Shiloh Hill received a major opportunity from John Cena, and he promised not to let the legend down. During the November 17, 2026, episode of WWE NXT, the company aired backstage footage of Cena personally inviting Hill to compete in the upcoming John Cena Classic.

Cena approached Hill backstage to present him with an official invitation for the December event. Hill expressed his gratitude for being personally selected, and Cena handed him the special John Cena Classic briefcase, a gesture he has repeated with other invited Superstars. Hill promised Cena that he would perform his best in the tournament.

Hill joins the list of NXT participants, which also includes Izzi Dame, who received her invitation during the same broadcast. Other confirmed names for the event are Cody Rhodes, Zilla Fatu, Noam Dar, Romeo Moreno, Kelani Jordan, Charlotte Flair, Jacy Jayne, Fallon Henley, Lainey Reid, and Liv Morgan. The John Cena Classic is scheduled for December 14, 2026, at the ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex near Orlando, Florida, featuring exhibition matches between main roster and NXT talent.
