Kevin Nash recalled a wild night in Japan where he nearly got Scott Hall into serious legal trouble by blowing into his court-ordered breathalyzer. Speaking on the Kliq This podcast, Nash explained that he was extremely drunk after a night out with Hall, a young Finn Bálor, Bryan Danielson and Mark Coleman, and decided to test his blood alcohol level on Hall's device, which was required due to a previous legal issue. Nash blew a .263, far above the legal limit, and had to call probation authorities himself to admit what he had done. Nash also recalled another chaotic night in Hamburg where Hall spotted a framed photo of himself in a bar and wanted it, leading Nash to smash the frame off the wall.
