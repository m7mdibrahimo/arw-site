A new Tables match has been added to the upcoming episode of TNA iMPACT following Bound for Glory. The show, which is being booked by Matt Hardy and Jeff Hardy, will feature Syx consisting of Joseph Sawyer and Samuel Shaw taking on The Righteous consisting of Vincent and Dutch. At Bound for Glory, Shaw and Sawyer secured a victory over The Hardys in a Falls Count Anywhere match with assistance from Erick Redbeard. Additionally, the card features Nic Nemeth facing Ryan Nemeth with the stipulation that if either competitor tries to throw the match, the Nemeth Brothers will relinquish the TNA World Tag Team Titles.
