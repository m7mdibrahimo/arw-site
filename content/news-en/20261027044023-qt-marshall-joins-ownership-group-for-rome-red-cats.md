QT Marshall has joined the ownership group of the Rome Red Cats, an indoor football team competing in the American Arena League. The organization officially announced that the AEW executive and professional wrestler, along with entrepreneur Tripp Bennett, have become partial owners of the franchise. Marshall and Bennett are already business partners through their One Fall Wrestling (1FW) promotion based in Georgia. Both men expressed their excitement about the opportunity to contribute to the growth of the team and strengthen its ties to the Rome community. Marshall noted that his positive experience bringing 1FW events to Rome inspired him to invest in the local organization. The new ownership partners intend to support existing leadership while exploring new avenues for fan engagement and community outreach.
