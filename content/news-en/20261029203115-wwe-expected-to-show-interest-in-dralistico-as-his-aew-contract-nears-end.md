WWE is reportedly interested in AEW talent Dralístico. According to WrestleVotes on Fightful Select, the company plans to pursue him once his availability becomes clear. His current contract with All Elite Wrestling is believed to run until December 2026. Dralístico is a former AAA World Tag Team Champion, having held the titles alongside his brother, current WWE wrestler Dragon Lee. The pair won the championships by defeating FTR on the night Dragon Lee announced his signing with WWE.
