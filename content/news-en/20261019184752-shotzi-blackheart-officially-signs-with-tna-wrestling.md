Shotzi Blackheart has officially signed with TNA Wrestling. The news was first reported by Fightful Select on October 14, and TNA subsequently confirmed the signing, noting it was initially reported by Busted Open on SiriusXM. Blackheart, a California native who made her professional wrestling debut in 2014, is a former NXT Women's Tag Team Champion alongside Ember Moon. She made appearances at TNA live events in Dothan, Alabama, in mid-October. TNA shared ten fun facts about Blackheart, including her lifelong love for riding motorcycles since the age of 18, her work as a paranormal investigator for her YouTube series CHAMBER OF HORRORS, her musical theatre major at AMDA in Hollywood, and her status as PWI's 2025 Independent Wrestler of the Year. The details also highlighted her personal milestones, such as getting married and wrestling in her wedding dress on the same day, alongside her proud Filipino heritage.
