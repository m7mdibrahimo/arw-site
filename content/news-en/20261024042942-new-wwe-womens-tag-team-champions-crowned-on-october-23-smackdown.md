The WWE Women's Tag Team Championship reign of Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley came to a sudden end during the October 23 episode of WWE SmackDown. The duo entered the show looking for their first successful title defense, but Michin and B-Fab walked out as the new champions following an intense matchup.

The bout began with B-Fab aggressively attacking Bliss before Paxley tagged in to regain control, even landing a moonsault on Michin outside the ring. Bliss returned to action and hit a DDT and blockbuster for a close two-count, but the champions lost momentum when Paxley became the legal competitor. After Paxley missed a 450 splash and failed to execute the Cemetery Drive, Jade Cargill secretly struck Paxley while the referee was distracted. The interference allowed Michin to deliver a Styles Clash and secure the pinfall victory.

This loss leaves Bliss and Paxley without the titles after failing in their very first defense. The unexpected outcome also raises serious questions regarding why Cargill decided to interfere and assist Michin and B-Fab in securing the championship gold.
