TNA Wrestling set the lineup for the December 3 episode of Thursday Night iMPACT, as the November 26 broadcast serves as a Thanksgiving special featuring unseen matches. The upcoming show features a title defense and the return of a signature multi-man stipulation.

Jason Hotch defends the TNA International Championship against Fabian Aichner. The event also marks the return of the Triple Threat Revolver to decide the next challenger for Leon Slater's TNA World Championship. The bout begins with three competitors under standard Triple Threat rules. Once an elimination occurs via pinfall or submission, a new wrestler enters based on their drawn entry number between 4 and 8. The cycle continues until all entrants have appeared, with the final fall deciding the winner.

Matt Hardy and Moose are the only confirmed entrants for the Triple Threat Revolver match so far, with additional participants set to be revealed.
