Kit Wilson has discussed how WWE Main Event helped him transition to a singles career following an injury to his former tag team partner, Elton Prince. In an interview with The Sportster, Wilson talked about the show's move to the Rumble platform and why he considers it an underrated program. "Look, as ever, WWE is expanding and growing, and that is now going onto a new platform, which is Rumble. So, Main Event every single week — and Main Event's always the little sleeper hit of a show; you know, it's not got the main stories that maybe Raw and SmackDown do, but it's got the superstars that you want to see, that you don't always see, attracting, fighting with, going against superstars that you want to see them go against. It's exciting stuff," he said. Wilson explained that Main Event gave him a platform to find himself as a singles competitor after partnering with Elton Prince, allowing him to face top-tier talent like Sheamus, Damian Priest, and Shinsuke Nakamura. He noted that the show also gave him the freedom to test out new ring gear and a fresh look away from the main roster shows.
