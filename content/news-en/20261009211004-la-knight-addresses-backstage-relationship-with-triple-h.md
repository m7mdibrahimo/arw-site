LA Knight has finally spoken about his professional relationship with Triple H following months of rumors regarding potential tension between them. His recent comments refute narratives previously circulated by others, specifically claims from former WWE writer Vince Russo that Triple H disliked Knight and mocked him backstage.

During an interview with Metro UK, Knight recalled a backstage conversation with Triple H that took place after Money in the Bank 2025, which was featured on WWE Unreal. Knight confirmed he listened closely to the feedback provided by Triple H, even though he felt the match had proceeded exactly as intended.

> "I’m just sitting there listening to it, like, ‘Okay, yeah, all right.’ Obviously I’m gonna listen to what’s coming at me. I’m thinking to myself, ‘Well, everything went off pretty much without a hitch, as far as anybody can tell, as far as even I could tell.’”

Knight noted that his relationship with Triple H is in a good place. He also explained that he and the WWE creative team have reached a mutual understanding regarding his performance style. He prefers the freedom to connect with the audience naturally rather than following a strictly scripted plan.

> "We’ve kinda gotten that figured out between me and creative, where it’s like, I’m not the type that wants to have everything mapped out from A to Z. I don’t want the audience to have to think as much. I want them to feel."
