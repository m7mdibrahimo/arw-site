Charlotte Flair has provided an update on her upcoming mental health podcast. Speaking with Going Ringside, Flair explained that she invested in a nonprofit organization while out with an injury, which inspired her to create a show addressing the pressures faced by athletes and high-level professionals.

Flair noted that she has already filmed eight episodes and is currently awaiting an official launch date.

> "To be honest, I didn't wake up and go, 'Oh man, I want to have a podcast.' But, when I was out injured, I took the opportunity to invest in a nonprofit organization, mental health is something very dear and important to my heart," Flair said.
