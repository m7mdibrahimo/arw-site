Jake Roberts recently revealed which current and former WWE stars remind him of himself during an interview with Nick Teodoro. When asked about wrestlers today who share traits with him, Roberts named Bray Wyatt and Randy Orton. "Bray Wyatt did," he said. "Randy Orton does. That's about it. They're different. Different. Randy's got a lot [of similarities]." This is not the first time Roberts has discussed Wyatt and Orton. In a 2017 interview with Sports Illustrated, he expressed concerns about WWE's creative direction for Wyatt, questioning why the promotion allowed fans to cheer for a heel character. Given how central character work was to his own career, Roberts struggled to understand fan support for a villain. He also noted that he previously attempted to reach out to WWE to work with Orton and help him refine his character, but management never responded, leaving him with lingering resentment. Roberts has since made a return to WWE in a limited capacity, appearing primarily at fan conventions while clarifying that he remains uncontracted.
