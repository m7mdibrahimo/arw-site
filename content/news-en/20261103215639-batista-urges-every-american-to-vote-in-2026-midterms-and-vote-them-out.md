Batista shared his political stance once again as Election Day 2026 arrived. The WWE legend cast his ballot in the midterms and immediately delivered a clear message to his followers on November 3 by writing on Instagram, "Vote! #votethemout 🇺🇸 I voted - please join me! Text VOTE to 63033 to find your polling place, early voting site, or drop box And finally, once you've voted, text 3 friends who might not have a plan to vote! 🙏🏼"

This message builds on his history of vocal criticism directed at Donald Trump, which included supporting Kamala Harris during the 2024 presidential election and mocking Trump's tough-guy persona. While he previously delivered a memorable rant on Jimmy Kimmel Live two years ago, Batista kept his message brief this time around and let the hashtag do the talking as Americans headed to the polls.
