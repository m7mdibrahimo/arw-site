Andre Fili is interested in a professional wrestling career after his time in MMA comes to an end. Following his knockout victory over Kai Kamaka III in the second round at UFC Fight Night on October 10th, Fili revealed on The Ariel Helwani Show that he plans to use steroids to build his physique for the squared circle. Fili stated, "When I'm done with my MMA career, I'm gonna get on a healthy amount of steroids, I'm gonna get big as f*ck and then you might see me in a pro wrestling ring. In some tights and some boots." Fili has competed professionally since 2009 and has been fighting in the UFC since 2013.
