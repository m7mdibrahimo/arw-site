Tony Khan has shared details about his recent conversations with Skydance CEO David Ellison following the company's acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery. Speaking during the AEW WrestleDream media call, Khan noted that Ellison is very excited and positive about AEW and believes in the promotion's future.

Khan acknowledged that changes are expected as AEW moves forward under the new corporate structure, but he remains confident. He specifically highlighted the continued presence of supporters like Channing Dungey and JB Perrette, alongside AEW's strong viewership and pay-per-view performance, as key reasons for optimism.
