Eva Marie admitted that she considered the possibility of joining another wrestling promotion after her departure from WWE, though she noted that any potential deal would have required a significant offer. Speaking on TMZ's Inside the Ring, Eva Marie explained that while she initially thought about exploring other options, her deep history with WWE made the idea feel complicated.

> "It was initially at the very beginning, and I kinda, like, thought about it. But it's also like, it would have to be done so good and not trying to recreate Eva Marie. And I feel — WWE was my home. So if I were to have to do something like that, the offer would have to be incredibly good. There would be a lot of zeros, and I would have to have some type of creative control."

Beyond financial compensation, Eva Marie emphasized that creative control was a non-negotiable requirement. She noted that her time in WWE taught her the value of having input into character development, as wrestlers often rely on writers to ensure their stories remain engaging and impactful. 

> "'Cause that's what I have learned in doing the business — it helps a lot if you have a lot more input, because a lot of us in WWE don't. You're basically hoping that the writers help you to the fullest and make the storyline that is juicy and good, and you get a payoff. And if you don't get that, it kinda sucks for your character."

Ultimately, Eva Marie did not pursue any serious negotiations with other promotions. She made it clear that while she is not ruling out wrestling outside of WWE entirely, any future suitor would need to meet her specific demands regarding compensation and creative freedom.
