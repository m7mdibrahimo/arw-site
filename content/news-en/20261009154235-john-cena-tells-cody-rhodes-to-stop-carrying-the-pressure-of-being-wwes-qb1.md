John Cena recently offered some advice to Cody Rhodes regarding the pressure of being the face of WWE. During an appearance on Busted Open Radio, Cena explained that Cody should stop worrying about labels like QB1 or being the company's top guy. Cena noted that he always viewed WWE as a team effort rather than a company built around one person, adding that becoming the top star is simply a byproduct of doing good work. He also recalled going thirteen years between marquee WrestleMania main events against The Rock and Cody Rhodes without ever feeling frustrated, emphasizing that his main goal was always just to do his best work regardless of his spot on the card.
