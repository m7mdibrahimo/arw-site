Matt Hardy recently explained why TNA is a better fit for him and Jeff Hardy compared to their previous run in AEW. Speaking on his podcast, Matt noted that they faced frustrations in AEW and lost momentum following Jeff's 2022 arrest and suspension, leading to their departure in June 2024. However, he emphasized that TNA has rejuvenated their love for wrestling. While Matt admitted they may never fully bounce back from their lost momentum in AEW, he refused to rule out a future return, citing the wrestling adage to never say never. He also praised AEW for raising the bar in the industry when WWE became stale. Looking toward the future, Matt discussed retirement plans, noting that Jeff is currently focusing on his music career while he plans to transition into a backstage role once his in-ring days conclude.
