WWE NXT talent Sean Legacy is crediting CM Punk for helping him improve his physique. Legacy shared on Instagram that a conversation with Punk at a live event several months ago provided the motivation he needed to change his habits.

> "A few months ago, I had a conversation with @cmpunk at a live event about his diet and workouts. I’ve looked up to him for years, but that conversation hit at a time when I really needed it. It made me want to demand more of myself and follow through."

Legacy noted that he has since been eating cleaner, training harder, and focusing on his conditioning. He emphasized that the transformation has been as much mental as it has been physical, stating that he feels more focused and confident than ever before.
