KC Navarro has earned a future opportunity at the TNA X-Division Championship after emerging victorious in a six-man Ultimate X match at TNA Destination X on Sunday, November 15, 2026. The contest featured AJ Francis, Cedric Alexander, John Skyler, Joe Alonzo, and TJP.

Travel issues impacting the event forced significant lineup changes, resulting in the removal of Rich Swann and BDE, while AJ Francis and TJP were added to the match. Once the action started, the competitors initially targeted Francis. As the match progressed, Alexander, Alonzo, and Skyler attempted to climb the structure, but were thwarted by Francis. TJP later sprayed red mist into Navarro's eyes during the chaos.

In the closing moments, Navarro successfully leaped onto the cables and unhooked the X despite resistance from Francis to secure the win.
