Goldberg has achieved nearly everything in professional wrestling, but his WWE career is not what matters most to him when thinking about his legacy. In an appearance on NewsNation's BIFF podcast, the WWE Hall of Famer discussed his life, his son Gage, and his perspective on aging. When asked about his own death and how he wants to be remembered, Goldberg emphasized that his in-ring accomplishments are secondary to his role as a father and husband.

> "The most important title that I have is father and husband. Goldberg is way down the road, but I was a father that cared more about his family than himself and would do anything and everything not only to protect them, but to arm them for what's to come."

Goldberg noted that while he has spent decades building a reputation as a dominant performer, his true priority is his family. He explained that he wants his son to know he will always be there for him, regardless of life's challenges. Reflecting on his own mortality, Goldberg stated that he hopes to be remembered as a good man who positively affected as many people as possible.

> "We're all going to expire here soon. We can't outrun death. You just want to make sure that once you look back on it that you did everything that you can and that you've positively affected as many people as humanly possible, but most importantly, you're a good father and a good husband."
