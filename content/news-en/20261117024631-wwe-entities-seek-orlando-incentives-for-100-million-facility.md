WWE is exploring the development of a new regional headquarters and performance center. According to the Orlando Business Journal, organizations affiliated with the promotion are requesting financial incentives from Orlando to construct a $100 million facility on vacant land near Inter.co Stadium. The city's community redevelopment agency advisory board is scheduled to vote on the proposal on November 18, with a subsequent review by the city council for final authorization.

City records identify the developer as WWE-Orlando Sports PC LLC, a partnership involving Orlando Performance Center SPE LLC and WWE PC Holdings LLC. The proposed site is a 98,000-square-foot structure located at 22 S. Terry Ave. Orlando, Stamford, and Las Vegas were the cities evaluated for this project.

Regarding the investment, an Orlando City spokesperson stated:

> "We are grateful to WWE for considering downtown Orlando for this investment. This project would advance the city’s continued efforts to revitalize downtown by attracting employers and high-wage jobs, bringing more visitors to experience downtown’s amenities, restaurants and local businesses while further establishing Orlando as a premier sports and entertainment destination."

The facility will feature training areas, office space, a fan zone, and an event studio. WWE P.C. Operating Company LLC is slated to operate the site under a 30-year lease. The agreement mandates at least 24 public events per year, local business participation in construction, and various community development initiatives.
