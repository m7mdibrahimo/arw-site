Game Changer Wrestling held the second night of its GCW Fight Club event on October 11 at The Showboat in Atlantic City, New Jersey. The event was broadcast on Triller TV+. 

The results for GCW Fight Club Night Two are as follows:

Jeffrey John def. Dustin Waller, Sal Mistretta, Kavron Kanyon, Devious Cass, and Hunter Drake in a Scramble Match.

Charles Mason def. Kylie Alexa.

Cowboy Way (1 Called Manders and Thomas Shire) def. Mane Event (Jay Lyon and Midas Black), Controlled Kaos (Kid Chaos and Papa Juice), and VNDL48 (Otis Cogar and Christian Napier).

Marcus Mathers def. Terry Yaki.

Marko Stunt def. Joey Janela.

During the event, Joseph Sawyer appeared via video to announce that he will defend the GCW Title against Atticus Cogar on November 7.

SLADE def. Eddy Only in a Deathmatch.

Vipress def. Mr. Danger, Anakin Murphy, Jamesen Shook, and Nick Gage in a Deathmatch.
