Sheamus has been the subject of speculation regarding a potential appearance at AEW Full Gear tonight. However, an update from Fightful Select indicates that Sheamus has not been spotted backstage at the event in Phoenix, Arizona. While this does not definitively rule out a surprise, there is currently no evidence suggesting he is involved in the show.

Speculation grew after Sheamus left his scheduled Big Event NY appearance early. Dave Meltzer previously reported that the former WWE Champion left due to a wedding, though fans continued to wonder about his status. Sheamus has been a free agent since his WWE contract expired in early October.

While stars like Claudio Castagnoli and Adam Copeland have expressed interest in him joining AEW, recent reports indicated that no deal was imminent. Sheamus recently told fans at a meet-and-greet that he is enjoying his time off and is not in any rush to sign with a new company, noting that his wife is currently making his career decisions. He has confirmed he is not retired and is simply taking an extended break, with TNA also reportedly interested in his services.
