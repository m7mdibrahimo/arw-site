Steve Maclin opened up about how much his wife, AEW star Deonna Purrazzo, means to him in a heartfelt post.

The former TNA World Champion celebrated their fourth wedding anniversary with a black-and-white photo from their wedding day, thanking her for her constant support.

In a deeply personal statement, Maclin wrote:
> "I know you hate when I say it, but the truth is, I wouldn’t be alive today if it wasn’t for you."

The couple wed on November 10, 2022, on a date that also marks the birthday of the United States Marine Corps.
