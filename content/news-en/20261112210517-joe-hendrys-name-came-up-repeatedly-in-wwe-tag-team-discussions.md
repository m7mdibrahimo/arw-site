WWE spent part of the summer of 2026 asking employees across various departments to brainstorm new, creative tag teams. The exercise was not limited to the creative team, as staff from multiple areas were encouraged to think outside the box and propose unusual pairings. According to WrestleVotes, there were no brand restrictions in place, and employees were specifically urged to avoid obvious combinations in an effort to strengthen the division. Joe Hendry’s name was mentioned numerous times throughout the process. While WrestleVotes did not reveal who the staff wanted to pair with Hendry, the frequency of his name suggests that people backstage see potential for an interesting tag team for him. The hosts noted that WWE has recently been open to experimenting with odd-couple pairings, such as Damian Priest and R-Truth. They also wondered whether Hendry's pairing with Danhausen came from similar thinking, though there is no confirmation that it did. The hosts also engaged in their own fantasy booking, with Joey suggesting LA Knight as a potential partner for Hendry.
