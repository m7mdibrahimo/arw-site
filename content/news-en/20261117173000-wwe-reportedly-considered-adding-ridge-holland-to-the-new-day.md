WWE had internal plans to add Ridge Holland to The New Day before the company ultimately canceled them. A recent edition of the "Wrestling Observer Newsletter" revealed that the idea was discussed when Kofi Kingston and Xavier Woods turned heel on Big E. Had the move gone through, Holland would have replaced Big E, the very wrestler he had previously injured in a match that resulted in Big E's retirement from in-ring competition. While The New Day never permanently added a new member, Grayson Waller did join Kingston and Woods during their final period in WWE. Holland's time with the promotion ended last November following a run in "NXT" after his move from the main roster. He was released after being asked to take a pay cut of over 50%, a request similar to the one that led The New Day to reject WWE's offer and depart for AEW. Holland, who previously recovered from neck surgery, returned to the ring this past July and has been competing for various independent promotions in the UK.
