WWE has no plans to utilize The Sphere in Las Vegas as a backup venue for WrestleMania 43. According to Fightful, the company is preparing to host its biggest annual event in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, in April 2027.

> "There is no indication that WWE is planning to use The Sphere in Las Vegas as a backup WrestleMania venue, despite recent speculation, @FightfulSelect has learned."

https://x.com/Fightful/status/2114869694557827365

WrestleMania 43 will mark the first time the event is held outside North America.
