WWE Main Event's transition to Rumble has achieved significant early success, with its premiere episode approaching half a million views. The October 14 broadcast, which aired on Rumble at 8:00 p.m. ET, reached approximately 498,000 views. The event featured matches between Sol Ruca and Lyra Valkyria, Dragon Lee and Austin Theory, Rey Fenix and Kit Wilson, as well as Nathan Frazer and Ricky Saints. Despite YouTube's massive subscriber base compared to Rumble, the new episode surpassed the viewership of WWE's final YouTube broadcast, which stands at around 380,000 views. The move follows WWE's new agreement to air Main Event on Rumble every Wednesday, placing it in a timeslot competing directly against AEW Dynamite and WWE's Evolve series on Tubi.
