Alexa Bliss recently shared the hilarious story of how her relationship with Ryan Cabrera began, revealing that he had no idea who she was when they first connected. During an appearance on Shot of Brandi with Brandi Rhodes, Bliss explained that Cabrera's social media managers kept interacting with her posts, leading people to assume they were dating. When The Miz reached out to Cabrera about the rumors, Cabrera famously asked if Alexa Bliss was a clothing brand. Although The Miz had to clarify that she was a WWE Superstar, it was actually the ongoing rumors that eventually prompted Bliss and Cabrera to start messaging each other. After discovering that Cabrera was traveling to Orlando for a show at Disney, where Bliss happened to live, the two finally met in person and eventually got married. While The Miz still tries to take full credit for bringing them together, Bliss noted that their actual connection blossomed through their own direct messages.
