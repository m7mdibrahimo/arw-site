WWE had planned for a showdown between Gunther and Demon Finn Balor at Crown Jewel, but the match was scrapped before the event. According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, WWE still intends to hold this match in the future. Meltzer reported that all the segments leading up to the bout had already been filmed, even though the match was never officially announced. The company has not abandoned the idea, as sources within WWE confirmed that the two will eventually face each other. Gunther’s decision not to travel to Saudi Arabia was the primary reason the match did not take place at Crown Jewel. Balor also had concerns about the trip, though it is believed he would have made the journey if Gunther had agreed to go. For now, the exact date and location for the match remain unknown.
