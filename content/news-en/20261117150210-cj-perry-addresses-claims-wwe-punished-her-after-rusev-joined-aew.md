CJ Perry is addressing claims that WWE punished her after her husband Rusev, known as Miro in AEW, joined the rival promotion in 2020. During a podcast appearance, she looked back at the period when she was repeatedly put through announce tables by Nia Jax with help from Shayna Baszler, which led fans to believe Vince McMahon was retaliating against her over her husband's departure.

Perry explained that those rumors were entirely false, emphasizing that she loved the storylines and grew excited every time she went through a table. She noted that she pushed for those opportunities and viewed them among her best experiences as a babyface, especially after fans rallied behind her all the way to her Survivor Series 2020 victory, prior to getting her revenge by defeating Nia Jax in a Tables Match on WWE RAW.
