The October 9 episode of WWE SmackDown serves as the final stop before the Money in the Bank Premium Live Event, featuring a stacked lineup to entertain fans. CM Punk is scheduled to make his return on the show after missing last week's episode, as he prepares to enter the men's Money in the Bank ladder match this weekend. In championship action, WWE Women's Tag Team Champions Fallon Henley and Lainey Reid of Fatal Influence will defend their titles against the team of Charlotte Flair and Tatum Paxley. The event takes place at the Cajundome in Lafayette, Louisiana, airing live at 8 PM ET on the USA Network and Netflix internationally.
