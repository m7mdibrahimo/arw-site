Vince Russo believes Ric Flair must prioritize his family following his recent health scares in Florida. Speaking on the Coach and Bro Show, Russo argued that the 77-year-old WWE Hall of Famer needs to consider the toll his actions take on his daughter, Charlotte Flair. Russo pointed out that with David Flair largely out of the spotlight and Reid Flair having passed away in 2013, Charlotte is the one who constantly faces public scrutiny regarding her father's behavior. "What he's gotta do is put his family first. I mean, it's that simple. He has to take into consideration what he is putting her through," Russo stated. Flair has experienced a difficult week in Tampa, including a fall on October 23 that led to a hospital stay for head and eye injuries. This occurred shortly after he revealed he was dealing with a leg blood clot, which forced him to cancel a Seattle appearance. Despite being released from the hospital on October 24, Flair returned to his usual hangout that same evening. Russo emphasized that Flair should be more conscious of his public choices, as Charlotte is likely the one left worrying about him around the clock.
