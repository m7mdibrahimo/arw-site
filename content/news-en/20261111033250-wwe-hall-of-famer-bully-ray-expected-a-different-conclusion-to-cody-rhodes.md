The intense rivalry between Cody Rhodes and Randy Orton concluded last weekend at WWE Crown Jewel, where Rhodes defeated Orton in a Last Man Standing match. However, WWE Hall of Famer Bully Ray believes the feud deserved a more significant stage for its finale. During an episode of "Busted Open Radio," Ray expressed that a blow-off match of this magnitude should have taken place at a major event rather than in Saudi Arabia. Ray suggested that Hell in a Cell would have been a more fitting environment for the final encounter between the two stars. He also proposed an alternative scenario based on Orton's history with Dusty Rhodes, suggesting a Texas Bullrope match to honor the 2007 feud that ended Dusty's WWE career.
