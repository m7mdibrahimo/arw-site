Johnny Gargano recently shared a fun behind-the-scenes detail about his family life with his fellow WWE Superstar wife, Candice LeRae. During an interview with TV Insider, Gargano revealed that their four-year-old son Quill is a huge fan of Randy Orton and frequently asks to FaceTime the WWE legend while his parents are at work. Gargano explained that when LeRae was recently on a video call with their son from a WWE event, Quill asked to speak with Orton, who happened to be sitting nearby. LeRae simply handed the phone to Orton, who greeted the young fan warmly.

Gargano admitted that the situation feels quite surreal, noting that while most children only watch WWE Superstars on television, his son gets to have direct conversations with them because of his parents' careers. He also mentioned that Quill is already talking about following in their footsteps and becoming a professional wrestler himself. For now, the four-year-old is simply enjoying the unique perks of having two WWE Superstars as parents.
