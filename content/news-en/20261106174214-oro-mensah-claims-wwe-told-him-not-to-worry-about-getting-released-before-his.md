Oro Mensah revealed that someone within WWE specifically told him not to worry about getting released shortly before he was let go. Speaking on the Haangout podcast, Mensah explained that he had a bad feeling as the usual post-WrestleMania release period approached, especially after being moved to EVOLVE. He decided to ask directly about his job security and was reassured that he was safe. However, he never truly believed the reassurance, noting that things started feeling strange again about a month later when Meta-Four was broken up on WWE NXT. Mensah was officially released in May 2025, receiving a brief 45-second phone call while standing inside Costco, which deeply hurt him after six and a half years with the company and a 15-year overall journey to reach WWE.
