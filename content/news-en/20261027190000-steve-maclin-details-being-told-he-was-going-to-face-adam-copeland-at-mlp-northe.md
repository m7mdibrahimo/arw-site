Steve Maclin recently opened up about his experience finding out he would face Adam Copeland in a Street Fight at MLP Northern Rising. The match served as the main event of the evening and saw Copeland emerge victorious over Maclin. In an interview with Sean Ross Sapp, Maclin revealed that he learned of the booking from his wife. He had originally been scheduled for a four-way Monster’s Ball match, but the plans changed after Copeland approached his wife with the idea. Maclin expressed his excitement, noting that as a lifelong fan of the Attitude Era, stepping into the ring with a legend like Copeland was a career-defining moment. He emphasized that the opportunity allowed him to prove his worth on a big stage, not just to the world, but to himself. Maclin also noted that he competed in the match just ten weeks after suffering a broken leg.
