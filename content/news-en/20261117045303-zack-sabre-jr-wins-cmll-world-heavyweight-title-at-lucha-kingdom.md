Zack Sabre Jr. captured the gold at the joint CMLL and New Japan Pro-Wrestling event, Lucha Kingdom, which took place at Arena México on November 16. In one of the marquee matches, Hechicero defended the CMLL World Heavyweight Championship against Zack Sabre Jr. Ultimately, Sabre Jr. managed to trap Hechicero in a pin to secure the victory and win the belt. This marks Sabre Jr.'s first-ever title win in CMLL. Meanwhile, in New Japan Pro-Wrestling, he is a two-time IWGP Heavyweight Champion.
