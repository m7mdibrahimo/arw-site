Nearly Average Wrestling announced NAW MERCY, a new arcade-style fighting game inspired by classics like Mortal Kombat. Actor and comedian Shane Hartline is expanding the comedy wrestling universe with a game developed in partnership with Leroy Patterson of TACKBOARD GAMES. Gameplay will feature up to 4-on-4 matches, a story mode, and independent superstars such as EFFY and Shane Haste. The game is currently in development, with an initial beta release exclusive to NAW Patreon subscribers before launching on Steam.
