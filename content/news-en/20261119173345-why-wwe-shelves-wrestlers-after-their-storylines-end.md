Sheamus recently opened up about his time away from television following his match with Rusev in Paris. Speaking on the Strength Required podcast, the former world champion explained that his absence was part of WWE's strategy to keep talent fresh once a program concludes.

Sheamus explained that the company follows an approach where performers are temporarily sidelined after wrapping up an angle.

> "Sometimes when the storyline ends, the character will be shelved for a little while to bring it back fresh."

During his time away, Sheamus dealt with lingering shoulder pain that made even basic gym workouts difficult. However, after about a month off, an unexpected injury to another wrestler prompted WWE to call him back sooner than planned. Determined not to lose momentum, Sheamus chose to work through the pain to protect his television spot.
