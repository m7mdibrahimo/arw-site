Jake Hager has shown an encouraging recovery following the severe eye injury he suffered at Power Slap 23 last month in Lubbock, Texas. After taking a devastating strike from Johnny Magna, Hager suffered a globe rupture and underwent emergency eye surgery. According to a recent update from Sinn Bodhi, Hager is now able to see out of his injured left eye. While the long-term outlook for his full vision remains uncertain and he may need additional surgeries, this progress marks a significant step forward for the former WWE star.
