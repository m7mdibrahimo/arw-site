EA Fight Night is set to return after a teaser photo was shared on Tuesday, though no further details were provided. The franchise's last title, Fight Night Champion, was released in 2011 before EA shifted its focus to the UFC. EA acquired the UFC license and subsequently launched five EA Sports UFC titles between 2014 and 2026. Across its history, the Fight Night series spans five games. Boxers like Tyson Fury and Anthony Joshua have appeared in the EA UFC series, and the two heavyweights are scheduled to clash in December in one of the most anticipated fights in decades. Meanwhile, EA is reportedly set to be sold to Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund, an entity deeply involved in Zuffa Boxing.
