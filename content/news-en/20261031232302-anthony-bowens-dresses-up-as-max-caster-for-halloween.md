Anthony Bowens had some fun on Halloween by dressing up as his former tag team partner, Max Caster. Bowens posted a video on X, formerly X, featuring himself wearing a fake mustache and holding a microphone to impersonate Caster. He began the video with Caster's signature catchphrase, "Yo, listen!" and had an associate chant, "Let's go Max, you're the best wrestler alive!" 

Bowens continued the bit by mimicking Caster's "five-minute open challenge" routine, repeatedly shouting for someone to start the clock. He also recreated a fan interaction by asking someone to back up so he could kiss their hand. The timing of the impression is notable given Caster's recent career struggles following his brief TNA run, which ended after his Bound for Glory rap referenced the Riyadh bombing. Bowens did not address the controversy, choosing instead to focus on the comedic tribute to their history as The Acclaimed.
