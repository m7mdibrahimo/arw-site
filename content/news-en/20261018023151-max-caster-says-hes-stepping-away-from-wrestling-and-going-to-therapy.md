Max Caster has announced that he is stepping away from the ring for an extended period and going to therapy following his recent departure from TNA Wrestling.

Caster made his debut at TNA Slammiversary by answering the open challenge issued by Moose. During his pre-match rap, he referenced the bombing in Riyadh, which led to TNA cutting ties with him the following day. Appearing at ACW, Caster addressed the situation, apologizing to the fans and expressing gratitude for their support. He stated that he does not know what his next move will be, but plans to focus on his well-being before returning to the ring.
