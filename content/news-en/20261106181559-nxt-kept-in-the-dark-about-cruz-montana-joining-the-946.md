Cruz Montana's move from NXT to help 946 attack Roman Reigns on RAW was kept secret even from the NXT brand. According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, Montana joining The 946 as their fifth member for WarGames against The Bloodline was a late decision that caught NXT officials off guard. Shawn Michaels noted that he rarely receives advance warning about main roster call-ups and must simply adjust. The sudden change disrupts NXT's plans, as Montana was slated to continue performing as a top babyface.
