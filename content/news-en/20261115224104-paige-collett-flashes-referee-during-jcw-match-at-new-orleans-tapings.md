Paige Collett found a creative way to distract a referee during a recent JCW taping in New Orleans. Collett jumped onto the ring apron, pulled down her top, and flashed the referee while a match was taking place.

She posted footage of the incident on Instagram, showing herself revealing nipple pasties on the apron. The referee immediately started arguing with her from inside the ring, while Kelis played in the background. Collett has a history of causing trouble at wrestling shows, including confronting Big Foot with a chainsaw in September.
