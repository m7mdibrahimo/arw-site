WWE talent scout Gabe Sapolsky is advising independent wrestlers to carefully consider their futures before signing any professional contracts. On November 10, 2026, Sapolsky shared seven key factors for talent to evaluate, emphasizing that being "signed" should never be the sole motivation for a deal. While he did not name a specific company, his comments followed recent online discourse regarding MLW agreements. Sapolsky urged wrestlers to investigate a promotion's specific plans for them and to assess whether a contract offers genuine career growth or upward mobility. He also highlighted the importance of understanding contract limitations and potential benefits, noting that wrestlers should trust their instincts. Sapolsky, who has extensive experience in ECW, ROH, Dragon Gate USA, and EVOLVE, currently works within WWE's talent development system. He concluded by acknowledging that his candid advice might draw criticism from those unfamiliar with the wrestling business. Sapolsky's core message remains that a contract is only as valuable as the plan behind it, and signing without a clear purpose could potentially hinder a wrestler's career.
