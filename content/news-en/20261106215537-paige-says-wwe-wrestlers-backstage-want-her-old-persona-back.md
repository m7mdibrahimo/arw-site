Paige is facing scrutiny regarding her return to WWE, with pressure coming from both fans and her peers. The former Divas Champion revealed that colleagues and backstage staff have been urging her to reclaim the persona that defined her stardom over a decade ago.

During an appearance on Busted Open Radio, Paige discussed her physical state following her recent bout against Kelani Jordan on WWE NXT. Tommy Dreamer commended her improved speed and conditioning, noting that she resembled the version of herself that first gained fame in NXT. Paige attributed her progress to eight years of sobriety and a strict focus on cardio and nutrition.

She acknowledged that her initial performances upon returning were not up to her own standards. Regarding the feedback she received, she stated:

> "And I've had fans and other wrestlers and backstage being like, 'We need the old page back. We need the old page.' And I think this kind of gave me the boot up the ass I needed today."

Paige believes her latest match was a vital step toward proving she can still compete at a high level.
