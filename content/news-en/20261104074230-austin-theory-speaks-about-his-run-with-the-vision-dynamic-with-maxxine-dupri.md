During a recent interview, WWE World Tag Team Champion Austin Theory opened up about his time with The Vision faction and his current dynamic with Maxxine Dupri. Theory discussed the group's struggles, noting that injuries and roster changes have consistently disrupted their momentum. He also reflected on how his perspective has shifted after returning from a hernia injury, as he now sees a new generation of rising stars taking center stage alongside veterans.

Theory expressed excitement about his pairing with Maxxine Dupri, pointing out that they share a similar background of lacking consistent opportunities and are eager to prove themselves. He stated, "Maxxine, someone that’s super talented, in the same kind of situation in a way of just not really seeing her get opportunities and then they kind of go away, and then you’re just hanging out, and I kind of saw myself in her in that situation." Theory and Bron Breakker are scheduled to defend the World Tag Team Titles against Je'Von Evans and Penta at WWE Crown Jewel on November 7th.
