TNA World Champion Leon Slater has made waves not only in TNA, but also through appearances in WWE due to the partnership between the two promotions. One of his most notable moments came on John Cena's retirement show, WWE Saturday Night's Main Event in Washington, DC, in December 2025, where he teamed with Je'Von Evans against AJ Styles and Dragon Lee.

Speaking with the Extreme Channel, according to Wrestling Inc., Slater recalled thinking he would fly back home to the UK during a break in his schedule. He was surprised when travel plans changed.

> "I was asking them, 'Oh, cool. I'll go home here then, yeah?'"

> "The travel lady at 'NXT' goes, 'No, you'll be in DC that weekend.' I go, 'Alright. Cool.' I didn't really think of it and I walked off, I was like, 'DC, like what's going on in DC this weekend?' Then I realized and I was like, 'Oh my God!'"

Slater described the experience as a dream come true, allowing him to meet his childhood heroes, and noted that the bout alongside Evans is the match he is most proud of. He also praised John Cena, mentioning that the star was among the first people he saw behind the curtain after the match.

> "For someone who has so much on his plate between the Hollywood stuff and all his other interests and his involvement in WWE, he's so giving with his time."

> "That was my biggest takeaway from meeting John... I saw him in the hallway earlier in the day and I said, obviously, like, 'So nice to meet you, sir, thank you for having me,' and he's like, 'No, no. Thank you for being here...' He was just a real standup guy and he couldn't have been more giving with his time and he couldn't have been more generous with his feedback and his critiques after the match."

Slater has enjoyed a stellar run in 2026, which peaked when he defeated Nic Nemeth to capture the company's top championship in the headliner of TNA Bound for Glory.
