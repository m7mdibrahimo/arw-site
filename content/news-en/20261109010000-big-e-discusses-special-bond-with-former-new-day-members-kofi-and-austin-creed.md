The New Day was a fan-favorite stable for years in WWE. While fans enjoyed watching them onscreen, the three men were also close friends behind the scenes. Austin Creed and Kofi are no longer with WWE, as they now compete in AEW, while Big E remains under contract with WWE as an occasional broadcaster. Despite this, Big E says their friendship has not waned.

Big E recently discussed his bond with Creed and Kofi during an appearance on the "HaaangEmHiigh" podcast. "Those two were my groomsmen, just a couple weeks ago. Those guys are family," he stated. Big E explained that he does not have any blood-related brothers, so Creed and Kofi filled that role for him. The trio first became close while working live events together before their onscreen debut as The New Day.

"We just built such a special bond, and man, I don't think people truly understand how much time and — the schedule's definitely changed, but at that time, we spent so much time around each other. We pretty much spent every waking moment that we were on the road [together], five days a week," he recalled. Their communication continued even when they were home. "One of the greatest honors of my career and my life was getting to be next to two guys, to two men, that I have so much respect for."

Big E also praised Austin Creed for his work in the video game community, noting that he often brought talent from opposing promotions together, a spirit he also brought to the WWE locker room. "There are so many people who got to have incredible experiences, who got to make thousands, if not tens of thousands of dollars, playing video games, having fun! He brought such a spirit to the locker room," Big E said. He also spoke highly of Kofi, calling him one of the best human beings you will ever meet.

Reflecting on the final segment Big E had with The New Day, which saw Creed and Kofi turn on him and kick him out of the group, he acknowledged the impact it had on fans. "As much as it hurt people to see us break up, I think just getting to do something that just hit people in the chest? I had so many grown men come up to me and say, 'Look, I know it's pro wrestling, but you broke my heart; y'all broke my heart!'" Big E concluded by reiterating his deep respect for them both as wrestlers and as human beings.
