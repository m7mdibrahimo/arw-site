D-Von Dudley opened up about feeling overlooked by WWE during their Black History Month celebrations. During an episode of D-Von and The Duke, the WWE Hall of Famer said he was frequently left off promotional posters and out of recognitions, even while other African-American talent were featured. Dudley recalled seeing other Black wrestlers highlighted while his own name was never brought up.

His perspective shifted after hearing directly from fans at comic conventions and in public, who told him how much the Dudley Boyz meant to them growing up. Younger Black wrestlers also approached him to share how his Attitude Era run inspired them to pursue careers in the business. Hearing those stories gave Dudley peace of mind, knowing his contributions had a real impact despite WWE's omissions.
