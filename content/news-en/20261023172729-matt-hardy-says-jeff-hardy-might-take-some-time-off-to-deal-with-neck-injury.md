Matt Hardy provided an update on Jeff Hardy's health status during a recent podcast appearance. Jeff has been dealing with a persistent neck injury since October 2025, when The Hardys faced DarkState at NXT Halloween Havoc.

Speaking to Jon Alba, Matt Hardy revealed that his brother might soon step away from the ring to properly address the injury. Matt stated that Jeff has done everything possible to manage the issue, but now needs time to fix the bulging disc that has been bothering him for a year.
