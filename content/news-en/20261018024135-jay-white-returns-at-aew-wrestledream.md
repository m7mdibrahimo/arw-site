Jay White has made his unexpected return to television at AEW WrestleDream Tailgate Brawl. White teamed up with The Gunns and The Conglomeration to face the Death Riders and The Dogs in a multi-man tag team match. He had previously been sidelined with an injury sustained at AEW All Out, with an initial recovery timeline of one to two months. The Death Riders and The Dogs secured the victory in this encounter.
