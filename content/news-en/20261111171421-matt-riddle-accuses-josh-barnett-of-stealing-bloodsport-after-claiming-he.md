Matt Riddle is publicly calling out Josh Barnett for taking credit for the creation of Bloodsport. Riddle, a former WWE Superstar, claims the concept, rules, and name of the event were entirely his idea from the beginning, and he is frustrated by the narrative that Barnett was the one who started it. On November 11, 2026, Riddle posted a promotional graphic for the original 2018 event on Instagram to clarify his role. "I’m very happy and proud of where blood sport went after I got signed to WWE but the rules, the idea and even the name all came from me. It was my show and my vision and WWN and GCW made it possible along with all the talented wrestlers that participated," Riddle stated. He further accused Barnett of hijacking the project after he moved to WWE, asserting, "One thing I can say with 100% certainty is Josh Barnett did not create or even help with the original bloodsport and then tried to take it for himself." Riddle also revealed that he confronted Barnett during WrestleMania week earlier this year after Barnett suggested there would be no more events. Riddle told him, "that’s not your choice, you don’t own blood sport." Despite the public accusation, Riddle noted that their conversation was not malicious, though he remains firm that the event was his creation.
