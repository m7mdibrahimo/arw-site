WWE NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan recently discussed the choices that led to her success in NXT, noting that dropping her easygoing attitude helped her reach the next level. During an interview on Casual Conversations, Jordan explained how developing a killer instinct and speaking up for herself earned her the NXT Women's North American Championship and eventually the NXT Women's Championship. Jordan admitted that it was frustrating not getting a title shot earlier, stating that she realized waiting in line patiently only gets you passed by. Despite her previous experience, she did not receive a televised NXT title shot until NXT Heatwave in August 2026, where she challenged Kendal Grey and secured the title with a split-legged moonsault. Jordan also credited WWE's partner promotion TNA Wrestling with boosting her microphone confidence. Because TNA filmed multiple shows in a single day, she often had to speak on the fly without overthinking, which helped her find a more natural tone that she has now brought over to NXT. Jordan made her TNA debut in September 2025 at Victory Road, where she won the vacant TNA Knockouts World Championship by defeating Lei Ying Lee. Her two-month title reign included successful defenses against Jordynne Grace, Indi Hartwell, Ash By Elegance, and Heather By Elegance.
