WWE star Je'Von Evans wants to reunite with Oba Femi and Trick Williams inside the ring on the main roster. The three performers previously shared the spotlight in WWE NXT, including headlining the Stand & Deliver event. While their interactions have been few since moving up, Evans is confident their paths will cross again in major storylines.

Speaking on the No-Contest Wrestling podcast, Evans revealed his dream scenario for when they meet again. He envisions a huge moment at WrestleMania with top titles involved.

> "Bro, I can't wait for us to take that same picture, and be in the same exact spots, and it's, like, WrestleMania. Probably with the WWE Championship or the World Heavyweight Championship on the line. But no bro, it's great bro. It's insane just to be able to say that I'm in the ring with, you know...actually, it's not even insane because Trick worked hard for that, you know what I mean? So it's great to be able to say that I'm in the ring with Trick Willie again, you feel me?? And if Oba was in the match, I would've been like 'Yo, it's great to say that the big three is in the match,' you feel me? But we going to get there. We going to get there."

Evans also imagined entering that situation with significant leverage of his own, having Williams as champion on one brand and Femi on another.

> "I feel like there's going to be a point where maybe, like, Trick has the WWE Championship, then Oba has, like, the World Heavyweight Championship, and I have the Money in the Bank again."
