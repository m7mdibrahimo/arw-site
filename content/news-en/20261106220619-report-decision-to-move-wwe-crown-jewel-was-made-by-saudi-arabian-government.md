Despite the ongoing conflict involving the United States, Israel, and Iran, WWE is moving forward with its scheduled events in Riyadh. WWE Crown Jewel will take place tomorrow at the ANB Arena, also known as The Venue. The event was originally slated for the Riyadh Season Stadium, but the location was changed on October 28. According to the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, the decision to relocate the show was made by the Saudi Arabian government rather than TKO, WWE's parent company. The move to the ANB Arena, which has a capacity of 6,000, is intended to demonstrate that major events can still proceed despite the region's security situation. Crown Jewel features four matches, including Roman Reigns defending the World Heavyweight Championship against Undisputed Champion Sami Zayn, and Women's World Champion Liv Morgan facing Chelsea Green. Additionally, World Tag Team Champions Bron Breakker and Austin Theory will defend their titles against Je'Von Evans and Penta, while Cody Rhodes and Randy Orton conclude their trilogy in a Last Man Standing match.
