WWE star Ethan Page recently found himself involved in an awkward confrontation with wrestling influencer Heel Girlfriend. The incident occurred on October 25 when Heel Girlfriend questioned his presence at the event, leading to a tense exchange where Page explained he was invited to create content. Feeling embarrassed by the public shouting, Page decided to walk away from the situation. The moment comes shortly after Page criticized modern wrestlers on Inside the Ring for engaging in unnecessary drama with fans online, arguing that today's talent is much softer and should focus on supporters rather than critics.
