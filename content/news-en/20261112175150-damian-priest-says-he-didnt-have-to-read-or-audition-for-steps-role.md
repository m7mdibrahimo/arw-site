Damian Priest did not have to audition for his role in the upcoming Netflix animated film Steps. Speaking with Complex Graps, Priest explained that Netflix contacted WWE directly to request him for the voice role, meaning he only had to give a yes or no answer. Since WWE approved and his schedule was not disrupted, the process moved forward smoothly and quickly. Steps is set to premiere on Netflix on Friday, November 20, and features a voice cast that includes Amanda Seyfried, Peter Dinklage, and Daniel Radcliffe.
