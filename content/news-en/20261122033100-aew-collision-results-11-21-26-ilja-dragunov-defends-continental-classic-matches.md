Tonight’s episode of AEW Collision on November 21, 2026, aired live from the Resch Center in Green Bay, Wisconsin. In the main event, The Cut’s "Switchblade" Jay White defeated The Demand’s Ricochet in an AEW Continental Classic Blue League Match. In the championship match, AEW National Champion "The Mad Dragon" Ilja Dragunov successfully defended his title against Don Callis Family’s El Clón.

Other Continental Classic matches saw Don Callis Family’s "The Jet" Kevin Knight defeat "The Psycho Killer" Tommaso Ciampa, and Brodido’s Brody King defeat The Death Riders’ Daniel Garcia by referee stoppage. In tag team action, Divine Dominion defeated Viva Van and VertVixen, while The Death Riders defeated Shane Taylor Promotions in an 8-person mixed tag team match. Additionally, “The Alpha” Konosuke Takeshita defeated Trent Beretta, and AZM defeated Laynie Luck.
