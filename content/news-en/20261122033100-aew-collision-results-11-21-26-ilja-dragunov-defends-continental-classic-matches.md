Tonight’s episode of AEW Collision, airing November 21, 2026, took place live from the Resch Center in Green Bay, Wisconsin. The show featured AEW National Champion "The Mad Dragon" Ilja Dragunov defending his title against Don Callis Family’s El Clón. Plus, The Demand’s Ricochet faced The Cut’s "Switchblade" Jay White in an AEW Continental Classic Blue League Match.

Tommaso Ciampa defeated Kevin Knight in an AEW Continental Classic Gold League Match via roll-up after a hard-fought battle.

Divine Dominion defeated Viva Van and VertVixen in a tag team match.

Brody King defeated Daniel Garcia in an AEW Continental Classic Blue League Match via referee stoppage after Garcia suffered an arm injury.

Ilja Dragunov defeated El Clón to retain the AEW National Championship.

Konosuke Takeshita defeated Trent Beretta in a singles match.

AZM defeated Laynie Luck in a singles match.

The Death Riders defeated Shane Taylor Promotions in an 8-person mixed tag team match.

Ricochet faced Jay White in the main event of the night in an AEW Continental Classic Blue League Match.
