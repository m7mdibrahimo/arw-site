Kevin Owens recently explained his decision to return to the ring for WWE NXT after nearly seven years away from the brand. Owens wanted the opportunity to work with the young talent shaping the company's future, such as Tony D'Angelo, Kendal Grey, and Shiloh Hill. Furthermore, he admitted that he could not say no when veteran wrestler and WWE Performance Center coach Steve Corino personally asked him to make the appearance at the October 17 live event in Gatineau, Quebec. Despite dealing with a tough travel schedule and physical issues involving his knee, neck, and shoulder, Owens made the trip to support Corino, a mentor who took him under his wing back in 2004. Owens last wrestled for NXT at TakeOver: WarGames in November 2019, but this latest return was focused entirely on supporting the next generation and honoring a long-standing friendship.
