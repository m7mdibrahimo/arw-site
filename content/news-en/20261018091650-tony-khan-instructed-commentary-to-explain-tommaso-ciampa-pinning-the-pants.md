AEW President Tony Khan addressed a humorous spot from the AEW WrestleDream event where Tommaso Ciampa attacked and pinned Andrade El Idolo's pants during their AEW National Championship match. Speaking at the post-show press conference, Khan explained that he instructed the commentary team to clarify the moment as psychological warfare rather than a legitimate pin attempt.

Khan compared Ciampa's actions to the mind games often utilized by Orange Cassidy, noting that Andrade also frequently plays mind games by taking selfies during matches. Khan stated that Ciampa was attempting to turn those mind games back on his opponent, emphasizing that while AEW allows room for fun, commentary needed to frame it within the context of a serious athletic contest.
