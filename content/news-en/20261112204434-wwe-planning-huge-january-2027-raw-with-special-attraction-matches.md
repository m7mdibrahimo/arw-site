WWE is already preparing for a big start to 2027. The company is planning to make the January 4 episode of Monday Night RAW a major event, potentially featuring special attraction matches to ensure it feels significant. According to WrestleVotes Radio, WWE creative is discussing plans for this episode, which will be held at the Frost Bank Center in San Antonio, Texas. The company aims to use the show to celebrate the start of RAW's third year on Netflix. WWE expects to give the episode a significant promotional push throughout December and the holiday season to help kick off the new year with one of the biggest editions of RAW on the calendar. While specific matches have not yet been announced, the company is looking into special attractions to make the night stand out. WWE has a history of big moments on January 4, such as Bret Hart's return to the company in 2010. While NJPW's Wrestle Kingdom also takes place on that date, it is unclear if WWE's plans are related to that event. WWE clearly intends to start 2027 with momentum as the Royal Rumble and WrestleMania season approach.
