TNA World Champion Leon Slater has rejected the notion that wrestlers use TNA merely as a stepping stone to reach larger promotions. While some have historically viewed the company as a platform to move elsewhere, Slater maintains a different perspective.

Slater stated that his opportunities within WWE helped him improve his skills and contribute to TNA's growth rather than simply serving as a path to a WWE contract. He noted that the lessons he gained during his time in NXT and on the main roster were invaluable to his current success.

He added that the partnership between TNA and WWE benefits both sides, as young stars in NXT learn from experienced TNA wrestlers like The Hardys and Nic Nemeth.
