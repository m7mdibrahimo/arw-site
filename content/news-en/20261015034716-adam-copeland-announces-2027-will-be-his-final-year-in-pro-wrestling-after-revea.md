Adam Copeland revealed during the October 14, 2026 episode of AEW Dynamite/Collision that 2027 will be his final year in professional wrestling. The announcement followed his victory over Josh Alexander in a WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match. Copeland disclosed that he suffered three broken ribs and a collapsed lung within the first five minutes of his Tables, Ladders and Chairs match against Christian Cage at AEW All Out. Despite the severity of these injuries, he continued to compete. Copeland stated that with his career coming to an end, he is now focused on winning the AEW World Championship. His victory over Alexander has positioned him closer to that goal as he prepares for his final chapter in the ring.
