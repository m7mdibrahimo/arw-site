Sheamus confirmed he has not retired from wrestling despite his departure from WWE and his current status as a free agent. During a recent interview, he clarified that he is simply enjoying a long vacation rather than being a former wrestler as some have suggested. His contract with WWE concluded at the beginning of October, making him one of the most prominent free agents available.

> "Did you say ex-luchador? I'm just taking time off. I'm just chilling, I'm relaxing, I'm on vacation — a long vacation."

His schedule has sparked speculation, as his appearance at Big Event NY on November 14 coincides with AEW Full Gear. Reports indicate he inquired about leaving the convention early to attend a wedding. While AEW and TNA have reportedly shown interest in him, there is no confirmation he has signed with any new promotion.
