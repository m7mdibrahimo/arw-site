Alexa Bliss revealed that her battle with anorexia took a dangerous turn during college after a cheerleading coach pressured her to lose weight to make the national championship team. Speaking on What's Your Story? With Steph McMahon, Bliss explained that she had previously recovered from anorexia, but the program's mandatory body-fat testing, public postings, and strict gym check-ins revived her old eating disorder. When she told the coach about her past struggles, she was told that reaching a specific weight would secure her spot as the only freshman on the national championship squad.

Bliss began losing weight rapidly due to her competitive drive. Although the coach eventually grew concerned and tried to encourage her to eat, Bliss soon lost control. She ultimately left college while still wearing her uniform, drove home, and admitted herself to the same hospital that had treated her as a teenager. Her month-long stay focused on the psychological reasons behind her eating disorder rather than just weight restoration, helping her eventually recover before moving into bodybuilding and her WWE career.
