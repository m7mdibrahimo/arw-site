Sami Zayn concluded this week's WWE Raw with an ambush on Roman Reigns, delivering a strong message to the WWE World Heavyweight Champion afterward. While heading to the back, Zayn fired up the crowd and addressed the trailing camera, questioning if Reigns was paying attention following his earlier comments.

> "You paying attention now? Do I have your attention now? They think this is a game. You think this is a game. This is my life. This is my whole life."

Zayn yelled angrily before walking away, looking mentally exhausted. The two opened the show with a verbal exchange where Zayn demanded the respect he deserved ahead of their upcoming match at Crown Jewel this weekend, but Reigns refused and told him and the audience to acknowledge him.

Late in the show, Reigns was ambushed by the 946 and their new member, Cruz Montana. Once the group left the ring after attacking The Bloodline, Zayn suddenly appeared and struck a Helluva Kick to close the broadcast, building toward their clash for the Crown Jewel Championship.
