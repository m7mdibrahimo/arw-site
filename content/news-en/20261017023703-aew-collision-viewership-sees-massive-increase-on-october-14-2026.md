The audience figures for the October 14, 2026 episode of AEW Collision on TBS have been released, showing a sharp increase compared to the previous week. According to Wrestlenomics, the show drew 514,000 viewers with a 0.1 rating in the key 18-49 demographic. This represents an increase of 142,000 viewers and up by 0.06 in the demo from the October 10 episode, which drew 372,000 viewers and a 0.04 rating. Compared to the same week in 2025, viewership increased by 28.5%, while the demo rating dropped by 9.1%. These numbers reflect only linear television viewership on TBS and do not include streaming or digital platforms.
