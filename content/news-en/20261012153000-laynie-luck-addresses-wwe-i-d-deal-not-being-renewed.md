Laynie Luck has spoken out regarding the decision not to renew her WWE I.D. contract. Following her loss of the WWE I.D. Women's Championship, it was confirmed that she would return to free agency. Luck held the title for more than 300 days.

In an interview with Sean Ross Sapp, Luck reflected on her tenure with the program.

> "It’s honestly surprising, obviously. That’s the biggest word you can use. I took this whole year and I showed what I can do and I feel like I built so much value and I feel, like I said, lucky. I feel like I got everything I possibly could have out of that program and I wanted to go there and build value and grab knowledge and that’s exactly what I did. I feel like I really launched myself this year."

Luck also discussed the support she received online, noting that she remained authentic throughout her time under contract.

> "I feel like it’s just validating of the choices that I’ve made… Getting the contract initially was, I felt like, a big win for people who stay true to themselves and maybe that’s even something to do with I never had to change my name. I got to be Laynie the whole way through, and so I feel like people just see that and people know that and I’ve just been here; I’ve been around, I’ve been with the girls, I’ve been with the people. They just know who I am and the things that I wanna stand for and I feel like I got to just stay so true to myself and that was a win for my people who care about me and wanna see me win as myself."

Luck has been an active wrestler since 2015, with appearances in AEW, ROH, and numerous other promotions during her 11-year career.
