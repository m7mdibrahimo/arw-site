WWE legend Steve Austin has concluded his inaugural season of off-road racing and is already preparing for a return to competition in 2027. Throughout 2026, Austin competed in the Stock Mod division with the Valley Off-Road Racing Association in Nevada as part of his Broken Skull Racing team, driving a Kawasaki race car featuring the number 316 in honor of his iconic catchphrase.

Following his final race at the Monster Dash 2026 event on October 24, Austin took to social media on October 28, 2026, to reflect on his rookie year and confirm his future plans:

> "The 2026 Broken Skull Racing season is over. We compete in Stock Mod division, and will stay there again for 2027 season. Thanks to @valleyoffroadracingassociation for the great races this year. We will run the VORRA schedule next year and will also hit KOH, Mint, V2R. Thanks to our sponsors for all the help."

Despite a strong start that included victories in his first two races, Austin faced challenges later in the year, including an accident at the Stomping Grounds 300 in August and an early exit in October. Nevertheless, he remains focused on growing his team and expanding his racing schedule next year, proving he has found a new arena for competition outside of the wrestling ring.
