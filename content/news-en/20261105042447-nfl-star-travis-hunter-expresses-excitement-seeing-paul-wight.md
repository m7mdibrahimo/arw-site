NFL star Travis Hunter showed great enthusiasm upon spotting AEW wrestler Paul Wight during a recent Jacksonville Jaguars game. The event took place on November 1 at EverBank Stadium in Jacksonville, where the Jaguars secured a dominant 42-7 victory over the Indianapolis Colts.

When Wight appeared on the stadium's big screen, Hunter, who plays as a cornerback and wide receiver, expressed his excitement by referring to the wrestler by his famous moniker, "The Big Show."

AEW President Tony Khan also serves as the Chief Football Strategy Officer for the Jacksonville Jaguars.
