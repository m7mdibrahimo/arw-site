Sami Zayn is preparing for his Champion vs. Champion match against Roman Reigns at WWE Crown Jewel 2026 on November 7. In an interview with Cathy Kelley, Zayn expressed confidence in his ability to defeat the Tribal Chief. Zayn argued that despite Reigns' dominant persona, he still experiences self-doubt as a human being. Zayn noted that he is currently performing at his highest level and believes he can secure a victory. Furthermore, Zayn addressed fans who have doubted his journey, stating that he has accomplished more with fewer advantages than anyone else in the history of the business. He also expressed frustration with fans who require constant validation to recognize his greatness, though he maintained that he will continue to be authentic to himself rather than adopting a persona like Reigns.
