On Monday, GCW announced Bloodsport Reborn without providing details on the event managed by Josh Barnett since 2019. Barnett previously stated at Bloodsport XV in April that the Bloodsport series would halt until further notice, clarifying that it was not finished for good but paused for now.

On Tuesday, Josh Barnett responded to an account noting a lack of consultation regarding the new show.

> "You and me both."

While Bloodsport was founded by Barnett, it remained part of GCW The Collective during WrestleMania weekends and carried GCW branding even during events in Europe and Japan. In an interview on Monday, Barnett revealed that he was shopping the concept to other parties and had received initial offers.
