John Cena is heading back to television for several appearances. WWE confirmed that he will appear live on the Friday, November 13 episode of SmackDown and the Monday, November 16 episode of Raw, according to Fightful.

Cena revealed plans for the Cena Classic on Monday, noting that he will also be on NXT and at Survivor Series. The tournament takes place on December 14 at Disney World and pits main roster talent against NXT superstars, with fans voting on the winner. Cody Rhodes is the first competitor announced for the event.
