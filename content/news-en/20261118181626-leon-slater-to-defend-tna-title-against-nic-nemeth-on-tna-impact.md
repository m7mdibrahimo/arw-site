TNA has announced a World Title match for the November 19 episode of TNA iMPACT! Leon Slater will defend his TNA World Championship against Nic Nemeth. Slater previously defeated Nemeth to capture the title at TNA Lockdown. The November 19 card also features a multi-woman tag team match pitting Indi Hartwell, Gabby Forza, Shotzi Blackheart, and Allie against the team of Apex (Shayna Baszler and Zoey Serrano) and Fire N Flava (Kiera Hogan and Tasha Steelz), along with an appearance by The Dollhouse (Mila Moore, Harley Hudson, and Taryn Terrell).
