Indiana Pacers star Tyrese Haliburton prepared for his return to the NBA on Wednesday against the New Orleans Pelicans. This game marked his first appearance since he suffered an Achilles tear during Game Seven of the 2025 NBA Finals.

Upon arriving at the arena, Haliburton wore a denim jacket identical to the one Triple H sported during his own return from a quad injury in May 2021 at Madison Square Garden.

https://x.com/NBA/status/2113021834702823905

Haliburton has openly expressed his passion for WWE and has appeared on the promotion's television programs multiple times in recent years, including a notable face-off against Jalen Brunson at the same Madison Square Garden venue.
