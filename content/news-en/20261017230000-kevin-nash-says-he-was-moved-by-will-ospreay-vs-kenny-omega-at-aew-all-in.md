During a recent episode of his podcast, WWE Hall of Famer Kevin Nash discussed his thoughts on the match between Will Ospreay and Kenny Omega at AEW All In, where Ospreay defeated Omega for the AEW Men's World Championship. Nash admitted that he was genuinely moved by the encounter despite it differing from his preferred style of wrestling. He also shared an idea for a dream tag team match pitting Ospreay and Omega against WWE stars Seth Rollins and Roman Reigns to test their contrasting styles together.
