Shayna Baszler is set to challenge Indi Hartwell for the Knockouts World Championship at TNA Destination X. During the October 29 episode of TNA iMPACT, TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Champions Shayna Baszler and Zoey Serrano interrupted proceedings at ringside. Serrano took out a security guard before Baszler grabbed a headset from the commentary team. Baszler announced that authority figure Daria Rae hired her to take the Knockouts World Title from Indi Hartwell at the upcoming event. The championship match was subsequently made official for the November 15 special.

The updated lineup for TNA Destination X features TNA World Champion Leon Slater defending his title against Trey Miguel, alongside the Women's and Men's Ultimate X matches to determine future title challengers. Additionally, the event will host two World X-Cup Fatal 4-Way matches and the World X-Cup Final, culminating with Indi Hartwell defending the Knockouts World Championship against Shayna Baszler.
