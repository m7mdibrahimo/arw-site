Katie Hebner is currently training to return to the wrestling ring. Gail Kim recently revealed on social media that Katie, who is the daughter of famous referee Earl Hebner, is working on her in-ring comeback. Fans might remember Katie from her memorable TNA Wrestling appearance when she was targeted by Awesome Kong and Raisha Saeed before Gail Kim and ODB made the save. Katie has previous training experience at RVA Wrestling Academy and has also worked as a referee for a few ROH matches.
