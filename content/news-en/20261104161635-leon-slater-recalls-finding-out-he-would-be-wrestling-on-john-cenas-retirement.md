Leon Slater recently opened up about how he found out he would be competing on John Cena's retirement show in Washington, DC, late last year. Speaking with the Extreme Channel, Slater explained that he tries to avoid treating major bookings as monumental events beforehand to keep his nerves in check and maintain his performance level. Slater recalled how he had a booking with RevPro around the same time and was sorting out his schedule between TNA and NXT dates when Melissa, a travel coordinator at NXT, informed him that he would actually be in DC that weekend. After initially missing the significance of the location, he quickly realized what event was taking place. Slater described the weekend as an incredible experience where he was surrounded by friends and childhood heroes, making it a true dream come true.
