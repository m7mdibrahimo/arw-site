UFC 334 is scheduled for Saturday, November 14, at Madison Square Garden in New York City. The event will air on Paramount+, featuring a main event between UFC Heavyweight Champion Ciryl Gane and Josh Hokit. Additionally, UFC Women’s Bantamweight Champion Kayla Harrison will face Amanda Nunes.

The card includes several other matchups, such as Caio Borralho vs. Yousri Belgaroui, Uros Medic vs. Kevin Holland, Bilal Hasan vs. Luis Gurule, Stephen Thompson vs. Charles Radtke, Drew Dober vs. Chris Duncan, and Jim Miller vs. Terrance McKinney. The lineup also features Donte Johnson vs. Cesar Almeida, Melquizael Costa vs. Jared Gordon, Bia Mesquita vs. Macy Chiasson, Heili Alateng vs. Juan Diaz, and Evan Elder vs. Samuel Sanches.
