Shannon Moore revealed he was so furious with John Laurinaitis during his WWE tenure that he wanted to jump across a table and get physical with him. According to Ringside News, Moore claimed Laurinaitis intentionally sabotaged his match against C.W. Anderson at an ECW show in the Hammerstein Ballroom by constantly changing instructions through the referee’s headset, switching him between babyface and heel roles.

> "Johnny played into that I think and that night in Hammerstein Ballroom he tried to sabotage my match by switching the call back and forth on the headset to the ref going, like, hey, tell Shannon that he’s a baby face, tell Shannon he’s a heel, tell Shannon he’s a baby face, tell him he’s a heel now, he’s a baby face now."

Moore was upset for himself and for Anderson, wanting his opponent to have a quality match. Upon returning backstage, he was prepared for a physical confrontation before other wrestlers intervened to stop him. While Moore admitted that contacting Vince McMahon directly to secure his return was not the proper corporate protocol, he maintains that Laurinaitis held a grudge and made him suffer the consequences.

> "I was wanting to jump across the table and like make it physical cuz I was really upset about that like not only for me but for C.W. Anderson."
