A tense verbal exchange took place backstage following WWE Raw between Sol Ruca and Lyra Valkyria. During an interview, Ruca was asked for her thoughts on the upcoming John Cena Classic next month, praising Cena for highlighting the youngest and newest talent in WWE.

Before Ruca could finish her sentence, Valkyria interrupted her with a disgruntled rant. Valkyria expressed her frustration, stating that she dislikes individuals like Ruca and Lola Vice who she claims get everything handed to them easily, while she faces stacked odds every week on Raw.

Ruca responded by pointing out that she has also endured tough times without making it everyone else's problem or blaming others. She then challenged Valkyria to settle their issues inside the ring, leaving Valkyria looking stunned and without an immediate answer to the challenge.
