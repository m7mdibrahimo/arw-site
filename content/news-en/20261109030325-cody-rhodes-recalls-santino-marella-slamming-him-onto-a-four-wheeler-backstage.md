Cody Rhodes learned that joking around with Santino Marella backstage was a mistake. During an interview on the Double Coverage Podcast, Rhodes revealed that a friendly wrestling session between the two turned into a real fight after he managed to take Marella down in the locker room.

Marella, who was a judo and mixed martial arts expert, did not appreciate being taken down. Rhodes explained that Marella became extremely angry and reacted with force.

> "It’s not — I’m not better at martial arts than Santino. But he was so angry that he picked me up over his head right as we came up, and he slammed me on a four-wheeler hard case in the back. And thank God he slammed me on that. Otherwise, I would have hit the cement."

WWE legend Arn Anderson witnessed the incident, and everyone in the room realized the situation had gone too far. Rhodes noted that while Marella played a comedic character on television, his legitimate combat skills made him someone not to be trifled with.
