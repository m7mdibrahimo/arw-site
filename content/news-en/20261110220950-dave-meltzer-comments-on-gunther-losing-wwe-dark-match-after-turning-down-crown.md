Dave Meltzer addressed GUNTHER losing a dark match to Trick Williams before WWE Raw following his decision not to travel to Crown Jewel in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. Meltzer noted that babyfaces typically win dark matches, and suggested the loss could be a form of punishment from management. He pointed out that WWE's stance on talent refusing Saudi Arabia trips has tightened over the years, often resulting in adjusted pushes or losing titles prior to the shows. While neither Meltzer nor Alvarez could independently confirm the punishment, Alvarez noted that GUNTHER had never previously lost a dark match in WWE.
