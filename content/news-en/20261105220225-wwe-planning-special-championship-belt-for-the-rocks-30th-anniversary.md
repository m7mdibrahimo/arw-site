WWE is preparing to celebrate the 30th anniversary of The Rock's debut, with special merchandise, including a commemorative championship belt, currently in the works. According to WrestleVotes, the company intends to honor The Rock throughout November 2026 to mark the milestone of his first appearance at Survivor Series 1996. The celebration will also feature social media campaigns revisiting his iconic career. While there is no official word on when the commemorative title will be released or what it will look like, WWE aims to make the anniversary a significant event. The Rock made his debut on November 17, 1996, at Survivor Series in Madison Square Garden as Rocky Maivia. He eventually became one of the biggest stars in the Attitude Era, famously feuding with Stone Cold Steve Austin and Triple H. There is currently no indication that The Rock will make a television appearance for the anniversary, as the focus remains on merchandise and content.
