Rey Pegasus discussed his experience meeting and competing against fellow independent wrestlers during the 2026 CMLL Torneo de Escuelas. Pegasus and his partner Tornado emerged as winners of the tournament, and he has since secured the Mexican National Middleweight Championship within the promotion. Speaking with Luis Pulido, Pegasus noted that while he had crossed paths with wrestlers like Karma I, Tornado, and Cerebro Negro Jr. on the independent circuit, their paths had not always aligned before the tournament.

> That’s right. I spent many years as an independent wrestler, and, obviously, I met a lot of fellow wrestlers—many from the wrestling scene. When we went to ask for a chance or to train at the wrestling schools, well, obviously, since we had different coaches, sometimes our schedules didn’t align. When the tryouts came around—and they do hold them to determine whether you’ll participate or not—it was a surprise to run into each other there and see familiar faces, specifically people I’d already faced and with whom I’d shared the locker room and the ring on the independent circuit. It was a surprise, more than anything, to realize, ‘Oh, look, here we are now, and we’re going to give it a shot.’ It’s really cool—it’s really cool—that so many of our fellow wrestlers who’ve been grinding it out in the independent circuit have made it here, been given this opportunity, and taken advantage of it. It’s a surprise, and it’s really cool to share the locker room with them right now because we know where we came from; we know what we struggled through before to make it to the best wrestling promotion.

Pegasus emphasized how rewarding it has been to see so many peers succeed after years of working on the independent scene. He also reflected on the significance of his victory in the tournament.
