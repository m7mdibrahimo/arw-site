WWE Crown Jewel 2026 is scheduled to take place on November 7 at the Riyadh Seasonal Stadium in Saudi Arabia, but internal discussions are reportedly taking place regarding whether the event should still be held in the country. According to Bryan Alvarez of Figure Four Weekly, discussions arose following reports from Reuters that Yemen's Houthi forces claimed attacks on the King Khalid International Airport and other energy facilities in Riyadh. The U.S. Embassy and the Department of State have issued warnings advising against travel to Saudi Arabia amidst escalating regional conflicts. WWE would reportedly not suffer financial losses if the event is relocated, though preparations at the stadium are already underway. Meanwhile, WWE remains committed to holding WWE WrestleMania 43 in Saudi Arabia next April.
