NJPW announced a new kickoff bout for the upcoming Taichi event, scheduled for October 17th at the wrestler's former middle school. The match features Young Lions Masatora Yasuda and Ryoma Tsukamoto.

Regarding the matchup, NJPW stated:

> "Before the main pay-per-view action gets underway, Masatora Yasuda goes one-on-one with Ryoma Tsukamoto. A product of Just Tap Out, Tsukamoto was a part of a notable six-man tag bout at last year's Ishikari school event, a six-man tag that turned incredibly heated, especially in exchanges with Yota Tsuji. Tsukamoto has been angling for a shot on this year's card for quite some time, and at Korakuen Hall made a direct appeal to Taichi. The Holy Emperor had an opponent ready in Hokkaido native Masatora Yasuda, who will be looking to assert himself as the true leader of NJPW's Young Lion guard with a victory against outsider opposition on the afternoon."

The event also features Ryusuke Taguchi vs. Dick Togo for the DDT Universal Championship and a Falls Count Anywhere match between Toru Yano and El Desperado against Unbound Co.
