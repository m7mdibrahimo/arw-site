WWE SmackDown took place on Friday, October 30, 2026, at the Charleston Coliseum in Charleston, WV. The event was themed "Danhausen's Very Nice, Very Evil SmackDown" in celebration of Halloween Eve.

During the broadcast, it was announced that United States Champion Trick Williams would challenge Sami Zayn for the Undisputed Championship. Additionally, Ricky Saints is set to challenge Rey Fénix for the AAA Cruiserweight Championship. Cody Rhodes addressed his upcoming Last Man Standing match against Randy Orton at Crown Jewel, emphasizing that the winner would likely emerge as the number one contender. 

Backstage, Tiffany Stratton discussed her plans to wait for the right moment to cash in her briefcase, while Chelsea Green, dressed as Nikki Bella, declined a trade offer from Danhausen. NXT North American Champion Zaria was spotted in the crowd during the title match.

Chelsea Green def. Nikki Bella to retain the WWE Women's Championship.
