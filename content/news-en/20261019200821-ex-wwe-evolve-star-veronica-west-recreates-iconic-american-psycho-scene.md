Former WWE EVOLVE star Veronica West recently recreated the famous murder scene from American Psycho on Instagram. Dressing up as Christian Bale's character Patrick Bateman, West utilized a clear raincoat and an axe while featuring another person as the unfortunate Paul Allen. During the video, West incorporated one of Bateman's most recognizable lines into her caption. This creative post comes as West continues her professional wrestling career under her current name rather than Fallyn Grey.
