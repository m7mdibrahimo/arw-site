Will Ospreay has expressed interest in joining Netflix's upcoming Assassin’s Creed series. The AEW star revealed that he has put his name forward for a potential role in the adaptation, noting that he would love to try his hand at acting. Ospreay has long drawn inspiration from the franchise for his wrestling persona, famously utilizing the Aerial Assassin moniker and the Hidden Blade elbow strike, and he has previously collaborated with Ubisoft on several custom entrances. While nothing has been agreed upon yet and he acknowledges that script reading might present a challenge, Ospreay hopes the opportunity could help further build his presence in AEW.
