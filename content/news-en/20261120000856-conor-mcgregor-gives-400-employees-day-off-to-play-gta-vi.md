Conor McGregor gave 400 employees working across his various businesses a full-day leave to celebrate the release of Grand Theft Auto VI. The former UFC Champion decided that the massive video game launch was enough reason to let his workforce in multiple countries skip their shifts and enjoy the game instead.

McGregor announced the decision personally, sending a message to his staff wishing them an enjoyable day away from work duties. Dublin's Black Forge Inn, one of his businesses, also announced that it would close on November 19, 2026, so its employees could experience the game.

The decision comes as McGregor continues to manage his business ventures and recover from surgery following a knee injury during his UFC return in July, while the door remains open for a potential future appearance in WWE.
