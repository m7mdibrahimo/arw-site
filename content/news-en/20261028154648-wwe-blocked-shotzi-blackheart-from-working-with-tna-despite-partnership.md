Shotzi Blackheart revealed that she wanted to work with TNA while she was still under contract with WWE, but the company blocked the move. During an interview, she explained that after returning from ACL surgery, she was eager to wrestle and saw WWE's partnership with TNA as an opportunity. She even spoke with Tommy Dreamer, who was open to the idea, but WWE officials ultimately denied permission. After her eventual release from WWE, Shotzi spent a year and a half on the independent circuit to rebuild her confidence before officially signing with TNA.
