Sami Zayn recently discussed his journey to the WWE Championship, admitting he believed his opportunity had vanished following his loss to Drew McIntyre at WWE Royal Rumble 2026. Zayn noted that while he had been involved in intense rivalries, such as his time with The Bloodline, he felt that the narrative of the underdog finally achieving his dream had concluded after that defeat.

Reflecting on these moments during an interview on the Insight podcast with Chris Van Vliet, Zayn explained that he briefly accepted that his window to win the title had closed. Despite this, he eventually secured the championship at WWE Night of Champions by winning a triple threat match against Gunther and Cody Rhodes.

Zayn is now a two-time world champion and is preparing to face Roman Reigns for the Crown Jewel Championship at WWE Crown Jewel.
