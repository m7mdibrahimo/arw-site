Elayna Black prefers to handle her own hair and makeup before wrestling matches rather than relying on professionals in WWE and TNA. During an interview with the Daily Star, she explained that taking care of her appearance herself helps her mentally prepare for her matches.

> "We have hair and makeup at TNA, but I've always done my own hair and makeup even when I was at WWE."

She noted that the routine gives her a chance to disconnect from backstage noise and focus by wearing headphones for an hour and a half without speaking to anyone.

Black added that the process keeps her mind occupied and prevents overthinking before performing, noting that having someone else do the work leaves her mind racing.
