WWE Crown Jewel remains scheduled for Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on November 7, according to TKO executive chair and CEO Ari Emanuel. During an appearance on Fox News, Emanuel addressed the escalating security concerns in the region following recent attacks and airport closures linked to the Houthis.

Emanuel noted that TKO is monitoring the situation daily and has a dedicated team preparing contingency plans in case the event needs to be relocated. While the show is still set for Riyadh, the company is actively considering alternative locations to ensure safety if conditions deteriorate further.
