India Sioux has shared her thoughts on the significance of winning the CMLL National Women's Championship. Sioux won the title in March 2025 by defeating Sanely on CMLL Viernes Espectacular, going on to defend it against competitors like Hera and Kira.

Speaking about the championship's prestige, Sioux highlighted the history and dedication required to hold it.

> "It meant a lot to me. One of CMLL’s most important championships is the Women’s National Championship, because of its history and all the women who have won it; it’s a championship that demands a lot of dedication."

Her reign lasted 511 days before she lost the belt to Thunder Rosa in July 2026. Reflecting on the loss, Sioux admitted it was a difficult night but expressed her determination to secure a rematch and reclaim the title in the future.
