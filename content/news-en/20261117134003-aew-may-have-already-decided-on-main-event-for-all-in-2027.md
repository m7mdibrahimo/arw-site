Will Ospreay’s position at the top of AEW is reportedly shaping major plans for the future. Speaking during a Self Made Session, Self Made Pro discussed the possibility of MJF eventually dethroning Ospreay and revealed a bigger backstage belief. According to him, sources within AEW firmly believe that Ospreay is already locked in for the 2027 All In main event. Self Made Pro noted that while there is plenty of uncertainty surrounding the World Championship picture and how other top stars like Andrade factor into the immediate future, people within the company are convinced that next year's main event is already booked around Ospreay.
