According to Front Office Sports, Pat McAfee frequently receives alarming messages, including death threats behind the scenes. A source revealed that the FBI told the ESPN star to consider wearing a bulletproof vest during the week following the assassination of Charlie Kirk at Utah Valley University in September 2025. These details help explain McAfee's recent distress during his visit to the University of Utah for College GameDay, where the institution shared a video of students mocking him.

McAfee stated that he had never experienced such intense discomfort. Following the incident, the University of Utah issued an apology and admitted that its attempt at humor missed the mark.
