Keanu Carver is set to appear in a Seminole County, Florida, court on Tuesday, October 20. Carver, whose real name is Kevin Robertson, was previously served with an order of protection that prohibits him from contacting an NXT talent whose identity has been withheld. The order was filed on September 14 following allegations that Carver had stalked the talent and engaged in domestic violence. A court hearing on September 22 ruled that the injunction would remain in effect, and the upcoming hearing will serve as a review of that order. Carver has not been charged with a crime, but he has since been released by WWE. Following the initial reports, Carver posted on X:

> "yall gone sound dumb ash in a month"
> "Fake ass virtue signaling mfs everything done inna dark come to light."
Those posts and his account have since been deleted.
