Ilja Dragunov is currently in Japan. The official X account for Sendai Girls' Pro Wrestling shared a photo showing All Elite Wrestling star Ilja Dragunov in the ring alongside several members of the Sendai Girls' roster, including Chihiro Hashimoto, during a visit to the Sendai Girls' dojo. Dragunov's partner is Lady Leigh, formerly known as Jin Tala during her time with WWE, who has been competing for Sendai Girls' since September. Meanwhile, on AEW programming, Dragunov and Tommaso Ciampa have recently found themselves on a collision course, sharing a segment on the October 28 episode of AEW Dynamite.
