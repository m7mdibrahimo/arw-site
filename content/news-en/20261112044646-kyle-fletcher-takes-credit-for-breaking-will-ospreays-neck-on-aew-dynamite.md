Kyle Fletcher and Will Ospreay exchanged heated words inside the ring on AEW Dynamite ahead of their upcoming match at AEW Full Gear. During the segment, Fletcher claimed he was responsible for breaking Will Ospreay's neck rather than Jon Moxley, threatening that he would drop him on his head again if given the chance.

Ospreay, the AEW World Champion, responded confidently, reminding Fletcher that he easily beat him twice despite having a broken neck. He questioned whether Fletcher even stood a chance at Full Gear now that Ospreay was back at full health. Fletcher slapped Ospreay during the segment, but the champion remained unfazed, and the segment ended with Ospreay claiming he was on another level.
