Cody Rhodes recently discussed his mother's involvement in his WrestleMania 40 storyline and made a striking claim about her qualifications. Speaking on Mythical Kitchen's Last Meals, Rhodes explained that Mama Rhodes has more real-world experience in the wrestling industry than many active competitors. He recalled how The Rock targeted her during their feud, noting that she never cried on the championship belt despite the intense pressure. Rhodes shared that her natural integration into the story came from the deep-rooted history between their families, transforming her into an accidental on-screen character who began contributing her own creative ideas. He ultimately argued that living the wrestling lifestyle alongside Dusty Rhodes for decades gave her an authentic understanding that surpasses many professional wrestlers today.
