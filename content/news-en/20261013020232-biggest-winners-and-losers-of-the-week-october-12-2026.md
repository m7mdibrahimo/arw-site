This week in professional wrestling featured major events from WWE and TNA, including Money in the Bank and Bound for Glory. Yota Tsuji successfully defended his IWGP Heavyweight Championship against Ryohei Oiwa at NJPW King of Pro Wrestling, setting up a potential showdown with Kenny Omega. Meanwhile, The Vision had a rough night at WWE Money in the Bank as Bron Breakker missed out on a title opportunity and Bronson Reed lost to Oba Femi. Amazing Red was inducted into the TNA Hall of Fame and won the X-Division Championship for the fourth time. Max Caster faced severe backlash and a loss to Moose at TNA Bound for Glory following controversial remarks. Leon Slater made history by defeating Nic Nemeth to become the youngest TNA World Champion.
