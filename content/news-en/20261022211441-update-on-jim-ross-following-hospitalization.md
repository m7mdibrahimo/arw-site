An update has been provided regarding the health of Jim Ross. Following a second brain procedure, Jim Ross had to return to hospital care due to low oxygen levels. Conrad Thompson shared an update on his podcast, stating that Jim Ross recently visited for an outpatient procedure and contracted a chest cold and infection. Although his oxygen was low and he spent the night in the hospital, he is now safely back home and recovering.
