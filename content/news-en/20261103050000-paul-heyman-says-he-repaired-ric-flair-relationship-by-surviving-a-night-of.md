Paul Heyman has revealed how he repaired his strained relationship with Ric Flair after creative differences in WCW. During an appearance on "In Depth with Graham Bensinger," the WWE Hall of Famer recalled that he survived a night of drinking with the 16-time World Champion in order to bury the hatchet.

Heyman explained that after checking into the same hotel, Flair invited him to the bar. Knowing that Flair could outdrink anyone, Heyman had no choice but to keep up. He noted that he felt terrible the next day and had to consume pickle juice and salted popcorn to absorb the alcohol, but he ultimately survived the night.

Since earning Flair's respect during that drinking session, the two have remained on good terms personally and professionally. Meanwhile, Flair is currently recovering from a bad fall in Tampa, Florida, where he suffered multiple injuries including cuts to his head and eye.
