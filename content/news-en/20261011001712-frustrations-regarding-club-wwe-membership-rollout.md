According to a report from WrestleVotes, WWE has faced significant criticism regarding the launch of its Club WWE membership program, specifically the $99 Founder’s Package. Internal sources have described the rollout of the membership as 'disorganized and uneventful.' The initiative, which was introduced in July, was designed to provide fans with various benefits including access to exclusive content, merchandise, rewards, community features, and ticket opportunities.
