Anthony Bowens recently opened up about his memories of the late Benjamin Satterley, also known as PAC. While speaking with Michael Fairman, Bowens recalled spending time with Satterley after shows and sharing conversations with him and other wrestlers. Bowens expressed his sadness over the loss of someone so young and talented, noting that Satterley was his first singles opponent in AEW. Bowens praised Satterley as a sweet, humble, and kind man, adding that he learned a great deal about wrestling psychology and pacing simply from sharing the ring with him for eight minutes. Bowens stated that he remains thankful for the experience and for Satterley's friendship, concluding by hoping he rests in peace.
