Mick Foley continues to show his admiration for Megan Bayne and Divine Dominion. Following the November 4 episode of AEW Dynamite: Blood & Guts, the WWE Hall of Famer shared a photo on Instagram of himself bowing down to Bayne. Despite being covered in blood from the brutal women's Blood & Guts match, Bayne was joined by Foley as he performed her signature pose. Foley embraced his role as Bayne's hardcore servant, writing that he gladly bows down before her. This moment follows a running joke that began earlier this year when Foley humorously misunderstood their demand to bow down during a July appearance. After Bayne and her team competed in a violent Chicago Street Fight at All Out in September, Foley posted another video of himself bowing to Divine Dominion. The Blood & Guts match provided him with yet another reason to show his respect after Bayne, Lena Kross, Thekla, Skye Blue, Julia Hart, and Persephone faced Kris Statlander, Harley Cameron, Willow Nightingale, Queen Aminata, Jamie Hayter, and Alex Windsor in a chaotic encounter featuring broken glass and barbed wire. Although Bayne's team came up short following Julia Hart's submission, Foley was right by her side to honor her violent performance.
