James Ellsworth went from being destroyed by Braun Strowman to becoming a regular on WWE SmackDown, but he recently revealed that WWE had no creative plans for him when Vince McMahon first hired him. Speaking on Developmentally Speaking, Ellsworth explained that his career took an unexpected turn after he impressed McMahon during his 2016 match against Strowman.

> "When they call and they're like, 'Hey, you're coming to SmackDown.' I'm like, 'All right, well, am I going to NXT?' Because that, you know, that's what everybody does. They're like, 'No, man. Vince wants you. Vince wants you on SmackDown.' They're like, 'We don't know what the future holds.'"

Ellsworth noted that because WWE had months of television already written, his sudden arrival forced the creative team to scramble to make room for him. He suspected his presence might have initially annoyed some people behind the scenes, as the company had invested significant time and money into wrestlers coming through NXT.

> "So they have all this television written for months and months and months. They have ideas for months. Now Vince is going into this writer room and going, 'Hey, you got to write stuff for this guy,' which means, you know, and you live and you learn, which means now they got to take somebody off the show to give me their time, and now they got to rewrite all this stuff just to put me on."

He recalled that his plans were constantly changing, shifting from working with The Miz to facing AJ Styles, which he found intimidating. Ellsworth added that the company simply planned his character week-to-week, eventually leading to his run as an ally to Carmella. Despite the lack of long-term direction, he stated that he has no regrets about his time in WWE.
