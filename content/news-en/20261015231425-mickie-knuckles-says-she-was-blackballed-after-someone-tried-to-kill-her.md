Mickie Knuckles recently reflected on her two-decade wrestling career, revealing two instances where she almost quit the industry. Speaking on Jasmin St. Claire’s Off the Rails podcast, Knuckles described a brutal hazing ritual during her early IWA training known as “hell month.” She claimed she was homeless and working two jobs at the time, and the constant abuse from peers made her feel unwelcome until Tracy Smothers and Necro Butcher intervened by refusing to participate.

Knuckles also detailed a more serious incident following her departure from IWA Mid-South, where she alleged someone “tried to kill” her. She stated that after attempting to report the incident, she was blackballed and pressured into silence because the individual involved held significant influence in the business. Knuckles credited Smothers for helping her return to the road and clearing her name with promoters. Despite these traumatic experiences, she remains in the industry, citing her passion for the physical storytelling of professional wrestling.
