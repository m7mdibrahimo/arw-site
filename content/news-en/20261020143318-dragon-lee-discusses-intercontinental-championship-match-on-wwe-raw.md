Dragon Lee has shared his thoughts on competing for the WWE Intercontinental Championship during the October 19, 2026, episode of WWE RAW. The championship bout featured titleholder Chad Gable defending against both Lee and Rey Mysterio in a triple threat match.

Gable successfully retained his title, and Ethan Page alongside Big Cass attacked the participants after the match concluded. According to Fightful, Dragon Lee took to social media to express his excitement about sharing the ring with Gable and Mysterio, describing the experience with Rey as a major career milestone.

> "I just wanna said how special this night was ..! Thank you San Diego and thank you Chad gable for been and incredible and inspired wrestler in all the ways ! And also wanna said that really means the world to me sharing the ring with Rey Misterio, dream come true!! as a 52 years old and keeping moving like 20s eeeasy!! he really Motivated me and I know all the world ! He’s the best luchador in te entire World! Tonight I really learn so much for both of them !"
