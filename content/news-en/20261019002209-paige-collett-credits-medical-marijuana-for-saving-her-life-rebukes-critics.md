Independent wrestler Paige Collett addressed criticism regarding her use of cannabis, stating that medical marijuana is the reason she can still pursue her professional wrestling career. Taking to social media on October 18, Collett pushed back against onlookers who assume she uses it recreationally or as a party habit, emphasizing that she is managing a severe chronic illness.

Collett explained that she specifically chose cannabis over ketamine treatments and opioid painkillers, and she voiced frustration over strangers making assumptions without knowing her health background.

> "People see me smoking and judge me & typecast me! I’m a strong advocate for medical marijuana. It has saved my life and it lets me continue to pursue my wrestling dream."

> "Because I’m brave enough to refuse ketamine infusions and opioids…I then have strangers see me smoke with other people and these other people do other drugs and they immediately think oh she’s a partier when in reality I’m a chronically ill woman that’s fighting for my life."

> "We need to end the stigma surrounding marijuana! 💚✅‼️"

Collett suffers from Complex Regional Pain Syndrome, a painful condition that began after a training accident under WWE Hall of Famer Rikishi that left her temporarily using a wheelchair. After years of rehabilitation to walk and return to the ring, she maintained that cannabis remains a crucial part of her pain management.
