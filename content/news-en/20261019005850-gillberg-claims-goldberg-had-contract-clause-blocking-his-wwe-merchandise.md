Gillberg believes WWE missed a significant opportunity by not producing more merchandise featuring his parody of Goldberg. He specifically wondered why the company never released a battle pack that included both of them, given that his gimmick was entirely based on mocking Goldberg. Gillberg recalled his surprise appearance during the famous Festival of Friendship segment involving Chris Jericho and Kevin Owens, noting that he was excluded from the related merchandise and imagery.

> "They — I don’t know how they didn’t make a battle pack of Goldberg-Gillberg."

> "I don’t understand. I don’t understand. I was on the Festival of Friends. They did the entire Festival of Friends — everybody on it but me. Even the picture, you know what I mean? But not me."

Gillberg suggested a potential reason for his absence from these products, claiming he heard that Goldberg had specific language in his contracts that prohibited WWE from granting Gillberg certain merchandise opportunities.

> "Because I heard that Goldberg had in his contracts that Gillberg gets nothing — no action figures, no video games, no nothing."

While Gillberg admitted he never personally saw the contract or confirmed the details, he believes this would explain why a character so closely linked to Goldberg never received the merchandise treatment he expected. He remains confused by the lack of crossover products, especially since he believes such merchandise would have sold itself.
