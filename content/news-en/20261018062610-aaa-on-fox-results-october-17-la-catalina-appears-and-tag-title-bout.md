Lucha Libre AAA broadcast a new episode of AAA on FOX on October 17 via Fox Latin America and YouTube. La Catalina opened the program to discuss her AAA Reina de Reinas Title victory, stating that she concluded Flammer's story and began her own.

Las Toxicas interrupted the segment, with Flammer insisting her story would restart. La Catalina questioned who was superior between Maravilla and La Hiedra for a future title defense, while Flammer mentioned she would consult with Rey Mysterio. Mysterio later announced he would determine the number one contender by the end of the night. After hearing cases from Flammer and La Hiedra, Mysterio decided that La Catalina will defend her championship against both Flammer and La Hiedra.

Taurus def. El Fiscal. Following the match, El Fiscal challenged Abismo Negro to a bout at Heroes Inmortales.

Los Wagner Brothers (El Hijo del Dr. Wagner Jr. & Galeon) def. El Mesias & Mecha Wolf to retain the AAA World Tag Team Championships.

After the main event, Perros Del Mal confronted the Wagner Brothers. El Mesias and Mecha Wolf attacked the champions, leading Nino Hamburguesa, La Parka, and Mr. Iguana to intervene and target Perros Del Mal, according to Fightful.
