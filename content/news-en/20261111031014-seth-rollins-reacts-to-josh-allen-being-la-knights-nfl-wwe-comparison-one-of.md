Seth Rollins and Becky Lynch appeared on The Kittle Things show hosted by George and Claire Kittle, where the San Francisco 49ers tight end initiated a game of NFL-WWE comparisons. When George suggested that Buffalo Bills quarterback Josh Allen is the NFL equivalent of LA Knight, Rollins offered his take. "I think he’s a babyface," Rollins said regarding Allen. "He hasn’t reached the summit yet. That’s the thing is he’s still on the come up." When pressed on the comparison to LA Knight, Rollins laughed and said, "Ooh. I shouldn’t say that. I shouldn’t say anything. I should just bite my tongue on that one. I was gonna say one of them is talented. I said it anyway."
