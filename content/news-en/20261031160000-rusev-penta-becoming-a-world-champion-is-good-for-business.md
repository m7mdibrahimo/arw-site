Rusev praised Penta in a new interview with TMZ Inside The Ring, revealing that he has been a fan of him since their time in AEW. Since returning to WWE in 2025, Rusev has shared the ring often with him and the Lucha Bros, describing them as looking like live action figures and comparing Penta to a Mortal Kombat character.

Rusev discussed Penta winning Money In The Bank and stated that a world champion run for him would benefit the business.

> "I think he deserves it 100%. I love working with Penta, I love kicking his ass. I know how entertaining he is and how cool he is. I'm a big fan of him. If he’s becoming a champion, I will be more than happy for him and for the business in general, it's good for the business."

He also highlighted Penta's charitable work in private conversations, noting that he secretly helps orphans and the homeless in Mexico with truckloads of toys and clothes. CJ Perry added during the interview that Rusev will play a villain in the film adaptation of her Bulletproof Angel novel.
