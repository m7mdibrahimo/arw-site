Jeff Hardy recently admitted he is open to having one more feud with CM Punk in WWE. Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Hardy reflected on his storied career and noted that his rivalry with Punk is one of his all-time favorites alongside his battles with Umaga. Watching Punk perform at a high level in WWE has sparked Hardy's interest in a potential rematch.

> "Naturally, when I see him in the WWE Universe now, and how good he looks and how good he's performing, it's like, wow, what would that look like? Maybe that's what I should start manifesting is a feud. One more feud between me and Punk."

Hardy also wondered how the crowd would react if they faced each other again in 2027. Punk previously addressed Hardy's past comments about wanting to retire him with some skepticism in February 2025, noting that there are many bridges to cross and expressing uncertainty about how much time he has left in his own career. Despite those past remarks, Hardy remains interested in exploring the idea.
