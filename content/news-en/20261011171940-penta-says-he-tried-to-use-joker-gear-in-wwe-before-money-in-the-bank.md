Penta revealed that he had previously tried to use his Joker gear in WWE before finally wearing it at Money In The Bank. During the event, Penta competed against Kevin Owens, CM Punk, Je'Von Evans, Bron Breakker, and Trick Williams in the men's ladder match and won the briefcase. Speaking to WWE Now after his victory, Penta explained that he had wanted to use the gear earlier, but was told he could not, so he saved it for the premium live event.
