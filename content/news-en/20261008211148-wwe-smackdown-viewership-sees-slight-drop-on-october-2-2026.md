WWE SmackDown viewership numbers for the October 2, 2026, episode on USA Network saw a minor decline compared to the previous week. According to data, the show drew 1,250,000 viewers and a 0.27 rating in the key P18–49 demographic.

This represents a decrease of 13,000 viewers from the September 25, 2026, episode, which recorded 1,263,000 viewers and a 0.27 rating. Despite the week-to-week dip, the show performed significantly better than the same week in 2025, showing a 21.4% increase in viewership and a 17.4% rise in the key demographic compared to the October 3, 2025, episode.

These figures account only for linear television viewership on USA Network and do not include digital or streaming metrics. The company continues to prepare for its next major live event.
