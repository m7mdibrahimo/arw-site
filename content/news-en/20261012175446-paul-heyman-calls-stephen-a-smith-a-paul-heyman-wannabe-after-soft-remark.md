Paul Heyman recently fired back at Stephen A. Smith after the ESPN host claimed the WWE Hall of Famer had "gone soft." Speaking on his YouTube channel, Heyman addressed the comments by dubbing Smith a "Paul Heyman wannabe," noting that Smith had previously admitted to admiring his style while appearing alongside Roman Reigns. Heyman laughed off the criticism about being soft, arguing that after four decades in the wrestling business, choosing not to yell constantly doesn't mean he has lost his edge. He then took a series of comedic, over-the-top jabs at Smith before concluding with a final lighthearted remark about how Smith misses him and loves him, while he misses and loves himself too.
