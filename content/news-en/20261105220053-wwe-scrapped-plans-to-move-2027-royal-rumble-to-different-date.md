WWE has confirmed that the 2027 Royal Rumble will take place on February 6, but internal discussions previously considered a different date. According to WrestleVotes Radio on Fightful Select, the company explored the possibility of moving the event to February 20 to capitalize on the massive promotional window surrounding the Super Bowl, which will be broadcast on ESPN for the first time in 2027. WWE believed that the week leading up to the game and the game-day coverage itself could have provided significant exposure for the Royal Rumble. However, the company ultimately decided against the change for two primary reasons. First, pushing the event back two weeks would have required extensive adjustments to WWE's existing calendar structure. Secondly, the host city for the 2027 Royal Rumble, Phoenix, Arizona, is already scheduled to host the NBA All-Star Weekend from February 19 to 21. Given these logistical conflicts, WWE opted to remain with the original date of February 6 at State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona.
