In a recent interview, The Miz revealed that he never envisioned himself as a professional wrestler in WWE during his childhood. He explained that he lacked the physical stature of legends from that era, such as Hulk Hogan.

> "Well, as a kid, I never thought I could be a WWE superstar. Like, the guys back then, Hulk Hogan, 6'7", you know… pythons, he had huge muscles and I didn't have that. I wasn't big by any means. So there was, that was out of the question."

The Miz noted that he aspired to be a baseball or football player when he was young, describing himself as a good athlete but not exceptional. His perspective shifted after he appeared on the reality show The Real World, which led him to believe he might have a path into wrestling.

> "I just never thought I could do it, and until I said, I'm gonna try it."

Since debuting in the mid-2000s, The Miz has become one of the most decorated performers in the company, earning the Grand Slam Champion title twice.
