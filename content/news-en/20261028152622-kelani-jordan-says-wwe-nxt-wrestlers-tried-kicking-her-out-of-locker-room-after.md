Kelani Jordan opened up about the hostile reaction she faced in the WWE NXT locker room after winning the TNA Knockouts Championship. During an interview on October 28, 2026, with The Wrestling Classic, Jordan revealed that some women in the locker room tried to kick her out despite not achieving as much as she had. Jordan explained that while she expected hostility from opponents wanting a title shot, she was surprised to face backlash from the roster and even fans who called her a traitor for bringing the title back to WWE. She noted that other wrestlers had received praise for similar achievements in the past. Although she understands that championship success naturally breeds jealousy, she made it clear that she was unhappy with being treated that way and now has even more reason to stand her ground as the NXT Women's Champion.
