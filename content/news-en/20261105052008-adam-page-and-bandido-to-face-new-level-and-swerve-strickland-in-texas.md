Adam Page has finally relented. Following another violent confrontation with Swerve Strickland, Bandido approached Page backstage to express his frustration with the disrespect Strickland has shown him. Bandido challenged Strickland and New Level to a Texas Deathmatch at AEW Full Gear, and after weeks of hesitation, Page agreed to the stipulation. The match was confirmed shortly thereafter by Tony Khan and the commentary team.
