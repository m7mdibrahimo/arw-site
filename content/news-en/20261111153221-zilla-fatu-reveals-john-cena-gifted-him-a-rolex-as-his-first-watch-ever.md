John Cena did not just invite Zilla Fatu to the John Cena Classic. The WWE legend also gifted the son of Umaga a Rolex, which turned out to be the first watch Fatu has ever owned in his life. Appearing on Busted Open After Dark, Fatu spoke with Bully Ray about his recent encounter with Cena on WWE NXT. While chatting, Bully noticed the watch on Fatu's wrist and asked if it was a Rolex, prompting Fatu to share the story behind it.

> "It's a John Cino Rolex my first ever watch. Isn't it crazy? Look at that dude."

Bully Ray was shocked by the revelation, and Fatu confirmed that Cena gave him the expensive timepiece, admitting that he even wears it to bed. 

> "He gave me a John Cena Rolex. Yes. My first ever watch in my life. Yes. Wow. I sleep in it. I'm, I'm sleeping in it."

This generous gift followed Cena's personal invitation to Fatu for the John Cena Classic during the November 4 episode of WWE NXT, where Cena became emotional while discussing his bond with Fatu's late father, Umaga. Fatu had previously shown fans the special package he received for the event, which included a briefcase, coins, merchandise, and a watch, and he has now confirmed that the Rolex came directly from Cena. The John Cena Classic is set for December 14, giving Fatu another chance to shine in WWE, but he is already walking away with a deeply treasured gift from a legend who gave him both an opportunity and his very first watch.
