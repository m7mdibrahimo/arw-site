Swerve Strickland made his frustration clear following his championship loss at AEW WrestleDream on October 17, 2026. After losing the AEW World Trios Championships to Hangman Adam Page, Brody King, and Bandido, Strickland refused to hand over the belts, instead dropping them on the canvas before exiting.

The defeat occurred during a Two-Out-of-Three Falls Match where Strickland, Austin Creed, and Kofi fell to their opponents. Despite an early lead for Strickland's team, Page, King, and Bandido rallied, with Bandido pinning Strickland to secure the deciding third fall and the titles.
