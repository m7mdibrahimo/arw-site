On the July 8, 2024 episode of WWE Raw, Zoey Serrano, Shayna Baszler, and Daria Rae aligned to become Pure Fusion Collective. The name faced immediate backlash from fans and talent alike. "Oh my goodness, that whole name is awful. I mean, come on. Not a great name, what are we? We pitched so many names!" Serrano told Chris Van Vliet on Insight. When asked about alternative ideas, she recalled one of the worst options considered. "I don’t remember. But you want to know one of the worst ones that I do remember is banshee. Isn’t it the girl who screams or something like that? I was like, definitely can’t do that," she said. Serrano explained that the group spent a month submitting lists of names back and forth with management before settling on the decision out of sheer exhaustion. PFC eventually ended when Rae and Baszler left WWE, while Baszler and Serrano later reunited in TNA as the reigning TNA Knockouts Tag Team Champions.
