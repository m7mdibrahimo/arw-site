Stephanie Vaquer has been medically cleared to return to WWE, but Becky Lynch is already predicting she will be sidelined again soon. After WWE posted a video on October 24 of Vaquer announcing her return, Lynch did not offer a warm welcome. Vaquer, who has been out of action since suffering an ankle injury at Money in the Bank, stated, "Becky, you wanted to know when I'm cleared? See you Monday." Lynch responded to the announcement with a blunt prediction regarding Vaquer's health.

> "I’ll give you three weeks and I’m sure you’ll be injured again. See you Monday 😘"

Vaquer previously lost the Women's World Championship to Liv Morgan at Money in the Bank, where she suffered the ankle injury. Although she initially expected a quick recovery, her absence lasted for several months. Both Lynch and Vaquer are now scheduled to appear on the October 26 episode of WWE RAW, where their confrontation will likely be anything but friendly.
