TNA Wrestling faced major travel disruptions ahead of Destination X on November 15, 2026, after the FAA ordered a nationwide flight ground stop on November 13. Despite cancellations and delays that left several wrestlers stranded, TNA managed to pull off the show with a much smaller budget than larger promotions. Adjustments were made to both Ultimate X matches, and the World Championship main event nearly fell through before Trey Miguel arrived just in time.

Following the event, TNA announcer Matthew Rehwoldt took to X/X to praise the roster and crew for overcoming these challenges. He highlighted that the company achieved a great show without millions of dollars or a private jet budget. “For all intents and purposes… tonight’s TNA show should not have happened. But it did. And it was a great show. It’s a testament to every talent, staff member, and part of our crew. We may not have millions of dollars and a private jet budget… but this crew has grit.” Rehwoldt expressed pride in what the team accomplished despite the obstacles.
