D-Von Dudley recently opened up about his time in WWE and expressed his belief that the company often treated the Dudley Boyz like an afterthought. During an appearance on the Duke LovesRasslin podcast, the WWE Hall of Famer explained that he and Bubba Ray Dudley were regularly left out of promotional material and commercials, even when other wrestlers lower down the card were featured to sell upcoming live events. D-Von noted that while top stars like Stone Cold Steve Austin were highlighted, fans were simply expected to know the Dudley Boyz would be there.

Despite becoming one of the most successful tag teams in wrestling history after starting with a unique gimmick in ECW inspired by the Hanson Brothers from the film Slap Shot, D-Von admitted that the lack of recognition always bothered him. He also shared that meeting fans at Comic-Cons eventually helped him realize the profound impact the team had on people during difficult times. Additionally, D-Von reflected on being left out of Black History Month celebrations by WWE and discussed his past separation and eventual reunion with Bubba Ray.
