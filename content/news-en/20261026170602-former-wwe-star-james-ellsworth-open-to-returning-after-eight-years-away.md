James Ellsworth believes his story with WWE might not be over, even though years have passed since he was a regular performer for the company. The former WWE star sees himself as a character who can return every few years and immediately get a big reaction, comparing his potential role to that of The Boogeyman.

Ellsworth reflected on his departure in 2017 and 2018, noting that WWE released him before quickly bringing him back on contract. He explained that NXT talent requires a massive investment of time and TV time, which makes it natural for outside talent to cycle out. He believes management views him as a reliable nostalgia act rather than someone who needs a weekly story.

> "I'm a guy like, 'This ain't really our guy. This is a character we could bring back every five or ten years, and people will remember it.' You know, I'm like The Boogeyman or something."

Despite the long absence, Ellsworth insists he is fully open to a return. He believes he could thrive as a full-time manager or an enforcer on TV, similar to his popular run alongside Carmella, where he helped get other talent over without needing to wrestle matches every week.
