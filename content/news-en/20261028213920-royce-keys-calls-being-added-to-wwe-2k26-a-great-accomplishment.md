Royce Keys recently discussed his inclusion in the WWE 2K26 roster, calling it a 'great accomplishment' and expressing his excitement about being a playable character. As part of the Season 6 Ringside Pass, Keys joins Earthquake, Typhoon, and Octagon Jr. as new additions to the game. In an interview with Bleacher Report, Keys explained how meaningful it is for him to finally be featured in a game he has spent years playing.

> "It's a great accomplishment because I think I've played almost every WWE video game, and I've always created myself. And now I don't have to do all the hard work of finding all the right moves, but it's definitely a very high accomplishment for me."

Keys also shared his long-standing passion for the WWE gaming franchise, noting that he enjoys playing current titles with his children. He recalled his favorite games from his childhood, specifically mentioning Royal Rumble on the Super Nintendo and No Mercy on the Nintendo 64. He also expressed his love for the SmackDown vs. Raw series, the 2K games, and even older titles like WrestleFest.
