New viewership data shows a slight increase in numbers for the WWE SmackDown episode that aired on Friday, October 30, 2026, on USA Network compared to the previous week. According to Ringside News, the broadcast brought in 1,162,000 viewers and secured a 0.26 rating in the key 18–49 demographic. By comparison, the October 23 episode attracted 1,100,000 viewers with a 0.24 demo rating, marking an increase of 62,000 viewers and a 0.02 rise in the key demographic. On a year-over-year basis, the October 31, 2025 episode drew 933,000 viewers with a 0.2 demo rating, representing a 24.5 percent increase in viewership and a 30 percent boost in the demo. These numbers reflect only linear television viewership on USA Network and do not include digital streaming platforms.
