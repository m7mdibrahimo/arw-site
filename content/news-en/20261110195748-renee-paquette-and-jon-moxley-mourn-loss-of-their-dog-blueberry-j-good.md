Renee Paquette and Jon Moxley are mourning the death of their beloved dog, Blue, who has passed away after nearly 11 years with the family. Paquette shared the news on Instagram, revealing that Blue died the previous night. She noted that while pet owners know this day will eventually come, Blue had always remained so full of life that she truly believed he might live forever. Blue had been a central part of their lives since long before their marriage, serving as one of only two attendees at their wedding and welcoming their daughter Nora home from the hospital as a constant, faithful shadow.

Paquette recalled many of the unique quirks and fond memories she will miss, from thunderstorms and windshield wipers on television to his distinct turkey gobble bark and forceful pawing for snacks. She also shared that while Blue loved affection from her, his time with Moxley involved wrestling around the house. Paquette concluded her tribute by expressing gratitude that they were able to say goodbye, noting there is now a giant Blue-shaped hole in their lives.
