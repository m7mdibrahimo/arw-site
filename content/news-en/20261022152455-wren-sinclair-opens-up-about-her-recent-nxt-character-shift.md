Wren Sinclair has discussed the recent changes to her on-screen persona, explaining her turn toward a more aggressive side in NXT. Sinclair is scheduled to challenge for the NXT Women's Championship at the Halloween Havoc event.

Speaking in an interview with TMZ Inside The Ring, Sinclair reflected on the frustration that led to her new attitude after repeatedly being pushed around on television and social media.

> "I definitely think so. I feel like we are people at the end of the day but when we are so publicly out there and on social media, the things that people say to you and the responses you get from people, it does affect you in a sense. Me to my core, I'm not going to let someone push me around. So, to get pushed around and eat it and to get pushed around and then eat it again, it definitely pulled on who I am as a person. So, I think I was very excited to finally bite back in lack of better terms."
