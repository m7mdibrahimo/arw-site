The Sandman is preparing for an in-ring return, as Battle Championship Wrestling in Australia announced a match between him and DCT on Friday, December 4, 2026, at BCW 10th Anniversary.

DCT requested the bout, expressing his desire to experience the atmosphere of the ECW legend known for his Singapore cane. This event marks The Sandman's first appearance in Battle Championship Wrestling, where he promised to bring his signature extreme style.

The match will take place under no-rules conditions at the Whitehorse Club.
