Ken Anderson recently discussed the creation of his "Mic Check" finishing move during an interview with Rewind, Recap, Relive. He explained that the move was developed after The Undertaker made it clear he would not take Anderson's previous finisher, the "Green Bay Plunge."

Anderson noted that while he was in the locker room, JBL asked The Undertaker if he would ever take the Green Bay Plunge, to which The Undertaker responded, "I'm not ever taking that move." Anderson realized he needed a move that any opponent could safely take. He mentioned that the circumstances behind the Green Bay Plunge were already unique, as it was originally a last-minute addition to his repertoire.

Following The Undertaker's refusal, Anderson worked with Johnny Ace, who suggested he use a flatliner. Anderson then refined the move by adding his own style. When it came time to name the new finisher, Jim Ross asked him what he was going to call it, and Anderson replied, "Mic Check one, two, three. Mic Check."
