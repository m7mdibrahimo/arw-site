John Cena made his WWE debut on the June 27, 2002, episode of WWE SmackDown, where he faced Kurt Angle. While the match launched one of the most storied careers in WWE history, Cena recently shared a story about the backstage hazing he endured that day. During a Q&A session in New York, Cena recalled, "In 2002, I went to Chicago as an extra talent–someone not to be used. I was then told I was going to have a match against an Olympic gold medalist. Randy Orton tied my boots together in a locker where I couldn’t even reach them, so I had to cut the laces and wrestle with half of my laces. I had to go off campus to get one of the worst haircuts that people watching on television had ever seen." Despite the rough start, Cena expressed gratitude for the experience, noting, "That was my welcome to the WWE, and it was better than I ever could have imagined. A moment that was supposed to be celebratory was also humbling. It was filled with tough love that belongs to our fraternity, and I was grateful to go through that." Orton and Cena went on to become major rivals throughout their careers. Cena is scheduled to appear on WWE RAW and WWE NXT this week.
