Bandido recently discussed teaming with Brody King, joking about how Brody always uses his body as a weapon during their matches. The duo has achieved significant success together, holding the AEW World Tag Team Championships as Brodido and becoming two-time AEW World Trios Champions alongside Hangman Adam Page, most recently winning the titles at AEW WrestleDream. Speaking in a new interview with Good Karma Wrestling, Bandido praised Brody's understanding of his style, noting that Brody learned lucha libre when he was young from a luchador trainer. In another recent interview, Bandido recalled his final conversation with PAC.
