Chad Gable recently shared details about WWE's initial plans for the El Grande Americano character before his injury halted the storyline. Speaking on a recent podcast, Gable explained that the gimmick was designed to mock lucha libre culture by falsely claiming he was the best luchador in the world. 

> "So from there, we continued kind of this assault, right, on the whole lucha libre culture and all this stuff. And I did it as both characters," Gable said.

Although an injury sidelined him and another performer took over the mask, Gable admitted he was grateful that the character continued to thrive on television while he recovered.
