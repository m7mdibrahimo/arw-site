WWE Hall of Famer Mick Foley recently took to his YouTube channel to share a direct message with Americans ahead of the midterm elections, urging voters to support Democratic candidates. Foley, who identifies as a registered independent voter, argued that Congress has failed to keep President Donald Trump in check and called him the most corrupt president in American history. He criticized lawmakers for lacking the courage to stand up to the administration, pointing to issues such as the handling of USAID, the Epstein files, and the war in Iran. Foley also reflected on his own career and physical toll, comparing his sacrifices in professional wrestling to the financial gains of figures like Elon Musk. He concluded by asking voters to temporarily put aside their normal party preferences to provide a necessary check on power in Washington.
