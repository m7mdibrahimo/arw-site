Former WWE Champion Big E has officially married Kris Yim in a ceremony attended by family, friends, and wrestling stars. A clip shared online showed the couple celebrating their big moment, with Seth Rollins and Becky Lynch among the familiar WWE faces present. The wedding comes nearly a year and a half after Big E announced their engagement in May 2025. Kris Yim is the sister of longtime WWE star Michin, connecting Big E even further to the wrestling family.
