During the 2013 World Series of Fighting 2 event, Anthony Johnson earned a unanimous decision victory over Andrei Arlovski. Arlovski faced a knockdown late in the opening round, though the bell provided him with a reprieve. Johnson mistakenly believed the bout had ended when the referee intervened, but the official was only signaling the conclusion of the round.

Despite suffering a broken jaw early in the contest, Arlovski managed to compete for another ten minutes and secured one round on the scorecards. Johnson later died in November 2022 from organ failure.

In an interview with David Guttman, Arlovski maintained his belief that he was the superior fighter that night.

> "Unfortunately, the judges gave him the victory. I'm the winner anyway. He's dead. I'm alive."

Arlovski remained an active MMA competitor until 2024.
