One of the security guards who helped separate Myles Borne and Tavion Heights during a heated segment on WWE NXT was actually a WWE prospect. The guard was identified as Conan Lycan, a wrestler with independent scene experience in promotions such as CSW, WWN, and HWA.

Lycan is currently part of the WWE ID program after receiving a contract from AJ Styles earlier this month. The segment featured security intervening as Heights and Borne exchanged insults, with Borne pointing to his NXT North American Championship victory over Ethan Page and Heights promising a fight.

Danhausen interrupted the confrontation to announce that Borne and Heights will settle their rivalry in a Weapons Wild Match at WWE NXT Halloween Havoc. While Lycan only played a background role in controlling the situation, his appearance marks an early television placement alongside established NXT talent following his WWE ID signing.
