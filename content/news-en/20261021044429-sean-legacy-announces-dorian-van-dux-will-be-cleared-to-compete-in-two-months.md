Sean Legacy provided an update on Dorian Van Dux during the October 20th episode of WWE NXT on The CW. Speaking alongside Romero Moreno, E.K. Prosper, Chazz 'Starboy' Hall, Noam Dar, and Saquon Shugars, Legacy shared that doctors expect Van Dux to be cleared to compete in two months. Van Dux had been sidelined following a punctured lung injury, though it was previously announced on September 27th that he was cleared. His last match took place on September 15th.
