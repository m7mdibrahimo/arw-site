TNA recently battled travel issues caused by FAA grounding, leading to reconfigured and impromptu matches at Destination X 2026. Wrestling Inc. staff shared their thoughts on the highs and lows of the show, highlighting both the successes and shortcomings of the event.

Among the things they hated were the lackluster pre-show and a controversial finish. The pre-show featured very little in-ring action, dedicating most of its time to video packages and travel updates, alongside a segment with Elijah and Vincent. Meanwhile, the Mustafa Ali versus Frankie Kazarian match ended in a no-contest when the referee called for the bell after they refused to break in the corner, which frustrated fans given the pay-per-view stakes. Another low point was the No Disqualification match between Matt Hardy and Joseph Sawyer, which critics felt relied too heavily on clumsy weapon spots.

On the positive side, the show had plenty of strong moments. The women's Ultimate X match for the Knockouts World Championship delivered fast-paced action and strong storytelling, highlighting the tension between M By Elegance and Heather By Elegance while keeping Shayna Baszler looking dominant. Additionally, despite the roster disruptions, TNA managed to put together a solid card, featuring the return of TJP for the first time since 2021 and the addition of AJ Francis, who brought unique match dynamics to the Men's Ultimate X contest.
