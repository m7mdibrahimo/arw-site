Erick Redbeard, formerly known as Erick Rowan in WWE, made his TNA debut at Bound for Glory 2026. He appeared to assist Syx, Joseph Sawyer, and Samuel Shaw during their falls count anywhere match against The Hardys. Redbeard previously worked with Sawyer and Shaw, then known as Dexter Lumis and Joe Gacy, in WWE's Wyatt Sicks stable before their release following WrestleMania 42.

Sawyer and Shaw debuted in TNA in late September after a series of backstage attacks on the roster. They revealed themselves as the culprits on the September 24 episode of TNA iMPACT, attacking Matt and Jeff Hardy. At Bound for Glory, the teams fought on the entrance ramp, where the Hardys positioned their opponents on two tables. Before the Hardys could climb the surrounding ladders, Redbeard interfered and sent them through the tables. Redbeard then carried Jeff Hardy to the ring, while Shaw handled Matt Hardy. Sawyer and Shaw finished off the Hardys in the ring, allowing Syx to secure the victory.
