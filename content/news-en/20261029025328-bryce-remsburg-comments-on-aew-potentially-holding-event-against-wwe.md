All Elite Wrestling has a significant opportunity to capitalize on the weekend of WWE WrestleMania 43, which is set to take place in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, next year. AEW could potentially host a major event in the United States during that same period. While AEW President Tony Khan has not confirmed any plans, he has acknowledged that it is "something to consider." In a recent update regarding what might happen in April 2027, AEW referee Bryce Remsburg spoke on "The Certified Mark Show" but remained noncommittal.

> "I think I can say that conversations are being had around what we might do that weekend, how about that? There are some factors out of our control that would contribute to our decisions, so we'll kind of see what happens."

It was reported in July that WWE anticipated a "significant presence" from AEW in the states during the WrestleMania weekend. WWE has already confirmed several events in New York City for that time, including a WrestleMania watch party, AAA Eternal Glory, and "WWE NXT" Stand & Deliver, with more fan experiences expected to be announced soon. Although official dates for WrestleMania 43 have not been set, reports indicate that the weekend of April 10 and 11 is being targeted. AEW has already scheduled events for early 2027, such as "AEW Dynamite: New Year's Smash" in Atlanta and AEW Dynasty in Liverpool on February 7.
