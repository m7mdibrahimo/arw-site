Deonna Purrazzo believes that simply increasing the number of women's matches on television is not enough to improve the division. In a recent interview with Mike Pendleton of BTC Studios/BTT Conversation alongside Steve Maclin, Purrazzo argued that the lack of character development is the primary issue. She explained that without vignettes, backstage interactions, and daily television segments, audiences are not given enough reason to invest in the performers.

Purrazzo cited her own time in AEW as a prime example, noting that she has not received a vignette in approximately 18 months. She argued that because viewers do not know enough about her persona as "The Virtuosa," they are less likely to connect with her during matches. Purrazzo emphasized that developing relatable or polarizing characters is the key to securing longer storylines, more television time, and main event opportunities for women in wrestling.
