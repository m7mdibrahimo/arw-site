Game Changer Wrestling held its GCW Dead On Arrival event on November 6, 2026, from Pop's Nightclub & Concert Venue in Sauget, Illinois. The event aired live on TrillerTV+. Full results from the show are detailed below.

Bustah & The Brain (Alec Price & Jordan Oliver) & Jeffrey John def. Unsigned And Don't Care (Anakin Murphy, Gary Jay & Aaron Williams)

Tim Bosby def. Marko Stunt

Haley J def. Leon King, Rachel Armstrong, and Nixi XS

Jimmy Lloyd, Sal Mistretta, Caine Carter & Kavron Kanyon def. Logan Cavazos, JJ Garrett, Bax & Blackwell

Joey Janela (w/ Ryan O'Neill) def. Juni Underwood

GCW World Tag Team Championships: KJ Orso & Sam Stackhouse (c) def. Vipress & Megan Bayne to retain the titles

VNDL48 (Otis Cogar & Christian Napier) def. Nino Extremo & Mr. Danger

Deathmatch: Sicko The Clown def. Neil Diamond Cutter
