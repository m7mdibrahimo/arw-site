Michael Cole recently opened up about the return of CM Punk to WWE, admitting he initially had questions regarding how Punk would fit into the current locker room environment. Despite their close relationship during Punk's first tenure, the two lost touch after Punk's departure, leading to years of silence between them. However, Cole stated that any uncertainty vanished once Punk returned, noting that their professional relationship resumed as if no time had passed.

> "Punk and I were real close when he was here the first time around. Obviously, he leaves the company. This business is weird because you can be best friends with someone while they're working here, and then they leave, and you might not talk to them for a few years. That was my situation with Punk. Listen, business is business. I learned that a long time ago when I started here. Never say never to anything in WWE. I was excited when Punk was going to come back. How was he going to act? I wasn't sure. How was our locker room going to take his return? I wasn't sure. I knew I was going to do business, and I've had no problem with him, and we talk all the time, and it's almost like we didn't miss a day."

Regarding past comments Punk made about him, Cole emphasized that he refuses to carry grudges, viewing them as detrimental to a career in professional wrestling. He explained that his priority has always been to focus on the company's success, including ticket sales and fan entertainment, rather than dwelling on old disagreements.

> "Listen, there were a few things directed toward me over the years that Punk had come out and said, but this is the wrestling business. You can't hold a grudge. If you take anything personal in our business, you won't survive. There's a reason I've been here for 30 years. You have to just let it slide down your back, move on with a smile on your face, and do business. That's what it's about."

Cole concluded that he is pleased to have Punk back, noting that Punk now has the unique opportunity to become the first person to win the Money in the Bank briefcase three times.
