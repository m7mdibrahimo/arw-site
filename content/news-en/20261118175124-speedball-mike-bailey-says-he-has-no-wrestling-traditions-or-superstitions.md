Speedball Mike Bailey stated that he does not believe in any traditions or superstitions regarding professional wrestling, emphasizing that he is entirely skeptical of such things.

During a new interview, Speedball Mike Bailey discussed daily challenges and continuous wrestling preparations, pointing out the difficulty of competing year-round without off-seasons between AEW Dynamite on Wednesday and AEW Collision on Saturday.

When asked about pre-match rituals or superstitions, he answered directly: "I'm a complete skeptic. I have no, like, traditions or superstitions." He added that he has traveled the globe, taking 19 years to reach AEW and become one of the top wrestlers in the world, asserting that his twenty years of experience make daily rituals unnecessary.
