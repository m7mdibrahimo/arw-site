Bandido endured a grueling experience at AEW Full Gear, and fans are now seeing the true physical toll of his Texas Death Match. The former AEW World Trios Champion suffered a deep head wound during the bloody title contest that required stitches. Following the November 14 event, Bandido shared a graphic photo on X revealing the extent of the damage, which needed to be closed with medical sutures.

This outcome is hardly surprising given the extreme nature of the match. Bandido teamed with Hangman Page and Brody King to defend their AEW World Trios Championships against Swerve Strickland, Kofi Kingston, and Austin Creed. The Texas Death Match featured an array of dangerous weapons, including barbed wire, thumbtacks, staple guns, and an exploding Power Glove. Bandido took significant punishment, including attacks with chains and padlocks, though he managed to strike back with a frog splash onto Strickland into thumbtacks.

The match eventually narrowed down to Strickland and Hangman Page. Page connected with a Buckshot Lariat, but Strickland got back up, stopped another attempt with a chair shot, and beat the referee's ten-count. Strickland then drilled Page with a House Call, and Page could not answer the ten-count, giving Strickland, Kingston, and Creed the win as the new AEW World Trios Champions. Bandido left the arena without the titles and with a stitched-up head, providing clear evidence of the brutality of the Texas Death Match.
