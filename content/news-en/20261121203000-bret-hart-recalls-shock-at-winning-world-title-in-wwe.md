Bret Hart looked back on his WWE Championship win against Ric Flair at a live event in Saskatoon during the fall of 1992. Speaking on the Johnny I Pro Show, Hart admitted he never anticipated capturing the belt, noting that the triumph completely transformed his career and finally brought him the recognition he always wanted.

> "I didn't see it coming."

> "Didn't expect it. And it was just...it was, for sure, going to change my life, and for sure going to...it was finally going to give me that credit that I maybe never got from anywhere else."

> "I was kind of, I was always like 'You hear Bret Hart's a good wrestler, is he really?' I don't know. it's like I think...I always wanted someone to verify that I was actually any good."

He also shared the mixed backstage reactions to his victory, mentioning that his brother Owen was thrilled, whereas Tom Billington, Hulk Hogan, and Davey Boy Smith reacted negatively. Hart viewed those responses as evidence of the intense competitiveness and professional rivalry among wrestlers behind the scenes.

> "Maybe in his case he was thinking 'It could've been me, but they picked him.'"

> "I could see it with Davey a little bit. But Dynamite was kind of already over...I always thought he would've been happy. Hogan was another one too. I always thought he would've been very happy...It just goes to show you that it's a very competitive business with stuff like that."
