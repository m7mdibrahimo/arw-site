Elayna Black once again fell short in her pursuit of the TNA Knockouts Television Championship due to the title's strict time limit. During the October 9 episode of TNA iMPACT, Black challenged the inaugural champion, Ash By Elegance. Despite Ash requesting that her Elegance Brand associates be banned from ringside, the champion still utilized underhanded tactics, including an attempt to stomp on Black's hand against the steel ring steps. The back-and-forth contest saw Black land a suplex on the floor, while Ash countered with a series of kicks and an arm bar submission. In the closing moments, Black delivered her signature Black Out maneuver, but the match ended before the referee could complete the pinfall count as the ten-minute time limit expired. This marks Black's second time limit draw in tournament competition, following her opening round bout against Wendy Choo. Ash By Elegance originally secured the title by defeating Jada Stone in a tournament final on August 27.
