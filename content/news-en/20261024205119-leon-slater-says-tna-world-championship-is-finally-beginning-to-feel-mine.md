Leon Slater recently discussed his historic TNA World Championship victory and how it has taken time to fully sink in. At TNA Bound for Glory, Slater defeated Nic Nemeth to become the youngest TNA World Champion in the company's history, achieving his major career goal five years ahead of his own predictions. Speaking on the I Love Wrestling Podcast, Slater opened up about the surreal experience of holding the prestigious title while relaxing at home. 

> It feels very surreal, man. It took a while for it to settle in because I kind of felt like I was carrying Nic Nemeth’s belt around for a while just because I’ve so used to seeing him with it, Slater said.

Slater shared a lighthearted story about playing video games with his friends on PlayStation while having the championship displayed on his TV table, realizing it finally belongs to him after seeing it in the same room where he has spent the last decade. Looking ahead, Slater is scheduled to defend the championship against Trey Miguel at TNA Destination X as he aims to steer the title into a brand new direction.
