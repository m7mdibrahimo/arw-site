AEW has started discussing potential activities to hold during the WrestleMania 43 weekend in 2027. With WWE taking the event to Saudi Arabia that year, AEW could find a solid opening to host its own show in the United States.

Speaking on The Certified Mark Show, according to Ringside News, AEW referee and travel department manager Bryce Remsburg was asked if the company is considering taking advantage of WrestleMania heading overseas. He confirmed that discussions are currently underway, though no official announcements have been made yet.

> "Yeah, I think I can say that conversations are being had around what we might do that weekend. How about that?"

AEW President Tony Khan previously showed interest in organizing a major event in the spring of 2027 during that same period. Since WrestleMania is moving to Saudi Arabia, the usual setup for independent wrestling shows in the United States will experience a noticeable change.
