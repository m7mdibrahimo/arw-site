Laynie Luck will continue to appear on WWE programming through the end of 2026 due to a backlog of recorded content. Luck's contract under the WWE I.D. program concluded after she lost the WWE I.D. Women's Championship to Monica Monroe. During her reign, Luck recorded several matches for the WWE EVOLVE program. She confirmed that due to the taping schedule and the release plan for these episodes, she will remain a fixture on the show for the rest of the year.

> "I mean, they keep putting stuff out weekly as the plan is — yeah — it’s definitely for the year. It’s saved up… It’s bittersweet maybe because it’s like, there’s some good stuff coming out and I wish I was able to be watching it in a different kind of scenario or whatever but, I am still very excited for the stories that are coming out. We’re doing good stuff on EVOLVE."

Luck held the WWE I.D. Women's Championship for over 300 days before her departure from the program.
