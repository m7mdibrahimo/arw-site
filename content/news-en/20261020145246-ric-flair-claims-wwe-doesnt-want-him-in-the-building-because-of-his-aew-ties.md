Ric Flair believes his relationship with AEW is the reason WWE no longer wants him backstage at their events. The WWE Hall of Famer explained that his connection to Tony Khan's company makes it difficult to attend shows where his daughter, Charlotte Flair, is wrestling. Speaking during an interview on NewsNation's BIFF, Flair noted that while he watches all of Charlotte's matches, attending in person has become an issue. He clarified that there are no personal issues with WWE talent or management, but rather a simple matter of corporate competition.
