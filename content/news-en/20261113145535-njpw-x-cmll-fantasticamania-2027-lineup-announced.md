New Japan Pro-Wrestling and Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre have announced the full participant list for the upcoming NJPW x CMLL Fantasticamania 2027 tour. The event is scheduled to kick off on February 17 at the Sendai Sun Plaza Hall, followed by a three-night residency at Korakuen Hall from February 25 to February 27.

The roster includes a mix of established stars and Fantasticamania debutants. Confirmed participants are Mistico, Mascara Dorada, Titán, Templario, Neón, Esfinge, Xelhua, Hijo del Pantera, Ultimo Guerrero, Hechicero, Soberano Jr., Averno, Black Tiger, YUTANI, OKUMURA, and El Coyote. Esfinge, Hijo del Pantera, YUTANI, and El Coyote are all set to make their first appearances at the event.
