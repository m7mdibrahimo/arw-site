WWE has officially added a singles match between Zilla Fatu and Dion Lennox to tonight's episode of WWE NXT, November 17, 2026. The two have been feuding for the past week, and the promotion confirmed the contest via its official NXT X account. The match follows a heated segment on the November 10 episode, where Fatu mocked Lennox for a recent loss, leading to a brawl involving BirthRight and Los Perros del Mal. Fatu, who was personally selected by John Cena for the upcoming John Cena Classic, is currently pursuing the NXT Championship held by Grayson Waller. Tonight's WWE NXT takes place at the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, and will be broadcast on The CW. The lineup also features Kelani Jordan defending the NXT Women's Championship against Kendal Grey, a match between Tony D'Angelo and Kam Hendrix, and a Triple Threat Match involving Tristan Angels, Sean Legacy and Saquon Shugars.
