WWE RAW has gone through various creative teams, roster changes, and a new era under Triple H, but its internal production format has remained virtually unchanged for over twenty years. Speaking on Wrestling Observer Radio, former WWE writer and MLW owner Court Bauer discussed how professional wrestling television has evolved. Bauer noted that while the overall presentation looks different today, the actual run sheet used to organize RAW's production is nearly identical to the format used when he worked for the company in the mid-2000s. Dave Meltzer compared this to Eric Bischoff's enduring influence on modern wrestling television, noting that Bischoff established a format away from squash matches toward weekly programs featuring major storylines, a blueprint that companies like WWE and WCW have followed for decades. Bauer expressed surprise that the internal structure has stayed untouched despite WWE transitioning into the streaming era and changing its creative leadership under Vince McMahon and Triple H.
