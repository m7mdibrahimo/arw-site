Mark Sterling, serving as legal counsel for MJF, has issued an official statement regarding an incident involving a fan at AEW Full Gear. Following the main event between Will Ospreay and Kyle Fletcher for the AEW World Championship, MJF made his return, struck Ospreay with the Triple B belt, and fled the ring as United Empire arrived to help. As he exited, he accidentally kicked a fan in the face.

MJF later addressed the incident on social media, writing,

> "Also tell that lady she should watch where she’s going."

Mark Sterling then released a formal statement from the legal counsel, explaining that following a thorough review, Maxwell Jacob Friedman has chosen not to pursue legal remedies against the fan who obstructed his path. Sterling noted that while his client had grounds for civil damages, he graciously decided against pressing charges at this time. The statement concluded by emphasizing that this decision is not an admission of fault or a waiver of legal rights, ending with,

> "You're welcome."
