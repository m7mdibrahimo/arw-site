Cody Rhodes recently stated that he would rather have Guile's haircut for a year than wear the Stardust face paint for a single day. The new Street Fighter film hit theaters on October 16, featuring Cody Rhodes as Guile and Roman Reigns as Akuma. Speaking with Overtime, Rhodes was asked whether he would prefer wearing the Stardust face paint for 14 days or keeping the Guile haircut for a full year. "I mean, I would rather always do the Guile haircut. I mean, a year is a long time, so you're committing. I can't see a world where I would want the Stardust face paint for one day. So I definitely would go with the Guile flat top," Rhodes said. Stardust was a character from Rhodes' early career in WWE, and he recently signed a five-year contract extension with the company.
