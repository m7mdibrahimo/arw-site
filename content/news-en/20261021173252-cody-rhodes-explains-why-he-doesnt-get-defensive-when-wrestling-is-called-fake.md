Cody Rhodes has explained why he chooses not to get defensive when people call professional wrestling "fake." During an appearance on Andrew Schulz's Flagrant podcast, Rhodes discussed how some wrestlers immediately react with stories about injuries when confronted with the term, but he prefers a different approach.

Rhodes noted that getting defensive can often halt an interview and turn it into an argument. Instead, he focuses on the genuine emotional reactions fans experience while watching live events, believing those real feelings are what truly matter. He also shared his philosophy on keeping his composure and delivering sound bites when opportunities arise during media appearances.
