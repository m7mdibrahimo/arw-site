Tommaso Ciampa has been with AEW for less than a year, but the "Psycho Killer" has made a major impact since joining the promotion in January. Beyond winning the AEW TNT Championship and competing on major cards, Ciampa is particularly pleased with how the move has benefited his family life. In a recent interview with SBNation, Ciampa discussed how the transition from WWE provided new opportunities for his wife and daughter, Willow.

> "The amount of times we've traveled together: London, Scotland, Montreal. My hope is to get them out to Mexico next time, or even France, because it was so cool out there too. We've been doing a lot of trips. Willow and my wife came to Chicago and she had her big on-screen moment with Okada. There's so many things that have happened in the last 10 months that wouldn't have happened if we didn't make the move when we did. Looking back at it all, it's incredible to think of the experiences that we're all gaining, specifically Willow, because a lot of what I do nowadays is pretty much all for her. It's just been a total blessing."

The AEW production team supported the family at AEW All Out 2026. In addition to being at ringside for his match against Kazuchika Okada, Willow received a special birthday entrance before the event, featuring her father's entrance music followed by a birthday song.
