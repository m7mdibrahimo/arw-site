A significant development has emerged regarding the murder of independent wrestler BRAXX, as a suspect was taken into custody months after the fatal shooting in Philadelphia. According to reports, Dennis Patton was arrested on July 24, 2026, during a traffic stop in Lebanon City, Pennsylvania, following an active warrant issued by Philadelphia police. The victim, Walter Holloway, known to fans as BRAXX, was 31 years old when he was fatally shot in the Point Breeze neighborhood on May 6, 2026. This arrest provides a measure of progress in a case that deeply affected the independent wrestling scene. BRAXX was a recognized performer who frequently competed for promotions such as Matt Tremont's H2O Wrestling, and his passing continues to be mourned by his peers.
