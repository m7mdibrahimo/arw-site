Michael Cole was missing from the October 16 episode of WWE SmackDown at the Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, California. According to PWInsider, Cole was simply on vacation and taking some time away from his duties. Vic Joseph stepped in to provide commentary alongside Wade Barrett in his absence. Cole is under a long-term agreement with WWE, and there is no indication that his absence is related to his future with the company.
