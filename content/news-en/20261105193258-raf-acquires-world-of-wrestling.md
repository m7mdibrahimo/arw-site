Real American Freestyle (RAF) has acquired World of Wrestling, a promotion focused on youth amateur wrestling that has been operating since 1979. The acquisition was announced via social media on Thursday, November 5. In a statement, RAF noted that World of Wrestling is the home of the Tulsa Nationals, Reno Worlds, and the Kickoff Classic, stating, "The Roller family built something that generations of wrestlers carry with them. We’re excited to work alongside them and build on what makes these tournaments so special." The promotion is scheduled to host various events in Abu Dhabi, Chicago, Long Island, and Charlotte over the coming weeks.
