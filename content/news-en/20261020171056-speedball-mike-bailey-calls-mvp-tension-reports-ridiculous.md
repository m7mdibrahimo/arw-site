Speedball Mike Bailey recently reflected on past internet reports suggesting that they and MVP did not get along, describing the claims as the most ridiculous premise on earth. The rumors began after the two joked about tension in online videos, where they frequently exchanged insults and playful banter. Bailey explained that the story gained significant traction online because some fans enjoy that type of drama, prompting them to lean into the joke with comedic videos. Despite the playful online rivalry, Bailey noted that they and MVP share a great mutual respect, even continuing to greet each other with humorous insults to this day.
