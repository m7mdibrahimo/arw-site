WWE Crown Jewel is currently taking place in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, but fans watching at home are experiencing a minor tape delay. According to False Finish, the broadcast is running on roughly a 10-to-15-minute delay compared to what the live crowd inside the arena is seeing. The delay is reportedly due to WWE's production setup for the event. With only a minimal production crew working on the ground in Riyadh, the majority of the production is being handled from Stamford. That extra buffer gives WWE enough time to ensure everything runs smoothly between Saudi Arabia and Connecticut. As a result, Cody Rhodes and Randy Orton kicked off the show with their Last Man Standing Match while the delay was already in effect. This also means results and spoilers could potentially leak online before home viewers see them on the broadcast.
