Longtime commentator Michael Cole has simplified his schedule in recent years by stepping away from several major backstage responsibilities in WWE to focus exclusively on his on-air duties. During an appearance with Bobby Burack of OutKick, Cole explained that his workload reached a point where he wanted to transition solely into on-screen talent after managing numerous roles over his decades with the company.

> "In my first 26, 27 years in the company, I was not only an announcer, but I had many different roles. I was involved in live-event production early on in my career. I ran the WWE website at one point in my career. I was basically in charge of all the announcers in the company for over 10 years. And a couple of years ago, I decided I just wanted to be a talent."

Cole noted that while removing those administrative duties made his routine easier, his travel schedule remains demanding with around 200 to 230 days on the road annually due to television shows, premium live events, and international tours. Beyond broadcasting, Cole spends significant time recording video game voiceovers and conducting extensive statistical research for broadcast preparation.
