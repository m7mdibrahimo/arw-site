Tommaso Ciampa recently revived his old obsession with hitting Randy Orton with an RKO. While in St. Louis for AEW Dynamite, Ciampa attempted to track down The Viper to finally execute the move. However, he mistakenly stalked and targeted Kyle Fletcher instead. After lunging for the move and realizing his mistake, Ciampa quickly walked away, leaving Fletcher confused and asking who he had mistaken him for. This incident follows a series of failed attempts from 2024 where Ciampa repeatedly tried to surprise Orton with his finisher.
