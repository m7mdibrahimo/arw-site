Revolution Pro Wrestling held its RevPro Live In London event on November 1 at Porchester Hall in London, England. The results were as follows:

Slater Dojo (HMD and Liam Slater) (w/ George Hunter) def. Contenders Division (Chicago Desailly and Isaac Murray) in a Great British Tag League qualifier.

Kanji def. Abigail.

CPF (Danny Black and Joe Lando) def. THE FRESHNAS (Martin Steers and Fabio) in a Great British Tag League qualifier.

Michael Oku (w/ Amira Blair) def. JJ Gale.

FRIENDS (Leon Cage and Maverick Mayhew) def. ACT TWO (Jack Knudson and Benjamin Harland) in a Great British Tag League qualifier.

Aluna def. Alexxis Falcon (w/ Shaun Jackson).

Jay Joshua def. Luke Jacobs (w/ Ethan Allen).

Connor Mills (c) def. Thomas Shire to retain the Undisputed British Heavyweight Championship.
