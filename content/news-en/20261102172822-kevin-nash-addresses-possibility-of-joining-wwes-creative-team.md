Kevin Nash discussed the possibility of joining WWE’s creative team during a recent podcast appearance, noting that while the idea sounds fun, he believes he would quickly irritate Triple H. Speaking on the Kliq This podcast, Nash responded to reports about Shawn Michaels potentially replacing Bruce Prichard on creative, leading him to joke about getting involved himself. Nash initially suggested he could work from home via Zoom calls to talk wrestling, but quickly realized that effective booking requires deep personal familiarity with the main-roster talent. He admitted that he would need to fully immerse himself in the product and spend time around the wrestlers rather than running creative from his couch. Furthermore, Nash acknowledged that his constant joking and desire to make people laugh would likely get on Triple H’s nerves over time. Ultimately, at 67 years old, Nash concluded that the daily work and travel required for the role simply do not appeal to him.
