WWE star Becky Lynch recently discussed her home life with husband Seth Rollins, detailing how the couple manages disagreements away from the wrestling ring. She emphasized that their domestic disputes never escalate into physical confrontations, contrary to what some fans might imagine. During an appearance on the program So You Think You Can Sports, Lynch praised Rollins as an incredible and amazing wrestler. When asked if their arguments ever involve using wrestling maneuvers at home, she firmly denied the possibility.

> "No, never."

Lynch explained that they rely solely on conversation and discussion to resolve any differences of opinion. She jokingly admitted that she might be the one who initiates arguments more frequently, while maintaining her deep respect for her husband both inside and outside the ring.
