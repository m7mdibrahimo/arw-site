Rey Horus is making his return to TNA as part of the World X-Cup festivities at Destination X, marking his first match for the promotion since August 2022. The promotion announced his participation on November 6, making him the only officially announced name for the tournament so far. The event takes place on November 15, featuring Leon Slater defending the TNA World Championship against Trey Miguel, Indi Hartwell defending the TNA Knockouts World Championship against Shayna Baszler, and Syx consisting of Joseph Sawyer and Samuel Shaw facing Matt Hardy and Moose.
