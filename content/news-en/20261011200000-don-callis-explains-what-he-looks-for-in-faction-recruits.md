In a recent appearance on Poisonrana, Don Callis discussed the criteria he uses when recruiting members for the AEW faction Don Callis Family. Callis stated that while physical potential matters, he primarily looks for a specific mental mindset and emotional stability in a talent. He pointed to Josh Alexander as an example, noting that Alexander previously struggled with imposter syndrome before joining the group. Callis explained that Alexander now benefits from a strong support system and is positioned to compete at a high level alongside members like Kyle Fletcher and Konosuke Takeshita. Callis also claimed that there are additional prospects associated with the faction who have not yet been revealed to the public. However, with the group's numbers currently high, Callis noted that he is not actively seeking new recruits at this time.
