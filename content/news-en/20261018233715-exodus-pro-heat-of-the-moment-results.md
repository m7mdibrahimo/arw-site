Exodus Pro Wrestling hosted its Exodus Pro Heat of the Moment event on October 16 at Collision Bend Brewery in Euclid, OH, according to Fightful. Dante Casanova defeated Devious Cass in singles action.

The Fun Police defeated The Exobros consisting of Conner Knox and Johnny Mahalo. LJ Cleary successfully defended the Irish Junior Heavyweight Championship against Ashton Day.

Brandon Barreta defeated J Rocc in a no disqualification match. Cowpoke Paul retained the Crown Royal Championship by defeating Handsome Harley T. Morris and Gunner Cain in a Cowpoke Paul Hoe Down match.

The Exodus Pro Women’s Championship match between J-Rod and Marti Belle ended in a double disqualification after both competitors struck special guest referee Scotti Sosa. Sosa stated that she is taking emergency custodial authority of the title to protect it.

Lord Crewe picked up a victory over Zachary Nystrom. In the main event Cleveland Cup match, Team Cleveland featuring EC3, Joshua Bishop, Atticus Cogar, Otis Cogar, and Christian Napiar defeated Team Northeast consisting of TJ Crawford, Love Doug, Anthony Greene, Richard Holliday, and Hayden Backlund, with Joshua Bishop and Christian Napiar remaining as the sole survivors.
