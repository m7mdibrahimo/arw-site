Cody Rhodes recently shared his thoughts on WWE's food options, declaring that the company offers the best catering in the entire entertainment industry. During his appearance on the Double Coverage Podcast, Rhodes praised the quality and variety available to the production staff and performers.

> "WWE show, we have an unreal catering situation."

Rhodes explained that the setup is remarkably convenient for everyone involved. 

> "There's no meal ticket. It's just open all day. I don't know who it's made for because some of it's really athletic-based. There's your chicken, there's your rice, and then other stuff is just today's taco day. It's all over the place and they can make you anything either. You don't wanna be one of those guys who asks for stuff but the few that they can make it. So I'm very spoiled as far as catering. It is meant to feed a small army that is the WWE production and I don't think there's a better catering in the world than WWE's, which I don't think I've ever said in an interview and I'ma put our flag in the sand. The best catering in all of entertainment is WWE."
