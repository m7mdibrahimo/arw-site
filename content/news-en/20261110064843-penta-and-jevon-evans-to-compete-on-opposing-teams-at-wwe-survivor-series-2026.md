Newly crowned World Tag Team Champions Penta and Je'Von Evans are set to battle on opposite sides at WWE Survivor Series 2026. The upcoming event features a classic five-on-five elimination bout pitting Team USA, captained by Cody Rhodes, against Rey Mysterio's Team Mexico.

On the latest episode of WWE Raw, both Rhodes and Mysterio declined to add Dominik Mysterio to their respective lineups, after which CM Punk offered to join forces with Rhodes. Backstage segments later confirmed the additions to the match, with Mysterio recruiting Penta to Team Mexico while Evans accepted an invitation from Rhodes to join Team USA. The tag team partners will now collide on Saturday, November 28 at Daikin Park in Houston.
