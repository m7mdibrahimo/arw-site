During an appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Sami Zayn reflected on his historic clash against Roman Reigns at WWE Elimination Chamber 2023 in Montreal. The showdown followed his run with The Bloodline and his betrayal of Reigns with a steel chair at Royal Rumble.

Asked if he should have captured the title in his hometown, Zayn pointed to Cody Rhodes and the timing of the storylines, but noted that his victory would not have been ridiculous given the massive fan backing he received.

“At the time, I thought there was a case to be made for it, but going back to the domino effect thing, I don’t know. I really don’t know. I don’t think it’s ludicrous to say I should have. If you were watching at that time, I think anybody could have made that argument. But it was just again timing, and Cody was also red hot at that time, and they were building to something. Then it leads to Cody the next year, and then it’s more gratifying. Whatever it is, just yeah, I don’t know. Could, should, would- would it have worked? I think so. But who’s to say?”

Zayn later headlined the first night of WrestleMania 39, joining forces with Kevin Owens to defeat The Usos (Jimmy Uso & Jey Uso) for the WWE Tag Team Championships.
