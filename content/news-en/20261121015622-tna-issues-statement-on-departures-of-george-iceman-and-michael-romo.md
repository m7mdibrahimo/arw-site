TNA Wrestling announced the departures of George Iceman, known as The Personal Concierge, and referee Michael Romo. According to Fightful, the company released a statement confirming an agreement to end The Personal Concierge's contract immediately while noting that Romo is leaving the promotion as well.

> "TNA Wrestling has come to terms on the release of The Personal Concierge, effective immediately. In addition, referee Michael Romo will no longer be working for TNA Wrestling. No further comment will be made. We wish both the best in their future endeavors."

The Personal Concierge previously performed as part of The Elegance Brand stable, which featured Ash by Elegance, Heather by Elegance, M by Elegance, and Mr. Elegance. Mr. Elegance exited the group previously, and Ash stepped away from competition due to health concerns while expecting a baby boy due in January 2027. The faction currently consists of M by Elegance and Heather, with a growing division between the two shown on TNA programming.
