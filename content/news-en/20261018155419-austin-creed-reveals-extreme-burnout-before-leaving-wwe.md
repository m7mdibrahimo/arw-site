Austin Creed revealed that his final years in WWE brought severe exhaustion after more than two decades of constant wrestling, travel, and digital content creation. During an interview in Australia, he shared that he used a three-month break after leaving the promotion to spend a full summer with his children for the first time in his 23-year career.

Creed explained that his packed schedule between wrestling, running his YouTube channel, and making appearances heavily drained his energy, though he always aimed to secure his future before his physical performance declined. After returning to the ring alongside Kofi Kingston at AEW All In late August and joining Swerve Strickland to capture the AEW World Trios Championship, Creed now maintains a much better balance between his career and personal life.
