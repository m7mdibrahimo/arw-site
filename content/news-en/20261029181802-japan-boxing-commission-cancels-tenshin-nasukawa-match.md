Tenshin Nasukawa will no longer participate in an upcoming bout. TEAM TENSHIN, the group backing the former MMA fighter, kickboxer, and active boxer, is staging an event on October 31st at Matsudo Central Park in Chiba, Japan.

A partnership with DDT Pro-Wrestling was promoted, featuring Nasukawa in a street pro wrestling match. He was scheduled to team with Yuki Ueno and Shinya Aoki in a six-man tag team contest. Following the spread of the announcement, the Japan Boxing Commission released a statement revealing that Nasukawa is barred from performing on the card.

Their rules prohibit professional boxers from taking part in other combat sports, matches, or similar events. Nasukawa holds an 8-2 record in boxing and retired from kickboxing in 2022 with an undefeated 42-0 record.
