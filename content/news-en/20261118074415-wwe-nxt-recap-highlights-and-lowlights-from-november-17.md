As WWE prepares for Survivor Series, the NXT brand is turning its attention toward its first developmental TLC event. The November 17 broadcast advanced two competitors toward the John Cena Classic and concluded with a contentious finish in the main event title match.

Among the negatives, Kam Hendrix defeated Tony D'Angelo after Myles Borne interfered by introducing a chair. Critics felt Hendrix needed more established victories to make this win impactful. Additionally, a three-way match between Sean Legacy, Saquon Shugars, and Tristan Angels was criticized for a lack of substance, ending when Shugars kicked Legacy to allow Angels to secure a pin. Finally, the ongoing storyline involving Thea Hail and Chantel Monroe, which incorporates Hail's real-life hair loss condition, was panned for its questionable creative direction.

On the positive side, Izzi Dame delivered a promo targeting NXT Women's North American Champion Zaria. Her performance was praised for its compelling, delusional character work that enhances their rivalry. Furthermore, the return of intergender wrestling was celebrated, with Myka Lockwood set to join an eight-person tag team match next week. She will team with Ricky Smokes, Brad Baylor, and Jackson Drake against Hank Walker, Tank Ledger, Noam Dar, and Romeo Moreno.

Lastly, the NXT Women's Championship match between Kelani Jordan and Kendal Grey ended in a disqualification. Jordan slapped the referee to escape an ankle lock, allowing her to retain the title via champion's advantage.
