WWE Hall of Famer Tammy "Sunny" Sytch, currently serving a 17-year prison sentence for a 2022 fatal traffic incident in Ormond Beach, Florida, has filed a motion to overturn her conviction. Sytch argues that her defense counsel provided ineffective assistance by failing to investigate the pre-existing cardiovascular and internal medical conditions of 75-year-old Julian Lasseter, who died following the collision. The motion claims that Lasseter's fractured ribs were caused by CPR administered at the scene rather than the low-speed impact itself, and suggests that a medical expert could have led to her acquittal of homicide charges.

Furthermore, Sytch alleges a conflict of interest, stating that the lead Assistant State Attorney, Ashley Terwilliger, was married to the physician who declared Lasseter's death, a relationship that was not disclosed during the trial. Judge Christian Miller of the Volusia County Circuit Court has dismissed the motion, noting that it lacked official facts and failed to comply with criminal procedure requirements. Judge Miller warned that pursuing further appeals could risk a longer sentence if she were to be retried. Sytch is currently scheduled for release in December 2039.
