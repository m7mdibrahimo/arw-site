Road Dogg has addressed reports claiming he was fired from WWE due to his support for Chelsea Green, dismissing the allegations as entirely false. Brian James clarified that the actual reason for his departure was that he resigned from the company.

Jonathan Coachman previously alleged on The Coach and Bro Show alongside Vince Russo that James was officially fired because he insisted on giving Green a larger role backstage. However, reports citing informed sources confirmed that James resigned rather than being terminated.

Road Dogg had expressed strong admiration for Chelsea Green on social media following her win of the Interim WWE Women's Championship at SummerSlam, noting that he chose to leave after clearly seeing what was happening backstage.
