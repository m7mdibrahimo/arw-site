WWE has announced that Baron Corbin will be sidelined indefinitely. The injury occurred during an impromptu match against Cody Rhodes on WWE SmackDown last week, which featured interference from Randy Orton and concluded with Rhodes delivering a punt kick to Corbin.

During the October 23 episode of WWE SmackDown, Michael Cole provided an update from the WWE medical team. He confirmed that the current United States Champion suffered a cervical muscle strain, an orbital bone contusion, and blurred vision. Consequently, Corbin is out of action for an indefinite period.
