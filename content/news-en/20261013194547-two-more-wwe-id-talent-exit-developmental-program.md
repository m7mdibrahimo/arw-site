The original class of the WWE ID program continues to see turnover. Following the recent departure of Sam Holloway, PWInsider reports that both Cappuccino Jones and Santi Rivera have officially left the developmental initiative. While neither wrestler has made a public statement regarding their exit, the report confirms their time in the program has concluded. Jones was a standout talent within the group, having held the WWE ID Men's Championship for 234 days between August 2025 and March 2026. He also maintains the Reality of Wrestling Texas Championship. Rivera, 24, has been active on the independent scene since 2021, competing for promotions such as Create A Pro Wrestling and Beyond Wrestling. These departures follow the recent exits of Marcus Mathers and Laynie Luck, adding to a wave of talent movement within the WWE ID roster. Despite these changes, WWE continues to recruit new talent to the program.
