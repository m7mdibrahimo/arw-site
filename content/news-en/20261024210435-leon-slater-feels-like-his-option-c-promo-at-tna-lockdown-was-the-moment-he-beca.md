Leon Slater recently reflected on his pivotal Option C promo at TNA Lockdown, where he defeated Cedric Alexander in a Last Man Standing Steel Cage match to capture the TNA X Division Title before relinquishing it to challenge Nic Nemeth for the TNA World Title. Speaking on the I Love Wrestling Podcast, Slater compared the career-defining moment to Austin's iconic 3:16 promo, noting that it was the turning point where he transitioned from a rising prospect to the top star of the company. He explained that after receiving the belt from Carlos Silva, he knew it was time to move on to the heavyweight division following a historic X Division run that included appearances on WWE television, SmackDown, and John Cena's final match. Slater successfully cashed in his opportunity at Bound for Glory, solidifying his status as a main eventer.
