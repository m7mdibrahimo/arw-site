Following the sudden passing of PAC in late September, AEW held a tribute show during the following episode of AEW Dynamite. Fans praised the event, particularly the emotional 5-on-5 tag team main event featuring the Death Riders and a one-time return by Bryan Danielson. Matt Hardy discussed the tribute on his podcast, "The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy."

> "I liked everything I saw. I thought it was very classy. I thought it was well done. It was very emotional," Hardy said.

Hardy watched the first 30 minutes and was impressed by the video tribute to PAC's life. He noted that the show provided fans and wrestlers with necessary hope while grieving. He also reflected on a promo by Jon Moxley following AEW All Out, which featured PAC in the final scene. Hardy admitted that, in hindsight, the promo felt eerie given the circumstances.
