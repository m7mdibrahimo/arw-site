CMLL's Amazonas Grand Prix is a global showcase featuring women from various promotions, including AEW, STARDOM, Maple Leaf Pro, MLW, and RevPro. The tournament takes place on October 23 at Arena Mexico. Shotzi Blackheart and Masha Slamovich were originally named as MLW's representatives, but Blackheart had to withdraw following the death of her brother. Blackheart expressed that competing in the tournament was a massive honor that she deeply cherished. CMLL announced that AEW's Marina Shafir will replace Blackheart, representing her home country of Moldova. CMLL highlighted Shafir's mixed martial arts background and aggressive style, marking her debut for the promotion. Shafir joins fellow AEW representatives Megan Bayne, Queen Aminata, and Harley Cameron, alongside other global participants.
