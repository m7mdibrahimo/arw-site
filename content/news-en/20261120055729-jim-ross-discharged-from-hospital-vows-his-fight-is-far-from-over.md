Jim Ross has shared an update regarding his health, confirming that he has been discharged from another hospital stay and is moving in with his family for a short period.

The legendary commentator wrote on social media about leaving the facility and heading straight to his daughter's home:

> "Just got out of another hospital stay today and came straight to Tulsa to move in with my daughter and her family for a few weeks. My family has been so supportive and I can’t thank them enough. Appreciate everyone’s positive thoughts and prayers. My fight is far from over."

Ross underwent successful shunt surgery on November 11. He has dealt with multiple medical setbacks this year, including brain surgery in August, a second brain procedure in October to resolve a shunt issue, and an additional hospital stay caused by low oxygen.
