At WWE WrestleMania 40, The Rock returned to headline the first night of the event, teaming with Roman Reigns to face Cody Rhodes and Seth Rollins. Since he was able to keep up with everyone in the ring, some wonder if he is capable of having one more match to wrap up his career, which is something Bully Ray believes.

While speaking on "Busted Open Radio," Bully Ray stated that The Rock losing a lot of muscle will decrease the physical strain on him inside the ring, adding that he believes in his ability to return with his current fitness level. He noted that the star has reduced his muscle mass compared to his peak, but this change may help him improve his performance, stressing his confidence that a retirement match will take place in front of massive crowds.

> "With all of that muscle that he's taken off, it'd be a lot less strain on him in the ring. Do I think he could get back into the ring in the shape that he is now? Yes I do"

Ever since convincing John Cena to turn heel, The Rock has been absent from any new storylines following heavy script changes. With his near-total focus on Hollywood and having not officially announced his retirement or entered the Hall of Fame yet, Bully Ray thinks holding a final match seems very logical, stressing that failing to have him face Roman Reigns in the future would represent a missed opportunity on the scale of a match between The Undertaker and Sting never happening.

> "I do think there is a Rock retirement match, in front of like a hundred and fifty-thousand people."
