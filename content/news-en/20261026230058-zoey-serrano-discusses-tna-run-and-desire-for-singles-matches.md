Zoey Serrano remains focused on pursuing a singles career despite her current tag team partnership with Shayna Baszler. The duo, who captured the TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Titles at the 2026 Bound for Glory event, reunited after Baszler officially joined the promotion.

During an appearance on Busted Open, Serrano discussed learning about Baszler’s signing, according to Fightful.

> "Honestly, I didn’t really know if — I mean, obviously Daria (Rae) was there and everything else but I didn’t know if Shayna (Baszler) was gonna come so when she told me that she finally signed and that we were gonna be a team together, that was really exciting. I found out, I think, like four days ahead of time or something."

After TNA Head of Creative Delirious paired them together, Serrano made it clear to him that she still wanted to compete as a singles wrestler to develop her own identity. She noted that she has spent her entire career in tag teams and wanted to evolve as a performer.

> "I wanted to do my own thing. I was in a tag for the last — my entire career, so I was like, ‘I wanna go out and do a singles and figure myself out before I go back into another team,’ and it wasn’t discussed. I wasn’t told anything. It just was kind of like, ‘Hey, I signed -’ when she told me — and then Delirious kinda just said, ‘Okay, you two are a team. Let’s think of tag names.’ But I did have a talk with Delirious. I was like, ‘I still wanna do singles stuff. I wanna do both, and be a workhorse more than anything else and find myself.’ That’s really the goal at this point in my career is just finding myself again. Have fun with everything."

Serrano praised Delirious for being receptive to her feedback and confirmed he assured her that she would still have opportunities for singles work. She expressed excitement about balancing both roles.

> "It’s so much easier to actually go up and talk to Delirious. I feel like everything I pitched to him, he was just like, ‘Yeah. We’ll do it on this show.’ We’re also probably taking some stuff off of his plate where he’s like, thank you, you know? Giving him some ideas. But yeah, I’ve definitely made that clear. He sent me a text saying, ‘Don’t worry. We’re gonna still do singles’ and everything so I’m excited to mix both of doing both singles route and tag… I love working with Shayna (Baszler) but, I definitely wanna see where I could go with the singles route."

On the October 22 episode of Thursday Night iMPACT, Baszler and Serrano attacked Knockouts World Champion Indi Hartwell, with Baszler holding the title to end the segment.
