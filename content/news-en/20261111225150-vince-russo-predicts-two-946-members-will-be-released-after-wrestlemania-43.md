Vince Russo is predicting a difficult future for some members of the 946 faction in WWE. Despite the group being involved in a major storyline leading up to Survivor Series, Russo believes that some members could be released from the company once the WrestleMania season concludes.

During the November 11 episode of The Coach and Bro Show, Russo criticized WWE for placing 946 in a prominent position without providing the members enough time to develop their personalities or deliver promos. He argued that the group is working alongside stars like Roman Reigns before they are prepared for that level.

> "It's almost like a rule — no one can have a personality. Don't let anyone talk or say anything. They're not ready to talk, not ready to cut a promo, they're not ready to pin Roman Reigns."

Russo stated that he expects at least two members of the group to be cut following WrestleMania, noting that the company typically makes roster changes during that time of year.

> "I promise you, at least two of these 946 members right now will be cut right after WrestleMania. I promise you."

Coachman also recently criticized the group's development, arguing that WWE skipped the necessary character work. He questioned the sudden transition of Cruz Montana from a babyface in NXT to a heel faction without a clear explanation and called for the motivations of members like Jaida Parker to be established.
