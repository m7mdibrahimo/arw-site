Paige is grateful to be wrestling again after eight years away, but she made it clear she is still going to fight for her television time. Speaking to Metro UK, the former Divas Champion opened up about her WWE return and what being back in the ring means to her, while acknowledging that advocating for herself might occasionally make her difficult to deal with backstage. "I do appreciate every single second that I get to be on TV and in that ring. And the fact that I managed to return after eight years, I am so grateful for that. But that doesn’t mean I won’t put my foot down when it comes to TV time," Paige said.

Her concerns extend beyond her own minutes, as she wants WWE’s women’s division as a whole to receive as much television exposure as possible. Paige admitted that management might occasionally find her vocal approach challenging, but she believes the company knew what to expect when it brought her back. Her comments follow an instance where one of her planned WWE segments was cut from television, as her promo addressing Nikki Bella on the August 14 episode of SmackDown never made the broadcast, prompting her frustration on social media over women losing television time.
