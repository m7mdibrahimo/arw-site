Tetsuya Naito successfully defended the GHC Heavyweight Championship against Kenoh in the main event of Pro Wrestling NOAH's event on November 9 at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo, Japan. The show also marked the start of the NOAH Jr. Grand Prix 2026 tournament. In tournament action, YO-HEY defeated Shuji Kondo, Sidney Akeem defeated Kid Lykos, and BUSHI defeated Kai Fujimura. Additionally, Masa Kitamiya, Tadasuke, and Takashi Sugiura defeated Hi69, Manabu Soya, and Yuki Iino. Alejandro, Dragon Bane, Kid Lykos II, Luis Mante, and Naomichi Marufuji defeated Atsushi Kotoge, Hiroto Tsuruya, Hiroya Namiki, Tetsuya Endo, and Yuto Koyanagi. Finally, Daiki Odashima, Kaito Kiyomiya, and Yoshiki Inamura defeated Alpha Wolf, Jun Masaoka, and OZAWA.
