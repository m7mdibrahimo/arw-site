Athena has set her sights on her next challenger. During the November 5 episode of ROH TV, ROH Women's Champion Athena officially challenged Hyan to a title match at ROH Final Battle. Athena, who has held the title for over 1,400 days since winning it at ROH Final Battle 2022, warned both Diamante and Billie Starkz while suggesting that Hyan could bring a fresh dynamic to the division. ROH Final Battle is set to take place on Friday, December 4, in San Antonio, Texas, marking Hyan's first opportunity to compete for the ROH Women's Championship.
