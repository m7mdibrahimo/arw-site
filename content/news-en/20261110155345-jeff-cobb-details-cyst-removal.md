In August 2026, Jeff Cobb revealed that he had a growth removed from his scalp. Speaking to Chris Van Vliet on Insight, Cobb detailed the experience, explaining that he first noticed the lump on the back of his head right before the pandemic. Since it was hidden by his hair, he did not think about it for years.

Eventually, while visiting friends in Japan and utilizing his remaining insurance coverage from the country, Cobb decided to have it checked out. The procedure took about 15 minutes and cost roughly 100 dollars. Cobb recalled looking at the removed tissue and joking with the doctor about whether it could be cancerous.

> I don’t think cancer because six years I would have felt something wrong?

After sending the tissue for testing, the doctor informed him that it was benign. Cobb noted that the issue turned out to be a benign cyst and advised anyone noticing unusual physical changes to get checked immediately rather than waiting for years.
