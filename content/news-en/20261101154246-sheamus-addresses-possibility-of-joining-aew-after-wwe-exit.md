Sheamus is addressing the possibility of joining AEW following his exit from WWE. During a recent meet-and-greet, a fan asked him directly about signing with AEW. He did not give a definitive answer, stating instead, "I’m just enjoying my time off right now." Sheamus also joked that fans should ask his wife about his future, saying, "Ask my wife, she's the one making all the decisions."

Speculation about Sheamus and AEW has grown because of his upcoming schedule. He is scheduled to appear at Big Event NY on November 14, the same day AEW holds Full Gear. Dave Meltzer previously reported in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter that Sheamus asked to leave the convention early to attend a wedding. Sheamus became a free agent after his WWE contract expired in early October, and several AEW veterans have pushed for Tony Khan to sign him, while TNA has also expressed interest.
