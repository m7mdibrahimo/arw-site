CM Punk is refusing to move on following his physical defeat against Kevin Owens on WWE SmackDown. After suffering a loss in their brutal No Holds Barred match, Punk made it clear that their intense rivalry is far from finished. Speaking during the WWE Crown Jewel Countdown show, he addressed the November 6 defeat and targeted his rival directly.

Owens secured the pinfall after wrapping a steel chain around his hand to knock Punk out. During the post-show appearance, Punk reflected on the outcome:

> "My takeaway was that I got beat by a human ham sandwich because he got his hand wrapped with a steel chain. That's what you sign up for. This is the job. I'm not complaining or making excuses like everyone else does. I need a rematch — anyone can beat me once."

The No Holds Barred contest featured chaotic action all over the venue, including tables, chairs, and trash cans. Punk drove Owens through a table with an elbow drop and slammed him onto chairs, but Owens responded with a fisherman's buster through ringside tables before using the weapon to score the three-count.
