Trick Williams has addressed the backlash surrounding his physical altercation with Sami Zayn on WWE SmackDown. During a recent appearance on Busted Open Radio, Williams made it clear that he does not view Zayn any differently despite his status as the Undisputed WWE Champion. Williams recalled taking the United States Championship from Zayn in the past and stated that a belt does not define a man. He further warned that Zayn was lucky the exchange only involved a headbutt, fueling speculation about their ongoing feud. Fans should not expect the tension between the two stars to cool down anytime soon.
