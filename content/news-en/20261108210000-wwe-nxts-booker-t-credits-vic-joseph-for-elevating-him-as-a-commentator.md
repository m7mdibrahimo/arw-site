Booker T has found major success at the commentary desk since joining WWE NXT, and he gives full credit to his broadcast partner, Vic Joseph. The WWE Hall of Famer moved to broadcasting after finishing his wrestling career, though it was never a path he initially planned for himself. He expressed gratitude to Michael Cole for giving him the confidence to try the role, admitting that working on the main roster was extremely difficult and featured plenty of struggles.

> "I didn't [think I would become a commentator]. You know, I really didn't. You know, I thank Michael Cole, you know, for bringing me on the team some years back. And I just didn't think I was the guy. I didn't think I fit in. And on the main roster, it was so freaking hard. I got fired a couple of times. One time, they told me to take me off commentary. I go, 'Guys, I was going to come talk to you guys about it,'" he joked in his interview with Notsam Wrestling. "They just stuck with me and stuck with me."

The turning point came with his move to WWE NXT, where teaming up with Joseph allowed him to fully embrace his natural style.

> "And then really, you know, getting the chance to go down to NXT and work with Vic kind of made me realize who I really was as an analyst, as a commentator, but more as a hype man than anything. Because Vic, he does so much work, and I just try to back him up, and it's been such a great tag team between him and me."

Booker T also remembered that his commentary roots began in WCW, when he used to do mock commentary backstage to entertain the locker room, before later doing it on screen in TNA.
