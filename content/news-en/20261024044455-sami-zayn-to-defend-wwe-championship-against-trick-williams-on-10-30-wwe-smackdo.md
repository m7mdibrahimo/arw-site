Sami Zayn is set to defend his WWE Championship in the upcoming episode of WWE SmackDown on October 30. During the October 23 show, Trick Williams interrupted a segment involving Zayn and Rey Fenix, which resulted in a tag team match pitting Williams and Fenix against Zayn and Gargano. Before that match, Williams told Cathy Kelley in a backstage interview that he would challenge Zayn for the title next week. The updated lineup for the October 30 WWE SmackDown includes: Undisputed WWE Championship: Sami Zayn (c) vs. Trick Williams; WWE Women’s Championship: Chelsea Green (c) vs. Nikki Bella.
