John Cena opened the November 13 episode of WWE SmackDown to reveal the next participant in the upcoming John Cena Classic tournament. Cena officially introduced Charlotte Flair as the latest wrestler added to the field.

Following her entrance, Flair reflected on a backstage conversation she shared with Cena at WrestleMania 41. She expressed her determination to perform at an excellent level throughout the tournament and stated her goal to win the entire competition. The segment concluded with Cena and Flair sharing a hug in the ring.

Flair joins a growing list of confirmed competitors for the tournament, which already features Cody Rhodes, Zilla Fatu, Noam Dar, Romeo Moreno, and Kelani Jordan.
