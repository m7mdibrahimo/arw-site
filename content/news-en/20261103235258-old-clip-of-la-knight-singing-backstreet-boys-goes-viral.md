LA Knight is trending online once more following the resurfacing of vintage footage. On Monday, November 2, @LAKnightsGame on X shared a video capturing him performing the hit track "Everybody" by the Backstreet Boys.

This recording dates back to his initial run in WWE NXT under the ring name Slate Randall. Near the conclusion of the clip, he delivered his trademark catchphrase to the beat.

Knight has been absent from television broadcasts since the October 12 episode of WWE Raw. His most recent in-ring appearance took place on October 29 alongside Oba Femi and Solo Sikoa against The Vision during a live event in Ohio.
