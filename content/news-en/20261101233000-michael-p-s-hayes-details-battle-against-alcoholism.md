Michael P.S. Hayes opened up about his long battle with alcohol abuse during an appearance on "What's Your Story? with Steph McMahon," looking back at his golden era with the Fabulous Freebirds in the 1980s. He explained that he began drinking at a very young age, noting that he managed to sustain his career because he was a functional alcoholic whose performances were never hindered by exhaustion. Hayes noted that Stephanie McMahon booked him into rehabilitation centers multiple times, but he realized that true sobriety requires firm faith and a genuine personal desire.

Following numerous stints in clinics that featured difficult times, Hayes finally succeeded at the Riverside center thanks to help from former wrestler Shannon Moore, who provided him with massive support. In the end of his discussion, Hayes clarified that he currently lives an alcohol-free lifestyle, stressing that he does not want people to view him as a saint but rather as a human being who conquered his old habit.
