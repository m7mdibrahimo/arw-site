Konnan has faced severe health battles this year, but a positive update has emerged just before his TNA Hall of Fame induction at Bound for Glory. Speaking on Wrestling Observer Radio, Dave Meltzer shared that he received encouraging news regarding the wrestling legend's condition. While Meltzer noted he does not expect Konnan to attend the ceremony in person, he shared a major relief. Meltzer stated that Konnan is past the worst of his medical crisis, adding that he went through a very difficult period. Bryan Alvarez reacted to the news and sent his best wishes to Konnan ahead of the upcoming Hall of Fame ceremony. Throughout 2026, Konnan has dealt with life-threatening health issues, including a reported second leg amputation and waiting for a kidney transplant as shared previously by Latin Lover. Despite these heavy struggles, this latest update brings a positive note as Konnan prepares to be honored alongside ODB and Amazing Red during the TNA Hall of Fame ceremony taking place before the main show.
