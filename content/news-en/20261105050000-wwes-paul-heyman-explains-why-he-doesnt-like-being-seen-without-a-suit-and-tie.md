Throughout most of his WWE career, Paul Heyman has been known for his trademark suit and tie. During a recent interview with Graham Bensinger, the WWE Hall of Famer explained why he rarely wears anything else.

Heyman recalled that during his ECW days, he wore a long coat and a hat before switching to a corporate look in WWE. He noted that fans now expect him to look a certain way at all times, joking about how uncomfortable he feels when spotted in casual clothes. "Yeah, it's what people expect of me. I've also been photographed like this in flip-flops and shorts running down to the local convenience store, and, you know, I had a torn-up T-shirt, some truck will go by and the guy will stop and go, 'Hey, Paul Heyman, how you doing? Hey, can we take a selfie?' And I'm not going to say no. I'm not going to be rude. So, I end up with a selfie, and it ends up on the internet, and there I am, you know, with a pair of shorts, with one testicle hanging out, and flip-flops," Heyman said. He added that being caught out of character bothers him because fans expect him to maintain his persona as the "Wiseman."

Heyman also explained how his signature look helps him when transitioning into more serious storylines, noting that his appearance enhances the emotional stakes when tensions rise on screen, as seen during Solo Sikoa's attempt to take control of The Bloodline.
