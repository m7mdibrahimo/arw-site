Paul Heyman recently discussed his complicated history with Ric Flair during an interview on In Depth with Graham Bensinger. Heyman noted that while he has always appreciated Flair's role as the main attraction for the first event he ever promoted, Wrestle Party '85, their relationship soured when Flair took over creative duties in WCW. Heyman admitted that the tension between them escalated quickly, eventually leading to his firing. Heyman revealed that he actually attempted to provoke Flair into a physical confrontation during that time. "I tried to lure him into fighting me. Everything I could to get him to swing and he didn't take the bait. I wanted him to swing. Because how can an executive at Turner punch somebody in the face and get away with it? Number one, would have made it an easy lawsuit. Number two, he'd have to be fired alongside with me."

When Heyman returned to WCW in May 1990, the two maintained a professional demeanor, with Flair even inviting Heyman out for drinks. Heyman described the experience of trying to keep up with Flair's legendary drinking habits as grueling, but noted that surviving the night at the bar helped repair their relationship. "He said, 'All right. Kid's not as bad as I thought he was.' From then on, we worked together fine."
