Charlie Dempsey reflected on his mini-tour with All Japan Pro Wrestling in late 2023, during which he challenged for the Triple Crown Heavyweight Championship. Appearing on Busted Open, Dempsey stated that the experience gave him a major confidence boost and changed his perspective in the ring.

> "It wasn’t talked about much but, in the beginning of 2024, while working for NXT, they had me go over to wrestle for All Japan, for the Triple Crown Championship, and I thought that was an incredible experience. Because that 90s King’s Road-style wrestling has always been some of my favorite of all time."

Dempsey also discussed facing Katsuhiko Nakajima, Hideki Suzuki, and 70-year-old Tatsumi Fujinami during his time in Japan, noting that Fujinami told him to hit him hard before the match. Dempsey is currently in his fourth year with WWE NXT as a member of the BirthRight faction.
