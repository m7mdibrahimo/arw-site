HollyHood Haley J secured another victory in Power Slap competition. She competed at Power Slap 24 on Friday against Gema, who entered the matchup with an undefeated record of 1-0, while HollyHood held a 2-1 record.

Following three rounds of action, HollyHood earned a unanimous decision victory from the judges after scoring a knockdown in the final round. Her record now stands at 3-1 with consecutive decision wins following a knockout loss at Power Slap 15.

https://x.com/powerslap/status/2108741489186762929

Alongside her Power Slap career, HollyHood continues performing on the independent wrestling scene for promotions including Juggalo Championship Wrestling, GCW, and AIW. She is not the only wrestler to step into Power Slap, as Jake Hager previously competed before suffering a brutal knockout in his most recent contest, according to Fightful.
