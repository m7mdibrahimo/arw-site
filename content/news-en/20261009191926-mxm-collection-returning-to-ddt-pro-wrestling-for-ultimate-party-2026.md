MxM Collection is set to make a return to DDT Pro-Wrestling. The duo consisting of Mansoor and Mason Madden will compete at the upcoming Ultimate Party 2026 event. The show is scheduled to take place on November 3rd at the Ryogoku Sumo Hall in Tokyo, Japan. This marks their first appearance for the promotion since they last wrestled there in June 2025.
