Former WWE and ECW star Perry Saturn has shared details about his recent health scare, revealing that he was hospitalized for roughly 48 hours due to severe respiratory issues and his third bout with COVID-19. Speaking on Chase's YouTube channel, Saturn explained that he contracted COVID-19 while dealing with an upper respiratory infection that left him struggling to breathe and losing his voice completely. He described the physical toll as feeling like having Yokozuna and Rikishi sitting on his chest. After beginning treatment at the emergency room, Saturn confirmed that he is now recovering, moving around better, and finally breathing easier, though he is still relying heavily on cough medicine.
