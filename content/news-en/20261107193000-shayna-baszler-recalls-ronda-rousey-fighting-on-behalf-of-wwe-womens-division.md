Shayna Baszler shared details about Ronda Rousey standing up for the WWE women's locker room behind the scenes during her run with the company. Speaking with Denise Salcedo, Baszler stated that Rousey rarely gets credit for what she did off-camera. She revealed an incident where WWE cut six minutes from Rousey's premium live event match because a previous bout went over time. Rousey responded by buying a flight to WWE headquarters to confront the creative team.

> "There was a time where Ronda had a pay-per-view match and got six minutes cut off her match because a match before had gone over their time... When that happened, she bought a ticket and flew herself to headquarters to have a meeting with creative at that time to be like, 'Guys. This keeps happening. The women keep losing time. I need to know right now, is this going to change? Because if it's not, I don't need to be here. I don't need to be here to make money. I don't need to be here to get famous. I'm here to help advance this, and if you're telling me right now that's never going to happen, then I'm done. I only want to improve this.'"

Baszler explained that Rousey bought her own travel ticket so officials could not cancel the meeting. She added that Rousey brought massive mainstream attention to WWE by drawing in MMA fans who otherwise dismissed professional wrestling.
