Ethan Page and Big Cass launched a brutal attack on Chad Gable, Rey Mysterio, and Dragon Lee following Gable's Intercontinental Championship defense on WWE RAW. Gable had just retained his title in a chaotic Triple Threat Match by pinning Mysterio with a sunset flip after hitting hard-fought moves, including Mysterio's 619 and frog splash, and a near-fall by Lee with a Styles Clash. After the match, the competitors showed mutual respect until Page stormed the ring to assault Gable and Mysterio, while Big Cass demolished Lee with a chokeslam onto the ringside barricade and delivered a massive boot to Mysterio. Page then planted Gable with a fisherman's buster, grabbed the Intercontinental Championship to hold it high, and hit Gable with a second fisherman's buster to leave the champion laid out.
