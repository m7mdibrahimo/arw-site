TNA Bound For Glory 2026 took place on October 11, 2026, at the Yuengling Center in Tampa, Florida. The event featured the TNA Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony, where Amazing Red, Konnan, and ODB were officially honored for their contributions to the industry. Following the ceremony, the main card delivered several championship changes and intense matchups.

In the opening singles match, Ricky Sosa def. Eddie Edwards. In the X-Division Championship 7-Way Scramble Match, Amazing Red def. Fabian Aichner, KC Navarro, Cedric Alexander, Rich Swann, Ryan Nemeth, and BDE to become the new champion. In the TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Championship match, Zoey Serrano and Shayna Baszler def. Allie and Kiera Hogan to win the titles. In the Call Your Shot Gauntlet Match, Frankie Kazarian emerged victorious. In the TNA International Championship 4-Way Match, Jason Hotch def. Mustafa Ali, John Skyler, and Special Agent Zero to capture the championship.
