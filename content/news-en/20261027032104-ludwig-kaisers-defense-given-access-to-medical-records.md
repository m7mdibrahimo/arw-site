Court records indicate that the legal team representing Ludwig Kaiser, also known as Marcel Barthel, has been granted access to the medical records of the alleged victim in his ongoing battery case. A filing submitted on October 26 stated that the State of Florida is making redacted medical records available to the defense upon the receipt of a blank DVD. Kaiser's legal team had previously been granted access to photographic evidence related to the case. A pre-trial conference is currently scheduled for December 17. Ludwig Kaiser, who performs as El Grande Americano on WWE television, was arrested in May following an incident in April where he allegedly punched a man in an elevator. Despite the ongoing legal proceedings, Kaiser has continued to wrestle for WWE.
