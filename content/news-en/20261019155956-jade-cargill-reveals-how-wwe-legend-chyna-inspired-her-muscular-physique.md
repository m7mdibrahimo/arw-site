Jade Cargill has revealed that WWE legend Chyna played a major role in inspiring her muscular physique and powerful presence. Speaking in a workout video on the Women's Health YouTube channel, Cargill explained that she has always been naturally muscular, but seeing Chyna command attention on television made her want to push her physique even further. She admired the way Chyna carried herself and commanded respect upon entering a room, qualities she later sought to emulate as she began strength training in college. Cargill also noted that while Chyna remains her biggest fitness inspiration, the female athletes she currently shares the ring with in WWE continue to motivate her to work harder every day.
