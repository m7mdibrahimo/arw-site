WWE is celebrating the 30th anniversary of The Rock, and Roman Reigns sent a message to his WrestleMania XL tag team partner on social media.

> "30 years of The Great One. Eternal love & respect for @therock. Thank you for paving the way for so many of us"

The Rock responded to The Tribal Chief, writing:

> "Love you my brother. You are one of one - the GOAT. Aiga always xo"

The Rock is preparing to return at WWE Survivor Series to celebrate his anniversary. It remains unknown if he will participate in additional matches at the event. Meanwhile, Reigns will headline Survivor Series by leading The Bloodline against The 946 in WarGames.
