Joe Hendry revealed that his popular WWE character was originally designed to be universally hated by fans. Speaking with TV Insider, Hendry discussed whether he could eventually show a darker side instead of sticking with his upbeat persona. He explained that when he created the gimmick and the music video, he expected the audience to reject him entirely. Instead, fans embraced him and his music enthusiastically, forcing him to let the character go wherever the audience wanted. Hendry noted that he is leaving the decision open for the future rather than forcing a change, and he remains happy to let the audience dictate where things go next.
