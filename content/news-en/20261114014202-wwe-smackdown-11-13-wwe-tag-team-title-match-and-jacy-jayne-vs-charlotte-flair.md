WWE has expanded the lineup for the November 13 episode of WWE SmackDown. The promotion announced that The MFTs (Tama Tonga and Talla Tonga) will defend their WWE Tag Team Championship against Bakusai (Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki) on Friday's show.

Additionally, a singles match between Jacy Jayne and Charlotte Flair has been added to the card. The show will also feature Finn Balor taking on Gunther, an appearance by WWE Women's Champion Tiffany Stratton, and an appearance by John Cena to discuss the John Cena Classic.
