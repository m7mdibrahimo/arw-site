Jim Duggan has expressed his frustration with professional wrestlers who complain about the business while appearing on the documentary series Dark Side Of The Ring. The Vice series has documented many of the industry's most troubled times over its seven seasons. During a recent interview with Clutchpoints, Duggan shared that he had a positive experience throughout his career and finds the negativity on the show to be unnecessary.

> "I had a fun time in the business, you know? I see so many guys on the Dark Side Of The Ring. They're crying and whining about wrestling. It's been a great business for me. I've been with my wife for 43 years. I never had to go to rehab for booze or drugs. I put two daughters through college. At 73 years old, I travel the world signing autographs. How can you gripe? I see these guys: ‘Oh, wrestling's so bad. It's horrible.’ I'm like, ‘it's what you make of it.’ Don't do an eight ball of cocaine every week and get three divorces, you know? And there are other success stories in wrestling. You just don't hear about them. Tito Santana."

Duggan emphasized that he believes success in the industry is largely determined by personal choices rather than the business itself.
