AEW has started teasing the arrival of a major new star with a recent mystery vignette on television. According to reports, the promotional videos were created for Ilja Dragunov. The former WWE star chose not to renew his contract when it expired in August and has reportedly already signed with AEW. The company has been working to resolve his visa situation before his official debut. A cryptic 22-second video recently aired featuring a man conducting an orchestra, a metronome on a ring mat, and a figure walking down steps. While an exact debut date has not been confirmed yet, the television teasers indicate that Dragunov's arrival is approaching.
