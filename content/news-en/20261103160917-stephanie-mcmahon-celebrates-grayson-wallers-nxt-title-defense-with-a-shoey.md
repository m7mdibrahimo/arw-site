Stephanie McMahon found a unique way to celebrate Grayson Waller successfully defending the NXT Championship at Halloween Havoc by taking part in a shoey. After Waller's title defense against Tony D’Angelo, McMahon posted a video from Waller’s podcast appearance on X. The clip showed McMahon drinking out of Waller’s boot alongside the NXT Champion. McMahon congratulated Waller on retaining his title and joked about the taste of the drink from his footwear. Waller has been performing shoeys for years as part of his wrestling persona, drawing inspiration from UFC fighter Tai Tuivasa and Formula 1 driver Daniel Ricciardo. Now, Waller can add Stephanie McMahon to the list of people he has introduced to the Australian tradition.
