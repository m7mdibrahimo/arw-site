John Cena's farewell run features the newly announced John Cena Classic tournament, but Jonathan Coachman is voicing frustration over how WWE is handling the event. Speaking on The Coach and Bro Show, Coachman criticized WWE for connecting Cena's traditional message of loyalty to a paid membership model. Cena revealed that voting for the tournament would be exclusive to Club WWE members, which Coachman sees as putting the process behind a paywall before fans even understand what they are voting on.

Coachman questioned how charging fans money rewards their long-standing dedication to Cena and the company. His co-host Vince Russo raised further doubts, questioning whether WWE would genuinely adjust its booking based on fan votes or if the results would be predetermined. Coachman clarified that he does not believe Cena created the setup as a personal cash grab, suggesting instead that Cena was convinced to heavily market Club WWE without realizing how the strategy appears to the audience.

The criticism comes amid lingering uncertainty regarding the tournament's format. Reports previously indicated that fans might vote on who advances in each round regardless of match outcomes, though logistical hurdles led WWE to reconsider that approach. With the rules still unconfirmed, the requirement of a paid membership to participate remains a key point of contention surrounding Cena's farewell tour.
