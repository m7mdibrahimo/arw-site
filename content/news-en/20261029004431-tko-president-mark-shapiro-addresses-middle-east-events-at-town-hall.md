TKO held a town hall meeting on October 28, where President and Chief Operating Officer Mark Shapiro spoke to employees from WWE, UFC, Zuffa Boxing, and On Location. During the session, Shapiro emphasized that the safety of all personnel is "paramount" regarding upcoming events in the Middle East.

This meeting occurred before the announcement that WWE Crown Jewel would change venues while remaining in Riyadh. The event is scheduled for Saturday, November 7. Additionally, WWE President Nick Khan praised Bron Breakker, Je'Von Evans, Sol Ruca, and Oba Femi, identifying them as rising stars with a promising future in the company.
