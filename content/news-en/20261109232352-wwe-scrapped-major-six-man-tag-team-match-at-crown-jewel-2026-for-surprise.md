WWE had planned a massive six-man tag team match for Crown Jewel 2026, but the company changed course to facilitate a title change. According to Dave Meltzer of the Wrestling Observer, WWE originally intended to book Oba Femi to team with Penta and Je'Von Evans against Bron Breakker, Austin Theory, and Bronson Reed at the November 7 event in Saudi Arabia.

Instead, WWE decided it was better to have Penta and Je'Von Evans challenge Breakker and Theory for the World Tag Team Championships, with Femi interfering to cause the title change. Meltzer reported, "The decision was changed because of the feeling that it was better to have Femi cause a title change to get him into the angle and it’s expected to lead to that trios match soon."

During the match, Bronson Reed attacked the challengers, but Oba Femi made a surprise appearance to stop him. This allowed Penta and Je'Von Evans to defeat Breakker and Theory to become the new World Tag Team Champions. WWE still expects to book the original six-man tag team match featuring Femi, Penta, and Evans against Breakker, Theory, and Reed in the near future.
