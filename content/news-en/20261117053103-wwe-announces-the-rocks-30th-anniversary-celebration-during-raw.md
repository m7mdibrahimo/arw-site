WWE has officially announced a celebration honoring 30 years of The Rock's career, alongside a dedicated merchandise line for the occasion. During the November 16, 2026, episode of WWE RAW, the company broadcast a special tribute video celebrating The Great One's 30th anniversary while advertising new merchandise on WWE Shop, including a commemorative championship belt and a special anniversary T-shirt.

This timing marks exactly 30 years since The Rock debuted in WWE at Survivor Series 1996 inside Madison Square Garden. Competing then as Rocky Maivia in a traditional Survivor Series elimination match, he later evolved into The Rock to become one of professional wrestling's top stars, with his feuds against legends like Stone Cold Steve Austin and Triple H helping shape the Attitude Era.

According to Ringside News, previous reports indicated that the company was already planning a commemorative championship belt, exclusive gear, and social media content to honor his career. As of now, there is no confirmation that The Rock will personally appear on WWE television for the celebration, leaving the company to honor his career through promotional content and special merchandise marking three decades since the debut of one of its most recognizable names.
