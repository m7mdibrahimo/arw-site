Tonight's TNA Impact served as the go-home show for Destination X, though it did not quite feel like the lead-in to a major event. Writing for Wrestling Inc., Max Everett and Brie Coder shared their thoughts on the highs and lows of the episode, ranging from the opening match to the main event.

Hated: Technical difficulties
- The TNA+ app lagged significantly and played a choppy version of the show, a widespread issue among viewers ahead of a major pay-per-view.
- The technical problems were only resolved late in the broadcast, leaving many fans frustrated with the subscription service.

Loved: A fantastic opener with some Beautiful People
- Heather by Elegance and Elayna Black delivered a well-paced opening Knockouts match that kept the action strong.
- The bout advanced their backstage storyline, highlighted by impressive moves and a strong performance from Black.

Hated: Who do we root for at Destination X?
- Video packages for Leon Slater and Trey Miguel highlighted their vulnerable sides and motivations.
- However, the lack of animosity or tension between the two challengers made it difficult for viewers to pick a side ahead of their title match.

Loved: Apex squashes the King Bees
- Shayna Baszler and Zoey Serrano scored a quick and dominant victory over Charity King and Danni Bee.
- The squash match served as a strong final statement to Indi Hartwell ahead of the Knockouts World Championship match at Destination X.

Hated: Mundane event
- The main event featured Nic Nemeth defending his World Championship rematch against Elijah in a match booked on the fly.
- Critics noted that the booking felt uninspired, with many other available talents left off the card.

Loved: The Dollhouse seemingly expands
- Taryn Terrell continued to expand her faction by recruiting Harley Hudson and setting her sights on Mila Moore.
- The return of the legendary stable signals positive momentum for the TNA Knockouts division.
