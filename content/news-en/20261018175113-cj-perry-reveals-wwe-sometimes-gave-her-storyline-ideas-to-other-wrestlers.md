CJ Perry recently discussed her time in WWE and revealed that some of the storyline ideas she pitched to management were later given to other Superstars instead. Speaking on Nick Club Wrestling Happy Hour, Perry explained that she regularly pitched creative concepts to Vince McMahon and members of the creative team. While many of her ideas were rejected, she noted that on multiple occasions, concepts she had suggested eventually appeared on television involving different performers. Rather than feeling frustrated by the situation, Perry chose to view it positively. She explained that she tricked her mind into believing that her ideas were simply too creative, and that the company liked the concepts even if she was not necessarily the right person to cast in the roles. As an example, Perry mentioned pitching a storyline where she would become the sixth wife of Ric Flair following her work with Bobby Lashley. While Lacey Evans later entered into a romantic storyline with Flair, Perry clarified that she believed Evans was a much better fit for that particular role.
