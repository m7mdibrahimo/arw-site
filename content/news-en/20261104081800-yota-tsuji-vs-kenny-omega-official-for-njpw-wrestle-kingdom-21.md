Following the conclusion of Power Struggle, New Japan Pro-Wrestling held a press conference on November 4 where company President Hiroshi Tanahashi officially announced the match card for Wrestle Kingdom 21. The event takes place at the Tokyo Dome on January 4.

The show will be headlined by IWGP Heavyweight Champion Yota Tsuji defending his title against Kenny Omega. Additionally, Shingo Takagi will face Jon Moxley in an Unbound Deathmatch.

The announced lineup includes:

Vacant IWGP Junior Heavyweight Tag Team Championship: Místico & TBA vs. Robbie X & Taiji Ishimori vs. Robbie Eagles & Kosei Fujita

Unbound Deathmatch: Jon Moxley vs. Shingo Takagi

IWGP Junior Heavyweight Championship: YOH vs. SHO (If YOH loses, he must leave NJPW. If SHO loses, he must leave HOUSE OF TORTURE and reunite with YOH to reform Roppongi 3K)

Will Ospreay vs. Callum Newman

MJF vs. Ryohei Oiwa

IWGP Global Heavyweight Championship: Gabe Kidd vs. Yuya Uemura

IWGP Heavyweight Championship: Yota Tsuji vs. Kenny Omega
