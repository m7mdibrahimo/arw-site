During the AEW WrestleDream press conference, Tony Khan addressed the presence of Tatevik and Rachel Ley alongside Andrade El Idolo on AEW programming. Khan noted that Tatevik has frequently been seen with Andrade and might be the keeper of his contract and belt. He praised Andrade's popularity among female fans, calling him a true Latino star. Fightful previously reported in January that Tatevik signed with AEW. Meanwhile, Andrade successfully defended the AEW National Title against Tommaso Ciampa at the event.
