Jeff Cobb recently admitted that his WWE departure hit him so hard he briefly considered retiring from professional wrestling. During an appearance on INSIGHT with Chris Van Vliet, Cobb explained that he lost his passion for the sport after being released following WrestleMania 42, noting that May was a particularly rough period. When Van Vliet asked if he had thought about walking away, Cobb confirmed that he did, but ultimately decided he did not want his career to end on that note. His motivation eventually returned after he started watching NJPW programming and reflecting on his history with the promotion, leading to his return during the G1 Climax 36 finale.
