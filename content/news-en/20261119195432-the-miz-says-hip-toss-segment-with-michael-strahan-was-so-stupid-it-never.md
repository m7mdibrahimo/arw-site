The Miz recently reflected on his WWE Raw segment with Michael Strahan back in November 2013, where Strahan hosted a Miz TV segment alongside Titus O'Neil. The segment concluded with Strahan executing a hip toss on both Miz and O'Neil. During an appearance at Hot Ones Game Night, Miz recalled the moment when asked to name his dumbest WWE storyline that should never have aired. 

> "I had a hip toss competition with Michael Strahan. A hip toss is you take a guy and you kinda toss him, they kinda do a flip. The higher they get, the better it looks. It can be [about height]. I mean, to be honest, I don't even know. It was in New York of all places. New Yorkers are very known as a crowd that like, they'll let you know exactly how they feel. If they don't respect you, they'll do nothing. That's the worst thing you could possibly have and they don't care. Michael Strahan’s always amazing on TV. Anytime you put him in any situation, it's great, except for a hip toss competition."
