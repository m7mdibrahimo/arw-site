Mike Bailey admitted that he was hesitant to join another group in AEW, explaining that his previous team did not end well and he initially found it interesting to be a lone wolf. However, after discussing the idea with Will Ospreay and seeing other members like Andrade, he decided to join United Empire. Bailey also noted that working with Ospreay will help him reach the next level in his career, recalling how inspired he was watching Ospreay face Kenny Omega at AEW All In.
