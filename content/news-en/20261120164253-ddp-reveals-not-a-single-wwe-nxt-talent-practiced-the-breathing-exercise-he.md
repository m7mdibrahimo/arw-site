Diamond Dallas Page recently spent two days working with WWE NXT talents on stress management, mindset, and controlled breathing. However, during his second session, DDP discovered that not a single talent had practiced the breathing exercises he assigned as homework. Speaking on Stephanie McMahon's What's Your Story? podcast, Page explained that he taught the roster specific breathing patterns to help them manage anxiety and stress. While his wife praised his talk as the best she had ever seen, DDP was left baffled when no one in the room raised their hand to confirm they had actually practiced the simple breathing techniques.
