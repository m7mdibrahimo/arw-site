Looking back on what could have been, Shayna Baszler recently reflected on her time in WWE alongside Zoey Serrano and Daria Rae as part of the Pure Fusion Collective stable. Speaking with Denise Salcedo, Baszler explained that the group had high expectations and hoped to fill a major spot on the roster as factions like The Judgment Day went through changes, but ultimately suffered from bad timing and a lack of proper storytelling time.

The stable was formed in the summer of 2024, uniting three stars with extensive MMA backgrounds. However, the alliance wrapped up in the spring of 2025 when Sonya Deville's contract expired and Baszler was released, while Serrano was later released following WrestleMania. All three have since landed in TNA, where Baszler and Serrano won the Knockouts World Tag Team Championship at Bound for Glory, and Rae serves as the on-screen Director of Operations.
