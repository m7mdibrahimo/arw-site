WWE WrestleMania 27 marked the first time The Miz headlined the company's biggest event of the year, defending his WWE Championship against John Cena. With assistance from Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson, Miz managed to retain his title and set up the following year's iconic "Once In A Lifetime" match. Despite achieving a massive career milestone, Miz revealed during an appearance on WWE Vault that he has mixed feelings about the match today.

Reflecting on the night, Miz recalled being surprised by the special entrance effects. "Main event, sold out! Georgia Dome, 65,000 people! And, they told me: 'Mike, you're gonna have pyro.' I was like: 'What? I never get pyro; I never get anything!'" However, the physical toll of the match is what ultimately left him with uncomfortable memories. After taking a hard bump on the concrete floor, his head accidentally slammed into the ground, resulting in a concussion that caused memory loss. "I wanted to be tackled into an audience, I've never seen that happen! And you know I was like, 'Do we have any extras? Can we just put seats there?' And they were like, 'Well, we can't do that.' I was like, 'You know, just spear me onto the thing!' That was a dumb move, because I got knocked out, do not remember any of this!"

Despite suffering a concussion early on, Miz successfully finished the match with the help of Cena, The Rock, and referee Mike Chioda. Although it remains a defining moment of his career, he admits he struggles to watch the bout back. "I don't like watching that match, even though, people always ask, 'What's your favorite match?' It's like, that one [WM XVII], and they're like 'Why?' Because it's the main event of WrestleMania and not everyone gets to main event WrestleMania," he stated.
