Chelsea Green admits she lives in constant fear that WWE might take her Women's Championship away at any moment. Speaking on Jeremy's Food Tour, Green opened up about battling severe imposter syndrome throughout her 13-year career despite her success. Even as champion, she frequently worries that she will arrive at work and be told she is no longer good enough to hold the title. Green explained that these insecurities stem from how unpredictably her wrestling career has unfolded over the years. Despite her accomplishments, those anxious thoughts never completely disappear, and she has simply learned to live with them.
