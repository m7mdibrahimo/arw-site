Titus O'Neil is among the names to be honored at the upcoming George Tragos/Lou Thesz Pro Wrestling Hall of Fame induction weekend in 2027. Five awards will be issued during the event, recognizing excellence in various aspects of professional wrestling and amateur pedigree. Charlie Haas will receive the Hall of Fame honor for his technical brilliance and amateur background, while Rick Martel is set to receive the Lou Thesz Award. Additionally, Al Snow will be given the Verne Gagne Trainer Award, Titus O'Neil will be presented with the Frank Gotch Award for his leadership and philanthropy, and Lance Russell will receive the Gordon Solie Award. O'Neil, who was moved to WWE's alumni section in September, last wrestled in 2020 and previously served as a Global Ambassador for WWE after being inducted into their Hall of Fame in 2020.
