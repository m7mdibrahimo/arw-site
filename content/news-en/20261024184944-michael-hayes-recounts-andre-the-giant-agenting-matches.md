In a recent appearance on Stephanie McMahon's What's Your Story? podcast, WWE producer Michael Hayes shared stories from his pro wrestling career, including working with André the Giant as a match agent. Hayes recalled an incident in Ohio when The Freebirds were booked for a show, but because he forgot, he drank beer and took valium the night before. His teammates Terry Gordy and Buddy Roberts had to carry him into the venue. Hayes also spoke about hiding from André at an airport after an earlier incident got him in trouble, and discussed his long tenure with WWE and a conversation he had with Chief Content Officer Paul 'Triple H' Levesque about his future.
