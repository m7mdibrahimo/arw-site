MJF fired back at a fan on social media who accused him of being too scared to leave AEW for WWE. After a user called AEW the safe bet, MJF defended his career and everything he has accomplished without needing WWE. He listed his major achievements, including main-eventing in front of 82,000 fans, headlining million-dollar houses, starring in feature films, and holding the AEW world championship three times. MJF clarified that he has no problem with WWE, but takes pride in building his legacy on his own terms.
