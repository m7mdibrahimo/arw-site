Dave Meltzer reported in the Wrestling Observer Daily Update that December 19 is currently the favorite date for the John Cena Classic, which would put it head-to-head with AEW Worlds End. WWE has already announced NXT TLC and Wrestlepalooza for December 12, making December 19 the most likely option while ruling out December 26 due to existing house shows. Fans will not have to wait much longer for details, as John Cena is returning on the November 2 episode of WWE RAW at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn to finally reveal the tournament's specifics.
