Chelsea Green played a key role in securing a partnership between WWE and cosmetics giant Maybelline. Speaking on Cody Rhodes' What Do You Wanna Talk About? podcast, Green shared that she was chosen to pitch the value of the women's division directly to company officials.

Green noted that she did not rely on any prepared scripts during the meeting, choosing instead to speak sincerely and spontaneously to convince them to invest in WWE's female talent.

> "I got to sit at headquarters and pitch to the Maybelline team that the women in WWE were worthy of a Maybelline deal. They chose me to tell them why. That's huge. They gave me no notes. No one prefaced the meeting with anything. I just went in there and I spoke from the heart and I was so proud when I came out and found out that we had a deal."

Green viewed these opportunities as proof of the company's belief in her importance, noting that she enjoys representing WWE in commercial settings. She added that accepting every available opportunity over the past four years directly contributed to her success, including winning the WWE Women's Championship at SummerSlam 2026.
