Adam Copeland recently reflected on the serious neck injury that forced his first retirement from wrestling after WrestleMania 27. Speaking on The Mick and Kenny Show, Copeland recalled not realizing he had broken his neck at the time, noting that his left arm began to atrophy. After consulting peers and undergoing medical evaluations, he discovered he had two ruptured spinal discs and required a double fusion surgery in 2001 or 2002. Although the procedure extended his career by a decade, it was plagued by constant pain from taking bumps against his spinal discs. Despite doctors telling him he would never wrestle again, Copeland eventually sought clearance years later, leading to his miraculous return at the 2020 Royal Rumble.
