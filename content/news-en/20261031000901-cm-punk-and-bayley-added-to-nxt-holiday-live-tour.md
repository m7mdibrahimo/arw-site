CM Punk is set to make his return to WWE NXT. WWE announced that Punk will participate in the NXT Holiday Live Tour, appearing in Detroit on November 14 and Chicago on November 15. Previously, Punk wrestled at an NXT Live event in Virginia this August, teaming up with Myles Borne and Tavion Heights to defeat The Vanity Project, which consisted of Brad Baylor, Jackson Drake, and Ricky Smokes. In addition to Punk, former NXT Women's Champion Bayley will join the tour, performing in Cleveland on November 13 and Detroit on November 14. Punk has frequently visited the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, initially for rehabilitation when he returned to WWE and later to mentor younger talent. Grayson Waller, despite previously calling out Punk for not asking permission to visit NXT, believes Punk has been a positive influence at the Performance Center. Furthermore, Punk is scheduled to face Kevin Owens on the November 7 episode of WWE SmackDown.
