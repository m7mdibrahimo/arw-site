Elayna Black is pushing back against those who claim her OnlyFans account diminishes her credibility as a professional wrestler. Since returning to TNA, Black has faced criticism from people who believe her presence on the platform is incompatible with being a serious athlete. In an interview with the Daily Star, Black emphasized that her personal choices regarding her body do not negate her professional accomplishments or her dedication to the sport.

Black noted that she originally joined OnlyFans following her release from WWE, and she remains active on the platform today. While she acknowledges that some people still hold outdated views, she believes the industry is becoming more progressive. She highlighted that many women, including athletes outside of wrestling, use the platform as a legitimate source of income without it affecting their professional status. 

Addressing the criticism, Black stated, "Yes, there's always going to be, you know, the shitty people who think that they can speak to you a certain way or that you're less of a wrestler because you have an OnlyFans or something like that." She also dismissed concerns about her role as a role model for younger fans, clarifying that she is not encouraging anyone to join the platform. Instead, her message is centered on bodily autonomy and self-confidence. She concluded that as long as she is not harming anyone, she does not see why anyone should have a problem with her career or personal decisions.
