Chad Gable recently discussed why he never tried to avoid comparisons to Kurt Angle, explaining that he considers them a major compliment. Speaking with Chris Van Vliet, the former Olympian noted that fans often draw parallels because of their shared amateur wrestling backgrounds and similar in-ring styles. Gable pointed out that trying to force a different style just to stand apart from Angle would feel disingenuous, preferring instead to embrace the similarities.

> "I never wanted to be the one that would fight the comparisons, first of all, because they're of the highest compliment to me, being compared to Kurt."

Rather than attempting to become the next Kurt Angle, Gable is simply focused on being himself while acknowledging that their shared foundation naturally leads to comparisons.
