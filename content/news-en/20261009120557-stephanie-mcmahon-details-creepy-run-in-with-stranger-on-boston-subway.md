Stephanie McMahon recently shared a disturbing public transit experience on her What's Your Story? podcast. She recalled riding a crowded subway train in Boston, where she stood near the entrance stairs holding onto a divider. While holding the rail, she felt an unsettling touch against her finger that immediately caused concern.

Looking to her right, she noticed a suspicious man wearing dark sunglasses who gave her a strange feeling. Although he did not do anything major, the brief and personal contact left her feeling uneasy while she was traveling alone. She chose to quietly remove her hand and avoid looking at him, noting that his sunglasses made it impossible to read his expression or intentions.

While the story lacks a dramatic ending, the strange encounter clearly left a lasting impression on her. She handled the situation safely by maintaining her distance and avoiding any confrontation on the packed train.
