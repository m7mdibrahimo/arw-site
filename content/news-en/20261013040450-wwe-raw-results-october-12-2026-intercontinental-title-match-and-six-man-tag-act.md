WWE Raw aired live from the Brookshire Grocery Arena in Bossier City, Louisiana, on October 12, 2026. The show opened with a recap of Money in the Bank, followed by an appearance from the new Men's Money in the Bank briefcase winner, Penta. Je'Von Evans interrupted to congratulate Penta, but was attacked by Bron Breakker. In the opening match, Rey Mysterio def. Big Cass. Roxanne Perez def. Chelsea Green in a match involving Judgment Day. In the Intercontinental Championship match, Chad Gable def. Rey Fenix. The main event, a six-man tag team match between Jey Uso, Jimmy Uso, and Solo Sikoa against The 946's Royce Keys, Lucien Price, and Bronco Nima, ended in a no contest after Carmelo Hayes interfered and attacked Solo Sikoa.
