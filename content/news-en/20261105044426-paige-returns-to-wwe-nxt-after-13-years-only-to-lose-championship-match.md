Paige made her long-awaited return to WWE NXT after more than a decade, but her comeback resulted in a championship loss. During the November 4, 2026, episode of WWE NXT, Kelani Jordan defended her NXT Women's Championship against a mystery challenger, who was revealed to be the inaugural NXT Women's Champion, Paige. The returning star immediately took the fight to Jordan, utilizing strikes, headbutts, and a suplex before landing her signature RamPaige finisher. However, Jordan kicked out of the pin attempt. Jordan eventually countered a second RamPaige attempt into a rollup, securing the three-count to retain her title. This loss prevented Paige from reclaiming the championship she helped establish during her original tenure. It remains unclear if Paige's appearance was a one-time event or the start of a longer run with the brand.
