New Japan Pro-Wrestling has officially added a new match to the upcoming NJPW Bound for Unbound event. The promotion announced that Yuto-Ice will face freelancer Fuminori Abe at the show on November 20.

According to NJPW, while OSKAR went after Lance Archer, Yuto-Ice looked for his own opponent and selected a close friend in Abe. Both competitors share a passion for fighting and have a history of overcoming difficult pasts. This will mark their first-ever match against each other.

The updated card for NJPW Bound for Unbound on November 20 includes Yota Tsuji vs. Ares, Drilla Moloney vs. Daiki Nagai, Natsupoi and Shingo Takagi vs. Chihiro Hashimoto and Jeff Cobb, Robbie X vs. Dragon Lee, OSKAR vs. Lance Archer, and Yuto-Ice vs. Fuminori Abe.
