WWE is returning to Australia and New Zealand this December with a series of live events. The tour begins in Melbourne on December 9, followed by a show in Brisbane on December 15, and concludes in Auckland, New Zealand, on December 17. These dates mark the first time WWE has visited Brisbane in seven years and Auckland in ten years. Tickets for these shows go on sale on October 15, with an exclusive presale starting on October 14. The events in Melbourne, Brisbane, and Auckland will feature a variety of top WWE talent, including Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, Sami Zayn, Chelsea Green, and Trick Williams. Additionally, WWE previously confirmed three events at the RAC Arena in Perth: SmackDown on December 11, Wrestlepalooza: Perth on December 12, and Raw on December 14. Fans can also purchase various VIP packages for the tour, offering premium seating and exclusive experiences.
