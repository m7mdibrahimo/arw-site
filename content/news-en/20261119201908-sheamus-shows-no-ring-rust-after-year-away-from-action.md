Sheamus (Stephen Farrelly) demonstrated his readiness to return to competition despite a lengthy absence from the ring. Taking to social media on November 19, he marked the one-year milestone since his previous match, which occurred on the November 17, 2025 episode of WWE Raw.

During that contest, he joined forces with Rey Mysterio and John Cena to battle The Judgment Day.

The update featured footage from a recent training session inside the squared circle, highlighting that he has not lost a step. Sheamus is currently a free agent following the expiration of his WWE deal in early October.

According to Fightful Select, performers such as Claudio Castagnoli and Adam Copeland are actively pushing for him to sign with AEW. A week following that update, Sheamus stated that he is currently on vacation.
