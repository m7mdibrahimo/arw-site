Reflecting on her meteoric rise in Ring of Honor, Zayda Steel recently opened up about competing in the main event of ROH Supercard of Honor just five months after making her debut on the Final Battle pre-show. Speaking on the "Muscle Memory" podcast, Steel described the surreal experience of wrestling in the historic first-ever Women's Survival of the Fittest match while receiving guidance from her mentor, Christopher Daniels. Although she was the first competitor eliminated after crashing through a chair courtesy of defending champion Athena, Steel embraced the learning curve, comparing her mindset at the time to being a kid in a candy store. Athena ultimately retained the championship by outlasting Maya World.

Steel also shared her positive experiences working for Tony Khan, who oversees both ROH and AEW. Praising Khan's genuine passion for the industry, Steel noted that his enthusiasm is evident behind the curtain as he reacts to matches from the Gorilla position. She emphasized that working for someone who shares her deep love for professional wrestling makes the journey all the more rewarding.
