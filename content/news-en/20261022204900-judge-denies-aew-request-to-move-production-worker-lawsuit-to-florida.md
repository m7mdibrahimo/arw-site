A judge denied a request from AEW and Jon Moxley to transfer a civil lawsuit to a Florida court. According to Brandon Thurston, the case filed by former production worker Christopher Dispensa in May 2025 will remain in Michigan, where the incident occurred in May 2023.

Dispensa accused AEW and Moxley of negligence, civil assault, and battery after he was pushed to the floor. AEW previously sought to move the case to Florida, citing the residence of Dispensa and his doctors. A second settlement conference is scheduled for December 22, 2026, following an initial meeting in July.
