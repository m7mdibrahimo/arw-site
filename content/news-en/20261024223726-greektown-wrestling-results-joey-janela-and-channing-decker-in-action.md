Greektown Wrestling held its latest event on October 23 from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, with the show streaming on TrillerTV+.

The results featured pre-show victories for Sydney West over Georgia South, as well as Abu, Miles Deville, and Shelby Wylder defeating Anthony Vecchio, Mike Zee, and Shane Constant. Jimmy Lloyd captured a Scramble match against Junior Benito, Vaughn Vertigo, Zach Nystrom, and Brent Banks, while CoKane defeated Isaiah Broner. Seleziya Sparx picked up a win over Jody Threat, and The League defeated Devious Cass and Don Freeze.

In the second half of the card, Salsa King and Sonny Kiss defeated The Production, while PUF, Luigi Primo, and Jermaine Marbury downed Wes Barkley, Sam Holloway, and Jeffrey John. ELP defeated Bill Collier in a number one contenders match for the Greektown Championship. In the main event, Channing Decker, Joey Janela, and Sloane Jacobs defeated Marcus Mathers, Aaron Rourke, and B3CCA.
