AAA on FOX returns on November 21, 2026, at the Gimnasio Olímpico in Mexico City, Mexico. The event is scheduled to begin at 8 p.m. ET in Mexico and 9 p.m. ET in the United States. Fans can watch the broadcast on FOX in Latin America or via the AAA YouTube channel in the United States. The card features the AAA Trios Championships match between Perros Del Mal (Daga, Angel & Berto) and the team of El Grande Americano, La Parka, & Rey Fenix. Additionally, the Wagner Brothers (El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr. & Galeno del Mal) will defend their AAA Tag Team Championships against El Mesías & Mecha Wolf. The event also includes a singles match between Flammer and Faby Apache.
