Tony Khan addressed the future of the AEW x NJPW Wrestle Dynasty event during the Full Gear media call on Thursday. When asked if the 2025 Tokyo Dome show would get a sequel, Khan indicated that a follow-up is unlikely. He explained that the original event was designed as a collaboration to support NJPW's Wrestle Kingdom by sending top AEW stars to help bolster the Tokyo Dome card. Khan noted that NJPW appears to have adopted this model for their current Wrestle Kingdom event, incorporating world-class talent from AEW to maximize the show's scale. He stated that the original concept was to build an event that complemented Wrestle Kingdom, but NJPW's current approach is to focus on making Wrestle Kingdom itself as big as possible by featuring top international stars. Looking ahead to Wrestle Kingdom 21 in January, Kenny Omega is scheduled to challenge Yota Tsuji for the IWGP Heavyweight Championship, Jon Moxley will face Shingo Takagi in an Unbound Deathmatch, and MJF is set to compete against Ryohei Oiwa.
