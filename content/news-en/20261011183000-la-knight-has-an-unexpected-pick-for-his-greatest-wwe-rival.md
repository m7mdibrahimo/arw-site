LA Knight has reflected on his time in WWE and named the opponents who stand out the most in his career. In an interview with Sporf, Knight pointed to Solo Sikoa and Logan Paul as his most prominent rivals. While he feels deeply connected to Sikoa, he believes his feud with Paul had the biggest impact on his trajectory. "I kind of had that rub up with him three years ago going into Money in the Bank... And that kind of put us on the same trajectory in a certain way," Knight said. Knight eventually defeated Paul to win the United States Championship at SummerSlam in 2024. Today, his career has come full circle as he works alongside Sikoa while continuing his ongoing conflicts with Roman Reigns.
