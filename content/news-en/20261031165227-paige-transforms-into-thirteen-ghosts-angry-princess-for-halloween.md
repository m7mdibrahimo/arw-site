Paige has made a tradition of going all out for Halloween, and she embraced a much darker theme for 2026 by dressing up as the Angry Princess from the 2001 horror film Thirteen Ghosts. On October 31, she shared photos on X showing her recreation of the character, complete with signature bloody cuts, a bathroom setting, and the character's kitchen knife. She accompanied the reveal with a line from the movie, writing, "We have ten minutes until the ectoplasmic shit hits the fan 🔪 Happy Halloween 🖤"

Over the past few years, Paige's Halloween costumes have evolved significantly. In 2023, while performing as Saraya, she portrayed Angelina Jolie’s Lisa Rowe from Girl, Interrupted, followed by a transformation into Lara Croft in 2025. With her latest tribute to horror cinema, her annual costume reveals have firmly established themselves as a yearly tradition for her fans.
