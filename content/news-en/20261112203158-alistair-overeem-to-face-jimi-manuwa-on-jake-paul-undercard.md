Alistair Overeem will return to combat sports on Thursday, December 24, when he faces Jimi Manuwa in a six-round professional heavyweight boxing match. The bout will take place at the Coca-Cola Arena in Dubai, serving as part of the undercard for the event headlined by Jake Paul against Cédric Doumbé. Most Valuable Promotions (MVP) announced the matchup today, noting that this will be Overeem's first fight since October 2022. Overeem, a former Strikeforce heavyweight champion and K-1 World Grand Prix winner, holds a professional MMA record of 47-19. He will make his professional boxing debut against Manuwa, a former UFC light heavyweight contender who holds a 17-6 MMA record and is 1-0 in professional boxing. Nakisa Bidarian, CEO of Most Valuable Promotions, stated, "Alistair is one of the most decorated heavyweights in history, and Jimi has built his career on delivering spectacular knockouts. Bringing these two legends together in a professional boxing match adds another compelling matchup to an already massive Christmas Eve event."
