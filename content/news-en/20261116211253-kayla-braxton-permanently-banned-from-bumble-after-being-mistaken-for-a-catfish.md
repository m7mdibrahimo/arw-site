Kayla Braxton recently attempted to return to the dating app Bumble, but she discovered she was permanently banned from the platform. Braxton revealed on her Instagram Story on November 16 that the ban occurred five or six years ago because users repeatedly reported her profile as a catfish, refusing to believe she was actually the former WWE personality. The ban notification stated her account was removed for having an "inauthentic profile," and she claimed Bumble informed her that once an account is blocked, there is no possibility of reinstatement regardless of the reason. Braxton also noted that she has no interest in using Raya, another dating app she has tried intermittently for years, describing it as the worst option available despite being the only one permitted for public figures.
