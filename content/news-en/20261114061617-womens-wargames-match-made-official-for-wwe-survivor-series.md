According to Fightful, a Women's WarGames bout has been locked in for Survivor Series. During the latest episode of WWE SmackDown, Charlotte Flair, Alexa Bliss, Tatum Paxley, Bayley, and Stephanie Vaquer laid down a challenge for the steel cage battle.

The company subsequently made the clash official for the upcoming premium live event. The contest will pit Charlotte and her allies against Jade Cargill, Michin and/or B-Fab, and Fatal Influence members Jacy Jayne, Fallon Henley, and Lainey Reid.
