New Japan Pro-Wrestling's NEVER Openweight Champion Aaron Wolf is hoping to bring Olympic gold medalist Christa Deguchi into the world of professional wrestling. During an event at the Aaron Wolf Cup in Katsushika, Japan, on October 25, Wolf sat down for a talk show with his former classmate. Deguchi, a two-time Judo World Champion and 2024 Paris Olympic gold medalist, retired from Judo in March 2026 after a 27-year career.

Wolf openly encouraged her to transition to the ring, though Deguchi admitted she is hesitant because she knows how difficult the profession is. While she has yet to commit to a move, Wolf extended a formal invitation for her to attend NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 21 on January 4, 2027, at the Tokyo Dome. Deguchi noted that she is interested in seeing him compete in such a special moment.
