According to Ringside News, fresh court documents show that Richard Reap alleges he suffered a concussion and a torn rotator cuff during his April altercation with WWE star Ludwig Kaiser, whose real name is Marcel Barthel. Reap stated during an October 16 pretrial hearing that he sought medical care after Kaiser struck him and pushed him to the ground.

> "At Pretrial Hearing on October 16, 2026, Mr. Reap testified that he suffered injuries—including a torn rotator cuff and a concussion—and sought medical care for those injuries."

Defense attorney Thomas D. Sommerville is seeking medical records to verify the injuries, though Reap and prosecutors object to handing over the documents. Kaiser maintains he acted in self-defense at an Orlando apartment complex, and his case proceeds toward a December 17 pretrial conference.
