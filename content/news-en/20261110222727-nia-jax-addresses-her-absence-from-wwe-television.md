Nia Jax has been absent from WWE television for nearly two months, but she clarified that there is no major mystery behind her time away. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, Jax explained that she simply goes wherever the company needs her, noting that she recently worked a live event in Hawaii and wrestled Giulia on WWE Main Event.

"Well, I was in Hawaii at one of our live events, which was incredible. I did Main Event versus Giulia. I had a really fun match with her. I go where I'm needed, so right now I'm just chilling," Jax said.

Jax has not appeared on WWE television since the September 11 episode of WWE SmackDown in Mexico City. During that appearance, she faced Chelsea Green in a WWE Women’s Championship match and attempted to draw heat by using "USA" chants, which led to a vulgar chant from the crowd. Jax continues to take bookings when called upon by WWE and appears content to wait until she is brought back to weekly television.
