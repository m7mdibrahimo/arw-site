The Syx revealed that a major surprise during their shocking TNA debut was meant for them as well as the fans. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, Shaw shared that much of what fans saw came from concepts he and Sawyer had pitched to TNA, though they had no idea the company would actually use them.

Shaw explained they were blown away upon arriving at the building to find brand-new Syx shirts waiting for them. Once they walked through the curtain and looked at the Tron, they realized TNA secretly included nearly every creative idea they discussed, including glitch effects, chainsaws, and blood.

The duo is now preparing to face The Hardys at Bound for Glory this week. This highlights the creative freedom they have received in TNA, seeing their ideas come to life in front of a live crowd.

> "Yeah, look, I’m wearing brand-new Syx T-shirts. So this was a concept—I drew this up on a napkin at first, and I was like, ‘You know, if we just had Syx and it looked like the chainsaw coming through one of the letters,’ and they were immediately like, ‘Excellent.’ They’re like, ‘Make it a little more gory. Put some blood there.’ It’s something I couldn’t have gotten through in WWE. We got a bit of an edge now. They were all about this image."

> "Our first day on the job, they have this shirt for me and Joe. Then we get out to the ring and we see up on the Tron all the ideas we pitched—like chainsaws, the glitching, the way that the lettering of the Syx on the screen—goosebumps again, because this is a reveal. But then I’m looking up at the Tron, and we didn’t even know if it was gonna be there. But that imagery, the things we had discussed, that’s all there. So amazing. We’re working in conjunction together to make this dream a reality."
