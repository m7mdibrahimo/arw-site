Fallon Henley discussed her alliance with Jacy Jayne as part of the Fatal Influence faction, highlighting the strength of their partnership. The two have been aligned since August 2024, initially joined by Jazmyn Nyx before Lainey Reid took her place. During this time, Henley has secured both singles and tag team championships, while Jayne captured the WWE Women's United States Championship on the main roster.

In a recent interview with Brad Gilmore and The Collection, Henley reflected on working with Jayne, noting that their long-standing history makes their collaboration effective.

> "Well, her and I have, we started way back when, when we were both on the independent scene, we started together, we started training together. So being alongside somebody that I've known so long, I mean, it makes a great partnership, and if our work doesn't speak for that, then I don't know what does."
