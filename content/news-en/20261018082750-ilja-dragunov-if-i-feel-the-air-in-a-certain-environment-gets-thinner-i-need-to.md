Following a teaser that aired on AEW Dynamite, Ilja Dragunov officially debuted as the newest member of the AEW roster at AEW WrestleDream. Dragunov recently left WWE in August, having not wrestled since April. During the WrestleDream press conference, he explained his reasons for making the jump to AEW. He noted that after nearly 14 years in the industry and various experiences across the independent scene, he felt a strong urge for fresh challenges and continuous competition. "If I feel that the air in a certain environment gets thinner for me, I need to breathe. That's what I did today, here," Dragunov stated regarding his decision to seek new opportunities.
