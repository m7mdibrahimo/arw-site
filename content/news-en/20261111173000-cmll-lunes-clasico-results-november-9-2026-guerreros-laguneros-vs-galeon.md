Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre held the CMLL Lunes Clásico event on November 9, 2026, at Arena Puebla in Puebla, México. 

Here are the results from the show:

Aguila Egipcia & Multy def. Centella Roja & El Hijo de Centella Roja, and Sombras Diabolicas (Sombra Diabolika & Sombra Diabolika Jr.) in a three-way match to become the #1 contenders for the CMLL Arena Puebla Tag Team Championship.

Fuego & Millenium def. Kheops & Prayer.

Hermanos Calavera (Calavera Jr. I & Calavera Jr. II) def. Fuerza Poblana (Arkalis & Stigma).

Persephone def. Tessa Blanchard in a Lightning Match.

Dulce Gardenia, Máscara Dorada & Xelhua def. Yutani & Hermanos Chávez (Ángel de Oro & Niebla Roja).

Guerreros Laguneros (Gran Guerrero, Stuka Jr. & Ultimo Guerrero) def. Galeón Fantasma (Zandokan Jr., Barboza & Difunto).
