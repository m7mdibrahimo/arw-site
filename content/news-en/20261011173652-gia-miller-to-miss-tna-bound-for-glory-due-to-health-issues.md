TNA Wrestling ring announcer Gia Miller will miss the upcoming TNA Bound for Glory event. Although she managed to travel to Tampa, Florida for the show, a sudden health decline forced her to return home.

> "Well, I made it to Tampa only for my health to nose dive overnight. I'm going to have to go home, regroup, and get well. I'm devastated to be missing BFG. To my TNA family, I love and miss you all, stay safe, and have a great show tonight,"

Miller shared on social media. This follows previous health challenges in September when she missed TNA iMPACT! tapings after visiting the emergency room twice and undergoing two minor surgeries.
