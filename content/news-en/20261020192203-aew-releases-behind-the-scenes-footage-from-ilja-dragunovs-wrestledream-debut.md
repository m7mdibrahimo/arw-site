AEW has shared behind-the-scenes footage on X showing Ilja Dragunov's arrival and debut at WrestleDream. Despite visa issues that delayed his arrival, Dragunov finally debuted after AEW teased him through vignettes. The video includes clips of him working out in a ring, preparing backstage, and hugging AEW owner Tony Khan, alongside comments from Dragunov expressing his gratitude. Additionally, AEW announced that Dragunov will make his in-ring wrestling debut on AEW Dynamite this week, marking his first match outside of WWE since 2022.
