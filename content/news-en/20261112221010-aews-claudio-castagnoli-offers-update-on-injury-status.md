Claudio Castagnoli has provided an update on his current injury status following his absence from AEW television. Castagnoli was last seen on the October 23 episode of "AEW Dynamite," where he was put through a table by "Switchblade" Jay White. While speaking with Paul Delos Santos on "The Load-In" during the League of Legends World Championships, Castagnoli revealed that he has not been cleared for action.

> "I'm not medically cleared right now so I was just sitting around at home, so [League of Legends] asked me if I wanted to come here, see Worlds and I'm like 'Yeah definitely, 100% I'll be there."

Castagnoli's last match took place on the October 24 episode of "AEW Collision," where he teamed with his fellow Death Riders to face Shane Taylor Promotions. As for the rest of the group, the lineup for the Anarchy in the Arena match at AEW Full Gear was confirmed on the November 11 episode of "AEW Dynamite." Jon Moxley, Gabe Kidd, and Daniel Garcia will represent the Death Riders alongside Orange Cassidy, Roderick Strong, and Kyle O'Reilly of The Conglomeration to face Jay White, Ace Austin, David Finlay, Clark Connors, Austin Gunn, and Colten Gunn.
