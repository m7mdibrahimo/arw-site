Game Changer Wrestling promoter Brett Lauderdale is cautioning wrestlers against signing contracts with Major League Wrestling. A recent report from Fightful Select indicated that MLW is planning a roster refresh and evaluating talent as part of an effort to clear payroll for potential high-profile signings. Lauderdale reacted to the news by arguing that signing with the organization provides little benefit to performers. He stated, "There is literally zero reason to sign a contract with MLW. They only do a handful of tapings per year and you dont make any money. Your career momentum stalls and you cant sign w/ aew or wwe unless they let you out." Lauderdale further noted that MLW contracts include a restrictive clause that prevents talent from appearing for GCW. He concluded by advising young wrestlers against committing years of their careers to the promotion without a guaranteed substantial income.
