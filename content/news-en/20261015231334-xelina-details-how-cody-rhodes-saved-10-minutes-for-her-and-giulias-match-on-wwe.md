Xelina, formerly known in WWE as Zelina Vega, recently shared that her August 2025 SmackDown match against Giulia is one of her favorite bouts from her WWE tenure. During an interview with D.S. Shin of Ring The Belle, Xelina explained that their original match time was dependent on the opening segment featuring John Cena and Cody Rhodes. Xelina feared their time would be cut significantly, but Rhodes intervened.

> "As we’re in Gorilla, Cody comes back and he goes, 'Hey, saved you guys an extra 10 minutes. Have fun.'" Xelina noted that while the extra time caused initial panic because they had not planned for a longer match, it allowed them to showcase their skills properly. She credited Rhodes and Cena for the opportunity, as she and Giulia relied on calling spots during the bout. Xelina, who was let go from WWE in April 2026, expressed deep admiration for Giulia, describing her as a close friend and a "wrestling soulmate."
