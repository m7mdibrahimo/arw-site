John Cena recently discussed what a potential appearance for him in GTA 6 would look like. Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to release next month after two delays, and a recent trailer featured backyard wrestling. Speaking in an interview alongside Jessica Biel, Cena imagined how his character would be integrated into the game. He joked about using his signature moves and memes from his 25-year career in a cameo that might eventually happen in a future installment.
