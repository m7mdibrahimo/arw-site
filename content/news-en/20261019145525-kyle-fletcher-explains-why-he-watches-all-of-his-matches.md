AEW star Kyle Fletcher revealed that he reviews every single match he wrestles to evaluate his performance and apply feedback to his future in the ring. Fletcher has emerged as a top rising talent in AEW over the past few years, establishing himself among the best competitors in the professional wrestling world.

During an interview with Westwood One Sports Night, the Australian wrestler explained that reviewing his bouts allows him to maintain an objective perspective away from the emotion of the moment. 

> "Yeah. I try and watch every single match of mine back. I think it's the- I think it's a very important part of the feedback loop, being able to watch myself objectively removed from it and look at it as a finished product and be able to take that feedback and apply it to myself."
