Sami Zayn recently opened up about his early days in WWE NXT and discussed his perspective on the industry's constant drive for achievement. Reflecting on The Undertaker's WWE Hall of Fame speech, Zayn noted that while he respects the legend immensely, he disagrees with the notion of never being satisfied. Zayn explained that this relentless mindset can lead to a locker room full of miserable people who fail to appreciate their own work. He recalled how NXT coaches pushed the narrative that wrestlers needed to strive to become the next John Cena or risk not belonging in the company. However, Zayn pointed out that Triple H always provided a much-needed perspective during those days, reminding everyone to enjoy the moment because it would eventually end.
