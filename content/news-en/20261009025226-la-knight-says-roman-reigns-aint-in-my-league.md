LA Knight recently addressed the comparison between his career path and Roman Reigns' ahead of their World Heavyweight Championship match at Money in the Bank. Knight acknowledged the success Reigns has achieved in WWE, but he emphasized the stark difference in how each man started his journey.

> "Look, here's the one thing that, again, for a guy who had no connections in the business. My dad loaded trucks for a living. And it's a respectable living, but it didn't do anything for me career-wise. You know what I mean?"

Knight noted that while Reigns benefited from a famous wrestling family, he had to build his career from scratch without any inherent advantages. Despite this, he credited Reigns for meeting the high expectations placed upon him.

> "However, when people are like, you know, there's a very small amount of people, if I'm honest, who are like, ‘Ah, you know, LA Knight's not in Roman Reigns' league.’ Huh? The guy came in the business here and now he's here. I came in the business here and now I'm here. You ask me, he ain't in my league."

Knight concluded by stating that the disparity in their entry points into the industry is the primary reason he considers himself superior. He is heading into the upcoming title match looking to prove that his hard-earned climb makes him the rightful champion.
