During an appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Cody Rhodes discussed the valuable insight he received from Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson regarding the intersection of professional wrestling and Hollywood acting. Rhodes noted that Johnson gets annoyed when wrestlers openly dismiss Hollywood opportunities.

Rhodes revealed what Johnson told him about that mindset:

> "One time, Rock told me he doesn't like when wrestlers would say, 'Oh, I don't want to be an actor, I always want to be a wrestler.' And he had good reasons; he said, 'Of course you love wrestling.' ... But also, wrestling is storytelling. And that's what he said: 'What I wish people would say is that they just want to grow as a storyteller."

Rhodes later used that exact philosophy during a general meeting with film studio executives in Los Angeles, emphasizing that professional wrestlers are naturally equipped for acting because storytelling is their core craft. Rhodes has recently expanded his own Hollywood presence, taking on the role of Guile in the upcoming "Street Fighter" movie adaptation.
