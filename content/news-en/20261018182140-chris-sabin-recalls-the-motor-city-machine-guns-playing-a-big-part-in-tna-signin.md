Chris Sabin recently reflected on how The Motor City Machine Guns played a major role in TNA signing The Young Bucks back when they wrestled as Generation Me. Speaking in an interview with MuscleManMalcolm, Sabin explained that after facing The Young Bucks at PWG, he and his tag team partner urged Terry Taylor in Talent Relations to bring them in for a tryout. Now that both legendary tag teams are part of the roster in All Elite Wrestling, Sabin expressed his excitement to reignite their classic rivalry and face them once again.
