A new feature film is currently in development based on the life story and book of Nattie Neidhart, according to Fightful. Jeff Bowler revealed that he is collaborating with Neidhart to adapt her memoir titled The Last Hart Beating: From the Dungeon to WWE, which was released in October 2025.

> "Excited to be developing this very special film. It's based on Nattie's book and life story, The Last Hart Beating. We're making magic! Taking studio meetings at Paramount and others, with the team after watching I Play Rocky at the Grove for inspiration, and Nattie signing with UTA. Writer Nate Skulnick, and the best team around it. It also brings me back full circle to the WWE, where I was an executive 25 years ago! So much more to come."

The production plan includes holding meetings with studios such as Paramount alongside writer Nate Skulnick and a team that drew inspiration from watching I Play Rocky at the Grove. The project also marks a return for Bowler to WWE, 25 years after he previously served as an executive for the company.
