WWE taped the November 6, 2026 episode of WWE SmackDown following the November 2, 2026 episode of WWE RAW. Before the taping, Trick Williams cut a promo, but Danhausen interrupted him. The show opened with Gunther, who addressed his actions during the Halloween episode. He claimed he was doing everyone a favor by taking out Danhausen and criticized Finn Balor for his involvement. When Balor confronted him, a brawl broke out, ending with Gunther retreating. Shinsuke Nakamura defeated Tama Tonga in a singles match. In a WWE Women's Tag Team Championship match, Fatal Influence faced B-Fab and Michin. The match ended in a brawl after Tatum Paxley attacked Jade Cargill, drawing out Bayley, Charlotte Flair, and Alexa Bliss to join the fight. SmackDown GM Nick Aldis later spoke with WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green, who admitted to struggling with self-doubt. Liv Morgan interrupted to mock Green, claiming she only won the title because Rhea Ripley was injured and declaring she would prove Green was a fluke at Crown Jewel. In a backstage interview, Trick Williams addressed his recent setback and his upcoming focus on Damian Priest. Damian Priest defeated Nathan Frazer in a two-minute match after the referee ruled Frazer could not continue following a collision with the announce table. Axiom attempted to intervene but was attacked by Priest. WWE Champion Sami Zayn addressed the crowd, discussing his recent defense of the title and his confrontation with Roman Reigns on WWE RAW. Jimmy Uso interrupted to warn Zayn that he had made things personal with Reigns, but Zayn welcomed the challenge. As Zayn left, 946 attacked Jimmy Uso from the crowd, and Zayn stood by and watched the assault before leaving Jimmy to be beaten. In the main event, CM Punk and Kevin Owens fought in a No Holds Barred match. The two brawled throughout the arena, using trash cans, steel chairs, and tables. After a chaotic fight that saw both men crash through tables, Owens struck Punk with a steel chain to score the pinfall victory.
