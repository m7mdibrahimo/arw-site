Tony D'Angelo recently discussed his character shift and the breakup of The Family within WWE NXT. The faction disbanded last July, which led to D'Angelo taking a hiatus before returning this past February with a new look, character, and speech pattern. In a new interview with Denise Salcedo, D'Angelo reflected on the transition.

> "It's been fun. You know, I enjoy it because, I've said this before, but you know, the Don character, so to speak is just one part of who I am and who I was. Now we got this Tony D'Angelo, who's a badass bruiser. Who's just, it's just another part of who I am. So I've had fun with both and I just, I'm thankful that the fans have, stuck with me, because sometimes transitions can be a little weird, with wrestlers. But it's been, I can't say, you know, it hasn't been fun. It's sometimes it is stressful for sure. It was a little difficult, but I don't have to stay clean shaven anymore. I got this beard now, so it's kind of cool." D'Angelo noted that backstage factors influenced the creative pivot.

> "Well, there were, I'll say this, there were a lot of behind the scenes stuff that caused the change and the breakup and all this other stuff, you know, nothing bad, but just, you know, backstage things happen. So now we we pivoted, I went on that little hiatus and now I am this version of Tony D and it's still fun. You know, I'm still an ass kicker. I just- I had to go to speech therapy. You know, I used to have an accent, but now, you know, some people had a hard time understanding what I was saying. So I had to- the WWE, thank you. They paid for it, and now I can speak like a normal human, apparently."
