AEW star Tommaso Ciampa recently revealed his aspirations to wrestle in NJPW, expressing a strong desire to face former IWGP Heavyweight Champion Shingo Takagi. In an interview with SBNation, Ciampa discussed his career goals and openness to a limited schedule.

> "Shingo always jumps out at me as someone I've always wanted to share a ring with," Ciampa said.

> "I don't know that my body or personal life at this point bodes well for a full-on tour or G1 tournament, but doing a show here or there, or three shows, I'd be 100% open to that."

Takagi has recently crossed paths with several AEW talents, including matches against the late Pac and Claudio Castagnoli. Meanwhile, Ciampa recently challenged for the AEW National Championship against Andrade El Idolo at AEW WrestleDream, coming up short in his bid for the title.
