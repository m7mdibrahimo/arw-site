Ric Flair is responding directly to people questioning how he is still alive following his recent fall. The 77-year-old Hall of Famer suffered a facial and eye injury after falling in Florida on October 23, requiring treatment at Tampa General Hospital. Rather than expressing concern about his health, Flair took to social media on October 30 to highlight the massive physical toll his body has endured throughout his life.

Flair listed his career accomplishments and survival stories, noting that he competed in over 5,000 matches, survived a major plane crash, and spent weeks on life support and in rehabilitation. He dismissed worries over his recent accident by comparing his extensive career workload to other major wrestling figures such as Brock Lesnar, Triple H, John Cena, and The Undertaker.

> "The question I keep getting asked is — how am I still alive? After 5,000 matches with 2,400 of them being one-hour draws and five 90-minute bouts. Round trip to Japan, 24 hours, only to wrestle three one-hour draws. Airplane crash, 31 days in the ICU and 14 days on life support. 35 days in rehab. And you are worried about me falling down and hitting my eye? WTF."

This response comes shortly after Flair revealed he visited the USF Health Eye Institute on October 28, where doctors determined he needs another procedure and a potential skin graft. Despite facing additional medical treatment alongside an existing blood clot in his leg, Flair remains determined and confident about his recovery.
