The newly crowned Women's Champion Tiffany Stratton is scheduled to appear on the November 13 episode of WWE SmackDown. This follows her successful title win at WWE Crown Jewel, where she attacked Chelsea Green to cash in her Money in the Bank briefcase.

Stratton struck Green with the briefcase immediately after Green secured the WWE Women's Crown Jewel Title. She then executed her signature moonsault to claim the championship. John Cena will also appear on the program to discuss the John Cena Classic.
