Big Boom AJ discussed his current contract status with AEW, revealing that his deal is set to expire at the end of February 2027. Speaking with Tom Campbell of Cultaholic, Big Boom noted that he has fulfilled his minimum four-match requirement for the year, having competed at AEW Revolution, AEW Double or Nothing, AEW All In, and most recently at AEW Full Gear. Expressing his desire to continue with the promotion, he stated that he is happy in AEW and hopes to extend his run, mentioning potential involvement around AEW Worlds End.
