Drew McIntyre has no problem speaking his mind about CM Punk, and he recently took things a step further by claiming he would easily defeat Punk in a real fight. During his appearance on the Raw Talk podcast, McIntyre discussed his intense rivalry with Punk and suggested that his opponent's UFC background would not help him in a genuine physical altercation. McIntyre stated that he avoids Punk backstage and that their on-screen animosity carries over into real life. He even dismissed the value of mixed martial arts or Brazilian jiu-jitsu experience against him, emphasizing his size and background. The two rivals previously engaged in a brutal feud throughout 2024, culminating in Punk defeating McIntyre inside Hell in a Cell at WWE Bad Blood 2024.
