Austin Theory and Maxxine Dupri have been drawing attention for their convincing WWE partnership, but Theory believes he knows exactly why their pairing irritates fans. According to Theory, people may dislike them, but they will eventually have to acknowledge their appeal.

Speaking on NotSam Wrestling, Theory addressed his relationship with Dupri after host Sam Roberts noted how well their appearances and personalities complement each other as heels. Theory embraced the arrogance that defines their on-screen dynamic, stating: 

> "It's infuriating, right? Yeah, I think that's what gets under the skin of people. Because it's like, you look at us and you can hate on us, but we both have abs, we both have beautiful faces, you know, we just got it. So it's one of those things where you're like, you just gotta hate, but at some point you're gonna have to really appreciate it. And I think that opportunity, when it's there, we're going to jump all over it and take it."

Theory argues that he and Dupri possess the traits others envy, making them easy targets for fan resentment. This fits the obnoxious confidence they have displayed since Dupri joined Theory following her split from Alpha Academy. Their chemistry has also gained traction on social media, with recent viral videos showing them in matching outfits, which only fueled fan speculation. Despite the online chatter, there is no indication of a real-life romance, but Theory seems to enjoy using the attention to further their heel persona.
