Pro Wrestling Illustrated revealed its annual rankings, placing Stephanie Vaquer at number one in the PWI Women's 250 for 2026. Vaquer experienced a notable year that featured holding and winning the WWE Women's Championship, entering the evaluation period as champion before capturing the belt again at WWE Money in the Bank, dropping it at WrestleMania, and missing four months of action due to an injury.

Mercedes Moné dropped to fourth place after claiming the top spot in 2025, with Willow Nightingale taking second and Thekla claiming third. Rounding out the top ten were Saya Kamitani, Liv Morgan, Persephone, Syuri, Rhea Ripley, and Kris Statlander.

Evaluations covered the span from October 1, 2025, to September 30, 2026. Judges evaluated candidates using criteria such as in-ring achievement, influence, technical ability, competition, and activity.
