Although WWE had promoted a singles match between SmackDown stars GUNTHER and Finn Balor for Crown Jewel, neither superstar traveled to Saudi Arabia for the event. According to WRKD Wrestling, management simply felt the rivalry required more buildup and that the four-match card was sufficient. Balor and GUNTHER have crossed paths since the SummerSlam four-way bout to crown a number one contender for the Undisputed WWE Championship, and tensions boiled over on the October 30 episode of WWE SmackDown when Balor deployed his Demon persona with Danhausen's assistance to ambush GUNTHER. While no new date has been confirmed, speculation points toward the upcoming WWE Survivor Series: WarGames event on November 28.
