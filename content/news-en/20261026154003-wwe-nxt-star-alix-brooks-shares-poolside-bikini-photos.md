Alix Brooks has not appeared on WWE NXT television yet, but she is already grabbing attention on social media. The new WWE star shared a series of bikini photos on her Instagram account, showing herself enjoying the sunshine by the pool.

Brooks wore a brown bikini paired with black sunglasses and a high ponytail while relaxing on a lounge chair surrounded by palm trees. She accompanied the pictures with a short caption reflecting her high confidence, writing:

> "Everything I want wants me even more"

Her followers quickly filled the comments section with reactions and praise. Brooks signed a contract with WWE earlier this year after coming through the NIL talent program. Although she originally planned to perform as Kylee Quinn, she settled on the name Alix Brooks ahead of her anticipated WWE NXT debut.
