Veteran wrestler Mercedes Martinez has officially undergone surgery to repair a torn ACL in her right knee, which she suffered during an August 15 match against Allie Katch. Lacking health insurance, Martinez launched a GoFundMe campaign to cover the medical costs. In a video update posted to X, she confirmed that fan donations successfully paid for the entire procedure. Martinez is now focused on her recovery at home, beginning rehabilitation exercises and managing her pain without heavy medication as she starts the long road back.
