WWE NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan has opened up about the main challenge she faced when transitioning from gymnastics to professional wrestling. Speaking on the Wrastlin' with E & Matt podcast, Jordan explained that her gymnastics background made high-flying moves and body control natural to her, but her biggest struggle was accepting lack of perfection. In gymnastics, athletes are judged strictly on perfection, making it difficult for her to adapt when things looked rough around the edges in the ring.

Jordan also highlighted other key differences between the two sports, noting that gymnastics is not a contact sport and does not require working directly with a partner. Furthermore, she explained that gymnastics taught her to tune out outside distractions, whereas professional wrestling requires athletes to listen to the crowd and react to their surroundings while staying in the moment, including during promo segments.

Despite these challenges, Jordan's athletic transition has been highly successful. Within just three years of her wrestling career, she has captured the NXT Women's Championship, the NXT Women's North American Championship, and the TNA Knockouts Championship, establishing herself as one of WWE's premier rising stars.
