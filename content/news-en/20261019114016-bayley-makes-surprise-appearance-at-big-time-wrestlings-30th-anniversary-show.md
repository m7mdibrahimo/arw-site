Bayley returned to the promotion where her career began during Big Time Wrestling's 30th anniversary show on October 18, 2026. She served as the special guest referee for Aaron Solo's match against Starboy Charlie, stopping Solo from using his belt and helping Charlie secure the victory with a moonsault. Afterward, Bayley received a warm welcome from the crowd in Newark, California, where she wrestled as Davina Rose in 2008, and fought back tears while addressing the fans.
