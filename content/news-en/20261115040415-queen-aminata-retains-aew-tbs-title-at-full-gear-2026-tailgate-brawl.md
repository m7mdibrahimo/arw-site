Before the AEW Full Gear 2026 pay-per-view officially began, the Tailgate Brawl portion aired on TNT and HBO MAX. The headline match featured Queen Aminata, who successfully defended her AEW TBS Championship. The bout underwent a last-minute change due to travel issues in the United States; while Aminata was originally scheduled to defend her title against Persephone and Maya World, Maya World was unable to attend. As a result, Hyan and Skye Blue were added to the match, making it a four-way contest.

Despite the increased competition, Aminata remained dominant. She attacked Skye, Persephone, and Dr. Britt Baker D.M.D. at ringside before delivering her "Off With Her Head" finisher to Hyan to secure the victory and her second successful title defense. In the opening contest, "Jungle" Jack Perry faced The Beast Mortos. Perry utilized his power to set up the finish, executing a Piledriver followed by a Running Knee. He then hit a Muscle Buster on Mortos to win and send a message to Samoa Joe.

Additionally, the Don Callis Family secured a victory in a trios match as Wardlow, El Clon, and Kevin Knight defeated RJ Wylde, Jordan Cruz, and Jaden Monroe, with Knight finishing the match with a UFO Splash. In an eight-man tag team match, Trent Beretta, Rocky Romero, Josh Alexander, and Lance Archer were defeated by QT Marshall, Big Boom AJ, Matt Sydal, and Darius Martin, after Sydal hit a Shooting Star Press on Romero.
