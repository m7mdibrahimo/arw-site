Kyle Fletcher recently shared his thoughts on Adam Copeland's looming retirement and expressed his desire to face him again inside the ring. Copeland announced that he will retire from professional wrestling soon, setting his sights on the AEW World Title before stepping away. Meanwhile, Fletcher is scheduled to challenge for the same championship at AEW Full Gear.

During an interview with TMZ Sports, Fletcher spoke about the wrestling icon's upcoming departure from the sport.

> "My thoughts are, I think he is an absolute legend in the sport, and I think that he, dude, he was a part of my childhood. He was a part of so many people's childhoods and one of the best things and the best feelings in wrestling is that nostalgia and remembering, how it made you feel when you're a kid, and I think he was probably one of the first wrestlers that I watched, you know? So for me, it's a lot of mixed emotions. I'm obviously very sad. I watched him retire in whatever it was, I believe 2010, and I thought that was going to be the end of it. I was watching live when he made his return and yeah, that was very emotional too. So I'm sure watching him retire will be very emotional as well."

Fletcher concluded by noting that he has already pinned Copeland previously, but he wants another match to prove his superiority before his career ends.
