Penta will compete on the upcoming episode of WWE Raw. General Manager Adam Pearce announced that the current Mr. Money in the Bank will face Bron Breakker in a singles match on the October 19 edition of the show. The two last shared the ring at the Money in the Bank event, where Penta secured the briefcase. The upcoming Raw will be held at the Pechanga Arena in San Diego, California. The updated card for the show includes Sami Zayn and Roman Reigns appearing face-to-face, JD McDonagh vs. Joe Hendry, and Penta vs. Bron Breakker.
