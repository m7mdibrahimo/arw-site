WWE was open to altering the schedule for Friday Night SmackDown to assist the WNBA in resolving a major arena conflict for the WNBA Finals, and Nick Khan had no objection to the shift. According to ESPN, WWE was asked to push SmackDown back from its usual 8 PM ET time slot to 9 PM ET on Friday, October 30, 2026. This adjustment would have permitted Game 6 between the Golden State Valkyries and the Atlanta Dream to broadcast on USA Network from 7 PM to 9 PM ET.

The scheduling dilemma arose because the WNBA needed a suitable time for Game 6 at State Farm Arena in Atlanta, while the Atlanta Hawks were already booked to face the Cleveland Cavaliers at the venue on Thursday, October 29. Amid urgent talks over the weekend, State Farm Arena and Atlanta Hawks CEO Steve Koonin contacted WWE President Nick Khan to see if the promotion could accommodate the basketball league. ESPN explained how WWE became involved in the discussions.

> "During the frantic negotiations over the weekend, an unlikely ally emerged — the WWE. At one point, sources told ESPN that Steve Koonin, the Hawks and State Farm Arena CEO, reached out to WWE president Nick Khan and asked if he'd be willing to move the telecast of this week's Smackdown to accommodate Game 6 of the WNBA Finals on Friday night."

Because both broadcasts air on USA Network, shifting SmackDown by an hour would have opened up the necessary television window. ESPN provided further details regarding Khan's response.

> "Smackdown usually airs at 8 p.m. ET on Friday nights on USA Network, which is the broadcast network for this game. If Game 6 were to be played from 7-9 p.m. at State Farm Arena, the WWE would have to move an hour later. Khan told him they'd be fine with the later broadcast window if it was needed to help the WNBA Finals play in a more appropriate arena."

Ultimately, the proposed plan did not happen. Shifting Game 6 to Friday would have pushed a potential Game 7 to Tuesday, November 3, conflicting with Election Night coverage, so the WNBA selected a different path. The league announced that Game 6 would occur on Thursday, October 29, at 12:30 PM ET, and the Hawks moved their game against Cleveland to 8 PM ET that night. Consequently, WWE SmackDown will air at its standard 8 PM ET start time on Friday, October 30, without any modifications.
