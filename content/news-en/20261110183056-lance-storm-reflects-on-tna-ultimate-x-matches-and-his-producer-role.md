Lance Storm recently reflected on whether he would have wanted to compete in an Ultimate X match in TNA during his in-ring career. The Ultimate X match has long been a defining staple of the promotion and its X-Division.

Speaking to Love Wrestling in a new interview, Storm shared his thoughts on the high-flying fixture.

> "Part of me is glad I never had to, but there is a part of me, if I remember back to my 20s and 30s, I think I would have really enjoyed it."

Addressing his responsibilities producing those matches, Storm explained that he views his role as an editor rather than imposing his own wrestling style on others. He emphasized that he wants performers like Trey Miguel and Jason Hotch to showcase their individual identities in the ring without trying to copy him.
