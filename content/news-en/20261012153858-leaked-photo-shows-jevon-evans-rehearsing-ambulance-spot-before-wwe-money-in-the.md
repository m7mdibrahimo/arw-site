A leaked backstage photo offers a rare look at how WWE rehearsed Je'Von Evans' unforgettable ambulance spot before Money in the Bank went live. The image, captured inside the Smoothie King Center, shows Evans standing atop a ladder near the briefcase during pre-show preparations. Other WWE Superstars are also visible in the photo, including Trick Williams talking with Bron Breakker in the ring, while Dominik Mysterio and JD McDonagh watch from ringside. Kevin Owens sits on a ladder nearby, and Sol Ruca, Roxanne Perez along with other women's ladder match competitors discuss their spots. The rehearsal highlights the extensive planning required to safely execute massive stunts, even as Evans still took a massive physical risk during the live broadcast.
