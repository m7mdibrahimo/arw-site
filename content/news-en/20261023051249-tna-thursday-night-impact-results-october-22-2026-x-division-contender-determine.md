TNA Thursday Night Impact aired on October 22, 2026, from the Dothan Civic Center in Dothan, Alabama. The show featured a Four-Way Number One Contender's Match for the X-Division Championship, following Amazing Red's title win at TNA Bound for Glory.

In the opening segment, Daria Rae returned to TNA and confronted Knockouts World Champion Indi Hartwell. This led to a title match between Hartwell and Xia Brookside. Hartwell retained her title, but was attacked by Shayna Baszler and Zoey Serrano after the match.

Cedric Alexander defeated Rich Swann, Fabian Aichner, and KC Navarro in a Four-Way Match to become the new number one contender for the X-Division Championship. Following the victory, he was confronted by the champion, Amazing Red.

In other action, Avery Styles defeated Joe Alonzo. Kiera Hogan and Allie addressed their recent title loss, which ended with Hogan turning on Allie and attacking her alongside Tasha Steelz. Shotzi Blackheart made the save for Allie.
