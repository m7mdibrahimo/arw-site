Mercedes Moné has faced accusations of wielding excessive creative control in AEW, with some believing she dictates her own booking and decides who loses to her. However, people who work directly with her have pushed back against these claims. During a recent Fightful Q&A podcast, Sean Ross Sapp addressed the rumors and revealed that he spoke with individuals inside AEW, people close to Moné, and independent promoters who booked her. None of them confirmed that she possesses creative control.

Her extensive championship collection, which included collecting titles across the independent scene, previously raised questions about who was making the decisions. However, independent promoters understood the arrangements when bringing her in. They paid big money for her star power and agreed to the title plans in advance, knowing she would win and eventually drop the belts. Tony Khan has also previously defended her booking, making it clear that building her as a dominant champion was a deliberate creative choice.
