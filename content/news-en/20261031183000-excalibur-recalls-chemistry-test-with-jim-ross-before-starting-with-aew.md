Before starting with AEW, Excalibur faced a chemistry test with Jim Ross. Excalibur revealed they were tested together before the promotion's first event, Double or Nothing, and that Ross was initially unsure about him wearing a mask.

"Before we even called a show together, they brought us in for a chemistry test," Excalibur said. "We all sit down behind the microphones, but I see there's cameras there, so I put on the mask. And JR looks at me and says 'You know, this isn't about you, right?' And I'm like 'Sir, please, I know. Just give me a chance.'"

Despite the initial hesitation, the two have worked together successfully for years, with Ross praising Excalibur as a great friend and asset. Excalibur explained that his main goal is always to keep the focus on the wrestlers and the action in the ring.
