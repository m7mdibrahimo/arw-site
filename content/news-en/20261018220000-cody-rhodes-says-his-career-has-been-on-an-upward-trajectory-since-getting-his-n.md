Cody Rhodes has addressed his neck tattoo, which depicts his "American Nightmare" logo, during a media tour for his new movie, "Street Fighter." Rhodes spoke about the ink's origins on Mythical Kitchen's "Last Meals," noting that he got it after his time in NJPW with Bullet Club. He explained that he wanted to move away from the faction's gun-themed imagery and create a unique logo.

> "I wanted to get away from that, no disrespect, I just wanted my own logo," he said. "So, when doing the American Nightmare logo, it's a bit derivative of Shadaloo, which is one of the main villains of the 'Street Fighter' cannon and M Bison's logo... When you're looking up in the Burlesque Hall, in one of the scenes in the movie because M Bison welcomes you to his World Warrior tournament, it's actually one of the examples I gave the artist who made this logo in the first place."

Since debuting the tattoo at AEW Revolution 2020, Rhodes has seen significant success in both Hollywood and professional wrestling. After returning to WWE in 2022, he has held the Undisputed WWE Championship three times. Despite some reservations about the tattoo's size and placement, Rhodes remains satisfied with the outcome of his career.

> "I'll say it's a regret because of maybe the logo itself, because of its size and its placement, but it's been nothing but a career on an upwards trajectory ever since," Rhodes said.
