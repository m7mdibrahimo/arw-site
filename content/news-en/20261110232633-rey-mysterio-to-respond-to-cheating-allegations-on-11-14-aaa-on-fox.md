Rey Mysterio will share his side of the story regarding recent accusations. Lucha Libre AAA announced that Mysterio is set to respond to the cheating allegations made by Dorian Roldan this past Saturday. Roldan accused Mysterio of having an affair with Marisela Peña. Following these accusations, Mysterio was suspended from his role as general manager of the promotion for allegedly creating an unsafe work environment. Additionally, Omos is scheduled to face Pimpinela Escarlata. The lineup for the November 14 episode of AAA on FOX includes Mysterio's response and the Omos vs. Pimpinela Escarlata match.
