Cody Rhodes discussed securing the role of Guile in the film "Street Fighter" during an appearance on The Nikki & Brie Show. He explained that producer Mary Parent left a strong impression during the meeting and informed him of their desire to award him the part.

Rhodes revealed that he received a text message immediately after leaving the meeting from Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson asking how things went. He noted that he was anxious about telling Triple H about the matter, but the conversation went much better than expected, confirming that his role in the film later expanded and included additional scenes alongside Roman Reigns, who portrays Akuma.
