WWE has announced a major showdown for next week, as CM Punk and Kevin Owens are set to collide in a No Holds Barred Match. The announcement was made during the October 30, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown, confirming the two stars will face each other on November 6.

A promotional graphic aired during the broadcast confirmed the match will take place on the November 6 edition of WWE SmackDown, which airs at 8 PM ET on the USA Network. The No Holds Barred stipulation removes the standard restrictions of a wrestling match, allowing both competitors to use weapons and fight outside the ring without the threat of disqualification.

This match provides Punk and Owens an opportunity to settle their personal differences without any limitations. With both wrestlers known for their intensity, the November 6 episode of WWE SmackDown is expected to feature a volatile encounter.
