Paul Walter Hauser, an Emmy award winning actor who also competes in the wrestling world for promotions like ROH, MLW, PROGRESS, and various independent companies, recently spoke about his early days in the industry. During an appearance with Alistair McGeorge of Metro, Hauser reflected on how people reacted when he first stepped into the ring. He stated, "I think that when I first started, everyone was just watching me to see if I get injured, if I could survive the matches, and I think I surprised some people." Hauser added that he later heard a certain high level wrestling star had been talking behind his back, noting, "They were basically alluding to the fact that I didn’t really belong in wrestling. They took a few shots at me in different ways. It was somebody I actually am a fan of and admire their work, so I was kind of bummed. But, it’s good to find out about that stuff because it puts a healthy chip on your shoulder, and it also lets you know who to help and who not to help when they call." In recent action, Hauser lost the PROGRESS Proteus Championship to Donovan Dijak at PROGRESS 199.
