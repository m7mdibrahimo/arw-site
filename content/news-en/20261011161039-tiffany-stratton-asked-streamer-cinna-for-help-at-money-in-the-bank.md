Tiffany Stratton sought assistance from online streamer Cinna during the women's ladder match at WWE Money in the Bank on Saturday. Six rising stars competed in the second match of the premium live event to retrieve the suspended briefcase.

Stratton successfully captured the victory to secure the briefcase while her friend Cinna watched from the front row. During the bout, Stratton briefly appeared on Cinna's live stream to ask for help before climbing a ladder to execute a dive, which the broadcast captured.

Later in the broadcast, Cinna headed backstage to celebrate with Stratton after the win. Cinna remained ringside for much of the event to react to the action, while Logan Paul shared social media reactions regarding the women's performances.
