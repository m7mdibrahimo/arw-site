Drew McIntyre spoke about the leadership differences between Vince McMahon and Triple H during a recent interview on WWE RAW Talk. McIntyre explained that McMahon viewed things in black and white, focusing on clear babyfaces and heels, while Triple H prefers to operate in shades of gray to reflect the real world. McIntyre also discussed how Triple H's creative approach inspired his own character's evolution into a complex persona over the past four years, contrasting it with McMahon's strict focus on the present moment.
