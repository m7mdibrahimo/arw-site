Beyond Wrestling hosted the latest episode of Wrestling Open on October 15 at White Eagle in Worcester, Massachusetts, with the event airing live on IWTV. In the opening matches, Armani Kayos defeated Will Kiedis, Alec Price defeated Don Freeze, and TJ Crawford defeated Jake Gray, while Rickey Shane Page got the better of Rain Conway. The Discovery Gauntlet saw Tiny Giant defeat Sal Mistretta, and Waves And Curls (Jaylen Brandyn and Traevon Jordan) picked up a win against Miracle Generation (Dustin Waller and Kylon King). In the battle for the WO Women's Championship, Kylie Alexa and Allie Katch fought to a double countout. Meanwhile, The Church Of Greatness (Brother Greatness, Ichiban, Sammy Diaz and Tyree Taylor) secured a victory over 23 Hazard, Peter Gianni and The Breadwinners (Cash McGuiness and Patrick Wheatman). In the hair versus hair main event, Big Business (Brad Hollister and Love Doug) defeated Brick City (Julio Cruz and Victor Chase).
