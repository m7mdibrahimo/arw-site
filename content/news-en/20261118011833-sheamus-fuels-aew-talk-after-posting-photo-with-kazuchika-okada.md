Sheamus continues to keep fans guessing regarding his future in professional wrestling, and a recent social media post has only intensified the speculation. The former WWE Champion, who has been a free agent since early October, shared a photo alongside AEW star Kazuchika Okada.

The two were dressed for the occasion, with Sheamus in a black suit and Okada wearing a patterned jacket. Sheamus captioned the image with a reference to the film franchise, writing:

> "The real life Hunger Games 👊👊 Sunrise on the Reaping #TheHungerGames50th"

The picture was taken while Sheamus was in New York City for the November 16 debut of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping. While the meeting appeared casual, the timing has revived discussions about a potential move to AEW. Sheamus recently stated he is enjoying his time away from the ring, joking that his wife is the one who will decide his next career move. Although several AEW talents have expressed interest in his arrival, there is no confirmation of any formal agreement.
