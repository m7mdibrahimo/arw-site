Earlier this month, AEW hosted its annual Blood & Guts show. The Young Bucks have competed in three of the six men's Blood & Guts matches, featuring creative weapons like shoes glued with thumbtacks. Matt Young Buck noted that it has been a challenging year with many high-stakes matches. Matt explained, "Today's Blood and Guts match will be our third one because we're a couple of psychopaths that say, 'hey, throw us in these crazy matches, Tony. Why not?'"

Nick interjected, "We say at the end of them, never again." A clip aired showing the last time they made that claim, highlighting how they always get pulled back in. Matt described the matches as scary and gory, while Nick asked Brandon Cutler for prayers before the bout.
