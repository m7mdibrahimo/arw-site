According to Wrestling Inc, Xelina recently discussed original plans for her WWE Women's United States Championship reign during an appearance on "Ring the Belle". She held the title for 63 days after defeating Chelsea Green in April 2025 before dropping it to Giulia.

Xelina explained that she and Green devised a storyline to turn fans against Green while drawing sympathy for herself. They planned for Xelina to win the title at a pay-per-view event and immediately grant a rematch on the following show.

> "I was like, 'What if we did something where it was like whatever pay-per-view or whatever it is, I win the title, but then, the very next show, we have a rematch.'"

Zoey Stark was slated to join Green's group and brutally attack Xelina during the rematch to generate severe fan backlash. Green loved the pitched concept, but Paul "Triple H" Levesque ultimately chose a different creative direction for the championship.

Xelina also expressed a desire to defend the belt across other promotions like AAA and Stardom to establish it as a premier workhorse title. Despite creative differences with management, she remains grateful for the overall championship experience.
