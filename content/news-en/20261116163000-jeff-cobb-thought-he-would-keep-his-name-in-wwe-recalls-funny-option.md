Jeff Cobb revealed that he originally hoped to retain his real name throughout his run in WWE after making his debut at Backlash in 2025. Hearing Michael Cole say his name on commentary gave him optimism, and he kept using it for nearly two weeks before management asked him to choose a new moniker.

Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Cobb explained that he tried to put off changing his name before settling on JC Mateo so he could keep using his gear with the initials "JC." When narrowing down the pitch list from the creative team, one name stood out enough to make both him and Drew McIntyre laugh:

> "So, I brought the list back to the locker room. And Drew was like, 'Oh, what's this?' I was [like], 'Oh, this is my list of names...' And he looked at them, and he's on the second page. He's like, 'I like this one.' I was like, 'Well, we're not using that...' But it was Jafar Smash. I said, 'What?' So, every time I saw Drew without hesitation, I was like, 'What's up, Drew?' He's like, 'What's up, Jafar?'"

Cobb also noted that the list included the name "JONAH." He pointed out that WWE's creative team did not realize the name had already been used by "Big" Bronson Reed during his previous tenure in NJPW.
