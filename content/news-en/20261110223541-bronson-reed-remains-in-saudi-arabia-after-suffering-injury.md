According to Fightful, Bronson Reed is still in Saudi Arabia following an injury. WWE has sent a senior international executive and a medical team member to assist him. 

> "Bronson Reed remains in Saudi Arabia after sustaining an injury. WWE has top international executive and a member of their medical team on-site with him."

Reed did not compete at WWE Crown Jewel, but he appeared during the WWE World Tag Team Title match. Oba Femi threw him off the stage and through tables.
