AEW World Champion Will Ospreay has opened up about his decision to perform the Black Arrow in memory of fellow wrestler Pac. The news of Pac's passing deeply affected the wrestling community and those who regularly shared the ring with him in AEW. Ospreay decided to honor his friend during his match against Ricochet on AEW Dynamite by executing Pac's signature move.

Speaking in a recent interview with Brandon Kravitz, Ospreay explained that he had to relearn the move after abandoning it years ago because of the risks involved. He revealed that a past near-miss had rattled him, making him hesitant to attempt it again.

> "After we lost him, we had the tribute match with myself and Ricochet. The amount of people that are hoping that one of us could pull off the Black Arrow, and I used to do it like years and years ago, but I haven't done it for years because it was just — I nearly didn't make the move, it had spooked me, and I never wanted to go near it again."

Ospreay noted that performing the move provided an emotional release for himself, his colleagues, and the fans. He admitted that he was unable to complete the move off the top rope during practice, making his execution during the match his first successful attempt in eight years. Ospreay added that learning of Pac's passing forced him to cancel his plans to attend a show in Las Vegas so he could grieve in private.
