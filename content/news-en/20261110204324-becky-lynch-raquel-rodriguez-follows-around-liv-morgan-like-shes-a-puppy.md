Becky Lynch recently addressed Raquel Rodriguez, criticizing her unwavering loyalty to Liv Morgan. Lynch compared Rodriguez's behavior to that of a puppy seeking an owner, questioning why someone of her stature would act in such a submissive manner. During an interview on the RAW Recap, Lynch discussed her recent partnership with IYO SKY, noting that she felt IYO needed a mentor figure similar to how Shawn Michaels influenced others. Lynch expressed her disdain for The Judgment Day, specifically targeting Roxanne Perez and Raquel Rodriguez. She highlighted Rodriguez's height, suggesting that she is physically imposing enough to dominate, yet chooses to follow Morgan instead. Lynch also criticized Morgan's personality, describing her voice as shrill and irritating, and claimed that the group is constantly propping her up because she cannot succeed on her own.
