Raj Dhesi has expressed his interest in facing Twitch streamer Amine in the ring. Amine organized the Final Slam wrestling event in France this past November, which featured Dhesi in a match against content creator Djilsi. In a conversation with MuscleManMalcolm, Dhesi discussed the content creators he would like to face.

He initially mistook streamer Lonni for a local French wrestler due to his athleticism and ring awareness. When asked about a specific target, Dhesi named Amine, noting, "He's actually same height as me. He's six foot five." Dhesi revealed that he visited Amine's studio, which is equipped with a ring, weights, and cardio machines.

Dhesi praised the production value of Amine's events, which have included football matches and racing events, and noted that Amine was dedicated to respecting the wrestling business and highlighting French talent, such as Kuro, Mecca, and Amale. The battle royal at the event was won by Nate (Prince).
