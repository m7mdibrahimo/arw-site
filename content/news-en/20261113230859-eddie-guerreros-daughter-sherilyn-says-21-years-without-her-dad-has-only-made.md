Sherilyn Guerrero, the daughter of the late Eddie Guerrero, says that losing her father has not become easier over the past 21 years. In fact, she revealed that the pain of his absence has only grown more intense as time goes on, despite the many cherished memories she still holds. On November 13, 2026, marking 21 years since Eddie’s passing, Sherilyn shared her struggles on Instagram. She explained that while his memory remains a central part of her life, dealing with the reality of his absence becomes increasingly difficult with each passing year.

> "I love you. It doesn’t get easier, it always gets harder but your spirit being so alive makes it all okay."

Sherilyn reminisced about her childhood, recalling moments like falling asleep on Eddie’s lap while he played Madden, watching movies together, and being allowed to steer the car. She also remembered how he would return home exhausted from his grueling wrestling schedule but still make time to watch her and her sisters perform for him.

> "In a quiet world. It’s just you and I. It’s falling asleep on your lap while you played Madden. It’s you letting me steer the wheel when I was a kid to let me drive. It was the pallets we made as a family and the memories we made watching movie after movie. It was putting on concerts for you and mom when you came back from the road and you watched and clapped even though you were so tired."

She emphasized that the Eddie Guerrero she knew was far more than the WWE Superstar millions of fans saw on television. To her, he was simply the father who dedicated his time to his daughters whenever he could. Eddie Guerrero passed away on November 13, 2005, at the age of 38. Sherilyn, who was a child at the time of his death, continues to honor his legacy as she prepares to follow in his footsteps as a professional wrestler.
