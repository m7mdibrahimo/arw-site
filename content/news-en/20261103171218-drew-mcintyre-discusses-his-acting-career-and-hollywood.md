Drew McIntyre stated that he is not chasing more Hollywood roles, noting that he takes things as they come and that the entertainment industry came to him. During his time away from WWE television, he took part in several projects including Hex, Highlander, and Agent of Shadows.

While speaking in an interview with CNN News 18, he discussed his future in acting.

> "I mean, again, I'm not chasing it. I know a few of our guys are chasing it. I think Drew McIntyre would say, Drew McIntyre didn't come to Hollywood. Hollywood came to Drew, and we're taking it as it comes."

He added that he will return to WWE, calling it his home, and thanked Nick Khan and Triple H for letting him explore this world.

> "I will be back to WWE. You know, WWE's my home, and I appreciate Nick Khan, Triple H, you know, letting me go out and explore this world. Hopefully it's beneficial to both worlds. But when the time is right, Hollywood Drew will be back. But the time's not right quite yet."
