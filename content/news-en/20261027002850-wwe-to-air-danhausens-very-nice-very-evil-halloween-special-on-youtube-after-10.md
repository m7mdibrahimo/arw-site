WWE is set to air a special Halloween program featuring Danhausen on its official YouTube channel. The special, titled Very Nice Very Evil, will broadcast immediately following the conclusion of the upcoming episode of WWE NXT on the CW Network. According to reports, the program will include spooky matches, scary superstars, and surprise guests. Danhausen is also scheduled to host WWE SmackDown and Halloween Havoc later in the month.
