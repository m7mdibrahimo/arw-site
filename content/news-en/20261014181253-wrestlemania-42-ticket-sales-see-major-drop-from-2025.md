WrestleMania 42 generated $36,000,093 in ticket revenue across two nights at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas on April 18 and 19. Night One, headlined by Cody Rhodes against Randy Orton, sold 42,215 tickets for $17,441,679, while Night Two, headlined by CM Punk against Roman Reigns, sold 48,158 tickets for $18,558,414. These figures mark a significant decline from WrestleMania 41 at the same venue in 2025, which sold 113,412 tickets and brought in $66,074,558. This represents roughly a 20% drop in ticket sales and a 45.5% drop in gate revenue year over year. The drop followed WWE's unusual decision to return to Las Vegas for a second consecutive year, after initially planning the event for New Orleans. WWE received $6 million from the Las Vegas Convention and Visitors Authority alongside $4.3 million in tax credits. Nick Khan admitted during an internal meeting that running WrestleMania in Las Vegas twice in a row might not have been the right decision, adding that the back-to-back strategy likely will not be repeated. Despite the decline, both nights still rank among the highest-grossing gates in company history when adjusted for inflation, with Night Two ranking sixth and Night One ranking ninth. WrestleMania 43 is scheduled to take place in Saudi Arabia in April 2027.
