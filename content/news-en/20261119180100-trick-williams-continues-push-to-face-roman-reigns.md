Trick Williams is not letting up on his quest to step into the ring with Roman Reigns. The reigning WWE United States Champion has openly campaigned to share the ring with the "OTC" since 2023, and he remains determined to secure that marquee matchup.

During an appearance on Six Feet Under, Williams was asked about his dream foe and pointed straight to Reigns as the benchmark.

> "Man honestly, it's probably cliche, I'm sure everybody say it. But right now, I feel like the top of the business is Roman Reigns. If I want to go down as one of the greatest, that's who I want my match against, Roman Reigns. Go against the best."

After The Undertaker encouraged him to take Reigns down from his lofty spot, Williams made it clear he is ready for the challenge.

> "Somebody got to do it. Why not me? I ain't scared. Go to the top. Don't waste your time with everything in between, let's see where you stand."

Reigns is scheduled for action at Survivor Series, joining forces with The Usos, Solo Sikoa, and Jacob Fatu in a WarGames bout against The 946 lineup of Carmelo Hayes, Bronco Niema, Royce Keys, Lucien Price, and Cruz Montana.
