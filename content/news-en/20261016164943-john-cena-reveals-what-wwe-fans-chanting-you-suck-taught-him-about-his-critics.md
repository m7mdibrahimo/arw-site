John Cena reflected on his years as a polarizing WWE star and what he learned from hostile crowds. During an appearance on TODAY’s Making Space with Hoda Kotb, Cena explained that wrestling in front of tiny audiences before his WWE days prepared him for criticism better than thousands of fans booing him. He recalled facing direct negative reactions while performing as The Prototype in Ultimate Pro Wrestling and OVW, noting that looking someone in the eye in a small gym takes more courage than shouting anonymously in a large arena. Cena added that trying to please everyone is impossible, which is why he chose to stay authentic to his real-life values rather than changing his character to appease critics.
