AJ Styles has shared his frustration regarding modern wrestling kicks, specifically criticizing the common reliance on leg slaps to create the sound of impact. During the premiere of The AJ Styles Podcast, Styles and Bayley reviewed her 2024 WWE Women’s Championship match against Meiko Satomura in Tokyo. Styles paused the footage to highlight Satomura’s technique, noting that her kicks sound authentic because she is actually making contact.

> "Hey Bake, give me a pause on that. So here's what I loved about those kicks is because there was no slapping my leg. She's kicking you."

Styles clarified that he does not want wrestlers to hurt each other, but he admires how Satomura makes strikes sound and look brutal without causing genuine harm.

> "And I mean, they're real, but she's so good. I don't know how to explain it, but man, really freaking awesome kicks and I wish more people would do them like that and understand what it takes. And the practice that goes into, you know, throwing a kick like that, making the sound because it's real, but not murdering you so you can't continue the match."

Styles praised Bayley for matching Satomura’s intensity throughout the match. He noted that when both performers react believably to each other and maintain high energy, it enhances the quality of the wrestling.

> "It's awesome. That's wrestling to me. I love it."

Styles concluded by emphasizing that he values the effort required to master realistic strikes rather than using the shortcut of a leg slap.
