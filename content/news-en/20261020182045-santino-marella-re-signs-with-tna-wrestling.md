Santino Marella has officially signed a one-year contract extension with TNA Wrestling. Speaking on The Johnny I Pro Show, the Director of Authority revealed that TNA President Carlos Silva approached him about staying as the company's momentum continues to grow. Marella highlighted the promotion's success since moving to AMC and the thriving locker room as key reasons for his return. He also touched upon his ongoing on-screen rivalry with Daria Rae, confirming that their power struggle over roster decisions will continue.
