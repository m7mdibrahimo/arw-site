Seth Rollins recently shared that he maintained an unusual shared custody arrangement for a Yorkshire Terrier named Kevin with an ex-girlfriend after their breakup. Appearing on The Kittle Things podcast with George and Claire Kittle, Rollins and Becky Lynch discussed relationship advice, where Rollins recalled that whenever he returned home from the road with WWE, Kevin would spend a few days with him before returning to his ex. Rollins noted that Lynch was completely fine with the arrangement. The shared custody eventually ended after Rollins and Lynch had their daughter, as managing the back-and-forth trips became too difficult, especially given that Kevin was getting older and dealing with health issues. Kevin has since passed away, but Rollins looks back on the arrangement fondly as an example of successful communication when dealing with an ex.
