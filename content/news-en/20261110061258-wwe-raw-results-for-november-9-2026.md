WWE RAW took place on Monday, November 9, 2026, at the Bon Secours Wellness Arena in Greenville, South Carolina, following the WWE Crown Jewel event. 

Oba Femi def. Bron Breakker via disqualification in a singles match after Logan Paul interfered by passing brass knuckles to Breakker, leading to an attack from The Vision.

Rey Fenix def. Big Cass and Chad Gable in a triple threat match to become the number one contender for the WWE Intercontinental Championship after hitting a splash.

Bayley def. Michin in a singles match after sending her into the ring and executing a flying elbow drop for the pin.

IYO SKY and Becky Lynch def. The Judgment Day consisting of Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez in the main event tag team match after SKY reversed Rodriguez's attempt and hit an over the moonsault to secure the pin.
