Shane Haste has opened up about his time as the GHC Heavyweight Champion. When Haste made his return to Pro Wrestling NOAH earlier this year, few fans anticipated him quickly capturing the top singles title. However, he proved critics wrong on May 2 at the Spring Mayhem Ryogoku event by defeating Yoshiki Inamura to win the GHC Heavyweight Championship. Haste held the belt throughout the summer before losing it to Tetsuya Naito at the Legacy Rise show on August 9.

In a recent interview with HypeTalkTV, Haste admitted that his confidence grew significantly after his title defense against KENTA on May 13.

> "Yeah, it felt surreal. That was a surreal thing and that match went really well but also, I was in there with someone great and it was an established show, it was a big show. So, the real test for me was the next match, which I had Kenta two weeks later. Kenta is over as hell, so that also made it help, but it was a smaller show. It was definitely on me going into it as the champion that I had to back it up. That match went fantastic, it was awesome, it was sick and everyone loved it. That's where like after that one, I'm like, alright, I'm the fucking champ. I'm good."

Haste last competed for the promotion on October 25, where he teamed with Mikey Nicholls to take on the Good Brothers and the Passionate RATEL'S.
