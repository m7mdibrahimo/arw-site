WWE recently honored the 30th anniversary of Dwayne 'The Rock' Johnson's debut at Survivor Series during an episode of WWE Raw. Chief Content Officer Paul 'Triple H' Levesque shared a tribute on X to commemorate the milestone.

> "The most electrifying career in all sports and entertainment started 30 years ago… today. November 17, 1996. Small towns, stadium sell-outs, bumps, bruises, and one hell of a ride. An honor and a pleasure to create with you. @TheRock"

Triple H highlighted the legendary career that began on November 17, 1996, expressing his pride and joy in working alongside the iconic superstar throughout the years.
