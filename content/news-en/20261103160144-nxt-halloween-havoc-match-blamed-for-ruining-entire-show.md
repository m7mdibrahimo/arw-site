WWE NXT Halloween Havoc did not satisfy everyone, as some attendees reportedly felt that one match ruined the entire event. The show took place on October 31 at the WWE Performance Center and featured an extended storyline between NARAKU and Shiloh Hill.

Speaking on Wrestling Observer Radio, Dave Meltzer discussed CMLL's recent events and revealed that he heard similar criticism from people who attended the NXT show. According to Meltzer, those in attendance felt the rivalry negatively impacted the atmosphere and destroyed the experience.

> "It's a diff. It's different. I mean better. Because there's nothing as stupid as a Naraku Shiloh Hill thing, which ruined that entire show, by the way. I heard from some people there and it was interesting, their comments to me because it was like their basic comment was that, yeah, Narako and Shiloh Hill ruined the entire show. That's what happened."

Despite WWE making the NARAKU and Shiloh Hill feud a central focus of the event, the execution failed to win over everyone. It remains unconfirmed whether management will consider the criticism as NXT continues experimenting with new characters and stories.
