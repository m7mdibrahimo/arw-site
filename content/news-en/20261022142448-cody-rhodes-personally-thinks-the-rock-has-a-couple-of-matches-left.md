During a recent interview on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Cody Rhodes shared his personal thoughts on whether The Rock will wrestle again in WWE. Rhodes noted that he believes The Rock likely has a couple of matches left in him following their high-profile encounter at WrestleMania 40. However, Rhodes also acknowledged that given The Rock's extensive commitments as a board member and performer, it will ultimately be up to him to decide if he steps back into the ring.

The Rock has not appeared on WWE television since the Elimination Chamber event in 2024, where Cody turned down his demand for his soul before being attacked by John Cena. While discussing his future in the industry, Cody made it clear that any opponent would jump at the chance to share the ring with a superstar of The Rock's caliber.
