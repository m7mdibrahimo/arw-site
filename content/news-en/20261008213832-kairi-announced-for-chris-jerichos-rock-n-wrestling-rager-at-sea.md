KAIRI is the latest star confirmed for the upcoming Rock N Wrestling Rager At Sea: Seventh Cruise of a Seventh Cruise. Chris Jericho officially announced her participation in the event.

> "SHE’S BAAAACK! Stoked to announce the addition of the #SaneSailor @official_kairi to the @jericho_cruise! She will be there to meet & greet, take pix and be a part of the overall festivities on the #SeventhCruiseOfTheSeventhCruise!!!"

KAIRI is currently a free agent following her WWE departure this past April. Jericho previously confirmed he will team with Matt and Jeff Hardy to face Blake Christian, Jay Lethal, and Nick Wayne. Additionally, an episode of AEW Collision will be filmed on the ship.
