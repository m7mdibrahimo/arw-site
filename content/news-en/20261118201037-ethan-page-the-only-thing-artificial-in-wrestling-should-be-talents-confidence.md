Ethan Page has criticized the use of artificial intelligence in professional wrestling, asserting that it should not be utilized for promotional materials like posters or other industry artwork. The use of AI has become widespread in recent years, with independent promotions and talent in major organizations employing it for match cards and graphics. This trend has faced significant backlash from both fans and wrestlers, leading some promoters to ban AI-generated content in designs and entrance videos.

On Wednesday, November 18, Page shared a post from Alpha-1 Wrestling, the promotion he founded and manages, to emphasize his stance that artificiality should be limited to a performer's self-assurance rather than creative work.

> "The only thing artificial in wrestling, should be the talent’s confidence … not the art. Good job @A1Wrestling"
