Independent wrestler Vinny Pacifico has built an acting career over the past two years alongside his work in professional wrestling. In an interview with Fightful Overbooked's In The Weeds, Pacifico discussed how he manages both fields simultaneously.

Pacifico explained that most film and television shoots take place on weekdays, while wrestling events are usually held on weekends. He cited his experience filming the movie The Hours That Keep Us alongside Paige VanZant and Mickey Gall in Massachusetts, where he filmed from Tuesday to Friday before traveling to wrestle on Saturday.

> "I think it's all about making it work. It's way busier because I'm not home really at all anymore."

Pacifico noted that balancing both careers requires strategic scheduling, especially as he tries to spend time at home to support his family and care for his grandmother. He added that his current schedule is far busier than his early years in wrestling, but he enjoys having the opportunity to pursue both passions.
