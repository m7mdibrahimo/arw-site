Major League Wrestling aired the latest episode of MLW Fusion on November 5 on Tubi. The show featured a match between Killer Kross and Josh Bishop, along with an Opera Cup quarterfinal between Big Damo and Oddyssey. The Good Brothers also confronted the Glasgow Boys On Top. In the main event, MLW World Heavyweight Champion Killer Kross defeated Donovan Dijak to retain his title. After the match, Josh Bishop came out to confront Kross, and Dijak slapped him for not sticking to the plan. Bishop responded by hitting the Exit 187 on Dijak through a table.
