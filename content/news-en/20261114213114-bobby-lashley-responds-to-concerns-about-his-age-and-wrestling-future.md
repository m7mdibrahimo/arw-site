Bobby Lashley emphasized that winning championships does not mean he is in his final days inside the wrestling ring, stressing that he has much more to offer after a long career. In recent comments, Lashley explained that he does not understand the constant obsession with young stars and the marginalization of experienced wrestlers, noting his ongoing desire to compete in strong matches and seize new opportunities.

Lashley’s actions reflected this vision on November 12 at 4th Rope Wrestling. He defeated Joe Alonzo to win the Heavyweight Championship in just minutes with his signature Spear. MVP celebrated with him, personally placing the title around his waist.
