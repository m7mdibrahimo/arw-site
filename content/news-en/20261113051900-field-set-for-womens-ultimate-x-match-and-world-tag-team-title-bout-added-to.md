TNA Wrestling has officially finalized the lineup for the Knockouts Ultimate X match at its upcoming Destination X event, scheduled for November 15, 2026, at the Edmonton EXPO Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. The company is completing the match card as the event date approaches.

The field for the Knockouts Ultimate X match will feature Tasha Steelz, TNA Knockouts Television Champion M by Elegance, Xia Brookside, Shotzi Blackheart, Gabby Forza, and Jada Stone. The winner of the match will earn a future opportunity at the TNA Knockouts World Championship, which is currently held by Indi Hartwell.

In another addition to the card, Director of Authority Santino Marella booked a three-way tag team match involving TNA World Tag Team Champions Ryan Nemeth and Nic Nemeth. The Nemeth Brothers will defend their titles against Syx (Joseph Sawyer and Samuel Shaw) as well as the team of Matt Hardy and Moose.
