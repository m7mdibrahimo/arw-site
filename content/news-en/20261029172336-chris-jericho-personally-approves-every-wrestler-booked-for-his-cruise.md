Chris Jericho revealed that he personally vets every participant for his annual wrestling cruise, ensuring they match the event's atmosphere rather than just relying on their popularity.

During an interview with Skratch N’ Sniff, the AEW star explained that he handles the selection process for all wrestlers, musicians, and comedians himself.

> "So every, you know, and I book everybody, like everybody that's on the cruise, every band, every wrestler, every comedian, I vetted and know kind of what their vibe is."

Jericho stated that his goal is to maintain a continuous party environment across the ship, which has helped the event reach seven editions with plans for an eighth already underway.
