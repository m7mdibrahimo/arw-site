Sami Zayn is set to confront Roman Reigns on the October 19 episode of WWE Raw to discuss their upcoming championship match. During the October 12 episode of WWE Raw, the company officially announced that Roman Reigns will challenge Sami Zayn for the Men’s Crown Jewel Championship. WWE had previously confirmed Reigns' appearance for next week's show, and Zayn confirmed during his promo on SmackDown that he will face the challenger to address their upcoming title defense. The updated lineup for the October 19 episode of WWE Raw also includes a singles match between JD McDonagh and Joe Hendry. Crown Jewel is scheduled to take place on November 7 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.
