Trick Williams has a unique method for preparing for the upcoming Money in the Bank ladder match. While training with Big E, Williams shared his humorous approach to overcoming a fear of heights.

> "I don't do the heights too much. To overcome my fear, with Lash being in the match herself, I go to Home Depot every single day and I bring home a ladder. We practice climbing the ladder and coming down. We go to the attic and come back down. We got to the roof and come back down. Every single day. I got her changing stuff, chandeliers, everything. Just climbing and getting used to it, then come back down. 'Hey, that wasn't fast enough. We gotta do it fast and beat that time.' Been climbing a lot of ladders."

Although Williams was being sarcastic, he noted that there is a grain of truth in his joking. Both Trick Williams and Lash Legend are set to compete in the Money in the Bank ladder matches this Saturday.
