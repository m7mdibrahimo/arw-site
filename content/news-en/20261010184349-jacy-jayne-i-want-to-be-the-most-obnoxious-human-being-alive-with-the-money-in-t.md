Jacy Jayne is looking forward to her opportunity to win the WWE Women's Money In The Bank briefcase at the upcoming Premium Live Event. Jayne, who currently holds the WWE Women's United States Championship, stated in a recent interview with Outkick that she plans to use the briefcase to create chaos if she wins. "Everyone’s a target. I want to keep everyone’s head on a swivel. I want them to not know what way I’m coming, when I’m coming. I might pretend I’m going to cash in 52 times before I actually cash in," Jayne said. "I want people to be paranoid – terrified, paranoid, all of the above. I want to be the most obnoxious human being alive with this briefcase." Jayne also noted that her time in WWE NXT with Chase U was instrumental in her growth as a professional wrestler.
