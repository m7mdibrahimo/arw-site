NJPW has officially announced the cessation of operations for its American subsidiary, resulting in the permanent closure of its Los Angeles training facility, known as the NJPW Academy. This decision concludes an eight-year tenure that helped develop many young talents currently competing in NJPW and AEW.

According to reports, the news was met with widespread sadness among those involved with the academy. However, some individuals expressed surprise that the closure did not occur sooner. Bryan Alvarez of the Wrestling Observer noted that while everyone felt the weight of an era ending, there was a consensus that the facility’s closure was not entirely unexpected.

> "From talking to multiple people who have been involved over the years, the feeling is that the NJPW USA Dojo closing up was no real surprise, a few even said they were shocked it hadn't happened earlier, but universally everyone was sad not only about the end of an era, but also because so many good people had worked and were still working there," Alvarez reported.

Established in 2018, the LA Dojo was led by head coach Katsuyori Shibata and produced notable stars such as Gabe Kidd, Clark Connors, Alex Coughlin, Ren Narita, Kevin Knight, and Yuya Uemura. Veterans including Rocky Romero, Fred Rosser, and KUSHIDA also contributed to the training efforts. Despite the closure, NJPW will continue to hold live events in the United States.
