Mercedes Moné used the ring ropes to secure the win and retain the AEW Women's World Championship against Marina Shafir at the 2026 Full Gear pay-per-view. The Death Riders' Marina Shafir challenged Mercedes Moné for the belt in what marked Moné's third defense of her current reign, which began at All In. During the match, Moné executed a dive to the outside, but Shafir countered into the Mother's Milk submission, causing Moné to tap out outside the ring. Shafir paid tribute to the Death Riders by utilizing the late Benjamin Satterley, a.k.a. PAC's 'Brutalizer' submission and Claudio Castagnoli's signature swing. Moné later applied the Brutalizer on Shafir, but Shafir reversed it. Moné ultimately capitalized by pinning Shafir while using the ring ropes for leverage without referee Aubrey Edwards noticing.
