Erik Watts is struggling to accept the passing of his father, WWE Hall of Fame legend Cowboy Bill Watts. Only a day after the wrestling icon died at 87 years old, Erik shared an emotional statement explaining that he is still in shock and unable to comprehend the tragedy.

Erik posted on Facebook addressing the death, which was initially reported by journalist Bill Apter on November 17. He revealed that he and his partner had planned to visit his father and stepmother this weekend, adding to the heartbreak. He emphasized that while the industry lost an icon, his relatives lost a beloved father.

> "TODAY A LEGEND PASSED AWAY. To me and my family, we lost a father! I can’t process it yet or really get my head around it. I am numb. My girlfriend and I were going to visit him and his wife this weekend! I will post a tribute in the near future but for now, I am posting out of respect to all the fans and friends that are reaching out to asking if it is true that Cowboy Bill Watts has died. The answer is a heart-wrenching YES! I am broken. Fans and friends, please send any pictures you may have of my father for the tribute. I will not be able to use them all but my family and I will cherish them all.

Cowboy Bill Watts leaves behind a massive legacy after building Mid-South Wrestling, developing numerous stars, and working behind the scenes in WCW, leading to his 2009 WWE Hall of Fame induction. WWE released a statement honoring him, Triple H paid tribute, and WWE NXT featured a tribute on November 17.
