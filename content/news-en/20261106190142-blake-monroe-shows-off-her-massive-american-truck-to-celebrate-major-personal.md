Blake Monroe is celebrating three years of living in the United States by showing off a massive pickup truck in Orlando, Florida. The WWE NXT star marked the anniversary of her move from England by posing beside the large gray vehicle in a tight-fitting outfit, sunglasses, and heels. Monroe shared her gratitude for the journey on Instagram, writing, "happy 3 years of living in america to me 🤍 blessed beyond belief to live out my dreams in this amazing country. every day is an adventure, grateful for everything and can’t wait to see what’s next. here’s me with my fat a$$ american truck to celebrate ususususus" This personal milestone follows her transition to WWE after a run in AEW, and she continues to enjoy her life outside the ring.
