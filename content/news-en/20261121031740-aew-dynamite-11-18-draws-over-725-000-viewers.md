According to Dave Meltzer of the Wrestling Observer, the November 18 episode of AEW Dynamite drew 727,000 viewers on TBS. This figure is down from the 742,000 viewers that the Dynamite portion of the three-hour episode drew on November 11. The full three-hour episode averaged 621,000 viewers.

Wednesday's episode drew a 0.19 rating in the 18 to 49 demographic, which is up from the 0.16 rating that the Dynamite portion drew the previous Wednesday. AEW Dynamite was simulcast on HBO Max, but viewership data for that platform is not available.
