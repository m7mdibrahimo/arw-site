U.S. Senator Ruben Gallego has identified himself as an AEW fan. The discussion began on social media when a fan posted a GIF of Kane (Glenn Jacobs) being beaten by Rey Mysterio, accompanied by the caption, “ruben gallego when he sees glenn jacobs on the senate floor.” Gallego replied to the post, stating: "I am more of an @AEW man."

Jacobs, who served an eight-year term as Knox County Mayor, concluded his time in office early this September. Following his departure, reports emerged suggesting he was considered for a potential appointment to the U.S. Senate. Jacobs is a WWE Hall of Famer and a legendary figure in the industry, having last stepped into the ring for the 2021 Men’s Royal Rumble match.
