Game Changer Wrestling promoter Brett Lauderdale has revealed that former WWE Intercontinental Champion Chad Gable was originally planned to compete at Josh Barnett's Bloodsport X in 2024. Lauderdale shared the promotional graphic that had been prepared for the event, explaining that the match ultimately fell through after plans changed on WWE's side.

According to Lauderdale, the intention was to feature a dream match between Gable and Jonathan Gresham. However, Gable opted to shift his complete focus toward WrestleMania, leading WWE to alter its course shortly before the show.

> "It was supposed to be Chad Gable vs Jonathan Gresham and it would have been one of the greatest matches of all time lol But alas wwe changed course at the last minute because chad wanted to focus on wrestlemania (which i understand) It ended up being a landmark event regardless"

Gable recently lost his WWE Intercontinental Championship to Ethan Page on an episode of WWE RAW.
