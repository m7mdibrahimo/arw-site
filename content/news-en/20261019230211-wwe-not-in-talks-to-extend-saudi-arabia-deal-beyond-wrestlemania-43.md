WWE may be slowing down its long-term commitments in Saudi Arabia, as a new report claims there are no meaningful discussions about extending the Riyadh Season partnership beyond WrestleMania 43. According to Blake Avignon, TKO executives Ari Emanuel and Mark Shapiro are currently focused on fulfilling existing financial agreements, which include site fees and the deposit for WrestleMania 43. While Zuffa Boxing maintains its ties to the region, TKO appears hesitant to lock WWE into new deals amid escalating security and political tensions. Crown Jewel is still scheduled for November 7 in Riyadh, but future plans remain uncertain until current contractual obligations are met.
