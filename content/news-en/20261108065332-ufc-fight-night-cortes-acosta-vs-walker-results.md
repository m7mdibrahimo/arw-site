UFC Fight Night: Cortes-Acosta vs. Walker took place on November 7 at the Meta APEX in Las Vegas, Nevada, and streamed live on Paramount+. Below are the full results from the event.

In the main event, Tatiana Suarez defeated Virna Jandiroba by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 29-28). In other action, Elijah Smith knocked out Davey Grant at 1:00 of round 1, and Lucas Fernando defeated Billy Elekana by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28).

Nina Milosevic defeated Priscila Cachoeira by TKO at 3:40 of round 2, while Mitch Raposo picked up a unanimous decision win over Marcos Degli (29-28, 29-28, 29-28). Ollie Schmid defeated Keiichiro Nakamura by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28), and Thomas Petersen stopped Gabriel Lorenco by TKO at 4:47 of round 3.

Karine Silva defeated Gabriella Fernandes by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28). Murtazali Magomedov defeated Jose Delano by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28), and Austin Bashi submitted Carlos Calderon via rear-naked choke at 1:07 of round 1.

Andre Petroski defeated Mantas Kondratavicius by TKO at 4:39 of round 2, Seokhyeon Ko defeated Wellington Turman by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 30-27), and Jose Souza knocked out Jonny Parsons at 4:16 of round 1.
