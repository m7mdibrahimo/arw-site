Hip-hop legend Nas was in attendance at the 4th Rope wrestling event on November 12th. The Grammy- and BMI-Award winning artist was pictured alongside former AEW World Champion Swerve Strickland. Additionally, AEW talent Cpt. Shawn Dean shared footage of Nas watching the show. The promotion, which is run by Westside Gunn, concluded its event with Bobby Lashley winning the 4th Rope Heavyweight Championship.
