Johnny Gargano revealed that his favorite Marvel-inspired ring gear was based on Wolverine, adding that he has more ideas for the future according to Fightful. In August 2019, Gargano wore Wolverine-inspired gear at NXT Takeover: Toronto, continuing a trend of comic book-themed attire that has included Thor, Star-Lord, and Iron Man. During an interview with LaughingPlace.com, Gargano pointed to that specific outfit when asked about his best Marvel gear.

> "The Wolverine one, I think, is the coolest I've ever looked."

Gargano noted that he has additional comic-inspired concepts in mind, though he plans to see how things develop before debuting them.

> "There's obviously ideas in the back of my head that are floating around that I would love to make come true. So we'll see. I'm just going to put it out in the universe and see what happens."
