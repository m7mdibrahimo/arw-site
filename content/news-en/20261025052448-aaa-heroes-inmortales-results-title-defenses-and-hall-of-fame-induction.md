AAA held its Heroes Inmortales event live from Gimnasio Olímpico Juan de la Barrera in Mexico City. The event featured four championship matches, including a title defense for the AAA Mega Championship, and the induction of Marisela Peñan into the AAA Hall of Fame.

In the opening title match, Los Perros del Mal defeated La Parka, Mr. Iguana, and Niño Hamburguesa to retain the AAA World Trios Championship. Daga hit La Parka with a low blow to secure the pinfall victory. Following the match, Big Mami made the save after the champions continued their assault on the challengers.

La Catalina successfully defended the AAA Reina de Reinas Championship in a Triple Threat Match against Flammer and La Hiedra. Lady Maravilla attempted to interfere on behalf of La Hiedra, but La Catalina capitalized on the confusion, hitting a running knee on Flammer to retain her title.
