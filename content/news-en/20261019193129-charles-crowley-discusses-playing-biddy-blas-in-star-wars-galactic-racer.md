Charles Crowley recently spoke about voicing the fan-favorite character Biddy Blas in Star Wars: Galactic Racer, noting that the intense vocal performance took a toll on his vocal cords. Crowley, who balances an independent wrestling career with acting, admitted that he quickly realized the character was resonating with fans shortly after the video game's release. Describing the character's direction, Crowley explained that he was asked to portray a small, anxious man who overcompensates, which required a demanding vocal effort. Despite leaving the recording booth dripping in sweat after every session, he expressed complete satisfaction with the unique character they built together.
