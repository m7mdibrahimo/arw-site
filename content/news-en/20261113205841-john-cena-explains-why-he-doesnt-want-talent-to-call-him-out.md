John Cena is avoiding setting expectations he knows he cannot meet. Since retiring from in-ring competition in December 2025, Cena has not engaged in verbal sparring with active WWE performers. Instead, he has used his television time to praise stars like Cody Rhodes and Zilla Fatu. In a new interview with Sports Illustrated, Cena explained that it would be pointless for other wrestlers to attempt to trash-talk him.

> "The only thing that matters is I control what I do, and if it's one of those things where a talent is like, 'Hey, man, maybe I can come out and talk some trash about you.' Okay, where's the payoff? Where are we building? You're wasting your air, you're wasting space. If you call me out, that's great. You have a few good zingers about me. Good for you, but you're building something, and you're influencing the consumer to something that you'll never get. We're never gonna deliver on this. So, use me as a conduit to call somebody else out."

Cena also discussed his recent change in attire on WWE programming. He is scheduled to appear on the November 13 episode of WWE SmackDown.
