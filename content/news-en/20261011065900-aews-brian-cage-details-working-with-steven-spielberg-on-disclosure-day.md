AEW star Brian Cage recently discussed his experience working on Steven Spielberg's Disclosure Day, which was released earlier this year. Cage appeared in the film's opening sequence alongside fellow wrestlers Lance Archer and Chavo Guerrero. During an appearance on the No But Seriously Though podcast, Cage explained how Guerrero helped secure the opportunity for him after another project was postponed. According to Cage, Spielberg appreciated his look and charisma, and even allowed him to choose Archer as his on-screen opponent. Looking ahead, Cage also expressed interest in landing a role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe or working alongside action star Jet Li.
