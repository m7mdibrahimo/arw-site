AJ Francis revealed that he faced backstage backlash during his time with WWE because a celebrity guest specifically asked to meet him instead of major stars like Brock Lesnar or Randy Orton. The incident occurred during WWE's Money in the Bank event in London, where the social media personality Expressions attended as a celebrity fan. Francis explained that when WWE staff asked Expressions which superstar he wanted to meet, Expressions chose Top Dolla, which surprised and upset backstage personnel.

> "I remember one time I got a little bit of heat in WWE because when Expressions was backstage as like a celebrity fan before Money in the Bank in London, they asked him who did he want to see and he could have saw Brock Lesnar, he could have saw Randy Orton and he said Top Dolla and they're like why do you want to see Top Dolla?"

Francis noted that the situation felt ridiculous and showed how his popularity sometimes worked against him in WWE. He contrasted that experience with his current run in TNA, where the promotion values his creativity and gives him opportunities to perform. Francis and Expressions later developed a close friendship after meeting during Super Bowl week in Las Vegas, which eventually led to Expressions making an appearance during Francis's entrance at TNA Slammiversary.
