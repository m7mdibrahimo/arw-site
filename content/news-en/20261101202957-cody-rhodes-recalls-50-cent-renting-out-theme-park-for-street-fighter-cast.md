WWE Champion Cody Rhodes revealed a story from the set of the upcoming Street Fighter movie involving famous rapper 50 Cent. Speaking on the Double Coverage Podcast, Rhodes shared how the cast and crew built a strong bond on set through various social activities and dinners organized by cast members.

Rhodes explained that he wanted to do something big for the team, so he rented out a yacht and set up specialty cocktails for the cast and production crew. However, 50 Cent appeared to take that gesture as a friendly challenge.

> "I did that and 50 Cent came, and we all took this great picture and apparently he took it as a challenge. The following weekend, he put an end to it with renting out an entire theme park, which is Luna Park in Australia. An entire theme park for all of the production, all of the cast and their families. So there was no more gift-giving after he put his flag in the sand. 50 Cent’s gonna do what he wants to do but he he did that and it was marvelous. It was, again, nobody needed to do dinner or anything after that he made it very clear that 50 Cent is 50 Cent and he's awesome in the movie too."

Rhodes noted that after 50 Cent rented Luna Park in Australia for everyone and their families, no one attempted to organize any more gift-giving events on set.
