Darby Allin and Steven Borden recently discussed the possibility of Steven wearing face paint in the ring in the future. Allin and Borden have been teaming since Borden debuted on the AEW roster, following in the footsteps of his father, Sting, who previously teamed with Allin. During a recent interview with Trent Osborne on 1010XL, Steven was asked if he would ever paint his face like his legendary father. Darby Allin immediately chimed in, saying, "Look how sexy he is. He doesn't need it." Steven explained that wearing face paint from day one might have drawn negative reactions from the crowd. He stated, "Yeah. The last thing I want to do is be playing the greatest hits. I think you can do callbacks when it makes sense at the right time. But I think if I were to have come out with face paint and a trench coat and a bat, I probably could have been booed out of the building in the beginning, and I'm still in the beginning right now. So kind of taking it week by week."
