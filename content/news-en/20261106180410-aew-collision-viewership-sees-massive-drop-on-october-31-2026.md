Fresh viewing figures emerged for the October 31, 2026 broadcast of AEW Collision on TNT, revealing a sharp decline from the previous week.

According to SpoilerTV, the program attracted 302,000 audience members alongside a 0.06 score within the vital P18–49 demographic. By contrast, the October 24, 2026 installment pulled in 449,000 watchers and a 0.1 demo score, marking a drop of 147,000 viewers and a 0.04 reduction in the demo.

When measured against the comparable week in 2025, viewership improved by 32.5% while the demographic score rose by 50.0%. These metrics solely represent linear television figures on TNT and exclude digital or streaming numbers.
