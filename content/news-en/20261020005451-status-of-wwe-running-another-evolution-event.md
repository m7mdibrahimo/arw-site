WWE fans hoping for another all-women's Evolution event should temper their expectations. According to WrestleVotes, there are no current plans for the company to make Evolution an annual tradition, and the event is not expected to return anytime soon.

> I haven’t heard anything about Evolution. They didn’t do one this year, so it won’t become an annual thing that people thought coming off last year’s show.

Reports suggest that WWE’s hesitance stems from the viewership numbers of the 2018 original event, which reportedly performed lower than the NXT TakeOver shows of that era.

> The only thing I can say about Evolution, going back to the first Evolution in 2018 — the running tale within WWE is the show drew lesser numbers on the WWE Network viewership, both live and replay, than most of the TakeOvers around that time, which they were not happy about.

While WWE brought the event back last year, it has since disappeared from the calendar. WrestleVotes suggests that WWE may prefer to keep the concept as an occasional special rather than a yearly requirement, noting that it does not currently attract the same audience levels as standard weekly programming like WWE RAW or WWE SmackDown.
