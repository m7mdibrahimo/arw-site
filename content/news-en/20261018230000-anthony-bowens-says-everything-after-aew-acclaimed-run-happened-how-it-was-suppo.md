Anthony Bowens addressed the aftermath of The Acclaimed's breakup, pushing back against the idea that his subsequent singles run was an unintentional failure. Speaking on the Michael Fairman Channel, Bowens clarified that the trajectory of his storyline was carefully designed by AEW rather than going off track.

> "They think the return was a flop, which it wasn't. It was made to be that way so I could go off the deep end, blame Billy for my not being able to win the big matches, blame the fans for choosing Caster over me, adding to his delusion of being the best wrestler alive. And then I would go into what the Pride of Pro Wrestling was."

Bowens admitted that while things did not fully progress the way he envisioned, the creative direction was part of a structured narrative before he became a free agent.

> "It was a lot of, like, real emotions balled up. And I was trying to bring it to screen. But you know, what happened happened. And you know, I don't like to dwell on anything. I'm with The Opps now, and hopefully...well I was with The Opps. We'll see how things work."
