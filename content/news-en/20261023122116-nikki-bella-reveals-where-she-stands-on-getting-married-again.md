Nikki Bella is currently not interested in getting married again, although she is open to romance. During a recent episode of The Nikki & Brie Show, the WWE Hall of Famer discussed the vision boards she and Brie Bella create to represent their goals. While Nikki admitted she has many dreams she wants to achieve, finding a husband is not one of them right now. However, after Brie suggested she needs a man, Nikki acknowledged that she might consider adding a good-looking man to her vision board.
