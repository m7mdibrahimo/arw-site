Royce Keys recently discussed Carmelo Hayes joining 946 on WWE RAW, explaining why the former NXT Champion was a natural fit for the group. Speaking to The Takedown on SI, Keys noted that his bond with Hayes goes back long before their time in WWE. He compared Hayes to a pit bull with a hidden aggressive side. Keys also praised the trust WWE has placed in the faction, while hinting that more surprises could be on the way as potential WarGames matches loom against The Bloodline. According to Keys, fans are about to see a completely different version of Hayes and the rest of the group under the motto that nobody is safe.
