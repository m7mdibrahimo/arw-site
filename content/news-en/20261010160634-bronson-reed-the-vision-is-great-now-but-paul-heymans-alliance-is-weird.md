Bronson Reed discussed the current state of The Vision, sharing his love for the faction's current iteration while calling Paul Heyman's involvement unusual.

Following his return in September, Reed addressed Heyman's fluctuating loyalty to the group in a recent interview with Rock And Pop FM, pointing to their recent interaction on WWE RAW. Reed stated that while he appreciates his stablemates like Logan Paul, Austin Theory, Maxxine, and Bron Breakker, Heyman's presence remains strange.

> &#8220;It was interesting to watch, I was sitting at home again, watching RAW every week as I'm rehabbing, as I'm getting ready to come back, and The Vision that once was is different now. I love the version of The Vision we have now. You know, I'm a big fan of, of Logan Paul, Austin Theory, Maxxine, obviously Bron is forever. But the only weird thing has been Paul Heyman's alliance with us. He's been a little hot and cold with The Vision, and that's not what I'm used to. I think you saw that on Monday. I approached him. I let him know what I'm going to do, and he warned me about Oba. But the thing is, you don't need to warn me. I'm already completely prepared for what Oba is going to bring, and as you said, I've been working on myself to make myself the best version I can be, and you're going to see that on Saturday at Money in the Bank.&#8221;

Reed also previewed his upcoming match against Oba, expressing full readiness for the challenge ahead.
