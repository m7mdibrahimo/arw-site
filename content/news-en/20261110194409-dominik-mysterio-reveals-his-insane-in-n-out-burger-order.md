Dominik Mysterio revealed his massive In-N-Out Burger order during a recent appearance. Speaking with Doug Does Fights, Dominik discussed his favorite food options and admitted to having a serious weakness for the fast-food chain. When asked about his order, he distinguished between behaving and misbehaving. For his misbehaving order, Dominik unloaded a massive list. "Misbehaving is a 4x4, spread only, mustard-fried, light grilled onions, light chopped chilies, with Animal Style fries. Hold the grill. And then we're also doing a Flying Dutchman, 3x3, with a vanilla shake and maybe a water cup on the side."
