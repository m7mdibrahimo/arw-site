WWE Raw is scheduled to air two hours early on November 2 as the go-home show for Crown Jewel. According to WrestleVotes Radio on Fightful Select, the episode will stream live on Netflix at 6 PM Eastern from the Barclays Center in Brooklyn. WWE will host a double show that night, with a SmackDown taping following Raw.
