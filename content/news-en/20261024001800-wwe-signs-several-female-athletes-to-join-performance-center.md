WWE has added four new female recruits to its roster who have already relocated to Orlando, Florida. According to Bryan Alvarez of the Wrestling Observer, the company signed Gabriela Martinez Asensi, Silvia Likki, Elly Finch, and Tayleigh Robertson after they participated in WWE tryouts. Gabriela Martinez Asensi is a former collegiate tennis player from the University of Missouri, while Silvia Likki brings a strong CrossFit and strength training background from Italy. Elly Finch competed in track and field at Michigan State University, and Tayleigh Robertson has experience in amateur wrestling and bodybuilding after attending Snow College and Utah Tech. All four athletes will now begin developing their professional wrestling skills at the Performance Center.
