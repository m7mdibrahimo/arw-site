Deonna Purrazzo recently discussed her historical reign as the inaugural ROH Women's Pure Champion, highlighting the deep connection she shares with Ring of Honor. Reflecting on her journey, Purrazzo noted how the promotion provided her with a platform to grow over the past eleven years, leading to major milestones like winning the Women's World Championship in 2022 and now holding the pure title. She expressed immense pride in representing the division alongside what she considers one of the strongest women's rosters in the world. Additionally, Purrazzo praised Lee Moriarty, calling him the greatest technical wrestler active today.
