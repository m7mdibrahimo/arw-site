Carmelo Hayes officially joined the 946 during the main event of WWE Raw, helping the faction take down the Usos and Solo Sikoa in a chaotic brawl. The match featured intense action both inside and outside the ring, with Jaida Parker interfering by taking out the referee and executing high-flying moves into the crowd. Sikoa dominated parts of the bout and attempted to use his signature spike, but a masked man wearing a bandana intercepted him. The mystery attacker was revealed as Hayes, who used a crowbar alongside the 946 to lay out the Usos and Sikoa and stand tall as the show went off the air.
