According to Ringside News, Dawn Marie recalled that she was still working as a stripper when she received a sudden phone call telling her to travel to Philadelphia within three hours for her ECW debut. Speaking on Velvet Ropes, she explained that the promotion urgently needed someone to face Tammy Sytch after plans changed for Terri Runnels, with Bubba Ray Dudley recommending her after remembering her from Jersey All Pro Wrestling.

> "So they called me. Well, he called me and he’s like, ‘You know, can you — are you interested in coming down?’ Blah blah blah blah. And I’m like, ‘Yeah, sure. When?’ He’s like, ‘You need to be here in three hours.’"

Because she had no custom wrestling gear ready, she had to use an outfit from her work bag. She noted that stripping carried a major social stigma back then. That quick phone call launched a career that later included a prominent run in WWE.
