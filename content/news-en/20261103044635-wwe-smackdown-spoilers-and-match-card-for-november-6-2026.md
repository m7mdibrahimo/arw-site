WWE SmackDown will air on November 6, 2026 with a lineup featuring Shinsuke Nakamura vs. Tama Tonga, Damian Priest vs. Nathan Frazer, and a WWE Women's Tag Team Championship match between Fatal Influence and B-Fab & Michin, according to Ringside News citing Fightful Select.

Backstage notes indicate that Jimmy Uso is slated to interrupt Sami Zayn's segment before the 946 attacks Jimmy, with Sami leaving him behind. Charlotte Flair, Alexa Bliss, Tatum Paxley, and Bayley are expected to get involved during the women's tag team match and later issue a WarGames challenge.

The main event features Kevin Owens vs. CM Punk, though no finish is listed in advance.
