Grayson Waller retained his NXT Championship against CM Punk in the main event of WWE NXT on The CW. The match was set up after Punk challenged Waller following the champion's victory over Tony D'Angelo at the Halloween Havoc event. During the closing moments of the match, Kevin Owens ran down to the ring and distracted Punk. Owens had been removed from the WWE Performance Center earlier in the day after attempting to fight Punk before the show. The distraction allowed Waller to roll Punk up for the pinfall victory to hold onto his title. Punk and Owens will face each other in a taped singles match scheduled to air on WWE SmackDown, while Punk is also scheduled for WWE NXT live events in Detroit and Chicago later this month.
