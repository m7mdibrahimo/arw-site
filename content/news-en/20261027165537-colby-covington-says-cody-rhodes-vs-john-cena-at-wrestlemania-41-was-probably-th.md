UFC star Colby Covington recently reflected on attending WrestleMania 41, sharing high praise for the main event match between John Cena and Cody Rhodes. Speaking in an interview, Covington described their WWE Championship bout as likely the greatest match he had ever witnessed, highlighting the incredible energy in the sold-out stadium. He also expressed a desire to face John Cena or Cody Rhodes in the future if given the opportunity.
