Chad Gable has maintained a positive relationship with Triple H since returning to WWE. Since re-signing with the company in June 2024, Gable has experienced a significant career boost, culminating in his victory over Penta at SummerSlam in August to win the Intercontinental Championship. This success is a major shift from his previous run as Shorty G in 2019 and 2020.

In a recent interview with Justin LaBar of TribLive, Gable revealed that Triple H discussed that gimmick during their contract negotiations in 2024. Gable stated, "He's delivered on everything that he's ever said to me. When I was in the process of negotiations and entertaining the idea of re-signing, he addressed the Shorty G thing with me upfront. He didn't say he was a fan of it, he made it very clear that it was not the best direction for me and made it clear that going forward, things would be much different than that and they have been."

Gable expressed deep respect for Triple H, noting his appreciation for the platform he has been given since Triple H took over creative control. He added, "I have nothing but the highest of regard for him and the most respect for him. There's something to me about a quality of somebody that shoots you straight and everything that they say, you can believe."
