Chelsea Green recently opened up about a uniquely awkward encounter she experienced during heart surgery, where two medical professionals recognized her as a WWE Superstar while she was exposed on the operating table. Speaking on What Do You Wanna Talk About? with Cody Rhodes, Green discussed her procedure for supraventricular tachycardia, which took place in May 2026 after her heart rate dangerously spiked to 228 beats per minute. While lying flat with tubes running through her arteries and unable to move, two guys in the operating room casually announced they were fans of hers. Green joked about whether the moment constituted a HIPAA violation, admitting that realizing staff could see her while recognizing her from WWE was the absolute last thing she needed in that vulnerable moment. Fortunately, the procedure was a success, and she returned to work just ten days later, though she acknowledged the entire ordeal was far more frightening than people realize.
