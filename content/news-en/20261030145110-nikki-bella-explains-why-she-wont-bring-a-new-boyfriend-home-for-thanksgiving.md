Nikki Bella might be single right now, but she has a strict rule about when a man gets an invitation to her family's Thanksgiving table. Speaking on an episode of The Nikki & Brie Show, Nikki explained that she prefers to wait at least six months before introducing a new partner to her family for the holidays. She noted that showing up together for Thanksgiving makes a relationship official and serious, so she would rather spend the holiday solo than rush into things with someone new. Brie joked about the situation and tried to play matchmaker later in the episode, but Nikki immediately shut down the idea and confirmed she is enjoying the holidays by herself.
