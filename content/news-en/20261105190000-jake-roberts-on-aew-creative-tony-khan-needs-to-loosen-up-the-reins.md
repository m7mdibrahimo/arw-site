WWE Hall of Famer Jake Roberts believes that AEW CEO Tony Khan should be more open to advice regarding the company's creative process. During an interview with Nick Teodoro, Roberts, who worked in AEW from 2019 until recently, praised Khan as a person but suggested that he needs to delegate more. "Tony Khan needs to loosen up the reins a little bit, take some advice," Roberts stated. "He's got a good product. He's got a lot of talent there. I think he's got to be able to be used better."

Roberts also discussed his time managing Lance Archer, describing their pairing as a "perfect" fit that never received the opportunities it deserved. He encouraged fans to petition for Archer to receive a championship run. Roberts, who previously managed La Faccion Ingobernable, confirmed he is no longer with AEW.
