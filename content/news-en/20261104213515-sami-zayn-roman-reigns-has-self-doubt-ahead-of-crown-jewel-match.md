WWE Champion Sami Zayn believes Roman Reigns is carrying internal self-doubt heading into their highly anticipated matchup at WWE Crown Jewel. The two stars are set to square off for the men's Crown Jewel Championship following weeks of tense verbal exchanges on WWE programming.

Speaking with Cathy Kelley on WWE's Crown Jewel Countdown show, Zayn explained that despite the bravado displayed by Reigns, human nature makes doubt inevitable for anyone entering the ring against a champion.

> "Roman can say he's 100% sure. I know there's that self-doubt because he's human. He's human. I don't care if you're The Tribal Chief. I don't care how much success you've had. You're still a human being. You cannot tell me that you're going into the ring with a WWE champion and there's zero self-doubt. I know there's self-doubt there. He can play it however he wants. I don't care. I know there's self-doubt. I'll tell you- I am telling you there's self-doubt. But there's also a crazy amount of confidence, and I'm operating at a higher level now than I ever have been. So yes, absolutely, I think I can beat him."

Zayn enters the contest performing at the highest level of his career, confident that his momentum will be enough to secure a decisive victory over Reigns in their upcoming showdown.
