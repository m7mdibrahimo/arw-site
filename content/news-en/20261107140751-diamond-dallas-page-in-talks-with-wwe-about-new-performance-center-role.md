Diamond Dallas Page has discussed a potential role at the WWE Performance Center aimed at helping younger wrestlers prevent injuries. Speaking on TMZ's Inside the Ring, DDP revealed that he has already talked with Triple H, WWE President Nick Khan, and Chief Communications Officer Chris Legentil about bringing his methods to the facility.

DDP wants to visit the Performance Center a few times a year to teach younger talent how to protect their bodies. He noted that injury prevention is one of the primary benefits of DDP Yoga, pointing to wrestlers like Chris Jericho and Shawn Michaels as early adopters of his methods.
