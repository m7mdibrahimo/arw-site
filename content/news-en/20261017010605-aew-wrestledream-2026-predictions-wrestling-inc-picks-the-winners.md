AEW WrestleDream 2026 takes place this Saturday in Orlando, Florida, featuring an eight-match main card with seven title bouts. Wrestling Inc. staff provided their predictions for the main card, looking to improve their record following a difficult night at WWE Money in the Bank. Key matches include Chris Jericho vs. Nick Wayne, with 75% of staff backing Wayne to win and end the feud. Andrade El Idolo is heavily favored at 92% to retain the AEW National Championship against Tommaso Ciampa. In a four-way match for the AEW TBS Championship, 75% of the staff expect Persephone to retain her title. Jon Moxley is predicted by 83% of the staff to successfully defend his AEW Continental Championship against Mark Davis. Finally, Swerve Strickland and The New Level are favored by 92% to win their Two Out of Three Falls match against Hangman Adam Page and Brodido for the AEW World Trios Championship.
