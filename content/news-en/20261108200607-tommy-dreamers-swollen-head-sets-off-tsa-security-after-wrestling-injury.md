Tommy Dreamer revealed that his recent head injury caused an unexpected airport security issue when the swelling triggered TSA scanners. Taking to Instagram, Dreamer shared a photo from an airport lounge displaying a visible bruise and cut on his head while having a drink. According to Dreamer, the swelling was severe enough to set off the screening equipment, prompting a TSA agent to request a pat-down. When the agent asked if there were any sensitive areas, Dreamer replied, "The guy said I have to pat your head down I said wear gloves He said any sensitive areas I said yes my head." Dreamer humorously blamed the Storm Wrestling Academy for what he called a traveling rib at Calgary Airport, joking, "If I can be serious for a moment I feel @stormwrestlingacademy is behind this traveling rib in Calgary Airport." Despite decades of taking hard bumps, having a swollen head trigger airport security is a first for the veteran.
