Rob Van Dam remains firm regarding his controversial remarks about the creative freedom within AEW, maintaining that wrestlers exert significant influence over television content. During a recent episode of his 1 Of A Kind podcast, the WWE Hall of Famer reiterated his view that the promotion functions as if the inmates are running the asylum.

> "yeah, I said it's it gives the impression that it's more like the inmates are running the asylum, which I yeah, I think they are. And so that's their That's what you get for their show."

Despite the backlash, Van Dam clarified that he does not necessarily view this creative freedom as making AEW better or worse, but rather that the input from the performers is evident in the product. He also addressed accusations that his stance shifted following his WWE Legends contract. Van Dam revealed that his agreement with WWE was already in place before Tony Khan contacted him about joining AEW.

He noted that Jack Perry initially reached out to him, which led to discussions with Khan about a larger role. Ultimately, Van Dam chose to protect his existing WWE Legends deal rather than pursue a more substantial commitment with AEW.
