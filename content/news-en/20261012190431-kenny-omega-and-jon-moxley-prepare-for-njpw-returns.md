New Japan Pro Wrestling held its annual King of Pro Wrestling event at Ryogoku Sumo Hall in Tokyo, featuring major announcements regarding the in-ring returns of Kenny Omega and Jon Moxley.

Shingo Takagi and Daiki Nagai def. Hirooki Goto and Tatsuya Matsumoto in the opening match. Following the bout, a video message from Jon Moxley aired to announce his upcoming return to NJPW in response to Shingo Takagi's open challenge.

https://x.com/njpwworld/status/2109544601224720406

Later, IWGP Heavyweight Champion Yota Tsuji successfully defended his title against G1 Climax winner Ryohei Oiwa in the main event. Kenny Omega appeared via video to challenge Tsuji before rushing the ring to attack both Tsuji and Boltin Oleg, proposing a three-way match before the end of the year to determine the number one contender for NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 21.
