The Miz and Maryse recently embraced 1990s comedy by recreating a famous scene from the 1994 film Dumb and Dumber. The couple donned matching suits, with The Miz wearing the bright orange outfit of Lloyd Christmas and Maryse sporting the powder-blue tuxedo of Harry Dunne.

They reenacted several comedic moments, including a mock cane fight and the film's famous spray gag, which caused Maryse to cough immediately. The Miz shared the video on Instagram with a brief caption, stating:

> "Dumb and Dumber but make it Miz & Mrs."
