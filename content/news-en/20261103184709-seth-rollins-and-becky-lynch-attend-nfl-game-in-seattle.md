Seth Rollins and Becky Lynch were spotted at Lumen Field in Seattle, Washington, on Monday night to attend the Chicago Bears' 34-17 victory over the Seattle Seahawks. The Bears' social media team shared a video of the WWE couple showing their support for the team. Rollins, usually known for his extravagant outfits on WWE RAW, opted for a more casual look with a Bears jacket and hat, while Lynch wore a black shirt and coat. In the video, Rollins declared, "We are here in Seattle, but we are Bears fans, Seth Rollins and Becky Lynch. We're gonna smash them Seahawks on Monday Night Football. You ready for it?" Lynch joined in with a spirited "Go Bears!" while Rollins cheered along. Lynch last competed on WWE RAW on October 28 in a Women's World Championship match against Liv Morgan, which ended in a disqualification due to interference from The Judgment Day. Stephanie Vaquer was originally scheduled for the match but was sidelined due to an illness. Rollins has been absent from WWE television since his loss to Roman Reigns at SummerSlam, though he has remained active with appearances on ESPN as part of his contract with the network.
