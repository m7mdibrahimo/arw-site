Anthony Bowens recently discussed his current status and future plans during a new interview with Michael Fairman. Earlier in October 2026, reports indicated that former AEW World Tag Team Champions Anthony Bowens and Max Caster became free agents. When asked about what lies ahead, Bowens avoided going into details to prevent immediate speculation.

> "Well, unfortunately I don’t think I can speak on any of that because as soon as I open my mouth, I know there will be 27 different dirt sheets about what I say," Bowens laughed. "I just don’t know where things are gonna go. I don’t wanna speculate. I’m taking everything one day at a time. I appreciate everybody’s support throughout all of this and I got a lot of 'stay,' I got a lot of different things, and I appreciate people’s feedback but I know deep down what I want to do and we’ll see if it works out." Bowens last competed for AEW during the Buy In pre-show at AEW All In 2026.
