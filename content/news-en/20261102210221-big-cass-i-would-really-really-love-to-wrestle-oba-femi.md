Big Cass discussed potential dream matches during a new interview, naming Roman Reigns and Oba Femi among the opponents he would love to face. Since returning to WWE earlier this year, Cass has mostly competed as a singles competitor while recently aligning with Ethan Page on WWE RAW. Speaking on the Sal Licata Show, Cass ruled out legends like The Undertaker, Stone Cold Steve Austin, and Kevin Nash while focusing on current top stars. "For what could possibly happen, obviously you want to, you want to wrestle Roman top dog. He's top of the food chain. You want to wrestle him one-on-one. I've wrestled him in other kinds of matches, but one-on-one would love to wrestle Roman and Ob. Me and Oba. I would really, really love to work that, to wrestle Oba. I think that would be very good," Cass said.
