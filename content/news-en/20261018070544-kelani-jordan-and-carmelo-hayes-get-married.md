Kelani Jordan and Carmelo Hayes are officially married. The couple tied the knot on October 17 in Miami, with photos captured by Photos By Reem. Hayes previously proposed to Jordan in July 2025 in Hawaii. Speaking about the wedding preparations in April, Hayes mentioned that Jordan handled most of the planning while he offered his input when needed, noting that many fans assumed they were already married. Kelani Jordan currently holds the NXT Women's Championship, while Carmelo Hayes appeared on WWE Raw this past Monday.
