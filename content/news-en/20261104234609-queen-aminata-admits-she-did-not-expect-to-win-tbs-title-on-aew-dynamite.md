Queen Aminata is still processing her recent victory as the new AEW TBS Champion. Days after winning the title in the main event of AEW Dynamite, she revealed that she entered the match without expecting to leave with the championship belt.

Speaking on her YouTube channel on Wednesday, November 4, Aminata reflected on her win over Persephone during the October 28 episode of AEW Dynamite. She noted that she was under significant stress leading up to the event and did not fully grasp the reality of her win until afterward.

> "I literally just got out of my room, and I feel like everything is sinking in now because throughout the day, like yesterday to today, I was under a lot of stress. Like, I was dealing with so much stuff."

Aminata admitted she initially thought the match was a precursor to a future opportunity. Despite interference from Britt Baker, she secured the win with a running knee.

> "And to be honest, not expecting to win today. I was expecting to have this match and maybe have another belt for the pay-per-view."

She expressed that winning in the main event made the moment feel perfect. 

> "But I feel like everything happens for a reason, and I couldn't have imagined a better way to win this belt than main eventing Dynamite and against Persephone. Like, this is perfect. This is amazing."

> "This is pretty. This is juicy. This is mine. I am your new TBS Champion."
