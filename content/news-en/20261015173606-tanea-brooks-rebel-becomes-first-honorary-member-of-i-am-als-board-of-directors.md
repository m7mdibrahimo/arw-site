Tanea Brooks, known to wrestling fans as Rebel, has been named the first honorary member of the I Am ALS Board of Directors. Brooks, a former AEW star who is living with ALS, joined the organization as an ambassador in June after sharing her diagnosis publicly. Her appointment follows the final congressional passage of the ACT for ALS reauthorization act. Brooks has used her platform and personal story to raise awareness and encourage fans to support the cause.
