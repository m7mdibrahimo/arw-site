Paul Heyman and Vince McMahon have a long history together, but Heyman says he has no intention of reaching out to his former boss. During a recent interview with Graham Bensinger, Heyman noted that they are currently on different life paths and he is not thinking about a reunion.

Heyman reflected on their complex working relationship in WWE, acknowledging that McMahon thrived on confrontation. "It's a lot of It's a lot of yous, yous. And he liked confrontation. So, if I said, you know, hey, you on this, and he he he he wouldn't he wouldn't go, 'Oh, how can you talk to me that way? I'm the chairman of the board. I'm going to fire you.' Oh, really? ME? YOU. YOU KNOW, it's like, 'Okay, here we go.' I mean, it it was just it was these childish battles."

He cited the 2006 ECW revival as their most difficult period, recalling how McMahon promised him control before stripping him of his authority, leading to six months of frustration. While the two eventually reconciled and worked together again in 2012, Heyman is uncertain about the future. "Well, I don't know. I mean, it it it's it's You know, it Our business is very, very funny. So, I Right now, we're on different paths in life. So, do I think we reconnect? I don't know."

Ultimately, Heyman emphasized that he is not focused on the possibility of reconnecting, and he doubts McMahon is either. "I don't I don't think about it and I can very much guarantee you neither does he."
