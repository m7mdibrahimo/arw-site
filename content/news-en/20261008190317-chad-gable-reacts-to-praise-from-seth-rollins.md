WWE Intercontinental Champion Chad Gable recently shared his emotional reaction to high praise from Seth Rollins, who called watching Gable grow into one of the best wrestlers on the planet an absolute treat. Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Gable revealed that hearing such kind words from a peer he deeply respects made him nearly tear up, especially knowing that Rollins does not simply hand out empty compliments. Gable reflected on his career journey, admitting there were moments during the Shorty G era and beyond when he wanted to walk away, but ultimately, his perseverance paid off.
