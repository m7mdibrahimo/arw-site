Big Cass has addressed recent reports regarding how WWE views him internally, stating that he is ready to do whatever the promotion asks of him during his current run. Cass made his return to WWE earlier this year and has become a regular presence on WWE RAW, regularly working opposite popular stars like Dragon Lee and Rey Mysterio. However, his win-loss record has led to online speculation about his long-term position in the company.

Speaking on The Sal Licata Show, Cass addressed the rumors and revealed that he recently discussed the matter with Paul Levesque, known as Triple H. He explained that he returned to WWE with a business-first mindset after falling short of his own personal expectations during his first stint.

> "Whatever the company needs is what they're going to get out of me. Whether the company needs me to come out first every night and open the show, that's what they're going to get 100% of. If they need me to close the show, they're going to get 100%. If they need me to do the popcorn match, they're going to get 100%."

Cass added that negative reports are designed to generate clicks and that he remains focused on doing the best job possible with the opportunities he receives from Triple H.
