Solo Sikoa delivered a promo during the October 26 episode of WWE RAW to send a warning to Royce Keys and mention The Bloodline, but fans quickly grew distracted by the background. Viewers noticed that Roman Reigns and The Usos standing behind Sikoa looked unusually fake, prompting immediate speculation on social media. Many fans took to X to suggest that WWE had used a green screen, artificial intelligence, or other production tricks to insert Sikoa into the parking lot setting. While no official confirmation has been made regarding the use of a green screen, the strange visual quality sparked widespread debate among the audience as to whether Roman Reigns and The Usos were actually present during the segment.
