Pro Wrestling EVE hosted EVE 156: eVe For Vendetta on November 6 at the Big Penny Socials in London, England. The event was broadcast on MyAEW.

During the show, Cory Zero announced that she was not medically cleared to compete. As a result, French Art, consisting of Zero and JGU, was forced to relinquish the EVE Tag Team Titles.

Kaiju Queens (Nyla Rose & Nightshade) def. Chantal Jordan & Stephanie Maze in an AAAW Tag Team Championships eliminator match.

Alexxis Falcon & Rhio def. Session Moth Martina & Lana Austin.

Skye Blue def. Kay Lee Ray.

Miyu Yamashita def. Molly Spartan in an EVE International Championship eliminator match.

HyperActive (Safire Reed & Anita Vaughan) def. NyteSkye (Violet Nyte & Skye Smitson) and Hai High Mate (Maika & HANAKO) to win the vacant EVE Tag Team Titles.
