AEW National Champion Andrade is looking to add the promotion's top prize to his collection and become a dual champion. During a recent appearance on Close Up with Renee Paquette, Andrade discussed his title defense against Tommaso Ciampa and made his future intentions clear. Although he already holds gold, he wants the AEW World Championship next.

> "But this is my title. This is my title. I want this title. I want the world title. So I want Andrade two belts."

Andrade has a future World Championship opportunity waiting, but he is in no rush to cash it in and wants to wait for the right moment. He stated that his return to AEW was motivated by a desire to prove he can represent the company at the highest level. While Will Ospreay's name was mentioned as a potential opponent, Andrade is willing to wait and see who holds the title when he decides to make his move.
