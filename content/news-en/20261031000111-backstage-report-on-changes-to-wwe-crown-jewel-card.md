WWE Crown Jewel 2026 was recently moved to The Venue in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, but that is not the only update made to the event. According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, several changes were made to the match lineup.

Posters around the original venue showed Finn Balor alongside GUNTHER. Meltzer noted this could indicate a singles match between the two, as Balor vs. GUNTHER was part of the original Crown Jewel plans. Additionally, the World Tag Team Championship match featuring Bron Breakker and Austin Theory defending against Je'Von Evans and Penta was originally planned as a six-man tag team match including Bronson Reed and Oba Femi. The decision was made to adjust the bout, as management felt it made little sense to run the six-man tag match before the title defense.

The rest of the event features World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns facing Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn for the men's Crown Jewel Championship, while WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green takes on Women's World Champion Liv Morgan. Cody Rhodes and Randy Orton are also scheduled to compete in a Last Man Standing match.
