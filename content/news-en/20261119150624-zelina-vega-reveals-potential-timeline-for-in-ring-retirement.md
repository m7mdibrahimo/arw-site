Zelina Vega shared her thoughts on her wrestling career and the potential timeframe she has considered for eventually stepping away from the ring. Speaking on D-Von Dudley's YouTube channel alongside Aleister Black, she noted that she is not planning to leave the ring anytime soon, pointing to Asuka as a major inspiration to keep going regardless of age.

Vega, who is 35, stated that she projects she could wrestle for roughly another seven years. Discussing what might come next after her in-ring days, she expressed interest in returning to managing roles like she previously did alongside Andrade, taking inspiration from Vickie Guerrero's career in the industry.
