Jordynne Grace has been absent from WWE television for months, but reports suggest this is not a sign of her departure. According to the latest backstage information, WWE simply lacks current creative plans for her. During a recent WrestleVotes Q&A, JoeyVotes and TC addressed her status, noting that SmackDown's women's division is currently crowded with ongoing storylines involving groups like Fatal Influence, Charlotte Flair’s faction, Jade Cargill’s team, and the pairing of Nikki, Paige, and Chelsea Green.

> "The thing that I’ve heard recently is she’s not in the creative plans. That doesn’t mean they don’t want plans for her."

TC emphasized that Grace is not on her way out of the company, attributing her absence to a simple numbers issue regarding limited television time. Grace has also been occupied with a bodybuilding competition, which WWE may be allowing her to complete before finalizing her return. TC compared her situation to Johnny Gargano, who was inactive for months before finding a spot in the main event scene. Previous reports had suggested a potential tag team with Ivy Nile, though the current status of those plans remains unclear. WWE reportedly still values Grace, and her absence is not an indication of her leaving.
