WWE star Chelsea Green recently reflected on her wrestling journey, identifying her WWE Women's Championship victory at SummerSlam as the most significant moment of her career. Green, who competed in a four-way match at the original All In 2018, feels that her recent title win holds the top spot, with her appearance at the 2018 event coming in as a very close second.

Green discussed these milestones during an appearance on Cody Rhodes' "What Do You Wanna Talk About?" podcast. Reflecting on All In, she noted, "I just feel like that was such a unique moment in not only my career, in the 13 years that I've done so many really cool things, but in wrestling history. You can't take that away from us. We filled that arena and we put on one of the best shows, I think, ever."

Regarding her SummerSlam win, Green admitted she felt like an underdog heading into the ladder match against stars like Charlotte Flair, Jade Cargill, and then-champion Tiffany Stratton. She credited the fans for helping her secure the victory, stating, "I do feel like because it was a culmination of my career and this underdog narrative and everything, the fans really helped me get there. And so it felt very similar to that show."
