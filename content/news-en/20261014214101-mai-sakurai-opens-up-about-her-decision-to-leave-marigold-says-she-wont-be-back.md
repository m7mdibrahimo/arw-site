Mai Sakurai has opened up about her upcoming departure from Dream Star Fighting Marigold, revealing that she plans to take a break from pro wrestling. Her final match with the promotion is scheduled for October 24th at the Marigold Grand Destiny show at Ryōgoku Sumo Hall in Tokyo.

Speaking to reporters via Tokyo Sports, Sakurai explained that her decision stems from health issues concerning her knees and neck, following a cervical sprain, alongside stress-related health struggles since early 2026. “As other wrestlers left the promotion and my own position changed, there were times when I was pushing myself too hard and trying to be something I wasn’t. I’ve been muddling through until now, but I’ve reached a point where I can no longer balance what’s expected of me with what I’m capable of,” she stated.

Sakurai added that she feels she has achieved everything possible in Marigold and confirmed she has no plans to return in the near future. “I feel I’ve done everything I could. I don’t know what the future holds, but since I’ve made the decision to leave, I won’t be returning to Marigold anytime soon,” she concluded.
