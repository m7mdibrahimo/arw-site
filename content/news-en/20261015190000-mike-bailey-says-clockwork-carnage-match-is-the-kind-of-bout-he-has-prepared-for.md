Mike Bailey is excited for his upcoming match at AEW WrestleDream, which will feature the company's first Clockwork Carnage match. Speaking in an interview, Bailey discussed his excitement for the bout, noting that while he would love to be defending the AEW World Championship in the main event, this is a great first step. Bailey also recalled hearing about the concept from Will Ospreay before it was officially announced on television, admitting that he loves complex and unusual match ideas. Having competed in numerous extreme and long-form matches throughout his independent career and time in DDT, Bailey feels that this unique stipulation is the exact kind of contest he has spent his career preparing for.
