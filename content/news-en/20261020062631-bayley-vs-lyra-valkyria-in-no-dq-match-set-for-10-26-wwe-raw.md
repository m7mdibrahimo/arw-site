The rivalry between Bayley and Lyra Valkyria is set to escalate. During the latest episode of WWE Monday Night Raw, a singles match took place between Lyra Valkyria and Lola Vice. Valkyria had been feeling paranoid throughout the night, believing that Bayley was following her backstage. While searching for Bayley, Valkyria heard a noise, turned around, and accidentally struck Lola Vice. Bayley later appeared during the match, distracting Valkyria and ultimately causing her to lose to Vice. Following the match, the two fought, and Bayley challenged Valkyria to a No Disqualification match for the October 26th episode of WWE Raw.
