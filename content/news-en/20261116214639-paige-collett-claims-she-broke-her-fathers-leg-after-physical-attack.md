Independent wrestler Paige Collett opened up about a personal ordeal during an appearance on Headlocks, Heels, & Havoc: Wrestling Unscripted, claiming she broke her father's leg and busted his lip when he physically attacked her on her birthday last year. She stated that she suffered abuse from him since childhood, noting that her family opposed her starting a wrestling career at age 18, which led to her being kicked out and becoming homeless.

Collett explained that the harsh treatment continued even after she developed complex regional pain syndrome (CRPS), a condition that kept her in a wheelchair for years. During the attack last year, she warned her father not to touch her due to her medical state, but he ignored her warning, prompting her to use her wrestling background to defend herself.

> "I literally armed the [ __ ] and he didn’t know how to take it obviously and broke his leg. And then I pulled him up and I was like, ‘You’re never allowed to hit me ever again.’ And I gave him one in the lip and bloody he busted."

Collett noted that her wrestling instincts took over despite the severe pain caused by her condition. She stated that this was the first time she publicly shared the details, revealing that the story will be included in an upcoming book detailing her challenges and her journey to return to the ring.
