Major League Wrestling aired the latest episode of MLW Fusion on October 22 via Tubi. In the opening match, Joe Coffey defeated Andrew Everett. Following the match, Coffey dedicated his victory to CJ Gallows and stated he is coming after Trevor Lee and the Southern Crown Championship. It was noted that CJ Gallows is currently out of action with a fractured jaw, concussion, and bruising in his lungs. In other action, Josh Bishop defeated Rich Royal, while Saya Kamitani picked up a victory over Lady Frost. In the Opera Cup quarterfinal match, Austin Aries secured a win against Trevor Lee. Meanwhile, Joseph Samael of CONTRA issued a threat to the entire roster of MLW, claiming that Masha Slamovich will conquer. Additionally, Glasgow Boys on Top were fined, and footage was shown of Austin Aries blowing off Matt Riddle. Josh Bishop targeted Killer Kross, set up a street fight between The Good Brothers and Glasgow Boys for Saturday Night SuperFight, and brawled with Kross into the streets. Shotzi Blackheart expressed that Kamitani is a dream match for her, Scarlett and Zamaya engaged in a brawl in the ring, Yota Tsuji issued an open challenge for Saturday Night SuperFight, and Tessa Blanchard announced her arrival to MLW.
