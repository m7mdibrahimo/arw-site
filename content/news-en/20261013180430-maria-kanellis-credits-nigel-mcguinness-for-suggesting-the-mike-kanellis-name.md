Maria Kanellis recently discussed her time working with her husband, Mike Bennett, on an episode of Insight with Chris Van Vliet. She revealed that Nigel McGuinness was the first person to suggest Bennett change his name to 'Mike Kanellis' while they were traveling back from Canada during their time in ROH.

> "Nigel McGuinness actually had come up with this idea of calling him Mike Kanellis when we were in Ring of Honor and we were on a car ride back from Canada, and he had brought up this idea he had, and lo and behold, WWE is the one that did it. He was fine with it because he thought there was going to be a payoff at the end. He thought he was going to be able to turn on me, and I always wanted to do the fight with him. I always thought that would be such a cool (idea). I really wanted it to be the three of us, where Matt Taven, Mike, and myself were kind of in this little power struggle. But yeah, Mike was fine with it because he is the consummate professional. He is a fantastic worker, and he was like, alright, that’s what I’m doing, and so he thought he was going to get a really good payoff for it, and unfortunately, it never happened."

Bennett initially agreed to the change because he expected a meaningful storyline payoff, including a potential conflict between them. While Bennett remains with AEW/ROH, Maria is no longer with the promotion.
