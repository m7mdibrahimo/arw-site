Brie Bella has shared an update on her recovery from a broken scapula, confirming that her latest CT scan showed her bones have not yet fused. Speaking on the latest episode of The Nikki & Brie Show, she explained that doctors view the three-month mark as the critical period for bone fusion, meaning she still has time to heal. Brie suffered the injury during a match at WWE SummerSlam on August 1 after crashing near the steel steps, though doctors determined she would not need surgery. Despite her ongoing physical therapy and rehabilitation, she clarified that fans should not expect an imminent in-ring return or any twin magic when she attends upcoming events. According to Ringside News, a potential return around the 2027 Royal Rumble remains a hopeful target depending on her continued healing progress.

> "So, on the solo episode, I told you guys all that I just got back from my scans. Well, the results came in and unfortunately my bones have not fused. So, that's okay. You know, it's like two months in though."

> "Yeah, they really do. It's really three months where they feel like the bones would be fused."

> "Yeah, I'm still on the right path of healing and still all the things, but no. So, if you see me pull into Sacramento with my mom, please don't assume I'm wrestling. There's no going to be no twin magic. Trust me, I wish."
