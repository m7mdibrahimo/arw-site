Lady Leigh has opened up about her departure from WWE one year after being released from the company. On October 10, 2025, while competing for the Evolve brand under the name Jin Tala, she was let go by the promotion. Reflecting on the anniversary of her release, she shared her experiences on social media.

> This day last year, I was released from WWE. The call taken at my boyfriend's birthday dinner … no reasons were given. It left me thinking I didn't have what it takes. But Im a stubborn mf, & chased a gut feeling that my potential hadn't reached a limit yet & that I would decide when I'm done. Now im in Japan, creating unforgettable memories & getting closer to the type of wrestling I love & to be loved by wrestling. I'm evolving, adapting, ever growing. Set your and watch it burn brightly & beautifully

Since her departure, Lady Leigh has competed for various independent promotions and recently made her debut for ROH.
