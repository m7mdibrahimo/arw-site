WWE is reportedly exploring ideas to bring back the Sin Cara character with a completely new wrestler under the mask. According to PWInsider, early internal discussions have taken place, though nothing has been finalized yet. The company is not planning a return for the original performer who previously portrayed the character. This development comes shortly after the former Sin Cara expressed interest in returning to WWE during an interview last month. While WWE has not yet committed to bringing the character back to television, the potential return could introduce a fresh face to the role.
