Maya Jama is stepping down as the host of Love Island UK after leading the show through nine series since 2023. With Jama leaving the role, WWE interviewer and personality Cathy Kelley has expressed interest in taking over the position. Kelley has been a part of WWE since 2016, with a brief absence from 2020 to 2022.
