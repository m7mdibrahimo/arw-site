Bron Breakker recently discussed the most impactful hits he has experienced in both his football and professional wrestling careers. Before his transition to WWE, Breakker played football at Kennesaw State University and had a brief stint with the Baltimore Ravens in 2020. During a recent interview with The Kickoff, he recalled the toughest hit he took on the gridiron.

> "Hardest hit I've taken in football was at preseason practice going into my senior year. Linebacker that transferred in was a great player, All-American, his name was Charlie Patrick. Really, really good player. That's the hardest I've ever been hit on the football field."

Breakker then shifted his focus to his time in the ring, identifying the hardest hit he has delivered to an opponent in WWE.

> "In WWE, the hardest I've ever hit somebody, we've gotta go with IShowSpeed. He hasn't been back since."
