WWE stopped at the Canada Life Centre in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada, on October 15 for a Road to Survivor Series live event.

The show featured several championship bouts alongside a tag team pairing of CM Punk and Danhausen. In the main event, Sami Zayn put his Undisputed WWE Championship on the line against longtime rival Kevin Owens.

Full results from the event are below.

WWE United States Championship: Trick Williams (c) def. Damian Priest

WWE Tag Team Championships: MFTs (Tama Tonga and Talla Tonga) (c) def. Bakusai (Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki)

Chelsea Green, Tatum Paxley, and Giulia def. Fatal Influence (Jacy Jayne, Fallon Henley, and Lainey Reid)

Street Fight: Cody Rhodes def. Gunther

AAA World Cruiserweight Championship: Rey Fenix (c) def. Ricky Saints

CM Punk and Danhausen def. The Miz and Kit Wilson

Undisputed WWE Championship: Sami Zayn (c) def. Kevin Owens
