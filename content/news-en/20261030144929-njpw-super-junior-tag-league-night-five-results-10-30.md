New Japan Pro-Wrestling held night five of its NJPW Super Junior Tag League tournament on October 30 at Gifu Memorial Center Fureai Dome in Gifu, Japan. The event was broadcast live on NJPW World.

Mistico, Masatora Yasuda & Shota Umino def. Ryusuke Taguchi, Kazuma Sumi & Tatsuya Matsumoto

HOUSE OF TORTURE (DOUKI, Yoshinobu Kanemaru & Yujiro Takahashi) def. Master Wato, KUUKAI & Yushin Hatazawa

Unbound Co. (Taiji Ishimori, Robbie X & Gedo) def. Nick Wayne, KUSHIDA & Taisei Nakahara

Aaron Wolf, Boltin Oleg & Toru Yano def. Satoshi Kojima, Yuya Uemura & Taichi

B Block: The Swirl (Lee Johnson & Blake Christian) def. HOUSE OF TORTURE (Dick Togo & SHO)

B Block: Hyo & YOH def. Daiki Nagai & Titán

B Block: Ichiban Sweet Boys (Kosei Fujita & Robbie Eagles) def. United Empire (Jakob Austin Young & Francesco Akira)
