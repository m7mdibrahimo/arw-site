WWE Women's United States Champion Jacy Jayne recently discussed her upcoming appearance in her first Women's Money in the Bank match at Saturday's Money in the Bank event. Speaking with Denise Salcedo, the Fatal Influence leader admitted she is feeling some nerves and anxiety about the bout, primarily because she is not a fan of heights.

> "I'm excited, I'm anxious," Jayne said. "I'm a little nervous, because [as] I told you before I am not a heights girly. I don't really like heights. So I'm just going to woosaw, my adrenaline's going to kick in, it's going to be good, but I am a little nervous."

According to Wrestling Inc., Jayne explained that the downtime before the weekend leads to overthinking, though she believes adrenaline will take over once the bell rings.

> "I think it's more so you're thinking about it and you're like 'Oh god, this could go wrong or that could go wrong, or what if this happens,'" Jayne said. "It's more of just, like, the waiting and the anxiety that comes with that, that's what makes me nervous. But once I'm out there, once I'm in the ring, I'm good. I'm indestructible once I'm out there."

Jayne joins Lash Legend, Sol Ruca, and Lola Vice as match rookies. Roxanne Perez and Tiffany Stratton are the only veterans, with Stratton having won in 2024 and cashed in for the WWE Women's Championship in early 2025.
