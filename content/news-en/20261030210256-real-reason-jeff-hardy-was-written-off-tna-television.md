Jeff Hardy is stepping away from TNA television once again following an angle on the October 29 episode of TNA iMPACT, where Joseph Sawyer and Samuel Shaw took him out. According to reports, TNA planned the angle knowing Hardy needed to miss upcoming shows in Canada due to his past legal issues. Additionally, Matt Hardy recently revealed that his brother has been dealing with bulging discs in his neck, which contributed to the decision to give him time to recover. While surgery is not currently required, the Canadian travel restrictions made this the ideal time to write him off. Sources indicate that Hardy is not expected to be gone long and should return for the December television tapings in Texas, while he remains scheduled for outside appearances such as the Jericho Cruise and WrestleCade.
