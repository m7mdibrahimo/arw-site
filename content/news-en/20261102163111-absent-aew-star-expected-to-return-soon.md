Nick Wayne could return to AEW television as soon as Wednesday, November 4. According to Dave Meltzer on Wrestling Observer Radio, Wayne is expected back in the United States in time for the AEW Blood & Guts special in Jacksonville, Florida. Wayne has been competing in Japan for New Japan Pro-Wrestling, but with the Super Juniors tournament now finished, his schedule has cleared for a comeback. Meltzer noted that Wayne is not on the card for NJPW's Tuesday show in Hiroshima, allowing him to fly back early. While AEW has not officially announced his appearance, his return is approaching and his exact role remains to be seen.
