Samoa Joe recently shared his thoughts on wrestlers slapping their thighs to enhance the sound of strikes, explaining that he has no issue with the technique itself as long as it remains hidden. Speaking in an interview, Joe stated that once fans can clearly see the slap happening, the illusion of the match is ruined. He admitted that it frustrates him to even have discussions about visible thigh slaps, noting that it means the trick has failed. For Joe, the line is simple: do it without letting the audience notice, because once the secret is out, part of the wrestling magic is lost.
