LJ Cleary is set to be out of action for the next 9 to 12 months after sustaining an injury that requires surgery. The 12-year veteran announced the news on social media, expressing his disappointment as the setback comes during a career-best year.

> "Absolutely breaks my heart to have to post this. As of this morning I've been diagnosed with an injury that requires surgery and will have me out for 9-12 months. There's never a good time to get hurt, but it particularly stings right now as I was on the roll of my career, having the best year of my career as a whole with some big things lined up. But this is the nature of the game, thank you for everyone that's ever supported me up till now, this is a minor speed bump in the grand scheme of this beautiful life it See you all soon x"

Cleary noted that while the timing is difficult, he views the injury as a minor obstacle in his professional journey.
