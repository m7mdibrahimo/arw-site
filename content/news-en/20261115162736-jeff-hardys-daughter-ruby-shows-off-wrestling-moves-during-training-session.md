Jeff Hardy’s daughter Ruby recently stepped inside the ring with her father for her first official training session. Ruby shared a video on Instagram showing herself working through wrestling basics and moves with Jeff, including running the ropes, collar-and-elbow tie-ups, and headlocks. She then moved on to more advanced moves, executing dropkicks, an enzuigiri, and shoulder tackles with her father's guidance. Ruby also paid homage to her dad by breaking out his signature dance right in front of him during the session.

She kept the caption simple, noting that it was her first time inside the ring with her father. While there is no indication that Ruby is beginning professional training to follow Jeff into the business, the moment offered a unique look at a daughter learning the ropes from a veteran who has spent decades in the industry.
