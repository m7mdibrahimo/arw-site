Zack Sabre Jr. discussed his special exhibition match against Katsuyori Shibata during the 2021 G1 Climax tournament in NJPW while appearing on CMLL's Esto es Lucha Podcast, according to Fightful.

Sabre Jr. looked back on the unexpected return of Shibata for his first NJPW match since 2017, emphasizing that he never encountered a reaction quite like the crowd's silent gasp under COVID-19 restrictions.

> "It was an exhibition, but, his return… it was obviously during the COVID restrictions where the Japanese fans were restricted from being able to vocally cheer or make any noise and I’ve never experienced anything like the silent gasp of when the first bars of his theme music played where it was like, I felt in sync with the audience. That epitomized the power of pro wrestling."

Shibata currently holds a contract with AEW and ROH, and he belongs to The Opps alongside Samoa Joe, HOOK, and ROH World Television Champion Action Andretti.
