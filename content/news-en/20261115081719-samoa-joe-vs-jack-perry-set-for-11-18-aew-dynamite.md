Jack Perry wanted a match against Samoa Joe, and the bout is now official. The two stars will face each other in a singles match on the upcoming episode of AEW Dynamite following the Full Gear pay-per-view. Joe missed the event due to a nationwide ground stop by the Federal Aviation Administration. Meanwhile, Perry defeated The Beast Mortos at Full Gear Zero Hour, then called out Joe directly to the camera and spat at it. In response, Joe promised in a video message that he would show up at Dynamite and choke Perry out. Additionally, the show will feature the start of the 2026 Continental Classic tournament, with participants to be announced on November 16.
