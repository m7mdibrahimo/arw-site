Mystery Wrestling held its Mystery Wrestling 30 event on October 11 at the Centre Aydelu in Gatineau, Quebec, Canada. The event was broadcast on Twitch.

Gabriel Fuerza def. Alexia Nicole, Jay Blast, and Pawn #87.

Le Tabarnak De Trio (Benjamin Tull, Mathieu St-Jacques, and Thomas Dubois) def. The Debt Collectors (Bray Dello Russo, Shane Pinto, and Tyler Nox).

Locked And Loaded (Jessie V and Mark Wheeler) def. Street Meat Express (HADDY and Top Dog).

A Fan (c) def. Cecil Nyx to retain the replica IWGP World Heavyweight Championship.

London Lightning def. Storm Ryder.

Psycho Mike def. Bruce Thunderstein.

Dreya Mitchell, Kristara, and Layla Wilde def. Katrina Creed, KC Diamond, and Kingsley.

Cool Ref def. Johnny Mystery (c) to win the Mystery Wrestling World Championship in a match featuring Sasquatch as the special referee.
