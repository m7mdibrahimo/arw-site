AEW announced a massive eight-man tag team match for the upcoming Winter Is Coming event, scheduled for December 9 in Monterrey, Mexico. The contest will feature Dax Harwood and Cash Wheeler of FTR teaming with Jacob and Drew Kasper to battle the duo of Matt Jackson and Nick Jackson alongside Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin. The Motor City Machine Guns proposed this bout on AEW Dynamite, with Stokely Hathaway subsequently accepting and confirming the date.
