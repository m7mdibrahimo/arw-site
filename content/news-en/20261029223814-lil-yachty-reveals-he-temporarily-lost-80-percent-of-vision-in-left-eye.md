Musician and WWE performer Lil Yachty recently shared that he experienced a significant health scare that left him temporarily blind in his left eye. During an interview with Complex, Yachty explained that he had been dealing with a severe, undisclosed illness for two weeks, which forced him into isolation. He described the moment he lost 80 percent of his vision, admitting that the experience was terrifying.

> "Scared the sh*t out of me. I literally thought I was gonna be blind forever. That type of sh*t… make you so grateful. All the sh*t we take for granted, like our eyesight, and you be like, ‘Oh my God bruh, I never knew.’ We get older in life and things become so minuscule to us… Majority of us have been blessed to have it our entire lives, and then it just becomes so minuscule. It is something to have your eyesight."

Despite the severity of the incident, Yachty confirmed that he has since recovered.

> "I’m fine."

Yachty last appeared on WWE programming during the September 11 episode of WWE SmackDown, where he was involved in a segment with Baron Corbin and Trick Williams.
