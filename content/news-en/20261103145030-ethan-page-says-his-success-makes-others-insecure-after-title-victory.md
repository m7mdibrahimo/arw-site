Ethan Page addressed his Intercontinental Championship win and the backlash surrounding it, claiming that his success causes other people to feel insecure. Appearing on WWE Now following his victory against Chad Gable on the November 2 edition of RAW, Page stated that his hard journey around the globe to reach WWE justifies his pride in his accomplishments.

Responding to questions about his lack of modesty, Page insisted that he possesses more humility than anyone else in the promotion. He added that his deep self-confidence creates discomfort in others because he fully understands who he is while many people do not.
