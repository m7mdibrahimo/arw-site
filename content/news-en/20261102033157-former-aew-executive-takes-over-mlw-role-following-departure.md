Major League Wrestling has appointed Rafael Morffi as its new Senior Director of Live Events, stepping into the role previously held by Mike Kitlas. Morffi, who previously worked in live event operations for AEW under Tony Khan, actually took over the position approximately one month ago. Kitlas, who had been with MLW since 2019 and was widely respected for his dedication behind the scenes, has departed the company to take on a new position with Real American Freestyle. According to Sean Ross Sapp of Fightful Select, the leadership change occurred quietly weeks before Kitlas' departure became public knowledge. With Morffi now managing the live events department and Kitlas starting a new chapter, MLW heads into the future with an experienced executive from AEW leading its operations.
