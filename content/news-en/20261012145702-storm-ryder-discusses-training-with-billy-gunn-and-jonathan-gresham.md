Independent wrestler Storm Ryder recently opened up about his training experiences and the mentors who shaped his career, including Billy Gunn and Jonathan Gresham. Speaking in a new interview, Ryder praised Billy Gunn as a great coach and human being, noting that training with him involved a healthy dose of tough love. Ryder explained that Gunn recognized his and his brother's dedication to professional wrestling during their time together at the Nightmare Factory.

Ryder also highlighted the immense impact of working with Jonathan Gresham, calling him one of the best wrestlers in the world. He shared that he frequently reaches out to Gresham for feedback and match analysis, drawing parallels between Gresham's insights and his own wrestling philosophy. Ryder credits his current success in the independent scene to the valuable lessons he learned from these experienced coaches.
