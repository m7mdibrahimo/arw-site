A major backstage figure in WWE is reportedly set to step down next year after WrestleMania 43. PWInsider reports that Bruce Prichard, executive director of creative writing and talent, plans to retire from his current position following the major event. Prichard is expected to transition into a consulting role with WWE once he steps away from day-to-day operations. His current tenure with the company began in 2019 when he rejoined the creative team, later taking on executive director duties for WWE RAW and WWE SmackDown in 2020. Prichard's extensive history with the company spans back to the late 1980s under his Brother Love persona, alongside multiple stints working behind the scenes and with other promotions like TNA and MLW. Currently, there is no official word on who will take over his responsibilities once he departs.
