Grayson Waller retained the NXT Championship against Tony D'Angelo at the 2026 NXT Halloween Havoc Premium Live Event using a crowbar. After the match, Danhausen announced that Waller's personal friend had a message for him. CM Punk appeared on the screen and challenged Waller for the NXT Championship on the Wednesday, November 4 episode of WWE NXT. The upcoming show will also feature Rey Fenix, Dragon Lee, and WWE Intercontinental Champion Chad Gable teaming up. Additionally, NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan will defend her title against a mystery opponent, with General Manager Robert Stone hinting the challenger will come from the main roster. The updated lineup includes Dion Lennox vs. Cruz Montana in an Unsanctioned Match with Michael Chandler as the special guest referee, Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno vs. Hank and Tank in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic Final presented by Cody Rhodes, an appearance by John Cena, Chad Gable, Rey Fenix, and Dragon Lee vs. Vanity Project, Kelani Jordan defending the NXT Women's Championship against a mystery opponent, and Grayson Waller defending the NXT Championship against CM Punk.
