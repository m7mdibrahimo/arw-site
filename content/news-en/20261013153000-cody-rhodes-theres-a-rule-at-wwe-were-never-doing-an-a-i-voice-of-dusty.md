Cody Rhodes has confirmed that there is a strict policy within WWE regarding the use of artificial intelligence. During a recent appearance on the Last Meal show to promote the upcoming Street Fighter movie, Rhodes explained that the company will not use A.I. to replicate his father's voice.

> "There’s a rule at WWE: no AI. We’re never doing an A.I. voice of Dusty, and here’s the backup: If we need to fill in a couple blanks, Bruce Prichard will just do it. Bruce Prichard or I’ll do it. That’s my promise, that’s my PSA. No A.I. Dusty."

While Rhodes' father, Dusty Rhodes, had his voice featured during Cody's entrance at SummerSlam in August 2025, Cody clarified that future projects will rely on human voice acting rather than technology. He emphasized that he or Bruce Prichard would step in to provide any necessary audio work instead of turning to A.I. software.
