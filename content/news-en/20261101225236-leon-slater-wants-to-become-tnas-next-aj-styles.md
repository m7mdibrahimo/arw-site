Many view TNA Wrestling as the house that AJ Styles built, but reigning TNA World Champion Leon Slater wants to establish himself as the company's new foundation.

During an interview with Wrestling Life, Leon Slater reflected on his memorable encounter with AJ Styles at TNA's 2025 Slammiversary event and expressed his wish to become the promotion's next star to carry the banner.

Leon Slater noted that AJ Styles served as the backbone of the organization for years, expressing his ambition to surpass that massive legacy and reach a similar legendary status.

He added that AJ Styles' return after a long absence was not limited to greeting the fans, as he made sure to praise and support him personally in the ring.

AJ Styles contributed during his lengthy career to establishing the X-Division, which highlights fast-paced matches, and praised names like Christopher Daniels, Frankie Kazarian, Amazing Red, and Homicide before declaring Leon Slater as the future of the division.

Leon Slater became the youngest TNA World Champion in history after defeating veteran Nic Nemeth at Bound for Glory.
