WWE RAW star Je'Von Evans spoke about his strong connection with Jacob Fatu, praising the Samoan Werewolf for guiding him through his transition to the main roster. In an interview with Stephanie McMahon on What's Your Story, Evans highlighted their shared roots on the independent scene and their aggressive in-ring styles.

> "He's somebody who helped me, especially when I first got up there, because we have a similar background when it comes to wrestling. We both came from the indies, so we know the grind. I think we met on the indies once and other than that, it was just us watching each other's matches. We were big fans of each other. I was a big fan of Jacob and now, to see how he is, where he's at, and what position he's in, it's great. It's just so cool to see."

Both performers are currently engaged in significant WWE storylines. Fatu is embroiled in a war alongside The Bloodline against the 946 group, recently being taken out after an attack by Jaida Parker. Evans is fresh off a standout performance in the Men's Money in the Bank ladder match, where he endured a mid-air spear from Bron Breakker before returning to the fray.
