Independent wrestler David Morales achieved a major milestone during the 2026 Midterm Elections by becoming the Mayor of Providence, Rhode Island. Ocean State Media reported that Morales, known for his progressive politics and bright suits, represents a new generation of leadership for the state's capital and promises a fresh political direction. Voters gave him a strong mandate, with the democratic socialist securing over 80% of the vote against Republican David Talan. In his victory speech, Morales stated, "Across Rhode Island, and right here in our capital city of Providence, our neighbors voted overwhelmingly for the change that they deserve." At 40, he becomes the youngest mayor ever elected in Providence, surpassing Joseph Paolino Jr., who was elected in 1984 at age 29. Morales has been involved with Rhode Island’s Renegade Wrestling Alliance since 2021, where he performs under the ring name "The Commissioner."
