Former WWE ring announcer Greg Hamilton has finally revealed the real reason he was fired by the company five years ago, claiming it had nothing to do with his online dispute with rapper Westside Gunn. Hamilton explained in an Instagram comment that he was let go after appearing in a music video for a Name, Image, and Likeness (NIL) project owned by WWE, stating he had no idea he was even in the video when Talent Relations called him.

Hamilton recalled that he received the unexpected call from Talent Relations while he was at home watching a Cincinnati Bearcats football game right after returning from a WWE trip to Saudi Arabia. Although many fans previously assumed his release in October 2021 was tied to his public threats of legal action against Westside Gunn for unauthorized use of his voice, Hamilton insisted that the music video appearance was the actual cause of his termination.
