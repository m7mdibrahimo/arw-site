Lucha Libre AAA presents week two of AAA Heroes Inmortales on October 31, 2026, live from Gimnasio Olímpico Juan de la Barrera in Mexico City, Mexico. The event starts at 8 pm ET in Mexico and 10 pm ET in the US, and fans can watch on FOX in Latin America and on YouTube in the US. The lineup features the AAA Tag Team Championships match with The Wagner Brothers defending against The Vipers, Pagano and El Carnicero facing Los Psycho Circus, and a AAA Cruiserweight Championship match between Rey Fenix and a TBA opponent. Fightful will provide coverage of the event beginning at 10 p.m. ET on Saturday.
