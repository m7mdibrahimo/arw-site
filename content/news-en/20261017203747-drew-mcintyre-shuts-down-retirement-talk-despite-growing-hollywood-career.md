Drew McIntyre has made it clear that his growing Hollywood career does not mean he is leaving WWE behind. During a recent podcast appearance, McIntyre addressed his ongoing absence from television while working on outside acting projects like Highlander and Agent of Shadows. He explained that wrestling remains his primary focus and that he has an understanding with company leadership that allows him to pursue acting opportunities without walking away from the ring.

McIntyre, who recently turned 41, emphasized that he plans to stay with WWE for years to come. He noted that his current five-month break is the longest of his career and was extended because filming wrapped up near other projects, leading management to keep him off television until shooting finished. Despite these new opportunities in the entertainment industry, McIntyre intends to balance both worlds rather than choosing one over the other.
