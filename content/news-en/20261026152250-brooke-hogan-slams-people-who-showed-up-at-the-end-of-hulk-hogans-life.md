Brooke Hogan is expressing intense anger toward individuals who she claims used her father, Hulk Hogan, until his death. In a series of posts on her Instagram Stories, Brooke defended her decision to distance herself from him, citing toxic behavior and greedy people. However, she directed her strongest criticism at those who arrived only at the end of his life, accusing them of being there to collect rather than offering genuine support. Brooke specifically targeted people for showing up late in his life, and she also referenced the sale of his Clearwater Beach mansion for 5.9 million dollars, accompanying a photo of the property with a sharp message. Earlier, she had criticized WWE Superstars and legends who participated in his televised tribute following his passing on July 24, 2025, claiming they barely visited him in the hospital.
