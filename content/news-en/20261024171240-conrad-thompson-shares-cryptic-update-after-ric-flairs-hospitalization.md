Conrad Thompson has shared a cryptic update on social media following Ric Flair's recent hospitalization after a fall in Florida. Thompson posted a short message reading, "He’s home. 🙏", which raised questions about whether the WWE Hall of Famer had been released from the hospital. However, Thompson also hosts a podcast with Jim Ross, who was recently hospitalized himself, leaving some uncertainty about who the message referred to. Flair's daughter Charlotte previously confirmed that her father is going to be okay after suffering cuts in the fall, which occurred just hours after he revealed a blood clot in his leg.
