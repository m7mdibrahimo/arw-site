Rhea Ripley revealed that she needed WWE's permission to cut her hair during her early days in WWE NXT. Speaking on The Wrestling Classic, Ripley recalled that she was dealing with a difficult personal breakup and constant criticism at the WWE Performance Center, which left her tired of trying to fit into a mold that did not represent her real personality. When she decided to cut her hair, company rules required her to seek official approval through the system.

Joe Belcastro, who was in charge at the time, noticed her distress and granted the request so she would feel better. Ripley joked that she considered that single approval valid for the rest of her life as her hair became progressively shorter week by week in NXT. This transformative look ultimately became a defining part of her wrestling identity, though she previously admitted she might have been fired if she had kept her long hair.
