At AEW Full Gear 2026, The Cut secured a victory in the Anarchy in the Arena match against The Death Riders and The Conglomeration. During the chaotic bout, various outside appearances took place, including KeMalito and Wheeler Yuta. Mercedes Moné interfered in the match before being taken out by Willow Nightingale, while Claudio Castagnoli also made his return. In the end, Clark Connors and David Finlay put Gabe Kidd through a flaming table, and Jay White pinned Jon Moxley after a Blade Runner from behind to win it for The Cut.
