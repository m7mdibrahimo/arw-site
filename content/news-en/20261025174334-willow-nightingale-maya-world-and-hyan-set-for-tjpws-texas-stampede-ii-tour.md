Members of the AEW roster, including Willow Nightingale, are set for the Tokyo Joshi Pro-Wrestling Texas Stampede II tour, which takes place from December 4th through the 6th. The promotion has announced the guest wrestlers for the show, featuring a lineup of talent such as Vert Vixen, Maya World, Hyan, Jazmin Allure, and Zara Zakher. TJPW shared the announcement on social media, noting that 16 wrestlers have also been announced to fly over from Japan for the events.
