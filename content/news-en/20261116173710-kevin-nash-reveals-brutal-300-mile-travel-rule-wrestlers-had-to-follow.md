Kevin Nash recently discussed the grueling travel schedule wrestlers endured during the busiest days of WWE and WCW, revealing a strict rule regarding driving distances. During an episode of the Kliq This podcast, Nash explained that if the next town was 300 miles away or less, wrestlers were expected to drive rather than fly. Nash noted that trips like Chicago to St. Louis meant driving 299 miles after a physical match, often leaving performers with only a few hours of sleep before repeating the routine. He also shared that they frequently had to search for hotels in the middle of the night without advance reservations, and mentioned that a Cadillac Sedan DeVille was his preferred vehicle for its horsepower and space when traveling with Scott Hall and Sean Waltman.
