Paige faced a major travel nightmare while trying to leave Scotland. After losing her seat, having her flight canceled due to a storm, and finding out that trains were not running, the WWE Superstar was left stranded. She shared the frustrating ordeal on social media, joking that Scotland does not want her to leave. Paige hopes to finally make her escape tomorrow and update fans. The travel headache comes shortly after her match on the October 16 episode of SmackDown, where she defeated Niki Bella.
