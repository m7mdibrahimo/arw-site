Paul Wight recently discussed the rising potential of Kyle Fletcher, noting that the Australian wrestler is building "incredible momentum." Fletcher is currently preparing for a high-profile match at AEW Full Gear, where he will face Will Ospreay for the AEW World Championship. While speaking to Going Ringside, Wight praised Fletcher's well-rounded skill set and professional attitude.

> "A guy I think is building incredible momentum and I see huge big star potential is Kyle Fletcher. He checks every box, interview ability, wrestling ability, tenacity, aggression. his attitude for the industry, his willingness to work hard, the way he presents himself, his interaction with guys in the locker room, you know, behind the scenes. He's very humble, very quiet, very good kid, and I like seeing what he does. I look at him, hmm, that guy's got money written on him."

Fletcher has been a frequent topic of conversation recently, including a humorous interaction in St. Louis where Tommaso Ciampa mistakenly attempted an RKO on him.
