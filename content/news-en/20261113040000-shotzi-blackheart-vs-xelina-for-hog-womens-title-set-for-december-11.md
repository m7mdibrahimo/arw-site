Shotzi Blackheart is preparing to defend her HOG Women's Championship against Xelina in an upcoming match. House of Glory officially announced that the title bout will take place on Friday, December 11, 2026, live from the NYC Arena in Jamaica, Queens. Shotzi has dominated the women's division, holding the championship for over 350 days.

Xelina earned this opportunity after teaming with Tommy End to defeat Shotzi and Lio Rush during the promotion's October event. Xelina now aims to end Shotzi's historic title reign, while the champion remains determined to defend her gold.
