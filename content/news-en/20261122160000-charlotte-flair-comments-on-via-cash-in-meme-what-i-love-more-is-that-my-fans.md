Charlotte Flair recently talked about the popular "via cash in" meme. During a split-screen interview on SmackDown in March 2025, Flair and Tiffany Stratton discussed Stratton's path to the title, with Flair dropping the line "via cash in." After Stratton captured the WWE Women's Championship at Crown Jewel 2026, Flair posted a reference to the moment on social media.

Appearing on the Ring the Belle podcast, Flair shared her thoughts on the clip going viral.

> "What I love more is that my fans that remembered that. When I put it up, I was like, maybe my diehard Queens Court fans will know this picture or remember this picture. But other people just be like, 'Oh, it's just like a typical cute glam pic.' No, but people remembered it. I thought it was great. The line," she said.

When asked if official merchandise based on the meme is being planned, Flair teased:

> "I may or may not have a shirt already."
