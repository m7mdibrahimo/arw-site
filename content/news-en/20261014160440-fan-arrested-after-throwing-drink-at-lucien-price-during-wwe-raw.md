During the latest episode of WWE RAW, a fan was arrested after throwing a drink at Lucien Price as the trio known as 946 made their entrance through the crowd. Price, Bronco Nima, and Royce Keys were heading to the ring for a six-man tag team match against Jimmy Uso, Jey Uso, and Solo Sikoa when the incident occurred. Security and police quickly intervened, escorting the individual out of the arena as nearby spectators watched. The disruption happened before the bell rang and did not affect the flow of the show, but the action inside the ring quickly escalated into complete chaos.

The match broke down after Jaida Parker pulled the referee out of the ring, spilling the brawl all over ringside and into the crowd. Solo Sikoa prepared to finish Royce Keys with the Samoan Spike, but the lights suddenly went out on The Bloodline. A hooded figure attacked Solo from behind with a tire iron and was revealed as Carmelo Hayes. Hayes aligned himself with The 946, helping Bronco Nima, Lucien Price, and Royce Keys lay out Jimmy, Jey, and Solo to close the broadcast.

The fan's actions quickly became a major talking point online, highlighting the ongoing issue of crowd interference. While wrestlers regularly face loud audiences, crossing the line into throwing physical objects remains unacceptable, and arena security took immediate action. WWE now heads toward Crown Jewel with the rivalry between The Bloodline and The 946 hotter than ever, while the disruptive fan ended their night in police custody.
