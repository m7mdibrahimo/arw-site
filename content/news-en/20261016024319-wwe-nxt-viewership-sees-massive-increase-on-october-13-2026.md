Viewership for WWE NXT on The CW Network saw a significant boost for the October 13, 2026 episode. According to Wrestlenomics, the show drew 732,000 viewers, achieving a 0.14 rating in the key 18-49 demographic. This marks a substantial improvement from the previous week's episode on October 6, which recorded 532,000 viewers and a 0 rating in the same demographic, reflecting an increase of 200,000 viewers and a 0.14 rise in the demo rating.

Compared to the same week in 2025, when the October 14 episode drew 690,000 viewers and a 0.12 demo rating, the 2026 figures show a year-over-year growth of 6.1% in total viewership and 16.7% in the key demographic. These figures account for linear television viewership on The CW Network and do not include streaming or digital metrics.
