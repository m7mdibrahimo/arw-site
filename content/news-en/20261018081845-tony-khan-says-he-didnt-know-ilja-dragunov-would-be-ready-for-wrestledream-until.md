Ilja Dragunov made his AEW debut at AEW WrestleDream after being introduced by Renee Paquette. Tony Khan explained at the post-show press conference that he was unsure if the debut would happen until the final moments due to visa processing.

> "Thanks to the process, it can be an unpredictable process of acquiring a visa. It's something that I wasn't sure until this Wednesday night, during a live broadcast, that I would be able to bring you. It's a great surprise, and it's something that I was so pleased that we were able to do tonight," Khan said. Dragunov had departed WWE earlier in August.
