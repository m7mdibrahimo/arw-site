Kay Lee Ray discussed returning to the independent wrestling scene following her departure from WWE in April 2026, marking her first match against Molly Spartan at Pro Wrestling EVE's 150th event in London.

During a new interview, Kay Lee Ray reflected on her last appearance for the promotion in 2019, according to WhatCulture Wrestling.

> "Like I said earlier, it's almost making sure that I can step up to all that. There's so many girls that I haven't worked before that I’m eager to get in the ring with and see what we can do in this crazy wrestling thing. For me, the person you're in with really dictates the matches for me. We're all individuals and it should be very different."

She added that this variety motivates her to constantly improve, pointing out the many prominent names around her. Since leaving WWE, Kay Lee Ray has also wrestled for promotions like wXw and PROGRESS.
