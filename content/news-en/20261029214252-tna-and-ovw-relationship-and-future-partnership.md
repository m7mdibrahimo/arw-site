The working relationship between TNA and Ohio Valley Wrestling is set to evolve significantly in 2027. TNA is currently considering a new plan to shift the production site for some of its weekly content. According to PWInsider, the promotion intends to begin taping Xplosion at the Davis Arena in Louisville, Kentucky, early next year.

This move marks a major departure from the current system, where Xplosion matches are recorded during TNA iMPACT events. The primary motivation for this change is to reduce touring and travel expenses by utilizing a fixed venue. 

This strategy coincides with ongoing discussions to expand the collaboration between the two organizations, which could see OVW function as a developmental system for TNA. While TNA talent have not yet received their 2027 schedules, moving the production of Xplosion to the Davis Arena represents a tangible step in strengthening the growing partnership between the two companies.
