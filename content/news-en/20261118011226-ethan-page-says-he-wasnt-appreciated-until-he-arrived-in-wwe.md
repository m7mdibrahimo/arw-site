Ethan Page has been wrestling professionally since 2006, but he didn't feel truly valued until he joined WWE in 2024. In an interview with Ringer Wrestling, the WWE Intercontinental Champion reflected on his career and the years he spent feeling undervalued.

> "I have always reached a point of knowing my value is not being appreciated in the company that I am currently in. Not the one right now. I'm very appreciated, but I will say every company before did not appreciate who I was and what I brought to the table," Page said.

Page believes his talent and potential made others uncomfortable because they saw him as a genuine threat. He explained that previous promotions tried to push him out, but he welcomed the changes and found success elsewhere.

> "I actually think that my skillset and my potential made people very uncomfortable. They didn't think that they could hold up against me. I was a real threat. So they essentially tried to push me out of everywhere that I was. I happily watched them open the door and saw success on the other side," he continued. "I upgraded every company that I left from. Now I am in the company. I started at the bottom and I have now upgraded in the only company left to be in."

Before signing with WWE, Page competed across the independent circuit in Canada, the United States, Japan, and the United Kingdom, while also making television appearances for TNA Wrestling, Ring of Honor, and All Elite Wrestling. He departed from AEW and ROH in early 2024 after feeling unfulfilled. Since arriving in WWE, Page has held the NXT Championship, the NXT North American Championship, and the WWE Intercontinental Championship, with plans to reach the top of the company and eventually retire there.
