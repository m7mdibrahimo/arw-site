Willow Nightingale shared a fresh update on her broken hand recovery, confirming she is continuing daily treatment to heal the injury. She posted a photo on her Instagram story behind the wheel while using a bone stimulator on her injured hand.

She explained that her condition has improved even though it is not completely healed yet, and she uses the device every day while stuck in traffic. Nightingale has kept appearing on AEW television despite the injury per the promotion's plans, facing Thekla at AEW All Out and returning on AEW Collision following her AEW Women's World Championship loss to Mercedes Moné at AEW All In.
