The go-home episode of WWE NXT for Halloween Havoc saw a significant drop in average viewership, recording the show's lowest numbers in five months. According to Wrestlenomics, the October 27, 2026, edition averaged 520,000 viewers, which is 21 percent lower than the previous week's 655,000 and 20 percent below the four-week average of 648,000. The last time the show drew a smaller audience was on May 19 with 517,000 viewers. Despite the drop in viewership, the key demographic rating fell only slightly from 0.10 to 0.09. Furthermore, the show performed better than it did in October of last year, with average viewership up 3 percent and ratings 10 percent higher. The episode finalized storylines for Halloween Havoc, featuring the rivalry between Grayson Waller and Tony D'Angelo, where Waller retained his title, as well as Zilla Fatu's feud with BirthRight leading to his match against Charlie Dempsey over the weekend. The upcoming November 4 episode will go head-to-head with AEW Dynamite, featuring major stars such as John Cena and CM Punk, with Punk scheduled to face Waller.
