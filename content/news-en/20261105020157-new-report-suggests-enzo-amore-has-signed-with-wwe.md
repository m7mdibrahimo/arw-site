Following months of speculation, a new report suggests that Enzo Amore has signed a contract with WWE within the last week. According to PWInsider Elite, there is a belief that the former Cruiserweight Champion is on his way back to the company, with sources pointing toward a potential role on WWE NXT. Amore previously visited the WWE Performance Center in May, ahead of the return of his former tag team partner Big Cass to WWE Raw in August.
