Kacy Catanzaro recently looked back on her memorable performance in the 2019 Royal Rumble, her first such match. Entering at number 19, she lasted ten minutes and created a lasting impression by avoiding elimination with a handstand on the floor. Speaking with Sean Ross Sapp, Catanzaro noted that fans still mention the moment years later.

"Definitely my 2019 Royal Rumble save. I would say that's one of my bigger ones just because I was so new and it was such a big moment and years later when I would meet people they'd be like, 'I loved your Rumble save.' [It was] just something that kind of stuck in people's minds and for how new I was, it was just such a big moment that I'm super proud of that," she said.

Catanzaro explained that she and the producers worked to find a creative way for her to showcase her athletic style despite the differences between the WWE main roster ring posts and the ones she used in NXT. She credited Lacey Evans and TJ Wilson for their support in helping her execute the spot. Catanzaro continues to look back on her time in WWE with pride, noting that she enjoyed challenging herself to find her limits.
