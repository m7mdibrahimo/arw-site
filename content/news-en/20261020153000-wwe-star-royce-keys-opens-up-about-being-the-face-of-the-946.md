WWE star Royce Keys recently opened up about his role as the leader and face of the 946 faction on WWE RAW. During an interview on Busted Open Radio, Keys discussed his responsibilities as a mentor and older brother figure to the group, which currently includes Bronco Nima, Lucien Price, Jaida Parker, and Carmelo Hayes. Keys noted that their unity goes beyond television, as they travel and train together as a single unit. The stable has recently made a major impact on the WWE RAW locker room by targeting The Bloodline, featuring high-profile attacks and official main roster debuts.
