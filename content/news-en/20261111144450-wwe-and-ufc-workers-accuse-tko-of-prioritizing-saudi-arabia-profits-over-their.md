TKO employees and workers are expressing frustration over the company's decision to continue holding events in Saudi Arabia despite ongoing safety concerns and missile and drone attacks in the region. According to a report by Ben Fowlkes of Uncrowned, multiple workers under the TKO umbrella feel that financial profits and fourth-quarter earnings are being prioritized over their well-being. Following WWE Crown Jewel in Riyadh on November 7, reports indicated that a missile attack hit Riyadh's financial district the following day. While TKO executives like CEO Ari Emanuel and President Mark Shapiro have addressed employee concerns by monitoring the situation and stating that events would be postponed if danger escalated, workers remain worried and critical of the lack of communication and safety plans regarding upcoming events, including a scheduled UFC event in Riyadh.
