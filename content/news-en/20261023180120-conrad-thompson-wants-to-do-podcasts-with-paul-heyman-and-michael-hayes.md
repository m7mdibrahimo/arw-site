Conrad Thompson revealed in a recent interview that Paul Heyman and Michael Hayes are his top dream podcast guests. Thompson praised Hayes for his extensive backstage experience and his involvement in critical historical moments in WWE and WCW, stating that he would love to reshape public perception of him.

> "Obviously Paul Heyman would be a big white whale for me, as would Michael Hayes."

Thompson currently hosts a podcast network featuring prominent names such as Jeff Jarrett, Tony Schiavone, Eric Bischoff, Jim Ross, and JBL. He believes Hayes would bring a unique perspective to the genre thanks to his natural entertainment skills and long-standing tenure with WWE.
