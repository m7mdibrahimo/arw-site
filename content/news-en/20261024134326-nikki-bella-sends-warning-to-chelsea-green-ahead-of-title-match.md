Nikki Bella is getting ready for a major challenge for the WWE Women's Championship on WWE SmackDown, where she will face champion Chelsea Green on October 30. Bella shared on X that she is fully confident in winning and taking the belt, sending a direct message to her opponent telling her to get ready to hear the new champion announced. 

> "So ready for next week! Oh @ImChelseaGreen AND NEW is whatcha gonna be hearing next week 💋 You got that 💅"

https://x.com/NikkiAndBrie/status/2113795464986341448

This challenge came shortly after Green successfully defended her title against Roxanne Perez in a match featuring numerous interventions from Raquel Rodriguez and IYO SKY before Green secured the win. The backstage area saw heated moments following an attack by Liv Morgan on Green and Tiffany Stratton attempting to cash in her Money in the Bank contract. 

This bout arrives at a crucial time since Green is scheduled to meet Women's World Champion Liv Morgan at Crown Jewel on November 7. Should Bella succeed in capturing the title, WWE's plans for that major match could change completely while Bella stays fully focused on claiming the championship.
