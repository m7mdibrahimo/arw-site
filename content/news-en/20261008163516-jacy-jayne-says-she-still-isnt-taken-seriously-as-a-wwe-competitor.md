Jacy Jayne feels that people still refuse to take her seriously as a competitor in WWE despite her championships and main roster presence. During an appearance on The Wrestling Classic, Jayne discussed feeling underestimated throughout her career. "Well, I feel like people just overlook me in general still to this day for some reason. I feel like I'm just not taken seriously as a competitor and I will never understand that," she said. She also opened up about her positioning on the card dropping after her time in Toxic Attraction ended, noting that she had to do a lot of hard work behind the scenes and train at another school to improve her craft.
