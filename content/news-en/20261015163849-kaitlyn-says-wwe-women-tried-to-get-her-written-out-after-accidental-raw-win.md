Former Divas Champion Kaitlyn recently spoke about her accidental breakthrough in WWE, revealing that some women on the roster tried to get her pushed out almost immediately after her unplanned victory on WWE RAW. Speaking with Chris Van Vliet on Insight, Kaitlyn explained that she was originally supposed to be eliminated early in a battle royal arranged by Fit Finlay, but a botched spot led her to accidentally eliminate Eve Torres instead. This unexpected win made her the number one contender and left her backstage wondering if she would be fired, though Vince McMahon found the entire situation hilarious. Kaitlyn noted that while one supportive writer was thrilled for her hard work, he also warned her that some of the female talent were already trying to have her written out of the storyline. At the time, television time for the division was extremely limited and there was only a single championship to chase, intensifying the backstage competition. WWE ultimately decided to stick with the result, launching the storyline that led to Kaitlyn capturing the title.
