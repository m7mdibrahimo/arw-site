The lineup for the October 27th episode of WWE NXT has been set as the go-home show ahead of Halloween Havoc on October 31st. Jackson Drake will defend the NXT North American Championship against E.K. Prosper. The show will also feature the semifinal matches in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic tournament, with Vanity Project facing Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno, and Hank and Tank taking on The Wagner Brothers.
