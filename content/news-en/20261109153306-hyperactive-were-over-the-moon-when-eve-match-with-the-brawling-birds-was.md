Hyperactive recently discussed their postponed match against The Brawling Birds in EVE, expressing their deep disappointment. Back in July, a match was announced for this past weekend featuring Hyperactive (Safire Reed and Anita Vaughan) taking on The Brawling Birds (Jamie Hayter and Alex Windsor). However, during an appearance on In The Weeds, EVE promoter Dann Read announced that the match had to be postponed because The Brawling Birds were dealing with injuries sustained during AEW's Blood And Guts match.

Speaking to Fightful Overbooked, both Safire Reed and Anita Vaughan shared their reactions to the sudden postponement. Safire Reed stated, 

> "Yeah, I mean, of course we're gutted. The fans are going to be absolutely gutted. This is a match we've wanted for the longest time, and when it was announced, we were over the moon."

Anita Vaughan added, 

> "Yeah, this was announced back in July as well, so it's like, not that like everyone else has been really expecting this match, really waiting for it, like we have been too, and we've been and yeah working towards it, and then, yeah, we got off the flight from Japan last night, got a bit of sleep, woke up this morning, get that news from Dann, and we're like.."

Despite the setback, Safire Reed noted that they wish Jamie Hayter the best and firmly believe the matchup will eventually happen when the time is right.
