The Miz recently discussed his lengthy losing streak in WWE, noting that he was unaware of it until someone pointed it out on social media. According to an interview with Z100 New York, he dropped 45 straight matches between August 26, 2025, and October 23, 2026, explaining that he always focuses only on his most recent match rather than his overall record.

The Miz noted that his streak ended on the October 23 episode of Main Event thanks to his tag team partner, Kit Wilson.

> "I didn't even pin the guy, by the way. My tag team partner did. So, I won by not even pinning a guy."

The 46-year-old veteran added that he has not won a singles match since February 2024 and still needs to pin an opponent on his own.
