Marcus Mathers has announced his departure from the WWE ID program. Mathers, who was among the original talents signed when the program launched in October 2024, shared the news on social media this week.

> "As of this week, my time with WWEID has come to an end. What an incredible two years it's been. I've learned so much, gained invaluable knowledge and made friendships and memories that I'll always cherish. I want to thank WWE for their time! Time is the most valuable thing any of us can give. So when this chapter closed, I did what I know how to do, l Kept moving. I rolled up to The Dungeon and trained with some of the hungriest people in the world. Today I got Amazing Red, Tomorrow Nick Gage, match Sunday and match Monday. Now my next chapter awaits and it's up to me and my hunger to take me there. I'm in the drivers seat. I'm going to show up EVERYWHERE to fight ANYONE."

Mathers previously appeared on WWE EVOLVE and competed in a dark match before WWE NXT on October 6. His departure follows the recent announcement that Laynie Luck has also exited the WWE ID program.
