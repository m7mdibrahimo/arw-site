Damian Priest is not taking back his previous comments about seeing the same stars constantly at the top of WWE. Speaking with Complex Graps, Priest explained that while he still feels the same way, he realized he needed to change things himself if he wanted a different outcome. He noted that he is no longer pointing fingers at Roman Reigns, Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, or Seth Rollins, but rather focusing on making an impact.

Priest's original comments gained heavy attention back in August, leading to Triple H addressing the situation during the SummerSlam post-show by stating that wrestlers must earn their spots rather than waiting for a turn. Reflecting on those remarks, Priest acknowledged the immense talent of WWE's top stars while expressing his desire to climb back to the main-event scene and reclaim a world championship.
