Matt Cardona is aiming for a much earlier return to the ring after WWE announced the John Cena Classic. Originally targeting the Royal Rumble in February 2027 following his triceps surgery, Cardona revealed on X that he is now trying to get medically cleared by December 14. That date marks the John Cena Classic at Disney World in Orlando, Florida. Speaking with Katie Nolan alongside Chelsea Green, Cardona had previously discussed beating his recovery timeline, but the Cena event changed his plans. With Cody Rhodes announced as the first participant, Cardona hopes to make his return at the special tournament.
