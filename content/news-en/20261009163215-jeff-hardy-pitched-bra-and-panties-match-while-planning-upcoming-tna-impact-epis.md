Matt Hardy revealed that he and his brother Jeff Hardy have been actively collaborating with the creative team to plan the upcoming episode of TNA iMPACT. While discussing potential match ideas for the show, Jeff jokingly suggested bringing back a classic bra and panties match from the Attitude Era. Matt explained that while the proposal was made entirely in jest and likely will not happen, their brainstorming sessions have also included reliable staples like ladder matches and tables. The Hardys are currently deeply involved in TNA storylines, including their ongoing rivalry heading into Bound for Glory. Matt promised that the October 15 episode will feature multiple exciting matches, unique vignettes, and a special gimmick match.
