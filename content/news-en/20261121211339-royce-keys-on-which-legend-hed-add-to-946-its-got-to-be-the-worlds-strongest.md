Royce Keys named the legend he would add to the 946 stable during an interview with Jeremy Jacobowitz. Keys leads the group on WWE television, which currently consists of himself, Lucien Price, Bronco Nima, Carmelo Hayes, Jaida Parker, and Cruz Montana. When asked which legend he would add to the stable, Keys responded, "Oh, it's got to be the World's Strongest Man, Mark Henry, without a doubt. Name: World's Strongest Man. Not 'Group of World's Strongest Men.' 'The,' which means one World's Strongest Man. So, hell yeah. Mark can join anytime." The 946 stable is scheduled to face The Bloodline in a WarGames match at Survivor Series. Cruz Montana secured the WarGames advantage for his team during the November 16 episode of WWE Raw.
