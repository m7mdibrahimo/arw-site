Josiah Jean has taken a major step toward WWE after receiving an official WWE ID contract, with Booker T personally delivering the news during a recent episode of Reality of Wrestling. Booker surprised Jean on the show to reveal the contract, praising the young wrestler's hard work and noting that he sees a lot of himself in him. Booker also shared that Jean recently impressed WWE officials during a tryout in Orlando, Florida, where he represented Reality of Wrestling. The announcement followed Jean addressing his fallout with AJ Francis and House Money, explaining that he felt used in the partnership. While Booker warned Jean about the ruthless nature of the wrestling business, he made it clear that this contract is only the beginning of the journey for the rising talent.
