Gabe Kidd recently opened up about why he signed with AEW full-time despite previously being strongly opposed to the company. Speaking with Chris Jericho, Kidd explained that his fierce loyalty to NJPW fueled his anti-AEW stance, admitting he did not care if people called him a hypocrite for changing his mind. He revealed that Jon Moxley played a major role in his arrival after Kidd approached him about joining the Death Riders. Kidd credited the group with helping him conquer personal struggles and gain a new level of confidence. When he eventually met with Tony Khan, Kidd felt that joining AEW and staying with the Death Riders was the natural path forward.
