Former WWE and TNA star Ken Anderson recently recalled how he met his wife while performing at a wrestling event in Australia, revealing that their initial interaction started with an in-character insult.

Speaking in an interview with Rewind, Recap, Relive, Anderson explained that his wife was not originally planning to attend the show until a friend offered her a front-row ticket. During his match, Anderson delivered a playful insult toward her from the ring as part of his villainous character, expecting a negative reaction.

> "I called her ugly in a roundabout way, and she was like, everybody else was mad about it, and she was like, 'he's funny.' The funny thing is, I heard Steve Irwin say this, that he had a picture of him and his wife the moment they met, and I have that."
