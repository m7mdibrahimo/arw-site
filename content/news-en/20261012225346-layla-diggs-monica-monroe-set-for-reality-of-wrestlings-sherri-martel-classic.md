NXT talent Layla Diggs and the new WWE I.D. Women's Champion Monica Monroe have been announced for the upcoming Reality of Wrestling Sherri Martel Classic tournament. The event is promoted by Booker T's Reality of Wrestling organization. Both competitors were confirmed for the field through official social media announcements.

> BREAKING NEWS! NXT Superstar Layla Diggs has officially been added to the SHERRI MARTEL CLASSIC TOURNAMENT! With an NXT Superstar now entering the mix the road to becoming wrestling’s next breakout star just got a whole lot tougher for the competition!

> TALENT ANNOUNCEMENT! Coming off the BIGGEST night of her career, becoming the NEW WWE ID WOMEN’S CHAMPION and officially earning a WWE ID contract! Monica Monroe is coming to the SHERRI MARTEL CLASSIC with a HUGE ANNOUNCEMENT for the ROW Nation!

The tournament field also includes Rachel Ley, Ruthie Jay, Brittnie Brooks, Kaylia Capri, Simone, Jazmyne Hao, Mia Friday, and Jazmin Allure.
