According to TMZ, newly released 911 audio provides heartbreaking details regarding the final moments before first responders arrived to help PAC, whose real name is Benjamin Satterley. The emergency call was placed on September 27 at approximately 3:53 p.m., about 15 minutes prior to Satterley being officially pronounced dead at 4:08 p.m.

The female caller initially mistook Satterley for a woman due to his long hair before correcting herself. She informed dispatchers that she and other bystanders had spent several minutes trying to get his attention while his vehicle was still running.

> "I’m at the CAVA on Mannheim Road in Des Plaines and I was getting in my car and the person next to me is passed out in their car. I don’t know. She doesn’t look too well and her car is running. It’s a man, I’m sorry."

She described Satterley as entirely unresponsive and stated she believed he was no longer breathing. 

> "We’ve been banging on the window for I want to say, like, five minutes now. His face is, like, straight down. Apparently their eyes are open, too. I don’t see his chest moving at all."

First responders arrived soon after the call and attempted life-saving measures. Police previously noted there were no visible injuries other than what appeared to be saliva on the front of his shirt, and officers recovered medication inside the vehicle. Authorities also confirmed his phone had Google Maps open with directions to Chicago O'Hare International Airport. The official cause of death remains pending while the Cook County Medical Examiner’s Office conducts additional testing.
