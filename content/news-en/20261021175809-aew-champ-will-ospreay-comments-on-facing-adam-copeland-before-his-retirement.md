AEW Men's World Champion Will Ospreay recently discussed his desire for a singles match against Adam Copeland before Copeland's anticipated retirement in 2027. Following their encounter at AEW WrestleDream, where Ospreay successfully retained his title in Clockwork Carnage, Ospreay praised Copeland during an interview with 1010XL-Jacksonville's Sports Radio. Ospreay expressed his gratitude for Copeland's support during his neck surgery and mentioned that he wants Copeland to save a date for a future match between them. The two are scheduled to team up on AEW Dynamite against Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher.
