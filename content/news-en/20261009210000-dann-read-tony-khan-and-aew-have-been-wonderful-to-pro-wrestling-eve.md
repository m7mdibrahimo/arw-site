Dann Read, a key figure in the British promotion Pro Wrestling EVE, recently praised Tony Khan and AEW for their significant support of the independent wrestling scene. Read noted that AEW stars have frequently appeared for EVE, an all-women's promotion where Will Ospreay has played a major role in creative development. During an interview, Read addressed the tribalism often found in wrestling discourse, emphasizing that the facts demonstrate AEW's genuine care for the industry.

> I mean, absolutely. Like, I know how it's going to look and I know how people like, there's going to be people that will twist how the things that I've said and how I come across and, you know, and I'll be like, because, you know, there's so much tribalism and agenda spending and everything along those lines, and I just try to, like, look at the facts as the facts are, and you said it there, and the facts are also that Tony and AEW have been wonderful to us. I cannot say enough good things about them. I cannot say enough and it's true and the evidence is there. It's just ignored if people deliberately choose to ignore it, that they care for the independent scene. They understand that wrestling does better where a rising tide raises all ships, and they understand that when the wrestling scene is hot, we all do well. The promotions, the wrestlers, the staff crew, we all do well when we're all doing well, and the support of Tony Khan, AEW, Chris Harrington, I have to throw that name because he's followed EVE for years, much prior to then. Wonderful, wonderful fellow.

Read also highlighted the contributions of AEW executive Chris Harrington, who has supported the promotion for years. Additionally, Read discussed Ospreay's creative influence, specifically mentioning his role in developing unique match stipulations like Clockwork Carnage.
