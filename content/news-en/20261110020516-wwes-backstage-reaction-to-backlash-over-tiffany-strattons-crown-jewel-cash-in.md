WWE faced heavy backlash and a high number of dislikes after Tiffany Stratton cashed in her Money in the Bank contract at Crown Jewel, but backstage figures are reportedly unfazed. According to WrestleVotes, the company views the negative online response and dislikes as positive engagement just like any likes, emphasizing that audience interaction is what matters most. Stratton’s YouTube video cashing in on Chelsea Green received significantly more dislikes than likes, reflecting a harsh reaction from fans who also criticized the predictability of her turning on Green. However, Stratton herself has embraced the reaction on social media. The controversy began at Crown Jewel when Stratton cashed in following Green's victory over Liv Morgan, ultimately leaving Riyadh as the WWE Women’s Champion. Sources indicate that putting the title on Stratton was part of WWE's plan to turn her heel again.
