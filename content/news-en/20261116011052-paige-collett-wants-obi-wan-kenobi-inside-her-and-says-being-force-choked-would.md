JCW star Paige Collett shared some wild thoughts about Star Wars character Obi-Wan Kenobi, admitting during an interview on Headlocks, Heels & Havoc that she has always wanted him inside her. Collett clarified that she is not talking about actor Ewan McGregor, but rather the character himself, whether in his cartoon, old man, or super old man version. She even confessed that being Force choked by the Jedi Master would make her life complete. When one of the hosts pointed out that Obi-Wan is on the light side and not known for Force choking, Collett suggested that him tapping into the dark side would be very rock and roll. Collett is known for wild moments, such as flashing a referee at a JCW event in New Orleans and getting chokeslammed after carrying a chainsaw at an independent show.
