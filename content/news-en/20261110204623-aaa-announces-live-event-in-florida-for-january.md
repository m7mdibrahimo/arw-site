Lucha Libre AAA is preparing for a new live event in Florida, officially announcing a major show on January 30 at the Fort Lauderdale War Memorial Auditorium in Fort Lauderdale. The event will feature top stars, including El Grande Americano, La Catalina, Mr. Iguana, Psycho Clown, and Los Perros del Mal, among others. General ticket sales begin on November 14 at 10 am, following a venue presale on November 13 using a specific discount code. This announcement is part of the promotion's current tour, which includes stops in Texas and California before heading to Florida.
