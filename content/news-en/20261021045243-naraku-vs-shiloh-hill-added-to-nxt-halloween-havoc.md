NARAKU has put Shiloh Hill on notice ahead of their upcoming match at WWE NXT Halloween Havoc on October 31st. The two have previously faced each other in two singles matches, with NARAKU winning the first encounter while the second ended in a no contest. During a video message aired on the October 20th episode of WWE NXT on The CW, NARAKU warned Hill that he is unprepared for the black mist that will be unleashed upon him. The updated lineup for the premium live event includes Kelani Jordan defending the NXT Women's Championship against Wren Sinclair, Grayson Waller defending the NXT Championship against Tony D'Angelo, ZARIA facing Izzi Dame, and Shiloh Hill taking on NARAKU.
