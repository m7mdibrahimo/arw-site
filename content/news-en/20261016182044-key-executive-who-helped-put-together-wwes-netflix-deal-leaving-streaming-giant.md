Netflix chief content officer Bela Bajaria is leaving the streaming giant after holding the position since 2023. Bajaria played a key role in the negotiations that brought WWE to Netflix. Her departure comes amid a wider shakeup at the company, which is laying off about six percent of its workforce. Meanwhile, former Disney and Fox executive Peter Rice is taking over Bajaria's position. Rice has a history with professional wrestling, having been involved in bringing WWE SmackDown to Fox back in 2018 when he was chairman and CEO of Fox Networks Group.
