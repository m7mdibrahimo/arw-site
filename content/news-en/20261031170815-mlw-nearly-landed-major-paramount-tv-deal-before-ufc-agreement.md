Major League Wrestling was close to securing a major television deal with Paramount that would have brought weekly live programming to the network in 2025. According to MLW founder Court Bauer, negotiations began around April 2025 during a trip to Los Angeles and progressed quickly, with plans for the company to increase its staff and production scale. However, the corporate landscape shifted following Paramount's acquisition by Skydance, alongside key personnel changes.

Everything changed when Paramount reached a media rights agreement with UFC valued at more than $7 billion. Bauer noted that the news caught the MLW team completely off guard, as the agreement was negotiated at a high level without the knowledge of people involved in other television discussions. Within weeks, MLW's negotiations stalled and ultimately fell apart.

Although MLW never signed a final contract, Bauer believes the promotion was on the verge of finalizing the deal before priorities changed. MLW has since continued its television distribution through Tubi and pay-per-view events with Live Nation's Veeps, but the missed opportunity would have marked a major milestone for the company.
