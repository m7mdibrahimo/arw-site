Kyle Fletcher recently discussed his current character in AEW, noting that it closely resembles what he envisioned for himself as a child dreaming of becoming a wrestler. During an interview with Westwood One Sports Night, Fletcher explained that his current persona is simply an accumulation of the things he loved growing up, such as tattoos, collecting Pokémon cards, music, and dressing nicely. He added that if a ten-year-old version of himself played a wrestling video game from 2009, he would likely create the exact same character he portrays on television today.
