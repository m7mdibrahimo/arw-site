Storm Ryder recently discussed his career goals and potential dream matches in a new interview. Ryder, who debuted in professional wrestling last year alongside his brother London Lightning, expressed a strong desire to challenge for the IWTV tag team titles again.

When asked about his ideal future opponents, Ryder highlighted a specific tag team rivalry he hopes to revisit.

> "Oh wow. Well, with the Mystery Wrestling Talent Visa, we now have so much opportunity presented in front of us, and I am very excited for that. NWA, like I said, we used to do the territories there, Crockett Cups and stuff. We pretended to wrestle out and it would always start the same way. We would always be play wrestling and then he'd give me a stiff one and then I would give him a stiff one, and then by the end of it, you know, someone's nose was bleeding. We were fighting each other. It was a full blown all out at that point. But let's see some people I'd like to face. I know I'd love to face Alec and Jordan Oliver again. We wrestled them once to see four and a six man. But I'd love to do that again, wrestle for the IWTV titles again. That'd be great, and we're going to need to coexist after the match because London and I, we got a lot of tag team bookings coming up after this. So let's hope everything is okay after we knock each other's heads a bit."

Ryder also noted that he balances his competitive drive with the need to maintain a professional relationship with his brother, as they have numerous tag team bookings scheduled for the future.
