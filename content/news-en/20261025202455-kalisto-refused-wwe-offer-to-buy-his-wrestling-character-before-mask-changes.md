Kalisto chose to protect his wrestling persona rather than hand over complete control when signing with WWE. The former United States Champion revealed that WWE offered to purchase the rights to his Samuray Del Sol ring name and mask design, but he rejected the proposal to maintain ownership of his identity. He explained during an interview with David Otunga that he took inspiration from Sin Cara's past mask disputes to ensure he retained the freedom to use his character outside the promotion.

> "When I first came in, they wanted to buy the Samurai soul name and design. I said no. I mean, I was flattered. I'm like, man, that's pretty cool, but uh God forbid if anything, I want to go back to the same, you know, I want to still use this all this stuff."

Despite keeping his intellectual property, Kalisto accepted changes mandated directly by Vince McMahon. The former WWE chairman insisted on exposing Kalisto's eyes and mouth, departing from the traditional full-face luchador mask. Kalisto noted that after years of competing with the altered gear, he grew so used to the open design that wrestling with full facial coverage now restricts his breathing.
