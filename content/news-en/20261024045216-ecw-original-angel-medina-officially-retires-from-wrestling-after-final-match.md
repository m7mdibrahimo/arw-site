Veteran wrestler Angel Medina, widely recognized as Angel of The Baldies, has officially retired following his final match on October 23, 2026. This concludes a career spanning over 25 years, which included a significant tenure during the most prominent era of ECW.

Medina competed in his farewell match at a FLEX Wrestling event held in Elmira, New York, where he faced his longtime colleague HC Loc. The encounter featured a nostalgic atmosphere as former teammate Tony DeVito served as the special guest referee, reuniting the trio to commemorate their shared history.

Trained by legend Johnny Rodz, Medina rose to prominence after joining ECW in 1999 as part of The Baldies alongside Tony DeVito, Vic Grimes, and Big Vito. Following the promotion's closure, he worked briefly before transitioning to a career in law enforcement in Kansas.
