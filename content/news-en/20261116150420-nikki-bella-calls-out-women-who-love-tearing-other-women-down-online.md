Nikki Bella recently opened up about the state of online discourse during an appearance on The Nikki & Brie Show with Not Skinny But Not Fat host Amanda Hirsch. Nikki discussed how gaining success in the public eye often brings a massive wave of opinions, drawing from her own experiences in the wrestling ring. Hirsch admitted that the internet feels noticeably harsher today, noting she frequently encounters posts on Threads where women viciously attack other women online. Nikki agreed, expressing her sadness that a large portion of the online vitriol comes from women targeting other women simply to feel better about themselves. Both discussed how the ease of going viral has amplified the negativity, with people saying outrageous things just to grab attention. Nikki and Hirsch both expressed hope that internet culture will eventually move past this phase and return to a more positive environment.
