Rusev has officially returned to the ring after spending nearly three months out of action due to an injury. He wrestled his first match back at a WWE live event in Honolulu, where he faced Joe Hendry in singles competition. Although Hendry secured the victory, the primary takeaway was Rusev's successful return to health following his lengthy recovery process. This match marked his first time competing since the July 27 episode of WWE RAW, where he suffered an awkward fall on his neck during a bout against Je'Von Evans. CJ Perry later clarified that Rusev was dealing with significant pain and sought immediate medical attention backstage. Perry subsequently confirmed that Rusev received medical clearance in September and spent time training at the WWE Performance Center and Natalya's Dungeon 2.0 to prepare for his eventual return.
