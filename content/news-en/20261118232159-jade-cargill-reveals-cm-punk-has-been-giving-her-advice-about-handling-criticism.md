Jade Cargill discussed the many opinions she faces in professional wrestling and explained that she has learned not to let those comments dictate her career. During an appearance on The Pin Down with Sports Illustrated's Kent Brown, Cargill was asked who gave her the best advice in her career. She noted that while many legends have tried to help her, CM Punk's guidance stands out the most.

Punk encouraged her to stop worrying about how other people view her and to focus instead on her own abilities and what she brings to the ring. Cargill added that negative comments and criticism give her a greater motivation to succeed and prove doubters wrong, explaining that she enjoys when someone tells her she cannot do something because it drives her to prove them wrong with full confidence.
