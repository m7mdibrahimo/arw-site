Penta recently opened up about the intense backstage chaos he experienced before his World Heavyweight Championship match against Roman Reigns in Mexico City. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, the Money in the Bank winner explained that dealing with his first-ever match against Reigns, his family's presence, and a delayed horse entrance created an overwhelming amount of stress. Despite the frantic preparation and the chaotic moments leading up to the bell, Penta expressed deep gratitude for the opportunity and praised Reigns for his incredible in-ring psychology and control.
