During a recent interview with The Ringer, WWE NXT superstar Wren Sinclair discussed her favorite wrestlers inside and outside of the company. Sinclair named Penta as her favorite within WWE, but revealed a surprising pick from the outside world. "If we go into Outer Space, I secretly love Thekla. That woman has made a fan out of me. Oh my gosh. She really won me over on the mic. She's just crazy. You never know what she's going to say. And she's so different than what you see. She's so raw and I think that's what drew me to her. I think she's great," Sinclair said. Sinclair specifically pointed to a promo where Thekla initially claimed she hadn't been fired from STARDOM before admitting that she actually had been dismissed, which fully won her over.
