Stephanie Vaquer has officially returned to the ring following a period of absence due to an ankle injury. The wrestler was previously scheduled to face Becky Lynch on Monday Night Raw to determine a contender for the WWE Women's World Championship, but she was pulled from the event because she had not fully recovered.

Vaquer made her return at a WWE house show in Portland, Oregon. In her absence, Becky Lynch faced Liv Morgan for the title, winning by disqualification, though Morgan retained the championship.
