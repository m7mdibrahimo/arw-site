Jake Roberts has cleared up confusion regarding his status with WWE, explaining that he is not actually under contract with the company. Just days after stating that he had re-signed with WWE, the Hall of Famer clarified that his relationship with the promotion is limited to occasional appearances, such as signing autographs and working on Topps trading cards. Roberts made the original comments on October 31 following the conclusion of his six-year run with AEW.

In addition to addressing his WWE status, Roberts provided a serious health update, confirming that he is currently using oxygen due to emphysema and COPD. He acknowledged that smoking for roughly 50 years contributed to his condition, but shared that he quit five weeks ago and remains committed to staying smoke-free. Roberts revealed that he hopes to undergo a medical procedure that could help him get off oxygen, provided he remains smoke-free for six months.
