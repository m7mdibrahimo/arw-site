Steven Borden is back in the mix at All Elite Wrestling after being sidelined since the 2026 All Out pay-per-view. He is scheduled to compete in the men's Blood & Guts match on the November 4 episode of AEW Dynamite. Borden and AEW TNT Champion Darby Allin appeared on First Coast News to promote the show, where Borden opened up about the concussion he suffered at All Out.

Reflecting on the injury, Borden said,

> "It feels great to be back. So I got concussed, and I was knocked out for a little bit." Allin interjected,

> "A little bit!? You were snoring for six minutes straight. I was there in the ring. We had to stop the match live on pay-per-view. Stop the match and he’s out there snoring and I was like, ‘Steven!?’ It wasn’t a little bit." Borden continued,

> "It’s weird though. With injuries, you’re sidelined. Normally, you can feel it. You tore something in your knee or whatever, and so you know as much as you wanna get back in the action, you can’t. But for me, I felt fine. So it’s just this weird thing of like, I wanted to be back out there but I couldn’t, and now that I’m back, it feels pretty dang good."

The men's Blood & Guts match on AEW Dynamite will feature Will Ospreay, Adam Copeland, Darby Allin, Steven Borden, Matt Jackson, and Nick Jackson taking on The Don Callis Family, represented by Kazuchika Okada, Kyle Fletcher, Wardlow, Kevin Knight, Mark Davis, and Jake Doyle. Meanwhile, the women's Blood & Guts match will pit Julia Hart, Skye Blue, Thekla, Megan Bayne, and Lena Kross against Willow Nightingale, Harley Cameron, Kris Statlander, Alex Windsor, and Jamie Hayter.
