Rob Van Dam and Katie Forbes have been together for years, but things were not always smooth between them. RVD admitted that early in their relationship, constant arguments made him consider walking away entirely. During an episode of One of a Kind with RVD, the couple discussed the adjustments they had to make after coming from previous relationships. RVD explained that their past experiences created different expectations that needed to be updated.

A major issue was their contrasting communication styles. Forbes noted that she would get heated quickly and move on just as fast, whereas RVD rarely got angry but stayed upset much longer once he reached that point. Because of a past unhealthy relationship, RVD had no desire to stay in any situation that could harm his mental health, which made even small arguments feel overwhelming at first. However, the good moments heavily outweighed the rough patches, and they eventually learned how to resolve their differences.

Forbes revealed that these arguments mostly happened during their first two years together, and such conflicts are now extremely rare, happening less than once a year. By understanding each other's communication methods, they successfully bridged the gap and left those early struggles behind.
