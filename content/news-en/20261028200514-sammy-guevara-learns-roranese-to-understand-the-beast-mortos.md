Sammy Guevara confirmed that he is now able to speak Roranese and understands what his teammate The Beast Mortos is saying, according to his latest vlog. Guevara, RUSH, and The Beast Mortos have been aligned for a year, with Guevara joining LFI in August before Death Before Dishonor. While The Beast Mortos typically communicates through roars that others find unintelligible, Guevara has found a way to interpret them and translated for RUSH during the episode.

> "My friend, don't you speak Roranese? Roranese, I've been studying it, so it might be a little off, but he said he's so happy we're finally back together!"

Guevara and his wife, Tay Melo, recently made headlines after their daughter was lost and subsequently found.
