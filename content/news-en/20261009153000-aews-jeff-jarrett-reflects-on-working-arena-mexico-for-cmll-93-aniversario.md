AEW star Jeff Jarrett recently reflected on his experience wrestling at the iconic Arena Mexico during CMLL's 93 Aniversario event on September 18, where he faced Lucha Libre legend Blue Panther on his 66th birthday. Speaking on his My World podcast, Jarrett admitted he never expected to perform in such a major venue against such a beloved name, calling the experience magical and describing the event as Mexico's equivalent to WrestleMania. Jarrett, who is celebrating his 40th year in professional wrestling, expressed deep gratitude for the opportunity to finally step foot in the historic arena after coming to terms with the idea that it would never happen.
