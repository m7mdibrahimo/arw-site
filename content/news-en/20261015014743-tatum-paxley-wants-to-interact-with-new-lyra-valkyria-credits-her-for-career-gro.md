Tatum Paxley recently reflected on her close on-screen partnership with Lyra Valkyria during their time in WWE NXT. While appearing on Off The Ropes, Paxley expressed a strong desire to interact with the new version of Valkyria currently featured on WWE RAW.

> "I knew she had it in her. That’s exactly why it was her all along. That’s why she had my heart. I just knew that she had this little quirk to her. I just knew it, okay? I love every second of it. I would love to have a long playdate with this Lyra Valkyria and get to know her."

Paxley also recalled the emotional moment when Valkyria returned to NXT to assist her, noting that she owes a significant portion of her career to that storyline.

> "When they told me Lyra was coming back for that episode, I cried. Picturing the moment of her running out after the win really warmed my heart. I owe so much of my career and where I am right now to that storyline that I had with her."

Paxley is now competing on the main roster and currently holds the WWE Women's Tag Team Championship alongside Alexa Bliss.
