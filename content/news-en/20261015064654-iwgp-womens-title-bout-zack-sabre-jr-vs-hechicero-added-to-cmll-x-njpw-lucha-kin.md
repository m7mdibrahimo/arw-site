The IWGP Women's Championship will be defended at the CMLL x NJPW Lucha Kingdom event on November 16th at Arena México. During the October 14th episode of CMLL Informa, it was announced that Kira will challenge champion Syuri for the title. Syuri has held the championship for a year, successfully defending it five times.

Also added to the card is a non-title singles match between Zack Sabre Jr. and CMLL World Heavyweight Champion Hechicero. The updated lineup for Lucha Kingdom includes Místico vs. El Desperado, a three-way tag team match for the NJPW STRONG Openweight Tag Team Championship, and other matchups featuring various NJPW and CMLL stars.
