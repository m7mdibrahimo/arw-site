Drew McIntyre has opened up about his intense rivalry with CM Punk, confirming that the onscreen animosity between them is entirely genuine. Speaking on The Joe Vulpis Podcast, McIntyre reflected on their brutal Hell in a Cell match at WWE Bad Blood 2024, comparing its intensity to the historic encounter between The Undertaker and Shawn Michaels. He expressed immense pride in the bout, describing it as the ultimate representation of his character work. "That's the one that, if it's a new fan, and there's a build-up video prior to that kind of helps tell the story, that they'll see everything they need to see to be like, 'Wow, that's what professional wrestling can be like?' Because that is professional wrestling at its best, is McIntyre-Punk," he stated. McIntyre acknowledged that despite their mutual dislike, both men are ultimate professionals who understood how to work together, likening their masterpiece feud to a perfect piece of art.
