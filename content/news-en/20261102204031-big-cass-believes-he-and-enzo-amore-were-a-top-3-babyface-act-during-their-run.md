Big Cass reflects on his and Enzo Amore's time together in WWE, noting that their massive popularity as a babyface act makes his current singles run as a heel much harder. Cass returned to WWE earlier this year alone, surprising fans who expected a reunion with Amore. While speaking to The Sal Licata Show, Cass explained that coming back as a heel is difficult because the audience still remembers him from one of the most popular babyface teams in the company, estimating they were a top-three act at the time. He noted that fans often shout old catchphrases at him to get under his skin. Furthermore, he addressed the initial rumors that circulated online when his signing was announced, which falsely suggested that he and Amore would immediately reunite on television. Despite the initial confusion, Cass feels he is doing a solid job generating heat by getting booed out of arenas.
