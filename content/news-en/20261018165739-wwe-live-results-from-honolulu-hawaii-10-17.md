WWE held a live event on October 17 at the Neal S. Blaisdell Arena in Honolulu, Hawaii. Nia Jax defeated Roxanne Perez. Dragon Lee defeated Big Cass. In a street fight, Bayley defeated Lyra Valkyria. Joe Hendry defeated JD McDonagh. The Usos and Je'Von Evans defeated 946, consisting of Royce Keys, Bronco Nima and Lucien Price, with Jaida Parker in their corner. Chad Gable and Rey Fenix defeated Ethan Page and Rusev. In a four-way match for the WWE Women's World Championship, Liv Morgan successfully defended her title against Stephanie Vaquer, Iyo Sky and Sol Ruca. In the main event, Oba Femi, LA Knight and Penta defeated The Vision, featuring Bronson Reed, Bron Breakker and Austin Theory, with Maxxine Dupri in their corner.
