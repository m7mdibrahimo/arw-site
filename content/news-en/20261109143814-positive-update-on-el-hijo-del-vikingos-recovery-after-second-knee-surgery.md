El Hijo del Vikingo shared a positive update on his ongoing recovery from a torn ACL and two knee surgeries. The AAA star posted a video on Instagram showing his intensive physical therapy sessions, which included weighted hip bridges, cycling, and resistance-band walks. Vikingo accompanied the footage with an encouraging caption about his progress, writing, "There is less and less time to go." This comes just a month after his second knee operation on October 6, following which he confirmed the procedure was successful. Before the second surgery, Vikingo had estimated he was about seven months away from returning to the ring, though no new return date has been announced.
