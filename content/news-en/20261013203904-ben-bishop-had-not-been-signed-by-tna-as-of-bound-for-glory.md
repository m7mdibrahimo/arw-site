Ben Bishop made his debut for TNA Wrestling during the Call Your Shot Gauntlet at Bound for Glory. During the match, Bishop shoved Jada Stone out of the way to take her spot, but Stone eventually returned to the bout and eliminated him. According to Fightful, Bishop had not been signed by TNA as of the day of the event, though he did make a positive impression on those within the company. The 2026 Call Your Shot Gauntlet was ultimately won by Frankie Kazarian, who has now won the match for three consecutive years. Kazarian announced that he intends to use his opportunity to challenge for the TNA World Tag Team Championship alongside Avery Styles.
