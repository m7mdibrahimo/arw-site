Royce Keys has discussed his career goals and how he hopes fans and peers will remember him, stating that he aims to be known for terrifying his rivals inside the squared circle.

Since debuting at Royal Rumble earlier in the year, Keys has established himself as a key member of the main roster in WWE. Although he missed a brief period to prepare for the arrival of The 946, the stable has quickly emerged as one of the most perilous units on the roster. The faction immediately targeted The Bloodline and their associates upon arrival, engaging in an ongoing feud with the group led by Roman Reigns.

During a recent interview with Soundsphere Magazine, Keys addressed his desired impact on the industry. 

> "Man, that's a good question. Probably, I think when it's all said and done, just being one of the most, feared, you know, just when someone's standing across the ring from me, you know, the first thing that comes to mind is fear."

He additionally hinted that the group might make appearances on shows beyond Monday Night RAW.
