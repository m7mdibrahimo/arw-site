Penta showcased his new custom briefcase on WWE Raw following his major victory at Money in the Bank. During the October 12 episode, he revealed a red-and-black case featuring his logo, the phrase 'Dinero En El Banco', and three pyro sparklers on each side.

After presenting the briefcase, Penta soaked in the crowd's reaction. He thanked his past opponents and noted that he has spent the last 20 years fighting for such opportunities.
