Bret Hart has achieved incredible success in professional wrestling, but he would gladly relive one specific day of his career. Speaking on the Johnny I Pro Show, the WWE Hall of Famer shared that winning his first world championship remains the most significant moment of his storied career.

Hart defeated Ric Flair on October 12, 1992, in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, to capture the WWF Championship. He noted that he never saw the victory coming after spending years trying to prove himself in the locker room, and he remained terrified that Vince McMahon might change his mind before the show.

His anxiety was so intense that he refused to call his family to share the news out of fear of jinxing the opportunity. McMahon ultimately stuck with the plan, allowing Hart to walk out as champion and culminate a lifetime of pressure and dedication as the son of Stu Hart.
