Karl Fredericks is looking forward to his upcoming singles match against Gabe Kidd at PRODUCE Vol. 6, which is co-produced by The Dogs. Speaking on the In The Weeds podcast with Fightful Overbooked, Fredericks stated that this encounter is the most significant of his career since his departure from New Japan in 2022.

"The most important fight I would say since I left New Japan in 2022, this is the most important fight coming up with Gabe Kidd," Fredericks said. "It's gonna tell you exactly what the future looks like for me. There's a lot of pressure, there's a lot of weight with certain things."

Previously, Kidd and Fredericks shared the ring at a West Coast Pro event in November 2025. PRODUCE Wrestling is set to return with its Season 2 premiere titled SKODEN on October 12 in Brooklyn, New York, co-produced by Fredericks to mark Indigenous People’s Day.
