Michin and B-Fab have reacted to winning the WWE Women's Tag Team Championship. During the October 23 episode of WWE SmackDown, Michin and B-Fab defeated Tatum Paxley and Alexa Bliss to capture the titles. Following their victory, both new champions shared their thoughts and emotions on social media.

Michin posted a heartfelt message on X, expressing gratitude for achieving this milestone in her hometown with her family watching from the stands. She also thanked her brother for introducing her to wrestling during their childhood and inspiring her dream. B-Fab reflected on her journey, emphasizing the importance of self-belief and perseverance through difficult times. This title win marks the first championship victory for both Michin and B-Fab in WWE, ending Bliss and Paxley's 14-day reign with the belts.
