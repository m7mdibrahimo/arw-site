UFC star Michael Chandler has teased a potential appearance on the upcoming episode of WWE NXT, which is scheduled for November 4 and will air against AEW Blood & Guts. Chandler reached out to NXT General Manager Robert Stone on social media, offering to assist with maintaining order during the Unsanctioned Match between Dion Lennox and Cruz Montana.

Stone quickly replied to the offer on his account:

> "Your making this too easy for me @MikeChandlerMMA"

The November 4 edition of WWE NXT features the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic final between Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno vs. Hank and Tank, with Cody Rhodes presenting the trophy. John Cena is also scheduled to appear on the show.
