Tony D'Angelo is set to compete for the NXT Championship after securing a victory on the October 13th episode of WWE NXT on The CW. D'Angelo defeated Myles Borne in a qualifying match to earn his title opportunity. During the contest, Borne was distracted by a cameraman on the ring apron, which allowed D'Angelo to gain the upper hand and win the match. Following the conclusion of the bout, it was revealed that the cameraman was Tavion Heights, who attacked Borne and powerbombed him through the announce desk. This incident served as retribution for Borne's previous attack on Heights in September. D'Angelo will now face Grayson Waller for the NXT Championship at the NXT Halloween Havoc event on October 31st. The updated card for NXT Halloween Havoc is as follows: NXT Women's Championship: Kelani Jordan (c) vs. Wren Sinclair; NXT Championship: Grayson Waller (c) vs. Tony D'Angelo.
