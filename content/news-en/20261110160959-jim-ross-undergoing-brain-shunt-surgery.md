WWE Hall of Famer Jim Ross is back in the hospital for another procedure after dealing with a series of recent health issues. On Tuesday, November 10, Conrad Thompson revealed on X that Ross is undergoing shunt surgery, asking fans to send him support and positive messages during his recovery.

> "Good ole JR is having shunt surgery this morning. Let’s all pray for his speedy recovery! 🙏 Jim deserves a break."

Thompson also encouraged fans to fill Ross's timeline with support so it is waiting for him when he leaves the operating room.

> "@JRsBBQ is tagged here so send some positive vibes his way so when he’s in recovery, his timeline is flooded with positive. ♥️"

The procedure follows Ross revealing in October that doctors had turned off his brain shunt after a CAT scan showed bleeding. Shutting off the device caused new problems with his balance and walking, though the shunt had previously improved those abilities. Ross explained that his oncologist reviewed the scans and confirmed the bleeding led to the decision. Despite dealing with severe headaches and additional tests after doctors found a spot on his kidney, Ross has remained optimistic about his recovery.
