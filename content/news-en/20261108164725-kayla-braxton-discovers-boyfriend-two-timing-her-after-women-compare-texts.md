Kayla Braxton recently shared a shocking dating story during an episode of Milk and Honeys, revealing that she discovered her boyfriend was seeing another woman behind her back. Braxton explained that she had been dating the man in Los Angeles for a short time after meeting him through the local nightlife scene. Despite recognizing the typical red flags, she grew to genuinely like him, introduced him to her friends, and even discussed him with their families. However, while visiting his workplace a few days later, Braxton noticed another woman sitting down the bar. After briefly crossing paths outside, the two women started talking and realized they were both dating the same man. They decided to compare messages and discovered he had been sending them identical romantic texts, songs, and deep conversations. When Braxton confronted him, he broke down in tears and insisted she was the only woman, but she ultimately cut ties with him. To make matters worse, the other woman called Braxton just ten minutes later to report that he had already contacted her as if nothing happened. Braxton expressed that her biggest frustration was feeling made to look foolish after initially trying to keep things casual before he pushed for a more serious relationship.
