During an appearance on TMZSports' Inside the Ring, WWE star Joe Hendry discussed his favorite wrestling entrance themes from the past and present. When asked about his all-time favorites, Hendry highlighted The Rock's Hollywood theme, Steve Austin's glass smash, and Motorhead's 'The Game.' Turning to the current roster, Hendry addressed the modern WWE themes produced by Def Rebel and praised Trick Williams' theme for its strong crowd interaction.

> "Okay, so [my] favorite theme of all time is The Rock's Hollywood theme," Hendry said. "That just, like, as a fan, it gave me goosebumps watching that. I think some really iconic ones? Obviously, Steve Austin's glass smash, and 'The Game' by Motorhead is iconic as well."

> "On the current roster, there are some great songs," Hendry said. "I think ultimately songs that are about crowd interaction [work best], so I think they did a really great job with Trick's song. There's so many good ones."
