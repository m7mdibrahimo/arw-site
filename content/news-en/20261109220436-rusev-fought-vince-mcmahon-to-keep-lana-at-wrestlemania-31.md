CJ Perry, better known as Lana, revealed that her appearance at WrestleMania 31 almost did not happen until Rusev intervened with Vince McMahon, according to Ringside News. Perry stated that her status remained uncertain until five days before the event, despite her role supporting Rusev throughout the year in his United States Championship feud against John Cena.

Perry explained that McMahon offered her a WWE Studios film alongside Edge, which left her worried she would lose her spot at WrestleMania. She believes McMahon wanted to reduce her screen time to focus more heat on Rusev as the primary heel before his showdown with Cena.

Despite the uncertainty, Rusev approached McMahon and insisted that she belonged on the show after building the character all year. McMahon agreed, allowing Perry to accompany Rusev during his memorable military tank entrance at WrestleMania 31.
