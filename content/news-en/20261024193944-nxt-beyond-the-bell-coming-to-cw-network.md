A new interview series called NXT Beyond The Bell is coming to the CW Network, according to Fightful. Vic Joseph announced that he and Blake Howard will host the program, though a premiere date was not revealed.

Joseph shared his excitement on social media about bringing in-depth stories on WWE NXT superstars to fans. He also posted a photo of himself doing a sit-down interview with Shiloh Hill.

https://www.instagram.com/p/De4fS_wlNNF/

Joseph serves as the lead play-by-play announcer for WWE NXT alongside Booker T. WWE NXT airs every Tuesday on the CW Network, and Fightful will share further updates as they become available.
