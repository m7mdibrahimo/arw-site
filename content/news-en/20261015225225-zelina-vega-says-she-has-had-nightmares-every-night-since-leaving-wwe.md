Zelina Vega says that leaving WWE has been a difficult emotional process, revealing that she has dealt with recurring nightmares since her departure. Speaking to Ring the Belle, Vega discussed the cinematic video she released after leaving the company, which symbolized her closing a chapter of her life. She explained that the move was not just a career change but an identity shift connected to her decade-long tenure.

> "I’ll be honest, I’ve had nightmares about leaving WWE every single night. Like it’s, it’s trash. Like it is."

Vega noted that her time in WWE was deeply intertwined with her personal life, including memories of her late father and her childhood wrestling dreams. She explained that she wanted to confront these past versions of herself in her new "Xelina" identity rather than ignoring them. By accepting what her time in WWE meant to her, Vega hopes to finally process her experiences and move forward with her career.
