TKO Group Holdings released its financial report for the third quarter of 2026, announcing revenue of $1.360 billion and a net income of $277.9 million. UFC revenue grew by $194.0 million to reach $519.2 million, while WWE revenue saw a slight decline of $2.8 million to $399.3 million.

Mark Shapiro, President and COO of TKO, noted that the company's assets are positioned at the center of the premium sports ecosystem. TKO also raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to between $5.790 billion and $5.830 billion. The company reported adjusted EBITDA of $555.7 million for the quarter, marking a 54% increase compared to the previous year.
