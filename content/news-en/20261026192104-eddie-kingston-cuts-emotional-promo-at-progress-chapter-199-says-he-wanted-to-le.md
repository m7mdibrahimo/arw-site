Eddie Kingston delivered an emotional promo at PROGRESS Chapter 199 following his match against Luke Jacobs, reflecting on the recent passings of Andy Williams, known as The Butcher, and Benjamin Satterley, known as PAC. Kingston shared how deeply the losses affected him, stating that he stayed home for five days and considered leaving the wrestling business entirely. He expressed his gratitude to the fans for their supportive messages about mental health and personal struggles, noting that their words helped him through the dark period. Kingston previously participated in an AEW tribute show honoring The Butcher.
