Sting's sons Steven and Garrett Borden made their debut as a tag team on November 8, 2026, defeating The 8th Day, made up of Timur The Great and Oday Al-Saleh, at Rhodes Wrestling Association's Fall Fury 2026 in Cedar Park, Texas.

The feud began in September after The 8th Day attacked Garrett following a singles victory. Steven ran down to help his brother, leading the duo to issue a challenge that Dustin Rhodes booked for the promotion's second-anniversary event. Steven is under contract with AEW and previously wrestled on Dynamite, while Garrett continues to train under Dustin Rhodes.
