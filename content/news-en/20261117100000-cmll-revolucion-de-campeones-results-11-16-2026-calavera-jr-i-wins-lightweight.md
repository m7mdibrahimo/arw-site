CMLL held "Revolución de Campeones" on November 16, 2026, at Arena Puebla in Puebla, Mexico. The event featured several championship bouts.

In the opening contest, CMLL Arena Puebla Openweight Champion Prayer successfully defended his title against Millenium. Following that, CMLL Arena Puebla Tag Team Champions Meyer and Tiger Boy retained their belts against the team of Aguila Egipcia and Multy.

The Copa Mujeres Revolucionarias Torneo Cibernético saw Princesa Sugehit emerge victorious over a field that included Astoreth, Diablita Roja, Garra Negra, India Sioux, Lady Metal, Lluvia, Marcela, Persephone, and Tabata.

In title changes, Calavera Jr. I defeated Stigma to win the CMLL World Lightweight Championship. In the main event, Volador Jr. successfully defended the CMLL World Historic Middleweight Championship against Villano III Jr.
