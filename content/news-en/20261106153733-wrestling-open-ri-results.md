Beyond Wrestling presented its Wrestling Open RI show on Monday, November 2, broadcasting live on IWTV from Rhodes On The Pawtuxet located in Cranston, Rhode Island.

Bobby Orlando def. DJ Powers
Anthony Vecchio def. Erik ChaCha
Bobby Casale def. Brett Ryan Gosselin
Dustin Waller def. Jermaine Marbury

Following action in the ring, Bryce Donovan confronted Bear Bronson and struck him with a low blow. Dustin Waller attempted to cash in his championship opportunity, but Bobby Orlando intervened to make the save.

Liviyah def. Paris Van Dale, Little Mean Kathleen, Ruthie Jay, Shannon LeVangie, and Nessa Ferrari in a six-woman rumble main event.
