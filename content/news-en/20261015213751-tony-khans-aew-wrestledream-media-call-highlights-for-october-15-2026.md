AEW President Tony Khan held a media call on October 15, 2026, to discuss the upcoming AEW WrestleDream pay-per-view. During the session, Khan addressed AEW's future under Skydance and David Ellison, expressing optimism about the new leadership, though he declined to discuss specific operational plans. He also confirmed that Channing Dungey and JB Perrette remain with the company.

Regarding the roster, Khan confirmed that he is aware of Adam Copeland’s plans to make 2027 his final year in professional wrestling, though he noted he does not know how far into the year Copeland will continue. Khan also addressed the current status of Christian Cage and Jamie Hayter, both of whom are currently sidelined with injuries. He praised Christian Cage and the late Ben “Pac” Satterley for their contributions to the company.

Khan discussed the status of the TBS Championship division, noting that Maya World is currently dealing with an injury while mourning the death of her brother. Khan stated that these circumstances, alongside Pac’s death, required several adjustments to the show’s plans. He expressed confidence in the upcoming four-way match and praised the locker room for their resilience. Additionally, Khan provided a positive update on Steven Borden and highlighted opportunities for talents like Hyan and Dani Luna.
