UFC Fight Night: Moicano vs. Nolan took place on October 31 from the Meta APEX in Las Vegas, NV. The event aired on Paramount+. Full results are below.

Women's Bantamweight: Lucia Szabova def. Tainara Lisboa by TKO (punches) at 3:20 of Round 3

Women's Strawweight: Piera Rodriguez def. Talita Alencar by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 30-27)

Middleweight: Joseph Kropschot def. Nick Klein by submission (armbar) at 1:25 of Round 2

Middleweight: Zachary Reese def. Mickey Gall by unanimous decision (29-27, 29-27, 29-27)

Featherweight: Julian Erosa def. JeongYeong Lee by knockout (punches) at 1:31 of Round 1

Welterweight: Rodrigo Sezinando def. Theodor Berggren by TKO (cut) at 5:00 of Round 2

Featherweight: Dakota Hope def. Francis Marshall by split decision (28-29, 29-28, 29-28)

Welterweight: Jean-Paul Lebosnoyani def. Farman Hasanov by TKO (cut) at 2:11 of Round 3

Women's Bantamweight: Luana Santos def. Yana Santos by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28)

Flyweight: Cody Durden def. Ode Osbourne by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28)
