Mike Bailey recently addressed whether he would ever sell foot content following a viral moment from a recent episode of AEW Collision featuring Gabe Kidd. During an interview, Sean Ross Sapp showed Bailey a photo of Kidd biting his toes during their match. Bailey responded by explaining that he has no intention of entering the foot content market, noting that his feet are already visible on national television every week for free. He added that making money from such content would require specific actions that he is not willing to perform. In the same interview, Bailey also discussed the upcoming Clockwork Carnage match.
