Heartbreaking details about PAC's final hours have emerged, showing how suddenly his life ended. His wife, Natalie Taylor, spoke with him less than three hours before his death and later called his phone while police were already at the scene. According to a new Cook County Medical Examiner report obtained by the New York Post, Taylor called PAC's phone on September 27 after he was found unresponsive in his rental car at a CAVA restaurant in Des Plaines, Illinois. Police answered the phone instead of PAC. Taylor told authorities she had spoken with her husband, Benjamin Satterley, less than three hours earlier. PAC had told her he was stopping for food before heading to O'Hare Airport. “Satterley told his wife that he was stopping at the restaurant CAVA before heading to O’Hare Airport.” Taylor also informed investigators that PAC had been diagnosed with a medical condition and prescribed medication, though the report redacted the specific diagnosis. She noted he had “no history of illicit drug or alcohol abuse.” Toxicology tests were unremarkable, and officials ruled that PAC died from bronchial asthma, classifying the death as natural. The report noted that inhalers were found at the scene, meaning he had access to them during the emergency. This is notable because Dave Meltzer had previously questioned whether PAC had an inhaler nearby. “Unfortunately you know, usually, you know, you would have the inhaler with you, and I don’t know, you know, you know, it may have happened so quick. There was stuff in his car, in the rental car, but he may not have been able to. It may have happened so quick. He wasn’t able to get it.” PAC was found slumped in the driver's seat of his running rental car shortly before 4 p.m. A witness who called 911 reported, “I don’t see his chest moving at all.” PAC had wrestled Andrade at AEW All Out the previous night and was preparing to fly home, but that trip never occurred.
