Jack Cartwheel secured his spot in the next round of the AAA Cruiserweight Title Contenders Tournament according to Fightful, defeating Lince Dorado and Kento on the November 14 episode of AAA on FOX. The conclusion of the match featured Cartwheel executing Jack's Arrow on Dorado to secure the win, making him the initial wrestler to advance in the competition. The tournament features four three-way bouts across upcoming weeks, with the four victors advancing to a four-way clash at AAA Guerra de Titanes on December 19. The winner of that four-way encounter will then meet Fenix to contend for the AAA Cruiserweight Championship on January 2 at the AAA event in Acapulco.
