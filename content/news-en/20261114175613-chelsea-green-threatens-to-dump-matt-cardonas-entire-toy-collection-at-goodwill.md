WWE Superstar Chelsea Green is ready to clean house and get rid of her husband Matt Cardona's large toy collection, threatening to dump everything at a local charity store.

During an appearance on the Good Karma Wrestling podcast, Green discussed her husband's obsession with buying collectibles. She explained that she confronted him about his many purchases, but he managed to escape the argument because she had to leave for an acting class. Green revealed that she told her husband what she plans to do once the cameras stopped recording, confirming her plan to rent a moving truck on January 2 to take everything in their garage to the charity shop.

> "After the cameras stopped recording, I told him January second I'm going to hire a U-Haul and every single thing that is in my garage is going to the Goodwill. So January third if you're looking for some new toys, we're in Florida."

Cardona's love for wrestling figures and memorabilia is nothing new, and Green frequently pokes fun at this collection. However, her latest threat to clear out the items might put the star in a real bind given how attached he is to his belongings.
