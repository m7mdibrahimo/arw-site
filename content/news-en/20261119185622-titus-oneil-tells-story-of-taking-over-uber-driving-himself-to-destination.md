WWE Hall of Famer Titus O'Neil shared a unique travel story on X, revealing how he took over driving duties during a two-hour Uber ride. O'Neil ordered a ride from Jupiter to Tampa, but after his driver repeatedly hit road reflectors, he offered to take the wheel. The driver agreed and fell asleep in the passenger seat, allowing O'Neil to drive himself the rest of the 2.5-hour journey home. O'Neil jokingly updated his status on social media, writing that he gave himself five stars as an Uber driver and even called out Guinness World Records.
