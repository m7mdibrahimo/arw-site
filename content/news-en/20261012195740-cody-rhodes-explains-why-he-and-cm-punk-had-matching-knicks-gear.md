Cody Rhodes recently explained the reasoning behind his and CM Punk's matching Knicks gear during their tag team match at Saturday Night's Main Event earlier this year. The duo teamed up in July to face Sami Zayn and Gunther in a high-stakes match with SummerSlam implications, ultimately securing the victory while wearing coordinated outfits. During an appearance on Complex's Sneaker Shopping, Rhodes discussed the rare instance of coordination with Punk. 

> "Me and Punk did the Knicks colors, and they're very big. I don't ever like to match. That's a big thing when you team, it's like 'oh they're gonna match? Are they gonna put their music together?' Like, no, I want to be me and him be him. But that was one where I think we both thought, 'yeah, let's match.' I love the Knicks gear."

Additionally, Rhodes touched upon Tyrese Haliburton's relationship with WWE and a specific rule given when gifted sneakers.
