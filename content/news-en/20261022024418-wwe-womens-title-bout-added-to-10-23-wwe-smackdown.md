A WWE Women's Championship match has been added to the upcoming episode of WWE SmackDown. SmackDown general manager Nick Aldis announced that Chelsea Green will defend her title against Judgment Day member Roxanne Perez. Perez earned the opportunity after pinning Chelsea in a non-title bout on the October 12 episode of WWE RAW. The updated lineup for Friday's show includes Giulia vs. Blake Monroe in a Street Fight, Tatum Paxley and Alexa Bliss defending the WWE Women's Tag Team Championship against B-Fab and Michin, Bakusai consisting of Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki facing The MFT featuring Tama Tonga and Talla Tonga, alongside the championship bout between Chelsea Green and Roxanne Perez. Fightful will provide live coverage of the event starting at 8 PM ET on Friday, followed by a post-show podcast.
