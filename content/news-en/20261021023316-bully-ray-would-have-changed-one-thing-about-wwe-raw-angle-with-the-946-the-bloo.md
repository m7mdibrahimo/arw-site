During a recent episode of "Busted Open Radio," WWE Hall of Famer Bully Ray discussed the brawl between The Bloodline and the 946 on "WWE Raw." Ray stated that he would have kept the entire segment the same, except for the final moments.

> "946 beats down The Bloodline. Roman's entrance, the John Wayne walk, the four-on-four in the middle, the big schmoz, everything," Ray said, "and just when you think The Bloodline are going to get the upper hand, everybody has spilled out of the ring, Roman Reigns just hit a big Superman Punch on whoever, Carmelo Hayes in the back door with that tire iron he was carrying to the ring. Crack Roman right in the back of the head ... and he goes down."

Ray explained that Carmelo Hayes should have struck Roman Reigns with a crowbar instead of targeting Jimmy Uso, Jey Uso, and Solo Sikoa. He added that his preferred scenario would allow the 946 to continue the attack on Roman Reigns before leaving the scene.
