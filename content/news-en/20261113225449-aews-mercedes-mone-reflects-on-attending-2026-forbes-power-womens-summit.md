AEW Women's World Champion Mercedes Moné shared her thoughts on attending the 2026 Forbes Power Women's Summit in New York City, describing the experience as incredible. Moné attended the event with her championship belt, aiming to showcase the value and global reach of women's professional wrestling alongside leaders from various industries.

Writing in her newsletter, Moné noted that she walked into the summit representing an entire division of women who deserve recognition on that level. She discussed negotiating, her role as the CEO of wrestling, and her current title reign with entrepreneur Haley Sacks. Moné highlighted the progress women's wrestling has made over the years, moving from fighting for basic screen time to headlining major events and building global brands.

Looking ahead, Moné expressed her desire to see future generations of female wrestlers occupy spaces among the world's most influential women. On the ring side, Moné is set to defend her AEW Women's World Championship against Marina Shafir at AEW Full Gear on November 14 in Phoenix.
