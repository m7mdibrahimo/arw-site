Drew McIntyre recently paid tribute to the late PAC, praising him as an amazing talent and person. During an interview with Dan Soder, McIntyre reflected on the passing of Benjamin Satterley at the age of forty. McIntyre shared that he first met Satterley in 2004 as a teenager in Carlisle, England, and noted that he remained the exact same genuine person right until the end. He also emphasized the importance of perspective and gratitude in life.
