WWE NXT wrestler Izzi Dame praised coach and wrestler Shawn Spears for his major role in her career, stating that he completely shifted her path. Speaking on Busted Open, Dame explained that Spears spoke to her in a way that made wrestling concepts and psychology clear and logical to her.

Dame discussed the short period she spent under his guidance before their split months ago, noting that she gained a lot from his deep experience and understanding of backstage details and ring secrets. Dame and Niko Vance kicked Spears out of The Culling in June 2026.
