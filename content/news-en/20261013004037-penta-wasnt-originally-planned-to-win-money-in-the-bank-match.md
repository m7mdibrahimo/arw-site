Penta's victory in the Money in the Bank ladder match reportedly caught many people within WWE by surprise. According to recent comments from JoeyVotes and TC of WrestleVotes, he was not expected to be in that position just one month before the show.

JoeyVotes explained that internal plans shifted significantly following Penta's performance against Roman Reigns during the Mexico City edition of WWE RAW, which caused his standing to improve rapidly. "A lot of plans changed post-Mexico City. He was the creative favorite. Those within thought Penta was going to win that match. Three weeks later, he ended up winning it. So he did a lot of good for himself at Mexico City. Don't know if that's gonna translate into him winning the title eventually, but he wasn't supposed to be in this spot a month ago."

TC confirmed that the push for Penta materialized much faster than most anticipated. This standout performance appears to have been the decisive factor in elevating him to one of the company's top spots, serving as a reminder of how quickly creative directions can shift in WWE.
