Austin Theory is aware of the criticism surrounding The Vision and has no intention of letting the group break up. While some fans are calling for WWE to end the alliance, Theory is confident that those same detractors will eventually be the ones buying the group's merchandise. Speaking on NotSam Wrestling, Theory addressed the negative feedback directly. "I love that everybody says, 'Oh, this group should stop. This group should end.' I can't wait for the moment that we're standing on top of the world and everybody knows the story," Theory stated. He compared the current backlash to other trends that start out being hated before becoming popular. Theory joked that the people currently complaining will eventually want items like "a Logan Paul shirt, a Vision shirt, an Austin Theory shirt, gotta get a Bron Breakker shirt." He remains unfazed by the criticism, viewing it as a natural part of the process while WWE continues to build the group.
