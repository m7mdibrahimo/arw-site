Players can earn a luchador mask in the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6. As fans look for details ahead of the game's release, it has been noted that GTA 6 includes a pro wrestling element. If users defeat a character known as 'The Gentrifier,' they will receive a luchador mask that can be used to hide their identity when they are wanted by law enforcement. A social media post noted, "If you wrestle and beat The Gentrifier, you can get a luchador mask! (You can also use it to conceal your face when committing crimes) #gta6 #wrestle #wrestling #luchador #ign"
