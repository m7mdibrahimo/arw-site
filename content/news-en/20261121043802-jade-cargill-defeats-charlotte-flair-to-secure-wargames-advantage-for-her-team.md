Jade Cargill defeated Charlotte Flair on the November 20 episode of WWE SmackDown to secure the WarGames advantage for her team. Cargill and Flair faced off in the advantage match, which ended with Cargill pinning Flair after sending her face-first into an exposed turnbuckle. 

At WWE Survivor Series, the Women's WarGames match will feature Charlotte Flair, Alexa Bliss, Tatum Paxley, Bayley, and Stephanie Vaquer against the team of Jade Cargill, Michin, and Fatal Influence (Jacy Jayne, Fallon Henley, and Lainey Reid). With this victory, Cargill's team earns the right to send its second wrestler into the match first.
