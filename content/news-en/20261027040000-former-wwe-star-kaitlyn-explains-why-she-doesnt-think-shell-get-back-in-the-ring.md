Retirement in professional wrestling is often temporary, as many performers eventually decide to return to the ring. However, former WWE Divas Champion Kaitlyn remains committed to her decision to stay away. In a recent interview with "Insight With Chris Van Vliet," Kaitlyn explained that she has no desire to compete again. "I hope no one hates me for this, but I don't really want to get back in the ring," she said. "I respect wrestling a lot, and I find it very entertaining. I love the artistry of it." She noted that her time as a wrestler gave her a deep appreciation for the performance, psychology, and storytelling involved in the sport, but she is satisfied with her career. Kaitlyn, whose real name is Celeste Bonin, last wrestled at a charity event in 2019. While she was invited to participate in the 2022 WWE Women's Royal Rumble, she did not end up appearing. Her initial WWE run lasted from 2010 to 2014, followed by a brief return for the 2018 Mae Young Classic.
