Tony Khan's recent meetings with Paramount Skydance may have left him optimistic, but a report suggests AEW could face challenges regarding its future. A former Warner Bros. Discovery executive reportedly believes Skydance's new leadership might not pick up AEW's option year if the promotion continues to offer only traditional wrestling programming. Blake Avignon shared that the former executive is concerned AEW has not sufficiently diversified its content. According to Avignon's source, "Their concern is that AEW hasn't diversified its content offering. If Tony Khan expects a renewal by simply delivering more of the same, he might be in for a rude awakening."

This perspective contrasts with recent reports. Dave Meltzer noted on October 25 that Khan was very pleased after his meetings with Skydance in Los Angeles. Khan himself recently confirmed he has had positive discussions with David Ellison about AEW's future. During the October 17 WrestleDream post-show press conference, Khan stated: "Yes, I’ve had a conversation with David Ellison about the future of AEW. I’ve had a few great conversations with David Ellison about AEW. He is very excited to be partnering with AEW."

There is currently no official announcement regarding AEW's option year, and it remains unclear what specific content changes Skydance might seek. For now, this reflects the perspective of one former WBD executive rather than a finalized decision.
