WWE has filed a new trademark with the United States Patent and Trademark Office. On November 4, the company filed paperwork to secure the rights to the term 'Marquis Journey'. According to the official filing, the trademark covers a wide variety of entertainment and media services, including professional wrestling exhibitions and live performances broadcast across television, radio, and the internet. It also encompasses the provision of wrestling news, information portals, fan club services, and online newsletters.
