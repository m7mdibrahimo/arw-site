John Cena has officially announced that the first-ever John Cena Classic tournament will take place on December 14 at Disney World. During his recent appearance on WWE RAW, Cena confirmed that Cody Rhodes and the newly signed Zilla Fatu will both compete in the tournament. Zilla Fatu shared his excitement about the invitation in a recent episode of his YouTube vlog, noting the significance of the moment. "The Main One has been invited to the John Cena Classic, man," Zilla said. "Disney World... Look how far we came? And we already brushed shoulders with John Cena, let alone invite us out to Disney World for the first John Cena Classic? It don't get better than this." Zilla also showcased a briefcase he received from Cena, speculating on the contents inside and revealing several items that relate to the tournament. Cena has stated in recent interviews that he will provide further details on the tournament's structure and his goals for the event soon.
