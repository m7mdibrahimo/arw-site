WWE NXT on October 13, 2026, featured the road to Halloween Havoc with Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic matches and Tony D'Angelo earning another NXT Title shot. On the negative side, Saquon Shugars surprisingly took a loss to Tristan Angels despite a backstage segment with Grayson Waller setting up a potential future storyline, Izzi Dame's obsession with Zaria felt confusing and poorly structured, and Tony D'Angelo securing another championship match against Grayson Waller felt repetitive and stalled the division. On the positive side, Mini Vikingo and Chazz "Starboy" Hall continued to shine in their underdog tag team run before falling to the Vanity Project, Romeo Moreno and Noam Dar pulled off a major upset against Fraxiom to advance in the tournament, and Kali Armstrong had a very solid showing against AAA Reina de Reinas Champion La Catalina.
