Chelsea Green has enjoyed several notable moments during her career, most notably winning the WWE Women's Championship in a ladder match at WWE SummerSlam. Throughout her 13-year tenure in the industry, she has participated in standout bouts across various promotions, including Lucha Underground, TNA, and at All In.

During a conversation with Cody Rhodes on his show, What Do You Want To Talk About?, Green was asked to identify her favorite match. She explained that her perspective has shifted since returning to the company.

> "Since I came back to WWE, none of those will ever be my favorite wrestling matches. I thought it would be the opposite. I thought I would be having all my best and most beloved wrestling matches in WWE, but I'm actually so much harder on myself in WWE. My standard has risen, and I haven't met my standard yet. Hopefully, I will. Hopefully the next time we talk I can say, 'That's the one.'"

Green recalled performing in cities like Pittsburgh, Milwaukee, and Texas, while highlighting her match against Penta in Lucha Underground as a career standout. The pair also discussed the All In event during the interview, according to Fightful.
