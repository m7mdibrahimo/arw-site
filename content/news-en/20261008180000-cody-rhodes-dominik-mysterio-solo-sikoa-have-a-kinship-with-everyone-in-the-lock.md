Cody Rhodes recently praised Dominik Mysterio and Solo Sikoa, highlighting their strong presence and leadership qualities behind the scenes in WWE. During an appearance on Flagrant, Rhodes discussed how both rising stars have connected deeply with the rest of the talent. “I make this joke all the time that Dom is the locker room leader. I make that joke about him and Solo Sikoa all the time, but I make it because they really are. They may not be the guys marching orders and saying, ‘Hey, pick up the locker room’ or ‘Hey, don't do that’, but Dom and Solo are in the locker room. They're feelingiting. They have a kinship with everyone,” Rhodes said. Additionally, Rhodes mentioned that he does not foresee himself winning the United States Title in the future.
