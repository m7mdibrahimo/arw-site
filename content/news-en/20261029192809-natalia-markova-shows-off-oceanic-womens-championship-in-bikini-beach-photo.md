Natalia Markova is getting ready to defend her title at the upcoming Jericho Cruise event, sharing a new beach photo to promote the appearance. She posted a picture on X wearing a blue and silver bikini while holding the Oceanic Women's Championship belt.

Markova confirmed in her post that she will put her title on the line when the Jericho Cruise returns next month, encouraging fans to book their spots.

> "Are you coming on the @jericho_cruise? I’ll be defending my Oceanic Women’s Championship! Book the cabin now! 🌟"

Markova has held the championship since February 2025, when she became the inaugural champion by winning a tournament during the cruise. She has not yet announced her challenger for the upcoming title defense.
