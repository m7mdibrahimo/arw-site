According to the latest update from Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, the cause of death for Benjamin Satterley, known as Pac in AEW and Neville in WWE, remains unknown as authorities await toxicology reports. The 40-year-old star was found dead in a rental car near Chicago's O'Hare International Airport on September 27, just a day after wrestling at AEW All Out 2026. Meltzer noted that the Cook County Medical Examiner's Office is conducting additional studies because the initial autopsy showed no signs of a natural death like a stroke or heart attack. Investigators also gathered details about his final hours, revealing that he had spoken to a woman on the phone to say he was heading to the airport but stopped for lunch at a restaurant after his flight to Newcastle was delayed. Tributes from across the wrestling world have poured in, including a dedicated episode of AEW Dynamite and a ten-bell salute from RevPro and Dragon Gate.
