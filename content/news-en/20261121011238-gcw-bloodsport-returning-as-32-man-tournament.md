Game Changer Wrestling is bringing back Bloodsport as a 32-man tournament starting in January in Chicago. GCW announced that "The Gauntlet for Glory" tournament will unfold over three different dates in three different cities, crowning the first-ever World of Bloodsport Champion, alongside an eight-woman tournament for the Women's World of Bloodsport Champion. GCW promoter Brett Lauderdale highlighted the promotion's combat sports history and stated, "No weight classes, no limitations, and no restrictions as to who can or can't compete. We aren't looking for wrestlers… we're looking for FIGHTERS." The tournament kicks off on Sunday, January 17, with fighters and further details to be released next week.
