Throughout his extensive career across multiple promotions, Samoa Joe has faced numerous formidable opponents. Recently returning to AEW and setting his sights on Will Ospreay's AEW World Championship, Joe participated in a bracket-style comparison on "SHAK Wrestling" to determine who hit him the hardest in the ring. Starting with a choice between Brock Lesnar and Kurt Angle, Joe acknowledged both men's remarkable techniques, noting, "Kurt, I think it's the battle of technique vs. raw, unbridled power." Ultimately, Lesnar advanced over Angle, Shinsuke Nakamura, Bryan Danielson, and Claudio Castagnoli, though Joe admitted he had to pause when comparing Lesnar to Castagnoli.

Lesnar also surpassed Nigel McGuinness, Necro Butcher, Minoru Suzuki, and Low Ki in Joe's evaluation. Explaining his reasoning for selecting Lesnar over Low Ki, Joe remarked, "We're going to go destructive potential, but extreme enthusiasm, and effort and a very, very close call, but we're going to stick with Brock." When finally compared to Kenta Kobashi, whom Joe faced later in Kobashi's career when he was slightly less explosive, Joe once again chose Lesnar, cementing "The Beast Incarnate" as the hardest-hitting opponent he has ever faced.
