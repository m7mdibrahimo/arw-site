Nixi XS has clarified a misconception regarding her background. After Grapsody's Righteous Reg released his annual BW500 list, which celebrates the top 500 black wrestlers in the world, Nixi XS found herself ranked at #176. However, Nixi is not black, a fact that surprised many of her fans and peers.

Nixi addressed the situation on social media, stating, "Huge thank you for including me in such great company! But to clarify, I’m not black. Just a pale bitch that’s really into Grace Jones. I’d hate to take a spot from a deserving black wrestler due to any confusion. I can’t believe I’m having to come out as white This is so humiliating, guys."

She has since responded to several individuals who were unaware of her actual heritage, confirming that this has been a long-standing belief held by some in the wrestling community. The matter is now resolved.
