TNA Wrestling star Harley Hudson recently shared that The Undertaker is her favorite wrestler of all time. Hudson, who signed with TNA in May 2025 and recently joined Taryn Terrell's The Dollhouse, discussed her inspirations in a new interview with Denise Salcedo. Hudson explained that she grew up watching The Undertaker and was always captivated by the spectacle of his WrestleMania matches. She noted, "My favorite wrestler ever is The Undertaker. I think the only similarities that we share is that I do the same leg drop on the apron that he does. Other than that, that's probably where it ends. But I think, in a way though, it's like someone so different, and I can just remember watching WrestleMania and just being blown away by the whole spectacle of every match that he'd have." Hudson recalled waking her mother up late at night to watch his matches during WrestleMania events. She expressed a desire to one day have a similar impact on fans, hoping to create moments that make people stop what they are doing to watch her perform.
