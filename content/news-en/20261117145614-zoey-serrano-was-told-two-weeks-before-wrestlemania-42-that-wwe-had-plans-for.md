Zoey Serrano, formerly known as Zoey Stark in WWE, recently spoke with Chris Van Vliet on Insight about her departure from the company and the events leading up to it. Serrano suffered a knee injury in May 2025 that kept her out of action for nearly a year. Although she was ready to return in early 2026, she was released by WWE on April 24, shortly after WrestleMania 42.

"So two weeks before Mania, and I’m being told, ‘[We’re] trying to get you started. ‘We have some [plans]. We’ve been talking about you. We have some game plans.’ I go to Mania, show face. Writers are telling me the same thing. Then the following week, I read online that cuts are happening, and just my gut, my heart sank, because something in me knew. I can’t explain what that feeling is, but I remember messaging Mia and Chelsea, ‘Pretty sure I’m getting released.’ Because they keep telling me I’m gonna get used, but then I’m not getting used, and now here we are, it’s after Mania, and I’ve had two knee surgeries. Writing’s kind of on the wall, even though I don’t want to accept it," Serrano said.

Serrano also recalled receiving the phone call about her release while she was working out at the gym. "I was at the gym, and I saw TR call me. I was doing my normal workout, trying to get ready, and when I saw the name pop up, I answered hoping it wasn’t the phone call. When he said the words, I broke down right then and there. I fully thought I was going into a panic attack. Started shaking, crying, just completely overwhelmed. Didn’t know what I was going to do next. As much as that hurt me, I always like to talk about the guy who gave me the phone call. It can’t be easy for him. That has to be a rough day at the office because I could tell on the other end of the phone he wasn’t happy to give that phone call. So it’s one of the hardest things to go through, but even though it was so hard, I am strangely happy it happened now," she added.

Following her release, Serrano made her in-ring return in July at RevPro Wrestling before eventually joining TNA Wrestling. During the interview, she also touched upon her former team name, the Pure Fusion Collective.
