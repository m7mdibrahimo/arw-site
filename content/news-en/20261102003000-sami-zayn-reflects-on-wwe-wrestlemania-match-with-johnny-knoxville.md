Looking back on his memorable WWE WrestleMania 38 match, Sami Zayn reflected on his anything-goes encounter with Jackass actor and stuntman Johnny Knoxville. Blending comedy and violence, the bout featured everything from a fire extinguisher and trash cans to a giant mouse trap. Zayn admitted that he even experienced self-doubt right before heading out to the curtain, wondering if the crowd would connect with the chaotic stipulation. Ultimately, Zayn acknowledged that Knoxville proved to be the perfect opponent for that specific scenario, making it one of his personal career favorites.
