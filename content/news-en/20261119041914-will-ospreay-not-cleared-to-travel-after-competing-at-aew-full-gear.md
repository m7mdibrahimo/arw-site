Will Ospreay is dealing with injuries following his recent performance at AEW Full Gear. Ospreay successfully defended his AEW World Championship against Kyle Fletcher in the main event, but he sustained multiple injuries during the bout.

During this week's episode of AEW Dynamite, commentary revealed that Ospreay has not received medical clearance to travel following the event. This update aired alongside an intense, emotional segment where Ospreay forgave Fletcher and advised him to distance himself from Don Callis.

Further details regarding Ospreay's health status and his return to AEW television are expected to be revealed in the coming period.
