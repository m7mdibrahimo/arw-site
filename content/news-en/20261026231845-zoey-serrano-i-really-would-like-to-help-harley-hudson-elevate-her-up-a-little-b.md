Zoey Serrano has expressed her desire to help Harley Hudson elevate her status in TNA Wrestling. During an appearance on Busted Open, Serrano discussed Hudson and noted that she sees a lot of potential in her.

Serrano stated, "So you mentioned ‘help someone.’ I really would like to help Harley (Hudson). I think she has a lot of potential so to help elevate her up a little bit more."

Since joining TNA Wrestling, Serrano has shared the ring with Hudson multiple times. Her first contracted match for the company involved teaming with Xia Brookside to face Hudson and Indi Hartwell. Currently, Serrano holds the TNA World Tag Team Championship alongside Shayna Baszler.
