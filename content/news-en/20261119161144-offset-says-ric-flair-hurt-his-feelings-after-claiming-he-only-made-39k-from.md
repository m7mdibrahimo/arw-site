Offset recently spoke with Bootleg Kev about his current relationship with Ric Flair and addressed claims made by the WWE Hall of Famer regarding their 6x platinum hit 'Ric Flair Drip'. In 2024, Flair claimed he earned just $39,000 from the track while alleging others pocketed nearly $50 million. Offset admitted that the comments hurt him, saying, "It's been a minute. Ric Flair hurt my feelings though, man. That's my brother but he hurt my feelings. He did like an interview and said I made like $70M off Ric Flair Drip and he made 40,000 or something."

Offset explained that he made sure Flair received payment even though WWE technically owns the name and noted that he included Flair in promotional appearances like Jimmy Fallon. Despite the frustration, Offset emphasized that there is still love between them.
