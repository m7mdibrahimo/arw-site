WWE star Cody Rhodes recently opened up about his frustrations with wrestling social media during an interview with Chris Van Vliet, explaining how online discussions can often manufacture entirely fake controversies out of nothing. Rhodes pointed out that internet chatter frequently blows things out of proportion, creating heat where none previously existed until fans and talent react to it. He admitted that even he has been guilty of fueling these situations in the past, referencing a previous discussion about Matt Cardona. Ultimately, Rhodes believes the most important voices are the fans inside the arena who give their time and energy to the product.
