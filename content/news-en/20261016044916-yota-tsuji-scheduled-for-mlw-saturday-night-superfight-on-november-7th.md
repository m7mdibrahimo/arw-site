Yota Tsuji is set to appear for MLW. The promotion has been teasing the arrival of the current IWGP Heavyweight Champion, and his appearance has now been confirmed. Tsuji announced in a video that he will be at the Saturday Night SuperFight event on November 7th in Cicero, Illinois.

> YOTA TSUJI (@njpw_yotatsuji ) IS COMING TO MLW?! Watch MLW FUSION: https://tubitv.com/series/300022585/mlw-fusion

Before that, Tsuji is scheduled to compete at NJPW Power Struggle on November 3rd. He will face Oleg Boltin and Kenny Omega in a three-way match, where a victory for Omega would earn him a shot at Tsuji's IWGP Heavyweight Championship.
