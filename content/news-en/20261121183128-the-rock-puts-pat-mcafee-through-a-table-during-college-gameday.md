Pat McAfee suffered a Rock Bottom through a table at the hands of The Rock after mocking Miami as soft while appearing on College GameDay. Things started when McAfee was discussing Miami's performance and complaining about officiating, describing their protests as the softest thing he had ever seen, before everyone was surprised by The Rock entering to his own music and heading straight for the analysis panel to confront him.

When The Rock asked him if he dared to say that again on their campus, McAfee stood by his words and asked whether the team really seemed a little soft, prompting The Rock to launch an immediate attack that smashed the table beneath him in front of the fans before delivering a strict message about his view on that softness.
