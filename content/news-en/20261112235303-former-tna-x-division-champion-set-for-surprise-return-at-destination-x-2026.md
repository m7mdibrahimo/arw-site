TNA Wrestling has announced another surprise for Destination X 2026, with a former X-Division Champion set to make a return on Sunday, November 15. While the identity of the returning star remains a mystery, it is unclear if this appearance will lead to an in-ring match.

Beyond the mystery return, TNA has scheduled a concert by former WWE Superstar Elijah during the Countdown pre-show. Additionally, the World X-Cup tournament will kick off during the Countdown and continue throughout the pay-per-view. The tournament will feature eight wrestlers from six countries competing in two Fatal Four-Way matches, with the winners advancing to the final later that night. Confirmed participants include TNA International Champion Jason Hotch for the United States, Rey Horus for Mexico, Ricky Sosa for Belgium, Fabian Aichner for Italy, The Home Town Man for Canada, and Amir Jordan for Pakistan.

Destination X will also feature two Ultimate X matches, one for the men and one for the Knockouts division, both serving as championship contender matches. The event, held at the Edmonton EXPO Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, begins with the Countdown pre-show at 6 PM ET, followed by the main card at 7 PM ET on TNA+ and traditional pay-per-view providers.
