New matches and appearances have been added to the upcoming episode of WWE Raw by general manager Adam Pearce. Newly crowned Women's World Champion Liv Morgan will make an appearance following her triple threat victory over Stephanie Vaquer and Becky Lynch at WWE Money in the Bank.

In addition, Rey Mysterio will face Big Cass in singles competition. Pearce also noted that a medical update regarding Je'Von Evans will be provided after Evans crashed through an ambulance at WWE Money in the Bank.
