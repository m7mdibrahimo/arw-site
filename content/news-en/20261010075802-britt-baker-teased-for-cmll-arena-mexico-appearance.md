Britt Baker is heading to CMLL. CMLL released a video featuring the reigning CMLL World Women's and AEW TBS Champion Persephone, who teased that her ally Britt Baker will soon make an appearance for CMLL. The two first joined forces at AEW All In, where Baker assisted Persephone in defeating Maya World to claim the AEW TBS Championship. Persephone has held the CMLL World Women's Championship for over 200 days.
