Kayla Braxton's parents were initially concerned when she told them she had landed a job with WWE. Braxton, who now uses the name Kayla Becker, explained that her parents did not understand the role of a ring announcer and assumed she would be parading in a bikini. During an appearance on the Milk & Honeys podcast with Vanessa Curry and Tim Chantarangsu, Braxton recalled how her family imagined her career path. "They assumed I was saying I was going to be a ring girl who would be holding a sign in a bikini," she said. Braxton admitted that her parents were genuinely worried, though she joked that she would have had to give up pizza to maintain the physique for such a role. Braxton began her WWE career as an NXT ring announcer in 2016 before transitioning to hosting and backstage interviews on SmackDown, The Bump, and premium live event kickoff shows. She left WWE in June 2024 after an eight-year tenure, though she noted in June 2026 that Triple H told her she could return at any time.
