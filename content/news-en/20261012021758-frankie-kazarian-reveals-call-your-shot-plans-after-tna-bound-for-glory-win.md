Frankie Kazarian has confirmed his strategy following his victory in the Call Your Shot gauntlet at TNA Bound for Glory on October 11, 2026. Rather than pursuing a singles championship, Kazarian revealed backstage that he intends to use the opportunity to challenge for the TNA World Tag Team Titles alongside Avery Styles. Kazarian stated that this decision is focused on the future of TNA and the growth of the company. By choosing to target the tag team titles instead of the TNA World Championship, Kazarian has immediately established a new direction for his win and presented a significant threat to the current tag team division. While he could have used the victory for a solo title shot, he opted to elevate his partnership with Styles. The timeline for when they will cash in their opportunity has not yet been announced.
