AEW presented its annual "Dynamite: Blood & Guts" episode this past Wednesday, which opened with the women's match for the second year in a row. During the contest, Willow Nightingale entered the caged ring carrying a backpack filled with toy bricks and broken glass. While many viewers likely assumed the items were LEGOs, the commentary team consistently referred to them as "toy bricks."

According to Bryan Alvarez on Wrestling Observer Radio, AEW was instructed to avoid using the brand name LEGO during the broadcast. Alvarez noted that officials were required to correct anyone who accidentally used the term, stating, "They made it very clear that they have to call them toy bricks. Every time somebody accidentally said the word LEGOs, they had to correct it to toy bricks. So toy bricks got involved here."

While the specific reason for this restriction was not disclosed, the LEGO brand maintains a strict Fair Play Policy regarding the use of its logo and trademark. AEW appears to be exercising caution by utilizing a generic term to avoid potential trademark issues.
