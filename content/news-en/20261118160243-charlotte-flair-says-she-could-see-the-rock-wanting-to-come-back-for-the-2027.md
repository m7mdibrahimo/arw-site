Charlotte Flair believes The Rock could make a return for the 2027 Royal Rumble match. The upcoming Royal Rumble event will mark the 40th anniversary of the historic WWE show. Speaking with Screen Chatter, Charlotte Flair discussed the landmark 40th anniversary of the Royal Rumble and shared her thoughts on potential participants. "Being the 40th, I could see The Rock wanting to come back for the Rumble. I'm not saying he would, but I'm saying the Rumble is that big, and because it's the 40th, you never know," Flair said. She also mentioned her father, Ric Flair, noting that he would love to participate, though she did not expect it to happen this year. Flair added that the unpredictable nature of the match is what makes it so exciting for fans.
