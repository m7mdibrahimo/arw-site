One of WWE's most memorable Attitude Era segments nearly looked very different, as Jason Sensation confirmed that Chyna was originally slated to play Owen Hart in D-Generation X's famous Nation of Domination parody. The plans changed after Chyna made it clear she was not interested in the role. Sensation, speaking with Chris Van Vliet, noted that he was initially intended to appear as himself to interview the DX members, rather than dressing up as a wrestler. "That's true. That's true. And she was just not into that. What the segment was going to be, just me as myself. That was going to be my introduction day as Jason Sensation," he explained. Sensation added that the original concept would have seen DX members in costume while he provided the voices for their impressions. However, Chyna refused the Owen Hart part because she felt it did not suit her character. Vince Russo agreed with her, leading the creative team to put Sensation in the costume instead. Sensation noted that Russo wrote most of the segment, though he contributed some jokes, including the line about smelling what The Rock was cooking, which was tied to Owen Hart's oversized fake nose. The decision to switch roles became a defining moment in Sensation's career.
