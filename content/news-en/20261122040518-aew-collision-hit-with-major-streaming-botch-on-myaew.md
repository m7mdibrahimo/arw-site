AEW experienced major technical difficulties on its MyAEW streaming platform before the in-ring action could properly begin. Fans tuning into the November 21, 2026 broadcast of AEW Collision discovered that the English and Spanish audio feeds had been swapped. Viewers who selected the English stream heard Spanish commentary instead, while those on the Spanish broadcast received English audio.

The unusual technical error forced subscribers to intentionally pick the incorrect language option just to hear their preferred announcers. Paying viewers wanting English commentary can bypass the issue by choosing the Spanish audio track until officials fix the mistake. The mix-up created a frustrating start to Saturday's show from Green Bay, Wisconsin, for paying subscribers who expected a seamless broadcast.
