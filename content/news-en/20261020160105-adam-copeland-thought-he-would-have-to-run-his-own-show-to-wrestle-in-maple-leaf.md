Adam Copeland opened up about finally getting the chance to wrestle in Maple Leaf Gardens, revealing the lengths he thought he would have to go to in order to make it happen. At Maple Leaf Wrestling's Northern Rising event on October 3, Copeland defeated Steve Maclin, checking off a legendary venue that sat at the very top of his career bucket list. Speaking during a YouTube documentary from MLP, Copeland explained why competing in the historic building meant so much to him and why he once believed it was an impossible dream.

> “This was easily the most important venue for me to wrestle in, over any other, over Wembley Stadium, over any of them. Maple Leaf Gardens has always been my one place that I wanted to get to. Here we are today. No, I never thought I'd wrestle in Maple Leaf Gardens. Between the fact that I had retired for nine years, and then even once I got my career back, just with the landscape of what wrestling is and where I was working, Maple Leaf Gardens was a pipe dream. I thought if I were going to ever wrestle there, I'd have to run my own show, and then Scott D'Amore and I, we started talking and realized, 'wait, this is now a possibility now that I'm with AEW, we could actually do this.'”

Meanwhile, Will Ospreay recently mentioned that he wants Copeland to save a date for a match before he retires in 2027.
