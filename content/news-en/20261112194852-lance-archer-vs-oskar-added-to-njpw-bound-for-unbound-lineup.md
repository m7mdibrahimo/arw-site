New Japan Pro-Wrestling has added a new singles match to its lineup for the Bound for Unbound event. The show is scheduled for November 20 at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo.

OSKAR issued an open challenge for the event in an effort to establish himself as the best monster wrestler in NJPW history, and Lance Archer answered the call. When OSKAR was a Young Lion, Archer offered him advice, but during last year's World Tag League, Archer and Alex Zayne defeated OSKAR and his partner. Now, the two giant competitors will square off in a singles environment.

The updated card for NJPW Bound for Unbound includes:

- Yota Tsuji vs. Ares
- Drilla Moloney vs. Daiki Nagai
- Natsupoi & Shingo Takagi vs. Chihiro Hashimoto & Jeff Cobb
- Robbie X vs. Dragon Lee
- OSKAR vs. Lance Archer
