Tiffany Stratton nearly cashed in her Money in the Bank contract during the October 23, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown, which would have forced Liv Morgan to defend her title. Stratton arrived after Chelsea Green successfully defended her WWE Women's Championship against Roxanne Perez, clearing the ring to prepare for her move. According to Ringside News, the plan did not unfold as expected.

Chelsea Green retained her title despite interference from Raquel Rodriguez, while IYO SKY later confronted Rodriguez at ringside. Following the match, Morgan and Rodriguez assaulted Green, with SKY and Perez attempting to intervene. Stratton then entered the ring with her briefcase, attacking the group before handing the case to the referee. However, she chose to strike Morgan with the briefcase first, which cost her the opportunity. Perez and Rodriguez pulled Morgan to safety, preventing the match from starting. Stratton retains her contract as Morgan prepares for Crown Jewel.
