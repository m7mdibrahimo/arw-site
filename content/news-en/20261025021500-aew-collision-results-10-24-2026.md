All Elite Wrestling broadcast an episode of Collision on TNT from the LJVM Coliseum in Winston-Salem, North Carolina. 

In the first match of a best-of-five series, FTR def. Motor City Machine Guns. Swerve Strickland picked up a victory over Myron Reed. Divine Dominion defeated Wrathlander in a tag team contest. 

The first hour featured segments with Jay White, Bang Bang Gang, and The Dogs. Jay White addressed Jon Moxley and announced that they would now be called The Last Outfit, while Kyle O'Reilly challenged him for later in the evening. Will Ospreay also cut a promo previewing Blood & Guts. 

Death Riders def. STP in an eight-person tag team match. In the main event, Josh Alexander def. Blade Brown.
