TKO released its financial results for the third quarter of 2026 on Wednesday, showing that WWE revenue fell 1% compared to the previous year, totaling $399.3 million. The company attributed this slight decline primarily to a reduction in the number of main roster Premium Live Events and lower ticket sales for SummerSlam.

According to TKO, the $2.8 million revenue drop was driven by a $24.8 million decrease in live events and hospitality revenue. This was partially offset by growth in other areas, including an $11.1 million increase in partnerships and marketing revenue, a $6.9 million rise in media rights, production, and content revenue, and a $4.0 million gain in consumer products licensing.

The decline in live events and hospitality revenue was mainly due to the timing and mix of events, specifically three fewer main roster Premium Live Events and lower SummerSlam sales compared to the same period in 2025. Conversely, media rights revenue grew due to higher fees, notably the impact of the distribution agreement with ESPN that began in September 2025.
