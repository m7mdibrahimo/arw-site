MJF made a surprise return at AEW Full Gear on November 14, 2026, attacking Will Ospreay after his successful title defense against Kyle Fletcher. Ospreay had just retained the AEW World Championship when a masked man attacked him from behind, revealing himself to be MJF. Holding his Triple B championship belt, MJF stood over Ospreay and made it clear he still considers himself the rightful champion before fleeing.

The return comes shortly after Sean Ross Sapp reported that MJF was expected back soon for a major storyline. MJF had been away from AEW television since AEW All Out on August 30. Back on September 24, MJF stated that he was waiting for the right moment to return and target whoever held the AEW World Championship to become the first undisputed world champion in the company's history. Now, MJF has made his intentions known, leaving Ospreay with a major challenge ahead.
