Kayla Braxton recently discussed a unique dating experiment from before her WWE tenure. She moved in with YouTuber Swoozie to participate in a "30-day dating challenge," but she confirmed that the two never slept together during that time. Braxton explained that Swoozie was celibate and saving himself at that stage in his life. She noted that Swoozie has been open about this aspect of his life publicly, so she felt comfortable sharing the detail. The experiment involved moving into Swoozie's parents' home in Orlando with him after they had barely met, an experience she described as "chaotic." While she initially approached the challenge as a way to launch a YouTube career, she admitted that real feelings eventually developed toward the end of the 30 days. Braxton ultimately decided to remove the footage of the experiment from the internet, a move she now views as a potential mistake given the dramatic nature of the experience.
