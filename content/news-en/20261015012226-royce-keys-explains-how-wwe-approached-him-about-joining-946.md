Royce Keys recently opened up about how WWE approached him regarding the new direction that helped him find his footing with the company and join The 946. Speaking on The Ebro Laura Rosenberg Show, Keys explained that after reaching out about his lack of connection with fans, officials assured him that the audience just needed to see his real personality. He noted that leaning into his naturally intimidating look changed everything for him. When discussing how he joined The 946, Keys stated that management simply asked for his input on a new direction, and he readily accepted whatever role they needed him to play. Now, alongside Bronco Nima, Lucien Price, Jaida Parker, and Carmelo Hayes, Keys believes the faction has become one of the most talked-about topics in professional wrestling.
