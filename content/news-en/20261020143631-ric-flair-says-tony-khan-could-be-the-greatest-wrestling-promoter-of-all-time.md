Ric Flair believes AEW President Tony Khan could go down as the greatest promoter in professional wrestling history. Speaking on NewsNation's BIFF on October 15, 2026, the WWE Hall of Famer praised Khan's generosity, hard work running both AEW and the Jacksonville Jaguars, and his approach to letting wrestlers showcase their athleticism. While acknowledging Vince McMahon's historic cleverness and decades of dominance, Flair argued that the only real difference between WWE and AEW is the length of time each company has been in business, suggesting Khan has the potential to reach unprecedented heights in the industry.
