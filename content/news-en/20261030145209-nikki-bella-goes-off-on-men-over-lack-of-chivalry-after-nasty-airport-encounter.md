WWE Hall of Famer Nikki Bella recently opened up about a frustrating airport encounter in Mexico City that prompted her to speak out about modern chivalry. Speaking on The Nikki & Brie Show, she recalled trying to pass through a crowded customs line when a man began aggressively slamming his luggage cart into the back of her ankles. She expressed her shock at his rude behavior, noting that an officer eventually intervened and let her through. The incident sparked a broader conversation about courtesy and respect, with Bella emphasizing that women advocating for equal pay and opportunities does not mean men should abandon basic manners. She argued that holding doors open and waiting one's turn should remain standard expectations for everyone.
