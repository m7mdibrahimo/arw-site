The linear television ratings for the November 11 episode of AEW Dynamite on TBS showed a minor decrease compared to the previous week. According to reports, the broadcast drew 742,000 viewers and achieved a 0.16 rating in the key 18-49 demographic. This reflects a drop of 50,000 viewers and a 0.08 decline in the key demographic compared to the November 4 episode, which drew 792,000 viewers with a 0.24 rating.

Despite the weekly decline, the show performed stronger when compared to the same period last year. The November 12, 2025 episode recorded 600,000 viewers and a 0.15 rating in the key demographic, marking a 23.7 percent increase in overall viewership and a 6.7 percent rise in the key demographic year-over-year. The four-week rolling average for the show currently stands at 703,750 viewers, with total viewership in 2026 ranging between a high of 899,000 viewers on September 30 and a low of 495,000 viewers on October 6.

These figures record only linear viewing on TBS and do not include streaming or digital numbers. AEW will look to maintain its audience momentum as it continues the build toward its upcoming major events.
