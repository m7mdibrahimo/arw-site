WWE has officially begun its promotional campaign for Royal Rumble 2027, with Roman Reigns featured prominently on the event's first poster. The graphic displays Reigns holding a championship belt against an Arizona desert backdrop, with State Farm Stadium visible in the distance.

Triple H announced on November 18, 2026, via X that tickets for the event are now available. The 40th annual Royal Rumble is scheduled for Saturday, February 6, 2027, at State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona. The show will feature both men's and women's Royal Rumble matches, with the winners earning championship opportunities at WrestleMania 43.

> "30 Superstars. One opportunity at greatness. Tickets for #RoyalRumble LIVE from Glendale, AZ are on sale NOW!"

Arizona will host the event for the third time, following editions in 2013 and 2019. Additionally, WWE SmackDown and AAA Road to Rey de Reyes are scheduled for February 5 in Glendale. While Reigns is featured on the poster, WWE has not confirmed his participation in the match.
