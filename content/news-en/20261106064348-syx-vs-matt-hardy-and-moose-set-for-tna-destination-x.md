Moose opened the November 5 episode of TNA iMPACT by addressing Syx (Joseph Sawyer and Samuel Shaw), following their victory over The Hardys in a Slaughterhouse Syx match on October 29. During that match, Syx attacked Jeff Hardy by placing a trash bin over his upper body and striking him with axes. When Moose called out Syx on the show, the duo responded and ambushed him, prompting Matt Hardy to run down and make the save.

Later in the broadcast, Matt Hardy was addressing Syx when Moose joined him on camera. TNA then officially announced that Moose and Matt Hardy will team up to face Joseph Sawyer and Samuel Shaw at TNA Destination X on November 15.

The updated lineup for TNA Destination X includes Leon Slater defending the TNA World Championship against Trey Miguel, alongside Indi Hartwell defending the TNA Knockouts World Championship against Shayna Baszler. The card will also feature both Men's and Women's Ultimate X matches, alongside World X-Cup tournament matches.
