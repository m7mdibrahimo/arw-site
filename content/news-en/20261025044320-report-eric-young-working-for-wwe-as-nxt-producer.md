Eric Young has returned to WWE, according to a report from Mike Johnson of PWInsider. The two-time TNA World Champion joined the company's backstage staff this past July following his departure from TNA during the summer.

Young is currently employed as a producer for the NXT brand. Johnson noted that this transition was widely expected by many in the industry after Young secured his release from TNA. Regarding his potential future in the ring, Johnson stated:

> "That was the assumption for a lot of people when Young asked for and received his TNA release and it was correct. He started working for WWE as a Producer for NXT in July. I don't know if he will wrestle."

Young previously rejoined WWE in 2022 but did not make any on-screen appearances before departing due to personal, professional, and moral concerns.
