Kyle Fletcher paid tribute to PAC in the latest episode of his vlog series by writing 'PAC Forever' on the Love Lock bridge in Paris. On September 27th, AEW announced the passing of Benjamin Satterley, known to fans as PAC, prompting the company to dedicate an episode of AEW Dynamite to him with various tribute matches and segments. During an episode of Kyle Fletcher's 'Protolog' series, Kyle and Skye Blue visited Paris and left the tribute at the Love Lock bridge. Meanwhile, PAC's wife, Natalie, shared a statement remembering her late husband on October 12th.
