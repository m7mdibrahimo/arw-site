Absolute Intense Wrestling is taking a break. The promotion announced on Monday that they will go on a brief hiatus following their Hell On Earth event on Friday, November 27. The current plan is to resume operations in late winter or early spring of 2027.

"After 11/27 we will be taking a bit of a break to allow everyone a chance to recharge including our fanbase. We will not be holding any events at the very least in December or January and the hope would be to return in late winter/ early spring of 2027."

Future plans regarding the promotion's return will be announced in due course.
