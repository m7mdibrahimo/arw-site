Damian Priest has responded to R-Truth blaming him for the injury that stopped their WWE SmackDown match on October 16. Priest joked that Truth nearly ended his own life just to beat him.

Speaking on Off the Ropes, Priest addressed Truth's medical update and hospital photo. He turned the accusation around, stating that Truth took the risk for the victory. Truth previously thanked fans for their support and kept his storyline going by claiming Priest tried to kill him.

Truth has not revealed the exact nature of his injury or a return timetable, but his message confirmed he is doing well.
