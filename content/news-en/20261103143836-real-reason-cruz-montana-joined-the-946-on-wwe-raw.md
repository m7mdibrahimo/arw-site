According to False Finish, Royce Keys was the one who brought Cruz Montana into The 946, with the decision to add the NXT star specifically described as a "Royce Keys decision." Keys previously made the call to bring Carmelo Hayes into the group, demonstrating his significant influence over team membership.

Montana debuted during Keys' match against Solo Sikoa on Monday, November 2, on WWE RAW, initially concealing his face with a mask during a brawl between The 946 and The Bloodline. He then unmasked, struck Roman Reigns with a steel chair to block a Spear, and attacked the WWE World Heavyweight Champion.
