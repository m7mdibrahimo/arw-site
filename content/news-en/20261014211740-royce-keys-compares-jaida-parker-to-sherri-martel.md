According to Fightful, Royce Keys spoke highly of Jaida Parker during an appearance on Busted Open, stating that she could be a modern-day Sherri Martel. 

> "She hits harder than some of the dudes. I feel she can be the modern-day Sherri Martel. You can’t be afraid in this business, especially as a female. A dude get up in your face, smack the hell out of him. She definitely will."

The updated WWE 946 faction features Keys, Bronco Nima, Lucien Price, Parker, and newest addition Carmelo Hayes. During a brawl between The 946 and The Bloodline, Parker climbed into the stands to deliver a crossbody onto The Bloodline on the floor below.
