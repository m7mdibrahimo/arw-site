Bret Hart opened up about the heavy physical toll his body paid throughout his long career inside the wrestling ring. The WWE Hall of Famer revealed that a doctor warned him during the 1980s that the condition of his knees was so poor it resembled that of an 80-year-old man.

During an appearance on the Johnny I Pro Show, Hart explained that he did not take the warning seriously at the time because he was still able to wrestle without feeling real problems. He confessed that the years of stress and falls left destructive effects he still deals with today, recalling a past warning from Harley Race about the severe damage sustained by Dynamite Kid.

Despite ongoing pain and physical issues, Hart insisted he has no regrets about giving everything he has to professional wrestling. He noted that this physical price was simply part of the cost of becoming one of the standout stars of his generation.
