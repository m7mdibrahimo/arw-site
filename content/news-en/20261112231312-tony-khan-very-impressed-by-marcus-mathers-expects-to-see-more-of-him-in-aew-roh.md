Independent wrestler Marcus Mathers recently competed at a Ring of Honor taping against Mark Davis and then wrestled Nick Wayne on the Jericho Cruise edition of AEW Collision. AEW President Tony Khan discussed Mathers during the Full Gear media call, stating that his strong performance at the ROH taping earned him the spot on Collision. Khan revealed that Christopher Daniels recommended Mathers and noted that he was unaware Mathers' WWE I.D. contract had expired.

Khan praised Mathers, saying that his work on ROH was exceptional and served as a great lesson for independent talent working the tapings. He added that Mathers came to the Jericho Cruise looking for work and delivered another impressive performance against Nick Wayne. Khan concluded that he was very impressed by the young wrestler and expects to see more of him across AEW and ROH programming.
