Sol Ruca has spent her WWE career showcasing a fun-loving persona to fans, yet she remains curious about the potential of exploring a completely different direction. The NXT star confirmed she would love to experiment with being a heel, even though she has been told previously that such a change might never occur.

During an appearance on The Finish, Ruca was asked if she could evolve her character beyond the bubbly babyface fans recognize. She admitted that she is very interested in attempting such a transition.

> "I have no idea. I think I would love to be able to try that."

The challenge lies in the fact that some people find it difficult to envision her as a villain. Ruca revealed that she has been informed she likely will not turn heel because of her natural personality.

> "I have been told in the past that I most likely will not be heel ever because of just how I am naturally. I am quite a bubbly and, like, [likable] person."

While Ruca has not abandoned the idea of showing a darker side, she believes it would require the right individual or storyline to bring it out. Although no current plans exist for this shift, she remains open to stepping out of her comfort zone if WWE provides the opportunity.
