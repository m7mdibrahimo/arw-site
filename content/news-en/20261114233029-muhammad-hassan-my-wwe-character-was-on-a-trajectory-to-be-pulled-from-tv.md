Muhammad Hassan believes his controversial WWE character was already on a path to be removed from television, regardless of the events surrounding the July 2005 London bombings. Following intense backlash after that tragedy, the character was eventually pulled from WWE programming. In an interview on Monte & The Pharaoh, Hassan reflected on the character's direction.

"I think regardless of what happened in July of 2005 in London, I think that character was on a trajectory that was going to get him pulled from TV. The world was changing," Hassan said. He noted that while he was portraying a heel, the cultural shift toward hypersensitivity made his character's approach increasingly difficult to sustain. Hassan explained that the turning point occurred when the character shifted from a "homegrown" sleeper cell narrative to one more heavily reliant on Islamic fanaticism.

He believes this shift drew significant attention because it moved away from holding a mirror up to American society and instead introduced religion into the narrative. Hassan suggested that even without the London bombings, the character likely would not have lasted in a world becoming more sensitive to ethnicity and religion.
