Brooke Hogan expressed her displeasure regarding the sale of her late father's Florida mansion in Clearwater Beach. Following the official sale of the beachfront property, Brooke shared a direct message on her Instagram Story reflecting on possessions, memories, and personal values. Although she did not name anyone specifically, she made it clear that parting with the house was not a decision she would have made. 

> "Some people inherit possessions. Others hold on to memories. I suppose the difference is in what we consider valuable. I would have held on to this one forever."

The 5,400-square-foot property sold for 5.9 million dollars, a significant drop from the 10.989 million dollar asking price when it first hit the market in January. Hulk Hogan purchased the home for 3.3 million dollars in 2012 and lived there until his passing on July 24, 2025. The home features five bedrooms and five-and-a-half bathrooms, alongside a 1,000-square-foot primary suite with views of the Gulf. Additionally, a neighboring 2,062-square-foot guest cottage was sold for 2.8 million dollars, though the buyer remains unconfirmed. The estate continues to navigate several ongoing legal and financial matters.
