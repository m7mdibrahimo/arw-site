Mick Foley recently revealed a unique superstition he maintained for over three decades of his life.

While speaking in an interview on Scaredycast, the WWE Hall of Famer shared that he used to wear his left sock inside out for more than 30 years. Foley explained that his routine eventually came to an end due to physical limitations before he underwent hip replacement surgery.

> "Did you know for about 30 years I wore my left sock inside out, but then Mother Nature decided to mess with me and put me in a situation where I couldn't cross one leg over the other until I had my hip replacement, and now I can do it. But for many years, I couldn't cross my leg more than this, and I found that trying to put my socks on was like almost like trying to like loop it over my leg, and I just stopped wearing socks. But for 30 years, yeah, I wore the left sock inside out."
