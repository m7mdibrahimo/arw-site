Charlotte Flair recently discussed her future in WWE and her ongoing chase to break the all-time world championship record currently held by John Cena at 17 title wins. Flair, a 14-time world champion, noted that she is currently aligned as a babyface alongside her tag team partners Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley. Speaking in a new interview with Good Karma Wrestling, Flair explained her plans to reach the top of the mountain once again alongside Bliss and Paxley. "Well, given that I am a two-time women's Royal Rumble winner, I would say the odds are forever in my favor. But even with all the competition that we have out there, you know, with Royal Rumble, you can have NXT call-ups. You never know a returning Bianca Belair, a Naomi, a Rhea. Like, Rumble, as hard as it is, imagine adding those three women to the Rumble, right? But then you have Elimination Chamber. Or, you know, back in the day, Charlotte used to walk in and be like, I want a title match. I was the cash in. So who knows, you know, if it's not the Rumble, where there's a will, there's a way, and I will find my way back to the top of the mountain somehow with Lexi and Tatum by my side."
