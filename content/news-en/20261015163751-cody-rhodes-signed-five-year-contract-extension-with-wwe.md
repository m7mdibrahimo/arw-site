Cody Rhodes recently signed a five-year contract extension with WWE. While making media rounds to promote the upcoming Street Fighter movie, Rhodes appeared on Andrew Schulz's FLAGRANT podcast and revealed his new deal. The news was also confirmed in a feature piece by the New York Times. Rhodes is a three-time Undisputed WWE Champion, with his most recent reign ending in June 2026 when he lost the title to Sami Zayn.
