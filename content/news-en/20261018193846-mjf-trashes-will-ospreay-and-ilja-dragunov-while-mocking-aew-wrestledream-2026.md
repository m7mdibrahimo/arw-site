Maxwell Jacob Friedman took to social media to criticize AEW WrestleDream 2026, targeting AEW World Champion Will Ospreay, newcomer Ilja Dragunov, and Chris Jericho. Following the October 17 event, MJF dismissed Ospreay's defense in the eight-man Clockwork Carnage Match, accusing the champion of cheating. He also mocked Dragunov's debut as an orchestral composer and noted Nick Wayne's victory over Jericho.
