Stephanie Vaquer suffered a legitimate injury during her Women's World Championship match against Liv Morgan at WWE Money in the Bank, according to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter. Footage of Vaquer struggling to walk and needing referee Jessika Carr to help her backstage raised immediate concerns. Meltzer confirmed the injury was real and not part of a storyline, but noted that she is not expected to be sidelined for an extended period.
