Gabe Kidd reflected on his close bond with the late Benjamin Satterley, better known as PAC, sharing memories of their time together in the Death Riders stable.

Kidd explained on "Talk Is Jericho" that he first encountered PAC in 2011 in Nottingham, United Kingdom, before they grew much closer during car rides in the weeks leading up to his passing.

> "You like dubstep? I love dubstep. We're connected."

Kidd revealed that they discussed forming a tag team reflecting their British roots while having dinner in Charleston, West Virginia. The two later united in AEW as part of the Death Riders, wrestling their final match together on the September 16 episode of AEW Dynamite alongside Jon Moxley against United Empire.
