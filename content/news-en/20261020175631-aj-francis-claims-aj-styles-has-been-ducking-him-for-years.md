AJ Francis is continuing to insist that he is the greatest AJ in TNA history, and he is now accusing AJ Styles of intentionally avoiding him. Speaking on The Wrestling Classic, Francis claimed that Styles knows he is the superior performer and has been dodging a confrontation for years. Francis also targeted Styles' son, Avery, suggesting that avoiding opponents might be a family trait. Francis claimed that Frankie Kazarian has been advising Avery to stay away from him. When asked about potentially facing Avery in the ring, Francis made it clear he has no intention of being gentle, promising to give him a severe beating if they ever meet. Despite Styles' legendary status and numerous world championships in TNA, Francis remains unimpressed and is determined to secure a match against someone in the Styles family.
