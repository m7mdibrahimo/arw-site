Former WWE Co-President George Barrios recently appeared on My World With Jeff Jarrett to discuss WWE's past business dealings with Saudi Arabia, detailing his unique experience traveling to Riyadh to finalize the major agreement.

Barrios recalled the tense atmosphere surrounding the trip, noting that he stepped up to make the journey because Vince McMahon and Michelle Wilson were not going. Upon arriving in Riyadh weeks after the killing of Jamal Khashoggi, he was greeted by armed guards with machine guns and described a bizarre meeting inside a dark compound with one of the senior princes wearing boxer shorts and smoking a hookah while shouting over deal terms.
