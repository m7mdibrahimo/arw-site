Kairi Sane's wrestling future continues to attract attention as speculation grows regarding a potential move to AEW. The former WWE star remains one of the most closely watched free agents on the market, with ongoing discussions keeping AEW firmly in the conversation. Adding to the rumors, Kairi is scheduled to appear on Chris Jericho's Rock 'N' Wrestling Rager at Sea cruise, placing her around AEW talent and fueling fan expectations. Despite the strong momentum, a deal is not yet finalized. During a recent Q&A session, Sean Ross Sapp clarified the current status of negotiations by stating, "She has not yet though. Just has not yet." Until a contract is signed, Kairi remains a free agent with her future open to multiple possibilities.
