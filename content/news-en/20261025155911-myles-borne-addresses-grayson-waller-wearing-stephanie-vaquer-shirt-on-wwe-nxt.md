Myles Borne is unfazed by Grayson Waller wearing a Stephanie Vaquer shirt on WWE NXT, believing the attempt to troll him backfired. Borne pointed out that Waller essentially spent his own money to support Vaquer, putting money on their table. During an appearance on The Finish with Corey Smith and Tor Smith, Borne explained that Waller's stunt ultimately made him look like a fan rather than an effective troll. Borne also suggested that Vaquer's shirt stole the entire segment, overshadowing whatever Waller was trying to say. Waller originally wore the shirt during the October 14 episode of WWE NXT while cutting a promo about Borne facing Tony D'Angelo for a shot at the NXT Championship. This followed WWE reportedly turning down another shirt idea featuring Vaquer's viral Shawn Michaels-inspired Instagram pose.
