Josh Barnett provided an update on the future of Bloodsport during a recent interview. At the conclusion of Bloodsport XV in April, Barnett announced that the promotion was going on hiatus to secure more capital. Now, a few months later, Barnett revealed that they have been shopping the concept around to potential investors and receiving strong interest. While the event is not overly expensive to produce compared to streaming or traditional media content, securing financial backing remains essential. Barnett emphasized that he would rather keep Bloodsport dormant than compromise his vision or allow it to be ruined by individuals lacking his dedication. Additionally, Barnett shared that he previously had major plans for the show, including a potential match against Bill Goldberg in Japan and a tournament featuring Mike O'Hearn, highlighting his strong connections and deep understanding of how to run the event successfully.
