Legendary wrestler Bret Hart honored his late father by taking part in the official ribbon-cutting ceremony for the Stu Hart Memorial Bridge in Calgary. According to the Calgary Herald, the new eastbound bridge opened on November 10 with Bret in attendance alongside family members, including his daughter and grandson.

Alberta Transportation and Economic Corridors Minister Devin Dreeshen stated that naming the structure after Stu recognized the Hart family's impact on putting Calgary on the wrestling map. Stu established Stampede Wrestling in 1948 and remained a prominent figure until his passing on October 16, 2003.

For Bret, the tribute carried deep emotional significance because the surrounding area was a major part of his childhood travels with family. Hart expressed joy at seeing his father commemorated in a location linked to happy memories with his siblings.
