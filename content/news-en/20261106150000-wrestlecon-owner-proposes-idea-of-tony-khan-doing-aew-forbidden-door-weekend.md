WrestleCon owner Michael Bochicchio recently discussed an idea for AEW to run a global weekend around AEW Forbidden Door in April 2027 while WWE presents WrestleMania 43 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. During a Sign-it-Live session with Mick Foley, Bochicchio pitched the concept of expanding the pay-per-view into a full weekend event to capture fan interest during that period. Foley noted that he is scaling back his outside appearances in 2027 to prioritize AEW, while WrestleCon's location for that year remains undetermined. Meanwhile, AEW referee Bryce Remsburg mentioned that discussions are already underway regarding what AEW might plan while WWE is in Saudi Arabia.
