Dave Meltzer has provided new details regarding the futures of Dragon Lee and his brother Dralistico. Meltzer confirmed on Wrestling Observer Radio that Dragon Lee has signed a five-year contract extension to stay with WWE. Meanwhile, regarding Dralistico, whose AEW contract expires in December, Meltzer noted that while there is speculation he might leave and join his brother in WWE, sources close to the wrestler suggest his recent social media post featuring prison bars was actually related to visa issues rather than an imminent departure.
