AEW World Tag Team Champions the Young Bucks recently discussed their ongoing run in AEW and praised their boss, Tony Khan. During an episode of Being the Elite, Matt and Nick Jackson reflected on their match against Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight at WrestleDream. Nick recalled telling Khan on camera that he was unsure how much longer he could perform at this high level, which led both brothers to express their appreciation for Khan as both a boss and a person. Nick stated that he would do anything for Khan and credited him for giving them so much throughout their careers, adding that Khan does not get enough praise and credit from them. Matt also mentioned texting Khan on his birthday to thank him for changing his life. Having been key figures in the launch of AEW in 2019 alongside Kenny Omega, Cody Rhodes, and Brandi Rhodes, the Young Bucks have remained at the top of the company, recently setting the record for the most AEW World Tag Team Championship reigns by winning the titles at All Out.
