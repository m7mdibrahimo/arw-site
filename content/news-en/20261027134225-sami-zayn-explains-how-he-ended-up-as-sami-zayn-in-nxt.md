Before adopting the name Sami Zayn in WWE NXT, Sami competed on the independent scene as El Generico. When joining WWE, he dropped the mask and considered various name and persona options. Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Sami discussed the categories of names he initially presented to management. He noted that he suggested Irish-themed names, French Canadian ideas, Arab-inspired options, and masked characters because he was unsure what WWE was looking for. Ultimately, he chose the name Sami Zayn because it provided a unique opportunity to represent his ethnicity and religion in Western culture without falling into the stereotypical, villainous tropes often portrayed in media.
