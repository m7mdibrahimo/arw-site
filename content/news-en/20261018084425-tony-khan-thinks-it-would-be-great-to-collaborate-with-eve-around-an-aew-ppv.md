Tony Khan discussed the ongoing relationship between AEW and Pro Wrestling EVE. The two promotions have collaborated for over a year, with EVE events airing on the MyAEW platform, Will Ospreay serving on the EVE creative team, and Kris Statlander currently holding the EVE Championship. At the WrestleDream press conference, Khan was asked about the possibility of a collaboration during an AEW pay-per-view in the United States.

> "That'd be a great thing around one of these pay-per-views. We've had events with Ring of Honor in conjunction with the pay-per-views. With EVE around a major PPV event could be good. We had success working with them around All In, and we've collaborated a lot. I love working with Will on those kinds things, including some of the things we've done with EVE and the wrestlers in AEW."

Will Ospreay added his support for the idea, stating, "If we can make it happen, it would be brilliant. I would love to do something."
