Former WWE writer and producer Robert Karpeles stated that Arn Anderson felt sidelined behind the scenes in AEW and that his extensive experience was not utilized to help young stars. Speaking on the Two Man Power Trip of Wrestling podcast, Karpeles noted that the promotion features many veteran minds who merely sit around without any true application of their vast knowledge.

Karpeles shared details of a conversation he had with Anderson backstage at AEW Dynamite, where he asked him if he was working as an agent with his previous level of activity. Anderson's reply was that he did nothing except deliver his television promo and then sit throughout the show without anyone asking him for any consultation.

These statements match what Anderson mentioned previously about his role losing its direction after Cody Rhodes left and joined WWE, which made him lose the main purpose of his presence alongside the talent on the show.
