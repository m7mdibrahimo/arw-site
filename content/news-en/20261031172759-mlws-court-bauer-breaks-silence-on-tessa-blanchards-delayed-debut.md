Major League Wrestling founder Court Bauer has finally addressed the delayed debut of Tessa Blanchard, explaining that an unexpected detour caused a roughly 60-day setback. Speaking on Wrestling Observer Radio, Bauer declined to reveal the specific behind-the-scenes details but confirmed that things eventually got back on track, leading to the same destination.

Blanchard was originally scheduled to appear for MLW in September after leaving TNA, with promotional material already released before her name suddenly disappeared. Bauer noted that original plans encountered an unexpected problem, though he stopped short of confirming CMLL's involvement in the delay. Despite the hiccup, Blanchard is now set to make her long-awaited debut against CMLL's Kira at MLW Saturday Night SuperFight on November 7.

Interestingly, Bauer also revealed that MLW's history with Blanchard actually dates back to 2017, when she was originally supposed to be involved in the promotion's very first show while dating Ricochet. Now, nearly a decade later, she is finally stepping into an MLW ring as part of the growing women's division, with potential future opportunities involving Masha Slamovich, Zamaya, and talent from STARDOM.
