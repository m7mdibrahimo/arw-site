Will Ospreay recently discussed Adam Copeland's impact on his wrestling career and revealed that he wants Copeland to save a date for him before he hangs up his boots. During a recent edition of AEW Dynamite, Copeland qualified for the Clockwork Carnage match at AEW WrestleDream and announced his plans to retire from professional wrestling in 2027. Speaking in an interview with 1010XL, Ospreay reflected on Copeland's guidance during his neck surgery and expressed his gratitude for the veteran's presence in AEW. Ospreay stated that while their upcoming focus remains on the championship match, he hopes to share the ring with Copeland one final time before his retirement.
