Jonathan Coachman is clarifying his recent comments regarding Road Dogg's departure from WWE. Coachman previously claimed that Road Dogg was fired due to his public support for Chelsea Green. Road Dogg has since insisted that he resigned, but Coachman maintains that the distinction is minor, as he believes Brian James was effectively pushed out of the company because of how he was treated.

Speaking on The Coach and Bro Show, Coachman explained that his comments were misinterpreted by viral clips. He clarified that he did not mean WWE fired James solely for supporting Green, but rather that his support for her contributed to the environment that led to his exit. Coachman questioned why James would choose to quit if the environment had not become untenable.

> "Well, I do need to address Road Dogg, because we went viral today. Because, you know, the clippers, they took the headlines and they say, ‘Coach says he was fired.’ What I said was, ‘I don't care what anyone says, he was fired because of his support.’ That's how he was supportive. I didn't say he was fired just because of that, but that's how it reads online."

Road Dogg responded to the controversy on November 11 by clarifying that he resigned, while reaffirming his stance on Green. 

> "I quit!!! @ImChelseaGreen is a national treasure NO MATTER THE NATION!!! I saw the writing on the wall I’m hearing others are being pushed out too lol Growth hurts sometimes But just the ones YOU HURT during it."

Fightful Select previously reported that several sources confirmed James resigned rather than being fired. Road Dogg has consistently been a vocal supporter of Green, famously questioning why WWE took so long to recognize her potential after she won the Interim WWE Women’s Championship at SummerSlam.
