Conrad Thompson has revealed that he would still love to launch an ECW podcast with Joey Styles when the time is right. Speaking with Sean Ross Sapp, Thompson explained that Styles is currently focused on milestones in his current career and prefers to avoid taking risks right now. Thompson compared Styles to Bruce Prichard and Tony Schiavone, noting that he could provide a unique perspective on ECW as someone who has not written a book or done many interviews.
