WWE NXT aired on October 27, 2026, live from the Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, on The CW and Netflix. The show featured action from the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic and a title defense.

In the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic, Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno defeated Vanity Project (Brad Baylor and Ricky Smokes) with Myka Lockwood. During the show, Tony D'Angelo left the building after discovering Grayson Waller was at his house, while Cruz Montana challenged Dion Lennox.

In other action, Chantel Monroe defeated Lizzy Rain, and Sean Legacy defeated Saquon Shugars. Izzi Dame also appeared in a vignette, promising to expose ZARIA. During the second hour, Myles Borne and Tavion Heights had a face-to-face, confirming a Weapons Wild Match for Halloween Havoc. Robert Stone announced that Dion Lennox will face Cruz Montana in an Unsanctioned Match next week. The show concluded with the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic match between Hank and Tank and The Wagner Brothers.
