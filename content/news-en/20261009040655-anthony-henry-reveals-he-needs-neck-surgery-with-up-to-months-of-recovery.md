Anthony Henry is facing a serious health setback as he revealed on social media that he requires neck surgery. The WorkHorsemen member explained that doctors discovered a herniated disc pressing against his spinal cord, a diagnosis he described as frightening.

> I haven't been active on here, but I at least wanted to give any fans of myself or the #Workhorsemen an update. My neck is kinda screwed. I will need surgery.

> The herniated disc is on the spinal cord itself, which makes this even more scary.

Regarding his recovery timeline, Henry stated that he expects to be sidelined for three to six months depending on the success of the procedure. For now, the focus remains on the surgery and ensuring the spinal issue is resolved safely before considering a return to the ring. Henry has been a key part of the WorkHorsemen tag team alongside JD Drake, but this injury will force him to take a significant break from professional wrestling.
