Joe Hendry stated that a potential heel turn in WWE remains entirely possible, according to Fightful, noting that anything can happen in the industry. He has spent recent years as a fan favorite during his time in TNA, NXT, and the WWE main roster, although his past work occasionally leaned toward villainous traits through satirical songs and other elements.

While speaking on the Michael Fairman Channel, Hendry was asked about the possibility of turning heel in WWE, responding that he would never rule it out and explaining that his current television persona was actually built to draw hatred.

> "Yes. I mean, never say never. You just never know. Anything can happen in the WWE. It's funny. I originally, this is the character that I've designed and the person that I'm portraying was designed to be hated and people just happened to like it you know so."
