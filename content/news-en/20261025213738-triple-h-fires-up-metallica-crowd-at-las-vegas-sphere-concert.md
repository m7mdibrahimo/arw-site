Triple H got a crowd fired up before Metallica took the stage at the Sphere in Las Vegas over the weekend. The WWE Chief Content Officer attended the band's residency alongside his wife, Stephanie McMahon, and was caught on camera rallying fans before the show started. Footage shared on Facebook showed him playing to thousands of fans, while Stephanie captured the moment on camera. Triple H also took to X on October 23 to share his reaction, writing, "Metallica at The Sphere… F’N INCREDIBLE!!!" Metallica's residency features 24 shows at the venue, with performances scheduled through March 13, 2027.
