Orange Cassidy has revealed that he built a ring in his backyard after making the decision to try out professional wrestling. Cassidy, known for his unique sloth style, has been active in the industry for over 22 years and has been a staple of AEW programming since its inception in 2019. Speaking in a new interview with Show Me St Louis, Cassidy discussed his early days in the business and how watching wrestling on television inspired him to take action. He explained that after seeing a show in person, he was completely hooked and decided to construct his own ring using scraps of wood to wrestle with his neighborhood friends.
