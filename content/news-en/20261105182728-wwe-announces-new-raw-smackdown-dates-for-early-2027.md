WWE has announced eleven new dates for early 2027, featuring nine new television tapings for WWE RAW and WWE SmackDown across January and February. Tickets for the events will go on sale Wednesday, November 11 at 10 a.m. local time, with an exclusive presale beginning on Tuesday, November 10. The newly added schedule includes stops in cities such as San Antonio, Chicago, Seattle, Dallas, and Atlanta, along with a WWE Holiday Tour event and a Road to Royal Rumble show in Vancouver, Canada.
