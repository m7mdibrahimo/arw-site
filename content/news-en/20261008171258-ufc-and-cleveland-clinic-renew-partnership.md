UFC and the Cleveland Clinic have announced a multiyear extension of their partnership, with UFC providing a one million dollar contribution to support the Professional Athletes Brain Health Study through 2030. WWE and Zuffa Boxing, which operate alongside UFC under TKO Group Holdings, will also join the research initiative through financial support and athlete participation. Led by Dr. Charles Bernick at the Lou Ruvo Center for Brain Health in Las Vegas, the study examines the long-term effects of repetitive head trauma and aims to identify indicators for chronic traumatic encephalopathy. Since joining the research in 2011, UFC has committed over three million dollars across four rounds of funding. The study has enrolled more than nine hundred active and retired athletes from various sports, offering voluntary and ongoing health assessments.
