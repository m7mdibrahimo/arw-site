Johnnie Robbie has signed a contract with CMLL. The five-year veteran confirmed the news to OutKick, stating that she is no longer a wrestler "living in purgatory." Robbie explained that she moved to Mexico to improve her skills, initially expecting no matches before her work ethic earned her more opportunities. She emphasized that she wants the world to see her abilities now that she has officially joined the promotion.

> "CMLL is where the best train, where the best perform, and I came here to compete with the best. Now that I've signed with CMLL, I want people to see who Johnnie Robbie is."

Robbie expressed her desire to carry the promotion and face top stars such as Persephone. She also noted her willingness to compete in AEW, Ring of Honor, and other CMLL partner promotions against names like Skadi, Olympia, AZM, and Athena. Robbie has been working dates for the company for a year, with her most recent appearance taking place on November 7.
