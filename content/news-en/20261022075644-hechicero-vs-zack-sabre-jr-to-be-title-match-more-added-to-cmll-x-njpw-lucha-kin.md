Zack Sabre Jr. will challenge Hechicero for the CMLL World Heavyweight Championship at the CMLL x NJPW Lucha Kingdom event on November 16th at Arena Mexico. This match was upgraded to a title contest after Sabre issued a challenge backstage at King of Pro-Wrestling and Hechicero successfully defended his championship against Liam Slater in the UK.

Sabre previously challenged Hechicero for the title at CMLL's Anniversary show in September in a three-way match that also included Claudio Castagnoli, but Hechicero retained his gold without Sabre being involved in the final decision. The event lineup also now includes a singles match between Soberano Jr. and Shota Umino. Additionally, an eight-man tag team match has been added, featuring Zandokan Jr., Difunto, Barboza, and Furia Roja facing off against KUSHIDA, Black Tiger, Yutani, and Shoma Kato.
