Billy Gunn's AEW tenure has involved a significant amount of in-ring action, far exceeding his initial expectations. Gunn, who originally joined the promotion primarily as a backstage coach, eventually found himself regularly competing alongside his sons, Austin and Colten, as part of The Gunn Club, and later teaming with The Acclaimed. Reflecting on his time with the company on "Inside The Ropes," the 62-year-old veteran admitted he did not anticipate a career resurgence at this stage of his life. Gunn noted that his initial contract was limited to 12 dates a year, but his role expanded rapidly. "Yeah, so my contract, I had 12 dates a year. Like, I really wasn't gonna wrestle, but I told Tony [Khan], 'If you need me to, I'll do some here and here and there,'" Gunn explained. He revealed he held two separate contracts—one as a performer and another for his backstage duties. "And then, lo and behold, they crack me out and then I'm working both shows. I'm working all the time, about to fall apart," he joked. Despite the heavy workload, Gunn remains confident in his ability to keep up with younger talent, crediting his consistent training regimen. "No matter what they do, I can still — it's going to hurt a little while — I can go if I need to," he said. Gunn emphasized that while he knows he is at an age where he is not expected to be in the ring, he continues to perform because he maintains his fitness and enjoys the work.
