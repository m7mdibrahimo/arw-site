Becky Lynch has been finishing her opponents with the Manhandle Slam for years, but she originally had a much more complicated setup in mind. During an appearance on So You Think You Can Sports with Elliott Glazer, Lynch explained that she initially used the move as a pumphandle maneuver similar to The Rock’s Rock Bottom.

She noted that her original version involved hooking her opponent’s arm, reaching under their legs to grab the other arm, and then executing the slam. However, she eventually dropped the more complicated setup because it was too difficult to position correctly. Instead, she decided to simplify the maneuver and adopt The Rock's finishing move as her own with his blessing.

> "The Rock's finishing maneuver. He hooks an arm here, and then I started doing it where I would get another arm, and I would grab it from under their legs, and then I would slam them."

> "I stopped doing that part because it was just kind of hard to get into, and then I just took The Rock's finishing maneuver and just took it and used it as my own. And kindly, he allowed me to do so. He's the best."
