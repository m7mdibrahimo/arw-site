As WWE Money in the Bank 2026 approaches, Wrestling Inc. staff analyzed the upcoming card, identifying the most anticipated matches and those that have failed to generate excitement. The event features two ladder matches, a grudge match between Bronson Reed and Oba Femi, and two world championship bouts. The Women's Money in the Bank ladder match is a major draw, showcasing a talented lineup of emerging stars including Sol Ruca, Lola Vice, Jacy Jayne, Lash Legend, Roxanne Perez, and Tiffany Stratton. Conversely, the triple threat match for the women's world title between Stephanie Vaquer, Liv Morgan, and Becky Lynch is viewed as a dud due to a lackluster build and a repetitive storyline. The Men's Money in the Bank ladder match is another highlight, featuring a competitive field of CM Punk, Kevin Owens, Bron Breakker, Je'Von Evans, Trick Williams, and Penta. Finally, the World Heavyweight Championship match between Roman Reigns and LA Knight is considered a dud, with critics citing a predictable outcome and a weak promotional buildup.
