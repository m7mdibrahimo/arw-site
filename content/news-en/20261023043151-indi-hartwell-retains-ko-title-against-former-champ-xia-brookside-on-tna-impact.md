Indi Hartwell successfully defended her Knockouts World Championship against former champion Xia Brookside in an impromptu match during the latest episode of TNA iMPACT. Hartwell, who recently captured the title at Bound for Glory, interrupted Daria Rae's return promo, leading Rae to immediately book the championship bout despite Hartwell not wearing ring gear. Brookside quickly targeted Hartwell's left arm, but the champion fought through the pain to hit a big boot and a slam for the winning pinfall. Following the match, Zoey Serrano and Shayna Baszler ambushed Hartwell, with Baszler choking out the champion while Rae watched from the entrance ramp.
