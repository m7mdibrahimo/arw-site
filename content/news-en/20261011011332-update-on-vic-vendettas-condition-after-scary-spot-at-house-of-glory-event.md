Fans have received positive news regarding Vic Vendetta following a frightening injury scare at the House of Glory Salvation event on October 9. During the show, Vendetta attempted a shooting star press but under-rotated, landing directly on his head and neck. The match was immediately halted as medical staff and referees rushed to the ring to provide aid. Due to New York State Athletic Commission regulations, the event was paused until a replacement ambulance arrived after the initial one transported Vendetta to the hospital. House of Glory issued an official statement on Twitter the following day confirming that Vendetta had been evaluated and is currently in recovery.

> "Following last night's event, Vic Vendetta was evaluated at the hospital. He's doing well and recovering. Thank you to everyone who checked in and sent their support. Wishing him a smooth recovery. ❤️" While the promotion did not disclose specific details regarding his injuries or a return timetable, the update confirms he is doing well. Fans can breathe a sigh of relief knowing he is recovering after a potentially life-threatening situation.
