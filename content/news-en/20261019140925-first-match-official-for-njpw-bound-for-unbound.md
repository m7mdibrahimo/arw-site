NJPW and Shingo Takagi announced the first official match for the upcoming NJPW Bound For Unbound event on November 20. Daiki Nagai will face his teammate Drilla Moloney in a singles contest.

Korakuen Hall will host the very first self-produced card by Unbound Co. on November 20. A video posted on Shingo Takagi's YouTube channel revealed details from the initial creative meeting, which led to this bout announcement.

Nagai challenged Moloney to the match with encouragement from his stablemates, stating his intent to finish the bout using the Drilla Killa. Although Moloney has mentored Nagai and previously permitted the Young Lion to use his signature move in tag team action, he intends to hold nothing back against his junior heavyweight rival in this special challenge.
