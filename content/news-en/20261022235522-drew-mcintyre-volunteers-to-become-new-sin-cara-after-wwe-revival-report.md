Drew McIntyre has joked about becoming the new Sin Cara after reports emerged that WWE has discussed bringing the masked character back with a new wrestler. McIntyre reacted to the news on X by sharing a recent video of himself performing a flying arm drag during training, along with the message, "I’m ready." The timing aligned with a video he posted earlier where he successfully executed the high-flying move and shouted "Olé!," followed by an Instagram joke about adding Lucha libre to his arsenal. According to PWInsider, there have been early internal pitches to resurrect the Sin Cara character, though nothing has been finalized and no specific talent has been chosen. Meanwhile, the previous Sin Cara recently expressed interest in returning to WWE to wear the mask again. While McIntyre is unlikely to don the mask himself, he continues to have fun with online rumors as he prepares for his eventual in-ring return.
