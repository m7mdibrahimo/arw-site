AEW had specific plans for Samoa Joe at AEW Full Gear, but nationwide travel disruption prevented him from making the show. Joe was scheduled to make a surprise appearance during Jack Perry's match against The Beast Mortos during the Full Gear Tailgate Brawl pre-show, but he was unable to travel to Phoenix.

Speaking on Wrestling Observer Radio, Dave Meltzer explained that Joe was among several talent affected by the widespread travel issues. Bryan Alvarez noted that Joe was not advertised for a match, but Meltzer clarified that the former AEW World Champion was set to interfere in Perry's bout as part of their ongoing storyline. The travel chaos stemmed from an FAA grounding of nationwide flights following a telecommunications outage, which created massive delays throughout the weekend. To get talent to Phoenix, AEW rented three private jets to transport stranded performers, though many roster members had already arrived earlier in the week following Dynamite and Collision tapings.

AEW President Tony Khan confirmed prior to the event that the main pay-per-view card remained intact, but acknowledged that the pre-show required changes due to travel delays. During the match itself, Perry defeated Mortos using Joe's signature Muscle Buster move, continuing their rivalry despite Joe's absence. The two are expected to continue their storyline on upcoming episodes of AEW Dynamite.
