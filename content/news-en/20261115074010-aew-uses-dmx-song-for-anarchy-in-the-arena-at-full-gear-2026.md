The 2026 AEW Full Gear pay-per-view featured an Anarchy in the Arena match between The Cut, consisting of Jay White, Ace Austin, Austin Gunn, Clark Connors, Colten Gunn, and David Finlay, and the team of Jon Moxley, Daniel Garcia, Gabe Kidd, Orange Cassidy, Kyle O’Reilly, and Roderick Strong, according to Fightful.

The bout began with Wild Thing playing before transitioning to Party Up by the late DMX. The music stopped after several cycles as fans chanted for it to return, coinciding with The Cut attempting to recruit Gabe Kidd, who refused their offer before the track resumed.
