Tyrese Haliburton debuted new D-Generation X-inspired shoes during a preseason game against the Milwaukee Bucks. The sneakers featured an all-green design with subtle black accents and the Puma logo. The Bucks ultimately won the game in overtime. Haliburton recorded 17 points and six assists, shooting 7-for-14 from the field. This is not the first time Haliburton has worn DX-themed footwear; in March 2025, he wore All-Pro NITRO PE DX shoes inspired by Triple H while playing against the same team. Haliburton is currently sidelined with a hamstring injury. He returned to action this season after missing the entire 2025-26 season due to a torn Achilles tendon, wearing a Triple H denim jacket for his first game back.
