According to PWInsider, LA Knight is backstage at the November 16, 2026 episode of WWE RAW in Raleigh, North Carolina, and is slated to make his television comeback. He is anticipated to join Team USA during Cody Rhodes' opening segment for the upcoming Survivor Series: WarGames match against Team Mexico. Knight has been absent from programming for more than a month following an assault by Bronson Reed. That absence occurred shortly after his World Heavyweight Championship match against Roman Reigns at Money in the Bank on October 10, 2026. Additionally, Raquel Rodriguez will defend the Women's Intercontinental Championship against IYO SKY, and The Usos will face Carmelo Hayes and Cruz Montana to decide which faction gains the WarGames advantage.
