ROH Tag Team Champions El Sky Team, consisting of Mistico and Mascara Dorada, successfully retained their titles against The Swirl, featuring Lee Johnson and Blake Christian, on "AEW Dynamite/Collision." This marked the fourth title defense for Mistico and Dorada since capturing the championships at "CMLL Viernes Espectacular" in June. Johnson and Christian, who are the reigning ROH Six-Man Tag Team Champions alongside Jay Lethal, were aiming to become double champions.

The champions started strong, but The Swirl eventually isolated Dorada to take control. The match opened up as Mistico tagged in, leading to a back-and-forth sequence. During the action, Mistico took a heavy-looking Destroyer on the apron, causing the referee to check on him. Mistico recovered to secure the victory for his team by countering Johnson with a La Magistral cradle for the pinfall.
