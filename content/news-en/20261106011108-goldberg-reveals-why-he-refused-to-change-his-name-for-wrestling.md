Goldberg has addressed why he chose to keep his real name when he started his professional wrestling career. During an appearance on the BIFF podcast, the WWE Hall of Famer explained that because he was already well-known from his time playing college football at the University of Georgia and in the NFL with the Atlanta Falcons, adopting a new persona would not have been believable. Beyond the branding aspect, Goldberg emphasized that he wanted to maintain his identity as a source of pride and a positive example for his community. He noted that he has no interest in hiding his Jewish heritage, especially given the rise in antisemitism he observes online today. Goldberg shared that while he finds the hostility he sees on the internet to be "disgusting," he prefers to focus his energy on his family and using his platform to promote logic and facts rather than engaging with online trolls. He also expressed deep concern regarding the impact of this hostility on students in schools and colleges, adding that he is working with friends to combat hatred and focus on education.
