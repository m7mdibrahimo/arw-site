WWE has signed former sumo wrestler and college football player Hidetora Hanada to an NXT contract, according to PWInsider. The 24-year-old Japanese athlete previously competed internationally in sumo before playing college football for Colorado State and participating in WWE's NIL program. Hanada attended the Royal Rumble in Indianapolis and expressed excitement about experiencing WWE's production and atmosphere firsthand. There is no official word yet on when he will make his NXT debut.
