WWE presents WWE NXT on October 27, 2026, from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, starting at 8 pm ET. Viewers in the United States can watch the show on the CW Network, while international fans can stream it on Netflix.

The match card features Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic action with Vanity Project taking on Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno, and Hank and Tank facing The Wagner Brothers. In addition, Jackson Drake defends the NXT North American Championship against E.K. Prosper, and Lizzy Rain goes one-on-one with Chantel Monroe.
