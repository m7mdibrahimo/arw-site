According to Wrestling Inc., AEW Fright Night Dynamite on October 28, 2026, emanated live from the Chaifetz Arena in St. Louis, Missouri. In tag team action, Jay White and David Finlay defeated Jon Moxley and Daniel Garcia after interference from Clark Connors, leading to a post-match challenge by Moxley for an Anarchy In The Arena Match at AEW Full Gear on November 14 against The Cut. Kyle Fletcher defeated Jack Perry in singles competition, after which Mark Davis and Jake Doyle hoisted Fletcher on his shoulders as he issued a challenge to AEW World Champion Will Ospreay for the world title at Full Gear. New Level appeared in an interview segment with Renee Paquette to discuss their upcoming AEW World Trios Championship match at AEW Full Gear, interrupted by Brody King, Adam Page, and Prince Nana revealing a bloodied Bandido. In match three of a best of five series, The Motor City Machine Guns defeated FTR, though FTR attacked them after the bell. Tommaso Ciampa addressed fans regarding his pursuit of the AEW National Championship before being interrupted by Ilja Dragunov. Marina Shafir defeated Rachel Armstrong via submission, followed by a confrontation with Mercedes Moné and a chaotic backstage brawl involving multiple stables. In the TNT Championship match, Darby Allin successfully defended his title against Josh Alexander with assistance from the returning Steven Borden in a clown mask. In the main event, Queen Aminata defeated Persephone with interference from Britt Baker to win the TBS Championship, celebrating with several roster members as the show concluded.
