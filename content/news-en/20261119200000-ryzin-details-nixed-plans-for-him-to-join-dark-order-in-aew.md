During his career in AEW, RYZIN made frequent appearances on AEW Dark and AEW Dark: Elevation, stepping into the ring against notable opponents like Jack Perry, Powerhouse Hobbs (Royce Keys), and the Lucha Brothers.

In a recent interview, RYZIN revealed that he was initially scheduled to join the Dark Order under a special designation, which involved changing his gear colors and dyeing a portion of his hair purple. However, those plans ultimately fell through due to various factors.

He additionally discussed a separate concept that would have teamed him up with Nyla Rose and Vickie Guerrero on Being The Elite, featuring comedic backstage segments, but management ultimately rejected the pitch because he lacked an official contract with the promotion.
