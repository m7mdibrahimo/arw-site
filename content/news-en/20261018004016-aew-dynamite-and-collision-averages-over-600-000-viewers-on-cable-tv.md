AEW Dynamite and Collision averaged 614,000 viewers on TBS for the October 14 episode, according to Wrestlenomics. This is an increase from the 495,000 viewers who tuned into the Tuesday episode on October 6, which competed with NXT. The Wednesday episode drew a 0.12 rating in the 18 to 49 demographic, up from 0.11 the previous week. Dynamite brought in 715,000 viewers with a 0.14 demo rating, while Collision pulled in 514,000 viewers with a 0.10 demo rating. The previous three-hour block on September 23 averaged 567,000 viewers and a 0.09 demo rating. Additionally, Dynamite was simulcast on HBO Max, though viewership data for that platform is not currently available.
