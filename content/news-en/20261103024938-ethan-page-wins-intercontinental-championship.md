Ethan Page pulled off a major upset on WWE RAW after defeating Chad Gable to become the new Intercontinental Champion. The opening match took place on Monday, November 2, 2026, at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn, New York, with Page ending Gable's championship reign.

Gable controlled the early going with his amateur wrestling background before Page turned the tide by attacking his neck and upper back. Gable fought back with submission attempts, but Page survived and ultimately connected with an Egoplex to secure the pinfall and the title.
