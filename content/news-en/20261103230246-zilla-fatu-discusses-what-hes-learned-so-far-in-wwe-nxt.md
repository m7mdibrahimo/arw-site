Zilla Fatu made a significant impact on "WWE NXT" in August by interrupting a number one contender's match for the NXT Championship. Since then, he has engaged in a feud with BirthRight and recently secured a victory over Charlie Dempsey at Halloween Havoc. In a recent appearance on "Busted Open Radio," Fatu discussed his transition from the independent scene, including his time in House of Glory, to WWE.

"I think the big transition is trying to get rid of these independent wrestling habits and trying to transition into the TV way," Fatu said. "I didn't realize how much detail that this business is. I didn't realize you've got to work certain roaming cameras. You gotta work this, you gotta know how to work your face at certain cameras at certain times... I thought I knew everything, but as soon as I got here on my first day, I find out I didn't know a damn thing."

Fatu noted that this realization has motivated him to learn as much as possible from his coaches. He is focused on absorbing knowledge from figures like Shawn Michaels and is working to improve both his in-ring performance and microphone skills. "You guys are witnessing Zilla come out of his caterpillar phase," he said. "I think me just wanting to spread my wings, I'm just trying to figure out who I am right now. That's what it is, I'm trying to figure out who Zilla Fatu is and that's the stage that we're at right now."
