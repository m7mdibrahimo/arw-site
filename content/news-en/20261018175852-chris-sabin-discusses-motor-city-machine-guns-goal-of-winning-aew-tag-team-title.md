Chris Sabin spoke about the Motor City Machine Guns potentially becoming the first team to hold tag team titles across major wrestling promotions. In a recent interview, Sabin noted that capturing the AEW World Tag Team Titles alongside their previous reigns in TNA, WWE, and NJPW would be a unique achievement. He stated that this remains their primary goal as a team. Additionally, Sabin discussed AEW's tag team division during the conversation.
