Josh Barnett recently shared his thoughts on Jake Hager's transition to Power Slap, questioning the decision during a new interview with Ariel Helwani. Hager signed with the company earlier this year and made his debut on April 17, later facing Johnny Magna in September for his third match. During that bout, Hager suffered an eye injury that led to a referee stoppage defeat. Barnett criticized Hager's involvement in the sport, pointing out his collegiate wrestling background as a Division 1 All-American, his tenure in WWE and AEW, and his fights in Bellator. Barnett questioned the logic behind the move, stating that there are much better career options available, including a return to MMA, and concluded that the risks simply are not worth it, especially after suffering an eye injury. Elsewhere in the conversation, Barnett discussed the future of Bloodsport.
