Matt Hardy recently discussed his experience booking an episode of TNA iMPACT and whether he wants to take on that role again. The Hardys handled the creative duties for the post-Bound for Glory edition of TNA iMPACT that aired on Thursday, October 15. Hardy explained on The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy that TNA booker Delirious proposed the idea to celebrate their 35th anniversary in the industry.

> "It was extremely interesting. So, it had been out that October 15th was like our wrestling anniversary," he began.

Once in charge, they immediately wanted a ladder match and planned to feature Leon Slater in the opening segment depending on the outcome of Bound for Glory. The brothers also brainstormed ideas for the Nemeths, who squared off in a singles match that evening.

> "It was fun. I enjoyed it," he said.

Hardy noted that the broadcast received positive feedback and a boost in viewership. He also expressed openness to becoming a lead booker down the line if the right opportunity presents itself.
