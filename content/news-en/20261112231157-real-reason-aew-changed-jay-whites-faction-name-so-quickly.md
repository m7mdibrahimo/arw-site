Tony Khan has addressed the rapid rebranding of Jay White’s new faction, which saw the group switch from "The Last Outfit" to "The Cut" almost as quickly as the first name was announced. Khan clarified that the change was not a reaction to negative fan feedback on social media. According to Khan, he had a clear vision for the group and consulted with the members to refine the name. During these discussions, the phrase "the last outfit" was suggested to describe the group as the last outfit of an operation. Khan noted that while fans seemed interested in the faction itself, something felt incomplete. He proposed the name "The Cut" to Jay White, who approved the change immediately. The decision was finalized on the same day, resulting in the quick transition that left fans surprised. Khan emphasized that he views the faction as a major force in AEW, noting the significant Bullet Club history shared by the members, including Jay White, Juice Robinson, Ace Austin, Austin Gunn, Colten Gunn, David Finlay, and Clark Connors. He expects the group to create significant challenges for top teams like the Death Riders and The Conglomeration.
