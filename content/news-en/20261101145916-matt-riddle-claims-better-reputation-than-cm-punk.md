Matt Riddle believes his overall reputation is much better than CM Punk. During an interview in New Zealand, Riddle discussed how his mixed martial arts career helped him transition into professional wrestling, pointing out his superior reputation both inside and outside the cage.

Commenting on the matter, Riddle stated:

> "Well, I got a better reputation than CM Punk just in general. Yeah, you know, but he's looking good these days. I'll say he's working out, you know. Good for him. Nothing but kudos."

This statement comes as a notable shift compared to his previous remarks in August 2025 when he criticized Punk's abilities. Despite the ongoing disagreement between the two, these comments arrived in a less harsh style accompanied by some praise.
