Former WWE and AEW star Maria Kanellis recently opened up about her professional wrestling future and her time away from the industry. During an appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Kanellis reflected on her departure from AEW and her personal health battles, noting that she has been busy working as a florist and event planner in Nashville while raising her children. Kanellis admitted that she spent considerable time pondering her next steps following her exit. "Because I was in AEW and I was in ROH and I had a tumor and I had skin cancer and I had this and I had that," Kanellis said. "So, it was a lot of self-reflection. Luckily, I have a husband that's just allowed me to take my time to figure out, 'What do I want to do next?' I finally have found something, So, now I'm all in, but before that, it was very much like, 'Eh. I don't know. I don't know. I miss performing and I miss this.' It was really hard." Regarding a potential return to the ring, Kanellis made it clear that she has not completely shut the door on the possibility, though a full-time schedule is unlikely. "I haven't closed the door, but it's not always up to the person," she said. "Wrestling, probably not, unless it's like the Royal Rumble or another Evolution pay-per-view or something like that. But, consistently wrestling? Probably not."
