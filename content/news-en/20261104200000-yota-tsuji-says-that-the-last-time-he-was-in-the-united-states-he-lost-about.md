IWGP Heavyweight Champion Yota Tsuji recently discussed his activities while visiting the United States for work, sharing his enjoyment of gambling during international trips. In a new interview with Fightful Overbooked's In The Weeds, Tsuji recalled a recent visit to a casino. "So one of his routine when he goes to shows internationally is that he goes to casino and, you know, enjoyed it a little bit. So last time he came to US for the Chicago G1 opener, he went to casino and he lost about $1,000 within like 30 minutes. So he needed to bounce off kind of early. So I'm trying to get early bets this time." When asked about his preferred casino games, Tsuji named poker and blackjack. He also spoke about his upcoming appearance with MLW in the same interview.
