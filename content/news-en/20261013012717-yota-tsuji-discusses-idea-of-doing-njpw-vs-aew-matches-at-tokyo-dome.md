Yota Tsuji suggested the idea of holding an inter-promotional event between NJPW and AEW at the Tokyo Dome, pointing out that top AEW talent has been appearing frequently in New Japan recently. Following the King of Pro-Wrestling event, where Kenny Omega and Jon Moxley returned and Omega ambushed Tsuji and Oleg Boltin, the NJPW star addressed the situation with the press.

"These days, AEW's top fighters are coming one after another. So, to establish a truly equal relationship, I think it would be interesting to hold a full-scale inter-promotional match at the Tokyo Dome," Tsuji said. He added, "If I beat Kenny in Hiroshima, I want AEW to select a 'true top of the top' once again and send them to face me."

Tsuji also noted that New Japan wrestlers are beginning to realize they cannot remain overshadowed by AEW. A non-title three-way match featuring Tsuji, Omega, and Oleg Boltin is scheduled for NJPW Power Struggle on November 3rd, where a victory for Omega would earn him an IWGP Heavyweight Title shot.
