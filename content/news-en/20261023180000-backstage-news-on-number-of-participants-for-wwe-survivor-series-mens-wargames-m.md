This year's Survivor Series will feature a WarGames match with five wrestlers on each team. Reports indicate that The Bloodline will likely face off against the 946 faction led by Royce Keys. Both groups have featured four members in their storyline so far, with Jacob Fatu and Sami Zayn as potential additions for The Bloodline. Survivor Series 2026 is scheduled to take place in Houston, Texas, on November 28.
