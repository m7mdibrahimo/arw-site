Footage has emerged showing Stephanie Vaquer struggling to walk and receiving assistance from referee Jessika Carr after WWE Money in the Bank. Vaquer was visibly limping and had to sit on the steel steps before heading backstage, following a match where she lost the Women's World Championship to Liv Morgan. Fans have raised concerns about a potential injury after Vaquer landed awkwardly during a crossbody to the outside earlier in the bout. WWE has not yet released an official statement regarding her condition, leaving it unclear if the injury is legitimate or simply the result of a brutal match.
