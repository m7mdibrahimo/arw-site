Action figures for the late Jeff Cannonball are currently in development, with all proceeds going to support his family. Cannonball passed away at the age of 39 following a battle with ALS, and his funeral was held on October 13th in New Jersey after tributes poured in from fans and wrestlers alike. Promoter and wrestler Danny Demanto announced the news on X, stating that the figures are coming soon and full reveals will be shared shortly. Cannonball began his in-ring career in 2010 and competed for GCW, MLW, CZW, and many other promotions before his final match in December 2024.
