The film Street Fighter, starring Roman Reigns, Cody Rhodes, and Hirooki Goto, is scheduled to hit theaters on Friday, October 16, with early preview showtimes beginning on Thursday. Paramount has lifted the critical review embargo for the movie, and with the first 29 reviews tallied, the film holds a 34% critical rating on Rotten Tomatoes. Additionally, the movie holds a 46/100 rating on Metacritic as of publication time.
