Darren Young is officially back in WWE in a very different role. Nine years after his release, Young revealed that he is now working with WWE NXT as a coach to help develop the next generation at the Performance Center. Young announced the news on X, noting that the date made his return even more emotional as it was exactly nine years to the day since his release. "Nine years ago today, I was released from @WWE. Today, I’m back at @wwenxt, fighting back tears of joy every time I pull up to the Performance Center and grateful for the opportunity to give back to the next generation."

Young shared photos from his return, including one with Shawn Michaels at the Performance Center, noting that he now gets to experience the developmental system firsthand. "I’m always hearing what WWE is looking for NOW I’m able to see it for myself and be a sponge in this environment." He also reflected on his past as a trainee and a member of The Nexus on the original NXT in 2010. "And when you talk about NXT, don’t forget the ORIGINAL NXT! MR. NO DAYS OFF baby! Nine years later, still standing, still inspiring, and STILL NO DAYS OFF!" Young thanked WWE for bringing him back and made it clear he is ready to guide the future stars.
