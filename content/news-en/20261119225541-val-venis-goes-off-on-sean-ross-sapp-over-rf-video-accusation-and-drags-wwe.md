Val Venis and Sean Ross Sapp are clashing on social media after Sapp brought up Venis' past association with RF Video, the company founded by Rob Feinstein. Sapp criticized Venis for accepting money to do an interview with someone involved in a 2004 online sting. Venis fired back with a lengthy post, arguing that hundreds of prominent wrestlers, including Bret Hart, Dusty Rhodes, Eddie Guerrero, AJ Styles, and Rob Van Dam, also did business with the company over the years.

Venis accused Sapp of guilt by association and questioned whether a journalist should be held responsible for the actions of individuals they have interviewed. The argument then shifted to political differences, with Venis criticizing Sapp's views on abortion. Venis has a history of social media disputes, but this time he used wrestling history to challenge Sapp's criticism.
