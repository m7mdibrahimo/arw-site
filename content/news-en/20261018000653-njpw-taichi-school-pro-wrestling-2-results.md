New Japan Pro-Wrestling hosted its NJPW Taichi School Pro Wrestling 2 event on October 17, 2026, at the Ishikari Municipal Hanakawa Junior High School Gymnasium in Ishikari, Hokkaido, Japan, according to Fightful. The show was broadcast live on NJPW World.

In the main event, Dangerous Tekkers secured the victory against their opponents. Full results from the event are listed below.

Masatora Yasuda def. Ryoma Tsukamoto
Ryusuke Taguchi def. Dick Togo
YOH def. Satoshi Kojima
Bishamon def. Kumagoro Hokkai & Lonely Wolf
Yuya Uemura def. TAKA Michinoku
El Desperado & Toru Yano def. Unbound Co.
Dangerous Tekkers def. Naomichi Marufuji & Yoshinobu Kanemaru
