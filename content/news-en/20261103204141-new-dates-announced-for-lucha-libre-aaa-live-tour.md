Lucha Libre AAA has added several new dates to its upcoming live tour. The following stops have been confirmed for the tour: November 29th at the Ector County Coliseum in Odessa, Texas; December 26th at the Sacramento Memorial Auditorium in Sacramento, California; and December 27th at the Selland Arena in Fresno, California. The next installment of AAA programming will be the third week of Héroes Inmortales, airing on November 7th.
