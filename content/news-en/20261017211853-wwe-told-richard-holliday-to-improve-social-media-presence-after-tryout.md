Richard Holliday revealed that WWE told him to improve his social media presence after he was not offered a contract following his September 2023 tryout. The tryout came just months after he finished chemotherapy for Hodgkin lymphoma. Although WWE passed on signing him, the advice motivated him to heavily invest in his online marketing. Now, Holliday considers himself one of the top personalities on the independent wrestling scene, noting that his social media efforts are directly resulting in actual bookings and financial gains.
