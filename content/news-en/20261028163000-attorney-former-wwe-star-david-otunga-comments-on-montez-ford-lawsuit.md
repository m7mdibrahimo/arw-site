Former WWE star and attorney David Otunga shared his legal perspective on the recent lawsuit involving Montez Ford. Noel Lucas, a security guard, filed a lawsuit against Ford alleging that he received an unwanted lap dance during the July 6 episode of WWE Raw. After reviewing new footage from a different angle, Otunga stated on his YouTube channel that Lucas has a valid civil case regarding liability and damages.

Otunga explained that while Ford was likely trying to entertain the crowd and make Lucas laugh, intent does not excuse crossing legal boundaries. He noted that the footage shows repeated contact without consent, which is sufficient for a battery claim since a bruise or broken bone is not required. Otunga added that the rules regarding battery apply equally to men and women, emphasizing that being a male security guard does not mean someone has to accept unwanted physical contact. He concluded that WWE could potentially settle the case to resolve the matter.
