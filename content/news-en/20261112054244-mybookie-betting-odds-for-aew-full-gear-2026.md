MyBookie has released the betting odds for All Elite Wrestling's upcoming 2026 Full Gear pay-per-view, which is scheduled for November 14th in Phoenix, Arizona. In the AEW World Championship match, Will Ospreay is listed at -300 against Kyle Fletcher at +200. For the AEW World Trios Championship Texas Death Match, Hangman Page, Brody King, and Bandido are -140 favorites over Swerve Strickland and New Level at +110. In the AEW Women's World Tag Team Championship bout, The Brawling Birds are -140 against Wrathlander at +110. The AEW World Tag Team Championship features The Young Bucks at -200 facing Mark Davis and Jake Doyle at +150. Finally, in the AEW Women's World Championship match, Mercedes Moné is -300 against Marina Shafir at +200.
