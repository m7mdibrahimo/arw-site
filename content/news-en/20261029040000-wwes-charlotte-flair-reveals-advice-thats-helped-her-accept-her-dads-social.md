WWE Hall of Famer Ric Flair has always been a fierce supporter of his daughter Charlotte Flair, frequently taking to social media or podcasts to criticize creative decisions that he feels do not elevate her properly. Despite being 77 years old, Ric continues to spark online controversy with his candid posts. Charlotte recently admitted on HeymanHustleTV's "The Roundtable" that she used to feel embarrassed by her father's social media activity, but a conversation with Natalya changed her perspective completely.

Charlotte recalled Natalya telling her that her father has earned the right to express himself, which helped her realize that Ric's posts stem from a place of genuine care and belief in her talent rather than just trying to stir up trouble. Following that discussion, Charlotte has eased up on her reactions, noting that she now understands his supportive intentions. Meanwhile, Ric was recently hospitalized following a fall over the weekend, but he has since been discharged and provided a health update while clarifying that his Facebook account is currently managed by a digital talent agency.
