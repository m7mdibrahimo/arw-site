AEW is facing significant travel issues just one day before Full Gear 2026, as a nationwide flight shutdown ordered by the FAA has left several wrestlers stranded. While some talent arrived in Phoenix following the November 11 Dynamite and Collision tapings, others have been unable to depart, raising concerns that they may not reach the pay-per-view on time.

Bryan Alvarez of the Wrestling Observer reported that the ground stop, triggered by a telecommunications outage affecting air traffic control, has caused major delays. "Regarding the FAA grounding all flights, many AEW wrestlers flew to the PPV straight from Dynamite, but others haven’t left yet and are stranded. The longer this goes the greater the likelihood of major delays and people not making it there at all."

Alvarez noted that some talent were warned the shutdown could last until 8 PM tonight. "There are people who have been told this ground stop may not be lifted until 8 PM tonight which would be a disaster."

This follows a week already marked by travel difficulties for AEW, as Tony Khan noted during his November 12 media call that flight issues stemming from the Chris Jericho Cruise had caused delays for talent arriving at the Dynamite and Collision tapings. AEW Full Gear 2026 is scheduled to begin at 8 PM ET on Saturday, November 14, at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona. While no matches have been canceled, the company is running out of time to resolve these logistics.
