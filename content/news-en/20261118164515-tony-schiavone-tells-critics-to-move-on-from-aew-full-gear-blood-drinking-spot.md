Tony Schiavone recently weighed in on the controversial blood-drinking moment from AEW Full Gear, admitting that while he thought it was gross, he does not understand why people are still making a fuss about it. During a recent episode of What Happened When with Conrad Thompson, Schiavone discussed the intense Texas Death Match between Swerve Strickland and Hangman Adam Page. Schiavone admitted he had managed to avoid the online backlash entirely, but he still shared his candid thoughts on the spot.

“Yeah, that was pretty gross, I have to admit. But hey, you're always going to do something that somebody doesn't like, and people love to amplify the controversial. That's what being in social media is all about. So I haven't heard any of it, and you know what? You just got to move on from it,” Schiavone said. He also pointed out that in a brutal Texas Death Match, it is nearly impossible to define where the line of going too far actually is.

Schiavone added that the blood-drinking spot was not even the wildest moment of the night for him, noting that seeing former New Day members with skewers in their heads made him realize they had truly entered AEW territory. Ultimately, Schiavone believes everyone should just accept the chaos of Full Gear and move on with their lives.
