Former AEW star Max Caster is currently taking a break from professional wrestling following the backlash he received for a rap line during his match against Moose at TNA Bound for Glory. During a recent live online signing with K&S WrestleFest, Caster addressed whether he expects the negative reactions he receives for his lyrics. He was specifically asked about the controversy surrounding a December 2022 episode of "AEW Dynamite," where he referenced Jeff Jarrett's marriage to Karen Jarrett, who was previously married to Kurt Angle. Caster admitted that he is accustomed to the criticism, stating that many people are angry at him for various reasons. When asked if he expects the heat, he replied that he has simply come to accept it. Caster's most recent controversy stemmed from his Bound for Glory appearance, where he referenced missile strikes on King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, which occurred shortly before the event. Following the match, TNA removed all references to Caster from their social media platforms and issued a statement confirming they had severed ties with him. Caster subsequently apologized online and revealed during an independent event on October 17 that he would be stepping away from the ring for an extended period.
