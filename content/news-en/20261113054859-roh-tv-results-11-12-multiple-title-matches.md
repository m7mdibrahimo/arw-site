Ring of Honor aired the latest episode of ROH TV on November 12, with the event broadcasting on HonorClub. The Premier Athletes (Ariya Daivari and Tony Nese) defeated Serpentico and Orlando Colon. In ROH Men’s Pure Championship action, Lee Moriarty successfully defended his title by defeating A.R. Fox. In an ROH Women’s Championship Proving Ground match, Athena retained her title against B3CCA, with Diamante and Billie Starkz accompanying her at ringside. Bryan Keith secured a victory over Matt Mako. Shane Taylor and Tehuti Miles teamed up to defeat Kiran Grey and Darian Bengston. The Infantry, consisting of Captain Shawn Dean and Carlie Bravo and accompanied by Christyan XO, Shane Taylor, and Tehuti Miles, defeated Isaiah Broner and Ren Jones. Robyn Renegade picked up a win against Sirena Linton. Big Boom AJ and QT Marshall issued a challenge to RPG Vice for the AEW Full Gear. In the main event for the ROH Championship, Bandido successfully defended his title against Bishop Kaun.
