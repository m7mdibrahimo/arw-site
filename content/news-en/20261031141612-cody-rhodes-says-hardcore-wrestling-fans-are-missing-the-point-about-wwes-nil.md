Cody Rhodes believes that hardcore wrestling fans are missing the bigger picture regarding WWE's NIL system. Speaking on Double Coverage, Cody explained that recruiting college athletes instead of exclusively chasing lifelong wrestling fans is essentially how the business has always operated. He pointed to historical examples like Ernie Ladd and the old territorial days, noting that promoters have always searched for athletes with the physical tools for wrestling. According to Cody, traditional wrestling fans will always find their way to the sport, using Johnny Gargano as an example of someone who was always destined to reach WWE. In contrast, the NIL system provides an opportunity to discover prospects from other sports who might never have considered professional wrestling otherwise.

Cody highlighted Oba Femi, who competed in track and field at the University of Alabama, as a prime example of an athlete discovering a passion for wrestling. He believes Femi has already established himself as someone fans can connect with long-term, comparing his high ceiling to Michael Jordan. Furthermore, Cody defended WWE's use of tryouts and the NIL program to find individuals from diverse athletic backgrounds, placing Femi alongside standout performers like Brock Lesnar, Trick Williams, and Bianca Belair. While acknowledging that athleticism alone does not guarantee stardom, Cody emphasized that exceptional physical attributes can provide a distinct advantage in the ring.
