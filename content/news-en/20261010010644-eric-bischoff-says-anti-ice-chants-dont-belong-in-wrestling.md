Former WCW President Eric Bischoff recently shared his thoughts on the growing presence of politics in professional wrestling, specifically addressing the anti-ICE chants heard during Brody King's matches in AEW. Bischoff stated that while patriotism and faith can be expressed respectfully, using religion, politics, race, or social issues simply to grab attention is disgusting and harmful to the business.

Bischoff questioned whether political messaging is genuinely what fans want to see, noting that while it may draw a reaction, it does not reflect what audiences actually pay to experience. He believes the entertainment industry has gone too far in weaponizing social and political topics, but expects the pendulum to swing back toward a more balanced culture free from forced political agendas.
