Kayla Becker recently shared high praise for Paul Heyman, noting that he paved the way for everyone in the wrestling industry who holds a microphone. During her tenure with WWE, Becker engaged in on-screen verbal sparring with Heyman on Talking Smack following its return in August 2020, which coincided with the launch of his partnership with Roman Reigns.

"It was great. Listen, we all know Paul paved the way for all of us microphone holders. It was really cool to get to spend that much like close time with him over the years. He taught me a lot. Did he get on my nerves all the freaking time? But hey, I love that man, thankful for him all the time."

Becker also named Heyman, Bayley, Thea / Zelina Vega, Sami Zayn, and Kevin Owens as the people who made her laugh the most during her time with the company. She recalled a humorous moment in Paris with Owens prior to a pay-per-view event where he chased ducks and baguettes instead of focusing on the task at hand.
