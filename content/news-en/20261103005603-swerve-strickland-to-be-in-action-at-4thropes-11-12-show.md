Swerve Strickland is scheduled for action at the upcoming 4THROPE Wrestling event. The show takes place on November 12th at the Melrose Ballroom in Queens, New York, where the former AEW World Champion will face Ninja Mack in a singles match. Before that event, Strickland teams up with The New Level, consisting of Kofi and Austin Creed, at AEW Full Gear to challenge Hangman Adam Page, Bandido, and Brody King for the AEW World Trios Titles.
