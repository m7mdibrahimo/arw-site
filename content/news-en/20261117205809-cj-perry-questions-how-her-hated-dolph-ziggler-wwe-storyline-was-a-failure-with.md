CJ Perry recently addressed the intense fan backlash toward her 2015 WWE storyline with Dolph Ziggler. Despite the angle being famously labeled as one of the worst of that year by Rolling Stone, Perry remains puzzled by the criticism given its massive reach. Speaking with Andro Mammo, Perry highlighted that the storyline was actually a major success in terms of engagement. "Yeah, it was the most viewed clip of 2015, which is so funny cuz it was also the most hated storyline by Rolling Stones," Perry stated. "And it's just like is it really that hated of it was like 85 million views." Perry admitted that the negative reception took a toll on her at the time, especially since she wanted the same respect she earned during her debut year alongside Rusev. She noted that while the storyline was polarizing, she now views that reaction as a sign of success. "It was like little bit more polarizing, which now I understand polarizing's good," she explained. "You know, John Cena's polarizing. It's okay not to be completely loved or completely hated to have mixed as long as people are talking about you." Behind the scenes, the angle was managed by Vince McMahon, who personally directed segments, including a kissing scene that the participants had to rehearse in front of Rusev. The romantic arc was eventually scrapped after Perry and Rusev’s real-life engagement became public. Reflecting on the experience, Perry concluded that she has learned not to be consumed by negative opinions, noting that performers must simply make the best of the creative direction provided to them.
