Spike Dudley has officially signed a WWE Legends deal, marking his first major business relationship with the company in over two decades. The former ECW fan favorite shared the news on Facebook, where he also hinted that new merchandise is in the works. In his announcement, Dudley expressed his gratitude to those who supported him, stating, "Very happy to announce I just signed a WWE Legends deal. Will keep you posted with merch. Thank you to all friends, family and fans that made it possible!!!!"

Spike Dudley originally joined WWE in 2001 after his successful run in ECW. He became a memorable part of the Dudley family, known for his resilience and high-risk style. During his WWE tenure, he captured the European, Hardcore, and Tag Team Championships, and in 2004, he defeated Rey Mysterio to win the Cruiserweight Championship. Following his departure in 2005, he continued his wrestling career elsewhere but did not maintain a major presence in WWE. While this new deal does not guarantee television appearances, fans can look forward to the upcoming merchandise.
