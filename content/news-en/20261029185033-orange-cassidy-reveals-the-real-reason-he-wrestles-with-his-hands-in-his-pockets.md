Orange Cassidy has built his career on a unique wrestling style, most notably his habit of competing with his hands in his pockets. During an interview on Going Ringside, Cassidy addressed why he does this and whether it makes performing moves difficult. He acknowledged that it is quite challenging, noting that people should try performing everyday tasks with their hands in their pockets to understand the difficulty. However, Cassidy explained that this behavior is a tactical choice. He uses it to make his opponents believe they have an easy advantage over him. "I just put my hands in my pockets to kind of disarm the person I'm fighting so that they think they can get the advantage of me. But then, uh, it's when I take them out of my pockets and I punch them in the face." This routine has become a signature part of his AEW persona, serving as a psychological tool to catch rivals off guard.
