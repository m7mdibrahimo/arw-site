During a recent episode of The Nikki & Brie Show, Nikki Bella recalled a chaotic night out with Brie Bella and Bryan Danielson after closing on a property for Bonita. After lingering over French champagne while waiting for Bryan in traffic, Nikki realized her phone was missing as they drove home. She took an Uber back to the speakeasy to retrieve it, only to realize she had left her wallet behind, carried her laptop instead of a purse, and brought their dog Remy without a leash or collar.

Stranded in the dark with a driver who did not speak English and a laptop that was not connected to Wi-Fi, Nikki worried about how she would manage. Fortunately, she quickly found her phone in the parking lot. The next morning, Bryan noted that the sisters had been drinking, prompting Nikki to text Brie with early-morning anxiety about how she appeared as a mother. While Bryan and Brie teased her, Nikki ultimately joked that losing her phone at least proved she was not addicted to it.
