In a recent interview on the Ring The Belle YouTube channel, Xelina, formerly known as Zelina Vega, opened up about her time in WWE and discussed a creative pitch involving Zoey Serrano, formerly known as Zoey Stark. According to Xelina, she and Chelsea Green pitched an idea where Serrano would cost her the WWE Women's United States Title and then align with Green.

> I win the title, but then, the very next show, we have a rematch and at that point, Zoey was supposed to be joining them.

Although Brian 'Road Dogg' James later discussed a similar idea on WWE: Unreal on Netflix and received criticism for it, Xelina clarified that the concept actually originated from her and Green. Creative differences ultimately led to a different direction, but Xelina expressed gratitude for holding the championship and experiencing that period in her career.
