AEW has announced a five-part documentary series titled The Toni Storm Tapes, set to premiere on Friday, November 27 at 10 a.m. ET on YouTube and MyAEW, amidst her ongoing mysterious disappearance. Storm has not been seen or heard from since March 18, 2026. According to the storyline, a suspicious package containing five analog cassette tapes arrived at AEW offices last Wednesday, featuring recordings from Storm's life prior to her disappearance. Renee Paquette narrated the announcement video, explaining that the tapes capture conversations, meetings, and moments from her former Timeless life. The series will delve into each tape across five episodes, offering a unique look at her character. The announcement concluded with a chilling message from Storm herself, stating, "If you are hearing this, I am already gone."
