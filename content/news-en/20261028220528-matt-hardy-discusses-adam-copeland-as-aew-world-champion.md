Adam Copeland recently shared his plan to retire by the end of next year, but he first wants to capture the AEW World Championship. Speaking on his podcast, The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy, his former WWE rival Matt Hardy expressed doubt that all AEW fans would warmly embrace Copeland as the champion.

> "It would be a great way to send him out. It's just sometimes I get so concerned, because even the AEW fans — somebody like Adam, who's still an amazing performer at his age, and he can still go and have good matches — a lot of times they go, 'Well, he's just an older guy,' or, 'He's a WWE guy.' ... I feel like they would be a little critical of him as champion."

Hardy noted personally that he would welcome a title reign for Copeland, believing he would not use backstage politics to win the belt and would continue supporting younger talent.

Copeland has received one title opportunity since his announcement, falling short in the Clockwork Carnage match at WrestleDream. Even so, according to Wrestling Inc., Hardy remains optimistic about Copeland's chances and agreed with co-host Jon Alba that defeating Will Ospreay for the championship might not be the ideal path.
