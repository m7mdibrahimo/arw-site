New Japan Pro-Wrestling has announced that the winners of the upcoming Super Junior Tag League will each receive a brand-new trophy to commemorate their victory. The tournament is scheduled to run from October 24 to November 3, kicking off in Saitama and concluding at the NJPW Power Struggle event in Hiroshima Sun Plaza Hall.

Twelve teams will compete for the redesigned trophies, which feature a double-helices design representing synchronization, verticality, and the speed of the junior heavyweight division. Fans can watch the tournament live in English on NJPW World or attend the events in person.
