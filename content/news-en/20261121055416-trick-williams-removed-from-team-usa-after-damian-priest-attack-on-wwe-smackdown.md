Trick Williams is no longer a member of Team USA for Survivor Series following a violent attack on WWE SmackDown. After Hacksaw Jim Duggan backed out of joining the team, Williams entered the ring and was added as the final member alongside Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, Je'Von Evans, and LA Knight after a proposal from Lil Yachty.

Later in the show, Williams faced Damian Priest in a singles match. During the action outside the ring, Williams got his leg caught in the barricade. Priest took advantage of the situation by picking up the timekeeper's bell and hitting Williams directly in the knee, causing the referee to stop the match. Priest continued to target the knee before heading backstage.

Following a medical evaluation of Williams, SmackDown General Manager Nick Aldis instructed Rhodes and Punk to find a replacement. While Danhausen pitched himself to lead Team USA, his idea did not materialize, leaving one open spot on the roster.
