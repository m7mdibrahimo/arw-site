The partnership between WWE and the New York Knicks continues with a new apparel release. The team is raising its 2026 NBA Championship banner at Madison Square Garden during a game against the Philadelphia 76ers.

To mark the occasion, HOMAGE launched an officially licensed shirt combining basketball and sports entertainment, available for one week. The design features the 2026 championship logo on a wrestling title belt, inspired by the crossover seen during WWE's Saturday Night's Main Event at the arena this summer. This shirt is part of a larger collection celebrating the team's historic season, which previously included appearances by Jalen Brunson and Karl-Anthony Towns at the WWE event.
