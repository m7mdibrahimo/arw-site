According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, WWE originally planned a six-man tag team match featuring Penta, Je'Von Evans and Oba Femi against Bron Breakker, Austin Theory and Bronson Reed for Crown Jewel 2026. However, the company ultimately decided it did not make sense to run the six-man tag before paying off the World Tag Team Title match between the champions and challengers. This led to Breakker and Theory defending their titles against Penta and Evans instead, while leaving Femi and Reed out of the mix for now.
