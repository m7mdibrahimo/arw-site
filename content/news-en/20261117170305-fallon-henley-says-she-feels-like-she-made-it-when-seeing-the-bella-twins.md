Fallon Henley revealed that she feels she has achieved her dream in professional wrestling every time she encounters The Bella Twins backstage in WWE.

Over the past year, Nikki and Brie Bella returned to the company for a final run. Although Brie is currently sidelined with a shoulder injury, she previously held the Women's Tag Team Titles with Paige before dropping them to Fallon Henley and Lainey Reid of Fatal Influence in July.

In a recent interview, Henley expressed her joy at sharing a locker room with the duo:

> "It's great I mean it, like I say, a lot of the verbiage I always come back to is surreal because walking in and just seeing them, I'm like, 'oh my gosh, like, I made it.' I did it, you know because because Total Divas and everything, I wasn't somebody who grew up watching wrestling every single weekend or with my family."

Henley noted that after watching Total Divas and clips from RAW and SmackDown, she began following the product religiously. She added that sharing the ring with these stars is a major accomplishment she is proud to show her family. Total Divas served as an entry point for many women into WWE, a sentiment Henley confirmed by describing the show as a source of inspiration.
