Major League Wrestling has lost another longtime member of its team, as Mike Kitlas, the promotion's Senior Director of Live Events, has departed after seven years. According to PWInsider, Kitlas is moving on to a new role with Real American Freestyle. His tenure with MLW began in 2019, and he played a crucial role in the company's live event operations. Before his time with MLW, Kitlas gained extensive experience in the live event industry working for Madison Square Garden. He was known for his dedication, often being one of the first to arrive at a venue and the last to leave. His departure follows the recent exit of actor Paul Walter Hauser, who left the promotion due to creative differences. MLW will now have to continue its live event operations without one of its most familiar behind-the-scenes figures.
