Ricky Sosa is sidelined from in-ring competition due to a severe back injury. During the October 22nd episode of TNA iMPACT on AMC, it was announced that Sosa is currently out of action. Sosa previously competed in a main event ladder match on the October 15th episode of TNA iMPACT for an opportunity at the TNA World Championship. During that match, Moose executed a powerbomb that sent Sosa to the outside, where his back grazed a propped-up ladder before landing directly on the floor. Updates regarding his return will be provided as they become available.
