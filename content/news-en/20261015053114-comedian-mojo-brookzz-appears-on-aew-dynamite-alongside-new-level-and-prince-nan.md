During the October 14 broadcast of AEW Dynamite and Collision from the Wolstein Center in Cleveland, Ohio, comedian Mojo Brookzz appeared on television. The segment featured AEW World Trios Champions, The New Level (Kofi and Austin Creed), accompanied by Prince Nana. Swerve Strickland was absent due to family matters, and Nana introduced Brookzz as the person Strickland sent to join the group. Brookzz accompanied the team to the ring to promote their upcoming 2-out-of-3 Falls match for the Trios Titles at AEW WrestleDream on October 17. Brookzz, who has millions of social media followers, is currently on tour until early December 2026.
