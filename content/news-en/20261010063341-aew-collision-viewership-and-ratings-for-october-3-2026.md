Following an emotional week that included tributes to the late Pac, the October 3 episode of AEW Collision steered the promotion back toward the upcoming AEW WrestleDream pay-per-view on October 17.

Kofi competed in his debut AEW singles bout against Lee Moriarty, Jon Moxley triumphed over Jake Doyle during an AEW Continental Championship Eliminator match, and the Clockwork Carnage match was officially booked for AEW WrestleDream.

According to Wrestlenomics, AEW Collision drew an average audience of 415,000 viewers, delivering a strong performance compared to recent weeks. Although this figure reflects a 16% decrease from the 493,000 viewers who tuned in for the September 23 episode that aired on a Wednesday following AEW Dynamite, it represents a substantial increase from the 339,000 viewers for the September 19 broadcast, which was the last installment in the regular timeslot, and rose 21% above the trailing four-week average.

As is standard for AEW programming, these figures exclude live viewers streaming on Max. The most significant success for AEW Collision occurred within the 18 to 49 demographic, where the program secured a 0.08 rating, matching the September 23 episode and doubling the 0.04 rating from September 19.

This rating also exceeded the trailing four-week average of 0.05 by 60% and doubled the performance of AEW Collision during the same period last year. Consequently, AEW Collision claimed the eighth position on the prime-time cable rankings for the evening, marking a rare top-ten placement for a Saturday night broadcast.
