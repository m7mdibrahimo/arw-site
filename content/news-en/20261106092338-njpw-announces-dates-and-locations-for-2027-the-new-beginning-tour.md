New Japan Pro-Wrestling has begun releasing details for its 2027 event schedule. Following Wrestle Kingdom 21 on January 4th, the promotion will launch The New Beginning tour. The tour is scheduled to run from January 18th through February 13th, culminating with The New Beginning in Osaka. The tour dates are as follows: January 18 and 19 at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo, January 29 at New Life Arena Ryugasaki in Ibaraki, January 30 at Saku City General Gymnasium in Nagano, January 31 at Iwaki City General Gymnasium in Fukushima, February 1 at Isle Arena Wing Hat Kasukabe in Saitama, February 4 at FUKAI SQUARE GARDEN Ashikaga in Tochigi, February 7 and 8 at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo, February 11 at Tosho Arena Anjo in Aichi, and the final event, The New Beginning in Osaka, on February 13 at Edion Arena Osaka.
