Andrade El Idolo recently provided an update on his status after his pants were targeted and beaten down by Tommaso Ciampa during their match at AEW WrestleDream. At the pay-per-view event, Andrade defended his AEW National Championship against Ciampa in singles competition, a bout that heavily featured comedic spots involving Andrade's pants, including Ciampa attacking the clothing item with several moves and pinning it for a near fall. Following significant discussion on social media regarding the unusual spot, Andrade posted an update claiming his pants were injured. He also took a jab at MJF over social media, telling him to keep playing with his toy championship.
