Dustin Rhodes recently expressed his high praise for Jimmy House, formerly known as CJ Valor. Following an interview conducted by Sean Ross Sapp of Fightful, Rhodes took to X to advocate for House to join the AEW locker room, calling him a one-of-a-kind, genuine person with an unmatched work ethic. Rhodes emphasized that House is explosive in the ring and stated that he would love to see his student in AEW. House began his professional wrestling career in 2023, has competed in Rhodes Wrestling Association, and has been part of the WWE I.D. program since March 2026.
