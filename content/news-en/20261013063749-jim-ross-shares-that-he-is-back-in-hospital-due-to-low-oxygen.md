AEW commentator Jim Ross has announced that he is back in the hospital due to low oxygen levels. Ross shared the news on social media, writing, "Back in the hospital due to low oxygen. Weighed 198. Need a break." This follows a successful brain procedure Ross underwent on October 7th to address a shunt issue. The 74-year-old commentator last called a match for AEW at the 2026 Double or Nothing pay-per-view, where he provided commentary for the Continental Championship bout between Jon Moxley and Kyle O'Reilly.
