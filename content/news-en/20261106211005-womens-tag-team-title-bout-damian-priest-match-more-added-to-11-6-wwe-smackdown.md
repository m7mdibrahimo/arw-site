The lineup for the November 6 episode of WWE SmackDown continues to grow. WWE announced that B-Fab and Michin will defend the WWE Women's Tag Team Championships against Fatal Influence, consisting of Lainey Reid and Fallon Henley. Additionally, Damian Priest is set to face Nathan Frazer, and Shinsuke Nakamura will go one-on-one with Tama Tonga. These bouts join the previously announced No Holds Barred match between Kevin Owens and CM Punk. Fightful will provide live coverage of the event on Friday, followed by a post-show podcast on our YouTube channel.
