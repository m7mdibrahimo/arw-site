Mickie James has been named the special guest referee for the Knockouts Championship match at TNA Bound For Glory. TNA President Carlos Silva confirmed that James will officiate the title bout between Xia Brookside and Indi Hartwell at the upcoming event. James was previously confirmed for an appearance at the show following the October 8 episode of TNA iMPACT. Earlier this year, James was involved in a storyline with Ash By Elegance and her brand, which included a Hardcore Country match at TNA Rebellion. TNA Bound For Glory is scheduled for Sunday, October 11, beginning at 4:30 PM ET.
