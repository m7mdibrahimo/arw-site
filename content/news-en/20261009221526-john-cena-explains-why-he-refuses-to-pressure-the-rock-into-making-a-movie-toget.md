John Cena is open to starring in a movie with The Rock, but he has no interest in pressuring Dwayne Johnson to make it happen. During an appearance on Happy Sad Confused, Cena explained that their conversations have become more personal over the years, and he refuses to use that time to pitch film ideas. Cena noted that he learned from his past WWE rivalry with Johnson that being overly aggressive was a mistake, and he now values their genuine interactions too much to complicate them with business proposals. While Cena would be eager to collaborate if Hollywood executives presented the right project, he prefers to let the opportunity arise naturally rather than forcing Johnson into a decision. Cena emphasized that he respects Johnson's extremely busy schedule and does not want to be another person demanding his time or attention.
