Moose has officially thrown his name into the mix for TNA Bound For Glory. During the October 8th episode of TNA iMPACT, a video package aired featuring Moose, who announced that he is issuing an open challenge for the upcoming pay-per-view event. TNA Bound For Glory is scheduled to take place on October 11th at the Yuengling Center in Tampa, Florida. The current lineup for the show includes Nic Nemeth defending the TNA World Championship against Leon Slater and Xia Brookside putting the TNA Knockouts World Championship on the line against Indi Hartwell. Additionally, the event will feature a six-way scramble for the TNA X Division Championship, a four-way match for the TNA International Championship, and a Falls Count Anywhere match between The Hardys and Syx.
