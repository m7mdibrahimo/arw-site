Deonna Purrazzo recently discussed what a potential singles match against Athena would mean to her. Both wrestlers are part of the Ring of Honor roster, with Purrazzo holding the Women's Pure Championship and Athena holding the Women's World Championship. During an interview with The Ledgera magazine, Purrazzo explained that a match between them would determine who truly rules the promotion. "I've said it before, a match between Deonna Purrazzo & Athena would be like the two keepers of the guard fighting for rights to the Kingdom," she stated. Purrazzo also noted that while she has spent most of her career in regular matches, Athena has never competed under Pure rules, raising questions about whether Athena can adapt her style.
