Management at the Tokyo Dome is weighing strict measures following the circulation of an unauthorized video on social media. The footage features a woman posing as an in-stadium beer vendor, pouring a drink for a customer using props. While the woman is seen exiting a building resembling the Tokyo Dome in her uniform, the explicit portions of the video were not filmed on the premises.

According to the Tokyo Reporter, a spokesperson for the venue confirmed that management investigated the matter alongside the agency responsible for stadium concessions. The venue strictly prohibits unauthorized filming for commercial purposes and disruptive behavior. The spokesperson stated that the individual in the video is not a member of their staff.

> "Following confirmation with the contractor that employs and manages our vendors, we have verified that the individual in this video is not a member of our stadium staff," the spokesperson said. "We are currently consulting with the contracting agency and considering strict measures, though we refrain from providing further details at this time."

The Tokyo Dome serves as the annual home for NJPW Wrestle Kingdom, which takes place every January 4.
