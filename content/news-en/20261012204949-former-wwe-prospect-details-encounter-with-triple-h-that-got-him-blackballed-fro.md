Former WWE prospect Jamin Olivencia recently shared the story of how a backstage encounter with Paul 'Triple H' Levesque led to him being blackballed from the company. Olivencia recalled that after briefly interacting with Stephanie McMahon and trying to hand in a promo, he approached Levesque backstage during an episode of WWE RAW. After Levesque initially said he did not have time, Olivencia approached him again the next day during WWE SmackDown while he was talking to Kofi Kingston. Olivencia explained that he thought he was showing initiative, but Levesque appeared agitated and directed him to Canyon Ceman before walking away with a sarcastic facial expression. Security soon approached Olivencia and told him to leave, falsely claiming he had followed Levesque into a bathroom and asked for a job, which Olivencia denied. Five years later, Olivencia was invited back to WWE RAW by Ceman, but a referee later pulled him aside to inform him that Mark Carrano stated he was not supposed to be there and would not be paid for traveling through a snowstorm. Reflecting on the events, Olivencia noted that he now takes full responsibility for putting himself in that position.
