Gabe Kidd officially challenged Yuya Uemura for the IWGP Global Heavyweight Championship at NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 21. Following his successful title defense against Taichi at NJPW Power Struggle 2026, Kidd insulted and struck NJPW President Hiroshi Tanahashi in the ring. This prompted Yuya Uemura to rush out, sparking a brawl that also involved Jon Moxley and Shingo Takagi. In his post-match comments, Kidd set the stage for his upcoming defense against Uemura on January 4, 2027.
