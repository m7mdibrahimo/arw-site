TNA Wrestling has announced that Tasha Steelz is sidelined with a knee injury and there is no timetable for her return. The injury stems from an incident during the Bound for Glory event where Mustafa Ali defended the TNA International Championship against Jason Hotch, John Skyler, and Agent Zero in a four-way match. During the bout, Mustafa Ali delivered a 450 splash onto Tasha Steelz and Agent Zero through a table. On the October 15 episode of TNA iMPACT, TNA confirmed the knee injury keeping Steelz out of action. Meanwhile, Jason Hotch won the four-way match to become a two-time International Champion.
