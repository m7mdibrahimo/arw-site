Former WWE star Xelina credited Cody Rhodes and John Cena for allowing her to get extra time in a WWE championship match that she considers one of her favorite bouts of her career. Speaking on the "Ring the Belle" podcast, Xelina, formerly known as Zelina Vega in WWE, explained that her August 1, 2025 "WWE SmackDown" match against Giulia for the Women's United States Championship only received extended time thanks to the two stars. She noted that the first 30 minutes of the show were originally dedicated to Rhodes and Cena, culminating in Cena's face turn ahead of WWE SummerSlam.

Xelina revealed that WWE officials initially told her the women would only have seven to eight minutes total around a commercial break, which was later cut down even further. However, while standing near the gorilla position, Rhodes informed them that he and Cena had saved an extra 10 minutes for their match. She stated that she and Giulia quickly adapted their plans to utilize the extended time. Xelina added that outside of her Backlash 2023 match in Puerto Rico, this Women's United States Championship bout against Giulia remains her favorite match of her career.
