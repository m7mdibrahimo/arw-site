The Bollywood Boyz are scheduled to make their Pro Wrestling Rebellion debut early next year. PWR will head to Talkatora Stadium in New Delhi, India, on January 22, 2027, and the promotion recently confirmed that the brothers will perform on the card. Reacting to the announcement on social media, the duo shared a heartfelt message about returning to their parents' home country, noting that their biggest break in wrestling happened in India fifteen years ago with Ring Ka King, and emphasizing that this will be their largest show in New Delhi in five years. The brothers previously competed in major promotions including WWE and AEW.
