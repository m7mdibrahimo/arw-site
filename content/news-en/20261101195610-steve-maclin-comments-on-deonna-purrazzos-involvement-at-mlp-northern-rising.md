Steve Maclin recently talked about Deonna Purrazzo's involvement at MLP Northern Rising, where he faced Adam Copeland. The match also featured appearances by wrestlers such as Rhino and Deonna Purrazzo. Speaking with Sean Ross Sapp, Maclin reflected on sharing the screen with his wife for the first time in Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling and noted how special the moment was for his family, including his mother who watched the match from home. He also mentioned that the door is now open for a potential mixed tag team match involving Adam Copeland against him and his wife.
