Former AEW wrestler and current ASÉ Wrestling commentator Aerial Big Swole Johnson is being honored at the Crohn's and Colitis Foundation's A Toast for A Cure gala as a new board member for the Tampa Bay chapter. Johnson expressed her gratitude for overcoming her health struggles and emphasized her dedication to helping others facing similar battles. To support the cause, TNA is donating an Ultimate TNA Fan Experience, signed memorabilia from The Hardys, a guitar neck from Elijah, a Slammiversary turnbuckle, and an event-used canvas for a silent auction and raffle. The gala takes place on Thursday at 5:30 PM ET.
