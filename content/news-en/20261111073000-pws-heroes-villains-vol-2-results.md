Pro Wrestling Symphony hosted its Heroes & Villains Vol. 2 show on November 7 at Station Camp High School in Gallatin, Tennessee. Colby Carter def. Jamesen Shook in the opening match.

Grayson Pierce and Corinne Joy def. Terry Yaki and Jazzy Yang. Kaia McKenna def. Corinne Joy and Alexis Littlefoot in a 3-way match to retain the PWS Women’s Championship.

Royal Prestige def. Fly Defeats Adrian Thomas won the PWS Championship number one contenders gauntlet match by defeating Jake Painter, Tyler Stevens, Tyler Shoop, Grayson Pierce, and Alejandro in the main event.
