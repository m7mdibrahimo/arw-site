Pro Wrestling Symphony held its Heroes & Villains Vol. 2 show on November 7 at Station Camp High School in Gallatin, Tennessee.

Colby Carter defeated Jamesen Shook in the opening match. Grayson Pierce and Corinne Joy defeated Terry Yaki and Jazzy Yang. Kaia McKenna retained the PWS Women's Championship by defeating Corinne Joy and Alexis Littlefoot in a 3-way match. Royal Prestige (TeVon Jordyn and Majestic) defeated Fly Def (Nasir Dey and Zack Zilla).

In the main event, Adrian Thomas won the PWS Championship number one contenders gauntlet match by defeating Jake Painter, Tyler Stevens, Tyler Shoop, Grayson Pierce, and Alejandro.
