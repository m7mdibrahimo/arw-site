Grayson Waller planned to wear a T-shirt featuring Stephanie Vaquer's viral Instagram photo on WWE NXT, but company officials blocked the idea. The photo in question showed Vaquer posing with only the Women's World Championship, paying tribute to a famous Shawn Michaels pose. Waller revealed that he had the shirt made, but it never made it to television because management felt it went too far. Although WWE rejected the shirt, Waller still owns it and believes a fan would pay a high price for it. He also admitted that he avoids trolling opponents unless they can face him in a ring, noting that his feud line with Vaquer meant he could not wear it. Vaquer originally shared the revealing image following the October 5 episode of RAW, and she later lost the title to Liv Morgan at Money in the Bank.
