As WWE Crown Jewel 2026 approaches this Saturday in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, the match card features four bouts, including three championship encounters. The most anticipated match on the lineup is the Last Man Standing match between Cody Rhodes and Randy Orton. Their rivalry resumed after Orton delivered an RKO to Rhodes at SummerSlam, followed by a deceptive low blow during their clash at Sunday Night's Main Event. Rhodes has since shown a more aggressive persona, recently targeting Baron Corbin with a punt kick. On the other hand, the match between WWE Women's World Champion Liv Morgan and WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green is viewed as the least compelling. The non-title contest for the ceremonial WWE Crown Jewel Championship has lacked strong storyline momentum, as Green's reign has been heavily overshadowed by involvement from Tiffany Stratton and The Judgment Day.
