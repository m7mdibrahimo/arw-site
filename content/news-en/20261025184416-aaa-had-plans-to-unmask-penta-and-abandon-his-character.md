Kalisto recently revealed that AAA had major plans to unmask Penta and retire his character entirely before Kalisto's move to WWE changed everything. Speaking on a podcast, Kalisto recalled his history with Penta in Mexico and explained that he was originally scheduled to remove Penta's mask in a match that would have ended the persona permanently, paving the way for Penta to return to his Dark Dragon gimmick. However, the plans fell through when Kalisto signed with WWE, allowing Penta to keep his mask, build the character into a massive success, and eventually carry it all the way to WWE, where he recently secured a Money in the Bank victory.
