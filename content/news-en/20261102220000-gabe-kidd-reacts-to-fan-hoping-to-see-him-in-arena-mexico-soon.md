Gabe Kidd recently reacted to a fan on Instagram expressing hope that he will soon appear in CMLL and Arena Mexico. Kidd stated that he still wants to end Soberano Jr. Over the past two years, Kidd has become a prominent figure in both NJPW and AEW, competing as a member of The Dogs and later The Death Riders. Although he has wrestled on several events co-produced by CMLL during his career, he has yet to compete inside Arena Mexico.
