Kyle Fletcher revealed that he felt like a "very nervous little boy" before competing in his first AEW Blood And Guts match. During the latest edition of his Protolog, Fletcher looked back at his debut inside the dual steel structure on AEW Dynamite. He secured a victory for his team after forcing Adam Copeland to surrender by threatening Will Ospersay with a screwdriver. Reflecting on his anxiety before the match, Fletcher shared his thoughts in a candid video.

> "I have to wrestle in a cage. I'm nervous. That's about it. I'm a very nervous boy. A very nervous little boy who hopes he doesn't bleed too much today. Or guts too much. Or too many guts. Guts would be bad. Let's hope there's no guts. Yeah, only blood, no guts. Only blood. I mean, I still hope there's no blood, but I feel like there's probably a 0% chance of that happening. It's everywhere. I feel like the people would be disappointed if there was no blood. Let's just hope there's more of everyone else's blood than there is my blood. I'm fucking terrified. Let's see how this goes."

Fletcher recently addressed comparisons made between himself and Randy Orton.
