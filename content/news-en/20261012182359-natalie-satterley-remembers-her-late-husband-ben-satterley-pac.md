Natalie Satterley has shared a heartfelt tribute to her late husband, Ben Satterley, known to wrestling fans as PAC, following his sudden passing which was announced by AEW on Sunday, September 27. Reflecting on their twenty-one years of friendship, nineteen years together, and eleven years of marriage, Natalie expressed her profound grief and disbelief over the loss. She shared details of the future they had planned together, including returning home, spending time with family, and enjoying simple moments. She concluded by promising that she and their loved ones will try their hardest to get through the nightmare while missing him every single day.
