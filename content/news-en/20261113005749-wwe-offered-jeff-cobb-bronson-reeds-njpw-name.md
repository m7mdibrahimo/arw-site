During his time in WWE, Jeff Cobb was known as JC Mateo. However, as Cobb revealed on "Insight With Chris Van Vliet," he was presented with a list of potential ring names before his debut, which coincided with Backlash 2025. One of those names was "JONAH," which had previously been used by current WWE star Bronson Reed during his tenure in New Japan Pro-Wrestling. Recalling the moment, Cobb said, "The first page, I'll say it was maybe 60 names on there. I was like okay, this is pretty cool, pretty cool." He continued, "One that stuck out made me giggle because it was all caps, JONAH. [That had been used] by Bronson Reed. The guy that's in your company. I was like, 'Wait a minute, are you guys even trying now?'" Reed performed as Jonah Rock on the independent scene before adopting the name JONAH following his 2021 WWE release, during which he wrestled for NJPW, TNA, and PROGRESS. He returned to WWE in 2022 under his current ring name, Bronson Reed. Cobb, who departed WWE in May 2026, currently competes for NJPW.
