Jonathan Coachman says he was told WWE's plans for WrestleMania include Cody Rhodes vs. CM Punk and Oba Femi vs. Roman Reigns as the main events for both nights. Speaking on 2 Count Tuesday, Coachman revealed that one of his sources claimed these two matches are 100% locked in for the big event. He noted that with Survivor Series and the Royal Rumble coming up before WrestleMania, WWE will likely spend the next six months building Femi up as a dominant monster, especially given his minimal losses in 2026. Regarding the other main event, JD suggested Cody might need to turn heel for his feud with Punk, and Coachman completely agreed with the idea while reiterating his confidence in the match lineup. WWE has not officially announced either match yet, leaving plenty of time for plans to change.
