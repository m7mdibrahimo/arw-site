NFL star Tyreek Hill recently had a starstruck moment when he met WWE legend The Undertaker. Fanatics shared a video on Instagram showing Hill spotting The Undertaker and immediately embracing him. Hill expressed his disbelief while The Deadman smiled during the interaction. Hill admitted that getting starstruck is not something that normally happens to him, noting that he grew up playing as The Undertaker in WWE video games on the Nintendo 64.

> "Yo, I've never been starstruck before. You talking about back in the day when I was playing the Nintendo 64? This is who I got, bruh. Y'all know who this is?" Fanatics summed up the moment in its caption, stating that everyone turns into a kid when they meet The Undertaker.
