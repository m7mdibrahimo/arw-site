Seri Yamaoka is set to return to the ring following a recovery from shoulder surgery. The Dream Star Fighting Marigold talent suffered a dislocated right shoulder in May 2026 and underwent arthroscopic stabilization surgery the following month. She was given a six-month recovery timeline and has been preparing for her return. Yamaoka will compete at Marigold Grand Destiny on October 24th, where she will team with Yuuka Yamazaki to face Komomo Minami and Nagisa Tachibana. The 19-year-old talent, who began her career when the promotion launched in 2024, previously held the Marigold Twin Star Titles alongside Nanae Takahashi for 111 days.
