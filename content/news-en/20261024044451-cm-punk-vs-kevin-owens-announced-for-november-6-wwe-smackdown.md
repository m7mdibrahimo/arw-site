CM Punk and Kevin Owens will finally face each other in the ring after blaming one another for their Undisputed WWE Championship issues. WWE officially announced the match for the November 6 episode of WWE SmackDown in Brooklyn, New York. The bout was confirmed during the October 23, 2026, episode when Nick Aldis appeared in a video alongside both men to break the news. During the segment, Punk made it clear he viewed the conversation as a waste of time and blamed Owens for costing him his championship opportunity. Owens fired back by arguing that Punk's actions ruined his own title chances and brought up Punk's previous time away from the company. With both men holding strong grudges and eager to settle the score, the confrontation is set for Friday, November 6, just one day before WWE Crown Jewel.
