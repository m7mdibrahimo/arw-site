RJ City's employment status with AEW remains a bit of a mystery. While he has appeared on the company's television and digital platforms, he has never received an official "All Elite" graphic. AEW recently updated its website to include biographies for various talents, and RJ City is among those featured.

The bio itself even acknowledges his lack of a graphic. The official AEW profile states: "Describing his pro wrestling career as “years of failure” perfectly encapsulates the self-deprecating nature of one of the hosts of AEW’s pay-per-view Buy In shows with fellow Toronto native Renee Paquette." The profile also highlights his online catalog of over 100 episodes of Hey!(EW), which ran from shortly after he joined the company in 2022 until December 2025. Despite his close relationship with "Timeless" Toni Storm and his frequent interviews with guests like Ben Mankiewicz, RJ City still lacks the official graphic confirming he is an official part of the company roster.
