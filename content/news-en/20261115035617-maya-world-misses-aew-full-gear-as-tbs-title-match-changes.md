AEW Full Gear underwent a sudden adjustment as Maya World was officially pulled from her championship match. Shortly before the event on Saturday, November 14, 2026, the promotion confirmed that World could not travel to Phoenix, necessitating a significant update to the TBS Championship bout.

AEW announced that World would no longer participate in the planned three-way contest against Queen Aminata and Persephone. Instead, the promotion expanded the match to include Hyan and Skye Blue. Queen Aminata is now set to put her TBS Championship on the line against Persephone, Hyan, and Skye Blue during the Full Gear Tailgate Brawl pre-show.

Following the announcement, World shared a broken-heart emoji on X to express her disappointment. This absence is particularly frustrating as she had received medical clearance in October after a September procedure, hoping to reclaim the title she previously vacated.
