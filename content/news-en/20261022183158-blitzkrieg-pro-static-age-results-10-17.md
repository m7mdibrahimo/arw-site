Blitzkrieg! Pro hosted its B!P Static Age event on October 17 in Enfield, Connecticut, with the show airing on YouTube according to Fightful. Angelo Carter defeated TJ Crawford to retain the Blitzkrieg! Pro Jensen Championship.

The Residency consisting of RJ Rude, Rex Lawless and Nick Robles wrestled Dante Drago, Allin Bayno and Brett Mettro with Jared Silberkleit, and Amity LaVey defeated Izzy Moreno. The Cowboy Way composed of 1 Called Manders and Thomas Shire defeated Dolphin Funeral featuring RULLO and JuleZee. In a scramble match, Oxx Adams defeated Jose Zamora, Javi, Angel Jacquez, Will Kiedis and Logan Black. Kalypto defeated Nat Castle with Sebastian Amor and Nikki Winters with Jay Kharma in a three-way match.

Ancestral City Shooters comprised of Kwesi Asante and Joseph Alexander defeated Miracle Generation consisting of Dustin Waller and Kylon King to retain the Blitzkrieg! Pro Tag Team Championships. Alec Price defeated Devious Cass. In the main event Bunkhouse Brawl, Bear Bronson, Bobby Orlando and Sammy Diaz defeated Charles Mason, Andy Brown and CPA.
