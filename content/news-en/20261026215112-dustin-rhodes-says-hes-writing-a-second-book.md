Dustin Rhodes is currently writing his second book, following his 2010 autobiography "Cross Rhodes: Goldust, Out of the Darkness." Dustin revealed the project on social media, noting that the new book will cover his entire career in greater depth, including the highs and lows. He stated that he is returning to glory and doing things his way, adding that his hands were tied with his previous company. Dustin is currently recovering from double knee replacement surgery that he underwent in August.
