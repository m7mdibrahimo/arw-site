A TBS Championship match is now official for the AEW Saturday Night Tailgate Brawl pre-show before Full Gear on Saturday, November 14. Champion Queen Aminata will defend her title in a triple threat match against Maya World and Persephone.

This bout marks the second title defense for Queen Aminata since she won the belt from Persephone on the October 28 episode of AEW Dynamite. Meanwhile, Maya World gets her first opportunity to reclaim the championship after losing it to Persephone at AEW All In.
