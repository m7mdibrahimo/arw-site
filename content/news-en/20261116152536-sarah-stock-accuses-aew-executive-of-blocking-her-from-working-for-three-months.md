Sarah Stock continues to air her grievances against AEW, claiming that she spent three months on the payroll while management repeatedly prevented her from working. According to Stock, she was hired and ready to start, but AEW Chief Legal Officer Megha Parekh allegedly withheld her paperwork from Tony Khan and offered constant excuses. Stock stated that she repeatedly contacted the legal and human resources departments, only to be told it was an issue for Parekh. She eventually bypassed the usual channels and called a former WWE colleague who needed female and Spanish-speaking coaches. The colleague was surprised to learn Stock had been hidden for months, and she began working that same Wednesday. This follows a previous incident where Stock claimed Tony Khan billed her for Jon Moxley's hotel room.
