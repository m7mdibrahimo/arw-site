Tony Khan has commented on Adam Copeland's upcoming retirement from professional wrestling. During a recent episode of AEW Dynamite, Copeland surprised fans worldwide by announcing that 2027 will be his final year in the ring. Following this announcement, Tony Khan spoke about the situation during the media call for AEW WrestleDream. Khan confirmed that Copeland had informed him of his decision in advance. However, he also stated that he is currently unsure of how far into 2027 Copeland plans to compete.
