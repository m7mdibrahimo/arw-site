Ricky Marvin is returning to AAA. A report from False Finish notes that Marvin has signed with Lucha Libre AAA as a trainer and occasional producer. "Sources tell False Finish that Ricky Marvin has signed with Lucha Libre AAA as a trainer and occasional producer. Marvin is a former AAA World Trios Champion alongside Averno and Chessman as part of Los OGTs" Marvin's in-ring career began in 1995 with CMLL, followed by stints in Japan, Pro Wrestling NOAH, Ring of Honor, and AAA. In December 2018, he participated in a WWE tryout in Chile, and he remains active on the independent scene.
