Dustin Rhodes has expanded his ongoing social media pizza feud to target New Jersey. After previously criticizing New York pizza and specific toppings, Rhodes responded to a fan's photo of a Jersey Shore slice on October 8. He did not hold back in his assessment of the food.

> "Disgusting greasy shit."

This latest comment follows a series of food-related critiques from the AEW veteran. Rhodes has previously expressed his strong dislike for various toppings, including black olives, pickles, pineapple, and vegetables, often using harsh language to describe them. He has also been a vocal advocate for New Haven Tavern style pizza, consistently declaring it superior to other regional varieties. Despite fans occasionally defending their local preferences, including John Silver's argument against toppings, Rhodes remains firm in his stance regarding what constitutes acceptable pizza.
