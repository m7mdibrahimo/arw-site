Drew McIntyre has intensified his ongoing feud with CM Punk by stating that he wants his rival out of WWE permanently. Speaking during an interview with Ella Jay while promoting the film Agent of Shadows, McIntyre did not hold back when asked about Punk.

> "That’s why we don’t send CM Punk to these things because he’s a piece of trash."

McIntyre compared Punk's influence on fans to Charles Manson, labeling him a bad person who only succeeds in acting when playing a version of himself. He further clarified that his constant focus on Punk is not due to obsession, but rather a desire to see him leave the company.

> "And I only talk about him all the time, not because obsession, because I just won’t be happy till he’s gone from WWE forever."

McIntyre’s co-star Chase Mattson noted that McIntyre had been equally vocal about his feelings for Punk behind the scenes. These comments follow McIntyre’s recent appearance on WWE Raw Talk, where he also described Punk as a "genuine piece of crap." Despite their intense personal animosity, McIntyre acknowledged that their rivalry has been effective for WWE television.
