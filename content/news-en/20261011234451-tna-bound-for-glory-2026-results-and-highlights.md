TNA Bound For Glory aired on October 11, 2026, live from the Yuengling Center in Tampa, Florida. The event featured the 2026 TNA Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony honoring Amazing Red, Konnan, and ODB. In the opening match, Ricky Sosa def. Eddie Edwards. Amazing Red def. Fabian Aichner, KC Navarro, Cedric Alexander, Rich Swann, Ryan Nemeth, and BDE in a 7-Way Scramble Match to win the TNA X-Division Championship. Zoey Serrano and Shayna Baszler def. Allie and Kiera Hogan to win the TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Championship. Frankie Kazarian won the Call Your Shot Gauntlet Match.
