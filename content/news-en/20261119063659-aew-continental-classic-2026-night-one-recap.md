The 2026 AEW Continental Classic tournament began on the November 18 episode of AEW Dynamite. In the Gold League, Orange Cassidy defeated Jon Moxley via pinfall after hitting three Orange Punches. Also in the Gold League, Nick Wayne secured a victory over 'Speedball' Mike Bailey by rolling him up with a handful of tights. The show closed with a Blue League match between 'Hangman' Adam Page and his trios partner, Bandido. Bandido won the main event after countering a Buckshot Lariat attempt, landing a knee to the face, and executing a 21 Plex on Page.
