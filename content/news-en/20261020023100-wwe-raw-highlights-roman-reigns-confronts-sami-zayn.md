The October 19, 2026 episode of WWE RAW took place at the Pechanga Arena in San Diego, California. The broadcast opened with a video package reviewing the rivalry between The 946 and The Bloodline, highlighting Carmelo Hayes joining Royce Keys' crew the previous week.

World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns arrived at the venue and encountered Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn, who headed into the ring. Zayn addressed the crowd before Reigns joined him. Reigns acknowledged Zayn, and the two embraced in the center of the ring. Zayn admitted that holding the Undisputed WWE Championship has brought heavy pressure. Reigns claimed that Zayn's past status as an Honorary Uce was bigger than his current title, questioning whether Zayn appeared out of greed or loyalty to The Bloodline. Zayn stated that he now sits at the head of his own table on SmackDown, while Reigns insisted the World Heavyweight Championship is the premier title in WWE. Reigns questioned Zayn's reign heading into Crown Jewel before departing.

Roman Reigns crossed paths with Penta during his exit, and Penta entered the ring to confront Zayn before his scheduled match.

Penta def. Bron Breakker

During the match, Maxxine Dupri and Austin Theory walked down to distract Penta. After Penta struck Breakker and Theory with superkicks, Breakker delivered a mid-air spear for a near fall. Je'Von Evans then intervened, taking out Theory and hitting Breakker with the Money in the Bank briefcase away from the referee's sight, allowing Penta to secure the victory.
