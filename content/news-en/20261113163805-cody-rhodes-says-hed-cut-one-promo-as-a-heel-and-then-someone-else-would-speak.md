Cody Rhodes addressed the ongoing speculation about a potential heel turn during a recent appearance on the Ebro, Laura, and Rosenberg Show. While noting that he is open to anything that moves WWE forward, Cody explained how he envisions a villainous run unfolding. 

> "I'm open to anything if it's something that moves the ball forward for WWE. I really am open to it. It's not a conversation of 'I would not be willing to.' So much has happened already. People already boo."

Cody emphasized that if he were to make a definitive turn, he would want to take it to an extreme level rather than keeping it entertaining for the fans. 

> "If I were to make this definitive turn, I'm letting you know it wouldn't be cool. 'Now I'm into him more as a babyface.' I would do my best to take it very far. We're on ESPN, Netflix, and Disney. I would not want to be rooted for."

He added that he would only explain his actions once before completely letting someone else handle his talking on television. 

> "The only insight I can give is, if it happened, I would tell the audience one time, and one time only, why it happened, and they'll never hear from me again. There wouldn't be a single promo ever cut from me. Somebody else would cut those promos. I'd give them one moment."

Cody previously suggested an idea where he could align with Ava, the daughter of The Rock, to speak on his behalf. As he prepares to lead Team USA against Team Mexico at WWE Survivor Series and compete in the John Cena Classic in December, he closed with a warning for fans clamoring for the change. 

> "Please take what I'm saying with full-blown caution. It's not going to be fun like you think. I mean that with no threatening nature. As always in wrestling, be careful with what you wish for."
