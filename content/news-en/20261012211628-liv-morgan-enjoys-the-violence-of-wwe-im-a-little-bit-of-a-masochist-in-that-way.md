WWE Women's World Champion Liv Morgan is no stranger to the physical toll of professional wrestling. During an appearance on "Hot Ones," Morgan shared her perspective on the injuries she sustains in the ring. "I love it," Morgan said. "I think it's an asset. I, like, am proud of them, because I'm like 'This is literally what we're putting our bodies through for your entertainment.' But I also like the physicality of it. I kind of like the violence a little bit. I'm a little bit of a masochist in that kind of way, and so I'm always like 'Yay!' But yeah, the hematoma is the only injury I've had where I'm like 'Yeah, I'm not happy about this.'" Morgan also described the relief she felt when her shoulder was popped back into place after dislocating it during a June 2025 match against Kairi Sane. "It feels great," Morgan said. "Honestly, it feels great. It's such a release, because when it gets popped out of the socket, there's like such an immense pressure. And it hurts so bad that it doesn't hurt, but there's just so much pressure. And you don't know what to do except to hold it to relieve it. So when they pop it back in, you almost think you're good as new. You're like 'Oh wow. I can go back out there.' That's typically not the case. But it's just a huge relief. Huge, huge relief."
