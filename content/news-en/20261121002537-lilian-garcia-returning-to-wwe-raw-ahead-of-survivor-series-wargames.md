Lilian Garcia is preparing to return to ring announcing duties on WWE Raw, confirming on her Instagram account that she will be behind the microphone for the November 23, 2026, episode in Wichita, Kansas, which will air live on Netflix.

This return comes just days before Survivor Series WarGames, making Garcia the official voice to present the matches on the final red-brand show before the anticipated event scheduled at Daikin Park in Houston, Texas.

> "HAVE YOU HEARD?! Next Monday, yours truly is BACK on the mic for WWE action with Monday Night RAW LIVE on Netflix."

> "WICHITA, KANSAS… are you READY?! See you LIVE!!"

Garcia has made several previous appearances on WWE shows since leaving her full-time position on WWE SmackDown in March 2025, having recently returned at various times to cover for absent announcers. Details have not yet clarified whether this participation is limited to a single night or part of a longer-term arrangement.
