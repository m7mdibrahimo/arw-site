Since the first AEW Double or Nothing in May 2019, Bryce Remsburg has been a key member of the AEW referee team and a travel manager for the promotion. Remsburg's consistency is no surprise, as he was a standout referee on the independent circuit for over a decade before joining AEW. During an appearance on "The Certified Mark Show," Remsburg admitted that the transition from the indies to a major television promotion was challenging.

"Refereeing on the independents and refereeing on television for AEW are two different beasts," Remsburg explained. "I did seventeen years on the indies from 2002 to 2019. And I feel like I had kind of gotten my bachelor's degree. But when AEW started, just really, I began my master's program."

A significant adjustment was the use of an earpiece, which allows Remsburg to receive real-time instructions from Tony Khan, agents, and producers. He noted that it took several months to feel comfortable with this level of communication. Remsburg added that the period during the global pandemic, when "Dynamite" was taped in Jacksonville without crowds, was instrumental in helping him gain confidence for television refereeing.
