Penelope Ford appeared backstage at AEW Dynamite, marking her first time behind the scenes in months of absence from AEW programming. Reports confirmed that Ford continues to be sidelined from the ring because of the injury she sustained during the Denver episode of AEW Dynamite, when she teamed with Megan Bayne to face Willow Nightingale and Harley Cameron for the AEW Women's World Tag Team Championship. That injury resulted in a long-term absence following an ankle sprain. Despite her recent backstage presence, it does not necessarily signal that her official in-ring return is imminent.
