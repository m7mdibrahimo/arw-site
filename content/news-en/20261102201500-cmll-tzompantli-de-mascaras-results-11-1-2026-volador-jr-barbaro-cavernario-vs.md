Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre held CMLL Tzompantli de Máscaras on Sunday, November 1, 2026, at Arena México in Mexico City, Distrito Federal, Mexico. Full results from the event are listed below.

Pólvora & Robin def. AtrapaSueños (Rey Cometa & Espíritu Negro)

Persephone def. Princesa Sugehit, Reyna Isis & Magnífica in a Tzompantli Cup match

Dragón Rojo Jr. def. Star Jr. in a lighting match

Akuma def. Furia Roja in a lighting match

Blue Panther def. Valiente & Virus in a three-way match

Infernales (Averno & Euforia) def. Guerreros Laguneros (Stuka Jr. & Último Guerrero) via disqualification in a two-out-of-three falls match

Volador Jr. & Bárbaro Cavernario def. Hermanos Chávez (Ángel de Oro & Niebla Roja) in a two-out-of-three falls match
