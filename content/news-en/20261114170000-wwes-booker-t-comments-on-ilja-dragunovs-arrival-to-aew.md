Following his departure from WWE, Ilja Dragunov made his AEW debut during WrestleDream on October 17. Since then, he has competed in two matches for the company, including a bout against Mike Bailey. On an episode of the Hall of Fame podcast, WWE NXT commentator Booker T shared his thoughts on Dragunov joining AEW.

Booker T praised Dragunov, saying he is a great entertainer and wrestler who can connect with fans from the moment he walks out. However, he noted that this will be a real test for Dragunov because he has to maintain that high level of performance regularly. Dragunov left WWE after letting his contract expire following the rejection of an extension offer, having not appeared on television since the April 17 episode of WWE SmackDown.

Booker T expressed his support for Dragunov, recalling how much he enjoyed calling his matches in WWE NXT and watching him perform.
