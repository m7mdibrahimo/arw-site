Bronson Reed has at last returned to his house after spending over seven weeks dealing with severe physical damage sustained during the WWE Crown Jewel event in Saudi Arabia. The WWE star had been trapped abroad after picking up several fractured ribs and a torn kidney, but he is now continuing his rehabilitation at his residence following an operation.

According to Bryan Alvarez, the competitor has safely made it back and is pushing forward with his recuperation. Reed was hurt on November 7 in Riyadh, despite not actually taking part in a match. He interfered during the World Tag Team Championship bout to assist The Vision, but things went terribly wrong when Oba Femi hurled him off the stage with a massive toss powerbomb through a table.

Wade Barrett verified on the November 9 episode of WWE Raw that Reed remained hospitalized, which sparked worries over the severity of his condition. Reports later clarified that he sustained multiple broken ribs and a lacerated kidney, shooting down rumors of a punctured lung. WWE has not issued a timetable for his return to the squared circle, but his safe arrival home marks an encouraging step forward.
