Roman Reigns maintains a rigorous, year-round diet to stay in top shape for his WWE schedule. In a recent appearance on Men's Health's "Gym & Fridge" series, the former Undisputed WWE Champion provided a breakdown of his meal-prep routine.

> "Well, when I'm locked in for a big event, we're always going to be in meal prep. So as you can see, we have a vast selection of meal prep. That makes it a lot easier for me. Typically, it'll be a vegetable, a carb, and a protein."

Reigns explained that his daily intake increases significantly during intense training periods, consisting of six meals and two protein shakes. He relies on calorie-free condiments to make the repetitive, bland meal-prep food more palatable. His routine begins with fasted cardio followed by a breakfast of scrambled eggs, oatmeal, and blueberries, which he considers his favorite meal of the day.
