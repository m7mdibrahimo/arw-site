AEW stars like Kenny Omega, Jon Moxley, MJF, and Will Ospreay have been appearing on recent NJPW shows to set up future matches, ahead of their expected presence at NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 21. However, former IWGP Heavyweight Champion Callum Newman is not happy about this trend, expressing his frustration in an interview with NJPW.

Newman criticized AEW wrestlers for treating NJPW as an easy way to build their resumes without putting in the grueling work required in the dojo. He pointed out that many of these stars fly in just for one show, skip the tough touring schedule, and jump straight into main events, which he believes is unfair to the full-time roster.
