A change has been made to the World X Cup match scheduled for tonight's TNA Destination X. The event was originally set to feature two three-way matches involving Fabian Aichner, Jason Hotch, Rey Horus, Amir Jordan, Home Town Man, and Ricky Sosa.

According to Fightful, Rey Horus will no longer participate. Consequently, the World X Cup will now be decided in an elimination match featuring Home Town Man, Ricky Sosa, Amir Jordan, Fabian Aichner, and Jason Hotch.
