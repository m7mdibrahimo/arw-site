Kris Statlander stepped in for Marina Shafir to team with Jon Moxley at a recent Warrior Wrestling event. Last year, during her reign as AEW Women's World Champion, the Death Riders attempted to recruit Statlander, but she rejected the offer by attacking Wheeler Yuta and flipping off Moxley. However, she briefly joined the group for one night only on October 30th. Moxley was originally scheduled to team with Shafir against Lance Archer and Tootie Lynn, but with Shafir sidelined, Statlander took her place. During the match, Statlander and Moxley executed the faction's signature corner clotheslines on Archer. Meanwhile, on AEW Dynamite, Moxley issued a challenge to The Cut for an Anarchy In The Arena match at AEW Full Gear.
