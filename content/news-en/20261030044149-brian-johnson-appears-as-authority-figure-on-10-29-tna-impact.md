Brian Johnson appeared as an authority figure on the October 29 episode of TNA iMPACT. Cameras caught backstage footage of Daria Rae on her phone before 'The Mecca' Brian Johnson walked into the frame. Rae mentioned that Johnson had been trying to secure a job with TNA for a full year. Johnson replied that he officially has a position now, having been appointed by the Board of Directors to keep a close watch on her. He explained that due to her physical altercation with Indi Hartwell and subsequent suspension, he is required to report back to the Board if any similar incident occurs. Rae stated she would contact the Board directly before walking away. Johnson has a 19-year wrestling career, which includes competing for Ring of Honor prior to its acquisition by Tony Khan from 2017 to 2021. Additionally, Johnson has wrestled in five TNA Xplosion matches since 2025.
