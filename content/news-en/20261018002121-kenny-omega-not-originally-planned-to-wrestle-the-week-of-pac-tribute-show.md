According to Fightful Select, Kenny Omega was not originally scheduled to wrestle during the week of the PAC tribute show, but he decided to compete on the day of the event. He returned to Japan shortly after the show concluded. Omega had been absent from television following his loss to Will Ospreay at AEW All In, where he teamed with the Young Bucks and Hangman Page for the tribute, and has not appeared on TV since.
