George Iceman has officially parted ways with TNA Wrestling after failing to reach a new contract agreement, according to PWInsider. Iceman, who served as The Concierge for The Elegance Brand and previously worked as a backstage insider, had been in talks regarding a new deal, but negotiations ultimately fell through. TNA had seemingly anticipated his departure, as recent backstage segments featuring The Elegance Brand aired without him. Despite his exit from TNA, interest in Iceman from other promotions is reportedly growing, and several other TNA contracts are set to expire in the coming months.
