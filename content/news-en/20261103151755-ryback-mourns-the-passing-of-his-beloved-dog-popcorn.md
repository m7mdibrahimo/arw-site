Former WWE star Ryback has shared an emotional update about his dog Popcorn passing away after a sudden medical crisis. He explained that her neurological condition worsened rapidly, leading to weakness in her front legs and difficulty breathing. Realizing she was suffering, Ryback made the heartbreaking decision to let her go peacefully at 11:30 p.m. Pacific Time.

Ryback adopted Popcorn from the Henderson Animal Shelter in Nevada on July 10, 2024. She was four years and four months old, and he noted that he never expected her condition to deteriorate so quickly. This loss compounds an already difficult year for Ryback, following the death of his mother earlier in 2026.

Expressing his gratitude for the overwhelming support, Ryback shared a heartfelt goodbye to his beloved companion, whom he affectionately called "Popcorn with extra butter." He concluded by acknowledging that while a piece of him will be gone forever, he finds peace in knowing she is no longer suffering.
