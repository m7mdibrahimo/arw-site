Andrade El Idolo and Tommaso Ciampa are set to square off in another match. During AEW Fright Night Collision, it was announced that Tommaso Ciampa will receive a rematch against El Idolo for the AEW National Championship next week. The two previously met at AEW WrestleDream, where Andrade successfully retained his title.

The upcoming episode of AEW Collision is scheduled for November 7 and will take place on Chris Jericho's Rock N Wrestling Rager.
