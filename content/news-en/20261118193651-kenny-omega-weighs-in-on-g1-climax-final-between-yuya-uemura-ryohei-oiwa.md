Kenny Omega recently shared his thoughts on the 2026 NJPW G1 Climax final between Yuya Uemura and Ryohei Oiwa. Omega, who did not watch the match live, was compelled to view it after it became a major topic of discussion on social media.

Omega told NJPW1972.com that while modern wrestling often trends toward increasing violence and danger, Uemura and Oiwa relied on fundamental techniques to convey emotion. He praised the bout for its uniqueness in the current landscape of the sport.

> "It was something that stood out from any wrestling we’ve seen in the last 10, 20 years. It was a beautiful match."

Omega expressed that the match gave him hope that technical, fundamental wrestling can still succeed in the modern era. He noted that while he strives to be an all-rounder, he has yet to showcase that fundamental aspect in AEW due to the promotion's more violent style. Omega is currently preparing to challenge Yota Tsuji for the IWGP Heavyweight Championship at Wrestle Kingdom 21.
