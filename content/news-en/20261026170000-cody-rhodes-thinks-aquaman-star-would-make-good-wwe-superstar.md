Cody Rhodes believes that actor Jason Momoa could eventually join WWE. In an appearance on ESPN's "The Rich Eisen Show," Rhodes noted that Momoa possesses the physical tools necessary to succeed as a WWE Superstar. Rhodes mentioned that he often speaks with his Street Fighter production colleagues about the possibility of collaborating with WWE and hosting celebrity guests. Rhodes also suggested that bodybuilder and actor Olivier Richters, who plays Zangief in the Street Fighter movie, would be an ideal fit for the ring. Rhodes joked about the prospect of slamming the seven-foot actor, similar to Hulk Hogan and Andre the Giant. He expressed his hope that many actors from his recent projects will eventually make their way to WWE.
