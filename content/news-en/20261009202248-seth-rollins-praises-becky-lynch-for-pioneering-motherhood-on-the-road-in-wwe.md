Seth Rollins spoke highly of his wife, Becky Lynch, reflecting on how they manage family life while touring. Since welcoming their first child in December 2020, the couple has traveled consistently with the help of a tour bus and a personal nanny. Rollins pointed out that Lynch helped pave the way for working mothers in the wrestling business.

In an interview with Vulture, Rollins highlighted how rare it was previously for female performers to balance motherhood with a full-time schedule.

> "You don’t want to be away from your kid, but you also don’t want to give up your career. Rebecca was one of the first to pioneer what it’s like to be a mom on the road and on the roster. You could count on one hand the number of women who did that before. The company has been very good about adjusting to the new normal too, which is great. It’s a major step toward equality in our industry."

Lynch is set for major action in the ring soon, as she and Liv Morgan challenge Stephanie Vaquer for the Women's World Championship at Money In The Bank.
