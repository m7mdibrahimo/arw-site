Fanatics sent word to Ringside News on November 9, 2026, announcing a new partnership with American Express to launch a line of credit cards. The cards feature trading card-inspired designs showcasing more than 500 athletes across WWE, the NFL, NBA, and UFC, allowing cardholders to select their favorite athlete to be printed directly on their card.

Initial designs include WWE star Cody Rhodes alongside other sports figures such as Karl-Anthony Towns and Joe Burrow. Cardholders can earn rewards redeemable for wrestling merchandise, collectibles, and event tickets across the Fanatics platform.

Fanatics Financial Services President Greg Lisiewski explained the goal behind the product.
