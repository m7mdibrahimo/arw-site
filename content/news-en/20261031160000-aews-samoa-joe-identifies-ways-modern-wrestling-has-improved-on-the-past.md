Samoa Joe recently discussed the next generation of professional wrestlers on an episode of SHAK Wrestling. Having built a career spanning nearly three decades, Joe pointed out two key areas where today's stars have surpassed past generations. According to Joe, adaptability and coordination have improved significantly over the years. He noted that modern performers often possess a more natural aptitude, comparing the trend to MMA where lighter weight classes display greater technical skill and closer matchups. During the podcast, Joe also named Brock Lesnar as the opponent who hit him the hardest throughout his lengthy career, while reflecting on other technical competitors he has worked with such as Bryan Danielson.
