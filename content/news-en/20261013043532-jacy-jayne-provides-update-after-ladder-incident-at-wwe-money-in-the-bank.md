Jacy Jayne has shared a status update following her participation in the 2026 women's Money in the Bank ladder match. Jayne competed in the bout alongside Tiffany Stratton, Lash Legend, Roxanne Perez, Sol Ruca, and Lola Vice. During the match, Jayne and Vice were battling on a ladder when they fell onto another ladder positioned between the ropes and the ring. The impact caused the ladder to shift, sending both competitors out of the ring and onto their backs and necks.

One day after the event, Jayne appeared at Collect-A-Con and addressed the incident. 
> "How many of you guys watched Money in the Bank last night? Did you guys see me almost die? I feel great today, in case you were wondering."

Tiffany Stratton emerged as the winner of the match. Stratton also suffered a difficult landing during the bout when the back of her head struck the ringside floor, and she provided an update on her condition following the show.
