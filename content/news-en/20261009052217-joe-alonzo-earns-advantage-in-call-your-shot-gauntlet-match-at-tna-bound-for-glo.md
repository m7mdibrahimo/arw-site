On the go-home episode of TNA iMPACT, Joe Alonzo secured the final entry spot in the upcoming Call Your Shot Gauntlet match at TNA Bound for Glory. By pinning Trey Miguel in an eight-man tag team match, Alonzo earned the number 20 position, while Miguel was relegated to the number one spot. The chaotic match saw AJ Francis eventually secure the win for his team by tagging in and delivering a TFL to Miguel.

This will mark Alonzo's first time competing in the Call Your Shot Gauntlet. The independent standout joined TNA this past August after a successful run in AAW. The winner of the gauntlet match receives a trophy that can be cashed in for any championship title at any time within one year. TNA Bound for Glory takes place this Sunday live from the Yuengling Center in Tampa, Florida, on TNA+.
