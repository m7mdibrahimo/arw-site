According to Wrestling Inc., wrestling veteran Bully Ray recently shared his thoughts on a potential storyline involving Judgment Day stablemates Liv Morgan and Roxanne Perez at WWE Money in the Bank. With Perez competing in the women's ladder match and Morgan challenging for the WWE Women's World Championship against champion Stephanie Vaquer and Becky Lynch, fans have speculated about a scenario where both win and Perez holds a title shot over her stablemate.

Speaking on Busted Open Radio, Bully Ray proposed a specific dramatic moment for the event rather than an immediate cash-in. According to Wrestling Inc., he envisioned a scenario where Morgan wins the triple threat match while her opponents are down, only for Perez to walk out with her briefcase.

> "Could you imagine if Liv Morgan wins, Becky's selling on the floor, the medical is attending to...Vaquer, and Liv is all by herself sitting in the corner, clutching that championship,"

> "She's out of breath, because she just won a big three-way dance. And Roxanne's music hits, and here she comes with the briefcase. Roxanne slides in, Roxanne has the briefcase, she's looking right down at Liv who's holding the championship, right?"

> "You get that moment, you get those eyes locking. Liv is scared, she doesn't know what's going on. Roxanne knows she's got that ace up her sleeve in the Money in the Bank. The referee is right there. 'Do you want me to ring the bell right now? Do you want me to ring the bell?' And then Roxanne helps up Liv. You have this moment of tension, maybe the embrace or maybe Roxanne walks away. You can really set the tone and the table for great storytelling moving forward right there."

To reach this point at WWE Money in the Bank, both competitors must first get past tough competition. Morgan faces Vaquer and Lynch, while Perez battles Lash Legend, Lola Vice, Jacy Jayne, Sol Ruca, and Tiffany Stratton in the women's ladder match.
