Dragon Lee is set to compete at WWE Survivor Series. During a backstage segment on Monday's episode of WWE Raw, Rey Mysterio announced that Dragon Lee will represent Team Mexico in their upcoming match against Team USA at the event.

With this announcement, Dragon Lee joins a lineup that already features Penta, Rey Fenix, and Rey Mysterio. This will mark Dragon Lee's first premium live event match since WrestleMania 42, where he participated in a ladder match for the Intercontinental Championship.

Team USA currently consists of Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, LA Knight, and Je'Von Evans. Both Team Mexico and Team USA have one spot remaining on their respective five-man rosters.
