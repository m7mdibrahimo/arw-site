WWE executive director of creative writing and talent Bruce Prichard is set to retire after WrestleMania 43 and remain with the company as a consultant. According to Wrestling Inc., Dave Meltzer provided details on Wrestling Observer Radio regarding Prichard needing major surgery from an injury dating back to 1991.

> "He never knew he broke his neck when it happened. This is like, 35 years later. Who was he working with...Ultimate Warrior? Probably messed up a lot of guys. I heard yesterday that it was back surgery. But either way, he's having one or two major surgeries. And going on the road every week, it's just going to be too difficult, makes all the sense in the world. He's staying with the company. He's going to be a consultant. He's planning on working through WrestleMania and then taking the time off the road."

Retiring will allow him to leave Stamford, Connecticut. Meanwhile, WWE Hall of Fame member Shawn Michaels is expected to take over his role.
