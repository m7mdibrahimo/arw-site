Former AEW World Champion Swerve Strickland believes professional wrestling fans will never see a joint event between WWE and AEW. When asked in an interview with Billboard if a cross-promotional show could happen during his lifetime, Strickland offered a firm response.

> "No. Not in my lifetime at least."

Strickland explained that corporate control and brand protection are the main barriers preventing such a collaboration. He compared WWE's approach to the NFL's handling of its brand, noting that WWE would never risk letting stars from another promotion look superior to their own talents.

> "It’s control. It’s all about marketing. It’s like the NFL, WWE has to protect their shield. There’s no way they’d allow any of our wrestlers to look better and be better than them."

He pointed out that WWE must strictly protect top performers like Cody Rhodes, Roman Reigns, Gunther, and Oba Femi, as well as women's division stars Rhea Ripley, Liv Morgan, and Chelsea Green. While Strickland noted that talent in the locker room would love to participate in dream matches regardless of who wins, he admitted that leverage and corporate politics make it impossible. Strickland also added that he no longer seeks personal dream matches for himself, stating that he now aims to be the fantasy match for others.
