Swerve Strickland recently discussed the moments in All Elite Wrestling that crossed the line, pointing to Jon Moxley attempting to drown Darby Allin as the time AEW went a little too far. During an interview with Sportskeeda, Strickland reflected on his own hardcore matches, including his brutal Texas Deathmatch and Lights Out Steel Cage match against Hangman Page, before addressing Moxley's segment with Allin. Strickland remarked on the striking incident by saying, "Darby getting drowned. I was like 'oh we're here.' Okay." When asked to name the craziest star in the company, Strickland immediately pointed to Allin, noting that he goes all out across the board and mentioning that he has visited Allin's home. Additionally, Strickland discussed several roster members he believes will eventually become the AEW World Champion, while noting that PAC was previously his go-to answer.
