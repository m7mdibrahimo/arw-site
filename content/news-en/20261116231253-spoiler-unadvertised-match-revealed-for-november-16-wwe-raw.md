WWE has an unadvertised match planned for tonight's episode of WWE RAW. According to a report, WWE Women's United States Champion Jacy Jayne is scheduled to face Stephanie Vaquer in a singles match at the Lenovo Center in Raleigh, North Carolina. While the bout was not included in the official lineup for the November 16, 2026, episode, it is listed on the early internal rundown. WWE could still adjust its plans before the show goes live.

Jayne and Vaquer have a significant history from their time in NXT, having battled for the NXT Women's Championship in May 2025. Vaquer recently returned to WWE television on the November 13 episode of WWE SmackDown, where she confronted Charlotte Flair, Fatal Influence, and other members of the women's division. During that segment, Jayne attempted to attack Vaquer but missed her kick. Vaquer is currently aligned with Charlotte Flair, Alexa Bliss, Bayley, and Tatum Paxley as they prepare to face Jade Cargill and her allies at Survivor Series on November 28. Tonight's match would allow Jayne and Vaquer to address their ongoing rivalry before the upcoming WarGames event.
