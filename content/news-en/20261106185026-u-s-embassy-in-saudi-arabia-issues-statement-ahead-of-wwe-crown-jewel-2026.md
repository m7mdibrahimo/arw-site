The United States Embassy in Saudi Arabia released an official statement highlighting WWE's role in international relations and sports diplomacy ahead of WWE Crown Jewel 2026. On Thursday, November 5, the embassy in Riyadh shared a message on X to promote the November 6 premium live event, noting that WWE is an American company headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut, and helps bring people together across borders.

> "American sports diplomacy comes to Riyadh! We are thrilled to share the excitement as WWE Crown Jewel returns to Saudi Arabia this weekend! Crown Jewel is entirely created, owned, and produced by @WWE, a wholly American professional wrestling promotion headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut. American sports entertainment exports create shared unforgettable experiences, strengthening the bonds between the American and Saudi people."

WWE has been organizing major shows in Saudi Arabia since 2018, and Crown Jewel has become an essential part of its international schedule. This partnership will reach a historic new milestone in 2027 when Saudi Arabia hosts WrestleMania 43 as the first edition held outside North America. This year's event takes place at ANB Arena in Riyadh, also known as The Venue, following a relocation from Riyadh Season Stadium at KAFD to the indoor arena, featuring top stars like Roman Reigns, Sami Zayn, Cody Rhodes, Randy Orton, and Liv Morgan.
