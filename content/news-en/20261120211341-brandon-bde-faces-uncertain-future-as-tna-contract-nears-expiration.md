TNA Wrestling may soon lose another talent, as YouTube star Brandon BDE (Brandon Collymore) is approaching the end of his contract. According to Fightful Select, his agreement is set to expire at the end of December 2026, with no confirmation that the promotion has initiated negotiations for an extension or established creative plans for him beyond that date.

BDE officially announced his signing in January 2026. At the time, he expressed his enthusiasm for the deal, stating:

> "Your boy has signed a contract with TNA Wrestling. What a time to be alive, man."

Since debuting at Bound for Glory in October 2025, he has competed in over two dozen matches. His recent work includes defeating Mr. Elegance to qualify for the X-Division Championship scramble at Bound for Glory 2026 and participating in the Ultimate X match at TNA Destination X on November 15. With ongoing independent bookings, BDE has options should he depart. TNA President Carlos Silva recently held a backstage meeting to address numerous expiring contracts. While Xia Brookside has already received an offer to remain with the company, BDE’s status remains unconfirmed as he nears the new year.
