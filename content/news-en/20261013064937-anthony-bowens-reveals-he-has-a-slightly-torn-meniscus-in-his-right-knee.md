Anthony Bowens recently discussed the various injuries he has sustained during his professional wrestling career in an interview with Michael Fairman. The AEW star shared that he is currently dealing with a slightly torn meniscus in his right knee. Bowens detailed his injury history, stating, "I’ve had a few diagnosed concussions. I’m sure there’s been undiagnosed. I’ve had a seizure in the ring. My MCL’s torn; it had to be repaired. I currently have a slightly torn meniscus in my right knee. My left meniscus got torn and I had to have that repaired. Let’s see, I bruised nerves in my lower back once." He added that there are likely many more injuries he has forgotten. This update comes after reports that Bowens is now a free agent following his tenure with All Elite Wrestling since 2020.
