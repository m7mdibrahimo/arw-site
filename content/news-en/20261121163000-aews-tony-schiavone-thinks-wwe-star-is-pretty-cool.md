AEW senior producer and play-by-play veteran Tony Schiavone spoke about Roman Reigns during an episode of his What Happened When podcast. When co-host Conrad Thompson asked if he disliked Reigns, the AEW broadcaster made it clear that he harbors no ill will toward the WWE star.

> "I've never met him, but I've always kind of thought he was pretty cool"

Reigns is currently preparing for the Men's WarGames match at Survivor Series, scheduled for Saturday, November 28, at Daikin Park in Houston, Texas.
