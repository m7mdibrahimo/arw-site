John Cena recently explained that staying in WWE longer would have been unfair to his wife, Shay Shariatzadeh. During an appearance on TODAY’s Making Space with Hoda Kotb, Cena discussed his retirement and how the physical demands of wrestling no longer aligned with his life priorities. At 49, he realized that continuing would require immense preparation and recovery, noting that he is no longer as fast as younger elite performers. Cena admitted that his focus shifted away from WWE as he grew older, making his relationship with his wife his main priority. He also considered the long-term physical impact of another serious injury, asking himself if it was fair to risk his health just for a few more cheers if it meant leaving an unnecessary burden on his partner later in life. Although he still loves and misses wrestling every day, Cena ultimately decided that walking away was the right choice for his future.
