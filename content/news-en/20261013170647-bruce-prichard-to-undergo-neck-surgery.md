Bruce Prichard is scheduled to undergo neck surgery. Conrad Thompson shared the news on the latest episode of the 83 Weeks podcast, noting that his co-host has been struggling with a neck injury for years. According to Thompson, the injury stems from a 1991 incident during a taping of WWF Superstars, where the Ultimate Warrior attacked Prichard. Prichard only recently discovered the extent of the damage.

> "I don’t know that Bruce has revealed it but Bruce has been dealing with a neck injury. It turns out that Bruce is gonna have to have some surgery on that rascal and he hadn’t been having the best quality of life recently. He’s on the mend; he’s doing rehab and all that jazz but, he’s hurting for certain… Do you remember way back when he was Brother Love and the Ultimate Warrior beat him up?" 

Thompson added,

> "He broke his neck and he just found out just recently. All these years later… He’s on the mend. He’s gonna be alright. But keep him in your thoughts and prayers." 

Prichard currently works as a member of the WWE creative team. The specific date for the upcoming surgery has not been disclosed.
