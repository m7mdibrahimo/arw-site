WWE NXT star Wren Sinclair joined the company in December 2023 after an independent career. Growing up, she watched professional wrestling before stepping away for a time. During an appearance on the No-Contest Wrestling Podcast, Sinclair explained how a former NXT star brought her back into the sport when she and her brother attended a live event in Austin, Texas.

 Sinclair recalled seeing a triple threat match featuring Enzo Amore and Big Cass, whom she did not know at the time. However, Amore's mic work immediately captivated her.

> "My name is Enzo Amore," she remembered. "The whole place just started saying every single word with him. I was like, 'Oh my gosh. This is power.' Like, I need that. That really kind of brought me back. I was like, 'How do I do this?" 

Amore debuted on NXT in May 2013 and teamed with Big Cass until their main roster call-up after WrestleMania 32 in April 2016. While Cass returned to WWE after a stint in AEW, Amore has not wrestled there since his January 2018 release, despite rumors of a reunion.
