Roman Reigns is bringing his Tribal Chief persona into the Street Fighter universe, though Cody Rhodes did not make the cut for his dinner party. In a video released by the official Street Fighter X account on October 18, Reigns was asked to name which characters he would invite to sit at the Head of the Table. Reigns, who portrays Akuma in the upcoming film, provided a breakdown of who he considers worthy of a seat.

Reigns welcomed Chun-Li, jokingly noting that he did not want to face her wrath, and praised Ken as a fun guest. However, he rejected Ryu, claiming the character would never stop fighting, and immediately dismissed Guile, who is played by Cody Rhodes in the film, saying, "No, you know why." Reigns also invited Dhalsim and Zangief, the latter out of fear of being put through the table, and welcomed M. Bison because of his mastery of the Dark Hado. Finally, Reigns declared that his own character, Akuma, is the true Head of the Table. Reigns noted that the role required intense martial arts training, but he enjoyed blending his WWE persona with the film's world.
