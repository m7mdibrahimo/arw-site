Lucha Libre AAA has officially added a major title defense and another marquee match to the November 21 episode of AAA on FOX. The promotion confirmed that the Wagner Brothers, featuring El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr. and Galeno del Mal, will put their AAA Tag Team Championships on the line against the formidable duo of El Mesías and Mecha Wolf.

In addition to the tag team championship clash, the card will feature women's division action as Flammer goes head-to-head against Faby Apache. These newly announced matchups round out an already loaded lineup set for Saturday's broadcast.

The updated lineup for the November 21 show also includes a defense of the AAA Trios Championships, where Perros del Mal (Daga, Angel, and Berto) face El Grande Americano, La Parka, and Rey Fenix.
