Queen Aminata captured the AEW TBS Championship by defeating Persephone during the October 28, 2026, episode of AEW Dynamite, which served as AEW's Fright Night Dynamite in St. Louis, Missouri. Despite repeated interference from Britt Baker, who distracted the challenger, targeted Aminata's injured neck, and struck her with the championship belt while the referee was distracted, Aminata persevered. After Persephone failed to put her away with a top-rope moonsault, Aminata recovered and delivered a headbutt followed by a running knee strike to secure the pinfall and the title.
