Blake Christian and Lee Johnson of The Swirl sent a strong warning to the tag team division in AEW while speaking backstage at NJPW Super Junior Tag League. Christian and Johnson currently hold a 3-1 record with 4 points in the tournament following their victory over House of Torture, featuring Dick Togo and SHO. "When Blake and I get back to AEW, all of you teams, you are fucked. Not only are you fucked, you are royally fucked because Blake and I have not only gotten a whole lot better, but we have gained a whole lot of confidence. With that said, we don't care if it's Edge and Christian. We don't care if it's your Young Bucks. We don't care if it's FTR. When we get back, you are royally fucked. Because we are not just a part of your story. We are the damn story. I hope the Young Bucks, because I would royally love to take those titles from them. But our mindset is on this IWGP tournament, winning those championships. That's our current mindset," Christian said. The Young Bucks currently hold the AEW World Tag Team Championship and are set to defend their titles against Mark Davis and Jake Doyle at AEW Full Gear in November. Meanwhile, The Swirl also holds the ROH World Six-Man Tag Team Championship alongside Jay Lethal.
