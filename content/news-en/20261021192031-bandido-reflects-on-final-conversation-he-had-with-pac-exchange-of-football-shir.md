Bandido recently reflected on his final conversation with PAC and the story of how they exchanged their respective football club shirts. PAC tragically passed away nearly a month ago, following his final match at AEW All Out as a member of the Death Riders faction. During an interview with Jimmy V3, Bandido shared warm memories of their time together in Dragongate and their last meeting. Bandido stated that PAC gifted him a Newcastle shirt, while he gave PAC a Santos Laguna shirt, recalling PAC's parting words and his desire to keep his memory alive.
