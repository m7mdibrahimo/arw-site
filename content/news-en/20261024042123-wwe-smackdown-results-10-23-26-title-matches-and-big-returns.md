WWE SmackDown aired on October 23, 2026, live from the Toyota Arena in Ontario, California, featuring championship matches and high-stakes rivalries. Chelsea Green successfully defended the Women's Championship against Roxanne Perez, while Michin and B-Fab captured the Women's Tag Team Championship by defeating Tatum Paxley and Alexa Bliss. In a brutal Street Fight, Blake Monroe emerged victorious against Giulia. Randy Orton confronted Cody Rhodes to set up a Last Man Standing match for the title at WWE Crown Jewel. Meanwhile, a chaotic six-man tag team main event saw Penta, Rey Fenix, and Trick Williams defeat Sami Zayn, Johnny Gargano, and Ricky Saints.
