WWE’s top stars are increasingly pursuing Hollywood opportunities, but that freedom is reportedly creating backstage tension. According to Jonathan Coachman speaking on The Two Count with JDfromNY206, a source claims that getting major names to commit to long-term storylines has become a genuine issue because they are constantly looking toward outside projects. While discussing talents like Batista, Drew McIntyre, Cody Rhodes, and Roman Reigns, Coachman noted that while nobody opposes outside ventures, complications arise when WWE requires stars locked into months of creative plans. Coachman referenced Rhodes' recent comments about the freedom of his new deal, warning that this flexibility could eventually impact ticket sales if fans planning months ahead cannot be certain which stars will actually appear.
