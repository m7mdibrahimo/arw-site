Reports indicate that Kevin Owens is slated to kick off tonight's WWE SmackDown broadcast from Evansville, Indiana, according to Viper Reports.

Internal rundowns reveal that Owens is positioned for the show's opening spot, though these early schedules remain subject to adjustment prior to airtime. Further specifics regarding his role or potential co-stars in the segment have not been detailed.

This comes as WWE gears up for its final blue-brand episode before Survivor Series WarGames. The card features Jade Cargill facing Charlotte Flair in a WarGames Advantage Match, alongside Damian Priest competing against Trick Williams in non-title action. Furthermore, Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki team up against The War Raiders, while Cody Rhodes unveils the final member joining Team USA.
