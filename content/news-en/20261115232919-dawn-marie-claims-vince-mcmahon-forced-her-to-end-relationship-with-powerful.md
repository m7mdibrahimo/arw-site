Dawn Marie revealed that during her time in WWE, the company's backstage environment made it difficult for women to openly date within the industry. She claimed that Vince McMahon personally intervened in one of her relationships, forcing her to end it because he disapproved. According to Marie, the mystery man she was dating held significant power in the industry, and he allegedly threatened to quit WWE if she were fired. Marie described the era as a "good old boys club" with unwritten rules that could end a woman's career for minor mistakes, including dating the wrong person. She also claimed WWE would use the brand split between Raw and SmackDown to separate couples the company suspected were dating, an experience she said she went through herself. Marie stated that the mystery individual she dated was willing to put their own career on the line to save her job, which she believes prevented her from being fired sooner. However, the ultimatum from McMahon remained clear: the relationship had to end immediately to ensure her continued employment.
