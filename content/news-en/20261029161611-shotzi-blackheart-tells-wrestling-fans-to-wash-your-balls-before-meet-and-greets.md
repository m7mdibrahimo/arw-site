Shotzi Blackheart has a blunt message for fans attending meet-and-greets: practice basic hygiene or do not expect a hug. Speaking on Knockouts and 3 Counts, the WWE star discussed locker-room etiquette and shared straightforward advice for both newcomers and attendees. While discussing pet peeves with the host, Shotzi highlighted the importance of cleanliness, stressing that fans meeting her must shower and wear deodorant. She noted that while locker-room etiquette is important, the real issue often stems from stinky fans approaching wrestlers at public appearances. "Man, I really like the ‘wash your balls’ one, because, man, there's, like, a lot of stinky people. I mean, this is more so for the fans and not, like, the locker room," she said. Shotzi reiterated her rule, stating that she will refuse hugs from anyone who fails to follow this basic hygiene advice before stepping up to meet her.
