Tsunagu Sato is sidelined following a head injury. Pro Wrestling NOAH originally planned to begin its Jr. Grand Prix tournament on November 9 at Korakuen Hall. While the event will proceed as scheduled, the tournament matches are now set to start on November 14.

Sato, who represents Michinoku Pro Wrestling, suffered a concussion on November 1 while competing for his home promotion. NOAH confirmed that he will miss the November 9 show and noted that his health status could also prevent him from participating on November 14. Sato is 21 years old and debuted in 2025.
