Roman Reigns addressed recent remarks from Conrad Thompson, who previously labeled him one of the most overrated stars in WWE history. The Tribal Chief made it clear that he does not care about the criticism at all.

Appearing on Club Shay Shay, Shannon Sharpe asked Reigns about Thompson's comments and Booker T's public defense. Reigns laughed off the question and stated that people simply use his name to generate attention and views.

> "No, because he—I mean, the truth is, are there a lot of people watching that guy's show? So you're going to say stuff. You already know. I'm trying to get—I'm a metric mover. That type of stuff, they know I give views and all that stuff, so it's fine. I mean, it is what it is."

He added that Thompson's background and associations likely influence his opinions. Reigns noted he has no interest in lengthy debates because he understands his massive drawing power.
