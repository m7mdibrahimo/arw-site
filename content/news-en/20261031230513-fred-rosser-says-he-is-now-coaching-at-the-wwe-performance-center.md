Fred Rosser, formerly known as Darren Young, has returned to WWE as a coach at the WWE Performance Center. Rosser, who was a member of the WWE roster from 2005 to 2017 and famously part of The Nexus stable alongside Wade Barrett, David Otunga, and others, announced the news on social media. "Nine years ago today, I was released from @WWE. Today, I’m back at @wwenxt, fighting back tears of joy every time I pull up to the Performance Center and grateful for the opportunity to give back to the next generation," Rosser wrote. He added that he is eager to share the experiences he gained during his time in the ring to help shape the future of the company. Rosser expressed his excitement about being back in the environment where he once trained before reaching the main roster. His last WWE match took place in January 2017 on an episode of Main Event, where he was defeated by Epico.
