The Young Bucks have paid an emotional tribute to PAC, reflecting on their long-standing friendship that began decades before their time together in AEW. Following the recent AEW Dynamite tribute show dedicated to PAC, Matt and Nick Jackson spoke about their bond with the late star on their Being The Elite vlog.

Matt Jackson shared memories of meeting PAC during their first trip to Japan in 2008, noting how PAC took care of them and showed them the ropes when they were young and overwhelmed. Matt also recalled a recent conversation where PAC pointed out that they had known each other for nearly twenty years and would be sixty in another twenty, expressing heartbreak that PAC will never reach that age.
