Wrestling legend Tiger Jeet Singh made a turbulent return to Japan after 14 years away when he struck a spectator in the head with his sword during Megumi Kudo's 40th anniversary show at Yoyogi National Stadium in Tokyo.

Working as a special observer, the 82-year-old veteran rushed toward the crowd with his saber and cut a fan's forehead, leaving him bleeding and requiring a hospital trip. Singh refused to apologize after the incident, blaming the injured man for failing to move out of his way. Former WWE star Tiger Ali Singh supported his father's stance, while event organizer Megumi Kudo issued an official apology for the dangerous mishap.
