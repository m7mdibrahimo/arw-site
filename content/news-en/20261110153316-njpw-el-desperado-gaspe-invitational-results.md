New Japan Pro-Wrestling presented the NJPW El Desperado GasPE Invitational card on November 9, broadcasting live on NJPW World from the Shin-Kiba 1st RING in Tokyo, Japan. 

Naruki Doi vs. Taiji Ishimori went to a 10-minute time limit draw.
Bishamon (Hirooki Goto & YOSHI-HASHI) def. MxM Collection (Mansoor & Mason Madden)
Mason Madden def. Amida Collection AB (Fuminori Abe), Danshoku Collection AF (Danshoku Dino), Hirai Collection WT (Master Wato), KUSHIDA Collection WKK (KUSHIDA), Mansoor, Matsumoto Collection YL (Tatsuya Matsumoto), Taguchi Collection RT (Ryusuke Taguchi), and YOH
El Desperado (Starlight Kid) & MxM Collection (Mansoor & Mason Madden) def. CIMA, Kazuki Hirata & Maki Ito
