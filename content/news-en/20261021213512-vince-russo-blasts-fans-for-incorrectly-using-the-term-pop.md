Former WWE writer Vince Russo recently criticized wrestling fans for misusing the term “pop,” explaining that a true pop requires buildup rather than just an instantaneous crowd reaction. Speaking on The Coach & Bro Show, Russo used Roman Reigns entering through the curtain as an example, noting that cheering during an entrance is simply a reaction and not a pop. Russo explained that wrestlers traditionally used the term to describe building a match or storyline toward one massive reaction, comparing the structural buildup to a sexual climax. He pointed to the classic hot-tag formula as a prime example of building toward a pop. Russo urged fans to stop misusing the word, admitting he does not know when fans began applying the term to entrances.
