AEW has officially scheduled the 2026 edition of Winter Is Coming for Wednesday, December 9, at Arena Monterrey in Monterrey, Mexico. The event will feature both AEW Dynamite and AEW Collision broadcasts from the same venue. AEW confirmed the details through social media, noting that presales begin October 13, with general ticket sales starting October 16 at 10 am CST.

Promotional materials for the event highlight several stars, including Andrade El Idolo, Bandido, Persephone, Thunder Rosa, and The New Level members Austin Creed and Kofi. This marks AEW's debut in Monterrey and follows the company's interest in expanding further into Mexico after the success of Grand Slam Mexico. Winter Is Coming has been a staple of the AEW calendar since 2020, previously hosting major moments like Sting's debut and Kenny Omega's world title win. The Monterrey show arrives during a busy end-of-year schedule for AEW, falling between Full Gear in Phoenix and Worlds End in Minneapolis.
