TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Champions Allie and Kiera Hogan have challenged Zoey Serrano to a title match at Bound For Glory. The challenge was issued during the October 8, 2026, episode of TNA iMPACT, with Serrano invited to select a partner of her choice for the showdown this Sunday at the Yuengling Center in Tampa, Florida.

While the match is not yet officially confirmed on the TNA card, it would add to an already stacked lineup for the promotion's biggest annual event. Bound For Glory currently features five championship bouts, including Nic Nemeth defending the TNA World Championship against Leon Slater and a four-way match for the TNA International Championship. The event will begin at 4:30 p.m. ET on October 11, following the Hall of Fame ceremony.
