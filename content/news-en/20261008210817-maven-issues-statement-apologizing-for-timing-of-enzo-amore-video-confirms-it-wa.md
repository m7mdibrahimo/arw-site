Maven has issued a formal apology regarding the timing of his recent YouTube video featuring Enzo Amore. The video, which included comments from Amore criticizing AEW, sparked backlash due to its release following the promotion's tribute show for PAC. Amore previously clarified on social media that the interview was recorded in March 2025, a point Maven confirmed in his statement.

> Hey guys, I wanted to address the Enzo Amore video. First, we want to apologize to Enzo and any fans who were upset or hurt by the timing of our latest upload. Nothing malicious was intended, but looking back, we fully understand why the timing bothered some people. Moving forward, we’ll make sure we’re more mindful about our content and when we release it.

Maven explained that the footage had been sitting unreleased for a long time after the team honored Amore's request to remove a specific portion of the interview. They decided to publish the edited version last Friday as supplementary content because their regular upload was shorter than usual. Maven emphasized that the sensitive portion remained excluded and that they are learning from the situation to improve their future content.
