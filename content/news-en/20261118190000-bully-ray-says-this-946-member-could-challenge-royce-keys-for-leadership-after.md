Bully Ray believes Cruz Montana could challenge Royce Keys for leadership of The 946 following a strong showing on WWE Raw. Montana and Carmelo Hayes defeated The Usos in the WarGames Advantage match, with Montana securing the pinfall. Speaking on "Busted Open," Ray praised Montana's leadership outside the ring and his overall career progression since moving up from NXT. Ray noted that Montana has the potential to eventually become a major star in WWE. This match marked Montana's first appearance on the main roster since his call-up, giving 946 a crucial advantage ahead of their WarGames match against The Bloodline.
