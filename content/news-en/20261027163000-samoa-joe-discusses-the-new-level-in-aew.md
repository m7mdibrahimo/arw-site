Samoa Joe asserted that the move to AEW will grant Austin Creed and Kofi a genuine opportunity to showcase their full capabilities following years spent in WWE. Joe, who achieved major success in Tony Khan's promotion, noted that fans will witness unprecedented style variety and flexibility that was not sufficiently visible before.

> "I think if anything, you're going to start seeing the kind of the range and the variety and the actual flexibility they have stylistically, You know, I think for a lot of years, you might have seen a more restrained version of what they were in the past [in WWE], and I think now you're going to kind of see them to their full potential."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1xvB7gCyA4A

For their part, the duo do not feel anxious about adapting to their new environment, emphasizing that their new team name reflects their desire to deliver strong and high-level competition.

The pair previously participated in reviving tag team competition inside WWE alongside teams like The Usos, and they aim to repeat that success in AEW to face tough teams like FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns, in addition to looking forward to a prospective rivalry against Kenny Omega and The Young Bucks.
