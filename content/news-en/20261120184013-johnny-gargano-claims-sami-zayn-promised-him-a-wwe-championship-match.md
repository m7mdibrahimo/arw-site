Johnny Gargano is reminding Sami Zayn of a promise regarding the Undisputed WWE Championship. In an interview with TV Insider, Gargano reflected on how CM Punk offered him a championship match in Cleveland, which led to his current alliance with Zayn. Gargano noted that Zayn treated him with respect when others mocked him. Gargano also pointed out their similar paths in independent wrestling and NXT, and stated that Zayn promised him a title opportunity.
