Ilja Dragunov recently spoke about the final stretch of his run with WWE and his decision to join AEW. The former WWE Superstar made his AEW debut at the AEW WrestleDream pay-per-view on October 17, and later secured a successful in-ring debut against Speedball Mike Bailey on AEW Dynamite. Reflecting on his departure after seven years with WWE, Dragunov admitted in an interview with The Takedown on Sports Illustrated that he felt the need for a change following his return from an injury last year. He stated that although winning the United States Championship upon his return was a memorable moment, he eventually felt things were different and decided to explore new opportunities. Dragunov also noted that WWE's contract renewal offer was not enough to convince him to stay.
