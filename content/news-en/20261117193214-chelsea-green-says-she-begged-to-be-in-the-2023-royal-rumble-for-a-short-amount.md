Chelsea Green reflected on her brief appearance in the 2023 Women's Royal Rumble match, revealing that she actually begged to be in and out of the match quickly. During the 2023 Royal Rumble match, Green made her return to WWE but lasted only five seconds before Rhea Ripley immediately tossed her over the top rope. Speaking with Brad Gilmore and The Collection, Green explained that she originally was scheduled to stay in the match much longer before being eliminated.

> "That was step one, like coming back and them saying, 'Hey, you're going to be in the Rumble, and at first being in it for about, I believe I was in it, you know, for about six numbers before I was going to be eliminated, and I, begged them to let me be in it for a short amount of time, and I'll never forget standing in front of Rhea right before she goes out, and I said to her, 'if we don't record for shortest amount of time in the Rumble, I'm going to be so mad at you because I'll run, I'll run right across the ring, but you know, you got to toss my ass out of there.' Now I'm like, man, that was really critical to setting the tone really for the next few years."

Green added that she asked Ripley to make sure they broke the record for the shortest time in the Rumble, noting that the memorable moment proved crucial in setting the tone for her character in the years following her return.
