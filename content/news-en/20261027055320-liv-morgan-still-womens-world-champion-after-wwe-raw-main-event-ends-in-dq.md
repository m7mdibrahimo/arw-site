Liv Morgan retained her WWE Women's World Championship in the main event of the October 26 episode of "WWE Raw" after her match against Becky Lynch ended in a disqualification. Lynch, who was added to the match after Stephanie Vaquer was pulled due to medical reasons, dominated much of the contest. Morgan escaped a Disarmher attempt thanks to interference from Roxanne Perez and Raquel Rodriguez, who distracted Lynch. The Judgment Day further complicated the match by attacking Lynch on the outside, leading to a brawl. Lynch eventually hit the Manhandle Slam on Morgan, but The Judgment Day broke up the pin, forcing the disqualification. Post-match, chaos ensued as Chelsea Green, IYO Sky, and Tiffany Stratton all confronted the champions. Stratton attempted to cash in her Money in the Bank briefcase, but Dominik Mysterio dragged Morgan to safety before the bell could ring.
