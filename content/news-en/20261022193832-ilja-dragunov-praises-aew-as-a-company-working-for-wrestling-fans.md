Ilja Dragunov recently shared his thoughts on AEW and why fans enjoy the product. Following his debut win against Mike Bailey on the October 21 episode of AEW Dynamite, Dragunov praised the promotion for its dedication to professional wrestling. In an interview with The Takedown on Sports Illustrated, he explained that the company focuses entirely on delivering high-level craftsmanship for fans who love the sport. Dragunov noted that audiences appreciate this clear dedication when watching Dynamite, Collision, or pay-per-view events.
