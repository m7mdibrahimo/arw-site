WWE NXT returns tonight, October 13, 2026, with a loaded card as the brand builds toward its next Premium Live Event. The show will feature a high-stakes match to determine the next challenger for the NXT Championship.

Tony D'Angelo and Myles Borne will face off in a No. 1 Contender’s Match. The winner will earn a future title shot against current NXT Champion Grayson Waller. This follows Waller's recent successful defense against EK Prosper.

The Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic continues with two quarterfinal bouts. Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno will battle Fraxiom, while Vanity Project takes on the team of Mini Vikingo and Chaz “Starboy” Hall. Additionally, Saquon Shugars will return to singles action against Tristan Angels.

WWE NXT airs live from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, at 8 PM ET on the CW Network. International fans can stream the event on Netflix.
