El Grande Americano successfully defended the AAA Mega Championship against Dominik Mysterio at the Lucha Libre AAA Heroes Inmortales 2026 event. During the match, Dorian Roldan attempted to interfere on behalf of Dominik by striking Americano with a chair. Later, Omos also intervened to aid Dominik, getting into an altercation at ringside with Pimpinela Escarlata before stopping the referee's three-count. AAA General Manager Rey Mysterio then came to the ring and struck Omos with a chair. When Dominik tried to take the chair from his father, Rey hit him with it as well, allowing El Grande Americano to capitalize and retain his title.
