Tessa Blanchard recently addressed a fan on social media who brought up past allegations of racism against her. The incident occurred on November 6, after Blanchard jokingly commented on a post about Mila Moore ending her relationship with Jason Hotch on TNA television. One fan responded to her comment by stating, "You spit on a black woman and you're calling someone else cruel??" Blanchard replied by stating she was simply commenting to a friend and emphasized that she has focused on personal growth. She wrote, "I’m just commenting to a friend. Please don’t turn that into something it’s not. I’ve worked hard to grow as a woman, and I’m still learning every day. I’m not interested in going back and forth. Let’s move past the same tired judgments. Por fin." These allegations stem from a 2020 incident where La Rosa Negra accused Blanchard of using a racial slur and spitting on her in Japan. While Blanchard has disputed those claims, she revealed in December 2025 that she and La Rosa Negra had reconciled and occasionally discuss wrestling. Blanchard opted not to engage in a prolonged online argument, signaling her desire to move forward.
