Cody Rhodes and Roman Reigns took on demanding stunt work for their roles in the Street Fighter movie. Rhodes, who portrays Guile, revealed on The Rich Eisen Show that the production team didn't assume the WWE Superstars were experts in film combat just because they are wrestlers. Rhodes admitted he required extensive training to adapt to the camera-focused choreography, which differs significantly from the in-ring style he is accustomed to in WWE. He specifically noted that Roman Reigns, who plays Akuma, was also heavily involved in complex stunt sequences. The stunt team ensured both men trained daily to meet the film's requirements, treating them like any other actors rather than relying on their wrestling backgrounds. With the movie now released, fans can see the results of their training and stunt work on screen.
