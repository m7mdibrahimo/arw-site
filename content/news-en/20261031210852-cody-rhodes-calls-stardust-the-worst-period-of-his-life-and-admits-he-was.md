Cody Rhodes reflected on his time portraying Stardust in WWE, admitting that it was the worst period of his entire life. During an appearance on the Double Coverage Podcast, Rhodes discussed the struggles he faced before leaving the company in 2016, noting that the gimmick made him feel like he would never reach the top of the wrestling business. He confessed that he regrets not speaking up more about the creative direction at the time, acknowledging that he shares some of the blame for not using his voice when it mattered most.
