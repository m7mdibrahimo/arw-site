Cash Wheeler believes that FTR has the advantage if their ongoing best-of-five series against the Motor City Machine Guns goes the full distance. Speaking on Going Ringside, Wheeler noted that FTR thrives in marathon-style matches. 

> “I mean, shockingly, I see it working out in FTR’s favor. Everybody knows that for us in particular, the longer a match goes or the longer a series goes, the better off we are. We thrive in those marathon style matches. We've done 58 minutes with Jay White and Juice Robinson. We've done 45 minutes with Mark and Jay Briscoe. When you get into those deep waters, when you get past the point of a normal match and you're in a new realm, I think we thrive there. I think that's our millu, I don’t know if Motor City can say the same thing. They might be more explosive in those first 15 minutes or those first 20 minutes, but once you get past that, which we are very good at making sure that we can drag that out a little bit to get that advantage. I think it plays to our favor.”

Wheeler also blamed their first-match loss at AEW All Out on the physical toll of a previous ladder match, noting that they are finally returning to full health. The third match between FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns is set for AEW Fright Night Dynamite.
