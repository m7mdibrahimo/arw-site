Karl Anderson revealed that Seth Rollins once had a planned loss changed after speaking directly to Vince McMahon. Speaking on the latest episode of Talk'n Shop, Anderson and Luke Gallows discussed the differences between working in WWE and NJPW. Anderson recalled that The Good Brothers were originally scheduled to pin Rollins while he was World Heavyweight Champion, but the finish was altered after Rollins talked to McMahon.

Anderson noted that Rollins returned with a new finish where Rollins rolled up Anderson instead, followed by a post-match beatdown. While acknowledging that the change might not have altered their careers entirely, Anderson admitted that pinning the reigning champion could have helped them at the time. He also questioned how plans discussed through a week of production meetings could change after a single conversation.
