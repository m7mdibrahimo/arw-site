In May 2025, Jacy Jayne captured the NXT Women's Championship by defeating Stephanie Vaquer with assistance from Fallon Henley and Jazmyn Nyx. Reflecting on the victory during an interview with Denise Salcedo, Jayne described the moment as surreal, stating that she felt like she was having an out-of-body experience due to the long-awaited nature of the achievement. Although the title change drew criticism from fans upset over Vaquer losing her 77-day reign through outside interference, Jayne remained unbothered by the negative reactions. She embraced her role as a hated figure, noting that the crowd's hostility and the underestimation of her abilities only drive her to work harder.
