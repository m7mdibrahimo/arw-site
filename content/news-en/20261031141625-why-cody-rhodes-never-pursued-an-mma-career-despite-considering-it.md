Cody Rhodes once thought about trying mixed martial arts, but the idea was immediately shot down at home. During an appearance on the Double Coverage podcast, Cody revealed that he mentioned the possibility to Brandi Rhodes, only to receive a firm refusal. Long before the UFC became a massive powerhouse, Cody had trained in Brazilian jiu-jitsu during his high school wrestling offseason. Although he described his skills as simply okay, his amateur wrestling background gave him a foundation, yet he ultimately stopped at the right time. When people discuss potential crossover stars, Cody points to Chad Gable as a much more logical choice due to his extensive amateur background as an Olympic alternate. Still, Cody briefly wondered how far he could go if he committed to training, but Brandi gave him a hard no without hesitation, knowing full well what might happen in the cage.
