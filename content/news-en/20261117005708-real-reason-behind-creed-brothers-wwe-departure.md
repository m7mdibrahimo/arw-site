The departure of the Creed Brothers from WWE was not merely a result of expiring contracts and an immediate move to AEW. According to reports, WWE management was fully aware of the potential outcome and accepted their exit as a natural business decision. WWE reportedly relies on metrics and merchandise sales to determine the value of investing in specific tag teams, leading the company to allow the duo's contracts to expire without renewal.

This development followed earlier backstage indications that their tenure was concluding after the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic. Their loss to Hank Walker and Tank Ledger on October 20 served as their final match before a mutual parting of ways. AEW quickly capitalized on the situation, with the brothers debuting at AEW Full Gear under the names Jacob and Drew Casper. They attacked The Young Bucks and the Motor City Machine Guns before aligning with FTR.
