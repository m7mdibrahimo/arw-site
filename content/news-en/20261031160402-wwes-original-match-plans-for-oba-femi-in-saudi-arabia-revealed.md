Oba Femi was initially slated to compete in a six-man tag team match at WWE's event in Saudi Arabia before management altered the plans and removed him completely. Dave Meltzer reported on Wrestling Observer Radio that WWE's initial plans featured Femi teaming with Penta and Je'Von Evans to battle Bron Breakker, Bronson Reed, and Austin Theory. This matchup would have given Femi a major opportunity to perform alongside top-tier talent on an international stage. Ultimately, company leadership adjusted the bout into a tag team championship match, which resulted in Femi and Reed being excluded from the announced card. Meltzer noted that this removal does not necessarily mean the company has abandoned the concept entirely, suggesting the possibility that Femi and Reed could interfere in the title match to set up the six-man encounter later.

> "It wouldn't shock me at all to see those two involved in that match to lead to the six man."
