The Miz believes fans obsessed with high-rated wrestling matches might be missing the broader point of entertainment. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, he defended Danhausen working with GUNTHER, explaining that the contrast brings a unique perspective. He noted that WWE needs variety, pointing out that comedy acts and merchandise sales are just as important as technical masterclasses. The Miz argued that chasing the same critically acclaimed style in every match makes the product repetitive. Instead, he values unique characters and moments that connect with younger fans.
