Sesame Street has released a new video featuring 17-time WWE World Champion John Cena and Elmo. The official Sesame Street social media account shared the clip, which shows the two discussing the importance of helping others.

> Elmo and John Cena agree: the best sunsets come after a day of helping others. 🧡

Additionally, John Cena and WWE are scheduled to host the John Cena Classic tournament this December. Further details regarding the specific date and location for the event have yet to be announced.
