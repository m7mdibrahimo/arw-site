UFC Fight Night: Cortes-Acosta vs. Walker took place on November 7 from the Meta APEX in Las Vegas, NV, and aired on Paramount+. In the main event, Johnny Walker defeated Waldo Cortes-Acosta by TKO at 2:27 of Round 3.

Full results:

Welterweight: Jose Souza def. Jonny Parsons by knockout at 4:16 of Round 1

Welterweight: Seokhyeon Ko def. Wellington Turman by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 30-27)

Middleweight: Andre Petroski def. Mantas Kondratavicius by TKO at 4:39 of Round 2

Featherweight: Austin Bashi def. Carlos Calderon by submission at 1:07 of Round 1

Featherweight: Murtazali Magomedov def. Jose Delano by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28)

Women's Flyweight: Karine Silva def. Gabriella Fernandes by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28)

Heavyweight: Thomas Petersen def. Gabriel Lorenco by TKO at 4:47 of Round 3

Featherweight: Ollie Schmid def. Keiichiro Nakamura by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28)

Flyweight: Mitch Raposo def. Marcos Degli by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28)

Women's Bantamweight: Nina Milosevic def. Priscila Cachoeira by TKO at 3:40 of Round 2

Light Heavyweight: Lucas Fernando def. Billy Elekana by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28)

Bantamweight: Elijah Smith def. Davey Grant by knockout at 1:00 of Round 1

Women's Strawweight: Tatiana Suarez def. Virna Jandiroba by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 29-28)
