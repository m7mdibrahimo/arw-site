John Cena opened up about his marriage to Shay Shariatzadeh and their choice not to have children, noting that the subject remains open for discussion despite coming up early in their relationship. Appearing on TODAY’s Making Space with Hoda Kotb, Cena explained that their partnership relies on teamwork, meaning a shift in perspective for one person does not create a problem for the other.

Cena shared that his aversion to having children dates back to when he was 13 or 14 years old, shaped heavily by family struggles and the difficulties he witnessed his parents face. Even so, he emphasized that the door remains open as they continue to grow and change together.

He noted that he lives a fulfilling life full of joy and curiosity without kids, asking for the same respect given to those who choose parenthood. Right now, Cena and his wife are focusing their energy on strengthening their marriage and allowing room for personal evolution.
