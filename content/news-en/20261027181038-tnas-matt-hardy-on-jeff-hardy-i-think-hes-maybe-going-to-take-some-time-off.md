As TNA prepares for its annual Canadian tour in November, Jeff Hardy is set to take some time away from the ring. While it is standard practice for Hardy to be written out of television during international travel due to past legal issues, he is also currently managing legitimate health concerns. Matt Hardy discussed his brother's situation on an episode of The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy, noting that Jeff has been struggling with a bulging disc for a year.

"It's been bugging him and he's going to, you know I think he's done everything he can in his power and he's going to take some time off and see what he can do to get it fixed up and correct it as much as possible," Matt said. Jeff is currently looking to address the injury to ensure a full recovery. Before the tour, The Hardys are scheduled to appear at the Chris Jericho Rock 'N' Rager at Sea from November 7-11, which will feature the first-ever live episode of AEW Collision from the cruise.
