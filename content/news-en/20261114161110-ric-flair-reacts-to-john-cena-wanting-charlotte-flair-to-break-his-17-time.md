Ric Flair recently shared his reaction after hearing John Cena express his desire for Charlotte Flair to break his legendary 17-time world title record. Following the November 13 episode of WWE SmackDown, the WWE Hall of Famer took to social media to thank Cena for showing immense respect toward his daughter. During the show, Cena brought Charlotte to the ring and announced her for the upcoming John Cena Classic, leading to an emotional exchange where Charlotte credited a simple piece of advice from Cena for helping her regain her confidence.

Cena praised Charlotte's incredible accomplishments and noted that despite carrying the famous Flair legacy, she made a difficult path look effortless throughout her career. The defining moment arrived when Cena revealed that while chasing his own record-breaking 17th world championship, he always envisioned shaking the hand of the person who would eventually reach 18, with Charlotte being that exact person. The discussion now raises the exciting prospect of whether WWE plans to turn this vision into a long-term storyline, putting a historic number on Charlotte's future in the ring.
