Yota Tsuji has responded to recent comments made by Callum Newman regarding the "new era of strong style." Newman, a member of the United Empire, had previously criticized both Tsuji and Yuta-Ice, questioning this modern interpretation of the wrestling style.

Speaking to Fightful's In The Weeds, Tsuji addressed the criticism, noting that every wrestler on the NJPW roster possesses a unique style. Tsuji stated he appreciates Newman's perspective, even though he disagrees with the idea that the roster is moving away from traditional wrestling. Tsuji emphasized that while styles may differ, the ultimate objective for everyone remains the same.

> "So I honestly appreciate that, you know, the guys like Callum Newman, who does all kinds of athletic moves, kind of questions, you know, the recent kind of movement in the New Japan roster that we are kind of, going towards more of the traditional moves, rather than these acrobatic, athletic moves. I think he is just like everybody else in the New Japan roster, they have they each have a unique fighting style to express themselves and but all the goals we have is to have a win. So I can't question myself as well as what strong style is, and I don't think anyone can have the definite answer of what it is, including myself. But for sure, the idea is that you have to win in the end, and the pro wrestling that we do is as simple as that. that the winner takes all. So whatever it takes to have secured win, I think that should be what strong style is. So I accept the Newman's comment and I understand why he's saying that, but you know, that's what it is, and I think it's good to have everybody's own unique style, just all going to try and secure the win is what the New Japan Pro Wrestling is."

Tsuji also discussed his upcoming appearance with MLW during the interview.
