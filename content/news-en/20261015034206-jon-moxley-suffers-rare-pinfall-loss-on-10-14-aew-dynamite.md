Jake Doyle scored a major upset victory over Jon Moxley on the October 14 episode of AEW Dynamite. The show opened with a six-man tag team match featuring the Death Riders, consisting of Jon Moxley, Gabe Kidd, and Daniel Garcia, facing off against the Don Callis Family, represented by Mark Davis, Jake Doyle, and Wardlow.

In a surprising finish, Jake Doyle pinned Jon Moxley clean in the middle of the ring. This victory comes shortly before Doyle and Moxley are scheduled to face each other for the AEW Continental Championship at AEW WrestleDream. The loss marks a rare pinfall defeat for Moxley on AEW television, as he had not been pinned on the promotion's programming since his match against Will Ospreay in April.
