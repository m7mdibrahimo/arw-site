Former WWE star Snitsky recently discussed his thoughts on a potential WWE Hall of Fame induction during an appearance on the It's Not Our Fault podcast. While he expressed gratitude that fans still remember memorable moments from his career, such as punting babies and his clothesline to Paul London, he doubts he will ever enter the Hall of Fame. Snitsky claimed that "certain people" currently within WWE could prevent it from happening, though he did not name anyone specifically.
