WWE Crown Jewel is being relocated to a new venue, according to an announcement from Riyadh Season. No additional details about the new location have been disclosed yet. Riyadh Season released a statement regarding the change, noting that ticket holders will receive full refunds and tickets will be put back on sale within the next 48 hours. WWE has not issued an official statement on the venue change at this time. Crown Jewel is currently scheduled to take place on Saturday, November 7. The event is set to feature championship matches, with Roman Reigns facing Sami Zayn for the men's title and Liv Morgan defending against Chelsea Green for the women's title. Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport has experienced disruptions and limited flights throughout most of October following attacks by the Houthis in Yemen on October 10.
