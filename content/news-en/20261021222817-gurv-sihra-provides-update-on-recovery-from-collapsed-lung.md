Gurv Sihra, formerly known as Sunil Singh, shared a health update regarding his recovery from a collapsed lung. He revealed that his lungs are nearly back to normal, though he is still not cleared to fly.

> "The road to recovery continues 💪🏾 X-Rays show my lungs are back to normal & there is very little air bubbles left in my chest-cavity from the collapsed lung I still can’t fly at the moment, however, I should be cleared soon 🙏🏽👍🏾 Thank you all for your continued love & support."
