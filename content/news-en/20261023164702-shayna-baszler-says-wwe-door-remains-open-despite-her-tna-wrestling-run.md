Shayna Baszler remains on good terms with WWE and could continue working behind the scenes despite her recent run in TNA Wrestling. During an interview with Denice Salcedo, the former NXT Women's Champion discussed her part-time coaching work at the WWE Performance Center, NXT, and EVOLVE following her departure in 2025. Baszler admitted that helping young wrestlers develop has become something she truly loves, stating that she has fallen in view of watching young athletes grow.

At the same time, Baszler recently teamed up with Zoey Serrano in TNA and captured the TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Championships at Bound for Glory. Despite her increased responsibilities with another promotion, she believes the door to coaching in WWE is still open. Baszler noted that she checked with people who mattered before making the move and received no objections, emphasizing that she has no intention of burning bridges with WWE.
