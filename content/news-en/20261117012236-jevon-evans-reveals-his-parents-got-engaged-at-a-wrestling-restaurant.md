Je’Von Evans recently shared a unique detail regarding his family's deep-rooted connection to professional wrestling. During a new interview on Great Day Houston, the wrestler known as the "Young OG" highlighted how his family's passion for the sport has spanned generations.

Evans noted that his parents became engaged at a wrestling-themed restaurant. He stated:

> "Yeah, for sure. Everybody watched wrestling. My grandma, my mom, my pops. I think my dad proposed to my mom in a wrestling restaurant if I’m not mistaken."

Historically, such venues included the WCW Nitro Grill in Las Vegas and WWF New York.
