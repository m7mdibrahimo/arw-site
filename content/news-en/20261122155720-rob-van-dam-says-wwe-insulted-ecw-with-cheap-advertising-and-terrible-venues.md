Rob Van Dam spoke openly about WWE's revival of ECW, stating that the company did not provide sufficient financial support for the venture. He explained that management handled the project with a very small advertising budget compared to its other shows.

Van Dam said on his podcast that he felt personally insulted because the company relied on the old promotion's reputation and loyal audience without making a serious investment in promotion. He added that choosing old, run-down venues proved that management did not understand the true secret behind ECW's unique success in the past.
