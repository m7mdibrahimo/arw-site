Nikki Bella revealed she is texting a mystery man she first noticed at a UFC event, and she hopes they can finally get together in December. Speaking during the November 20 episode of The Nikki & Brie Show, Nikki stated that she wants to go on a date with him, but their schedules are busy.

> "There's one person I really wanna go on a date with, but our schedules don't add up."

Nikki explained that she tracked down his phone number after watching him at the event, noting that he works behind the scenes rather than in front of the camera. A planned meeting for last weekend was called off due to travel issues, leaving her aiming for December. Brie Bella supported her sister during the discussion, expressing optimism that the date will happen.
