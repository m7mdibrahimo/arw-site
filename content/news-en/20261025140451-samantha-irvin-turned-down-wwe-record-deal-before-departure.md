Samantha Irvin is addressing details of her WWE exit, stating that she turned down a recording contract offer from the company because her sole desire was to manage wrestlers. She explained via X that WWE offered to produce musical works for her, but she politely declined the offer due to a lack of interest in that field.

Irvin noted that the songs she released last year came from a spontaneous collaboration with her former Cirque du Soleil colleagues and were meant only for her old fans, denying any professional singing aspirations. Despite previous reports from Bryan Alvarez and Dave Meltzer at the time of her October 2024 departure indicating she wanted to focus on music, she emphasized that her primary goal was and remains working as a wrestling manager.
