Josh Bishop was in attendance at TNA Wrestling's 2026 Lockdown pay-per-view to support his friend, Joe Alonzo. During the event, Alonzo faced The Home Town Man in a dark match and emerged victorious, which earned him a contract with TNA. Bishop, who currently holds the MLW World Heavyweight Championship, was seen in the front row and later explained to Sean Ross Sapp that his presence was purely to back his friend. Bishop expressed his enjoyment of the event, noting that he had a great experience and enjoyed seeing Carlos Silva and the crew. He also shared his admiration for Jeff Hardy, stating that he is his all-time favorite wrestler, which made the prospect of seeing The Hardys compete in a Steel Cage match particularly exciting.
