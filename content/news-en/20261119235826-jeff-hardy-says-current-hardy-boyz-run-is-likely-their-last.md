Jeff Hardy discussed the future of The Hardy Boyz, admitting that his current tag team run alongside his brother Matt Hardy will likely be their final extended chapter together. Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Hardy explained that he would define this stage as their most extreme run because it is likely their last, adding that he plays things by ear and plans to continue as long as he can.

Despite viewing this as the duo's final run, Hardy clarified that he has no intention of quitting the ring anytime soon. He noted that he still intends to perform stunts for as long as possible, revealing a long-held goal to wrestle until at least age 50 and expressing a desire to stretch his career to between 55 and 60.
