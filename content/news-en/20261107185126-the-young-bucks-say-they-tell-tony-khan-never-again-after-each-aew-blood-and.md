The Young Bucks discussed their ongoing involvement in AEW Blood And Guts matches, revealing that they routinely tell Tony Khan 'never again' after each appearance. Having participated in three such matches, including the 2026 edition, Matt Jackson and Nick Jackson reflected on the brutal nature of the stipulation while speaking on an episode of Being The Elite. Matt Jackson noted that they are often the ones asking to be included in these chaotic encounters, despite immediately regretting the toll they take. Nick Jackson emphasized the intense stress involved, while Matt Jackson described the matches as scary and gory. Elsewhere on Being The Elite, Brandon Cutler mocked the duo for their recent squash match loss against Davis and Doyle.
