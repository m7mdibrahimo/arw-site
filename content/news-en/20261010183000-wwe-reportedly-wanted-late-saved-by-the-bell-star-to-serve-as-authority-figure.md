Dennis Haskins, the actor best known for his role as Mr. Richard Belding on "Saved By the Bell," passed away on September 27 at the age of 75. Haskins was a known fan of professional wrestling and made appearances on various online series, including "Zack Ryder's Z! True Long Island Story" and "The JBL & Cole Show."

According to Dave Meltzer on Wrestling Observer Radio, WWE previously explored the possibility of bringing Haskins into the company in a significant role. Meltzer noted that the company had discussed having him serve as a General Manager, though the actor ultimately declined the offer due to the travel requirements involved.

> He was Pro Wrestling Syndicate commissioner and WWE actually at one point wanted him to be General Manager, but he didn't want the travel. But yeah, he was a lifelong wrestling fan and I think he was on Raw.

Beyond his television work, Haskins maintained a close friendship with wrestler Austin Aries. Meltzer shared that the two were frequently seen together, often going out to dinner or participating in karaoke nights.
