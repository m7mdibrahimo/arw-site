During a recent interview with ESPN New York, Seth Rollins discussed his 2026 SummerSlam match against Roman Reigns, calling it a pivotal moment in his career. Rollins noted that the encounter helped define the last decade of WWE, especially given the absence of Jon Moxley. He also reflected on the heavy pressure to meet fan expectations and deliver a memorable story alongside his former Shield stablemate. Reigns ultimately defeated Rollins to retain the World Heavyweight Championship, and Rollins has not appeared on WWE television since the event.
