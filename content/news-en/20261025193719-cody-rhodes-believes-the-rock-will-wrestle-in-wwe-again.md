Cody Rhodes recently discussed the in-ring future of Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson, expressing his belief that the WWE legend still has more matches left in his career despite no singles bout occurring between them yet. During an appearance on a podcast, Rhodes clarified that he is not avoiding the topic, but rather feels that The Rock still has a few performances remaining.

> "It's not that I'm evading that answer. It's that I don't really know how to answer because I think personally he has a couple of matches probably left to do."

Rhodes noted that The Rock remains dedicated as a board member, though the final decision rests with him. The Rock last competed at WrestleMania 40, where he teamed with Roman Reigns to defeat Cody Rhodes and Seth Rollins.
