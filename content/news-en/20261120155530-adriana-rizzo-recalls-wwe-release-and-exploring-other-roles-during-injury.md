Following a four-year tenure with WWE, Adriana Rizzo was released by the company on September 4. Rizzo spent her time in the promotion as part of the D'Angelo Family, but she also dealt with significant injuries, including a torn Achilles and a torn ACL. She missed nearly a full year before returning to the ring in July 2026.

During an appearance on Talk is Jericho with Chris Jericho, Rizzo recalled receiving her release call while driving to Savannah, Georgia. She noted that the conversation was brief and described the call as "Cold."

She explained that during her time on the sideline, she expressed interest in taking on different roles, such as conducting interviews and hosting Rizzo Rants following shows. Rizzo served as an interviewer for WWE EVOLVE and worked as an authority figure at NXT live events while recovering. She stated that she felt ready to contribute in the ring and with her character just as she was let go.
