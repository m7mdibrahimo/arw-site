The November 4 episode of WWE NXT features major fallout from Halloween Havoc, headlined by Grayson Waller defending the NXT Championship against CM Punk. Kelani Jordan defends the NXT Women's Championship against a mystery opponent, while Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno face Hank and Tank in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic final presented by Cody Rhodes. Additionally, Cruz Montana and Dion Lennox meet in an Unsanctioned Match with Michael Chandler as the special guest referee, and Chad Gable, Rey Fenix, and Dragon Lee take on The Vanity Project. John Cena will also make a special appearance. The show airs live at 8 PM ET on the CW Network from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, with streaming available on Netflix.
