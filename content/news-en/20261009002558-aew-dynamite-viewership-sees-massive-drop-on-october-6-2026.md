AEW Dynamite on TBS experienced a significant decline in audience numbers for the October 6, 2026, episode. According to data from Wrestlenomics, the show recorded 495,000 viewers and a 0.11 rating in the key P18–49 demographic.

This performance marks a sharp decrease from the September 30, 2026, episode, which drew 899,000 viewers and a 0.22 rating in the same demographic. The drop represents a loss of 404,000 viewers and a 0.11 decline in the key rating. Despite the week-to-week decline, the figures show a year-over-year improvement compared to the October 7, 2025, episode, which drew 321,000 viewers and a 0.07 demo rating.

These statistics account only for linear television viewership on TBS and exclude digital or streaming numbers. The company continues to monitor these trends as it prepares for upcoming live events.
