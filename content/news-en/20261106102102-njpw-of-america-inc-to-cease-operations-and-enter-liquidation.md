New Japan Pro-Wrestling has announced that its North American subsidiary, NJPW of America Inc., will cease operations and enter liquidation. The decision comes as the promotion looks to optimize its management resources in a rapidly changing business environment. The subsidiary was originally founded in 2019. Despite the closure, New Japan confirmed that it will continue to hold events in North America, which will now be fully supported by the TV Asahi group as part of its ongoing global expansion efforts. A statement from the promotion reads: "New Japan Pro-Wrestling hereby announces that subsidiary New Japan Pro-Wrestling of America Inc will cease operations and enter liquidation. As NJPW continues to optimize allocation of management resources in a rapidly changing business environment, the decision was made to close the subsidiary, which was founded in 2019. Moving forward, NJPW events in North America will continue to be held with the full backing and resources of the TV Asahi group, as part of a new chapter in NJPW's ongoing global expansion." The final event held under the NJPW of America banner was Resurgence, which took place in May 2025 at the Toyota Arena in Ontario, California.
