After signing with WWE through the NIL program in 2023, Kendal Grey rose to the top of the NXT women's division, holding the NXT Women's Championship for 62 days before dropping it to Kelani Jordan in August. A reported injury likely contributed to her loss. Grey has been in a tag team with Wren Sinclair since last year, with Sinclair supporting her partner throughout and even challenging Jordan for the title on Grey's behalf.

In a recent interview on "No-Contest Wrestling," Sinclair discussed their friendship. "When I first got here, Kendal was here in the Performance Center before me, and she kind of welcomed me in," she stated. Sinclair contrasted this with her independent wrestling experience, where she found the locker rooms less welcoming. "She's a real genuine person. So to finally get to be able to show that onscreen? I feel like people can tell that we are friends and that the way that we support each other is genuine; it's not something that was forced together."

Sinclair noted that the two spend time together outside of wrestling, a new experience for her, as wrestlers often only get along for storylines. She added, "She is probably the best women's wrestler in the industry right now, in the ring. It is actually insane how fast she picked this up." Following an absence from television, Grey has returned to NXT, reuniting with Sinclair onscreen.
