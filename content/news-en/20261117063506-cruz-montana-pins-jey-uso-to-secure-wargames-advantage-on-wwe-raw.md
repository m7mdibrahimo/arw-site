According to Fightful, Cruz Montana secured his first victory on WWE Raw in the main event tag team match pitting The Usos against The 946 members Carmelo Hayes and Cruz Montana. The victorious team earned the crucial advantage for the upcoming WarGames match at WWE Survivor Series.

During the finishing sequence, Cruz Montana attempted his signature lariat on Jey Uso, but Jey reversed it into a spear. Jey then climbed to the top rope to deliver his signature splash, but Cruz raised his knees at the last second. Recovering to his feet, Cruz successfully landed the lariat on Jey Uso to score the win for his team.
