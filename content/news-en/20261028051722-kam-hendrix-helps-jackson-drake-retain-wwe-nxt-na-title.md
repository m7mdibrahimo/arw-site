Jackson Drake successfully defended the NXT North American Championship against EK Prosper on WWE NXT, thanks to some outside interference. Drake entered the match with his Vanity Project stablemates Brad Baylor, Ricky Smokes, and Myka Lockwood. During the bout, Lockwood distracted the referee, allowing Kam Hendrix to push Prosper off the top rope. This opened the door for Drake to hit a Shining Wizard and secure the pinfall victory. Earlier in the broadcast, Baylor and Smokes dropped their NXT Tag Team Championship opportunities in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic semi-finals against Romeo Moreno and Noam Dar.
