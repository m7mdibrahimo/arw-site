Ilja Dragunov is drawing attention to Bandido as he prepares for his in-ring debut in All Elite Wrestling. Ahead of facing 'Speedball' Mike Bailey on AEW Dynamite, Dragunov appeared on TMZ's Inside The Ring Podcast and praised the ROH World Champion and AEW World Trios Champion. Dragunov expressed his amazement at Bandido's abilities, stating that what he can do feels like something straight out of a video game. Bandido recently teamed with Hangman Adam Page and Brody King at AEW WrestleDream to defeat Swerve Strickland and The New Level, capturing the World Trios Titles on the same night Dragunov made his debut.
