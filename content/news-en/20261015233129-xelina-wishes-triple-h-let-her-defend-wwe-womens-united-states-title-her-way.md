Xelina, the second holder of the WWE Women's United States Championship, recently reflected on her 63-day reign with the title. During an interview on Ring The Belle, she expressed that she wished WWE Chief Content Officer Paul 'Triple H' Levesque had allowed her more creative freedom in how she defended the belt.

> "I wish that he (Triple H) would have loved it on me enough to let me defend it the way I wanted to defend it. But it’s fine, because I wanted to go and bring it to AAA and f*cking STARDOM. Let me bring it to different places and really make this the workhorse title if that’s what we were gonna do."

Before dropping the championship to Giulia, Xelina made one successful title defense. The Women's United States Championship served as her first singles title in WWE, building upon her previous experience as a Women's Tag Team Champion.
