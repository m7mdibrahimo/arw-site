John Cena recently addressed fan concerns that WWE is wasting Gunther following their final match at Saturday Night’s Main Event, where Gunther forced Cena to tap out and end his legendary in-ring career. During an appearance on Busted Open Radio, Cena explained that he approaches creative decisions as challenges rather than questioning management. He chose to submit immediately to give his character a definitive ending and hand Gunther a massive push. Regarding criticism that WWE has not properly capitalized on that momentum, Cena noted that while fans might feel that way, there could be a larger creative vision behind the scenes that is not yet visible. He emphasized that his job was to make Gunther look as strong as possible, while the future direction remains in the hands of WWE creative.
