The WWE Universal Championship match between Roman Reigns and Sami Zayn at WWE Elimination Chamber 2023 remains one of the most impactful defenses during Reigns' 1,316-day reign. Zayn competed in his hometown of Montreal shortly after turning on The Bloodline at the Royal Rumble.

During an appearance on "Insight with Chris Van Vliet," Zayn described the atmosphere as exceptional, noting that the crowd was left despondent following Reigns' victory. When asked if he believed he should have won the match, Zayn stated:

> "At the time I thought there was a case to be made for it. But, going back to the domino effect thing, I don't know... I really don't know. I don't think it's ludicrous to say that I should have. I think if you were watching at that time, anybody could have made that argument. But, again, timing and Cody was also red-hot at the time and they were building to something... Could, should, would. Would it have worked? I think so. But, who's to say."

Zayn compared the conclusion of the bout to the 1997 Survivor Series match between Bret Hart and Shawn Michaels, known as the Montreal Screwjob. He reflected on the power of professional wrestling, saying:

> "What a gift to be able to do that. Because that's what we really do, we play with people's emotions."
