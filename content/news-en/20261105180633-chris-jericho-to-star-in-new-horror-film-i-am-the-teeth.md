Chris Jericho is continuing his acting career in Hollywood. Deadline reports that Jericho has been cast in a horror-action film titled 'I Am The Teeth' for Epic Pictures Group. The project is set to be pitched to international buyers at the upcoming American Film Market. In the film, Jericho will portray a mysterious and ruthless bounty hunter called "The Hunter." The cast also features Hope Jordan and Thomas Parobek, with Scott B. Hansen serving as director. A proof-of-concept presentation has already been filmed for the American Film Market, while the main production is scheduled for the first quarter of 2027. Jericho recently appeared as Gorgeous George in the Amazon Prime series 'The Greatest'.
