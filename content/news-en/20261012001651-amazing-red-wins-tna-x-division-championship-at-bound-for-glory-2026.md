Amazing Red made a stunning return to championship glory at TNA Bound for Glory 2026 by winning the vacant TNA X-Division Championship over 16 years after his last reign. During his TNA Hall of Fame speech earlier in the evening, Red surprised fans by entering the seven-man scramble match for the vacant title, which was available after Leon Slater invoked Option C to challenge for the TNA World Championship. Red capitalized on the opportunity by hitting a Code Red on KC Navarro to secure the pinfall and capture the title for the fourth time. This historic victory comes exactly 16 years and a few months after his previous reign ended on September 25, 2010. The momentous night capped off an unforgettable evening for the legend, who transitioned straight from his Hall of Fame induction to championship gold.
