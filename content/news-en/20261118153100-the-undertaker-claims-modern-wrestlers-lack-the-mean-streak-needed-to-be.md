The Undertaker believes modern wrestlers have become too focused on entertaining fans while losing the essential believability of a real fight. Speaking on the Six Feet Under podcast with Trick Williams, The Deadman discussed the difference between learning wrestling mechanics and possessing the necessary aggression. He noted that while anyone can learn holds and moves, true stardom requires a certain mean streak. Williams recalled advice he received from Undertaker about balancing charisma with the ability to turn on a fierce, aggressive switch when it is time to fight. Undertaker emphasized that relying solely on being an entertainer can create a perception problem among fans. He believes that combining personality with a relentless fighting spirit is what ultimately elevates a wrestler to the next level.
