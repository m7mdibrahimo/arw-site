Mercedes Moné will defend her AEW Women's World Championship against Marina Shafir at the upcoming AEW Full Gear pay-per-view. The match was officially announced following a recent segment on AEW Fright Night Dynamite, where Shafir challenged Moné, leading to a physical altercation between the two. During the same broadcast, Jon Moxley was attacked by Ace Austin, David Finlay, and Clark Conners while he was providing commentary for Shafir’s match. The updated card for AEW Full Gear 2026, scheduled for November 14, includes Will Ospreay defending the AEW World Championship against Kyle Fletcher, The Young Bucks defending the AEW World Tag Team Championship against Mark Davis and Jake Doyle, and Adam Page and Brodido defending the AEW World Trios Championship against Swerve Strickland and New Level.
