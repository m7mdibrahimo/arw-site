During a recent interview on the "notsam" podcast to promote his new film "Street Fighter," Roman Reigns opened up about his frustrations with his WWE run during the 2010s. The former Undisputed WWE Universal Champion admitted that he was unhappy with his creative direction and how he was portrayed before the Bloodline storyline began, particularly highlighting the polarizing period between 2015 and 2019 when fans often gave him hostile reactions. Reigns pointed to a specific low point late in 2019 and early 2020 during his feud with Baron Corbin, which featured an angle where he was covered in dog food alongside Dolph Ziggler. He noted that things finally clicked for him right before the COVID-19 pandemic, leading him to take complete control of his career and evolve into the Tribal Chief persona.
