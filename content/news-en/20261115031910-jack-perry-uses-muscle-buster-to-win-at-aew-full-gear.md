Jack Perry continues to target Samoa Joe, successfully defeating The Beast Mortos in the opening match of the AEW Full Gear Tailgate Brawl. Perry appeared close to victory after hitting a piledriver and a running knee, but he decided to escalate the situation by executing a muscle buster to secure the win. Before performing the maneuver that Samoa Joe famously used to win championships, Perry looked into the camera and said:

> "Samoa Joe, this is for you, you son of a bitch."

Following the match, Perry continued to challenge Joe, demanding that he "kill him." Perry has spent months seeking Joe's respect and a handshake.
