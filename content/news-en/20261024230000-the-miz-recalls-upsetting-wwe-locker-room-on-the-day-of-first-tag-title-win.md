The Miz recently reflected on the day he won his first WWE tag team championship alongside John Morrison, revealing that their actions backstage and during the match upset many of the veterans in the locker room. Speaking on WWE Vault, The Miz explained that they appeared on commentary for ECW before SmackDown, where they openly criticized the match, and later celebrated their tag team title victory with excessive enthusiasm, which did not sit well with the roster.

Despite the initial backstage heat, the management was pleased with their work and even asked them to return to commentary the following week. The duo eventually launched 'The Dirt Sheet' web show, which became so popular that it transitioned to live television, though it continued to ruffle feathers among their peers.
