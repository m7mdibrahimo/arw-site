Paul Heyman believes Brock Lesnar's UFC run would have remained unblemished if not for health issues. During an interview with Graham Bensinger, Heyman stated that Lesnar, who won the UFC Heavyweight Championship in his fourth fight, would have stayed undefeated had he not battled diverticulitis. "If he didn't have diverticulitis, he would've never been defeated in UFC. Never. He'd still be champion today at 49," Heyman said. Heyman also discussed Lesnar's transition from WWE to the Minnesota Vikings training camp, noting that the team was hesitant to release his combine numbers due to his professional wrestling background. Heyman explained that Lesnar was performing at an Olympic qualifying level despite suffering from a fractured pelvis, a torn groin, and a broken jaw at the time. Lesnar, who retired from in-ring competition following his final bout against Oba Femi at WWE SummerSlam in August, is the subject of recent podcast interest, with Conrad Thompson expressing a desire to host Heyman.
