Mini Vikingo will undergo surgery for an elbow injury. During the first week of Lucha Libre AAA's Héroes Inmortales, it was revealed that Vikingo was injured during his recent appearance on NXT. Although he was originally scheduled to face Rey Fenix for the AAA World Cruiserweight Championship, he was not cleared to compete. Vikingo confirmed that doctors informed him that surgery is necessary for his elbow. The 18-year-old last wrestled on the October 13 edition of NXT on The CW, where he and Chazz 'Starboy' Hall were defeated by Vanity Project in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic.
