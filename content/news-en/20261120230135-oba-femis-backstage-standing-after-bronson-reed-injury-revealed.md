Oba Femi left Bronson Reed with broken ribs and a damaged kidney following a brutal spot at WWE Crown Jewel, raising questions about whether he was in hot water backstage. However, Sean Ross Sapp reported on a Fightful Q&A podcast that WWE is not holding Femi responsible for the incident, which occurred when Femi launched Reed off the stage and through a table during the World Tag Team Championship match. Sapp stated that he had not heard of any heat against Femi, describing the situation as an unfortunate accident.

Reed underwent surgery and returned home on November 17, with no clear timeline for his in-ring return. Femi previously drew backstage chatter following an unscripted moment on the September 7 episode of RAW, but there is no indication that the Crown Jewel incident has negatively impacted his standing in WWE.
