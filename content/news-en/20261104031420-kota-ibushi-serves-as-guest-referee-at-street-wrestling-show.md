Kota Ibushi continues to stay involved in professional wrestling despite not competing in the ring. After undergoing a second surgery to address complications from a fractured femur, Ibushi noted that an in-ring return currently seems impossible, though he remains determined to return eventually. While he is not actively wrestling, Ibushi recently made an appearance as a guest referee at a street pro-wrestling event. His last official match took place in October 2025. Since that time, he has made various appearances for DDT Pro-Wrestling and is scheduled to appear at a P.P.P. Tokyo show on November 26th.
