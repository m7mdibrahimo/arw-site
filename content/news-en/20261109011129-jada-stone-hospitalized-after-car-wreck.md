Jada Stone has been hospitalized following a car wreck. Stone provided an update on her Instagram stories, noting that she is doing okay and feeling blessed to be alive after the accident. She shared photos of her hospital stay and her vehicle, which sustained significant damage to both the front and back. Stone has notified promoters regarding the cancellation of her upcoming bookings. Stone, who performs for TNA Wrestling, last competed on the October 29 episode of TNA iMPACT!, where she challenged M By Elegance for the TNA Television Championship. She also works for various independent promotions such as Limitless and PWF. We wish Jada the best in her recovery.
