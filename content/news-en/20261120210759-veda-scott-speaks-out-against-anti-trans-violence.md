Veda Scott addressed the violence facing transgender individuals with a candid message posted to X on Friday, November 20, 2026, in observance of Trans Day of Remembrance. Scott highlighted the lives lost to hatred and bigotry, emphasizing that mere remembrance is insufficient while the violence persists. She utilized the platform to honor victims while demanding safety, respect, and dignity for all.

> "Today is Trans Day of Remembrance We honor and celebrate our friends, family, and neighbors stolen by acts of bigotry and violence. We cherish their memories and carry forward the joy they gave the world. And we continue the work to ensure safety, respect, and dignity to all. You are so loved 💕"

Trans Day of Remembrance is observed annually on November 20 to honor those killed by anti-transgender violence and to draw attention to ongoing discrimination. Scott concluded her message by offering support to those currently facing hostility, reminding them that they are loved.
