Former WWE star Drake Maverick recently reflected on his time in the 24/7 Championship division, describing the chaotic backstage era as a highlight of his career. Maverick, who served as the '205 Live' General Manager at the time, was not originally slated to compete for the title but found himself consistently involved in the segments while traveling between WWE RAW and WWE SmackDown.

> "When I look back at it, didn't realize in the moment [they were] the good ol' days," he said. "When I look back on it, it's just the best. Wasn't even supposed to be involved in it. I got brought to 'Raw' every week because I was the '205 Live' general manager at the time... I was always brought to TV on a Monday just in case they needed me for anything, whether it be digital or stuff."

Maverick credited Adam Pearce for advocating for his inclusion in the division, as he was often overlooked for other storylines. He highlighted one specific segment involving Titus O'Neil, Bobby Lashley, and Baron Corbin as a turning point where he decided to seize the opportunity.

> "I remember we busted a Bobby Lashley and a Baron Corbin backstage, everyone runs through," he said. "This was, not going into business for myself, I don't want to call it, but seizing the moment. But, believe me, I went into a lot of business for myself with this. I stopped, looked at Bobby, looked at Corbin, went, 'Sorry.' And just kept on running."
