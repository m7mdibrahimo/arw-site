The WWE Crown Jewel Championship is crafted with real gold and diamonds, but the winner does not get to keep the actual belt. During an appearance on the Good Karma Wrestling podcast, Chelsea Green revealed that the championship remains in Saudi Arabia, likely with members of the royal family. Instead of the belt, winners receive a custom-designed ring made with real gold and diamonds, which they can take home. Green compared the tradition to winning the Stanley Cup or Super Bowl.

Green explained that normal WWE championships work differently, as incoming champions take possession of the titles while past champions must rely on replicas. Unfortunately for Green, she has not even been able to keep her Crown Jewel ring. Following her victory over Liv Morgan to win the Women's Crown Jewel Championship on November 7, Tiffany Stratton cashed in her Money in the Bank contract and allegedly took the ring right off Green's finger during the betrayal.
