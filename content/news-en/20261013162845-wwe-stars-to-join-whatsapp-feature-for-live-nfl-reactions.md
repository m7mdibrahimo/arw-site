Wrestling fans can connect with WWE talent through a live chat feature from WhatsApp and OffBall during NFL games. According to a news release obtained by OutKick Dropkick, this collaboration begins with the Green Bay Packers versus Dallas Cowboys game on October 18.

Cody Rhodes and United States Champion Trick Williams will represent the Cowboys, while WWE Women's World Champion Liv Morgan will support the Packers. The Miz will also provide additional commentary for the matchup.

For the November 1 game between the Philadelphia Eagles and the Washington Commanders, Nikki Bella and Brie Bella will support the Eagles. Meanwhile, L.A. Knight will back the Commanders.
