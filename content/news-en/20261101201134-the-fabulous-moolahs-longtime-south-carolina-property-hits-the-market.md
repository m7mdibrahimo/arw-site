A piece of professional wrestling history is now available for purchase in South Carolina. The longtime Columbia property once owned by WWE Hall of Famer The Fabulous Moolah has been listed for sale at an asking price of $224,900. The 9.11-acre property at 101 Moolah Drive is positioned as a waterfront development opportunity, situated on a private peninsula surrounded by two private ponds, mature hardwoods, and pine trees.

The official listing directly highlights the land's deep roots in wrestling history, noting that it served as Moolah's residence for many years. While the original residential structures—which previously included two three-bedroom homes and six efficiency residences—have been removed, the land offers a clear opportunity for redevelopment. The property retains three storage buildings sold in as-is condition and features R-5 zoning with access to public water and sewer services.

Represented by Alecia Porter and brokered by NorthGroup Real Estate LLC, the property is located approximately five minutes from Fort Jackson with direct access to major interstates. Despite the removal of the original buildings, the address and land maintain a permanent connection to one of the most prominent and discussed figures in women's wrestling history.
