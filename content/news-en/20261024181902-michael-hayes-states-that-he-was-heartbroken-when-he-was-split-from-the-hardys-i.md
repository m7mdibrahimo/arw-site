Michael Hayes revealed that he was heartbroken when he was separated from The Hardys in WWE. During an appearance on the What's Your Story? podcast with Stephanie McMahon, Hayes recalled working as a producer and manager for Matt Hardy and Jeff Hardy in 1999 when they won their first WWF Tag Team Championship. Hayes explained that Vince McMahon changed his mind about his aggressive character direction after he was removed from the group, which left him devastated because he was deeply invested in their success. Both Matt and Jeff have publicly expressed their gratitude to Hayes over the years.
