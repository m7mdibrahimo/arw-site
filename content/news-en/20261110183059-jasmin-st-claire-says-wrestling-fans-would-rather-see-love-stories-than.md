Jasmin St. Claire believes that deathmatch wrestling has gone too far and that fans would rather watch a compelling love story than a wrestler getting smashed through multiple light tubes. Speaking on the November 9, 2026, episode of her Off the Rails podcast, the former ECW and XPW personality discussed the heavy reliance on dangerous weapons in independent wrestling today. She questioned how promoters can build excitement effectively when opening matches feature extreme violence right from the start.

St. Claire argued that storytelling and emotional drama hold far more value than performers continually trying to top each other with increasingly hazardous stunts. She emphasized that fans ultimately want something that feels real and engaging rather than excessive bloodshed. Recalling her time working with XPW and managing deathmatch veterans, she noted that the constant use of light tubes and barbed wire eventually makes the violence repetitive instead of meaningful.
