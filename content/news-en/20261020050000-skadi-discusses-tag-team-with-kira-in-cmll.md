Skadi recently reflected on her successful partnership with Kira in CMLL. The duo won the CMLL Mexican National Women's Tag Team Championship in March 2024 by defeating Las Chicas Indomables, holding the titles for 931 days before losing them to Las Valientitas at CMLL Noche De Campeones 2026. Skadi told Fightful that the pairing was completely organic and formed spontaneously after a single match. She emphasized that their chemistry was immediate and that they have since broken new ground in women's wrestling. Skadi noted that it is important for younger generations to see women main-eventing and succeeding in the sport. Despite losing the titles, the team remains together and is now setting their sights on world championships and international exposure. Skadi also commented on her career path and following in her mother's footsteps.
