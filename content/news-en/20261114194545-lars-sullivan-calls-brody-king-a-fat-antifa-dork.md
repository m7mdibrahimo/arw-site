Former WWE star Lars Sullivan weighed in on the ongoing controversy surrounding Brody King, labeling him a "Fat Antifa dork" amid continued fan reaction to his vocal opposition to U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement.

Sullivan was replying to a fan who attacked King on social media over his politics and his wrestling. King's opposition to ICE has spilled into AEW events, where crowds repeatedly broke into anti-ICE chants during AEW Grand Slam Australia on February 14 and episodes of Dynamite, even interrupting a backstage promo featuring him.

During the AEW Revolution media call, Tony Khan defended King, praising his genuine personality and noting that he built a strong reputation inside and outside the ring long before mainstream media attention arrived. Khan also supported the right of fans to express their opinions as part of the live event experience.

Sullivan's brief remark left the door open to see whether King will respond to him.
