WWE superstar Rusev and his wife, former WWE star CJ Perry, recently revealed they are expecting their first child, a son they plan to name Bear, this coming April. Rusev expressed his excitement regarding his upcoming role as a father, noting that it has provided him with fresh motivation to make a significant impact in the ring. During a recent interview with "TMZ Sports' Inside the Ring," Rusev stated his intention to pursue the WWE Intercontinental Championship, framing the goal as a life lesson for his future son.

> "I had been following the Intercontinental Title for over a year now, and I believe when the time is right I will bring it back home," Rusev said. "Whether it's before Bear or after Bear, this title will be mine. And now, especially knowing that Bear is coming, I have a lot more to fight for."

He added that he wants to model the right way to live for his child. 

> "I have to teach my son how to be a man, how to stand up for himself, how to show his power where he needs to be, not just spread his power everywhere. Because you can, you have to concentrate your strength. So now this is going to be my next task, my next mission is to show my life to my child and how to behave when he grows up when I'm not here."

Rusev has been absent from WWE television since July due to a neck injury that caused numbness in his hands, followed by an additional illness. While there is no confirmed date for his return, Rusev remains confident that he will maintain his momentum.
