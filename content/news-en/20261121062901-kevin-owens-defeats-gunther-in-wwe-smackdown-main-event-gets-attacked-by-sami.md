Kevin Owens defeated Gunther in the main event of WWE SmackDown, before being attacked by WWE Champion Sami Zayn.

Kevin Owens started the show continuing his pursuit of the WWE Championship, asking Nick Aldis to book a match for him against Sami Zayn, but Gunther interrupted him to demand that he leave the ring so he could state his own case for a title opportunity. Owens returned with Aldis to make his match against Gunther official for the main event as an unofficial match to determine the number one contender for the title.

Gunther attempted to trick Owens by pushing him into the referee, much like he did with Finn Balor the previous week, but Owens avoided it before Candice LeRae and Johnny Gargano interfered in the match. Owens surprised Gargano with a powerbomb, causing the referee to check on him, which allowed Balor to intervene and deliver a low blow to Gunther. Owens capitalized on the moment to execute a Stunner and pin Gunther for the victory.

Following the match, Owens was hit by a surprise attack from Sami Zayn, who landed a Helluva Kick and berated him at length while holding the WWE championship belt aloft to close the show.
