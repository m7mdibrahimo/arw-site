Zack Sabre Jr. has held championships in the United Kingdom, the United States, and Japan, but he added Mexico to that list on November 16 at CMLL x NJPW Lucha Kingdom. He defeated Hechicero to become the new CMLL World Heavyweight Champion. Following the loss, Hechicero has expressed his desire for a rematch, with the Tokyo Dome appearing to be his preferred destination.

During a recent episode of "CMLL Informa," Hechicero discussed the defeat and emphasized his determination to reclaim the championship. He noted that NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 21, scheduled for January 4 at the Tokyo Dome, would be the ideal setting for their next encounter, provided NJPW officials approve the match.

Sabre Jr. addressed the challenge, confirming he is open to defending his new title at the Tokyo Dome. He expressed a preference for facing the next generation of luchadors, though he mentioned Blue Panther as a potential opponent to give the legendary star a moment in the Tokyo Dome. If finalized, this would mark their sixth one-on-one meeting, and their first in Japan.
