Cincinnati Bengals wide receiver Ja'Marr Chase has successfully appealed the fines he received for his touchdown celebration. Chase scored two touchdowns during a game against the Houston Texans on September 20, and he later claimed his celebration was a tribute to The Rock's "people's elbow." NFL reporter Ian Rapoport announced on October 13 that the appeals office overturned both fines. In his appeal, Chase noted that he had received no prior warning that such a celebration was prohibited. The Bengals currently hold a 3-2 record and are preparing for upcoming games against the Baltimore Ravens, the Tennessee Titans, and the Atlanta Falcons.
