Richard Holliday does not believe that AEW deserves credit for the success of independent wrestling. During a recent podcast appearance, he argued that giving a major promotion credit for the entire independent scene disrespects the wrestlers and promoters who grind every week. While he acknowledges that having an AEW star on a card can bring extra attention, he views it as a mere bonus rather than the driving force behind the industry's survival. Holliday pointed to his own busy schedule, featuring over 120 matches throughout 2026 across various promotions, as proof that independent wrestling is thriving. He also emphasized that success does not require filling large arenas, as smaller companies drawing hundreds of fans can still be profitable and successful. Furthermore, Holliday believes wrestlers can create more opportunities for themselves by actively utilizing social media to attract new fans to independent shows.
