Jeff Hardy has expressed a strong desire to return to his classic "No More Words" entrance theme for a potential WWE appearance. During a fan Q&A on Chris Jericho's cruise, which was featured on The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy podcast, Jeff Hardy named the Endeverafter track as his favorite entrance song of all time. He specifically mentioned that he would love for the iconic drums of the song to hit during a future Royal Rumble entrance. Hardy noted that he hopes this could be a possibility in the near future. The wrestler previously pushed for the return of "No More Words" during his 2021 WWE run, though his time with the company concluded shortly after its reintroduction. Since reuniting with his brother Matt Hardy, Jeff has been using "Loaded," the classic Hardy Boyz theme. However, Jeff Hardy believes that hearing "No More Words" start unexpectedly at a Royal Rumble would be an incredibly exciting moment for fans.
