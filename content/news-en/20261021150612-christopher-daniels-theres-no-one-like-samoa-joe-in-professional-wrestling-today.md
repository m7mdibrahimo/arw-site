Christopher Daniels recently spoke about Samoa Joe's unique aura and presence in the professional wrestling industry, emphasizing that there is no one like him today. Throughout his career, Daniels has faced many top names in TNA, including memorable rivalries with AJ Styles and Samoa Joe. Speaking in a new interview with Going Ringside, Daniels praised Joe as one of the toughest opponents he has ever faced and someone who truly defines presence and aura in wrestling. Additionally, Daniels discussed recognizing AJ Styles' special talent from the very beginning of their time working together.
