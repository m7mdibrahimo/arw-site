Zilla Fatu overcame Charlie Dempsey and significant outside interference to secure a victory in their match at WWE NXT Halloween Havoc on October 31, 2026. The bout featured intense back-and-forth action, but the contest turned chaotic when Stacks and Uriah Connors interfered on behalf of BirthRight. Despite Lexis King accidentally sliding a shovel to Fatu, Dempsey used the weapon to target Fatu's arm and hand. Dempsey maintained control with a steel chain and an octopus stretch, but Fatu mounted a late comeback with a series of power moves. After clearing the ring of interference, Fatu countered a shovel attack from Dempsey to hit a Triple Z and a final Frog Splash for the pinfall victory.
