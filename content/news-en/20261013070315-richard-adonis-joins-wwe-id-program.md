Richard Adonis has officially joined the WWE ID program. During a WWN Live event held on October 12 in Clearwater, Florida, WWE coach and EVOLVE General Manager Timothy Thatcher recruited Adonis to the initiative. Adonis confirmed the news on social media, posting, "It’s official WWE ID." The 25-year-old wrestler has been active since 2017, having competed for AEW, ROH, MLW, TNA, and the NWA, and he recently participated in a dark match for WWE NXT in early September 2026. Meanwhile, Laynie Luck is no longer part of the WWE ID program, as her contract was not renewed.
