Due to election coverage, the November 4 episode of WWE NXT on The CW will air on Wednesday instead of its usual Tuesday night slot, going head-to-head with AEW Blood & Guts. The broadcast will feature a special appearance by 17-time WWE World Champion John Cena, who is also scheduled to appear on WWE RAW earlier that week.

Also scheduled for the event is the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic tournament final, where Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno will face Hank Walker and Tank Ledger. Undisputed WWE Champion Cody Rhodes will be present to hand the trophy to the winning team.

Additionally, the rivalry between Dion Lennox and Cruz Montana will continue as they face off in an Unsanctioned match.
