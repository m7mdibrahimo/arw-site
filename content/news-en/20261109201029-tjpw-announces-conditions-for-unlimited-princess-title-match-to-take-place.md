Tokyo Joshi Pro-Wrestling has revealed the rules for the newly introduced Unlimited Princess Championship. The belt is designed to be defended at any time, provided a referee is present. A Battle Royal will crown the inaugural champion at TJPW's event at Korakuen Hall on December 26.

To initiate a title match, a challenger must post an announcement and their desired rules on social media within 24 hours of the challenge. The match is officially sanctioned once the tournament office posts an "approval" on social media. While the interval between the announcement and approval can be as short as one second, ambush challenges without prior notice are prohibited. Matches do not need to follow standard rules, provided they are presented and approved by the office, and every defense must be broadcast on social media, even if it takes place in a ring.
