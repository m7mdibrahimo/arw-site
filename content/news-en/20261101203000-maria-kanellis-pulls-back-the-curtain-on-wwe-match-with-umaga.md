Maria Kanellis recently recalled a terrifying moment from November 2006 when Eric Bischoff booked her in a match against Umaga on WWE Raw. Kanellis admitted that she was visibly shaking before entering the ring, though she noted that Umaga took great care of her and made sure she did not feel the impact of the mat. However, she revealed that the following hip attack was much more painful and left her feeling as though she might be paralyzed. Despite her ditzy character at the time, Kanellis felt her presentation worked to her advantage by contrasting with the physicality she faced from male opponents. Months prior, she had also found herself on the receiving end of an Angle Slam and a potential Ankle Lock from Kurt Angle before John Cena rushed to her rescue.
