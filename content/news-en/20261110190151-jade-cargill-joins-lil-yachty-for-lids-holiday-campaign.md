Jade Cargill is taking part in the new 2026 holiday campaign from Lids, teaming up with rapper Lil Yachty and other figures from sports and entertainment. According to Ringside News, the campaign is titled "It's The Gift That Counts" and features comedy videos about the challenges of finding proper gifts while poking fun at artificial intelligence.

Alongside Cargill and Lil Yachty, the promotion includes Eladio Carrión, Dixie D'Amelio, Chase B, Tim Chantarangsu and Chris Brickley. Lids also confirmed that the Concrete x WWE "It's Us" Collection will launch on November 20, alongside apparel and hats from New Era, '47 and Mitchell & Ness throughout November and December.
