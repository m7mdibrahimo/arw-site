John Cena says he never viewed himself as a locker room leader during his lengthy tenure as the face of WWE. Speaking on The Sam Roberts Show, the 17-time world champion admitted he was uncomfortable with that specific label.

> "I never considered myself a locker room leader in WWE at all. People said that about me, which I’m very grateful for, but I don’t want to be a leader. I want to continue to create, and I want to work with people who are great, and I want to work with people who are passionate. I just want to go to work, dude."

Cena explained that the term feels misplaced because he believes wrestlers perform for the audience, not for their peers in the locker room.

> "Locker room leader is a tough thing to say to me because we don’t work for the locker room. We work for the audience."

While he acknowledges the importance of leadership, Cena stated he does not care who receives the credit as long as the focus remains on delivering the best possible show for the fans.

> "Leadership is important, but I don’t care who’s pulling the sled. Let’s just do the best we can for the audience."
