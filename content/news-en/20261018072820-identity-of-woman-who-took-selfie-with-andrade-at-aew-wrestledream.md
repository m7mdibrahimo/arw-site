Andrade El Idolo continued his recent ringside routine at AEW WrestleDream by stopping mid-match to take a selfie and share a kiss with a fan in the crowd. Following the event, the mystery fan was identified as independent wrestler Madisyn Maxxwell, who confirmed her involvement by sharing a photo with Andrade on her Instagram Story.

Maxxwell is an active competitor on the independent scene, having appeared for promotions like LPW, WOW, and CCW. This marks the third time Andrade has engaged in this specific interaction during a match. He previously took a selfie with independent wrestler Kelly Madan during the October 14 episode of AEW Dynamite, and shared a similar moment with Instagram personality bad.oce at AEW Grand Slam France, where he also kissed the fan during his match against Michael Oku.
