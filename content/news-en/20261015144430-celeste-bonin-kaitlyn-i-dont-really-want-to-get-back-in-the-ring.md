Former WWE Divas Champion Celeste Bonin, also known as Kaitlyn, recently spoke about her current stance on wrestling and whether she plans to step back into the ring. During an interview with Chris Van Vliet, Bonin revealed that she has no desire to return to active competition.

"I hope no one hates me for this, but I don't really want to get back in the ring," Bonin said. She explained that she deeply respects the art form and appreciates the various performance aspects, psychology, and storytelling involved, but she simply does not have the burning desire to compete again. Bonin last wrestled in December 2019, following a brief return in 2018 for the Mae Young Classic tournament where she was eliminated in the second round by Michin.
