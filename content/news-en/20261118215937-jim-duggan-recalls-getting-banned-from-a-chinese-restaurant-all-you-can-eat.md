Jim Duggan recently shared a story about being banned from a Chinese all-you-can-eat buffet while he was training in Hawaii early in his career. Duggan, who wrestled from 1979 until his retirement in 2019, told Sporf that he was sent to Hawaii by Vince McMahon Sr. to train under 'High Chief' Peter Maivia after he had wrestled Hulk Hogan and Sergeant Slaughter in the WWF. Duggan explained that he was a young wrestler at the time and was working alongside his friend Haku, also known as Meng. The two were earning about $40 per week during their stint in Hawaii. Duggan recalled, "The Chinese restaurant banned us from the all-you-can-eat buffet."
