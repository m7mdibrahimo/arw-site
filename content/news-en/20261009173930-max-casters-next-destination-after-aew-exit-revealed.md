Max Caster has confirmed his next destination following his departure from AEW. The former star announced that he will make his TNA debut this weekend at Bound for Glory to answer Moose's open challenge. Caster recently became a free agent alongside Anthony Bowens after their AEW contracts expired. In a social media rap, Caster targeted Moose and outlined his plans to make an immediate impact in TNA. Bound for Glory will mark his first appearance for the company since leaving AEW.
