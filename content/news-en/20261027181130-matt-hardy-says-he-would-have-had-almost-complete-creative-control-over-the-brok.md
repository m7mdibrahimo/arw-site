Matt Hardy recently spoke about the level of creative control he would have had over his iconic 'Broken' character if he had chosen to stay with TNA instead of returning to WWE in 2017. During an interview with The Stunner, Hardy explained that he worked closely with Jeremy Borash on the character's development and had significant input while at home. He noted that a return to TNA was considered after Jeff Jarrett came back to the company, but a disagreement over previously agreed-upon money and a falling out led them back to WWE instead. Hardy stated that had he remained with TNA, he likely would have held nearly complete creative control over the persona.
