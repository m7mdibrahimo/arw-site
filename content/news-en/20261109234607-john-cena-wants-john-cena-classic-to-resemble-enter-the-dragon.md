John Cena is hoping his new tournament, the John Cena Classic, will capture the spirit of the 1973 Bruce Lee film 'Enter the Dragon'. In the movie, the antagonist Han organizes a martial arts tournament as a cover for his criminal operations. Speaking in a new interview with Uncrowned, Cena explained that he wants his event to mirror the tournament structure of the film while keeping the focus on the competition. 

> "Sometimes all people need is just to get out there. Honestly, that's the motivation behind the John Cena Classic. I want to create an annual event where we showcase the best and brightest from NXT against the best and brightest from WWE. Kind of like 'Enter the Dragon' without all the death. I want to put together a heck of a card and I want to let the future of the business run."

In a separate interview, Cena discussed the selection process for the tournament, specifically mentioning Cody Rhodes and Zilla Fatu.
