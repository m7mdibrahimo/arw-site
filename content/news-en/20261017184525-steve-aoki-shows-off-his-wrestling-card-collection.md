DJ and producer Steve Aoki recently showed off his impressive WWE wrestling card collection in a Topps Motion video. Aoki, a known wrestling fan, revealed several rare items, including a card of The Rock that he purchased at a Gold Auction for 90,000 dollars, and a John Cena rookie card graded PSA 10 that remains hidden from public view. He also named the legendary Undertaker as his favorite wrestler of all time. His collection features high-grade vintage items, such as a 1973 Wrestling Annual Andre the Giant card graded PSA 10, alongside a signed Hulk Hogan card featuring a personal inscription. Aoki previously made headlines in the wrestling world when he appeared at a WWE live event in Boston in December 2023, where he threw a cake at Ludwig Kaiser.
