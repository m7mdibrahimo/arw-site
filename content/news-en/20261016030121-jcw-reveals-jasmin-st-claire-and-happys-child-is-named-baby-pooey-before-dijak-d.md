On the October 15 episode of JCW Lunacy, the ongoing pregnancy storyline involving Jasmin St. Claire took a bizarre turn when the name of her child with Happy was revealed as "Baby Pooey." The announcement quickly devolved into chaos in the ring. Jasmin began berating Happy, blaming him for her physical appearance after birth and declaring her intention to make his life miserable. 

> "From here on out, I’m going to make your life a living hell."

Happy responded with a gesture, prompting a physical confrontation. Dijak, who was accompanying Jasmin, was struck by Happy with a stroller. Dijak retaliated by kicking Happy and beating him with the stroller. In a shocking moment, Dijak placed the storyline baby in the ring and dropped an elbow on it. Happy attempted to shield the child, and commentator Mac Davis later confirmed the baby was unharmed. This incident marks the latest escalation in a family feud that has been developing on JCW Lunacy for several months.
