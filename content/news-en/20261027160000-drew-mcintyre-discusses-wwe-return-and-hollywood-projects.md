Drew McIntyre recently discussed why WWE has become more flexible in allowing its stars to take on Hollywood movie roles, while also hinting at his impending return to the ring. McIntyre is currently away from WWE television due to filming commitments for movies like "The Last Druid" and "Highlander," joining other wrestlers such as Roman Reigns, Becky Lynch, and Cody Rhodes who have recently branched out into acting.

McIntyre explained that the current management, led by Nick Khan and Paul Levesque, holds a completely different mindset compared to the previous administration. He noted that Vince McMahon used to require a constant presence at shows because of the heavy schedule of live events, whereas the current leadership supports opportunities that benefit both the individual wrestler and the company brand.

Reflecting on his early days in WWE when he competed against legends like Triple H, Shawn Michaels, and Ric Flair, McIntyre emphasized that he is only 41 years old and feels his physical fitness and speed are at their absolute peak. He warned his peers that he will soon return strongly to WWE programming once he wraps up his time in Hollywood.
> "I remind everyone my age all the time. I just turned 41 the other week. I'm 285 pounds. I'm the biggest I've ever been. You see the way I'll be hitting ropes, I'm the fastest I've ever been. My body feels fantastic. My mind's right. My body's right. It's just whenever I decide to bring Hollywood Drew back to WWE, it's going to get rolling."
