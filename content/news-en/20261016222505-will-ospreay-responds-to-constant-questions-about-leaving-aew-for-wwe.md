Will Ospreay is frequently asked about a potential jump to WWE, but he maintains that he is not concerned with those rumors. Speaking with Brandon Kravitz, Ospreay explained that he accepts such questions as part of being a top-tier wrestler. 

> "Uh, it’s — I just, I know it’s a part of the gig. Like, people always ask, and, like, I can only give my opinion. I don’t really care about it."

Ospreay emphasized that his focus remains entirely on AEW and the goals he wants to achieve within the promotion. He expressed pride in the company's impact on the industry, noting that he is fully invested in what they present to fans.

> "And, like, what I believe AEW is, is changing the face of professional wrestling. And I love what we present. I love what we do. I love our wrestling. I love everything that, like, we’re all about."

Regarding the ongoing debate between AEW and WWE, Ospreay noted that fans comparing companies is simply part of the business. He reiterated that he is not interested in monitoring other promotions, as he is content with his current position in AEW.
