Cruz Montana made a significant impact during his surprise debut on the November 2 episode of WWE RAW. Montana was revealed as the masked individual who attacked Roman Reigns and assisted the group known as 946 in their assault on The Bloodline. Reflecting on his journey, the former AEW and TNA star responded to social media praise regarding his transition from NXT to the main roster. On November 3, Montana shared his thoughts on X, stating, "NEVER let anyone say you can’t do it. You see the vision. You put in the WORK. YOU MAKE IT HAPPEN! I’ll bet on myself every day of the week." After departing AEW and gaining experience in TNA, Montana has now aligned himself with The 946, signaling a major shift in his career. While he previously performed as a babyface in NXT, his main roster arrival immediately positioned him as a heel. With The 946 and The Bloodline currently on a collision course ahead of Survivor Series, Montana is positioned for a prominent role in the coming weeks.
