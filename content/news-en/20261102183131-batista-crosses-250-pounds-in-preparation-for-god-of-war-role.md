Batista has highlighted his physical progress as he works on building muscle to portray Kratos in an upcoming Amazon Prime series, successfully surpassing 250 pounds.

As his acting career grew, the former professional wrestler previously shed much of his heavy muscle mass to improve his overall health for various film roles. However, he secured the iconic video game role in late August following an injury to Ryan Hurst, who was initially announced for the part in January 2026 before suffering a torn bicep that required surgery and threatened to delay production restarts into 2027.

In the weeks following his casting, Batista dedicated himself to packing on significant size to match the character's appearance. Sharing an update via an Instagram story on Sunday evening, he revealed that he has now crossed the 250-pound milestone, adding 20 pounds of muscle so far in preparation for the project.
