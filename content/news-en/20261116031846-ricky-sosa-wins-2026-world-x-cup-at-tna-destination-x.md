Ricky Sosa earned a major career milestone by winning the 2026 World X-Cup at TNA Destination X on November 15, 2026. Sosa emerged victorious from a five-way elimination match that included Fabian Aichner, Home Town Man, Amir Jordan, and Jason Hotch.

The match began with Jordan and Hotch trading early offense before Aichner entered to dominate the action. Home Town Man eventually took control, eliminating Jordan with his "Home Town Slice" finisher, but he was quickly removed from the match by Aichner.

Following Hotch's elimination by Aichner's moonsault, the match came down to a final showdown between Sosa and Aichner. Despite absorbing a series of suplexes and a DDT, Sosa managed to kick out of every pin attempt. After several back-and-forth sequences, Sosa caught Aichner with two uppercuts and secured the pinfall to win the tournament. This victory marks a significant achievement for the young competitor as he continues to build his momentum in TNA.
