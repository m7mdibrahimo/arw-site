Zoey Serrano recently discussed her sushi restaurant segment with IYO SKY on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, noting that she faced online backlash after throwing food during the skit. While that bizarre segment made it to television, Serrano revealed that WWE considered an even stranger idea that was ultimately scrapped. The company reportedly thought about having her scuba diving in a swimming pool while secretly stalking SKY in a weird follow-up. Serrano admitted she is glad that particular underwater segment never happened because she is unsure if she would have agreed to it.
