WWE broadcast the latest episode of WWE EVOLVE on Wednesday, November 4, from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida. The event was available for viewing on Tubi.

CJ Valor def. Ulka Sasaki. Following the match, Charlie Dempsey confronted the MOG Squad.

Nikkita Lyons and Sloane Jacobs def. their opponents.

Laynie Luck vs. Alix Brooks ended in a no contest after Aaron Rourke and Kale Dixon interfered in the ring.

During the show, footage aired of Timothy Thatcher offering Richard Adonis a WWE ID deal. Meanwhile, Thea Hail, Wendy Choo, and Skylar Raye discussed their belief that Nikkita Lyons should lose her championship.

Shido Ash def. Chazz “Starboy” Hall. During the contest, Viktor Zanov attacked Cappuccino Jones at ringside.

Harley Riggins suggested that if Harlem Lewis is not medically cleared, he should vacate his title.

In the main event, Nikkita Lyons def. Thea Hail, Wendy Choo, and Skylar Raye in a Fatal Four-Way match to retain the WWE EVOLVE Women’s Championship. Vanta the Unknown interfered during the match.
