Max Caster addressed the fallout from his appearance at TNA Bound for Glory 2026, where he answered Moose's open challenge. During his entrance rap, Caster delivered a controversial line referencing Riyadh, which led TNA to issue a condemning statement, remove the match from the TNA+ feed, and scrub all related posts and photos from their platforms. Caster took to Instagram to discuss the situation, stating that TNA President Carlos Silva was very generous and professional. Caster apologized to Silva and anyone he felt he let down, but noted that his raps are meant for entertainment rather than to cause upset. He accepted the consequences of his actions, acknowledging that he was fired after just one match, while emphasizing that he is moving forward and appreciates the support from fans.
