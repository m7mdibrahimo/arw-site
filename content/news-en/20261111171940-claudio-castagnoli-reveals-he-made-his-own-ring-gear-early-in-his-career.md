Claudio Castagnoli revealed that he created his own ring gear when he first began his wrestling career, utilizing sewing skills he acquired during his school years. According to Fightful, the AEW star noted that he continued this practice even after moving to the United States and appearing on television.

> "When I started wrestling, when I moved to the United States and even up to the point where I was on TV, I used to make my own ring gear. So I can actually, I mean, I sewed it, I cut it, I made it all myself."

He explained that he initially lacked contacts and found it more cost-effective to produce his own attire. Castagnoli eventually stopped making his gear once his travel schedule increased and he found professionals who could produce higher-quality work.
