Mini Vikingo missed his scheduled match on WWE NXT after suffering a real elbow injury. He was supposed to team with EK Prosper and Chazz Hall to face Jackson Drake, Ricky Smokes and Brad Baylor of Vanity Project, but General Manager Robert Stone announced that he lacked medical clearance.

According to reports, the injury is legitimate and not part of any storyline, forcing WWE to remove him from the bout immediately. No exact details regarding the severity of the injury or his expected absence duration have been released yet, putting his upcoming appearances in question.

These developments arrive just days before his anticipated match against Rey Fenix for the AAA World Cruiserweight Championship at AAA Heroes Inmortales on October 24. Despite his removal from NXT, the title match has not been officially canceled as fans await his final status.
