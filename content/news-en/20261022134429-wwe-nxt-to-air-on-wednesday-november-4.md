WWE NXT will temporarily shift to Wednesday, November 4, facing a direct head-to-head clash with the annual Blood & Guts episode of AEW Dynamite. This schedule change happens because the CW Network will air coverage of the midterm elections on Tuesday, November 3, moving NXT from its usual slot. This marks the first Wednesday clash between the two shows since 2021, as recent yearly showdowns have taken place on Tuesday nights when AEW shifted for the MLB playoffs.
