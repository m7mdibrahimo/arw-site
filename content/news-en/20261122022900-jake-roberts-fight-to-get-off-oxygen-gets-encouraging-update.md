WWE Hall of Famer Jake "The Snake" Roberts has finally managed to quit smoking, marking a significant milestone in his ongoing battle with serious respiratory issues. Speaking with Stephanie McMahon on What's Your Story?, Diamond Dallas Page shared an update on Roberts' progress, noting that after decades of struggling with the habit, Roberts successfully quit over a month ago. Page also highlighted Roberts' broader recovery, noting that the wrestling legend has now been sober for 14 years.

Roberts, who suffers from emphysema and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), previously shared that approximately 50 years of smoking contributed to his condition. While he currently relies on oxygen, he is working toward a medical procedure that could help him get off it. Doctors have required him to remain smoke-free for six months before he can be cleared for the operation. Roberts has already navigated several health challenges this year, including hip revision surgery and the implantation of a Watchman device to prevent strokes.
