New details have emerged regarding the upcoming Anarchy in the Arena match at AEW Full Gear. According to Fightful Select, the contest will feature more participants than the names already advertised. The scheduled lineup for the match pits The Death Riders, consisting of Jon Moxley, Daniel Garcia, and Gabe Kidd, alongside The Conglomeration, featuring Orange Cassidy, Kyle O’Reilly, and Roderick Strong, against The Cut, which includes Jay White, David Finlay, Clark Connors, Austin Gunn, Colten Gunn, and Ace Austin.
