Former AEW Women's World Champion Nyla Rose addressed her recent social media interactions with Sean Ross Sapp in a new video. Rose highlighted several personal updates from her daily life that she felt were being overlooked by media coverage. Among these updates, she shared that she received a phone number from a gentleman and successfully secured a special deal on taquitos. Rose has been a member of the All Elite Wrestling roster since the promotion's inception in 2019 and holds the distinction of being the second-ever AEW Women's World Champion.
