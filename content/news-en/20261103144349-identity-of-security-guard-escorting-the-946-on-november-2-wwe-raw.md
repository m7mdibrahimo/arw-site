Independent wrestler Jay "The Key" Evans was revealed as one of the security guards accompanying The 946 during the Monday, November 2 episode of RAW, according to Ringside News. Evans confirmed his cameo on his Instagram story after temporarily trading his in-ring gear for a security uniform. He has previously performed for promotions such as Worldwide Dojo, TCW, SPO and Gotham Wrestling.

Security proved to be a minor element of the segment as Royce Keys' match against Solo Sikoa dissolved into chaos. Interference from Bronco Nima, Lucien Price and Carmelo Hayes drew Roman Reigns and The Usos into the brawl.

An unidentified masked individual suddenly intervened to prevent Reigns from delivering a Spear to Keys. After striking Reigns with a steel chair and executing a clothesline, the attacker unmasked to expose Cruz Montana. Montana aligned with The 946 to overwhelm The Bloodline, launching his main roster career by immediately inserting himself into a major program with Reigns.
