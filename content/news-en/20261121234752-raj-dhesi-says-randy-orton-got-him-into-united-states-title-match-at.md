Raj Dhesi recently discussed his participation in the United States Championship match at WrestleMania 34, a contest he won in a four-way bout against Randy Orton, Bobby Roode, and Rusev. In an interview with MuscleManMalcolm, Dhesi described the victory as a "dream come true" moment, noting that he entered the match as a significant underdog. He recalled that betting odds heavily favored Rusev, which made the surprise victory even more special for him. Dhesi noted that he did not have a lengthy run with the title, as he was drafted to WWE RAW the following week, but he fondly remembered losing the belt to Jeff Hardy.

Furthermore, Dhesi revealed that he was not originally planned for the match, which was intended to be a contest between Randy Orton and Bobby Roode. He explained that Orton, who was dealing with back or knee issues, requested that Dhesi be added to the match to lighten his physical workload. According to Dhesi, Orton approached him at a live event in New Mexico to inform him of the plan, and Dhesi was added to the program the following week.
