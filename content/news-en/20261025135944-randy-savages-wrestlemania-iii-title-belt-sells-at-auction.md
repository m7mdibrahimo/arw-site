An iconic WWE championship belt used during Randy Savage and Ricky Steamboat's legendary WrestleMania III match sold for $716,750 at Heritage Auctions. The historic title was active between 1985 and 1988, held by multiple WWE legends before Steamboat defeated Savage at the Pontiac Silverdome on March 29, 1987.

https://x.com/darrenrovell/status/2114226133424279693

This massive sale follows other high-priced vintage WWE memorabilia transactions at the same auction house this year. The belt passed through four 1980s Hall of Famers, drawing a massive buyer willing to pay a premium for a true piece of wrestling history.
