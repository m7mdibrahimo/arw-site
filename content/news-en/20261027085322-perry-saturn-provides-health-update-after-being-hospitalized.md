Perry Saturn provided a health update in a video published on his official YouTube channel alongside his show co-host Maven. Saturn explained his recent absence by revealing that he was hospitalized after contracting COVID-19 for the third time, despite being vaccinated. He also dealt with an upper respiratory infection that was successfully treated, though he noted he was still having trouble talking. Saturn joked about his co-host Maven speaking about him as if delivering a eulogy while he was in the hospital, noting that friends and family reached out to check on him.
