Steven Borden made his return to AEW on this week's episode of AEW Dynamite. The show featured a tag team match where Adam Copeland and Will Ospreay faced the Don Callis Family's Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher. Fletcher secured the win for his team by pinning Copeland following a belt shot from Kazuchika Okada and a brainbuster. The Don Callis Family continued their assault on Copeland and Ospreay after the match, but the situation shifted when Darby Allin arrived. Allin introduced Steven Borden, who had been absent since suffering an injury at AEW All Out. With help from the Young Bucks, Borden and Allin cleared the ring. Borden then grabbed the microphone to announce his return, specifically targeting the Don Callis Family for a Blood & Guts match.
