Viewership and demographic ratings are in for the Saturday, October 31, 2026, episode of WWE NXT Halloween Havoc on The CW Network, according to Programming Insider.

The show maintained a level close to the previous week by drawing 461,000 viewers, alongside a 0.07 rating in the key 18-49 demographic.

These numbers reflect linear television viewership on The CW Network only and exclude digital or streaming platforms. This figure marks the highest viewership achieved by WWE NXT so far in 2026 as the company continues preparations for upcoming major live events.
