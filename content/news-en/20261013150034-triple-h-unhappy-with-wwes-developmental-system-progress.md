Triple H has been working to revamp the WWE developmental pipeline, but reports suggest he is not satisfied with the current results. According to Self Made Pro, Triple H has expressed frustration regarding the progress of male NIL recruits, leading the company to prioritize the WWE ID program and the recruitment of experienced independent wrestlers instead.

> "You know why that got deemphasized and why they rushed to do ID? He was not happy. Hunter didn't like the progress of the men's wrestlers, nor did he have interest in calling up any of them."

Triple H reportedly prefers bringing in wrestlers who already possess professional experience, allowing WWE to focus on teaching them the company's specific style. However, concerns persist among main roster talent that recent NXT call-ups are not as prepared as previous generations, particularly regarding the women's division.

> "It exists amongst the men's division, but even more so with the women. There is this feeling that NXT has not properly prepared a lot of talents."

Some wrestlers feel there is a noticeable gap between current graduates and those from a decade ago. While WWE continues to refine its approach, the progress of the WWE ID program has also been slower than initially expected, keeping the system under constant scrutiny.
