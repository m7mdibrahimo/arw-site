Kairi Sane made a surprise in-ring return during Chris Jericho’s Rock ‘N’ Wrestling Rager at Sea on November 11. Following the main event, Sane emerged to a massive ovation and immediately got physical, clearing the ring and delivering an Insane Elbow to Jay Lethal. The appearance marked her first time competing since her WWE release earlier this year. Fans chanted her name as she celebrated with Chris Jericho, Carlito, and others on the cruise. While Tony Khan previously acknowledged her free-agent status and expressed interest in October, and Sane has teased that various things are in motion, no official signing with AEW or any other promotion has been confirmed.
