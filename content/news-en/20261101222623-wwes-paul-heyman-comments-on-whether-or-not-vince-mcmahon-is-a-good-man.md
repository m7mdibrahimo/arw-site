Speaking on In Depth With Graham Bensinger, Paul Heyman opened up about what it was like working under Vince McMahon, sharing his perspective on whether the former WWE CEO viewed himself as a moral person. Heyman stated that he does not believe McMahon ever considered himself a good man, nor did he care about such judgments, viewing his life's purpose differently. Heyman also discussed the intense demands of keeping up with McMahon's relentless work ethic and vision, describing it as an inspiring yet paralyzing experience. Later, Heyman recalled their turbulent meetings during the 2006 ECW relaunch, describing how McMahon frequently stripped him of authority despite initial promises, which ultimately hurt the product and its fans before ECW closed in 2010.
