Joseph Sawyer remains the GCW World Champion. During GCW Fight, promoter Brett Lauderdale suggested stripping Sawyer of the title, but Sawyer appeared on screen to address the situation. Sawyer expressed frustration with the GCW fanbase, citing the negative reaction he received after suffering a knee injury during his title defense against John Jeffrey at GCW Score on September 26.

> Why would I want to come back?

Sawyer criticized the locker room and the fans, claiming he does not need them. He also called out Atticus Cogar for wasting his time in the promotion. Despite his grievances, Sawyer agreed to defend the GCW World Title against Cogar on November 7 in a match with no rules, stating that he intends to leave with the championship after winning.
