Former WWE star Nic Nemeth wants the 946 faction to succeed, but he feels the company needs to provide a clear explanation for why they are attacking everyone. Since debuting in WWE, 946 has ambushed The Bloodline multiple times and recently attacked several stars on WWE NXT. Nemeth discussed the group's storyline on "Busted Open," noting that their actions currently lack a clear mission.

> "I'd like to see, at some point, an in-depth reason, or a reason why. Not just like, 'Oh, he wears a bandana sometimes too. All right, he's in,'"

Nemeth praised the potential of the individual members but emphasized the need for a defined purpose behind their aggression. He compared their current lack of direction to the established history of other factions, such as The Bloodline. 

> "And we need to see some mission statement other than 'no one's safe.' We need something that stands out. And even if it's one of those ones that's the formulaic opening to 'Raw' for 20 years in a row, like, there's a faction of six guys, so they're all going to talk for two minutes, and then Triple H is going to talk for 10, and then we're going to go, it's going to be 25 minutes in. Like, even with that formulaic, I think we need to see it."

Nemeth added that while the group is physically dominant, the audience needs to understand their motivations to remain invested in their rise.
