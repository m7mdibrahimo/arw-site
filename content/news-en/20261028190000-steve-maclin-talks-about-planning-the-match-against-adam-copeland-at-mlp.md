Steve Maclin recently opened up about planning his street fight against Adam Copeland at the MLP Northern Rising event, marking their first-time singles matchup. In an interview with Sean Ross Sapp of Fightful, Maclin explained how he envisioned the bout, noting that he suspected it would be the main event because of Copeland's long-held dream to wrestle at Maple Leaf Gardens. Maclin shared that they wanted to honor their childhoods and professional wrestling history by incorporating signature moves from wrestlers Copeland had previously faced or teamed with. He described the atmosphere as incredible, praising the passionate Canadian crowd and noting that he felt a rare sense of calm and comfort inside the ring throughout the night.
