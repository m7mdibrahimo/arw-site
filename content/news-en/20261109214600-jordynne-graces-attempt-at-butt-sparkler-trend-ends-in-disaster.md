Jordynne Grace is currently absent from WWE television, but the star found a dangerous way to spend her free time. Grace attempted a viral Instagram trend that involves placing a lit sparkler between the buttocks. The experiment failed almost immediately when the sparkler fell onto her foot. Grace scrambled away while yelping, and her friend laughed at the situation. Grace appeared to take the mishap well afterward. She captioned the video: "Trying the viral sparkler trick gone wrong 😭 That’s what I get I guess 😭😭😭" Grace has been off WWE television for some time, which led to speculation about her status when "WWE Superstar" was briefly removed from her social media bio. Fightful Select reported that Grace remains with WWE and the bio change was not a sign of her departure. During her time away, Grace has been preparing for a bodybuilding competition, but she has not been involved in WWE's recent creative plans.
