The lineup for the November 7 episode of AEW Collision is taking shape with several big matches announced. The best-of-five series between FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns continues with match four. Motor City Machine Guns currently hold a 2-1 lead in the series, meaning a victory over Dax Harwood and Cash Wheeler will secure them the overall series win.

Also set for the show, Persephone will face Mina Shirakawa just days after participating in the Women's Blood & Guts match. Additionally, Tommaso Ciampa will challenge Andrade El Idolo for the AEW National Championship.
