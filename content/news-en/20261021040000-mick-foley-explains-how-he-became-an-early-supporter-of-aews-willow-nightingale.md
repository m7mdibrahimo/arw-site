As Willow Nightingale has risen through the ranks in AEW, winning the TBS and AEW Women's World Championships, she has frequently cited WWE Hall of Famer Mick Foley as a major inspiration. Now that they are co-workers, Foley has had the chance to share his admiration for her. During an appearance on "The Mick and Kenny Show," Foley explained that his support began not just because of her talent, but due to her kindness toward his daughter, Noelle, during her own wrestling training.

> "When it was clear that Noelle's body wasn't going to stand up to the, you know, the day in, day out...she [Willow] was so good to my daughter," Foley said. "And I told Noelle, I think I've told you this before, I said 'Noelle, if you have one match, it should be with Willow as a way of thanking her for everything you did.' So for me, to see you get first signed, [I was] extraordinarily happy. And to see the level of success you've had? Incredible."

Their professional bond has continued to grow in AEW. Foley admitted he had a rare "mark out" moment after watching Nightingale's acclaimed match against Mercedes Moné at AEW All In. After sending her a congratulatory text, he was surprised when she invited him to meet with her, leaving the "Hardcore Legend" feeling like a fan himself.
