Rey Pegasus discussed his experience working in the CMLL group Los Amos del Aire alongside Tornado and Magia Blanca, who also serves as their trainer. Speaking with Fightful's Luis Pulido, he described sharing the ring with his teacher as a rewarding opportunity.

> "It’s very rewarding. The fact that your teacher is now your classmate is really great because, while he was already sharing his knowledge with us in the classes he taught at school, now, as classmates, he’s always attentive, always supporting us, and always helping us prepare even more because he knows the responsibility that comes with this after having been a teacher who’s been with CMLL for many years. I’m very happy, and I am making the most of it, taking full advantage of his expertise, and my partner Tornado and I are also working closely together. We’re always trying to find ways to bring out the best, to please the audience, and it was a huge surprise that they put together a concept for us so quickly within the company as well. And well, what could be better than doing it with my teacher? So I’m very happy with how things have gone so far."

Pegasus also noted that he and Tornado are constantly striving to improve their performances to entertain the fans, expressing satisfaction with how quickly CMLL developed their group concept.
