WWE Hall of Famer Glenn Jacobs, known in professional wrestling as Kane, has emerged as the leading contender to replace Senator Marsha Blackburn in the U.S. Senate representing Tennessee, according to Ringside News. The potential opportunity comes following Blackburn's victory in the 2026 Tennessee gubernatorial election. She is scheduled to assume her new office on January 16, 2027, which leaves a Senate vacancy that she will have the authority to fill.

Jacobs previously served two terms as the mayor of Knox County. He spent months strengthening his political relationship with Blackburn and her team, while also actively participating in her campaign events across the state. Reports indicate his chances improved after Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth was removed from consideration for the Senate post.

Amid growing speculation, Jacobs is viewed as a candidate who can connect with party leadership and voters alike. He is also supported by the widespread name recognition built over his decades-long career in WWE.
