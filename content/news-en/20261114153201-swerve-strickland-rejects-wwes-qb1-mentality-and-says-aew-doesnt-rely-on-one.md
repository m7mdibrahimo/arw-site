Swerve Strickland recently discussed how AEW differs from WWE in its approach to top stars, rejecting the concept of a single "QB1." Appearing on The Radio Big Mack Show, Strickland argued that while WWE traditionally relies on a pyramid structure with one clear top star, AEW features a more diverse group at the top. He referred to this group as a "Fab Five," noting that multiple wrestlers rotate through major stories. Strickland pointed to himself, Will Ospreay, and Hangman Page as examples of top talents whose paths regularly intersect in various combinations rather than one person carrying the entire company.
