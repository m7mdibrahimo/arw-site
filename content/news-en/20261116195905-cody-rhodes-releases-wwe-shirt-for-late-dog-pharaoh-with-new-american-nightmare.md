Cody Rhodes’ late dog, Pharaoh, now has his own official WWE T-shirt featuring a newly redesigned version of the American Nightmare logo. Following the passing of the beloved Siberian Husky, WWE Shop launched merchandise showcasing Pharaoh’s face alongside a logo inspired by the dog. The design incorporates a husky-inspired skull while maintaining the signature wings and patriotic color scheme. 

Rhodes shared the announcement on his social media, signaling the personal significance of the new design. The release follows his announcement of Pharaoh’s death on September 8, 2026. Pharaoh was a fixture in the wrestling world for nearly 16 years, appearing at various WWE and AEW events. Rhodes previously revealed plans to modify the logo to honor his companion, and the new merchandise now serves as a tribute to his legacy.
