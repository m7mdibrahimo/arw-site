Val Venis recently discussed a backstage political conversation with Triple H during his WWE career that he believes may have negatively impacted his standing in the company. Appearing on a podcast, Venis recalled a discussion about taxes involving himself, Kane, and another wrestler where he argued that direct taxation is theft. When Triple H overheard and mentioned that his father was a government contractor his whole life, Venis responded by stating that his father technically collected taxpayer money. Venis admitted he is not sure of the exact timeline regarding his championship runs at the time, but he remains convinced that the awkward exchange did not help his career.

> Well, Triple H heard that, and he kind of turned around to me, and he goes, 'Hey, Val, my dad was a government contractor my whole life.' And I just politely looked at him, and I go, 'Well, technically, your dad collected welfare his whole life.'

Venis believes Triple H was genuinely offended by the truth of the statement because he is disconnected from standard political debates about welfare and taxation. While it is impossible to know how much the interaction actually affected his WWE run, Venis looks back at the moment as a backstage debate that likely carried lasting professional consequences.
