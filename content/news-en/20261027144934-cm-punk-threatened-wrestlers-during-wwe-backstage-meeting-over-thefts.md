Booker T recently discussed a time in WWE when wrestlers held a backstage meeting to deal with a series of thefts in the locker room. During an episode of his podcast, Booker recalled that items like wallets and iPads were disappearing, leading veterans to take matters into their own hands. Booker, Big Show, and CM Punk all addressed the roster with very different approaches. Big Show and Booker both tried a diplomatic route, appealing to the locker room's sense of family. CM Punk, however, took a much more direct and threatening stance to warn the culprit.
