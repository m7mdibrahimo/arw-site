Kyle Fletcher refuses to set a timeline for a future AEW World Championship victory, despite being heavily favored as a future world champion following his past reigns with the TNT Championship and International Championship. During a recent interview with Forbes, Fletcher addressed his proximity to the company's top prize.

> "I feel like this is going to be a very hard question to answer. I think I feel ready. I feel ready for it. I want the pressure that comes with it. I want to be the guy that is the face of the company, and I think the guy who has that championship, by proxy, becomes the face of the company."

He noted that he feels closer than ever to the world title and wants to represent the belt everywhere, though he acknowledges the decision ultimately rests with management rather than himself.
