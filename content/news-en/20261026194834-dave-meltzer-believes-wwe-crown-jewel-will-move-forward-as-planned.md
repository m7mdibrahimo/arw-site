Despite ongoing geopolitical tensions in the region and warnings from the US State Department, Dave Meltzer reports that WWE remains committed to holding WWE Crown Jewel in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on November 7. On "Wrestling Observer Radio," Meltzer stated that he expects the event to proceed as scheduled regardless of the situation. Meltzer noted that while he previously hoped for a venue change, he now believes the company's focus on financial gain will prevent any relocation.

> "The people who run the company, the way they're wired, they always want to make money. Money is in front of everything, okay, so they're going to make that decision. I mean, they could bomb the airport the day before, they're still going to go. That's just how it is."
