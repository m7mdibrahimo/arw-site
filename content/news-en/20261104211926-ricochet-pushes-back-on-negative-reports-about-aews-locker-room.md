Ricochet has addressed recent negative reports surrounding AEW's locker room environment, dismissing claims of backstage chaos and ego clashes. Speaking with Zach Heydorn on The Takedown on Sports Illustrated, Ricochet noted that the backstage atmosphere actually reminds him of his early days on the independent circuit.

He acknowledged that wrestlers have distinct personalities and egos, but emphasized that the overwhelming majority of the roster shares the same goal.

> "Obviously everyone has their egos, everyone has their personalities, everyone has their attitudes, whatever you want to say, but at the end of the day, I think 97% of what we got in our locker room, I think everyone wants to make AEW the best, the biggest that it can be."

Ricochet also pointed out that despite occasional negative rumors online, talent routinely set their egos aside for the benefit of storytelling, especially during emotionally challenging periods. He added that everyone understands their role and works together for the collective success of the company.
