C.J. Perry recently reflected on her experience working as a background dancer in the 2011 film 'Big Mommas: Like Father, Like Son.' Perry, who was a professional dancer at the time, appeared in a cafeteria scene filmed in Atlanta. She recalled being fascinated by the stunt work she witnessed on set, specifically noting a scene where a character was sent through a table. Perry admitted she was impressed by the action sequences, telling Sean Ross Sapp, "That was the first time I saw a stunt move and an action move like that and him going through the table and the stuntman and I just thought it was so cool." She joked that at the time, she had no idea that her own future in wrestling would involve being put through tables by Nia Jax. Perry is currently promoting her new book, 'Bulletproof Angel.'
