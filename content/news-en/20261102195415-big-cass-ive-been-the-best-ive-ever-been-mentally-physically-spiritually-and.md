Big Cass discusses how he has been feeling lately, noting he is the best he has ever been mentally, physically, spiritually and emotionally. Earlier this year, Cass made his return to WWE, where he has targeted wrestlers like Je'Von Evans, Rey Mysterio and Dragon Lee. His second run follows a difficult end to his first tenure, during which he previously admitted he was not at his best. Speaking in a new interview on The Sal Licata Show, Cass shared that he feels entirely at the top of his game across all aspects of his life and career, emphasizing the importance of keeping his mind healthy above all else.
