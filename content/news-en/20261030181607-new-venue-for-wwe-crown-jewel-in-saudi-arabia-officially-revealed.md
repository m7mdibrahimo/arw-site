WWE Crown Jewel is set to take place in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on November 7. In recent weeks, concerns regarding the safety of staff traveling to the region have been raised, particularly following a U.S. intelligence memo warning of potential threats to Americans in the Middle East, which coincided with the withdrawal of U.S. diplomats and their families from the country.

On Wednesday, the Riyadh Season social media account announced that the event would move to a new location. It was confirmed on Friday that Crown Jewel will now be held at "The Venue," a modern indoor facility in the heart of Riyadh designed for sports, entertainment, and corporate events. The Venue is located minutes from major city landmarks and approximately 35 minutes from King Khalid International Airport.

According to Dave Meltzer, the change was made due to safety concerns, as the original outdoor arena was replaced by a more secure indoor environment. Meltzer noted that safety reports from those currently in the region have been positive. He stated that unless a major incident occurs before next Tuesday, the event will proceed as scheduled. He previously discussed the logistical and financial incentives for both WWE and TKO to maintain their presence in Saudi Arabia.
