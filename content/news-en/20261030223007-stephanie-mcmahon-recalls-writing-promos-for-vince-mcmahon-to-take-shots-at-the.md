Stephanie McMahon recently recalled shadowing her father, Vince McMahon, and helping him write promos during trips to the United Kingdom. Speaking on What's Your Story with Grayson Waller, Waller joked that no one wants WrestleMania in the rain, which prompted Stephanie to look back on her early days in WWE. She explained that she used to shadow her father and work on his promos, particularly those that involved burying the UK. She noted that it was a very fun experience and added that Waller's jokes about the UK fans enjoying the banter were accurate. John Cena has also continued to advocate for holding WrestleMania in the UK.
