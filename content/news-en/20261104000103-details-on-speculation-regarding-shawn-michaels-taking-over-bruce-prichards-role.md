Shawn Michaels is not set to take over Bruce Prichard's role within WWE. According to Fightful Select, sources within the company have clarified that speculation regarding such a transition is inaccurate. Furthermore, reports suggesting potential changes involving WWE, NXT, Evolve, and ID are either incorrect or far from being finalized, as any succession plan would require significant restructuring. It had previously been reported that Michaels was expected to step into Prichard's position as executive director once Prichard took a step back following the WrestleMania season.
