The field for the 2026 AEW Continental Classic will be revealed this Monday. Tony Khan announced that the participants for the tournament will be unveiled during the halftime show of Monday Night Football. The announcements are set to be made via AEW social media channels. The Monday Night Football game featuring the Baltimore Ravens and Los Angeles Chargers kicks off at 8:15 PM ET, with halftime expected between 9:30 and 10:00 PM ET. Jon Moxley is the only guaranteed participant, as he is the reigning Continental Champion. Moxley won the 2025 Continental Classic and later secured the Continental Challenge Cup at AEW All In. The tournament is scheduled to begin on Wednesday's episode of AEW Dynamite, with the finals traditionally taking place at Worlds End, which is set for December 19.
