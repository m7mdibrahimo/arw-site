TNA Wrestling faced a major travel crisis at Destination X on Sunday, November 15, 2026, in Edmonton, Alberta, after widespread flight cancellations in the United States left numerous wrestlers stranded.

Speaking on The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy, Matt Hardy revealed that the company managed to put on a solid event despite having only 60 percent of its roster available due to the nationwide FAA flight shutdown on November 13.

> "I'm going to put it out there like TNA did a hell of a job putting together a good competent show, especially when only 60% of the roster was there and they made it make as much sense as possible and everybody went out and busted their ass and had their working boots on."

The travel issues forced significant changes, including Indi Hartwell missing her scheduled Knockouts World Championship defense against Shayna Baszler, along with the absences of Moose and The Nemeth Brothers, plus the cancellation of the TNA World Tag Team Championship match. Trey Miguel nearly missed his match after a connecting flight cancellation, but he arrived in Edmonton just in time to keep his TNA World Championship match against Leon Slater intact as the main event.
