Vince Russo has addressed recent rumors regarding a potential return to WWE, though he stopped short of confirming or denying them. Speaking on The Coach & Bro Show, Russo expressed frustration at reporters for circulating stories about him without contacting him directly. Reports had surfaced earlier this summer, including claims from Conrad Thompson and Fightful Select, suggesting Russo had held talks with WWE President Nick Khan. Russo stated that he has nothing to discuss currently and criticized the journalists involved for failing to verify their information. He emphasized that if he has something to say, he will announce it himself. Russo did not explicitly deny that conversations with Khan occurred, but he insisted that no official role or deal has been established, noting that WWE sources indicated no progress was made toward hiring him in 2026.
