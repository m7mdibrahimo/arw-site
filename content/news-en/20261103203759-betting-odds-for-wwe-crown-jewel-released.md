WWE Crown Jewel is scheduled for Saturday, November 7, in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The event will be broadcast on ESPN Unlimited and Netflix. MyBookie has released the betting odds for the upcoming matches. In the World Tag Team Championship match, the team of Penta and Je’Von Evans is favored at -140, while the champions Bron Breakker and Austin Theory are at +100. For the Last Man Standing match, Cody Rhodes is a -250 favorite against Randy Orton, who is at +170. Chelsea Green is favored at -200 to defeat Liv Morgan (+150) for the Crown Jewel Women’s Championship. Finally, Roman Reigns is a -300 favorite over Sami Zayn (+200) for the Crown Jewel Men’s Championship. Fightful will provide full coverage of the event on Saturday.
