Eva Marie remains open to a potential return to WWE, expressing her continued affection for the company that launched her career. During a recent interview with Page Six, she confirmed her willingness to step back into the ring.

> "Of course, yes and yes. So, WWE will always hold a special place in my heart because, like I said, they took a chance on a no-name kid who never wrestled a day in her life."

Reflecting on her background, Eva noted that soccer was her primary sport before transitioning to professional wrestling, having watched WWE during its WWF era with her three older brothers. She also looked back fondly on her 2021 run on WWE RAW, where she fully embraced her role as a heel.

> "I think like I played a bad guy on So, I was a heel in WWE, and there's nothing nothing better to play. Like when I went back in 2021, I had the best time ever because I really embraced that character."

Although her previous stint ended later that year and she did not announce any active talks with WWE, her openness to another run leaves the door ajar for a future comeback.
