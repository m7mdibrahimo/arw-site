AEW Collision experienced a significant ratings surge for its November 11, 2026 broadcast on TBS. According to Wrestlenomics, the episode drew 500,000 viewers and scored a 0.12 rating in the key 18 to 49 demographic. This marks a noticeable rise from the November 7 episode, which garnered 376,000 viewers and a 0.08 rating in the same demographic, reflecting a gain of 124,000 viewers.

The year-over-year figures also demonstrated substantial growth for the brand. The November 15, 2025 broadcast drew 219,000 viewers with a 0.03 demo rating, meaning the 2026 episode surged by 128.3 percent in total viewership and 300 percent in the key demographic. Over the past four weeks, AEW Collision has maintained a rolling average of 406,750 viewers.

These reported totals only account for traditional linear television on TBS and exclude digital or streaming metrics. So far in 2026, the high mark for the show stands at 616,000 viewers recorded on March 22, while the lowest reached 253,000 on January 24.
