Joe Hendry recently opened up about his plans to feud with Logan Paul in WWE and explained why the rivalry never fully materialized. During an interview about his career, Hendry discussed his time on WWE RAW and revealed that Paul's injury halted their planned storyline. Hendry had already targeted Paul through music, including his song Fire Logan Paul, but the injury forced him to shift his focus. Aside from Logan Paul, Hendry has also found himself at odds with The Vision and Judgment Day. Despite facing challenges and having plenty of enemies, Hendry's ultimate goal remains clear. He wants to become a top star and secure championship gold in WWE. Having already won the NXT Championship, Hendry believes he has what it takes to succeed on the main roster. He is now looking closely at the Intercontinental Championship picture as a potential path for his career. Hendry will now have to decide whether to finish what he started with Logan Paul or pursue the Intercontinental Championship.
