Cody Rhodes discussed his wrestling future during an appearance on Chris Van Vliet's Insight podcast. Rhodes revealed that he recently signed a new contract with WWE, noting that it will likely be his final deal. When asked about how much longer he plans to compete, Cody estimated he has about six or seven years left in the ring. "I just signed my last contract. I say last contract, but that's going to go for a while, and if I'm able to do what I've been able to do... probably seven years. Six, seven?" he said. Rhodes also stated that he does not want to continue wrestling into his fifties, explaining that he wants to leave the industry while he is still on top and before overstaying his welcome.
