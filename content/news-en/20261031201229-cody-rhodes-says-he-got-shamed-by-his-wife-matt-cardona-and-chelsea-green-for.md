Cody Rhodes explained why he always wears suits on television, noting that he took the style from Ric Flair. Speaking on the Double Coverage Podcast, Rhodes discussed how Flair represented the flashy lifestyle of a pro wrestler in the 1980s. Rhodes added that he now wears suits almost exclusively after facing gentle teasing from Chelsea Green, Matt Cardona, and his wife for wearing polos that they considered too fancy. Rhodes is currently scheduled to appear on the November 4 episode of WWE NXT.
