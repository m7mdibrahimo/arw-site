Bronson Reed is dismissing concerns that his recent 50-pound weight loss has negatively impacted his wrestling persona. During an appearance on Cheap Heat Live, Reed explained that he previously focused on getting as heavy as possible to stand out as an attraction, reaching 350 pounds at one point. However, he now believes that shedding weight allows him to showcase a more dynamic version of himself that WWE fans have yet to fully experience.

> Pretty much. Yeah, pretty much. You know, I think I have a lot of tricks in my bag from years prior to my experience on the indies and in Japan and stuff. And, you know, I sort of became an attraction by getting bigger and bigger and bigger. And I got to 350 pounds at one point. And I think, you know, seeing a man that size move around, do things, is always something spectacular to see. But there’s so much more that I can offer.

Reed noted that the physical transformation has significantly improved his quality of life, making daily tasks and playing with his daughter much easier. Addressing the effectiveness of his Tsunami finisher, he laughed off suggestions that he would lose impact, arguing that he is now faster and capable of getting more airtime. Reed remains focused on his upcoming matches, eager to prove that he is still a dominant heavyweight with a deeper skill set than previously shown.
