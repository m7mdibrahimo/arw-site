Amazing Red successfully defended the X-Division Championship against Cedric Alexander during the latest episode of TNA Impact. Red originally captured the title during a seven-way scramble at Slammiversary, an unforgettable night that also featured his induction into the TNA Hall of Fame. Alexander earned his title shot by winning a four-way match on the previous show against Fabian Aichner, Rich Swann, and KC Navarro. Alexander started the match strong, grounding the champion before hitting a Death Valley Driver that resulted in a near-fall. Red fought back with a tornado DDT, followed by a thrust kick to the gut, and eventually secured the victory with a Code Red after executing a leaping flatliner.
