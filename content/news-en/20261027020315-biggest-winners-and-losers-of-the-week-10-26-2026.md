It has been another busy week in professional wrestling as major promotions build toward upcoming events. On WWE RAW, Roman Reigns joined his family's fight against The 946, teasing a future WarGames match. Meanwhile, AEW has been focused on building toward AEW Full Gear and the Blood and Guts special on AEW Dynamite.

Joe Hendry is a loser this week after his bizarre Funko Pop-themed match against JD McDonagh on WWE RAW. Despite his viral success in TNA, Hendry's momentum has stalled on the main roster, and losing this comedy match did him no favors.

The Bloodline are winners after Roman Reigns finally intervened to help his family against The 946 on WWE RAW. By driving the group through the crowd, Reigns has signaled his full investment in the rivalry heading into the upcoming WarGames match.

The New Level are losers this week in AEW. While Austin Creed and Kofi are talented, their role as glorified sidekicks to Swerve Strickland in his feud with Hangman Page has sidelined their own potential as a top-tier team.

Mark Davis and Jake Doyle are winners after a dominant squash victory over AEW World Tag Team Champions The Young Bucks on AEW Dynamite. The win establishes them as a credible threat to the tag team division moving forward.

Finally, the Blood and Guts edition of AEW Dynamite is a loser this week. The event feels forced and lacks the narrative build that made past iterations special, with the announced matches appearing thrown together.
