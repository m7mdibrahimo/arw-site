The Rock celebrated his mother Ata Johnson's 78th birthday with a family gathering that included a traditional Samoan Taualuga dance. Dwayne Johnson shared videos from the celebration on Instagram, showing his mother dancing and spending time with relatives. Johnson noted that as her only child, his main priority is keeping his mother happy and smiling. He also joked about failing to fulfill one of her birthday requests. The celebration focused entirely on ensuring his mother enjoyed her special day surrounded by family.
