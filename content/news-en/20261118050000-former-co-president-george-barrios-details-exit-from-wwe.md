Former WWE Co-President George Barrios recently opened up about the details surrounding his departure from the company in 2020. Speaking on the My World podcast with Jeff Jarrett and Conrad Thompson, Barrios explained how he and fellow Co-President Michelle Wilson mutually decided that their time running the company was coming to an end. Barrios detailed their emotional meeting with Executive Chairman Vince McMahon to inform him of their decision, recalling that McMahon became visibly upset and emotional during the discussion.

Barrios noted that McMahon initially tried to persuade them to stay, but eventually tensions grew as they attempted to negotiate a new operating model to speed up their workflow. Barrios recalled that McMahon ultimately decided they needed to leave, telling them they had to pull the band-aid off. Barrios and Wilson later returned to WWE as board members in January 2023 during McMahon's brief return, before departing permanently following the company's merger with UFC into TKO Group Holdings.
