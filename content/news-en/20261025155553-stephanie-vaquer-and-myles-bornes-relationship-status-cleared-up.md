Stephanie Vaquer and Myles Borne are still together despite recent breakup rumors. Fans previously suspected a split after the two WWE stars unfollowed each other on Instagram and deleted their shared photos. However, the speculation ended after they were recently spotted together and shared new photos of themselves working out at the gym. Borne previously revealed that they started dating after he was too nervous to talk to her backstage, leading Vaquer to invite him to dinner. Neither wrestler has publicly addressed the social media changes, but their recent activity confirms they are still a couple.
