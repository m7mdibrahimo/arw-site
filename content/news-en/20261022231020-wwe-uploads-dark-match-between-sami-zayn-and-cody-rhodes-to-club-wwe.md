Following the conclusion of Monday's WWE Raw in San Diego, California, the promotion hosted a special dark match featuring Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn defending his title against Cody Rhodes. According to False Finish and Fightful, this contest is now available for subscribers on the Club WWE platform. This marks the second time in recent months that WWE has shared such exclusive footage, following the release of the Liv Morgan vs. Stephanie Vaquer match from Chile.
