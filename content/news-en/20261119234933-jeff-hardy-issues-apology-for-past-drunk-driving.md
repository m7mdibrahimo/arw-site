Jeff Hardy offered an emotional apology for his past drunk driving arrests, acknowledging that his reckless behavior easily could have ended someone else's life. According to Ringside News, the former WWE Champion stated that he still feels intense shame and guilt whenever he recalls those mistakes.

During his appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Hardy explained that the emotions returned strongly after his oldest daughter, Ruby, recently started driving. He mentioned that he had been joking with her about driving before he could due to the revocation of his license, but the conversation turned serious as he remembered the danger of his past actions.

> "I'm so embarrassed of that. It's such a stupid act, such a childish thing to do, and I'm so ashamed. And that that guilt will will kill you if you let it. So, I just got to keep putting moving forward. But, yeah, I got my I really screwed up my driving situation."

> "I could have actually hurt or killed somebody. And I just want to say that for I'm very sorry for those times I went out there and drove under the influence. It was so childish and unprofessional. So it it makes me feel better when I do apologize just in front of crowds like this. So I apologize."

The situation traces back to Hardy's arrest in Florida in 2022 for driving under the influence, an incident that resulted in a ten-year driver's license suspension. Hardy's apology received warm applause and strong support from the audience present in the studio.
