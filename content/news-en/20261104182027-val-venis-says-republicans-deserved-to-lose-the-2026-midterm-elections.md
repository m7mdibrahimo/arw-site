Former WWE star Val Venis reacted to the November 3 election results by stating that Republicans deserved to lose the midterms. Writing on X, Venis did not blame Democrats for the Republican losses, but instead directed sharp criticism at the party's leadership. He connected his frustration to the Charlie Kirk murder case, pushing an unsubstantiated claim about the incident and criticizing the official narrative. Venis' comments followed the Democratic takeover of Congress in the 2026 midterms. Meanwhile, Mick Foley expressed a very different view of the election results, posting that he was happy with the outcome and calling on Americans to focus on unity.
