Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Raquel Rodriguez reflected on her moment with John Cena at WWE Survivor Series, where she helped Dominik Mysterio win the WWE United States Championship by hitting a Tejana bomb on Cena.

She shared childhood memories of supporting Cena since high school and called sharing a PLE moment with him mind-blowing. She expressed gratitude for learning from his experience.

Rodriguez is the WWE Women's Intercontinental Champion. She was recently in action on WWE Raw, a show that also featured Cena.
