NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 21 could feature a major team reunion. A masked individual appeared on the screen during NJPW Power Struggle to challenge YOH for the IWGP Junior Heavyweight Title, stipulating that a loss would permanently ban YOH from competing in NJPW.

The masked attacker attempted to jump YOH, though YOH blocked the attack, delivered a low blow, and unmasked him to expose SHO. YOH agreed to the bout under the condition that if he wins, SHO must exit HOUSE OF TORTURE and Roppongi 3K will reform.

SHO and YOH previously held the IWGP Junior Heavyweight Tag Team Championship five times as Roppongi 3K in NJPW.
