WWE SmackDown viewership for the October 2 episode experienced a minor dip according to Wrestlenomics. The show drew 1.250 million viewers on USA Network, falling slightly from the 1.263 million viewers recorded on September 25. Despite the drop in total viewership, the program maintained a 0.27 rating in the key 18 to 49 demographic, matching the score from the previous week.

The most viewed clips from the episode on YouTube included the main event qualifying match featuring Kevin Owens, as well as segments involving Trick Williams, Gunther, Finn Bálor, Penta, and Je'Von Evans. Other popular highlights featured a confrontation between Paige and Nikki Bella, a warning from Gunther to Danhausen, and a segment showcasing Damian Priest.
