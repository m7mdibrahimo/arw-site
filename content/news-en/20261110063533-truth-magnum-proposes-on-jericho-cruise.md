Truth Magnum is engaged. During the seventh edition of the Chris Jericho Rock ‘N’ Wrestling Rager at Sea, Magnum proposed to his girlfriend in the middle of the ring. She accepted the proposal. The couple embraced while Turbo Floyd, Dalton Castle, and the crowd celebrated the moment. Magnum, who performs as part of the Outrunners alongside Turbo Floyd, is a former ROH Six-Man Tag Team Champion. He and Floyd are regular performers on ROH TV and have made multiple appearances on AEW programming over the years.
