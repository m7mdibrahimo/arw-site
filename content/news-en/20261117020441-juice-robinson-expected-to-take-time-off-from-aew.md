A report ahead of AEW Full Gear confirmed that Juice Robinson is scheduled to take time off from the company. Robinson was written off television following an attack by The Cut's Anarchy in the Arena opponents during the November 7 episode of AEW Collision. According to Fightful Select, Robinson is expected to begin his leave soon. He was absent from the Full Gear pay-per-view, where The Cut faced the Death Riders and the Conglomeration in an Anarchy in the Arena match. Jay White secured the victory for his team after delivering a Blade Runner to Jon Moxley in a match that lasted nearly 36 minutes. It is possible Robinson is taking this time to spend with his wife, Toni Storm. The former AEW Women's Champion has been inactive since mid-March after being attacked in her dressing room. While reports at the time suggested she would be away for the remainder of 2026, AEW has recently teased her return through social media videos featuring packaged cassette tapes. Dave Meltzer of Wrestling Observer Radio noted that while Storm's in-ring return is not considered imminent, she could potentially be involved in programming in other ways.
