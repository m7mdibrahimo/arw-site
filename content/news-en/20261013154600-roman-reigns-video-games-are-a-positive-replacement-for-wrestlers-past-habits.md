Roman Reigns recently discussed the growing popularity of video games within professional wrestling locker rooms. During an interview on the red carpet for the Street Fighter event, Reigns noted that gaming has become a common way for modern talent to spend their downtime. He highlighted that this trend serves as a healthy alternative to the types of extracurricular activities wrestlers were known to engage in during previous eras. According to Reigns, gaming helps performers stay in their hotels rather than exploring cities excessively while on the road. The WWE star admitted he is not particularly skilled at gaming himself, but acknowledged its significant presence among his colleagues. Reigns is currently scheduled to return to WWE RAW on the upcoming episode of the show.
