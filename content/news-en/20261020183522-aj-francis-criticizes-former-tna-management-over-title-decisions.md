AJ Francis expressed strong frustration with how TNA was previously managed, arguing that the former regime made flawed decisions regarding championship opportunities. During an appearance on The Wrestling Classic, Francis contrasted the past leadership with current head Carlos Silva, whom he praised for securing the company television deal on AMC and running the promotion like a legitimate sports organization.

Francis stated that the previous administration relied on outdated professional wrestling habits, often handing titles to newer talent who had not put in enough work instead of rewarding established performers who consistently delivered.

> "The old wrestling leadership style would be like, 'Well, he's still got a lot to prove, but let's put a title on this guy who's been here six months who nobody gives a shit about.' Like that's the, that's the old wrestling style, right? And Carlos, Carlos recognizes what's in front of his face."

Francis noted that it has been two years since he last held a championship in TNA. He made it clear that his ultimate goal remains winning the TNA World Heavyweight Championship, believing his hard work will soon lead to that opportunity under Silva's management.
