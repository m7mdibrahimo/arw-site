Former WWE star Kalisto recently reflected on the passing of AEW star PAC, real name Benjamin Satterley, who died on September 27. Speaking with TMZ Sports, Kalisto recalled working with Satterley in WWE NXT and praised his unique in-ring style, admitting he wishes they could have faced each other more often. Kalisto noted that everything stars in both AEW and WWE are saying about PAC is true, adding that he looked up to the late star a great deal.
