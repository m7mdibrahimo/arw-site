Former All Japan Pro Wrestling World Tag Team Champion Xyon is heading back to the rugby field for an exhibition match in Wales on October 17th at The Wern Sports Stadium.

https://x.com/alljapan_pw/status/2109949653005504930

> "I’m really excited to put on my rugby boots again. Whether I’m in the ring or on the rugby field, my style is the same—I never leave a single drop of fuel in the tank. I always give it my all—not just with my body, but with my entire soul."

Xyon has played rugby since 2009 and represented the United States national team. In AJPW, he teams with Go Shiozaki, Oddyssey, and Shotaro Ashino in the HAVOC stable. He recently wrestled on the October 12th show, teaming with Oddyssey to defeat MUSASHI and Ren Ayabe. He also reached the semifinals of the 2026 Royal Road tournament before falling to winner Ryuki Honda.
