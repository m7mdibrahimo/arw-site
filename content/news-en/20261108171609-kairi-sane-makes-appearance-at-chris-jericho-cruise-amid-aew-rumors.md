Kairi Sane has made her first major wrestling-related appearance since leaving WWE earlier this year, showing up aboard Chris Jericho’s Rock ‘N’ Wrestling Rager at Sea. Sane shared a photo of herself on X alongside Kofi Kingston and Austin Creed while speculation continues regarding her next destination in the wrestling world. Her appearance on Jericho’s cruise has heightened AEW rumors, especially after Tony Khan addressed her during the WrestleDream media scrum on October 18, stating that he is aware she is a free agent and intrigued by the possibility. While Sane has not signed with AEW and recently told fans that various things are in motion regarding her future, her latest public outing has certainly kept everyone guessing about where she will eventually return to the ring.
