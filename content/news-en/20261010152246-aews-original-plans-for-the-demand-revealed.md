Bishop Kaun recently revealed that he and Toa Liona were not originally planned to join Ricochet in forming The Demand. Speaking on the Certified Mark Show, Kaun explained that they forced their way into the group by delivering standout performances and refusing to wait for backstage approval. After noticing Ricochet was looking for allies, Kaun and Toa Liona capitalized on their matches while Ricochet watched from commentary. Their dominant efforts impressed both Ricochet and management, ultimately securing their spots over two other unnamed wrestlers who Kaun hinted are actually part of the group now anyway.
