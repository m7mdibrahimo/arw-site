Cody Rhodes has finally addressed a well-known quote from Bryan Danielson about learning how to lie, believing people completely missed the joke. Speaking on The Nikki & Brie Show, Cody discussed his longtime friendship and travels with Danielson, which eventually brought up Danielson's 2022 story about Cody teaching him how much fun it is to lie straight to someone's face. Cody explained that they were joking about ridiculous locker-room stories rather than anything serious.

Giving an example, Cody described the kind of absurd lies wrestlers tell to pass time on the road, such as convincing someone that another wrestler secretly wore a hairpiece and adding increasingly specific details. Danielson originally told The Masked Man Show in 2022 that riding with Cody helped turn him into a better liar by showing him how fun it is to make up something completely absurd. Four years later, Cody still dislikes how often that quote is used against him whenever he says something people find hard to believe, especially in media interviews where saying something silly can easily happen.
