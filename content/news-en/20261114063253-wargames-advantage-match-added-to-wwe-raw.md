WWE officially announced a WarGames advantage match for the upcoming episode of WWE Raw, according to Fightful. The bout builds toward WWE Survivor Series, where The Bloodline consisting of Roman Reigns, Jacob Fatu, Jey Uso, Jimmy Uso, and Solo Sikoa will collide with The 946 composed of Royce Keys, Bronco Nima, Carmelo Hayes, Cruz Montana, and Lucien Price.

During WWE SmackDown, it was revealed that The Usos will square off against Carmelo Hayes and Cruz Montana. Here is the updated lineup for the show:

WarGames advantage match: The Usos vs. Carmelo Hayes & Cruz Montana

Women's Intercontinental Championship: Raquel Rodriguez vs. IYO SKY

John Cena will appear
