WWE Hall of Famer D-Von Dudley has firmly rejected the idea of competing in Power Slap, stating that his opposition goes beyond watching clips online. He revealed that the physical toll is a major concern, especially after witnessing the long-term impact on one of his own wrestling students.

During an episode of the D-Von and The Duke podcast, the topic arose following Jake Hager’s recent surgery for a detached retina. When asked about his own interest in participating, D-Von was clear.

> "Hold on. Why would I want to sit there and let somebody slap the s*out of me and then think I can stand up? It’s bad enough that I go in professional wrestling and got to know that they’re going to slam me and do all of that. Now, I’m just supposed to sit there and let somebody slap me? F* no. You asking to be embarrassed. No way whatsoever."

D-Von noted that one of his students competed in Power Slap about six months ago. Although the student won via disqualification, he has yet to be medically cleared to return to wrestling training.

He contrasted the sport with boxing, where athletes at least attempt to defend themselves, and concluded that the brutality of Power Slap is simply too much.
