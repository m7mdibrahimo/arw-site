Cain Velasquez has revealed that people are currently trying to convince him to return to combat sports. Velasquez has been retired from MMA since his final fight against Francis Ngannou in February 2019. Following his MMA career, he had a brief stint in professional wrestling before his legal issues, which led to him spending time in jail until being released on parole earlier this year. In a new interview with Ariel Helwani, Velasquez was asked if anyone is attempting to bring him out of retirement. Velasquez responded, "They’re trying to, they're kicking the cans. Yeah, they are." He explained that he is currently focused on his role as the General Manager for Combat U, stating, "Just like in fighting when I did fight, when I get really passionate about something, I'm laser focused on that. That's what I’m doing." Velasquez last wrestled for AAA on December 3, 2022, after receiving court permission to participate in the match.
