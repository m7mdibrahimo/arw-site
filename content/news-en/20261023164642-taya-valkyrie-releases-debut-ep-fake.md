Taya Valkyrie is expanding her artistic career by stepping into the music industry, according to Fightful. Following weeks of promotion on social media, she launched her debut EP titled "Fake" on Friday, October 23.

The project includes tracks such as "Witch", "Treat", "Fight", and "Fake". Taya shared that her birthday wish is for supporters to listen to the album and add it to their playlists, with the project now available on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and other platforms.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WjgDDy4FtlE

Valkyrie recently departed "The Traitors: Canada" following her elimination earlier in the month. Records indicate she has not competed in a wrestling match since January 2026, and Fightful will provide updates when she eventually steps back into the ring.
