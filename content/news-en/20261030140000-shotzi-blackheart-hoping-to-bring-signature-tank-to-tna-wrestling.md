Shotzi Blackheart is hoping to bring her signature tank with her to TNA Wrestling following her recent return to the promotion. The newest addition to the Knockouts division made her comeback on the October 22 episode of TNA iMPACT, where she saved Allie from an attack by Kiera Hogan and Tasha Steelz of Fire 'N Flava.

During a recent appearance on Knockouts and 3 Counts, Blackheart was asked whether her trademark tank would be making an appearance during her TNA run. She expressed optimism about bringing it back to television.

> "Oh my God, you know, I am hoping so. I am really hoping that maybe there will be a reunion with me and Ole Tanky because it’s been a minute," Blackheart said. "It’s been a minute."

Blackheart is scheduled to return to the ring on the November 5 episode of TNA iMPACT. She will team with Allie to face Hogan and Steelz in a tag team match.
