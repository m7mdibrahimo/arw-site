Kiana James is preparing to join AAA. During the November 21 episode of AAA on FOX, a promotional vignette aired for James featuring the caption "Coming Soon."

Her last appearance on WWE television took place on the October 6 episode of WWE NXT, where she competed in a battle royal to determine the number one contender for the WWE NXT Women's Championship. Her previous main roster match occurred in July, resulting in a loss against Chelsea Green in an interim WWE Women's Championship qualifying match. James is best recognized for her main roster run alongside Giulia before their partnership ended.
