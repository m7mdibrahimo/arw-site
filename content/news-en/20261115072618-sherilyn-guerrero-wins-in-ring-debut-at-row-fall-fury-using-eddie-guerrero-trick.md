Sherilyn Guerrero, the daughter of WWE Hall of Famer Eddie Guerrero, made her official in-ring debut at Reality of Wrestling’s Fall Fury event on November 14. Facing Mystii Marks in her first real match, Sherilyn looked comfortable once the action got underway, delivering a balanced back-and-forth contest.

The match ended with a nod to her family heritage. Marks attempted to hit Sherilyn with a Gory Special, but Sherilyn escaped the hold and countered with a roll-up. To ensure the win, she grabbed a handful of Marks' tights, holding her down for the three-count to secure her first victory. The clever tactic echoed Eddie Guerrero's famous persona, prompting a loud celebration from the crowd.

The rivalry between the two began in October during the Sherri Martel Classic, where Marks blamed Sherilyn for a loss and struck her, leading to a direct challenge. Before her debut, Sherilyn prepared for her career by attending Bayley's Lodestone camp and training at the WWE Performance Center in Orlando.
