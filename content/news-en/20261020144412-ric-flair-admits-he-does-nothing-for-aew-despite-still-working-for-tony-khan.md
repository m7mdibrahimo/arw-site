Ric Flair recently admitted that he still works for AEW despite doing absolutely nothing for the company. During an interview on NewsNation's BIFF, the WWE Hall of Famer discussed his relationship with AEW President Tony Khan. Flair praised Khan for his generosity and stated that the billionaire promoter continues to take care of him out of pure respect for his legendary career. Flair signed a multi-year agreement with AEW in November 2023, initially appearing as Tony Khan's special gift to Sting and contributing to Sting's retirement storyline. Although he has not been a regular presence on television since then, Flair remains loyal to AEW and recently dismissed rumors about returning to WWE, noting that Khan allows him to pursue outside business opportunities while treating him as a personal friend.
