AEW star Chris Jericho joined musician Teddy Swims on stage for a lighthearted moment during a recent concert. The performance took place on Oct. 25 at the Benchmark International Arena in Tampa, Florida, where Jericho made a surprise appearance to help Swims launch hot dogs into the audience.

Swims also shares a friendship with TNA Wrestling's Trey Miguel, having walked him to the ring at No Surrender when Miguel won the TNA International Championship. Meanwhile, Jericho has been involved in a feud with Nick Wayne in AEW, while Wayne is currently competing in Japan on NJPW's Super Jr. Tag League tour.
