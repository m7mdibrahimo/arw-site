Royce Keys has provided insight into why The 946 have focused their efforts on The Bloodline, asserting that his group is responsible for the faction's recent struggles. Since emerging on the WWE main roster, The 946 has engaged in a series of intense battles with The Bloodline.

During a recent episode of WWE Monday Night RAW, Cruz Montana officially joined the group, aligning with Keys during a heated altercation with The Bloodline. In an interview with Hot 97, Keys discussed his motivations for targeting Roman Reigns and his family.

> "You know, I look at it like, back growing up in Cali, in the Bay. You know what I'm saying? So it's- I done did my dirt, did a little time, you know, don't wanna tell too much on the radio. But, you know, my group, 946, there's a bunch of us just feel like we've been held down. You know, so I now go after the top of the food chain, which is The Bloodline. The Bloodline probably is one of the greatest factions to do this all, I mean. Ain't nobody spilled the blood of the Bloodline but us. We put them down."
