Nikki Bella recently discussed Chelsea Green impersonating her at a WWE live event ahead of their WWE Women's Championship match on WWE SmackDown.

Speaking in an interview with Justin Dorazio prior to last week's episode of WWE SmackDown, Nikki was asked about her reaction to Green's impression.

> "Oh my God, of course. It makes you feel like so iconic, especially I'm seeing everyone hit the booty shake and it's like, you get super flattered. It honestly made me smile."

Nikki explained that she was busy traveling on a red-eye flight and handling school arrangements for her son when her mother called to tell her the news, adding that the gesture brought a big smile to her face and made her feel wonderful.
