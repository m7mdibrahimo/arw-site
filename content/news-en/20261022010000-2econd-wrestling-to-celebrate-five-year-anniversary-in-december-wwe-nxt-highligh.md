2econd Wrestling will celebrate its five-year anniversary on December 3. Meanwhile, full highlights from the October 20, 2026, episode of WWE NXT are now available, alongside news that State Champs will face Misery Inc at the upcoming Wrestling Open x Worldwide Dojo event. Josh Alexander and Jake Doyle spent time on the beach before AEW in Miami, and Corinne Joy has been announced for Crimson City Fights.

In other updates, Byron Saxton worked as a scare actor for Sir Henry's Haunted Trail in Florida, and Ron Bass Jr. was announced for Paradigm Pro's upcoming return show. A major update was also provided regarding the future match between Zachary Wentz and Cole Radrick, while Carlie Bravo and Jay Lethal will clash in a title-versus-title match at 1FW Rome Rampage.

Steph De Lander is set to return to Focus Pro Wrestling in November, and tickets are currently on sale for the JCW Series Of Survivals. Additional features include Clark Connors and Dan Le Batard, a story on The Boogeyman from 2005 to 2006, and a full match between Stone Cold Steve Austin and The Rock for the WWE Title at Rebellion 2001.
