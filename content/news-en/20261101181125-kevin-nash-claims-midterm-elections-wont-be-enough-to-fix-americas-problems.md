Kevin Nash believes the upcoming midterm elections will not magically fix America's deep-rooted issues. Speaking on his Kliq This podcast, the WWE Hall of Famer compared hoping for change through the midterms to getting an oil change on a severely damaged car.

Nash also addressed partisan politics, arguing that both Democrats and Republicans contribute to the ongoing divide through tactics like gerrymandering. He further discussed his concerns regarding military leadership and global tensions, admitting he has never seen the world in such a troubling state.
