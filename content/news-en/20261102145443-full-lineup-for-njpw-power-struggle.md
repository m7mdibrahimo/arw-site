New Japan Pro-Wrestling has announced the complete match card for NJPW Power Struggle on November 3. Gabe Kidd will defend the IWGP Global Heavyweight Championship against Taichi in the main event.

The co-main event features the Super Junior Tag League Final between Unbound Co. and Ichiban Sweet Boys. In addition, a three-way match will pit Yota Tsuji against Boltin Oleg and Kenny Omega, with Omega earning an IWGP Heavyweight Title match at Wrestle Kingdom 21 if he wins.

The lineup also includes Knockout Bros. defending the IWGP Tag Team Championships against United Empire, according to Fightful.
