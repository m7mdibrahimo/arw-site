Former WWE star Shotzi Blackheart signed with TNA last month and debuted on the October 22 episode of "TNA iMPACT." Since leaving WWE in 2025, she has competed for promotions including MLW, where she held the Women's Featherweight Championship, GCW, and HOG. Blackheart told "Busted Open Radio" that her arrival in TNA was a long time in the making.

> "I had a couple matches back in like 2019, maybe 2018, with TNA hoping I would become a Knockout then, and you know, I'm finally here and it feels good."

Blackheart expressed excitement about the TNA roster, specifically naming Zoey Serrano, formerly known as Zoey Stark in WWE, as a preferred opponent. She cited their past chemistry and Serrano's aggressive in-ring style as reasons for wanting to face her again. She also praised Jaida Stone, noting that her current in-ring abilities at such a young age are impressive. Blackheart's debut saw her make a save for Allie after a post-match attack by Kierra Hogan and Tasha Steelz, followed by a singles match against Allie.
