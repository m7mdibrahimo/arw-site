Brad Baylor and Ricky Smokes of Vanity Project successfully defended their Tag Team Championship against Hank Walker and Tank Ledger during the latest episode of "WWE NXT." Hank and Tank were named as the replacement challengers after Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno were sidelined following an attack by the champions. The match saw the challengers gain momentum early, but interference from North American Champion Jackson Drake and Myka Lockwood ultimately helped Vanity Project secure the win. This victory marks the first title defense for Baylor and Smokes since they reclaimed the belts from Tavion Heights and Myles Borne at Heatwave in August.
