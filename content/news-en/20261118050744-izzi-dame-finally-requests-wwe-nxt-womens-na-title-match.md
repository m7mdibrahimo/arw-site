Izzi Dame finally challenged Zaria to a Women's North American Championship match on WWE NXT. Dame previously defeated Zaria in a non-title match at Halloween Havoc after earlier passing on a title opportunity because she aimed to prove the champion was not good.

During the show, Dame received an official invitation to the John Cena Classic tournament. Zaria watched the segment from the locker room, which angered her and led her to head to the ring to call out Dame. Dame walked out to the ring, and following a back-and-forth exchange, she decided the moment had arrived to capture the title and earn her second championship reign.

Zaria stated that the championship was not a free handout like the tournament invite and expressed her belief that Dame lacks the ability to strip the title away from her. She added that Dame would receive her title opportunity and promised to make her regret every single word once the opening bell rings. The segment concluded with Zaria hoisting Dame up and driving her down with an F-5 while Dame smiled, showing she achieved her desired outcome.
