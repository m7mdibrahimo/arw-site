Bronson Reed discussed his body transformation journey while recovering from a torn bicep injury he suffered this past February. Prior to his comeback at Sunday Night's Main Event, Reed posted a photo online displaying his new physical fitness, explaining the reasons that drove him to change his appearance and the amount of weight he lost.

> "The way I did it is, I really just locked in. You know, I've had serious injuries before, but this was a serious one with my bicep. I was like, what can I do to sort of stand out when I return? Last time I got injured, I joined The Vision...I thought, you know what, I'm just going to get into great shape. I have a coach in Australia that helps me. He helped me with my nutrition and my strength and conditioning. I was able to drop 50 pounds in those six months when I was injured."

Despite failing to secure a win over Oba Femi at Money in the Bank, that loss only increased his determination to stand out among his teammates in The Vision, with stablemates Bron Breakker and Austin Theory currently holding the World Tag Team Championship.
