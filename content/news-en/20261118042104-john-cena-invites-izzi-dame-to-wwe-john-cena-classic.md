John Cena expanded his upcoming tournament roster by personally approaching Izzi Dame backstage with an invitation during the November 17, 2026, episode of WWE NXT. Dame expressed excitement and received a custom briefcase from the 17-time World Champion.

She joins a roster that already includes Liv Morgan, Lainey Reid, Fallon Henley, Jacy Jayne, Charlotte Flair, Kelani Jordan, Romeo Moreno, Noam Dar, Zilla Fatu, and Cody Rhodes. The tournament will take place on December 14, 2026, at the ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex near Orlando, Florida, featuring exhibition bouts between main roster and developmental talent alongside Club WWE fan voting.
