Bronson Reed discussed his victory over Roman Reigns at WWE Crown Jewel: Perth last year, calling the major win a defining milestone in his wrestling career.

Reed recalled facing Reigns one on one in his native Australia, noting that despite heavy interference, he hit The Tsunami to secure his biggest victory while playing the underdog.

He added that defeating a star of that magnitude in front of his home crowd made the moment unforgettable, especially since many fans doubted he could pull off the upset.

> "I think it's very important. It's sort of become almost a big staple of my career, the fact that I did beat Roman Reigns. I beat him in a one-on-one contest and I did it in Australia as well. I did it in my home country, and it's one of those things I feel like even… as big as bad as I am going against someone like Roman Reigns, you're still the underdog. So people just don't believe that you can beat him."
