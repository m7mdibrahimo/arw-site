Kyle Fletcher has experienced a significant rise within AEW, a journey he believes has profoundly impacted both his career and personal life. The 27-year-old, viewed by many as a future star of the industry, has developed considerably since joining the promotion in 2022.

In a recent interview, Fletcher acknowledged that his lifestyle has undergone a complete transformation, yet his ambition to succeed and capture the world title remains constant. He noted that while fans may perceive him as a successful star living in a large home, he still feels like the young boy putting on wrestling gear for the first time, emphasizing that he will never forget his roots.

Fletcher credited his success to both his own hard work and the influence of his on-screen manager, Don Callis, who helped build his confidence. He is now preparing for a highly anticipated match against Will Ospreay at Full Gear with his sights set on the AEW World Championship.
