Liv Morgan holds the WWE record for the most money spent on sneaker shopping, according to Cody Rhodes. During an appearance at ComplexCon, Rhodes revealed that WWE tracks how much stars spend on the show Sneaker Shopping. He noted that Paul Heyman recently passed him on the spending list, but Morgan remains the all-time leader after spending over 11,000 dollars. Rhodes added that he is not trying to beat her record after purchasing three pairs of sneakers for around 1,300 dollars.
