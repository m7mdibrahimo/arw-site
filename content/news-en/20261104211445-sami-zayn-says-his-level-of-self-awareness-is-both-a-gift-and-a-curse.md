Sami Zayn recently spoke about his level of self-awareness in professional wrestling, describing it as both "a gift and a curse." Zayn, who is the current WWE Champion, is scheduled to face World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns for the Crown Jewel title this coming weekend.

During an interview with Cathy Kelley for the WWE Crown Jewel Countdown show, Zayn discussed what it means to be self-aware as WWE Champion. He stated, "It's a gift and a curse, and I myself have been blessed or cursed, depending on how you look at it, with a high intellect, a high emotional sensitivity, and a very strong sense of morality, and that is a recipe for disaster." He added that he sometimes envies other WWE superstars who navigate life without overthinking, noting that while it feels like a heavy load to carry, it is also the reason he ultimately reached the championship level.

In another recent interview, Zayn also reflected on his fears that the opportunity to become WWE Champion had passed him by following the 2026 Royal Rumble event.
