Nearly two years after his appearance at WWE Elimination Chamber, The Rock is set to return to WWE programming. The Rock announced earlier this week that he will be in town for WWE Survivor Series: WarGames in Houston next weekend. However, his exact role at the event remains unclear.

Speaking on Wrestling Observer Radio, Dave Meltzer stated that he is unsure what The Rock will be doing at the show, only confirming that he is scheduled to make an appearance. Meltzer expressed skepticism that whatever The Rock does will lead to an active storyline. Instead, he believes the appearance is centered around celebrating the 30th anniversary of The Rock's WWE debut at Survivor Series 1996, while also helping WWE sell merchandise and tickets.

While WWE Survivor Series: WarGames has already drawn 30,000 fans at Daikin Park, Meltzer expects The Rock's presence to boost ticket sales further over the coming days. The clarification comes amid ongoing speculation about a potential larger angle involving Roman Reigns or Cody Rhodes, though Meltzer maintains the visit is primarily a promotional appearance.
