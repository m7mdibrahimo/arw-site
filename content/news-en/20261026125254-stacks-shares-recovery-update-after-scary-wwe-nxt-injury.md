Channing Stacks Lorenzo shared an encouraging recovery update following his recent scary injury at a WWE NXT live event. The WWE star posted photos from both the hospital and the gym, showing his quick return to training just over a week after the frightening incident.

Stacks appeared in a hospital photo wearing a neck brace while maintaining his sense of humor with a cheeky message.

> "Doc was concerned about my 'attitude'.. Told 'em it's a preexisting condition. The show goes on."

The injury occurred on October 16 at an NXT live event in Sudbury, Ontario, after he took a back body drop over the top rope during an eight-man tag team match. The situation caused WWE to stop the match while medical personnel checked on him before placing him on a stretcher and transporting him to a hospital. Although WWE never confirmed the exact injury officially, teammate Uriah Connors previously stated he was doing well. Stacks is now back in the gym, though his official in-ring return date remains unconfirmed.
