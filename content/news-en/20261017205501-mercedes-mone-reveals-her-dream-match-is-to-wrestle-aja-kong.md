AEW Women's World Champion Mercedes Mone recently took to social media to reveal her ultimate career goal. Mone shared a post expressing her desire to share the ring with legendary Japanese wrestler Aja Kong. Kong made her debut in 1986 with All Japan Women's Pro-Wrestling and has captured numerous championships throughout her storied career. Mone is currently preparing to defend her title against Thekla at AEW WrestleDream.
