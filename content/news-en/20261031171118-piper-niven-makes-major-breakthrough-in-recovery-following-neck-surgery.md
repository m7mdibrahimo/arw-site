Piper Niven shared an encouraging update on her recovery, showing herself back in the gym and looking visibly slimmer as she continues to heal from neck surgery. Niven posted training photos on X, marking another major step forward after previously revealing that her 12-week scans showed her neck was healing beautifully. Although she noted that the recovery process remains challenging, she remains determined, writing, "The tribulations continue but so do I. 👹" Niven has not wrestled since facing Charlotte Flair on WWE SmackDown in Dublin in August 2025, and there is still no confirmed timetable for her WWE return as she takes her time training and recovering.
