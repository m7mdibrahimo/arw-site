Deonna Purrazzo stated that her time in TNA Wrestling shaped her the most as a performer and gave her the confidence to lead a division. Speaking with The Ledgera magazine, Purrazzo reflected on her career and explained how her run in TNA changed her perspective after she previously questioned her future in the industry. During her time with the promotion, she captured multiple world championships and held tag team gold with Chelsea Green.

> "My time in TNA/IMPACT shaped me the most. In April/May 2020, I didn't even know if I wanted to continue to wrestle so when TNA called, I was hesitant. From the moment I walked in the doors on my first day, I felt like I was home. Besides winning 5 World Championships, main eventing countless PPVs and TV shows, for the first time in my career, I had a team of people who wanted to see me succeed and were willing to work alongside me to see me do so. My time in TNA gave me the confidence to know I could lead a division AND do it without compromising who I am and what I stood for."

Purrazzo wrestled for TNA across two stints from 2014 to 2017 and from 2020 to 2023, establishing herself as a top star. Today, she performs for AEW and ROH while continuing to look ahead at potential high-profile matchups in her career.
