Nic Nemeth recently discussed why he publicly shouldered the blame for the confusion surrounding his advertised match against Jeff Hardy in TNA, according to Fightful. During an interview with TVInsider, Nemeth stated that his decision stemmed from viewing himself as the primary representative for TNA at this stage.

> "It made sense to me because I feel like I’m the main representative for TNA at this point on a regular basis"

He emphasized that a true champion must actively work to boost public interest, grow social media engagement, and maximize every media opportunity. Looking ahead, Nemeth is scheduled to put his TNA World Championship on the line against Leon Slater at the upcoming Bound for Glory event.
