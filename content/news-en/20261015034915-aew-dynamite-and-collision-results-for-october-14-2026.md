The October 14, 2026, episode of AEW Dynamite and Collision emanated from the Wolstein Center in Cleveland, Ohio. The show featured several AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying matches.

Don Callis Family (Wardlow, Jake Doyle, and Mark Davis) def. The Death Riders (Jon Moxley, Gabe Kidd, and Daniel Garcia) in a trios match.

Adam Copeland def. Josh Alexander in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match.

Konosuke Takeshita def. Brian Cage in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match.

Kazuchika Okada def. Myron Reed to retain the AEW International Championship in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match.

Triangle of Madness (Thekla, Julia Hart, and Skye Blue) def. Mercedes Moné, Megan Bayne, and Lena Kross in a trios match.

Samoa Joe and Tommaso Ciampa def. Will Ospreay and Andrade El Ídolo in a tag team match.

Ricochet def. Ace Austin in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match.

Dani Luna and Hyan def. Persephone and Britt Baker in a tag team match.

Darby Allin def. Hechicero to retain the AEW TNT Championship in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match.

Following the main event, a chaotic brawl broke out involving multiple stars, including Will Ospreay, Kazuchika Okada, Adam Copeland, and Samoa Joe, as security and officials struggled to maintain order.
