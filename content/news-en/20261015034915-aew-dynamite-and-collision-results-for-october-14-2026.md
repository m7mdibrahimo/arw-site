The October 14, 2026, episode of AEW Dynamite and Collision aired from the Wolstein Center in Cleveland, Ohio. In the main event, AEW TNT Champion Darby Allin defended his title against Hechicero in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match. The broadcast featured several qualifying matches for the upcoming pay-per-view event.

Don Callis Family (Wardlow, Jake Doyle and Mark Davis) def. The Death Riders (Jon Moxley, Gabe Kidd and Daniel Garcia) in a trios match.
Adam Copeland def. Josh Alexander in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match.
Konosuke Takeshita def. Brian Cage in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match.
Kazuchika Okada def. Myron Reed in an AEW International Championship AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match.
Triangle of Madness (Thekla, Julia Hart and Skye Blue) def. Mercedes Moné, Megan Bayne and Lena Kross in a trios match.
Samoa Joe and Tommaso Ciampa def. Will Ospreay and Andrade El Ídolo in a tag team match.
Ricochet def. Ace Austin in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match.
Dani Luna and Hyan def. Persephone and Britt Baker in a tag team match.

Following his victory, Adam Copeland announced that 2027 will be his final year in professional wrestling. Later, Chris Jericho and Nick Wayne agreed to a rubber match for AEW WrestleDream. The show concluded with Darby Allin defending his championship against Hechicero.
