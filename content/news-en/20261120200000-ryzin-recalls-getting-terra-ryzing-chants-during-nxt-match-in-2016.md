RYZIN recently recalled an amusing moment from his appearance on WWE NXT in 2016, where fans chanted "Terra Ryzing" during his match. Speaking on an episode of Fightful Overbooked's In The Weeds with Jeremy Lambert and SP3, RYZIN shared how Triple H reacted when the chants started. Before his time as a WWE executive, Triple H wrestled under the ring name Terra Ryzing in WCW back in 1994 before changing it to Hunter Hearst Helmsley when he joined WWF in 1995.

During his NXT appearance, RYZIN wrestled a tag team match against Johnny Gargano and Tommaso Ciampa with Triple H overseeing everything backstage. Although enhancement talent usually receive new ring names, RYZIN was allowed to keep his name. One of the producers asked Triple H if they should change RYZIN's name, but Triple H dismissed the idea, stating that nobody would remember Terra Ryzing. However, as soon as the bell rang and RYZIN locked up with Gargano, the crowd began chanting "Terra Ryzing" immediately.
