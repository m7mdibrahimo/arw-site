AEW announcer Excalibur recently reflected on his career in an interview with the Ballast Point Brewing Company, highlighting the historic match between Kenny Omega and Will Ospreay at AEW All In. Excalibur recalled how the broadcast team stayed silent for the first ten minutes of the bout to let the incredible atmosphere at Wembley Stadium speak for itself. He explained that after the initial stats and rivalry background were established, the encounter evolved into a battle for supremacy. "And it was the best professional wrestling match that I have ever called as a broadcaster and maybe the best professional wrestling match that I've ever seen," Excalibur stated.
