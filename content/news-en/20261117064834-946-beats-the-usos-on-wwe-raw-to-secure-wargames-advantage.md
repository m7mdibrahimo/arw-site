946 secured the WarGames advantage after defeating The Usos in the main event of WWE Raw. Jey and Montana traded blows in the crowd while Jimmy and Hayes fought on the opposite side, leading to Jimmy holding the rope down so Jey could dive onto their opponents. Hayes attempted to bring chairs into the ring, but Montana told Hayes to focus because "we need this."

946 maintained control for most of the bout. Jey landed a hip attack for a two-count, and Montana hit a Death Valley Driver. Montana delivered a cutter followed by Hayes hitting Dirty Diana. After Jey tagged in Jimmy, Jimmy connected with a Samoan Splash. Montana pulled Jimmy out of the ring, and Hayes rolled up Jey before executing First 48, followed by Montana landing a Frog Splash that Jey kicked out of at the last second. The Usos gained momentum as Jey hit Superkicks on both opponents and they landed 1D on Montana, but Hayes broke up the pin. Jimmy then drove Hayes into the uncovered announce table, where Hayes hit Nothin' But Net. Back inside, Jimmy blocked Spin the Block with a Superkick, and Jey speared Hayes, who kicked out. Jey knocked Hayes and Montana from the turnbuckle, but Montana got his knees up and finally connected with Spin the Block for the win. Following the match, 946 celebrated in the crowd as Solo Sikoa and Jacob Fatu entered the ring, with Fatu staring down the victors from the top rope.
