Ric Flair is still dealing with the aftermath of his recent nasty fall in Florida. The WWE Hall of Famer revealed that he needs another surgery and possibly a skin graft before his recovery is complete. Flair shared an update from the USF Health Eye Institute, expressing optimism despite his ongoing health challenges. The update came just five days after he suffered a bloody fall outside a Florida establishment, which sent him to Tampa General Hospital for treatment to his face and eye injuries. Flair had already been managing a serious blood clot in his leg that forced him to cancel a scheduled appearance in Seattle. Meanwhile, reports indicated he had visited a bar near his home before and after the accident. Flair remains focused on his healing process and thanked the medical staff for their care.
