Tonight's WWE SmackDown, airing October 16, 2026, emanated live from the Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, California. The show kicked off with Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn addressing the locker room before a match was set between Trick Williams and Johnny Gargano. Non-Title Match: Trick Williams def. Johnny Gargano. In women's division action, Paige squared off against WWE Hall of Famer Nikki Bella. Singles Match: Paige def. WWE Hall of Famer Nikki Bella. Later in the night, R-Truth took on Damian Priest in singles action. Singles Match: Damian Priest def. R-Truth via referee stoppage. In championship action, Rey Fénix defended his title against Ricky Saints. AAA World Cruiserweight Championship Match: Rey Fénix (c) def. Ricky Saints via disqualification. In the main event, Cody Rhodes went one-on-one with Baron Corbin before Randy Orton made his presence felt. Singles Match: Cody Rhodes def. Baron Corbin via disqualification.
