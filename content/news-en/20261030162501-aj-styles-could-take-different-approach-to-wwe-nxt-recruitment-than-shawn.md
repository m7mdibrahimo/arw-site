Matt Hardy believes that if AJ Styles takes a more active role in WWE NXT, he could change how the brand scouts and recruits talent. On The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy podcast, Hardy and Jon Alba discussed reports that Shawn Michaels might eventually move into Bruce Prichard's role, with Styles potentially stepping into a more hands-on position with NXT. Alba questioned whether Styles' unique career path would influence his evaluation of developmental wrestlers. Unlike those who spent their entire careers within WWE, Styles spent years working through various promotions before finding success in TNA, NJPW, and WWE. Hardy stated, "I mean, I do think AJ would be a lot more focused on indies than a lot of other guys would be just because he was such a child of the independent scene, you know, when it was all said and done."

Hardy argues that Styles' history makes him more likely to recognize the value of independent talent and more willing to give them opportunities compared to Michaels. He added, "I think he's definitely much more on the pulse of indie workers, and he would be much more willing to give someone a chance than someone like Shawn Michaels would be because I don't think Shawn is quite on that same pulse." Beyond scouting, Hardy believes Styles' extensive experience adapting to different wrestling styles would make him an excellent mentor for improving in-ring work. While no official announcement has been made regarding a change in leadership, Hardy sees Styles' background as a major asset for the future of NXT.
