Dreamwave Wrestling hosted its Trick or Treat event on October 10 at the Kaycee Club in LaSalle, Illinois. The card featured multiple championship bouts and a featured gauntlet match, according to Fightful.

B3CCA def. Rebecca J. Scott, Tori Fox and Laynie Luck for the Uprising Alternative Championship
AMB def. Kody Kaiser to retain the Dreamwave Alternative Championship
Brittnie Brooks def. Lillith Kahn
Baron Von Raschke won the Sidney Bakabella’s Halloween Ball Gauntlet Extravaganza
Hartenbower & Arik Cannon def. Twist and Flip
Rebecca J. Scott vs. Megan Bayne ended in a no contest
Kevin Thorn & Sirena Veil def. Pinky Montoya & Alfonso Gonzalez
Bobby Orlando def. Connor Hopkins to win the Dreamwave Championship

The main event saw a title change as Bobby Orlando defeated Connor Hopkins to capture the Dreamwave Championship. Earlier in the evening, the Women’s Championship match between Rebecca J. Scott and Megan Bayne concluded without a winner due to outside interference from Brittnie Brooks and B3CCA.
