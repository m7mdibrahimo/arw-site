Major League Wrestling has signed independent wrestler Terry 'The Flip' Sequoyah. According to Fightful Select, Sequoyah is currently sidelined with an injury but will join the active roster once he is cleared to compete. Sequoyah previously appeared for MLW during their May tapings and is based in the Carolina and Virginia region.
