A memorial event honoring Rick Achberger, widely recognized by fans as the WWE Sign Guy, is scheduled for later this month. Achberger died on September 27, prompting numerous WWE performers to share tributes and memories of him on social media platforms.

According to PWInsider, a celebration of life for the late fan will take place on November 27. The gathering is set for 12 pm to 2 pm at Kelly's Restaurant & Bar in Frisco, Texas. The event will be held in a private room, and those who knew him are welcome to attend.
