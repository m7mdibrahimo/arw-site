Cody Rhodes discussed how a WWE pre-show segment sparked unexpected backstage tension involving Roman Reigns. Rhodes noted that Peter Rosenberg brought up Roman during the broadcast and suggested a rivalry regarding who held the top spot, even though Rhodes and Reigns were not scheduled to face each other. Rhodes revealed that he texted Rosenberg about the comments and later addressed the situation in person during the post-show to smooth things over. Rhodes pointed out that the comments fueled rival fan bases and created an unnecessary distraction for the talent behind the scenes.
