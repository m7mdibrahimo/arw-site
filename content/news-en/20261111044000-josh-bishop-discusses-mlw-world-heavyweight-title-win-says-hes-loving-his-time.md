Josh Bishop is enjoying his tenure in MLW. At the Saturday Night SuperFight event, Bishop defeated Killer Kross in a Last Man Standing match to capture the MLW World Heavyweight Championship. Following his title victory, Bishop spoke with Sean Ross Sapp and shared his thoughts on the win and his experience in the promotion so far.

> "I mean, I’m not surprised. Of course it went well. All I said is I’m winning the MLW World Title to everyone that would listen."

Bishop reflected on his return to MLW in January and his journey to the top. He noted that while he did not win the Battle Riot, he remained confident in his path to the championship. He also commented on the evolution of the Skyscraper storyline and his current status as the promotion's top champion.

> "I mean, I love it. I’ve been having a great time. I came back to MLW in January… when I came back, I just had one goal was to become the champion obviously, and so, I didn’t win the Battle Riot. But I knew I was still gonna win the title… I just had to rock and roll. Obviously, did a little Skyscraper story, little deferred. Obviously, Skyscrapers fell apart. There’s just one left. Only one left at MLW now, the MLW World Champion."

Bishop has been a professional wrestler for nine years and currently holds three other championships across various promotions.
