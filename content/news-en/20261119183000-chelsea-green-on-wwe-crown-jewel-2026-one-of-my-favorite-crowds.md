WWE's Chelsea Green recently shared her thoughts on her experience wrestling at Crown Jewel. Green competed against Liv Morgan for the Crown Jewel Championship and emerged victorious, though she later lost the WWE Women's Championship to Tiffany Stratton, who cashed in her Money in the Bank briefcase.

Green discussed the atmosphere during an interview with "Good Karma Wrestling." "So it's funny because we love going to Saudi Arabia and putting on those one-piece outfits. Like, the girls love wearing them, love designing them, love performing in them. But we never know how the fans are going to react to us as females in the ring. We never know, are they going to give us exactly what we think they're going to give us?" she said.

She continued, "Usually we can predict what American fans and what European fans are going to give us in terms of reaction, but we never know. But I would say this is one of my favorite crowds, specifically this crowd at Crown Jewel. Like, they were hot from start to finish. They were singing everyone's songs. They were reacting to my entire match. Like, I can't speak for anyone else's, but throughout my entire match, they were giving Liv and me what we wanted. And that's special. So I have no notes on the Saudi Arabian crowd at Crown Jewel. I, in fact, would go back and do it over again."

Crown Jewel took place in a smaller venue this year due to the ongoing regional conflict, yet the audience remained enthusiastic. Green also noted that despite the long travel time, she and her colleagues use the flights to catch up on much-needed rest.
