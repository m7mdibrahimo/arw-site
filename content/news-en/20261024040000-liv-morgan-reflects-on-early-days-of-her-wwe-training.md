Women's World Champion Liv Morgan, who recently defended her title against Stephanie Vaquer and Becky Lynch at Money in the Bank 2026, shared insights into her early WWE career on First We Feast's "Hot Ones." Morgan noted that her start was defined by a simple instruction: 

> "Shut up and keep your eyes open."

She explained that trainees were not permitted to enter the ring for the first 30 days, forcing them to observe from the sidelines. Reflecting on her initial three-day tryout, Morgan admitted she felt intimidated by the physical demands, especially given her background in competitive cheerleading rather than wrestling. Despite the early challenges, Morgan has since climbed to the top of the women's division. She is now preparing to face WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green for the Women's Crown Jewel Championship at Night of Champions, while keeping an eye on Ms. Money in the Bank Tiffany Stratton, who remains a constant threat to any champion.
