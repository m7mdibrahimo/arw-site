Kevin Owens recently opened up about his upcoming participation in the WWE Money in the Bank ladder match, addressing concerns surrounding his past neck injury and the steel plates now in his spine. During an interview with WWE, Owens explained how he sustained the injury in January 2025 without realizing its severity, going on to wrestle Cody Rhodes at Royal Rumble and Sami Zayn at Elimination Chamber before undergoing surgery. Regarding his current physical condition and the dangerous nature of a ladder match, Owens joked that the surgical hardware might actually make him stronger than ever. "I think the neck injury happened well before the match [with Cody Rhodes in February 2025], which obviously we weren't aware of," Owens said. "I went into that match half-broken and I didn't even know, and I almost won. Now I have steel plates and screws in my neck. So in theory, if you think about it, stronger than ever, really. I'm not sure that logic would be agreed upon by the medical staff, but to me it makes sense. So yeah, I think that actually plays in my favor." Owens also noted that his family has accepted his unrelenting drive to compete, despite the inherent risks involved in high-flying stipulations. "It was really scary for them, but there was no world where I wasn't coming back if I had the chance, and they knew that," Owens said. "I don't think they would want me to not be doing this when they know that this is where I belong, you know? Of course, there's concerns and there's fear, especially considering the elements of a Ladder Match and my past and ladder matches. My wife, my kids, my parents, I think they've accepted that slowing down is just not really an option for me. I've told them all, if I have five years left, three years, two years, whatever it is, I'm gonna give it all for that amount of time and they know. I think they're good with it. So yeah, we'll see what happens." At the upcoming WWE Money in the Bank premium live event in New Orleans, Owens will compete against Je'Von Evans, Penta, Trick Williams, Bron Breakker, and CM Punk in the men's ladder match.
