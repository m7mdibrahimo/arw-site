Major League Wrestling hosted its MLW Saturday Night SuperFight event on November 7 at Cicero Stadium in Chicago, with the show airing on VEEPS. The main event featured a Last Man Standing match for the MLW Championship, where Josh Bishop defeated Killer Kross to capture the title.

In other action, the Good Brothers (Karl Anderson and Doc Gallows) successfully defended their MLW Tag Team Championships against Glasgow Boys On Top (Joe Coffey, Mark Coffey, and Wolfgang) in a street fight. Masha defeated ZAMAYA, while Tessa Blanchard earned a victory over Keyra. Blanchard noted after her match that she was pleased to be competing in MLW.

The MLW National Openweight Championship match between Austin Aries and Matt Riddle ended in a 30-minute time limit draw. Following the bell, Riddle knocked out Aries and took the title. Additionally, Ultimo Guerrero won a triple threat match against Templario and Titan, and Yota Tsuji defeated Trevor Lee.

Several incidents occurred throughout the night. Big Damo and Brick Savage attacked Mistico before his scheduled match, and Josh Bishop brawled with Donovan Dijak backstage. Tessa Blanchard also confronted ZAMAYA behind the scenes. During the event, Court Bauer welcomed the audience and introduced NJPW Chairman Naoki Sugabayashi. Bauer announced that MLW, NJPW, and CMLL are collaborating on a significant surprise for 2027.
