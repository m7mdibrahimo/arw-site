NXT star Wren Sinclair revealed that she was told she was "too old for WWE" and should look elsewhere before finally signing with the company in 2023. Speaking on TMZ's Inside the Ring, Sinclair admitted that injuries and rejection letters made her question whether continuing her wrestling career was worth it. She noted that she is unsure if she would still be wrestling today if WWE had not eventually signed her, as her mental health and future hung in the balance. Now that she is officially with the company, Sinclair joked that WWE will have a hard time getting rid of her.
