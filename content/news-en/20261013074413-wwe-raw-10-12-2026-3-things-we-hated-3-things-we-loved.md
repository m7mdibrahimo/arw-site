Welcome to Wrestling Inc.'s weekly review of WWE Raw, featuring a new member added to the 946 group, the men's Intercontinental Championship match, Oba Femi's promo with Paul Heyman, and Chelsea Green's loss to Roxanne Perez. Here are three things we hated and three things we loved about the October 12, 2026 episode of WWE Raw. Hated: Big bore WWE. Big Cass's return has been baffling, quickly reduced to a laughingstock through repetitive booking and losses, while commentary like Corey Graves buries him instead of helping. Hated: Way to sell a match, guys. Liv Morgan and Chelsea Green's build to Crown Jewel felt all over the place with petty insults, and having Green lose to Roxanne Perez after a distraction made the champion look weak. Hated: That time of year. WWE is building a champion versus champion match between Sami Zayn and Roman Reigns for Crown Jewel without any presence from Reigns, undermining the world title and suspending actual roster progression. Loved: Oba Femi turns down Paul Heyman. Oba Femi showed intelligence and self-reliance by grabbing the microphone from Paul Heyman and declaring himself an Oba Femi guy, bringing renewed logic to their storyline. Loved: Chad Gable and Rey Fénix put on a clinic. Chad Gable defended the Intercontinental Championship against Rey Fénix in a standout match that highlighted both men's strengths and their strong lucha libre styles. Loved: Carmelo Hayes joins 946 to aid in chaotic main event brawl. In a chaotic main event brawl between the 946 and the Bloodline, the returning Carmelo Hayes was revealed as the man behind the bandana, helping his new group stand tall to end the show.
