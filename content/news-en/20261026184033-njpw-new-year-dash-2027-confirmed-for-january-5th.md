New Japan Pro-Wrestling has officially confirmed the date for the 2027 installment of New Year Dash. The event will take place on January 5th at the EBARA WAVE Arena Ota in Tokyo, right after the Wrestle Kingdom 21 show at the Tokyo Dome on January 4th. Former IWGP Heavyweight Champion Kenny Omega has returned to NJPW and aims to reclaim the belt at Wrestle Kingdom. First, he is scheduled for a non-title three-way match at Power Struggle on November 3rd against Oleg Boltin and IWGP Heavyweight Champion Yota Tsuji. If Omega wins, he will secure a title match against Tsuji.
