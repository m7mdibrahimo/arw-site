Former WWE star Jaxson Ryker has explained his decision to retire from professional wrestling after a 23-year career, stating that his body feels much better now that he has stopped wrestling. Ryker revealed that ongoing neck and lower back issues, which caused numbness in his arm and hand during his final days in WWE, ultimately led him to walk away from the ring.

Although he briefly returned for a final independent run in 2022 that concluded in August 2023, Ryker confirmed he has turned down subsequent offers to wrestle again. Emphasizing that he is content in his retirement, Ryker noted that avoiding further physical punishment and preserving his health were far more important than stepping back under the lights.
