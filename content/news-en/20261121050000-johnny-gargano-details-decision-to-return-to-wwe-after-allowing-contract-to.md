Johnny Gargano allowed his WWE contract to run out in late 2021, taking several months away from wrestling before returning to the company in August 2022. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, Gargano explained what pushed him to come back, emphasizing how much storytelling drives his passion for the industry.

> "The storytelling stuff is what really gets me going. That time period where I let my contract expire, and I wasn't anywhere, and I was a free agent, and Candice [LeRae] was pregnant, I remember sitting at home and ... I watched the build-up to Shawn [Michaels] and Undertaker with that 'Running Up That Hill' song from WrestleMania. ... I remember sitting there thinking, 'Oh, I've got to go back to WWE. That's why I got in the business, is this. I want to do this, and that's the place to do it.' That's what made my mind up there."

After returning from his nine-month hiatus, Gargano reunited with Tommaso Ciampa in the tag team DIY for a multi-year run. Following Ciampa's departure from WWE in early 2026, Gargano transitioned through a storyline slump before reigniting his momentum in a SmackDown match against CM Punk for the Undisputed WWE Championship. Despite falling short in that title bout, he has maintained a regular presence on the brand and formed an alliance with current champion Sami Zayn.
