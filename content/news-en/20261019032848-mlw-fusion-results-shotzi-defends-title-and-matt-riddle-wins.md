Major League Wrestling aired the latest episode of MLW Fusion on October 15, featuring matches and segments taped in June and broadcast on Tubi. Killer Kross opened the show by stating that MLW does not hold back talent, including Josh Bishop. Bishop and Donovan Dijak interrupted, with Dijak demanding a title shot, which Kross accepted. In the ring, Zamaya defeated Tiara James. Joe Dombrowski informed fans that CJ Gallows never made it to the building despite being scheduled to debut, while a vignette aired for Masha. In a Lightning Match, Okamura defeated Love, Doug. Austin Aries stated he would not be pressured into giving Matt Riddle a title shot. In the first round of the Opera Cup, Matt Riddle defeated Mark Coffey. A vignette aired for Tessa Blanchard, and it was announced that Killer Kross will defend the MLW Title against Josh Bishop in a Last Man Standing Match at Saturday SuperFight. Josef Samael told Zamaya she caught someone's attention, and footage showed Gallus attacking CJ Gallows. In the main event, MLW Women's Champion Shotzi Blackheart defeated Rina to retain the title. Additionally, it was announced that Yota Tsuji will appear at MLW Saturday SuperFight.
