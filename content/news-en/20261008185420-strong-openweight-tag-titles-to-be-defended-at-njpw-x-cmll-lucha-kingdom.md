The STRONG Openweight Tag Team Championships will be defended at NJPW x CMLL Lucha Kingdom on November 16 at Arena Mexico. Current champions Los Hermanos Chavez, featuring Ángel de Oro and Niebla Roja, will put their titles on the line against the teams of Templario and Francesco Akira, as well as Titan and Taiji Ishimori. Los Hermanos Chavez won the championships in Mexico last November by defeating TJP and Templario. Templario is now teaming with Francesco Akira to bring the tag team titles to United Empire, while Titan and Taiji Ishimori also enter the mix for a high-speed encounter. The event will also feature a singles match between Mistico and El Desperado.
