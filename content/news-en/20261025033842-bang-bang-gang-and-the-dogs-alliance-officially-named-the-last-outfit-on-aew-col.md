The Bang Bang Gang and The Dogs have officially formed a new alliance under the name The Last Outfit. The groups joined forces following a coordinated attack on the Death Riders, featuring David Finlay, Clark Connors, Jay White, Juice Robinson, Ace Austin, Austin Gunn, and Colten Gunn.

During the October 24 episode of AEW Collision, Jay White addressed the union, noting that the collective required a unified identity. After teasing a potential Bullet Club reference to the crowd, White revealed the official name as The Last Outfit.
