Kelani Jordan successfully defended her NXT Women's Championship at the 2026 Halloween Havoc Premium Live Event. Jordan faced challenger Wren Sinclair in what was the champion's second title defense. Jordan secured the victory and kept her championship by using the ring ropes to gain leverage during the pinfall. This win marks another successful outing for the NXT Women's Champion.
