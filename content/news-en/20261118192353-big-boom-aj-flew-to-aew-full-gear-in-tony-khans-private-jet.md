Big Boom AJ discussed how Tony Khan ensured his presence at AEW Full Gear amid recent travel disruptions. Speaking to Tom Campbell of Cultaholic, AJ explained that he traveled on a private jet alongside the debuting Kasper Brothers.

> "There's no stopping Tony Khan. He's the greatest guy in the world. I can't say enough great things about Tony... there was no stopping Tony from making this happen. There was just never a doubt. I mean, there was no way Tony would ever say to the world, 'Sorry, we're pushing Full Gear.' As long as there was a resource available, he was going to use it."

He noted that flights included a trip out of Orlando, another from North Carolina carrying Adam Page and Adam Copeland, and a third from New York, while The Young Bucks drove from Los Angeles to arrive on time.

AJ said he was thrilled to be part of AEW and thanked the Phoenix crowd for their energy throughout the event.
