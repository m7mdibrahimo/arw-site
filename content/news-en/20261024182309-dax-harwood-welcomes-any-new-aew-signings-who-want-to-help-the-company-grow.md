Dax Harwood recently spoke about the influx of new talent joining AEW in 2026, stating that he will welcome anyone who wants to help the company grow. Speaking in an interview with The Danny Ocean Show and 3NT Wrestling, Harwood praised the recent additions to the roster, including Maya World, Hyan, Motor City Machine Guns, Ilja Dragunov, and The New Level, noting that they bring additional eyeballs to the product. Harwood emphasized that helping AEW grow is always a positive thing, and as long as new signings share that goal, he welcomes them. Elsewhere in the interview, Dax also discussed the second match in FTR and Motor City Machine Guns' best-of-five series.
