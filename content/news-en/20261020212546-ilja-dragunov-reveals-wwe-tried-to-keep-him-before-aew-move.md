Ilja Dragunov recently opened up about his departure from WWE, revealing that the company actually tried to keep him before he ultimately decided to sign with AEW. Speaking on The Ariel Helwani Show, Dragunov explained that when he wrestled his final WWE match in April at the Andre the Giant Memorial Battle Royal ahead of WrestleMania 42, he had no idea it would be his last appearance for the promotion. He noted that things simply felt stuck over time as his contract ran down, leading him to realize he needed a change of scenery. Dragunov confirmed that WWE officials wanted to re-sign him and had no intention of letting him walk away easily. However, after weighing creative fulfillment, business factors, and the fact that he spent most of his 20s under the WWE umbrella, he decided that exploring a new environment in AEW was the right path for his career.
