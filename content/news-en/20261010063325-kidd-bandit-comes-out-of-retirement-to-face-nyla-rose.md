Kidd Bandit is returning to the ring for one night only in December at T4T Wrestling: Wild Card.

Bandit previously announced her retirement from professional wrestling in April.

T4T Wrestling shared a statement regarding the upcoming bout.

> "Match Announcement! Nyla Rose takes on a returning Kidd Bandit in a showdown that will rival the duel between the Big Iron Arizona Ranger and the Outlaw Texas Red! Get your tickets today! Event proceeds will be going towards the Funny Bone Fundraiser"

https://x.com/T4TWrestle/status/2107498931928371321

Bandit created the promotion, which held its debut event on July 3.
