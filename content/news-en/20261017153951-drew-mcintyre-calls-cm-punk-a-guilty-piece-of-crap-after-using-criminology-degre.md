Drew McIntyre recently appeared on Stephanie McMahon’s podcast and used his criminology degree to evaluate CM Punk, joking that his longtime rival would make a great case study. McIntyre compared Punk’s ability to win over fans to Charles Manson, urging people to trust someone who has always been there for them instead. When Stephanie brought up the idea of being guilty beyond a reasonable doubt, McIntyre delivered a blunt verdict, calling him a guilty piece of crap. McIntyre has previously stated he wants Punk gone from WWE entirely, and his latest remarks show their intense rivalry remains as personal as ever.
