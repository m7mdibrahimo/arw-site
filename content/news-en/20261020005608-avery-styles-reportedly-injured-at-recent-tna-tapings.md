Avery Styles may face a brief slowdown in his momentum. According to Fightful Select, he was seen wearing a walking boot following recent TNA tapings in Dothan, Alabama.

The son of AJ Styles joined TNA in August at Lockdown and recently competed in the Call Your Shot gauntlet match at Bound for Glory. He defeated Joe Alonzo in a singles match on October 13 that has not yet aired on TNA iMPACT.
