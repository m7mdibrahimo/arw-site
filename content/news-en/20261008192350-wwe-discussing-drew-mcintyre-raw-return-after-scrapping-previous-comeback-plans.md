WWE is reportedly discussing plans for Drew McIntyre to return as a member of the WWE RAW roster, following earlier plans that were scrapped in late August. According to WrestleVotes, creative discussions are taking place regarding McIntyre landing on WWE RAW rather than WWE SmackDown, as the company believes the red brand could benefit more from his star power.

McIntyre has been absent from television since losing to Jacob Fatu at WrestleMania XLII in April 2026, during which time he filmed three movies. Recently, he visited WWE headquarters in Stamford, Connecticut, where he met with officials and filmed promotional material for upcoming micro dramas alongside Jacob Fatu and Joe Hendry. Although previous comeback plans were dropped before materializing, McIntyre has recently expressed a strong desire to return to action, and current discussions indicate that his long-awaited comeback to WWE programming could finally be taking shape.
