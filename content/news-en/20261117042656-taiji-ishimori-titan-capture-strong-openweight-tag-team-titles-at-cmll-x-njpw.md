Taiji Ishimori and Titán captured the NJPW STRONG Openweight Tag Team Championships at the CMLL x NJPW Lucha Kingdom event held on November 16 at Arena México. The title match was a three-way bout where Los Hermanos Chavez, consisting of Ángel de Oro and Niebla Roja, defended their titles against United Empire's Francesco Akira and Templario, alongside the duo of Unbound Co., featuring Taiji Ishimori and Titán. In the end, Titán and Taiji Ishimori emerged victorious to win the titles. The STRONG Tag Team Championships were originally part of the now-defunct NJPW of America branch, though New Japan has announced plans to continue running events in the United States, following their last US show, Resurgence, which took place in Ontario, California, in May 2025.
