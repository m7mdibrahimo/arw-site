WWE held an NXT live event on October 17 in Gatineau, Quebec, Canada. Hank Walker and Tank Ledger defeated DarkState, consisting of Cutler James and Osiris Griffin. Thea Hail secured a victory over Karmen Petrovic. In a title match, NXT North American Champion Jackson Drake, accompanied by Vanity Project, successfully defended his title against Saquon Shugars and Tavion Heights. Myka Lockwood defeated Lizzy Rain, while Cruz Montana picked up a win over Dion Lennox via disqualification. In another title match, NXT Tag Team Champions Vanity Project, featuring Brad Baylor and Ricky Smokes with Myka Lockwood and Jackson Drake, retained their titles against Los Americanos, comprising Rayo and Bravo. Additionally, NXT Women’s North American Champion Zaria successfully defended her title against Izzi Dame. In the main event of the evening, Kevin Owens, Tony D’Angelo, Shiloh Hill and Kendal Grey triumphed over BirthRight, which included Lexis King, Uriah Connors, Charlie Dempsey and Arianna Grace.
