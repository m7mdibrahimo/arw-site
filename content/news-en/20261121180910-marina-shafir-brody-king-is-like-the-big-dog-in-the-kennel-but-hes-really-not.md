Marina Shafir recently discussed Brody King, comparing him to a big dog in a kennel while also noting that he is not really that big. During an interview with WBAY TV-2, Shafir spoke about the AEW Continental Classic tournament and Daniel Garcia's upcoming match against Brody King on AEW Collision, sharing her thoughts on his physical presence and intimidating nature.

> "My boy Daniel Garcia is going to shine like a bright star tomorrow. I can feel it. I mean, he's… Daniel's going to be wrestling Brody King, and Brody, he's like… I don't know. He's like the big dog in the kennel, but he's really not that big. The bark is not as big as his bite, I don't think, maybe. I mean, some other people maybe think otherwise, but yeah, I'm pretty excited about that one."
