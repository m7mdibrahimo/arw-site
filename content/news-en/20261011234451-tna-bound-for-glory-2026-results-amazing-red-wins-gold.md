TNA Bound For Glory took place on October 11, 2026, at the Yuengling Center in Tampa, Florida. The event featured the TNA Hall of Fame induction ceremony, honoring Amazing Red, Konnan, and ODB. The night saw several title bouts and high-stakes matches, with live coverage provided throughout the event.

Ricky Sosa def. Eddie Edwards in a singles match.

Amazing Red def. Fabian Aichner, KC Navarro, Cedric Alexander, Rich Swann, Ryan Nemeth, and BDE in a 7-Way Scramble match to win the TNA X-Division Championship.

In the main event, TNA World Champion Nic Nemeth is scheduled to defend his title against Leon Slater, while The Hardys face syX in a Falls Count Anywhere match. Other scheduled bouts include Allie and Kiera Hogan defending the TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Championship against Zoey Serrano and Shayna Baszler, Moose vs. Max Caster, Mustafa Ali defending the TNA International Championship in a 4-way match, and Xia Brookside defending the TNA Knockouts World Championship against Indi Hartwell.
