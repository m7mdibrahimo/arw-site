Alice Crowley is set to compete in the 2026 CMLL Women's Grand Prix. In an interview with Fightful, Crowley discussed her enthusiasm for the upcoming tournament and the circumstances that led to her selection. 

> "I'm so excited; there's just no other way to put it. I'm just super, super excited, super, super thankful to be here, and I don't think I ever could have told myself, “Hey, guess what you get to do?” you know? I get to wrestle in Arena México, like, that's awesome and, honestly, I can't thank everyone enough. So yes, I'm very, very, very excited to be here."

Crowley explained that the opportunity arose following Taylor Rising's departure from Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling. After receiving a mysterious call from Scott regarding her availability, she initially expected a standard Maple Leaf booking. Upon learning she would be representing the promotion in CMLL, she expressed her gratitude for the chance to compete in such a prestigious tournament.
