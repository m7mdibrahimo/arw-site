AAA held its special Heroes Inmortales 2026 event on October 24 at the Gimnasio Olímpico Juan de la Barrera in Mexico City. The event featured four championship matches, a singles bout, and the induction of Marisela Peñan into the AAA Hall of Fame.

In the main event, AAA Mega Champion El Grande Americano successfully defended his title against Dominik Mysterio. The match saw heavy interference from Omos, Dorian Roldán, and Rey Mysterio, but Americano ultimately secured the win with an Aztec Headbutt.

In the AAA World Trios Championship match, Los Perros del Mal defeated La Parka, Mr. Iguana, and Niño Hamburguesa to retain their titles after Daga hit a low blow on La Parka. Following the match, Big Mami made a save for the challengers when Los Perros del Mal attempted a post-match assault.

La Catalina retained the AAA Reina de Reinas Championship in a Triple Threat Match against Flammer and La Hiedra. The finish saw Catalina hit a running knee on Flammer to secure the pinfall victory after the two challengers failed to coordinate.

In the AAA World Cruiserweight Championship match, Rey Fenix defended his title against Mini Vikingo. Additionally, El Fiscal faced Abismo Negro Jr. in a singles match.
