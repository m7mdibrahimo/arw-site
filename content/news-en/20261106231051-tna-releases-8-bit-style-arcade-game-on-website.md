TNA Wrestling has launched a new 8-bit arcade-style mini-game on its official website, allowing fans to experience various singles matches with ten different wrestlers. The available male competitors include Leon Slater, Nic Nemeth, Trey Miguel, Mr. Elegance, Jeff Hardy, and Moose, while the Knockouts division features Xia Brookside, Indi Hartwell, Rosemary, and Tasha Steelz.

Keyboard controls are utilized to play the game, which incorporates a health bar and a two-minute time limit for each contest. Total Nonstop Action previously launched its only console video game in 2008 titled TNA iMPACT! Produced by Midway, that release featured Kurt Angle, Samoa Joe, AJ Styles, Sting, Jeff Jarrett, and others, along with the story mode character Suicide who eventually transitioned into a real-life wrestler. A sequel to that console game was never published.
