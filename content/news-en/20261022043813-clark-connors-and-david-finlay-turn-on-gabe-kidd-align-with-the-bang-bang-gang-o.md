During this week's episode of AEW Dynamite, Jon Moxley defeated Roderick Strong in an AEW Continental Championship Eliminator Match. Following the contest, Clark Connors and David Finlay entered the ring to attack Strong, but Moxley intervened to stop them. The situation escalated when Connors and Finlay betrayed Moxley, attacking him from behind. When Gabe Kidd rushed to the ring to confront The Dogs, they turned their aggression toward him as well.

Jay White, Orange Cassidy, and Kyle O'Reilly arrived to drive The Dogs away. However, White immediately revealed his true intentions by striking Cassidy with his Blade Runner finisher. The rest of the Bang Bang Gang then joined the fray to assault the members of the Death Riders and The Conglomeration. The segment concluded with Clark Connors and David Finlay officially aligning themselves with the Bang Bang Gang.
