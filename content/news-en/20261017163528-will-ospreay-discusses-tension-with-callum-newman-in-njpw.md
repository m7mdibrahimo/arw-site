Will Ospreay recently spoke about the growing tension between himself and NJPW's United Empire leader Callum Newman. During an interview with 1010XL, Ospreay addressed his relationship with Newman and the changes within the faction.

> He's stuck in his ways and he thinks that he's owed a thank you and he thinks he's a leader, and I just see him as a little bit of a dickhead right now and he's changed everything that I love about the faction that I created.

Ospreay noted that they have not spoken much recently and mentioned that Newman has even brandished a sword during their ongoing feud. He also expressed hope that they can eventually resolve their differences, while adding that he would like Adam Copeland to save him a date before retirement.
