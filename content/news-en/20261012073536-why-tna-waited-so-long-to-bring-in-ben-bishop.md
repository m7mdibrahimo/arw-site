Ben Bishop finally made his TNA debut at Bound for Glory on October 11, 2026, but the move was a long time in the making. According to reports, TNA had been tracking Bishop since last year, with his name surfacing during previous management regimes. He was a regular topic in discussions regarding unsigned independent talent, though he had several unsuccessful WWE tryouts before landing on TNA television.

The delay was primarily due to internal debates over his readiness and his social media presence. While some staff members felt his online clips were a major asset, others, including TNA veteran Tommy Dreamer, were concerned that his content might hinder his chances with major promotions.

> "I’ve been working with Ben a lot lately on trying to better him. I like him. He had a tryout that didn’t go well a long time ago."

> "I told him I don’t think his social media is helping him get hired other places. He has improved in ring so much, hopefully someone picks him up."

Bishop eventually crashed the Call Your Shot Gauntlet at Bound for Glory as the 19th entrant, eliminating Jada Stone to secure his spot. While his debut appeared sudden, it followed a lengthy period of improvement and internal evaluation. It remains unconfirmed if Bishop has signed a full-time deal with TNA, leaving his future with the promotion uncertain.
