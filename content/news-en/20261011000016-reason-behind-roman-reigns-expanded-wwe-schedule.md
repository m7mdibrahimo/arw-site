Roman Reigns is preparing for one of his busiest periods in years, with a backstage report clarifying the driving force behind his increased appearances. According to a report, the decision for Reigns to work a heavier schedule came directly at the request of TKO.

WWE recently confirmed a series of dates for Reigns, including appearances on the October 19, November 2, November 9, November 23, and November 30 editions of WWE RAW, as well as his participation in Crown Jewel and Survivor Series. This shift represents a significant change from the lighter schedule he has maintained in recent years.

The increase in his workload became a topic of internal discussion following the public announcement of his fall schedule. While specific reasons for TKO's request remain undisclosed, Reigns is now set to remain a central figure as WWE moves into a hectic stretch of its calendar.
