IYO SKY could leave WWE RAW tonight with the Women's Intercontinental Championship around her waist. She faces defending champion Raquel Rodriguez in a title match on the November 16 episode of WWE RAW, and reports suggest WWE may trigger a title change on weekly television.

During a recent Q&A session, WrestleVotes discussed the possibility of Sky defeating Rodriguez for the title tonight. Joey Votes noted that a title change is certainly possible. It was also highlighted that WWE has made a conscious effort to feature more title changes on weekly broadcasts rather than saving them exclusively for premium live events, referencing Ethan Page recently winning the Men's Intercontinental Championship on WWE RAW.

There may also be a strategic motivation for a title change tonight. WWE RAW goes head-to-head with Monday Night Football featuring the Chargers and Ravens, and delivering a major title change could help retain viewers throughout the broadcast. While a title change is not guaranteed, WWE's recent approach makes the outcome of tonight's match far more unpredictable.
