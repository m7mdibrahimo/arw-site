AEW Collision is set to deliver an action-packed show tonight, October 24, 2026, building towards the Full Gear pay-per-view. The lineup features the second match in the Best-of-Five Series between FTR and Motor City Machine Guns, where Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin currently hold a 1-0 lead. In singles action, Swerve Strickland will face Myron Reed following his successful title defense at WrestleDream. Additionally, Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron's Divine Dominion will take on Megan Bayne and Lena Kross in a tag team match, while Jay White is scheduled to address the fans following the Bang Bang Gang's recent alignment with The Dogs. The event will air live from the LJVM Coliseum in Winston-Salem, North Carolina, starting at 8 PM Eastern time on TNT and streaming on MAX.
