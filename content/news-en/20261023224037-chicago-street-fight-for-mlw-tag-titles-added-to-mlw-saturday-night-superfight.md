Major League Wrestling announced that The Good Brothers will defend the MLW Tag Team Titles against Glasgow Boys On Top in a Chicago Street Fight at MLW Saturday Night SuperFight on November 7. The match was added after Glasgow Boys On Top attacked Doc Gallows' 18-year-old son, CJ Gallows, during his singles debut, leaving him hospitalized with multiple injuries. Doc Gallows and Karl Anderson responded by putting their titles on the line to get their revenge in a match with no disqualifications.
