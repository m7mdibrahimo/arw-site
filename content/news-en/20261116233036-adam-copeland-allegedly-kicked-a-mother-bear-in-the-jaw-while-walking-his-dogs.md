Adam Copeland had a strange encounter before AEW Full Gear on November 14, 2026, when he came face-to-face with an angry mother bear while walking his dogs. According to Bryan Alvarez of Wrestling Observer, Copeland kicked the animal in the jaw after it approached him while he was holding his dogs' leashes. The kick caused the bear to retreat, allowing Copeland and his pets to escape safely. Copeland later told people that his shin was in a lot of pain from the impact. Despite the injury, he made it to AEW Full Gear to face Kazuchika Okada for the AEW International Championship in the opening match. Copeland lost to Okada after taking a Rainmaker. In addition to the bear encounter, Copeland faced travel delays when nationwide flight disruptions forced Tony Khan to send a plane to get him to Phoenix.
