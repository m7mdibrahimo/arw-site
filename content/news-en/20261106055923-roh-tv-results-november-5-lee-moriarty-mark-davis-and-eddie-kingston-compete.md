Ring of Honor broadcast the latest episode of ROH TV on November 5 via the HonorClub platform. The event featured several matches and segments, with the results listed below.

In the opening Proving Ground match for the ROH Men's Pure Championship, Lee Moriarty and AR Fox fought to a time limit draw. As a result, Fox secured a future title opportunity.

Athena issued a challenge to Hyan for the upcoming ROH Final Battle event. In other action, Robyn Renegade defeated Rachael Ellering, while Adam Priest and ELP picked up a win against Kiran Grey and Dante Casanova.

Shane Taylor Promotions, consisting of Shane Taylor, Tehuti Miles, Capt. Shawn Dean, and Carlie Bravo, emerged victorious over the team of TJ Crawford, Ori Gold, Hadar Horvitz, and Sean Maluta. Mark Davis defeated Marcus Mathers, and Darius Martin earned a win over QT Marshall.

In the Proving Ground match for the ROH Women's Pure Championship, Deonna Purrazzo successfully defended her position by defeating Jody Threat. Additionally, SkyFlight, Boom & Doom, and Bustah & The Brain shared comments regarding The Kingdom.

In the main event, Eddie Kingston and Ortiz defeated RPG Vice, which included Rocky Romero and Trent Beretta.
