Stu Grayson recently reflected on his Maple Leaf Pro match against Josh Alexander and the explosive Canadian style they brought to the ring. At Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling Northern Rising on October 3rd, Grayson and Alexander faced off in a Canadian Championship unification match, which Alexander won.

Speaking on Fightful Overbooked's In The Weeds, Grayson discussed their chemistry and shared philosophy.

> "So what we like to do is what I now call the new style of Canadian pro wrestling, the explosive Canadian technical style... We're very, very explosive. So our styles works really well, because we kind of do the same thing, but the foundation is the same."

Grayson also opened up about the intense and physical nature of the bout.

> "The match itself was extremely physical, and we told each other, we are going out there and fucking brace yourself... I know that Josh needed to show that he could do it because he just came back from a knee injury, and he didn't get as much in the ring time as he would have wanted."

Despite the brutal nature of the match—which left Grayson with cuts and a bloody pillow the next morning—he noted that he had a great time testing himself against Alexander.
