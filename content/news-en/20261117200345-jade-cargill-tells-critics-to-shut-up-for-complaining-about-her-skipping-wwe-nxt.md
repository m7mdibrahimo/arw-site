Jade Cargill is officially tired of hearing complaints from fans about her skipping WWE NXT before jumping straight to the main roster. During a recent Snapchat stream, Cargill addressed her critics directly, telling them to stop obsessing over her career and focusing on something that pays their bills. Cargill explained that she did not make the decision to bypass NXT, stating that the call was made by WWE management and was above her pay grade. 

Cargill also pointed out that nobody knows for a fact whether she would actually be a better wrestler today if she had spent time in developmental. While she has previously acknowledged missing out on certain aspects of camaraderie in NXT and the adjustment to WWE's heavy schedule, she made it clear that she has no regrets. Ultimately, Cargill told fans who continue to complain about her path to stop wasting their time offering opinions on a decision she had no part in making.
