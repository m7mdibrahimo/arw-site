Kyle Fletcher has faced frequent comparisons to Randy Orton in recent years. These parallels are not based on their wrestling styles or career achievements, but rather on their physical builds, as Fletcher has shaved his head and focused on increasing his muscle mass.

Speaking on the What Happened When podcast with Conrad Thompson and Tony Schiavone, Fletcher addressed the situation.

> "When I first started hearing it, it was that reaction of, 'Aw man, Randy Orton is great. I love Randy Orton.' To be mentioned in the same sentence as him was awesome. At the same time, any wrestler should want to carve their own path. I think that I've started to do so."

Fletcher noted that he does not view the comparison negatively. Instead, it serves as motivation to forge his own legacy, acknowledging that it is difficult to compare himself to a veteran with 30 years of experience and 14 world championships. Fletcher is currently preparing to challenge Will Ospreay for the AEW World Title at AEW Full Gear.
