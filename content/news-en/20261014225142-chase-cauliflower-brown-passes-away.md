Wrestler Chase Brown, known by the nickname 'Cauliflower', has passed away at the age of 41. The news was shared by fellow wrestler Cheeseburger, who paid tribute to Brown's career and character. Brown began his professional wrestling journey in 2012 and was a familiar face to many during the mid-2010s through his work with ROH, where he participated in tryouts and served on the ring crew. He was known for his unique character, which included bringing a giant cauliflower to the ring to take a bite from, a name he reportedly received from Terry Funk due to his ear. Cheeseburger wrote the following tribute: 
> RIP “Cauliflower” Chase Brown Truly a one of a kind professional wrestler and a legitimate genuinely good person in this crazy business. 
Brown, who was remembered by peers as a tough and talented performer, appeared for ROH in 2015 and 2016.
