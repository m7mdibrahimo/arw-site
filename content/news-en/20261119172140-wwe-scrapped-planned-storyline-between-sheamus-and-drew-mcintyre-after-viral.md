Sheamus and Drew McIntyre were originally scheduled to enter a storyline together following their viral 'burger after burger' promo, but the plan was quietly dropped. Speaking on the Strength Required podcast, Sheamus revealed that after the segment took off, he suddenly disappeared from television and never received another in-ring promo.

The famous line actually originated from Sheamus, who decided to lean into the online criticism regarding his physique after returning to WWE. He suggested that his close friend McIntyre use the joke during their segment. Although McIntyre initially hesitated, he delivered the line, resulting in an instant viral moment that amused fans.

Despite the segment's success, WWE changed direction. Sheamus noted that he had previously been pushing for a WrestleMania match against Gunther for the Intercontinental Championship, but creative opted for a different path. Following that period, Sheamus returned to television heavier than planned, but the situation ultimately motivated him to refine his training and get back into top shape.
