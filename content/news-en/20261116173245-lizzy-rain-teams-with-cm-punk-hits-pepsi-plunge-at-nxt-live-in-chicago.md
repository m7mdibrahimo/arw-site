Lizzy Rain had a memorable night in Chicago during a live event. In the main event, she teamed up with CM Punk to face Jackson Drake and Myka Lockwood of the Vanity Project. During the closing moments of the match, Lizzy Rain executed the Pepsi Plunge on Drake. CM Punk then followed up with the GTS, and Rain secured the victory by pinning him with Thunderstruck. The Pepsi Plunge is a top rope pedigree that Punk frequently used in the earlier stages of his career, though he rarely performed it in WWE as he grew older. Punk wrestled at two NXT live events over the weekend, having previously appeared in Detroit to team with Bayley.
