Tetsuya Naito has his next GHC Heavyweight Championship defense lined up following his successful defense against OZAWA at Pro Wrestling NOAH Wrestle Odyssey II. Naito will put his title on the line against Kenoh at the upcoming November 9th event at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo, Japan, according to Fightful.

The November 9th show will also kick off the NOAH Jr. Grand Prix tournament. The advertised tournament matches for the opening night feature Kai Fujimura vs. BUSHI, Sidney Akeem vs. Kid Lykos I, Kid Lykos II vs. Tsunagu Sato, and Shuji Kondo vs. YO-HEY.
