Tony D'Angelo revealed that being the NXT Champion comes with unique pressure, noting that he is the first person management looks at when television ratings drop. During an appearance on the Battleground Podcast, D'Angelo explained that holding the title means carrying the brand, which involves handling media responsibilities and answering for the show's viewership numbers. Reflecting on his championship run after winning the belt at Stand & Deliver, he admitted that the role is far more stressful than expected.

He recalled telling himself that he was the main guy for the brand, carrying everything on his shoulders. While acknowledging the pressure, D'Angelo expressed gratitude for Shawn Michaels and the NXT management team for believing in his ability to handle the position. He noted that they assured him his shoulders were strong enough to carry the brand, which is why he won the title. Despite the weight of television ratings falling on the champion, D'Angelo accepted the responsibility that comes with being at the top of the brand.
