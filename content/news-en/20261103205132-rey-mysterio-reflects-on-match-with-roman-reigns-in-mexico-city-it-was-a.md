Rey Mysterio recently looked back on his rare singles encounter with Roman Reigns in Mexico City. The two have only shared the ring in one-on-one competition three times throughout their careers. Their most recent meeting took place during a WWE live event in Mexico City in July 2023.

In a conversation with Adrian Hernandez of Unlikely, Mysterio described the experience as a significant highlight. "Yes I do, yes I do. I remember it was a special night, a special moment for me. Not only for Mexico City, but for me and my career to have this opportunity to main event versus Roman Reigns. Anytime I get to step into Mexico and be part of such an important match like that one, it's just so special."

Roman Reigns is scheduled to face Sami Zayn at WWE Crown Jewel.
