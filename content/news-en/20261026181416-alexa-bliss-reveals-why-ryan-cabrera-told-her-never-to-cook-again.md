Alexa Bliss revealed that her husband Ryan Cabrera once told her never to cook again after a disastrous breakfast attempt early in their relationship. During an appearance on Shot of Brandi with Brandi Rhodes, Bliss recalled trying to surprise Cabrera with scrambled eggs and a bagel before he left for golf. She admitted she knew the eggs looked like burnt pellets, but Cabrera ate everything without complaining. The truth came out later that night during a game night with friends when Cabrera publicly exposed her terrible cooking, telling her he loved the sentiment but she was never cooking again. Bliss admitted she was actually relieved by the ban since she knew the food was awful. She noted that her busy travel schedule with WWE made it hard to keep up cooking habits she formed during her bodybuilding days. However, after successfully handling the bacon during her appearance on the show, Bliss might finally have a chance to challenge the old rule.
