During a 1997 episode of WWE's Badd Blood: In Your House, Matt Hardy witnessed a historic moment as The Undertaker and Shawn Michaels competed in the first-ever Hell in a Cell match. The event also featured the debut of Kane. Hardy, who was 23 at the time and early in his career, recently reflected on the match during his podcast, The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy.

> "It was impressive, yeah," Hardy said. "I'll never forget sitting back and watching that match, and that was the first time I was like, 'Whoa.' Shawn Michaels is like — we knew he was great, but he was on another level in that match. Such a stand-out. It was so incredible to watch."

Hardy also praised The Undertaker's performance, noting how he treated Michaels differently than other opponents. Additionally, Hardy recalled seeing Kane backstage without his mask, noting their history with Glenn Jacobs from their time in Smokey Mountain Wrestling. "We worked with him in Smokey Mountain when he was Unabomber," he added. "It was so weird, because we're like, 'This is crazy, this guy's getting this amazing character, he's Undertaker's brother. We were around him when he was Isaac Yankem.'"
