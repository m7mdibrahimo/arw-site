Chelsea Green still hasn't received her promised free hotel stay from New York City's Plaza Hotel after being mistakenly accused of being an escort, and Cody Rhodes is now stepping in to help. More than two years after the incident, Rhodes wants the hotel to make things right. Green revisited the story during her appearance on Cody Rhodes' What Do You Wanna Talk About? podcast, confirming the situation remains unresolved.

Green explained that after she posted a photo of the security personnel who removed her, a Plaza Hotel representative contacted her, promised the situation would be addressed, and offered a free stay anywhere in the world. However, when she attempted to redeem the offer last year, it did not exist. Rhodes urged the hotel to honor its promise, noting that the story is fully public.
