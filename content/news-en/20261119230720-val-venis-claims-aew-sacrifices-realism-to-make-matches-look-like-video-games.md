Val Venis recently criticized AEW for moving away from believable professional wrestling, comparing some of its modern violence to a demonic horror movie. Speaking on social media, Venis argued that the promotion caters primarily to hardcore gamers who prefer video-game-style action over realism. While acknowledging that there is a legitimate market for this style, he admitted it no longer keeps him engaged. His comments follow recent criticism of AEW's Full Gear event, which featured controversial spots during the Texas Death Match between Swerve Strickland and Hangman Adam Page, as well as the Anarchy in the Arena match.
