Toni Storm's mysterious cassette tapes have sparked rumors about her AEW comeback, but according to Fightful Select, her in-ring return is not imminent. AEW initially had major plans for her through 2026, yet following her bout against Marina Shafir at AEW Revolution in March, officials learned she would be unavailable until late in the year at the earliest. She was subsequently removed from television, creating a storyline mystery surrounding her backstage attack.

The situation led to cassette tapes surfacing at AEW WrestleDream on Friday, October 17, 2026, after being intentionally handed to select media members. Content creator Astrid Pizarro explained how she received hers, stating:

> "I was handed out the tape at WrestleDream by somebody. I opened it, it just said 'play me.' Thankfully my dad and my brother have a cassette player, so I was able to play it today. I was the only one in my area that got it, and it says one out of five. I don't know who the other four people are."

The first tape featured Renee Paquette narrating recordings taken from a hidden device, with the final fifth cassette labeled "Death." Tony Khan addressed her status during the post-show media scrum, confirming her eventual comeback without providing a specific date.

> "I can't say when or where but I know 'Timeless' Toni Storm will be back in AEW."
