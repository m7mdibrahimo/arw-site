According to Ringside News, WWE has finalized the main event for the November 17 episode of WWE NXT, with the NXT Women's Championship match scheduled to close the show. Kelani Jordan will defend her title against Kendal Grey in what will be the final match of the night.

Grey earned her championship opportunity by making a surprise return on the November 10 episode of WWE NXT, confronting Jordan in the ring and setting up a rematch. Their unfinished business stems from Heatwave on August 30, where Jordan captured the NXT Women's Championship after Grey hit an exposed turnbuckle, leading to Grey being sidelined with vertigo and other medical issues. Although Jordan had planned to take some time off from defending her title before competing in the John Cena Classic, General Manager Robert Stone officially booked the rematch.

In addition to the championship main event, WWE has announced more matches for the November 17 lineup. Zilla Fatu will face Dion Lennox following their recent confrontation, and a Triple Threat match will feature Tristan Angels, Sean Legacy, and Saquon Shugars, alongside Tony D'Angelo against Kam Hendrix. The show will emanate live from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, starting at 8 PM ET on The CW.
