Liv Morgan captured the Women’s World Championship at WWE Money in the Bank 2026 by outlasting Stephanie Vaquer and Becky Lynch in a chaotic triple threat match in New Orleans. The bout featured intense action both inside and outside the ring, with tables and ring steps coming into play as all three competitors fought hard for control. Vaquer dominated large portions of the match, dropping both opponents with suplexes and hitting a twisting moonsault after clearing the ringside area. However, the finish was disrupted when Becky Lynch pulled the referee out of the ring to prevent Vaquer from securing the three-count. Capitalizing on the chaos, Morgan quickly hit the Oblivion on Vaquer and pinned her to claim the championship gold.
