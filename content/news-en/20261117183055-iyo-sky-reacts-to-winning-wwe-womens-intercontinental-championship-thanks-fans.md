IYO SKY reacted to winning the WWE Women's Intercontinental Championship on Monday Night RAW, expressing gratitude to the fans. In recent weeks, SKY had been pursuing the title on the RAW brand, and on the November 16 episode of WWE Monday Night RAW, she challenged Raquel Rodriguez in a singles match. SKY secured the victory, adding the championship to her extensive list of accomplishments in professional wrestling. Previously, she had challenged for the title against Becky Lynch and participated in the inaugural tournament to crown the first champion, falling short on both occasions. Following her victory, IYO SKY took to social media to celebrate finally becoming champion and thanked the WWE universe for their unwavering support.

> "FINALLY….I became the Women’s Intercontinental Championship!!! I am so thankful to the WWE universe for always believing in me!!!! Love y'all"
