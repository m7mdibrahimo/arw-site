Independent wrestler Jadis Quinn claims they were attacked by four people at a bar in Hagerstown following an ACW show on Friday night. Quinn stated that the incident began after being targeted with homophobic slurs by three men and a woman. According to Quinn, the situation worsened when security guards intervened and sided with the alleged attackers, throwing Quinn to the ground for defending themselves. Quinn, known in the independent scene as The Twink Temptress, expressed shock and frustration over the incident on social media, questioning how someone could be targeted simply for being themselves. The allegations have not been independently verified, and no statements have been made by the bar or the accused individuals.
