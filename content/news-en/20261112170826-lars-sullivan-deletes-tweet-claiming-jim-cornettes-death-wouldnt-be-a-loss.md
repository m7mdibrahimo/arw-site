Lars Sullivan recently returned to X with a harsh message for Jim Cornette before quickly removing it. The former WWE star attacked Cornette and his wife personally, even speculating about what would occur following the longtime wrestling personality's eventual death. Sullivan wrote, "When Jim Cornette dies, nothing of value is lost. He's leaving behind a fat, ugly wife. That's all."

Sullivan deleted the post shortly after, without providing any explanation for the outburst or his decision to take it down. Cornette is known for his polarizing opinions on wrestlers and modern wrestling promotions, which have frequently resulted in public disputes. Sullivan has largely avoided the wrestling industry since leaving WWE, making this sudden attack particularly unexpected. He has not commented further on the situation, leaving fans to speculate about what might have triggered his anger.
