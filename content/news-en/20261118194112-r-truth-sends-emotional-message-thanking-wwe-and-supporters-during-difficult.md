R-Truth is currently navigating a challenging period and has taken the opportunity to express his gratitude to those who have supported him. The WWE veteran shared a heartfelt message on X, thanking his family, friends, colleagues, and WWE management for their help during these difficult moments.

This message follows the passing of his father, Aaron Killings, on November 8, 2026. Truth emphasized that having people by his side during tough times has been invaluable.

> "There aren’t any words to describe or express how grateful and blessed I am to have people like ALL of you in my life. Friends, family, acquaintances, WWE, work colleagues, work bosses, ALL OF YOU. I never met a stranger, I love people. YOU people have been there for me every time I’ve been knocked down or dealt a hard blow from life. I want to say THANK YOU to each and every one of you from the bottom of my heart and soul🙏🏽❤️ There’s healing power in people."

Truth is also managing health concerns following an injury sustained during his October 16 match against Damian Priest on WWE SmackDown. While he did not specify the exact reason for his latest post, his inclusion of WWE and his bosses highlights how much their support means to him.
