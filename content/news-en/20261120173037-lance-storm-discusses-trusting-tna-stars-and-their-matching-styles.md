Lance Storm places a great deal of confidence in the TNA Wrestling roster. Since rejoining the promotion as a producer in 2022, Storm has helped produce hundreds of bouts, yet he prefers that wrestlers avoid copying his vintage wrestling style.

During a recent appearance on the Battleground podcast, Lance shared a story about working with Trey Miguel and Jason Hotch, where he initially hesitated over a spot due to his age before allowing them to proceed. He recalled:

> "I think it was Trey and Jason Hotch. I've got multiple matches in the day, so you talk and you leave, you talk and you leave and you come back. They had this one sequence or spot where it's like, my 57 year old brain was like, ‘That’s a bit much.’ But, I know it's sort of in their realm. I mentioned it and Trey is like, ‘No, I think I'm good.’ I'm like, ‘Alright bro.’ I trust to let you do it. It was so rewarding because it went the way he wanted it to and it worked, 57 year old brain was wrong."

Storm added that he was mistaken and praised the successful outcome, emphasizing that he trusts the younger talent and collaborates with them as a team. He concluded that he wants every performer to have a bout that reflects their own personality and unique approach rather than forcing them into his mold.
