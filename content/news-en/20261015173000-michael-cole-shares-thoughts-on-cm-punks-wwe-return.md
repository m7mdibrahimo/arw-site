Veteran WWE commentator Michael Cole recently discussed CM Punk's return to the company, noting that he welcomed the star with open arms despite losing touch with him for several years following his initial departure.

Cole mentioned that while relationships in the wrestling industry often fade when someone leaves, he was uncertain about how the locker room or Punk himself would act upon the comeback.

> "Punk and I were real close when he was here the first time around. Obviously, he leaves the company. This business is weird because you can be best friends with someone while they're working here and then they leave and you might not talk to them for a few years. That was my situation with Punk"

He emphasized that holding grudges has no place in professional wrestling and that moving past old disagreements is essential for a long career.

> "You, you can't hold a grudge. If you take anything personal in our business, you won't survive. There's a reason I've been here for 30 years. You have to just let it slide down your back, move on with a smile on your face, and do business"
