TNA Bound For Glory took place on October 11, 2026, at the Yuengling Center in Tampa, Florida. The event featured the TNA Hall of Fame induction ceremony, honoring Amazing Red, Konnan, and ODB. During the ceremony, Amazing Red announced he would be the seventh entrant in the TNA X-Division Championship 6-Way Scramble Match later that night. The opening match of the main card saw Ricky Sosa face Eddie Edwards. The event was broadcast on YouTube and TNA+.
