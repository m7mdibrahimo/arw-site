Josh Bishop is gaining attention for more than just his wrestling prowess. In late 2025, rapper Westside Gunn updated the artwork for his "Heels Have Eyes" albums, incorporating several photos of Bishop. The choice resonated strongly with the wrestling community. In an interview with The Wrestling Classic, Bishop discussed how he handles the situation when peers in the locker room try to tease him about the feature. "It's cool and it’s just funny. It's just cool, it's funny because then of course you hear people try to chirp about you in the locker room because of it," Bishop said. "It's like, okay, what album are you on? What vinyls are you signing? None, because nobody cares about you because you're a jabroni." Bishop is currently preparing for his next major challenge, where he will face Killer Kross for the MLW World Heavyweight Title at MLW SuperFight.
