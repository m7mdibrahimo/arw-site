Lucha Libre AAA presents AAA on FOX Latin America on October 10, 2026, live from Domo de la Feria in Leon, Mexico. The broadcast begins at 8 pm ET in Mexico and 10 pm ET in the US, with fans able to watch on FOX in Latin America or via YouTube in the US. The lineup features an appearance by Pagano and a tag team match pitting Los Americanos against Tokyo Bad Boys. Additionally, a number one contender four-way match features Nobu San, Joaquin Wilde, Jack Cartwheel, and Mini Vikingo, where the winner earns a shot at Rey Fenix for the AAA Cruiserweight Title at Heroes Inmortales.
