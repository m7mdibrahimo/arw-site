Chris Jericho has taken to social media to reveal his dramatic transformation into wrestling legend Gorgeous George for the new Prime Video limited series about Muhammad Ali titled The Greatest.

Jericho shared a photo of himself in character on Instagram, sporting George's iconic blond curls and elaborate style to promote the eight-episode series that is now streaming.

> "Are you ready to get GORGEOUS? #TheGreatest out NOW on @primevideo! #GorgeousGeorge @muhammadali"

The casting of Jericho was first announced in October. Gorgeous George was a pioneer of television wrestling whose flamboyant persona and theatrical entrances helped shape modern sports entertainment and famously inspired a young Muhammad Ali. Jericho makes his first appearance in the role during the show's fourth episode.
