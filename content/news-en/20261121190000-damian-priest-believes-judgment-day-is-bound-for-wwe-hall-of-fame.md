Former The Judgment Day member Damian Priest spoke positively about his former group, asserting that they deserve a spot in the Hall of Fame. Priest explained that the stable managed to prove its strength, persistence, and growth despite the disbandment of many other groups around it, noting that he was the first star to join the faction alongside its founder, Adam Copeland.

Speaking on Complex Graps, Priest revealed how he planned to bring in the rest of the future members, such as Rhea Ripley, Finn Balor, Dominik Mysterio, and JD McDonagh. He pointed to the special period when multiple members held titles at the same time, as he held the Money in the Bank briefcase and the undisputed tag team championship, while Ripley was the women's world champion and Dominik was the North American champion.

Priest concluded by emphasizing that these moments gave them the confidence that they are not only on their way to entering the Hall of Fame as individuals, but also as a unit that will go down as one of the greatest factions in WWE history.
