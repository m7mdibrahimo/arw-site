Shannon Moore recently revealed that he struggled with feelings of guilt after the passing of former WWE Superstar Umaga in 2009. During an appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Moore became emotional as he recalled the period following his friend's death. At the time, Moore was battling his own addiction issues and found it difficult to reconcile why he was still alive while Umaga, a father of children, had passed away.

> "I remember sitting at home in my recliner and just wishing that it would have been me."

Moore shared that he had spent time with Umaga during Hulk Hogan's Hulkamania tour in Australia just before the tragedy. During that tour, Umaga had spoken fondly about his desire to return home to his children and his plans to take them on trips. These memories made the news of Umaga's death even harder for Moore to process.

> "I remember, you know, when I got that phone call, I remember sitting in the living room and just begging going like God like why? Like why him? Like this dude, I don't have kids. Like why couldn't this been me?"

Umaga, real name Edward Fatu, died on December 4, 2009, at age 36 after suffering cardiac problems. A medical examiner later ruled his death accidental, caused by acute toxicity from hydrocodone, carisoprodol, and diazepam. Moore noted that this tragedy occurred during a dark time in his own life, and he struggled for years before finally seeking help for his addiction in 2017 with the support of Road Dogg. He has now been sober for approximately nine years and dedicates his time to helping others.
