WWE star Drew McIntyre has praised Elijah Holyfield following their work together in the spy micro-drama "Agent of Shadows," which was released on ReelShort on October 23. The project also featured fellow WWE talent Jacob Fatu, Booker T, and Joe Hendry.

Speaking on "Insight with Chris Van Vliet," McIntyre discussed working with Holyfield, who joined WWE in 2024 after a football career at the University of Georgia and a four-season stint in the NFL. McIntyre stated, "He looks great. I've honestly never seen him work, like hopefully he can get it done in the ring, but his presence, he's a great, great face. He's got an aura about him. Solid. Built like a brick you-know-what and he was excellent in the stuff we did together, so I see big things in the future for him if he can put together a half decent match."

Holyfield previously competed on the first season of "WWE LFG" as part of The Undertaker's team. While he has yet to make his televised debut on "NXT," he has worked a dark match against Kam Hendrix and performs regularly on live events and "WWE EVOLVE" on Wednesdays.
