President Donald Trump has offered the position of White House Press Secretary to UFC President Dana White. President Trump announced the offer on Saturday, stating, "I am pleased to announce that the Great Dana White, a violent Fighter and Warrior, has been offered the job of Press Secretary of the United States." Trump also requested that White bring assistants, including Islam Makhachev, Khabib Nurmagomedov, Justin Gaethje, Daniel Cormier, Amanda Nunes, and Kayla Harris, to help defend against his political opponents. Dana White and Donald Trump are longtime friends who previously collaborated on a UFC event held at the White House in July. White responded to the news on Instagram by posting, "On to the next chapter." It remains unclear how serious the offer is, as Trump recently nominated Stephen Miller to be his new White House chief of staff.
