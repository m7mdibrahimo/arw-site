Despite Cody Rhodes having a clear plan for a potential heel turn, key officials within TKO are firmly against the idea. According to WrestleVotes Radio, leadership views Rhodes as a quintessential babyface whose current persona is highly profitable due to merchandise sales and lucrative marketing deals. TKO believes the risks of changing his image outweigh any potential creative gains. Rhodes recently discussed his openness to turning heel on The Ebro Laura Rosenberg Show, noting he would offer a brief explanation before ceasing all promos. He added:

> "I would not want to be rooted for. I don’t know what a bad guy is supposed to be anymore. We’re living in a different time."
