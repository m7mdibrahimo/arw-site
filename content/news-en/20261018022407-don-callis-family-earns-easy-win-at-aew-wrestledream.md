The Don Callis Family picked up a quick victory during the AEW WrestleDream Tailgate Brawl. Jake Doyle, Josh Alexander, and Wardlow easily defeated Alex Breeze, Papi D, and Rhys Maddox.

Wardlow laid out all the competitors with a powerbomb before every member of the Family pinned their opponent with a foot on the chest.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C93VvnylRd0

Wardlow and Doyle previously teamed with Mark Davis on AEW Dynamite to defeat Death Riders, featuring Daniel Garcia, Gabe Kidd, and Jon Moxley.
