Darby Allin has shared a new photo from Mont Blanc, signaling a return to mountain climbing. Last year, the AEW star successfully summited Mount Everest, an experience he described as deeply emotional while listening to country music. Now, Allin has posted an image on his Instagram Stories from Chamonix, stating, "Nice to be in the mountains again." Mont Blanc stands as the highest peak in the Alps and Western Europe. Allin's last AEW appearance took place at AEW Grand Slam: France, where he successfully defended his TNT Championship against Kyle Fletcher. Meanwhile, Paul Wight recently expressed interest in working with Allin on AEW television as a heel.
