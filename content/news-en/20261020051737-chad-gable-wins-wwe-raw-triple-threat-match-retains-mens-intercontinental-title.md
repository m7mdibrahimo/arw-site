Chad Gable successfully defended the Men's Intercontinental Championship against Rey Mysterio and Dragon Lee on the October 19, 2026, episode of WWE RAW. The hard-fought triple threat match featured continuous action and shifting momentum, with Lee and Mysterio trading moves and targeting Gable throughout the contest. In the closing moments, Gable managed to capitalize on the chaos, rolling up Mysterio to secure the pinfall victory and retain his title. Following the match, Mysterio celebrated in the ring before both he and Lee were blindsided by attacks from Ethan Page and Big Cass respectively.
