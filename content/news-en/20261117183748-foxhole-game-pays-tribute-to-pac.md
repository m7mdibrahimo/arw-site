The multiplayer online sandbox wargame Foxhole is honoring the late PAC. The developers have introduced a new weapon to the game called the P-A-C "Bastard" Burst Launcher. According to the game's official description, the weapon fires a rapid barrage of three White Ash rockets, serving as a powerful anti-tank tool for the Warden infantry. The weapon's name, "Bastard," was chosen to reflect the dangerous development process required to create the White Ash canisters used in the launcher. PAC, whose real name was Benjamin Satterley, passed away on September 27.
