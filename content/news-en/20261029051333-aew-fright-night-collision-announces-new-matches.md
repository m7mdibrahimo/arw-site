AEW announced new matches for AEW Fright Night Collision. In trios action, The Conglomeration consisting of Roderick Strong, Kyle O'Reilly, and Orange Cassidy will face The Cut, featuring Juice Robinson, Austin Gunn, and Colten Gunn.

In singles competition, Tommaso Ciampa will take on Matt Sydal, and Ilja Dragunov will square off against The Beast Mortos. Additionally, the Artist Of Stardom Championships will be defended as Triangle Of Madness puts the titles on the line against Hazuki, Koguma, and Fuwa-Chan.
