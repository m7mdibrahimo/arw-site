TNA Wrestling faces further travel disruptions ahead of Sunday's Destination X pay-per-view. Samuel Shaw is officially unable to reach Edmonton for the event, forcing changes to the advertised World Tag Team Championship match. While his Syx tag team partner, Joseph Sawyer, arrived safely, Shaw's absence prevents the originally scheduled three-way title match against Matt Hardy, Moose, and champions Nic and Ryan Nemeth from proceeding as planned. The Nemeth Brothers' status remains uncertain, as both were pulled from pre-show meet-and-greets, fueling speculation that they may also miss the event. Earlier, Matt Hardy confirmed his arrival in Edmonton, suggesting his title match plans are in flux. TNA is now working to replace the tag team championship bout entirely. The list of talent impacted by the nationwide FAA flight shutdown on November 13 continues to grow, with nine wrestlers now confirmed out, removed from matches, or expected to miss the show. Indi Hartwell has confirmed she cannot defend her Knockouts World Championship against Shayna Baszler, and TNA has already announced major changes to both Ultimate X matches. Despite these setbacks, TNA officials are reportedly confident that Trey Miguel will reach Edmonton in time to challenge Leon Slater for the TNA World Championship. Destination X is still scheduled to proceed at the Edmonton EXPO Centre.
