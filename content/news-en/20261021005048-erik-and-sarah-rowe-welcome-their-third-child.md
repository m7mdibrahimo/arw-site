Erik and Sarah Rowe have officially welcomed their third child. Months after the WWE star announced they were expecting, Erik revealed that their first daughter arrived on October 20, and both mother and baby are doing well.

> "Dena Elizabeth Rowe Born at 2:13 PM on 10/20/26. 9lbs and 21in long."

Erik shared the news on Instagram, confirming that Dena Elizabeth Rowe weighed nine pounds and measured 21 inches. He added, "Both mama and baby are healthy and safe. Gods gifts are truly endless."

Back in March, the War Raiders member expressed his excitement about becoming a "girl dad" after welcoming two sons, Raymond Cash Rowe in 2021 and Ezekiel Joseph Rowe in 2024. This birth marks a new chapter for Sarah, who has focused on motherhood since her WWE contract was not renewed in 2025.
