Kurt Angle has turned his wild Cameo motivational videos toward a newborn baby, joking that he wants to hit her with an Angle Slam and put her in an Ankle Lock. A fan uploaded a video on TikTok with the caption, "POV: You just had a baby," featuring the WWE Hall of Famer.

Angle started by congratulating the fan on baby Madison, but things quickly escalated. "I wanna congratulate you on baby Madison. That is awesome. I just wanna tell you that your baby lacks the three I's — Intensity, Integrity, and Intelligence," Angle said. He then declared that the baby "sucks" and joked about executing his wrestling finishers on her. "And by the way, the baby sucks. And I just wanna hit it with an Angle Slam and slap an Ankle Lock on her. Oh, it's true. It's damn true."

This follows another recent Cameo where Angle roasted a fan over his cheeseburger habits and reminded him of winning an Olympic gold medal with a broken neck. With these unhinged messages, it seems no fan is safe from the legendary wrestler's brutal humor.
