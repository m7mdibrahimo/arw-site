Christopher Daniels recently discussed tribalism in professional wrestling during an interview with Going Ringside, sharing his perspective that fans should support the entire industry rather than taking sides in the perceived AEW versus WWE rivalry. He acknowledged that while WWE is the oldest promotion in the business, AEW has grown rapidly and consistently delivers great professional wrestling week after week. Daniels also touched upon his thoughts on Samoa Joe during the conversation.
