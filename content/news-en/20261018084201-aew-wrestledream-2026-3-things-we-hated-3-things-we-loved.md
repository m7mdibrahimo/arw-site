AEW WrestleDream 2026 presented a packed night of action in Orlando, featuring title changes, intense battles, and high-profile debuts. Wrestling Inc. evaluated the major highlights and low points of the event, breaking down three aspects that disappointed and three that truly stood out.

3 Things We Hated:

- Enhancement match on a major show: Jake Doyle, Josh Alexander, and Wardlow easily defeated Alex Breeze, Papi D, and Rhys Maddox in under three minutes during the Tailgate Brawl. Such a brief, one-sided match provided little value for Wardlow and took time away from other potential matchups.

- Flawed finish to Chris Jericho vs. Nick Wayne: While Chris Jericho and Nick Wayne delivered a solid match, the ending descended into confusion. Low blows, the legal use of a protective cup as a weapon, and an awkward roll-up finish ruined the momentum.

- Clockwork Carnage match in the main event: The new Clockwork Carnage concept created by Will Ospreay lacked urgency during its timed pinfall segments. Placing an experimental match in the main event spot over the women's world title bout proved to be a misstep.

3 Things We Loved:

- Mercedes Moné and Thekla show out: Mercedes Moné retained her AEW Women's World Championship against Thekla in an engaging opening match that featured great in-ring psychology and storytelling.

- Andrade vs. Tommaso Ciampa comedy and action: Tommaso Ciampa and Andrade delivered a highly entertaining match that combined fast-paced action with hilarious antics, including Ciampa attempting to pin Andrade's removed trousers.

- Jon Moxley vs. Mark Davis physical brawl: Jon Moxley and Mark Davis engaged in a brutal, hard-hitting contest that highlighted Moxley's emotional struggle following recent events, supported by interventions from Marina Shafir and Daniel Garcia.
