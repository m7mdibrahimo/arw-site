Jey Uso has expressed his desire to participate in the upcoming John Cena Classic event. After John Cena detailed the rules for the unique WWE showcase, Jey Uso took to his Instagram Story to publicly demand a spot in the competition and confidently predicted his victory. "Put me in the Cena classic. I'll win that shi."

While Jey Uso has not been officially confirmed for the show, he is already volunteering his services, joining Cody Rhodes as a main-roster star expressing interest. John Cena recently explained to Rick Ucchino of The Takedown on Sports Illustrated that the December 14 event will not feature a traditional tournament bracket. Instead, it will consist of exhibition matches between current main-roster stars and NXT talent. Cena noted that the format is intended to provide a unique experience, adding, "It’s very much like Saturday Night’s Main Event."

The event will crown two champions—one man and one woman—based on votes from Club WWE members. Cena hopes to establish this pilot show as an annual tradition. For now, Jey Uso has made his interest known, even claiming he already knows who should walk away with the men’s championship.
