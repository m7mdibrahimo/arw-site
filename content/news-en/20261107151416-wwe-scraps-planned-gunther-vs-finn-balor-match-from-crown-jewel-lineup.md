WWE Crown Jewel is moving forward without a scheduled match between Gunther and Finn Balor, even though the rivalry had been built up on television. Dave Meltzer reported on Wrestling Observer Radio that the bout was originally included in a five-match lineup, but the company ultimately advertised just four matches for the event. Meltzer noted that WWE's promotional material established Crown Jewel as a four-match show, indicating a behind-the-scenes change. Despite advancing their storyline on RAW, WWE removed the encounter and has not provided an explanation. It remains unclear whether the match will be rescheduled for a future episode of RAW or another event.
