Indi Hartwell is open to reuniting with Samuel Shaw in TNA now that they share a roster once again. The two previously worked together in WWE NXT as part of The Way and even got married on screen. In a recent interview, Hartwell noted that fans still bring up their past storyline and expressed interest in revisiting it since they never got a real conclusion.
