Michael Cole recently discussed his broadcasting future and potential successors on the WWE commentary desk. Speaking on OutKick, Cole named Wade Barrett, The Miz, and Seth Rollins as current stars who could easily transition into analyst roles after their in-ring careers. Cole also reflected on his favorite past broadcast partners, highlighting Tazz, Corey Graves, and Pat McAfee.
