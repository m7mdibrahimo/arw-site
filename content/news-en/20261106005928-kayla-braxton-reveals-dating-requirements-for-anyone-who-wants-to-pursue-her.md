Kayla Braxton has shared the specific requirements for anyone interested in dating her next. The former WWE personality posted a list on her Instagram Story, making it clear that she is no longer willing to teach the basics of dating to adults. Her criteria include matching her energy, zero tolerance for dishonesty, prioritizing actions over promises, and maintaining basic table manners.

Braxton also revealed that she uses playful teasing while developing feelings, and promised absolute loyalty and protection once she is in a relationship. Anyone planning to pursue her has been given clear guidelines, and failing to meet them will rest entirely on the applicant.
