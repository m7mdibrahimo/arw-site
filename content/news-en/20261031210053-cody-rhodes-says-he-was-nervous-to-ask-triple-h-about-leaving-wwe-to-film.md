Cody Rhodes revealed that he was nervous to ask Triple H for permission to leave WWE temporarily to film the Street Fighter movie, in which he played the character Guile. Speaking on the Double Coverage Podcast, Rhodes explained that he was anxious about approaching the head of creative because wrestling is always the number one priority for the company and for Triple H, who is dedicated to pleasing all fans across RAW, SmackDown, NXT, and PLEs. Taking the WWE champion away to shoot a movie was a major request, but Rhodes was pleasantly surprised when Triple H agreed, helped work out the dates, and even encouraged him to return with fresh ideas rather than falling back on traditional tropes. Rhodes praised Triple H, noting that his experience and worldview make him the ideal and only person currently capable of leading WWE.
