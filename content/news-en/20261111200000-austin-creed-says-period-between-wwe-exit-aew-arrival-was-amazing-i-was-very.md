Austin Creed recently reflected on the time between his departure from WWE and his debut in All Elite Wrestling, describing the period as amazing because he was severely burnt out. After spending nearly two decades with WWE alongside Kofi, the former New Day members chose to leave the company and eventually signed with AEW. Creed and Kofi made their debut for AEW as The New Level alongside Swerve Strickland at AEW All In 2026, where they captured the World Trios Titles.

Speaking with Fightful, Creed discussed how taking that time off allowed him to enjoy a proper summer at home for the first time in his 23-year career. He explained that being able to spend quality time with his children and establish a better work-life balance was crucial for him after years of constant wrestling, creating content, and making public appearances. Creed noted that this break helped him figure out his priorities, set boundaries, and approach his career with a healthier mindset. Following their arrival, Creed, Kofi, and Strickland have already successfully defended the AEW World Trios Titles four times.
