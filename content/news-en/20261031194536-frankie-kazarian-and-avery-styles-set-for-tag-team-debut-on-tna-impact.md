Frankie Kazarian and Avery Styles are set to compete as a tag team on TNA iMPACT! TNA Wrestling announced that the duo will make their official tag team debut on the November 5 episode.

Both competitors have maintained an alliance since Styles made his debut for the promotion at Lockdown.

The updated lineup for the show features Harley Hudson's initiation into The Righteous, as well as Eddie Edwards vs. Bear Bronson. If Edwards wins, Bronson must fall in line within The System, but if Bronson wins, he gets to call the shots in the faction.

Additionally, The Nemeth Brothers defense of the TNA World Tag Team Championship against Leon Slater & Amazing Red is set, alongside Fire 'N Flava vs. Shotzi Blackheart & Allie.
