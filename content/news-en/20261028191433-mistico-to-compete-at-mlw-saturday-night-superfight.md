Major League Wrestling has officially announced that Místico will appear at Saturday Night SuperFight. The event is scheduled for Saturday, November 7, live from a sold-out Cicero Stadium in Chicago. The broadcast will begin at 8 p.m. ET and will be available exclusively on VEEPS.

His participation adds significant momentum to the promotion's biggest show of the year, sparking numerous questions regarding the internationally renowned wrestler's plans in Chicago. With many of the sport's top stars in attendance, speculation is growing about whether he will issue a new challenge or deliver a powerful message to the wrestling world.

The event features championship bouts, intense rivalries, and major debuts. Místico will have a global stage to showcase his spectacular aerial offense before the entire MLW locker room.
