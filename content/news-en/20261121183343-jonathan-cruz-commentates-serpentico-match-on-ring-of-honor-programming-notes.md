During a recent episode of Ring Of Honor programming, professional wrestler Jonathan Cruz served as a commentator for a match involving Serpentico. Cruz, who has competed under both his own ring name and as the masked Serpentico without capturing many victories in AEW and Ring Of Honor, expressed his strong dislike for masked wrestlers while on commentary during tapings from the 2026 Jericho Cruise. 

> "You know, if there's one thing I can't stand, it's masked wrestling, and this is the epitome of a masked wrestler. I do not… Oh, I'm sorry, I'm sorry, I'm sorry, I'm sorry. (To Excalibur) I mean, I didn't mean that. Currently active masked wrestlers."
