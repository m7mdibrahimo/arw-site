NWA aired a new episode of NWA Powerrr on October 24 on Comet TV. The show opened with King Trevor the First and his Royal Court, who demanded applause. Kerry Morton also spoke about his upcoming bout against Colby Corino.

In the opening contest, The Royal Court, consisting of Sir Michael, Duke of Knox, and Squire Tyler Franks, defeated Christiano Argento and The Man They Call Frank. Next, Daisy Kill, accompanied by Big Cuzzo, defeated Charlie Adams, who was accompanied by Eric Smalls. In the following match, Blunt Force Trauma, featuring Carnage and Damage, defeated the team of Jack n’ Eddie, comprised of Jack Bálor and Eddie Grayson, before being attacked by The Slimeballz.

In the main event, Kerry Morton defeated Colby Corino.
