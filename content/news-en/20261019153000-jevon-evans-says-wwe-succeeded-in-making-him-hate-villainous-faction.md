During a recent appearance on Stephanie McMahon's podcast, current WWE star Je'Von Evans discussed his childhood reaction to Seth Rollins turning on his Shield stablemates, Roman Reigns and Dean Ambrose, to join The Authority. Evans recalled how much he hated the villainous faction, particularly after the shocking betrayal. McMahon explained the reasoning behind aligning Rollins with the group, noting that it allowed them to maximize his individual potential within the storyline.
