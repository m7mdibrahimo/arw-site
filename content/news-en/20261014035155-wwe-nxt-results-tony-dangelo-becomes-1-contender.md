The October 13, 2026, episode of WWE NXT aired live from the Capitol Wrestling Center in Orlando, Florida. The show featured a high-stakes main event to determine the next challenger for the WWE NXT Championship, alongside opening round action in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic tournament.

In the tournament quarterfinal matches, The Vanity Project (Ricky Smokes and Brad Baylor) defeated Mini Vikingo and Chazz “Starboy” Hall, while Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno secured a victory over Fraxiom (Nathan Frazer and Axiom). In singles competition, Tristan Angels defeated Saquon Shugars, and La Catalina successfully defended her AAA Reina de Reinas Championship against Kali Armstrong.

Following a dispute regarding the recent Women's Championship #1 Contender’s Battle Royal, NXT General Manager Robert Stone officially confirmed that Wren Sinclair will challenge for the title at Halloween Havoc. The main event saw Tony D’Angelo defeat Myles Borne to become the new #1 Contender for the WWE NXT Championship. Following the match, Tavion Heights made a surprise appearance, attacking Borne and putting him through a table to close the show.
