World Series Wrestling held its WSW MegaMania Adelaide event on October 31, 2026, at the Adelaide Entertainment Centre in Hindmarsh, South Australia. The event was streamed on YouTube. In the main event, Killer Kross, accompanied by Scarlett Bordeaux, defeated Tetsuya Naito to retain the WSW World Championship in an Anything Goes match. In other championship action, Brian Cage successfully defended the WSW Australian Championship against BUSHI. The Good Brothers, Karl Anderson and Doc Gallows, defeated United Empire's Andrade El Idolo and HENARE to win the WSW Tag Team Championships. Additionally, Myla Grace retained the WSW Women's Championship against Cassie Lee. Other matches on the card included Matt Riddle defeating Ricky Sosa, Zoey Serrano defeating Shayna Baszler, Sammy Guevara defeating Matt Hayter, and Moose defeating Dean Brady and Ryusei.
