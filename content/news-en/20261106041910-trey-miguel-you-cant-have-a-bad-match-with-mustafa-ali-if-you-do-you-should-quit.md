Trey Miguel praised Mustafa Ali during a recent interview on Busted Open, stating that anyone who has a bad match with Ali should quit. Miguel recalled their TNA Rebellion 2026 match as one of his all-time favorites, noting that Ali constantly checked on him after he broke his kneecap. Miguel highlighted the hours of detailed preparation Ali puts into every match, from facial expressions to camera angles and psychological pacing. Miguel is scheduled to challenge Leon Slater for the TNA World Championship at Destination X on November 15th.
