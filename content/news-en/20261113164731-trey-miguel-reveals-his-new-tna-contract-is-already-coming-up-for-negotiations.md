Trey Miguel recently re-signed with TNA Wrestling, but he is already preparing to enter contract negotiations once again. The former X-Division Champion revealed that his latest deal was only for six months. After signing two short-term contracts in 2026, he hopes TNA will offer him a longer agreement. During an interview with Tom Campbell of Cultaholic Wrestling, Miguel explained that he signed a six-month deal in January before agreeing to another six-month extension in June. This means he will return to the negotiating table before the end of 2026, though he made it clear he has no desire to leave TNA. Miguel admitted that his contract situation became particularly stressful when he broke his knee earlier this year, sidelining him for about four months while his previous deal was nearing its end. He feared TNA might decide against bringing him back, but the company instead reached out with a new contract while he was still recovering. Miguel suffered a fractured patella during his match against Mustafa Ali at TNA Rebellion in April and returned to the ring in August. Having signed five or six short-term TNA deals at this point, he is hoping his next contract will provide three years of stability.
