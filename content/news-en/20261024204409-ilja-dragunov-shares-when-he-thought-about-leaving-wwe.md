Ilja Dragunov recently discussed the timeline of when he first considered leaving WWE before his contract expired in August. Dragunov noted that after returning from a torn ACL and quickly winning a championship, things shifted creatively. Around April, or perhaps a month prior, he began thinking about his future. He emphasized that this never affected his work ethic because he loves what he does. Dragunov subsequently finished his time with WWE and made his AEW debut at AEW WrestleDream, later defeating "Speedball" Mike Bailey on AEW Dynamite.
