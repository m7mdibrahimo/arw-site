Karl Fredericks recently discussed his upcoming involvement in co-producing PRODUCE Volume 5, scheduled for Monday, October 12. In a new interview, Fredericks explained that he was initially hesitant and nervous when first approached with the idea, as he was focused on restarting his career following his time in NXT as Eddy Thorpe. However, after learning more about the collaborative nature of the events and seeing that others like Rocky and the War Dogs were involved, his concerns were quickly put to ease. The card will feature notable names such as Nyla Rose, Janai Kai, Jonathan Gresham, Lio Rush, Lee Moriarty, The Blade, Fred Rosser, and EFFY, alongside younger indie and indigenous talent like Pat Sawyer. Fredericks emphasized that he wanted to avoid making the show feel like a one-night-only themed event, noting that the creative heavy lifting was handled for him while he focused on curating talent, including Dante Chen and Javi. Embracing the pressure of being a face of the event on Indigenous People's Day, Fredericks expressed excitement for the high stakes and the opportunity to wrestle and host an after-party.
