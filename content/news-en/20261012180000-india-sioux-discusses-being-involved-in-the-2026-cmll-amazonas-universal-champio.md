India Sioux recently spoke about her involvement in the 2026 CMLL Amazonas Universal Championship tournament, noting that she initially thought she would not compete because she is the reigning champion. "At one point, I thought I wouldn’t have this opportunity anymore precisely because I currently hold the championship title, but now that I know this, I know they’re going to give me the chance to be there and try to become a two-time champion," Sioux said. She highlighted the unique challenge of facing fellow tecnicas who know each other's strengths so well. Meanwhile, on the October 9 edition of CMLL Viernes Espectacular, Skadi and Olympia were confirmed for the tournament finals.
