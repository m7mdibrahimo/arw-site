WWE Raw aired on November 16, 2026, from the Lenovo Center in Raleigh, North Carolina. The show featured an appearance by John Cena and two WarGames advantage matches.

In the first hour, CM Punk and Cody Rhodes announced the fourth member of Team USA for WarGames. The Miz attempted to lobby for the spot but was replaced by LA Knight. LA Knight then defeated The Miz in a singles match. John Cena announced that Liv Morgan was entering the John Cena Classic, leading to a confrontation with Becky Lynch, who vowed to take the title from Morgan. 946 previewed their upcoming match against The Usos.

In the second hour, Stephanie Vaquer and Jacy Jayne fought to a No-Contest after Fatal Influence attacked both women. The Usos, Solo Sikoa, and Jacob Fatu expressed frustration to Adam Pearce backstage. Rey Mysterio announced that Dragon Lee would join Team Mexico at Survivor Series. Je'Von Evans thanked Oba Femi for his recent help, but their conversation was interrupted by JD McDonagh and Dominik Mysterio, who insulted LA Knight.

In the Women's Intercontinental Championship match, IYO SKY defeated Raquel Rodriguez to become the new champion. In the third hour, Ethan Page confronted Rey Fenix backstage. In the main event, 946 (Carmelo Hayes and Cruz Montana) defeated The Usos (Jey Uso and Jimmy Uso) in a WarGames advantage match.
