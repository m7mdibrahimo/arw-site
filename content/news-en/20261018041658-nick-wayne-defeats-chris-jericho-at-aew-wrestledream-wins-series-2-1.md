Nick Wayne secured a victory over Chris Jericho at AEW WrestleDream. This marked the third singles match between the two stars, following their initial encounter on the September 16 episode of AEW Dynamite, which Jericho won, and Wayne's equalizer at AEW Grand Slam France.

The rubber match took place at the Orlando event. During the final moments, a referee bump occurred, leading Wayne to land a low blow on Jericho. However, Jericho was prepared with a groin protector. Jericho then attempted a low blow of his own followed by a Judas Effect, but he missed. Wayne capitalized on the opening by rolling up Jericho to win the match and the series 2-1.
