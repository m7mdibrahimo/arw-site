Ric Flair shared an update with fans following his recent hospitalization after a fall in Florida. The WWE Hall of Famer took to social media to express gratitude to the medical staff at Tampa General Hospital and reassure everyone about his condition. He stated that he is currently focusing entirely on his recovery and confirmed he will be okay. Flair also clarified that he no longer owns his personal Facebook page and apologized for any inappropriate posting activity that occurred while he was receiving medical care.
