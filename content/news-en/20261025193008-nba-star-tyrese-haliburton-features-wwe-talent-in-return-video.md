Indiana Pacers guard Tyrese Haliburton is preparing for his return to the court this Wednesday, according to Wrestling Inc. He published a video across his social media pages documenting his recovery journey after tearing his right Achilles tendon during Game 7 of the NBA Finals.

https://x.com/Hali/status/2113702908479070388

The footage included clips of WWE stars Charlotte Flair, Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, and Seth Rollins, highlighting their paths to overcome severe injuries and return to the ring. The clip concluded with WWE Chief Content Officer Paul Triple H Levesque delivering his famous catchphrase.

Haliburton is a noted wrestling fan who has appeared on multiple WWE shows in the past. He and the Pacers will play against the New Orleans Pelicans at the Smoothie King Center this Wednesday.
