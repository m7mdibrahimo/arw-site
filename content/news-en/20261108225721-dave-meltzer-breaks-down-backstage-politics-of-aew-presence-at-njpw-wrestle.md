As NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 21 approaches at the Tokyo Dome on January 4, a number of top AEW stars are scheduled to appear, prompting Dave Meltzer to analyze the backstage politics in the latest Wrestling Observer Newsletter. The card features Kenny Omega challenging Yota Tsuji for the IWGP Heavyweight Championship, Gabe Kidd defending the IWGP Global Heavyweight Championship against Yuya Uemura, and a three-way match for the IWGP Junior Heavyweight Tag Team Championships involving Mistico and a mystery partner against Taiji Ishimori and Robbie X, as well as Kosei Fujita and Robbie Eagles. In non-title action, Jon Moxley will face Shingo Takagi, MJF will take on Ryohei Oiwa, and current AEW World Champion Will Ospreay will square off against Callum Newman.

Meltzer suggests that to keep all parties satisfied, each company should secure three victories. He envisions Uemura defeating Kidd and Mistico's team losing, while MJF should beat Oiwa and Ospreay must defeat Newman to protect the AEW World Championship picture. The remaining bouts between Moxley and Takagi, and the main event between Omega and Tsuji, present strategic choices for NJPW's business and storytelling. A Tsuji win over a top foreign star like Omega, on network television in Japan, would cement him as the country's top domestic star. An Omega win, on the other hand, could give NJPW a business boost for early 2027 and would repeat the story NJPW told with Hiroshi Tanahashi eighteen years ago, when he lost to Keiji Mutoh before winning the rematch and becoming the company's top babyface. Meltzer also notes that NJPW aims to surpass 25,000 attendees this year, bolstered by the addition of AEW talent, though potential appearances by Adam Copeland or Mercedes Moné were not finalized ahead of the recent Power Struggle event.
