Rob Van Dam has addressed criticism over his comments on Jon Moxley’s weapon use in AEW, defending his stance and arguing that AEW's creative freedom can make wrestling look amateurish. Speaking on Rip Rogers' podcast, the ECW legend explained that his disapproval of weapon-heavy wrestling is consistent, noting that he always considered that style of wrestling to be 'stupid,' even during his famous ECW days. RVD also dismissed comparisons to his own past angles in TNA, such as being attacked with a weapon by Abyss, explaining that he did not pitch the idea and was simply fulfilling his contract. Furthermore, RVD criticized AEW for giving wrestlers too much creative control, comparing the overall style to backyard wrestling compared to WWE's story-driven approach.
