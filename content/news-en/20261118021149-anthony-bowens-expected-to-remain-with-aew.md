Anthony Bowens may not be leaving AEW, as reports indicate the promotion wants to keep him on the roster. Although the former AEW World Tag Team Champion has not confirmed a new deal since his contract expired at the beginning of October, recent updates suggest he has either already signed or is close to finalizing a new agreement.

According to reports on November 17, 2026, sources within AEW expect Bowens to stay with the company rather than seek opportunities elsewhere. Unlike his former The Acclaimed partner Max Caster, who was removed from the official AEW roster page after his contract ended, Bowens remains listed on the website.

There was internal confidence regarding Bowens' return, though negotiations were not a top priority at the time due to the company mourning the deaths of PAC and The Butcher. Bowens addressed his future in an interview last October:

> "I appreciate everybody’s support throughout all of this and I got a lot of ‘stay,’ I got a lot of different things, and I appreciate people’s feedback but I know deep down what I want to do and we’ll see if it works out."

While there is no word on when he might return to television, Bowens appears set to continue his career with Tony Khan's promotion.
