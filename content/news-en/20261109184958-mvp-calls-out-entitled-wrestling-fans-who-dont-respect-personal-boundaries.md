MVP has no problem taking a photo with fans, but some people need a reminder that buying a wrestling ticket does not mean they bought access to him as well. The former WWE star explained that basic manners go a long way and that fans who fail to respect personal boundaries should not expect much from him. Speaking on a recent podcast, MVP called out individuals who act as though supporting a celebrity grants them ownership over that person, pushing back against the familiar argument that buying a ticket equates to paying a salary. He stated that purchasing a ticket simply grants entry to the show and nothing more.

Despite this stance, MVP noted that he has never turned down a fan who politely asks for a photo in public, emphasizing the importance of basic manners like saying please and thank you. He added that he does not agree with the notion that celebrities should automatically be grateful whenever someone demands a picture. Ultimately, his rule is simple: approach with respect, ask nicely, and he will gladly take the photo, whereas acting entitled to his time will yield a very different response.
