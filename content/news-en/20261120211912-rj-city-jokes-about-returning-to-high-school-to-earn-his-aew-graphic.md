It appears that RJ City will never receive his desired AEW graphic. Since joining the promotion in 2023, City has been pursuing the graphic typically reserved for talent, but the company recently provided him with a humorous website bio instead.

During a new interview, RJ City joked that he plans to return to high school to earn a degree so he can finally secure his graphic. According to Fightful, he stated:

> "I'm working on various idiotic things and I still don't have an All Elite graphic, which is fine, I'm not worried about it. This is what I'm going to high school for, he said if I get my degree, I can [have it]."

It is worth noting that attending high school to earn a degree is unusual, highlighting the satirical nature of his comments.
