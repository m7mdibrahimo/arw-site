WWE is preparing a major television comeback that could significantly impact the women's WarGames match at Survivor Series. Stephanie Vaquer is anticipated to reappear on programming, with arrangements pointing toward her alignment with the fan-favorite squad for the November 28 premium live event.

Based on the preliminary schedule for the November 13, 2026 episode of WWE SmackDown, Vaquer is slated to appear during a segment concerning the women's WarGames bout. This development is arranged to take place after Charlotte Flair wrestles Jacy Jayne, with Vaquer coming in on the babyface side. This appearance represents Vaquer's initial television showing since being removed from the October 26 installment of WWE Raw, where her match against Becky Lynch was canceled because she lacked medical clearance.

According to Ringside News, her absence stemmed from an illness instead of an injury, leaving her television status uncertain at the time without a definite recovery timeline. Positive developments occurred subsequently when Vaquer stepped back into the ring at a WWE live event in Portland, Oregon, on November 5, though she has not yet appeared on television. Meanwhile, the promotion continues to develop the storyline for the women's WarGames encounter, featuring Charlotte Flair, Bayley, Alexa Bliss, and Tatum Paxley amid tensions involving Jade Cargill and Fatal Influence. Survivor Series: WarGames is scheduled for November 28 at Daikin Park in Houston, Texas.
