Grayson Waller has initiated personal attacks against Tony D’Angelo ahead of his upcoming NXT Championship defense. With the Halloween Havoc event scheduled for October 31, the champion took aim at his challenger during a recent interview with Tyler Walsh.

Waller expressed confidence in his current championship run and dismissed the idea that D’Angelo could end his reign. He then resorted to insults regarding his opponent's weight and Italian heritage.

> "I'm the king of NXT, and I'm gonna be the champion for a long time coming, lads. As everyone knows, October 31st — unfortunately, I don't get to celebrate Halloween properly, but that's probably a good thing — I'm taking on Tony D'Angelo. He would actually fit in well here in New York. He's a fat Italian who's about to get drowned when Grayson Waller defends the NXT Championship against him."

Continuing his taunts, Waller mocked D’Angelo’s diet and suggested he enjoy his final days as a contender before the title match.

> "Keep enjoying the cannolis, keep enjoying the pastrami, the salami, the pasta — all those things, because I'm gonna be enjoying the victory October 31st at Halloween Havoc."

This rivalry follows a recent Triple Threat Match on the September 15 episode of NXT, where Mason Rook defeated Tony D’Angelo and Saquon Shugars to earn a future title opportunity. Despite having another challenger waiting, Waller remains focused on his upcoming bout at Halloween Havoc.
