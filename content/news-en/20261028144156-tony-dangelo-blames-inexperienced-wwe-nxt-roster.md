Tony D'Angelo discussed his time as WWE NXT Champion during an interview with Denise Salcedo, explaining why he believes his title reign did not receive adequate recognition. The former champion noted that the promotion of several NXT stars to the main roster left the brand in a difficult position, forcing him to lead the show while most of the roster was still learning.

> "I didn't have a whole lot to work with, so to speak. A lot of people with inexperience, which is not their fault. They, as in the main roster, took a bunch of people, and those were all the people that had experience and had been in main events and long matches and knew how, you know, their promos were tip-top."

He clarified that he did not blame the young wrestlers for their lack of experience, but rather felt that the timing of his championship run placed him in a complex situation that other titleholders did not experience.

> "I think had I been champion in a different era, I think people would talk differently about it for sure, but I'm not offended. It is what it is."

Despite those difficulties, D'Angelo believes he proved his ability to lead the brand through a challenging transition phase, recalling a conversation with Shawn Michaels acknowledging that NXT had lost many prominent stars at the time.

> "I think I still showed that I can be champion and carry a brand, and it was a difficult time because, like Shawn [Michaels] said, everybody left. So I held it together when we were in this little transition period. And I'm okay with that. I really am."
