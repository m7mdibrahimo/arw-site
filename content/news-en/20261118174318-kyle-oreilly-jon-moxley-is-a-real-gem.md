Kyle O'Reilly recently praised Jon Moxley during an appearance on What Happened When with Tony Schiavone and Conrad Thompson. O'Reilly highlighted Moxley's role as a locker room leader, stating that he leads by example, works hard, and is always willing to offer advice. He described Moxley as a real gem and expressed gratitude for having him in AEW. The two have faced each other in several memorable battles and recently teamed up at AEW Full Gear in Anarchy in the Arena.
