Chelsea Green has achieved notable success in WWE by holding multiple titles, yet she admits that proving herself in the business remains a constant challenge. Appearing on the Good Karma Wrestling podcast, Green reflected on her ongoing struggles with confidence and explained how professional wrestling has kept her humble despite her achievements.

Green noted that while she has always felt confident in other sports and daily life, wrestling presents a completely different environment. She acknowledged the immense talent across the women's division, noting that being surrounded by so many skilled performers often leads her to question her own abilities. She also highlighted the physical demands of the ring and the reactions of the fans as constant reminders of how difficult the industry truly is.

Addressing how she views her place in the company, Green rejected the idea of acting overly confident or pretending to be the best performer in WWE. She explained that she views herself as an ordinary person working an extraordinary job, fighting every week on WWE SmackDown to maintain her spot. Her comments follow recent setbacks, including losing the WWE Women's Championship after a betrayal by Tiffany Stratton, which she admitted impacted her confidence as she works to rebuild her position.
