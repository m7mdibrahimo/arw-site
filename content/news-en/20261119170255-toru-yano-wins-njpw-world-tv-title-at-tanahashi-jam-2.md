Toru Yano added another championship to his career after capturing the NJPW WORLD TV Championship at NJPW Tanahashi Jam 2 on November 19 by defeating Shun Skywalker. The conclusion of the match featured Yano outsmarting his opponent by delivering a low blow before shoving him hard into the exposed corner and rolling him up for the victory.

As is typical for House of Torture matches, the encounter saw outside interference, but Yano received backup and support from NJPW President Hiroshi Tanahashi. Following the end of the match, Yano was laid out by Yoshinobu Kanemaru.

This marks the first time Yano has won the NJPW WORLD TV Championship, while Skywalker's reign with the title ended at 53 days with zero successful title defenses.
