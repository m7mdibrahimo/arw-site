Former WWE star CJ Perry celebrated Halloween this year by dressing up as Catwoman and sharing the look with her fans. She posted an Instagram video showcasing her outfit, which featured a dark latex-style top paired with matching high-waisted bottoms, lengthy gloves, laced boots reaching the thigh, and the iconic mask associated with the character.

The footage displayed Perry both wearing the mask and without it, inviting her followers to select their preferred version. She wrote in her post:

> "Mama Catwoman has entered the chat 🐈‍⬛🖤 Mask on or mask off? You decide… 😼🎃 Happy Halloween! Use code FALL26 for 50% off—link in bio! 🕸️"

Known for her creative style outside the ring, Perry fully embraced the character to engage with her audience.
