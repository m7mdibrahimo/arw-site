As the Bloodline and the 946 storyline continues on WWE programming, anticipation is growing for the return of Naomi to support her husband, Jimmy Uso, and her family. During a recent episode of Busted Open Radio, WWE Hall of Famer Bully Ray shared his thoughts on Naomi's potential comeback, noting that she should not immediately return to the women's division while her family is facing ongoing threats. Instead, he believes her return needs to be a dramatic moment that directly impacts the ongoing feud with Jaida Parker.
