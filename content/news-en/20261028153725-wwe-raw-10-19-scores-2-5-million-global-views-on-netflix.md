Tudum reports that the October 19 episode of WWE Raw drew 2.5 million global views on Netflix, ranking ninth globally while missing the top ten in the United States. For comparison, the previous week's episode on October 12 drew 2.4 million global views, placing tenth globally and eighth in the United States. Meanwhile, Tudum also highlighted the top five most-viewed WWE Raw videos on YouTube. Roman Reigns and The Bloodline bickered and brawled with The 946, drawing 3.7 million views. Roman Reigns and Sami Zayn engaged in a fiery war of words, generating 1.3 million views. Penta defeated Bron Breakker with assistance from Je’Von Evans, pulling in 567,000 views. Chelsea Green and IYO SKY faced Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez in a tag team match that reached 380,000 views. Finally, Ethan Page and Big Cass launched an ambush on Chad Gable, Rey Mysterio, and Dragon Lee, garnering 361,000 views.
