Danhausen explained that he trademarked his face paint to protect himself. He previously told Chris Van Vliet on Insight that he decided to get the trademark after seeing a Magic: The Gathering card that looked similar to his face paint. On social media, Danhausen provided more details about his decision, writing, "If you’re mad about this, you’re stupid. This isn’t so Danhausen can Willy nilly try to sue people. It’s literally so no one can yell at Danhausen." Since joining WWE in February, the company has filed for several Danhausen-related trademarks.
