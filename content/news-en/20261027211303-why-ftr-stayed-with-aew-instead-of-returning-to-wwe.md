Cash Wheeler recently discussed why FTR decided to stay with AEW rather than returning to WWE. Speaking on Going Ringside, Wheeler explained that AEW offers unique opportunities, including the best tag team division in wrestling and the excitement of helping build a growing company. He noted that AEW provides wrestlers with crucial leverage and job opportunities throughout the industry, while also allowing FTR the freedom to compete in promotions like New Japan Pro-Wrestling and matches in Mexico. For Wheeler, this combination of great competition and career freedom makes staying with AEW an easy decision.
