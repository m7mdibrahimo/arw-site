Cody Rhodes recently emphasized that his appearance at WrestleMania 27 against Rey Mysterio served as a major turning point in his career, providing him with legitimacy on the main roster. During a recent interview on "Rowdy Ryder Reviews," he expressed his deep gratitude toward the veterans who supported his journey.

> "It's such a significant moment, and it meant a lot to me. WrestleMania [27] changed my life, and Rey making the decision really to have me at WrestleMania with him, changed my life."

Rhodes also reflected on his time at SmackDown, noting that he enjoyed significant creative freedom to grow, which he credited to Michael P. Hayes. He felt the pressure was lower than on RAW, allowing him to excel. This era included his masked character, which he adopted after Mysterio legitimately broke his nose.
