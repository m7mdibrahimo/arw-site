Alternative footage of the Montez Ford interaction at the center of a lawsuit against WWE has surfaced online, highlighting the security guard's direct reaction. Noel Lucas can be seen sitting still as Ford dances in front of him during The Street Profits' entrance at the July 6 episode of WWE RAW in Rosemont, Illinois. Lucas, an off-duty Cook County Sheriff's officer, is suing WWE, Ford, and Event Services, LLC. The new video offers a clearer view of the interaction and captures Lucas looking visibly upset and shaking his head after Ford moves away. The lawsuit accuses Ford of performing an unconsented sexualized lap dance, bringing 14 counts including assault and battery against the defendants.
