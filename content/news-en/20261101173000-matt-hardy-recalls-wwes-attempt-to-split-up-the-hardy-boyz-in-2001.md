Matt Hardy recently recalled WWE's attempt to break up The Hardy Boyz in 2001, an angle that also involved Lita. Speaking on "The Stunner," Matt Hardy revealed that the split was short-lived because Jeff Hardy was uncomfortable with the direction WWE wanted to take, leading the company to drop the storyline and punish them by keeping them off television for a while. "There was a storyline for our characters, and they wanted us to split up," Hardy said. "And there was a couple things that we were asked to do, and Jeff was uncomfortable and didn't want to do it. So we were off TV for awhile, as a punishment I guess. But we still did live events. And then they put us back together because we knew we weren't ready to separate and split up as a team yet." Hardy confirmed he knew what creative ideas his brother disliked, though he did not elaborate further, strongly hinting it involved Lita. Following the aborted angle, The Hardys continued to team for several more months before officially splitting up in the summer of 2002 after Matt turned on Jeff.
