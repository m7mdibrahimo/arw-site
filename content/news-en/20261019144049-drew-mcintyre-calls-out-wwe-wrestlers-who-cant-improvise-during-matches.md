Drew McIntyre has voiced his frustration with WWE wrestlers who rely entirely on pre-planned, choreographed matches instead of improvising in the ring. Speaking on Raw Talk, McIntyre explained that depending on a strict sequence is a disaster waiting to happen when crowds react differently than expected, noting that some talent freeze up and panic when things go off script. He also pointed out that WWE's reduced non-televised live event schedule leaves younger wrestlers with fewer opportunities to gain real ring experience, though he believes television performers should already meet a high standard. While clarifying he was joking about getting physically violent with anyone who makes him look bad, McIntyre made it clear that wrestlers on WWE TV must know how to adapt when a match is not working.
