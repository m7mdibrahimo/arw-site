Tag team Hyperactive, consisting of Safire Reed and Anita Vaughan, recently discussed their experiences wrestling in Japan since signing with Sendai Girls. After both received WWE tryouts, the duo signed full-time contracts with the Japanese promotion earlier this year. Speaking to Fightful Overbooked's In The Weeds, Vaughan explained how the Japanese wrestling style challenges performers in ways they have not experienced elsewhere. Reed added that training and living in Japan full-time has forced them to grow as professionals, noting the immense dedication required by the local culture. Vaughan described their daily routine, which includes intense training at the dojo and traveling across the country for events in front of large crowds. The team also addressed the postponement of their match against The Brawling Birds during the same interview.
