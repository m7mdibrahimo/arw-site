WWE is reportedly planning to continue its collaboration with Danhausen throughout the Christmas season following his successful marketing performance during Halloween. According to WrestleVotes Radio on Fightful Select, the company intends to rebrand the star as "Santahausen" for the coming months. The decision follows data showing that Danhausen was a major draw during the Halloween period, leading WWE to prepare a wide range of holiday-themed merchandise. The planned lineup for the Santahausen brand is extensive, with sources noting that WWE has high hopes for the project. Fans can expect various products including hoodies, T-shirts, magnets, and puppets. This follows Danhausen's significant involvement in WWE programming last month, which included his own YouTube special and appearances surrounding WWE SmackDown and NXT Halloween Havoc. WWE appears to have found a unique niche for the performer, allowing him to be repackaged for different holidays without requiring complex storytelling explanations.
