Drew McIntyre recently revealed that Triple H came up with his famous 'I prayed for this' line following CM Punk's injury at the 2024 Royal Rumble. Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, McIntyre explained that his own initial idea would have offended people, but Triple H toned it down and delivered the cadence. McIntyre added that their 2024 feud was voted the best of the 2020s, culminating inside Hell in a Cell at WWE Bad Blood.
