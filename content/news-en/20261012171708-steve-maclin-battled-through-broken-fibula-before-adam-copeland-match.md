Steve Maclin faced Adam Copeland in the main event of MLP Northern Rising on October 3, fulfilling a childhood dream of wrestling the star he once pretended to be using a WWE action figure.

According to Fightful, Maclin suffered a broken fibula ten weeks prior during a match against Isaiah Broner at NCG Drive To Glory when a choke backbreaker went wrong. Paramedics checked on him after the bout was stopped, and while he initially believed he had only rolled or fractured his ankle, medical evaluation revealed the broken bone.

He spent ten weeks in rehabilitation to prepare for the bout against Copeland, who ultimately emerged victorious in a match featuring interference from Rhino and Deonna Purrazzo.
