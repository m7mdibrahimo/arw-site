Fallon Henley credits wrestling for saving her life during a time when she felt lost. Before making her in-ring debut in 2017, Henley attended the University of South Florida, where she earned a bachelor's degree in Criminology and a certificate in Business. During her time in school, she was unsure of her future until she discovered professional wrestling. In a recent interview with the Toronto Sun, Henley explained her journey.

> "This is something that kind of sets me apart a little bit, I wasn't a day one kid coming from the indies like a lot of people. I found it while I was in college. I was working on my degrees, I had no clue what I wanted to do. I ended up graduating, but I was working on criminology and a business degree at USF. I was just kind of like, I don't know, I'm just kind of doing this because I'm supposed to be doing this. I don't really want to go into these fields but I didn’t know what else to do."

Henley discovered the sport through the reality series Total Divas. She had little prior knowledge of wrestling but was captivated by the athletes she saw on SmackDown and Raw. Seeing the female stars as beautiful, athletic, and tough inspired her to pursue the career. She began making connections and eventually found her way into the industry. Reflecting on that period, Henley said the path changed her life.

> "In a way, I love to say it kind of saved my life because I was just like a lost puppy dog in college not knowing what to do. That was kind of the first thing once I got into my first schools and everything, it just projected me in."
