Rob Van Dam believes modern wrestling has lost some of its old-school boundaries, particularly when it comes to backstage etiquette. Speaking on his 1 Of A Kind podcast, RVD explained that the line separating the backstage area from the fans has become far too blurry.

He specifically criticized the practice of allowing friends, family members, and various guests into the locker room while wrestlers are changing or reviewing their matches. While RVD noted he would change this if he had the power, he admitted he does not mind younger wrestlers asking him for photos backstage. Ultimately, he feels wrestlers deserve a private space away from large crowds of visitors.
