The November 6, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown took place at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn, New York, featuring intense action ahead of WWE Crown Jewel.

In singles competition, Shinsuke Nakamura, accompanied by Kyoki, faced Tama Tonga, who was accompanied by Talla Tonga and Haku. Nakamura secured the victory.

Michin and B-Fab defended their WWE Women's Tag Team Championship against Fatal Influence's Lainey Reid and Fallon Henley, who were accompanied by Jacy Jayne. The match ended in a no contest following an unexpected interference by Tatum Paxley and Alexa Bliss.

Damian Priest squared off against Nathan Frazer in a match that concluded via referee stoppage.

The main event featured a violent No Holds Barred Match between Kevin Owens and CM Punk. The contest saw both men battle inside and outside the ring, with Owens ultimately pinning Punk to win.
