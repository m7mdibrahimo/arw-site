John Cena revealed in a new interview that he still owns the first car he ever bought with his own money, a 1989 Jeep Wrangler. Cena explained that he purchased the vehicle at Santa Monica Motors and traded in his old Lincoln Continental. He noted that the Jeep now has 185,000 miles on it, and while the exterior still shows its original sun patina and rust, the underside is fully restored and brand new. Cena also discussed the parallels between stand-up comedy and professional wrestling during the conversation.
