Wrestling podcaster Conrad Thompson recently shared his perspective on why he considers WWE star Roman Reigns to be overrated. Appearing on "Busted Open Radio" this Wednesday, Thompson noted that his opinion was sparked by comparisons suggesting Reigns had surpassed legends like Hulk Hogan due to his WrestleMania main event appearances. While acknowledging Reigns' financial success, Thompson argued that it does not equate to being a superior performer.

> "I'm thrilled for his success. But I don't think he belongs in the conversation. I've not had a conversation with one wrestling fan ever where we have our dreaded Mt. Rushmore conversation, where they start with Roman Reigns."

Thompson also highlighted that Reigns struggled to connect with fans for years until he aligned with Paul Heyman, The Usos, and Sami Zayn. He believes this reliance on others for success differentiates Reigns from icons like Hogan, Steve Austin, and Ric Flair. Thompson concluded that while Reigns has enjoyed a phenomenal career, he ultimately does not possess the same level of memorable, career-defining moments as those legends.
