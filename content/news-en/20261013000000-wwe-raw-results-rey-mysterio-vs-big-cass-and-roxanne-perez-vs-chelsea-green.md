WWE Raw aired on October 12, 2026, from the Brookshire Grocery Arena in Bossier City, Louisiana. The show opened with a recap of the Money In The Bank event, followed by Penta discussing his victory in the 2026 Men's Money In The Bank ladder match. Je'Von Evans appeared to congratulate Penta before Bron Breakker attacked him. 

Rey Mysterio def. Big Cass in a singles match.

Roxanne Perez def. Chelsea Green in a singles match.

Following the match between Perez and Green, Liv Morgan attacked Green, and The Judgment Day stood tall to close the segment.
