Chavo Guerrero recently spoke about the unique challenges of working with both Hollywood actors and professional wrestlers, comparing the experience to comparing apples and oranges. Guerrero noted that while both groups have their strengths, teaching an actor how to wrestle for a scene can sometimes be easier than getting a wrestler to act.

Reflecting on his past projects, Guerrero recalled working with Ryan Nemeth and LA Knight for the fourth season of GLOW, noting how smoothly that process went because both men already understood the wrestling business and the Hollywood space. He also shared a behind-the-scenes story involving Lance Archer during the filming of Disclosure Day, where Archer was entirely comfortable with the wrestling side of things but had to adjust to the quiet environment required on a film set when dialogue is being recorded.
