Stephanie Vaquer’s return to WWE SmackDown was not a random occurrence following her recent time away from television. According to reports, WWE has a specific strategy for keeping her off WWE RAW, with Becky Lynch playing a significant role in that decision.

A recent report indicates that WWE is intentionally keeping Vaquer and Lynch separated. The company wants to avoid any confrontation between them following Vaquer’s surprise appearance on WWE SmackDown, effectively keeping her away from the WWE RAW title scene until the promotion decides it is time for her to return there.

> "They are going to be avoiding each other now that Stephanie Vaquer showed up on SmackDown unannounced. It's just a way to get her away from the RAW title scene until they want her back."

This approach explains why WWE did not resume the storyline that was in place before Vaquer disappeared. She was originally scheduled to face Lynch in a match to determine the number one contender for Liv Morgan’s Women’s World Championship on the October 26 WWE RAW, but the match was pulled after Vaquer was not medically cleared due to illness. Vaquer finally resurfaced on the November 13 WWE SmackDown, joining Charlotte Flair, Alexa Bliss, Tatum Paxley, and Bayley for the WarGames announcement.
