John Cena's upcoming "John Cena Classic" project is shaping up to be much more than a simple tribute to his career. Ahead of Cena's return to WWE RAW on November 2 in Brooklyn, reports indicate that the company has spent months developing the concept. WWE has held numerous creative meetings to ensure the project feels fresh and distinct from standard tournament formats.

According to WrestleVotes, WWE reached outside its typical creative circle to help shape the project. The company consulted with individuals who do not usually weigh in on these types of initiatives, including NXT's Shawn Michaels and AJ Styles. Their involvement is seen as particularly noteworthy given the scope of the project.

Originally pitched as a milestone program for Club WWE, the company is now considering a larger streaming platform to maximize visibility and reach. While the exact format remains under wraps, Cena teased that speculation will end when he returns to WWE RAW at the Barclays Center. "Make sure not to miss #WWERaw next week. SPECIAL START TIME OF 6PM EST! The wait is over, the speculation ends 11/2. Let's build this 'Never Seen' CLASSIC! OUR time is NOW!"
