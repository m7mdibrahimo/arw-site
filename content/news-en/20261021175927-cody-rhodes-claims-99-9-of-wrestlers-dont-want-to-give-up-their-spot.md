During a recent appearance, Cody Rhodes discussed John Cena's final WWE run and explained that 99.9% of wrestlers ultimately struggle to give up their spot. Cody noted that Cena spent an entire year elevating multiple stars rather than picking just one person, distinguishing between simply losing a match and truly doing the honors. He also credited veterans like Triple H and The Undertaker for giving back to the business, while acknowledging the natural selfishness that comes with reaching the top of the industry.
