Roman Reigns reacted to his victory at WWE Money In The Bank by declaring himself the greatest of all time. At WWE Money In The Bank, Reigns defended his WWE World Title against LA Knight and retained the championship after hitting a spear. Following the event, Reigns posted a video on social media addressing his detractors.

> "Maybe I'm delusional. Maybe I believe my shit too much. But can't nobody touch me. I'm the greatest of all time baby. Now go bitch about that."

Following his title retention, Oba Femi made it clear that he will be coming for Reigns.
