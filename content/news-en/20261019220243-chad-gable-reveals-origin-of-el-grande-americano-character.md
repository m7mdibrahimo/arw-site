During an interview on "Insight with Chris Van Vliet," Chad Gable discussed the creation of his El Grande Americano persona in WWE. Gable stated that he came up with the original concept late last year to intensify his feuds with luchadores.

> "I was doing some stuff with some of the luchadores in, I think, like September/October [2024] ... as Chad Gable, and I thought: 'Man, we can get a lot out of this, I think; a lot more than we already right now. We can turn this into something special,'"

Gable mentioned that the goal was not comedy, but rather generating heat and elevating the lucha libre culture. Later, after Gable suffered an injury, Ludwig Kaiser took over the role, leading up to a Mask vs. Mask match at Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide's Noche de los Grandes.

> "Entertaining? Sure, but no it was never a 'ha-ha funny' type thing; I think it was kind of mean-spirited to jab at these [luchadores]."
