Roman Reigns shared his professional wrestling Mount Rushmore during an appearance on Club Shay Shay, focusing strictly on business impact and revenue generation rather than match quality. Speaking about his criteria, Reigns explained that his father taught him early on that the wrestling business is entirely about money. He selected four legendary figures whom he believes completely transformed the industry and paved the way for modern success. "So I would probably have to say Hulk, Steve, Dwayne and John. Those are my—I mean, yeah, you could definitely throw some other guys in there for sure, but we only got so many slots," Reigns said, naming Hulk Hogan, Stone Cold Steve Austin, The Rock and John Cena. Despite his own historic championship accomplishments, Reigns left himself off the list, maintaining that these four icons are the true foundation of the business.
