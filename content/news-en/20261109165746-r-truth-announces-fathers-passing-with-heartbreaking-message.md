WWE star R-Truth is mourning the passing of his father, Aaron Killings, who died on November 8. Truth shared the heartbreaking news on social media, posting several photos of himself with his father and describing him as his hero. In his message, Truth reflected on the unique pain of losing a parent and urged his followers to express their love to their family members while they still have the chance. Truth did not disclose any details regarding his father's cause of death, focusing his tribute entirely on his love for Aaron. The news comes after Truth previously discussed the sacrifices required by his professional wrestling career, including missing major family milestones while on the road, and how those conversations resonated with him and his son. It remains unconfirmed whether the family will hold a public service or if Truth will take a temporary leave from WWE following his loss.
