According to Fightful, interim AAA general manager Dorian Roldan announced a championship bout for the upcoming program. Perros Del Mal members Daga, Angel, and Berto will put their AAA Trios Titles on the line against a trio consisting of the promotion's top titleholders: AAA Latin American Champion La Parka, AAA Cruiserweight Champion Rey Fénix, and AAA Mega Champion El Grande Americano.

This championship match will happen on the November 21 episode of AAA on FOX. The booking followed an angle in the main event between Pimpinela Escarlata and Omos, where Americano interfered and took a beating from Perros Del Mal before La Parka and Rey Fénix ran in to make the save.
