Nikki Bella recently spoke about several wrestlers in WWE she would still like to share the ring with before she retires, including Blake Monroe. During a new interview with Justin Dorazio, Bella discussed various talents on the WWE roster she hopes to team or work with, such as Chelsea Green, Maxxine Dupri, Liv Morgan, Lola Vice, and Rhea Ripley. Bella also mentioned that teaming up with Blake Monroe would be very entertaining, noting that they have both portrayed heels. The interview also touched upon a potential singles match between Nikki and her twin sister Brie before their in-ring retirements.
