Stephanie Vaquer was absent from this week's episode of WWE Raw due to an illness rather than an injury. Dave Meltzer reported on Wrestling Observer Radio that while her condition is not considered terribly serious, she remains out indefinitely. Meltzer noted that the situation is currently indeterminate regarding her return timeline. The illness was sudden, leading to her missing the scheduled match against Becky Lynch on Monday night. WWE opted for a disqualification finish in the subsequent match between Becky Lynch and Liv Morgan to protect Lynch's standing. It remains unclear if Vaquer would have been the challenger for Morgan's Women's World Championship had she been medically cleared to compete.
