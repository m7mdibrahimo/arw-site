Jonathan Coachman recalled that John Cena was drinking alcohol for ten straight days and had only a moment's notice before his legendary 60-minute match against Shawn Michaels on WWE RAW. Speaking on The Coach & Bro Show, Coachman explained that Dean Malenko walked up to Cena with the news just the night before the event in the UK. Cena put down his beer, walked to the elevator, and prepared for the iconic encounter. Despite the lack of preparation and the lingering effects of the continuous drinking, Cena went on to deliver an unforgettable performance in the April 23, 2007 episode in London, which saw Michaels emerge victorious after nearly an hour.
