Former writer Vince Russo has attacked the planning for the upcoming John Cena Classic before it even takes place. Although WWE continues to add prominent names to the special event scheduled for December, Russo confirmed that he does not understand the purpose of the event or how the matches are being organized.

During his press comments, Vince Russo stated that he finds the tournament concept very confusing due to the variety of participants. He questioned the reason for combining main roster stars with WWE NXT wrestlers in the same event. He noted that the show includes Cody Rhodes and Charlotte Flair alongside younger names, asserting that this mix will not be successful with the audience and will be a major disappointment.

> "The John Cena Classic, which I don't even understand. I swear to God, bro. We've got NXT people in it, we got Cody in it, we got Charlotte Flair in it, and then we got a tandem of three girls? I don't understand. So that's gonna be a fart in church."

These criticisms come as John Cena continues to personally select participants for the December 14 event. He has extended personal invitations to Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno on WWE NXT, in addition to the participation of Zilla Fatu and WWE NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan. Charlotte Flair and Fatal Influence also joined during the latest WWE SmackDown. It has been confirmed that the matches will not rely on a traditional tournament bracket, but will feature multi-person matches to provide opportunities for the largest number of stars.
