AEW star Swerve Strickland recently spoke about his career in professional wrestling and hip-hop during an interview with Billboard. Reflecting on his journey, Strickland stated that his in-ring ability is the primary driver of his success rather than outside factors.

> "Not me being a caricature of hip-hop, and not me being a caricature of a Black wrestler," Strickland said. "I'm being me. My success in pro-wrestling is genuinely because of my ability in the ring. It's not because they're trying to push an agenda, it's because I perform at a really, really high level — probably the highest level you could see of a Black wrestler in the history of the game."

Strickland acknowledged past Black stars like Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson and Booker T, but noted they rarely held the title of the best worker on their shows while competing against legends like Shawn Michaels and Eddie Guerreros. He added that he feels he competes at the same elite level as top stars in AEW today, including Kazuchika Okada, Kenny Omega, Will Ospreay, and Adam Page.
