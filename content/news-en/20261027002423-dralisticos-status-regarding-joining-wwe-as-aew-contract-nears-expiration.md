Reports indicate that wrestler Dralistico intends to join WWE once his current AEW contract concludes, primarily to work alongside his brother, Dragon Lee. During a recent Q&A, WrestleVotes confirmed that the opportunity to travel and perform with his brother is a major motivation. The outlet suggested that WWE would likely have no trouble finding a suitable role for him, especially given the company's ongoing collaboration with AAA and its focus on luchadors.

> "Yeah, the rumblings are that he wants to come over, and he would like to travel and perform with his brother. He can be used great. It fits with AAA and everything WWE has been doing with luchas as of late."

Although his original AEW deal was set to expire, the promotion extended it through December due to injury time. Despite discussions about a renewal, speculation regarding his departure continues. This follows his recent social media post featuring him behind bars, which fans linked to his contract status, and his absence from AEW rings since his last match at the June 6 Collision taping.
