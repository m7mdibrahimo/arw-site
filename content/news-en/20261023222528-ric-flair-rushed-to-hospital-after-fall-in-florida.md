Ric Flair was rushed to a Florida hospital on Friday, October 23, following a severe fall that caused facial bleeding. According to TMZ, the WWE Hall of Famer was at a restaurant in Tampa when he suddenly fell, suffering cuts to his head and eye alongside notable bleeding. Emergency medical personnel arrived at the scene to provide treatment before transporting him to the hospital via ambulance.

This medical emergency occurred only hours after Flair announced on X that he had to cancel an appearance because doctors discovered a blood clot in his leg. He explained that he would undergo a procedure to treat the affected vein. Fans and observers continue to follow updates regarding the wrestling legend's health status.
