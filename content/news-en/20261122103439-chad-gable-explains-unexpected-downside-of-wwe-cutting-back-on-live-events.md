Chad Gable initially thought WWE reducing its live event schedule would benefit his body, but he soon discovered the opposite. Speaking with TribSport, Gable explained that wrestling less actually made him feel more sore because he lost the physical conditioning he built from performing every weekend.

After returning to a more active live event schedule, Gable realized how much he missed the atmosphere, the road routine, and the locker room camaraderie. He noted that he is not alone, as a specific group of WWE stars shares his excitement for performing at house shows once again.
