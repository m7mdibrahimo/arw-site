Jimmy House is no longer part of the WWE ID program. Fightful Select confirmed that House was released from the program, and he confirmed the news himself. House, who competed under the name CJ Valor on WWE EVOLVE, had been signed to the program in March 2026 after participating in the Orlando tryouts in January.
