Roman Reigns and Sami Zayn exchanged words during the October 19th episode of WWE RAW. Reigns pulled up in a truck, followed by his Crown Jewel opponent, Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn. After a brief acknowledgment, Zayn headed to the ring, where Reigns later joined him for a segment. The two champions bonded as Zayn expressed that he finally understood the burden of being champion and the disrespect that comes with it, recalling Reigns' time as The Tribal Chief. Reigns noted that Zayn's most important title was being the 'Honorary Uce,' and asked if he was there to serve The Bloodline. Zayn replied that he respects his time in The Bloodline, but he now heads his own table. Reigns argued that his belt is the true title, eventually telling Zayn that perhaps the Undisputed WWE Title has lost its meaning since he lost it at WrestleMania 40. Zayn countered that Reigns knows he is a threat despite the lack of seriousness. Reigns warned that Zayn might not even hold the title by the time Crown Jewel arrives on November 7th. As Reigns left, Mr. Money in the Bank Penta appeared and went face-to-face with both Reigns and Zayn in the ring.
