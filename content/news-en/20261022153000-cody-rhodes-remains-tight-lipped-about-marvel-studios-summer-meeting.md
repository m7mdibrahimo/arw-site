WWE Undisputed Champion Cody Rhodes is staying quiet about his potential future with Marvel Studios following a summer visit to their offices. In an interview on The Brandon Davis Show, Rhodes expressed his lifelong fandom for Marvel characters but declined to confirm any official acting roles within the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Rhodes explained that he grew up collecting Marvel trading cards and watching the animated series, making the visit to the studio deeply meaningful. However, he clarified that he has not revealed any secrets regarding potential characters or projects, noting that the experience was simply a great meeting with wonderful people.

> "There's one guy in particular, who was sitting next to me on this couch pretty much all day, who is teasing things that aren't real, and if they were real, I wouldn't say anything, and guessing characters that are great, great characters, because I grew up... I learned every Marvel character you could from the trading cards, and then, of course, I had the animated series, and we have it again now. It's been heavily discussed, but I never discuss it. That's the beauty. I've said nothing other than that was just a photo at a wonderful office after a wonderful meeting with wonderful people."

Meanwhile, Rhodes continues his intense rivalry in the ring. Following his loss to Randy Orton at Sunday Night's Main Event in September, Rhodes attacked Orton with a Cody Cutter on WWE SmackDown before Baron Corbin intervened.
