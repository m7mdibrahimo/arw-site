Danhausen’s experiment on the October 30, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown took a chaotic turn when Gunther interrupted the segment and attacked him. Just as Gunther prepared to finish off Danhausen, the arena went dark and Finn Balor’s Demon persona made a surprise appearance to confront him. Gunther initially attempted to avoid the fight, but The Demon quickly took control, delivering flying forearms and kicks. Balor then hit a Sling Blade and a running dropkick that sent Gunther through a table. After knocking Gunther to the outside, Balor climbed a ladder and delivered a Coup de Grace, standing tall to close the segment.
