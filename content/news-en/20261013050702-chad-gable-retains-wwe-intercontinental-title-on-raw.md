Chad Gable successfully defended the WWE Intercontinental Championship against AAA Cruiserweight Champion Rey Fenix on the latest episode of WWE Raw. Gable, who has held the title since defeating Penta at SummerSlam in August, countered Fenix's high-flying offense and locked in the ankle lock to force a submission.

Throughout the fast-paced match, both competitors traded near-falls and high-impact maneuvers, including a moonsault and a frog splash from Fenix. Ultimately, Gable caught Fenix in the decisive ankle lock once again, leaving his opponent with no escape and securing the victory to retain his championship.
