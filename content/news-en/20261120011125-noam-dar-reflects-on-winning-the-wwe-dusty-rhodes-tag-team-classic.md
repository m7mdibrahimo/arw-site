Noam Dar has won plenty of trophies in WWE, but his latest achievement holds special meaning after a difficult stretch in his career. Speaking on The Certified Mark Show, Dar reflected on winning the 2026 Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic alongside Romeo Moreno, earning the Dusty Cup and a future shot at the NXT Tag Team Championship.

Dar explained how much this victory meant following two major injuries that kept him sidelined for a long time:

> "There's a lot of validation that's came from this period, but more specifically just to the more recent timeline. I essentially sat out for two years due to two long-term injuries. So coming back from one and then getting reinjured and having to go through that whole process again, especially with the majority of my wrestling career not really having that much time off. The second one really felt like that was it for me; it's probably time to hang up the boots. And then a year later, I'm winning the Dusty Cup that's got shiny silver boots on it. So, as of right now, the boots are staying on."

Dar had suffered a leg injury that put him on the shelf from May 2024 until April 2025. Just two months after his return, he was forced to vacate the NXT Heritage Cup due to another injury setback. The veteran wrestler mounted a comeback in the spring of 2026 by teaming with rising star Romeo Moreno. The duo defeated Hank Walker and Tank Ledger in the finals of the tournament, securing an NXT Tag Team Championship match against Vanity Project members Brad Baylor and Ricky Smokes.
