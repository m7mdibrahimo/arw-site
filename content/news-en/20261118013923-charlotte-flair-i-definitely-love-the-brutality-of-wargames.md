Charlotte Flair is preparing for her third WarGames match later this month. She is set to lead her team at the November 28th event, where they will face the squad led by Jade Cargill, which also includes Bayley, Alexa Bliss, Tatum Paxley, and Stephanie Vaquer. In a recent interview with Brad Gilmore, Flair shared her thoughts on the match type, expressing her appreciation for its intensity. "I would agree it's enjoyable, but it's so different than the Rumble. I mean, there's weapons, there's two cages, there's two teams. You're working with other people versus Rumble. It's just you and 29 other people. In WarGames, at least you have people backing you up or knowing that your teammates beside you against the other team. I definitely love the brutality of it. I love how creative some of the WarGames moments can be," Flair stated. While she enjoys the unique elements of the match, she noted that she still values traditional wrestling. "I don't know if this year, whether we're going to have entrances or- I love the shark tanks. I'll never forget when Roman and Punk had that moment with each other. 'Are they going to get along in there?' Like I do like those elements. but also I'm old fashioned too. I love a good old singles match. No weapons have to tell a great story just with straight emotion and fire in your fist. But, I'm looking forward to WarGames. It'll be my third one."
