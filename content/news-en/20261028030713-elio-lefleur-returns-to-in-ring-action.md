Elio LeFleur has made his return to the ring. Prior to the October 27th episode of WWE NXT on The CW, dark matches were held for the fans in attendance at the Performance Center. One of these bouts featured the in-ring return of former WWE Men's Speed Champion Elio LeFleur. Back in March 2026, LeFleur disclosed that he suffered a shoulder injury and underwent surgery in early April. He held the now-defunct Men's Speed Title and had to vacate the championship as a result of his injury.
