Rey Fenix is set to host an open challenge for the AAA World Cruiserweight Championship at the second week of Héroes Inmortales. Originally, Mini Vikingo was scheduled to challenge Fenix for the title at the event. However, during the first week of the two-part special, Vikingo revealed in a segment that he suffered an injury while competing for WWE NXT and would require surgery. Due to his injury, the scheduled title match was canceled. Fenix announced that he will instead defend his championship in an open challenge during the second week of Héroes Inmortales on October 31. The current card for the second week features the following match: AAA World Cruiserweight Championship — Open Challenge: Rey Fenix (c) vs. TBA.
