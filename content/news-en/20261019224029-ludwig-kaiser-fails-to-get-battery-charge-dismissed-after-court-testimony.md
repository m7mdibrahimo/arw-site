WWE Superstar Ludwig Kaiser was unsuccessful in his bid to have a misdemeanor battery charge against him dismissed. Following a court hearing on October 19 in Orlando, Florida, the presiding judge rejected the motion filed by Kaiser's defense team. The ruling means the case will move forward, with a pretrial conference set for December 17, 2026.

Kaiser, whose real name is Marcel Barthel, took the stand during the proceedings to give his testimony, while his accuser Richard Reap also testified. Prosecutors introduced multiple pieces of evidence, including four photographs and a CD. The defense had argued that Kaiser acted in self-defense during the April 23 apartment complex altercation under Florida's Stand Your Ground law, but that argument did not convince the judge to dismiss the charge.
