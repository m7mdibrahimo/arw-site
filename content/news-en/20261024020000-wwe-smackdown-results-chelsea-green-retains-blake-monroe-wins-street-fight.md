The October 23, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown took place at the Toyota Arena in Ontario, California. In the opening title match, Chelsea Green successfully defended her Women's Championship against Roxanne Perez. The contest featured outside interference before Green ultimately secured the victory.

The main event featured a brutal Street Fight between rivals Giulia and Blake Monroe. The two competitors brawled throughout the ringside area and into the backstage facilities, utilizing various weapons before Monroe emerged victorious.
