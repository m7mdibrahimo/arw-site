The November 11, 2026, episode of AEW Dynamite/Collision aired live from the Maverik Center in West Valley City, Utah. The show featured the AEW Full Gear go-home card, including a title defense and a high-stakes mixed tag team match.

In the opening segment, Will Ospreay confronted Kyle Fletcher. Fletcher expressed his jealousy over Ospreay's success since his neck surgery, leading to an intense exchange where Fletcher slapped Ospreay, setting the stage for their upcoming match at Full Gear.

Tag Team Match: The New Level ("Awesome" Austin Creed and Kofi) vs. Brodido (Brody King and ROH World Champion Bandido) ended in a Double Count Out.

Singles Match: The Cut's David Finlay def. Orange Cassidy.

Non-Title Match: CMLL World Women’s Champion Persephone def. Gypsy Mac.

Tag Team Match (Match 5 In A Best Of 5 Series): Motor City Machine Guns (Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin) def. FTR (Cash Wheeler and Dax "The Axe" Harwood). With this win, the Motor City Machine Guns won the Best of 5 series and earned a future shot at the World Tag Team Championship.
