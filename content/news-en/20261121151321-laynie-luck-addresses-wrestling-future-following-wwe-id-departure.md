Laynie Luck is looking toward her next steps after her run with WWE ID came to an end. Now an official free agent, Luck has begun discussing where she hopes to wrestle next, with her comments pointing toward a return to the AEW and ROH environment.

Speaking with Ella J for Wrestling Gal, Luck reflected on her time as one of the prominent female stars in WWE ID. She explained that her focus is set on getting back to weekly tapings and working alongside familiar faces in the ring.

> "Oh my gosh. I'm—I have—wow, we're back to tunnel vision. I have tunnel vision right now. I want to be where the sickos are. I miss my friends. I want to be around them every week. I want to get more black eyes from these tapings. I want to come away beat up, and I want to put out—like, I just want to really feel it again."

When asked about her singles goals for the upcoming year, Luck pointed to her previous match against Red Velvet for the ROH Women's World Television Championship as a major turning point in her career. She expressed a strong desire to challenge for that title once again.

> "Singles goal? I mean, I think about, like, a match that really kind of, like, it was a turning point for me for sure. The match I had with Red Velvet for her ROH TV title. so, I mean, if she's around and available, would love to get another opportunity in that aspect because I respect what Deonna's doing with her Pure title. I could brush up on the Pure rules, but that's definitely her wheelhouse."

Luck added that winning the ROH Women's World Television Championship is currently her main goal as she enters free agency. Her exit from WWE ID came after a 320-day reign with the WWE ID Women's Championship, which ended when she dropped the title to Monica Monroe before her contract was not renewed.
