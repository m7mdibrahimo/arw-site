AEW star Anna Jay revealed that she recently underwent shoulder surgery to fix a significant bone loss issue caused by years of recurring dislocations. Speaking on her Twitch stream, Jay explained that she was missing roughly 20% of the bone on her shoulder tee, which forced doctors to perform a Latarjet procedure by attaching a bone graft secured with two screws. Although she experienced considerable pain during the first couple of weeks and will need to wear a sling for a total of six weeks, Jay confirmed that the procedure went well and that she is ready to begin physical therapy. She first noted on October 12 that surgery had become unavoidable after consulting multiple doctors regarding the long-standing injury, and there is currently no timetable for her return to the ring.
