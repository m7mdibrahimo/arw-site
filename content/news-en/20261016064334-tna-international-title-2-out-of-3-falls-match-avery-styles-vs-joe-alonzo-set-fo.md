The rivalry over the TNA International Championship continues to heat up. Jason Hotch captured the title in a four-way match at Bound for Glory, defeating Mustafa Ali to become a two-time champion. On the recent TNA iMPACT, Ali interfered to prevent Hotch from securing the number one contendership for the TNA World Championship. As a result, a 2-out-of-3 Falls match has been scheduled for the next TNA iMPACT between Hotch and Ali with the International title on the line. Additionally, a four-way match will take place to determine the first challenger for TNA Hall of Famer Amazing Red and his X Division Championship, featuring Rich Swann, K.C. Navarro, Cedric Alexander, and Fabian Aichner. Avery Styles is also scheduled for singles action against Joe Alonzo on the card.
