Cody Rhodes originally landed a smaller part as Guile in the upcoming Street Fighter movie, but according to Ringside News, his performance quickly prompted producers to significantly expand his screen time.

Appearing on the Flagrant podcast, Andrew Schulz shared that Rhodes completely blew away the cast and crew once filming began. Schulz praised the WWE star's deep preparation and ability to play with scenes on the fly rather than just reciting lines from the script.

Rhodes noted that he approached the role seriously by researching military history from 1980 to 1994 to find realistic motivations for the character's signature look and background. According to Ringside News, director Kitao Sakurai and the production team noticed the strong on-screen chemistry and responded by writing extra scenes, keeping Rhodes on set for an additional week, and essentially doubling his time in the film.
