The Rock recently discussed how his time in WWE gave him a significant advantage when he transitioned into a Hollywood acting career. Speaking with Elmo Lovano, he reflected on early acting lessons where he was tasked with memorizing and performing lengthy monologues on short notice. He noted that his years of delivering long, unscripted promos in front of live wrestling crowds prepared him for the pressure of performing on set. The Rock explained that he learned to read live audiences and adjust his delivery in real-time, skills that were directly transferable to acting. He also recalled an early coach telling him that he already possessed the necessary instincts to succeed in any genre, even if he did not fully grasp the comment at the time. Reflecting on the advice, he now understands that while training is valuable, the ability to perform instinctively is something that comes from within. He concluded that his wrestling experience provided him with the foundation to handle the demands of a successful acting career.
