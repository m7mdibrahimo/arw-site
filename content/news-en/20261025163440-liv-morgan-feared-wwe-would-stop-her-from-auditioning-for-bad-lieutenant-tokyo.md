Liv Morgan revealed she feared WWE would prevent her from auditioning for the movie Bad Lieutenant: Tokyo due to its mature content. The WWE Women's World Champion explained during an interview with Real Sound that she was initially prepared for the company to reject the idea completely. However, she discovered that director Takashi Miike has several big fans among WWE executives, who ultimately gave her the green light. Morgan secured the role and traveled to Japan in May 2025 to film her scenes as Megan alongside a cast featuring Shun Oguri, Lily James, and Shotaro Mamiya.
