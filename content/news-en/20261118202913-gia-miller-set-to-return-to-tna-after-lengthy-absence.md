Gia Miller is returning to TNA Wrestling following a lengthy absence caused by difficult health issues. After being away for more than a month, Miller announced through her Instagram Story that she is officially back for the tapings taking place today at the WinSport Event Centre in Calgary, Alberta.

Miller shared her excitement about being healthy enough to work again with a humorous message:

> "Surprise bitch I lived. Time to finally get back to @tnawrestling."

This return follows a challenging period that included two emergency room visits and two minor surgeries. Miller had previously traveled to Tampa for Bound for Glory on October 11, but a sudden health setback forced her to return home instead of working the event. She expressed her devastation at missing that show at the time. Now, Miller has recovered and is ready to resume her duties with the promotion.
