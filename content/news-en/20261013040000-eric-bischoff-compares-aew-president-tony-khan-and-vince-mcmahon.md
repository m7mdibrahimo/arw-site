Former WCW Senior Vice President Eric Bischoff recently discussed the similarities between AEW President Tony Khan and former WWE CEO and Chairman Vince McMahon, noting their shared dedication to the professional wrestling industry.

According to Wrestling Inc, Bischoff spoke about both leaders on his "83 Weeks" podcast, highlighting their intense passion for the business.

> "Yes, I think Vince was proud of his passion for the product and expected everybody around him to be as passionate as him," Bischoff began on his "83 Weeks" podcast. "My impression was Vince would not understand if you're working in WWE, in particular, and you don't have a passion for the product. I think he would have a hard time understanding or even being willing to tolerate you being a part of WWE...Tony probably is of similar nature. We know he is, based on...anecdotal information that we've all heard from people that we know and respect. He loves what he's doing."

Bischoff added that significant differences exist between them, though he did not elaborate further. McMahon left his role in 2024 following legal allegations, while Khan continues expanding AEW.
