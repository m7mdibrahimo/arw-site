Kayla Braxton recently dealt with a stressful security breach when her social media account was hacked overnight. The intruder went as far as changing the phone number associated with the account. Braxton managed to recover control of the account before further damage could be done. After regaining access, she issued a warning to her followers, stating, "Someone hacked my account overnight and even changed my phone number. Got it back, but if you received any weird messages or links from me, DO NOT CLICK THEM. Wasn't me." Once the situation was under control, Braxton took a lighter approach to the incident. She suggested the hacker could use their technical skills for more productive purposes, adding, "I hate hackers. At least do something useful with your talents. Wipe out some student loans or something." Braxton did not share details regarding any other compromised information or the specific method used to recover her account, but she is now focused on ensuring her followers remain safe from any malicious activity that might have occurred while she was locked out.
