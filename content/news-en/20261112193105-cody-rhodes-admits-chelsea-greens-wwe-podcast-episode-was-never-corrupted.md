Cody Rhodes has admitted the truth regarding the missing episode of his WWE talk show featuring Chelsea Green. Green's previous appearance on What Do You Wanna Talk About? was scrapped from airing after she was told that the footage had been corrupted, which turned out not to be the case.

During a new appearance on the podcast, Green confronted Rhodes about the scrapped recording. She explained that she recorded the original interview on her birthday while drinking and never believed the corrupted file excuse. Rhodes admitted that the file was never corrupt.

Rhodes indicated that the interview was kept off the air due to Green's behavior, as she spent the time joking around and dodging serious questions about her career. He explained that the decision was made to protect both her and the show, though he expressed hope that brief clips could be released in the future rather than the full recording.
