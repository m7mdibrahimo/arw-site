Max Caster’s controversial appearance at TNA Bound for Glory may have negatively impacted his future prospects with WWE. According to Bryan Alvarez on Wrestling Observer Live, the issue stems from a reputation Caster developed during his time in AEW of being difficult to manage and ignoring creative instructions. 

> "He got in trouble so many times over the same thing in AEW. The way it was described to me, Max Caster is a very hard-headed guy, and if you tell him no, he's gonna do it anyway. And he got in trouble many times in AEW for this."

Alvarez suggested that WWE might now be hesitant to sign Caster, as the promotion values wrestlers who follow directions. 

> "Now the same thing in TNA, and after this, it's not sure if WWE will consider hiring him. It's not even so much what was said, as opposed to what WWE does not want is someone—if you say don't do that, they will do it anyway. And Max has a tendency to do just that."

This follows reports that Caster’s rap lyrics during the event were neither scripted nor approved by TNA officials. The company has since removed the segment from its social media channels as it evaluates its plans for the wrestler.
