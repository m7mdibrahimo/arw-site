Roman Reigns recently discussed his experience working on the upcoming Street Fighter movie, in which he portrays the character Akuma. During a media appearance alongside Eric Andre and David Dastmalchian, Reigns was asked how film combat compares to his physical performances in WWE. He expressed gratitude for stunt doubles and humorously noted that he did not have to take any heavy falls during production, contrasting it with the grueling nature of performing live inside the ring. The film is scheduled to hit theaters on October 16 and will also feature Cody Rhodes as Guile and Hirooki Goto as E. Honda. In his WWE career, Reigns previously defeated LA Knight at a recent premium live event.
