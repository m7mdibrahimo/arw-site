Gunther will face Finn Balor on the November 13, 2026 episode of WWE SmackDown, just one week after their planned match at Crown Jewel was scrapped. WWE officially announced the bout following Gunther's decision not to travel to Saudi Arabia for the November 7 event. According to Ringside News and Dave Meltzer of the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, WWE had already filmed promotional segments for the match before travel concerns disrupted the plans for both men. The upcoming SmackDown event at the Amerant Bank Arena in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, will also feature John Cena returning for the John Cena Classic and Tiffany Stratton's first appearance as WWE Women's Champion.
