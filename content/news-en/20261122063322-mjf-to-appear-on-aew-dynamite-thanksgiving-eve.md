MJF is set to make an appearance on next week's edition of AEW Dynamite. Following his return at AEW Full Gear, MJF challenged Will Ospreay to a match for the AEW World Championship at the upcoming Worlds End pay-per-view. While that title bout has not yet been made official by AEW, MJF will speak on the Thanksgiving Eve episode of AEW Dynamite, which takes place in Omaha, Nebraska. Here is the updated card for the show: Kyle Fletcher vs. Andrade El Idolo; AEW Women’s Tag Team Championships: Wrathlander (Harley Cameron & Kris Statlander) (c) vs. Divine Dominion (Megan Bayne & Lena Kross); and an appearance by MJF.
