ODB has revealed the three notable figures who will induct her into the TNA Hall of Fame this weekend ahead of Bound For Glory. The veteran star, who returned to TNA earlier this year, spoke about her upcoming induction and named her longtime friend Traci Brooks, her mentor Al Snow, and legendary referee Earl Hebner as the individuals who will be part of the ceremony.

"On Sunday, right before the pay-per-view at 4:00 PM, the pre-show, I will be inducted into the 2026 TNA Hall of Fame with Konnan and Amazing Red," she said. "I have three people involved in my induction there. So first, my BFF, Traci Brooks, Hall of Famer herself. And my mentor since the beginning, since I started wrestling, Mr. Al Snow. That guy needs to go into the Hall of Fame, too. He's trained so many of the greats. And then the legendary Hall of Famer himself, Mr. Earl Hebner will be walking me out."

Reflecting on the milestone, ODB admitted she even purchased a special dress for the occasion but remains undecided on whether she will wear it instead of her signature casual look. She will become the fifth female star to enter the TNA Hall of Fame, following previous inductees Gail Kim, Awesome Kong, Traci Brooks, and Mickie James.
