As WWE Crown Jewel approaches, criticism regarding the event continuing to be held in Saudi Arabia despite ongoing regional threats has intensified. On a recent episode of "The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy," the TNA star addressed the situation, admitting the scenario is complex. "It's a really wild scenario, no doubt about it, because as you said, there's definitely some active war and there's some...they had their airport hit awhile back, which is pretty scary," Hardy said. "So I don't know man...It's certainly complicated, no doubt about it."

When asked by co-host Jon Alba if he would be willing to perform on the show, Hardy remained noncommittal, contrasting with former co-worker Perry Saturn's more definitive stance. "I don't know if I would be crazy about going," Hardy stated. "But I mean, if they were doing it, and they could somehow guarantee it was going to be as safe as it could possibly be...I don't know. They would have to make that guarantee. I don't know."

Hardy acknowledged that his hesitation was partly due to his nature as a "company guy" who tries to be a model employee. He concluded by expressing relief that he is not currently forced to make that choice. "I'm also a big company guy too," Hardy said. "Whatever company I'm working for, usually I try to do whatever I can to be the most model employee I can be too...I'm glad I'm not in that dilemma, I'll tell you that right now."
