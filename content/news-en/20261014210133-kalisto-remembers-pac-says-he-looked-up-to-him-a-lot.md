Kalisto recently reflected on the late PAC, recalling their time working together and noting that he looked up to him a lot. During an interview with TMZ's Inside The Ring, Kalisto paid tribute to PAC following his tragic passing last month around the time of AEW All Out.

Kalisto shared memories of their matches together, starting with their first encounter at the Arnold Classic, which was non-televised, and later facing off on WWE SmackDown for the United States Championship. He praised PAC's unique style, which was inspired by his work in Dragongate, and described him as a wonderful person to work with.
