The U.S. government has ordered non-emergency diplomatic personnel and their families to leave Saudi Arabia amid ongoing security threats and Houthi attacks, raising questions ahead of WWE Crown Jewel 2026 in Riyadh on November 7. Reuters reported that the State Department ordered the departures due to increased threats connected to the Iran-aligned Houthis in Yemen, while AFP cited a diplomatic source claiming all American embassy staff and families had been ordered to leave. Meanwhile, a recent drone attack near the Shaybah oil field killed two foreign workers, and disruptions continue at Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport. WWE has not announced any changes or cancellation for the event, leaving the schedule intact as the company prepares to move its production into the country.
