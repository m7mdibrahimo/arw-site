After securing a spot in the AAA Reina de Reinas Championship match following a strong performance in last week's 20-woman battle royal, Kali Armstrong challenged La Catalina for the title on this week's episode of WWE NXT. The champion controlled much of the early action, taunting her challenger while using her agility to counter Armstrong's power game. Armstrong managed to turn the tide briefly by knocking the champion from the top rope and landing a spear, but Catalina fought back with a missile dropkick and a series of hip attacks.

Late in the match, Armstrong attempted to use the exposed concrete floor to her advantage, but Catalina countered by sending her opponent crashing into the steps. After returning to the ring, Catalina secured the victory by hitting a shining wizard, successfully retaining her Reina de Reinas title.
