During a recent appearance on Hot Ones with Sean Evans, WWE star Liv Morgan discussed how legitimate personal friction and disrespect between wrestlers can sometimes bleed directly into television matches and unscripted promos. Morgan explained that viewers can occasionally tell when performers genuinely dislike each other through awkward in-ring chemistry or unauthorized remarks during promos, though she emphasized that things eventually get resolved. She also noted that despite any personal differences, she remains a total professional committed to delivering an incredible match regardless of backstage tensions.
