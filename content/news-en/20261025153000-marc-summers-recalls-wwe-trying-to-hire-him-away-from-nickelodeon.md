Legendary TV host Marc Summers recently recalled that WWE once attempted to hire him away from Nickelodeon to work as an announcer. During an interview about his upcoming 40-year "Double Dare" reunion, Summers shared his fond memories of the show's crossover episodes featuring WWF stars. He specifically highlighted a match between Gorilla Monsoon and Bobby "The Brain" Heenan as his all-time favorite moment. According to Summers, WWE wanted to bring him on board during that era, but the offer included zero financial compensation, leading him to turn it down. Instead, he continued his successful television career, which later included hosting a show on the Food Network and returning to Nickelodeon for the 2008 "Double Dare" revival.
