Carmelo Hayes has not appeared on WWE television for several weeks, and his recent activity on social media has sparked new speculation among fans. On October 11, Hayes changed his Twitter profile picture to a black image. While he offered no explanation for the change, the timing has drawn attention due to his ongoing absence from WWE programming. Hayes has not been seen on television since the August 14 episode of WWE SmackDown, where he was defeated by Baron Corbin. Despite online rumors, backstage reports have suggested that WWE management still views him as a dependable and professional talent with significant potential. Even AEW star Andrade previously addressed the situation on social media, tweeting,

> "Where are you, amigo?" While the change to his profile picture may not indicate any major news, the lack of an official update regarding his status continues to leave fans guessing about his future.
