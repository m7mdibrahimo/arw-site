According to PWInsider, the December 24, 2026 episode of TNA iMPACT will not air on AMC. They said: "TNA Thursday Night Impact will not air on Christmas Eve 12/24 on AMC, PWInsider.com has confirmed." The program will also be preempted on Thanksgiving. Fans can still watch episodes on TNA+ and AMC+.
