Josh Bishop recently discussed the importance of good advice in professional wrestling during an interview with The Wrestling Classic. At 29 years old, Bishop reflected on the worst guidance he received early in his career from people who had never stepped into a ring, noting that young wrestlers should avoid listening to foolish suggestions. However, he credited Johnny Gargano with providing the most valuable guidance. Bishop stated that Gargano, his original trainer, advised him simply to be a good person, which has carried him further in the industry than anything else despite how rare that trait can be among performers. During the same interview, Bishop also touched upon his Westside Gunn album covers.
