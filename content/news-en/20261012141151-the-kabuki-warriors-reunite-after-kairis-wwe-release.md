KAIRI and Asuka recently reunited in a new video uploaded to Asuka's YouTube channel, marking their first on-camera meeting since KAIRI's departure from WWE earlier this year. The heartwarming reunion coincided with KAIRI's birthday, featuring celebrations, gift exchanges, and plenty of laughs between the longtime friends. This comes shortly after Chris Jericho announced that KAIRI will appear on his upcoming Rock 'N' Wrestling Rager at Sea cruise, while Asuka recently dispelled rumors of her quiet exit from WWE by confirming she remains with the company. While it remains uncertain if the duo will ever wrestle together again, fans are thrilled to see the former Kabuki Warriors back together.
