Bruce Prichard is reportedly preparing to step away from his full-time role with WWE following WrestleMania 43. According to PWInsider, the company plans to promote Shawn Michaels to take over Prichard's position as Executive Director of Creative Writing and Talent. Michaels, who currently runs WWE NXT, is expected to transition into the backstage creative role. Sources indicate that a succession plan has been in place for some time, giving Michaels months to prepare for the new responsibilities. Prichard, who is 63, is not leaving WWE entirely and is expected to work as a consultant from his home in Texas. His departure comes as he prepares for upcoming neck surgery related to an injury from a 1991 WWF storyline involving The Ultimate Warrior. It remains unconfirmed who will take over the day-to-day operations of WWE NXT once Michaels moves into his new role.
