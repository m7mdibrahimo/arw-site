Former wrestler Julio Barrera, also known as Jumbo Baretta, has passed away. The Cauliflower Alley Club shared the news on X, stating that he passed away after a career that began in 1986. Barrera trained under Boris Malenko, the father of Joe and Dean Malenko, and competed across NWA territories, WCW, and WWF, with his final match taking place in 2013.
