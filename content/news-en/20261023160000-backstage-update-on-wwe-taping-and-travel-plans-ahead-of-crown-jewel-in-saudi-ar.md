WWE is proceeding with its travel plans to Saudi Arabia for Crown Jewel despite regional conflicts and a Level 3 travel advisory issued by the US government. According to PWInsider, the company plans to hold the event in the Middle East on November 7 as scheduled. Meanwhile, Fightful Select reports that WWE will tape the November 2 episode of WWE SmackDown on the same day WWE Raw airs from the Barclays Center in New York, with Raw set to air at 6 p.m. ET. Dave Meltzer notes that the decision to move forward with the show is primarily financial, as TKO expects to generate between 70 and 90 million dollars from its three scheduled Saudi Arabian events.
