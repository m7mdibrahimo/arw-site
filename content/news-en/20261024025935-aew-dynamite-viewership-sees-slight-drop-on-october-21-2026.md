New ratings data for the October 21, 2026 episode of AEW Dynamite on TBS shows a minor week-to-week decline. According to Wrestlenomics, the broadcast drew 661,000 viewers, with a 0.15 rating in the key P18–49 demographic. This represents a decrease of 54,000 viewers from the October 14 episode, which drew 715,000 viewers and a 0.14 demo rating. Compared to the same week in 2025, when the October 22 episode drew 477,000 viewers and a 0.08 demo rating, this week's performance marks a year-over-year increase of 38.6% in total viewership and 87.5% in the key demographic. These figures represent linear TV viewership on TBS and do not include streaming or digital metrics.
