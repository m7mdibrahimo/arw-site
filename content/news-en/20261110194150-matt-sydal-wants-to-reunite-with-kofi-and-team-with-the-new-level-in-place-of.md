Matt Sydal is interested in reuniting Air Boom with Kofi Kingston in AEW, pitching a potential trio alongside the New Level where he would replace Swerve Strickland. Speaking in a new interview with MuscleManMalcolm, Sydal discussed the idea of teaming up with Kingston.

> "I mean, I think I would be a great teammate. They're running into some problems with Swerve because he's – heavyweight champion. He's on his own trajectory. Austin Creed, Kofi Kingston, plus Matt Sydal. That's a trio that the family can see. Bring your grandma. Bring your kids. Come watch us. We'll have some fun."

Sydal also recalled seeing Kingston in the locker room and noting how timeless he looks, adding that the core energy of Air Boom is still simmering in the AEW locker room.
