Through a cross-promotional partnership, a number of TNA Wrestling stars have been able to appear on WWE NXT, and vice versa. Such was the case for Leon Slater, who made several trips to WWE's developmental brand in 2026. On an episode of The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy, Slater explained how those opportunities emerged as a huge benefit to him both in the ring and outside of it.

> "That really changed the game for me," he said. "Just being in that environment, being around that caliber of people, and being able to learn from people like a Matt Bloom or Terry Taylor or Shawn Michaels, it was a life-changing experience."

Slater made his NXT debut on September 30, 2025, teaming with Je'Von Evans against Shawn Spears and Niko Vance. He later vied for the NXT Tag Team and NXT Championships, with his latest appearance coming against then-NXT Champion Oba Femi at NXT New Year's Evil in January 2026. Slater eventually returned to TNA as the X-Division Champion and now holds the TNA World Title.
