Darby Allin is preparing for an appearance in CMLL. It is officially confirmed that CMLL World Heavyweight Champion Hechicero will face AEW's Darby Allin in a non-title match at Arena México on October 30.

Both wrestlers are under contract with AEW, while Hechicero maintains a dual deal with CMLL and is a member of the Don Callis Family. Allin currently holds the AEW TNT Championship.
