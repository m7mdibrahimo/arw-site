Bully Ray believes that Cody Rhodes' popularity with the WWE audience is slowly declining. Speaking on "Busted Open Radio," the wrestling veteran suggested that while Rhodes still has support, it is not as strong as it once was. Bully Ray compared the situation to the Grand Canyon, describing the decline in Rhodes' support as a slow erosion rather than an overnight change. He noted that the crowd reactions have become less enthusiastic over time. Bully Ray pointed to the moment Rhodes chose not to strike Randy Orton as the turning point that began this shift in fan perception. Furthermore, Bully Ray contrasted Rhodes' current reception with the reaction John Cena received during his peak. While he acknowledged that fans still engaged with Cena despite the boos, he feels that the current audience has become indifferent to Rhodes, expressing frustration with his ongoing run as a babyface.
