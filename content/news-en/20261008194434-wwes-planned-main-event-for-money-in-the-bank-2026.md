WWE has reportedly finalized its planned closing match for the upcoming Money in the Bank premium live event. According to WrestleVotes Radio on Fightful Select, the current plan features Roman Reigns defending the World Heavyweight Championship against LA Knight as the final bout of the evening.

> "Speaking of WWE Money in the Bank, a source tells WrestleVotes on Fightful Select that the main event of Money in the Bank PLE is L.A. Knight challenging Roman Reigns for the WWE World Heavyweight Championship."

Company officials previously discussed putting the men's ladder match on last, but that idea was ultimately shelved. While WWE could still implement last-minute changes, the championship bout remains the current choice to close the show.
