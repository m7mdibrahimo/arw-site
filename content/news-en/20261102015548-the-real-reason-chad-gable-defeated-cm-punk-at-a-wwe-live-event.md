CM Punk’s clean pinfall loss to Chad Gable at a recent WWE house show generated significant buzz, appearing as a completely unexpected moment. According to reports, WWE management intentionally booked this match on October 29 to help Chad Gable secure a victory as part of a broader strategy to make live events feel unpredictable and worth attending.

The company aimed to create viral moments that circulate online, ensuring fans no longer assume house show results are predetermined. During the Road to Survivor Series: WarGames event in Youngstown, Ohio, Chad Gable successfully pinned CM Punk without any outside interference. Following the match, CM Punk added to the moment by shaking Chad Gable’s hand and raising his arm in victory.
