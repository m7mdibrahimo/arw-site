Tiffany Stratton’s heel turn has already yielded a WWE Women’s Championship, and WWE reportedly views this as a significant long-term run rather than a brief experiment. According to reports, there is a strong belief internally within WWE that Stratton will hold the title all the way through WrestleMania 43. While not finalized as a creative lock, this belief reflects how the company currently values her new title reign.

Reports also indicate that Stratton’s transition to a heel champion was mapped out well in advance of Crown Jewel. Sources noted that WWE specifically chose Stratton over Roxanne for the Money in the Bank briefcase because management intended to turn Tiffy heel. Stratton capitalized on that plan at Crown Jewel by betraying Green immediately after Green defeated Liv Morgan, cashing in, and hitting the Prettiest Moonsault Ever to secure the championship.

Now, WWE appears ready to give heel Tiffy ample time to establish her new persona on top. If these internal creative expectations hold true, Stratton will head into WrestleMania with the WWE Women’s Championship still firmly in her possession.
