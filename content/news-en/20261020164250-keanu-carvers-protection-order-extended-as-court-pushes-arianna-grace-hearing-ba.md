Keanu Carver's legal proceedings will continue as his protection order case involving Arianna Grace has been pushed back. According to court records, Kevin Robertson, formerly known as Keanu Carver in WWE NXT, is now scheduled to appear before Judge Bryanna Bynum at the Criminal Justice Center in Sanford, Florida, on November 17, 2026. The temporary injunction remains in place, prohibiting Robertson from contacting Grace, whose real name is Bianca Carelli, and requiring him to stay at least 500 feet away from her. Carelli filed the petition on September 14, accusing Robertson of stalking, harassment, and domestic violence. Meanwhile, reports indicate that Robertson was released by WWE earlier in October.
