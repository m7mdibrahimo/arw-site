During a recent appearance on the Club Shay Shay show with Shannon Sharpe, Roman Reigns discussed the advice he previously gave to Logan Paul. Reigns recalled telling Paul not to try to fit in as a traditional wrestler, but rather to embrace his unique personality and charisma. The Undisputed WWE Championship match between the two took place at the Crown Jewel Premium Live Event back in 2022. Reigns noted that Paul is a great athlete, but his standout trait is his individuality. Paul is currently a member of The Vision faction on WWE RAW and is recovering from a triceps tear suffered in May.
