According to Wrestling Inc, Dave Meltzer reported in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter that AEW Full Gear 2026 generated between 160,000 and 180,000 pay-per-view buys. Meltzer noted that these figures align the event with major shows this year like AEW Revolution and AEW All Out, keeping it among the promotion's top recent events.

During the post-show press conference, AEW President Tony Khan stated that this year's pay-per-view surpassed last year's total of approximately 140,000 buys. Khan also highlighted the ongoing growth in the company's pay-per-view buy rates amid recent discussions regarding the Skydance merger.
