Maria Kanellis revealed that her 2017 WWE return almost took a different path when she and Mike Bennett expected to start in WWE NXT. According to Maria, they were excited about that direction until word reached them that Vince McMahon intervened. “We thought we were going to NXT and we were excited about that, but then rumor has it Vince got wind of that and was like, ‘No, Maria's going straight to the main roster.’” Maria believes Vince assumed she did not need WWE NXT because of her previous tenure with the company. After waiting for months following their clearance, the duo finally debuted at Money in the Bank in June 2017.
