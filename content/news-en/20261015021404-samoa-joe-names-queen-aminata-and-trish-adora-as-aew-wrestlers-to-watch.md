Samoa Joe shared his thoughts on rising talent within AEW during a recent interview with TV Insider. When asked which wrestlers deserve more attention, Joe highlighted two women from the roster.

> "I think Queen Aminata. There is a ton of potential that is kind of unrealized there. I would say the same thing with Trish Adora. I think those are two talents that I consistently see where maybe their best is still kind of in front of them."

Joe also praised his faction mate, ROH World Television Champion Action Andretti. He suggested that providing Andretti with more opportunities could help him become a major force in the company. Queen Aminata recently competed for the AEW Women's World Championship against Mercedes Moné at AEW Grand Slam: France.
