Je'Von Evans explained why he avoided doing promos during his time on the independent circuit before joining WWE. Speaking on the No Contest Wrestling Podcast, the 22-year-old rising star noted that his young age made it difficult to cut promos against veteran wrestlers. 

> “When it comes to my mic skills, I've been trying to work hard on just talking. On the indies, I started at 13. On the indies, I wasn't able to cut promos. I’m not gonna say I wasn't able to, I'll say I didn't like to because I was young, I was a kid. I'm going against grown adults, so I can't be saying, ‘I’m gonna beat you up and I'm gonna win.’ Nobody’s gonna believe that. I didn't like my voice.”

Since signing with WWE, Evans has embraced the challenge and improved significantly, noting that promos become straightforward when treated like matches. He also credits veteran Paul Heyman for offering valuable advice to help him develop his microphone skills.
