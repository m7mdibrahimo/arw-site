Mustafa Ali could soon be a free agent, as his contract with TNA Wrestling is scheduled to expire at the end of 2026. According to Ringside News, TNA management faces a decision regarding whether to offer the former TNA International Champion a new deal before 2027. Ali has become a significant member of the TNA roster, contributing both in the ring and as a mentor to talent like Jason Hotch. Beyond his on-screen work, Ali has reportedly been informally scouting wrestlers and recommending potential signings to TNA management. While there is no confirmation of a new agreement, he is held in high regard behind the scenes. Whether Ali chooses to stay or explore opportunities in WWE, AEW, or elsewhere remains to be seen as his contract nears its conclusion.
