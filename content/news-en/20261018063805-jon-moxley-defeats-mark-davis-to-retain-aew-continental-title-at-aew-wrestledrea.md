Jon Moxley successfully defended the AEW Continental Championship against Mark Davis of the Don Callis Family at AEW WrestleDream. The two wrestlers had crossed paths multiple times recently, including a match on the October 14 edition of AEW Dynamite where the Don Callis Family emerged victorious. During the bout, Moxley suffered a cut on his head, which Davis targeted throughout the match by applying relentless pressure and even biting the wound. Davis later executed a piledriver on the apron, taking Moxley down for a significant period. Although Moxley managed to beat the referee's count back into the ring, Davis immediately dropped him with a kick, sending him back outside. As a countout seemed imminent, Marina Shafir and Daniel Garcia motivated the champion from ringside, helping him power through and re-enter the ring. In the closing moments, Moxley paid tribute to Pac by executing The Brutalizer before transitioning into a rear naked choke, forcing Davis to submit and securing the victory.
