Rey Fenix has officially joined Team Mexico for Survivor Series. On the November 13 episode of WWE SmackDown, Team Mexico captain Rey Mysterio appeared in a backstage segment with CM Punk and Team USA captain Cody Rhodes, where he introduced Fenix as the newest member of his team.

Fenix follows his brother Penta, who was added to Team Mexico on the November 9 episode of WWE Raw. With Fenix on board, two spots remain open for both Team Mexico and Team USA as the event approaches.
