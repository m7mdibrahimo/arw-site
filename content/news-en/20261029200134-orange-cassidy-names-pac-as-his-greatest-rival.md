Orange Cassidy recently reflected on his long-standing rivalry with PAC. Between 2020 and 2025, the two wrestlers faced each other in five high-profile singles matches in AEW. While all of their encounters were well-received, many fans consider their bout at Revolution 2020 to be their finest moment. During an interview with Going Ringside, Cassidy was asked to identify his best rival in AEW. After acknowledging other major opponents, Cassidy highlighted his history with PAC. "There are multiple. Obviously the stuff with the Death Riders and Moxley and Yuta. Chris Jericho is a very important one to me. But recently, I was reminded of the rivalry that I had with PAC. It'll be one that I don't think anybody will ever top. He and I, when we got in the ring together, it was special. I feel like he’ll be my greatest rival."
