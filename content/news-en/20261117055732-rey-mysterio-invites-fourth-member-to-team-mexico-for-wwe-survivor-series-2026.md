During the latest episode of "WWE Raw," Rey Mysterio announced that Dragon Lee will be the fourth member of Team Mexico for the upcoming Survivor Series elimination tag match. This traditional five-on-five contest, which is set to return this year, will feature Team USA against Team Mexico and will take place between the Men's and Women's WarGames matches. Cody Rhodes, the captain of Team USA, previously added LA Knight to his squad—which also includes himself, CM Punk, and Je'Von Evans—after Knight defeated The Miz for the spot. In a backstage segment, Mysterio confirmed that Dragon Lee will join his team, which already consists of Mysterio, Penta, and Rey Fenix. One final member for each team remains to be determined before Survivor Series on November 28. The elimination match was a staple of the event for decades, starting in 1987, though it has been absent from the card for the past four years as WarGames matches took priority.
