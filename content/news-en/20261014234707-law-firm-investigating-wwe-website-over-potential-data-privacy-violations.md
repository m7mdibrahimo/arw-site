A law firm is investigating WWE.com regarding potential data privacy violations. Levi & Korsinsky is looking into whether the company shared visitors' personal information with third parties without obtaining proper consent. The firm is examining the use of tracking technologies such as cookies, pixels, and analytics software that may have collected data on user activity, including watched videos and visited articles.

> "Our firm is investigating whether WWE.com shared visitor information with third parties without consumers fully understanding or agreeing to it."

The investigation is not limited to those who purchased items or created accounts, as the firm is inviting anyone who used the website or its digital services to determine their eligibility for compensation. There is no cost or obligation to participate, and the firm operates on a contingency basis. While this does not mean WWE has been found guilty, the investigation continues to look for potentially affected users.
