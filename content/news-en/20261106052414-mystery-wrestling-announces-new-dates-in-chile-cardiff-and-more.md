Mystery Wrestling is expanding its global footprint with a series of newly announced dates and venues. The promotion confirmed the following schedule: January 7 at Tokyo Square in Itabashi, Japan; February 4 at Cathay's Community Centre in Cardiff, Wales; February 6 at Mountford Hall in Liverpool, England; and March 20 at Teatro Roma in Santiago, Chile. The Great Sasuke is set to appear at the Tokyo Square event on January 7. Additionally, the first season of Mystery is currently available on MyAEW, with the second season scheduled to debut on YouTube on November 9.
