During a recent interview, WWE NXT star Jacy Jayne discussed the differences between independent wrestlers and collegiate athletes in the wrestling business. Jayne explained that while indie wrestlers tend to copy things they have watched before, athletes bring a fresh perspective because they lack that prior exposure. She noted that athletes often invent unique moves that experienced indie performers might not think of since they have never seen them executed.

Jayne also highlighted how both groups benefit each other by sharing knowledge and working together like a team. In addition to her comments, it was noted that Jayne is scheduled to compete in the Women's Money In The Bank match this year.
