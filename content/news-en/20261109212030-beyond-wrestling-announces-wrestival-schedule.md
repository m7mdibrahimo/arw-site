Wrestival will return on Thursday, December 31 at the White Eagle in Worcester, Massachusetts, with shows running across Thursday, Friday, and Saturday. All events will air on IWTV, according to Fightful.

Beyond Wrestling announced the schedule for Wrestival 2026, featuring Blitzkrieg Pro and Wrestling Open/Beyond Wrestling on Thursday, Blood Sweat Tears Wrestling and CZW on Friday, and Rickey Shane Page Presents alongside Pro Wrestling Junkie on Saturday.

Events will reportedly take place at 4 pm ET and 8 pm ET, while Wrestling Revolver and WWR+ will not participate this year. The White Eagle also hosts weekly episodes of Wrestling Open.
