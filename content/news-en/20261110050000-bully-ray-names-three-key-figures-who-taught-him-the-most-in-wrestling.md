WWE Hall of Famer Bully Ray recently shared insights into his career during an episode of Busted Open Radio, naming the three figures who had the biggest influence on his development as a main-event wrestler. Ray pointed to Paul Heyman, Vince McMahon, and Pat Patterson as the mentors who taught him the most throughout his time in the wrestling industry.

Recalling a specific moment with Pat Patterson, Ray described planning a tag team match with his long-time partner D-Von Dudley against Chris Jericho and Christian. The four wrestlers approached Patterson in the arena stands to present their detailed plan for the encounter. Patterson listened, took a drag from his cigarette, and instantly offered an alternative approach that completely transformed the structure of the match.

According to Ray, Patterson spotted aspects of the match within seconds that the wrestlers had failed to see despite discussing it for hours. Ray praised Patterson's vision and ability to elevate a match effortlessly, calling his insight unbelievable.
