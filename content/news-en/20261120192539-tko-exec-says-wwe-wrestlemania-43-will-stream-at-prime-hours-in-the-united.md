More details regarding WWE WrestleMania 43 have been revealed. During TKO's quarterly investors call, Chief Financial Officer Andrew Schleimer confirmed that the 2027 premium live event, which will take place in Saudi Arabia, is scheduled to stream during prime time hours for viewers in the United States. Schleimer noted that this timing was a specific requirement within WWE's deal with ESPN, ensuring the event receives the same media value as a domestic premium live event.

While the show might begin slightly earlier than typical U.S.-based events, TKO does not anticipate any issues with their broadcast partners. The company also plans to collaborate with local partners in Saudi Arabia to sell local sponsorships. WrestleMania 43 will be the first time the grand event is held in Saudi Arabia.

Unlike previous WWE events in the region, which typically aired between 12 pm and 2 pm ET, this change will allow the United States audience to watch the event in the evening. It remains unconfirmed whether the event will be broadcast live or on a tape delay. WWE has not yet announced official dates, though reports suggest April 10 and 11, 2027.
