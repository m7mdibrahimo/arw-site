Former WWE star Shotzi announced the passing of her brother, Dean, late Sunday night in a heartbreaking update shared on X. She explained that recent days have been especially difficult because she was away from her relatives while fulfilling her wrestling commitments.

> "Late Sunday night, I lost my brother Dean. Being away from my family these last few days has been hard, but they've been so supportive and encouraged me to fulfill my commitments this week."

Because of this tragedy, Shotzi confirmed her withdrawal from next week's CMLL tour to return home, be with her family, and honor her late brother's memory. She noted that deciding to pull out was difficult, particularly after being chosen for the prestigious CMLL Grand Prix.

> "I will, however, need to withdraw from my CMLL tour next week to be with my family and honor my brother's memory. It was a huge honor to be selected for the CMLL Grand Prix, and I don't take that opportunity lightly. Thank you to everyone at CMLL and the fans for understanding how hard all this is for me."
