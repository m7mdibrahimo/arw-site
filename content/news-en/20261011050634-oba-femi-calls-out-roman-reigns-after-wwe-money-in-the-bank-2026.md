Oba Femi made a bold statement following his victory at WWE Money in the Bank 2026, where he defeated Bronson Reed despite interference from The Vision. During the post-show media session, after fans in the background began chanting for Roman Reigns, Femi issued a direct challenge to the World Heavyweight Champion, telling him to stop running and stating that the time has come for a showdown. Reigns had earlier successfully defended his title against LA Knight, making Femi one of the top rising stars to openly step forward for a championship opportunity.
