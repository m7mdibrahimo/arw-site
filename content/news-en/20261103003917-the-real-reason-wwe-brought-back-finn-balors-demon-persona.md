Finn Bálor’s return as The Demon was not just a Halloween surprise, but a strategic decision to elevate his current storyline. According to WrestleVotes, WWE officials believe that The Demon vs. GUNTHER is a much more significant attraction than a standard match between Finn Bálor and the World Heavyweight Champion. A report during a Q&A session confirmed the reasoning behind the creative choice: "Yeah, it's to go into a program and an official match with GUNTHER. It opens a lot of eyeballs than just Finn Bálor vs. GUNTHER. The Demon vs. GUNTHER is a marquee match — that's the reason."

This transformation provides context to the events of the October 30 episode of WWE SmackDown. After GUNTHER interrupted a segment involving Danhausen and decimated the clones produced by a cloning machine, the lights went out, signaling the arrival of The Demon. Bálor successfully countered GUNTHER’s offense, eventually driving him through the announce table with a running dropkick and a massive Coup de Grace from a ladder. WWE reportedly views GUNTHER as the type of opponent who justifies the return of this specific persona, with the current confrontation appearing to be part of a long-term plan.
