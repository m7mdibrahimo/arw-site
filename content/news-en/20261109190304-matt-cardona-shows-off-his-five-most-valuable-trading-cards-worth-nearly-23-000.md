Matt Cardona has built an impressive wrestling and pop culture collection, revealing his five most valuable trading cards with a total value of nearly $23,000. Taking to Instagram, Cardona showcased the items, starting with a 1985 Topps Hulk Hogan card valued at $781 at number five. For number four, he featured a Topps Garbage Pail Kids card of his wife, Chelsea Green, worth $1,098. His collection then jumped significantly with a 1998 Comic Images card of The Rock valued at $2,323 at number three. Moving beyond wrestling for his second most valuable item, Cardona showed a 1995 SkyBox Woody card from Toy Story, which he called Woody's rookie card, valued at $3,651. Topping the list at number one is a 1999 Topps WCW Macho Man Randy Savage autograph card worth a massive $15,082.
