Stephanie Vaquer is speaking out about fans who obtain private phone numbers and expect personalized videos for free. The WWE star explained on her Instagram Story that these requests take up significant time, noting that recording custom content is actually part of her paid professional work. Vaquer shared a screenshot of a WhatsApp conversation where a fan contacted someone whose private number had been given out by a producer, asking for a video shoutout for a school team. Although the person agreed to help, Vaquer emphasized that creating these videos requires hours of work and proper permissions for her image, meaning they cannot be handed out casually.
