According to Fightful Select, there is no immediate urgency to bring Sheamus into AEW. However, top stars like Claudio Castagnoli and Adam Copeland have reportedly expressed interest in having him join the company. While Sheamus has engaged in talks with other promotions, there is no indication that a deal with AEW is imminent. On July 10, the former WWE star changed his social media handles to his real name, Stephen Farrelly, and posted a farewell to WWE. Sheamus, who has been with WWE since 2006, has been sidelined since December following shoulder surgery. His last televised match took place on the November 17 episode of WWE Raw, where he teamed with John Cena and Rey Mysterio to defeat The Judgment Day.
