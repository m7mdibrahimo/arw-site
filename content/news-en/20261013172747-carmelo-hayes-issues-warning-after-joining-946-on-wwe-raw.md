Carmelo Hayes has broken his silence following his shocking alliance with 946 on WWE RAW. Hayes posted a warning on Instagram after making his surprise return and attacking Solo Sikoa with a tire iron. The attack allowed 946 to leave Jimmy Uso, Jey Uso, and Solo laid out to close the show. Hayes had been absent from WWE television since August after losing to Baron Corbin. Reports indicate that his move to join Bronco Nima, Lucien Price, and Royce Keys had been in the works since July.
