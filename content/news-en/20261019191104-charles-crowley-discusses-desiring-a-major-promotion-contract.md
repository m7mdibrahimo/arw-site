Charles Crowley recently discussed his potential goals of signing with a major wrestling promotion to secure a platform that showcases his true abilities to fans.

Crowley stands as a prominent star on the independent scene across Britain and the world, and he also works as a skilled actor and voice actor. He recently joined the Star Wars franchise to voice the character Biddy Blass in a new racing video game. During a recent interview, Crowley expressed his hopes of joining a major promotion soon.

> "Of course I would absolutely love to get signed. We all want to be on WrestleMania. TNA has such a cool aesthetic, and the vibe of AEW is unmatched. I’ve spent a lot of time, effort, energy, and money on my promos as and when I can, and I can’t always do that because I’m a struggling artist, but it’s more just the platform to be able to show what I’m capable of… And I believe it will happen. Just got to keep grinding away, keep getting better every day, and just do my best. When I got into wrestling, someone said to me, ‘It’s all been done before, so don’t try and like recreate the wheel’. And I hated that. I’ve never fucking forgotten it, ever since then, I’ve tried to prove it wrong."
