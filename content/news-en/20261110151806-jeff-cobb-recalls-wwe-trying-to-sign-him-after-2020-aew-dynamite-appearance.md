Jeff Cobb recalled that WWE tried to sign him following his surprise appearance on the February 12, 2020, episode of AEW Dynamite, where he worked as a mercenary for Chris Jericho against Jon Moxley. Speaking to Chris Van Vliet on Insight, Cobb explained that he received phone calls the very next day asking about his availability. However, he had already committed to a one-year deal with New Japan Pro-Wrestling, and soon after, the pandemic hit, causing him to pass on the opportunity. Cobb eventually signed with WWE in May 2025 before being released a year later. During the interview, he also noted that WWE considered several potential names for him before settling on JC Mateo.
