WWE SmackDown aired on October 30, 2026, from the Charleston Coliseum in Charleston, West Virginia. Danhausen hosted the "Danhausen's Very Nice, Very Evil SmackDown" on the eve of Halloween. Cody Rhodes opened the show to discuss his upcoming Last Man Standing match against Randy Orton at Crown Jewel. In the night's title match, Chelsea Green successfully defended the WWE Women's Championship against Nikki Bella after rolling her up for the pin. Following the match, Liv Morgan confronted Green, while Roxanne Perez attacked Tiffany Stratton, and IYO SKY made the save for the Judgment Day. In a AAA Cruiserweight Championship match, Rey Fénix defeated Ricky Saints. Jade Cargill, accompanied by the new tag champions, defeated Tatum Paxley with the Jaded. Danhausen's experiment resulted in the arrival of Demon Balor, who attacked GUNTHER after he destroyed Danhausen's set. In the main event, Trick Williams defeated Sami Zayn by disqualification after Johnny Gargano pulled the referee out of the ring, meaning Zayn retained the Undisputed Championship. Nick Aldis then restarted the match as a No Disqualification bout, during which Williams hit Zayn with the Trick Stick to secure the win.
