Je'Von Evans spoke about his bond with Penta on No-Contest Wrestling. Evans described his working relationship with the former Men's Intercontinental Champion, expressing his honor at working alongside someone he once studied during his formative independent scene years. Evans noted that they have built a brotherhood, though they both know they share goals they want to achieve in wrestling. The two men previously teamed up to defeat The Vision's Austin Theory and Bron Breakker last Monday on WWE Raw, before standing in opposition at Money in the Bank where Penta captured the briefcase.
