Shiloh Hill recently received a personal invitation from John Cena for a major WWE opportunity, but his biggest challenge this week is coming from Grand Theft Auto VI. Hill shared his thoughts on X about Rockstar's latest release, which launched worldwide after a 13-year wait, expressing frustration with its realistic criminal consequences. 

> “i think GTA lost the plot a little because why am i experiencing realistic consequences of my crime? i already have that in real life”

Hill's experience highlights new features in the game, such as a more reactive Wanted system designed by Rockstar to make players feel the impact of their actions. The game now tracks appearances, vehicles, and criminal profiles, making it harder for players to simply evade the police by driving away. Reviewers have praised the added challenge, contrasting with Hill's humorous take just days after John Cena invited him to compete in the John Cena Classic on December 14.
