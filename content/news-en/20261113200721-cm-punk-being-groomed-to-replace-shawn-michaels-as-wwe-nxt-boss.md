CM Punk may be preparing for a significant new role within WWE that does not involve pursuing another championship. According to reports, the company is internally readying Punk to eventually take over the management of WWE NXT, succeeding Shawn Michaels after WrestleMania 43. Michaels is expected to transition into a larger role at that time.

In recent weeks, Punk has been present at most WWE NXT television broadcasts alongside Michaels. These appearances are considered part of his preparation for the position, as he has been spending considerable time learning directly from Michaels. The report confirms that Punk is currently the company's preferred candidate to lead the developmental brand.

WWE has not yet decided if this transition will require Punk to retire from in-ring competition or simply reduce his wrestling schedule. There is no official date for the change, as the company wants to ensure the arrangement is effective. These plans follow previous reports that Michaels will replace Bruce Prichard as Executive Director of Creative Writing & Talent in April 2027.
