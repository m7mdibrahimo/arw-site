Former WWE Tag Team Champion Spike Dudley has announced that he has signed a Legends deal with WWE. Speaking on Busted Open, Dudley discussed his return to the company fold. "I’ve been out of it for a while. I didn’t know what my standing was with the company because I haven’t had any interaction aside from going to Philadelphia a couple of years ago. So for them to make the call out and go, ‘Hey, we’re interested,’ it means that there’s something that they think they can make money from, meaning that I have some value, historical value… it’s a great honor. It’s really cool."

Regarding potential merchandise, Dudley noted that they are in the early stages. "We just signed so we haven’t gotten to that point. I’ll be honest, with (Microsoft) Outlook being all freaked out and like we haven’t gotten to anything of like, ‘These are the plans.’ But I know when I was initially signing — the next video game, whenever that comes out — they were talking about getting me into it, but there’s no definitive anything at this point." WWE filed to trademark the "Spike Dudley" name one week prior to his announcement.
