Kazuchika Okada remains the AEW International Champion after defeating Myron Reed on the October 15 episode of AEW Dynamite. This victory also earned Okada a spot in the Clockwork Carnage match at AEW WrestleDream.

The match began with back-and-forth action. Reed knocked Okada out of the ring with an enziguri, but "The Rainmaker" quickly regained control by sending his challenger face-first into the mat. Despite Reed's high-flying attempts, including a 450 splash from the ringside steps, Okada managed to avoid the major impact. Okada ultimately secured the win by hitting a Rainmaker after dodging a final splash attempt from Reed.
