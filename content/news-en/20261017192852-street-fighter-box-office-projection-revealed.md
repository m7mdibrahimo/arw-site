Street Fighter is on pace to capture the top spot at the domestic box office, although its opening weekend is tracking slightly below Paramount's original expectations. According to The Hollywood Reporter, the video game adaptation collected $13 million on Friday, which included Thursday previews, and is projected to finish the weekend with approximately $31 million across 3,575 North American theaters. Paramount had anticipated an opening in the mid-$30 million range, placing the early figures beneath that target despite the film leading the rest of the field.

Produced on an $82 million budget, the picture serves as the initial release under Paramount’s distribution agreement with Legendary, following a plot based on the Capcom fighting game franchise. The project also features notable WWE participation, with Roman Reigns portraying Akuma and Cody Rhodes taking on the role of Guile. Early audience reception has been mixed, as the film secured a B- CinemaScore alongside an 82 percent audience score on Rotten Tomatoes compared to a 42 percent critic score, leaving the film's post-opening performance as its primary upcoming challenge.
