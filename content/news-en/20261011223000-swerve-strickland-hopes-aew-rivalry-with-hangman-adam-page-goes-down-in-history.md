Swerve Strickland has expressed his desire for his intense storyline with Hangman Adam Page to be remembered among the greatest feuds in wrestling history. The personal conflict between the two stars in AEW has included extreme moments, such as Page burning down Strickland's childhood home and Strickland invading Page's family residence.

Speaking in an interview with The Takedown on Sports Illustrated, Strickland highlighted the unique electric energy they generate whenever they share the ring. He noted that the entire arena stands up the moment they tag in against each other, comparing the anticipation to a lit fuse ready to explode.

> "I hope one day it goes down as one of the greatest in wrestling history."

Strickland explained that their shared emotional vulnerability makes the rivalry so captivating for the audience. He emphasized that both men know exactly how to push each other's buttons, allowing them to take fans on an unforgettable emotional ride whenever their paths cross.
