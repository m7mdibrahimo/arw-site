WWE SmackDown aired on October 30, 2026, from the Charleston Coliseum in Charleston, WV. Danhausen hosted "Danhausen's Very Nice, Very Evil SmackDown." Cody Rhodes opened the show to discuss his upcoming Last Man Standing match against Randy Orton at WWE Crown Jewel. In the main event, United States Champion Trick Williams challenged Sami Zayn for the Undisputed Championship. In the women's division, Chelsea Green defended her WWE Women's Championship against Nikki Bella, with the stipulation that if Bella won, she would face Liv Morgan for the Women's Crown Jewel Championship. Green defeated Bella to retain her title. Elsewhere on the card, Rey Fénix defeated Ricky Saints to retain the AAA Cruiserweight Championship. The show also featured segments involving Jade Cargill, B-Fab, Michin, and Fatal Influence.
