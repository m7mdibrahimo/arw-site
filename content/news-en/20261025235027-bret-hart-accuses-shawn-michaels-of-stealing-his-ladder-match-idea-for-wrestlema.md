Bret Hart recently opened up about his frustrations regarding the iconic ladder match between Shawn Michaels and Razor Ramon at WrestleMania X, revealing that he is still bothered by the situation after more than thirty years. According to Hart, he originally conceived the ladder match concept back in 1985 and pitched it to Vince McMahon multiple times over the years. Hart explained that he even demonstrated the match layout to McMahon backstage with Shawn Michaels as his partner during a television taping, only for McMahon to miss the demonstration. Hart claims that Michaels and Ramon later took his idea and presented it to McMahon for WrestleMania X, leaving Hart feeling betrayed and admitting that he still loses sleep over the incident.
