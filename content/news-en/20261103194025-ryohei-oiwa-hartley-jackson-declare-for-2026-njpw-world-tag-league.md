Ryohei Oiwa and Hartley Jackson are looking to add a major accolade to their resumes. Following their victory over The Swirl (Lee Johnson and Blake Christian) at NJPW Power Struggle 2026, the TMDK duo announced their intention to compete in the upcoming World Tag League tournament. Their goal is to earn a shot at the IWGP Tag Team Championship before the year concludes.

Following the match, Oiwa was confronted via video message by MJF, who officially challenged him to a match at Wrestle Kingdom 21 on January 4, 2027, at the Tokyo Dome. Oiwa accepted the challenge. Regarding the tournament, Hartley Jackson expressed his drive to succeed despite his 27-year career, stating: "It's time for the WORLD TAG LEAUGE with ROY! Lets go."

Additionally, NEVER Openweight Champion Aaron Wolf has announced his entry into the World Tag League, where he plans to team with Oleg Boltin.
