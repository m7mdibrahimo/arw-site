The audience figures for the October 9 episode of WWE SmackDown on USA Network showed a slight increase compared to the previous week. The show drew 1,352,000 viewers and achieved a 0.32 rating in the key 18 to 49 demographic.

This represents an increase from the October 2 episode, which drew 1,250,000 viewers with a 0.27 rating in the key demographic. Compared to the same week in 2025, viewership rose significantly from 1,025,000 viewers and a 0.22 rating. These figures reflect television viewership on USA Network and do not include digital or streaming platforms.
