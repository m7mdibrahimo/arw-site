Marina Shafir is not confirming that Ronda Rousey will join AEW as a member of the Death Riders, but she is also not ruling out the possibility. In an interview with TheSportster, Shafir was asked about her longtime friend and former MMA teammate potentially joining her in AEW. Shafir suggested that in professional wrestling, anything is possible until it is officially off the table.

> "Here's the thing. This is what I like to say about things that haven't happened yet. That means that there's a pretty good chance that things could happen. And it may not seem likely right now, but, you know, that's one of the things that I love about professional wrestling."

Shafir compared the unpredictability of wrestling to her choice not to find out the sex of her child before birth, as she enjoys being surprised. She believes that keeping secrets from the fans makes for a better live experience. Rousey previously made a surprise appearance at AEW Revolution earlier this year to support Shafir, and Tony Khan has stated that the door remains open for her to return. However, Rousey noted in May that she has no interest in the travel requirements of a full-time wrestling schedule. Despite this, Shafir seems content to let fans speculate on whether the circumstances might one day align for another appearance.
