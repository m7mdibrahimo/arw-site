The November 4, 2026, edition of WWE NXT emanated from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, airing on The CW and Netflix. The main event featured NXT Champion Grayson Waller defending his title against SmackDown star CM Punk, a match arranged by Danhausen following Waller's victory over Tony D'Angelo at Halloween Havoc.

NXT General Manager Robert Stone ordered NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan to defend her title against a mystery opponent. This decision served as a penalty for Jordan's recent unauthorized appearances on Raw and SmackDown.

The 2026 Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic concluded with Romeo Moreno and Noam Dar facing Hank Walker and Tank Ledger. Cody Rhodes was present to award the Dusty Rhodes Cup to the victors of the tournament.

Cruz Montana battled Dion Lennox in an Unsanctioned Match. The contest followed weeks of personal animosity between the two, which previously included Lennox driving Montana into exposed concrete.

In a six-man tag team match, North American Champion Jackson Drake teamed with NXT Tag Team Champions Ricky Smokes and Brad Baylor to face Intercontinental Champion Chad Gable, Dragon Lee, and AAA Cruiserweight Champion Rey Fenix. This matchup was finalized backstage during Halloween Havoc.

John Cena appeared to announce the NXT participants for the upcoming John Cena Classic. The tournament is scheduled for December 14 at Walt Disney World in Orlando, Florida, as part of a special double feature edition of Raw.
