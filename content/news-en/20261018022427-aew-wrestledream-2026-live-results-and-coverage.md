AEW WrestleDream took place on October 17, 2026, live from the Addition Financial Arena in Orlando, Florida. In the main event Clockwork Carnage Match, Will Ospreay successfully defended the AEW World Championship against Samoa Joe, "Speedball" Mike Bailey, Adam Copeland, Konosuke Takeshita, Kazuchika Okada, Ricochet, and Darby Allin. Mercedes Mone retained the AEW Women's World Championship by defeating Thekla. The Motor City Machine Guns defeated FTR in the first match of their Best of Five Series. Nick Wayne secured a win over Chris Jericho. The Young Bucks successfully defended the AEW World Tag Team Championship against The Don Callis Family's Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight. Persephone retained the TBS Championship in a Four-Way Match against Hyan, Queen Aminata, and Dani Luna. Andrade El Idolo successfully defended the National Championship against Tommaso Ciampa. "Hangman" Adam Page, Brody King, and Bandido defeated Swerve Strickland and New Level in a 2-Out-Of-3 Falls Match to win the AEW World Trios Championship. Jon Moxley successfully defended the Continental Championship against Mark Davis. In the Saturday Tailgate Brawl matches, Divine Dominion defeated Sisters of Sin, The Don Callis Family defeated Papi D, Rhys Maddox, and Alex Breeze, and The Death Riders and The Dogs defeated The Bang Bang Gang and The Conglomeration.
