WWE SmackDown is preparing for a strong episode in Evansville, Indiana, with Survivor Series WarGames approaching. According to Viper Reports, an early backstage rundown lists United States Champion Trick Williams vs. Baron Corbin as the main event for tonight.

Plans remain subject to change before the broadcast, particularly because WWE previously advertised Williams in a non-title match against Damian Priest. Williams and Corbin have a long-running rivalry spanning months, beginning when Williams won the championship on September 6, followed by a Steel Cage match involving interference from Lil Yachty.

Tonight's event also features Jade Cargill vs. Charlotte Flair in a WarGames Advantage Match, a tag team match pitting Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki against The War Raiders, and Cody Rhodes revealing the final member of Team USA.
