Ring of Honor broadcast the latest episode of ROH TV on October 29, with the event airing on HonorClub according to Fightful. The show included footage from AEW Dynamite showing Swerve Strickland making ROH Champion Bandido bleed.

Brandon Cutler def. Soleil

During the show, The Kingdom explained their artificial intelligence assistant to The Grizzled Young Veterans.

Scorpio Sky def. Oro

Lacey Lane def. Cassie Lee

Los Colons & Serpentico def. Dante Casanova, Chad Skywalker, and Saeed El Sabbah

Following the match, The IInspiration stated that Lacey Lane will need to deal with Steph De Lander.

Lio Rush def. Charles Crowley

In the main event, Alec Price, Jordan Oliver, and Top Flight defeated The Grizzled Young Veterans and The Kingdom, with Sparta accompanying the opposing team.
