WWE is reportedly discussing potential plans for Rey Mysterio's retirement, with backstage conversations pointing toward 2027 as the possible year for his final run. According to WrestleVotes Radio, sources within the company have indicated that the legendary luchador could wrap up his active in-ring career in the coming years. Although nothing is officially scheduled yet, discussions have taken place regarding how to handle his eventual farewell. Officials also believe that Mysterio will likely remain with WWE and AAA in a non-wrestling capacity to help develop the next generation of talent, much like AJ Styles. Despite these ongoing reports, Mysterio recently stated that he is unsure when he will hang up his boots, noting that he is currently enjoying his time inside the ring and as a general manager.
