Maya World has been cleared to return to competition. During the 2026 AEW WrestleDream BUY-IN, hosted by Renee Paquette, Jeff Jarrett, and R.J. City, former TBS Champion Maya World appeared as a guest. World has been out of action since the September 9 edition of AEW Dynamite. She officially announced during the broadcast that she is cleared to compete. World previously held the TBS Championship for 35 days before losing the title at AEW All In, where interference from Britt Baker led to her defeat against the current champion, Persephone. Persephone is scheduled to defend the TBS Title at WrestleDream in a four-way match against Queen Aminata, Dani Luna, and Hyan, who is Maya World's partner.
