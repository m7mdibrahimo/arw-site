CJ Perry recently discussed her potential return to WWE, explaining the specific conditions that would bring her back to television. During an appearance on Nick Club Wrestling Happy Hour, the former Lana stated that she would love another opportunity in the company if the creative direction is right. However, she made it clear that she is not interested in competing for championships, believing instead that her greatest value lies in working as a manager or an on-screen character.

Perry pointed out that WWE already features a strong women's division with plenty of talent. Drawing a parallel to veteran figures like Paul Heyman, she believes her experience on the microphone and in storytelling can still contribute significantly to the product. While she remains focused on keeping her dreams alive and even takes acting classes in preparation, she understands that creative decisions involve many backstage factors beyond a performer's control.
