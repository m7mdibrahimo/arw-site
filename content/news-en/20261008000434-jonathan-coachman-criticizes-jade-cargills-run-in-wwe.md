Former broadcaster Jonathan Coachman recently shared a harsh assessment of Jade Cargill, claiming according to Ringside News that she lacks the necessary skills for the top level of the company. During an episode of The Coach and Bro Show on October 7, Coachman was asked if moving from SmackDown to RAW would help Cargill, but he dismissed the idea entirely.

> "No, because they listen to us! Remember when I caught all that heat for saying she’s just not good enough? She’s nowhere to be found."

Coachman argued that not everyone is suited for WWE's highest tier and suggested critics often hold back out of hesitation to insult female talent. He stated that Cargill has struggled since her debut and that her signing was largely driven by her appearance.

> "Bro, this is the top of the game, top of the business. Jade — she hasn’t been there since day one. They signed her more because of how she looks. She looks like a superhero."

Back in March, Cargill addressed earlier critics who questioned her wrestling ability by noting she had already captured championships across multiple companies and achieved success both inside and outside the ring. Despite ongoing debates about her in-ring skills, Cargill remains one of the most heavily discussed female wrestlers on the current roster.
