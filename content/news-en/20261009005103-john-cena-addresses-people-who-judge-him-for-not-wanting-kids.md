John Cena recently opened up about his decision not to have children, addressing the criticism he faces for that choice. In a conversation with Trevor Noah, Cena explained that his decision is based on biology and a desire for the freedom that allows him to pursue his personal growth. He pushed back against the idea that being a good role model or having a caring personality obligates him to become a father.

> "Just because someone thinks I would be good at something… is that reason enough to do it? And it is not a commitment where, like, four months in, I’m like, ‘You know, this wasn’t my thing.’”

Cena emphasized that his life path has allowed him to develop the wisdom and nuance he currently possesses. He noted that he and his wife, Shay Shariatzadeh, share a relationship built on trust and independence, which supports his current lifestyle. He also spoke candidly about his relationship with his father, admitting that he spent decades wanting his dad to be someone he wasn't.

> "And, man, even my dad was a great dad. He raised five of us, worked his ass off, yet me growing up, I wanted him to be somebody he wasn’t. And, like, I’m talking grown up, to, like, 45 years old."

At age 46, Cena decided to accept his father as he was, which significantly improved their relationship. He concluded that he is at peace with his decision to remain childless and prefers to stay in his own lane despite external judgment.
