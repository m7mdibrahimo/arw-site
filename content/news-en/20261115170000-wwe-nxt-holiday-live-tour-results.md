WWE put on a live show on November 14 at the Fox Theatre located in Detroit, Michigan, according to Fightful and Wrestling Bodyslam. Sean Legacy defeated Tristan Angels in singles action.

In a match for the NXT Tag Team Championships, champions The Vanity Project consisting of Brad Baylor and Ricky Smokes successfully defended their titles against Tavion Heights and Elijah Holyfield. Brooks Jensen earned a win over Harley Riggins. Wren Sinclair and Kendal Grey picked up a victory over Kali Armstrong and Chantel Monroe.

In a street fight, Saquon Shugars, Zilla Fatu, and Mason Rook emerged victorious against DarkState, which featured Dion Lennox, Cutler James, and Osiris Griffin. EK Prosper and Shiloh Hill defeated Kam Hendrix and Grayson Waller.

Zaria successfully defended the NXT Women's North American Championship against Lizzy Rain and Izzi Dame. In the main event, CM Punk and Bayley teamed up to defeat Jackson Drake and Myka Lockwood.
