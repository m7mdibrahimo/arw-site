UFC President Dana White has turned down an offer to serve as White House Press Secretary. On Monday, White officially declined the role, which President Donald Trump had offered him over the weekend. On Saturday, Trump had publicly stated, "I am pleased to announce that the Great Dana White, a violent Fighter and Warrior, has been offered the job of Press Secretary of the United States. I have requested that he bring with him assistants, even though some are not from our Country, of Islam Makhachev, Khabib Nurmagomedov, Justin Gaethje, Daniel Cormier, Amanda Nunes, and Kayla Harris. We need these people to defend us against the Lunatic Left. Dana will be a Press Secretary like none other. Dana, please take the job! President DONALD J. TRUMP." While Trump later claimed on Sunday that he was "only kidding," he noted that Dana White was interested in the idea.
