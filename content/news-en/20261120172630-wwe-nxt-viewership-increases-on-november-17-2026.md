WWE NXT experienced a noticeable improvement in television viewership on November 17, 2026, across The CW Network, achieving positive growth compared to previous weeks.

According to audience data, the November 17, 2026, episode of WWE NXT brought in 676,000 viewers on The CW Network, securing a 0.11 rating in the key 18–49 demographic. In comparison, the November 10, 2026, episode attracted 588,000 viewers with the same rating, representing an increase of 88,000 viewers.

On a yearly basis, the show registered a 4.0 percent increase compared to the corresponding week in 2025, which drew 650,000 viewers. These figures strictly reflect live television viewership on The CW Network and do not account for digital streaming platforms.
