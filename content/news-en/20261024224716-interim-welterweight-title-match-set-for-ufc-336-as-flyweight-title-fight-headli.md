Two massive championship matches are official for UFC 336. The promotion announced during UFC 333 that Carlos Prates will clash with Michael Morales for the Interim Welterweight Championship. The event takes place on January 30 in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Reigning Welterweight Champion Islam Makhachev was unable to compete on that date due to Ramadan.

Prates enters the contest on an impressive streak, securing three straight knockout victories along with three consecutive Performance of the Night bonuses. His recent victories came against Geoff Neal, Leon Edwards, and Jack Della Maddalena. Morales boasts an undefeated 19-0 career record and has won his last three fights by first-round TKO, stopping Neil Magny, Gilbert Burns, and Sean Brady.

In the main event of the night, Joshua Van will defend his Flyweight Championship against Manel Kape. Van comes in riding an eight-fight winning streak and has successfully defended his belt twice, most recently defeating Alexandre Pantoja. Meanwhile, Kape has won four consecutive fights, securing victories over Bruno Gustavo da Silva, Asu Almabayev, Brandon Royval, and Kyoji Horiguchi.
