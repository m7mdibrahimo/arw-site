Despite ongoing armed conflict in Saudi Arabia and travel warnings from the United States State Department, WWE has made it clear that they still plan to hold Crown Jewel in Riyadh next month. Dave Meltzer reported on Wrestling Observer Radio that a recent tweet from Saudi General Entertainment Authority director Turki Al-Sheikh was seen as a message to WWE, helping solidify the decision. Meltzer noted that Ari Emanuel will make the final call, with TKO heavily tied to Al-Sheikh due to funding for Zuffa Boxing. While families, talents, and backstage staff are reportedly unhappy and concerned for their safety, Meltzer believes WWE will exhaust every option to make the trip happen because of the massive financial incentives involved.
