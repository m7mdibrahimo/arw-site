Logan Paul recently shared his thoughts on EK Prosper executing a dangerous announce table springboard moonsault during his WWE NXT Championship match against Grayson Waller on September 29th. While comparing it to a similar high-risk spot he performed in the past, Paul acknowledged the effort and raw talent of the rising star. 

"It's always humbling when I see these young bucks copying the Jedi," Paul said during his latest vlog. Although he noted that the NXT arena and setup are smaller than the main roster venues, he praised Prosper's fearlessness and dedication, adding, "Brother, you got balls, and I know because I did that shit."

Paul also expressed his desire to face Joe Hendry first once he receives medical clearance to return to the ring.
