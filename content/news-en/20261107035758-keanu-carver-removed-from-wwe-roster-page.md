Keanu Carver's WWE departure appears confirmed after he was removed from the promotion's public roster page on Friday, November 6, 2026, following earlier reports of his release, according to Ringside News. The 27-year-old wrestler, whose real name is Kevin Robertson, previously competed in the WWE NXT developmental system before his exit.

His departure coincides with an ongoing civil protection-order case involving fellow NXT talent Arianna Grace, whose real name is Bianca Carelli. Carelli filed a petition seeking protection, accusing Robertson of stalking and harassing her after their relationship ended, alongside allegations of domestic violence.

Court filings included text messages showing Carelli repeatedly telling Robertson to cease contacting her and respect her boundaries. A judge previously issued a temporary protection order pending further proceedings, while Robertson had not faced criminal charges tied to the allegations.

WWE has not publicly explained the circumstances of Carver's release or whether the legal matter influenced the decision, leaving Robertson's professional future uncertain.
