Kofi Kingston appeared on The Mick and Kenny Show to discuss his historic KofiMania run in WWE, according to Wrestling Inc. In 2019, Kofi defeated Bryan Danielson at WrestleMania 35 to become WWE Champion.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKH_iYGN9S0

Kingston explained that the period was incredible, noting that his success came at the expense of Mustafa Ali. He wondered if the storyline would have changed if Ali had remained healthy and become the champion instead. He stated that capturing the title fulfilled a childhood dream he always imagined while playing with neighborhood friends, adding that being the first African-born champion in company history is a major milestone.
