John Cena has returned to his traditional WWE appearance, though he clarified that this change does not signal a desire to compete in another match. After experimenting with a different wardrobe during his retirement, Cena realized that wearing suits created more complications than it resolved. He originally chose suits to signal to fans that the wrestler they recognized had officially retired. 

However, the suits did not stop fans from chanting for one more match. Additionally, Cena found that fitted suits and dress shoes were impractical for his habit of running to the ring and interacting with the crowd, as he feared splitting his pants. This led him to return to his preferred attire of cargo shorts, sneakers, T-shirts, and his signature cap. 

Despite the familiar look, Cena emphasized that his actual wrestling gear remains retired. He is wearing casual clothes without wristbands, towels, or knee pads, and he remains firm in his commitment to stay retired from the ring.
