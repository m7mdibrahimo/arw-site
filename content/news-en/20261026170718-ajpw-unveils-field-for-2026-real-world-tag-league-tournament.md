All Japan Pro Wrestling has announced the field and blocks for the 2026 Real World Tag League tournament. The tournament is scheduled to begin on November 15th, featuring teams divided into Block A and Block B. The final match will take place on December 6th at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo. The reigning AJPW World Tag Team Champions, the Saito Brothers, are among the participating teams.
