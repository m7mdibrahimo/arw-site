LA Knight returned on WWE Raw, defeating The Miz to secure his spot on Team USA for WWE Survivor Series, according to Fightful. The show began with Cody Rhodes appearing to announce a new team member before CM Punk interrupted him. A discussion followed involving Rhodes, Punk, The Miz, and Kit Wilson.

The Miz asked to join Team USA, while Punk claimed he had already chosen a fourth team member alongside Rhodes, leaving Rhodes confused. LA Knight then walked to the ring as the intended pick and challenged The Miz for the available spot.

LA Knight def. The Miz

Knight pinned The Miz following a BFT finisher to claim the spot on the team.
