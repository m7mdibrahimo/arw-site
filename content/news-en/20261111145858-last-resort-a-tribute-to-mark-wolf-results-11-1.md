Last Resort: A Tribute To Mark Wolf took place on November 1 from the Irving Theater in Indianapolis, Indiana. The event was broadcast on IWTV. 

Brian Gorta and Trik Davis defeated Bang And Matthews (August Matthews and Davey Bang).

Gwen Neodonna defeated Lauren Lockett.

In a scramble match, Rachel Armstrong defeated Ethan Heyre, Hans Carden, Jackie Thad, Valerie Kennedy, and xOMGx.

In an Old School IWA Rules Handicap Match, IWA Originals (2 Tuff Tony, Corporal Robinson, Hy-Zaya, Mad Man Pondo, Nate Webb, and Todd Morton) defeated Jordan Dye and Kavron Kanyon.

The Rejects (John Wayne Murdoch and Reed Bentley) defeated Death Divas (Anakin Murphy and Davina Thorne).

In an IWA World Championship match with Jimmy Jacobs serving as the special enforcer, Connor Hopkins defeated Aaron Williams to retain the title.

Eddie Kingston defeated Calvin Tankman.
