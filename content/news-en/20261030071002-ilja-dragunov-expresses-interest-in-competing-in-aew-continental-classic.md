Ilja Dragunov recently addressed the possibility of entering the upcoming AEW Continental Classic tournament. During an appearance on AEW Advance, Dragunov discussed the tournament and reflected on its past winners, including Eddie Kingston, Kazuchika Okada, and Jon Moxley. Dragunov stated that he and Kingston are due for another match, recalling their history and mentioning Kingston's past comments about him. He emphasized that the tournament represents strength, resilience, and intensity, and noted that his presence is set to change the landscape of AEW. Dragunov originally arrived in AEW at the WrestleDream pay-per-view.
