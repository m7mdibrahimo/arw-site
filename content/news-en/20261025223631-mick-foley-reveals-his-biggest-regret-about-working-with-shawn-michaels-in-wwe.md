Mick Foley recently looked back at his memorable WWE career and revealed his single biggest regret regarding his historic rivalry with Shawn Michaels. During a fan Q&A session in Germany, Foley reflected on their legendary match at WWE In Your House: Mind Games in 1996, which he ranks as his second favorite match of all time right behind his 2004 encounter with Randy Orton. Foley explained that Michaels was carrying an enormous amount of pressure as the WWE Champion at the time, dealing with a heavy media schedule and the demand to constantly deliver great matches, which left him physically exhausted. Despite how well they worked together and how easily Michaels accepted his creative ideas, Foley wishes they had been given the chance to develop a much larger storyline. Specifically, he admitted that he would have loved to face Michaels on the biggest stage of them all at WrestleMania. Although their Mind Games encounter remains one of the most celebrated matches of his career, Foley feels that a full-scale rivalry culminating at WrestleMania would have given fans another true classic.
