LA Knight has clarified his relationship status following a flirty WhatsApp exchange with Nikki Bella. Knight revealed that he has been single for well over a year, noting that his long-term relationship ended in July 2025. He explained that he never made a public announcement about the breakup because he deliberately maintains a boundary between his television career and his personal life.

The recent interaction on a WWE WhatsApp group chat, which included a Bella Twin and an influencer, sparked speculation from fans who wondered if he was married or dating. Addressing the rumors, Knight stated that he is neither married nor in a relationship. While he accepts public speculation as part of being a public figure, he drew the line at fans directing questions and rumors toward his former girlfriend, emphasizing that she did not sign up for the spotlight and asking fans to respect that boundary.
