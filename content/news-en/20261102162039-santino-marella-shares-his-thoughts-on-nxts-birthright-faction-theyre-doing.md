Santino Marella recently shared his thoughts on the NXT Birthright faction, a stable consisting of multi-generational NXT talent, which includes his daughter Arianna Grace. Formed earlier this year in March, the group currently features five members: Uriah Connors, Charlie Dempsey, Arianna Grace, Stacks, and Lexis King. The faction has recently been involved in a feud with Zilla Fatu. Speaking in a new interview with the We R Wrestling podcast, Marella discussed the group and the unique pressure they face. "It's interesting because, it comes with some pressure too. You have to perform and, but the pressure is interesting. It's not just to live up to your parent's expectations, you have to exceed expectations that anybody puts on you because you don't want anybody to be able to say to you that you're only here because of your dad. So they have to, they generally outwork everybody. Everyone in the faction is working really hard to take away people's ability to say that you're only here because your parents, you know, and they're getting the spotlight. They're getting a lot of time. It seems to be a featured storyline and and they're capitalizing on the opportunity, they're doing really well."
