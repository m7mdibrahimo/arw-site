Matt Hardy shared that he personally sewed all the ring gear that he and his brother Jeff Hardy wore on WWE television during the 1990s. Before achieving legendary status as one of the most iconic tag teams in professional wrestling history, the pair spent much of that decade working as enhancement talent to help build up other tag teams in the company.

Speaking on The Stunner, Matt explained that money was tight during their early days in the business, making purchased gear unaffordable. He recalled learning how to use an old family sewing machine after their mother passed away, teaching himself the craft to create their custom ring tights for their early appearances.

> "I sewed all of the tights we wore. All of the tights that you saw us wear on WWE TV in the 90s, I sewed all of those as well. So we didn't have a ton of money, and tights were so expensive back then. So our mom had died when we were younger. I dusted off our old sewing machine and taught myself how to sew, and then I started making our tights."
