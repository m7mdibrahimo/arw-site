Ric Flair turned his recent hospital photo into a Halloween post. After sharing an image of himself wearing an eyepatch during medical treatment for an eye injury, The Nature Boy used artificial intelligence to transform the photo into a pirate look. Flair shared the image on Instagram, showing off the pirate version complete with an eyepatch, white shirt, pirate hat, and a mug of beer alongside the caption, "Happy Halloween! WOOOOO! 🎃". The post came after Flair spent the previous day telling people to stop worrying about him. Following questions about how he is still alive after his latest accident, Flair pointed to everything he has survived during his life and wrestling career, including 5,000 matches, an airplane crash, and a serious health crisis in intensive care. He noted that he still needs another surgery and a possible skin graft before fully recovering.
