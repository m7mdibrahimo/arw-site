Izzi Dame and WWE NXT Women's North American Champion Zaria are set to face each other at WWE NXT Halloween Havoc on Saturday, October 31. Zaria's title will not be on the line during the premium live event. Dame has spent recent weeks digging into Zaria's past and threatening to expose her true identity. Following a match on WWE NXT, Zaria confronted Dame and challenged her to a bout, which Dame accepted.
