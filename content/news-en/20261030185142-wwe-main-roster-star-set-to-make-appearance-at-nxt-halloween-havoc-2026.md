Tatum Paxley is officially returning to NXT for Halloween Havoc on October 31. Despite being a member of the SmackDown roster, Paxley announced on X that she will be joining the show as a panelist.

"Yup yup yup it sure doesn’t seem right to have Tater Paxley NOT be at #HalloweenHavoc …. SOOOO this year I’m coming to be one of the panelist cause i REFUSE to miss out on the show that gave me one of my best moments of my career! See you Saturday @WWENXT 🖤"

Paxley spent years in NXT, going through various storylines including her time with Diamond Mine, her friendship with Lyra Valkyria, and her role in The Culling. While she is now on the SmackDown brand, the Halloween Havoc event holds special significance for her career. There is no indication that this appearance marks a full-time return to NXT.
