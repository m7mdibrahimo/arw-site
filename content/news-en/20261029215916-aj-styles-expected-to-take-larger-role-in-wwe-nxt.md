Shawn Michaels is expected to transition into a significantly larger role within WWE following WrestleMania 43, leaving questions about who will oversee the NXT brand. According to False Finish, sources at the WWE Performance Center anticipate that AJ Styles will become more involved with NXT once Michaels moves into his new position.

While Styles has not been officially named as a replacement, there is an expectation within the Performance Center that he will assume greater responsibilities. Styles brings decades of experience from his runs in TNA, NJPW, and WWE, which could prove valuable for developing younger talent.

This development follows reports that WWE plans to promote Michaels to the position of Executive Director of Creative Writing and Talent, succeeding Bruce Prichard after WrestleMania 43 in April 2027. Prichard is expected to shift into a consultant role. PWInsider reports that WWE has been preparing for this transition for some time. As Michaels has managed NXT's creative direction and talent development since September 2021, his departure will be a major change for the brand. While it remains unclear exactly what Styles' role might entail, his increased presence in NXT is a situation to watch.
