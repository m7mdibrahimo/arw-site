AEW Collision: Fright Night took place on Saturday, October 31, 2026, at the Chaifetz Arena in St. Louis, Missouri. The event featured several high-profile matches and championship defenses.

Tommaso Ciampa def. Matt Sydal

Hurt Syndicate def. Soloroto

Triangle of Madness def. TRINITY

Ilja Dragunov def. The Beast Mortos

Speedball Mike Bailey def. Darius Martin

Divine Dominion def. Nixi XS and Leela Fiest

Konosuke Takeshita def. Adam Priest

During the broadcast, Persephone and Queen Aminata were announced for the Blood and Guts match. Andrade El Idolo was confirmed to defend the AEW National Championship against Tommaso Ciampa at Jericho Cruise, while Mercedes Moné will defend the AEW Women's World Championship against Marina Shafir at Full Gear.
