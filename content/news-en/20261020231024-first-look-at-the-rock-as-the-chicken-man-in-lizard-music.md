Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson is portraying a 70-year-old character known as the Chicken Man in the upcoming film 'Lizard Music'. Directed by Benny Safdie, the movie is currently scheduled for a theatrical release on December 25, 2027. Production recently shifted from Trilith Studios near Atlanta to New York, where new photos of Johnson in character were captured on Tuesday, October 20.

The film's makeup effects feature industry veterans Glen P. Griffin, Kazu Hiro, and Robin Pritchard. Back in September, Johnson revealed that he underwent a significant physical transformation for the role, stating that he lost 50 pounds to play the character.
