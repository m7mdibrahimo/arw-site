AEW Collision Grand Slam France takes place on October 10, 2026, at the Adidas Arena in Paris, France, starting at 8 p.m. ET on TNT and HBO Max. The match card features a loaded lineup of championship matches and high-stakes showdowns.

The Young Bucks defend the AEW Tag Team Championships against The Dogs, consisting of Clark Connors and David Finlay. Andrade El Idolo puts the AEW National Championship on the line against Michael Oku. In the women's division, a four-way tag team contenders match features Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron against Sisters of Sin, Julia Hart and Skye Blue, and Divine Dominion, featuring Lena Kross and Megan Bayne.

Additional matches on the card include Nick Wayne facing Chris Jericho, Swerve Strickland taking on Carlie Bravo, and a Clockwork Carnage qualifying match between Mike Bailey and Lee Moriarty. In eight-person tag team action, The Death Riders unit of Claudio Castagnoli, Wheeler Yuta, Daniel Garcia, and Marina Shafir battles The Conglomeration, featuring Willow Nightingale, Roderick Strong, Kyle O’Reilly, and Orange Cassidy.
