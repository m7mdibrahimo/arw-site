AEW CEO Tony Khan has addressed the recent absence of The Hurt Syndicate from the promotion, explaining their sporadic appearances. Speaking during the WrestleDream media call, Khan noted that the group operates as hired guns in AEW and could return at any time for the right price. He praised their involvement in major events like Double or Nothing and AEW All In London, and mentioned that they were happy to work with Big Boom A.J., Big Justice, and The Rizzler. Khan also expressed his respect for Bobby Lashley, Shelton Benjamin, and MVP, indicating they remain available to work with anyone under the right circumstances.
