Roman Reigns recently discussed his experience working with Andrew Koji on the set of the upcoming 'Street Fighter' film. Reigns, who portrays Akuma, shared the screen with Koji, who plays Ryu, for one of the movie's most intense action sequences. Reigns noted that the experience felt remarkably similar to his time in WWE.

"It was awesome. That was kind of a part of the difficulty because we trained so much with John Valera’s team and I was training a lot with Andrew’s stunt double. I was starting to really get his chemistry and his timing and his rhythm, because Andrew’s shooting the whole time. So, as I’m training and trying to get in, he's already been doing all the work. By the time got together, I had to adjust a little bit and it felt like being in wrestling. It felt like being in the WWE. He's a tough guy. He actually had to deal with some adversity and he just trucked right through it. But yeah, he'd get in there and make some contact, he’d lay it in there, which is good because that's what I'm used to anyways. It brings that authentic feeling to the movie."

'Street Fighter' is scheduled to hit theaters on October 16. The film also features appearances by Cody Rhodes as Guile and Hirooki Goto as E. Honda.
