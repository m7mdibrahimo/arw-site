TNA Wrestling has officially revealed the match card for the upcoming November 12 episode of TNA iMPACT!, following the events of the November 5 show. The lineup features four scheduled matches, highlighted by a high-stakes singles encounter between Mustafa Ali and Frankie Kazarian.

The announced card also features Moose taking on Samuel Shaw, who will have Joseph Sawyer in his corner. Additionally, Heather by Elegance will clash with Elayna Black, while Elijah is scheduled to face Nic Nemeth in singles action.
