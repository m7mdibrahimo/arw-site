The Undertaker believes that Trick Williams may be the final person he ever delivers a chokeslam to. Speaking on the Six Feet Under podcast, The Deadman looked back at their July 22, 2025 confrontation on WWE NXT, where Williams was on the receiving end of the move. Williams recalled that Shawn Michaels told him he was officially famous backstage after taking the slam. When asked about it, Undertaker confirmed that Williams is likely the last person to receive the move from him, noting that he does not foresee himself executing another chokeslam in the future. Williams previously revealed in August that he did not clear the trash talk with Undertaker beforehand, prompting The Deadman to say he would make him famous before delivering the chokeslam.
