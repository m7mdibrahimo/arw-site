NJPW President Hiroshi Tanahashi revealed during NJPW King of Pro-Wrestling that Tanahashi Jam 2 is officially returning.

The upcoming event will take place on November 19 at Korakuen Hall, roughly one year and four months after the first show at Aichi Prefectural Gymnasium featured special matches and outside talent.

CS TV Asahi Channel will broadcast the show live and exclusively, as fans await the matches and surprises planned by the NJPW leader for this new edition.
