John Cena has returned from retirement for a unique challenge. Sesame Street released a new video featuring Cena in a cookie eating contest against the Cookie Monster. While Cena refused to quit, he was soundly defeated by the beast. Cena then presented the Cookie Monster with a Cookie Championship Title, which the winner attempted to consume. Cena had previously appeared alongside Elmo. The John Cena Classic is currently being developed, with Cody Rhodes and Zilla Fatu already confirmed for the event.
