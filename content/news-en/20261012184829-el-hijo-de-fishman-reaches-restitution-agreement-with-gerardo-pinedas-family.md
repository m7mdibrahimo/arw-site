El Hijo de Fishman has reached a financial restitution agreement with the family of late referee Gerardo Pineda to avoid potential manslaughter charges. Pineda tragically passed away following a piledriver from Hijo de Fishman during an independent match against Mascara Sagrada NG. According to reporter Antonio Nieto, Hijo de Fishman agreed to pay 585,525 pesos to Pineda's family, which will be distributed among Pineda's five children. The agreement was crucial for Hijo de Fishman to avoid criminal charges and prison time, as the deal required the unanimous consent of all five children. Neither Hijo de Fishman nor the Pineda family has issued a public statement regarding the settlement.
