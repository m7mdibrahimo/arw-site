Steph De Lander discussed her departure from TNA Wrestling and her current status with ROH during an appearance on TMZSports Inside the Ring. The Australian star requested and received her release earlier this year and quickly transitioned to performing for ROH after recovering from a severe neck injury in April.

De Lander noted that she is enjoying creative freedom in ROH, where she recently challenged Deonna Purrazzo for the Ring of Honor Women's Pure Championship at Death Before Dishonor. She also clarified that there is currently no communication between her and TNA management following her departure.

> "At the moment, there is no relationship. There is no communication. There has been no communication. Like, the last communication there was me saying 'Send me my release papers right now.' And I got them in an email, and that was that."

Despite appearing on major events, De Lander revealed that she is not under an exclusive contract with AEW or ROH at this time, working instead on a pay-per-appearance basis.
