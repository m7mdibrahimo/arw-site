Kendal Grey had herself quite the weekend after recently returning to the ring following an injury layoff. The NXT star took to Instagram to share a photo dump covering her time away from work, which included moments from a recent NXT live event. In several photos, Grey posed by the pool wearing a black bikini top paired with jeans, sunglasses, and a waist chain, while also showing off her abs. Another photo featured a completely different vibe, showing her at the beach in a pink string bikini while holding a tiny teacup pig. The post comes as Grey is back doing what she loves, having returned to the ring at WWE's October 15 live event in Kitchener and defeating Karmen Petrovic in her first match since August 30. For now, Grey is enjoying her time back on the road and having fun between shows.
