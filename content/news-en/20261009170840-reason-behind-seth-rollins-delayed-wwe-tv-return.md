Seth Rollins has been away from WWE television since SummerSlam following his loss of the World Heavyweight Championship to Roman Reigns. Although he has competed in non-televised events during tours of South America and Mexico, he has not yet returned to WWE RAW. According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, Rollins is dealing with a minor injury, but this is not the primary reason for his absence. WWE reportedly has the option to bring him back immediately if necessary, but the creative team is waiting for the right moment to maximize his impact. This approach aligns with Rollins' own statements that he is taking his time and waiting for the right storyline to present itself.
