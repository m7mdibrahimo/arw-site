JBL recently recalled his interactions with the late star PAC and the plans that Vince McMahon had for him during his time in WWE. Speaking on the Something to Wrestle podcast, JBL discussed PAC's incredible talent and noted that McMahon originally wanted him to portray the superhero cartoon character Mighty Mouse. However, JBL explained that this plan never happened because WWE already had Rey Mysterio in that role, though he believes PAC would have been a perfect fit for the character.

JBL also highlighted PAC's early popularity in the company, remembering how the star generated double the social media impressions of anyone else when he first arrived. Praising PAC as a world-class talent and a great person behind the scenes, JBL shared fond memories of their time together before PAC ultimately left WWE in 2017.
