AEW Dynamite aired live on October 21, 2026, from the Watsco Center in Coral Gables, Florida. Following his debut at AEW WrestleDream, Ilja Dragunov was introduced by Renee Paquette and is set to face "Speedball" Mike Bailey later. 

In an AEW World Tag Team Championship Eliminator Match, Mark Davis and Jake Doyle def. The Young Bucks. 

In a tag team match, Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight def. Will Ospreay and Adam Copeland. Following the match, a beatdown ensued involving Kazuchika Okada, Fletcher, Knight, Davis, Doyle, and Wardlow. The segment concluded with Steven Borden and Darby Allin making the save, leading Borden to challenge The Don Callis Family to a Blood & Guts Match.

Additionally, it was announced that Maya World will face Queen Aminata in a Number One Contender's Match for the TBS Championship.
