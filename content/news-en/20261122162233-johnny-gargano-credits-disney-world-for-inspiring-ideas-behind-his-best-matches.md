Johnny Gargano has revealed that several of his best career matches were planned while visiting Disney World. Professional wrestlers often enjoy traveling to different destinations around the globe, with many stars in WWE and AEW being avid fans of theme parks ran by Disney both in the United States and internationally.

Gargano and his wife, Candice LeRae, are well known for their love of Disney World, even holding their wedding ceremony at the park. Speaking to LaughingPlace.com, the WWE star explained how being in that environment stimulates his creativity when designing matches.

> "I think I planned and had ideas for some of my best matches at Disney World. That was crazy to say, but it's just like the energy in the building, I think, and the creativity all around me that sparks that for me."

Gargano continues to apply that creative energy in WWE, where he remains a prominent fixture in the ring alongside his tag team partner Tommaso Ciampa.
