Callum Newman recently opened up about taking over the leadership of United Empire after Will Ospreay departed NJPW for AEW in February 2024. Newman explained that while Ospreay was the main guy, others stepped up before injuries opened the door for him to take charge and steer the group forward. Despite his sacrifices and efforts to keep the faction strong, Newman expressed frustration upon learning that Ospreay formed a United States chapter of United Empire featuring Mike Bailey, Alex Windsor, and Jamie Hayter without notifying him. Newman and Ospreay are scheduled to team up at NJPW Power Struggle to challenge for the IWGP Tag Team Championships.
