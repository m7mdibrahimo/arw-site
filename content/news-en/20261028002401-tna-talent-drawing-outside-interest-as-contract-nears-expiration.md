George Iceman may soon face some big career decisions. According to a report from False Finish, the TNA personality's contract is nearing its end, and there is reportedly interest in him from other promotions. While no specific companies were named, the report suggests that Iceman is evaluating his options as his current deal approaches its expiration.

Iceman has been a staple of TNA television, particularly as the Personal Concierge for the Elegance Brand, which includes Ash By Elegance, Heather By Elegance, M By Elegance, and Mr. Elegance. Beyond his role as a mouthpiece for the group, he has also stepped into the ring, including a debut appearance at Sacrifice 2025. This is not the first time his contract status has been a talking point, as reports of his deal expiring surfaced back in June.

At that time, Iceman addressed the rumors on social media, simply stating that "the show must go on." TNA President Carlos Silva has recently been in discussions with the roster regarding new contract negotiations, with many deals set to expire in December. As of now, it remains unclear whether Iceman intends to stay with TNA or explore new opportunities elsewhere.
