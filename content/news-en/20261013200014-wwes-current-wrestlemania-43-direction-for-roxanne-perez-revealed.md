Although Roxanne Perez left Money in the Bank without the briefcase, WWE reportedly has significant plans for her moving forward. According to a report from Self Made Pro on a Self Made Session, the decision to have Tiffany Stratton win the briefcase instead of Perez was a strategic move to build audience sympathy.

> "And so they said that they wanted to surprise the audience and also surprise the audience in a way that would make Roxanne a bigger babyface because the audience would feel that she was cheated." The goal is to establish Perez as a top babyface as the company approaches the WrestleMania season. Rather than holding the briefcase, WWE intends to have her pursue the Women's World Championship directly.

> "So they want to make the audience rally around Roxanne by feeling sympathetic for her, thus making her a big babyface as she chases the belt going into WrestleMania season." Self Made Pro noted that internal discussions suggest these plans are already focused on WrestleMania 43.

> "So it was implied to me that she's going to do a Mania-season title chase." This shift replaces her original trajectory of winning the Money in the Bank briefcase, aiming to maintain her momentum through a championship chase instead.
