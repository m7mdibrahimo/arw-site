Titus O’Neil has officially signed with Stoked Sports & Entertainment Expansion for management to continue building his career outside the ring, according to Ringside News. Although he has not wrestled a WWE match in six years, the former star remains with the company as a WWE Global Ambassador.

The agency is headed by Tammy Hunt and represents clients such as Patrick Patterson, Darius Miller, Norris Cole, and Shannon Briggs. Hunt discussed bringing O'Neil aboard by stating,

> "I have spent my career helping talent build careers with staying power and Stoked is the platform I always wanted to build from. Titus and this class are exactly the kind of talent we want to grow with, on the field and well beyond it."

O’Neil noted that the company's focus on life outside the spotlight was a major selling point for him. Stoked co-founder Jarnell Stokes also expressed confidence that the new partnership will help elevate the company.
