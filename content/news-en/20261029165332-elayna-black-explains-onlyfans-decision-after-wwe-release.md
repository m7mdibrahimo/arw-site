Elayna Black has opened up about her decision to join OnlyFans following her release from WWE, according to Ringside News. In a documentary published on TNA Wrestling's YouTube channel, she explained that the move was about securing her financial future after losing the career she had pursued since childhood.

> "I didn't grow up wanting to be an OnlyFans model. I grew up wanting to be a wrestler. And unfortunately for me, my dream was ripped out from under my feet with no control."

Black noted that wrestling was her sole ambition from the age of eight, leaving her unprepared for other business ventures until WWE let her go at age 24. Her choice to launch an OnlyFans account also earned support from her former trainer Mustafa Ali, who stated that he does not judge her career path and compared it to professional wrestling.

> "Her decision to do that is her decision. Uh, the way I look at everything very unattached. And, you know, it's no secret I'm a man of faith. I'm a Muslim. And I think people were kind of shocked about my stance or how I, what I'm about to tell you is this: what she's doing, how's that any different than wrestling?"

Despite finding financial success outside the ring, Black ultimately returned to TNA Wrestling to continue her in-ring career.
