Gail Kim and Alisha Edwards are joining forces to organize a special charity auction to support Rebel. Kim announced that the two will host an event titled "Rebel with a Cause" this December at 7 PM on the Whatnot platform, with all proceeds directed to help Rebel during her ongoing battle with ALS.

The auction will feature several unique items, including a TNA Knockouts Championship belt autographed by past and present stars, signed merchandise, and gear worn in the ring by Rebel. Bidding begins this week on Edwards' official Whatnot page.

Rebel, who joined the I AM ALS movement as an ambassador back in June, previously shared an emotional message about her fight:

> "ALS may be a terminal diagnosis, but I’m showing up to fight as an Ambassador for I AM ALS. This patient-led movement is stepping into the ring every day to drive real progress and passing laws like the ACT for ALS for research and treatments. I’m proud to join I AM ALS and I’m asking you to step into the ring with us. Please take action."
