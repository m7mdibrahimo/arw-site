Adam Cole's wrestling career may be over, and the latest update on his AEW future is concerning. The former TNT Champion has been sidelined for over a year with serious concussion-related issues, leading to growing doubt that he will ever wrestle again. At 37 years old, Cole faces the possibility of ending his career without a proper farewell match.

During a recent Q&A, Sean Ross Sapp discussed Cole's health, noting that regular travel is difficult for his recovery and that an in-ring return is not expected anytime soon. Cole has wrestled only 37 matches over the past four and a half years and was forced to surrender the TNT Championship at AEW All In: Texas in July 2025 due to medical issues. In April 2026, it was reported that AEW had no plans for him to return or travel, and while The Young Bucks provided an encouraging update in June, he remains un-cleared.
