Kevin Owens recently shared his plans to win the men's Money in the Bank briefcase and secure a world title opportunity fairly. He joins a ladder match featuring Je'Von Evans, Trick Williams, Bron Breakker, Penta, and CM Punk.

During an interview with Cathy Kelley for WWE, Owens stated that he refuses to use sneak attacks on fallen champions. He wants his opponent to be fully aware when he challenges them.

> "I'm going to get that briefcase, and then I will, whenever the time comes, I'll cash it in the right way, not with some sort of sneak attack when the champion's already down, because I want him to see me coming"

Reflecting on his Universal Championship win a decade ago, Owens noted that Triple H handed him the belt. He is now determined to earn his next world title entirely on his own merit.

> "And I don't want what happened 10 years ago when I won the Universal title to happen again, because I think I deserved the Universal title, but with the way I won it that night, I didn't earn it"
