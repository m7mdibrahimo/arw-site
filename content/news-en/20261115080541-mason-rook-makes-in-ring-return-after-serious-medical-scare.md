Mason Rook has officially returned to in-ring competition following a health issue that kept him out of action for nearly two months. Days after making his return to WWE NXT television, Rook worked his first match since September at a WWE NXT live event in Detroit on November 14. He competed in a Motor City Street Fight, teaming with Saquon Shugars and Zilla Fatu.

Rook was originally scheduled to challenge Grayson Waller for the NXT Championship on September 29, but WWE pulled him from the match after he failed to receive medical clearance. Reports in October suggested the medical situation could lead to a lengthy absence, though details about his condition were kept private. Rook made a surprise appearance on the November 10 episode of WWE NXT, going straight after Grayson Waller to restart their rivalry before making his physical comeback in the ring.
