Mara Sadè discussed her recent career, emphasizing that she feels an entirely new launch and great liberation since leaving TNA and continuously traveling to wrestle in various parts of the world, according to Fightful.

Sadè entered free agency in July after her contract with TNA expired, concluding a stint with the company that followed her departure from WWE. Since then, she has competed on the independent scene, and reports indicate she has signed with AEW.

In a new interview, Mara Sadè expressed her great happiness regarding the major development in her career and traveling without stopping, stating:

> "I feel like I'm like bursting out the box. Like I feel like I was in a box and I'm out the box. You know what I'm saying? I've been to places I never thought I'd be versus the other place, and like whenever that event happens, it's like a turning point, quite frankly. It was a chapter change, and so my first match wrestling again since then was in Paris. You know what I'm saying? So for me, that's a ginormous achievement, and I've been to London, I've been to, I mean, where have I been? I've been all over the place, and I've been traveling non-stop and it's crazy because I never could have expected it, but the fact that my momentum is still going, I'm not surprised, but I also knew just because I know the work ethic I put in, I'm saying? I put in the work, I'm a go-getter for real. So with that being said, like, I'm always going to shoot for what I want and what I want is to be successful in wrestling and win that gold. Like everybody wants gold. I want the fricking gold. So that's my goal, and I'm going to keep fighting. I'm going to keep working. I'm going to keep traveling. I'm keep doing whatever it takes to get there."
