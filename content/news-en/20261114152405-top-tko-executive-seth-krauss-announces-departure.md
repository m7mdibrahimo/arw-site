According to Ringside News, TKO is parting ways with a key official who assisted in uniting WWE and UFC. Seth Krauss, who contributed significantly to the 21 billion dollar merger forming TKO in 2023, shared that he is exiting the organization with his final day scheduled for January 15, 2027.

Krauss posted the announcement on LinkedIn, expressing gratitude following a tenure of over 12 years across Endeavor and TKO. 

> "The time has come to turn the page. While I will have more to say about my future soon, I wanted to take a moment to express my deep appreciation for all those colleagues (current AND former), partners and clients that have made the last 4500+ days an amazing journey. I’ll be transitioning out over the next couple of months, and hope to connect with as many as I can to say thank you. I am proud of what we accomplished and excited for the future here even as I move on to other adventures."

Although his departure becomes official in January, he already stepped down from his executive capacity. A filing submitted to the US Securities and Exchange Commission indicated that Krauss informed TKO of his choice on October 29 and formally relinquished his duties as Chief Administrative Officer and Senior Counsel on November 4, remaining as Senior Counsel until mid-January.

Krauss departs nearly a year prior to his contract expiration set for December 2027, a deal that featured a 2 million dollar annual salary alongside a matching target bonus. Krauss did not disclose his reasons for leaving or his upcoming plans, and TKO has not yet named a replacement for his former duties.
