Emily Jaye is taking a brief break from her independent wrestling career to enjoy a tropical vacation. The standout competitor headed to the beach in the Bahamas to soak up the sun, sharing photos of herself on X wearing a grey-green bikini and sunglasses. Posing near a tree with the ocean in the background, she kept her caption simple by writing, “a little piece of paradise.” It is a well-deserved escape for Jaye, who has built a strong reputation on the indie scene and gained broader exposure this year through appearances on AEW television. For now, wrestling is on hold as she enjoys her time away.
