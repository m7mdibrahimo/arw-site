TNA producer and wrestling coach Lance Storm revealed the key trait he values most when assessing young talent. Speaking in an interview with The Wrestling Classic, Storm shared that unselfishness and the ability to focus on making opponents look good is the most crucial quality in a rising wrestler.

Storm highlighted how wrestlers should pay attention to their actions even when the crowd's focus is on their opponent. He cited Rob Van Dam as a prime example of this mentality. While RVD is famous for his innovative offense, Storm noted that his willingness to take incredible bumps to elevate his opponents was what truly set him apart.

According to Storm, young wrestlers often focus heavily on their own success during their first couple of years in the business. However, he emphasized that real progress happens when a performer realizes the importance of teamwork and understands what is best for the opponent and the overall show.
