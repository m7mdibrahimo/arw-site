Grayson Waller recently reflected on his social media habits and shared the best advice he ever received from AJ Styles regarding online feedback. During an appearance on Stephanie McMahon's podcast What's Your Story, Waller recalled how Styles visited WWE NXT years ago and told him to ignore both positive and negative comments online, advising him that the only opinions that truly matter are those of Hunter, Shawn, and Bloom because they have lived the experience and make the decisions. Waller noted how difficult it is not to check social media when praised, but emphasized that wrestlers today are navigating unprecedented territory. Additionally, Waller praised Styles for being very hands-on with the talent in WWE NXT.
