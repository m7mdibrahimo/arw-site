C4 Wrestling announced its final event at the Preston Event Center in Ottawa will take place on November 27. This show will celebrate the promotion's 19th anniversary, though officials confirmed the company is not closing.

https://x.com/C4Wrestling/status/2112283562632196554

The promotion stated that leaving the venue after nine years was due to circumstances beyond its control. Management expressed gratitude for the history made at the location while focusing on future operations and their upcoming twentieth anniversary.
