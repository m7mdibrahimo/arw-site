Rich Swann's current contract with TNA is reportedly set to expire on December 31. According to Fightful Select, he signed a short-term deal earlier this year after returning to the promotion in the summer of 2025. Throughout this recent run, he has challenged for the World, X-Division, and International titles, while it remains unclear if the two sides have discussed a new agreement.

Fightful previously reported that TNA President Carlos Silva informed talent during a meeting that the company is working to finalize multiple deals expiring in December to allow for creative planning ahead. Additionally, Mustafa Ali's contract is coming up soon, and the promotion wants to re-sign him. Swann is a former TNA World Champion and a former WWE Cruiserweight Champion.
