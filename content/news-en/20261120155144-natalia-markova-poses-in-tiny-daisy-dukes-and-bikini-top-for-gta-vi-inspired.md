Natalia Markova has joined the internet's Grand Theft Auto VI excitement by sharing a beach photo with a strong Vice City vibe. Markova posted a picture on X showing herself posing next to a red sports car on the beach. She wore a black bikini top and very short Daisy Dukes, leaning into the GTA-inspired atmosphere with a direct question for her followers: "How is GTA VI treating you?"

The timing aligns with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, which ended a 13-year wait since GTA V and quickly dominated gaming discussions across social media. The crossover has also reached professional wrestling, as Rockstar included backyard wrestling features in the game, featuring moves like the Rock Bottom, Spear, German Suplex, and Attitude Adjustment. Markova chose to skip performing wrestling moves in her post, relying instead on a red sports car, a beach setting, and a GTA VI question to capture the right mood for the week.
