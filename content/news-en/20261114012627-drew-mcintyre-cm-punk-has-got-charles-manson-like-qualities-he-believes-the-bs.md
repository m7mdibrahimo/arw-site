During a recent appearance on AwayDays for their Shirt Shopping series, Drew McIntyre discussed his past feud with CM Punk. After talking about Henrik Larsson, McIntyre brought up Punk, recalling how he prayed for Punk to get injured. McIntyre did not hold back his criticism, comparing Punk to Charles Manson.

> "He's got Charles Manson-like qualities. That, I'll give it to him. Because he believes the BS he says and everyone follows him like freaking zombies. I'm like, 'Wow, you are totally Charles Manson.'"

McIntyre stated that he constantly pointed out the facts and even has a degree in criminology. He added that people still listen to Punk despite everything, comparing him to Manson again.
