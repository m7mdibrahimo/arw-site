Speedball Mike Bailey recently spoke about who he would like to face in AEW, noting that he wants another singles match with Kenny Omega. Bailey has faced several of All Elite Wrestling's top stars since making his debut with the promotion, but has not faced Kenny Omega outside of a triple threat match at AEW Dynasty last year. The star previously squared off with Omega in 2015 in PWG, and would like a rematch in AEW. 

Speaking in a new interview with 9NEWS, Bailey was asked if there was anyone in AEW he still wants to face, highlighting his desire for a singles match with Omega. "There are so many. There are way too many to name. We have legends in AEW. A great inspiration of mine who's another Canadian pro wrestler, his name is Kenny Omega, and he and I have faced before a long time ago. We had a three-way match in AEW, but Kenny Omega is, in my opinion, one of the best wrestlers in the entire world, and I would love to have another singles match with him in AEW."
