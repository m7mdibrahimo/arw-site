Ludwig Kaiser's battery case has taken a complicated turn as his accuser fights to block the release of medical records. Kaiser's defense team is seeking access to the documents to examine the alleged injuries, but prosecutors have refused to hand them over following an objection from the accuser. According to court documents filed on November 10, 2026, in Orange County, Florida, Kaiser's attorney, Thomas D. Sommerville, filed a motion to compel the release of medical records belonging to Richard Reap. Reap is the individual accusing Kaiser of battery following an altercation on April 23 at an Orlando apartment complex. Reap previously testified under oath that he sustained injuries during the confrontation and sought medical treatment afterward. Kaiser's defense maintains that reviewing these records could reveal whether Reap suffered the claimed injuries or if they resulted from a different cause. Prosecutors initially indicated the records were being prepared for production, but reversed course after Reap's civil attorney, Bruce Mounts, confirmed that Reap objects to the release. The defense is now asking a judge to intervene so the documents can be examined before Kaiser's upcoming pretrial conference on December 17, 2026, with a potential trial window between January 4 and January 22, 2027.
