Kevin Owens is scheduled to appear at a WWE NXT live event on October 17. The show will be held at the Slush Puppie Centre in Gatineau, Quebec, Canada. Other stars advertised for the event include Grayson Waller, Tony D'Angelo, Cruz Montana, Zaria, Shiloh Hill, and the Vanity Project trio of Jackson Drake, Ricky Smokes, and Brad Baylor. Owens is a former NXT Champion, but he has not competed in an NXT ring since the 2019 NXT TakeOver: WarGames event, where he served as the surprise partner for Donovan Dijak, Keith Lee, and Tommaso Ciampa.
