A former WWE extra ruined their chances of returning to the company after attempting to take a photo with The Undertaker. Former WWE Superstar James Ellsworth revealed the story during a recent interview on Developmentally Speaking, recalling his early days working as an extra before signing a full-time contract in 2016. Ellsworth explained that being backstage can be overwhelming for newcomers when legends like Vince McMahon, Triple H, Stephanie McMahon, and The Undertaker are present. Ellsworth credited his prior independent wrestling experience for helping him stay calm around major stars, noting that younger talent might struggle with this. He shared that another extra could not resist asking The Undertaker for a picture, which resulted in consequences. "The first time I went up there I took it all in. I was like, this is cool, but after that again I was, you know, mentally prepared for it. Thank God I didn't go when I was younger cuz I knew how to keep my cool and not, you know, like, 'Oh my God, it's The Undertaker. I need to get a picture,' or anything stupid like that, which it's funny somebody did that. That's why I say it," Ellsworth stated. The individual was never invited back to work backstage for WWE again. Ellsworth emphasized that extras are not supposed to treat the workplace like a meet-and-greet, warning that asking major stars for pictures leads to immediate dismissal. "You know, they were never invited. They were never invited back again. And, you know, those things like you don't want to go there and be like, 'Wow, it's The Rock. I got to get a picture.' Or you're just an extra. They're going to, you know, tell you here's the door," Ellsworth added. While Ellsworth did not name the person or specify when the incident occurred, he highlighted how easily an opportunity can be lost in the wrestling industry.
