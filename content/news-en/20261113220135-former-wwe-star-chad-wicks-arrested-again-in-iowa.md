Legal troubles continue to follow former WWE star Chad Wicks after he was arrested again in Iowa. This marks the third time the 48-year-old has been detained in less than eight weeks. Wicks is currently being held at the Winnebago County Jail in Forest City, though officials have not yet disclosed the specific charges related to this latest incident.

Wicks, known for his time as Chad Dick on WWE SmackDown, has faced recurring legal issues recently. He was arrested on November 4 for failure to appear in court before being released on bond. Additionally, he was detained in late September regarding multiple traffic violations, including driving with a suspended license and failing to provide proof of insurance.

Wicks previously joined the WWE developmental system through OVW before debuting on SmackDown in 2005 as part of The Dicks alongside James Dick. The duo departed the company in 2006, and Wicks retired from professional wrestling in 2007.
