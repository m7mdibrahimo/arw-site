Trey Miguel spoke about his current deal with TNA Wrestling and his future with the company as his contract approaches its expiration. Miguel explained that he has been associated with TNA for nearly a decade, renewing his contract at the start of the year after AEW withdrew its offer, before signing a new deal in June that is set to end at the conclusion of 2026.

In an interview with Cultaholic, Miguel expressed his great happiness working with the promotion and his desire to stay for a long time, noting that he wants to sign a three-year deal instead of the short-term contracts he recently signed. He added that he considers TNA his home and feels grateful for being given repeated opportunities to renew his contract following his knee injury earlier this year.

This follows Miguel's significant success as a singles star and his capture of the TNA International Championship, in addition to recently challenging TNA World Champion Leon Slater, following the departure of the other members of The Rascalz to AEW.
