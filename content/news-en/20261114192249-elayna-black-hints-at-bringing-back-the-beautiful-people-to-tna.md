Elayna Black recently spoke about her ideas for TNA, revealing she has a plan to bring back The Beautiful People if she ever needs backup. Speaking with Women's Wrestling Talk, Black explained that if she grows tired of being nice and needs to handle business, she would consider making that call. She also mentioned the newly formed tag team of Zoey Serrano and Shayna Baszler, noting that their attitude might just give her the exact excuse she needs to reach out to Velvet Sky and Angelina Love. While Black stopped short of confirming any official return, she made it clear she already has a strategy in place should things get too crazy.
