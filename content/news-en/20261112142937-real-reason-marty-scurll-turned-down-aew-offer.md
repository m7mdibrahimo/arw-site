Marty Scurll had the opportunity to sign with AEW during the company's early days in 2019, but he ultimately chose to remain with Ring of Honor. Speaking on Pro Wrestling Culture, Scurll revealed that while money played a role, the deciding factor was that ROH offered him the position of head booker. "Obviously, money was a big part of it, but the biggest part for me was Ring of Honor offered me essentially the head booker position for Ring of Honor," Scurll said. At around 30 years old, he viewed the chance to work in creative for a major promotion as a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity.

Scurll acknowledged that many people might find his decision strange given how AEW subsequently grew, but he wanted to experience the business from behind the camera after years of wrestling. He felt that AEW could have provided another place to wrestle, whereas ROH offered the chance to help run the show.
