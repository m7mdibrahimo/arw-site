Queen Aminata is set to challenge Persephone for the TBS Championship at AEW Fright Night Dynamite. Aminata earned this opportunity after defeating Maya World on this week's episode to become the number one contender for the title. Additionally, the best-of-five series between FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns will continue with their third encounter. The announced card for AEW Fright Night Dynamite on October 24 includes the third match in the best-of-five series between FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns, and Persephone defending the TBS Championship against Queen Aminata.
