Former World Championship Wrestling executive Jim Herd has died at the age of 87. Conrad Thompson first reported that Herd passed away in October. Herd served as the Executive Vice President of WCW from 1988 through 1992 following Turner Broadcasting's acquisition of Jim Crockett Promotions. During his tenure, he was frequently criticized for his creative ideas, many of which relied on celebrities or movie-themed gimmicks, though he was also credited with securing promotional deals that aided the company's business growth. Among his most controversial decisions were his attempts to rebrand Ric Flair with a Spartacus gimmick and his 1991 firing of the legendary wrestler. Herd resigned from his executive role on January 8, 1992, and remained largely out of the wrestling industry thereafter, aside from occasional interviews.
