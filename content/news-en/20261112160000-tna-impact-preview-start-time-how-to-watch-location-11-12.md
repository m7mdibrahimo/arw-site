TNA Wrestling presents a new episode of TNA iMPACT! on Thursday, November 12, 2026. The show takes place at the Dothan Civic Center in Dothan, Alabama, and will air at 9 p.m. ET on AMC and AMC+.

The announced card includes several high-profile matches. Heather By Elegance takes on Elayna Black in singles action, while Mustafa Ali faces Frankie Kazarian. Elsewhere on the card, Moose goes head-to-head with Samuel Shaw, who will have Joseph Sawyer in his corner. Additionally, Elijah squares off against Nic Nemeth.

The lineup also features the TNA Knockouts Tag Team Champions Apex, consisting of Shayna Baszler and Zoey Serrano, who will be in action. Fans will also see the next installment of Destination Next featuring Trey Miguel and Leon Slater.
