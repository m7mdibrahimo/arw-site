Game Changer Wrestling hosted its GCW Bedlam in Boise event on November 15 at the Treefort Music Hall in Boise, Idaho. The show was broadcast on TrillerTV+. The full results from the event are as follows:

Sal Mistretta def. Devious Cass
Team Idaho (Rusty Livings, Jonathan Monroe, and Nick Nightrain) def. Team Utah (Dan Macher, Andrew So Well, and Dalton Hood)
Anakin Murphy def. CPA
Atticus Cogar def. Juni Underwood to retain the GCW World Championship
Rebecca J. Scott def. Manny Lemons
Nick Gage def. Cheeseburger
The Cowboy Way (1 Called Manders and Thomas Shire) def. Alec Price and Marcus Mathers
Megan Bayne def. KJ Orso
