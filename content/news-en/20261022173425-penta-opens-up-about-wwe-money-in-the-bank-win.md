Penta is reflecting on his recent career trajectory after winning the Money In The Bank briefcase just weeks after failing to capture the World Heavyweight Championship from Roman Reigns. Speaking in a new interview, Penta admitted that he sometimes still cannot believe what is happening, but he now fully understands his responsibility to the fans and children who wear his merchandise and mimic his gestures. He also noted that his brother is now on WWE SmackDown while he competes on WWE RAW, making the family dynamic even more exciting.

Regarding the briefcase itself, Penta feels he holds incredible power and intends to cash it in whenever he feels the exact right moment has arrived. He expressed a desire to target Roman Reigns eventually, though he is currently enjoying every moment of carrying the briefcase and performing on television. The full interview provides further details on his mindset leading up to his matches on WWE RAW.
