WWE has bolstered its developmental roster by signing Elly Finch, a former track and field athlete from Michigan State University. According to PWInsider, the 23-year-old secured a developmental contract after participating in tryouts held in January and August 2026. She is now headed to the WWE Performance Center to begin her professional wrestling training.

> "WWE has signed Elly Finch, 23, who took part in January and August 2026 tryouts to a WWE developmental deal, PWInsider.com has confirmed."

Finch brings a diverse athletic background to the company, having competed in shot put and serving as the first girl to play varsity football at Royal Oak High School in Michigan. During the August tryouts, she worked alongside other talents like Josh Bishop and Titus Alexander before earning her spot in the system.
