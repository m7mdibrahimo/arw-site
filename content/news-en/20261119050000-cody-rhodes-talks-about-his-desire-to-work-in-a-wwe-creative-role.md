Cody Rhodes has done a lot in wrestling, from main-event star and multi-time champion to co-founder of AEW. Now he has revealed one more goal for his future: working on the creative team that writes the storylines.

On the latest episode of his show "What Do You Wanna Talk About," Rhodes looked back on helping put together the first All In event in 2018 and called creative work his "American dream." Speaking to his guest Chelsea Green, he explained that the interest goes back years:

> "That was an independent show we made ourselves. One of the things that made me so happy that day was that you were the first person to ask me what I wanted. I remember thinking, oh my God, I have to give her an answer. It was a dream to contribute even a small part to the vision of the show. Stephanie McMahon asked me on her show if I had any interest in that, and I think part of that interest faded, but it is still there inside me."

Rhodes thanked Green for encouraging him to explore his creative side. He also recalled a non-televised 2023 event in Hidalgo, Texas, that he and Seth Rollins produced themselves after bad weather kept the producers away, an experience that brought back his passion for the work. The Undisputed WWE Champion has said in past interviews that his current contract will be his last as a wrestler, and he hopes WWE will give him chances to work behind the scenes after he retires.
