Former WWE star Scotty 2 Hotty recently recalled how his signature dance move, the worm, became a massive hit with fans during his time in the company as part of Too Cool alongside Grandmaster Sexay and Rikishi. Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Garland explained that he first learned the move in the fourth grade and started performing it during house shows when he and Brian Lawler were known as Too Much. He noticed the strong crowd reaction immediately and brought it to WWE TV, where announcer Jerry Lawler helped cement its popularity by spelling out the word on commentary. Garland noted that success often happens organically rather than through strict planning, which is why he always encourages young talent to experiment and see what connects with the audience.
