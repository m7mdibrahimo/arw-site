WWE may be protecting Roman Reigns from taking a pinfall loss as the company builds toward a massive showdown against Oba Femi. Dave Meltzer of Wrestling Observer Radio recently discussed the future championship picture, noting that while Penta is widely expected to eventually defeat Sami Zayn for the Undisputed WWE Championship, WWE seems intent on keeping Reigns strong until Femi enters the title scene. Meltzer and Bryan Alvarez highlighted recent events, including Femi's surprise interference at WWE Crown Jewel to help Penta and Je'Von Evans capture the WWE World Tag Team Championships. Reports from earlier in the fall indicated that WWE was leaning toward a marquee match between Reigns and Femi as the Sunday main event of WrestleMania 43 in Saudi Arabia. With tension already brewing between the two following Femi's post-match challenge at WWE Money in the Bank, speculation continues to grow that WWE is deliberately preserving Reigns' status for a historic clash with Femi on the grandest stage.
