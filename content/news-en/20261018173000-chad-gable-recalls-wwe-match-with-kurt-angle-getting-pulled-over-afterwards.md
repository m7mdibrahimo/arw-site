During a recent appearance on "Insight with Chris Van Vliet," WWE Intercontinental Champion Chad Gable reflected on his memorable singles match against WWE Hall of Famer Kurt Angle on the March 18, 2019, episode of WWE RAW in Chicago, Illinois. Gable noted that the bout felt like a full-circle moment, having met Angle years earlier at an autograph signing and declaring they would one day wrestle in WWE.

Gable revealed that he and Angle benefited from a rare occurrence during Vince McMahon's era of running WWE RAW, as they were given extra time on live television to just wrestle on the fly. While the match and the energetic Chicago crowd made it a wonderful night, Gable added that the experience was only slightly marred by receiving a speeding ticket on his drive home.
