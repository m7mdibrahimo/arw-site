Lance Storm discussed Avery Styles and his career growth, stating according to Fightful that he holds a high opinion of him at this stage. It has been a busy year for Avery Styles, having made his in-ring debut earlier this year before signing with TNA and becoming a regular on their programming.

While speaking in an interview with Love Wrestling, Lance Storm talked about working with Avery and shared his thoughts on the young wrestler.

> "Yeah, it's great because very few people know this. I wrestled AJ Styles once. It was a tag match in Europe. Oh, cool. For like 1PW. It was supposed to be the Impact players against AJ Styles and Christopher Daniels, and Justin didn't make his flight. So we didn't get to have the match we wanted, but I did wrestle AJ Styles on that show. So I've been in the ring with AJ, and I have produced an Avery Styles match or two. So it's great to see the second generation of Styles, and he's a really nice kid, a very respectful kid. I think I… think the world of him. Again, I don't know him well, but every experience I've had has been fantastic, and I'm really curious to see how he carves his own path while also following in his father's footsteps."

Avery Styles was recently dealing with an injury.
