Tonight's WWE SmackDown on October 16, 2026, emanated live from the Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, California, airing on USA Network and Netflix. The show kicked off with Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn addressing the locker room alongside Johnny Gargano and Candice LeRae, discussing his rival and announcing his plans to confront Roman Reigns on WWE RAW next week. United States Champion Trick Williams then interrupted Zayn, leading to a heated verbal exchange about respect and survival. This confrontation culminated in Williams challenging Johnny Gargano to a match later in the evening. In the night's action, WWE United States Champion Trick Williams defeated Johnny Gargano in a non-title match. Following the match, backstage segments featured Nick Aldis addressing Blake Monroe and Chelsea Green, announcing a Street Fight between Monroe and Giulia for next week. Additionally, Nikki Bella confronted Chelsea Green, leading to a future title match being set between them.
