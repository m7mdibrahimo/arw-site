Brooke Hogan revealed that her relationship with her brother Nick has fallen apart following Hulk Hogan's death, stating that his betrayal hurts her more than anyone else's. During an appearance on the Leave Then Cleave podcast, Brooke explained that she feels punished because she went no-contact with both of her parents, noting that Nick has not checked on her or offered her any personal items belonging to their father. Brooke contrasted her dedication during Hulk's fifteen surgeries, where she paid her own way while waiting tables, with Nick living in Las Vegas where their dad had to pay his travel expenses to visit. She described seeing Nick attend WWE events honoring Hulk without inviting her as a dagger in the heart, while still expressing that she wants him to be happy.
