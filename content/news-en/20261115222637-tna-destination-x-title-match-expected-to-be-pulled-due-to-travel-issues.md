TNA Wrestling's Destination X card is facing a major shakeup as the Knockouts World Championship match is in serious jeopardy. Indi Hartwell is no longer expected to reach Edmonton for Sunday's pay-per-view, which means her scheduled title defense against Shayna Baszler could be moved to this week's TNA iMPACT. According to PWInsider, both Hartwell and Xia Brookside are considered extremely unlikely to make it to the show due to ongoing travel disruptions caused by Friday's nationwide FAA flight shutdown. TNA has discussed rescheduling Hartwell and Baszler's championship match for the November 19 episode of iMPACT, while Baszler would instead replace Brookside in the Knockouts Ultimate X match at Destination X. This change clarifies earlier adjustments to the Ultimate X lineup, which had removed Brookside, Gabby Forza, and Jada Stone in favor of Heather by Elegance, Kiera Hogan, and Baszler. The travel crisis has forced TNA to make multiple changes, with flight cancellations and delays leaving several wrestlers stranded across the United States while attempting to travel to Canada. The Knockouts Championship is not the only match affected, as TNA has prepared a backup main event in case Trey Miguel cannot arrive in time for his World Championship match against Leon Slater. Destination X is scheduled for Sunday, November 15, at the Edmonton EXPO Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, with coverage available on TNA+.
