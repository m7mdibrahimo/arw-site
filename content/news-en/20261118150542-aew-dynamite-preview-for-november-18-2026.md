AEW Dynamite airs tonight as a key stop to review the fallout from the Full Gear pay-per-view, featuring a strong lineup of matches and events from start to finish. Tonight features the first appearance of MJF following his surprise return at Full Gear, where he revealed himself as the mystery attacker who targeted AEW World Champion Will Ospreay after he successfully defended his title against Kyle Fletcher.

Continental Classic tournament matches get underway, highlighted by Hangman Adam Page vs. Bandido in the Blue League, while reigning Continental Champion Jon Moxley meets Orange Cassidy and “Speedball” Mike Bailey faces Nick Wayne in Gold League action. In the women's division, TBS Champion Queen Aminata defends her title in a four-way match against AZM, Maya World and Mina Shirakawa. In a long-awaited showdown, Samoa Joe faces Jack Perry after travel problems prevented their scheduled match at Full Gear.

AEW Dynamite takes place tonight at the Mission Ballroom in Denver, Colorado, with television broadcast on TBS and digital streaming on MAX.
