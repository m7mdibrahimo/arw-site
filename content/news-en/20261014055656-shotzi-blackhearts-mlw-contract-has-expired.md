Shotzi Blackheart's run with Major League Wrestling has come to an end after her contract expired last week. According to Mike Johnson of PWInsider, MLW sources reported that they had a positive experience working with her and hope to continue their partnership in the future. Blackheart previously held the MLW Women's World Featherweight Title for over 200 days. Meanwhile, she recently made an appearance at TNA Wrestling's post-Bound for Glory television tapings in Dothan, Alabama, where she competed in a match. Her history with the promotion includes wrestling two matches under the IMPACT Wrestling banner back in 2019, which included a bout against Madison Rayne.
