WWE aired the latest episode of WWE Main Event on October 21 via Rumble. In the opening match, Chelsea Green defeated Lainey Reid. Next, MFT consisting of Talla Tonga and Tama Tonga accompanied by Haku secured a victory against Fraxiom formed by Nathan Frazer and Axiom. Sol Ruca quickly defeated Candice LeRae in just four seconds, prompting Candice to demand a rematch, which Sol Ruca won again. In backstage developments, Bakusai featuring Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki told MFT to leave their father at home, while Penta and Je'Von Evans stated they are not afraid of The Vision. In the final match of the card, Austin Theory defeated Otis.
