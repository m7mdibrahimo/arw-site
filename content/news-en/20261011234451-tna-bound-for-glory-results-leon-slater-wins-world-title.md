TNA Bound for Glory took place on October 11, 2026, at the Yuengling Center in Tampa, Florida. The event featured the induction of Amazing Red, Konnan, and ODB into the TNA Hall of Fame. In the main event, Leon Slater defeated Nic Nemeth to capture the TNA World Championship.

Results:
Ricky Sosa def. Eddie Edwards
Amazing Red def. Fabian Aichner, KC Navarro, Cedric Alexander, Rich Swann, Ryan Nemeth, and BDE
Zoey Serrano and Shayna Baszler def. Allie and Kiera Hogan
Frankie Kazarian won the Call Your Shot Gauntlet Match
Jason Hotch def. Mustafa Ali, John Skyler, and Special Agent Zero
Indi Hartwell def. Xia Brookside
Moose def. Max Caster
syX def. The Hardys
Leon Slater def. Nic Nemeth
