According to Wrestlenomics, WWE NXT brought in 520,000 viewers on the CW Network on Tuesday, October 27, marking a decrease from the previous week's audience of 655,000.

The program attained a 0.09 rating in the 18 to 49 demographic, slipping from a 0.10 demo score recorded the prior week, as it aired opposite Game 4 of the World Series featuring the Milwaukee Brewers and Tampa Bay Rays.
