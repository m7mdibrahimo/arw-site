Top candidates have emerged within WWE creative to win the upcoming Men's and Women's Money in the Bank ladder matches in New Orleans. Reports indicate that Penta and Bron Breakker are the primary favorites for the men's contract, while Roxanne Perez and Lash Legend lead the contention for the women's briefcase. WWE United States Champion Trick Williams was also previously discussed as a potential winner, though it remains uncertain if he is still being considered.

Neither CM Punk nor Kevin Owens are expected to win the men's ladder match. Their current storyline with Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn on WWE SmackDown keeps them as top title challengers without needing the briefcase. WWE aims to preserve as many direct contenders for Zayn as possible while using the Money in the Bank winner to add fresh energy to the WWE SmackDown championship scene.
