Cody Rhodes recently shared his thoughts on the possibility of turning heel in WWE, suggesting that such a shift would look different than many fans expect. Since returning to WWE at WrestleMania 38, Rhodes has maintained a strong position as a top babyface, holding multiple championships during his run. During a recent interview on Unsportsmanlike, Rhodes discussed the potential for a character change, including the prospect of aligning with Paul Heyman.

Rhodes noted that his relationship with Heyman is unique because of Heyman's relationship with his father. He emphasized that he is open to any direction that serves the best interests of WWE, whether as a heel or a babyface. Rhodes also mentioned that he might already be perceived as a heel by some, depending on the audience's perspective. He pointed to a recent moment where he expressed satisfaction in punting Randy Orton, which resulted in cheers rather than the boos he anticipated, highlighting the unpredictable nature of the fan base.

Regarding a potential heel turn, Rhodes stated that it would likely change the nature of his current work. He expressed concern that a villainous version of himself might be less focused on the diplomatic responsibilities he currently holds, such as his work involving Team USA and Team Mexico. Rhodes acknowledged the current climate of outrage and cancel culture, suggesting that a heel version of his character might not fit the tone of shows like Unsportsmanlike. He concluded by noting that Triple H is guiding the creative direction, and if the time is right for a turn, the opportunity will not be missed.
