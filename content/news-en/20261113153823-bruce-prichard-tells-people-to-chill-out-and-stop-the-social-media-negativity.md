Bruce Prichard has had enough of the negativity on social media. The longtime WWE executive took to X on Friday, November 13, to share a blunt message about the fragility of life and the futility of arguing over trivial matters online. While Prichard did not name anyone specifically, he made it clear that he is tired of the constant noise and hostility, particularly when people are dealing with real-life losses.

> "You know what. We are all just human beings. Chill out. There are things in this world more important than social media. Life is fragile. When you lose family and friends, there really is little comfort. Stop with the noise and negativity. Appreciate life and what it delivers."

Prichard did not specify what prompted the post, though he has recently faced personal challenges, including neck issues that have complicated his travel schedule. He has been dealing with a neck injury dating back to 1991 that may require surgery, though he did not link his comments to his own health situation. Prichard emphasized that people should focus on appreciating those in their lives rather than engaging in arguments on social media.
