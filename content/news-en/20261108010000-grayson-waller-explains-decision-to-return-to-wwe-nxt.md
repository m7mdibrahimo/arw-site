NXT Champion Grayson Waller has addressed his decision to return to WWE NXT and revealed what might have happened had he remained on the main roster. Waller came back to the brand earlier this year after spending several years on the main roster, making the move following an invitation from NXT boss Shawn Michaels.

Speaking on Insight, Waller explained that Michaels gave him a choice, which led to his decision to return. He added that he did not view the move as a demotion since he appears on television every week, noting that his current run has been the best he has felt in WWE since his previous time on the brand.

> "Shawn called me and asked me to come and meet up with him. I was given an option by Shawn, and I chose to go and do that."

Waller admitted he likely would have been released from WWE if he had not returned to NXT, citing the talented stars being called up to the main roster while his own television presence diminished.

> "I could see the writing on the wall. They're bringing up these kids from NXT who are super talented and giving them all the chances, while I'm in the background, disappearing. You go lower and lower. You get more forgotten about. I was gone for sure. So, when I say I took a risk to go to NXT, it's a risk because you never know if it's going to work out. But I trust in my ability, and I just needed a platform."
