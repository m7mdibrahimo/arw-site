Carlito believes WWE made a major mistake by having Brock Lesnar end The Undertaker’s legendary 21-0 WrestleMania streak at WrestleMania 30 in 2014. During an appearance on the Hitting the Turnbuckle Podcast, the former Intercontinental Champion argued that the historic victory was wasted on Lesnar, who was already an established star. Carlito suggested that Roman Reigns would have been a better choice to benefit from such a monumental accomplishment.

> "It should have ended with Roman, not with Brock. What did it really do for Brock? Brock didn't need it."

Carlito explained that beating The Undertaker did not make Lesnar any more credible or dangerous than he already was. He felt the company had a singular opportunity to end the streak and should have used it to elevate a younger talent. While he acknowledged that Reigns eventually defeated The Undertaker at WrestleMania 33 in 2017, he noted that the streak was already gone by that time. Carlito’s perspective aligns with others in the industry, including Edge and The Undertaker himself, who have previously questioned whether Lesnar was the right person for the moment.
