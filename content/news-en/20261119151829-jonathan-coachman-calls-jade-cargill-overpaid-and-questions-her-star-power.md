Jonathan Coachman and Vince Russo have criticized Jade Cargill following her recent public comments dismissing suggestions that she should have spent time in NXT. Speaking on The Coach & Bro Show, Coachman questioned Cargill's star power and argued that her current earnings do not match her level of experience in professional wrestling.

Coachman reacted directly to Cargill's claim that she makes too much money not to be featured on WWE television, while also criticizing her advice for unhappy viewers to look away from the screen. He argued that claiming one's name equals money traditionally means moving merchandise and driving ticket sales, comparing her situation to top draws like The Rock, CM Punk, and Cody Rhodes. Russo added that Cargill appears to lack an understanding of fundamental wrestling traditions, noting that the responsibility for her lack of developmental training lies both with her and WWE management.

> "I do agree with her on one thing she said there, Vince. She does make too much money."

> "Because in wrestling, Vince, when you say my name equals money, what does that mean from a wrestling perspective? If a talent like Rock or Punk or Cody say my name equals money, that means they sell what?"

> "Every time she gets into a big match, she messes something up. That's just fact. That's not me hating. That's fact. Go watch the video."

Coachman also highlighted Cargill's limited ring experience, pointing out that she has been wrestling for less than four years and had her first match on television without working on the independent circuit. He suggested that her in-ring mistakes during major matches are a key reason WWE has not pushed her further, while Russo attributed her strong public responses to insecurity over ongoing criticism of her work.
