TNA star Matt Hardy recently addressed reports regarding Shawn Michaels potentially replacing Bruce Prichard in WWE. Drawing from his previous work with Michaels in WWE NXT through the TNA partnership, Hardy noted that Michaels places a heavy emphasis on fast-paced action inside the ring.

Hardy stated that Michaels prefers a rapid tempo with younger talent rather than slowing down the pace of matches. He added that giving Michaels broader authority on the main roster would ultimately benefit fans who enjoy high-energy and athletic bouts.
