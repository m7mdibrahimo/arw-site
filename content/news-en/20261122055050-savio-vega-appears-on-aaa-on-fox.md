Savio Vega might take on a fresh position within AAA. On the November 21 episode of AAA on Fox, Vega was seen backstage talking with Mesias, Mecha Wolf, and Lince Dorado.

The group was discussing the necessity of altering things to return to the win column before Vega approached them and stated that any changes would happen under his direction.

Vega previously made an appearance for AAA as an announcer in May and currently works behind the scenes for the promotion. He also competed as a prominent WWF star from 1993 to 1998.
