Kendal Grey has made a surprise return to WWE NXT, setting her sights on the NXT Women's Championship. During the November 10 episode of WWE NXT, Grey confronted champion Kelani Jordan, demanding a rematch for the title. Jordan, who had been celebrating her recent victory over Paige and her selection for the John Cena Classic, was initially dismissive of the challenge. She insisted that she had already defeated Grey and her associate, and expressed a desire to focus on preparing for the upcoming tournament rather than defending her title again.

However, Grey remained persistent, reminding Jordan of their past encounter and her desire to settle their unfinished business. The situation escalated into a heated argument until NXT authority figure Robert Stone intervened. Stone officially sanctioned the rematch, scheduling it for the November 17 episode of WWE NXT. This development disrupts Jordan's plans for a break, forcing the champion to defend her title against a determined challenger who is eager to take the championship from her before the John Cena Classic begins.
