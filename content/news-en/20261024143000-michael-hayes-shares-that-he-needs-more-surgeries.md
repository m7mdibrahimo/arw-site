WWE producer Michael Hayes spoke about his health and past operations during a recent episode of Stephanie McMahon's What's Your Story? podcast, revealing that he requires additional surgeries moving forward.

> "I had the hip replaced in ‘20, I had this replaced in ‘22, I had this replaced in ‘24. I’m so sick of surgeries, and I need more."

Hayes explained that his shoulders are in very poor condition and mentioned he may need another operation on his previously repaired left hip.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yaJFjnpz9Io

He credited Diamond Dallas Page's DDP Yoga program and losing 50 pounds with significantly improving his mobility. Additionally, Hayes shared that WWE Chief Content Officer Paul 'Triple H' Levesque wants Bobby Roode to succeed him as a producer.
