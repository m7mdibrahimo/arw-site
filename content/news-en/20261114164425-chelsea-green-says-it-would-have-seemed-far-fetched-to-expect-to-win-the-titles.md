Chelsea Green reflected on her current run in WWE during a recent interview with Good Karma Wrestling, admitting that her success would have seemed "far fetched" when she first re-signed with the company. Since returning for her second stint with WWE, Green has captured the WWE Women's Tag Team Championship, the WWE Women's United States Championship, and the WWE Women's Championship. However, she admitted that achieving all of this came as a major surprise to her.

Recalling her release during the pandemic, Green noted that she had lowered her expectations and simply wanted to make the most of any opportunity if she ever returned to WWE. She explained that she never imagined she would accomplish everything she missed out on during her first run in such a short span of time.
