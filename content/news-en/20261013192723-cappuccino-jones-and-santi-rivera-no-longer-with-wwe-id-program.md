Cappuccino Jones and Santi Rivera are no longer part of the WWE I.D. program, following multiple departures from the initiative. According to Mike Johnson of PWInsider, both talents have exited the program but have yet to publicly announce their departures. Jones previously served as the WWE I.D. Men's Champion for 234 days, holding the title from August 2025 until March 2026, and is the current Reality of Wrestling Texas Champion. Rivera, who is 24 years old and has been wrestling since 2021, has competed for promotions such as Create A Pro, Beyond Wrestling, and Reality of Wrestling. Other names, including Laynie Luck, Sam Holloway, and Marcus Mathers, have also departed the WWE I.D. program.
