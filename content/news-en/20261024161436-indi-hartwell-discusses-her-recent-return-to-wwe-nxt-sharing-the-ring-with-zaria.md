Indi Hartwell recently discussed her brief return to WWE NXT and getting the opportunity to share the ring with Zaria. During an appearance, Hartwell spoke about her appearance on the October 6th edition of WWE NXT, where several TNA names competed in a number one contenders battle royal for the NXT Women's Championship. Hartwell admitted she was initially worried fans might not remember her or care since the wrestling business moves fast, but noted that the return gave her a sense of closure. She added that it was special to face fellow Australian Zaria in the ring during the broadcast.
