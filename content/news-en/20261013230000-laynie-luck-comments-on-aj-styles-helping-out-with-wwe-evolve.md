Laynie Luck recently discussed AJ Styles and his backstage contributions to WWE EVOLVE. Following his transition away from in-ring competition, Styles has taken on a behind-the-scenes role with WWE, working as a producer for NXT and EVOLVE. The former WWE I.D. Women's Champion spoke about having Styles around to assist with creative ideas. "When A.J. Styles started popping up, we were like, ‘Oh my freaking God. A.J. Styles is gonna help us with some of these ideas.’ Just the way that he could take something. Like, here’s the vision. How do we get there? He will know how to get you there."

Luck recently lost the WWE I.D. Women's Title after holding the championship for 320 days, and her contract was subsequently not renewed.
