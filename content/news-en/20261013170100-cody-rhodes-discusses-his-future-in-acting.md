Cody Rhodes recently shared insights into his acting career, indicating that the coming months will bring new developments to his filmography. According to Fightful, Rhodes noted that this year will be a productive one for both announcements and taking on additional projects. This follows his notable appearances in The Naked Gun and his upcoming role as Guile in the Street Fighter movie, which premieres this week.

During an interview with ExtraTV, Rhodes expressed a strong desire to refine his craft and embrace new challenges. He credited his motivation to his time on set and conversations with Noah Centineo regarding the film Union County.

> "I think you will, and I think this is going to be a good year for announcements and a good year for doing more. I'd hope to stay Guile forever, but the bug, and I've been talking about this while I was on set, Noah Centineo screened Union County for me, and I was blown away by the depth they were going, the questions they were asking, and I'd love to take a swing, take a challenge, because without films and things like this, I'm not going to get any good at it. So I want to flex that muscle."
