Kit Wilson has shared his thoughts on his WWE partnership with The Miz, explaining that they have formed a strong bond despite their contrasting personalities. Wilson began teaming with The Miz following his split from Elton Prince, and the two have since appeared in numerous comedic segments on "WWE SmackDown."

Wilson expressed deep respect for The Miz's extensive experience and his ability to create opportunities. "It's been unbelievable. As people, we are different. He is loud, arrogant, and brash, and everything that people describe him as. I'm physically strong, but emotionally stronger. I'm a bit of a sensitive, introverted, clever guy. You wouldn't really think we'd get along, but we both want to always put on the most entertaining segment possible. So, we've bonded through that, and we've become really close now. We just had a little holiday vacation in Australia. So, we're closer than ever. But the man is full of wisdom. The man has been doing this for 20 years. He's done it with the best. He beat the best. And he just has so much knowledge," Wilson told "The Sportster."

He added, "He makes opportunities for himself, and he's making opportunities, therefore, for me, and he's teaching me how to make an opportunity and how to take an opportunity."

Wilson, who has been working under The Miz since March, believes this mentorship will elevate his career. He expects significant growth over the next six to 12 months. Wilson noted that while he feels his partner does not always receive the respect he deserves, he is proud to work alongside him in WWE.
