Gurv Sihra of The Bollywood Boyz and formerly of WWE has announced that he received medical clearance to return to the ring following a collapsed lung injury sustained at an independent event.

Sihra was rushed to the emergency room after experiencing breathing difficulties during a Pure Power Wrestling match for the tag team titles alongside his brother Harv. After a month of recovery showing his lungs had returned to normal, his trauma doctor officially cleared him.

> "It's a big thumbs up. 👍🏽 I'm happy to report I officially got cleared by my trauma Doctor today from my pneumothorax injury. Thank you to everyone, both far and near, that has reached out with well wishes, prayers and positive vibes. It's been a strange 2.5 months with some uncertainties, however, anything is possible when you keep a positive mindset & keep the faith. 'Conquer your mind. Conquer the world,'"

Reflecting on his 22-year career, Sihra expressed his ongoing ambitions and eagerness to continue wrestling.
