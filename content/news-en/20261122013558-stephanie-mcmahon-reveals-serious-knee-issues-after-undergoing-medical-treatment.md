Stephanie McMahon has been dealing with some serious knee problems in both of her knees, revealing that they are bone-on-bone. During a conversation on What's Your Story? With Steph McMahon with Diamond Dallas Page, McMahon opened up about her recent medical procedures, explaining that she recently received her first gel injections.

Instead of spreading the treatments out over multiple visits, she chose to get everything done at once. McMahon admitted that the procedure was a bit daunting, especially when she realized the doctors intended to inject both knees simultaneously, but she noted that the experience was ultimately not as bad as she anticipated. She also mentioned that she had previously attempted to improve her mobility through stretching, but pushed herself too hard and caused her knees to swell. McMahon has previously stated that she does not believe she can keep up with the current women's division in another WWE match, and while knee replacement surgery is not currently on the table, she is hoping the injections provide the relief she needs.
