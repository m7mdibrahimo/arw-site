Sunil Singh, known as Gurv Sihra, has announced that he has been cleared following a collapsed lung injury he suffered during a match at a Pure Power Wrestling event in Lethbridge, Alberta, Canada, on September 21. Singh shared the update on November 3, expressing gratitude to everyone who reached out with well wishes and prayers. He noted that the past two and a half months were strange and filled with uncertainties, but emphasized that staying positive and keeping the faith makes anything possible. Singh stated that he is looking forward to returning to the ring and continuing his wrestling journey, adding that he hopes fans will continue to support The Bollywood Boyz as he prepares to make towns again. He also mentioned that he could not ask for a better way to start Diwali week than receiving this medical clearance, having previously noted that he was lucky to be alive following the injury. It remains unknown when he will officially return to action.
