Another week has passed in the world of professional wrestling, culminating in a Halloween weekend featuring the Fright Night edition of AEW Dynamite and WWE NXT's Halloween Havoc. The focus for this week's winners and losers spans several notable moments, from Tiffany Stratton's multiple failed cash-in attempts to Nick Wayne's new character development. Queen Aminata stands out as a winner after capturing the AEW TBS Championship by defeating Persephone in the main event of Dynamite on October 28, capping off a stellar run since July. On the other hand, Tiffany Stratton takes a spot as a loser following three consecutive failed Money in the Bank cash-in attempts across Raw and SmackDown. Nick Wayne earns a win for his refreshed, cocky persona that blends modern streaming culture with his ongoing rivalry with Chris Jericho. Meanwhile, Chelsea Green is listed as a loser as she builds toward her match against Liv Morgan at WWE Crown Jewel 2026 amid low fan anticipation and creative struggles. Finally, Danhausen rounds out the winners for his successful hosting duties during Halloween week across NXT and a special YouTube release, which also advanced storylines involving GUNTHER and Finn Balor.
