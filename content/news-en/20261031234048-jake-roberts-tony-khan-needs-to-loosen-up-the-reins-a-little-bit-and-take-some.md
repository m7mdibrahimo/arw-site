Jake Roberts discussed his thoughts on Tony Khan during a recent interview. Roberts confirmed his departure from All Elite Wrestling in July 2026 after making his debut for the company in 2020. Speaking with Nick Teodoro on the Nick Club Wrestling Happy Hour, Roberts compared his time in WWE and AEW while addressing Khan's leadership style. "There's a lot of differences. Creative number one, no doubt. Tony Khan needs to loosen up the reins a little bit and take some advice," Roberts said. He added that the promotion has a strong product and plenty of talent, believing that the roster can be utilized better. Roberts praised Khan as a sweet guy and stated that he enjoyed working for him. During the interview, Roberts also mentioned that he has re-signed with WWE.
