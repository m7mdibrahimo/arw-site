WWE Hall of Famer Ricky Morton revealed that his Instagram account, @Thebabyface_ricky_morton, was hacked and that the hacker demanded $350 in cryptocurrency to return it. Morton stated that he received no warning or emails from Instagram regarding his two-factor authentication and criticized Meta's lack of support. He explained that losing access to the platform he has built for years is heartbreaking because it is his main way to connect with wrestling fans, share memories, and promote appearances. Morton asked Meta and Instagram for help and warned his followers not to click any suspicious links or send money from the compromised account.
