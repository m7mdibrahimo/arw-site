Dave Meltzer has revealed the financial compensation WWE is set to receive for hosting WrestleMania in Riyadh. Speaking on Wrestling Observer Radio, Meltzer explained that the price for the event is approximately $150 million, noting that WWE wants to protect its lucrative relationship with Saudi Arabia. Despite travel advisories issued by the United States government, WWE Crown Jewel remains scheduled for Riyadh on November 7, 2026. Meltzer noted that if the travel advisory level increases, it could force a cancellation that would serve as a bail-out for the company without straining relations with Turki Al-Sheikh, chairman of the General Entertainment Authority.
