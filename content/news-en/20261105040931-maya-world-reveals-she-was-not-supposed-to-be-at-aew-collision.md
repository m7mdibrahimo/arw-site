Maya World and Hyan discussed the backstage situation regarding Nixon Newell and Miranda Alize leaving an episode of AEW Collision a year ago, according to Fightful.

Speaking on Agent 00's stream, Maya World said, "It kind of just happened. If you know, you know. One day we were just working in Houston It was about a year ago. I might get in trouble for saying this, I don’t know, but two girls — we were not signed to the company yet — two women who were considered to be signed, they walked out and so we got an opportunity to step up. We were not tag partners. When they let us, we stepped up, and Tony [Khan] was like, ‘They’re good. Come back.’"

Hyan added that they were not scheduled to compete, and Maya confirmed she was not originally supposed to be present.
