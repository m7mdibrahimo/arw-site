Juicy Finau has signed on to compete at World Elite Sumo's Pro Era V event, scheduled for November 21 at the Caribe Royale Orlando in Florida. The 30-year-old wrestler, who has appeared for AAA and Major League Wrestling, joins a 12-man open-weight tournament field that includes athletes from various combat sports backgrounds. The promotion announced seven new competitors for the event, including Brian Dina, Duke Webb, and Jahsua Marsh, while confirming the withdrawal of Brennen Moorefield and Jakob Alekseyev due to injuries or undisclosed reasons. Finau, who has also competed in Power Slap, returns to the promotion alongside fellow veterans Rui, Jr., Josh Tuitupou, and Gilberto De La Torre.
