Indi Hartwell captured the TNA Knockouts World Championship at TNA Bound For Glory on October 11, 2026, by defeating Xia Brookside. The intense matchup featured Mickie James as the special guest referee. Throughout the bout, Brookside frequently clashed with James, leading to a chaotic environment that included an exposed turnbuckle and the use of the championship belt as a weapon. Despite these distractions and Brookside's attempts to manipulate the referee, Hartwell remained focused. After surviving a series of attacks and a close near-fall following Brookside's Dark Side maneuver, Hartwell capitalized on an error. She sent Brookside into the exposed turnbuckle and executed the Hurts Donut to secure the pinfall. This victory marks a significant milestone for Hartwell, who leaves Tampa as the new champion of the TNA Knockouts division.
