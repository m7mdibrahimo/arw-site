Avery Styles was seen wearing a walking boot following TNA tapings held earlier this week. The promotion hosted back-to-back television tapings on October 13 and October 14 at the Dothan Civic Center. The Hardys, Matt Hardy and Jeff Hardy, were given full creative control over the first night of tapings, part of which aired during the October 15 broadcast of TNA iMPACT.

According to reports, Styles was not the only talent dealing with physical setbacks after the events. A few other wrestlers were also banged up following the tapings, with Styles requiring a walking boot after participating in the shows.
