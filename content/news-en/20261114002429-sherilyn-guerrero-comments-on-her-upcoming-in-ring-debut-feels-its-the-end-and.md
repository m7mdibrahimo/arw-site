Sherilyn Guerrero has shared her thoughts ahead of her upcoming in-ring debut. On November 14, at Reality of Wrestling's Fall Fury show, Guerrero will step into the ring for the very first match of her career against Mystii Marks. This milestone arrives just one day after the anniversary of the passing of her father, WWE Hall of Famer Eddie Guerrero, on November 13.

Taking to social media, Guerrero opened up about what this moment means to her, stating:

> "A lot of people have asked me what this debut match means to me. I feel like it's the end and beginning of such a big chapter that's to come. Especially the fact that it's a day after my dad's anniversary is pretty surreal. I feel like it's time for me to show everyone that the vulnerable, naive girl that was here at the beginning of the year through this process is now ready to just make my mark as best as I can, and which better way to do it than in the ring? It's gonna be good."
