TNA confirmed it has ended its relationship with former AEW star Max Caster following his debut at TNA Bound for Glory 2026, where he faced Moose. Before the match, Caster delivered a rap that included a reference to a missile strike on Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport, which resulted in 12 deaths and over 300 injuries over the weekend. Fans noticed that TNA deleted all social media posts regarding Caster from the event, and the promotion later issued an official statement announcing his departure.

> "During Sunday's Bound for Glory pay-per-view, insensitive comments were made by Max Caster referencing attacks in Riyadh that killed and injured many people," the statement read. "Making light of violent attacks is wrong. This should never have made it into the show, and that responsibility is ours. Our thoughts are with the victims, their families, and everyone affected. We sincerely apologize."

TNA stated that it removed the segment from event replays and social media channels while taking steps to prevent similar incidents in the future. Former TNA International Champion Mustafa Ali shared the statement on his X account along with an apology.

> "Our sincere apologies about this incident," Ali wrote. "Thank you to management for addressing this matter."

Prior to the event, Moose issued an open challenge that was answered by Caster on social media. Caster recently became a free agent after his AEW contract expired. In 2021, Caster was previously suspended by AEW and underwent sensitivity training following a controversial rap segment.
