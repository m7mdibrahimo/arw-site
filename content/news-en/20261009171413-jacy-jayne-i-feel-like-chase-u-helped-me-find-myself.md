Jacy Jayne recently reflected on her time working with Chase U in WWE NXT, explaining how the experience shaped her career. Jayne was part of the group from late 2023 to early 2024 before eventually splitting off and entering a feud with Thea Hail. Following her departure from the stable, she went on to capture both the TNA Knockouts World Championship and the NXT Women's Championship. Speaking in a recent interview, Jayne noted that working alongside the group pushed her out of her comfort zone since she was accustomed to playing a mean character. She explained that trying out a supportive role helped her discover her true identity outside of tag-team wrestling.

> "Getting to play off of that many people at one time and the stories that we were telling, I thought it was so much fun and I kind of had to test myself a little bit, because I always was the evil, mean girl. So trying to be like the supportive friend was a little different for me. … and honestly, I really feel like Chase U helped motivate me and help me find myself as just me, Jacy Jayne, and not me as a tag-team wrestler."

In addition to discussing her time in the stable, Jayne also touched upon the differences between independent wrestlers and collegiate athletes. Looking ahead, she is set to compete in the Women's Money In The Bank match this year.
