Paul Wight considers Darby Allin to be the ultimate babyface in professional wrestling. In a recent interview with First Coast Living, Wight expressed his admiration for the current TNT Champion, noting that he is a "huge fan" of the young star. Wight, a multi-time world champion, praised Allin's business focus, character work, and toughness. He also jokingly noted that if he were to return to a villainous persona, Allin would be his ideal opponent. Wight stated, "Darby is the ultimate. We have a term in our business called baby face. Darby is the ultimate babyface. I'm such a huge fan of that kid."
