Andrade El Idolo continued his usual ringside selfie routine during AEW Collision before getting ambushed by Tommaso Ciampa. During the event, Andrade stopped by the front row to take a photo with a fan, but the moment was interrupted when Ciampa suddenly attacked him and turned the interaction back into a competitive feud. After the show ended, reports revealed the identity of Andrade's latest selfie partner. Independent wrestler Leah Night confirmed through her Instagram Story that she was the woman sitting at ringside. Night has competed in various independent wrestling promotions such as Marvelous, SSPW, and ASE, joining a growing list of independent female wrestlers who have participated in Andrade's recent ringside segments, which previously featured Madisyn Maxxwell and Kelly Madan.
