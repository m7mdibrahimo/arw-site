Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron captured the AEW Women's World Tag Team Championships at AEW Full Gear on November 14, 2026, ending the title reign of The Brawling Birds. In the title match, Statlander and Harley Cameron defeated Jamie Hayter and Alex Windsor to secure the gold.

The contest saw both teams trade momentum early, with The Brawling Birds utilizing their tag-team experience to manage the pace. As the match progressed, the action intensified with Hayter and Windsor focusing their attacks on Cameron, only for her to fight back and tag in Statlander. The final moments turned chaotic when The Brawling Birds attempted their "Two Birds One Stone" finisher on Statlander, but Cameron intervened to pull Hayter out of the ring.

With the champions distracted, Statlander capitalized by hitting Windsor with "Saturday Night's Fever" to secure the pinfall victory. The win marks the first time Statlander and Cameron have held the AEW Women's World Tag Team Championships together.
