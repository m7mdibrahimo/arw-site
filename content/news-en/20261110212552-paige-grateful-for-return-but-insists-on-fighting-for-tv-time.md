Paige returned to WWE at WrestleMania 42, winning the WWE Women's Tag Team Titles alongside Brie Bella. Their reign ended at WWE SummerSlam, which triggered a turn by the Bellas and led to a feud between Nikki Bella and Paige. After seeing segment times reduced during the story, Paige addressed the situation publicly.

> "I do appreciate every single second that I get to be on TV and in that ring. The fact that I managed to return after eight years, I am so grateful for that. But that doesn’t mean I won’t put my foot down when it comes to TV time," Paige told Metro. "The more, the merrier with them on TV. They work their ass off. The men work their ass off too, but you know how it is. I am vocal, and this time around I’m always gonna be that person. I think WWE knows that already. And I feel like they love me for it. At the same time, it might be a pain in the ass to deal with me sometimes!"

According to Fightful, Paige last competed on the November 4 episode of WWE NXT, where she challenged Kelani Jordan for the NXT Women's Championship.
