During a recent appearance on Busted Open Radio, WWE star Joe Hendry faced a tough dilemma when Nic Nemeth asked him whether he would rather have Logan Paul fired from the company or win a WWE world title himself. Hendry admitted it was a difficult choice, noting that both scenarios would be incredible for the business. He explained that firing Paul would change the course of history, but ultimately confessed his own ambition to become world champion. Hendry's rivalry with Paul began when he joined the WWE RAW brand in the spring of 2026, even releasing a music video about the social media star. While Hendry has previously held world championship gold in TNA, he is still chasing his first world title in WWE.
