Jade Cargill discussed her core training routine and fitness approach in a recent video for Women's Health. The WWE Superstar explained that exercising helps her clear her mind and generate ideas while preparing her body for the ring. She trains her core five days a week with exercises like planks and bicycles. Cargill noted that while working out is therapeutic, physical appearance is also a welcome benefit.
