Tony D'Angelo recently opened up about the emotional challenge of watching other wrestlers get called up to WWE RAW and SmackDown while he remains in WWE NXT. Speaking on Busted Open After Dark following his victory over Myles Borne on the October 13 episode of WWE NXT, D'Angelo admitted that it is a natural human reaction to feel frustrated when peers bypass you.

> "I mean, yeah, that's—I feel like that's a natural human reaction, to see somebody kind of bypass you. But I also think that it's out of my control to an extent." Despite the wait, D'Angelo confirmed that company officials like Shawn Michaels, Matt Bloom, and Johnny Russo have assured him that his prolonged stay in WWE NXT is strictly a matter of timing rather than his performance. He also credited Terry Taylor for his continued guidance and emphasized that quitting has never been an option for him, regardless of how long it takes to reach the main roster.
