Roman Reigns commented on his World Heavyweight Title defense against LA Knight at Money in the Bank, where he successfully retained his belt. Following the match, Reigns spoke with Cathy Kelley about his current focus and the pressures of being champion.

> "At this point in time, the main thing is just recover, try to get back to a place of health where I feel good because like you said, I got the whole world coming after me. Everybody wants what I have. This is the position. This is the burden. This is where you want to be. You want to be center stage. You want to be the one that holds the spotlight. Can't you feel it? Can't you feel the envy and the jealousy that just seeps back here? Everybody wants this. Before not too long ago, this was second prize. This was second fiddle. Now it's the number one most coveted prize in the game because the Tribal Chief made it that way. But this is what it is. This is the position and this is what I was born for. Always ready."

Reigns also discussed his busy weekend traveling to New Orleans and his thoughts on facing LA Knight.

> "It's a big weekend. You know what I mean? I knocked this out straight to LA for the Street Fighter premiere. It's a hell of a weekend. But this was priority right here. If I didn't come to New Orleans, if I didn't hold it down, if I didn't whoop LA Knight, which I gotta give him his respect. He runs his mouth, but he came in there and he backed it up, but he just ran into a better competitor today. I don't want to see him down the road, but man, this was the prerequisite to get to Monday, and I'm just happy I was able to do it."
