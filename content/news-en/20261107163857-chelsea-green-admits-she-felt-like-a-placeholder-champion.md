Chelsea Green acknowledged that she previously felt like a placeholder champion, admitting to lingering self-doubt. Green currently holds the WWE Women's Championship, though she initially served as an interim titleholder while Rhea Ripley recovered from a knee injury sustained at WWE Clash In Italy.

During WWE Crown Jewel, Green meets WWE Women's World Champion Liv Morgan for the Crown Jewel Championship, following build-up where Morgan applied the placeholder label to her. Speaking with Corey Graves on WWE's Countdown show, Green discussed her mindset.

> "I hate to say this, but I also felt that way. You know, like there was this little nugget of doubt in my mind. I'm thinking of myself as the underdog, and if Liv beats the underdog, what did she do? She just beat the underdog. But if I beat Liv, I beat the greatest champion of all time. I need to prove that I am the permanent champion. I need to prove it to myself."

Separately, Nikki Bella discussed in another interview how Chelsea dressed like her during a WWE Live event and for their recent WWE Women's Championship match.
