Veronica West has confirmed that her run with WWE EVOLVE concluded following her match on the October 7 episode. West announced the news shortly after the event, expressing gratitude to her opponent, Airica Demia, for their final encounter.

> Tonight was my final match on WWE Evolve. I’m grateful it was against you 💚🤍🫀🪽 @Airica_Demia Free on Tubi!

West clarified that while her time with the WWE developmental pipeline is over, she remains fully committed to her professional wrestling career. She encouraged fans to continue following her journey as she returns to the independent circuit, where she previously competed under the name Fallyn Grey. West, who is 22 years old and has been wrestling since 2023, noted that she looks forward to further progression in the industry.

Prior to her recent departure, West had been part of the WWE ID program, where she was known as Veronica Haven. Her time in the industry has included championship success, such as holding the Arizona Wrestling Federation Women’s Championship and the Fighting Spirit Pro Radiance Championship. West indicated that she intends to keep her fan base, the Angel Army, with her as she pursues new opportunities on the independent scene.
