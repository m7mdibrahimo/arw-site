Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre hosted its Domingo Familiar event on Sunday, November 15, 2026, at Arena Mexico in Mexico City. The card featured several matches in front of a live crowd.

Kaligua, Shockercito & Último Dragoncito def. Metales Explosivos (Full Metal, Pequeño Pólvora & Rostro de Acero)
Shoma Kato & Okumura def. AtrapaSueños (Rey Cometa & Espíritu Negro)
Kira & India Sioux def. God's Eye (Syuri & Ami Sohrei)
Dulce Gardenia, Volcano & Fuego def. Máquina de la Destrucción (Terrible, Elemental & Máquina Letal)
Hermanos Chávez (Ángel de Oro & Niebla Roja) def. Reyes del Inframundo (Guerrero Maya Jr. & Xelhua)

Following the fifth match, House of Torture launched an attack on Reyes del Inframundo.

Hechicero, Volador Jr. & Bárbaro Cavernario def. Esfinge, Neón & Templario (w/ Francesco Akira)
