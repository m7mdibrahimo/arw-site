AEW has confirmed that every wrestler scheduled for the main Full Gear pay-per-view card has successfully arrived in Phoenix, following a nationwide flight disruption. Tony Khan announced that the main card remains unchanged, avoiding the need for any last-minute adjustments. While the main event talent arrived, Khan revealed that the 7 PM ET Tailgate Brawl has been affected by travel delays. Khan noted, "On the Tailgate Brawl, there have been some delays and changes. I am gonna have to change some plans around, and the brawl is coming up very soon." The travel issues stemmed from a nationwide FAA ground stop on November 13 caused by a telecommunications outage. Despite the backlog, AEW utilized charter flights to ensure the main card talent reached Phoenix in time.
