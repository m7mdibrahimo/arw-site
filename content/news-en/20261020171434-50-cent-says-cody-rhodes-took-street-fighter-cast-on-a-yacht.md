The cast of the upcoming Street Fighter film, including rapper 50 Cent and actor Noah Centineo, recently shared high praise for WWE superstar Cody Rhodes and his work on set. Speaking on Not Sam Roberts, Centineo noted that Rhodes improvised much of his dialogue and brought great humor to his role as Guile. Meanwhile, 50 Cent, who portrays Balrog in the movie, jokingly recalled how Rhodes treated the cast to a luxury yacht outing, leaving him stunned by the grand gesture. The film also features appearances by fellow WWE superstar Roman Reigns and former IWGP Heavyweight Champion Hirooki Goto.
