Seth Rollins recently shared his thoughts on the upcoming World Heavyweight Championship match at WWE Money in the Bank, stating that Roman Reigns could easily defeat LA Knight in just thirty seconds. Speaking on ESPN's Get Up ahead of the event in New Orleans, Rollins explained that Reigns is currently operating on an entirely different level.

Rollins knows Reigns well after facing him in the main event at SummerSlam 2026, where Reigns retained his title. Betting odds currently reflect Rollins' prediction, with Reigns heavily favored to win. However, LA Knight remains confident and has consistently targeted the champion during recent weeks on WWE RAW.

> “Oh man. I mean, this is kind of one of those matches where if there were Vegas oddsmakers on this one, Roman’s a heavy favorite here. Heavy favorite in my book.”

Rollins believes that while Knight is a tough competitor, the current version of Reigns is simply too dominant. Fans will see whether Knight can pull off a major upset or if Rollins' prediction turns out to be accurate.
