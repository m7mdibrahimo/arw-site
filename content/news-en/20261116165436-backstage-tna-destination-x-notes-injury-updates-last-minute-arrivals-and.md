TNA faced major backstage chaos leading into TNA Destination X in Edmonton, Alberta, dealing with multiple flight cancellations and injuries. According to PWInsider, TNA X-Division Champion The Amazing Red was sidelined with an undisclosed injury, while Avery Styles also suffered an ankle injury. The travel situation proved equally stressful, as Trey Miguel barely made the show after his connecting flight was canceled, arriving just in time to challenge Leon Slater for the TNA World Championship. Meanwhile, TJP and John Skyler also arrived at the arena at the last possible minute to compete in the Ultimate X match, which was won by KC Navarro.

Other notable talent could not make it to Canada at all due to stranded flights. Moose, Nic Nemeth, Ryan Nemeth, Xia Brookside, and Indi Hartwell were all forced to miss the event. Consequently, Hartwell's planned defense of the Knockouts World Championship against Shayna Baszler was pulled, and the scheduled TNA World Tag Team Championship match was scrapped entirely alongside adjustments to both Ultimate X matches. TNA now hopes the roster can assemble smoothly for television tapings on Wednesday and Thursday in Calgary.
