Monica Monroe recently reflected on a viral moment involving Booker T. Back in July 2025, Booker T accidentally referred to Blake Monroe as Monica Monroe during an episode of WWE NXT. The mistake quickly gained traction online, and Monica herself eventually addressed the mix-up.

In a new interview with Love Wrestling, Monica Monroe shared that the incident provided a significant boost to her online presence. She stated, "Yeah, I loved it. Honestly, Booker got me a lot of followers on X from that, just for saying the name."

Monroe also discussed the lighthearted "Monroe Sisters" dynamic that emerged from the confusion. She noted, "There's the three Monroes [Me, Blake, & Chantel], everyone wants a faction. I got to say I was the first one though. I was the first. Five years ago, I was Lady Bird Monroe." Additionally, Monroe revealed that AJ Styles was the individual who extended an invitation for her to attend a tryout during SummerSlam weekend.
