Stigma is finally set to defend his title. CMLL announced that Stigma will defend the CMLL World Lightweight Championship against Calavera Jr. I. This will mark his first title defense since July 2022. Stigma successfully defended the championship three times prior to this upcoming match. As of this writing, his current title reign has lasted for over 1,700 days.
