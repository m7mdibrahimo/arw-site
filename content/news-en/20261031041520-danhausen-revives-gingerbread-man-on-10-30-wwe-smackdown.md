Danhausen revived the Gingerbread Man on the October 30 episode of WWE SmackDown. The character was originally killed by WWE Champion Sami Zayn on the May 1 edition of the show, and a subsequent funeral for him was crashed by Zayn. Danhausen eventually secured possession of the Gingerbread Man. During this week's episode, Trick Williams and Lil Yachty were interviewed by Cathy Kelley in a backstage segment. As they left, Yachty discovered a room marked "Top Secret." Inside, he found Danhausen standing with the revived Gingerbread Man.
