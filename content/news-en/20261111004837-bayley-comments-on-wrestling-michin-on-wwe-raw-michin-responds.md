With Survivor Series: WarGames approaching, tensions remain high across the WWE women's division. Despite this, Bayley recently took to social media to share high praise for her recent opponent, Michin, following their encounter on the November 9 episode of WWE Raw. Bayley wrote that she had waited a long time for her match against Michin, calling her one of the most talented women on the roster, someone who fights for every opportunity, and a future world champion. However, she jokingly added that it was too bad Michin has now turned to the dark side. Michin quickly responded on social media, keeping their on-screen rivalry alive while teasing a future clash. She wrote that she cannot wait to deliver another chest-crushing dropkick. The two wrestlers previously met in a Women's Money in the Bank qualifying match in 2023, and earlier that same year, Bayley defeated Michin in a singles bout on WWE Raw. Before signing with WWE, the pair also teamed up in SHIMMER under the names Davina Rose and Mia Yim. In recent weeks, Michin has aligned with Fatal Influence alongside Jade Cargill and B-Fab, while Bayley has received backing from Charlotte Flair, Alexa Bliss, and Tatum Paxley amid their cross-brand feud.
