WWE Hall of Famer Booker T believes CM Punk's current tenure in WWE is superior to his initial run with the company. Speaking on his "Hall of Fame" podcast, Booker T praised Punk as a natural leader and a role model, noting that the star is now in a much better position to succeed. "Guys like CM Punk, you need guys like that," Booker T said. "Look at his career now. This run is better than his first run in so many aspects. And I think he would probably say the same thing in so many aspects because the things he was worried about the first time around, he's not worried about those things anymore." Since his return at the 2023 Survivor Series, Punk has secured two World Heavyweight Title reigns, become a three-time Undisputed Champion, and competed in high-profile matches, including a recent tag team appearance with his wife, AJ Lee. Last week, Punk challenged Grayson Waller for the NXT Championship on a special episode of WWE NXT and faced Kevin Owens on WWE SmackDown to address their long-standing rivalry.
