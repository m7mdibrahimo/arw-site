Former WWE NXT wrestler Bobby Steveson, formerly known as Damon Kemp, is set to release a new song with Grammy-nominated singer Jimmie Allen. Steveson shared a teaser on his Instagram page featuring a phone call from Allen, with the caption, "We're ready, are you?" Allen was nominated for Best New Artist at the 2022 GRAMMY Awards and won the Country Music Association Award for New Artist of the Year in 2021, while releasing his latest album, Tulip Drive, in 2022. Steveson's WWE contract expired in July 2024 and was not renewed, after which he has competed at various independent events and survived a lightning strike.
