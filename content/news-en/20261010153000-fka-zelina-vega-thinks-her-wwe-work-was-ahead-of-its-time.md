Former WWE Superstar Zelina Vega, now known as Xelina, believes her character work and physical presentation in WWE were ahead of their time. Known for constantly changing her hair color and style for matches, Xelina expressed frustration that fans were not fully able to keep up with her evolution. She hopes that viewers watching her past matches will eventually appreciate her innovative approach and recognize that her creative vision was far ahead of its era.

"Eventually, down the line, you guys will look back and go, 'Wow, Zelina was actually way ahead of her time,'" she said on her podcast with Dakota Kai. Xelina was released from WWE on April 24, 2026. As an independent competitor, she has since wrestled for House of Glory, defeating Brittnie Brooks and Shayna Baszler, and has appeared twice for PROGRESS Wrestling in England against Myla Grace and Nikki Storm. She and Storm are scheduled to face Grace and Alexxis Falcon at PROGRESS Wrestling Chapter 199: Bonestorm on October 25.
