Following the merger of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery, CBS Sports and TNT Sports are folding into a single operation under Skydance, leading to expected layoffs and job consolidation, according to Ringside News. With the company aiming for massive financial savings, industry insiders warn that labor cuts are unavoidable due to overlapping staff and executives.

David Berson will lead the combined sports division and must remain direct with employees while the new structure takes shape over the coming weeks. Alongside staffing reductions, Skydance is reviewing its vast portfolio of broadcasting rights to trim unnecessary expenses. While major properties like the NFL, March Madness, and UFC are considered untouchable, other deals face close scrutiny. Meanwhile, AEW’s future within the newly merged media landscape remains uncertain as the company awaits upcoming contract decisions.
