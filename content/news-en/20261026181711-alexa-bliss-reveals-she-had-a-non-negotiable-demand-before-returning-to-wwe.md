Alexa Bliss shared that having her daughter Hendrix travel with her was a non-negotiable demand before returning to WWE in 2025. Speaking on Shot of Brandi, Bliss explained that leaving her young daughter behind for several days every week was not an option, especially with her husband Ryan Cabrera constantly touring as a musician. Bliss noted that Hendrix is already getting comfortable with the backstage environment, even learning how to count a pinfall.
