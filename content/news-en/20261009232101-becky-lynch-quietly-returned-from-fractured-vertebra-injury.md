Becky Lynch has been dealing with a more serious injury than previously known. Her husband, Seth Rollins, revealed during an appearance on The Rich Eisen Show that Lynch was sidelined for approximately six weeks due to a fractured vertebra.

> "She had a little fracture in one of her vertebrae that—so she was out of action. She couldn't wrestle for six weeks or so."

Rollins admitted that he was not sure if he was supposed to disclose the information, as it was not widely publicized by WWE. He noted that he feels concerned about her back whenever she competes, especially given the physical nature of her upcoming Triple Threat match for the Women's World Championship at WWE Money in the Bank.

Lynch recently returned to the ring on WWE RAW, where she faced Liv Morgan. Rollins confirmed that the match against Morgan was her first bout back since the injury. Despite the recent recovery, Lynch is moving straight into a high-stakes title match involving Stephanie Vaquer.
