Johnny Gargano discussed the differences between the opportunities available to the current NXT roster and those from his time on the brand. Gargano was a mainstay of WWE NXT from 2015 to 2021, holding the NXT Championship and featuring in several of the era's top feuds and matches. However, he believes today's generation of NXT stars enjoys superior main roster opportunities compared to his tenure. During a recent interview with MickeyBlog.com, Johnny Gargano stated that the current NXT roster receives better opportunities than he and his peers did.

> "Yeah, man, it's such a different time period from when we were in NXT compared to now. Guys get such a better opportunity. We would actually kill for a chance like this to have like a, co-promotion with Raw and SmackDown. We were begging for stuff like that during the Black and Gold time. To be able to do that now, it's so, so great for these young guys and girls that are able to just kind of get such a great spotlight that they deserve because they work incredibly hard and they're incredibly talented."
