Tony Khan quickly shut down a false claim on social media regarding the ratings battle between AEW Dynamite and WWE NXT. Ahead of the official numbers for the November 4 episode, a X account falsely claimed that WWE NXT drew 921,000 viewers with a 0.30 demo, while AEW Dynamite finished with 609,000 viewers and a 0.26 demo, calling it a massive victory for WWE. However, the actual published ratings revealed the exact opposite. AEW Dynamite drew 792,000 viewers on TBS with a 0.24 rating in the 18-49 demographic, while WWE NXT drew 552,000 viewers on The CW with a 0.13 rating. This gave AEW Dynamite a 240,000 viewer lead over WWE NXT, showing a significant increase from the previous week's 620,000 viewers. Upon seeing the bogus claim, Tony Khan responded by posting a GIF with four words:

> "You’re a pretty good liar."
