The October 24, 2026, episode of AEW Collision took place at the LJVM Coliseum in Winston-Salem, North Carolina, featuring intense matches and ongoing rivalries. Jay White appeared with the Bang Bang Gang and The Dogs to announce that his group is now known as The Last Outfit. He issued a challenge to Jon Moxley, while Kyle O'Reilly confronted White later in the evening. Josh Alexander challenged Darby Allin for the TNT Title before Adam Copeland attacked Alexander and PRG Vice, only to be laid out by Mark Davis and Jake Doyle.

FTR def. Motor City Machine Guns
Death Riders def. STP
Josh Alexander def. Blade Brown
Swerve Strickland def. Myron Reed
United Empire def. Steve Allen & Robert Allen
Wrathlander def. Divine Dominion via disqualification

Following the United Empire match, Tommaso Ciampa attacked Andrade El Idolo, but Mike Bailey made the save. In the main event, Wrathlander secured a victory by disqualification against Divine Dominion. After the match, the Triangle of Madness assaulted Wrathlander. Willow Nightingale attempted to intervene, but she was ultimately laid out by Divine Dominion.
