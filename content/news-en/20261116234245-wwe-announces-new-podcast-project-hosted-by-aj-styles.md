WWE announced that a new podcast hosted by AJ Styles will premiere on Wednesday, November 18. The upcoming show, produced as an original project by WWE and Fanatics, will feature Styles revisiting iconic matches from wrestling history. Episodes will be released weekly on YouTube and major podcast platforms.

The announcement comes nearly two months after Styles suddenly ended his previous independent podcast, The AJ Styles Show, in September. He originally launched the project in March alongside producer Tony Giles, using it to discuss news, video games, and his behind-the-scenes scouting responsibilities with WWE. When the original show was canceled after seven months, Giles stated that the channel would be repurposed, though neither he nor Styles explained the reason for the split.

While neither WWE nor Styles has officially confirmed that the new deal caused the September shutdown, the timing aligns with his transition to an official WWE-backed production. The new format moves away from general wrestling discussions to focus strictly on historic matches.
