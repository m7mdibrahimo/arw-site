WWE star Alexa Bliss recently shared her experience participating as a scare actor in the Wyatt-themed haunted house at Universal Studios Halloween Horror Nights. Bliss reflected on the emotional weight of stepping into the attraction, noting that Bray Wyatt had always wanted to have a haunted house at the event.

She described walking through it for the first time as feeling like a gut punch, especially knowing he never got to see it come to life. Bliss also discussed getting to work on the Firefly Funhouse scene, where she stood next to a television playing footage of Windham Rotunda over and over.

While she felt honored to be part of the tribute, she admitted that it was deeply heartbreaking. Bliss shared that the experience felt like a personal way of saying goodbye and returning to where her dark journey with Wyatt truly began.
