The final edition of TNA Impact before TNA Bound For Glory featured multiple title matches, high-stakes gauntlet qualifying action, and major news regarding Shayna Baszler joining the promotion. Wrestling Inc. breaks down three things we hated and three things we loved from the show.

What we hated:
- The backstage segment between Zoey Serrano, Allie, and Kiera Hogan felt like an unnecessary detour before their tag team match announcement, though the eventual match setup is intriguing.
- Fabian Aichner and KC Navarro delivered an incredible match, but it simply ended much too soon.
- The main event featuring the Hardys and the Nemeths felt slow and uninspired, culminating in a predictable attack by the Wyatt Sicks.

What we loved:
- The eight-man tag match with Call Your Shot Gauntlet implications featured fantastic heel work and engaging dynamics, particularly from Joe Alonzo and Trey Miguel.
- The Knockouts Television Championship match between Ash By Elegance and Elayna Black delivered simple yet effective storytelling with another time-limit draw.
- Shayna Baszler is officially heading to TNA to team with Zoey Serrano, which will be a tremendous addition to the Knockouts division.
