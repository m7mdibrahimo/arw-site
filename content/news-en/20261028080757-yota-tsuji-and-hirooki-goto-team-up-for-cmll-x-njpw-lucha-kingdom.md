CMLL and NJPW added new matches to the upcoming Lucha Kingdom event, which is scheduled to take place at Arena México on November 16th, according to Fightful. The updates feature IWGP Heavyweight Champion Yota Tsuji joining forces with Hirooki Goto to battle Místico and Último Guerrero. In addition, a singles contest between Bárbaro Cavernario and Konosuke Takeshita was officially announced for the lineup.

Los Hermanos Chávez (Ángel de Oro & Niebla Roja) (c) vs. United Empire (Francesco Akira & Templario) vs. Unbound Co. (Taiji Ishimori & Titán)

Syuri (c) vs. Kira

Konosuke Takeshita vs. Bárbaro Cavernario
