CJ Perry recently opened up about her transition from managing Rusev to becoming an in-ring performer in WWE, revealing that she faced unexpected locker room challenges once she started wrestling. Speaking on Milk and Honeys with Kayla Braxton, Perry explained that wrestling was by far the harder side of the business for her, noting that she always felt more confident on the microphone and as a manager. Once WWE put her in the ring, she realized she was far from polished and was essentially learning how to wrestle in real time while millions of fans watched on television.

While she understood why fans might question her readiness, the online criticism was not the only hurdle she faced. Perry admitted that she never really experienced locker room issues until her matches began taking up valuable television time, which can cost around $100,000 a minute. Although she did not name anyone specific or accuse anyone of resenting her, she understood why the situation created tension, as turning down an opportunity was simply not an option. Instead of walking away, Perry chose to work extremely hard, spending her days off training at NXT to make the most of every opportunity.
