WWE Hall of Famer Mick Foley recently criticized President Donald Trump on Instagram regarding the possibility of declaring a national emergency ahead of the midterm elections. Foley stated there is no crisis to justify such an action and expressed concern that many Americans seem comfortable with the idea, calling it a sad statement on the state of the country. The discussion surrounding an emergency declaration originated in August when conservative commentator Wayne Allyn Root suggested that Trump use emergency powers to implement changes like photo ID requirements for voting without congressional approval. While Trump did not commit at the time, he has since left the door open to the possibility in an interview with Time magazine. Foley previously criticized Trump earlier this month over comments regarding Los Angeles and San Diego, noting that he feels strongly enough about the situation to continue speaking out.
