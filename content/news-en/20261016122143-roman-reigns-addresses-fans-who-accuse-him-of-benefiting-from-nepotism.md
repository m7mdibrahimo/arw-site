Roman Reigns recently discussed the criticism surrounding his family's legacy and the accusations of nepotism during an appearance on Club Shay Shay. Reigns acknowledged that the Anoa'i name has played a significant role in his wrestling journey, but emphasized that carrying such a legacy brings immense pressure and responsibility.

> "I mean, you know, that’s the way we look at it. This is our family business. We want to always be a part of it. Sometimes you can do that in, you know, like a quick stint and a flash and just boom — you know, like, you can be very popular — and sometimes you have a different route, like Rikishi, where you go through a lot of different phases."

Reigns explained that he views WWE as his family business and believes every generation has contributed to keeping their connection to the industry alive. He noted that stepping into the ring carries the weight of his entire lineage and the groundwork laid by his ancestors, rather than just representing himself.

> "I’m representing my entire, you know, lineage, right? Everyone that came before me, all my ancestors that laid down the groundwork that I could do what I do and live at this — you know, live this life that I’m living — it’s all due to that. So, I try to rep that every day."
