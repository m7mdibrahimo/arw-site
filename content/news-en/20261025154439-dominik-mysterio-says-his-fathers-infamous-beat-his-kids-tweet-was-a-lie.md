Dominik Mysterio addressed an infamous tweet from his father, Rey Mysterio, where he stated he was planning to beat his kids. While speaking with Ring Rust Radio, Dominik explained that the social media post from over a decade ago was completely false, adding that his father has never defeated him in a video game. Dominik joked that his father is a compulsive liar regarding the claim. Elsewhere in another interview, Dominik discussed his current championship aspirations in WWE.
