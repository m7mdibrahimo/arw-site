Steven Borden may not be ready to return to the ring just yet, but his recent appearance offers an encouraging sign in his recovery process. According to PWInsider, Borden was spotted in Cleveland ahead of the latest episode of AEW Dynamite. While fans quickly began to wonder if the second-generation star was preparing for a surprise appearance following his scary injury at AEW All Out, reports indicate that his presence in town does not necessarily mean he is returning to television.

AEW frequently brings injured talent to show tapings to meet with company medical staff for evaluations, which is believed to be the primary reason for his trip. Borden has been away from television since suffering a frightening head injury at AEW All Out that forced his match to be stopped. Shortly after the incident, Tony Schiavone shared that Borden was feeling fine, experienced no headaches or dizziness, and was mainly worried about ruining the pay-per-view.

While there is still no official timeline for when Borden will be medically cleared to compete, seeing him back around the promotion marks another positive step forward in his recovery. Fans should temper their expectations regarding an immediate in-ring return, but his presence in Cleveland highlights his ongoing progress following one of the most concerning moments of the year.
