Lucha Libre AAA aired the second week of AAA Heroes Inmortales on October 31 from the Gimnasio Olímpico Juan de la Barrera in Mexico City. The event was broadcast on FOX Latin America and YouTube.

In the opening segment, Laredo Kid accepted an open challenge from Rey Fenix, but Jack Cartwheel interrupted, claiming he was the one answering the challenge. Takuma then entered, stating he was also participating. Fenix ultimately declared he would face all three men.

In other pre-match developments, Pagano gave Psycho Clown one final chance to leave AAA. Psycho Clown refused and was subsequently attacked by El Carnicero.

In the results:

AAA World Tag Team Championships: The Wagner Brothers (El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr. and Galeno) def. Los Vipers (Histeria and Taurus) to retain the titles.

El Carnicero and Pagano def. Los Psycho Circus (Dave The Clown and Murder Clown). Following the match, Pagano and Carnicero continued their attack. Psycho Clown attempted to make a save but was also taken out.

AAA World Cruiserweight Championship - Four-Way Match: Rey Fenix def. Jack Cartwheel, Takuma, and Laredo Kid to retain the title.

During the event, Dorian Roldan was seen on the phone inquiring if he would receive an answer by next week.
