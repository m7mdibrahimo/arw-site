Jonathan Coachman has expressed major concerns about how WWE is presenting the faction 946 on television. Ahead of Survivor Series, Coachman argued that WWE has skipped over vital character details and urged the group's members to push back against the creative team.

Coachman pointed out several glaring narrative gaps, noting that Cruz Montana was abruptly shifted from a babyface in NXT to a heel faction without any explanation, and questioned whether casual viewers even know the names of the group's powerhouse members. He also highlighted Jaida Parker's recent remark about being 'misunderstood,' stating that it perfectly sums up the lack of backstory provided for the faction.

With Survivor Series only weeks away, Coachman believes WWE needs to quickly give fans a reason to care about who these people are before the storyline progresses any further.
