WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green successfully defended her title against Nikki Bella on the October 30 episode of WWE SmackDown. Out of storyline, the WWE Hall of Famer was flattered by Green dressing up as her "Fearless Nikki" persona for Halloween. Bella revealed to Justin Dorazio of SportsRadio 94WIP that she was thrilled by the tribute, especially seeing fans and wrestlers recreate her signature moves.

> "Especially when I'm seeing everyone hit the booty shake, and it's like, you get super flattered," Bella said.

Bella added that her mother called her on the way to the airport to alert her about Green's live appearance, which brought a genuine smile to her face. Meanwhile, Green prepares to challenge for the Crown Jewel Championship in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, against Women's World Champion Liv Morgan.
