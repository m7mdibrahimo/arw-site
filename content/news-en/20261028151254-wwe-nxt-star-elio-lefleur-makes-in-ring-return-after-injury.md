Elio LeFleur has officially returned to the ring after a lengthy recovery period following surgery. Before the October 27 episode of WWE NXT at the Performance Center, LeFleur competed in a dark match, marking his first appearance since suffering an injury in March.

Following the match, he confirmed his status on social media:

> "Cleared."

LeFleur sustained the injury at a Lakeland live event, which forced him to vacate the WWE Speed Championship and step away from television. After undergoing surgery on April 7, he is now healthy enough to compete again, though his official return date to NXT television remains unannounced.
