Jeff Cobb recently discussed his arrival in WWE and the lengthy list of potential ring names he was given before settling on JC Mateo. During an appearance on INSIGHT with Chris Van Vliet, Cobb recalled receiving two pages of suggestions backstage at WWE Backlash in May 2025. The first page featured around 60 names inspired by his Guam and Hawaii background. However, the second page included some bizarre choices, such as JONAH, a name already made famous by Bronson Reed. Drew McIntyre also inspected the list and found a personal favorite that left everyone amused. "He on the second page he saw one he's like I like this one and I was like well we're not using that and he's like oh he signed it so it's a Drew McIntyre authentic signature which I'm not going to sell cuz it's funny but it was uh Jafar Smash." Cobb noted that McIntyre continued to call him Jafar whenever they crossed paths. Ultimately, WWE debuted him as JC Mateo on the May 16, 2025 episode of WWE SmackDown.
