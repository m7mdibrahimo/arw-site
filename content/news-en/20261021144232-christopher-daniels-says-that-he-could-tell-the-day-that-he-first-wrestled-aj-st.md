Christopher Daniels reflected on the early days of his rivalry with AJ Styles and noted that he knew early on that Styles was destined for greatness. Daniels and Styles have shared a storied history both as opponents and tag team partners across TNA and various promotions worldwide, with both currently retired from full-time in-ring competition. Speaking in an interview, Daniels praised his history with Styles and recalled their first encounter at an NWA anniversary show before TNA was even established. "All good AJ Styles stories, man. Every time I got to wrestle him was a pleasure. I wrestled him all around the world. I tagged with him all around the world," Daniels said. He added, "I saw greatness in him early on. Before TNA was a thing, I got to wrestle him at the NWA tournament, 53rd anniversary show. That was the first time I ever wrestled him. You could tell that day that he was going to be something special."
