Willie Mack has opened up about his current status with AEW, revealing that he is no longer under a guaranteed salary and has been moved to a per-appearance pay structure. Speaking with Wrestling Epicenter, Mack explained that he is technically still under contract with the company but is simply waiting for them to call him. However, those calls have been rare, noting that he has not been used by AEW since December.

Mack discussed his financial struggles after losing his regular salary, recalling that he previously asked AEW for at least two bookings a month just to cover his rent, which ultimately never happened. Despite being shifted to per-appearance pay, Mack claimed that AEW still restricts him from taking certain outside independent bookings. He added that independent dates do not make up for the guaranteed money he lost from his previous AEW deal, leaving him in an uncertain limbo without regular pay or full freedom.
