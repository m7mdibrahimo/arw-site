Kurt Angle has gone viral for his response to a person who recently came out of the closet. In a Cameo video, the WWE Hall of Famer encouraged the individual to embrace who they are before sharing some signature words of wisdom. "It's okay to suck sometimes. If you're going to suck, do it with intensity, intelligence, and integrity."

Angle is no stranger to internet virality, having captured public attention over the years through his expressive facial reactions and heartfelt advice to fans. In September, reports revealed he was dealing with medical issues that prevented him from traveling to the UK.
