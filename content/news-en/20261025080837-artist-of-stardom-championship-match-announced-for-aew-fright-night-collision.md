The Artist of Stardom Championship will be defended during an upcoming AEW broadcast. According to Fightful, the promotion is taping a title match for AEW Collision at the Chaifetz Arena in St. Louis, Missouri, coinciding with the AEW: Fright Night Dynamite event on October 29.

Triangle of Madness, consisting of Thekla, Julia Hart, and Skye Blue, will defend their titles against STARDOM representatives Hazuki, Koguma, and Fuwa-chan. The champions previously retained their gold at Stardom To The World in September, where a post-match brawl with the challengers set the stage for this contest.
