Following Max Caster's controversial departure from TNA after the Bound for Glory 2026 event, reports have emerged regarding whether WWE played a role in the decision. Caster was released shortly after his debut due to a rap performance that referenced missile strikes in Riyadh, the site of the upcoming WWE Crown Jewel event. However, according to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, sources within WWE have denied any involvement in TNA's decision to cut ties with the former AEW star. Meltzer noted that key personnel in WWE were reportedly unaware of the situation until TNA President Carlos Silva had already finalized Caster's release. The report also highlighted that TNA producers failed to review the content of Caster's rap beforehand, which led to the incident. Caster, who was working under a per-date agreement, previously faced disciplinary action in AEW for offensive content in his raps.
