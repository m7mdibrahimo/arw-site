Drew McIntyre remains grateful for his fans, but he has set a firm boundary when it comes to autograph hunters at airports. During an appearance on AwayDays' Shirt Shopping, McIntyre reflected on his two-decade career, noting that he still values interacting with genuine supporters. "That's what Drew Hollywood does. I still have time for my fans. I'll never forget them. I've been doing this for 20 years, and I still feel the same way. I'm incredibly lucky to have my fans because there was a time when nobody came to see me or cared about me. I wasn't in a good position on the show. I got fired," he said, referencing his 2014 release. However, McIntyre draws the line at people who approach him with stacks of photos or merchandise intended for resale. "And I know they got me to where I am. And I see how some people behave now, and I get it, sometimes they go too far. Don't come to the airport with a bunch of pictures. That's ridiculous," he stated. He clarified that he is happy to chat or take a photo if someone runs into him naturally, provided he is not occupied with private matters, but he urged fans to avoid treating airports like a marketplace for autographs.
