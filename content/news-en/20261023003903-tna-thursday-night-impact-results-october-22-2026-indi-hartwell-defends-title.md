TNA Thursday Night Impact aired on October 22, 2026, from the Dothan Civic Center in Dothan, Alabama. In the main event, Indi Hartwell successfully defended her Knockouts World Championship against Xia Brookside. After the match, Shayna Baszler and Zoey Serrano attacked Hartwell, with Baszler putting the champion to sleep using the Kirifuda Clutch.

Cedric Alexander emerged as the new number one contender for the X-Division Championship by defeating Rich Swann, Fabian Aichner, and KC Navarro in a Four-Way Match. Alexander was assisted by Brian Myers and Bear Bronson during the contest. Amazing Red, the current X-Division Champion, confronted Alexander following his victory.

Additionally, Avery Styles defeated Joe Alonzo in his first match on the program. Earlier in the night, TNA Director of Operations Daria Rae returned from her suspension and engaged in a verbal confrontation with Indi Hartwell. In other segments, Jada Stone was granted a Knockouts Television Championship match by Ash By Elegance, and Jason Hotch was announced to defend his International Championship against Mustafa Ali in a 2-Out-Of-3 Falls Match.
