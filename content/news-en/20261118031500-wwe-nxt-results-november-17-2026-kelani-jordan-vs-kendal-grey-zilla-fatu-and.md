The November 17, 2026, episode of WWE NXT took place at the Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, and aired on The CW and Netflix. The main event featured Kelani Jordan facing Kendal Grey. During the first hour, Kam Hendrix defeated Tony D'Angelo. John Cena invited Izzi Dame to the John Cena Classic, while ZARIA confronted Dame after the latter bragged about her tournament spot and challenged ZARIA for the NXT Women's North American Title. ZARIA responded by hitting Dame with an F5. Additionally, John Cena invited Shiloh Hill to participate in the John Cena Classic. Chantel Monroe defeated Layla Diggs, and Grayson Waller interrupted a conversation between Mason Rook and Robert Stone to advocate for Shiloh Hill as a worthy contender for the NXT Title.

In the second hour, Zilla Fatu defeated Dion Lennox, who was accompanied by Cutler James and Osiris Griffin. Elsewhere, Chantel Monroe attempted to mock Thea Hail regarding her alopecia. WWE also presented a tribute to the late Bill Watts. Other results included Tristan Angels winning a Triple Threat match against Saquon Shugars and Sean Legacy.
