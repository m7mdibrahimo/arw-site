WWE has announced the lineup for the October 19 episode of WWE Raw. Roman Reigns is scheduled to return to the red brand following his victory over LA Knight at Money In The Bank. Reigns is expected to address his upcoming match against Sami Zayn at WWE Crown Jewel.

Additionally, the show will feature a singles match between Joe Hendry and JD McDonagh. Hendry has spent the last several weeks mocking McDonagh regarding the size of his head.
