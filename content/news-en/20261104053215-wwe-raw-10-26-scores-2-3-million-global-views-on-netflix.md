Tudum reports that the October 26 episode of WWE Raw drew 2.3 million global views on Netflix, ranking seventh globally and eighth in the United States. This marks the lowest viewership since August 31, when the show did not place in the top ten with the tenth-place finisher drawing 1.8 million views. For comparison, the October 19 episode drew 2.5 million global views, ranking ninth globally while failing to chart in the United States. The top five most viewed WWE Raw videos on YouTube included Solo Sikoa addressing The 946 with 1.6 million views, Tiffany Stratton attempting a Money in the Bank cash-in on Liv Morgan with 683,000 views, and Sami Zayn defeating Je’Von Evans following an attack by Bron Breakker on Penta with 533,000 views. Rounding out the top videos were Liv Morgan facing Becky Lynch in a Women's World Title match with 462,000 views and Bayley defeating Lyra Valkyria in a No Disqualification match with 432,000 views.
