Rob Van Dam recently opened up about his complicated relationship with the late WWE Hall of Famer Scott Hall, explaining that he refuses to rewrite history or pretend everyone he met in the wrestling business was a great person. Speaking on his podcast, Van Dam recalled several uncomfortable encounters with Hall over the years, including a backstage incident where Hall interrupted a conversation with Paige while seemingly intoxicated, and an awkward bar meeting in England where Hall talked about personal struggles. Van Dam also shared a story from Katie Forbes about how Hall abruptly ended a conversation the moment Van Dam approached. While acknowledging that many fans would have loved to spend time with Hall, Van Dam made it clear that his personal experiences with him as a peer weren't always positive.
