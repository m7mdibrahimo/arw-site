TNA Wrestling has removed Rich Swann and BDE from the lineup for Sunday's Destination X pay-per-view, further complicating the event as the company deals with ongoing travel issues. According to PWInsider, the Men's Ultimate X match, which was originally set to feature six competitors, will now include only Joe Alonzo, Cedric Alexander, John Skyler, and KC Navarro. The reason for the removal of Swann and BDE has not been officially confirmed, but the show has been heavily impacted by the nationwide FAA flight shutdown that occurred on November 13, leaving many wrestlers struggling to reach Edmonton, Alberta.

The travel disruptions have also forced major changes to the Knockouts Ultimate X match. Fightful Select reported that Xia Brookside, Gabby Forza, and Jada Stone were removed from the card, with Shayna Baszler, Heather by Elegance, and Kiera Hogan named as replacements. Additionally, Indi Hartwell has confirmed she is unable to travel to Edmonton to defend her Knockouts World Championship against Baszler. TNA is currently discussing moving that title defense to the upcoming episode of TNA iMPACT.

The main event for the TNA World Championship is also facing uncertainty. Trey Miguel has been attempting to reach Edmonton for his match against Leon Slater, and TNA has reportedly been preparing a backup main event. TNA President Carlos Silva stated on November 14 that the company is forced to adjust the show due to the travel nightmare. Despite these challenges, TNA is moving forward with the event, which now includes a match between Mustafa Ali and Frankie Kazarian. Destination X is scheduled for Sunday, November 15, at the Edmonton EXPO Centre.
