The John Cena Classic is currently being organized, with John Cena personally inviting participants across WWE's major television programs. While stars like The Miz have publicly expressed interest in joining the tournament, Ethan Page feels that participating would be a mistake. During an interview on Ringer Wrestling, Page criticized the format, which allows fans to determine the winners of bouts through voting rather than in-ring outcomes.

> "If I am to be invited to a tournament that, even if I physically decimate my opponent, I can still lose because of the voting power of our moronic fans, I would be an idiot to enter this tournament."

Despite his frustration, Page noted that he would evaluate his options if he were to receive a formal invitation. He also voiced his displeasure regarding the gift packages sent to invitees, which include tournament merchandise and a watch. "I am not in the hurry to be awarded merchandise with another man's name on it and a free watch," he stated, adding that he prefers wearing gear with his own name. Page, who is currently the WWE Intercontinental Champion, noted that he is still determining his future path within the company.
