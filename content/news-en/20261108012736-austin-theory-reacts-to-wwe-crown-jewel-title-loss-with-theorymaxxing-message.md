Austin Theory recently lost the WWE World Tag Team Championships at Crown Jewel, but he made it clear that the defeat has not ruined his evening. Following the loss alongside Bron Breakker to Penta and Je'Von Evans, Theory took to social media to share a carefree reaction centered on his confidence, appearance, and dinner plans. Despite a chaotic finish that saw interference from Maxxine Dupri, Bronson Reed, and a surprise appearance by Oba Femi who took out Reed, Penta and Evans capitalized to secure the victory and the titles. Theory opted not to complain about the chaotic circumstances, focusing instead on his post-match mindset.
