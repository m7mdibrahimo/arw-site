WWE is launching the John Cena Classic on December 14 at the ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex in Orlando, Florida. According to Wrestling Observer Radio, the event features main roster talent facing NXT wrestlers, and club members will vote for the male and female winners during commercial breaks.

Dave Meltzer and Bryan Alvarez discussed the tournament, noting that match outcomes will not dictate the winners since fans decide through a popularity contest. A competitor could even lose a match and still be named a champion. Meltzer stated that the bouts are exhibitions that will not lead to guaranteed title shots or storyline advancements.

> "The point is, is that it doesn't matter in the sense that the winner is not defending a championship. The winner is not going to be leading to a match. They made a point of exhibition matches. Exhibition meaning that they don't count, supposedly."

Cody Rhodes and Zilla Fatu are confirmed for the lineup. Meltzer indicated the company might use the showcase for NXT talent already lined up for main roster moves, though those decisions depend on existing plans rather than fan votes.
