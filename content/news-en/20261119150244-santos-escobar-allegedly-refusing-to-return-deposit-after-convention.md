Things are tense between former WWE star Santos Escobar, now known as Romeo Malverde, and promoter Christopher Ruffus after a flight cancellation turned into a financial dispute over a $500 deposit. Salvatore Wrestling Promotions publicly criticized Malverde on Facebook, stating that Ruffus booked him directly and paid the deposit in September for an appearance at The Big Event NY. Widespread flight issues prevented Malverde from reaching New York, and though he attempted to change his flight to Saturday, he ultimately remained stuck in Orlando.

Ruffus stated that Malverde refuses to honor their previous agreement or return the deposit for an uncompleted job caused by the travel stoppage, leaving him stuck with $400 worth of merchandise. In messages, Malverde argued that bookings require a 50 percent retainer and asserted that a potential March date would be a new event with different terms rather than a simple rescheduling. The dispute escalated when Malverde informed the promoter that his attorney would be reaching out.
