Shiloh Hill secured a victory over NARAKU at NXT Halloween Havoc. The singles match was originally scheduled to take place earlier in the show, but NARAKU ambushed Hill in the backstage area as he walked to the ring. Both wrestlers spat orange mist at each other, transporting them into a cinematic setting where they experienced hallucinations. After finally crossing paths within this surreal environment, they crashed through a wall that led them straight to ringside. The match ended when Hill used fog to counter NARAKU's mist before defeating him to claim the win.
