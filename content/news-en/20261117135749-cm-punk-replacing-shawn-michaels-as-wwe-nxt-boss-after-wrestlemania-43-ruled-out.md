Reports claiming CM Punk is preparing to take over WWE NXT from Shawn Michaels following WrestleMania 43 are inaccurate, according to Dave Meltzer on Wrestling Observer Radio. Meltzer explained that Punk's current multi-million dollar WWE contract, which has five years remaining, prevents him from leaving his top-star status to become head coach.

Meltzer noted that Bruce Prichard's administrative duties could not be handled entirely by Michaels, with Ed Koskey and Bobby Roode stepping into those roles. Additionally, Punk recently informed fans at a live event in Detroit that he plans to wrestle for another five years.

Meanwhile, AJ Styles has been linked to changes within NXT, though his expected role focuses on scouting and the WWE ID program rather than running the brand entirely. This leaves Punk active as a WWE Superstar for the foreseeable future.
