Shelley Martinez has walked back her recent comments regarding Maria Kanellis after realizing she was mistaken about who she was discussing. Martinez previously used her TMI podcast to revisit past grievances with Kanellis, speculating that she was the woman involved in a controversy mentioned by Barbie Blank, known to WWE fans as Kelly Kelly. However, Martinez revealed that after the episode went viral, industry contacts informed her that Kanellis was not the individual Blank was referring to.

> "Amongst everyone texting me in the business, I found out who it was that that not Maria that Barbie was talking about and it wasn't Maria."

Martinez admitted she was wrong but maintained she never stated her suspicion as a confirmed fact. She also shared that Kanellis and Blank have since mended their relationship, with Blank even assisting Kanellis in finding a fertility doctor. Martinez noted that had she known this information beforehand, she would not have brought up their history involving an ex-boyfriend. Despite the error, she defended her decision to speak out, claiming that several high-profile individuals in the wrestling business reached out to thank her for addressing topics they felt uncomfortable discussing publicly. Martinez stated she has found closure regarding her long-standing resentment toward Kanellis and is ready to move on.
