Chad Gable recently opened up about losing his Intercontinental Championship to Ethan Page on the November 2 episode of WWE RAW. Speaking in an Instagram Q&A video after finishing a workout on his current road trip, Gable acknowledged the setback but promised fans he would reclaim the title. Reflecting on his career, he also shared his favorite tag team match with former partner Jason Jordan. Gable recalled a memorable night in Blackpool, England, where the lively crowd chanted his name continuously before the bell even rang, creating an unforgettable atmosphere that helped them connect deeply with the audience.
