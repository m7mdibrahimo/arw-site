WWE Hall of Famer Booker T recently shared his thoughts on Adam Copeland and his upcoming in-ring retirement during an episode of the Hall of Fame podcast. Booker expressed great respect for how Copeland handled his career, noting his successful return following a serious neck injury and his subsequent run in AEW.

> "He did it his way. You got to respect that, man," Booker said.

Copeland previously retired in 2011 due to spinal stenosis before making his dramatic return at the 2020 WWE Royal Rumble. After leaving WWE for AEW, he held both the TNT Championship and the AEW World Tag Team Championship alongside Christian Cage, and he now aims to capture the AEW World Championship before hanging up his boots in 2027.
