Samantha Irvin is defending her account of her WWE exit, directly responding to fans who accused her of lying about her career path. The exchange began after Irvin reiterated that she did not leave WWE to pursue music. A fan pointed to the songs she released after her departure, arguing that her timeline did not make sense. Irvin quickly pushed back, explaining that making art independently is not the same as entering the music business.

> "the chick who has independently released songs since 2013 and had a 2 year non compete made art. just cause your nana knitted you a sweater doesn’t mean she’s started a clothing line, bruh."

Irvin previously clarified that WWE actually offered her a record deal before her exit in October 2024, which she declined because her true passion was managing wrestlers. She noted that her recent songs were just independent projects created for fun with former Cirque du Soleil colleagues. Her explanation counters reports from Bryan Alvarez and Dave Meltzer at the time of her departure, which suggested she left to focus on her music career.
