Skadi recently opened up about the heavy burden and honor of carrying on the legacy of her mother, Marcela, who is widely considered one of the greatest female wrestlers in Mexican wrestling history. Marcela holds the record for five reigns as the CMLL World Women's Champion and famously paved the way for women in the industry, traveling the world and competing in Japan. In an interview, Skadi reflected on how difficult it was at the beginning of her career to step out of her mother's massive shadow and deal with constant comparisons regarding her career and experience. Despite the physical and mental toll of living up to such a legendary figure, Skadi expressed immense gratitude for her mother's guidance, support, and the freedom she allowed her. She emphasized that she is proud to be Marcela's daughter and is fully committed to honoring her legacy with great pride. Additionally, Skadi touched upon facing Olympia ahead of the 2026 CMLL Amazonas Universal Championship Tournament finals.
