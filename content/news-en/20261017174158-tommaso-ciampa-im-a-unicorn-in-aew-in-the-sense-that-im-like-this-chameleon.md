Tommaso Ciampa discussed his ability to transition between being a babyface and a heel in a short amount of time during a recent interview with The Sportster. Since joining All Elite Wrestling, Ciampa has operated without a fixed place on either side of the roster, adapting his alignment based on his ongoing rivalries, such as his recent programs with Kazuchika Okada and Andrade El Idolo.

> "I'm a unicorn in AEW in the sense that I'm like this chameleon. I can be a bad guy on Monday and a good guy on Friday. It's crazy to me. With Okada, I kind of was more the good guy, and now with Andrade, I'll probably be a little bit more of the bad guy. It's very interesting. I haven't really aligned myself with a group. It's cool. I'm in my own lane, and I'm really getting to just explore that, so I really enjoy that a lot."

In the interview, Ciampa noted that he enjoys staying in his own lane without aligning with a specific group. He also touched upon the exceptional talent of Andrade El Idolo during another recent conversation.
