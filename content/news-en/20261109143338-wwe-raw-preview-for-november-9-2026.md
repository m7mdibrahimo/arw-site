The November 9, 2026 episode of Monday Night RAW will feature fallout from the Crown Jewel event. Roman Reigns faces multiple issues after chaos erupted on the November 2 show when The 946 attacked The Bloodline. Cruz Montana has since joined the group alongside Royce Keys, Carmelo Hayes, Bronco Nima, Lucien Price and Jaida Parker as Survivor Series approaches.

Sami Zayn is also a focal point after he hit Reigns with a Helluva Kick prior to their match at Crown Jewel, which saw Reigns emerge victorious. Reigns must now address those Brooklyn actions alongside the aftermath of that bout.

The program emanates live from the Bon Secours Wellness Arena located in Greenville, South Carolina, kicking off at 8 PM ET. Viewers can stream the entire broadcast live on Netflix.
