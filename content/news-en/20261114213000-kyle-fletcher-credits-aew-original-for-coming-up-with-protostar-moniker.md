During a recent appearance on "What Happened When," Kyle Fletcher revealed how he came up with his "Protostar" moniker while serving as the ROH World Television Champion. Fletcher explained that he was discussing names for a young star with producer Jimmy Jacobs and AEW star Angelico, who then searched online and suggested the term "protostar."

Fletcher added that the nickname caught on organically after he used it in a promo, eventually becoming a defining part of his identity. Around the same time, he also started using the phrase "say my full name" after receiving a text message suggesting he incorporate it into his promos.
