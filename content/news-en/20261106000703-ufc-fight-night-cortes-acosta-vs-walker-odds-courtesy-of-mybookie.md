UFC Fight Night: Cortes-Acosta vs. Walker is scheduled for Saturday, November 7, at the Meta APEX in Las Vegas, Nevada. The event will be available for streaming on Paramount+. Betting odds for the card are provided by MyBookie. 

The main event features Waldo Cortes-Acosta (-238) facing Johnny Walker (+181) in the heavyweight division. In the women's strawweight division, Virna Jandiroba (+167) will take on Tatiana Suarez (-218). Other bouts include Davey Grant (+242) vs. Elijah Smith (-330) in the bantamweight class, and Seok Hyeon Ko (-752) vs. Wellington Turman (+470) at welterweight. The event also features Billy Elekana (+250) vs. Lucas Fernando (-340) at light heavyweight, Andre Petroski (+234) vs. Mantas Kondratavičius (-319) at middleweight, and Thomas Petersen (-163) vs. Gabriel Lorenço (+127) in the heavyweight division. 

Additional matchups include flyweight bouts featuring Karine Silva (-240) against Gabriella Fernandes (+182), and Mitch Raposo (+183) against Marcos Degli (-240). The featherweight division sees Jose Delano (-123) face Murtazali Magomedov (-103), while Ollie Schmid (+246) meets Keiichiro Nakamura (-340), and Austin Bashi (-625) takes on Carlos Calderon (+400). Finally, Priscila Cachoeira (+360) faces Nina Milosevic (-526) at bantamweight, and Jose Souza (-240) meets Jonny Parsons (+182) at welterweight. Fightful will provide full coverage of the event on Saturday.
