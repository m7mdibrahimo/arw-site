WWE star Cody Rhodes recently discussed his future in the ring and admitted that retirement might happen sooner than later. Speaking in an interview, Rhodes explained that his decision is largely driven by his family and wanting to be there as his daughter grows up. He also praised John Cena's retirement approach, noting how much he respected the finality and honesty of Cena's farewell. Furthermore, Rhodes expressed his hope to eventually step away while helping put over a rising star on his way out.
