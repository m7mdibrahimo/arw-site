All Elite Wrestling is marking Thanksgiving alongside familiar faces such as Glacier, Curry Man, and Scotty 2 Hotty. Paul Wight led a holiday dinner featuring special guests like Mercedes Moné, Darby Allin, Steven Borden, Divine Dominion made up of Megan Bayne and Lena Kross, and Scotty 2 Hotty, with Curry Man and Glacier joining Scotty for the event.

Trent Beretta and Rocky Romero arrived with only a single turkey leg, which disappointed Wight. Alternatively, New Level consisting of Kofi and Austin Creed provided a proper turkey that served everyone present.

AEW Dynamite is scheduled to take place on Thanksgiving Eve, whereas AEW Collision will air on Thanksgiving night according to Fightful.
