Dylan Bostic released a new diss track titled Cancel Me, taking direct aim at Joey Janela and mocking his Bad Boy nickname following their real-life brawl in Indianapolis. Bostic used footage from their January 2025 fight to promote the song on social media. In the lyrics, Bostic referenced Janela's tough talk and the moment he flipped a table during their confrontation, boasting that it took a whole group to break up the fight. The bad blood between the two dates back to December 2020, when Bostic accused AEW of cutting his pay, prompting Janela to mock his wrestling career. After years of online tension and Janela refusing a paid boxing or MMA fight, the two finally clashed on January 31, 2025, during GCW weekend when Bostic approached Janela and flipped a table onto him. While Janela later claimed Bostic begged for mercy, Bostic has now turned the altercation and his rival's nickname into the punchline of a new song.
