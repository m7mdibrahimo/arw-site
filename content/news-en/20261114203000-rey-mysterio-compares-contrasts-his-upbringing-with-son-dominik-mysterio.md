Despite the current tension between Rey Mysterio and his son Dominik, Rey remains proud of Dominik's journey. In a recent appearance on "What's Your Story? With Steph McMahon," Rey compared their early lives, noting that neither of them cared much for school. Rey explained that he only attended school because it was required, eventually making a deal with his mother to finish his education before fully dedicating himself to wrestling. However, he left after his 11th grade to sign with Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide after the promotion had extensive discussions with his mother.

> "He showed no interest in education, and I was the same way. I went to school because I had to go to school."

> "That was a part of my bargain, until I left and never came back."

> "Dom is like a spitting image of how I grew up, in the sense of not liking school that much."

Rey credited himself for opening the doors for his son, but emphasized that Dominik put in the hard work to establish his own career. He reflected on the differences in their paths, noting that he had to struggle to make a name for himself, whereas Dominik's path was different. Rey recalled that he met Konnan at age 11 and trained at his uncle's gym before Konnan helped him and Psicosis break into Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide. Rey admitted he was surprised Dominik chose wrestling at all, especially since he seemed to lose interest around age 11 or 12. He expressed that he wants the best for his children and believes Dominik has reached his current level through his own efforts. Now, Rey hopes to see his son reach the next level in his evolution.
