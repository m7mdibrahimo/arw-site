A new tag team match has been added to the card for Major League Wrestling's Saturday Night SuperFight event. The show is scheduled for November 7 at Cicero Stadium in Cicero, Illinois.

Following the November 5 episode of MLW Fusion, officials announced that Templario and Titán will face the team of Black Tiger and Último Guerrero. This addition brings the current lineup for SuperFight to seven matches.

The updated card for Saturday Night SuperFight includes the following:

- Templario & Titán vs. Black Tiger & Último Guerrero
- Keyra vs. Tessa Blanchard
- MLW World Tag Team Championship: The Good Brothers (Doc Gallows & Karl Anderson) (c) vs. Glasgow Boys On Top (Joe Coffey & Mark Coffey) (w/ Wolfgang)
- MLW National Openweight Championship: Austin Aries (c) vs. Matt Riddle
- Yota Tsuji vs. Trevor Lee
- Masha Slamovich vs. ZAMAYA
- MLW World Heavyweight Championship: Killer Kross (c) vs. Josh Bishop (Last Man Standing Match)
