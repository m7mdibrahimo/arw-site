AEW Collision on November 7, 2026, features a stacked card building toward the Full Gear pay-per-view. Andrade El Idolo defends the AEW National Championship against Tommaso Ciampa in a rematch. FTR faces the Motor City Machine Guns in Match Four of their best-of-five series, with Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin leading 2-1. Persephone takes on Mina Shirakawa in singles action following Blood & Guts, and Chris Jericho meets Blake Christian one-on-one. The show airs live from a ship in Miami, Florida, on TNT and MAX.
