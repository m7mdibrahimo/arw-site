Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron led their team to a hard-fought victory during the women's Blood & Guts match on this week's episode of AEW Dynamite. The contest featured a clash between the group of Thekla, Julia Hart, Skye Blue, Megan Bayne, Lena Kross, and Persephone and the squad consisting of Alex Windsor, Jamie Hayter, Statlander, Cameron, Willow Nightingale, and Queen Aminata.

The bout opened with a brutal exchange between Bayne and Statlander involving trash cans and chairs. As more competitors entered the cage, the intensity escalated with the use of light tubes, broken glass, and LEGO bricks. Once all participants had joined the fray, Nightingale's team gained control, highlighted by Cameron hitting a Canadian Destroyer on Bayne. Ultimately, Statlander and Cameron secured the win by forcing Hart to submit to a half crab while applying barbed wire to her face.
