Baron Von Raschke made history at Dreamwave Wrestling by becoming the oldest male professional wrestler to compete at 86 years old. He entered the Halloween Ball Gauntlet Extravaganza hosted by Sidney Bakabella, securing the victory in his first match in three decades.

https://x.com/SBakabella/status/2109007120767111191

Von Raschke surpassed the previous men's record held by Kendo Nagasaki, who wrestled at 84 years old in November 2025. Mae Young holds the overall record for competing at age 87 in 2010. Von Raschke began his career in 1966 with AWA and wrestled for WWE, Jim Crockett Promotions, NWA, and WCW while winning multiple championships.
