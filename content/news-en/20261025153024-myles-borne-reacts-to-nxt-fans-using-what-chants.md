Myles Borne addressed the famous What chants from WWE NXT fans, revealing a funny issue with their plan because he could not hear them at all.

Borne did not notice the chants live and only learned about them later by watching the recording with closed captions. Appearing on The Finish with Corey Smith and Tor Smith, he explained that he was not annoyed by the situation and found it humorous, noting that the crowd wasted their energy trying to get a reaction he could not give.

> "I mean, the only reason I even knew there was a ‘What?’ chant is because when I watched it back, there was closed captioning, so I was able to pick up on it. But, I mean, to me, that just shows me, you're really doing ‘What?’ chants to a guy that can't even hear? Like, you know, you're wasting your energy. You're going to have to be a lot louder than that if you want to try to get my attention."
