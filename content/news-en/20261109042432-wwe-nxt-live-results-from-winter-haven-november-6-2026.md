WWE held an NXT live event on November 6, 2026, at the Winter Haven Armory in Winter Haven, Florida. According to results from Wrestling Bodyslam, the following matches took place.

In an elimination match, the team of Elijah Holyfield, Elio LeFleur, Shiloh Hill, and Tate Wilder defeated Brooks Jensen, Harley Riggins, Shido Ash, and Viktor Zanov. Kenzie secured a victory over Andi Summers.

BirthRight, consisting of Channing Lorenzo and Uriah Connors, defeated The Dropouts, Jackson Goonrey and Tyran Tuckey, with Arianna Grace in their corner. Kali Armstrong picked up a win against Chantel Monroe.

In an NXT North American Championship match, Jackson Drake successfully defended his title against Zilla Fatu. Kendal Grey and Wren Sinclair teamed up to defeat Arianna Grace and Myka Lockwood. Tristan Angels emerged victorious in a match against Saquon Shugars and Sean Legacy.

In the title matches, Zaria retained the NXT Women's North American Championship against Vanta The Unknown, and Kelani Jordan successfully defended the NXT Women's Championship against Layla Diggs.
