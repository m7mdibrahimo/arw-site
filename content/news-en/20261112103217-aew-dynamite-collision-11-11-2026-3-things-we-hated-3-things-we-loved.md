AEW Dynamite collided with its sister program, Collision, on Wednesday night ahead of the Full Gear pay-per-view. The Wrestling Inc. staff shared their favorite and least favorite moments from the double header. On the negative side, the staff disliked Persephone's unnecessary squash match against a jobber prior to the main event, and the chaotic non-finish of the match between Brodido and New Level ahead of their Texas Death match for the AEW World Trios Championships. Additionally, they disliked Queen Aminata's first title defenses coming in three-way matches, with her next one placed on the Tailgate Brawl pre-show, where it feels like an afterthought.

On the positive side, Mike Bailey delivered an impressive performance against Tommaso Ciampa for the AEW National Championship, leading to a surprise appearance by Ilja Dragunov as the next challenger. The main event featured Queen Aminata retaining her TBS Championship against Hyan and Skye Blue in an entertaining multi-woman match. Finally, the Motor City Machine Guns defeated FTR 3-2 in a hard-fought best-of-five series to earn a shot at the AEW World Tag Team Championships.
