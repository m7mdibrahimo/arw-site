Former WWE stars Doc Gallows and Karl Anderson, known as the Good Brothers, recently remembered the late AEW star PAC during a live recording of their "Talk N' Shop" podcast. The two shared fond memories of working alongside PAC in WWE between 2016 and 2017, praising his incredible talent and the impact he made in both WWE and AEW.

Gallows recalled that PAC had an outstanding tryout with WWE years ago, but WWE did not hire him at the time, and he claimed then-executive Canyon Ceman turned PAC down for not being handsome enough. "He blew the tryout out of the water," Gallows said. "And then we didn't see him, and somebody goes 'Well, why didn't they hire PAC?' And they go 'Canyon Ceman said that he wasn't handsome enough to be hired.' I'm like 'This is professional wrestling.'"

Anderson also shared a conversation he had with PAC shortly before the latter's departure from WWE in 2017, noting that PAC made a bold and successful decision to bet on himself despite having limited options at the time. "He goes 'I can't wait to get the f* out of here,'" Anderson said. "I remember thinking, and I probably even said it to him, I was like 'Where the f*'s he going to go? What's he going to do?' But he did it. Most important is he had the balls [to do it]."
