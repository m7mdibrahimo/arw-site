Michael Cole believes that the Money In The Bank premium live event has reached a status nearly matching major shows like Royal Rumble and Survivor Series. During a recent interview, the WWE announcer explained how the event shapes careers and stands just behind WrestleMania and SummerSlam in importance.

> "Money in the Bank, for us, is right up there. Almost at the same level as the Royal Rumble, Survivor Series," he said.

Cole noted that climbing the ladder to grab the briefcase offers a career-changing contract that can be cashed in at any time. He also highlighted the mix of veterans and newcomers competing in this year's matchups.
