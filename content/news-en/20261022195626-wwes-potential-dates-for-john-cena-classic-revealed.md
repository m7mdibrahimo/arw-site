WWE is currently weighing two potential dates for the upcoming John Cena Classic, while the calendar for December continues to fill up. According to WrestleVotes Radio on Fightful Select, officials have narrowed their focus down to December 12 and December 19, though the exact date remains undecided.

"As for the rumored John Cena Classic — which Cena himself has said will take place in December — a source couldn't confirm whether the event is more likely to follow WrestlePalooza on December 12 with a stateside event that evening or take place the following weekend on December 19 — which would put it up against AEW Worlds End, as well as AAA Guerra De Titanes later that same evening."

A December 12 date could potentially feature a massive doubleheader, with WWE holding WrestlePalooza overseas earlier in the day before running the John Cena Classic in the United States that night. On the other hand, December 19 would place the event directly opposite AEW Worlds End and AAA Guerra De Titanes.

Other dates have already been ruled out or filled. December 5 is unlikely because WWE wants to avoid running major events on three consecutive weekends, while December 26 is already booked for the company's traditional holiday show at Madison Square Garden.

"Additionally, a source noted that the voting aspect of the event is still something that John Cena would like to see happen. We're told that several ideas have been floated internally to make that a possibility."

While the fan-voting concept has not yet been finalized, creative discussions are ongoing. WWE still has several details to iron out before the event becomes official.
