AEW Dynamite aired live from the Watsco Center in Coral Gables, Florida, on October 21, 2026. Following WrestleDream, Ilja Dragunov made his AEW debut, introduced by Renee Paquette, and later defeated "Speedball" Mike Bailey in his first match for the promotion.

In the opening match, Mark Davis and Jake Doyle of The Don Callis Family defeated The Young Bucks in an AEW World Tag Team Championship Eliminator Match. Later, Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight defeated Will Ospreay and Adam Copeland. Following the match, a brawl ensued involving The Don Callis Family, Wardlow, and Kazuyuki Okada. Steven Borden and Darby Allin arrived to assist Ospreay and Copeland, with Borden challenging the opposing group to a Blood & Guts Match.

Swerve Strickland defeated Tom Filip. Orange Cassidy and Jay White defeated The Death Riders. In the Continental Championship Eliminator Match, Jon Moxley defeated Roderick Strong. Post-match, David Finlay, Clark Connors, and Gabe Kidd were involved in a chaotic segment that revealed an alliance between The Dogs and a heel Bang Bang Gang.

Nick Wayne demanded an apology from Chris Jericho for his loss at WrestleDream. Jericho eventually apologized, telling Wayne that this would be a turning point in his career. In a Number One Contender’s Match for the TBS Championship, Queen Aminata defeated Maya World.
