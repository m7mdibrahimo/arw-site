AEW star Zayda Steel recently opened up about facing her idol, Mercedes Moné, during an appearance on MuscleManMalcolm's Muscle Memory. Steel reflected on her match against the current AEW Women's World Champion on the August 15 episode of AEW Collision, admitting that she was so emotional she nearly cried during her entrance. She praised Mone as a longtime comfort figure in her life and expressed confidence that she will improve significantly after her first year in AEW, vowing to eventually run it back with Mone. Steel also discussed another surreal moment from 2026 when she crossed paths with Jon Moxley in a mixed trios match. Having edited videos of Moxley in the past and owned his merchandise during his WWE days as Dean Ambrose, she admitted to being extremely nervous and humorously worried she might have accidentally hurt him during the bout.
