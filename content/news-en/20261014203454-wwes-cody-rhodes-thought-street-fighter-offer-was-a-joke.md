WWE star Cody Rhodes revealed that he initially thought the offer to star as Guile in the upcoming Street Fighter film adaptation was a joke. Rhodes shared that during what he expected to be a routine general meeting, film executives surprised him with the role. Caught off guard, he immediately assured the production team that WWE would approve his time off for filming. Since landing the part, Rhodes has fully embraced the character, even sporting Guile's signature haircut at public appearances and at WWE SummerSlam 2026. His dedication earned praise from John Cena, who encouraged WWE fans to support the movie when it hits theaters.
