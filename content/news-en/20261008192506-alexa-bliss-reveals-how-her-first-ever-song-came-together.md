Alexa Bliss recently shared the unique story behind her debut single, explaining how years of humorous text messages sent to her husband, Ryan Cabrera, inspired the track. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, Bliss revealed that Cabrera had secretly saved some of her most outlandish texts over the years, initially planning to use them for a book or art piece. After Cabrera jokingly called her a drama queen, Bliss replied that she was just drunk, which sparked the idea for the chorus. Cabrera spent several days arranging the texts, composing melodies, and testing voice notes before surprising her with the finished demo.

Although Bliss initially feared the song might embarrass her, she ultimately loved the catchy result and headed into the studio to record her vocals alongside notable musicians. The track, titled “I'm Just Drunk (Lexi's Texts),” features contributions from artists like Jaret from Bowling for Soup and drummer Tommy Carlucci, with Cabrera handling the guitar work. Fans can expect a fun and lighthearted look into the couple's private conversations as the song officially releases.
