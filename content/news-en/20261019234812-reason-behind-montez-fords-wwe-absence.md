Montez Ford’s recent absence from WWE television is unrelated to the ongoing lawsuit involving an interaction with a security guard during Monday Night RAW. According to WrestleVotes, Ford is currently away from the ring for personal family reasons. He and his wife, Bianca Belair, recently welcomed a new child.

> "Montez Ford and Bianca Belair recently welcomed an addition to their family. So, according to people we speak to, that’s the reason he’s off TV." Backstage reports confirm this family matter is entirely separate from the legal situation.
