AJ Francis recently discussed his rivalry with Elijah, which spanned from March through August and featured several matches on television and pay-per-view. The feud concluded at TNA Lockdown, where Francis secured a victory in a Steel Cage match. In an interview with The Wrestling Classic, Francis expressed his belief that the program was the standout storyline of the year. "Just look at the feud I had with Elijah. From the stuff we did in the studio where he's like haunting my mind to me stealing his name, image, and likeness, to us having the match and me hitting Expressions in the head with a guitar and me getting the Expressions medical fund. Just the opportunity to be creative and do different things. That was probably the most — if you start from the beginning of me and Elijah’s storyline to the end of it at Lockdown, I mean that's probably the best storyline in wrestling this year. It's the best, most creative storyline in wrestling this year. I stand on that. Not only that, but we had banger fucking matched at Slammiversary and at Lockdown. It should be up there for feud of the year."
