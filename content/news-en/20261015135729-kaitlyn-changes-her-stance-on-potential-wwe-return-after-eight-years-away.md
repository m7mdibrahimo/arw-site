Kaitlyn is no longer as interested in returning to a WWE ring as she once was. After previously stating she was open to coming back for an appearance like the Royal Rumble, the former Divas Champion now says she does not think she will wrestle again because the desire is simply gone. Speaking with Chris Van Vliet, Kaitlyn admitted that she hopes fans will not be upset by her answer, but getting back into the ring is not something she is particularly interested in doing.

Despite her stance on competing, Kaitlyn made it clear that she still respects the wrestling business deeply and appreciates its artistic nature. She pointed out that performing requires managing many details, such as knowing camera angles, working within time limits, and adapting to the live crowd. Although she occasionally watches matches involving friends, she no longer follows the product regularly and feels content leaving her career behind.
