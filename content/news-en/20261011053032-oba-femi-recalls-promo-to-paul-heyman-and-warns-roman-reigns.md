Oba Femi knew he would exceed expectations in WWE. Following his victory over Bronson Reed despite interference at Money in the Bank, Femi reflected on his rapid rise from the NIL program to stardom.

During the post-show press conference, Femi recalled a specific promo he cut to Paul Heyman. He stated that he told Heyman and everyone backstage in Gorilla Position to forget about second-generation stars and recognize that he is the real deal who prepared all his life.

Femi also issued a warning by calling out Roman Reigns during the same post-show appearance.
