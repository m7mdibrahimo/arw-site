Jim Duggan recently recalled Vince McMahon banning card games in the WWE locker room because they distracted the wrestlers. While modern talent often passes time with video games backstage, stars in the 1980s and 1990s played card games. Sgt. Slaughter even created elaborate tournament brackets before McMahon ultimately prohibited them.

Duggan explained that these games caused wrestlers to miss their entrance music. He shared a funny story about forgetting a pack of cards inside his knee pad during a match, which flew everywhere when he hit his finisher.

> "Skaaland's like, ‘Duggan, where'd those cards come from?' I said, Fan must have thrown them in the ring."
