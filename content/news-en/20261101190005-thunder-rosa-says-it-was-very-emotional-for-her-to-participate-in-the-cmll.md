Thunder Rosa recently reflected on her experience competing in the CMLL Grand Prix, noting that the occasion was very emotional for her. The tournament concluded on October 23rd for the women's division, with Skadi pinning former AEW Women's World Tag Team Champion Megan Bayne to secure the victory for Team CMLL, which also featured Thunder Rosa.

During a new interview with Paltrocast, Thunder Rosa opened up about what representing the promotion meant to her. She stated that hearing the national anthem brought her to tears and left her deeply moved. "Yes, it was. It was very emotional for me, to hear the national anthem. I was actually crying. I said, they actually took a couple of pictures and put me in there. I was really moved because, it was my first time doing the Grand Prix, it just feels so nice to represent my country, and it was weird because I was representing CMLL. / But at the same time, I have to remember that I am a champion in CMLL, so it only makes sense. So, yeah, it was very special for me."
