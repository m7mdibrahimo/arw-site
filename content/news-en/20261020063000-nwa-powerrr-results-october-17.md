NWA aired a new episode of NWA Powerrr on October 17 on Comet TV. Colby Corino confronted Kerry Morton, demanding a rematch, but Morton insisted that Corino must admit he is the greatest if he wins. Sirena Veil issued an open challenge for her Women's TV Title. In the opening match, Sirena Veil successfully defended her NWA Women’s Television Championship against Bobbi Thunder. MZRY also retained the NWA Television Championship after defeating Tony Leyenda. In other action, Axton Ray and Shawn Carlson defeated Pretty Boy Smooth and Effy. Additionally, Gretta picked up a victory over Ruby Red. In the main event, Da Pope defeated Big Strong Mims. Elsewhere, Kenzie Paige expressed doubt about continuing to team with her sister Kylie Paige, while Brandon McCord attempted to recruit Blunt Force Trauma.
