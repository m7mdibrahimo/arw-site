Kamille remains under contract with AEW despite a prolonged absence from television programming. Reports confirm she is still employed by the promotion following a brief return earlier this year. Her comeback occurred at Dynasty on April 12, 2026, where she attacked Willow Nightingale before defeating Big Anne on the Zero Hour pre-show. However, her return was short-lived, with her most recent AEW match taking place on the April 15 episode of Dynamite, where Willow Nightingale defeated her in a match for the TBS Championship. Since then, Kamille has worked only once outside the company, losing to Bobbi Thunder in Diamond Championship Wrestling. While she has engaged in promotional work and other projects, creative plans for her return to AEW television remain uncertain as her contract continues.
