Danielle Fishel, known for her role as Topanga Lawrence on Boy Meets World, has shared that she finally received the WWE Championship belt she believes she deserves. Fishel posted a photo on Instagram on Saturday, November 14, showing herself posing with the title belt over her shoulder while flexing. The championship features a custom design with a SiriusXM logo to promote WWE's radio station on the platform. Fishel wrote in her post, "AND NEW!!!!!! Thanks to @siriusxm and @wwe for finally giving me the title I deserve. Excited to listen to the new station…" The post drew responses from WWE stars, including referee Jessika Carr, who joked about raising her hand, and Bayley, who called her "MY CHAMP." Fishel has long been a fan of the company, frequently attending events, appearing on The New Day's Feel the Power podcast, and participating in backstage segments, including a notable appearance during the WWE RAW Netflix debut in January 2025.
