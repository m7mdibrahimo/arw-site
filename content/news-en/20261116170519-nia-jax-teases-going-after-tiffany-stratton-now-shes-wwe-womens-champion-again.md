Nia Jax discussed a potential future feud with Tiffany Stratton during a recent interview with WPLG Local 10. Jax reflected on various legends and past opponents she has faced throughout her career with WWE, including Trish Stratus and the Four Horsewomen. When asked about her dream opponents, Jax mentioned legends like Aja Kong and Chyna, noting that she prefers not to judge potential matches until she steps into the ring with someone to see their chemistry. "Like, I loved Aja Kong. Like, she just was such a powerhouse back in the day. I mean, Chyna. obviously like if that was something that I could have a match against, I never want to just like- cause I never know what it's going to be like until I get in the ring with them. Cause sometimes like you're actually surprised by your opponent. You're like, 'oh, we have such good chemistry.' Like the stories we can tell with each other are different from, I can tell from anybody else. So I just, I take on whoever comes my way and I try to make it the best and try to have as much fun and entertain the crowd. So I don't really have one particular, but, you know, Tiffy's got the title now, so who knows? I may be coming for her again."
