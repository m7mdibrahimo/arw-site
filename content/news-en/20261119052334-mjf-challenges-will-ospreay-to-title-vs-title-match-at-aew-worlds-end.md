MJF aims to become the undisputed champion. Following his return at AEW Full Gear, Friedman appeared on the Wednesday episode of AEW Dynamite to provoke Will Ospreay. He mocked Ospreay's absence and criticized his "restore the feeling" mantra, declaring that he is the true feeling himself.

Friedman further accused Ospreay of stealing his accolades, titles, and his life. He concluded the segment by challenging Ospreay to a match at AEW Worlds End for both versions of the AEW World Championship.
