Tommaso Ciampa recently captured the AEW National Championship, and speculation has already begun regarding who his first challenger might be. Following his title victory over Andrade El Ídolo on the November 7 episode of AEW Collision, reports suggest that Ciampa could soon enter a feud with Ilja Dragunov, with whom he shares some unfinished business. The match itself took place under challenging conditions aboard Chris Jericho's Rock 'N' Wrestling Rager at Sea cruise, where a slippery ring caused by rain led to a momentary scare before Ciampa secured the win.

During an episode of Wrestling Observer Radio, Dave Meltzer and Bryan Alvarez discussed the title change and its implications for the division. Meltzer pointed out that Ciampa's victory seems to set up a natural rivalry with Dragunov, recalling their previous confrontation on the October 28 episode of AEW Dynamite when Dragunov interrupted Ciampa's promo to issue a challenge. Meanwhile, Alvarez noted that Andrade still holds a guaranteed opportunity at the AEW World Championship thanks to his Casino Gauntlet victory at All In: London, which may allow him to shift his full focus away from the national title and toward the world championship scene.
