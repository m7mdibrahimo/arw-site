Kevin Nash believes the United States needs to secure its borders, but he is strongly critical of the methods used by ICE. Speaking on a recent episode of his Kliq This podcast, Nash argued that the agency has lost its focus and should target dangerous criminals instead of long-term residents. Nash described an incident where an officer allegedly pointed a firearm at a civilian's forehead and questioned the aggressive tactics being used. He also shared concerns about families facing deportation after decades in the country. Nash emphasized that the United States should prioritize securing the border while maintaining honor and diplomacy rather than bullying civilians.
