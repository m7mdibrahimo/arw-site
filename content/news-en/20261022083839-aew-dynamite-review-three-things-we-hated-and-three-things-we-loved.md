The latest edition of AEW Dynamite featured major developments following WrestleDream, highlighted by high-profile debuts and escalating rivalries. While several segments delivered exceptional action, other storylines left fans frustrated with their pacing and direction.

Among the negatives, the ongoing feud between Nick Wayne and Chris Jericho continues to drag at an agonizingly slow pace despite Wayne having already defeated Jericho twice. Additionally, there remains noticeable confusion surrounding the women's Blood & Guts structure, as a backstage brawl set up tension without making the match or its participants officially clear. Furthermore, the continuation of the AEW World Trios Championship feud between Swerve Strickland and the team of Hangman Adam Page, Brody King, and Bandido felt repetitive after their definitive rivalry should have concluded at WrestleDream.

On the positive side, Mark Davis and Jake Doyle scored an impressive and dominant victory over The Young Bucks to earn a title opportunity, immediately establishing themselves as a serious threat in the tag team division. Fans also witnessed an effective heel realignment as Jay White and the Bang Bang Gang turned on the babyfaces to stage a Bullet Club reunion alongside The Dogs. Finally, Ilja Dragunov made a spectacular in-ring debut in the main event, delivering a brutal, hard-hitting clinic against Speedball Mike Bailey to prove he is an elite addition to the roster.
