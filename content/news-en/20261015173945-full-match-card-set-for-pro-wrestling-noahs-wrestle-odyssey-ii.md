Pro Wrestling NOAH has finalized the complete match card for its upcoming Wrestle Odyssey II event. The show is scheduled to take place on October 25th at Ryogoku Sumo Hall in Tokyo, Japan. The lineup features several high-profile championship matches and special bouts, including a GHC Heavyweight Championship defense between champion Tetsuya Naito and OZAWA, as well as a GHC Tag Team Championship three-way match.

The full match card for the event includes a dark match featuring Atsushi Kotoge and Hi69 against Hiroto Tsuruya and Hiroya Namiki, alongside a GHC Junior Heavyweight Tag Team Championship bout where Team 2000X members YO-HEY and Jun Masaoka defend against Alejandro and Daiki Odashima. Additionally, a four-way tag team match will pit Team 2000X against Tetsuya Endo and Yuto Koyanagi, Los Tranquilos de Japon, and Kazuyuki Fujita with Black Menso-re.

Further action on the card features Maybach Taniguchi and KENTA taking on Masa Kitamiya and Takashi Sugiura, while HAYATA defends the GHC200 Championship against Shuji Kondo. Kaito Kiyomiya and Yoshiki Inamura will face Kenoh and Kouga, and Naomichi Marufuji puts the GHC National Championship on the line against Luis Mante. Rounding out the card, Yuki Iino and Manabu Soya defend the GHC Tag Team Championship in a three-way match against The Good Brothers and T.M.D.K., and Kai Fujimura defends the GHC Junior Heavyweight Championship against Dragon Bane.
