John Cena made a surprise appearance on WWE NXT to announce Zilla Fatu as the second participant in the John Cena Classic. The emotional segment featured a backstage production decision to highlight a specific moment in the ring.

According to Fightful Select, WWE production received instructions to elevate the audio levels at a precise point during Fatu's promo. While the exact moment was not specified, the segment featured significant high-energy points between the two performers.

During the appearance, Cena spoke about his extensive history with Fatu's family and expressed his deep respect for Fatu's late father, Umaga. The son of Umaga broke down in tears during the speech, eventually thanking Cena and embracing him in the ring.
