John Cena recently opened up about his personal life, admitting that his relationship with his family is currently strained due to his busy schedule. Speaking on a recent podcast, Cena shouldered the responsibility and explained that work has often served as an excuse for him over the years. Despite the distance with his four brothers and blood-related family members, Cena noted that he is actively trying to work through his workaholic tendencies and improve those bonds.

While his family ties remain complicated, Cena emphasized that he relies on a tight-knit inner circle of about six to eight people for support and guidance. At the very top of that list is his wife, Shay Shariatzadeh, whom he described as the anchor of his personal life. Cena made it clear that he is not making excuses for the strained family dynamics and continues to strive to be a better brother, son, and uncle.
