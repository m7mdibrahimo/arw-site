AEW Collision: Fright Night took place on October 31, 2026, at the Chaifetz Arena in St. Louis, Missouri. On Dynamite, Tommaso Ciampa claimed to be the best signing in AEW history, a sentiment Ilja Dragunov took personally. Tonight, Ciampa faced Matt Sydal, while Dragunov took on The Beast Mortos. Additionally, TRINITY challenged Triangle of Madness for the Artist of STARDOM Championship. The Death Riders, now without Claudio Castagnoli, addressed the formation of their group and their rivalry with The Conglomeration. Darius Martin competed in his first singles match in nearly three years against United Empire's Speedball Mike Bailey, while Konosuke Takeshita faced Adam Priest. The Hurt Syndicate also saw action.

Tommaso Ciampa def. Matt Sydal.

Hurt Syndicate def. Soloroto.
