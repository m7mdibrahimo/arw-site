All Elite Wrestling officially revealed the return of the annual New Year's Smash event, featuring AEW Dynamite on January 1 and AEW Collision on January 2. Gateway Arena in Atlanta, Georgia, will host both shows.

This annual tradition began in 2021 at Daily's Place in Jacksonville, Florida. Last year's event took place on December 31 in Omaha, Nebraska, and featured Mercedes Moné losing the AEW TBS Title to Willow Nightingale.
