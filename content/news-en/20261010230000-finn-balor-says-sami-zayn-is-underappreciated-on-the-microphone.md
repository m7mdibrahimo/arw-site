Finn Balor believes that Sami Zayn's promo skills are often overlooked despite his long-standing reputation for excellence. During an interview on "Rosenberg Wrestling," Balor highlighted that while stars like Roman Reigns and CM Punk frequently receive praise for their mic work, Zayn consistently delivers high-level performances that deserve more recognition.

> "I think someone who's very underappreciated on the microphone is Sami Zayn. You know, people always talk about Roman and Punk, but, you know, for me, Sami's promos, even since the NXT days, have been off the charts. And he really dives into, you know, the character and really, like, peels back the layers. So, for me, he's someone who I think is vastly underappreciated."

Zayn's ability to connect with the audience has earned him consistent praise from industry veterans like Mark Henry, who has labeled him one of the best talkers in the business since 2019. Zayn himself attributes his effectiveness on the microphone to years of experience, noting that he has learned how to say less while achieving a greater impact during his segments.
