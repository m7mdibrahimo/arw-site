Je'Von Evans crashing through the roof of an ambulance was a major talking point coming out of Money in the Bank. According to WrestleVotes, WWE had specific reasons for the stunt involving the 22-year-old. JoeyVotes explained that the primary goal was to create a viral moment that would generate significant online engagement.

> "The reason for it was to have a big spot where people would react and lose their minds and share the video a million times on social media. It was just to be viral."

TC added that the spot also served a narrative purpose, as WWE needed to write Evans out of the Men's Money in the Bank Ladder Match since he was not scheduled to win. By executing the stunt, the company protected Evans and added a highlight to his career.

> "And take him out of the match since he could not win it. And kinda protect him — like, okay, he's gonna lose this match. He's 22 years old, he has a huge future ahead of him, and now you add a big spot to his résumé that he can use going forward as one of his video package moments and iconic scenes. It was like, hey, get him out of the match with an ambulance, and it will be like, wow, what a great spot for Je'Von. And you kinda take the focus off that he lost another match in a big spot yet again."
