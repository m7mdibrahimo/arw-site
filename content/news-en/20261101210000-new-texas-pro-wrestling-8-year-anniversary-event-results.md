New Texas Pro Wrestling presented its New Texas Pro 8 Year Anniversary Event on October 30, 2026, live from Houston, TX, with streaming coverage on IWTV.

Chris Marcell (w/ Isaiah Trevino, AQA, & Big Marc) def. Gavin Ash, LVJ, Isaac Morales (w/ Eduardo Garcia, Ronin Reyes & Shawn Hendrix), and Dustin Nguyen Marvelous Mack in a ladder match to retain the Texas Contenders Series Championship.

Dream Team (Danny King & KC Kr’eme) def. La Familia (Diego de la Cruz & Jonathan Vega) to retain the New Texas Pro Tag Team Championships.

Kylie Rae def. Kitti Hisatomi.

Carter BlaQ def. Izzy James.

Oday Al Saleh def. Jesse Funaki, Timur The Great and Shimbashi to retain the Lonestar Championship.

The King Bees (Charity King & Danni Bee) def. Raychell Rose & Londyn Dior.

Katsuyori Shibata def. Buzzsaw Samuels (w/ Teflon Jon).

Bryan Keith def. Danny Orion to retain the New Texas Pro World Championship.
