Bear Bronson gave Beyond Wrestling fans a scare at the end of the Rent Free event, but fortunately, the situation was not as serious as it initially appeared. After needing assistance to get to the back following a brutal main event against Rickey Shane Page in a Rhode Island Red Death Match, Bronson provided a health update to concerned fans.

During the chaotic match, which required a pinfall or submission after drawing blood, Bronson sent Page head-first through a 70-inch television monitor. Following a series of brutal spots involving tacks, doors, and chairs, Bronson secured the victory with a powerbomb. However, concern grew when Gabby Forza and referee Kevin Quinn had to help Bronson backstage after the match.

In response to a worried fan who expressed severe concern, Bronson quickly offered reassurance on social media, writing: "Just a scare! I’ll be okay in no time. Thank you!" He later confirmed to another fan that he was not seriously hurt, adding: "I’ll be okay!" and agreeing that the match conclusion had indeed been a scary moment.
