The AEW World Trios Championships will be defended in a Texas Death match at AEW Full Gear 2026. Champions Hangman Adam Page, Brody King, and Bandido accepted the challenge issued by Swerve Strickland during Wednesday's episode of AEW Dynamite.

Page had spent recent weeks attempting to avoid the stipulation due to his previous experience with Strickland in that match type. However, tensions escalated when Strickland abducted and bloodied Bandido backstage. During this week's broadcast, Bandido attacked Kofi, sparking a brawl with Strickland. Austin Creed intervened by hitting a spear on Bandido, drawing King and Page into the fight.

Despite Page's final attempt to convince his partners to decline the match, Bandido insisted the situation had evolved beyond just himself. After King signaled his agreement, Page officially accepted the challenge. Strickland and Creed were observed smiling at ringside as the segment concluded.
