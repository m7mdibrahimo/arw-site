Lucha Libre AAA presents AAA Heroes Inmortales tonight, October 24, 2026, live from Gimnasio Olímpico Juan de la Barrera in Mexico City, Mexico. The event starts at 8 pm local time in Mexico and 10 pm ET for viewers in the US, with broadcast options including FOX in Latin America and YouTube in the US.

The match lineup features three championship bouts. In the first title match, AAA Trios Champions Perros Del Mal, represented by Angel, Berto, and Daga, defend their titles against La Parka, Mr. Iguana, and Nino Hamburguesa. In another major clash, AAA Mega Champion El Grande Americano defends the title against Dominik Mysterio. Finally, AAA Reina de Reinas Champion La Catalina puts her title on the line in a triple threat match against Flammer and La Hiedra. Fightful will provide coverage of the event starting at 10 pm ET on Saturday.
