TNA Hall of Famer Gail Kim recently responded to a social media user who accused her of selling out by marrying celebrity chef Robert Irvine. Following the 2026 midterm elections, Kim celebrated Democratic victories after arriving in Texas, posting on X that she had landed in a blue state. A user replied by questioning why she married an older white man instead of a man of her own culture, calling her a sellout.

Kim quickly fired back, noting that she shares many Democratic values and pointing out that Irvine is only 11 years older than her. She added that being happy with her soulmate matters far more than an anonymous critic's opinion, noting that she and Irvine have been married since 2012.
