Adam Pearce has explained why he avoids answering fan emails. The WWE Raw General Manager shared a strange message from a man who claimed to be Liv Morgan's husband, though Pearce did not believe it. Pearce posted a video on Instagram addressing an email from a person named Richard Nelson. The email had no subject line and began with a bold claim: "Hi, this is Liv Morgan's husband. Please call me ASAP." It also included a phone number, which Pearce did not share publicly.

Pearce used the message as an example of the odd emails that reach his inbox. He told the sender, "This is why I don't reply to your emails. No subject, as per usual, but Richard Nelson says, ‘Hi, this is Liv Morgan's husband. Please call me ASAP,’ and he gives a complete stranger a phone number." Pearce then spoke directly to Richard, noting the obvious flaw in his claim. "Richard, this is slightly awkward. You are, in fact, NOT Liv Morgan's husband, and because of that fact — it is a fact that you are not Liv Morgan's husband — I will not be calling you at the phone number that you gave a complete stranger."

Pearce kept his usual dry tone, wishing Richard a good weekend before explaining that such messages are why he does not respond to random emails. "So I hope you have a wonderful weekend, and I bid you adieu. And I want you to know, Richard, that you and people like you that I get emails from every day are, in fact, the reason I do not reply to emails. Love somebody today." Pearce did not mention what Richard wanted to discuss, and since he had no plan to call the number, the matter remains unresolved.
