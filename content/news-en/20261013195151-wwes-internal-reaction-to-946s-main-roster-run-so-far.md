The 946 faction has made a strong impression on WWE management since arriving on the main roster. According to False Finish, officials are very pleased with the group's presentation, believing that Royce Keys, Bronco Nima, and Lucien Price have surpassed expectations. 946 has quickly established itself as a dominant force through a violent rivalry with The Bloodline. The group has been involved in several major moments, including their recent attack on Jimmy and Jey Uso, and the kidnapping of Solo Sikoa. On the October 12 episode of WWE RAW, Carmelo Hayes joined the faction by helping them assault Jimmy, Jey, and Solo. With management's confidence and the addition of Hayes, 946 has become one of WWE's most successful acts of the year.
