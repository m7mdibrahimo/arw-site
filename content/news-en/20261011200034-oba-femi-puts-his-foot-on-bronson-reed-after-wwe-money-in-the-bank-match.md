Oba Femi secured a hard-hitting victory against Bronson Reed at WWE Money in the Bank, followed by a subtle yet powerful post-match statement. Footage circulating online captured Femi placing his foot on a downed Reed in the ring, replicating a moment from the September 7 episode of WWE RAW where Reed did the exact same thing to him. Unlike their previous encounter, Reed did not push the foot away this time as Femi stood tall to celebrate his win. The exchange was omitted from the live broadcast, but it served as a direct callback to their ongoing rivalry.
