Jake Hager's Power Slap injury has turned into a permanent medical issue, leaving him with only partial vision in his left eye following a brutal series of events at Power Slap 23. During the event in Texas, Hager suffered a ruptured eyeball and a detached retina after taking a severe strike from Johnny Magna, which immediately stopped the fight and caused bleeding from his eye and ear. Hager underwent emergency surgery for the globe rupture, and although initial signs were positive when doctors found he could still see, a subsequent diagnosis of a detached retina required a second operation.

His fiancée, Patricia Blackburn, previously noted that the second procedure went better than expected, but his recovery remained sensitive with no guarantees of a full visual recovery. Months later, Hager's team confirmed that despite undergoing two surgeries, he only retained partial sight in his left eye and sustained lasting damage. While he managed to avoid complete blindness in the eye, he continues to live with the long-term consequences of that single strike.
