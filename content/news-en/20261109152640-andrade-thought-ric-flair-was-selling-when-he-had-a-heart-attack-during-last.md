Ric Flair competed in his final match on July 31, 2022, teaming with Andrade El Idolo against Jay Lethal and Jeff Jarrett. During the match, Flair passed out, and it was later revealed that he suffered a heart attack. Speaking with Chris Jericho on Talk Is Jericho, Andrade reflected on the bout and his initial belief that Flair was merely selling in the ring. "I was with him in the corner. I was a little scared. I heard a couple months later. At the time, I thought he was selling. I said, 'Sir, are you okay?' 'Let's go. Let's go,'" Andrade said. He recalled being anxious about Flair's well-being given his age and training, but ultimately felt relieved when Flair was fine afterward. Flair has not returned to the ring since the match.
