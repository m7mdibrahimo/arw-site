Former WWE talent Zoey Serrano, who wrestled under the name Zoey Stark, revealed that she pitched an updated version of the Attitude Era stable Right to Censor during her time with the company. Serrano explained that the idea came directly from current WWE star Liv Morgan, who suggested the concept while they were working a live event together.

Serrano shared how Morgan encouraged her to post social media content criticizing the attire and styles of other female performers, including Morgan herself, Bianca Belair, and Rhea Ripley. Despite generating strong online engagement with over ten million views, WWE creative chose not to use the character on television.

After five years with WWE, Serrano was released this past April following WrestleMania. She has since joined TNA, where she recently won the Knockouts World Tag Team Championship alongside former WWE star Shayna Baszler under the team name Apex.
