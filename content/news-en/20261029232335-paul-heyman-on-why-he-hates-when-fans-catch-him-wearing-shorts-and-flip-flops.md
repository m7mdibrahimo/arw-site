Paul Heyman is serious about maintaining his WWE persona at all times, even when he is off the clock. The Wise Man explained that it genuinely bothers him when fans spot him in public wearing casual attire like shorts and flip-flops, as he feels it ruins the image he has carefully cultivated over decades. Speaking with Graham Bensinger, Heyman admitted that while he knows people expect to see him in a suit and tie, he occasionally heads to a local convenience store in more relaxed clothing.

> "Yeah, it's what people expect of me. I've also been photographed like this in flip-flops and shorts, running down to the local convenience store."

Heyman noted that he never turns down a fan who asks for a selfie, even when he is not dressed for the cameras. However, he worries about those images circulating online, where they clash with his television character. He joked about the potential for embarrassing photos ending up on the internet, but his concern is more about the fans' perception. He prefers to keep the illusion alive that he is always the character they see on WWE television.

> "Because I'm out of character. That's who they pay to see. If you ask a fan of Paul Heyman, their image of me is I swim in this."

Heyman suggested that many fans prefer to believe he wears a suit at all times, and he is happy to let them continue thinking so. He views protecting that image as part of his job, even if it means sacrificing comfort during a quick trip to the store.
