WWE star Ethan Page advised fans who spot him in public to simply keep walking. Speaking on The Mask Man Show, Page addressed questions about whether he is actually a nice person, stating that he does not care to convince anyone of his personality outside the ring and noting that interrupting his dinner puts the responsibility entirely on the person who starts the conversation.

Page stated that anyone who decides to approach him must accept whatever reaction follows, while leaving him alone guarantees a quiet and pleasant time for everyone involved. He pointed to a recent incident where he faced an unwanted confrontation from an influencer, though he added that he does not mind interacting if a fan feels the risk is worth taking.
