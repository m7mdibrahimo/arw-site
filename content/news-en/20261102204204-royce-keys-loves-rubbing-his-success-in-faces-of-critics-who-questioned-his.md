Royce Keys remembers the doubts people expressed when he moved from AEW to WWE and did not immediately become a top television star. Now that he has secured a regular spot on Monday Night RAW alongside The 946, he is enjoying the chance to prove his critics wrong. Speaking with HOT 97, Keys addressed the skepticism he faced during his early days in WWE.

> "Oh, yeah. I'm petty. I like rubbing stuff in your face when you don't believe in me or when you've done me wrong. So it's like I've always believed in my ability."

Keys is now using his platform to showcase a character deeply rooted in his personal history. He explained that his current persona is inspired by his upbringing in California, his late older brother, and the people his grandmother once tried to steer him away from.

> "I'm acting like my older brother, you know, RIP. I'm acting like the people my grandma tried to keep me away from. Like, everything you see is me turned up. It's me watching people, how I grew up. So that's just me bringing East Palo Alto, East Oakland to the mainstream."

Having successfully established himself on Monday Night RAW, Keys is not looking to move past the early criticism. Instead, he finds that proving those doubters wrong has made his current success even more rewarding.
