Bobby Lashley is the new 4th Rope Heavyweight Champion. At the December 12 4th Rope Wrestling event, he defeated Joe Alonzo in just two minutes to capture the title. Lashley is currently signed to AEW and is a member of The Hurt Syndicate alongside MVP and Shelton Benjamin. He is also a former AEW World Tag Team Champion with Benjamin.
