Nikkita Lyons has addressed the recurring criticism that her appearance is the primary reason she secured a spot in WWE. Speaking during an appearance on Booker T’s Hall of Fame podcast, Lyons made it clear that she is focused on proving her doubters wrong through her in-ring performance. 

Lyons noted that she is used to facing criticism regarding her body and physical appearance, recalling that she dealt with similar judgments from a very young age in the entertainment industry. She explained that she has developed thick skin over the years and is determined to let her hard work speak for itself rather than engaging with critics.
