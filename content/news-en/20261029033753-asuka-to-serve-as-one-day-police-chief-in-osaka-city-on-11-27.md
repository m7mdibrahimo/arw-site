Asuka is staying active despite her absence from WWE since May. On October 27, she announced that she will serve as the one-day police chief for the Oyodo Police Station in the Kita Ward of Osaka City, Japan, on Friday, November 27. During the event, Asuka plans to distribute autographed police merchandise and sign items for fans. She shared the following on social media: 

> "On Friday, November 27, I will be serving as the one-day chief of the Oyodo Police Station in Kita Ward, Osaka City. On the day of the event, I’ll be giving away autographed police merchandise and signing any items you bring along. If you’d like to attend, please leave a comment on this post! Venue: Kita Ward Community Hall, Osaka City Start Time: Morning (details to follow)"

Earlier this month, Asuka clarified that she remains with WWE.
