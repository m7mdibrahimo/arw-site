NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan recently reflected on making her WWE RAW in-ring debut in September during a Money in the Bank qualifier against Raquel Rodriguez and Lola Vice. Speaking with Justin Dhillon of The Wrestling Classic ahead of her defense against Wren Sinclair at NXT Halloween Havoc, Jordan described the moment as a huge turning point in her career. 

> "It was so cool. It’s just crazy to see the difference," Jordan said. "It was just an awesome experience to be able to be on a big stage, something that you worked for your whole career so being on Raw and being on Netflix, it was a super big turning point in my career, and I think also, it just allowed people to get more eyes on me."

Reports later indicated that Jordan is planned for a main roster run following the conclusion of her time as NXT Women's Champion. Jordan expressed her eagerness to compete regularly on the main roster when the right time comes, though her current focus remains fully on her championship reign and legacy in NXT.
