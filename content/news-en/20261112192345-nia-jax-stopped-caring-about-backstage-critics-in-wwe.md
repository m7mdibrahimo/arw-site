Nia Jax was determined not to let backstage critics ruin her second stint in WWE. Returning to the promotion in September 2023, she felt fully secure in her ring work and had no interest in hearing negative comments. That marked a clear shift from her initial WWE run.

Speaking on the Battleground Podcast, Jax reflected on what she realized about herself during her 2023 return. She admitted that during her first run, she did not fully grasp her own power in the ring. She spent years trying to understand her character, noting that even established legends told her it took them nearly ten years to feel completely comfortable in the ring.

When she got another chance with WWE, Jax felt confident in what she brought to the show. She explained that she stopped worrying about backstage critics, whom she referred to as chirpers.
