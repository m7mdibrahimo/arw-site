Ric Flair recently opened up about his negative experiences with agents, claiming his current representative owes him a staggering $1.25 million. Speaking on NewsNation, the wrestling legend advised anyone in the business never to mix friendship with management, stating that agents often tell clients what they want to hear while secretly looking out for themselves. Flair also reflected on his severe health crisis in 2017 when he spent 31 days in intensive care, alleging that people took advantage of his vulnerable state. He claimed a former agent stole $150,000 from him during that period and that someone attempted to pressure Wendy into signing over his intellectual property rights while he was on life support.
