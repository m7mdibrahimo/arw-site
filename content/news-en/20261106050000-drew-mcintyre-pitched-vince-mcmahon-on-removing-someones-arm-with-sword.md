In 2020, Drew McIntyre began carrying a Scottish claymore to the ring, incorporating it into his presentation. During an appearance on "Insight with Chris Van Vliet," McIntyre recalled pitching a brutal scene to Vince McMahon that was inspired by "Saving Private Ryan." 

> "I was like, '[In] "Saving Private Ryan," they use actors — amputees, et cetera — they lop off the limbs, they're actually missing; very realistic. I think at 'Mania or something, we should have a fight sequence where we end up backstage. Then we hit the pre-tape where I swing the sword at somebody — woosh, arm comes off.' Finally, I use the sword, pay it off!"

McIntyre noted that he felt frustrated having the prop for so long without ever using it, believing that actually utilizing it would have kept the sword relevant. However, the idea was never approved. McIntyre eventually stopped carrying the weapon because it no longer fit his character's evolution. He explained that as his story became more focused on personal trauma and PTSD, standing there with a giant sword felt out of place. Despite this, he remains open to bringing it back for special entrances, as he enjoyed representing his Scottish roots with the sword, which is named Angela after his late mother.
