Donovan Dijak is now a free agent after announcing his departure from MLW. In a statement on social media, Dijak confirmed that his appearances this past weekend were his final ones for the promotion. He expressed gratitude for the platform he was given over the last two years to compete as both a singles and tag team wrestler. During his tenure, Dijak captured the MLW Tag Team Championship alongside his partner Baron Corbin and shared the ring with opponents such as Killer Kross, Josh Bishop, Matt Riddle, Krule, Kevin Knight, The Good Brothers, and Kenta. Dijak thanked Court Bauer for the opportunity and stated that he is ready to bet on himself as he enters the next chapter of his career. He plans to continue working with independent companies like Progress, Limitless, Juggalo Championship, PW Rebellion, and Blood Sweat Tears. Dijak also revealed that he will be debuting a new and unique character presentation soon, noting that he feels he is currently at his best in the ring. He has been with MLW since 2024.
