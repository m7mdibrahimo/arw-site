The professional wrestling world is mourning the passing of former WCW executive and Mid-South Wrestling promoter "Cowboy" Bill Watts. Following the news, WWE Hall of Famer Ricky Morton paid tribute to the legendary promoter, recalling how Watts gave him and Robert Gibson their first major opportunity in the wrestling industry.

Speaking on a recent edition of "Busted Open Radio," Morton shared details about his early interactions with Watts, noting that their relationship included some tough love from the promoter.

> "I'll never forget, Bill Watts told me, 'Man, you'll never make it in this business, you're too little.' I said, 'Yes sir, but I'm going to try.' When we went to Louisiana and did our thing, it was absolutely good."

Morton also praised Watts as one of the greatest promoters in wrestling history, pointing out how he helped expand their events from National Guard armories to massive venues like the Superdome. Morton added that he had been thinking about Watts recently and spoke with his son, Eric Watts, who assured him of how much his father loved and respected him.
