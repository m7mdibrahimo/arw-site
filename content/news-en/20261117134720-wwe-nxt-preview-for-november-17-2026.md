The November 17 episode of WWE NXT delivers strong bouts as it builds toward the NXT TLC event. Kelani Jordan defends her NXT Women’s Championship against Kendal Grey after General Manager Robert Stone made the rematch official following their recent confrontation. In another match, Tony D’Angelo faces Kam Hendrix as Hendrix seeks to prove himself against a top name on the roster. The program takes place at the WWE Performance Center located in Orlando, Florida, beginning at 8 PM ET on the CW Network, with international streaming available on Netflix.
