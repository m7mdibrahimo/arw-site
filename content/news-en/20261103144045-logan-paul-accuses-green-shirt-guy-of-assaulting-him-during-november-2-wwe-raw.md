Logan Paul and Green Shirt Guy's ongoing feud took another heated turn during the November 2 episode of WWE RAW. Green Shirt Guy shared a video on Instagram showing Paul confronting him at ringside. Paul got extremely close and demanded that the fan be banned from WWE events, accusing him of assault.

> "You should be banned from these events. You should be banned. By the way, you just assaulted me — you should be banned from these events. I don't know how you even get these seats."

Paul then walked away looking furious, adding another bizarre chapter to their running ringside rivalry that has previously featured encounters in Mexico City, Riyadh, and Paris.
