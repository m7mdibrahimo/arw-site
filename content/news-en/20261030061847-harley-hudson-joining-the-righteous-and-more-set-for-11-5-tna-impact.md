TNA Wrestling has announced the lineup for the November 5 episode of TNA iMPACT. Harley Hudson is set to be initiated into The Righteous after accepting the duo's invitation. Meanwhile, Eddie Edwards will face Bear Bronson in a singles match; if Edwards wins, Bronson must follow his lead within The System, but if Bronson wins, he will take charge of the faction. In addition, the first-ever Knockouts World Tag Team Champions, Fire 'N Flava, will team up to take on Allie and Shotzi Blackheart. The advertised card also features the Nemeth Brothers, Nic Nemeth and Ryan Nemeth, going up against Leon Slater and Amazing Red.
