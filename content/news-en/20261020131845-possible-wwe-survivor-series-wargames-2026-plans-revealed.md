Possible plans for a WarGames match at WWE Survivor Series have been revealed following a major angle on WWE RAW. According to Dave Meltzer on Wrestling Observer Radio, WWE is planning to use the ongoing feud between Roman Reigns, The Bloodline, and The 946 for a WarGames match. The rivalry escalated during the October 19 episode of WWE RAW when Roman Reigns reunited with Jimmy Uso, Jey Uso, and Solo Sikoa to confront Royce Keys, Bronco Nima, Lucien Price, Carmelo Hayes, and Jaida Parker. Bryan Alvarez noted the strong crowd reaction to Reigns' return, and Meltzer confirmed that this storyline is indeed heading toward WarGames at Survivor Series on November 28.
