Cody Rhodes is planning to introduce a new variation of the American Nightmare logo featuring a wolf skull to honor his dog Pharaoh, who recently passed away after nearly 16 years. During an appearance on the Flagrant podcast, Rhodes explained that the updated design will feature a wolf skull turned in side profile with wings out to the side.

“I'm about to change – not change the logo because we can't change the tattoo. But for the first time ever, the way the American Nightmare, exactly this logo, which is on everything we do, it’s gonna be a wolf skull that's turned with the wings out to the side. I just lost my husky, Pharaoh, after almost 16 years. I thought I'm gonna give him a little run. See that logo, and just turn it side profile, husky wolf, dire wolf type looking thing. See what they come up with, but it should be pretty cool,” Rhodes said.

During the podcast appearance, Rhodes also discussed the concept of a Cody lie.
