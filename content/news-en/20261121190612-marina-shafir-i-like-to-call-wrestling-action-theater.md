Marina Shafir recently discussed how she describes professional wrestling, sharing that she prefers to call the industry action theater. During an interview with WFRV Local 5, she was asked about AEW Collision in Green Bay, expressing her excitement for the live audience and their energy.

> "No, I like to call it action theater. Yeah, it's like action improv theater, and we do our best that way, I think."
