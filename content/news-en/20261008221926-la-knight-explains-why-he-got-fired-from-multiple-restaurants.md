WWE Superstar LA Knight recently opened up about his past working in the restaurant industry before his rise to wrestling stardom. During an appearance on TMZ’s Inside the Ring, Knight shared his "Mount Rushmore" of restaurant jobs, detailing the challenges he faced while working at chains like Ruby Tuesday, TGI Fridays, and Applebee’s.

Knight admitted that he was fired from several of these jobs, though he clarified that the reasons were typically minor, such as arriving late. "I never got fired for anything crazy. It was usually like I was late too many times or something like that," he noted. He also discussed his time at the Cheesecake Factory in Brentwood, California, which he described as the most difficult serving experience of his life.

According to Knight, the staffing levels at that location made it nearly impossible to earn a living wage. "I think that was probably the one job that I had where I had to take out payday loans, like... a little too regularly to pay my rent. They didn’t want any of us having any more than three tables at a time," he explained. Today, the WWE star has moved far beyond those struggles, focusing on his current career in the ring.
