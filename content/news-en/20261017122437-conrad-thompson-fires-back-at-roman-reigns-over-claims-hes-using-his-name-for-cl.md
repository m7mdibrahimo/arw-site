Conrad Thompson has responded to Roman Reigns after the WWE star accused him of using his name for views. Thompson previously labeled Reigns overrated and an industry plant, prompting Reigns to laugh off the comments on Club Shay Shay and suggest Thompson was chasing clicks. Speaking to ALLCITY Wrestling, Thompson defended his remarks, explaining that interviewers constantly ask him about Reigns, and he simply answers their questions. Thompson also denied that his criticism stems from company loyalty, maintaining that he remains neutral in the WWE and AEW landscape. While he acknowledged Reigns as a major business success and praised his SummerSlam performance, Thompson stood by his belief that Roman does not belong on wrestling's Mount Rushmore alongside all-time greats.
