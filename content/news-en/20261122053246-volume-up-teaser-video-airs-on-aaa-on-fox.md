A new teaser titled Volume Up aired during the November 21 episode of Lucha Libre AAA. The video featured music playing loudly at an increased volume before concluding with the Spanish word for coming soon, while no further information was provided.

Joaquin Wilde and Cruz Del Toro of the LWO have hinted at a potential split recently. Wilde previously performed as DJ Z in TNA Wrestling and brought back that appearance in April on WWE Main Event, writing on social media, 

> "Not a reinvention. This is a correction."

It remains uncertain whether the teaser has any connection to the situation.
