Adriana Rizzo is not finished with professional wrestling following her recent release from WWE. The former NXT talent is currently waiting out her 30-day non-compete clause before exploring new opportunities. Speaking with Pario Magazine, Rizzo confirmed that she is not retiring from the ring. 

> "I think, um, right now I’m still within my 30 days, so I haven’t — once I get past the 30 days, I’ll really kind of start looking into options and see what’s out there. So that’s — I’m just kind of waiting until those 30 days are up right now. Yeah."

Rizzo's time in NXT was hampered by consecutive knee injuries, which stalled her momentum. Dave Meltzer of Wrestling Observer Radio noted that her departure was also influenced by the creative direction of Tony D'Angelo, as there was no longer a clear spot for her in his storyline. While Meltzer suggested AEW or Ring of Honor as potential destinations, Rizzo has not confirmed any interest from other promotions.
