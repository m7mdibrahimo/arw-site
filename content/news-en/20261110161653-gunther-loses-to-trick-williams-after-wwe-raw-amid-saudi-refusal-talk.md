Gunther suffered a pinfall loss to Trick Williams following Monday Night Raw, raising questions among fans due to the timing. The untelevised main event took place after the November 9, 2026, episode in Greenville, South Carolina, where Williams secured the victory by hitting The Bookend.

The defeat arrives shortly after reports emerged that Gunther refused to travel to Saudi Arabia for Crown Jewel. Dave Meltzer previously revealed that Gunther was originally scheduled to wrestle Demon Finn Balor on November 7, but those plans were dropped after Gunther decided against making the trip.

While some fans wonder if the loss is a punishment, others suggest a simpler explanation. Williams is from nearby Columbia, South Carolina, meaning the win could simply have been a way to reward the local favorite in front of his home-state crowd, and Gunther has taken untelevised losses before without any confirmed backstage heat.
