UFC Fight Night: Moicano vs. Nolan will take place on Saturday, October 31 from the Meta APEX in Las Vegas, Nevada. The event will air on Paramount+. MyBookie has released the betting odds for the entire fight card.

In the main event at 155 pounds, Renato Moicano is listed at -126 against Tom Nolan at -101. Other matches include Jacobe Smith at -280 facing Randy Brown at +208 in the 170-pound division, Luana Santos at -350 taking on Yana Santos at +250 in the 135-pound division, and Cody Durden at -121 meeting Ode Osbourne at -105 in the 125-pound division.

Additional matchups feature Zach Reese at -391 against Mickey Gall at +280 at 185 pounds, Jean-Paul Lebosnoyani at -319 facing Farman Hasanov at +234 at 170 pounds, Francis Marshall at -228 taking on Dakota Hope at +174 at 145 pounds, and Rodrigo Sezinando at -181 meeting Theodor Berggren at +141 at 170 pounds.

The card also features Jeong Yeong Lee at -169 against Julian Erosa at +131 at 145 pounds, Joe Kropschot at -340 facing Nick Klein at +248 at 185 pounds, Piera Rodriguez at -295 taking on Talita Alencar at +218 at 115 pounds, and Lucia Szabova at -847 meeting Tainara Lisboa at +490 at 135 pounds.
