Following Vince Russo’s departure from Juggalo Championship Wrestling in September, promotion owner Violent J has stepped back in to handle the creative side directly. JCW American Champion Facade revealed during an appearance on Rob Van Dam's 1 Of A Kind podcast that Violent J, who previously oversaw creative before Russo joined, has resumed full control as the final decision-maker.

Facade praised Russo's willingness to adapt storylines based on crowd reactions rather than sticking rigidly to pre-planned ideas during their time working together. Russo officially announced his exit on September 22, 2026, after growing unhappy with his position, though he and Violent J have since discussed potential occasional appearances. For now, Violent J remains firmly in control of JCW creative without bringing in a replacement.
