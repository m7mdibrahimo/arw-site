Enzo Amore has added fuel to the rumors regarding a potential WWE return. With speculation growing that the former Cruiserweight Champion has already signed a new deal, Amore posted an extensive video on Instagram on November 10, 2026, where he discussed his career, his controversial exit, and the prospect of a comeback.

While Amore did not confirm a signed contract, he notably used the phrase "never say never" throughout the promo. Amore also addressed the ongoing speculation, stating, "I'm the last threat left. I have been shrouded in controversy. I faced adversity, back against the wall, lost it all. And I stood tall, did it my way, dawg."

He also took a jab at Cody Rhodes, referring to him as "Dusty Rhodes' son," and claimed, "The truest kick in the nuts is going to be when he breaks his leg on Geez Nuts." Amore further boasted about his status in the industry, declaring himself the "real world champ" and asserting that he remains one of the best performers in the business.

Reports have swirled for months regarding Amore's connection to WWE. On November 4, PWInsider reported that some within the company believed Amore had recently signed a new contract, with NXT being a potential destination. Additionally, Amore and TNA wrestler Joe Alonzo were spotted visiting the WWE Performance Center on November 4. Amore's former tag team partner, Big Cass, is already back with WWE on Monday Night RAW, though reports suggest WWE may keep the duo apart for now.
