ESPN recently aired a graphic highlighting notable professional wrestlers who also played college football, but current WWE World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns was notably absent from the list. The network featured names like Ron Simmons, Steve Austin, The Rock, Bill Goldberg, and Bron Breakker, overlooking Reigns despite his successful career as a three-year starter and First Team All-ACC defensive tackle at Georgia Tech. Fans quickly took to social media to question the omission, especially given Reigns' accomplished football background and WWE's recent prominent utilization of his Georgia Tech history.
