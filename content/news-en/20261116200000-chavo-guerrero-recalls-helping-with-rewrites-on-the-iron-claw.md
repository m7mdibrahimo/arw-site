Chavo Guerrero has become the go-to person in Hollywood for authentic wrestling depictions, lending his expertise in choreography, scripts, and set design to projects like 'Disclosure Day', 'Margo's Got Money Troubles', and 'The Iron Claw'. In a recent interview with Fightful, Guerrero recalled a specific moment on the set of 'The Iron Claw' where he assisted with on-the-spot script rewrites. Guerrero explained that he noticed a disconnect between the choreography and the dialogue during filming, prompting him to speak with director Sean Durkin.

> "I’m involved with everything, whether it be helping cast the part, choreography, wardrobe, set design, scripts. I mean, there was a time in ‘The Iron Claw’ when something wasn't matching the choreography and the verbiage. We're shooting, I looked at Sean Durkin, the director, and he's like, ‘What’s wrong?’ Man, this isn't fitting. He's like, ‘Well, what do you think?’ I started telling him and he's like, ‘No, just tell me everything.’ We rewrote the scene in a sense eight there, or at least the verbiage. He looked at Zac Efron like, ‘Hey man, can you do this right now?’ Of course, Zac looks it over and it's like, ‘Yup, we got it.’ So we just re-wrote it right there, made it fit, you know? I've been doing that since day one, I should be getting writing credit, you know?"

Guerrero also noted that he provided significant input for the Netflix series 'GLOW', which ran from 2017 to 2019.

> "A lot on GLOW, they were doing a lot of stories — a lot of stories on GLOW were my stories that we just tweaked a little bit. I was in the writing room talking to the writers and they were kind of just brainstorming off me before the season started. Next thing I'm reading the script like, ‘Hey, wait a minute, that's my story.’ They start laughing, yep. So, I'm involved kind of in all of it. Anything with the wrestling part, I'm involved with the entire process of it and that's what they get when they hire me."
