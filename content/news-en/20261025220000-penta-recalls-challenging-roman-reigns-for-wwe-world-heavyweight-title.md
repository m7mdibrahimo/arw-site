Penta may have fallen to Roman Reigns on WWE RAW last month, but he left the ring feeling victorious. During a recent appearance on Busted Open Radio, the high-flying star praised the experience of sharing the ring with "The Tribal Chief" in his hometown of Mexico City.

> "I learned a lot facing Roman Reigns because, to be honest, I know now why he is one of the best wrestlers, or the best wrestler in the universe. The character, the Shakespeare, every move, you can breathe, you can enjoy every move."

Penta described the atmosphere as magical, noting how significant it felt to face Reigns in front of his home crowd. The match, which took place in the main event of WWE RAW on September 14, saw Reigns successfully defend his WWE World Heavyweight Championship after countering a Mexican Destroyer from Penta with a spear.

This was the first singles encounter between the two stars and Penta's first televised world title opportunity in WWE. As the 2026 Money in the Bank winner, Penta retains the option to challenge for a rematch at any time and location he chooses.
