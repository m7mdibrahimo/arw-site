WWE Hall of Famer Jim Ross has received positive news regarding his health, as his scheduled brain surgery has been postponed. Medical professionals had prepared to operate on his brain shunt on November 10, but a favorable CT scan prompted a last-minute change in plans.

Conrad Thompson provided an update on X on November 11, 2026, following fan inquiries about the legendary announcer's status. Thompson explained the situation regarding the procedure:

> "They postponed it after a good CT scan. Hoping for another good report this morning with a new CT scan!"

This development offers relief for Ross, who has faced numerous medical challenges in recent months. The surgery was intended to address complications with his shunt, which is used to drain excess fluid. While the situation remains under medical observation, it is currently unclear if the operation has been canceled entirely or merely delayed pending further evaluation.
