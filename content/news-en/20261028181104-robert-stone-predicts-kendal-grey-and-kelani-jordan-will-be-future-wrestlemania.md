Robert Stone has predicted that Kendal Grey and Kelani Jordan will one day headline WrestleMania. Speaking in an interview with Pro Wrestling Illustrated, the WWE NXT General Manager praised the two rising stars, highlighting their performances and impact on the women's division. Stone stated that their consistent delivery in high-profile matches and their ability to raise the bar make them the future of women's wrestling.
