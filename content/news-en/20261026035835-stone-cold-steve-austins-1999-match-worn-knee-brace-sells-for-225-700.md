A match-worn and signed knee brace used by Stone Cold Steve Austin around 1999 has been sold at auction for $225,700 through Heritage Auctions. The DonJoy brace features Austin's signature in gold paint pen, the date May 8, 1999, and a WWF authentication sticker. It comes accompanied by a WWE letter signed by Linda McMahon, along with authentication letters from Heritage Auctions, PSA/DNA, and Beckett Authentication Services. This sale sets a new record, surpassing a previous auction of another Austin knee brace that sold for $115,900 in May 2026.
