El Hijo del Vikingo is set to be featured on Netflix's upcoming Mexican competition series titled Secreto Millonario. The show is a spin-off of their show Million Dollar Secret. The cast includes current WWE talent and former AAA Mega Champion El Hijo del Vikingo, alongside Lupita Villalobos, Paola Suárez, Yordi Rosado, Sergio Mayer, Gusgri, Susana Zabaleta, Paola Rojas, Kike Vázquez, Maryfer Centeno, Emmanuel Román Morales, and Miguel Martínez. Vikingo is currently recovering from knee surgery.
