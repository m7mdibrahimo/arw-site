Tatum Paxley recently discussed her journey in WWE and her experience working alongside her main roster mentor and tag team partner, Alexa Bliss. During an interview with No Holds Barred, Paxley explained how Bliss inspired her to pursue professional wrestling and how surreal it is to now work beside her. Paxley also reflected on her character evolution, noting that after transitioning away from her early powerlifting gimmick, she embraced a darker persona that helped her find confidence and connect with fans. She and Bliss captured the WWE Women's Tag Team Championships earlier this month by defeating Fallon Henley and Lainey Reid on WWE SmackDown.
