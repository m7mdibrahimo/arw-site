Pro Wrestling NOAH has announced the launch of the Neo Green Boys recruitment program and a special rookie tournament. The audition project aims to attract aspiring male and female wrestlers aged 16 to 25, with plans to stream the process on YouTube and other platforms. Akihiro Takeda, director of NOAH's parent company CyberFight, stated that the goal is to build recognition for participants before their official debut. The application deadline is November 30, with final judging scheduled for March 2027 by a panel featuring Keiji Muto, Naomichi Marufuji, and Kenoh, followed by training at the NOAH Dojo for successful candidates. In addition, NOAH has organized the Neo Green Boys League for current rookie talent, running from December 5 through December 18 with matches between Hiroya Namiki, Yuto Koyanagi, Hiroto Tsuruya, and RYUSEI, culminating in a final match on January 1, 2027, at Korakuen Hall where the winner will head overseas.
