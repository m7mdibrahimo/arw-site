Tonight's AEW Dynamite/Collision aired live from the Maverik Center in West Valley City, Utah, as the go-home show for AEW Full Gear. AEW National Champion Tommaso Ciampa defended his title against Mike Bailey, while Mercedes Moné and Jay White faced The Death Riders in a Mixed Tag Team Match. In the opening match, The New Level and Brodido fought to a Double Count Out. David Finlay defeated Orange Cassidy. Persephone defeated Gypsy Mac in a non-title match. Motor City Machine Guns defeated FTR to win their Best of 5 Series. Tommaso Ciampa defeated Mike Bailey to retain the AEW National Championship. The Death Riders defeated Mercedes Moné and Jay White. The Elite defeated the Don Callis Family. Wardlow defeated Nick Comoroto. El Sky Team defeated The Swirl to retain the ROH World Tag Team Championship. In the main event, Queen Aminata defeated Skye Blue and Hyan in a 3-Way Match to retain the AEW TBS Championship.
