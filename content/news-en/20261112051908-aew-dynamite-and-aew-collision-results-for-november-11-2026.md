Tonight's AEW Dynamite and AEW Collision airing November 11, 2026, emanated live from the Maverik Center in West Valley City, Utah. The go-home show for AEW Full Gear featured a loaded card, highlighted by Tommaso Ciampa defending the AEW National Championship against Mike Bailey, and a mixed tag team match where Mercedes Moné and Jay White faced Marina Shafir and Jon Moxley. Will Ospreay and Kyle Fletcher engaged in a heated face-to-face confrontation in the ring to build toward their world title match at the upcoming pay-per-view.

In the opening match of the night, The New Level faced Brodido in a tag team match that ended in a double count-out as brawling spilled everywhere. Next, David Finlay defeated Orange Cassidy in a singles match after using a shillelagh behind the referee's back. In non-title action, CMLL World Women’s Champion Persephone defeated Gypsy Mac. In the fifth match of their best-of-five series, Motor City Machine Guns defeated FTR to win the series. Tommaso Ciampa successfully defended the AEW National Championship against Mike Bailey, followed by Ilja Dragunov confronting the champion. The Death Riders defeated Mercedes Moné and Jay White in a mixed tag team match when Marina Shafir forced Moné to submit. The Elite defeated the Don Callis Family in a trios match, and Wardlow defeated Nick Comoroto in singles competition. In the ROH World Tag Team Championship match, El Sky Team successfully defended their titles against The Swirl. The main event saw Queen Aminata defend the AEW TBS Championship against Skye Blue and Hyan in a three-way match.
