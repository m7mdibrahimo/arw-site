Sheamus is proud of his in-ring physicality, noting that he has embraced a more aggressive style in recent years. This approach has been a key factor in his late-career resurgence, which included high-profile matches against stars like Gunther and Drew McIntyre. In an interview with Strength Required, Sheamus stated that he believes he is the most physical wrestler in the industry.

> "My style is very physical, probably more physical than anybody in the business. I pride myself on that, but it comes at a cost because when I dish it out to my opponent, I expect them to dish it back. I enjoy that part of it, I'm kind of weird. I remember Grayson Waller said I'm a lunatic who just enjoys pain and stuff, maybe I do on some level. But I also enjoy the fact that I can get a crowd intrigued and hypnotized by what happens, kind of second guessing like, ‘Well, I thought this was entertainment’ when they see a Sheamus match."
