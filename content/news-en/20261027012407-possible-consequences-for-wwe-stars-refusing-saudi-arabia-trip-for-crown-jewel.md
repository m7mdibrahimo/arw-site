WWE talent can technically decline the trip to Saudi Arabia for Crown Jewel, but there may be professional repercussions for those who choose to stay home. With the November 7 event approaching, WrestleVotes addressed the potential fallout during a recent Q&A session. While no wrestler is being physically forced onto a plane, those who do not attend will not be paid for the show.

Beyond the loss of pay, there could be a lasting stigma associated with the decision. WrestleVotes suggested that a wrestler's standing within the company could change, stating:

> "They could say they're not going, and they won't get paid, and the rest of their career will be reflective of that. Not the pay, but the stigma — the position that they might find themselves no longer in. They can say, ‘I'm staying home. I'm watching it on my couch. You can figure it out,’ but things may be different when they go back to work."

Backstage morale remains complex, with sources previously describing the situation as "absolute disarray." While WWE and TKO executives, including Ari Emanuel, are monitoring the daily situation to explore potential alternatives if necessary, the event is currently scheduled for Riyadh Season Stadium. For now, the promotion continues to market the event as planned, including the main event of Roman Reigns vs. Sami Zayn for the WWE Crown Jewel Championship.
