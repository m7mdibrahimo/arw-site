John Cena has faced many tough opponents in his WWE career, but he recently met his match in a very specific challenge. Cena attempted to out-eat Cookie Monster in a contest involving cookies and milk, a showdown that the official Sesame Street X account teased with a warning for the 17-time World Champion: "John Cena’s time is up, Cookie Monster’s time is now!! 🍪🏆"

Before the eating began, the two had a face-off reminiscent of a WrestleMania main event, with Cookie Monster even incorporating Cena's famous catchphrases into his trash talk. "You no can see me. Cookiemania is running wild!" Cookie Monster said. Once the cookies were served, Cena quickly realized he was in over his head, as Cookie Monster dominated the competition.

Cookie Monster emerged victorious and was presented with a giant cookie-shaped championship belt. When Cena remarked, "The Champ is here," Cookie Monster immediately began eating his new title. Cena had to intervene with some advice, telling him, "Cookie, it doesn't work like that." Cookie Monster's celebration, which included trying to eat the prize, was a fitting end to a unique title win.
