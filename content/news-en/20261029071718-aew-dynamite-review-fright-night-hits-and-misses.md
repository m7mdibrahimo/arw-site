AEW presented a special Fright Night edition of "AEW Dynamite" featuring title matches and major storyline developments. Despite the in-ring action, several aspects of the show drew mixed reactions from the staff.

On the negative side, the Halloween theme fell flat because the show featured zero spooky stipulation matches due to athletic commission restrictions and preparations for Blood & Guts. Additionally, the build for Mercedes Moné and Marina Shafir over the AEW Women's World Championship was overshadowed by a chaotic brawl involving The Cut and the Death Riders. Furthermore, the build for the women's Blood & Guts match felt rushed and disjointed compared to the men's counterpart.

On the positive side, the Texas Death Match storyline continued to heat up with Swerve Strickland demanding a rematch against "Hangman" Adam Page, Brody King, and Bandido, culminating in a bloody backstage scene involving Strickland and Bandido. Meanwhile, Darby Allin successfully defended his TNT Championship against Josh Alexander in a hard-fought encounter just one week before Blood & Guts. In the main event, Queen Aminata captured the AEW TBS Championship by defeating Persephone following an intense contest.
