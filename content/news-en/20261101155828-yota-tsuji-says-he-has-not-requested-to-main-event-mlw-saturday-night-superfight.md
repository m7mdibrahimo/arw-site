IWGP Heavyweight Champion Yota Tsuji addressed his placement on the card for the upcoming MLW Saturday Night SuperFight event. Tsuji is scheduled to face Trevor Lee in a singles match on November 7th in Cicero, Illinois. Speaking in a new interview with Fightful Overbooked's In The Weeds, Tsuji clarified through a translator that he has not asked to be in the main event of the show. Tsuji explained that his previous remarks regarding the main event were simply intended to ensure the IWGP Heavyweight Championship receives the same level of respect as other major titles. "So I guess you guys are referring to the fact that I mentioned about the main event. I think just because to put the IWGP Heavyweight title on the show, wanted to- our IWGP belt championship as treated us as respectfully as other promotions," Tsuji stated. He added that he is grateful to be featured on MLW's biggest pay-per-view event of the year and would gladly accept a main event spot if offered, though he has not made any official requests. "So I don't personally have the request to my match to be like a main event or otherwise I want less than that. I'm not like in that kind of attitude, but you know, like for, this MLW show, which is their biggest show on pay-per-view of the year, so I am really grateful to be part of that, and I really would like to be thankful to be involved in. But if and only if MLW is willing to treat me in such a respectful manner, I would gladly accept to be involved in the main event, but I don't personally make requests and I have not so far, I have not been told what match order it is going to be, but regardless, I'm happy to be involved in the show," he said.
