Speedball Mike Bailey recently discussed his United Empire faction teammate Will Ospreay, emphasizing that the AEW World Champion does not hesitate in the ring. Speaking to The Wrestling Classic, Bailey noted that Ospreay operates at full intensity, which is a trait he first observed when they wrestled at PWG in 2015.

> "There are absolutely no half measures with Will Ospreay. There is nothing that he does, not a single movement, not a decision that he hesitates on. Everything is 100%, and I mean, that gets him in trouble sometimes. You see him overcommit, make decisions a bit too fast, and that might be his one flaw. But I mean, watching him go is absolutely impressive. The commitment and force with which he moves forwards, and that's something that the first time we wrestled, which was at PWG in 2015, I could feel about Will Ospreay, and it's something that he has maintained to this day. That has taken him to where he is now, which is AEW World Champion, the best wrestler in the world."
