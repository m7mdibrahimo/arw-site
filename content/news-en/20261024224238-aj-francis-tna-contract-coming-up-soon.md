AJ Francis could be heading into free agency soon, but he is expected to stay with TNA Wrestling. According to Sean Ross Sapp of Fightful Select, his current deal expires at the end of the year, but both sides want to continue their relationship. Francis is interested in remaining with the company, and management wants to keep him. A new agreement is anticipated though nothing is finalized yet. During his three years with TNA, Francis won the TNA Digital Media Championship, brought back the Canadian International Heavyweight Championship, and worked in major storylines with Joe Hendry, Mike Santana and Elijah. In an interview with The Wrestling Classic, Francis expressed his happiness and appreciation within the company.

> "I felt like I have a home in TNA because they recognize what I bring to the table."

Francis also praised the creative freedom he receives and his active role in shaping his storylines.

> "I'm calling the plays. You know what I'm saying? And they sending me out there first to set the tone."

Before his TNA run, Francis competed in WWE as Top Dolla and made appearances for NWA and MLW. Currently, there are no signs of him leaving, and negotiations point toward a new deal.
