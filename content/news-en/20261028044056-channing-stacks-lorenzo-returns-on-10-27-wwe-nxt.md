Channing 'Stacks' Lorenzo has returned to WWE NXT. During the October 27th episode of WWE NXT on The CW, Zilla Fatu appeared on stage to spin the Halloween Havoc wheel to determine his match type against Charlie Dempsey at the upcoming Premium Live Event. Before he could do so, Arianna Grace distracted Fatu, allowing her BirthRight stablemates Lexis King, Charlie Dempsey, Uriah Connors, and Channing 'Stacks' Lorenzo to ambush him. They targeted Fatu's previously injured hand and smashed it with a shovel. Stacks made his return following an absence after being stretchered off at an NXT house show, later providing an update on social media confirming he is back to regular programming.
