Following the passing of Benjamin Pac Satterley on September 27, the wrestling community united to honor him. AEW held a tribute episode on AEW Dynamite, while RevPro and Dragon Gate hosted ten-bell salutes during their first shows following his death.

Becky Lynch shared her memories, admitting she felt intimidated by him when she joined WWE NXT in the 2010s due to his immense skills and high professionalism. She sent her regards to his family, especially his wife Natalie. Finn Balor recalled their first meeting in Newcastle and their travels around the world, thanking his friend for the good times.

William Regal praised Pac's career, viewing him as Britain's top talent and commending him for staying true to himself even after leaving WWE in 2017. Regal stated that he will miss him every day.
