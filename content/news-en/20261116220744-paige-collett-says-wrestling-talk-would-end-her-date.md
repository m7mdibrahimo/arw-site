Paige Collett stated that discussing professional wrestling during a romantic outing is the quickest way to prevent a second encounter. The JCW star revealed on Headlocks, Heels, & Havoc: Wrestling Unscripted that she has no interest in dating fans of the sport and would terminate an outing immediately if her companion brought up the industry.

> "If I was on a date and someone brought up professional wrestling, the date would be over."

Collett noted that between promoting her bouts, advancing her career, and raising awareness for complex regional pain syndrome, she spends sufficient time talking about the business. She added that being single while managing a chronic illness complicates romantic relationships, noting that individuals outside the industry struggle to comprehend her lifestyle.
