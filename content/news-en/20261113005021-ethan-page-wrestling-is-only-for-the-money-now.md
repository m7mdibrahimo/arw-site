WWE Intercontinental Champion Ethan Page has confirmed that he is no longer interested in pursuing wrestling dreams. He stated that he has achieved everything he wanted from the sport and is currently only participating for financial gain.

In a recent interview, Page was asked about his remaining goals following his victory over Chad Gable to win the Intercontinental Championship. He explained that he no longer has personal objectives, noting that his priorities have shifted and his former passion has completely faded.

Page emphasized that WWE cannot motivate him with new accomplishments. He added that his future decisions will be strictly political and financial, and he is prepared to turn on anyone if it serves his material interests.
