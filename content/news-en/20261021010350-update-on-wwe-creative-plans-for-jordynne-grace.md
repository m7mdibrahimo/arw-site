According to Fightful, WWE developed tentative creative plans for Jordynne Grace in September. The company intended to begin teasing a tag team featuring Grace and Ivy Nile that month, utilizing vignettes that were filmed previously.

Grace has not competed in a wrestling match since June, when she appeared on WWE Main Event. Since that time, she has returned to participating in bodybuilding competitions.
