Zelina Vega took her role as Andrade's manager very seriously during their time in WWE, both on-screen and behind the scenes. In a recent appearance on Dakota Kai's YouTube channel, Vega revealed that she often had to intervene when other women sought Andrade's attention backstage. She referred to these women as "sucias." "He was, you know, he's a very attractive person, and so there's a lot of sucias. I would call them dirty," Vega explained. She admitted that she would actively prevent these interactions, especially when they interfered with work obligations. "Whenever we would get to a new place, like a new city, I would say, 'No more sucias, please. Like, can we not do this today?'" Vega noted that she was unapologetic about her behavior, adding, "Oh my God, I was such a little cockblocker. I did not care." Vega would even interrupt conversations if Andrade was needed for pre-tapes. She recalled, "I would kind of move her out of the way, and I would just be like, 'Hey, um, no, no sucias, please. We have to get this done, okay?'" According to Vega, Andrade was aware of her actions and found them amusing rather than offensive, viewing their dynamic as more of a sibling relationship.
