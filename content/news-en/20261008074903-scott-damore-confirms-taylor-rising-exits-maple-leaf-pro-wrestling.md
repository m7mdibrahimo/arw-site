Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling owner Scott D'Amore made a surprise cameo appearance on a recent episode of CMLL Informa to share a major update regarding his promotion's roster. During the broadcast, D'Amore announced that Taylor Rising and Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling have officially parted ways.

As a result of her departure, Rising will no longer participate in the upcoming CMLL Women's Grand Prix. D'Amore confirmed that Alice Crowley has been selected to replace Rising in the tournament line-up.
