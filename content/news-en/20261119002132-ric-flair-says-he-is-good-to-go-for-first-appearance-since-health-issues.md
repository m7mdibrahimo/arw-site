Ric Flair has recovered from a difficult health period involving a severe fall and an eye injury, and he is preparing to make his return. NGC Wrestling revealed on Instagram that Flair is scheduled to appear at NGC Wrestling: The Grand Stage at Harpos Concert Theatre in Detroit on November 21, marking his first appearance since his recent medical struggles. During an interview with Brandon Hudson of FOX 2 Detroit, Flair stated that he feels completely ready for the comeback.

> "I'm good to go. I love Detroit. Had a lot of fun there, wrestled there many times."

At 77 years old, Flair explained that he continues to take pleasure in traveling and engaging with fans as he gets older.

This upcoming appearance follows his October 23 fall in Florida, which caused facial and eye injuries that required treatment at Tampa General Hospital. Despite the significant challenges he has faced throughout his life, Flair remains determined to keep traveling and connecting with his supporters at various events.
