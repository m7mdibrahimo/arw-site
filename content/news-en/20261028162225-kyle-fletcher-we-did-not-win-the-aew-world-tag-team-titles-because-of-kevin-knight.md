Kyle Fletcher says he and Kevin Knight fell short in their AEW World Tag Team Title challenge at AEW WrestleDream because of Knight.

At AEW WrestleDream on October 17, The Young Bucks defended the AEW World Tag Team Titles against the Don Callis Family duo of Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher. The Young Bucks won the hard-fought match after a spat between the two partners, which Knight started.

Speaking on his latest Protolog, Fletcher looked back on the match and blamed his partner for the loss.

> "We did not win the championships because Kevin Knight is a stupid poo-poo head, and I'm gonna have some very choice words with that man. Because I should be a tag team champion of the world. But I digress. I'm now very tired. Today is over. Cut me some slack. I got super kicked at least 10 times in the face."
