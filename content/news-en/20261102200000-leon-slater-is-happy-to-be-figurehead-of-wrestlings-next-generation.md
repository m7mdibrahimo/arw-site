TNA World Champion Leon Slater recently talked about the rising opportunities for young wrestlers and expressed his willingness to represent his generation. Over the past year, many young stars across TNA, WWE, and AEW have received significant pushes, with Slater standing out as one of the most successful after capturing the world title. The young competitor believes his championship victory could serve as a catalyst for other youngsters to secure bigger opportunities.

During an interview with Wrestling Life Online, Leon Slater discussed his historic milestone.

> "It's not only being the TNA World Champion, being at the stage of my career that I'm in and at the age that I'm at, like, it's wider than TNA. I am the youngest world champion in professional wrestling history, especially at least in the last 100 years or so"

He noted that this could mark the beginning of a new movement, pointing to young talent such as Je'Von Evans, Roxanne Perez, Nick Wayne, and Kyle Fletcher. Slater emphasized that performers under 25 and 30 years old are establishing themselves as the present of the industry rather than just the future.

The TNA star stated that he accepts being the face of this emerging generation and welcomes the associated pressure. He concluded with his thoughts on being trusted by his company.

> "And to be the first person of that generation to win a world title and be trusted by their company to represent them, I could say it's a burden. But again, it's a privilege that I welcome, and it's a pressure that I welcome, and I'm glad that I can be the figurehead for this next generation"

Slater remains focused on achieving massive success in the industry and recently explained that he aims to emulate TNA legend AJ Styles while hoping to surpass him in the promotion.
