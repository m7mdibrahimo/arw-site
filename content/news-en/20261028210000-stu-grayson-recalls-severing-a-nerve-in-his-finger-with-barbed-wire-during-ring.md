During a recent interview, Stu Grayson looked back at a gruesome injury he suffered during a Ring of Honor match against John Silver and the Righteous. Grayson explained that while he was wrapping his knee in barbed wire to deliver a knee strike, the wire accidentally caught his finger and cut a nerve. The injury caused him to temporarily lose feeling in his finger for about a month, though doctors assured him it would heal naturally. Grayson noted that the painful incident went completely unnoticed by anyone else in the ring or the fans watching.
