Cody Rhodes recently shared his thoughts on the current state of professional wrestling, highlighting its growing cultural impact both inside and outside the ring. Rhodes, who appeared in the Street Fighter movie alongside fellow wrestlers Roman Reigns and Hirooki Goto, expressed his pride in seeing multiple wrestlers make the jump to mainstream entertainment. He noted that their distinct personalities proved to be a hit with audiences, comparing the experience to watching Dave Batista's breakout moments in the Guardians of the Galaxy films. Reflecting on the business as a whole, Rhodes stated that the industry is currently in the best shape of its history. He praised the leadership of WWE President Nick Khan and Chief Content Officer Triple H, emphasizing that wrestling no longer needs to fight for mainstream recognition. Rhodes also echoed the sentiments of his friend John Cena, predicting that strong performances will continue to open doors for more wrestlers in future entertainment projects.
