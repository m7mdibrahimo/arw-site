WWE Superstar Jacob Fatu is returning to his roots for a special one-night-only appearance. Fatu will headline Oasis Pro Wrestling's "YEAR 4Atuuuu" anniversary event on November 14 at the Grand Temple Arena in Berkeley, California. Before rising to prominence in WWE, Fatu served as the promotion's head coach, making this homecoming a significant moment for both the wrestler and the promotion.

Oasis Pro Wrestling announced the news on Instagram, stating, "We are proud to announce that our former head coach & current WWE Superstar JACOB FATU is returning HOME for 1 night only!" The event will feature a meet-and-greet session where fans can get photos and signed items. With only 150 general admission tickets available, the promotion expects an intimate setting for the celebration.

Fatu also shared his excitement on social media, telling fans in Berkeley to prepare for his return. "Berkeley, California, November 14, Oasis Pro — the Samoan Werewolf is coming home. Pull up on us. All gas, no brakes!" This appearance marks a return to the promotion that helped shape his career before his current WWE success.
