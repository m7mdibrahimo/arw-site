Sherilyn Guerrero recently discussed the meaning behind her "Latina Heat" wrestling moniker. During the November 14 event held by Reality of Wrestling, Guerrero competed in the main event against Mystii Marks, marking her first wrestling match. She carries on a family legacy as the daughter of the late Eddie Guerrero, who famously used the "Latino Heat" nickname during his career.

Speaking with Brad Gilmore, Guerrero explained why the nickname suits her. "Yes, Latina Heat was always something I even used as like a nickname or something because there's this interview and my dad's documentary that they did on him," Guerrero stated. She added that she had a sense of what it meant even as a child, noting her confidence during that interview and feeling that she has been using the term since she was young.

Guerrero further elaborated on why the moniker fits her personality and career. "I think it's actually a perfect representation because when I think of heat, I think of a drive that nobody can put out," she said. She described feeling like a Hot Wheels car with flames coming out of the back, noting that she has needed that inner fire throughout her young life to establish her own identity. Guerrero admitted she is still slightly surprised by the level of strength and drive she was able to pull from within for her debut, expressing pride in her readiness and how right the name feels for her.
