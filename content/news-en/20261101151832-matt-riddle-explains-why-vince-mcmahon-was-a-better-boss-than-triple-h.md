Matt Riddle recently discussed his experiences working with major figures in professional wrestling and mixed martial arts, explaining why he considers Vince McMahon the best boss he ever had. During an interview in New Zealand, Riddle was asked to evaluate working under prominent names like Dana White, Triple H, Shawn Michaels, and Vince McMahon.

Riddle stated that he prefers absolute honesty in a work environment, pointing out that McMahon addressed mistakes directly and without hesitation. He explained that Vince did not sugarcoat anything, telling the wrestler right to his face when a mistake happened and how to fix it to ensure his continued presence in the promotion.

> "You know, I'm a big thing when it comes to bosses. I love brutal honesty. And for me, out of all of them, Vince was the best boss. If he did something he didn't like, he didn't wait. He didn't say, ‘Good work.’ He told you right to your face, and he told you you messed up and you need to fix this or you're not going to be here much longer. And you adapt, right?"

In contrast, Riddle recalled difficult memories with Dana White in UFC, noting that public criticisms against him came after his departure from the promotion, and he confirmed that his public support for cannabis negatively affected his relationship with management at the time due to alcohol sponsorships and other factors. Despite the ups and downs of his WWE career, Riddle indicated that he preferred the direct clarity that characterized McMahon throughout their time working together.
