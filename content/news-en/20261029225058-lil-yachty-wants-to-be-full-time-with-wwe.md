Lil Yachty says he is ready to step away from his music career if his WWE run progresses as he hopes. The Grammy-nominated artist, who currently serves as an ally to United States Champion Trick Williams, told Complex that the discipline required in WWE is unlike anything he has experienced in the music industry. Yachty explained that while he controls his own schedule in music, the militant environment of WWE demands a level of focus and discipline that he finds captivating. He noted that he is actively trying to transition into a full-time role with the company. Yachty stated that if he successfully climbs the ranks in WWE and secures the pay he desires, he would be willing to give up his music career entirely to focus on wrestling. He joked that he would be happy to return to the ring, get thrown through tables, and perform until his body can no longer keep up. Yachty has not appeared on WWE programming since being attacked by Baron Corbin on the September 11 episode of WWE SmackDown, though he did make his in-ring debut against Corbin at Sunday Night's Main Event.
