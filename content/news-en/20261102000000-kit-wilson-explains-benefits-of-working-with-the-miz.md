Kit Wilson stated that his partnership with The Miz in WWE helped advance his career significantly after his tag team partner Elton Prince suffered an injury. Speaking on the Battleground Podcast, Wilson explained that The Miz teaches him many lessons and leads by example, noting that the most important thing he learned is to be prepared for anything at any moment, according to Wrestling Inc.

Wilson discussed the ongoing adjustments the duo faced in their booking schedule, recalling the backstage details of interrupting John Cena at WrestleMania after their original plans left them without a match. He noted that The Miz took the initiative to create a special moment despite there being no prior arrangement.

The pair actually faced John Cena and Danhausen at WrestleMania this year, and the match ended with Wilson and The Miz taking low blows from Danhausen and his Mini-Hausens. Although Wilson has been absent from the main television shows recently, he continues to compete at live events and on WWE Main Event.
