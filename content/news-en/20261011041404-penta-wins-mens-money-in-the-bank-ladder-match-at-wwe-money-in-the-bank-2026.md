Penta secured the biggest victory of his WWE career by winning the men’s Money in the Bank ladder match at WWE Money in the Bank 2026. He outlasted Bron Breakker, Trick Williams, Je’Von Evans, CM Punk, and Kevin Owens in a chaotic encounter filled with high-flying action and brutal spots. Evans suffered a scary moment after taking a midair spear from Breakker and being stretchered into an ambulance, only to shock everyone by returning later in the match before crashing through the ambulance roof. In the dramatic finish, Penta dropped Breakker with a destroyer onto a bridged ladder before climbing to pull down the briefcase and guarantee himself a future world championship match.
