Cody Rhodes has been exploring the acting world recently, most notably playing the video game character Guile in the 2026 adaptation of Street Fighter. Balancing his role as a top WWE star with a growing Hollywood career, Rhodes believes the two fields share distinct similarities.

"It's all part of the art of storytelling," he explained on his podcast, What Do You Wanna Talk About? With Cody Rhodes. "And the biggest thing with Street Fighter that I couldn't believe was: this is just wrestling!" He noted that actors often have conversations behind the scenes that closely mirror backstage discussions in WWE. Dwayne Johnson, who shares a close bond with Rhodes despite their heated rivalry leading up to WrestleMania XL, originally inspired him to pursue acting.

Reflecting on his Hollywood journey, Rhodes pointed out that WWE's production value remains much higher than most film sets he has experienced. He hopes both wrestlers and critics will eventually recognize the unique value performers bring to the entertainment industry.
