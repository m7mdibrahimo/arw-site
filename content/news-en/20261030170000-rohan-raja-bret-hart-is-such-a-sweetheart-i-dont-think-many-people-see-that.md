Rohan Raja has shared details about his relationship with Bret Hart, noting that the legendary wrestler maintains strong connections with many independent performers in Canada. Raja, an emerging talent currently competing in Maple Leaf Pro, first met Hart at a Dungeon Wrestling show run by Hart's son in Calgary back in 2023. 

Speaking on Fightful Overbooked's "In The Weeds" podcast, Raja explained that they have developed a genuine bond over time. He noted that Hart often takes time out of his day to chat with him and ask about his life. Raja said, "When they say the stuff like don't meet your heroes, Bret, I have nothing but good things to say about Bret. He doesn't get enough credit with how much he cares — even with fans."

Raja also shared an anecdote about seeing Hart at his themed establishment, Hitman’s Bar, in Calgary. He recalled that even when Hart’s wife attempted to get him to leave, he insisted on stopping to talk to every fan who approached him. Raja concluded, "He's such a sweetheart and I just don't think many people see that side of him."
