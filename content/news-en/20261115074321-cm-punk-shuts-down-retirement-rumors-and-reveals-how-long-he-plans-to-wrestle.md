CM Punk has addressed speculation that his age means he is nearing the end of his career, making it clear that he has no plans to hang up his boots anytime soon. Following the WWE NXT live event in Detroit on November 14, Punk spoke out against rumors suggesting he is wrapping things up at 48 years old.

"There's a lot of rumors out there that maybe because I'm 48, I'm wrapping shit up. I'm here to tell you guys that I am not. Rumors are always rumors, and I don't like addressing certain things since it gives them life, but let's put that to bed right now."

Punk went even further, revealing a timeline that guarantees fans will be seeing him in the ring for at least five more years.

"Y'all are stuck with me for at least five more years, and I will go as hard as I am currently until the wheels fall off."

This follows comments Punk made back in July on Busted Open Radio ahead of SummerSlam, where he explained that his retirement will not be measured by a contract or a specific number of matches, but rather by his health and how his body feels. With no plans to slow down, Punk is firmly focused on keeping his current WWE run going for the foreseeable future.
