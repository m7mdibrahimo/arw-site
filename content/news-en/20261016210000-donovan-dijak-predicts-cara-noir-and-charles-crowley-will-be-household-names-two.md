Donovan Dijak recently discussed his tour with PROGRESS Wrestling, where he worked with talents like Tommy End, Cara Noir, and Charles Crowley. In an interview with Fightful Overbooked's In The Weeds, Dijak praised his long-standing history with Tommy End, recalling how End welcomed him during his first trip to Europe with WXW and later served as his first opponent in WWE. Dijak also reflected on meeting Cara Noir ten years ago and praised his unique character. Furthermore, Dijak predicted that both Cara Noir and Charles Crowley will become major household names in professional wrestling within two years, highlighting their exceptional in-ring abilities and distinctive personas.
