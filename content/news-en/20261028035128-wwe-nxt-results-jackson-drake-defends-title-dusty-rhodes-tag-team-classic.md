The October 27, 2026, episode of WWE NXT aired live from the Capitol Wrestling Center in Orlando, Florida, serving as the final show before WWE NXT Halloween Havoc. The event featured a title defense and the conclusion of the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic semifinal matches.

In the first Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic semifinal, Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno def. The Vanity Project (Brad Baylor and Ricky Smokes). Following the match, Tony D'Angelo expressed his anger over Grayson Waller's home invasion in an office segment, while Cruz Montana addressed his recent past in a backstage vignette.

In a singles match, Chantel Monroe def. Lizzy Rain. Izzi Dame later explained her decision to turn down an NXT Women’s North American Championship shot while vowing to expose ZARIA in a non-title match at WWE NXT Halloween Havoc.

In another singles contest, "Super" Sean Legacy def. Saquon Shugars. Backstage, Myles Borne and Tavion Heights had a heated confrontation that was interrupted by Danhausen, who announced a Weapons Wild Match between them at WWE NXT Halloween Havoc. Robert Stone also confirmed that Dion Lennox will face Cruz Montana in an Unsanctioned Match next week.

In the second Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic semifinal, Hank Walker and Tank Ledger def. The Wagner Brothers (Galeno and El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr.). Elsewhere, Kelani Jordan and IYO SKY had a tense exchange regarding the title picture, and it was announced that John Cena will appear on next week’s show. Zilla Fatu was ambushed by BirthRight during an interview segment on the stage.

In the main event, WWE NXT North American Champion Jackson Drake def. EK Prosper to retain his title.
