WWE readied a major lineup for Monday Night RAW on October 26 as General Manager Adam Pearce officially added three fresh bouts to the card. These additions join the previously confirmed number one contender's match between Becky Lynch and Stephanie Vaquer following their social media feud regarding an ankle injury.

Bayley vs. Lyra Valkyria — No Disqualification Match

Raquel Rodriguez (c) vs. Iyo Sky — Women's Intercontinental Championship

Chad Gable & Dragon Lee vs. Big Cass & Ethan Page

Becky Lynch vs. Stephanie Vaquer — Number One Contender's Match
