Adriana Rizzo revealed that she suffered a partial ACL tear just four days before her television and PLE debut at NXT Vengeance Day 2024. Rizzo competed in a six-person mixed tag team match alongside Channing Lorenzo and Tony D'Angelo against Out The Mud. Despite dealing with severe knee pain and hitting the ropes, she managed to push through the event and even performed a jump on her opponents. Rizzo noted that she wrestled for another year and a half with the partial tear before fully tearing her ACL and meniscus in August 2025. After returning to the ring in July 2026, she was subsequently released by WWE in September.
