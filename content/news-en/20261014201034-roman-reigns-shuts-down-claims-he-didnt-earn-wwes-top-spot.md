Roman Reigns recently addressed the ongoing debates about his legacy and whether he truly earned his top position in WWE. Speaking on NotSam Wrestling, Reigns explained that his name is frequently used as clickbait because it generates views and attention in the wrestling world. He noted that even his past work as the Big Dog moved more merchandise years ago than top stars like LA Knight do today.

Reigns pushed back against the notion that management simply handed him his success, emphasizing that his business numbers have consistently justified the company's faith in him. He pointed out that decisions inside WWE are driven by business metrics that the public rarely sees, rather than online arguments. Ultimately, Reigns believes the constant discussions about him only prove his enduring value to the industry.
