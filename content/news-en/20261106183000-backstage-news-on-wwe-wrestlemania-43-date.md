According to Fightful Select, this weekend's Crown Jewel premium live event in Saudi Arabia could feature an official announcement regarding the date for WWE WrestleMania 43 following earlier doubts caused by regional conditions.

The event is expected to take place on April 10 and 11 in Saudi Arabia, matching previous rumors. TKO holds a backup plan to relocate if necessary, though the organization strongly desires to keep the show in the kingdom.

Meanwhile, WWE is currently locking down top matches for the card. Speculation points to a potential clash between WWE World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns and King of the Ring winner Oba Femi.
