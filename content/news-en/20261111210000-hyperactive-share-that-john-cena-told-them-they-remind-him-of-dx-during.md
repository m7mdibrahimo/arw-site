Hyperactive recently discussed their experience portraying D-Generation X during a Multiverse Rumble match in EVE, revealing that the idea came from John Cena. Anita Vaughan and Safire Reed, who make up the tag team, attended one of Bayley's Lodestone camps for independent women's wrestlers. John Cena was also in attendance at the camp to watch matches and offer feedback to the participants. Vaughan explained that Cena's input left a lasting impression on them. "It was great. We got to do a tag team match in front of him. After we got feedback from him as well, which is like, we talked about it before, but just like how incredible that was that it was like, he was so close to retirement and he still came out and spent like such a generous amount of time giving such detailed feedback," Vaughan said. She noted that Cena specifically compared them to the legendary DX duo. "One of the things he said, he was like, you guys remind me of DX. I was like, that kind of a comment just sticks with you. You're like, John Cena said that. That's really crazy. Thank you, Mr. Cena. Thank you," Vaughan added. Inspired by his comment, the team decided to portray Triple H and Shawn Michaels for the Multiverse Rumble event.
