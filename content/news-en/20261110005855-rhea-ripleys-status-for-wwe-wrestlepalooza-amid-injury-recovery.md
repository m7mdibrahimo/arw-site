Rhea Ripley might not be ready to return to the ring just yet, but WWE reportedly wants her on the road very soon. According to WrestleVotes, Ripley is currently scheduled to travel with WWE to Australia for WrestlePalooza next month, regardless of whether she receives medical clearance by then.

“Rhea Ripley — all signs indicate she's part of the traveling roster to WrestlePalooza. Couldn't tell you if she will get cleared in time. All we know is she's getting on a plane and getting to Australia. She's scheduled to travel,” WrestleVotes reported.

While officials backstage are keen to have her present for the tour, there is still no guarantee she will be cleared to compete. Ripley has been sidelined since undergoing surgery for a torn meniscus in August, and although she has made significant progress, she recently cautioned fans not to rush her return following footage of her walking without a brace.
