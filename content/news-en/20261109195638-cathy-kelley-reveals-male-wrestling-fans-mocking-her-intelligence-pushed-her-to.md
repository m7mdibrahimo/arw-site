Cathy Kelley revealed that constant insults from male wrestling fans regarding her intelligence pushed her to take the Mensa test to prove her critics wrong. During an appearance on The Nikki & Brie Show, the WWE backstage interviewer explained that early in her career, some fans criticized her knowledge of the product and used derogatory language toward her.

Kelley admitted that she was heavily affected by external opinions at the time, leading her to seek personal validation through the high-IQ society. While she successfully passed and received her membership card, she noted that her membership eventually lapsed because getting the proof was more important to her than maintaining affiliation.

She also credited Stephanie McMahon with encouraging her to embrace her intelligence professionally rather than hiding it due to outdated industry narratives. Kelley has since become a prominent backstage personality in WWE, with the company exploring expanded roles for her.
