Nia Jax opened up about portraying a heel in modern WWE, explaining that being booed by fans is something she truly enjoys and thrives on. Throughout her career with the company, Jax has primarily worked as a heel, embracing the negative reactions from live crowds.

During a recent interview with WPLG Local 10, Jax discussed her mindset as a villain on television and why audience hostility motivates her. 

> "Oh, I love it. I love it. It's, it's music to my ears. It's my job, it's something I thrive in, and the more reaction I can get out of the fans, the better. I feel as though like once you kind of master, I mean, not master, but once you kind of get in the groove of your wrestling and like what you can put in there, like it's- it adds another layer when you can kind of like dictate the crowd and how they react."

Jax believes that once a performer finds their rhythm in the ring, drawing strong responses from the audience adds a valuable layer to their work by allowing them to control the crowd's energy.
