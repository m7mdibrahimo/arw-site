Bully Ray has defended the announce table spot from the match between LA Knight and Roman Reigns at WWE Money in the Bank, where Knight needed two attempts to execute a powerbomb on the champion. Speaking on Busted Open, Ray argued that the struggle made the moment more realistic and heightened the drama for the fans.

> "Nothing went wrong with the announce table spot. Not everything is supposed to be perfect. Not everything is supposed to be smooth. It's supposed to look like a struggle. It's supposed to look like a fight."

Ray dismissed critics who labeled the moment a botch, noting that it made the near-fall even more effective because viewers did not expect Knight to easily dominate Reigns. He was so impressed with the performance that he expressed a desire to see a rematch between the two competitors.
