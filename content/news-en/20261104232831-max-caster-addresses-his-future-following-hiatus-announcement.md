Former AEW star Max Caster has confirmed he is taking a hiatus from professional wrestling following his controversial rap performance at TNA Bound for Glory. After apologizing for the incident, Caster addressed his future during a virtual signing with K&S WrestleFest. When asked about the possibility of returning to AEW, Caster remained non-committal. "It's wrestling, right?," Caster said. "You never say never. However, I am saying no to wrestling bookings right now, and that would be a wrestling booking. So I don't know."

Caster noted that the virtual signing would be his final wrestling-related appearance, as he had committed to the event before the TNA controversy occurred. He provided no timeline for his potential return. Caster, who gained notoriety for his rap performances and his success as one-half of The Acclaimed alongside Anthony Bowens, leaves behind a polarizing legacy that includes capturing both the AEW World Tag Team Championships and the AEW World Trios Championships. While Bowens' contract with AEW recently expired, Caster expressed doubt that his former partner has left the company.
