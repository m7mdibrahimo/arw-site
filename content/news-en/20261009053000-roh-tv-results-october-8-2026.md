ROH TV aired on October 8 via HonorClub. HOOK defeated Serpentico in the opening contest. Jordan Oliver picked up a win over Rocky Romero. VertVixen and Viva Van secured a victory against Tiara James and Kelsey Raegan. Adam Priest defeated Alex Zayne. In a backstage segment, Caprice Coleman spoke with Preston Vance, who expressed his desire for Jon Silver's approval and forgiveness. Elsewhere, Aaron Solo, Nick Comoroto, and Griff Garrison discussed their need to emulate the moves of Hangman, Bandido, and Brody King. The team of MxM Collection (Mason Madden and Mansoor) and Johnny TV defeated Aaron Solo, Nick Comoroto, and Griff Garrison. Hyan defeated Billie Starkz, prompting Athena to comment that Hyan should have been the main event. In the main event, Mark Davis defeated Ortiz.
