Independent wrestling is facing significant challenges due to rising travel costs, a smaller pool of available stars, and booking restrictions from AEW. During Wrestling Observer Radio, Dave Meltzer discussed the difficulties facing independent promotions, noting that companies like Absolute Intense Wrestling (AIW) recently suspended operations until the spring.

Meltzer explained that the traditional independent business model, which relied on flying in recognizable names from across the country for major matches, has become increasingly unsustainable. With many top stars now signed to major television promotions and the rising cost of flights, promoters struggle to turn a profit. Additionally, Meltzer highlighted the complications introduced by AEW, noting that Tony Khan must approve match outcomes for his contracted talent on independent shows. While wrestlers can take outside bookings, the restrictions on wins and losses make it difficult for promoters to build effective storylines.
