Cody Rhodes recently opened up about his experience performing stunt work while on the set of the Street Fighter movie. While many people assume that professional wrestlers are naturally prepared for stunt performing due to their background in the ring, Rhodes explained that the reality was quite different. He noted that the stunt team, led by John Valera, quickly realized that the wrestlers needed formal training to perform the sequences safely and correctly. Rhodes emphasized that filming for the movie was a much more intimate experience compared to performing in front of thousands of fans in large arenas, requiring a different approach to preparation. In a separate interview, Rhodes also touched upon his future aspirations in the acting industry.
