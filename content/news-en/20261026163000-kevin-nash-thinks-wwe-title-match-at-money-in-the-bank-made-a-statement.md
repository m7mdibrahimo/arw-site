Roman Reigns defended his WWE World Heavyweight Championship against LA Knight at Money in the Bank, a match Kevin Nash believes set a strong standard for the night. The bout opened the premium live event, delivering a performance that kept fans thoroughly engaged. Nash mentioned on his podcast that he enjoyed the contest despite knowing beforehand that Reigns would not lose.

Nash explained that the opening slot made the outcome clear, while praising both Reigns and Knight alongside the match's convincing false finishes. He also pointed to an outside factor that might have influenced creative decisions: the upcoming Street Fighter movie, in which Reigns stars as Akuma.

Nash noted that management likely wanted Reigns walking the red carpet as the reigning world champion to maintain his star status for movie promotions. Meanwhile, Reigns and The Bloodline have transitioned into new rivalries as WWE prepares for upcoming events.
