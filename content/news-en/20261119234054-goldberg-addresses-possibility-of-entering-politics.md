Goldberg has had a long and varied career, ranging from his time in the NFL to becoming one of the most iconic stars in wrestling history. However, there is one path he has no interest in pursuing: politics. When asked if he had ever considered running for office, Goldberg was adamant in his refusal.

Speaking with Biff Poggi, he explained that his past as a "crazy guy" would not hold up to the scrutiny of a political campaign. "Hell no. Hell no. For one reason. I mean, for a thousand reasons, but first and foremost, they look into my background. I was a crazy guy as a kid. I wasn't the responsible parent that I am now," Goldberg said. He views his past mistakes as part of his evolution, helping him appreciate his current life and responsibilities.

He takes his role as a public figure and WWE Hall of Famer seriously, especially regarding how his son views him. "Most importantly, my son. So every day is a struggle. Every day I got to make sure that he's proud of what I do," he added. Goldberg noted that he is now more selective about his public reactions, preferring to be a product of the logical upbringing his parents provided. He is content with his current responsibilities and made it clear that adding a political career to his life is not in the cards.
