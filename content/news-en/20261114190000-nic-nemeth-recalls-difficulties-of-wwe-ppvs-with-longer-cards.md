Nic Nemeth discussed the challenges he faced during his WWE career regarding premium live events with long match cards. Speaking on "Busted Open Radio" following WWE Crown Jewel, the former WWE star, once known as Dolph Ziggler, explained that he is still getting used to cards that do not feature eight matches.

Nemeth recalled his time in WWE, noting that match times were sometimes cut from twenty minutes down to four minutes or less because time ran short or older stars went long. He shared a story about a match against Kevin Owens following months of build, which was originally scheduled for twenty-five minutes before management nearly removed it entirely from the card.

> "We got a story here. This went from 25 to zero. I can get this done in 90 seconds and have the story be finished. Can we f'ing do this, yes or no?"

Following a brief thought, Vince McMahon agreed to the idea and told them to go do it.
