Bronson Reed's injury situation has been clarified following his appearance at WWE Crown Jewel. While initial reports suggested he suffered a punctured lung, Bryan Alvarez confirmed that the WWE Superstar is actually dealing with multiple broken ribs and a lacerated kidney. Alvarez stated that the earlier information was incorrect, wishing him a speedy recovery. Reed sustained the injuries during Crown Jewel on November 7, despite not competing in a match. He interfered in the World Tag Team Championship match to assist The Vision, but the situation escalated when Oba Femi attacked him and launched him off the stage through a table with a massive powerbomb. The spot left Reed hospitalized in Saudi Arabia while the rest of the roster returned home. Wade Barrett later confirmed during the November 9 episode of WWE RAW that Reed remained hospitalized. Alvarez also stressed during Wrestling Observer Live that the injury is entirely legitimate and not a storyline, noting that Reed remains in serious condition. The timing is a major setback for Reed, who previously missed over six months after fracturing his talus bone in November 2024 and underwent surgery in February 2026. He had only recently returned to WWE television in September before suffering this latest injury. There is currently no update on when he will be cleared to travel or return to the ring.
