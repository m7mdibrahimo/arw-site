WWE Undisputed Champion Sami Zayn says he often feels misunderstood and underappreciated, telling anyone who will listen that he does not get the respect he deserves. Zayn, who currently bills himself as "The Last Real Good Guy," told WWE that his self-awareness is both a gift and a curse.

Zayn explained that he possesses high emotional sensitivity, a strong sense of morality, and is highly intellectual. He described the combination of these traits as a "disaster." 

> "It is very hard to get through the day sometimes when you are a little bit too smart for your own good and you feel a little too strong and you're too aware of your surroundings."

Zayn admitted he is "almost jealous" of those who can remain blissfully unaware of their surroundings, but he believes these traits are exactly what led him to become a champion. He noted that the rest of the locker room does not have to deal with these internal struggles.

> "It's one of the only explanations for how a guy who looks like me, who acts like me, who talks like me, who acts like me, who thinks like me, who believes in what he believes in the way I do, ends up with this championship because it doesn't make sense. It doesn't make sense, but those are the intangibles that led me to this."
