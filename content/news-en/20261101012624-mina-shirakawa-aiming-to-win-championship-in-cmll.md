Mina Shirakawa is setting her sights high in CMLL. Ahead of her match at the October 30th CMLL show against India Sioux, Shirakawa spoke with Fightful's Luis Pulido about her future goals in the promotion and made it clear that she wants to capture gold. "Hmm, if I win that much, of course I hope. I will get the opportunity to challenge for something, or a belt should I win that match on this Friday. I want to wrestle in Mexico more and more, and finally, I want to be a champion in CMLL," she stated.
