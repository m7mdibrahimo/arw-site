Cody Rhodes has expressed frustration with WWE wrestlers who leave shows early without informing management. Rhodes discussed the topic on his "What Do You Wanna Talk About?" podcast with Chelsea Green, who pointed out that Rhodes himself often stays late at ringside to interact with fans. During the conversation, Rhodes admitted he had left a show early the previous night but emphasized that he followed protocol by checking in with the Gorilla position before departing. He clarified that his main issue is not with wrestlers needing to leave early for legitimate reasons, but rather with those who make a scene or disappear without notifying the staff who might still need them. Rhodes advised wrestlers to quietly inform the appropriate people in Gorilla so that no one backstage wastes time searching for them. Green, however, jokingly maintained that she prefers to make a grand exit, ensuring everyone knows she is leaving. Rhodes reiterated that professional courtesy is key to maintaining a smooth backstage environment.
