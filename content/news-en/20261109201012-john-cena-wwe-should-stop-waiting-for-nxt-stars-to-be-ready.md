John Cena believes that WWE needs to stop waiting for NXT talent to be fully prepared before moving them to the main roster. The 17-time World Champion stated that there is no such thing as being completely ready for that transition, and the only way to evaluate a wrestler is to provide an opportunity.

Speaking to Yahoo Sports Uncrowned, Cena explained that the John Cena Classic is intended to showcase the next generation rather than waiting for a perfect moment. He described the event's purpose by saying:

> "Sometimes all people need is just to get out there. Honestly, that's the motivation behind the John Cena Classic. I want to create an annual event where we showcase the best and brightest from NXT against the best and brightest from WWE. Kind of like 'Enter the Dragon' without all the death. I want to put together a heck of a card and I want to let the future of the business run."

Cena recalled his own early career, where an illness involving The Undertaker allowed him to face Kurt Angle. He noted that the move from NXT to the main roster is difficult, even after years of development. He added:

> "It is very difficult to matriculate from NXT to WWE. There are folks that have been down there for three years. That's a long time in development, and granted, all those reps are just getting fluency and mastery, which I appreciate. But I believe no one is ever ready, and I believe you've just got to bring them up and see what happens, if that's where they truly find an authentic sense of self."

The John Cena Classic will take place on December 14, 2026, in Orlando, Florida, with Cody Rhodes and Zilla Fatu already announced for the event.
