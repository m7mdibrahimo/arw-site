Hulk Hogan's death was officially ruled as natural months ago, but a newly surfaced audio recording provides a troubling glimpse into his final weeks. The audio, which was released on X by Bubba the Love Sponge on November 13, 2026, appears to have been recorded roughly three weeks before Hogan's passing on July 24, 2025. In the clip, Hogan is heard telling his wife, Sky Daily, that he knew he had suffered a stroke. Sky Daily questions why Hogan was driving a vehicle if he had indeed suffered a stroke. Hogan responds by saying, "Because I'm trying to drive." Sky Daily also notes that Hogan was having difficulty using a razor, which Hogan disputes, though the conversation suggests he was struggling with dexterity. Bubba the Love Sponge presented the audio, which was emailed to him under the title "Hogan's Last Days," for his audience to hear. While Hogan's claim of a stroke is not a medical diagnosis, the recording adds to the questions that surrounded his final days. In June 2026, the Clearwater Police Department concluded an 11-month investigation into Hogan's death, finding no evidence of criminal wrongdoing and confirming his death was an "attended natural death" following complications from multiple surgeries.
