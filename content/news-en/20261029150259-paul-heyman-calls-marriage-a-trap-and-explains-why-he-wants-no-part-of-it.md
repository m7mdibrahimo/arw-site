Paul Heyman recently explained why he views marriage as a trap and has no interest in getting married. Speaking in an interview, Heyman stated that he does not like the idea of staying in a relationship simply because a piece of paper makes it obligatory. He noted that even a prenuptial agreement does not change his perspective, as it turns a relationship into a financial arrangement with specific terms. Heyman added that his priorities lie with his children and his professional pursuits, leaving little room for a romantic partner. He described his dedication to his work as spiritually fulfilling, noting that it provides a thrill that goes beyond emotional or physical companionship.
