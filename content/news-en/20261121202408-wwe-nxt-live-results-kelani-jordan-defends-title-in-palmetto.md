WWE held a NXT live event on November 20 at the Bradenton Convention Center in Palmetto, Florida. Full results from the event are as follows: Elio LeFleur def. Brooks Jensen; Kali Armstrong def. Andi Summers; Harley Riggins and Jax Presley def. Milosh Jovic and Shiloh Hill; Lizzy Rain def. Alix Brooks; Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno def. Harlem Lewis and Niko Vance; Tristan Angels def. Tate Wilder; Thea Hail def. Chantel Monroe; BirthRight (Channing Lorenzo, Lexis King, and Uriah Connors) def. Mason Rook and The Dropouts (Jackson Goonrey and Tyran Tuckey); and Kelani Jordan def. Kendal Grey in a Two-Out-Of-Three Falls match to retain the NXT Women's Championship.
