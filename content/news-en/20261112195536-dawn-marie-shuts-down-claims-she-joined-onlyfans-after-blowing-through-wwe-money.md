Dawn Marie has addressed the assumption that she joined OnlyFans because she burned through her wrestling money, explaining that there was no giant wrestling fortune to blow through in the first place. Speaking to Soundsphere, Marie recalled making $75,000 per year while working a grueling schedule on the road for 275 days annually during her time with WWE, noting that she actually makes more money now as a nurse. She emphasized that joining OnlyFans was a deliberate and empowered choice rather than a financial last resort, stating that she is currently in her 50s and embracing her sexuality and confidence.

Marie also pushed back against the narrative that her platform is an attempt to cling to her past fame, pointing out that she deliberately stepped away from the wrestling business for 14 years and turned down all appearances during that period. Today, she runs her OnlyFans as a full-time career with a studio built inside her home, creating content that satisfies her creative drive without needing to recreate her television days.
