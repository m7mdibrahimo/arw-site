During a recent appearance on the Flagrant podcast, Cody Rhodes reflected on his historic victory at WWE WrestleMania XL against Roman Reigns. Rhodes stated that the iconic moment was not about him, instead giving credit to his rival. 'Roman, on that night, changed our industry,' Rhodes said, adding that the event had nothing to do with him and pointing to the massive internal changes WWE experienced during that period. He also emphasized that the star attraction was ultimately responsible for drawing the audience, reiterating that the historic night belonged entirely to Reigns.
