WWE has made a major change to tonight's Monday Night RAW by adding Rey Mysterio to the Intercontinental Championship match. Moments before the show, a backstage segment showed Chad Gable interrupting Rey Mysterio and Dragon Lee in the locker room to reveal that Mysterio has been added to the bout. What was originally scheduled as a standard title defense for Gable against Dragon Lee is now a Triple Threat Match. Mysterio now has an opportunity to capture the title in front of his hometown crowd in San Diego. Monday Night RAW airs live from the Pechanga Arena on Netflix.
