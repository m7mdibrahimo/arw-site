WWE is preparing for further backstage adjustments within its NXT brand as 2027 approaches. Recent reports suggest that Bayley and CM Punk may take on expanded roles as the company continues to restructure its developmental operations.

According to Bryan Alvarez of Wrestling Observer, these changes follow the transition of Shawn Michaels to the role of Executive Director of Creative Writing & Talent after WrestleMania 43 in April 2027. AJ Styles is expected to assume the responsibilities currently held by Michaels within NXT.

Regarding Bayley, her potential involvement remains dependent on her own career plans. Meanwhile, CM Punk is anticipated to exert more influence over the brand without necessarily holding a specific administrative title. Additionally, WWE plans to refresh its ID program in early 2027, signaling a new approach to managing independent wrestling talent as part of a broader organizational shift.
