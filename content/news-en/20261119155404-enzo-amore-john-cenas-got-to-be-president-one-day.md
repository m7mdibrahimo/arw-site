Enzo Amore recently discussed his time working with John Cena in WWE, declaring that the 17-time world champion should run for president in the future. Speaking with MuscleManMalcolm, Amore recalled sharing the ring with Cena during a peak moment in his career, praising Cena's ability to read a live crowd and let the fans guide the action. Amore described working with Cena as a major highlight, pointing to a match at WWE Battleground and their ring gear as the pinnacle of his run.

> "(sings) Can you take me higher? No. Never got any higher. That was when the kid peaked. How you doing? Glad you guys witnessed my peak. It doesn't get any better than being out there with John. He's the best, and he did what I did tonight better than anyone in the world, and that's just getting the ring, and let the people tell them what we should be doing. So learned a lot from John. Blessed to have known him in this business, and as a human, just watching him from afar. Guy's got to be president one day. Who else, right? He should be. I think he's on his way, bro. I don't care what anybody says, man. John Cena for president, though. Lucky I got to work with that guy. Battleground was the peak, man. It was the coolest it ever got. Probably the flyest ring gear maybe ever in the WWE."
