Hangman Adam Page and the team of Brodido captured the AEW World Trios Championship at AEW WrestleDream. The trio defeated Swerve Strickland and The New Level, consisting of Kofi and Austin Creed, in a two out of three falls match.

The New Level secured the first fall after pinning Bandido. Hangman Adam Page prevented a second fall, allowing Brody King and Bandido to even the score by pinning Creed following a leg-launched 450 splash.

In the closing moments, Bandido countered a Big Pressure attempt from Strickland into a rollup to secure the deciding fall. This victory ended the 48-day reign of Strickland and The New Level.
