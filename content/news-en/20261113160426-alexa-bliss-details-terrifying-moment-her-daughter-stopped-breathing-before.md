Alexa Bliss opened up about a terrifying parenting scare when her young daughter Hendrix suddenly stopped breathing and turned blue. During her conversation with Stephanie McMahon on What's Your Story?, Bliss recalled the moment she realized something was seriously wrong while staying at their California home. Hendrix had spiked a fever of around 103.7 degrees, and Bliss noticed her staring off and hallucinating.

Bliss's mother spotted the alarming symptoms through FaceTime and urged her to call 911 immediately as Hendrix's lips turned blue and she became completely unresponsive. After Bliss laid her on the couch, Hendrix suffered a febrile seizure, which eventually broke the fever. Paramedics soon arrived at the house and reassured Bliss that such seizures can happen with children under five, though it did little to erase the terror of seeing her child stop breathing. Hendrix also took her first ambulance ride during the ordeal.

Stephanie McMahon checked on Bliss during the scary situation, offering support and advice, including suggesting a cool cloth to help bring down the fever when medicine alone was not enough. Bliss expressed her gratitude for Stephanie's kindness and simple remedies during one of the most frightening moments she has faced as a mother.
