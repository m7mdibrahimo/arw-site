Royce Keys claims that Jacob Fatu is entirely responsible for the creation of The 946, which includes Bronco Nima, Lucien Price, Jaida Parker, and the newly added Carmelo Hayes. Speaking on Notsam Wrestling, Keys discussed how Fatu's actions pushed him back to his roots. "I went back to what I used to be. You know, that's all before the positive things. Like, I left Oakland, East Palo Alto. I left all that behind. And you know, there's something that triggered where I had to bring it back...It might have been when Jacob turned his back on me," Keys said. After their street fight on WWE RAW, Keys noted that tasting his own blood triggered feral tendencies that he plans to unleash once Fatu returns to the ring.
