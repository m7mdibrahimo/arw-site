Roman Reigns stated that WWE will eventually need a younger star to step up and replace both him and Cody Rhodes. During an appearance on Club Shay Shay, Reigns noted that having a single face for the company is not enough and that multiple top stars are required to carry different parts of the business. He explained that he and Rhodes belong to the same generation, meaning a new generation of talent must emerge behind them.

Reigns pointed out that developing into a legitimate top-level star takes time, extensive repetition, and the ability to survive the physical grind of the business. He noted that young talent must go through thousands of hours of work and withstand the regular injuries that come with a demanding schedule. Reigns and Rhodes famously headlined WrestleMania 40, where Cody ended Roman's historic 1,316-day championship reign.
