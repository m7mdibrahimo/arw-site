Reports circulated on Monday claiming that ROH had released Cole Karter due to him allegedly lying about a family issue to miss television tapings. However, subsequent updates have contradicted these claims, with an AEW source confirming that Karter remains employed by the promotion.

Sean Ross Sapp reported that a source within AEW stated Karter is still with the company, despite rumors of his departure circulating among talent. It appears these rumors may have originated from comments made by Karter himself. Karter has not appeared on ROH programming since April, and his former stable, The Frat House, has since disbanded.

Karter joined AEW in 2022 shortly after his departure from WWE, signing with the company following a match against Ricky Starks.
