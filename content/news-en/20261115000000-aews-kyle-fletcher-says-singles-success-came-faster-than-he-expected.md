Kyle Fletcher has made a rapid rise in AEW as a singles competitor, holding both the TNT Championship and the International Championship. Ahead of his AEW World Championship match against Will Ospreay this weekend, Fletcher appeared on Tony Schiavone's podcast and admitted that his ascent has happened much faster than he anticipated.

Fletcher compared his career momentum to a snowball rolling down a hill, growing bigger and faster until things moved at the speed of light. He explained that he has made the most of every opportunity given to him, putting him in the exact position he always wanted to be in throughout his life.

While Fletcher initially came to prominence in NJPW as part of Aussie Open alongside Mark Davis, and both are now members of The Don Callis Family, Fletcher has primarily focused on his singles career. He noted that although he always believed he would eventually transition to singles competition, the success arrived far sooner than expected.
