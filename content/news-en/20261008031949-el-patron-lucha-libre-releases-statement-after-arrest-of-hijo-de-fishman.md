El Patron Lucha Libre has released an official statement following the arrest of independent wrestler Hijo de Fishman in connection with the death of referee Gerardo German Pineda, known as El Buho. The arrest occurred on Wednesday after Fishman was charged with manslaughter stemming from a July 11 incident where he struck the referee with a weapon during a match, leaving him unconscious.

Following the news, El Patron Espectacuolos released a statement addressing the legal situation and offering condolences to the family of the late referee, according to Fightful.

> "Given the unfortunate events that occurred during the early hours of this Wednesday, October 7, in Mineral de la Reforma, Hidalgo, during a wrestling event with the independent wrestler Hijo de Fishman and the referee Gerardo Germán Pineda, “El Búho”, who unfortunately lost his life, El Patrón Espectáculos informs:"

> "Our company is attentive to the developing situation and has arranged accompaniment and legal assistance for Hijo de Fishman, so that the corresponding process is carried out in accordance with the law and before the competent authorities."

> "The lawyer and legal representative of the company maintains contact with the family of Mr. Gerardo Germán Pineda, “El Búho”, and with his legal representatives, to handle the case in the best way and guarantee respect for all parties involved."

> "The legal process is still ongoing, so, out of respect for the authorities and due process, this company will refrain from issuing additional information that could interfere with or prejudice the facts."

> "We ask the fans, media and members of the guild to act with prudence, discretion and respect, and avoid the spread of rumors or unconfirmed information, so as not to hinder the investigations and out of respect for all parties involved and their families."

> "We deeply regret the loss of a life and express our solidarity with his family, friends and colleagues in the wrestling business."

> "Sincerely, Patron Espectáculos."
