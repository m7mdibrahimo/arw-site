During WWE Raw, John Cena appeared to reveal the latest participant for the John Cena Classic, describing the event as an evening of opportunity featuring epic matches where fans hold the power.

Cena recalled his early days nearly getting fired before creating his famous identity, then introduced Liv Morgan as the newest invitee because she is a longshot like him.

After Cena left the ring, Morgan's Survivor Series opponent Becky Lynch arrived to criticize her for reaching the top with the help of others. The segment ended with Morgan attempting to strike Lynch as the two exchanged heated words.
