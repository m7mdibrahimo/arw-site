The Rock's return at Survivor Series has sparked speculation regarding his potential involvement at WrestleMania 43. According to Ringside News, there have been no changes to his status for the event, as his commitments to filming the next Fast & Furious movie remain in place. Production is expected to begin in December, potentially conflicting with the WrestleMania season. While The Rock successfully adjusted his promotional schedule to appear at Survivor Series, working around a major film production is a different challenge. Although his ability to rearrange commitments makes a future appearance possible, there is currently no indication that his schedule has opened up for WrestleMania 43.
