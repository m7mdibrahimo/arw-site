Danielle Petrow is speaking out about how her relationship with Matt Riddle ended, claiming she broke up with the former WWE Superstar after he allegedly sent her an explicit video of himself with a 19-year-old woman. Speaking with Monte & The Pharaoh, Petrow explained that she was the one who initiated the split, not Riddle. She revealed that the breaking point came when Riddle sent her a video of himself with a teenager whom she later identified as Ivy Malibu, noting that Riddle seemingly expected her to be happy about it. Petrow also shared that she reached out to the woman involved to try to warn her.
