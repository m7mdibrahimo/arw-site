According to Connected MMA, Gable Steveson will step back into the Octagon on January 30 in Toronto at UFC 336. He is scheduled to face Terrance Chatman in the upcoming event.

Steveson experienced his first mixed martial arts defeat at UFC 331 in September, getting knocked out by Sean Sharaf. Meanwhile, Chatman enters the matchup following a submission loss against Anthony Wint in August.

Steveson holds a 4-1 record in his MMA career, having started his journey at UFC 329 with a victory over Elisha Ellison.
