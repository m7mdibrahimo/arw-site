Cody Rhodes feels modern WWE stems from WrestleMania 40. At WrestleMania 40, Cody Rhodes finally defeated Roman Reigns for the Undisputed WWE Universal Championship and ended his story.

Speaking on the Double Coverage Podcast, Rhodes said that WrestleMania 40 changed the whole company. He noted that there is new management, new people in the corporate world, the comms department, and photography, meaning the whole company changed at that period of time.

> "The other thing is, it's such a powerful moment. We're still talking about it because all of modern WWE stems out of it. It's new management. There's new people in the corporate world. There's new people in the comms department. There's new people in photography. The whole company changed at that period of time. So to be part of that and to be part of that change was just, that's a real honor."
