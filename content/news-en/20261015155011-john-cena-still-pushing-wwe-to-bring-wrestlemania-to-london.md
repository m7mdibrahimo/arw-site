John Cena is still advocating for WrestleMania to take place in London, believing the city has earned the right to host WWE's biggest event. Speaking on a recent podcast appearance, Cena discussed his current reduced role with the company and noted that he continues to campaign for the UK capital whenever he gets the opportunity. He pointed to WWE taking WrestleMania outside North America for the first time as proof that traditional markets are no longer the only option. Cena admitted he does not know the current status of any official talks between WWE and the city, but he remains determined to keep the idea alive and hopes to return to the O2 to rally the fans once again.
