Damian Priest revealed that he has undergone medical procedures to deal with ongoing pain after wrestling with injuries for a long time. Speaking on Off the Ropes, the WWE star noted that he never missed a day of work while addressing his physical issues. Priest explained that the treatments have helped ease his pain, making him feel a lot better and allowing him to train daily and return to his previous physical shape. He also admitted that he is currently in a much better mental space after realizing he needed to adjust his mindset following a challenging period last year when he dealt with shoulder, knee, and back injuries.
