Drew McIntyre has gained valuable insights from working alongside Russell Crowe on the upcoming films 'Highlander' and 'The Last Druid.' Reflecting on their collaboration, McIntyre now views the actor as a mentor.

> "I mean, that's a career — I don’t think many people are going to be able to achieve the level that Russell is at. But, yeah, he's a mentor for sure." McIntyre explained that he spent 12 weeks with Crowe while filming 'Highlander' and took every opportunity to observe him at work. When he initially received a call from agents about a role in a movie, he was skeptical until it was confirmed that Crowe personally requested him. After receiving approval from Nick Khan and Triple H, McIntyre spent nearly three months learning from Crowe. He believes these lessons are applicable to his work in WWE and his life in general, noting that Crowe even gifted him his first watch. McIntyre is currently focusing on his "Hollywood Drew" persona.
