Ash By Elegance successfully defended her TNA Knockouts Television Championship against Jada Stone on the latest episode of TNA iMPACT, overcoming her previous challenger in a hard-fought rematch. Stone pushed the champion with a springboard hurricanrana and a 619, while M countered with high-impact offense including a superkick and a piledriver. During the match, Mr. Elegance refused M's request to hand her the title belt and seemingly quit The Elegance brand, while Heather distracted the referee. M persevered to secure the win and retain her title following her previous victory over Stone on August 27.
