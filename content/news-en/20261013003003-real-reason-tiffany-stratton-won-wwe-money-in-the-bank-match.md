Many fans were surprised when Tiffany Stratton won the Money in the Bank briefcase instead of the heavily favored Roxanne Perez. According to reports, WWE specifically chose Stratton to avoid predictable outcomes. As TC of WrestleVotes explained, the company is prioritizing staying ahead of fan expectations, and once Roxanne Perez became the consensus pick, WWE pivoted to a surprise choice.

> The zigzags come in. A lot of people started to get on the Roxanne Perez train, and now, more so than ever, WWE wants to stay one step ahead of everyone's predictions and thought process. It goes even so far as WWE Radio on SiriusXM — a lot of the contributors on that program had those people picked as most likely to win.

Stratton's victory was designed to catch viewers off guard. Additionally, the decision was not solely for shock value, as WWE has existing creative plans involving Stratton and Chelsea Green. 

> Not a lot of people had Tiffany to come in and win that briefcase again. It was a 'doing different to be different.' Yes, there is a story there with her and Chelsea Green, and I'm sure we'll see that play out.

While the decision sparked immediate backlash on social media, WWE successfully achieved the unpredictable reaction they intended.
