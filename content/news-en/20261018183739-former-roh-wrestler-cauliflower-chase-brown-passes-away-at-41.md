The independent wrestling community is mourning the loss of former Ring of Honor wrestler "Cauliflower" Chase Brown, who passed away on October 13 at the age of 41. The tragic news was announced by the Carolina Wrestling Channel, which highlighted his diverse career in wrestling, music, and acting.

Throughout his career in the Southeast, Brown competed for numerous independent promotions such as Trans-South Wrestling, Action Packed Wrestling, PWX Wrestling, and Pro Wrestling Union, alongside his appearances for Ring of Honor and WWN. Outside the ring, he was the subject of the independent short documentary The Aria of Babyface Cauliflower Brown and landed television roles in shows including The Righteous Gemstones, while also being featured in a 2017 New York Times video documentary.

Behind his on-screen persona, Brown was a devoted husband and a father to his daughter. While no cause of death has been publicly disclosed, tributes have poured in from fans and colleagues remembering his contributions to the business and the arts.
