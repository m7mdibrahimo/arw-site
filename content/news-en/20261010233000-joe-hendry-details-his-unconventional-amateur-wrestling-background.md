Joe Hendry has established himself as a WWE star after years of competing on the independent scene, in TNA, and in ROH. In a recent appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Hendry discussed his amateur wrestling background. Hendry noted that he took up amateur wrestling because he wanted to be taken seriously as a professional wrestler. He initially began training in grappling to add legitimacy to his act. Hendry recalled,

> "I think the thing about my amateur wrestling days is — it's very me, it's very unconventional."

He gained experience by visiting MMA gyms in the United Kingdom, where he frequently tested his skills against others. Hendry eventually trained at a more serious facility in Scotland, where he faced opponents like former UFC fighter Paul Craig. After defeating a wrestler scheduled for the Commonwealth Games, Hendry entered the competition himself and won the British Championship in freestyle wrestling. He later joined Team Scotland for the 2018 Commonwealth Games in Australia, where he was initially set to face UFC Middleweight Champion Robert Whittaker. The match did not occur because Whittaker withdrew to avoid vacating his UFC title. Hendry eventually shifted his focus entirely to professional wrestling, leading to his WWE signing in 2025.
