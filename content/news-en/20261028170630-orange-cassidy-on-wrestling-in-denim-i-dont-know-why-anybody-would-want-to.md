Orange Cassidy recently explained why he prefers wrestling in denim jeans, noting that he does not understand why other wrestlers choose anything else. Speaking in a new interview with The Rizzuto Show, Cassidy discussed his signature ring gear, which includes a denim jacket and jeans instead of traditional trunks or tights.

> "Yeah, it's like… I don't know why anybody would want to wrestle in anything else. It's the most comfortable, durable fabric."

Cassidy also shared why he opts for a drawstring instead of a belt when competing in jeans, explaining that belts can be uncomfortable during matches. Elsewhere, the star recently offered advice to the competitors in this year's AEW Blood And Guts match.
