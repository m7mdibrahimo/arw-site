WWE presented a live episode of NXT on October 27, 2026, from the Performance Center in Orlando, Florida. Matches in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic highlighted the broadcast.

Noam Dar & Romeo Moreno def. Vanity Project (Brad Baylor & Ricky Smokes)
Chantel Monroe def. Lizzy Rain
Sean Legacy def. Saquon Shugars

During the broadcast, Tony D'Angelo departed the arena upon discovering Grayson Waller was at his residence. Elsewhere, Cruz Montana called out Dion Lennox, while Izzi Dame declared in a vignette that she would expose ZARIA. A promotional video featuring Kam Hendrix playing basketball also aired.

In the second hour, a face-to-face confrontation between Myles Borne and Tavion Heights led to the announcement of a Weapons Wild Match for Halloween Havoc. Additionally, Robert Stone scheduled an Unsanctioned Match between Dion Lennox and Cruz Montana for the following week.

The Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic tournament continued with Hank and Tank (Hank Walker & Tank Ledger) taking on The Wagner Brothers (Galeno & El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr.). The show also featured an NXT North American Championship defense with Jackson Drake (c) vs. EK Prosper.
