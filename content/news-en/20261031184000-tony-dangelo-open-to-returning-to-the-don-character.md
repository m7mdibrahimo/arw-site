Tony D'Angelo stated that he would be completely open to returning to his The Don character in the future. Throughout his time in WWE NXT this year, his persona underwent a major transition to reflect his real-life personality more closely.

During a recent interview, Tony D'Angelo discussed the shift in WWE NXT and noted the possibility of revisiting The Don down the line.

> "I'd say it worked out. So like, I'm happy with it. If there's ever a universe where maybe we could get back to the Don stuff or maybe morph, the two, I'd be a hundred percent open to it. I've thoroughly enjoyed, that part of my career. But until then we're going to keep rocking with the Master of Fate."
