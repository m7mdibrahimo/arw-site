Rey Fenix reportedly sustained a hand injury during recent television tapings for AAA. According to False Finish, he managed to complete his match despite the issue, though he favored his hand throughout the contest.

The timing is problematic because AAA is actively scheduling his upcoming appearances. Dorian Roldán announced during the November 14 show on FOX that Fenix is booked for a six-man tag team match next week alongside the promotion's Mega Champion, El Grande Americano, and Latin American Champion, La Parka, to face the Trios Champions, Perros del Mal.

Furthermore, AAA is building a major storyline around his Cruiserweight Championship. The promotion arranged a twelve-man tournament featuring four triple threat bouts, with the victors advancing to a fatal four-way match at Guerra de Titanes on December 19. The winner of that four-way will challenge Fenix for the title on January 2, 2027, in Acapulco.

This title picture has already faced misfortune. Fenix was originally scheduled to defend the championship against Mini Vikingo at Héroes Inmortales, but Vikingo suffered an elbow injury, leading Fenix to defend against Jack Cartwheel, Laredo Kid and Takuma instead. Even though Fenix finished his latest bout, observers will monitor his hand closely as his next matches approach.
