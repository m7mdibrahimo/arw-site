WWE Superstar Liv Morgan recently spent $500 on a puppy she found on the side of the road in Florida and is already showing off her new pet. Morgan shared photos on Instagram on Saturday, November 14, holding the brown puppy near a roadside and revealing its cost in her caption. The puppy appears to be an American Bully or pit bull mix with tan fur and white paws, though Morgan did not confirm its breed or name. The post quickly drew reactions from fans and fellow WWE personalities, including referee Jessika Carr. Morgan, who already owns a farm and a donkey named Billy, has not yet explained how the roadside purchase came about, but she clearly went home with a new companion.
