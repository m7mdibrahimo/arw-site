The suspended Wren Sinclair is scheduled to appear on tonight's episode of WWE NXT. Sinclair snuck into a 20-woman Battle Royal last week disguised as Lady Shani and won to earn a women's title opportunity before revealing her identity.

General Manager Robert Stone will appear to address Sinclair's actions. The lineup also features a number one contender's match between Myles Borne and Tony D'Angelo, Saquon Shugars vs. Tristan Angels, Vanity Project vs. Mini Vikingo and Chazz Hall, and Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno vs. Fraxiom in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic.
