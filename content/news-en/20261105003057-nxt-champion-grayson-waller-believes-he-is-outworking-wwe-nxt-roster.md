Since returning to "NXT" several months ago, Grayson Waller has been outspoken and self-confident, traits that have carried him to his current reign as NXT Champion. Waller believes he deserves his spot more than anyone else. In an interview on "Busted Open After Dark" following a confrontation with his Halloween Havoc challenger, Tony D'Angelo, Waller explained why he believes he is superior to the rest of the roster.

> "I'm outworking everyone here. I'm not just saying it, I'm doing it. And then I'm also planning it for everyone. Let me ask you Bully; let's go back to when you're on the 'Raw' roster. Some new guy comes from a different company, and he comes in and he calls your roster soft, and he makes fun of your kid and says that you don't belong there and I deserve everything. How far through Gorilla would he get before you smacked him across the face...Exactly."

Waller also addressed the delay in D'Angelo finally retaliating against him.

> "And he didn't hit me till tonight. What's that, four months? It took him four months and all that before he finally hit me. Like, that's why I keep calling these guys soft. Like, you're going to let me come out? I can't believe I get away with this. I went to his house, and all he did was give me a double leg. If I went to your house, Bully, I have a feeling I wouldn't be leaving that house, you know what I mean?"

Waller retained his championship against D'Angelo at Halloween Havoc. He now faces a new challenge against CM Punk, whom he has been compared to in recent months. The two are scheduled to compete on tonight's special Wednesday edition of "NXT" for the title.
