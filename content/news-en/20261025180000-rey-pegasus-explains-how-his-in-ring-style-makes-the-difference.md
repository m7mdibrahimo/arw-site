Rey Pegasus opened up about his unique in-ring style and how it has helped him stand out in the promotion. Pegasus first made a name for himself by winning the 2026 Torneo de Escuelas alongside his partner Tornado, defeating teams like Estudiante Jr. & Feroz and Pantera Jr. & Psycotic along the way. Speaking in an interview, Pegasus explained that he deliberately focused on developing aerial moves that were entirely different from anything previously seen in the company to captivate the audience and media.

Pegasus noted that while wrestling has many established elements, he and Tornado constantly look for that extra edge to make everything look fresh and grab public attention. He added that introducing these new maneuvers has been instrumental to their success, ensuring they continue to make a distinct impact that keeps fans talking.
