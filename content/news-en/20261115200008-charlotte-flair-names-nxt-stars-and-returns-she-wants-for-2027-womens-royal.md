Charlotte Flair recently named several WWE NXT women and returning stars she hopes to see in the 2027 Women's Royal Rumble match, according to Fightful. The 2027 Royal Rumble Premium Live Event will take place in Glendale, Arizona, generating extra intrigue around potential returns and appearances by developmental roster members.

During an interview with Good Day Sacramento, Flair shared her picks from the developmental brand.

> "I'd love to see Izzi Dame. I'd love to see Kelani (Jordan). I'd love to see a repeat from Kiana James and her incredible performance last year. The roster is so deep within NXT, but also I'd love to see the returns of Bianca Belair, who is a new mother. Naomi, a new mother who didn't lose her title, who left due to being pregnant. I'd love to see her make a return. Also, Rhea Ripley has been out. I'd love to see her make a return. I mean, this year's 2027 Women's Royal Rumble could be probably one of the biggest if you look at it from who could potentially return. It'd be that big."
