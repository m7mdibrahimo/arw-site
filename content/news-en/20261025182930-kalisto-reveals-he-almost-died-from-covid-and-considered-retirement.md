Former WWE star Kalisto revealed that he nearly died from COVID-19 following his April 2021 release from WWE, spending roughly two and a half months in the hospital and seriously considering retirement from professional wrestling.

During an interview with David Otunga, Kalisto discussed his terrifying battle with the virus, alongside a previous incident earlier in his career when he landed on his head during a match in Mexico. He explained that he was ready to step away from the ring for good before everything changed after receiving an invitation from Mojo Rawley to participate in a special charity event for children with autism and their families.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EHEdm8lb2Zk

Kalisto competed in the main event alongside a young fan named Leo, and the child ultimately secured the winning pinfall for their team. Seeing the joy on the children's faces brought happiness back to his heart and convinced him to change his mind about retiring. He confirmed that he is currently preparing for a return to the ring despite previous injuries he has faced.
