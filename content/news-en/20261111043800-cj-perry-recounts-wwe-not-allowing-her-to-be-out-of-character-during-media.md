CJ Perry recalled that during her time with WWE, the company maintained strict rules requiring her to stay in character during all media appearances and red-carpet events. Perry shared that some fans still believe she is genuinely Russian, noting that the Lana character made a massive impact on the audience. She added that even after the storyline involving Bobby Lashley, where she spoke with an American accent, people remained confused. Perry noted that she always found this humorous and encouraging. Furthermore, she explained that Vince McMahon and WWE protected her character intensely at the time, which meant she could never break character publicly. Perry previously finished up her run with AEW in 2024.
