Erick Redbeard made a surprise debut at TNA Bound for Glory on October 11, 2026, during a violent Falls Count Anywhere Match. Redbeard interfered in the chaotic contest by attacking Matt and Jeff Hardy, allowing the team of Sawyer and Samuel Shaw to secure a victory. The match featured extensive brawling throughout the venue, including tables, chairs, and ladders, with Jeff Hardy landing a Swanton Bomb from a balcony through tables. Redbeard’s intervention proved decisive as he put both Hardys through tables, shifting the momentum entirely. Following the ambush, Syx finished the match by powerbombing Samuel Shaw onto the incapacitated Hardys to score the pinfall. This debut establishes a clear alliance between Redbeard and Syx, signaling a significant shift in the TNA tag team division.
