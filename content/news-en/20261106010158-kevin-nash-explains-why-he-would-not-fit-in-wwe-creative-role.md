Kevin Nash's close friends are currently holding major backstage roles in WWE. Paul "Triple H" Levesque has served as WWE's Chief Content Officer since 2022, while Shawn Michaels oversees creative for NXT as Senior Vice President of Talent Development and Creative. With reports suggesting Michaels could eventually take over Bruce Prichard's duties, questions have surfaced about whether Nash might join his former peers.

Speaking on his Kliq This podcast, Nash addressed the idea of working remotely.

> "I mean, if it was Zoom, like I could four-hour Zoom every day?"

> "The first thing I would have to do is, I would have to completely immerse myself in the product."

Nash noted that booking requires in-person dedication, stating that he would need to be there in person to talk with talent because someone cannot book performers without having a feel for them.

While open to the NXT environment, Nash dismissed joining main roster creative.

> "For me? No."

Nash explained that his humorous demeanor would likely irritate Levesque. Ultimately, the veteran admitted that he does not want to work at age 67, emphasizing that he is much older than both Levesque and Michaels.
