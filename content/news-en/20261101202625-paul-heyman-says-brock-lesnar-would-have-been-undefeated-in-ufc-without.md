Hall of Famer Paul Heyman spent 24 years alongside Brock Lesnar in WWE, helping build the image of "The Beast Incarnate." During his career, Lesnar also transitioned to mixed martial arts, capturing the UFC Heavyweight Championship with a record of five wins, three losses, and one no contest. Looking back at that era, Heyman believes Lesnar could have accomplished far more in MMA if not for serious medical issues.

Speaking on In Depth With Graham Bensinger, Heyman stated that diverticulitis was the only thing that halted Lesnar's dominance in the octagon.

> "He is the greatest athlete on the face of the planet, and the only reason why he doesn't get the credit he deserves is number one, there is a preconceived notion of what a professional wrestler is. Number two, because diverticulitis robbed him of so much more great athletic accomplishment. If he didn't have diverticulitis, he would've never been defeated in UFC. Never. He'd still be champion today at 49. There's nobody on this planet that could hang with him. No one. No one would get out of the first round with him, there's no way."

After his MMA run, Lesnar returned to pro wrestling for another massive stint. At WrestleMania 42, he teased retirement following a loss to Oba Femi. Four months later, after completing their trilogy match, Lesnar officially passed the torch to Femi as the next dominant force, confirming that his final official match took place at SummerSlam.
