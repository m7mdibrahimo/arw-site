Samantha Irvin is still addressing fan speculation regarding her departure from WWE, specifically denying claims that she breached her contract or spoke negatively about the company. The issue arose after a fan on social media suggested she could have been featured in a video of Alexa Bliss if she had not "burned bridges" with WWE by allegedly breaking her agreement and trash-talking the organization.

Irvin responded directly to the accusation, stating, "Didn’t breach, didn’t talk trash, didn’t want to do the above." This follows previous attempts by Irvin to clarify the circumstances of her October 2024 exit. She recently pushed back against claims that she left to pursue a music career, noting that she has been releasing music independently since 2013 and that creating art does not equate to wanting to join the music industry.

Furthermore, Irvin revealed that WWE had offered her a record deal before she left, which she declined because her professional interest was in managing wrestlers. She expressed frustration that fans continue to push narratives about her departure that contradict her own account.
