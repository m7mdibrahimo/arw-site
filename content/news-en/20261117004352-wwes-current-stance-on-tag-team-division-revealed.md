WWE currently has a large number of tag teams on its roster, but the company is reportedly not convinced that investing heavily in the division is worth the effort. According to WrestleVotes, internal analytics suggest that viewers often stop watching when tag team matches air, which has left management skeptical about the division's value. "WWE is indicating that, 'We are not sold on investing in the tag team division.' They are not really believing in it. Maybe it's analytics, which it's believed to be, that people tune out when tag team wrestling is on the screen. Maybe it's not the right teams. Maybe they don't have the right players in place."

This lack of confidence persists even as WWE has attempted to revitalize the division throughout 2026. On WWE RAW, the World Tag Team Titles are held by Penta and Je’Von Evans, a team formed by pairing two singles wrestlers rather than developing an established unit. Meanwhile, on WWE SmackDown, the WWE Tag Team Titles are held by Tama Tonga and Talla Tonga. Other featured acts include the pairing of Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki, alongside Fraxiom. Despite these efforts, neither brand has consistently established tag team wrestling as a primary attraction. The ongoing challenge for WWE remains whether these teams can connect with the audience, as management appears hesitant to increase television time for a division that data suggests is struggling to retain viewers.
