AEW Full Gear took place in Phoenix, Arizona, but winter weather and travel disruptions briefly threatened the weekend's events. The FAA grounded flights for several hours on Friday, which impacted several shows across wrestling and sports. Dave Meltzer reported on "Wrestling Observer Radio" that while the main show went ahead as planned with all booked talent present, the pre-show required several adjustments. Meltzer noted that the "AEW Tailgate Brawl" underwent multiple changes due to flight cancellations, though he only confirmed the alteration to the TBS Championship match.

Samoa Joe was one of the notable absences caused by the travel issues. He appeared in a pre-taped promo during the pay-per-view to reveal he was stranded and could not make it to the venue. While it remains unconfirmed if he was scheduled for a match, it is widely believed he would have engaged in a segment with his rival Jack Perry, whom he is set to face on AEW Dynamite. The other confirmed change involved the TBS Championship match. Originally, Queen Aminata was scheduled to defend the title in a three-way match against Persephone and Maya World. However, because Maya World could not make the flight, the bout was transformed into a four-way match featuring Persephone, Skye Blue, and World's tag team partner Hyan.
