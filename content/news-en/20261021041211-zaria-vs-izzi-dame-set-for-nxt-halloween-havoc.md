Izzi Dame is set to face ZARIA at NXT Halloween Havoc. Over the past few weeks, Dame has targeted NXT Women's North American Champion ZARIA, digging up past photos and videos without her face paint to uncover her background. Following Dame's victory over Layla Diggs on the October 20 episode of WWE NXT on The CW, ZARIA confronted her in the ring. ZARIA expressed frustration with the games and challenged Dame to a title match at Halloween Havoc on October 31. Dame accepted the match but noted that ZARIA does not need to put her championship on the line. The updated card for Halloween Havoc includes Kelani Jordan defending the NXT Women's Championship against Wren Sinclair, Grayson Waller defending the NXT Championship against Tony D'Angelo, and ZARIA vs. Izzi Dame.
