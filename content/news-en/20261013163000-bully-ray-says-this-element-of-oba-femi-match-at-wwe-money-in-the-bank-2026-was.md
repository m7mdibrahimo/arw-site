Oba Femi secured a victory over Bronson Reed at WWE Money in the Bank, but WWE legend Bully Ray took issue with a specific sequence during the bout. During the match, The Judgment Day interfered, and Reed used brass knuckles on Femi twice before hitting his Tsunami finisher. Despite these attacks, Femi kicked out, which left Ray questioning the logic behind the booking.

> "I've been loving everything from Oba up until Saturday night. They lost me a little bit on Saturday. Two shots to the jaw with brass knuckles? You don't think that's a little much? Forget about the Tsunami, because we know that's going to be there. Even though Bronson Reed has lost weight, would you still say that is a very large man who probably packs one hell of a punch without brass knuckles? Two shots to the jaw with brass knuckles? You don't think that's a little much?"

Ray argued that while it is professional wrestling, the sequence strained believability. He suggested that Femi kicking out of three Tsunamis would have been more plausible than surviving two direct hits with brass knuckles. Femi ultimately won the match after executing a moonsault and his Fall From Grace maneuver.
