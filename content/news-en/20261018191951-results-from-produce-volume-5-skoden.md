PRODUCE Wrestling held its PRODUCE Volume 5: Skoden event on October 12, 2026, at the Roulette Intermedium in New York City. The event was broadcast on MyAEW, according to Fightful.

The Limit (Janai Kai & Nixi XS) def. Kalypto & Tiara James. Dos Gringos Locos (Gringo Loco & KJ Orso) def. Julezee & Tiny Giant. Matt Mako def. Pat Sawyer. Hyan def. Jordan Blade in a Luna Vachon Invitational semifinal match. Lio Rush def. Jonathan Gresham by disqualification. Dante Chen def. Warhorse. JTG def. Man Like DeReiss. Dark Sheik def. Corinne Joy in a Luna Vachon Invitational semifinal match. Tracy Williams def. The Blade. Nyla Rose def. Nashoba Naira. Karl Fredericks def. Javi. Drilla Moloney def. Oni King. Effy & Fred Rosser def. Mad Dog Connelly & Slade by disqualification.

In the main event, Bryan Keith, Cowboy Way (1 Called Manders & Thomas Shire), and Lee Moriarty def. Adam Priest, Darian Bengston, Rudy Lockhart & Tim Bosby.
