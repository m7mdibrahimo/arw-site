The Good Brothers have been added to the GHC Tag Team Title match at the upcoming Pro Wrestling NOAH Wrestle Odyssey II event. The show is scheduled for October 25th from Ryōgoku Sumo Hall in Tokyo, Japan. The title match was originally advertised as T.M.D.K. challenging champions Yuki Iino and Manabu Soya. However, Doc Gallows and Karl Anderson returned to NOAH and inserted themselves into the title picture, turning the bout into a three-way tag team match.
