Will Ospreay recently quipped about his high level of cardiovascular endurance, noting that fans can ask his wife, Alex Windsor, to verify it. Speaking with WKMG News 6 ahead of AEW WrestleDream, Ospreay discussed defending his AEW World Title in the unique Clockwork Carnage match. Entering the bout first, he managed to survive until the final stage and secured the victory by hitting the Hidden Blade on Adam Copeland, pinning him for the win with just two seconds remaining on the clock. Reflecting on his physical conditioning for the demanding stipulation, Ospreay joked about his stamina.

> "I mean, it is, but thankfully, I'm a cardio machine. I'm very good at that. Ask the wife."
