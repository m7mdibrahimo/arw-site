Mark Briscoe has been absent from AEW television for more than three months, and reports indicate he is still not ready to return to the ring. During Wrestling Observer Radio, Dave Meltzer discussed Ilja Dragunov's upcoming appearance on AEW Dynamite and briefly addressed Briscoe's ongoing hiatus, stating that he is not yet cleared to compete. Briscoe last wrestled on the July 1 episode of AEW Dynamite, where he challenged MJF for the AEW World Championship shortly after competing in a brutal steel cage match at Forbidden Door. AEW later confirmed on Collision that Briscoe was dealing with a legitimate injury, though specific details have not been publicly disclosed. This follows another injury-related absence earlier in the year following a match in March. While Briscoe remains under contract with AEW, there is currently no official timetable for his return to action.
