John Bradshaw Layfield has been named the new president of the George Tragos/Lou Thesz Professional Wrestling Hall of Fame board of directors, taking over for Gerry Brisco. Brisco, who has served as president since the institution was established in 1999, is stepping down and moving into an advisory role. Layfield takes on the position immediately and plans to expand the organization and build the Global Pro Wrestling Hall of Fame brand alongside the Dan Gable Museum. Brisco expressed confidence in the transition, stating that the Hall of Fame will continue to grow under JBL's leadership.
