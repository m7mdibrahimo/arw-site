Jim Cornette recently addressed comments made by Vince Russo regarding the creation of The Rock, stating that Russo does not deserve credit for the megastar's success. Russo had claimed that he was not properly recognized for his role in helping build one of WWE's biggest stars.

Cornette compared the situation to Downtown Bruno, who took in a young Dwayne Johnson when he was struggling in Tennessee, noting that Russo was merely a paid writer doing his job rather than a mentor. Cornette also pointed out that Russo joined the WWE creative team months after Rocky Maivia debuted, and argued that The Rock's success ultimately came from his own unique charisma, look, and natural talent.
