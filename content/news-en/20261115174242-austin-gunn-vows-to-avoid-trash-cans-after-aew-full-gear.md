Austin Gunn expressed deep frustration regarding trash cans after taking repeated hits from them lately and at AEW Full Gear. In recent weeks, the metal bins turned into a major problem for him, including an episode of AEW Dynamite where he tried to rush Claudio Castagnoli. Castagnoli responded by throwing a trash can directly at his head and dropping him to the ramp.

At AEW Full Gear, Castagnoli came back and used the same weapon against him once more by placing it over his head before hitting a giant swing. Following the Anarchy In The Arena match at the pay-per-view event, Austin shared a social media post declaring that he will litter for the rest of his life and avoid trash cans entirely.

Austin also put out a second message strongly rejecting any notion of him and the bins sharing a bond as wrestling soulmates.
