Darby Allin recently spoke about his desire to inspire the next generation of professional wrestlers to stay true to themselves rather than changing who they are to achieve success. Allin has remained a prominent figure in AEW since the promotion's launch in 2019, consistently showcasing his high-risk wrestling style without altering his personal identity or performance style, despite critics suggesting he tone it down.

During a new interview with Trent Osborne on 1010XL, Allin explained that maintaining his authenticity was his primary goal from his very first day at wrestling school back in 2014. He noted that he would rather have faced homelessness again than compromise his beliefs and look in the mirror at someone he no longer recognizes. He stated that his ultimate objective in AEW is to encourage upcoming wrestlers to bring their true selves to the ring every single night.
