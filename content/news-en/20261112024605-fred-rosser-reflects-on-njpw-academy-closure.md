Fred Rosser has spoken out regarding the recent closure of the NJPW Academy. This announcement followed the news that NJPW America was also shutting down. On Wednesday, Rosser took to Instagram to express his gratitude toward the students he coached at the facility.

> "What's up wrestling world, you know what time it is. For the past three and a half years, I've had the privilege of coaching and mentoring so many incredible young men and women from around the world through NJPW Academy. The wins, the championships, and the accomplishments are great, but watching people grow in confidence, character, and belief in themselves is the real reward for me. To every student who walked through those dojo doors, thank you for allowing me to be a part of your journey. I hope I gave you even half as much as you gave me. The dojo might closing its doors, but the lessons, relationships, and the memories will live with me forever. Coaching was never just a job to me, it was and will always be my purpose. You know what time it is baby? It's time to go to work."

Rosser was an inaugural member of the NJPW Academy when it opened in early 2023. He was also a prominent figure in NJPW America, where he held the NJPW STRONG Openweight Championship for nearly 300 days.
