Former WWE prospect Sirena Linton, who recently debuted for Ring of Honor, was hospitalized on November 4 due to severe and persistent eye irritation. Linton sought medical care in Jacksonville, Florida, after the pain became unbearable.

Linton shared an update on X regarding her condition:

> "going to the hospital bc my eyes won’t stop burning wasn’t the plan today 😭 also it’s NOT MY EYELASHES SO DONT COME FOR ME"

She further expressed the severity of the situation in a follow-up post, stating:

> "i cant see 😭😭😭😭"

This health scare occurs as Linton works to establish her career following her time with WWE. She has not yet disclosed a diagnosis from doctors or confirmed how this issue might impact her upcoming wrestling schedule.
