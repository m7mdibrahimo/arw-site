Adam Page has updated his entrance music. Following his intense battles with Swerve Strickland and the New Level at AEW Full Gear, Adam Page competed in the main event of this week's AEW Dynamite in a Continental Classic match against Bandido. As he made his way to the ring, Page introduced 'Bronco' by Orville Peck as his new theme song. The track is from Orville Peck’s 2022 album of the same name and has already garnered over 3 million streams on Spotify and 280,000 views on YouTube.
