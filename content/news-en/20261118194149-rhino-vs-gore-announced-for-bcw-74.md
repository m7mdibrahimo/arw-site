Battle Championship Wrestling has announced that Rhino will face Gore at the BCW 74: 10th Anniversary Show on December 4, 2026. The match, billed as the "Battle of Gore," was set up after Rhino appeared on screen at BCW 73 and challenged Gore. Rhino, a former ECW World Heavyweight Champion and NWA Worlds Heavyweight Champion, is looking to face the former BCW Heavyweight Champion and Triple Crown Champion. The event will be held at the Whitehorse Club.
