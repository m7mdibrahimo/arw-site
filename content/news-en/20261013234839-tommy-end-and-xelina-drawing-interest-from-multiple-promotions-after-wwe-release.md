Tommy End and Xelina are currently attracting significant interest from multiple wrestling promotions following their departures from WWE earlier this year. According to PWInsider, several companies are exploring the possibility of signing the couple as a duo. The two have been in discussions with various promotions over the past month, though no final agreements have been reached yet.

End and Xelina were released by WWE in April 2026, shortly after WrestleMania 42. Since their departure, both have returned to the independent scene. End, formerly known as Aleister Black, returned to PROGRESS Wrestling and held the Men's World Championship before losing it to Cara Noir on September 27. He has since officially reverted to the Tommy End name. Xelina, previously known as Zelina Vega, has also appeared for PROGRESS and House of Glory. While no exclusive deals have been confirmed, the pair is actively looking for their next opportunity to work together.
