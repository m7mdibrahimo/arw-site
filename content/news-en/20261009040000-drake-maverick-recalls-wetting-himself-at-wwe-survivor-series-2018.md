WWE star Drake Maverick recently looked back at a memorable moment from the 2018 Survivor Series event, where he was asked to urinate on himself during a segment with the Big Show. Maverick explained that the special device given to him for the bit malfunctioned, forcing him to make a quick decision in the moment to pull off the spot successfully.

"Yeah, I don't trust contraptions," Maverick said, recalling how he improvised on the spot while wearing gray pants. He admitted that after the device failed, he simply closed his eyes and committed to the bit without wearing underwear. Backstage, figures like Paul "Triple H" Levesque, Brian James, and Vince McMahon found the situation hilarious, appreciating his dedication to the role.

Maverick proved his reliability through such moments, leading to a later backstage role with the company. After previous releases, he returned to WWE in 2022 and currently works as a creative writer and producer for WWE RAW.
