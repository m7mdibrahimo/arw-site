As AEW continues to grow following its 2019 inception, Executive Vice Presidents Matt and Nick Jackson are sharing more about the company's origins. During a Q&A session ahead of AEW Full Gear on the latest episode of "Being the Elite," Nick Jackson addressed the audience, asking if there were any fans of Pro Wrestling Guerrilla (PWG) present. The Young Bucks were a major part of PWG from 2007 to 2018. "I like to say AEW became PWG on illegal steroids," Nick joked. When Matt added, "not the doctor-prescribed ones?" Nick continued, "That's kind of what we envisioned this could be. Sure enough, eight years later, every show we do, we have PWG-style matches booked and the crowd gets hotter and hotter. That would be my description of AEW and what we envisioned it as." AEW commentator Excalibur, who was the voice of PWG, has previously noted that around 80 AEW employees worked at least one match in PWG, including Kenny Omega and Ricochet. The Young Bucks debuted in PWG in June 2007 and held the PWG World Tag Team Championships four times for a total of 631 days.
