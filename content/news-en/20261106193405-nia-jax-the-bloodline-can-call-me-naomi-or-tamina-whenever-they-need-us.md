Nia Jax remains ready to join The Bloodline whenever she is called upon. In a new interview with Keep It Aloha, Jax addressed her current status with the faction. She noted that while she is technically part of the group, it all depends on the creative direction of the storylines. Jax also stated that if the boys ever need reinforcement, they can always reach out to her, Naomi, or Tamina.
