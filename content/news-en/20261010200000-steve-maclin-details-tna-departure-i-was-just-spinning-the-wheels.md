Steve Maclin recently opened up about his decision to leave TNA after five years, citing significant creative differences. Despite winning the TNA International Championship twice and holding the TNA World Heavyweight Championship, Maclin felt his career had stagnated.

> I kind of felt towards the end of my fifth year I was just spinning the wheels where I was throwing ideas to creative and they were taking them and then nothing would come of it.

Maclin explained that his frustration grew because his pitches for the International Championship, such as defending it in Mexico or Japan, were ignored. He felt he was constantly fighting an uphill battle to improve the product, which led him to request his release in June. Since leaving, Maclin has been competing on the independent circuit and has expressed interest in joining AEW or ROH, where his wife, Deonna Purrazzo, currently competes.
