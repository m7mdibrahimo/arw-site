NWA Worlds Heavyweight Champion Silas Mason has revealed that his biggest inspiration isn't another wrestler, as he hopes to leave behind a legacy similar to country music legend Dolly Parton. During a recent interview with Lucha Libre Online, Mason explained that he admires Parton not just for her awards and success, but for her generosity and how much she gave back to others. Mason stated that he wants to be remembered for helping people and supporting the community, much like Parton did throughout her life. Putting these words into action, Mason noted that whenever the NWA holds territory shows with Joe Cazana Promotions, they donate to local wrestling schools and athletic departments. For Mason, supporting the next generation of athletes is just as important as his dominance inside the ring during his championship reign.
