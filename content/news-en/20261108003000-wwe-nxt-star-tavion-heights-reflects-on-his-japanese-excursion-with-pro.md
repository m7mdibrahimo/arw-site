In the summer of 2024, WWE NXT star Tavion Heights spent a month in Japan on an excursion with Pro Wrestling NOAH. Speaking on the No-Contest Wrestling podcast, Heights described the trip as a critical moment in his development, explaining that stepping into the ring with opponents who spoke no English forced him to adapt quickly and trust his instincts.

During his time in Japan, Heights competed against NOAH regulars including Yoshiki Inamura, Kenoh, and Manabu Soya. He also trained alongside WWE Hall of Famer The Great Muta and NOAH veteran Naomichi Marufuji. Heights recalled receiving memorable praise from Muta, who complimented the unique intensity and execution of his signature belly-to-belly suplex.

> "He just said like 'your belly-to-belly [suplex] is so unique and it's your own. The intensity that which you hit it with and the arch cannot be replicated.'"

Heights signed with WWE in 2022 following a background in amateur wrestling and has since become a regular competitor on the NXT brand. Most recently, he faced Myles Borne in a Weapons Wild Match at NXT Halloween Havoc.
