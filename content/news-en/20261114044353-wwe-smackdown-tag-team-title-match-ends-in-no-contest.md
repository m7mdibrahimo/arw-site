The MFTs kept their Tag Team Championship by technicality after the War Raiders attacked during the title match on WWE SmackDown. Tama Tonga and Talla Tonga controlled the early action against Kyoki before Shinsuke Nakamura entered to turn the momentum. Nakamura nearly secured the win with a Kinshasa before the War Raiders interfered to cause a disqualification. This marks the second time Bakusai has faced the MFTs and won via disqualification following their initial encounter in September.
