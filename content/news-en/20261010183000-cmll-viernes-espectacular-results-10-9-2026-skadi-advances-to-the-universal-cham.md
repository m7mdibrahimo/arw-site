The October 9, 2026, episode of CMLL Viernes Espectacular took place at Arena Mexico in Mexico City. The event was broadcast on the CMLL YouTube membership tier.

Here are the results from the show:

Hecate, Johnnie Robbie, and Nautica def. Candela, Lady Metal, and Valkiria.
Fuerza Poblana (Guerrero Maya Jr. and Xelhua) def. Galeon Fantasma (Barboza and Difunto) in a Lightning Tag Team match.
Blue Panther def. Averno by disqualification in 9:18 in a Lightning match celebrating Blue Panther's 48th anniversary.
Las Valientes (Hera and Olympia) def. Persephone and Reyna Isis in a 2/3 falls match.
Esfinge, Mascara Dorada, and Titan (with KeMonito) def. Guerreros Laguneros (Gran Guerrero, Stuka Jr., and Ultimo Guerrero) (with KeMalito) in a 2/3 falls match.
Skadi won the CMLL Amazonas Universal Championship second phase, defeating India Sioux, Kira, La Jarochita, Tessa Blanchard, Garra Negra, La Magnifica, Lluvia, Marcela, and Tabata.
