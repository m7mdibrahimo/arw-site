During a recent appearance on the "What's Your Story? With Steph McMahon" podcast, Drew McIntyre discussed the highs and lows of his WWE career. While he has achieved significant milestones, including becoming the 31st Triple Crown Champion and winning the Royal Rumble, he has also faced difficult periods. When asked if there was any specific moment he would change, McIntyre pointed to his 2020 Hell in a Cell match against Randy Orton.

> "Wish I could take back falling off the Hell in the Cell," he recalled.

McIntyre explained that the drop was much higher than expected, noting, "I believe it's 20 foot -the cell now- it was 10 feet up. I'm 6'7. When I looked back, I did not like heights. I went, 'What have I done?' and I fell off, hit the table, bit through my tongue."

Despite the pain, McIntyre acknowledged that the spot served the storyline well, especially due to Orton's ability to sell the moment. "But when you're in the ring with somebody like Randy, and you know what you're doing, you know your character, and you're presented with a big moment, you know, that puts over the brother – especially with a big babyface," he added. "It was a cool moment to be in sync like that, and it worked into the finish, but I wish I could take that fall back because it was horrendous." McIntyre confirmed that he suffered no permanent damage and feels great today thanks to his training since 2025.
