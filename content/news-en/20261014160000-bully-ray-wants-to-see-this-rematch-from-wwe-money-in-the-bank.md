Bully Ray recently praised WWE star LA Knight following his impressive performance against Roman Reigns at WWE Money in the Bank. Speaking on Busted Open, Ray argued that Knight deserves another world title opportunity after proving he can deliver in the main event. Ray admitted he now wants to see a rematch between LA Knight and Roman Reigns, even preferring it over a potential clash between Reigns and Penta. However, Ray emphasized that Knight must maintain consistent high-level performances alongside top stars like Cody Rhodes, Seth Rollins, CM Punk, Sami Zayn, and Kevin Kevin Owens to secure his spot permanently.
