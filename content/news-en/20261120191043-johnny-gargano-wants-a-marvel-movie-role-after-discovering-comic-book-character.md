WWE Superstar Johnny Gargano is interested in joining the Marvel Cinematic Universe, specifically to play a character that bears a striking resemblance to him. Gargano recently discovered Repent, a mutant character in Marvel's X-Men comics, and noted the visual similarities in a social media post on August 27, 2026. During a November 20, 2026, interview with TV Insider, Gargano expressed his openness to the idea of portraying the character if Marvel ever brings him to the screen.

> "I can never see myself as someone. But there was a character introduced in a Marvel comic book recently [Repent]. If he ever makes it on screen, we’ll see if I posted about it, I think as well. Look, I’m always open to it, I’d love to do it. If it happens, that’d be great."

Gargano's interest in Marvel extends to WWE's upcoming collaboration with Disney for the Avengers: Doomsday-themed Worlds Collide event on December 15, 2026. He mentioned that the partnership has allowed him to brainstorm creative ideas for his wrestling gear that he has wanted to explore since his time in NXT. Gargano is a self-described comic book and Marvel fan who often makes special arrangements during his travel schedule to keep up with new releases.
