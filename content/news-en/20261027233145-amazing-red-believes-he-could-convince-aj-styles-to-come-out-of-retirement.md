Amazing Red is holding out hope for one more match against his long-time rival, AJ Styles. Following his recent TNA Hall of Fame induction and X-Division Championship win, Red discussed his career and future opponents during an interview with Sports Matters TV in association with Wrestle Slam. Red noted that he has been watching Styles train and help his son at the dojo, which suggests to him that Styles has not completely moved on from the wrestling world. Red compared wrestling to an ex-partner that is difficult to leave behind, noting that he himself came out of retirement after being pushed by Will Ospreay. "I totally agree with you. I've been thinking the same thing. The way Will Ospreay took me out of retirement and just kept pushing me, pushing me, and then I kind of came back." Red admitted that if the timing is right, he might even try to reach Styles through his son. He even mused about the possibility of facing Styles' son for the X-Division title in the future, calling the prospect "crazy."
