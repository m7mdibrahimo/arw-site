Reckless driving charges against former WWE chairman Vince McMahon have been officially dropped following the completion of his probationary period. According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, the legal matter concluded after McMahon satisfied all court-mandated conditions.

The charges originated from a July 2025 crash in Westport, Connecticut, where McMahon totaled his Bentley while traveling at high speeds. As part of his one-year probation, he maintained valid insurance and licensure, avoided further legal issues, and provided a $1,000 charitable donation. The case is now closed.
