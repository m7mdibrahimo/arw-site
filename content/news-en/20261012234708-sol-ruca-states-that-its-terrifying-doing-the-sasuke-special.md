Sol Ruca recently discussed the challenges of performing certain maneuvers in the ring during her appearance on Notsam Wrestling Live!. When asked about her repertoire, Ruca admitted that performing the Sasuke Special is a terrifying experience for her every time.

> The Sasuke Special for example is absolutely terrifying to me. Every time I do it, I’m like, ‘Why am I doing this?’ But when I nail it, I’m like, ‘Hell yeah. That was so sick’ and I have hit my feet on the ropes before and I remember the first few times I did it, I did it very clean and then the first time I hit my feet on the ropes, I was like, oh my God, that was absolutely terrifying.

Ruca explained that she maintains a positive mentality even when things go wrong, noting that mistakes are part of performing live. She emphasized that she continues to practice the move to improve her consistency. Ruca recently competed in the 2026 women's Money in the Bank Ladder match, which was won by Tiffany Stratton.
