Tyler Breeze recalled his early NXT evaluation with Bill DeMott, where he pitched teaming up with Xavier Woods. Breeze thought he was getting fired after a poor review, but when he brought up the tag team pitch, DeMott claimed they had told Woods about it. Breeze opened the door and asked Woods, who confirmed he was never told anything. Breeze then used the moment to pitch multiple other ideas before Canyon Ceman cut him off. Breeze and Woods previously teamed as Hollywood Express in FCW and later reunited on UpUpDownDown. Woods is currently signed to AEW as Austin Creed, while Breeze is a free agent.
