Mick Foley recently discussed his conversation with Darby Allin regarding his violent wrestling style during a Highspots Sign-it-Live session, according to Fightful. Foley confirmed that he spoke with Allin about his approach in the ring, noting that the star thanked him but chose not to change his ways.

> "Well, I did (to try to talk to Darby Allin about his wrestling style). I did, and he thanked me for my feedback and told me he was going to continue — I didn’t say stop. I said, ‘You might wanna calm down.’ He’s like, ‘Well, I appreciate you talking to me, Mick. I really do.’"

Foley pointed out that Allin was moving carefully following the men's Blood & Guts match in AEW, where Wardlow threw him off the top of the cage through tables below after his team lost.

> "So somebody asked, ‘I heard you were gonna talk to Darby?’ I did, and he’s gonna keep doing what he does. I will tell you, he was walking very — a little gingerly last night."

Allin teamed with Will Ospreay, Adam Copeland, Steven Borden, Matt Jackson, and Nick Jackson in the bout, losing to The Don Callis Family of Kazuchika Okada, Kyle Fletcher, Wardlow, Kevin Knight, Mark Davis, and Jake Doyle after Copeland surrendered to save Ospreay.
