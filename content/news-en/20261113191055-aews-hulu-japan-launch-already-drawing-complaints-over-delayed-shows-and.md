AEW recently debuted on Hulu Japan, but the launch has been met with immediate backlash from fans. While the move was intended to streamline access to Dynamite and Collision, Japanese viewers are frustrated by delayed episodes, a lack of local commentary on weekly broadcasts, and missing Japanese subtitles. Dave Meltzer reported in the latest Wrestling Observer Newsletter that these issues surfaced almost immediately after the service began streaming this week. Fans are particularly disappointed because they previously had access to better viewing options via MyAEW and New Japan World, which offered live, commercial-free broadcasts along with Japanese-language features. Now, viewers are forced to wait days for content that has already aired in the United States. Hulu Japan has since announced plans to release unsubtitled weekly episodes sooner, with AI-generated Japanese subtitles to follow approximately one week later. Additionally, the upcoming Full Gear pay-per-view will be streamed live with Japanese commentary, provided it is purchased separately. This rollout stands in stark contrast to WWE’s recent move to Netflix in Japan, where programming is available live with a dedicated Japanese announcing team. As it stands, AEW has secured a dedicated home in Japan, but the current experience has left many long-time viewers unhappy.
