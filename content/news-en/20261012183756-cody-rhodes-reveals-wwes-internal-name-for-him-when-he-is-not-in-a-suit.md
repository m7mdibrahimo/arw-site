Cody Rhodes has revealed the internal name that WWE uses for him when he appears on television without his trademark suit. While speaking in a new episode of Sneaker Shopping, Cody explained that the company calls him "Cozy Rhodes" whenever he is wearing casual wear or sweatsuits instead of formal attire. He noted that staff members will often say he is "doing cozy" when he appears in comfortable outfits that require sneakers.
