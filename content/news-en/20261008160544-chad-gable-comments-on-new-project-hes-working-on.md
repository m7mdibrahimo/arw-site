WWE Intercontinental Champion Chad Gable recently appeared as a guest on Insight with Chris Van Vliet to discuss his career. During the conversation, Gable revealed that he is currently working on a new project documenting his professional journey. While reviewing different phases of his career, he reflected on his time performing as Shorty G and whether to include that era in his upcoming work. He noted that acknowledging the difficult periods is necessary to truly appreciate the high points, adding that the gimmick was simply a character that failed to resonate with the audience. Gable has been holding the Intercontinental Championship since SummerSlam.
