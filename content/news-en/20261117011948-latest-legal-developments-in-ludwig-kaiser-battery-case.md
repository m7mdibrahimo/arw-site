WWE star Ludwig Kaiser, also known as El Grande Americano, continues to face misdemeanor battery charges following his April arrest after a physical altercation with Richard Reap in a Florida hotel elevator. Recent legal filings indicate that Kaiser's defense team is attempting to subpoena Reap to unseal his medical records, aiming to challenge the severity of the injuries Reap claims to have sustained.

According to reports, Kaiser's defense hopes these records will prove that Reap's injuries were not as serious as initially alleged. Assistant State Attorney Juanda Padgett initially agreed to provide the records but withdrew after objections from Reap's representatives, leading the defense to issue a subpoena.

Reap claims he suffered a concussion and a torn rotator cuff after confronting Kaiser and his girlfriend, Andrea Bazarte, regarding their behavior in the elevator. Reap alleges that Kaiser pushed him to the ground and punched him, while Kaiser admits to only shoving Reap after the man approached him aggressively and directed racist remarks toward Bazarte. A Florida court denied a motion to dismiss the charges under the state's Stand Your Ground law in October. A pre-trial conference is set for December, with a potential trial date in January if no settlement is reached.
