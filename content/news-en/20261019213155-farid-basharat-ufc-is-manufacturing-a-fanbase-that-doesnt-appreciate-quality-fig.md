Undefeated bantamweight Farid Basharat was released by UFC after his contract was not renewed, despite boasting a 16-0 career record and a 7-0 record inside the promotion. Basharat believes his careful fighting style did not align with the company's preference for brawls and early finishes. In a video on his YouTube channel, he criticized the direction of UFC, stating that focusing solely on blood and quick finishes could eventually delegitimize the sport. Dana White responded to his departure by noting that there was definitely a reason behind the decision given his undefeated streak.
