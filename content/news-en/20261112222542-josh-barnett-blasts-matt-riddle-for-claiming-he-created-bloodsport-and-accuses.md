Josh Barnett has fired back at Matt Riddle after Riddle claimed he was the creator of the Bloodsport event series. Barnett disputed Riddle's account of a conversation during WrestleMania week, stating, "Let's go with conversations that never happened for $500 Alex. This is hilarious. Last we saw each other was backstage at Natural Born Killers and none of that was said." While Barnett acknowledged he had no role in Riddle's initial 2018 event, he criticized the quality of that show, calling it "second rate." Barnett further accused Riddle of plagiarizing the 1988 film Bloodsport, noting that Riddle used the movie’s font, name, and theme song. "When I came on board I immediately told Brett that not only did this thing need so much help, but that there's no way we're going to sit here and use all this IP from some company that we don't own because that's absolutely stupid," Barnett explained. He argued that the original 2018 event was merely an amateurish execution of an idea and that he was responsible for rebuilding the brand into what it is today. Barnett concluded by asserting that the modern identity of the event belongs to his vision, adding, "There is only one Josh Barnett's: Bloodsport because there is only one Josh Barnett — the person behind its heart and soul."
