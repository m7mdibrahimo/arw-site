John Cena recently shared a bizarre story on Trevor Noah's "What Now" podcast about a trip to Ireland where a volcanic eruption left the WWE roster stranded. With no flights available, the wrestlers were stuck in the country with time to kill. Cena explained that his friends Dylan Postl, known as Hornswoggle, and Christian Cage were traveling together. After a night of drinking, Hornswoggle vomited on his room service hamburger, and Christian left the tray in the hallway. Hours later, a hungry Cena found the burger and, thinking it was untouched, ate it. Cena noted, "I ate the thing." The next morning, his friends revealed the true origin of the meal. In other news, as the one-year anniversary of his final match against GUNTHER at Saturday Night's Main Event approaches on December 13, plans are moving forward for the "John Cena Classic" tournament, which is currently targeted for December 12.
