AEW referee Bryce Remsburg recently revealed that while most wrestlers cooperate with instructions during live television matches, some talent occasionally choose to ignore them. Speaking on The Certified Mark Show, Remsburg explained the constant stream of instructions referees receive through their earpieces from Tony Khan, the show's director, producers, and medical staff. Remsburg noted that these instructions primarily involve timing and production cues, such as adjusting positioning for the hard camera or managing commercial breaks to keep the broadcast on schedule. He added that if an opening match runs significantly over time, it negatively impacts the rest of the show, making coordination essential. While Remsburg emphasized that this issue does not happen frequently, he acknowledged that certain wrestlers simply decide not to follow the given messages when they are in the ring.
