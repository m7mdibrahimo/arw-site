A new agency named Not Too Small has been launched to represent wrestlers in both the sports and Hollywood industries. Chavo Guerrero Jr. confirmed to Fightful that he and longtime wrestling attorney Brad Small have joined forces to form the firm. The agency aims to negotiate wrestling contracts while also helping talent secure opportunities in movies, television shows, commercials, stunt work, and brand deals.

Guerrero brings over 30 years of industry experience from his time in WWE, WCW, TNA, AEW, and Lucha Underground. He has also successfully transitioned into Hollywood, working on productions like The Iron Claw and the television series Margot’s Got Money Troubles. Brad Small, who has served as Guerrero's lawyer and agent since 2003, adds decades of legal expertise representing major industry players.

Guerrero explained the need for a specialized agency in a recent interview:

> "Brad Small, he’s been my lawyer/agent since 2003, doing most of my WWE/WWF stuff and of course, all my TNA and Lucha stuff. Definitely all of my Hollywood stuff. Brad’s just — he’s represented all the big players in the industry and we were talking, with his connections and my connections, why are we not representing wrestlers on our own and doing our own agency?"

Guerrero emphasized the importance of having representation that understands the specific nuances of professional wrestling contracts, which differ significantly from standard entertainment agreements. The agency has already reached out to AEW and WWE to discuss their new venture, with Guerrero noting that the initial feedback from Tony Khan and WWE representatives has been positive.
