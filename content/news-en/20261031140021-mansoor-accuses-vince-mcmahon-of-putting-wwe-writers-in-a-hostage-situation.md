Mansoor recently discussed the chaotic creative environment during the Vince McMahon era in WWE, arguing that the writers were often unfairly blamed by fans for bizarre television moments. Speaking on Self-Made Sitdown, he explained that the writers had little freedom when instructions came directly from McMahon and were frequently stuck in the middle of backstage drama. He described their role by saying, 

> "Those poor writers, man. Yeah, it was like they were in a hostage situation."

Mansoor noted that writers often just wanted wrestlers to accept their scripts to keep things moving rather than facing McMahon with complaints, recalling how they would plead with talent to just take the paper and nod. He also defended them against heavy online criticism, particularly around 2019, pointing out that their main job was simply writing dialogue that McMahon did not want to write himself. 

Sharing an example of McMahon's direct involvement, Mansoor recalled a backstage incident where Sami Zayn rushed in to discuss a match while McMahon was talking to someone else. After McMahon abruptly told him to get out, the interaction ended up directly in Zayn's television script as a highlighted stage direction instructing him to interrupt the interview like a rude person.
