Paul Heyman says there is no one else he could have followed up his run with Brock Lesnar in WWE with than Roman Reigns. Heyman was first paired with Reigns in August 2020 as the latter began his run as The Tribal Chief, and the two worked together for five years before parting ways. Speaking on In Depth with Graham Bensinger, Heyman was asked why he considers Reigns the most transformative talent in his career.

> "Because there is no one else I could have followed up my run with Brock Lesnar with than Roman Reigns. As an athlete, top tier, 100%. Top tier. Great athlete. As a performer, his knowledge of being inside that ring is second to none. I've learned more from Roman Reigns than Roman Reigns has learned from his Wise Man. He knows what to say. I think I added when to say it. But I've learned more from him than he has ever learned from me. He is the Tribal Chief. It was the easiest name I've ever come up with. He is a Tribal Chief and the WWE Universe is his tribe. He has created an island of relevancy that, when you are with him, you are relevant because you are in the presence of Roman Reigns."

Heyman also discussed other topics during the interview, including trying to get Ric Flair to wrestle him.
