John Cena has addressed the upcoming John Cena Classic, admitting that if the inaugural event fails to impress, it will not return. The event is scheduled for December 14 at Disney World, featuring a mix of WWE main roster and NXT stars competing in exhibition matches. Winners of the men's and women's championships will be determined by fan votes on Club WWE.

Speaking to SI's The Takedown, Cena explained his mindset for the experimental show. "I gotta thank Paul Levesque, and I gotta thank Nick Khan for being like, 'Yeah, man, we'll try it.' Saturday Night's Main Event seemed to work. We got Sol Ruca, we got Je'Von Evans, and we got Oba Femi, and those three are thriving right now. So, what do we got to lose? We'll try it, and if it stinks, like, whatever. We don't have to do it again next year."

Cena also discussed the inclusion of NXT talent, noting his desire to provide opportunities for those who have been in the developmental system for several years. He expressed how much the opportunity meant to the performers, noting that many were moved to tears.
