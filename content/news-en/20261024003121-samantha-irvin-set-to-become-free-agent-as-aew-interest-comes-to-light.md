Samantha Irvin is nearing the end of her non-compete agreement, and AEW has expressed interest in bringing her into the company. The former WWE ring announcer has spent two years under a non-compete clause following her departure. While she is set to become a free agent in October 2026, reports indicate that an immediate debut on AEW Dynamite is not currently planned. If she does join the promotion, it would likely be in an on-screen role, though she has previously stated she has little interest in returning to ring announcing. Irvin has remained a regular presence backstage at AEW events to support her husband, Ricochet, and maintains a strong relationship with the organization. As she prepares to explore new opportunities, the decision regarding her professional future remains entirely her own.
