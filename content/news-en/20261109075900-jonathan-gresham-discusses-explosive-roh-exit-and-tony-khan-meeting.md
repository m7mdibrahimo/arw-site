Jonathan Gresham spoke on "Grapsody" about his departure from AEW and his tense final conversation with Tony Khan. The former ROH World Champion explained that he spent a long time feeling depressed about the situation because the resulting rumors damaged the reputation he had built.

Frustrations grew over creative changes to his character after Khan bought Ring of Honor in 2022, prompting Gresham to seek a meeting. After multiple failed attempts to connect, he confronted Khan backstage, leading to a heated discussion where he quit on the spot while agreeing to finish his commitments by dropping the ROH World title to Claudio Castagnoli.
