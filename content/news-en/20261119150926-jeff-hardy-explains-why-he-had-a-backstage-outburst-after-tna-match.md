Jeff Hardy has opened up about a backstage outburst he had following a TNA match, revealing that the chaos of filming multiple weeks of television left him without enough time to properly apply his iconic face paint. Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Hardy explained that ahead of a main event match against Nic Nemeth, he had already painted his face before realizing he needed to appear at ringside during Matt Hardy's match. With only about 15 minutes left before his own match, he attempted to quickly cover the paint up with purple, but the tight window made the task nearly impossible.

Following the match, Hardy admitted that his frustration boiled over behind the scenes.

> "And after that match with Nick, I just went, I like went raging because there was no time throughout the structure of those tapings to where I would've had time to paint my face like again or to get it cleaned off or whatever."

Hardy clarified that his anger had nothing to do with Nemeth or their performance in the ring, noting that Nemeth thought he was upset about the match. Instead, Hardy praised Nemeth as a great worker and ring general who helped everything come together smoothly despite the lack of preparation.

Hardy emphasized that face paint remains a massive part of his identity and presentation as a performer, noting that he typically needs at least 30 minutes to get it right. Because TNA's busy taping schedule compresses weeks of television into a very short window, that necessary time was simply not available on that particular night, leading to a rare moment where chairs took the brunt of his frustration.
