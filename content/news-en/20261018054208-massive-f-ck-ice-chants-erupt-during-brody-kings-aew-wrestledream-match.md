Fans at AEW WrestleDream on October 17, 2026, launched into loud anti-ICE chants as Brody King entered the arena for the AEW World Trios Championship match. The chants were so intense that the commentary team remained silent for several minutes, allowing the audience's reaction to be clearly heard on the broadcast. King, who has been vocal about his opposition to immigration enforcement, was participating in a Two-Out-of-Three Falls match alongside Hangman Adam Page and Bandido as they challenged Swerve Strickland and his team for the titles. This is not the first time King has faced such a reaction; similar chants have occurred throughout 2026, including during episodes of AEW Dynamite and at AEW Grand Slam Australia. King has previously stated that the crowd's support for his stance on the issue has been surprising but motivating. AEW has not officially addressed the recurring chants, leaving the commentary team to let the crowd's reaction play out on air.
