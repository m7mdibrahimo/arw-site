Mason Rook made a surprise return to WWE NXT on the November 10 episode, ending weeks of speculation regarding his health. Rook appeared during the show's opening segment to ambush NXT Champion Grayson Waller, who was in the ring mocking CM Punk. Rook's return comes just six weeks after WWE pulled him from a scheduled NXT Championship match on September 29 due to a medical issue that was never publicly disclosed. Following that withdrawal, reports from Bryan Alvarez suggested the situation could be serious enough to keep Rook sidelined for an extended period. Despite those concerns, Rook is back and has immediately inserted himself into the NXT title picture alongside Shiloh Hill, who also confronted Waller during the same segment. Waller now faces threats from both Rook and Hill as he continues his reign as champion.
