Zoey Serrano discussed her recovery from a major knee injury, revealing that she has no physical limitations anymore. Serrano tore her ACL, MCL, and meniscus in May 2025 and was sidelined until April 2026. Although she was cleared, she was released by WWE at the end of the month before returning to the ring in July. Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Serrano shared details about her testing process and the mental toll of being sidelined.

> "No, not anymore. No, full range of motion, able to go full strength, all of it. But I barely passed the WWE testing, like by a hair. It was one of those, just the strength wasn’t completely there, and this was really hard to come back from," she said. Discussing the rehabilitation process, she admitted that dealing with the mental aspect and the feeling of being forgotten was more difficult than the physical pain. She also mentioned that watching wrestling during the first six months was extremely painful and emotional for her.
