Bishop Kaun is looking to build his own individual legacy within the wrestling world. After spending much of his career competing in tag teams and factions, Kaun is now focused on pursuing Bandido and the ROH World Championship.

In a recent interview, Kaun explained his shift toward singles gold, emphasizing that he wants to move beyond his past success as a trios champion. He stated:

> "I think it’s just time. My legacy in Ring Of Honor has always been the greatest six man champion of all time. It's awesome to be a tag champion, a trios champion, but I think it's time to define my own legacy and why not take Bandido’s championship?"

Kaun reassured fans of The Demand that he remains a member of the group, but he feels it is the right moment to showcase his individual skills and establish his own dynasty in ROH.
