Sol Ruca recently discussed her potential as a heel character in WWE during an interview. Known for her lively and bubbly persona on TV, Sol admitted that she is unsure if she will ever turn heel. She explained that she has frequently been told in the past that her natural personality makes a heel turn very unlikely unless a very specific storyline brings it out of her. Her most recent televised appearance came in the women's Money in the Bank ladder match, and she has since competed on WWE Main Event.
