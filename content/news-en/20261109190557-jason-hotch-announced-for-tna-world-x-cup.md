Jason Hotch is joining the upcoming World X-Cup tournament. According to Fightful, TNA Wrestling revealed that the International Champion will compete for the United States at TNA Destination X on November 15.

He joins Rey Horus as the second participant named for the X-Cup bracket, which consists of a pair of four-way bouts leading to a final match between the victors. TNA Destination X will also feature Leon Slater vs. Trey Miguel for the TNA World Championship, Indi Hartwell vs. Shayna Baszler for the TNA Knockouts World Championship, and Syx against Matt Hardy and Moose, alongside the men's and women's Ultimate X matches.
