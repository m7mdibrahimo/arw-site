Dax Harwood recently spoke about the rivalry between FTR and The New Level, expressing his excitement to face them in AEW. At AEW All In 2026, The New Level, consisting of Austin Creed and Kofi, made their promotional debut by teaming with Swerve Strickland to win the Trios Championships. Later that evening, the duo shared the ring with several top tag teams during a face-off segment.

Speaking on the Danny Ocean Show and 3NT Wrestling, Harwood praised their history together. He noted that they share a checkered past, featuring memorable matches and a ladder match that remains one of his all-time favorites. However, Harwood emphasized that FTR is currently reigning supreme in the tag team division and is looking forward to seeing The New Level test themselves against the greatest of all time.
