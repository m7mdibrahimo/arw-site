During a recent appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Scotty 2 Hotty recalled how Stone Cold Steve Austin accompanied him to his first doctor's appointment before his neck surgery. Scotty explained that he needed cervical fusion surgery due to severe wear on his neck discs, noting that several other wrestlers underwent similar procedures at the time, including Edge, Lita, and Chris Benoit. He shared that Austin even reviewed his x-rays before the doctor entered the room. Scotty credited Austin for his incredible support during that difficult period.
