Carlito discussed his experiences in the United Kingdom, asserting that British cuisine suffers from a lack of sufficient flavor and seasoning compared to other foods. During an appearance on the Hitting the Turnbuckle Podcast, Carlito explained that the stereotype regarding British food is entirely accurate. He noted that he sampled several dishes during his recent wrestling appearances in England and Wales, but found them unremarkable and not particularly high-quality.

Despite his appreciation for the wrestling atmosphere and fans there, he relies on the Nando's chicken chain during every trip, ensuring he visits regularly.
