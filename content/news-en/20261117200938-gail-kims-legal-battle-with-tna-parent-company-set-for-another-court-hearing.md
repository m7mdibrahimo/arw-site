Gail Kim’s ongoing legal dispute with TNA Wrestling’s parent company, Anthem Wrestling Exhibitions, LLC, is set to continue with another court date. Nashville Chancery Court records confirm that a status conference is scheduled for November 20, 2026. This marks the third such conference since July, with no public indication that the two sides have reached a settlement.

The conflict began in January 2026, when Anthem filed a lawsuit against the TNA Hall of Famer. This followed Kim informing the company of her belief that she had potential legal claims under the Florida Private Whistleblower Act after her departure in March 2025. Anthem’s filing sought a court determination on which state’s laws govern the dispute and whether Kim was an independent contractor or an employee. The company contends that Kim was an independent contractor based on a 2022 Services Agreement, citing her work-from-home status and outside projects like her appearances on The Amazing Race Canada and The Traitors Canada.

Anthem is seeking to block Kim from pursuing employment-related claims under Florida law and is requesting attorney's fees and court costs. Kim has not publicly detailed her specific claims, but she has expressed significant dissatisfaction with TNA's current leadership. In March, she stated that a return to the promotion was not possible under the current ownership, adding, "It's not about burying the hatchet. It's about right and wrong. The truth always comes out eventually with time."
