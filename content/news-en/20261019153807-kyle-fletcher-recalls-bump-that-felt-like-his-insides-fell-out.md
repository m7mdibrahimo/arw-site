During a recent interview with Westwood One Sports Night, AEW star Kyle Fletcher recalled the nastiest bump he has ever taken in his career, which happened during a match against Mark Briscoe. Fletcher explained that taking a suplex from the top of the guard rail to the floor caused him to land directly on his tailbone, leaving him with a bizarre and painful sensation. He noted that he briefly had to lie on the mat for about 30 seconds to ensure he was okay before continuing the match. Elsewhere in the interview, Fletcher discussed his current character in AEW.
