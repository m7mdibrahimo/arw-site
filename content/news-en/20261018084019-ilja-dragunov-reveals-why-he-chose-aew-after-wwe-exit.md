Ilja Dragunov has explained his decision to join AEW following his departure from WWE, noting that he wanted a fresh environment where he could breathe and satisfy his hunger for competition. Speaking at the post-AEW WrestleDream media scrum after making his debut at the event, Dragunov reflected on his 14-year career and the feeling of growing stuck in a limited environment. He stated that he still has a strong urge to fight and challenge himself constantly, which led him to AEW for a new landscape. Dragunov's contract with WWE expired in August, and after a delay due to visa matters, he officially arrived at AEW WrestleDream, where he was introduced by Renee Paquette before AEW President Tony Khan confirmed his signing.
