Becky Lynch has criticized her rival Liv Morgan, suggesting that the WWE Women's World Champion lacks the desire to compete. Lynch pointed to Morgan's title reign earlier this year, during which the champion did not defend the title for three months. Speaking on "Raw Recap," Lynch stated, "Like, her biggest claim to fame is that she didn't defend her title for 100 days. That's the only thing that people have been talking about with Liv. That's it." Lynch argued that Morgan is not defined by her in-ring work or her ability to elevate opponents, but rather by her inactivity. While the hosts noted that Morgan is capable of delivering strong performances, such as her match against IYO SKY at SummerSlam, Lynch maintained that Morgan is not interested in such efforts. "She's capable. But that's not what she wants to be known for. She doesn't want to do fights. She has to be coaxed into it," Lynch added. Lynch will soon see Morgan face more frequent challenges, as the champion is scheduled to defend her title against Lynch at Survivor Series later this month.
