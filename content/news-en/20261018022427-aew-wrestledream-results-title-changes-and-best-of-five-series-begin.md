AEW WrestleDream took place on October 17, 2026, from the Addition Financial Arena in Orlando, Florida. In the main event, Will Ospreay defended the AEW World Championship against Samoa Joe, Mike Bailey, Adam Copeland, Konosuke Takeshita, Kazuchika Okada, Ricochet, and Darby Allin in a Clockwork Carnage Match. Mercedes Mone successfully defended her AEW Women's World Championship against Thekla. In a 2-Out-Of-3 Falls Match, Swerve Strickland and New Level defeated Hangman Adam Page, Brody King, and Bandido to retain the AEW World Trios Championship. The Young Bucks retained their AEW World Tag Team Championship against Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight. Persephone defeated Hyan, Queen Aminata, and Dani Luna to retain the TBS Championship. Jon Moxley successfully defended the Continental Championship against Mark Davis. Andrade El Idolo retained his National Championship against Tommaso Ciampa. Chris Jericho defeated Nick Wayne. In the Saturday Tailgate Brawl portion of the event, The Motor City Machine Guns defeated FTR in the first match of a Best of Five Series. Divine Dominion defeated Sisters of Sin, The Don Callis Family defeated Papi D, Rhys Maddox, and Alex Breeze, and The Death Riders and The Dogs defeated The Bang Bang Gang and The Conglomeration.
