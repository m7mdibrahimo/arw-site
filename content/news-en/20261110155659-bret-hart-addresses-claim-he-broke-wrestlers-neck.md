Bret Hart has responded to an accusation from a former enhancement talent who claimed that The Hitman broke his neck with a piledriver during a match in New Jersey. Speaking on the Johnny I Pro Show, Hart stated that keeping opponents safe was his greatest career achievement, noting that he never abused the trust placed in him inside the ring.

Hart revealed that he discovered a video about six months ago featuring the wrestler making the claim. While he does not remember the match, Hart argued that anyone working on WWF television needs to know how to protect themselves and take moves safely, maintaining that he should not be held responsible for the alleged injury.
