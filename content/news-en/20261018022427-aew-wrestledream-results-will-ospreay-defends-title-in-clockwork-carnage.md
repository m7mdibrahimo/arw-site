AEW WrestleDream took place on October 17, 2026, at the Addition Financial Arena in Orlando, Florida. In the main event, Will Ospreay successfully defended his AEW World Championship in a Clockwork Carnage Match against Samoa Joe, Mike Bailey, Adam Copeland, Konosuke Takeshita, Kazuchika Okada, Ricochet, and Darby Allin. Mercedes Mone retained the AEW Women's World Championship by defeating Thekla. The Young Bucks retained the AEW World Tag Team Championship against Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight. Persephone retained the TBS Championship in a Four-Way Match against Hyan, Queen Aminata, and Dani Luna. Andrade El Idolo retained the National Championship against Tommaso Ciampa. In a 2-Out-Of-3 Falls Match, Adam Page, Brody King, and Bandido defeated Swerve Strickland and New Level to become the new AEW World Trios Champions. Jon Moxley retained the Continental Championship against Mark Davis. Nick Wayne defeated Chris Jericho. In the Saturday Tailgate Brawl, The Motor City Machine Guns defeated FTR in the first match of a Best of Five series, Divine Dominion defeated Sisters of Sin, The Don Callis Family defeated Papi D, Rhys Maddox, and Alex Breeze, and The Death Riders and The Dogs defeated The Bang Bang Gang and The Conglomeration.
