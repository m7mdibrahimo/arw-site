The October 20, 2026, episode of WWE NXT emanated from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida. Tony D'Angelo opened the broadcast by calling Grayson Waller to the ring. The two rivals exchanged heated words regarding their history, with D'Angelo promising to defeat Waller at Halloween Havoc.

In a quarterfinal match for the 2026 Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic, El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr. and Galeno def. BirthRight. During the bout, Wagner Jr. used Galeno's spine as a platform to launch himself over the top rope, striking their opponents and Uriah Connors on the floor.
