The mother of NWA National Heavyweight Champion Bryan Idol, Antoinette Nazar, took matters into her own hands after a confrontation with NWA wrestler Mike Mondo. The conflict began at an NWA event when Mondo confronted Nazar at ringside, which resulted in her falling backward into the crowd. Nazar told WMDT that the incident occurred while she was calling out Mondo for cheating. Idol was outraged that his family was involved, stating, "Mondo, you put my hands on my mother, not only am I going to step in there with you, I want to hurt the ones that are near and dear to you." Nazar later sought her own revenge at a subsequent event in Philadelphia. When Mondo confronted her again, she slapped him across the face. Idol ultimately retained his championship against Mondo and Slade in a triple threat ladder match. Reflecting on his title reign, Idol noted, "One of my personal favorites had this championship, Rowdy Roddy Piper, so it's really cool to have the National Heavyweight Champion share a legacy with a name like that."
