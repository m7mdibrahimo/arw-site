Je’Von Evans has not forgotten those who doubted his ability to compete for championships, and he is now responding to them with sarcasm following his first major WWE achievement. After his victory at WWE Crown Jewel 2026, Evans posted a message on X highlighting past comments from critics who belittled his potential to reach the top of the card.

Evans and Penta won the World Tag Team Championship by defeating Bron Breakker and Austin Theory. The match featured interference from Bronson Reed, but a surprise appearance by Oba Femi, who put Reed through a table, provided the opening for Evans and Penta to secure the gold.

Evans shared an old post questioning his place in the title picture alongside a photo of himself with his new championship. He clearly enjoyed the moment, especially following their heated rivalry with The Vision.
