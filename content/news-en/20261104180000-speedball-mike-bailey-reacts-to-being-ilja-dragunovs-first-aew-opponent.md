Mike Bailey discussed his defeat against Ilja Dragunov during the latter's in-ring debut in AEW. Dragunov made his debut on the October 21 edition of AEW Dynamite, facing Bailey in a high-flying encounter. Following the match, Bailey shared his thoughts on his vlog, expressing disappointment over the outcome while praising his opponent's performance. Bailey noted that while he continues to fall short in pursuits for singles gold, such as the AEW World Championship, TNT Championship, National Championship, and International Championship, he remains determined to eventually achieve his goals.
