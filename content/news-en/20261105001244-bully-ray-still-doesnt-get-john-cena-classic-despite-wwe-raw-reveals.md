John Cena shared more details about the John Cena Classic on "WWE Raw" this past Monday, including the tournament's location, timing, and the first entrant, Cody Rhodes. Despite these updates, many questions remain regarding the tournament's purpose. On "Busted Open Radio," Bully Ray expressed confusion, questioning the stakes beyond a championship belt. "I'm not a huge fan of inconsequential rewards," he said. "This seems a little light to me, like you're just going to win this championship. Now, OG King of the Ring, you were deemed the best wrestler of that tournament, right? You got a cape and a crown and a saber, you were King of the Ring. This feels similar... We're fighting for this belt that is named after this guy with a picture of this guy on it. What are we fighting for? I get it, it's entertainment, but what are we fighting for?" Bully Ray noted that past King and Queen of the Ring winners earned title opportunities. He argued that if a title shot were involved, Cena would have announced it on Monday. He hopes for a tangible reward, though he suggested one potential outcome that would intrigue him. "If this is all being done for Cody Rhodes to win the whole thing, only to be presented with... that beautiful John Cena belt, for Cody to crack John in the face," he said. "For Cody to turn heel, I'm on board, brother."
