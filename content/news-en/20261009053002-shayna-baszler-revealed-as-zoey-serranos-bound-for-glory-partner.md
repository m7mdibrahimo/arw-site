Zoey Serrano acted quickly to secure a formidable partner for her upcoming match at Bound For Glory. During the October 8, 2026, episode of TNA iMPACT, Serrano responded to a challenge issued by Knockouts World Tag Team Champions Allie and Kiera Hogan by making a pivotal phone call.

The mystery individual on the other end was revealed to be former WWE star Shayna Baszler. Baszler agreed to join Serrano for the title match this Sunday, provided she could fulfill her specific requirement.

> "As long as I get to break bones."
