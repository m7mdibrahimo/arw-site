Former UFC Champion Merab Dvalishvili was assaulted by a group led by Usman Nurmagomedov in Abu Dhabi. Dvalishvili shared a video on social media explaining the incident, stating that Nurmagomedov and his associates attacked him from behind while he was conducting an interview in a hotel lobby. Dvalishvili said, "Was doing an interview for RAF in hotel lobby and Usman and his buddies came up from behind and started punching me and my young friend. Security was holding me - They kept kicking us when we were down like losers." Shortly after the post, former UFC Lightweight Champion Ilia Topuria shared footage of the altercation, condemning the actions of the attackers. Dvalishvili previously defeated Usman's brother, Umar Nurmagomedov, by unanimous decision at UFC 311 in January 2025.
