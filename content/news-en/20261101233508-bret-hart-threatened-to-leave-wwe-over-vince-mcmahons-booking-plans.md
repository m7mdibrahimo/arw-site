Bret Hart revealed he threatened to leave WWE after feeling that management's promises of giving him a major singles push might vanish.

Speaking to Inside The Ropes, Hart recalled the period when the Hart Foundation lost the Tag Team Championships to the Nasty Boys, after which Vince McMahon promised him a full opportunity as a singles wrestler. However, upon arriving at the WWE television tapings in Las Vegas, he was surprised by management's desire to place him in an immediate match against Curt Hennig, who had just captured the Intercontinental Championship at WrestleMania.

Although Hart admitted his threat to walk away was merely a bluff, he stood his ground to avoid an early loss that would damage his image before the fans. Consequently, the company altered its plans and assigned him another opponent to begin building him up gradually, before he ultimately achieved his ultimate goal by defeating Hennig for the title at SummerSlam.
