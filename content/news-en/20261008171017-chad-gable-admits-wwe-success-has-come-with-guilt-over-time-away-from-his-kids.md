WWE Intercontinental Champion Chad Gable recently opened up about the personal toll of his career, admitting that achieving success in the ring has come with feelings of guilt over the time he spends away from his wife and children. During an interview with Chris Van Vliet, Gable discussed the difficulty of balancing rigorous training, constant travel, and his family life, noting that fatherhood forced him to cut back on personal priorities like fitness and nutrition.

> "I had to let a lot of that go. And I think it’s something I’m still dealing with as far as like I put certain things on a pedestal as far as fitness, nutrition, all these things I make matter to a degree that I think is too much."

Gable explained that his guilt stems from feeling like he works too much rather than too little, often wishing he could spend more time with his kids. He noted that while their busy schedules with sports and activities help pull him away from the gym, true balance at an elite level is ultimately impossible to achieve without making significant sacrifices.

> "If you want to reach the kind of levels that we’re talking about reaching, right? If you want to be the best at what you do, I’m sorry, you’re just not going to have that kind of balance. There’s going to be sacrifice made somewhere, unfortunately. That’s just the way it is. And I—I have paid that price many times, and I still am."
