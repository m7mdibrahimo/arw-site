According to Fightful, MyBookie has released betting odds regarding which wrestler will be the next to pin or submit WWE King of the Ring champion Oba Femi. Bron Breakker leads the list at -110, followed by Roman Reigns at +100, Drew McIntyre at +400, Cody Rhodes at +600, GUNTHER at +800, Bronson Reed at +1000, CM Punk at +1200, Jacob Fatu at +1400, Seth Rollins at +1600, Royce Keys and Randy Orton at +1800, Damian Priest at +2200, L.A. Knight at +2500, and Danhausen at +3000.

Femi's last defeat on WWE programming occurred against Brock Lesnar at Clash in Italy. Before that, he remained undefeated since his time in NXT, where he last lost to Ricky Saints in an NXT Championship match at No Mercy.
