WWE Hall of Famer Jim Duggan recently spoke about his generation of professional wrestlers in comparison with the current generation, highlighting the strengths of both eras. In an interview with Clutchpoints, Duggan noted that while his generation may have been more creative, today's wrestlers are much more athletic.

Duggan explained that wrestlers in the past had to come up with their own gimmicks and navigate a different environment before the sports entertainment era. He recalled how locker room disputes and business matters were often handled directly among wrestlers, contrasting it with the modern landscape where today's athletes showcase incredible physical skills inside the ring.
