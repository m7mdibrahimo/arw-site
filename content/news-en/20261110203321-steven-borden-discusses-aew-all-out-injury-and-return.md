Steven Borden recently spoke about the injury he suffered at AEW All Out and his swift return to the ring at Blood and Guts during a media interview.

At AEW All Out, Borden made his pay-per-view match debut alongside Darby Allin to face Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher in a bout where the victors would earn a tag team title opportunity. The two competitors collided mid-air during the encounter, leading to an in-ring evaluation by Doc Sampson and a stoppage of the match before Borden was transported to a local hospital.

Tony Khan later stated that Borden was conscious and that his CT scan and medical tests returned positive results. Discussing the incident, Borden stated,

> "I guess it would be called an injury. Obviously the brain is pretty important. But physically it's a weird one because the moment I woke up, I felt okay. You know, I didn't hurt my back or my neck. So I feel fine. And I think for me, you don't want to run from difficult things or pressure."

Allin added that when returning from an injury, a competitor should advance at full speed rather than with caution, mirroring the approach Borden took for Blood and Guts.
