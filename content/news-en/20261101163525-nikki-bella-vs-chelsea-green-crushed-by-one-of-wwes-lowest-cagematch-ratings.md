Nikki Bella and Chelsea Green's title match on WWE SmackDown has received an extremely low rating on Cagematch, currently sitting at 1.15 out of 10. Fans heavily criticized the match, the lack of crowd reaction, the chemistry in the ring, and the controversial finish where Green put her foot on the ropes during a roll-up pin. Viewers also expressed frustration over WWE's handling of returning legends and Hall of Famers.
