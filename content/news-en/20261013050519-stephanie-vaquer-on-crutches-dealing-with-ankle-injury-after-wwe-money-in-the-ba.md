Stephanie Vaquer is currently dealing with an ankle injury following her participation at WWE Money In The Bank. Vaquer challenged Becky Lynch and Liv Morgan for the Women's World Championship during the premium live event. In the closing moments of the bout, Morgan regained her title after delivering an Oblivion to Vaquer. During the latest episode of WWE Raw, Jackie Redmond interviewed Vaquer backstage regarding her condition. Despite being seen on crutches, Vaquer insisted to Becky Lynch that she will recover in a few days.
