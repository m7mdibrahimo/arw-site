Roman Reigns recently addressed criticism regarding his reduced WWE schedule during an appearance on Club Shay Shay, explaining why he no longer wrestles at non-televised live events. The Tribal Chief noted that between 2012 and 2018, he regularly worked between 150 and 200 matches a year to hone his craft and gain essential experience. However, with WWE's current business model heavily reliant on massive television rights, streaming deals, and premium live events, the company can no longer risk its biggest stars getting injured at house shows. While Reigns believes that live events remain crucial for talent development, he acknowledges that the financial stakes for Monday Night RAW, Friday Night SmackDown, and PLEs mean top attractions must be preserved for television.
