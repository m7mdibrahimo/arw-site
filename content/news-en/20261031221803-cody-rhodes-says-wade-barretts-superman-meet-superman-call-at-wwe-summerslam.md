Cody Rhodes recently discussed commentary during an appearance on the Double Coverage Podcast, highlighting a memorable call by Wade Barrett during his Undisputed WWE Championship match against John Cena at WWE SummerSlam 2025. Rhodes recalled how Barrett reacted when he kicked out of Cena's Attitude Adjustment, exclaiming "Superman meet Superman." Rhodes praised the moment as high validation, noting that it felt like a gift of a job well done when the fans, commentary, and fellow wrestlers all truly believe in the action.
