Dragon Lee has reportedly agreed to a new contract with WWE, keeping him with the company for the foreseeable future. According to PWInsider, the luchador has signed what is believed to be a five-year deal. This agreement puts an end to speculation about his future, especially given that his previous contract was nearing its expiration and his brother Dralistico is approaching the end of his AEW deal. Self Made Pro previously reported that WWE officials did not expect Lee to leave, and it now appears those expectations were correct. Lee originally joined WWE in December 2022 and moved to the main roster in 2023. Throughout 2026, he has actively chased the Intercontinental Championship, challenging for the title four times, including an unsuccessful attempt on the October 19 episode of WWE RAW.
