WWE Hall of Famer Kevin Nash expressed intense anger toward US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth following an incident at Arlington National Cemetery on Veterans Day. Hegseth was near the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier with his wife and advisers when a sentinel intervened to remind the group to maintain silence and respect.

This occurred shortly after House Republicans moved to block impeachment proceedings introduced against Hegseth by Rep. Thomas Massie. Nash posted a furious message on X attacking Hegseth's behavior and demanding his immediate resignation.

Nash had previously called for the removal of Hegseth and other officials during an episode of his Kliq This podcast. He argued at the time that discussions and internal movements regarding Hegseth's impeachment were already underway before these recent events.
