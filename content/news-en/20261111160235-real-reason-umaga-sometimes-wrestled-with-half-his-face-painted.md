Umaga stands out as one of the most terrifying figures in WWE history, but his son Zilla Fatu recently shared a hidden detail regarding his iconic face paint. Fatu explained on Busted Open After Dark with Bully Ray that instances of his father wrestling with only half his face painted did not stem from creative choices, but rather from running late and lacking sufficient time to finish before reaching the arena.

Fatu noted that his mother constantly pushed him to leave early and arrive at venues on time. Bully Ray confessed that he had no clue, assuming the varying designs were simply creative expressions. Fatu added that he attempts to incorporate his father's distinct facial expressions and emotional communication into his current career in WWE NXT.
