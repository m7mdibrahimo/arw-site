WWE has announced a new collaboration with Marvel to promote the upcoming film "Avengers Doomsday." The company will host a "Worlds Collide" special on December 15 at the ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex, just days before the movie's December 18 release. This event will feature talent from WWE, "NXT," and AAA, continuing the tradition of past "Worlds Collide" specials. The partnership aims to merge the storytelling of both companies, with WWE World also taking place that weekend at the same venue in conjunction with Fanatics. Fans attending WWE World will have access to photo and autograph opportunities, as well as exclusive co-branded WWE and "Avengers" merchandise, including title belts, which will also be available at WWEShop and select Disney retail stores.
