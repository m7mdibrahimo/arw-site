Brie Bella is eager to return to a WWE ring, but her shoulder recovery is keeping her on the sidelines. The WWE Hall of Famer recently underwent a CT scan to assess her progress, though she is still dealing with broken bones resulting from her injury at SummerSlam on August 1.

Speaking on OutKick Dropkick, Brie revealed that her shoulder blade has not yet healed after being broken in five places. She hopes for better news during her next medical checkup in December while continuing her physical therapy routine.

> "Well, right now, I still have some breaks, unfortunately. We just got my CT scan back, but I’m hoping beginning of December, I’ll have better news. So, I’m just keeping up with my physical therapy. Finally, I got to kind of go up the next level of physical therapy and that was really fun. It was really challenging so far this week. I was like, whoa, I haven’t been this sore like since July."

Brie is aiming to resume in-ring training in January 2027, which could position her for a potential return during the Royal Rumble season. However, doctors will not clear her to wrestle until her shoulder blade is fully fused. Despite her frustration with the slow pace, doctors have assured her that her recovery is proceeding on schedule, with her next key evaluation set for December.
