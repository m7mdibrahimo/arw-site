The 2019 WWE Crown Jewel flight delay has been the subject of speculation for years, and former WWE co-president George Barrios is offering his perspective on the situation. Speaking on My World with Jeff Jarrett, Barrios addressed the incident where WWE talent were left stranded in Saudi Arabia. While he did not claim to have all the details, he suggested the situation was overblown.

> "Yeah, I think it was kind of — I don't know what the right phrase is — I think it was exaggerated to some degree."

Barrios recalled a separate travel issue in another country where a plane was delayed due to a sudden airport fee. He noted that he simply authorized the payment to ensure the talent returned home, joking about legal compliance while emphasizing that WWE was merely settling a local fee. Regarding the Saudi Arabia incident, Barrios admitted he did not know the exact cause of the delay, but he dismissed the more dramatic theories that suggested talent were held against their will. The event remains a point of contention, having previously led to a $39 million shareholder settlement in 2020.
