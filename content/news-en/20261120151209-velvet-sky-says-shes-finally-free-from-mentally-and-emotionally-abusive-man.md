Former TNA Knockouts Champion Velvet Sky has shared a personal update, revealing that she is finally free from a man she accused of subjecting her to years of mental and emotional abuse. Sky took to her Instagram Story to post an emotional statement, noting that the state had finally set her free after a difficult two-and-a-half-year period. She did not name the individual or specify the legal context, but expressed immense relief and gratitude toward her family and close friends for their unwavering support as she begins to reclaim her peace.
