The October 16, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown emanated from the Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, California. The broadcast featured Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn opening the show alongside Johnny Gargano and Candice LeRae. After a verbal confrontation with United States Champion Trick Williams, a match between Williams and Gargano was sanctioned.

Trick Williams def. Johnny Gargano in a non-title match.

Paige def. Nikki Bella in a singles match.

During the event, General Manager Nick Aldis announced a future Street Fight between Blake Monroe and Giulia, while Chelsea Green and Nikki Bella agreed to a match scheduled for two weeks out. Additionally, Zayn declared his intention to confront Roman Reigns on the upcoming episode of Monday Night RAW.
