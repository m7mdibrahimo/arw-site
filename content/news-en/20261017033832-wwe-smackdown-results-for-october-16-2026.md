The October 16, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown emanated from the Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, California. The event featured several high-stakes matchups following the recent Money in the Bank show.

In non-title action, United States Champion Trick Williams defeated Johnny Gargano after hitting a Book End. The match included interference from Candice LeRae, but Williams managed to secure the victory.

Paige faced WWE Hall of Famer Nikki Bella in a singles match. After a back-and-forth contest that saw both competitors utilize the ringside barricades, Paige used the ropes to secure a pinfall victory over Bella.

R-Truth took on Damian Priest in a singles bout. The match ended prematurely when the referee stopped the contest to check on R-Truth following an awkward landing.

In the main event, Rey Fénix defended his AAA World Cruiserweight Championship against "Absolute" Ricky Saints. Rey Fénix retained his title after the match ended in a disqualification when Saints interfered with the champion on the ropes, drawing the referee's intervention.
