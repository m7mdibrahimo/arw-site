Charlotte Flair recently discussed the influence her legendary father, Ric Flair, had on her professional discipline and fitness routine. Speaking to The Screen Chatter Interview Page, the WWE Superstar expressed her gratitude for the work ethic she inherited from the "Nature Boy."

> "No, but he has instilled in me such a dedicated work ethic to the gym and to taking care of myself and then seeing him at his age still be cognizant of like what he eats."

While Flair admires her father's health-conscious approach, she jokingly suggested that his current training intensity could be higher. 

> "Yeah. Or, you know, do I think he could be in the gym? Probably a little bit more. But, you know, my dad likes to have fun. That's his gym time."

Charlotte emphasized that while she appreciates his example, she maintains a much stricter training schedule than he does, noting that she never misses a day of preparation. She credited both her parents for instilling the habits that have helped her maintain a high level of performance throughout her career.
