Ethan Page spoke about his current rival, Chad Gable, noting that he sees him as a strange mix of wrestling legends Kurt Angle and Bret Hart.

In a new interview, Ethan Page mentioned that he has wrestled Chad Gable many times, describing him by stating:

> "People absorb things a lot differently and you can see the influences in his wrestling. Maybe he’s studying matches of, I don’t know, he reminds me of a lot of like a Kurt Angle or Bret Hart, or maybe both wrestlers mixed together in the weirdest way possible."

However, Page criticized his rival for focusing only on studying maneuvers and attacks rather than monitoring the full picture of all wrestlers and preparing for every possibility. He added that he watches everyone to calculate his steps carefully, just like how he views Roman Reigns as the biggest threat to his Intercontinental Championship.
