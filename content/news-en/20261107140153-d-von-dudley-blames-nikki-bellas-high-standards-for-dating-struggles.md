Nikki Bella has been open about the challenges of re-entering the dating pool, and according to D-Von Dudley, her celebrity lifestyle and high standards complicate finding the right partner. Speaking on the DukeLovesRasslin podcast, D-Von discussed Nikki's recent admission that she tried to shoot her shot with a man on a flight, only to discover he was married, and pointed to her past relationship with John Cena.

> "Well, when you were married, when you were together with John Cena for as long as you have, that's some pretty big shoes to fill."

He explained that the real issue is finding someone who can handle the entertainment industry, noting that not everyone is cut out for that world.

D-Von added that Nikki knows exactly what she wants after her experiences in wrestling and entertainment, and he believes she holds very high standards. While joking about helping her find a date through a matchmaking show, he admitted he would never pitch it to her directly for fear of her physical reaction.
