R-Truth has provided a health update after suffering a scary injury during his match against Damian Priest on the October 16 episode of WWE SmackDown. On October 19, 2026, Truth shared a photo on social media from a hospital room while sitting in front of an MRI machine, reassuring fans that he is doing okay.

> "I AM OK, Thank you ALL for your support, concerns and get well wishes , Wow, It truly feels amazing to feel and be loved I'm grateful for you all."

Truth also added some humor to the situation, joking about his former tag team partner before confirming his plans to return.

> "…On 10/16/2026 I almost passed away… Damien Priest tried to kill me,, I didn't think he was ThAt serious… But ok, I'll be back"

The injury occurred when Truth landed awkwardly after delivering a missile dropkick from the top rope, leading the referee to immediately stop the match. Wrestling Observer's Bryan Alvarez reported that Truth was set to undergo further medical evaluation. Although an official return timetable has not been announced yet, Truth's positive message shows he is ready to get back into the ring.
