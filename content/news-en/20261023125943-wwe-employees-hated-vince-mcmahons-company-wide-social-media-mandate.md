Stephanie McMahon recently revealed that employees inside WWE strongly disliked Vince McMahon's strict company-wide social media mandate. Speaking with Michael Hayes, Stephanie explained that what started as her idea to share digital responsibilities across departments quickly escalated under her father. Vince decided that social media should be a hundred percent of every department's focus instead of just a fraction. This led to department heads having to regularly report their social media activities, leaving many workers frustrated and confused about why online posting was suddenly tied to their core jobs. Stephanie also recalled that Vince ultimately placed Michael Cole in charge of overseeing WWE's social media efforts during that period.
