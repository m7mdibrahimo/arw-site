Paul Wight is still working to bring back Captain Insano to AEW. Speaking to Going Ringside, Wight confirmed that plans for the character are ongoing and that he wants the comeback to be presented in the best possible format. Wight first played the character in Adam Sandler’s 1998 movie The Waterboy. AEW previously filed a trademark for the character in January 2025 covering merchandise, action figures, and televised appearances. Captain Insano has previously made appearances in AEW through The Acclaimed’s music video and an MJF and Adam Cole skit, as well as at the Country Music Awards in 2024. Wight has also discussed getting physically ready for a bigger return, noting that weight loss and cardio are part of the process and that the outfit might need an update.
