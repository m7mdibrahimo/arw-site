Jonathan Coachman revealed that his early tenure at ESPN involved a harsh lesson, as he claims network executives sabotaged his relationship with current WWE President Nick Khan, resulting in significant financial losses. During an appearance on the Boots to Boots podcast with Deonna Purrazzo and Steve Maclin, Coachman explained that Khan was a prominent talent agent when an ESPN colleague informed him that Khan was interested in representing him.

Although Coachman initially believed he had secured one of the industry's top agents, the situation deteriorated after ESPN executives, who reportedly disrespected his WWE background, began spreading negative rumors about him to Khan. Coachman noted that Khan eventually negotiated his second ESPN contract, which included a $60,000 pay cut. He was left with an ultimatum to accept the terms during a period that coincided with the birth of his second child.

Coachman later learned from another of Khan's clients that Khan had been told Coachman was insulting his business practices. This misinformation led to a subsequent contract negotiation that resulted in an additional $120,000 pay cut. While he has come to terms with the situation, Coachman expressed frustration that a single phone call could have resolved the misunderstandings before his paychecks were impacted.
