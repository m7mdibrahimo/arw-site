Callum Newman competed in the G1 Climax tournament while dealing with a dislocated shoulder after suffering the injury at NJPW Dominion. Despite the physical setback, he participated in every match and reached the semifinals before losing to Ryohei Oiwa.

While speaking to NJPW, according to Fightful, Newman detailed what happened during his bout against Aaron Wolf, stating, "He gave me one of those judo throws and it popped- not popped out but I felt it move and then I felt this bolt of electricity down my arm."

He also noted that facing Oiwa was difficult because of his skill with armdrags and arm holds. Despite the injury, Newman rebounded by defeating HENARE on the final night of block action to secure his spot in the semifinals.
