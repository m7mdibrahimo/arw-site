WWE Hall of Famer Booker T recently reflected on the infamous 1993 WCW incident involving The Shockmaster. Appearing on "Insight with Chris Van Vliet," Booker explained that the segment was intended to build toward his first main event match, which was set to feature himself, Stevie Ray, and Sid Vicious against The Shockmaster, Sting, and Davey Boy Smith.

Booker attributed the disastrous fall to a production change made to the wall during the day. "The thing is, a lot of people don't realize, we rehearsed that thing earlier," Booker said. "We went over it. But somehow, I think the wall that they had, they wanted it to be a little more sturdier. So they put that board down at the bottom. And I don't think anybody told Fred. And Fred...Fred had the mask on. He couldn't see nothing. It wasn't a great idea, you know, from jump street, even though we just used it around here not too long ago."

Recalling the aftermath, Booker noted how difficult it was to remain in character as Sid Vicious reacted to the mishap. "I was so green and so young I just tried to keep a straight face," Booker said. "I just tried to keep my composure. And Sid goes 'I told ya!' That's what Sid said. And I didn't want to laugh. It was one of those moments. I felt really, really bad for Fred, because the helmet came off, the glitter went off. And I hate glitter. I hate...getting glitter on me. It's just one of my phobias. It was all over the place. And then you just knew things were going to change for him after that."
