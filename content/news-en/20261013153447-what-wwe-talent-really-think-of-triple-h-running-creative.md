Despite online criticism of WWE creative decisions, a new report suggests that Triple H maintains strong support within the locker room. According to JoeyVotes and TC of WrestleVotes, wrestlers rarely blame Triple H when storylines fail to meet expectations. Joey stated, "I've never heard a bad word about Triple H. Talent like him. We talked to a talent this week that praised Triple H. Triple H is never the problem. Contracts? Not Triple H. Things like that? Not Triple H." TC noted that talent understands Triple H never had a period of full creative control before the TKO takeover, viewing him as an advocate for the roster rather than the final decision-maker. "That's kind of a nice shield for him. It's like, hey, I like you, we have plans for you, but it's big bad TKO that's handcuffing me," TC explained. Joey estimated that approximately 95% of the current roster supports Triple H, adding that while wrestlers recognize his creative limitations, they remain confident that he will advocate for them whenever possible.
