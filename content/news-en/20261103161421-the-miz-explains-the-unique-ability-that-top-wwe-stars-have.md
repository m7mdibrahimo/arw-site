The Miz recently discussed the unique trait that sets top WWE superstars apart from the rest of the roster. During an interview with Q104.3 New York, he noted that handling intense pressure is essential for long-term success at the highest level. 

"The Roman Reigns' is the CM Punk's, the Cody Rhodes'. There's something about them. The reason they're always at the top of the tippity top is because they're able to handle the pressure that nobody else understands what it takes to be a huge success in WWE, and Sami Zayn's finding that out right now," The Miz stated. He also shared insights on his own career and path to the company.
