WWE continues to grow its reach in India through its partnership with Netflix, even as the nation implements strict new regulations on online gambling. While fans in India now access Raw, SmackDown, NXT, and Premium Live Events via the streaming service, the government has enacted the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, which took effect in May 2026. This legislation imposes nationwide restrictions on online money gaming, including activities involving financial stakes, while distinguishing them from permitted social gaming and esports.

WWE's transition to Netflix in India occurred on April 1, 2025, ending a two-decade broadcasting partnership with Sony Pictures Networks India. The new deal provides subscribers with weekly television, major events, Hindi commentary, and archival content. WWE President Nick Khan previously emphasized the importance of the Indian market, stating, "At the top of that list was India. If it's important to Netflix, assume it's important to us." Khan has also noted that the company is considering hosting a major Premium Live Event in the country.

While the regulatory environment for online money gaming has tightened, these changes do not appear to impact WWE's distribution agreement. Netflix remains a subscription entertainment service, and WWE's programming is not categorized under the gaming restrictions. The company maintains its international expansion strategy, with the Netflix partnership serving as a primary platform for its continued growth in the region.
