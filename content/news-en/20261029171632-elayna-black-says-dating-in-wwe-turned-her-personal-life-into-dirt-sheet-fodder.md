Elayna Black learned quickly that dating while working for WWE meant her personal life would not stay private. The former Cora Jade discussed in a documentary on TNA Wrestling's YouTube channel how signing with WWE at nineteen years old brought intense public scrutiny. She explained that strangers immediately began having opinions about everything she posted online. Black admitted that her relationship with WWE star Bron Breakker and later speculation involving Harlem Lewis and Thea Hail drew unwanted attention from fans and news websites. Beyond her dating life, she also noted how being in the public eye made her insecure about her appearance. Now twenty-five and competing in TNA Wrestling, Black realizes that achieving fame at a young age came with a significant cost to her privacy.
