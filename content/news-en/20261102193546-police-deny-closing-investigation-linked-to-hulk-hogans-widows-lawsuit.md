Clearwater police have denied claims that their investigation linked to a lawsuit involving Hulk Hogan’s widow, Sky Daily, and her son Hayden Hitt has been closed. According to TMZ, a Jane Doe plaintiff alleged in her lawsuit that police quickly shut down the case due to Daily’s standing within the Church of Scientology after Hitt allegedly became violent with her. However, the police department stated that the investigation began in March and was referred to the Pinellas-Pasco State Attorney’s Office for potential prosecution, emphasizing that the case remains very much open. The lawsuit details disturbing allegations regarding a March 28 party where Hitt allegedly shoved Jane Doe through a doorway and into a clothes rack, while Daily reportedly told her to forgive him. A second lawsuit filed by a minor, Jane B. Doe, alleges she was sexually assaulted by another minor at the same party while Hitt guarded the door, with both Daily and Hitt facing negligence claims. While these allegations remain unproven in court, police insisted on publicly clarifying that the investigation is ongoing.
