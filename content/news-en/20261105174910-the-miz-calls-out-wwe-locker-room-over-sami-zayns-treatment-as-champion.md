The Miz is speaking out against the lack of respect shown toward Sami Zayn during his current reign as WWE Champion. In a recent interview with Z100 New York, The Miz expressed his frustration with how fans and other superstars continue to undervalue Zayn's work, noting that he could lose the title at any moment on SmackDown due to the number of challengers chasing him.

> "Everyone else is like, 'Ah, Sami's a joke. Are you kidding me? Why is this guy He Everything revolves around what he is doing with the WWE championship.' And there are so many people that are vying for that championship that you literally any given moment, any given SmackDown, he could lose it."

Comparing Zayn's situation to his own WWE Championship run in 2011, The Miz argued that the constant criticism from social media bullies is unwarranted. He emphasized that he would rather stand with Zayn than pursue the championship himself, believing that Zayn has earned the respect of the entire locker room for his ability to carry the show.

> "He is so underappreciated, undervalued, and I believe that a Sami Zayn deserves the respect from not only the audience, but all the WWE superstars in the locker room, Raw, SmackDown, and what he's able to do in carrying the show."
