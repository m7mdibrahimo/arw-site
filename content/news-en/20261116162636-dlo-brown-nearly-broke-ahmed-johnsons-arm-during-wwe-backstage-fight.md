The Godfather is looking back at a violent Attitude Era backstage fight in WWE, stating that Ahmed Johnson was fortunate to avoid a broken arm. According to the former Nation of Domination member, D'Lo Brown trapped Johnson in such a severe submission hold that he and Ron Simmons had to intervene to stop the escalation.

Speaking in an interview, The Godfather discussed the notorious altercation between Johnson and Brown. He recalled Johnson getting angry backstage and making threats while speaking with unclear words, leading Brown to challenge him. He emphasized that Brown possessed tremendous toughness and physical strength, allowing him to take Johnson down after Johnson attempted to hit him with a soda can.

The Godfather added that Brown locked Johnson in a dangerous position that could have fractured his arm. This prompted him and Ron Simmons to urge Brown to release Johnson immediately, fearing that Brown would get fired because Vince McMahon had high confidence in Johnson and was heavily pushing him at the time. Meanwhile, Johnson has repeatedly denied this version of events in a past interview, questioning why such a serious incident did not leak when it happened. This leaves two contrasting accounts of what transpired inside the WWE locker room.
