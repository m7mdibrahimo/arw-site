WWE star Rey Fénix recently opened up about the severe physical toll his wrestling career has taken on his body, revealing that a serious right hip injury remains the worst he has ever suffered. During a discussion on his YouTube channel, Fénix detailed how a risky dive to the outside resulted in a bad landing that severely damaged the cartilage in his right hip and leg junction. Although the injury is technically behind him, the muscles in the area remain extremely sensitive, forcing him to stretch every morning and night to prevent atrophy and maintain normal mobility. Fénix explained that the recovery process was brutal, involving MRIs, X-rays, and painful platelet-rich plasma (PRP) injections directly into his hip joint despite local anesthesia. Today, that demanding maintenance routine is a permanent part of his daily life, gym sessions, and wrestling training to ensure the tightness and pain never return.
