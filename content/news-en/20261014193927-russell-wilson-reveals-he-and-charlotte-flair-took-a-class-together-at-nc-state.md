Russell Wilson and Charlotte Flair share a connection from their time at North Carolina State University. During a recent guest appearance on the Set, Hut! podcast, hosted by Russell Wilson and Kyle Long, WWE star Charlotte Flair joined the program. As the discussion progressed, Wilson shared that he and Flair were classmates during their studies at NC State. Flair is currently active on the WWE SmackDown brand, where she is aligned with Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley, the reigning WWE Women's Tag Team Champions.
