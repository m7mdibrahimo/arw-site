Former WWE star Shotzi Blackheart has opened up about her transition to the independent scene and her recent arrival in TNA following her release from WWE in May of last year. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, Blackheart explained why she immediately wanted to step out of her comfort zone by competing in a grueling death match for her first indie appearance, facing GCW's death match champion and going through glass while bleeding freely to show fans she is entirely unleashed. During the interview, ECW original Bully Ray jokingly asked if she would face him if he came out of retirement. Bully Ray retired alongside D-Von Dudley after their match against the Hardys at TNA Bound for Glory 2025. Blackheart enthusiastically accepted the hypothetical challenge, stating she would gladly step into the ring with the WWE Hall of Famer even if he lit a table on fire and power bombed her through it.
