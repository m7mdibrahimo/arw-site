WWE NXT star Lexis King has elevated his feud with Zilla Fatu by releasing a diss track titled Zilla Vanilla on X. The music video features an ice cream truck and dancers, but the lyrics take personal shots at Fatu. King mocks his opponent's tattoos, his past six-year prison sentence, and his interactions with women, even referencing an incident with Arianna Grace.

The track also brings up their family lineages, comparing King's late father, Brian Pillman, to Fatu's late father, Umaga. King boasts about injuring Fatu's hand during a recent attack with steel steps, which followed weeks of conflict after Fatu turned down an offer to join King's faction, BirthRight. Fatu had previously defeated King in a singles match on October 6 despite interference.

According to Ringside News, the rivalry began on the September 1 episode of NXT when Fatu rejected BirthRight and superkicked King. Fatu has not yet responded to the newly released song.
