Indi Hartwell has officially confirmed that she will miss TNA Destination X, resulting in the cancellation of her Knockouts World Championship defense against Shayna Baszler. Hartwell spent the weekend attempting to travel to Edmonton, but she admitted that despite assistance from several people within TNA, she was unable to secure a flight due to the nationwide FAA flight shutdown.

Addressing her absence on X, Hartwell sent a playful message to her challenger:

> "Good luck trying to break these arms and take this title today @qosbaszler! Because despite efforts from myself and multiple people at TNA, I cannot make it to Edmonton for today's Destination X, to defend my KO title. I'll be watching closely, though 🏆👀❌"

Reports previously indicated that Hartwell and Xia Brookside were unlikely to reach Edmonton, and TNA officials have discussed moving the title match to the November 19 episode of TNA iMPACT. The Knockouts Ultimate X match has also undergone changes, with Shayna Baszler, Heather by Elegance, and Kiera Hogan replacing the original participants. Additionally, Trey Miguel is struggling to reach Edmonton for his TNA World Championship match against Leon Slater, leading the promotion to prepare a backup main event for the show at the Edmonton EXPO Centre.
