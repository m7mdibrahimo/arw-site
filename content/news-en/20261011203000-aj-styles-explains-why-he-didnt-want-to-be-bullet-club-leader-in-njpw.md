During a recent appearance on Cody Rhodes' podcast, AJ Styles discussed his time in NJPW and explained why he never wanted to be considered the leader of Bullet Club. Styles stated that he never claimed the leadership role because he wanted the group to be about everyone rather than just one individual. He explained that their philosophy was that no one follows anyone, which helped keep all members important. Styles noted that this unique dynamic and the members enjoying themselves made the group popular in Japan. Bullet Club was quietly disbanded at the beginning of 2026, with remaining members moving on to other opportunities or joining Yota Tsuji's new stable.
