WWE Survivor Series 2026 is scheduled for Saturday, November 28, at Daikin Park in Houston, Texas, featuring four major high-stakes matches on the card. Updated betting odds from BetOnline show significant shifts for several of the company's top stars ahead of the event.

In the men's WarGames match, The 946 enters as a -250 favorite against The Bloodline, who sit at +170. The 946 secured the WarGames advantage after Carmelo Hayes and Cruz Montana defeated The Usos on WWE Raw. The event will also feature an appearance by The Rock, though his potential involvement in the match remains unconfirmed.

In title and team action, Women's World Champion Liv Morgan is a heavy -500 favorite to retain her title against Becky Lynch at +300. The team of Charlotte Flair, Stephanie Vaquer, Alexa Bliss, Bayley, and Tatum Paxley holds a -500 advantage over Jade Cargill, Michin, and Fatal Influence (+300). Meanwhile, the traditional Survivor Series elimination match between Team USA (Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, Je'Von Evans, and LA Knight) and Team Mexico (Rey Mysterio, Penta, Rey Fenix, and Dragon Lee) stands at even -120 odds for both sides.
