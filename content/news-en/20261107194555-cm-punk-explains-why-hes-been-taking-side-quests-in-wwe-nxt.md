CM Punk has explained why he has been taking unexpected appearances and working outside his usual schedule, noting that he is actively chasing new goals and challenges. Speaking on the WWE Crown Jewel Countdown show, Punk stated that he is not waiting around for Roman Reigns to return to television. Punk noted that he has been heading down to WWE NXT, working AAA shows, and taking on extra matches whenever possible to stay sharp.

One of those appearances brought Punk back to a WWE NXT ring on November 4 for the first time in over sixteen years, where he challenged Grayson Waller for the NXT Championship following Waller's controversial win over Tony D’Angelo at Halloween Havoc. Punk came close to winning the title before Kevin Owens interfered, distracting him and allowing Waller to secure the victory with a rollup.

Despite the loss, Punk confirmed that his approach remains unchanged. He views these appearances as valuable opportunities to conquer new challenges and keep his skills sharp rather than waiting for his next main roster storyline.
