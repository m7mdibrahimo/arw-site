Paul Wight is putting in significant effort to revive his Captain Insano character. The persona, which originated in the film The Waterboy, has become a staple of Wight's career, with the wrestler incorporating elements of the character into his work for years. Since joining AEW, Wight has brought Captain Insano to the screen on multiple occasions, including segments involving Adam Cole and MJF. In a recent interview with Going Ringside, Wight emphasized that he remains dedicated to the character. "Captain (Insano) is a lot of fun. We’re still working on that, actually. I have a lot of fun with it. Of course, that character was iconic in the movie The Waterboy. I’m going through a lot of hard work to bring that character out, to present that character in its best possible format," Wight stated. During the same conversation, Wight also discussed the future potential of Kyle Fletcher in professional wrestling.
