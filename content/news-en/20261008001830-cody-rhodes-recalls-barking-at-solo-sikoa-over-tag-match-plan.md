Cody Rhodes shared a humorous backstage story about Solo Sikoa attempting to plan a tag team match that was never actually going to take place, according to Fightful.

During an interview on Flagrant, Rhodes discussed the April 3, 2023, episode of WWE Raw, where he and Brock Lesnar were scheduled to face Roman Reigns and Solo Sikoa. The contest ultimately never happened because Lesnar launched a brutal attack on Rhodes before the opening bell.

Rhodes explained that Sikoa was still relatively new at the time and was unaware that high-profile matches are sometimes kept under wraps even from the participants. Sikoa was deeply nervous backstage, wondering how to approach a unique competitor like Lesnar in the ring, according to Fightful.

> "Great story where we did a match with me and Brock Lesnar vs. Roman and Solo. The match was never going to happen. We were going to do a big angle at the end, so there wasn't going to be a match but Solo was new. He didn't know the nuance of sometimes maybe the high level of the game where people are not saying things for a reason. There's producers who don't even know what we’re about to pull, this may be a limited number of people that need to know. I thought on his side of things, he would've been privy. He’s planning to do a full tag match with me and Brock. Brock is a unique animal to approach, he doesn't even lock up. So, how do you get in there with Brock? What do you do, do you just run up and kick him? Do you run up and let him clothesline you, what do you do? He’s backstage very nervous, which he's never nervous anymore because he's really assumed the locker room role now. But, he's backstage and I walked right by him, I didn't realize what I did. He goes, ‘Hey, what are we doing out there?’ I turn around and [bark], then I keep going. He, at the beginning of this match that doesn't happen, is basically getting ready to jump us, not aware of what's about to happen because he thought that's the only way you could do it with a guy like this, you jump on him and get what you can and then he'll take back over. It was a classic case of maybe the bark almost went the other wrong way. He should've got the information."

Rhodes noted that Sikoa was ready to jump them because he lacked the necessary information about the segment, leading to the bizarre and comical interaction, according to Fightful.
