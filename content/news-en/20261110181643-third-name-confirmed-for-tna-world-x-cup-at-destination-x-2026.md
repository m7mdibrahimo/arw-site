TNA Wrestling announced that The Home Town Man will join the TNA World X-Cup tournament at Destination X 2026. The event takes place on November 15 at the Edmonton EXPO Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, and will air live on pay-per-view and TNA+.

The Home Town Man joins TNA International Champion Jason Hotch and Rey Horus as the third confirmed competitor in the eight-wrestler tournament. The tournament will feature two Fatal Four-Way matches, with the winners facing each other in the finals later that night, leaving five spots still open.

Destination X will also feature Leon Slater defending the TNA World Championship against Trey Miguel, and Indi Hartwell challenging Shayna Baszler for the Knockouts World Championship. Additionally, men's and women's Ultimate X matches are scheduled, along with Matt Hardy and Moose facing Joseph Sawyer and Samuel Shaw of Syx.
