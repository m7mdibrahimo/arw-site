WWE Hall of Famer D-Von Dudley revealed that he is nearing retirement because ongoing airline delays and airport headaches have made constant travel exhausting. He explained that while he still enjoys meeting fans at events, the travel fatigue has him seriously considering stepping away and pursuing opportunities in Hollywood.

Dudley also shared that during a recent airport encounter with WWE star Damian Priest, Priest confirmed that active roster members face the exact same struggles with grueling travel schedules and persistent transportation issues.
