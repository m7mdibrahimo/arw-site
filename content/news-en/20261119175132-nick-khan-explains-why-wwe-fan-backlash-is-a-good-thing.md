WWE President Nick Khan addressed the vocal nature of the company's fanbase during an appearance on Bloomberg's The Deal podcast at the Screentime conference in Los Angeles. Speaking with hosts Jason Kelly and Alex Rodriguez, Khan stated that he does not view fan frustration as a negative, but rather as a positive sign of their deep investment in the product.

Khan compared the WWE audience to fans of major sports teams like the New York Yankees or Los Angeles Lakers, noting that supporters often speak about their teams with a sense of personal ownership. He explained that hearing from fans when things are not going well is part of the business, as it shows they care.

Regarding the company's distribution, Khan emphasized the need for simple communication, citing how WWE wrestlers created videos to guide fans through the process of accessing programs on ESPN. He reaffirmed that maintaining the core fanbase remains the company's top priority, pointing to the decision to bring SummerSlam to Minnesota as a way to serve a historically significant wrestling region.
