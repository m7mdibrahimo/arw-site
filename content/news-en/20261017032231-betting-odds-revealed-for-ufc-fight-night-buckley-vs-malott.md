Betting odds have been released for the upcoming UFC Fight Night event scheduled for Saturday, October 16, at Rogers Place in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. The card is set to stream live on Paramount+, featuring key bouts across multiple divisions.

In the welterweight main event, Mike Malott opens as a slight favorite at -133 against Joaquin Buckley, who sits at +104. The women's flyweight matchup sees Erin Blanchfield heavily favored at -350 against Jasmine Jasudavicius (+250). Other notable lines include Cristian Perez (-360) favored over Kyle Nelson (+260), and Kyle Daukaus (-208) favored against Marc-Andre Barriault (+160).

Additional bouts on the slate feature Louis Jourdain (-188) against Timmy Cuamba (+146), Mandel Nallo (-300) facing Nate Landwehr (+222), and heavy favorite Nick Galanti (-725) competing against Julien Leblanc (+450).
