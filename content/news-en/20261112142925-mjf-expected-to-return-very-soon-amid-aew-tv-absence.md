MJF could be returning to AEW television very soon. According to Sean Ross Sapp on The Hump, the former AEW World Champion is nearing his comeback after more than two months away, and the company already has a top-level program planned for him. MJF has not appeared on AEW programming since the AEW All In event on August 30.

Despite his television absence, MJF recently made a special guest referee appearance at Create A Pro Wrestling Academy on October 25, where he humorously tucked his Dynamite Diamond Ring into his pants during his entrance. He previously teased that his return depends on waiting for the right moment, making it clear that he intends to immediately target the AEW World Championship and become the first undisputed world champion in company history.
