WWE announced the lineup for the November 20 episode of WWE SmackDown. Charlotte Flair will face Jade Cargill in a WarGames advantage match. Flair will compete for her team alongside Stephanie Vaquer, Alexa Bliss, Tatum Paxley, and Bayley at Survivor Series WarGames, while Cargill will represent her squad with Michin and Fatal Influence (Jacy Jayne, Fallon Henley, and Lainey Reid). Additionally, Trick Williams is set to face Damian Priest in a non-title match, and Bakusai (Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki) will take on the War Raiders (Erik and Ivar). WWE SmackDown airs tonight.
