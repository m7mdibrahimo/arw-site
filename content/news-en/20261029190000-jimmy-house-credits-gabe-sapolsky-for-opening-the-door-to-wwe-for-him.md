Jimmy House discussed his path to joining the WWE ID program, revealing that he began contacting WWE years before finally securing a tryout. After earning his black belt in jiu-jitsu, House reached out to WWE's recruitment department and eventually moved to Texas to start from the bottom on the independent circuit.

Following a year on the independents, House received the call to prepare for a WWE tryout. He spent eight weeks training intensely and felt completely prepared when he arrived. House expressed confidence that he stood out during the tryout despite obstacles like his age, height, and past knee injuries.

House gave strong credit to Gabe Sapolsky for making the opportunity possible, stating that Sapolsky was the first person to open the WWE door for him through one-on-one consultation calls.
