The blood-drinking spot at AEW Full Gear upset many fans, while reports indicate that some individuals inside AEW were unhappy with it as well. Press remarks confirmed that the criticism was not limited to social media, but included genuine objections from people working within the promotion.

The report explained that a group of individuals inside the company questioned the nature of what Swerve Strickland and the other participants did in the spot, along with their stance on it.

Former referee Jimmy Korderas sharply criticized the Texas Death match, pointing out that the excessive use of weapons and blood across four different matches during the event stripped those moments of their impact. He added that the blood-drinking spot became the primary talking point for the audience, overshadowing the rest of the matches.

The question remains whether these internal criticisms will alter AEW's policy in dealing with such spots during upcoming shows.
