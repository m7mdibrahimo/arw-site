Cody Rhodes recently shared how John Cena keeps him grounded with his unique brand of mentorship. After Cena praised Rhodes as WWE's "QB1" and suggested he was performing the role better than anyone before, he quickly shifted gears. Just days later, Cena told Rhodes that their match at WrestleMania 41 in Las Vegas "stunk the joint out."

Rhodes admitted that the initial praise brought him to tears, but he appreciates Cena's ability to keep him from getting too comfortable. "That's what the great mentors do," Rhodes said. "They never can give you the full [praise], and they shouldn't. I'm mean to mine."

Cena shared his own perspective on the match during a recent interview with Uncrowned. While he celebrated winning his record-setting 17th world championship that night, he was blunt about the quality of the match itself. "I took a big swing at WrestleMania and we stunk up the joint. We failed," Cena admitted. However, he noted that the experience taught them how to course-correct, leading to a much more satisfying conclusion to their story at SummerSlam.
