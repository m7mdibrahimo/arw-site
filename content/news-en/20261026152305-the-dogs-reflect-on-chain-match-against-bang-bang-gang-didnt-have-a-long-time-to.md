At AEW Redemption, the Dogs consisting of David Finlay and Clark Connors faced the Bang Bang Gang featuring Jay White and Juice Robinson in a double chain match. Although the Dogs won, the match received a negative reception from fans. Appearing on What Happened When, the duo discussed the encounter and the lack of preparation time. Finlay and Connors noted that they wanted to approach the stipulation with an old-school mindset, ensuring they did not overshadow other violent bouts on the card. Connors highlighted a specific top-rope bump onto steel steps that he felt was missed due to a camera cut. Following the event, the Dogs and the Bang Bang Gang have formed an alliance under the name the Cut.
