Zilla Fatu revealed that he came close to crying during his emotional encounter with John Cena on the November 4 episode of WWE NXT, when Cena spoke about his late father, Umaga. Speaking on Busted Open After Dark, Fatu admitted that he has struggled with keeping his emotions bottled up throughout his life. Reflecting on the moment, he noted that he wishes he had simply allowed himself to express his feelings. Cena had personally invited him into the John Cena Classic before discussing his connection to the Anoa'i family. Fatu recalled that seeing Cena interact with Umaga years earlier made their recent reunion feel deeply meaningful.
