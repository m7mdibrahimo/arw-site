Current WWE NXT Champion Grayson Waller recently shared that he feared for his job earlier this year when several performers left the company. During an appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, he explained that the departures of other stars made him feel that his own tenure might be coming to an end.

> "I could feel it. Especially when The New Day left, I could feel it."

Waller noted that he was sidelined for a month due to an injury sustained during a match against Oba Femi. He realized his position was precarious but refused to wait for his potential release.

> "I was like, 'Not a great position to be in, and I'm smart enough to know.'"

> "That would upset me, if I didn't do something."

Instead of giving up, he returned to NXT and won the championship in August. Since then, he has successfully defended the title against EK Prosper, Tony D'Angelo, and CM Punk.
