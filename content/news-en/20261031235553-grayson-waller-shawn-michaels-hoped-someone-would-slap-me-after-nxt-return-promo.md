Grayson Waller returned to WWE NXT in late July after being off television for several months, with his last major appearance being a loss to Oba Femi on WWE Raw in April. In his return promo, Waller expressed his frustration with his lack of usage and called out the men's roster. Speaking to Stephanie McMahon on "What's Your Story," Waller shared the details behind the segment. "I told Shawn what I was going to say, just to make sure and cover my bases so it doesn't go off the chain too much. What I said to Shawn was, 'When I come backstage, I hope one of these kids comes up to me and says something.' Shawn said, 'I hope one of these kids slaps you across the face.' He wasn't joking. He wanted someone to hit me. No one did. No one came up to me or said anything. I came backstage to silence, awkward silence. I know people said things, but no one said it to me. Some of the kids have taken it on board and I can see the change in them," Waller said. He added that he believes the promo challenged the roster to step up. Waller is scheduled to face Tony D'Angelo for the NXT Championship at NXT Halloween Havoc.
