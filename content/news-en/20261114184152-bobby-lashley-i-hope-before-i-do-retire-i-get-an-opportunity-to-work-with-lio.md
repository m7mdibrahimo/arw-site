Bobby Lashley recently spoke about Lio Rush, expressing his hope to work with him again before he retires. Lashley and Rush previously spent a substantial amount of time paired together in WWE. Although they are currently on the same roster in AEW, they have not worked together in the company outside of a backstage segment.

During an interview with MuscleManMalcolm, Lashley shared his high praise for Rush, stating, "He is one of my favorite people in the world. You know how talented he is? Everything he does, everything he touches is gold." Lashley added, "He could do anything in this business, and I hope before I do retire, I get an opportunity to work with him again."
