Max Caster appeared at TNA Bound for Glory to answer Moose's open challenge, but he was not the company's first choice for the spot. According to False Finish, TNA originally considered Sheamus for the appearance. "Before Max Caster accepted Moose’s open challenge, sources indicate to False Finish that Sheamus (Stephen Farrelly) was the primary name that TNA was considering for the spot at #TNA Bound for Glory." This revelation adds context to Caster's debut, which became controversial after he rapped, "I drop bombs, you don’t even have to fly to Riyadh," a day after an attack at King Khalid International Airport. TNA confirmed they did not approve the lyric, subsequently removed the footage from social media, and reportedly ended their working relationship with Caster. It remains unclear why the deal with Sheamus did not materialize, but it is evident that Caster was a backup plan that resulted in an immediate controversy.
