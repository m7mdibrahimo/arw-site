Independent wrestler Big Ben Bishop is mocking fans who are spending their money on the newly released Grand Theft Auto VI. Bishop took to social media to claim he is too busy earning real money in the wrestling business to play video games. He then revealed that his upcoming payday for a weekend battle royal is just $20.

Bishop posted an Instagram video making fun of the game's release, asking if "GTA" was a new wrestling company. He told gamers to go play their "little video games" while he focuses on his "grown man business." The video was intended as a joke about the financial realities of independent wrestling.

This is not the first time Bishop has used comedy to poke fun at the industry; he has previously mocked the controversy surrounding CM Punk at WrestleMania 42 and Will Ospreay's comments. For now, while millions of gamers explore the new release, Bishop continues to chase his $20 battle royal payday.
