Real American Freestyle, the promotion founded in 2025 by WWE Hall of Famer Eric Bischoff and the late Hulk Hogan, announced a major expansion on Thursday. The promotion revealed on social media that it has acquired World of Wrestling, bringing the Tulsa Nationals, Reno Worlds, and the Kickoff Classic under its banner. These events are widely considered among the most significant tournaments for young, amateur wrestlers in the United States. "If you grew up wrestling, you know what these tournaments mean," the promotion stated in its announcement. "The road trips with your family. The nerves before your first match. The trophy you still have. The Roller family built something that generations of wrestlers carry with them. We're excited to work alongside them and build on what makes these tournaments so special." While the future of these specific tournaments under the RAF banner remains to be detailed, the promotion is preparing for its next event in Abu Dhabi on November 14, featuring a championship match between Khamzat Chimaev and Colby Covington. Bischoff currently serves as the chief media officer for RAF, which held its inaugural event in Cleveland in 2025.
