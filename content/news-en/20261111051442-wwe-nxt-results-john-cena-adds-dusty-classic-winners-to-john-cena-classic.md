On the November 10, 2026, episode of WWE NXT, live from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, the show opened with Grayson Waller discussing his recent victory over CM Punk. Shiloh Hill interrupted him, leading to a confrontation that was broken up by the return of Mason Rook, who attacked Waller. John Cena appeared backstage to officially invite 2026 Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic winners Romeo Moreno and Noam Dar, as well as NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan, to participate in The John Cena Classic.

In the ring, EK Prosper defeated Kam Hendrix in a singles match. Izzi Dame secured a victory over Wren Sinclair, after which Women's North American Champion Zaria attacked Dame with a spear and an F5. Lizzy Rain defeated Chantel Monroe in another singles matchup.

In the main event, NXT Tag Team Champions Vanity Project successfully defended their titles against Hank Walker and Tank Ledger. The finish occurred after Romeo Moreno and Noam Dar appeared at ringside, distracting the challengers and allowing Vanity Project to retain. Following the match, Zilla Fatu's promo was interrupted by a brawl involving DarkState, BirthRight, and Los Perros del Mal, which required security to intervene as the show concluded.

Next week's "NXT" will feature Kelani Jordan defending the NXT Women's Championship against Kendal Grey, along with a match between Tony D'Angelo and Kam Hendrix.
