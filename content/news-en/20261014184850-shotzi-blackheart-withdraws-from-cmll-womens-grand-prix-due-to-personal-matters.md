Shotzi Blackheart has withdrawn from her upcoming tour with CMLL for the Women's Grand Prix due to a personal family matter. In a statement shared on social media, she revealed that she recently lost her brother Dean. She expressed her gratitude to CMLL and the fans for their understanding while she takes time to be with her family. Prior to this, Blackheart recently made an appearance for TNA Wrestling during their post-Bound for Glory television tapings.
