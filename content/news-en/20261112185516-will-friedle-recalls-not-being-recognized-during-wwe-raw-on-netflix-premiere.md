Will Friedle may be a familiar face from Boy Meets World, but he is rarely recognized in public. During a recent appearance on the Zach Sang Show, the actor shared how his co-star Danielle Fishel regularly steals the spotlight whenever they travel together, often leaving him to take pictures for her fans.

Friedle explained that the same situation played out during the debut episode of WWE Raw on Netflix. While sitting in the front row with Fishel, the broadcast graphic identified her by name while leaving Friedle completely uncredited as he waved to the camera.

> "I never get recognized. I mean, to a comical point. We talk about it on the show — one of the things we do is wherever we’re traveling together, I always take the pictures of Danielle with the fan. So, they'll go, ‘Will you take a picture of us together?’ I go, ‘Sure, no problem, I take a picture.’"

> "Later that week, we went to the opening of the WWE on Netflix. We had the greatest seats ever. They're like, ‘We’d like you to come to the front row.’ So, they'll cut to the thing and there is Danielle and I like this and underneath it says Danielle Fishell. Under me, nothing. I'm just looking and [waving] like this."
