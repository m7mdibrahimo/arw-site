The October 24 episode of AEW Collision featured a new challenge for a Women's Blood and Guts match. During the event, Divine Dominion, consisting of Megan Bayne and Lena Kross, faced Wrathlander, featuring Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron. The match ended in a disqualification following interference from Sisters of Sin, as Julia Hart and Skye Blue attacked Statlander and Cameron.

Willow Nightingale arrived to even the odds. Later, in a backstage segment, Nightingale and Wrathlander expressed their desire to challenge Divine Dominion and Triangle of Madness to a Blood and Guts match, with The Brawling Birds also seeking to join the fray. AEW has not yet officially confirmed the contest.
