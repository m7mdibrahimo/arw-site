Karmen Petrovic revealed that she was first contacted about joining the Perros del Mal faction in November 2025, according to Fightful. The group made its official return under WWE's presentation of Lucha Libre AAA in June 2026, originally consisting of Daga, Petrovic, Bronco Nima, Angel, and Berto before Nima was later kicked out.

While speaking to the Toronto Sun, Petrovic explained that management initially planned an earlier debut but wanted to find the perfect timing given the stable's rich legacy in Mexico.

> "I was approached, I believe it was sometime last year actually. It was around maybe November time of last year. So they actually had plans to debut us a lot earlier but it was just about understanding when the best timeframe would be to debut, especially this faction that has so much history in Mexico and the legacy and everything around it so they wanted to make sure it was perfect."

She added that waiting was difficult, but she was thrilled about performing in front of Mexican fans for the first time.
