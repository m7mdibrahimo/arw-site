AEW World Champion Will Ospreay recently discussed how watching WCW cruiserweights on television and practicing on a backyard trampoline shaped his high-flying wrestling style. Speaking with WKMG News 6 ahead of his successful title defense at AEW WrestleDream 2026, Ospreay explained that his mother installed a trampoline in the garden to keep him safe after he started getting into trouble. He recalled studying the movements and maneuvers of the WCW cruiserweights before attempting them outside. Ospreay joked about how testing those moves in his backyard must have sounded to the neighbors. Reflecting on his career, he noted that a neck surgery in September 2025 required him to adjust his style, though those modifications did not stop him from capturing the AEW World Championship at Wembley Stadium during AEW All In London 2026.
