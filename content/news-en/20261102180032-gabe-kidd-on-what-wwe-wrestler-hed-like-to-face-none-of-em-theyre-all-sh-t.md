Gabe Kidd was recently asked which WWE wrestler he would like to face in the ring, with the Death Riders member offering a blunt dismissal of the idea. Known for never holding back his thoughts on social media, Kidd took a direct shot at AEW's main competition during a new Instagram Q and A session while waiting for a layover flight. When asked about who he would want to wrestle in WWE, Kidd emphatically rejected the possibility, stating, "None of 'em, they're all shit."

In recent All Elite Wrestling events, Kidd's former Dogs teammates, David Finlay and Clark Connors, turned on him to align with The Bang Bang Gang and form 'The Cut.'
