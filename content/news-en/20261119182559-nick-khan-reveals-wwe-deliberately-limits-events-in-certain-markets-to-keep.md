Nick Khan confirmed that WWE intentionally regulates the frequency of events in specific markets to prevent fan saturation, which helps maintain high demand for their shows. During the Bloomberg The Deal podcast at the Screentime conference in Los Angeles, Khan explained that the company balances profitability with preserving its core fan base. He highlighted the selection of Minnesota for SummerSlam as a decision made to honor the region's wrestling history, even though other cities might have generated more revenue. Khan also expressed surprise at the continued growth in interest for live events, noting that the live entertainment sector remains resilient against technological advancements.
