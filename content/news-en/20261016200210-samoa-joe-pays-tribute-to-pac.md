Samoa Joe recently shared his thoughts on the passing of PAC, remembering his remarkable international journey and the impact he left behind. In an interview with Sports Illustrated, Joe reflected on his late friend, stating that it is difficult to return to work without him around.

> "Ben was a guy that came up with a lot of us in that who we watched just have this meteoric rise internationally, probably from one of the most improbable places at the time when you think about the scene where there was actually people were getting exposure. But he was that good." Joe also praised Satterley as a wonderful human being whose talent was recognized everywhere he went.

> "If anything for us it's just it's it's just a big empty hole. We really miss having him around. We miss having an excuse to see him every week is kind of what we say. It's tough going back to work and not seeing him there." Meanwhile, Samoa Joe is preparing to compete in the Clockwork Carnage match at AEW WrestleDream.
