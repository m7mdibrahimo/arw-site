Former WWE Superstar Ruby Soho recently surprised fans by sharing photos of a dramatic new look on Instagram. After spending more than two years away from AEW television following her pregnancy leave in February 2024, Soho resurfaced with jet-black curls, bangs, and noticeable facial changes from a photo shoot with Shayne Culp. Although she was medically cleared to return in June 2026, AEW has not confirmed any creative plans or whether this makeover signals an upcoming comeback.
