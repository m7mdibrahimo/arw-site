Jim Ross has successfully undergone his latest brain shunt surgery following a last-minute postponement. Doctors initially delayed the procedure due to encouraging CT scan results, but plans changed on November 11, 2026, and Ross went through with the operation. Conrad Thompson, who co-hosts the Grilling JR podcast, provided regular updates on the situation. Thompson confirmed on X that Ross had made it through the procedure without any issues. "JR is out of surgery and everything went well! Praise God!" The operation comes after a challenging few months for the 74-year-old wrestling legend, who initially had a shunt inserted in August to help with balance and memory issues before facing complications and a chest infection. Ross now faces a recovery period with the goal of returning to the commentary desk for AEW when his health allows.
