Boxer and former kickboxer and MMA fighter Tenshin Nasukawa is set to compete in a match for DDT Pro-Wrestling. Team Tenshin is holding a festival on October 31st at Matsudo Central Park in Chiba, Japan, featuring a collaboration with DDT Pro-Wrestling that will include a street pro-wrestling match. Nasukawa will team up with Yuki Ueno and Shinya Aoki to face Danshoku Dieno, Takeshi Masada, and To-y.
