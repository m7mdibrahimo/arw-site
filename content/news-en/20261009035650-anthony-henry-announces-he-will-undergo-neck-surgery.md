Anthony Henry has announced that he will be undergoing neck surgery. He shared the update on X, explaining that he has been inactive due to his health. Henry stated that the procedure is necessary because a herniated disc is pressing on his spinal cord, which he described as a frightening situation. He expects the recovery period to last between three and six months. Henry previously underwent surgery in August 2026 to address lingering issues from a 2025 bicep injury.

> I haven’t been active on here, but I at least wanted to give any fans of myself or the #Workhorsemen an update. My neck is kinda screwed. I will need surgery. The herniated disc is on the spinal cord itself, which makes this even more scary. Recovery is anywhere from 3-6 months.
