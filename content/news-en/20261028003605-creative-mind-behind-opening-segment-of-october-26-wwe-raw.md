Paul Heyman played a major role in shaping the opening segment of the October 26 WWE RAW. According to a backstage report, Heyman pitched the entire sequence involving Sami Zayn, Je'Von Evans, Bron Breakker, and Bronson Reed, though he did not write the promos or produce the segments himself. Zayn opened the show by addressing Adam Pearce and various WWE stars before being interrupted by Evans, which led to an impromptu Undisputed WWE Championship match where Zayn successfully defended his title. The segment escalated further when Breakker attacked Evans, followed by interventions from Penta and Reed, leaving Evans and Penta laid out. The report notes that Heyman exerted significant influence over the creative direction of the overall show.
