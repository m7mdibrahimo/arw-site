Ricochet is drawing inspiration from The Joker for his persona in AEW. Since joining the promotion in 2024, Ricochet has primarily performed as a heel. He confirmed that the chaotic nature of his current character is intentional. In an interview with Sports Illustrated, the former AEW National Champion stated that he looks to The Joker for creative direction.

> "The Joker, who obviously thrives in chaos, is somebody I take a lot of inspiration from. Because Ricochet in AEW just likes to cause chaos. Whether that leads to something or not, he just wants to cause chaos. So that’s something I connect with… There’s a method behind his madness, and that’s also something I pull from."

Ricochet last wrestled on October 17, when he competed in the Clockwork Carnage match at AEW WrestleDream.
