Kofi stated that The New Level arriving at AEW All In was an open secret online. Earlier this year, The New Level members Kofi and Austin Creed appeared for All Elite Wrestling, joining forces with Swerve Strickland to secure the Trios Championships.

During a recent appearance on The Mick and Kenny show, Kofi explained that official contracts were only signed under 24 hours before the event began, noting that their success manifested a long-held vision to compete against top tag teams.

He added that they achieved a historic milestone by becoming the first talent to capture a title on their opening night in AEW, a feat never accomplished before.
