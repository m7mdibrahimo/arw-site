Kevin Nash believes there should be consequences for certain administration officials following the midterm elections, naming three individuals he wants to see removed from office. Speaking on the latest episode of Kliq This, the WWE Hall of Famer stated that Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, FBI Director Kash Patel, and Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. need to be impeached and removed from their positions. Nash explained that while he does not want Democrats to focus on a political revenge tour against Donald Trump, he feels strongly about holding these specific officials accountable, particularly pointing to Kennedy's record on vaccines and the importance of restoring trust in institutions like the CDC. Regarding Hegseth, Nash noted that he was likely heading toward serious trouble in Congress before the recess, and pointed out that Republican support already exists for pursuing impeachment articles against him.
