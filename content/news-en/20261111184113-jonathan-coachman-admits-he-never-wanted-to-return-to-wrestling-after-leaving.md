Jonathan Coachman recently discussed his past determination to leave professional wrestling behind after departing WWE for a position at ESPN. During an appearance on Boots To Boots with Deonna Purrazzo and Steve Maclin, Coachman explained that he aimed to establish himself as a legitimate sports broadcaster, though his history in wrestling continued to follow him.

> "I will say this, that when I went to ESPN, I said I will never do wrestling again, and I meant it. I meant it. But when you are in the public eye all the time, and you're always on shows, or I'm doing SportsCenter, I'm doing I had my own radio show for a long time at ESPN, those sound bites live, or at least they live in people's heads."

Career setbacks at CBS and the Professional Fighters League forced him to reconsider his future. Encouraged by friends, he eventually launched a podcast with Vince Russo. Coachman now recognizes that relying on traditional networks is risky, and he has shifted his stance, admitting he would consider new opportunities with WWE or AEW.
