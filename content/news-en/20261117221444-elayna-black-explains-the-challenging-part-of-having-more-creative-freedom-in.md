Elayna Black, who joined TNA Wrestling in January 2026 following her WWE release, discussed the differences in creative freedom between the two promotions. In an interview on The Wrestling Classic's Casual Conversations, Black noted that while WWE's Performance Center allowed for some input, the final vision rested with the company. "It's been great. It has been a little bit challenging. Being at WWE and the PC, you do have some input and ideas, but you don't get the final say," she explained. In TNA, she finds the process more collaborative. "In TNA, it's kind of up to me and who I want Elayna Black to be. Obviously, they have their vision for their storylines and the show, but they are more giving in that I can take Elayna Black and make it who I want her to be and just create," she said. Black admitted that this level of autonomy requires more personal effort, stating, "I'm like, 'Oh shit, I have to think about it and put more effort into the character,' which is challenging, but I'm having fun with it and I'm grateful for it because it makes it even more me." She added that she values the trust the company has placed in her to shape her character.
