Penta, the winner of the men's Money in the Bank ladder match, recently shared his strategy regarding his championship contract. During an interview on WWE Now, the Mexican star stated he is not rushing to use the briefcase, preferring to rest and scout his potential opponents first.

> "But not yet. Not too soon."

> "This is a big surprise because I was thinking something when I climbed to the top and the ladder, this is the real key for [when] I will become a world champion, right? Now I decide when, I decide where, and I decide against who."

He did not rule out challenging Roman Reigns again, noting his strong performance against the champion in his home country. Penta also warned all titleholders on WWE Raw to stay alert.
