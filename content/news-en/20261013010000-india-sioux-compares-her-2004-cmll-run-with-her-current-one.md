India Sioux has reflected on her career journey within CMLL, comparing her initial stint with the promotion in 2004 to her return sixteen years later in 2024. Sioux, who currently holds the CMLL-Japan Women’s Title, noted how the sport has evolved over the last two decades. She observed that wrestling was previously more intense and grounded in classic styles, whereas the modern landscape favors more aerial and flashy action. Sioux also highlighted her role in the early efforts to establish a women's division within the World Wrestling Council. She emphasized that those foundational efforts paved the way for current opportunities, including the recent increase in high-profile matches for women on the card. Sioux also shared that winning the CMLL National Women's Title holds significant personal value to her as she continues her career.
