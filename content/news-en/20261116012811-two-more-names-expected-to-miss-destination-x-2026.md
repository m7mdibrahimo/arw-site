TNA Wrestling may be missing another championship match at Destination X, as the status of the Tag Team Champions remains uncertain. Nic Nemeth and Ryan Nemeth were pulled from their scheduled post-show meet-and-greets in Edmonton, casting doubt on their participation in the pay-per-view. The Nemeth Brothers were slated to defend the TNA World Tag Team Championship in a three-way match against Matt Hardy, Moose, and Syx. TNA previously announced that Matt Hardy had arrived in Edmonton, but the removal of the champions from their appearances suggests they may not reach the event. TNA has not confirmed if the title defense will proceed or provided an update on the champions' status. These complications are part of a broader travel disruption that has forced TNA to revise several matches at Destination X. A nationwide flight shutdown on November 13 left numerous wrestlers stranded, with ongoing cancellations impacting the weekend schedule. Indi Hartwell has already confirmed she cannot attend to defend her Knockouts World Championship against Shayna Baszler, and both Ultimate X matches have been significantly altered. Destination X is scheduled for Sunday, November 15, at the Edmonton EXPO Centre.
