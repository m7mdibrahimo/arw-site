TNA Wrestling has announced that Amir Jordan will represent Pakistan in the World X-Cup at TNA Destination X on November 15. Jordan is the third competitor announced for the tournament, joining Rey Horus, Home Town Man, and Jason Hotch. The World X-Cup features two four-way matches, with the winners meeting in the finals.
