Tony Khan has shared his thoughts on the recent AEW run of Austin Creed and Kofi, highlighting his experience collaborating with them. Since joining AEW, the duo has presented a different side of their characters, which was on full display at AEW Full Gear during their brutal Texas Deathmatch against Brodido and Adam Page. The match, which saw the pair successfully defend the AEW Trios Championship alongside Swerve Strickland, was praised by Khan for its intensity and the team's active contributions.

"I really enjoyed the match [Trios title match at Full Gear], it was very hard-hitting, it was certainly a team effort, and they were very involved in it [Kofi and Creed]," Khan stated. "And in terms of the ideas, they were very active and they had great ideas. I've been very, very involved with them since they came, and it was a big change."

Khan expressed his satisfaction with the partnership, noting how well the former WWE stars have integrated into the AEW roster. "And then over the last few months, I've really enjoyed getting to know Austin and Kofi," Khan added. "I've really enjoyed collaborating with them. I've worked with Swerve for years, and it's the first time I've worked with Austin and Kofi, but I really enjoy it. And they have completely different experiences than I do, and I love talking to them, and I think it's great for everybody to be able to contribute and brainstorm, and so far they've been a great fit in AEW." The AEW CEO concluded by highlighting the success of the Texas Deathmatch and his happiness with the current reign of The New Level as Trios Champions.
