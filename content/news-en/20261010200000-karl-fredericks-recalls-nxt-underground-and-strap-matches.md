Karl Fredericks, known as Eddy Thorpe during his time in WWE, recently reflected on his experiences competing in stipulation matches within NXT. According to Fightful, Fredericks participated in three NXT Underground bouts and two strap matches throughout his tenure with the brand. He initially maintained an undefeated streak in Underground contests, securing victories over Dijak and Damon Kemp before Trick Williams ended that run on the March 18, 2025, episode of NXT.

Fredericks expressed disappointment regarding the short duration of his match against Williams, noting he wished they had more time to perform. He also discussed his background in Muay Thai, which he learned under Katsuyori Shibata at the NJPW Dojo, and noted that his first Underground match against Kemp relied heavily on improvisation.

Reflecting on his 2023 feud with Dijak, Fredericks described the program as deeply personal due to its cultural themes. He recalled that having his family in the front row for their strap match added an emotional layer to the performance, particularly given the historical context of the stipulation. He ultimately praised Dijak as a professional and a great talent to work with.
