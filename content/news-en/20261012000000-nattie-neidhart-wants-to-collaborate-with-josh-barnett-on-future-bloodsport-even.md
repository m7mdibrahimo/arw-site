Since making her debut as a fighter in Josh Barnett's hybrid Bloodsport event in April 2025, Nattie Neidhart has developed a significant passion for the discipline. Having competed in three events and maintaining an undefeated 3-0 record, Nattie is now interested in partnering with Barnett to revive the unique concept, which she credits for helping her evolve in her professional wrestling career. During an interview with "Allenownz Wrasslin," Nattie shared her vision for a potential collaboration.

> "Well, I would love to partner with Josh in, like, bringing back Bloodsport. I'm so putting Josh Barnett on the spot. Imagine if we did Nattie and Josh's Bloodsport, and we brought it back and we did something really cool for it. I'm trying to get Josh out of retirement... I have so much respect for Josh. He's a huge catalyst in the Nattie character."

Following WrestleMania 42, Barnett announced that Bloodsport would be taking an indefinite hiatus due to the challenges of finding a consistent venue. While he has paused the project, Barnett has stated he is not willing to hand the brand over to anyone else.
