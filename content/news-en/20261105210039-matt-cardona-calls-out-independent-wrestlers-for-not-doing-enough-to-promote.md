Matt Cardona recently shared his thoughts on the state of independent wrestling, questioning why more performers are not taking advantage of opportunities to make money and promote their appearances. Writing on social media on November 5, 2026, Cardona addressed concerns about the indie scene, noting that while he has not worked an independent show in a year, trusted colleagues assure him the business is far from dead. He urged independent wrestlers to embrace live social shopping to boost their income and better connect with fans. Cardona also criticized wrestlers who fail to use free social media advertising to promote their bookings, arguing that performers must treat their matches like a big deal if they expect fans to care.
