Drew McIntyre made a stop at Pro Wrestling EVE in London, sharing a video on his social media channels of his arrival at the arena and watching the matches up close.

> "It was incredible to get to an independent show during my time in London and just remember why I love this so much. The lassies killed it"

He added that he missed the independent environment and its enthusiastic fans. The Scottish wrestler stated that he deeply misses these vibes and looks forward to returning in the future, noting that everyone wrestled as if they were at WrestleMania because wrestling is their life.

Drew McIntyre has been off television since WrestleMania 41.
