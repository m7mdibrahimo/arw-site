Royce Keys, leader of the 946 faction in WWE, believes his group is destined for major success and can reach the status of legendary groups like D-Generation X, the Four Horsemen, and The Bloodline. Keys made his WWE debut at the start of the year and formed the faction in August, recently adding Cruz Montana as its newest member.

In an interview with The Sportstar, the former AEW star expressed confidence in the group's trajectory, noting that their unique backgrounds set them apart from other stables. Keys stated that unlike factions such as The Bloodline, members of the 946 were not born into the wrestling business, and each member brings a distinct element to the group. He specifically highlighted Carmelo Hayes showing a more aggressive side and Jaida Parker bringing impressive physical strength.
