Royce Keys, leader of the faction known as The 946, recently appeared on Busted Open Radio to discuss the group's recent expansion. The stable, which includes Lucien Price and Bronco Nima, recently added Jaida Parker and, most recently, Carmelo Hayes on an episode of WWE RAW. Keys shared his thoughts on welcoming Hayes to the group.

> "That my dog, you know, way, way before I even set foot in the door. He's a great addition. He's got a chip on his shoulder like we all do. There's more to come... More to come with the destruction and chaos. People are genuinely afraid of us, so we like that."

Hayes made his presence felt on RAW by participating in a beatdown of The Usos and Solo Sikoa. Keys, who has been a prominent figure since his SummerSlam involvement with LA Knight, expressed that his current run on the main roster is a dream come true. He remains focused on staying prepared for whatever the company asks of him.

> "You know, you gotta always stay ready. When they call the play for you, you may only get one shot at it. I'm ready to run any play they throw at me."
