Josh Alexander opened up about participating in the emotional tribute show dedicated to Pac on AEW Dynamite and sharing the ring with Bryan Danielson. Danielson made his in-ring return during the September 30 episode of Dynamite, rejoining the Death Riders for a main event match against the Don Callis Family. Alexander revealed that Danielson personally informed him about the match during the afternoon prior to the event.

> "I mean, it was Wednesday afternoon. Bryan actually approached me. He said hi, and he's like, 'Did you hear about the match?' I go, 'No.' And he goes, 'It's Death Riders against DCF.' And I was like, 'Okay.' And in my head I was like, I knew Mox was wrestling already and I was just like, 'Who's the other guy?' And he's like, 'Oh, it's me.' And I just moved away and then I went, 'Whoa, what? Did you say you're in the match?' He's like, 'Yeah.' And I went, 'Okay.' And he just starts laughing."

Alexander described the experience as bittersweet, noting that the locker room was mourning both Pac and Andy Williams. He felt that performing on the tribute show provided a cathartic release for everyone involved while honoring Pac's memory.

> "It's like cathartic for us to go out and do what we love to do and that we know that the person that passed away loved to do and try to honor them."

The recent losses of his colleagues also served as a reminder for Alexander to cherish his loved ones and express his appreciation to them backstage.
