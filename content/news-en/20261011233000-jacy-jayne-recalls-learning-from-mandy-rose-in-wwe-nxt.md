Jacy Jayne recently discussed the mentorship she received from Mandy Rose during their time together in the Toxic Attraction faction on WWE NXT. Jayne credited Rose for helping her gain confidence and teaching her how to be authentic both on and off the screen.

> "I learned how to be comfortable in my own skin," Jayne said. "I learned that playing a character, per se, is not really going to play the way you think it is on screen. You more so have to find who you are and apply that to everything, in each situation. Mandy, honestly, really taught me that, because there is no one who is more confident and comfortable in their own skin than Mandy."

Jayne noted that Rose was incredibly kind backstage, contrary to her on-screen persona. Toxic Attraction, which also included Gigi Dolin, rose to prominence in 2021. Jayne admitted it took her time to feel fully comfortable, eventually finding her stride during the group's second title reign. Following Rose's departure and the eventual split of Toxic Attraction, Jayne went on to lead her own stable, Fatal Influence, on WWE SmackDown, while achieving individual success as a multiple-time champion.
