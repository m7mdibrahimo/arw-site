Kofi Kingston and Austin Creed missed the October 21 episode of AEW Dynamite because they were attending Big E's wedding to Kris Yim. According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, the two members of The New Level traveled to celebrate with their longtime friend and former New Day partner. Their absence was noticeable on television as Swerve Strickland appeared without them during a backstage segment with AEW World Trios Champions "Hangman" Adam Page and Brodido. Strickland demanded a rematch under Texas Death rules after losing the titles, but Page rejected the stipulation, noting that Swerve's motivation went beyond the championships.
