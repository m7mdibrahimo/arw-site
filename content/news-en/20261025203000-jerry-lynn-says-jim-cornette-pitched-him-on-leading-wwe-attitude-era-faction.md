Jerry Lynn recalled that Jim Cornette once pitched him to lead a memorable faction in WWE during the Attitude Era. Speaking on "What Happened When", Lynn explained that Cornette wanted to bring him in to lead The Brood before Gangrel was chosen. "Jim was trying to bring me in — it's funny — he was trying to bring me into WWF to be in The Brood before they brought in Gangrel, but I was too small," Lynn said. Ultimately, Gangrel led the group alongside Edge and Christian, while Lynn went on to wrestle for ECW, TNA, and Ring of Honor before retiring from the ring in 2013 and starting a new career as a coach and producer in AEW.
