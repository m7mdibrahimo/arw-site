Amazing Red is motivated by Leon Slater after his impressive victory at TNA Bound for Glory. Slater shocked the wrestling world by defeating Nic Nemeth to capture the TNA World Championship during the event. While Slater relinquished the X-Division Championship to secure his world title opportunity, Amazing Red went on to win the vacant title later in the show.

Speaking with The Wrestling Classic, Amazing Red addressed the possibility of pursuing the TNA World Heavyweight Championship next. "Again, it was something that was not foreseen in my league, but I saw Leon capture it and brought it, I would be like this [waves], hey, I would like to be next for sure," he said. He praised Slater's remarkable achievements at such a young age, adding, "He motivates me to get better and to do better. Just seeing someone like that win the title, it makes me feel like, you know what, I could do that too. I'm gonna try that too. But, we’ll go step by step, little by little. X-Division title, like I said, it's a hard thing to hold on to right now."
