A federal judge has rejected the latest attempt by WWE, TKO, Vince McMahon, and Linda McMahon to dismiss the long-standing ring boy lawsuit. U.S. District Judge James K. Bredar handed down the decision, allowing the seven former ring boys to move their case toward the discovery phase.

The defense had argued that applying Maryland's Child Victims Act to allegations dating back about four decades violated their rights under the U.S. Constitution, especially since several key figures, including Mel Phillips, Pat Patterson, and Terry Garvin, have passed away. However, the judge found that states hold broad authority in setting legal deadlines and that the defendants failed to prove the law was arbitrary or irrational.

While this ruling is not a verdict on the merits of the allegations, which the defendants strongly deny, it clears the path for both sides to begin exchanging evidence. A teleconference is scheduled for November 17 to set future deadlines, while a potential trial remains several months away.
