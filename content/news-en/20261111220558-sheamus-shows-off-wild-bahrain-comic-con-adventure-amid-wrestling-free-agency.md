Sheamus is currently enjoying his time away from the ring while his wrestling future remains uncertain. The former WWE star recently traveled to the Middle East to appear at Bahrain Comic Con 2026, where he shared several highlights from the event on X. During his visit, Sheamus spent time meeting fans and even playfully put one attendee into position for his "White Noise" finisher, though he stopped short of actually delivering the move on the convention floor. Beyond his interactions with fans, the Celtic Warrior also tried his hand at go-kart racing and documented his experience trying a massive burger, which he described as "incredible."

Sheamus became a free agent when his WWE contract expired at the beginning of October. While there has been speculation linking him to both AEW and TNA, nothing has been confirmed. When recently asked about his potential interest in joining AEW, Sheamus indicated that he is simply focused on his time off. "I’m just enjoying my time off right now. Ask my wife, she’s the one making all the decisions," he stated. For now, the former WWE champion seems content to continue his travels and enjoy his hiatus before returning to full-time competition.
