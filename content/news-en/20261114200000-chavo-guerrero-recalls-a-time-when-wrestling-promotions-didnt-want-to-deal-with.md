Chavo Guerrero believes that agents play an important role in the professional wrestling industry. In a new interview with Sean Ross Sapp, Chavo Guerrero recalled that promotions previously looked down on wrestlers who used agents. He explained that lawyers write contracts rather than wrestlers, meaning talent needs legal representation to secure the best possible deals. Guerrero noted that agencies fight for better financial compensation, three-month non-compete clauses, travel expenses, and guaranteed downside pay. He added that the emergence of WCW forced WWE to introduce guaranteed downside contracts, which ultimately changed how wrestlers are treated.
