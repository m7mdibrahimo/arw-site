WWE producer Michael Hayes recently appeared on an episode of Stephanie McMahon's podcast, What's Your Story?, where he discussed his rocky relationship with former Chairman Vince McMahon. Hayes recalled that Vince initially hated him and swore he would never have a job in WWE. However, Hayes eventually became one of Vince's right-hand men, describing the achievement with strong language. Hayes noted that he earned Vince's trust through relentless hard work and dependability, always ensuring that tasks were completed without needing follow-up. Hayes is 67 years old and has been with the company since 1995.
