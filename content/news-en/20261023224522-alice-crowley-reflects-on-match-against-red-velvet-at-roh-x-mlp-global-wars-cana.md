Alice Crowley recently revisited her high-profile bout against Red Velvet, which took place at the ROH x MLP Global Wars Canada event in March 2026. The two wrestlers competed for the ROH Women's World Television Championship during the show. Speaking with Luis Pulido, Crowley expressed how significant the experience was for her career.

> That was probably one of the biggest matches I have ever had in my career thus far: that Red Velvet match, and it is probably one of my best matches that I've had, and it's a match that I take pride in for sure. If you guys haven't watched that match, please go back and watch that match cause it's really good, but yeah, I was very, very happy to be put in that position and to be put in that position very early on into working with Maple Leaf; that was not very far into- I wanna say it was maybe my second match working for Scott Dore. Before I got put in this title match, it was probably one of the best achievements of my career thus far, for sure, for sure, even though I didn't win the title, even though that doesn't matter; it's just the fact that I was able to be able to have a title match like that on a platform like that.

Crowley noted that the opportunity came quite early in her tenure with Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling. Although she did not secure the championship, she considers the platform and the quality of the match to be major milestones. Looking ahead, Crowley is scheduled to compete in the 2026 CMLL Women's Grand Prix.
