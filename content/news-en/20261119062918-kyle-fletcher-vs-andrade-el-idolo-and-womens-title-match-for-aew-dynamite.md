AEW announced new matches for the Dynamite Thanksgiving Eve episode on November 25. The card features a singles bout between Kyle Fletcher and Andrade El Idolo, arranged following their verbal confrontation during Andrade's segment on this week's program. Additionally, the event includes the inaugural title defense for the AEW Women's Tag Team Champions, Harley Cameron and Kris Statlander, who will face Divine Dominion. The promotion is expected to reveal further matches and segments for the show in the coming days.
