Reports have provided an update regarding the status of former WWE and STARDOM star Kairi as AEW Full Gear approaches. Kairi has been a free agent since her second departure from WWE in April. Despite receiving interest from various promotions, she has not signed a contract with any organization.

Kairi made her first appearance following her WWE exit during Chris Jericho's Rock N' Wrestling Rager cruise on November 10. According to Fightful Select, she met with Tony Khan while the cruise hosted the most recent episode of AEW Collision. However, she has not signed an official contract and does not currently possess a work visa for the United States.

The same report noted that Sheamus, who became a free agent after his WWE contract expired on October 1, has not been spotted backstage at AEW Full Gear. This comes despite significant speculation regarding his potential move to AEW.
