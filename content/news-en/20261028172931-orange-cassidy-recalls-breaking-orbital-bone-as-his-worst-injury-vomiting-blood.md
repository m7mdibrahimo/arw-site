Orange Cassidy recently opened up about the worst injury he has suffered during his career in professional wrestling, pointing to a severe facial fracture. While speaking on The Rizzuto Show, Cassidy recalled breaking his orbital bone and the unsettling aftermath of the accident. He explained that rupturing his sinus caused blood to flow down his throat and into his stomach, leading to severe nausea. Cassidy noted that after stepping away backstage at a small venue, a friend captured video footage of him vomiting. Describing the gruesome scene, he compared the blood to the famous film The Exorcist because it was shooting straight out after coming up from his stomach, all resulting from taking a knee directly to the face.
