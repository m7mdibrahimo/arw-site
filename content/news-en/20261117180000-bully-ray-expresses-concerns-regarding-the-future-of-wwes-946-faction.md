WWE legend Bully Ray has raised questions about the direction of the 946 faction, demanding clarity on their current storyline. Speaking on Busted Open ahead of their upcoming WarGames match against The Bloodline at Survivor Series, Ray noted that the additions of Cruz Montana and Carmelo Hayes to the group remain unexplained, a point previously raised by Nic Nemeth.

> "We still don't have a why on Carmelo and Cruz. The why has never been answered. I'm still giving you the benefit of the doubt, WWE and The 946, but please tell me why."

Reports indicate Montana's inclusion was unexpected, as WWE NXT reportedly had different plans for him. Ray urged WWE to provide answers rather than treating the audience like they are unintelligent. He also expressed concern that the faction might exist solely to lose to The Bloodline, questioning if there are long-term plans for members like Royce Keys and Cruz Montana following the event.
