TNA star Nic Nemeth is content with his single lifestyle and attributes his current relationship status to his deep commitment to professional wrestling. In an interview with TV Insider, Nemeth explained that he made a conscious decision years ago to prioritize his career over building a family. While he acknowledged that his parents occasionally pressure him to settle down, he remains focused on his professional goals.

> "Occasionally, there’s some pressure there, but I never felt like I wanted to because I really wanted to dedicate myself to wrestling. I chose that."

Nemeth noted that dating is often challenging because potential partners struggle to understand the intensity of his work schedule. He admitted that while many people initially claim they value their own careers, they often become frustrated when they realize how much of his life revolves around the ring. Despite this, he is not seeking to change his routine, as he currently feels he is performing at a high level and wants to maximize his time as a viable world champion before his body forces him to slow down.
