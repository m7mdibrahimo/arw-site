Nia Jax recently discussed how it can sometimes take longer than expected to fully understand a character in professional wrestling. Speaking on the Battleground podcast, Jax noted that it took her about a decade to truly grasp her persona in the ring. "I always, when we talk about it backstage, I tell girls like sometimes it takes like a full 10 years to understand your character and know who you are," Jax said. She added that upon returning in 2023, she had a clear understanding of her identity and what she brings to the company.
