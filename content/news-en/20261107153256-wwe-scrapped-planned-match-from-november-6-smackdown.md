WWE reversed course on a singles match scheduled for the November 6 episode of Friday Night SmackDown, as Rey Fenix was originally slated to wrestle Ricky Saints prior to a last-minute change. According to PWInsider, the company planned the matchup during the SmackDown taping on Monday, November 2, in New York City, but the plans were dropped on the day of the taping without any official explanation.

The broadcast also featured multiple adjustments to the running order compared to the live arena experience. The future of the bout between Rey Fenix and Ricky Saints remains uncertain regarding whether WWE will reschedule it or drop the idea completely.
