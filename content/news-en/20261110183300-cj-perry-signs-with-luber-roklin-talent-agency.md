CJ Perry has signed with the Los Angeles-based talent agency Luber Roklin. Perry, also known as Lana, is currently making media appearances to promote her book 'Bulletproof Angel.' During an interview with Sean Ross Sapp of Fightful, she revealed her new representation and discussed her future plans. "I can focus on producing movies. Right now, I’m with — I just signed with Luber Roklin management company and we’re working on a lot of different projects, trying to find the right projects for me to produce, and get attached to and so that’s the next venture, and that also takes time," Perry said. In addition to her new agency signing and book promotion, Perry and her partner Miro are expecting their first child together, with their baby boy due in April 2027.
