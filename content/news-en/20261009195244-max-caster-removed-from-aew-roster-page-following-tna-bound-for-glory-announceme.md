Max Caster has been officially removed from the AEW online roster page following recent developments regarding his contract status. Reports earlier this month indicated that both Caster and his longtime tag team partner Anthony Bowens had reached free agent status.

The removal comes shortly after Caster revealed he will face Moose at the TNA Bound For Glory event. It remains unclear whether Caster has signed an exclusive contract with TNA or is working under a short-term agreement. Meanwhile, Bowens is still listed on the AEW roster page as speculation continues surrounding the future of The Acclaimed.
