Drew McIntyre recently opened up about his ongoing animosity with CM Punk, claiming that the star has become much better at hiding his true personality backstage. Speaking on Raw Talk, McIntyre made it clear that his issues with Punk extend far beyond WWE television and that he avoids him whenever possible.

McIntyre stated that Punk plays a corporate role now because he knows he has nowhere else to go and this is his last chance in wrestling. He added, "He’s better at hiding it now than he used to be. Like, back in the day, everybody couldn’t stand him. He was so full of himself." Despite their genuine dislike for each other, McIntyre acknowledged that their real-life tension translates into great television when they work together.
