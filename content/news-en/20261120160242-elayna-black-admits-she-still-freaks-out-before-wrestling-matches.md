Elayna Black revealed in a recent interview that she still experiences intense anxiety and panic before wrestling matches, despite having years of experience in the ring. Speaking to the Daily Star, the former WWE star explained that she has developed a specific routine to help calm her nerves before performing. She typically spends the day before and the day of a show working out, listening to music, and watching old wrestling promos to get into the right mindset.

However, all that preparation does not stop her from feeling overwhelmed once she arrives at the venue. "And then I get there and just freak out until it's time to go out, and then I'm good," Black said. She previously opened up about suffering panic attacks following her WWE departure, which eventually led her to step away from the sport before making her return to TNA in January 2026.
