A new missile attack in Riyadh on November 8, occurring just one day after WWE Crown Jewel 2026, has raised concerns regarding the safety of the company's personnel. Despite immediate worries, PWInsider reported that the WWE roster had already departed the Saudi Arabian capital before the incident took place.

> "For those who have asked about the news that there was another missile attack on Riyadh, Saudi Arabia today and were concerned, the WWE roster was long gone before the attack happened."

While some production staff may have remained in the city to dismantle the ring and equipment, there have been no reports of anyone associated with the company being harmed, according to the outlet. It was further revealed that most WWE talent did not fly directly into Riyadh, but instead traveled through Bahrain and took buses into Saudi Arabia. Although a few individuals used private planes, this was an exception rather than the standard procedure. WWE had previously implemented security adjustments for Crown Jewel, including moving the event indoors and handling parts of the production remotely.
