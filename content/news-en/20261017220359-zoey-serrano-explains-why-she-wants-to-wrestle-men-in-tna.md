Zoey Serrano discussed her experience with intergender wrestling during a recent Q&A session on her YouTube channel, explaining that she often felt more comfortable going all out against male opponents compared to female competitors. Serrano noted that some of the best matches of her career before joining WWE took place against men, highlighting her memorable bout against Matt Vandagriff at FSW as a personal favorite. Now competing in TNA, Serrano expressed her enthusiasm for potentially stepping back into the ring with male wrestlers, pointing out the promotion's history with intergender competition and her readiness to deliver high-energy matches.
