Shawn Michaels has discussed the signing of Zilla Fatu and what he brings to the table in WWE NXT. Michaels appeared on Busted Open to talk about Fatu's development in NXT, noting that while he had not seen much of Fatu before his arrival, commentator Booker T spoke highly of him. Michaels explained that although there were some initial issues to navigate, the creative team wanted to give Fatu time to develop. He praised Fatu's potential, charisma, and background in the Samoan dynasty.

Michaels added that Fatu's passion and humor reflect the traits common throughout his family. He stated that he wants to help Fatu improve his promo skills and learn to handle multiple segments effectively.
