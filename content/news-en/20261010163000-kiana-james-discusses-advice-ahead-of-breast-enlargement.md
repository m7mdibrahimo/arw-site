During a recent appearance on the YouTube channel Ari and Emily, hosted by WWE star Ivy Nile and her husband Ari Levy, WWE star Kiana James discussed her experience and concerns ahead of undergoing a breast augmentation procedure. James explained that she specifically requested to have her implants placed over the muscle rather than under it to protect her upper body strength for her wrestling career. "Typically they like to put your implant under the muscle because then it looks more natural," James said. "It looks more natural because what you're seeing is the person's skin and flesh, and the fake part is like, way under. It just depends on how your body recovers, because they rip your muscle off the bone and put something underneath... I wanted mine over. I was like, 'Just slide it in there, don't mess with my muscle.' They're like, 'Well, it could look a little bit more artificial.' But I was like, 'Don't touch my muscle.' Three weeks after surgery, I was doing push-ups, pull-ups." James previously spent over a year away from the ring due to a leg injury following her draft to WWE RAW in April 2024, eventually returning on WWE SmackDown in July 2025 alongside Giulia.
