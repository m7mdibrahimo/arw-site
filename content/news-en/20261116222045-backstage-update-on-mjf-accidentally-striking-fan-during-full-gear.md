Maxwell Jacob Friedman made his return at AEW Full Gear on Saturday after Will Ospreay successfully defended his AEW World Championship against Kyle Fletcher. Wearing a mask, MJF interrupted the post-celebration of the match.

While fleeing the ring, MJF leapt over the barricade and accidentally struck a fan in the front row in the face with his foot. AEW officials and Nigel McGuinness immediately approached her to check on her condition. According to Fightful Select, the fan is doing well, and although she felt some initial shock and minor pain, she laughed off the incident afterward.

MJF showed no sympathy, blaming the fan for standing in his path.

His legal representative Mark Sterling stated that he would not press charges against her.

MJF continues to claim he is the true World Champion, ignoring his loss to Kenny Omega, and is now targeting Ospreay. Tony Khan announced that MJF will appear on AEW Dynamite.
