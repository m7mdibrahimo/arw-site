Chad Gable recently opened up about suffering a serious shoulder injury during his match against El Hijo del Vikingo at Worlds Collide 2025. He explained that the setback happened right at the peak of his career, forcing him to undergo surgery while Ludwig Kaiser took over the El Grande Americano gimmick. Gable admitted that watching Kaiser succeed did not cause jealousy, but instead ignited a powerful competitive fire within him, ultimately leading to his Intercontinental Championship win at SummerSlam after returning as a babyface. He shared these thoughts during an appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet.
