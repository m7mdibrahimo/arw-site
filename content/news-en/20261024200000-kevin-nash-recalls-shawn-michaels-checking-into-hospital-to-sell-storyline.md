WWE Hall of Famer Kevin Nash recently recalled a memorable storyline involving Shawn Michaels, where the legendary wrestler was seemingly checked into a hospital to make the angle feel completely real. Speaking on his "Kliq This" podcast, Nash explained how he pitched the idea to ensure fans would believe Michaels was genuinely injured following an attack. The segment caused real concern among fans, with many calling the hospital to check on Michaels' condition. Nash emphasized that in professional wrestling, you can make things real, but you cannot make them half real.
