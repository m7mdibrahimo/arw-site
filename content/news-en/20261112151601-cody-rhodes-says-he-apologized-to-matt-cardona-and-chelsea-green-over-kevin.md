Cody Rhodes recently revealed that he had to apologize to Matt Cardona and Chelsea Green regarding comments made by Kevin Owens. During an episode of What Do You Want To Talk About? in June 2025, Owens joked that Cardona was calling himself the King of the Indies while constantly talking about a return to WWE. Cardona eventually returned to WWE in December 2025 and ribbed Cody about the remarks and the lack of defense from Cody during that podcast appearance. On a recent episode featuring Chelsea Green, Cody discussed past unaired episodes, including one with Green, and acknowledged the situation. "Of course you do because I had to apologize to you and Matt over the Kevin one," Cody said. Cardona is currently sidelined with an injury.
