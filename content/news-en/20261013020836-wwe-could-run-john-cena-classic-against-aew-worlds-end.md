John Cena has confirmed that the John Cena Classic is scheduled for December, though the exact date remains undecided. According to reports from WrestleVotes, WWE is considering two potential options for the event. While rumors previously suggested it might be part of WrestlePalooza, that is not expected to happen. 

> "I know that's the rumor out there, but I don't believe so."

Instead, WWE is weighing whether to run the tournament head-to-head against AEW Worlds End on December 19, or to hold a massive day on December 12 featuring WrestlePalooza in the morning and the John Cena Classic in the evening. 

> "We've been hearing some speculation that they would potentially run against Worlds End on December 19th. Also, potentially make December 12th a giant day where, in the morning, you can have WrestlePalooza, and once you get to 12 hours here in the US, have the John Cena Classic. A bit of double-dipping."

WWE has not made a final decision, but the tournament is being positioned as one of the year's major attractions regardless of the chosen date. 

> "Don't know which one of those will prevail, but the Cena Classic won't happen at WrestlePalooza."
