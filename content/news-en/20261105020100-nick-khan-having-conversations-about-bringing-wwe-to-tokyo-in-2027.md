WWE is looking to host events in Tokyo, Japan, in 2027. During the TKO third quarter 2026 financial call, TKO President Mark Shapiro discussed the company's efforts to explore new international markets for both UFC and WWE.

Shapiro highlighted positive discussions with Abu Dhabi regarding a UFC deal before addressing future plans for WWE. He concluded his remarks by stating:

> "I'm also pretty bullish about some conversations that Nick Khan is having about bringing WWE to Tokyo next year."

WWE programming began streaming on Netflix in Japan this past October, and the promotion last visited the country in October 2026.
