John Cena's retirement tour featured the legend crossing paths with stars like CM Punk, Brock Lesnar, and Randy Orton, while teaming with Rey Mysterio and Sheamus. He even had one last clash with AJ Styles. However, many fans felt the matchups were incomplete because he never faced rivals like Batista or Adam "Edge" Copeland.

Copeland recently appeared on the "The Mick and Kenny Show" podcast with Mick Foley, where he noted that a WWE return for the match would not have been an issue. "I know I was open to it," Copeland admitted. "I know Tony [Khan] was open to it."

The veteran previously commented on his and Khan's willingness to allow a one-off WWE appearance, stating it would have been up to WWE to pursue it. Copeland acknowledged the importance of his history with Cena but expressed doubt that WWE would have made it happen after his years in AEW. Cena has also spoken highly of his history with Copeland and Orton, describing them as his two greatest rivals.

Although the tribute match did not happen, Copeland honored Cena during the AEW All Out 2025 opener by hitting Cash Wheeler with both the "Five-Knuckle Shuffle" and the "Attitude Adjustment."
