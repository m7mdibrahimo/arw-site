Cody Rhodes has always respected those who paved the way in the wrestling industry. During a recent episode of the "What Happened When?" podcast, former ECW World Heavyweight Champion Jerry Lynn revealed that Rhodes frequently pressured him to return to the ring. Lynn shared, "I couldn't tell you how many times Cody asked me 'Come on, one more match.' I told him I would need a good divorce lawyer." Lynn noted that while he has been retired since 2013, he might consider a return if the offer were substantial enough to cover personal expenses like his wife's car. Lynn has made sporadic on-screen appearances for AEW, most notably in 2023 when he brought Rob Van Dam into the company to challenge Jack Perry for the FTW Championship.
