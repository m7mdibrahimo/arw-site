The United Empire has officially welcomed two new members to its ranks. During their entrance at AEW Collision Grand Slam: France, it was announced that the Brawling Birds, consisting of Jamie Hayter and Alex Windsor, have officially joined the stable. The AEW Women's Tag Team Champions displayed the signature crown gesture as they made their way to the ring. Previously, the Brawling Birds assisted Will Ospreay at AEW All Out during his match against Jon Moxley. Ospreay launched a fresh chapter for the stable after capturing the AEW World Title at AEW All In, with Mike Bailey serving as the first official addition to this new iteration.
