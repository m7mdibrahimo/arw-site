Rey Mysterio discussed the backstage details of his WWE arrival and how he was surprised to discover he could wear his mask in the ring again. During an appearance on the What's Your Story? With Steph McMahon podcast, he explained that someone asked about his gear before his debut, and he learned that Vince McMahon wanted him to put the mask back on after it was removed in WCW.

Mysterio expressed his great happiness about returning in the mask and noted his astonishment that fans accepted him despite his previous unmasking, which was a first in Lucha Libre history. He previously criticized WCW for removing wrestlers' masks without understanding their historical value to their legacy.
