Yota Tsuji successfully defended the IWGP Heavyweight Title by defeating Ryohei Oiwa in the main event at NJPW King of Pro-Wrestling. While Tsuji spoke to the fans after his victory, the lights went out and Kenny Omega appeared on video to criticize Tsuji for skipping Forbidden Door. Omega pointed out that the letter I in IWGP stands for international, but Tsuji refused to travel.

Omega stated he was ready to face Tsuji on his own terms, while Tsuji replied that Omega must travel to Japan for a match. Boltin Oleg stepped out to confront Tsuji, but Omega blindsided him from behind before dropping Tsuji with a V-Trigger and a One Winged Angel. Omega noted he would speak with NJPW President Hiroshi Tanahashi to arrange the bout.
