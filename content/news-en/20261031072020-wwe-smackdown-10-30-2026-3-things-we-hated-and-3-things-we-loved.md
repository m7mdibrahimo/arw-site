The October 30, 2026 episode of WWE SmackDown delivered a special Halloween-themed night filled with title defenses, surprises, and heated confrontations.

Things We Hated:
- The women's division segment fell flat, with a sluggish title match between Chelsea Green and Nikki Bella followed by repetitive promos from Liv Morgan and yet another Money in the Bank tease from Tiffany Stratton.
- Ricky Saints remains completely directionless on the main roster despite a solid showing against Rey Fenix for the AAA Cruiserweight Championship.
- The main event between Sami Zayn and Trick Williams turned messy, featuring a cheap disqualification, an impromptu restart, and excessive outside interference that overshadowed the wrestling.

Things We Loved:
- Cody Rhodes delivered a direct and serious promo, apologizing to Randy Orton's family in advance for the violence planned for their Last Man Standing match.
- Danhausen shined in his Halloween hosting role, leading to the shocking return of Finn Balor as The Demon to take down GUNTHER.
- WWE set up an exciting No Holds Barred collision between Kevin Owens and CM Punk for the following week, keeping their fierce rivalry at a boiling point.
