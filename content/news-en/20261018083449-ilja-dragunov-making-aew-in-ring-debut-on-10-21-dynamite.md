All Elite Wrestling officially confirmed the signing of Ilja Dragunov at the WrestleDream pay-per-view. Following the event, Dragunov appeared at the post-show media scrum alongside Renee Paquette and AEW President Tony Khan.

Khan and Dragunov agreed that his first in-ring match will take place on the 10/21 Dynamite at the Watsco Center in Coral Gables, Florida.
