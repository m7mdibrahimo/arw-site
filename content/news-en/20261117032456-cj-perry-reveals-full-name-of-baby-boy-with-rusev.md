CJ Perry has revealed the full name she and WWE star Rusev have chosen for their baby boy, confirming he will be called Bear Petrov Barnyashev. Appearing on The Joe Vulpis Podcast, Perry shared that Rusev made most of the decision and picked the first name a decade ago.

> "I'm 16 weeks pregnant. It's a boy, and we're naming him Bear Petrov Barnyashev. Very Bulgarian. Very strong Bulgarian name. Rusev pretty much picked it all."

The name features special family meaning, with Petrov honoring Rusev's father as the first grandchild in the family. Perry recalled that wanting a family was something she and Rusev bonded over since their early days together in WWE, and they are now preparing to welcome their first child.
