Rey Fénix is now the No. 1 contender for the Intercontinental Championship after winning a triple threat match against Chad Gable and Big Cass on WWE RAW. The contest saw intense back-and-forth action, with Cass dominating early with his power. Fénix secured the victory by hitting a Frog Splash on Gable to break up a pin attempt, pinning him for the win. Following the match, the group known as 946 assaulted all three competitors, leaving them lying in the ring. During the aftermath, Royce Keys warned Roman Reigns that this would be the fate of his family if he did not come out to acknowledge them.
