Ric Flair believes WWE's developmental system is failing young talent because wrestlers spend too much time training against each other instead of gaining real-world experience. Speaking on NewsNation, Flair compared WWE and AEW, noting that the main difference is how long the businesses have been around rather than a talent gap. He argued that WWE keeps its product in a PG rating while AEW allows more freedom, but pointed out that WWE's 120 developmental wrestlers do not get enough opportunities to work with different opponents in front of live crowds. Flair contrasted this with his own career, where he traveled across the country and wrestled legends like Bruiser Brody and Harley Race to learn the business.
