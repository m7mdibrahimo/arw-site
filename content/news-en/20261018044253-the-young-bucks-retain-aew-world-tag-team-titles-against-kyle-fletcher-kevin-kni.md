The Young Bucks successfully defended their AEW World Tag Team Titles at the 2026 WrestleDream event. Matt and Nick Jackson faced the Don Callis Family's Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight in a high-stakes championship match. This victory marked the second defense of their current title reign, which began following their ladder match win at All Out. During the contest, Don Callis interfered by choking Nick Jackson with a cord. Despite Fletcher building momentum with several moves, Knight tagged himself in only to be taken out by the champions. The Young Bucks eventually baited Knight into position, hit him with a B.T.E. Trigger, and secured the win to retain their championships.
