Ahead of his TNA World Championship defense at Bound for Glory, Nic Nemeth spoke about his upcoming challenger Leon Slater and his own storied career. Nemeth praised Slater's incredible talent and athleticism, acknowledging that the young star is ready for the main event scene right now rather than in the future. However, Nemeth expressed confidence in his own vast experience and physical conditioning after more than two decades in the industry. 

> "I'm 23 years in and I'm in better shape than most people who are 23 years old. So those reps have only made me better and I think that's a split second decision making to where I can assess a situation and boom, I got something to go. He has to hesitate and I think there's still a little bit of light behind his eyes, so he has a little glimmer, a little twinkle in there."

Nemeth explained that his decades of accumulated reps allow him to make instantaneous decisions inside the ring, whereas Slater might still hesitate in high-pressure moments. The champion also discussed his experiences defending the TNA World Championship outside of the promotion as he prepares for the high-profile pay-per-view clash on October 11.
