Ilja Dragunov has spoken about his transition from WWE to AEW, noting that the agreement was finalized shortly after his WWE contract ended. Speaking on "TMZ's Inside The Ring," Dragunov explained that the process was rapid once he became a free agent. "I can say it came together quite quickly after my contract expired. That's something that happened in very much of a fragment of a time," he said. Dragunov noted that he had been questioning his position within WWE for some time, even while enjoying his seven-year tenure with the company. He emphasized that he values his gut instincts and prefers to be honest about his career trajectory. "I felt the respect from the other side [AEW] pretty quickly. I was asking myself if I am at the right place [in WWE] for quite a while already... So, when I feel like something is not going in the direction that I wanted anymore, it doesn't feel like a place where I want to stick anymore," he added. Dragunov ultimately decided to seek new opportunities, leading to his debut at AEW WrestleDream.
