Nia Jax recently discussed how attending live events can convert non-fans into dedicated supporters of professional wrestling. According to Fightful, she made these comments during an interview on the Battleground podcast, highlighting the unmatched atmosphere created by the audience.

> "The electricity. I mean, you know, our wrestling fans are second to none. They come in and they're so excited. They're invested in all the characters. You know, if you were to come with somebody who's never watched wrestling before they're going to leave a fan because of how incredible our fans are. Like, they're just electric. So if I say that to anybody, I was like, oh, if you bring a friend who's not a wrestling fan to an actual show in person, they're going to leave a wrestling fan."
