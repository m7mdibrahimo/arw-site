Val Venis recently criticized Max Caster's rapping skills following Caster's abrupt departure from TNA after the Bound for Glory pay-per-view. Caster faced significant backlash over a controversial entrance rap referencing Riyadh during his debut match against Moose, leading TNA President Carlos Silva to cut ties with him. Taking to social media, Venis shared a video of himself rapping with fans, declaring that Caster lacks rapping ability and demonstrating his own style by incorporating classic wrestling references and his Big Valbowski persona.
