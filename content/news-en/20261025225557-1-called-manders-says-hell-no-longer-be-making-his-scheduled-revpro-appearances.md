1 Called Manders has canceled his scheduled RevPro appearances due to an injury. According to the wrestler, he suffered an injury while competing in a match in Boston on October 24, resulting in a head laceration that prevents him from performing at the RevPro show on October 25. Manders shared an update on social media, expressing his disappointment about missing the dates in England and stating that he needs time to recover and step away from social media for a while. Fightful wishes Manders the best as he recovers.
