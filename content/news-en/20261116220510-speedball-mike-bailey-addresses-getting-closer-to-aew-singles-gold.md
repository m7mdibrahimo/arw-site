Speedball Mike Bailey discussed moving ever closer to his goal of winning singles gold in All Elite Wrestling following Full Gear. Despite competing for almost every singles title the company has to offer throughout his run, Bailey has yet to capture a singles championship in the promotion. 

Most recently, Bailey challenged Tommaso Ciampa for the AEW National Championship on Dynamite, but came up short. 

While speaking on his latest vlog, Bailey addressed feeling like he is getting closer to his goal of becoming a singles champion in AEW.

> "What an absolutely incredible, long, and arduous week. A lot has happened. Lots of travel. Lots of excitement, and I am reaching and moving forward, ever closer to my goal of singles gold in AEW. After a fantastic full gear. I am excited. I am pumped. I am motivated, and that's perfect because next week we're in Denver for the start of the most exciting season of the year. The Continental Classic."
