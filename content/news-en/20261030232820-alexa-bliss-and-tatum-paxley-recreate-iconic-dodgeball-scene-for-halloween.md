Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley decided to get into the Halloween spirit by recreating a memorable scene from the 2004 comedy Dodgeball: A True Underdog Story. Bliss shared a video on Instagram showing herself and Paxley dressed as characters from the film. Paxley held a dodgeball and delivered the movie's famous line, "If you can dodge a wrench, you can dodge a ball," before launching the ball directly at the cameraman, who went crashing down to complete the recreation.
