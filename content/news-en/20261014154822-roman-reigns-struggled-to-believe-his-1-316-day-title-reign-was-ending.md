Roman Reigns admitted that it was difficult for him to believe his historic 1,316-day championship reign was actually coming to an end when Cody Rhodes defeated him at WrestleMania 40. Reflecting during a recent interview, the Tribal Chief explained that holding the title for nearly four years had simply become a comfortable part of his daily life and routine. Rather than focusing on matches or championship defenses, Reigns highlighted his favorite memories as the quiet moments behind the scenes with his family. He recalled sitting in the Gorilla Position with Jimmy Uso, Jey Uso and Solo Sikoa before an entrance rehearsal, valuing the chance to share his career with the people he grew up with. Ultimately, Reigns remains deeply grateful for the journey, the opportunity to represent his family legacy, and the unforgettable experiences shared with The Bloodline throughout his historic run on top.
