Shuji Kondo captured the GHC200 Championship at Pro Wrestling NOAH event Wrestle Odyssey II on October 25th, according to Fightful. The unique title matches last only 200 seconds, with HAYATA defending his belt for the second time against Kondo. Near the end of the bout, Kondo knocked HAYATA off the apron, causing him to suffer a knee injury that prevented him from standing. HAYATA was counted out, making Kondo the new champion and only the second wrestler to hold the belt.
