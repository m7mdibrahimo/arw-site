Mini Vikingo was not medically cleared to compete on the October 20th episode of WWE NXT on The CW. The show was scheduled to feature a six-man tag team match between Vanity Project (Jackson Drake, Ricky Smokes, and Brad Baylor) and the trio of E.K. Prosper, Mini Vikingo, and Chazz 'Starboy' Hall. This match was booked following a backstage altercation between the two sides, stemming from Vanity Project eliminating Vikingo and Hall from the Dusty Classic tournament. During the broadcast, NXT General Manager Robert Stone announced that Vikingo could not participate. Despite this, Mini Vikingo is still scheduled to compete at AAA's Héroes Inmortales on October 24th, where he will challenge Rey Fenix for the AAA World Cruiserweight Championship.
