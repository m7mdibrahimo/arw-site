WWE NXT star Shiloh Hill revealed that former World Heavyweight Champion GUNTHER is his dream opponent for WrestleMania. Hill is currently feuding with Naraku on WWE NXT. Speaking on Busted Open After Dark, Hill discussed his interaction with GUNTHER when the latter appeared on WWE LFG. Hill said, "When he came to 'LFG,' even, being able to interact with him and being around the presence that he carries. I think he brings a certain amount of legitimacy to the ring. I want to go in there and show I can mix it up with one of the best, so he would be the one I would love to do it with." WWE LFG coach Bully Ray noted that GUNTHER's presence completely changed the energy of the room, a sentiment echoed by Hill.
