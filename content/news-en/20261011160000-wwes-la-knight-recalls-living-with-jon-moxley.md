LA Knight recently looked back on his early days in the professional wrestling industry, recalling the time he shared a one-bedroom apartment with Jon Moxley. The two stars lived together in Ohio when they started out, eventually moving into a two-bedroom apartment after sleeping on a small futon couch in the living room at first. Knight noted that they had many great and wild nights together, though many memories remain private. Both wrestlers have achieved massive success since those early days, with Knight challenging for world titles in WWE and Moxley holding championship gold in AEW.
