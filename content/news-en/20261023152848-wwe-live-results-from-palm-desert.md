WWE hosted a Road To Survivor Series live show on October 22 at the Acrisure Arena in Palm Desert, California, according to Fightful. Trick Williams def. Damian Priest for the United States Championship. Chelsea Green, Alexa Bliss, and Tatum Paxley def. Jade Cargill, Michin, and B-Fab. MFTs (Tama Tonga and Talla Tonga) def. Bakusai (Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki) for the WWE Tag Team Championships. Cody Rhodes def. Randy Orton in a Street Fight. Rey Fenix def. Ricky Saints for the AAA Cruiserweight Championship. LA Knight, Solo Sikoa, and Oba Femi def. The Vision (Bronson Reed, Austin Theory, and Bron Breakker). CM Punk def. Sami Zayn via DQ for the Undisputed WWE Championship.
