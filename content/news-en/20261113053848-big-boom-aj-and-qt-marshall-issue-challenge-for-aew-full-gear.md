Big Boom AJ and QT Marshall are heading to AEW Full Gear. On the November 12 episode of ROH TV, AJ and QT challenged Trent Beretta and Rocky Romero to a match at the upcoming event.

Rather than a standard tag team bout, they told RPG Vice to bring some companions while they do the same. AJ noted that Big Justice and The Rizzler will accompany them in their corner.

Boom & Doom previously defeated RPG Vice in a tag team encounter at AEW Full Gear 2025. Additionally, Big Boom AJ remains undefeated inside an AEW ring, according to Fightful.
