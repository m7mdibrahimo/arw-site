WWE is preparing for its Crown Jewel Premium Live Event, which takes place on November 7 at the ANB Arena in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. According to reports, a significant portion of the broadcast will be managed remotely from the company's headquarters in Stamford, Connecticut, where production and satellite trucks will be stationed.

While a sizeable production crew will still be present in Saudi Arabia, the total number of staff traveling will be lower than usual. On-site personnel will handle the setup of cameras, sound equipment, and other technical elements, transmitting the signals to the United States. This allows the production team at headquarters to manage the broadcast as if they were on-site. Additionally, WWE offered $3,000 in hazard pay to crew members traveling to the event.
