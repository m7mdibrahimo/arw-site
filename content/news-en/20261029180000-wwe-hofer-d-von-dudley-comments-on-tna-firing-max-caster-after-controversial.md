WWE Hall of Famer D-Von Dudley recently addressed the firing of Max Caster from TNA Wrestling following a controversial rap during his debut. Caster was released on the night of his first appearance after performing a rap that ridiculed the bombings in Saudi Arabia. Speaking on his podcast, Dudley argued that society has become overly sensitive, noting that promos from previous eras would not survive today's standards. Dudley expressed sympathy for Caster, hoping he can return to professional wrestling and reassuring him that the community is offering its support.
