Cody Rhodes recently discussed Haku, noting that while he is widely considered the toughest wrestler of all time, he is also the sweetest person you will ever meet. Haku has made a return to WWE television this year as part of The MFT presentation. During an interview with Comedy Shorts, Rhodes shared a story about Haku from his early days in Macon, Georgia, highlighting his legendary toughness alongside his kind nature. Elsewhere, Rhodes also spoke about the impact of the upcoming Street Fighter release on the wrestling world.
