Jacy Jayne is fully confident as she transitions to the WWE main roster, boldly declaring herself the company's new top star. Speaking on The Wrestling Classic, Jayne explained that achieving success on the main roster feels like the realization of a lifelong dream she has pictured for years.

Jayne believes her extensive tenure in NXT prepared her for any challenge WWE might present. She noted that she no longer feels fear when stepping through the curtain and views every appearance as a chance to establish her dominance. Having previously held the NXT Women's Championship and the TNA Knockouts World Championship, Jayne aims to make the current phase of her career revolve entirely around her.
