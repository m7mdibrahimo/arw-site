The Creed Brothers (Julius Creed and Brutus Creed) have been moved to the alumni section of the official WWE website following their departure from the company. According to Fightful Select, their exit appears to be a mutual parting of ways after their contract expired. The duo returned to WWE NXT at the beginning of September by attacking Noam Dar, Romeo Moreno, Sean Legacy, and Dorian Van Dux. They later competed in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic, where they lost to Hank and Tank (Hank Walker and Tank Ledger) in the quarterfinals. While they had not wrestled on the main roster since May, they recently made appearances on WWE Main Event and AAA on Fox Latin. The Creed Brothers originally joined WWE in 2021 through NXT after transitioning from amateur wrestling backgrounds.
