Full results for the November 11, 2026, edition of AEW Dynamite on TBS, broadcast live from the Maverik Center in West Valley City, Utah. Will Ospreay and Kyle Fletcher met face-to-face ahead of their upcoming match on AEW Full Gear. Fletcher thanked Ospreay for the lessons but vowed to end Ospreay's reign. Ospreay acknowledged Fletcher's improvement since their last encounter. After showing footage from AEW All In London, Ospreay asked Fletcher how he really felt after winning the title. Fletcher admitted he was jealous but knew what was coming next, vowing to take everything from Ospreay. Despite being slapped by Fletcher, Ospreay remained composed and stated he would prove he was on another level.

Brodido (Bandido and Brody King) faced New Level (Austin Creed and Kofi) in a match that ended in a count-out. Wrathlander and Brawling Birds argued backstage following AEW Blood and Guts. David Finlay defeated Orange Cassidy. Andrade El Idolo expressed his disappointment over losing the AEW National Championship and stated his sights are now set on the World Title. Big Stoke cut a promo predicting that FTR will win the Best of 5 Series. Persephone, accompanied by Britt Baker, defeated Gypsy Mac. Hangman Page and Bandido were interviewed backstage, with Bandido warning that Swerve Strickland and New Level will meet their fate at AEW Full Gear.

In the fifth match of the Best of 5 Series, Motor City Machine Guns (Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin) defeated FTR (Dax Harwood and Cash Wheeler). Tommaso Ciampa defeated “Speedball” Mike Bailey to retain the AEW National Championship. Following the match, a future bout between Ilja Dragunov and Ciampa was announced for AEW Full Gear. In a mixed tag team match, Marina Shafir and Jon Moxley defeated Mercedes Moné and Jay White in a mixed tag team match, which led to a post-match brawl involving The Conglomeration, Death Riders, and The Cut.

In other action, The Elite (Jack Perry, Matt Jackson, and Nick Jackson) faced Kevin Knight, El Clon, and Brian Cage. Divine Dominion declared they would bring the violence following their loss at AEW Blood and Guts. During a backstage interview, The Young Bucks and Jack Perry spoke, with Perry expressing his desire for a rematch with Samoa Joe. Wardlow defeated Nick Comorotto. Sky Team (Mistico and Mascara Dorada) retained the ROH World Tag Team Championship against The Swirl (Blake Christian and Lee Johnson) when Mistico pinned Lee Johnson with La Magistral.
