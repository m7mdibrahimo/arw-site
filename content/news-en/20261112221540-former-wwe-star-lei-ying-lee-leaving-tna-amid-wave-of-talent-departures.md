TNA Wrestling is losing another former champion as Lei Ying Lee, known in WWE as Xia Li, officially departs the promotion. Lee is now a free agent and has reportedly begun discussions with other major wrestling companies regarding her future. She had been absent from TNA television since July, having spent time in China visiting family, but her exit is now confirmed. Lee, who made history as the first Chinese woman to compete in a WWE ring, spent seven years with WWE before her release in April 2024. She joined TNA later that year, where she found the championship success that eluded her in WWE, capturing the TNA Knockouts World Championship twice. Her final match for the promotion took place in July in a losing effort against Xia Brookside. Lee’s departure follows a series of talent exits from TNA throughout 2026, including Steve Maclin, Myla Grace, Tessa Blanchard, Tommy Dreamer, Eric Young, and Alicia Fox. Additionally, George Iceman recently departed the company following a contract dispute. With Rich Swann and Mustafa Ali also facing potential contract expirations by the end of the year, further changes to the TNA roster remain possible.
