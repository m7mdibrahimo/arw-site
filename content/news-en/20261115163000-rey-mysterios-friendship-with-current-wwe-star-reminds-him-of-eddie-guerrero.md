Rey Mysterio recently reflected on his bond with Dragon Lee, sharing that it reminds him of his past friendship with the late Eddie Guerrero. Speaking on Steph McMahon's podcast, Mysterio noted that Dragon Lee often seeks his advice and guidance, drawing a parallel to the wisdom Guerrero used to share with him. He explained that their conversations frequently center around family life, as both men are married with two children, and praised Dragon Lee's strong dedication to his loved ones.
