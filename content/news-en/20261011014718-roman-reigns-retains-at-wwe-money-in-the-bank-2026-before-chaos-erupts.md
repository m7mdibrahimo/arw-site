Roman Reigns successfully defended his World Heavyweight Championship at WWE Money in the Bank 2026, but the victory was immediately overshadowed by a massive brawl. After defeating LA Knight to retain his title, chaos erupted when members of 9:46 appeared in the crowd. Solo Sikoa and The Usos quickly ambushed them from behind, triggering a violent fight that spilled through the arena and into the backstage area. The brawl continued outside the building until Jaida Parker arrived in an SUV, allowing 9:46 to escape and bringing the wild post-match ambush to an end.
