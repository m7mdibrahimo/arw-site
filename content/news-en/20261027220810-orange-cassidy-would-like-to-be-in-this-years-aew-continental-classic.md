Orange Cassidy has expressed interest in competing in this year's AEW Continental Classic tournament. Speaking in a new interview with STL Sports Central, Cassidy noted that he enjoys the tournament's round-robin format because it simplifies his preparation. Cassidy stated, "You see, that's the thing. I don't really think too far ahead and I don't ask too far ahead. I just show up and they tell me what to do. The Continental Classic is nice because you kind of know what the deal is, you know, I don't have to think too much. I just know what, what I'm doing, what match I'm wrestling. So, I would like to be in it."
