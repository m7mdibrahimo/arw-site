WWE has filed a new trademark for 'The Vision', covering entertainment services, wrestling exhibitions, and related digital and fan club content. Filed on October 9, the trademark filing is associated with a group that includes Bron Breakker, Logan Paul, Austin Theory, Bronson Reed, and Maxxine Dupri.
