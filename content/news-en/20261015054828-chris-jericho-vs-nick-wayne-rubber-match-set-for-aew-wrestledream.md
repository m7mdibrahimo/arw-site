Chris Jericho and Nick Wayne are set to face off in a rubber match at AEW WrestleDream this Saturday. The match was confirmed during the October 14 episode of AEW Dynamite and AEW Collision after a segment involving both wrestlers. Wayne celebrated his recent win over Jericho with a video package, only to be interrupted by Jericho, who presented his own video showing Wayne's use of a low blow. With their series tied at 1-1, Jericho challenged Wayne to a third match at AEW WrestleDream, which Wayne accepted under the condition that Jericho apologize to him if he wins. The segment concluded with Jericho delivering a low blow to Wayne, intensifying their rivalry ahead of the event.
