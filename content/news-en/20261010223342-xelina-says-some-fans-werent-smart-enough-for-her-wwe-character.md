Xelina believes that some fans were not smart enough to keep up with her character work during her time in WWE. During an episode of the ZELVX and CHARLIE GIRL podcast, Xelina and Charlie discussed how fans often struggled to understand the changes they made to their characters. Xelina frequently changed her hair color and style while in WWE, which caused confusion among viewers regarding her persona. Charlie shared a similar experience, noting that fans could not easily process her own hair color changes. Xelina explained her perspective on the situation, stating: "I did for a while (keeping the orange hair), but then it just became like, 'We don’t get it. Like, what are you? You don’t understand what kind of character you are.' I was like, 'Or you’re just not smart enough to keep up.' Eventually down the line, you guys will look back and go, 'Wow, Xelina was actually way ahead of her time.'" Additionally, Xelina opened up about feeling miserable toward the end of her final run with the company.
