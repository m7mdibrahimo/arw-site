Jeff Cobb, who now goes by JC Mateo in WWE after signing with the company in May 2025 and debuting at WWE Backlash alongside Solo Sikoa as part of the new Bloodline, opened up about joining the group that later became known as The MFTs alongside Solo, Tama Tonga, and Tonga Loa. Speaking with Chris Van Vliet on Insight, Cobb addressed being paired with the stable despite not belonging to the Samoan family tree.

> "Well, my strong family lineage, right? I didn’t even know that was the pitch to begin with because I heard a couple other rumors out there as well. I mean, just rumors from fans fantasy booking that I was like, oh, they’re gonna put you with Bronson Reed and be like the new Natural Disasters. I was like, oh man, so it would be like tsunami and volcano or something?"

Reed ultimately aligned with The Vision. Later in the interview, Cobb shared that JONAH, which was Reed's ring name before joining WWE, was included on the list of names that he could have used for himself in the company.
