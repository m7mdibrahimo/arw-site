Mila Moore has joined The Dollhouse faction on TNA Wrestling. During the November 5 episode of TNA iMPACT, Taryn Terrell announced the return of the stable she originally formed in 2015, with Harley Hudson being the first recruit. On the November 12 episode of TNA iMPACT, Mila Moore was revealed as the latest addition to the group. Moore was previously a member of the Order 4 faction, which has since disbanded.
