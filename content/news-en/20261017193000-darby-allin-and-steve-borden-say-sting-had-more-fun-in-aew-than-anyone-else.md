During a recent interview with Q101, Darby Allin and Sting's son, Steven Borden, discussed the legendary icon's final run in AEW. Steven Borden noted that his father truly enjoyed his time in the promotion more than anywhere else, attributing that happiness to his relationships with the backstage talent and AEW President Tony Khan. Darby Allin echoed those sentiments, calling AEW a place where wrestlers are encouraged to live their best lives and focus on their personal wellbeing.

Reflecting on how Sting's return came together, Allin recalled that it did not take much convincing after the veteran took a few bumps and felt good during their early training sessions. Allin initially brought Sting in for cinematic matches, but eventually convinced him to perform live in front of crowds because he believed the icon still had plenty left in the tank. Allin also expressed his admiration for how humble Sting was when he debuted during the pandemic.
