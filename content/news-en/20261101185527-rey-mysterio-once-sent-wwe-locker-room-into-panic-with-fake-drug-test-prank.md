Mike Chioda recalled a time when Rey Mysterio pulled a prank on the WWE roster that caused a genuine panic while the crew was touring overseas in China. According to Chioda, fake drug-testing signs were posted everywhere around the arena and the locker rooms upon their arrival, immediately catching the wrestlers off guard. Many performers, some of whom had taken sleeping medication during the long flight, panicked instantly and feared failing a surprise screening. Chioda noted that desperation quickly set in as wrestlers started frantically running around looking for anything they believed could help flush their systems, with some even chugging vinegar without knowing why. For Chioda, the prank hit especially hard since he had already served a suspension under WWE's drug-testing policy, making him genuinely worry that his career was over. While Chioda can laugh about the chaotic backstage moment today, watching his career flash before his eyes while coworkers desperately drank vinegar was far from funny at the time.
