Carmella has been open about how becoming a mother changed her perspective on health, admitting it makes getting back in the ring much less appealing. During her Hot Mom Sh*t podcast, she discussed dealing with postpartum anxiety and intrusive thoughts about injuries. Before having children, she never worried about getting hurt while traveling and wrestling around the world. Now, she constantly worries about something happening to her while alone with her kids. She wonders if these fears are the real reason she is not open to returning to the ring, as she wants to stay healthy for her children. Carmella also worries about her husband, Corey Graves, and what would happen during his work travels if she suffered a medical emergency alone at home. While she is not definitively closing the door on wrestling, her priority is making sure she can be there for her kids.
