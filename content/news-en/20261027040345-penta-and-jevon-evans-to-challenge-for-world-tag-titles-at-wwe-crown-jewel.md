A major title match has been set for WWE Crown Jewel. On this week's episode of WWE Raw, Adam Pearce announced that Penta and Je'Von Evans will challenge The Vision, consisting of Bron Breakker and Austin Theory, for the WWE World Tag Team Championships. The match was granted after Penta and Evans requested the opportunity following an attack by The Vision earlier in the show.
