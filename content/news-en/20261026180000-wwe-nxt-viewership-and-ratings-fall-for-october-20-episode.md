WWE NXT experienced a drop in both viewership and ratings following a six-week high the previous week. According to reports, the developmental brand averaged 655,000 viewers for its October 20 broadcast, marking an 11% decline from the prior week's total of 732,000 viewers.

Despite the decrease, the total viewership figure managed to stay above the show's four-week average of 641,000 viewers. However, the key 18-49 demographic rating suffered a larger drop, falling from a 0.14 to a 0.10. This rating fell below the recent monthly average of 0.11.

When compared to the same period last year, October numbers remain up overall, with total viewership rising by 8% and the key demographic rating up by 10%. Last week's broadcast featured the final two quarterfinal matches of the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic, which saw Hank and Tank alongside The Wagner Brothers advance to the semifinals.

The show also included a segment featuring NXT Champion Grayson Waller and top contender Tony D'Angelo to build toward their match at Halloween Havoc. The main event ended in a no contest between Cruz Montana and Dion Lennox after Montana was arrested amidst chaotic scenes.
