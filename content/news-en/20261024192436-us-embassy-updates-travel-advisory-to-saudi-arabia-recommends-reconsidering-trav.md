The US Embassy has updated its travel advisory for Saudi Arabia due to the threat of increased Iranian-supported Houthi regional aggression, armed conflict, terrorism, exit bans, and local laws regarding social media activity. The Department of State ordered non-emergency US government employees and their family members to leave the country. Despite the updated advisory, WWE Crown Jewel remains scheduled to take place in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on November 7.
