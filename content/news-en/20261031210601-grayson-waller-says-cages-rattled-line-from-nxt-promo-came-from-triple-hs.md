During a recent appearance on What's Your Story with Stephanie McMahon, Grayson Waller reflected on his return to WWE NXT in July and discussed the origin of his promo line about rattling cages. Waller explained that he borrowed the phrase from Triple H's interview with Cody Rhodes, where Triple H spoke about how Cody had to rattle cages after leaving WWE. Waller realized that he had been too quiet and polite backstage, taking Triple H's advice to heart to make people pay attention. Waller is scheduled to defend his NXT Championship against Tony D'Angelo at WWE NXT Halloween Havoc.
