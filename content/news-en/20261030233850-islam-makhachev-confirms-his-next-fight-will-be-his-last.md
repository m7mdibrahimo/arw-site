UFC Welterweight Champion Islam Makhachev has announced his intention to compete in only one more fight before retiring from the sport. Makhachev currently holds the record for the longest winning streak in UFC history, having secured 17 consecutive victories, most recently against Ian Machado Garry.

Makhachev stated that his upcoming bout will be among the final appearances of his career, expressing a desire to face top contenders to prove his dominance. Carlos Prates and Michael Morales are scheduled to compete for the Interim Welterweight Title in January, as Makhachev opted out of fighting that month due to Ramadan. Although he previously aimed for a 20-fight win streak, the date for his next contest remains unconfirmed.
