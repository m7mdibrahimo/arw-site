World Wonder Ring Stardom held its Crimson Nightmare event on October 29, 2026, at the Ota City General Gymnasium in Tokyo, Japan. New Japan Pro-Wrestling stars Shingo Takagi and Hirooki Goto both saw action on the card. In the opening match, Saori Anou, Aya Sakura, Sayaka Kurara, and Anne Kanaya defeated God's Eye, represented by Lady C, Tomoka Inaba, Ranna Yagami, and Kiyoka Kotatsu. This was followed by a three-way tag team match where Maki Itoh, Kikyo Furusawa, and Rian defeated God's Eye members Syuri, Ami Sohrei, and Hina, alongside the team of Mi Vida Loca, consisting of Rina Yamashita, Itsuki Aoki, and Akira Kurogane. Next, a 16-woman elimination tag team match saw STARS, featuring Hanan, Saya Iida, Maika, Utami Hayashishita, Hanako, Waka Tsukiyama, Yuria Hime, and Matoi Hamabe, secure a victory over H.A.T.E., which included Natsuko Tora, Momo Watanabe, Saya Kamitani, Konami, Rina, Ruaka, and Azusa Inaba. In mixed tag team action, Natsupoi and Shingo Takagi teamed up to defeat Yuna Mizumori and Hirooki Goto. In the main event, Suzu Suzuki successfully defended the World of Stardom Championship by defeating Starlight Kid.
