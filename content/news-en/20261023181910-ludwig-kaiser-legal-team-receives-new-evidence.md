Ludwig Kaiser's defense team has gained access to new evidence in his ongoing criminal case, featuring four photographs documenting the alleged injuries of the man accusing him of assault. According to a filing from Assistant State Attorney Juanda Padgett, the Florida state prosecution provided the defense with four images regarding Richard Reap, matching the photos presented during an October 19 hearing when a judge denied a motion to dismiss the charge. Kaiser, whose real name is Marcel Barthel, is contesting the charge under Florida's self-defense law following an April 23 altercation. A pretrial conference is scheduled for December 17, 2026, with a potential trial period from January 4 through January 22, 2027.
