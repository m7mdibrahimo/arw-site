Laynie Luck spoke about her experience under the WWE I.D. program after losing the WWE I.D. Women's Championship to Monica Monroe following a 320-day reign. According to Fightful Select, Luck entered free agency as her deal was not renewed. During an interview with Sean Ross Sapp, Luck addressed whether WWE imposed booking restrictions on her independent appearances. "No," Luck responded when asked if WWE I.D. came with booking restrictions. "I didn’t feel like there was really too much control or anything. The letters on that belt were very helpful to me and my bookings and it made people finally see me, and then I get there and I’m like, 'Hey guys, I’ve been here. Just so you know, I’m out here and I’ve been here.' Honestly, it was nothing but an upside for me. I didn’t lose anything. I didn’t lose my name. I didn’t lose any bookings. There wasn’t really any beef anywhere with anything… Just a positive. Just a nice, tall platform for me to climb up on to." Luck has been wrestling for 11 years.
