Bayley made the most of WWE's recent trip to Hawaii by sharing a vacation video that included jumping off a boat into the ocean. The former WWE Women's Champion posted a vacation recap on Instagram featuring a clip of herself doing a somersault into the water while wearing a black bikini top and blue bikini bottoms. She captioned the post with a collection of emojis summarizing her activities. The trip also included highlights and photos from WWE's live event in Honolulu alongside hiking, drinks, and dolphin sightings. This follows a similar vacation post she shared in June from Capri, Italy, which previously caught the attention of AEW personality Renee Paquette.
