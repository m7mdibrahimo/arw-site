Jason Sensation recalled working WWE house shows during the Attitude Era, where he impersonated top stars to warm up the crowd before matches. While the audience enjoyed the act, backstage concerns grew that he was taking crowd reactions meant for the actual performers. Sensation noted that Road Dogg personally warned him about the situation, explaining that his use of catchphrases before the wrestlers entered the ring could frustrate the locker room. Sensation also shared that Vince McMahon reportedly believed audiences had a strictly limited number of major reactions to give during a show, meaning WWE preferred to reserve those pops for the talent themselves.
