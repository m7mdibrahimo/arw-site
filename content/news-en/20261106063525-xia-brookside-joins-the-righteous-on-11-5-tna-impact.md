During the November 5th episode of TNA iMPACT, The Righteous (Vincent and Dutch) held an initiation ceremony for Harley Hudson. As Vincent directed Dutch to crown Hudson, he hesitated, questioning her commitment. After forcing Hudson to recite lines, it was revealed that Xia Brookside had been waiting behind her. Brookside attacked Hudson and officially joined The Righteous, becoming the second woman to align with the group, following Vita VonStarr.
