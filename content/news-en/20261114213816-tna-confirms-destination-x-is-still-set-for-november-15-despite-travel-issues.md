TNA Wrestling has officially confirmed that Destination X will take place as scheduled on Sunday, November 15, at the Edmonton EXPO Centre, despite extensive travel disruptions caused by the Federal Aviation Administration's nationwide grounding of flights across US airports.

TNA President Carlos Silva stated that the match card may change due to travel challenges affecting everyone, but he expressed pride in the roster's unity to deliver an exceptional event. He noted that two stars offered to take a domestic flight within the US and then drive over 600 miles to participate in the show.
