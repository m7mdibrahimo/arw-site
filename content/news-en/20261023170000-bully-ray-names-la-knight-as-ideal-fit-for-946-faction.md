WWE Hall of Famer Bully Ray believes LA Knight is the ideal choice to fill the final spot in the 946 faction ahead of their Survivor Series clash with The Bloodline. Ray dismissed the idea of Drew McIntyre joining, calling him a poor fit and labeling suggestions of McIntyre or Roman Reigns as forced and illogical. Instead, Ray pointed to Knight because his ongoing storyline with Solo Sikoa provides a natural narrative bridge. Sikoa recently assured Knight he would not return to The Bloodline, and Sikoa's subsequent pledge to his family leaves Knight justifiably aggrieved. Ray noted that this betrayal sets up Knight to seek retribution, potentially aligning with The 946 and adopting a heel persona without needing a drastic betrayal storyline.
