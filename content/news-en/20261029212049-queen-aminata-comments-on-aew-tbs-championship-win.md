Queen Aminata experienced a memorable night at AEW Fright Night Dynamite on Wednesday, defeating Persephone in the main event to become the new AEW TBS Champion. Following her victory, Aminata shared her emotions on X.

She dedicated the win to her late father, who passed away in late 2023 during her recovery from an injury. In a previous interview with Renee Paquette, Aminata revealed she had considered retiring from wrestling after his death, but found motivation after discovering photos of her wrestling in Japan on his phone.

> "Dad...I did it," Aminata tweeted. "I love you and I miss you so much."

Later, she posted a photo from her hotel room smiling with the TBS Championship.

> "I still can't believe this, but I am your NEW TBS CHAMPION," Aminata tweeted. "The one and only is your brand new TBS Champion...omg!"

On Thursday morning, she shared a video of the AEW women's locker room celebrating her win.

> "Women empowerment," Aminata tweeted. "I love all my AEW women."

While Aminata previously held titles on the independent circuit, such as the Deadlock Pro Wrestling Women's World Championship, this marks her first title in AEW. She gained initial notoriety competing on AEW Dark and AEW Dark: Elevation before officially signing with the promotion in early 2024.
