Mick Foley expressed his admiration for AEW and Tony Khan's management style, contrasting it with his past experience in WWE. Foley left WWE after growing frustrated with the company's ties to Donald Trump. During an appearance on Battleground Podcast, Foley discussed why he relates better to Khan's leadership approach.

"Oh, I love it [in AEW]. I love it, you know, Tony Khan makes it clear that he really appreciates his talent. He's got a great board, and it's just — it's a different management style. And it's one that I take to, you know, better," Foley said. He also credited AEW with pushing WWE to offer better salaries through competition, noting that wrestlers now have alternative options in the industry.

Foley stated he has no regrets about leaving WWE and has made sporadic appearances on AEW television and pre-show panels in recent months.
