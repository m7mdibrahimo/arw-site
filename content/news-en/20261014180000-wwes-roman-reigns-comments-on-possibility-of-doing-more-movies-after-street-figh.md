WWE World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns recently discussed his plans to star in more movies following his role as Akuma in the upcoming Street Fighter film. During an interview with extratv at the movie premiere, Reigns explained that while his family remains his top priority and WWE supported him early in his career, pro wrestling is a tough way to make a living. He expressed his desire to continue exploring acting and contributing to the creative process of the performing arts. Reigns transitioned to a part-time schedule with WWE a few years ago, allowing him to pursue acting opportunities alongside his former rival Cody Rhodes, who also appears in Street Fighter. His recent film credits also include a minor role in The Pickup starring Eddie Murphy and voice acting as a police officer in Zootopia 2 alongside another former rival, CM Punk.
