The November 10, 2026, episode of WWE NXT aired live from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, on Netflix. The show featured the 2026 Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic winners, Romeo Moreno and Noam Dar, accepting an invitation from John Cena to participate in The John Cena Classic. Additionally, NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan was invited to the tournament, and a championship match between Jordan and Kendal Grey was set for next week.

EK Prosper def. Kam Hendrix

Izzi Dame def. Wren Sinclair

Grayson Waller opened the show, claiming to be the best in the world after beating CM Punk, before being interrupted by Shiloh Hill. Mason Rook made his return to NXT, attacking Waller. Vanity Project, the NXT Tag Team Champions, attacked Moreno and Dar before their scheduled title defense. Following Izzi Dame's victory over Wren Sinclair, Women's North American Champion Zaria attacked Dame, leaving her laid out in the ring.
