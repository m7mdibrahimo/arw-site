Jasper Troy recently opened up about his frustrations regarding his utilization during his time with WWE NXT, prior to his departure from the company in early September.

Speaking with The Takedown on Sports Illustrated, Troy discussed how he was repeatedly told to stay patient by management despite his concerns. He explained that after February, the lack of consistent television matches made it difficult to maintain his performance rhythm, noting that work rate cannot replace actual repetitions in the ring. Troy also admitted that missing out on premium live events following WWE The Great American Bash in 2025 tested his patience, though he ultimately tried to stay focused on what he could control.

Troy, who won the first season of WWE LFG, officially parted ways with WWE last month as his tenure with the developmental brand came to an end.
