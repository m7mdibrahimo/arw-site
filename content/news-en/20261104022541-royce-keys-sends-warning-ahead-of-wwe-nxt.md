Royce Keys issued a stern warning ahead of the upcoming episode of WWE NXT. The show will air on Wednesday, November 4, due to Midterm Elections coverage. The event features an Unsanctioned match between Dion Lennox and Cruz Montana, with UFC fighter Michael Chandler serving as the guest referee.

Cruz Montana debuted on the WWE main roster on Monday, November 2, as the newest member of the 946 faction, which includes Royce Keys, Carmelo Hayes, Jaida Parker, Bronco Nima, and Lucien Price. Montana targeted World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns during his debut. Following these events, Keys posted a warning on X:

> "#WWENXT. Tomorrow night. Ain’t nobody safe."
