Tessa Blanchard is preparing for her debut match in MLW against Keyra at the MLW Saturday Night SuperFight event. The show is scheduled for November 7 at the sold-out Cicero Stadium, starting at 8 p.m. ET.

Blanchard enters the match with significant international experience from her time in CMLL, where she won the World Women’s Tag Team Championship with Lluvia and the Women’s International Grand Prix. Keyra aims to spoil her opponent's debut and capture the spotlight in her own first appearance for the promotion.
