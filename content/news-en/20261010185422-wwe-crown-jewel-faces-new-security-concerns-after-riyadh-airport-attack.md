WWE Crown Jewel faces new security concerns after an attack on King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh forced an evacuation and temporarily halted flights. According to the Associated Press, residents reported hearing explosions during the incident, which comes just days after previous strikes claimed by Yemen's Houthi movement. In response to the growing security situation, airlines such as Lufthansa, Etihad Airways, and Flydubai have canceled or suspended flights to the Saudi capital.

Despite the unrest and a recent security alert issued by the U.S. Embassy, WWE has not announced any changes to its plans. The Crown Jewel event is still scheduled for November 7 at Riyadh Season Stadium, leaving talent and staff concerned as the company monitors the situation over the coming weeks.
