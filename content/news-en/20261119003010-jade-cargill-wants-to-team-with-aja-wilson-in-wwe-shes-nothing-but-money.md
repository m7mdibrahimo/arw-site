Jade Cargill recently revealed that she wants to team with WNBA star A'Ja Wilson in WWE. In a new interview with The Pin Down on Sports Illustrated, Cargill discussed her college basketball background at Jacksonville University before entering the world of wrestling and was asked to name one WNBA star she would like to team with in WWE.

"A'Ja Wilson, of course. The MVP. We could do nothing but make money, make some noise, and create that currency that we do. She's nothing but money," Cargill said. When Kent Brown brought up that Wilson might have trouble playing a heel, Cargill assured that it would not be an issue and that Wilson would eventually grow to enjoy it.
