Portland Trail Blazers star Damian Lillard shared his personal ranking of top WWE wrestlers from the 1990s in a recently released video. The NBA point guard placed 'Stone Cold' Steve Austin at the very top of his list as the greatest of all time.

In the tier directly below Austin, Lillard ranked The Rock, Ric Flair, and The Undertaker. His next tier featured Shawn Michaels, Triple H, and Bret Hart. Lillard has frequently shown his love for professional wrestling over the years, having previously dressed up as Steve Austin for a game in 2018 and later as 'Macho Man' Randy Savage for Halloween in 2025.

After missing the entirety of last season due to a torn Achilles tendon, the basketball star is set to make his return to the court this year.
