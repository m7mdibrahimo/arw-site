Spike Dudley recently spoke about signing a Legends deal with WWE during an appearance on "Busted Open Radio" alongside Bully Ray. Dudley explained that fans frequently approached him at conventions asking why they couldn't buy official merchandise for his past championship career, so he told them to let WWE know. Those voices finally led to WWE reaching out to him a couple of weeks ago. Bully Ray expressed his excitement for the news and noted that he has long advocated for Spike to be inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame. Dudley began his career in 1995 before joining ECW, where he eventually became the final Dudley standing before moving to WWE alongside Bubba and D-Von.
