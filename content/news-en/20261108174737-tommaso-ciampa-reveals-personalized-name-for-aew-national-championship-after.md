Tommaso Ciampa has given his newly won AEW National Championship a personalized name following his victory aboard Chris Jericho's Rock 'N' Wrestling Rager at Sea cruise. During AEW Collision on November 7, Ciampa defeated Andrade El Ídolo in a rematch from AEW WrestleDream to claim the title, marking his second championship reign since joining AEW.

Following the match, Ciampa expressed his pride in making history and introducing a unique identity for the belt. "Ten months in AEW, and I’m rewriting the damn playbook. You can call me the history maker. My debut here, first man ever to win a singles title in his first ever match at the company. Now, I am the first man in the history of professional wrestling to win a title on television on a cruise ship. Tommaso Ciampa, ten months, TNT champion, National champion. I said it from day one. I am the new belt collector and I’m taking every damn title until I take the world title. This one right here, this one right here is special, and she tells me her name is Ruby. Say hello to Ruby. Isn’t she lovely? Isn’t she lovely?" Ciampa said in a digital exclusive.
