Maya World has shared her plans for Wednesday night, which involve both professional success and personal leisure. Maya has been pursuing the AEW TBS Championship since she lost it at AEW All In. After her rematch at Full Gear was delayed due to a nationwide ground stop, she is now scheduled to challenge for the title on the November 18th episode of AEW Dynamite. The match will be a fatal four-way featuring champion Queen Aminata, AZM, and Mina Shirakawa. Maya stated on social media that her goal for the evening is to reclaim the championship and then return to her hotel room to play GTA 6 immediately upon its midnight release.
