Podcaster Conrad Thompson shared a hostile email from a Roman Reigns fan on October 8, 2026, after reiterating his stance that Reigns does not belong on a wrestling Mount Rushmore. Thompson posted the message on Twitter with a sarcastic caption, treating the backlash as a joke. The email, sent by a fan named Lena Dee, criticized Thompson for repeatedly targeting Reigns and accused him of seeking clout and engagement. The fan wrote, "At this point, anything you say about Roman goes beyond just giving an opinion. You’re attacking the guy for no real reason, and continue to badmouth him when you don’t have to, other than for clout, views, and likes." The fan further stated that Reigns has accomplished more than Thompson ever will and praised the wrestler's character. This is not the first time Thompson has faced criticism regarding Reigns; he previously labeled the star as the most overrated main-eventer in WWE history in August. Thompson remains unfazed by the negative feedback as the debate surrounding his comments continues.
