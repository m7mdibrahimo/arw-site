World Series of Wrestling announced that Buddy Matthews will miss the WSW Megamania tour because of a scheduling conflict. The run was scheduled to take place from October 29 through November 2.

> "We have just learnt, that due to scheduling conflicts, Buddy Matthews will no longer be appearing on the WSW MEGAMANIA Tour. We’re really disappointed that Buddy won’t be joining us this time around, but we completely understand that schedules can change. We hope to welcome Buddy back to World Series Wrestling on a future tour!"

https://www.instagram.com/p/De78xbWzdLG/

Matthews is signed to AEW and was cleared from an ankle injury in August, though he has not wrestled yet.
