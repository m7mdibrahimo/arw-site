Drew McIntyre's Hollywood career could bring a major name into WWE, as actor Russell Crowe apparently wants to work with him at WrestleMania. Speaking on The Joe Vulpis Podcast, McIntyre revealed that the Oscar-winning actor has discussed getting involved more than once, even suggesting they appear as a team with a manager or assistant in a heel dynamic. McIntyre and Crowe became close while filming The Last Druid in Spain. While there is no official word from WWE regarding an appearance, McIntyre noted that the interest from Crowe is genuine.
