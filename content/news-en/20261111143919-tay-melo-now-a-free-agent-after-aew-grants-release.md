Tay Melo is officially a free agent after AEW granted her release. According to Tony Khan during the AEW Redemption media call, Melo had requested her release following her match on the June 6 episode of AEW Collision, stating she wanted to step away from both AEW and wrestling. While Khan admitted the request came as a surprise, there was no backstage drama. Melo later clarified on social media that she has not retired.
