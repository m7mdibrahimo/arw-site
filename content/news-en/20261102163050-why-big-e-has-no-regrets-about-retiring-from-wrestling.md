Big E recently discussed his retirement from wrestling and explained why he has no regrets about walking away from the ring. Speaking on The Haangout, he noted that working on meditation and mindfulness in 2020 helped him accept that ending his in-ring career was the right decision. A major reality check came after he broke his neck, when he listed all of his previous injuries to doctors and realized the immense toll his body had taken by his mid-30s. He also shared that a spine specialist advised him against ever wrestling again, emphasizing the severe risks of paralysis or worse.

Leaving the ring, however, has not meant leaving WWE behind entirely. Big E has embraced a new role in broadcasting, finding satisfaction in staying connected to the product from a different angle. Rather than risking his long-term health and quality of life for another run, he is content with protecting his future and moving forward.
