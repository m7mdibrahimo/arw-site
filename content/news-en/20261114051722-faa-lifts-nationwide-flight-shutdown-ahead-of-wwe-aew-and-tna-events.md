Domestic flights across the United States have been cleared to resume after the Federal Aviation Administration lifted a nationwide ground stop on Friday, November 13, 2026. The shutdown was triggered by a telecommunications outage that severely disrupted air traffic control operations, leaving thousands of flights delayed or canceled and threatening travel plans for several major wrestling promotions ahead of a busy weekend.

AEW faced significant travel disruptions as multiple wrestlers were stranded while trying to reach Phoenix for Saturday's Full Gear pay-per-view. Bryan Alvarez of the Wrestling Observer reported that while some talent had already traveled following Wednesday's Dynamite, others were left waiting at airports with concerns over whether they would make it in time for the show. Now that air travel is resuming, those stranded talent have a much better chance of arriving in Phoenix before the event begins.

TNA Wrestling was also facing potential disruptions ahead of Destination X on Sunday, November 15, in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, where most scheduled performers were not set to fly in until Saturday. Additionally, WWE has scheduled NXT live events across the Midwest featuring main roster talent, while independent performers nationwide were also at risk of missing scheduled bookings. Although the ground stop has been lifted, ongoing flight delays and cancellations could still impact travel schedules over the weekend.
