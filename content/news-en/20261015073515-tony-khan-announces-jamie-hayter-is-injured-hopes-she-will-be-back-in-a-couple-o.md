AEW President Tony Khan has provided an update on the status of Jamie Hayter. Following the three-hour broadcast of AEW Dynamite/Collision, Khan announced on X that one-half of the AEW Women's World Tag Team Champions is currently sidelined with an injury.

> "I was hoping The Brawling Birds would be able to defend the Tag Team Championship but Jamie Hayter — from that Chicago Street Fight — is still out injured. Hopefully Jamie Hayter will be back in a couple of weeks. But that was a major injury."

Hayter last competed at All Out, where she and tag team partner Alex Windsor successfully defended their titles against Divine Dominion. The duo was recently seen in a backstage segment on the October 14th edition of AEW Dynamite/Collision alongside fellow United Empire stablemates 'Speedball' Mike Bailey, Andrade El Idolo, and Will Ospreay. AEW's next pay-per-view, WrestleDream, is scheduled for October 17th.
