Cody Rhodes is planning a major update to his iconic branding to honor his late dog, Pharaoh, according to Ringside News. The announcement comes just weeks after Rhodes shared the emotional news that his beloved husky had passed away in September at nearly 16 years of age.

During an appearance on the Flagrant podcast, Rhodes explained that while his physical tattoo will remain the same, the logo featured on his merchandise and other projects is getting a significant redesign. The updated design will replace the traditional skull with a side-profile husky or direwolf-style skull while keeping the signature wings associated with the American Nightmare.

> "I’m about to change—not change the logo because we can’t change the tattoo—but for the first time ever, the way the American Nightmare, exactly this logo which is on everything we do, I’m going to—it’s going to be a wolf skull that’s turned with the wings out to the side because I just lost my husky Pharaoh after almost 16 years."

Pharaoh was far more than a pet to Rhodes, frequently joining him on his wrestling journey, appearing at events, and becoming a beloved figure among fans. With this new logo alteration, Pharaoh's memory will continue to live on every time the American Nightmare branding is displayed.
