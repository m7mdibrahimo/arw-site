WWE Hall of Famer Bully Ray recently shared his thoughts on the women's division of WWE Raw, highlighting the impressive performances on the red brand. Ray discussed the recent matches from the October 26 broadcast, noting that the talent on Raw is giving the NXT women strong competition. He specifically praised the main event title match between WWE Women's World Champion Liv Morgan and Becky Lynch, commending Morgan for stepping up her performance against top-tier opponents. The match ended in a disqualification after interference from Roxanne Perez and Raquel Rodriguez, allowing Morgan to retain her title.
