Vince Russo recently criticized WWE agents following a dangerous neck landing by Bron Breakker during a live event. During an appearance on The Coach & Bro Show, Jonathan Coachman and Russo discussed the incident where Breakker attempted his signature Frankensteiner on LA Knight, resulting in a frightening fall. Coachman questioned why such a risky move was performed at a non-televised show, while Russo argued that modern agents fail to protect top talent the way legends like Pat Patterson once did.
