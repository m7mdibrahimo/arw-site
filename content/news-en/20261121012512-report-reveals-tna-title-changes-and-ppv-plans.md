Recent reports have revealed that TNA title changes took place during the tapings for upcoming episodes of TNA iMPACT. According to PWInsider Elite, the tapings held on November 19 featured AJ Francis defeating Jason Hotch to win the TNA International Championship.

Elayna Black, formerly known as Cora Jade in WWE NXT, captured the TNA Knockouts Television Champion after dethroning M By Elegance. The company has not yet confirmed the specific episodes on which these matches will air.

The promotion confirmed that the Thanksgiving episode will not air on AMC on November 26, though it will be available as usual on AMC+, TNA+, and Sportsnet 360 in Canada. Reports also indicated that TNA Final Resolution has been canceled for December this year as part of a plan to reduce the number of pay-per-view events next year.
