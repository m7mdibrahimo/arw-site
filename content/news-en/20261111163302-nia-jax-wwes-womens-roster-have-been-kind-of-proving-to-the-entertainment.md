Nia Jax stated that WWE's women's roster has been proving to the entertainment industry that women are capable of holding the top spot. Jax, who has been a prominent member of WWE's women's division since the mid-2010s from her early days in NXT to the main roster, reflected on the company's commitment to highlighting female competitors. During a recent interview with Jimmy V3, Jax discussed how WWE has elevated women to the main event scene.

> "I agree because I was there in the black and gold when Hunter was really making the women… kind of just making the show about how good the women are, he really did invest in us and like prove to like the whole wrestling industry that women are just as good as an athlete as the men, and if not sometimes better and storytelling in the ring. So, it's been about like, I want to say a decade of us women in WWE kind of just like taking over, like being in the main event picture and sometimes being the best match on the card over the men. So, we have been kind of proving into the entertainment industry that women can hold that top spot."

Elsewhere in the conversation, Jax shared her thoughts on Tiffany Stratton's Money In The Bank strategy.
