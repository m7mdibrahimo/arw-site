Ricochet has shared his thoughts on Je'Von Evans' impressive performance during the 2026 men's Money In The Bank ladder match. During the bout, Evans took a brutal spear from Bron Breakker while attempting a splash, which temporarily removed him from the match via an ambulance. However, Evans dramatically returned by driving the ambulance into the arena, ambushing Breakker, and climbing a massive ladder to chase the briefcase. The chaos ended when Breakker pushed Evans off the ladder, sending him crashing violently through the roof of the vehicle. Following the event, AEW star Ricochet took to social media to express his admiration for Evans, making it clear that he is now a fan of the young star after his unforgettable ladder match performance.
