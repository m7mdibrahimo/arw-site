Tiffany Stratton recently won the Women's Money in the Bank Ladder Match, but former briefcase holder Carmella quickly stepped in to correct a claim Stratton made afterward. Following Money in the Bank 2026, Stratton claimed to be the first-ever two-time Women's Money in the Bank winner. Carmella took to social media to remind everyone that she reached that milestone first, sharing a graphic showing she achieved the feat before anyone else. The graphic also noted that Carmella is only the third superstar overall to win the briefcase twice, joining CM Punk and Seth Rollins. Carmella offered a playful joke at Stratton's expense after the brutal ladder match, writing, "I think she hit her head a little too hard last night 🫨 but congrats on the win, babe! 👏🏼👏🏼". While the response was lighthearted rather than hostile, Carmella made sure that WWE history remained accurate.
