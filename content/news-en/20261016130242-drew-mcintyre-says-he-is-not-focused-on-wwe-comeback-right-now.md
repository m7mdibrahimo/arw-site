Drew McIntyre says he is not focused on his WWE comeback right now, as he is currently busy promoting his new movie Agent of Shadows. During a recent interview with Collider alongside Chase DeMoor, McIntyre praised DeMoor's performance in the film's action scenes and even pitched the idea of them forming a tag team in WWE one day. However, when the conversation turned toward his own return to the ring, McIntyre made it clear that his current focus remains entirely on his Hollywood career. McIntyre has been spending time away from WWE due to his growing acting schedule, which includes roles in Highlander and a project with Russell Crowe. Although he recently shared footage of himself training in the ring, he has not announced an official return date.
