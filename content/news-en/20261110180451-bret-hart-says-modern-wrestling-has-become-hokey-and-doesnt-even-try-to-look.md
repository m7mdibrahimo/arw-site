WWE Hall of Famer Bret Hart recently shared his candid thoughts on the state of modern professional wrestling, expressing his frustration with how much the industry has changed. During an appearance on the Johnny I Pro Show, Hart explained that today's product relies too heavily on over-the-top production, explosions, and flashy moves rather than making fans believe they are watching a genuine athletic contest.

Hart noted that while he understands today's fans know the business is scripted, he believes wrestlers should still treat their performances seriously and try to make the action look legitimate. He contrasted the current style with the black-and-white matches of legends like Buddy Rogers, Killer Kowalski, and Gene Kiniski, praising those veterans for their ability to make everything look believable. Hart admitted that he no longer gets the same enjoyment from watching modern wrestling and wishes today's performers would focus more on basic holds, storytelling, and psychology.
