Drew McIntyre's return to WWE television might be delayed longer than expected. The Scottish wrestler has been absent for six months, and Nov. 7 had emerged as a potential comeback date. However, WWE has now confirmed he will be appearing in London on the very same night that Crown Jewel is set to take place in Saudi Arabia.

On Oct. 24, WWE's official UK account announced that McIntyre is scheduled to appear at Zuffa Boxing 12 on Nov. 7 at the Copper Box Arena in London alongside Bayley. This scheduled appearance significantly decreases the chances of him returning at Crown Jewel at Riyadh Season Stadium on that date. While WWE has not officially ruled him out of the event, being advertised in another country makes an unannounced appearance unlikely.

McIntyre has not been seen on WWE programming since Night One of WrestleMania 42, where he was defeated by Jacob Fatu in an Unsanctioned Match. He spent his time away filming Hollywood projects, and following the completion of those commitments, he recently noted that things are in motion regarding his eventual comeback.
