Mason Rook might be sidelined from the ring for an extended period due to a medical situation that appears more severe than initially anticipated. According to Ringside News, the rising WWE star was forced to miss his scheduled NXT Championship match after failing to receive medical clearance.

When Rook was removed from the September 29 episode of NXT, he told fans that the issue was merely a speed bump rather than a roadblock. WWE provided minimal details at the time, announcing only that he was not medically cleared to compete.

According to Ringside News, Bryan Alvarez reported on Wrestling Observer Live on October 7 that the rising star could face a lengthy absence from television. 

> "I have heard… whatever’s going on with Mason Rook, I have been told could be pretty serious. I’ve been told he could be out a long time — could be a very long time. No one seems to be really talking about what the issue is. Whatever it is, it’s a real issue, and he could be out for a long time."

Neither WWE nor Rook has disclosed the specific nature of the medical problem. Until an official update is provided, his return to NXT remains uncertain.
