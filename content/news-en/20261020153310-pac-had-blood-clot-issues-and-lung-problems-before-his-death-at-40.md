PAC's sudden death at 40 shocked the wrestling world, but new details reveal he had health problems years before his passing. Dave Meltzer reported on Wrestling Observer Radio that PAC dealt with blood clot issues in 2023 and had a history of lung problems. The Cook County Medical Examiner's Office previously confirmed that Benjamin Satterley, known as PAC, died from natural causes due to bronchial asthma on September 27. Meltzer noted that friends were aware of his lung issues, and while it is unclear if he could reach his inhaler, the fatal attack happened shortly after his match against Andrade at AEW All Out.
