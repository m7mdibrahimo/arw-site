Veteran wrestler Matt Hardy recently discussed the initial attempt by WWE management to break up his famous tag team with his brother, Jeff Hardy. According to Fightful, Matt confirmed that Jeff's refusal to participate in certain creative pitches led to the duo being removed from television for several weeks.

Matt explained that the creative team planned a storyline to split the brothers, but Jeff felt uncomfortable with the proposed directions. This resistance resulted in what Matt described as a punishment.

> "There was a storyline for our characters and they wanted us to split up and there was a couple things that we were asked to do and Jeff was uncomfortable and didn't want to do it so we were off TV for a while as a punishment, I guess. But we still did live events, and then they put us back together because we knew we weren't ready to separate and split up as a team yet."
