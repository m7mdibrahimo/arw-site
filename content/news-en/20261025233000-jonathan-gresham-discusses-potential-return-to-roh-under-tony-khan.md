Jonathan Gresham recently addressed the possibility of returning to ROH under AEW President Tony Khan during an appearance on the Grapsody Podcast. Gresham reflected on their intense 2022 disagreement, which occurred when he was instructed to lose the ROH World Championship to Claudio Castagnoli shortly before his match at Death Before Dishonor.

Although Gresham requested his release following that event, he noted that he and Khan have not spoken since, despite his repeated attempts to apologize. He explained that he does not permanently rule out a return, even after his previous efforts to reconcile in Atlanta were rejected.

> "I'm never going to say never, but I've reached out several times to apologize in person when they've come here to Atlanta...I'm just going to say 'no' was the answer that I got. And that's fair."
