Bayley secured a significant victory on WWE Raw by defeating Lyra Valkyria in a No Disqualification match. The bout added another chapter to their ongoing rivalry, following Valkyria's previous wins over Bayley in July and September. As the match progressed, various weapons came into play, culminating when Bayley executed a powerbomb on Valkyria into a trash can at ringside. Bayley then placed Valkyria on a ringside table and delivered her signature elbow drop from the top rope. After rolling her back into the ring, Bayley forced Valkyria to tap out using a kendo stick-assisted crossface. This marks Bayley's first televised singles victory since April.
