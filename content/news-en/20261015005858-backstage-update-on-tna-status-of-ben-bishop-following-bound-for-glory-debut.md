Ben Bishop made a notable appearance at TNA Bound for Glory this past Sunday, entering the Call Your Shot gauntlet match as the penultimate participant. Despite failing to secure an elimination, his presence at 6'10" caught the attention of those within the company. Fightful Select reports that Bishop has not signed a contract with TNA, although management was impressed by his performance.

Bishop addressed the reports on X, joking that his lack of a contract explains why TNA denied him entry to their television tapings in Dothan, Alabama, this week. TNA had reportedly tracked Bishop for some time, but previous attempts to sign him were allegedly blocked by former booker Tommy Dreamer, who reportedly disliked Bishop's social media content regarding character concepts. Dreamer has denied these claims, stating he holds no ill will and that past hiring failures were due to a poor tryout years ago.

Bishop remains active on the independent circuit and has previously appeared in the MLW Battle Riot. He also competed in several AEW Dark matches between 2021 and 2022, and faced Roderick Strong on a 2024 episode of AEW Rampage.
