Lola Vice discussed the dangerous ladder incident that occurred during the women's Money in the Bank Ladder match at the 2026 WWE Money in the Bank Premium Live Event. During the match, Vice and WWE Women's United States Champion Jacy Jayne were fighting on a ladder when Vice applied a chokehold to Jayne. As they fell, the ladder shifted and sent both competitors out of the ring and onto the floor. Reflecting on the moment during her appearance on WWE Ahora, Vice stated:

> "I had Jacy (Jayne) in a choke and when I put her to sleep, she fell back and I fell with her. I thought that was the end of it, but suddenly the ladder went backwards, and literally, it got us out of the ring. Because of that, I didn't win Money in the Bank. It hurt a lot and was very scary, but looking back, thank God it came out like this because it could have been very ugly. I think an injury in a Ladder match would have been rough. They are not for everyone, but thank God I'm strong and I was able to do it." Jayne made an appearance the following day, and her own comments regarding the bump have been previously reported.
