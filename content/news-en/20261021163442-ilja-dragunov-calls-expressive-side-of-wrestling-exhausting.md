Ilja Dragunov stated that the expressive side of professional wrestling is the most exhausting part of the industry, noting that it is harder than executing physical moves. Speaking on the Battleground podcast, he explained that vocal expressions and facial reactions require immense effort to engage the crowd. 

> "It is definitely the part of expressing yourself. It is the vocality. It is the expressions. This part of portraying what you're doing is at times much harder than any move that you can do."
