Tony D'Angelo discussed the importance of versatility and adaptability in WWE NXT during a recent interview with Denise Salcedo. Having spent several years in the developmental brand, D'Angelo has witnessed many wrestlers come and go, including newer names like Cruz Montana and Grayson Waller.

Reflecting on the shifting roster, D'Angelo emphasized that adapting quickly is essential for long-term success.

> "Yeah. I think you have to be versatile and you have to be able to adjust, especially if you want to be in this job for a long time, you know, and I have seen a lot of people come and go," he said. He noted that wrestlers who get called up too early without enough experience often struggle, while those who fail to evolve eventually get passed over. D'Angelo also highlighted the importance of being coachable, admitting that while he can be stubborn at times, he prides himself on adapting to any situation.
