Kam Hendrix is confident in his ability to compete in the upcoming John Cena Classic. The WWE NXT wrestler took to social media during the November 2 episode of WWE RAW to state that he could defeat any opponent who enters the tournament. John Cena announced that the event will take place on December 14 at Disney World in Orlando, Florida, while revealing a new championship for the winner and confirming Cody Rhodes as the first official participant.

Shortly after Cena shared the details of the tournament, Hendrix expressed his stance on social media with a clear statement.

> "I’d cook anybody in the John Cena Classic 🤣"

Hendrix did not name specific wrestlers, indicating instead that he feels ready to face the entire field of competitors. WWE has not yet confirmed Hendrix as an entrant in the tournament, leaving it unannounced whether he will receive an official spot in the lineup.
