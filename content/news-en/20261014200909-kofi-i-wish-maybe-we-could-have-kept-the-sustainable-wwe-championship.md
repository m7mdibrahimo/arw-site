Kofi spoke about the sustainable WWE Championship belt that Daniel Bryan used during his final run with the title. At WrestleMania 35, Kofi defeated Daniel Bryan to win the WWE Championship, bringing back the traditional design that fans were used to after Bryan introduced the wooden version he described as sustainable. During a recent interview on The Mick and Kenny show, Kofi shared that he would have liked to keep that unique design for his own title reign.

"Yeah, that was the sustainable championship. It was really, really cool. It was a really cool looking one with the wooden belt. Oh man, that was the one thing I wish maybe we could have kept because it was so unique."

Elsewhere in the interview, Kofi discussed how he was unsure if he would have won the championship if Mustafa Ali had not suffered an injury leading up to WrestleMania 35.
