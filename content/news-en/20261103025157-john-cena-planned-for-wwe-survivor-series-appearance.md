John Cena is preparing to appear at WWE Survivor Series in Houston to continue scouting talent for the John Cena Classic in December, according to Fightful Select. The appearance is part of WWE's plans to have Cena promote the tournament through Monday Night RAW.

The John Cena Classic is scheduled for December 14 at Walt Disney World. The tournament will take place during a special episode of Monday Night RAW and will feature Cena introducing a new championship. Cena is playing an administrative role in preparing for the event without any plans to return to the ring.
