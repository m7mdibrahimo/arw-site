John Cena recently cleared up any confusion regarding his eyewear during an appearance on Slice Joint, part of First We Feast. While reading a cue card, Cena used a pair of handheld reading glasses rather than a regular pair. The host mistakenly believed Cena was wearing a monocle, prompting Cena to joke, "IN THIS ECONOMY?!?!?!" Cena explained that the transformation of the glasses is part of a grand reveal. He is currently making media appearances to promote his new film Matchbox, which is available on Apple TV.
