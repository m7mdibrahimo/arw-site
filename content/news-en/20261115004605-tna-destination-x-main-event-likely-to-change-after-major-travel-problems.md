TNA Wrestling may be forced to change the main event of Destination X as travel issues threaten the scheduled TNA World Championship match. Trey Miguel is currently unlikely to reach Edmonton, Canada, for Sunday's pay-per-view, putting his title challenge against champion Leon Slater at risk. According to PWInsider, TNA talent were informed on Saturday, November 14, that it would take a miracle for Miguel to arrive in time. A nationwide flight shutdown on Friday has left several wrestlers stranded, with connecting flights into Canada causing additional complications. The situation is worsening as the hours pass, and TNA's creative team is preparing for significant lineup changes. Officials are currently assessing which wrestlers will arrive in Edmonton before finalizing adjustments to the card. TNA President Carlos Silva addressed the situation on November 14, stating that the company has no choice but to make changes due to travel predicaments affecting travelers worldwide. While some wrestlers have attempted to fly into alternative cities and drive over 600 miles to reach the show, limited flight availability and cancellations have hindered these efforts. TNA has not yet officially announced any changes to the World Championship match. Destination X is scheduled for Sunday, November 15, at the Edmonton EXPO Centre.
