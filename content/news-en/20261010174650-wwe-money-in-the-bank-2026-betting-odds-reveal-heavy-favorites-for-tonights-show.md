BetOnline has released betting odds for WWE Money in the Bank 2026, revealing several clear favorites for tonight's event at the Smoothie King Center in New Orleans. Roman Reigns is the biggest favorite of the night at -900 to retain the World Heavyweight Championship against LA Knight. In the men's ladder match, Bron Breakker leads the odds at -200, followed closely by Penta and Kevin Owens at +200. Roxanne Perez is heavily favored in the women's ladder match at -400. Additionally, Oba Femi is favored at -400 against Bronson Reed, and Stephanie Vaquer sits at -400 to retain her Women's World Championship in a triple threat match against Liv Morgan and Becky Lynch.
