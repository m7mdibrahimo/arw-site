Mike Chioda spent years building trust with WWE stars, but he firmly refused to act as a snitch for management. He explained that company officials sometimes expected referees to keep tabs on the talent.

Chioda stated that officials like Mark Carrano and John Laurinaitis wanted referees to track wrestlers' arrival times at shows and report anyone who was thirty minutes late. He considered this request unreasonable, noting that he traveled with these wrestlers around 300 days a year and trusted them inside the ring.

He added that he also pushed back when younger referees were put in the same position so they would not bear the wrestlers' anger. Drug tests also created awkward situations when referees were tasked with finding selected wrestlers to ensure they completed the tests.
