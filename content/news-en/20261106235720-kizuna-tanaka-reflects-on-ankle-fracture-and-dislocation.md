Dream Star Fighting Marigold talent Kizuna Tanaka has returned to action following a severe injury that sidelined her in January. Tanaka suffered an ankle fracture and dislocation, which required three surgeries after doctors warned that the bone was at risk of piercing her skin. Reflecting on the incident during an interview with ENCOUNT, Tanaka stated:

> "I went to the hospital right after the match, but the bone in my ankle had completely dislocated… and it was very close to going all the way through my skin. As a result, I ended up having three surgeries."

She further recalled the immediate aftermath of the injury, noting that she experienced a memory lapse due to the medical procedures. Tanaka, 21, recently competed at an independent show in Kanagawa, Japan, on November 3rd.
