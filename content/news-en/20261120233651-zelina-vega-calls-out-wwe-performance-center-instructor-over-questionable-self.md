Dakota Kai and Zelina Vega recently shared some ridiculous memories from WWE's Performance Center, including a self-defense class that left Vega questioning if any of the techniques would actually work against a real attacker. Speaking on their ZELVX and CHARLIE GIRL YouTube channel, Vega recalled an unnamed instructor who demonstrated moves that seemed entirely unrealistic for someone of her size against a larger opponent.

Vega specifically criticized a wall-defense sequence that required fumbling for an attacker's collar and wrapping fabric, noting that a real attacker would simply punch you in the face during the complicated setup. She remembered telling the instructor, "No, it's f not going to work," which immediately got her called out during the demonstration.

Dakota Kai also recalled another bizarre exercise where the instructor told them to stick out a hand and scream as loudly as possible if someone approached with ill intent. Kai remembered a wrestler, likely Xia Li, fully committing to the loud stance with an exaggerated posture that they still laugh about today. While they praised professional training sessions from the Gracies and Eve Torres, these specific self-defense lessons left them with lasting doubts about the practicality of the advice.
