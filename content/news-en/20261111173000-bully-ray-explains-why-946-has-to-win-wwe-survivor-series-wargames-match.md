946 and The Bloodline are set to collide in the men's WarGames match, and Bully Ray believes the newcomer faction should emerge victorious. Led by Royce Keys, the group has dominated WWE Raw since arriving, and a win at Survivor Series would solidify their status as a major threat.

Speaking on Busted Open, Ray argued that a loss would hurt their credibility as a dominant force. He noted that WWE is historically a babyface territory, but 946 needs this decisive win to establish themselves as truly intimidating heels without harming The Bloodline's standing.
