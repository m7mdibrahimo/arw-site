Ilja Dragunov recently shared his thoughts on a potential rematch with Kyle Fletcher, explaining why the matchup interests him. Dragunov made his debut for the promotion at AEW WrestleDream, where he walked out to be presented to the crowd. His ring debut is set for tonight's episode of AEW Dynamite against Speedball Mike Bailey.

Speaking on the Battleground podcast, Dragunov addressed Fletcher's recent comments about facing him again. The two previously shared the ring on the German independent circuit, particularly in wXw, before either signed with a major promotion.

> "Because he's simply just an entirely progressed version of the last time that I've seen him. We spend a lot of time on the German indies because in times where he wasn't in the tag team as the Aussie Open. They spent some time in Germany. I spent my time there with wXw as well. We were touring together. It was like years ago, and then when our way split it up and I signed, he signed, he did his thing. Like to see from the person that he was to the person that he has become, it's like incredible. Like he definitely made the most out of everything that he got, and I did the same thing. I grew. I'm a different performer than I have been, and to see what happens when these two performers will, go at it one more time, another time it's intriguing."

Dragunov noted that both competitors have grown significantly since their early days in Germany, making a potential future encounter an exciting prospect.
