Andrade El Idolo has opened up about his departure from WWE, revealing the main reasons behind his decision to leave the promotion after a year and a half during his second run. Speaking on Talk Is Jericho, the Mexican star explained that his situation backstage became awkward following his divorce from Charlotte Flair, making him feel uncomfortable at work.

Andrade stated that creative management failed to provide him with a clear storyline or direction. WWE officials eventually proposed sending him to work in AAA in Mexico, but he turned down the offer. He explained that he joined WWE to perform on a global stage rather than return to Mexico, where he was already an established name.

> "I moved here to work with WWE. I know AAA, I respect AAA, I respect all the companies. But the WWE is worldwide and AAA is just in Mexico. People in Mexico know me, so why [should I] work in AAA. I want people to know me around the world."

After leaving WWE, Andrade returned to AEW and immediately targeted Kenny Omega, securing a victory over him as part of his goal to face top-level talent. He also noted that he was unaware of a one-year non-compete clause in his release contract, as he had assumed it was the standard 90-day period.
