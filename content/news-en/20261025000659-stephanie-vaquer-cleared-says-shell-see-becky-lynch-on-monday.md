Stephanie Vaquer is ready to resume her rivalry with Becky Lynch. On social media, Vaquer announced she has been medically cleared to return to action, posting a video where she tossed her crutches aside. She stated she will be at WWE Raw this Monday to confront Lynch. The two had a tense interaction in the trainer's room last Monday, and General Manager Adam Pearce previously noted their match would be scheduled once Vaquer was cleared. While no official bout has been confirmed by Pearce yet, the tension remains high following their last encounter at WWE Money in the Bank, where Liv Morgan defeated both women to win the WWE Women's World Championship. Monday's WWE Raw lineup also includes a No DQ match between Bayley and Lyra Valkyria.
