Kevin Knight revealed that he spent the night in the hospital with Steven Borden following an injury during their match at AEW All Out. The event featured a tag team bout where Darby Allin and Steven Borden faced Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight. During the match, Knight and Borden attempted a mid-air collision spot, but Borden landed awkwardly as the back of his head struck the canvas. The match was stopped, and Knight and Fletcher were declared the winners, while Borden was stretchered out of the arena. Speaking in an interview, Knight explained that the spot was rehearsed beforehand and emphasized that he stayed with Borden to ensure his well-being. Knight noted that despite the scary accident, they discussed turning the situation into an opportunity for future growth. Additional updates on Borden were also mentioned by Tony Schiavone.
