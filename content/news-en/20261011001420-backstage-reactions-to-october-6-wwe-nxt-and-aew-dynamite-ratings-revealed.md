The latest television ratings for WWE NXT and AEW Dynamite have sparked internal discussions within both promotions. According to reports, AEW was pleased with the performance of Dynamite despite a notable week-to-week decline in viewership. Company personnel found encouragement in the fact that the episode still showed a significant increase compared to the pre-empted episode from the same time last year, viewing that as a more accurate metric for success.

On the WWE side, the internal focus was centered on competition. Reports indicate that company officials were satisfied that WWE NXT finished ahead of AEW Dynamite in head-to-head television viewership, a result that was internally highlighted once the figures were finalized. The final numbers showed WWE NXT drawing 532,000 viewers, while AEW Dynamite drew 495,000 viewers.

While viewership for both programs decreased compared to the previous week, both companies identified specific reasons to maintain a positive outlook. Although neither promotion is likely celebrating the overall downward trend, the backstage reactions suggest that both organizations believe they have a foundation to build upon moving forward.
