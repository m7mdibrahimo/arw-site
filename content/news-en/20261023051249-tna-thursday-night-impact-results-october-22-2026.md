TNA Thursday Night Impact took place on October 22, 2026, at the Dothan Civic Center in Dothan, Alabama. The event featured high-stakes championship action and a number one contender match for the X-Division Championship.

Indi Hartwell def. Xia Brookside to retain the Knockouts World Championship.

Cedric Alexander def. Rich Swann, Fabian Aichner, and KC Navarro in a Four-Way Number One Contenders Match for the X-Division Championship.

Avery Styles def. Joe Alonzo.

Jason Hotch def. Mustafa Ali in a 2-Out-Of-3 Falls Match to retain the International Championship.
