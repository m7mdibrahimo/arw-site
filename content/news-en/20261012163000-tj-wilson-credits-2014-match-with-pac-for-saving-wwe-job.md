TJ Wilson recalled how a 2014 match with Pac in WWE NXT helped him keep his job with WWE. Speaking in a video on the official WWE YouTube channel, Wilson stated that his singles match at the first WWE NXT TakeOver prevented him from being released in a round of cuts two weeks later.

> "I believe the TakeOver match I had with Ben, and of course, the four-way following, but that singles match at that first TakeOver, I believe that match really saved my job at that time."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XS-ujHCbCtY

Wilson noted that Kevin Owens messaged him to say Pac was the reason their matches were so great. He added that Pac made everyone look incredible in the ring and easily covered up any mistakes.
