TNA added a new six-woman tag team match to the upcoming TNA iMPACT card on October 15. The special event celebrates The Hardys and their 35 years in wrestling, with Matt and Jeff Hardy organizing the card and making the matches.

Xia Brookside joins new Knockouts World Tag Team Champions Zoey Serrano and Shayna Baszler to face Gabby Forza, Harley Hudson, and new Knockouts World Champion Indi Hartwell.

The lineup also features Nic Nemeth vs. Ryan Nemeth, where any attempt to throw the match means the Nemeth Brothers relinquish the TNA World Tag Team Titles. In addition, Syx (Joseph Sawyer & Samuel Shaw) faces The Righteous (Vincent & Dutch) in a tables match.
