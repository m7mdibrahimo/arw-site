The October 14, 2026, episode of AEW Dynamite/Collision aired live from the Wolstein Center in Cleveland, Ohio. In the opening match, the Don Callis Family (Wardlow, Jake Doyle, and Mark Davis) defeated The Death Riders (Jon Moxley, Gabe Kidd, and Daniel Garcia) in a trios match, with Davis securing the win after hitting a piledriver on Moxley. In the second match, Adam Copeland defeated Josh Alexander in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage Qualifying Match after hitting a spear. Following the match, Copeland addressed the crowd, revealing that 2027 will be his final year in professional wrestling.
