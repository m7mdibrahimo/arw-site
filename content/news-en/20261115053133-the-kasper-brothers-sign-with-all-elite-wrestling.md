Jacob and Drew Kasper have signed with All Elite Wrestling. The brothers, formerly known as Julius and Brutus Creed in WWE, made their surprise debut at the AEW Full Gear pay-per-view on November 14. They immediately targeted the Motor City Machine Guns and the AEW World Tag Team Champions, The Young Bucks. The duo also confronted FTR before Big Stoke, who was paired with them in WWE NXT as part of Diamond Mine, calmed things down. Following the chaos, AEW President Tony Khan officially announced their signing. The Kasper Brothers finished their run with WWE in early November.
