The October 16 WWE NXT live event in Sudbury, Ontario, concluded in a frightening manner when Channing "Stacks" Lorenzo suffered a severe fall during the main event and required a stretcher to leave the arena.

Stacks was wrestling in an eight-man tag team match alongside Birthright against Tony D'Angelo, Tavion Heights, Shiloh Hill, and Shawn Spears when the action suddenly halted. During the bout, Stacks took a back body drop to the floor, appearing to strike his face on the ring post before landing heavily on his back.

The official immediately signaled for a stoppage, prompting medical personnel to rush ringside and end the match rather than continuing the main event. Stacks was then loaded onto a stretcher and taken away, bringing the show to a sudden halt, though no official updates regarding his condition have been released yet.
