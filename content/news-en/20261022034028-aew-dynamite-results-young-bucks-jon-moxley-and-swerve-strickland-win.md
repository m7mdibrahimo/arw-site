AEW Dynamite aired live from the Watsco Center in Coral Gables, Florida, on October 21, 2026. Following AEW WrestleDream, Ilja Dragunov made his debut, introduced by Renee Paquette. In the opening match, AEW World Tag Team Champions The Young Bucks defeated Mark Davis and Jake Doyle in an Eliminator Match. Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight then defeated Adam Copeland and Will Ospreay after an interference by Kazuchika Okada. Swerve Strickland defeated Tome Filip, and Orange Cassidy and Jay White defeated The Death Riders. In the Continental Championship Eliminator Match, Jon Moxley defeated Roderick Strong. Post-match, a brawl involving David Finlay, Clark Connors, Gabe Kidd, and The Bang Bang Gang saw Finlay align with The Dogs. Finally, Queen Aminata defeated Maya World to become the number one contender for the TBS Championship.
