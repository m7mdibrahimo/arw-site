Kevin Knight has reflected on his time working as both a hero and a villain in AEW, revealing that he dislikes those traditional labels.

Speaking to Bleacher Report, the Don Callis Family member explained that every competitor shares the same motive of securing victories and championships.

> "Nobody got into this business to be a heel or a babyface. We all got into this business to win, and it's all about how you go about doing that."

Knight added that portraying a villain provides greater creative freedom since performers do not need audience validation.

Knight is scheduled to partner with Kyle Fletcher against the Young Bucks for the AEW World Tag Team Championship at WrestleDream.
