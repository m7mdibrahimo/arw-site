Harley Hudson was prepared to join The Righteous (Vincent and Dutch) on the November 5th episode of TNA iMPACT. However, Vincent and Dutch were displeased that Hudson made them wait for an answer. While she was deciding, former TNA Knockouts World Champion Xia Brookside attacked her. Later in the show, Hudson spoke to her mother on the phone about her struggle to find a home in TNA, noting she was not ready to return to the U.K. yet. She then discovered a note that read, "Come play with me." Upon following it, she found Taryn Terrell waiting by a dollhouse, who welcomed her to it. The Dollhouse was a faction in TNA from 2015 to 2016, originally founded by Terrell and featuring members such as Michin, Marti Belle, Rebel, and Kia Stevens (Awesome Kong).
