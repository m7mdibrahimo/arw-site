Perry Saturn does not plan to work as a producer in AEW soon. After watching Scotty 2 Hotty and Glacier work behind the scenes during Chris Jericho's cruise, Saturn realized he might quickly face issues with the human resources department.

Saturn talked during an interview on Chase's YouTube channel about observing his former colleagues, explaining that despite his extensive experience in putting together finishes, his personality does not fit with current locker room atmospheres. Saturn stated:

> "Now, maybe not so much because now they're all so sensitive. I'd definitely say something, hurt somebody's f feelings."

When Chase suggested that the wrestling world needs someone who speaks their mind, Saturn completely dismissed the idea, emphasizing that to avoid trouble and complicated situations with management, he prefers to stay away from that field and not try to change his personality.
