Elayna Black has nothing but love for what she has found in TNA, but she is not interested in burying WWE on her way out. When the topic of whether her previous locker rooms were toxic came up during an interview with the Daily Star, Black shut that description down immediately.

Black praised the creative freedom she has received in TNA, noting that it has allowed wrestlers to collaborate and bounce ideas off each other. She specifically mentioned Mustafa Ali as someone she regularly turns to for advice and feedback on her matches. While she acknowledged that every wrestling locker room has its own share of issues and drama, she firmly stated that she has never experienced a toxic environment in any company she has worked for.

When comparing the two promotions, Black pointed out that the main difference lies in creative control. While wrestlers can offer input in WWE, the final decision ultimately rests with management. In TNA, however, she feels she has a greater opportunity to collaborate and contribute to her creative direction, creating a more cohesive environment.
