Lance Storm says he will not return to the ring for another run, nearly a decade after his last match. Speaking to the Daily Star ahead of TNA Destination X, Storm explained that his body is simply done with active competition. He noted that he got out in good shape and does not want to risk his health just for another match. Storm also emphasized his high standards, stating that he refuses to put on a shirt and have an okay match just because fans would enjoy seeing him. He admitted that longtime friend Chris Jericho could have tempted him to break his retirement, but Jericho simply lasted too long and remains active. Ultimately, Storm confirmed that he and his wife have agreed his in-ring career is finished, meaning his match against Matt Hardy a decade ago will remain his last.
