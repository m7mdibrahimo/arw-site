Shayna Baszler made her TNA debut at TNA Bound for Glory 2026 alongside her former WWE stablemate Zoey Serrano, formerly known as Zoey Stark. The duo made an immediate impact by defeating Allie and Kierra Hogan to become the new TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Champions. Baszler opened the match by dominating Allie before tagging in Serrano to maintain control over Hogan with various holds. Despite a brief rally from Hogan, the challengers continued their assault. After Serrano neutralized Allie outside the ring, Baszler hit a knee strike on Allie and locked in the Kirifuda clutch. The referee called for the bell when Allie passed out, crowning Baszler and Serrano as the new champions.
