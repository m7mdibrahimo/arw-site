Chelsea Green will defend her title against Nikki Bella on the October 30 episode of WWE SmackDown in Charleston, West Virginia. The match was set after Nikki Bella confronted Green backstage last week, calling her a “Bootleg Nikki Bella” and criticizing the championship. Green initially offered a title shot for next week, but Nikki Bella requested the later date, which Green accepted. The following match is scheduled for the October 30 edition of WWE SmackDown: WWE Women’s Championship: Chelsea Green (c) vs. Nikki Bella.
