Grayson Waller recently criticized the WWE NXT roster, calling the talent soft, lazy, and complacent. Speaking with Chris Van Vliet, Waller explained that many wrestlers waste their opportunities and only realize their value after leaving the brand. He noted that newcomers often have a false sense of readiness for the main roster, comparing NXT to college where talent is heavily supported compared to the high-pressure environment of RAW and SmackDown. Waller also praised athletes like Kelani Jordan and Kendal Grey for showing genuine passion after transitioning from college sports to WWE.
