Josh Bishop is grateful for the experience of participating in the WWE tryouts during the 2026 SummerSlam weekend. The current MLW World Heavyweight Champion spoke to Sean Ross Sapp about the opportunity, expressing his appreciation. Bishop stated, "It was a great experience. I’m extremely grateful for the opportunity. Again, I really appreciated the opportunity. I had a great time, and really the best part was hanging out with Steve Corino for five days." Bishop recently won the MLW World Heavyweight Title at Saturday Night SuperFight by defeating Killer Kross in a Last Man Standing match.
