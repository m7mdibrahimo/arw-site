FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns have agreed to a best-of-five series to determine the top tag team in AEW, with the first match set for AEW WrestleDream. The challenge was issued during the October 14, 2026, episode of AEW Dynamite. Dax Harwood and Stokely Hathaway addressed the crowd, with Harwood expressing his desire for another shot at the AEW World Tag Team Championship. The Motor City Machine Guns, Chris Sabin and Alex Shelley, interrupted to challenge FTR directly. Sabin argued that the current state of the tag team division is what matters, and Shelley proposed the best-of-five series, drawing a comparison to FTR's past rivalry with The Young Bucks. Stokely Hathaway accepted the series on behalf of FTR but insisted that the competition must begin on a major stage like AEW WrestleDream.
