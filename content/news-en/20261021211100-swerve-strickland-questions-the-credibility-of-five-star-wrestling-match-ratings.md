AEW star Swerve Strickland recently shared his thoughts on the credibility of Wrestling Observer's famous five-star match ratings. Speaking with Billboard while discussing his career in hip-hop, Strickland compared wrestling match ratings to album reviews, arguing that both are entirely subjective. He noted that a match considered five stars by the publication might only be a four-star bout to anyone outside of that specific bubble. Strickland explained that true classics are rare and must stand the test of time, drawing a parallel to creating a legendary five-mic hip-hop album that resonates with critics, fans, and non-fans alike for years.
