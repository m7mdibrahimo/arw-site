AEW star Matt Jackson recently addressed the running joke among fans regarding The Young Bucks frequently using the same promotional photos for their match graphics across multiple events.

Speaking on an episode of Being The Elite, Matt jokingly explained that his brother Nick Jackson simply detests taking promotional pictures, a trait that dates back to his childhood days.

> "He hates the direction. He used to hate school picture day, yearbook day, and all that stuff. He's always complaining to my mom, like, 'God, I got to do these freaking photos, Mom? Yeah, you have to. Everyone has to, Nick. You go to the school? Yeah, you got to take a picture.' Look at the point. Look at his face."

Matt added that fans often notice the lack of updates in their graphics throughout the year simply because Nick refuses to sit through photo shoots.

> "This will be the only time you see us do this in 2026. When you're complaining and you're like, God, these photos have been used for 20 of the matches. You know what everyone goes, 'all the Young Bucks matches that I love this year, it's like the same freaking graphic,' because he doesn't want to do photos."
