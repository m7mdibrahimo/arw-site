AEW National Champion Andrade has warned his fans about a fake social media account impersonating him online. The impostor profile was using his nickname and advertising various professional opportunities, including meet-and-greets and wrestling bookings. After a fan alerted him to the fraudulent profile, Andrade shared a screenshot on his Instagram Story to confirm that the account had nothing to do with him and urged supporters to stay vigilant.
