Kofi Kingston recently discussed the build-up to his iconic KofiMania moment, expressing his belief that the historic run would not have happened if Mustafa Ali had not suffered an injury.

Speaking on The Mick and Kenny show, Kingston explained that Ali's unfortunate injury opened the door for him to enter the crucial gauntlet match and Elimination Chamber matches leading into WrestleMania 35, where he ultimately defeated Daniel Bryan to win the WWE championship.

Kingston acknowledged that while everything happened at the expense of Ali's setback, he believes everything happens for a reason, while also expressing admiration for Ali as a person.
