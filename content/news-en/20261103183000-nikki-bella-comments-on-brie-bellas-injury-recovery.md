Nikki Bella has provided an update on her sister Brie Bella's recovery following the injury she sustained at SummerSlam. Brie, who teamed with Nikki and Paige to face Fatal Influence, suffered a fractured scapula during the match. Nikki explained that the recovery process has been slow because Brie opted against surgery. "It's a slow process 'cause she didn't have surgery, but she couldn't [know] where the location of the breaks were. But she's doing well. She's in PT a few days a week. So it's kind of one of those things that you're just waiting for those bones to heal. And it's like, I think she gets another X-ray here soon to see, like, more of the growth. But she's so frustrated because when it's the scapula, like, she can't work out at all right now. So that's been really tough on her," Nikki told Justin Dorazio. Addressing the possibility of a singles match against her sister, Nikki downplayed the idea, noting that they are both nearing the end of their in-ring careers and prefer to work as a team. "I mean, now, like, after our injuries, I don't think so. I just don't think we're so, like, at the end of our in-ring careers that I feel like it just has to be together at this point," she added.
