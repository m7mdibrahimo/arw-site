Stu Grayson recently recalled a severe injury he suffered while setting up a ring and how he treated it himself before a match. Speaking in an interview, Grayson discussed his history of using makeshift medical treatments like duct tape and super glue instead of getting stitches. He shared a shocking incident involving his hand, explaining that he stabbed himself deeply with a knife while cutting a rope during ring setup. Despite the severe wound, he pulled the knife out, applied super glue and duct tape, and went on to wrestle.
