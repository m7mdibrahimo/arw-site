Triple H spoke highly of LA Knight following his performance at WWE Money In The Bank. Knight challenged Roman Reigns for the WWE World Heavyweight Title in the opening match of the event, and while he did not secure the win, his effort drew significant praise from the Chief Content Officer.

> LA Knight, to me, left this match greater than he went in. LA Knight proved that he is a megastar. He proved that he belongs in that title scene and that he is a top attraction in this business. Roman Reigns proved there is only one Roman Reigns.

Knight challenged Reigns with his full arsenal, including a BFT, but ultimately fell short after receiving multiple Superman Punches followed by a spear. Triple H emphasized that despite the loss, Knight solidified his status as a top-tier performer in the industry.
