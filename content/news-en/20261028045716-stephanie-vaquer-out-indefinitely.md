New reports have emerged regarding the absence of Stephanie Vaquer from WWE programming. Although Vaquer announced on Saturday that she was medically cleared from an ankle injury to face Becky Lynch on Monday's WWE RAW, general manager Adam Pearce opened the show by stating she was not cleared to compete.

According to Mike Johnson of PWInsider, Vaquer's absence is attributed to an illness. Cory Hays of False Finish reported that she will be out indefinitely.

> "out indefinitely."

Vaquer last wrestled during the WWE Hawaii Live Events on October 16 and 17. Previously, she competed in a triple threat match at WWE Money in the Bank against Liv Morgan and Becky Lynch, where she lost the WWE Women's World Title to Morgan.
