Hall of Famer Bully Ray recently discussed what he would like to see if the documentary series WWE Unreal returns for a fourth season. Ray noted that he would be particularly interested in the backstage lead-up to the 2026 Money in the Bank match between WWE World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns and LA Knight. "All I need to see is the day of," Ray said on Busted Open Radio. "I would be interested to know if anybody got in LA Knight's ear and was the voice of 'Hey, man, it's now or never tonight,' where it actually resonated." Ray added that he would love a look at the 24-hour build to the bout, praising the encounter itself. At the event in New Orleans, Roman Reigns defeated LA Knight using a Superman Punch and a spear to retain the World Heavyweight Championship, though Ray believes Knight also proved his worth as a top contender.
