Cody Rhodes is set to lead Team USA against Rey Mysterio and Team Mexico in the traditional Survivor Series Match at the upcoming Survivor Series: WarGames premium live event. Speaking on "Unsportsmanlike," Rhodes acknowledged the potential for real-life tensions to influence the cross-country showdown. "To kick it over to let's do Survivor Series where we just say, let's go team USA versus Team Mexico, now that's a positive, but there's room for some trouble along the way," Rhodes stated. He expressed concern that Team USA might be perceived as the "bad guys" in Houston, Texas, despite his intent to keep the storyline professional. Rhodes, who is currently joined by CM Punk and Je'Von Evans, emphasized that his team respects the other nations involved in the event, including Ireland and Samoa.

Rhodes specifically highlighted the addition of Je'Von Evans to his squad. Evans, who currently holds the WWE World Tag Team Championships with Penta, is viewed by Rhodes as a cornerstone of the company's future. "He's just the future of the business. I've been on here before and talked about Bron Breakker being the future of the business, but Bron's more traditional in terms of what you think pro wrestler is. If you come to a show and don't enjoy and a good feeling from watching Je'Von, it's crazy," Rhodes said. Evans transitioned to the WWE main roster in early 2026 and recently gained significant attention for his high-flying performance at the Men's Money in the Bank Ladder Match.
