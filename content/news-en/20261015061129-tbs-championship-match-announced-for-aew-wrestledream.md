AEW has officially confirmed a new title match for the upcoming AEW WrestleDream event. During the latest episode of AEW Collision, Dani Luna and Hyan faced off against Britt Baker and Persephone in tag team action.

Dani Luna secured a major upset by pinning Britt Baker, marking her first victory within the promotion. Following the match, the commentary team announced that Luna, Hyan, and Queen Aminata will challenge Persephone for the TBS Championship at the event.

Persephone enters the bout with momentum after defeating Luna and Hyan in September. Queen Aminata also carries strong momentum, having won five of her last six singles matches.
