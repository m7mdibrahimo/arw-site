Darby Allin recently spoke about completing the New York Marathon over the weekend with zero preparation, adding another feat to his list of achievements outside of professional wrestling. He finished the grueling run in just over five and a half hours.

Now, Allin turns his focus to competing tonight on AEW Dynamite in the violent Blood And Guts match, despite being only days removed from his marathon run. During an interview with Trent Osborne on 1010XL alongside Steven Borden, he discussed the physical toll of the race while heading into the marquee AEW event.

> "Yeah, thank you. Yeah, dude, I'm feeling every ounce of that. Because everyone's like, yo, congratulations, and then I was like, dude, I wish I trained for that. I didn't train once for it, and then… little did I know it was only going to be a few days removed from Blood and Guts. So I- and Blood and Guts is the most violent, crazy night of the year for AEW, and my legs are feeling all 26.2 miles of that marathon."
