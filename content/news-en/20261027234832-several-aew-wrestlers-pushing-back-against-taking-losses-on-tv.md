A recent backstage report indicates that certain wrestlers in AEW are pushing back against taking losses on television. According to Self Made Pro speaking on a Self Made Session, some talents in the midcard are unhappy with losing every week on Dynamite, with some even preferring to be pushed in Ring of Honor instead. The report also claims that members of the Don Callis Family who have not won a single match this year have turned down taking pins on television.

Self Made Pro noted that established main eventers like Will Ospreay and Hangman Page have no issues being team players because their spots are secure, unlike wrestlers fighting to keep their position. Concerns are further heightened by reports that lower-card AEW contracts might simply be allowed to expire rather than being renewed, leading some talents to protect their stock rather than agree to frequent losses.
