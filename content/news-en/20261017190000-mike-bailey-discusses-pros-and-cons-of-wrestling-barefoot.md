In a recent interview, Mike Bailey discussed the advantages and disadvantages of competing barefoot in the ring. Bailey explained that wrestling without shoes gives him extra balance, especially when grabbing the ropes or performing springboards, and he praised AEW for embracing his individuality. He also noted that he has very few foot injury issues compared to his Taekwondo days, though he admitted that running fast can sometimes be slightly more challenging depending on the canvas surface.
