During a recent interview, Billy Gunn reflected on Triple H taking a shot at AEW during the 2019 WWE Hall of Fame ceremony when D-Generation X was inducted. Gunn, who was already working with AEW at the time, admitted that he knew some mention of the company was coming because it was a hot topic. When fans chanted AEW, Triple H responded by joking that Vince McMahon would buy that company just to fire Gunn again. Gunn stated that while he expected a reaction, he felt the remark was unnecessary and ultimately hurt Triple H more than anyone else.
