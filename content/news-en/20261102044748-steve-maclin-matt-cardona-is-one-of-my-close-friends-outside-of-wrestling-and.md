Steve Maclin recently commented on his relationship with Matt Cardona during an interview with Sean Ross Sapp of Fightful. Maclin and Cardona previously faced each other in the ring on an episode of TNA iMPACT in April 2025. Maclin recalled a conversation he had with Cardona while they were visiting Epcot together. "Obviously he was like, 'I'm so pumped for you.' We were at Epcot walking around together and doing Disney Day and we were talking about it. He's like, 'You pumped for this?' I'm like, 'Yeah.' Of course he's like, 'How's the ankle? How's that feeling?' But at the same time, I think we were on the same par where Cardona and I know we're to go out there and we're going to kill it. We don't have to tell each other how to do things. We have that respect factor, especially being in the ring together," Maclin said. Maclin also expressed that he feels he and Cardona could have accomplished a lot more together in TNA, though it ultimately did not happen. "I felt he and I had so much mileage we could have done in TNA together, but that didn't come to fruition. Hopefully at some point that'd be an awesome feud to have because I loved working with Matt and he's one of my close friends outside of wrestling and he's somebody that I go to for advice," he added.
