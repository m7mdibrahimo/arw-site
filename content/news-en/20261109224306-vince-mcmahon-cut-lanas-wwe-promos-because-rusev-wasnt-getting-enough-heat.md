CJ Perry revealed that Vince McMahon deliberately reduced her speaking role as Lana in WWE because Rusev wasn't getting enough negative crowd reactions during their rivalry with John Cena. Speaking on Wrestling with Heart with Stanley Karr, Perry explained that her outspoken Russian character was drawing too much heat away from Rusev, who needed to carry more of the negative reaction as the heel stepping into the ring against Cena. Perry noted that unlike male managers such as Paul Heyman, she couldn't take physical bumps from Cena during that era, prompting McMahon to adjust her television time around October and November so Rusev could do more of the talking.

This reduction in her role eventually led to her babyface turn. When Perry missed a television appearance, Rusev brought out a Chicago lawyer who received a poor reaction, prompting the live crowd to chant for Lana. Perry recalled that hearing the fans chant for her gave McMahon the idea to turn her babyface. Ahead of WrestleMania 31, Perry was unsure of her status until Rusev personally asked McMahon to include her in his tank entrance, though Cena ultimately defeated him for the United States Championship.
