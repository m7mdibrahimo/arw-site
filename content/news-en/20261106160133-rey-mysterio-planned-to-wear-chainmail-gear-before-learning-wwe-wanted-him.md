Rey Mysterio unmasked at WCW SuperBrawl 1999 and competed without his mask for the remainder of his WCW tenure. When he joined WWE in June 2002 following the company's acquisition of WCW, he debuted wearing his mask once again. During an appearance on What's Your Story? with Stephanie McMahon, Mysterio revealed the original ring gear he had prepared before discovering that Vince McMahon wanted him to wear the mask. 

> "I went to OVW a week prior to my debut and I was training there. I forgot who came up to me, but they asked me if I knew what I was going to wear. 'I'm going to wear baggy pants.' At the time, I was using the [chainmail] headgear that Big Poppa Pump [Scott Steiner] would wear. I had the vest. 'I'm thinking of wearing that.' They were like, 'What about the mask?' 'I'm not wrestling with the mask anymore.' 'No, Vince wants you with the mask back on.' 'Nobody ever told me that; that's even better.' I had something made and that's when I came out with the red outfit. It was really cool to bring the mask back," he said.

Mysterio made his official WWE debut on the July 25, 2002, episode of SmackDown, defeating Chavo Guerrero.
