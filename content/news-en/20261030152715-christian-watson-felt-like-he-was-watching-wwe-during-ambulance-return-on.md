During Thursday Night Football, the Green Bay Packers secured a 27-19 victory over the Carolina Panthers. In the second quarter, Packers wide receiver Christian Watson suffered a chest injury and was taken to the hospital in an ambulance. In a surprising turn of events, Watson returned to the game during the third quarter and was once again caught on camera exiting the ambulance. Speaking with reporters after the game, Watson compared the surreal experience to watching WWE. "I used to watch WWE as a kid, I felt like I was watching WWE SmackDown vs. Raw or something out there. Seeing the cameras on me coming out the ambulance, that was a little crazy. It was a surreal experience, no doubt. Just to go through that and have the fans cheering for me being back out there was definitely exciting."

Following his return, Watson added 2 receptions for 13 yards, finishing the night with 8 catches for 113 yards in total.
