During his appearance on LiMPiN AiNT EASY, Truth Magnum of The Outrunners discussed how he and Turbo Floyd secured their AEW contracts. Magnum revealed that after persistently taking extra work and driving hours to make it to shows, AEW President Tony Khan eventually assumed they were already signed. Magnum also shared the surreal moment he received a message from Khan asking if he wanted to wrestle Sting the next day.

Magnum added that former AEW coach Mark Henry strongly advocated for their signing. According to Magnum, Henry praised their tag team style and spoke with Tony Khan twice on their behalf to urge him to sign them. The Outrunners officially signed with AEW in 2024 and later had a run as the ROH World Six-Man Tag Team Champions alongside Dalton Castle, which ended in August 2026.
