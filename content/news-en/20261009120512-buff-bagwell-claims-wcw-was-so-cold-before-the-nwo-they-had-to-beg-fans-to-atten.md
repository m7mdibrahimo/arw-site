Buff Bagwell recently opened up about the difficult times WCW faced before the nWo completely transformed the company. According to Bagwell, the promotion struggled to fill the small Center Stage venue in Atlanta and had to give away prizes like bicycles during marathon television tapings just to keep fans in their seats.

Bagwell revealed that WCW even hired a man and a woman for seventy-five dollars to sit in camera-friendly spots so television viewers would see presentable smiles during commercial breaks. Despite having major stars like Sting, Lex Luger, the Nasty Boys, Randy Savage, and Hulk Hogan on the roster, the company initially struggled to draw interest.

Everything changed once the nWo arrived and propelled WCW to massive success, eventually selling out venues like the Georgia Dome. Bagwell noted that this historic shift defined the biggest stretch of his career and turned the promotion into a global phenomenon.
