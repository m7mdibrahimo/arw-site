The Miz is promoting the John Cena Classic, but he has not received an invitation to compete in it yet. During an interview with Brandon Kravitz, he complained that Cena has already given special coins and letters to other Superstars. Miz pointed to his 20 years in WWE and his WrestleMania 27 victory over Cena for the WWE Championship to argue he deserves a spot. He also noted that he asked Cena about getting physical at WrestleMania, but Cena told him that his in-ring days in WWE are over. Despite feeling snubbed, Miz praised the tournament's format, which pairs Raw and SmackDown stars with NXT talent and allows Club members to vote for the show-stealing Superstar.
