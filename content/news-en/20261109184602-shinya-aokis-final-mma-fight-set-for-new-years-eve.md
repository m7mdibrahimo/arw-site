Shinya Aoki is stepping away from mixed martial arts and is scheduled to have his final MMA bout on December 31, 2026. The 49-13-1 fighter will compete at Super RIZIN 6. Aoki recently signed a contract with DDT Pro-Wrestling in October 2026 after working as a freelancer with the promotion since 2018. His last two MMA fights ended in losses.
