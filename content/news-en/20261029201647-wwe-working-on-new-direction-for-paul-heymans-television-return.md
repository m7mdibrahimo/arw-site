Paul Heyman has been absent from WWE television for a few weeks, which raised questions about his current status. Reports indicate that WWE creative is working on a new idea for his return to television in the near future. The plan involves expanding his role rather than returning him to the exact same spot he occupied before.

According to backstage reports, WWE wants Heyman to branch out into another program outside of The Vision faction. However, he will not completely disconnect from the group. Bron Breakker is expected to serve as Heyman's main connection to The Vision, as WWE considers Heyman an important part of Breakker's ongoing presentation on television.

Heyman was last seen on the October 12 episode of WWE RAW after previously pushing Bronson Reed to handle Oba Femi on the October 5 show. While details about the new storyline or potential partners remain unknown, WWE aims to keep him active with fresh creative plans while maintaining his ties to Breakker.
