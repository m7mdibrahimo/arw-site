Mickie Knuckles has opened up about the dangerous flaming casket spot that landed her in a hospital burn unit, stating that she was explicitly promised the fire would go out. Speaking on Jasmin St. Claire’s Off the Rails podcast, Knuckles explained that she voiced her concerns before agreeing to the stunt, but trusted assurances that it would be safe. Instead, the flames did not extinguish, leaving her with severe second- and third-degree burns.

Knuckles recalled that during the chaotic moment inside the fire, her shoulder popped out of socket as she tried to pull herself up. She credited a man named Steven with saving her life by grabbing her ankle and pulling her out before the smoke could damage her esophagus. Following the harrowing incident, Knuckles admitted that after 24 years in wrestling, she is finally learning that she has the right to say no to dangerous stunts.
