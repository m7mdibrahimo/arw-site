Nia Jax recently addressed accusations of nepotism regarding her WWE career, explaining that while she is proud of her famous wrestling family, she has earned her own success. During an appearance on the Battleground Podcast, Jax revealed that she originally tried to keep her connection to The Rock and the Anoa'i family quiet when she first tried out for WWE to ensure she could succeed on her own merits.

Jax noted that having famous family members is a great thing and she refuses to downplay their accomplishments, pointing out that they do not get in the ring, travel, or get injured for her. She also compared wrestling families to other multi-generational talents like Cody Rhodes and Randy Orton, noting that growing up around the business provides a distinct advantage in learning how the industry works before ever stepping foot in a ring.
