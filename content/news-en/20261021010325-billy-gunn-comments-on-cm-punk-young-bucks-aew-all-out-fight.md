AEW star and coach Billy Gunn addressed the famous backstage brawl between CM Punk and The Elite that took place after the All Out 2022 press conference. Gunn acknowledged that while he was not present during the incident, he understood how tensions boiled over behind the scenes.

Gunn explained that younger talent must respect veteran boundaries in the professional wrestling business. He noted that certain experienced performers will not tolerate disrespect from younger stars.

> "If you're a young little buckaroo and you want to poke the bear, get ready to get bit."

Reflecting on his tenure in WWE under Vince McMahon, Gunn mentioned that backstage physical altercations were rare because management maintained strict control. He added that veterans understood how to handle personal differences professionally without letting them disrupt business.
