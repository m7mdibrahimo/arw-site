AEW Women's World Champion Mercedes Moné shared a heartbreaking update in her Moné Mag newsletter, revealing that she lost her younger sister, Alexis, in September, leaving behind two young children. Moné reflected on their cherished childhood memories together, including attending WrestleMania in Atlanta. She then paid tribute to the late PAC, real name Benjamin Satterley, who passed away at age 40 following AEW All Out. Moné, who worked with PAC when he was known as Neville in WWE NXT and had known him since their FCW days, recalled their shared memories playing games on UpUpDownDown and listening to Sami Zayn's stories on road trips. She also discussed her participation in the tribute show on AEW Dynamite, noting how emotionally difficult the experience was for her but emphasizing that being in the ring was the best way to honor him.
