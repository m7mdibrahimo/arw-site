Cody Rhodes is keeping track of which wrestlers supported his new Street Fighter movie. Speaking on The Rich Eisen Show, the WWE star admitted he noticed who showed up, who promoted the film, and who didn't. During the conversation, Rhodes mentioned Seth Rollins attending the premiere and praised him for it. Rhodes explained that he feels strongly about this because the movie's success can help push the entire wrestling industry forward.
