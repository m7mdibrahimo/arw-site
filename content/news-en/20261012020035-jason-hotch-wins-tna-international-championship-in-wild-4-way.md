Jason Hotch captured the TNA International Championship at TNA Bound for Glory 2026, defeating Mustafa Ali, Special Agent Zero, and John Skyler in a chaotic four-way match. The contest began with Hotch, Skyler, and Zero temporarily aligning to neutralize Ali. The action escalated quickly as participants utilized ringside surroundings, including a table and steel steps. During the match, Tasha Steelz attempted to interfere but was neutralized by Mila Moore, who laid Steelz and Zero across a table before Ali struck them with a 450 splash. In the final moments, Hotch executed a superplex on Ali from the steel steps. Hotch ultimately secured the victory by rolling up Ali for the pinfall after trading heavy strikes with his opponents.
