Nixon Newell is voicing her frustration after fans discovered her private home address and began sending unsolicited gifts. The former WWE star, who has been vocal about her upcoming retirement, took to her Instagram Story to address the situation.

> "While I’m incredibly appreciative of any gifts I’m bought, what’s unacceptable is finding my address & sending random stuff to my home."

Newell acknowledged that the fans likely intended to be kind, but she emphasized that the breach of her privacy was inappropriate and unsettling. 

> "I appreciate the gesture but idk how you found my address & it’s a little creepy."

This incident occurs as Newell approaches the end of her wrestling career. In February 2026, she announced that she expects her final match to take place in December 2026. Newell had previously stated her desire to enjoy her final year in the ring before transitioning to the next chapter of her life.
