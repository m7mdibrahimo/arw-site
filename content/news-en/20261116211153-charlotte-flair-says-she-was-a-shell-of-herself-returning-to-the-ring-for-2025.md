Charlotte Flair recently discussed her two Royal Rumble victories, noting that she felt like a different person during her 2025 return. Flair returned from a lengthy knee injury to win the women's Royal Rumble match for the second time in her career at the 2025 Royal Rumble event.

Speaking on a new episode of Outkick, Flair reflected on the contrasting feelings between her two Rumble wins. "The first Royal Rumble that I won in Houston, it definitely was a ‘this is my division’ (moment), I actually could not believe I won. It was such a surreal moment. I felt it in all my being. But when I came back the second time in 2025, after my knee injury, I very much was a shell of myself. Hadn't been in the ring in a year, year and a half. Were people gonna remember ‘The Queen?’ Even with my accolades, ‘cause I always think, you know, you're just as good as your last match."

Flair admitted that the pressure of the return made her nervous about how the crowd would receive her. "So, I was very nervous. Wasn't sure what the reception was gonna be, but knowing that I had crossed the finish lines in terms of when I was away, being back in that ring was all that I thought about. So, all that preparation to get back and how hard it was to get back to me on a professional and personal level meant the world to me."
