Shakur Stevenson has officially secured his release from Zuffa Boxing after never fighting for the promotion. Stevenson announced the news on Monday, explaining that he requested his release following delays in getting a fight booked.

> “I’ve been working my ass off,” Stevenson said. “Staying focused, staying in shape. I’ve been in camp for, like, eight weeks preparing for fights that I was told was going to happen and it has not happened but look, it is what it is. This is the business of boxing. I’m not mad at anyone, I’m not pointing no fingers, but upon all of these issues and things that’s been going on, I requested to be released from Zuffa Boxing.”

Stevenson is now a free agent and plans to start his own promotional company while remaining open to working with other promoters. He signed a multi-fight deal with Zuffa Boxing in July, boasts a 25-0 career record, and holds the WBO title at 140 pounds after last fighting in January when he defeated Teofimo Lopez.
