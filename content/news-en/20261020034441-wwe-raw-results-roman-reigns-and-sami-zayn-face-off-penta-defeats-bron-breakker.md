The October 19, 2026, episode of WWE RAW at the Pechanga Arena in San Diego featured a major confrontation between Roman Reigns and Sami Zayn. Reigns, who arrived at the arena earlier in the night, encountered Zayn in the ring. The two discussed their history, with Reigns suggesting that Zayn’s Honorary Uce title was more significant than the Undisputed WWE Championship. Reigns also dismissed the value of the Undisputed title since WrestleMania XL, insisting the World Heavyweight Championship is now the premier title. Zayn defended his achievements, leading to a tense exchange before Reigns departed.

In the opening match, "Mr. Money in the Bank" Penta defeated WWE World Tag Team Champion Bron Breakker. The finish saw Je'Von Evans interfere by striking Breakker with the briefcase behind the referee's back, allowing Penta to secure the pin. Later, WWE Women’s Champion Chelsea Green and IYO SKY defeated The Judgment Day's Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez when SKY rolled up Rodriguez for the victory.

In other action, JD McDonagh defeated Joe Hendry after Dominik Mysterio assisted with a guitar smash. Finally, Lola Vice defeated Lyra Valkyria following interference from Bayley, who grabbed Valkyria's leg from under the ring.

Matches and Results:
Singles Match: Penta def. Bron Breakker
Tag Team Match: Chelsea Green and IYO SKY def. Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez
Singles Match: JD McDonagh def. Joe Hendry
Singles Match: Lola Vice def. Lyra Valkyria
