Former WWE NXT and AEW wrestler Amber Flora, also known on the independent scene as Amber Nova, is facing legal issues following her arrest in Orlando, Florida. According to Ringside News, official documents confirm that the 34-year-old was detained on Friday on charges of driving under the influence and refusing to submit to testing. She was released after posting a $2,000 bond.

Police reports indicate that officers stopped Flora near a major city road without any traffic accident occurring. She was transported to a testing facility, but records show that no valid breath samples were collected, resulting in no official blood alcohol concentration reading.

Flora, who has appeared for WWE NXT, AEW, and Impact Wrestling and held the SHINE Nova Championship in 2023, attended a mandatory court appearance. A future date has been set for the legal proceedings to continue.
