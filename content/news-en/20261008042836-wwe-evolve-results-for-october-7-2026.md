WWE presented the latest episode of WWE EVOLVE on October 7, broadcasting from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, via the Tubi platform. The event opened with Marcus Mathers expressing his frustration with DarkState and Elijah Holyfield, leading to a confrontation with Cutler James.

Cutler James def. Marcus Mathers
Anya Rune def. Veronica Haven

Following the second match, Sloane Jacobs and Nikkita Lyons attacked Anya Rune, placing a trash can over her head before assaulting her. Nikkita Lyons declared she would defeat Wendy Choo next week, prompting PJ Vasa to challenge her to try that against her instead. Backstage, AJ Styles provided Conan Lycan with a WWE ID deal, while The MOG Squad expressed dissatisfaction regarding a mystery opponent for Max Abrams.

Aaron Rourke def. Kale Dixon by DQ

Wendy Choo stated her intention to regain her title next week. Max Abrams revealed he remains injured, leading CJ Valor to take his place against a mystery opponent.

Jasper Troy def. CJ Valor

Following the main event, Aaron Rourke promised to bring destruction to Kale Dixon. Additionally, Harley Riggins and Harlem Lewis engaged in a brawl around the ringside area.
