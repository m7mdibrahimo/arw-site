WWE plans to alter a major match ahead of Survivor Series: WarGames, according to Ringside News. The SmackDown run sheet indicates that Trick Williams will leave Team USA because he failed to receive medical clearance to participate in the men's WarGames bout. Danhausen will step forward to take Williams's spot, but the company has different plans for the opening. Cody Rhodes will announce later that the fifth member of Team USA will be unveiled on the November 23 episode of WWE RAW instead of the original plan. This adjustment shakes up the roster days prior to Survivor Series, leaving the identity of the final participant a mystery.
