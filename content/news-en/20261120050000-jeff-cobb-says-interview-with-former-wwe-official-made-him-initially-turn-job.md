Jeff Cobb, formerly known as JC Mateo in WWE, revealed that an interview with former WWE talent development head Canyon Ceman made him initially turn down job offers from the promotion. In a recent appearance on Chris Van Vliet's 'Insight' podcast, Cobb recalled a 2021 video call where Ceman appeared in bed wearing boxer shorts and a T-shirt, which he felt lacked professionalism. Cobb also took issue with Ceman mentioning that WWE pays its talent during the pandemic compared to other companies. Despite being a huge fan of WWE in his youth, Cobb rejected multiple offers, including one after a 2020 match against Jon Moxley in AEW and another following the Ceman interview. He eventually signed a three-year deal with NJPW and joined them in 2025.
