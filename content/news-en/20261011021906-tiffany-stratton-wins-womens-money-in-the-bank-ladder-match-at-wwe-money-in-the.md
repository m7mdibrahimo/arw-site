Tiffany Stratton secured a future world title opportunity after winning a chaotic Women’s Money in the Bank ladder match at WWE Money in the Bank 2026. She outlasted Jacy Jayne, Lola Vice, Lash Legend, Roxanne Perez, and Sol Ruca in a brutal encounter featuring high-risk moves and ladder collisions. Stratton survived heavy blows and executed a dangerous swanton dive to the outside early in the match. Down the stretch, she battled Lash Legend at the top of the ladder before striking her with the briefcase to knock her down. Stratton then unhooked the briefcase to become the winner and earn a championship match anytime she chooses.
