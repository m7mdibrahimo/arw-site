Major League Wrestling held its MLW Saturday Night SuperFight event on Saturday, November 7 from Cicero Stadium in Chicago, Illinois, with the show streaming on VEEPS.

In an opening title contest, The Good Brothers (Karl Anderson and Doc Gallows) defended the MLW Tag Team Championship against Glasgow Boys On Top (Joe Coffey, Mark Coffey, and Wolfgang) in a street fight to retain their titles. Later in the broadcast, Masha defeated Zamaya in singles action. Before Mistico could compete in his scheduled match, he was ambushed and attacked by Big Damo and Brick Savage. In another backstage development, Josh Bishop and Donovan Dijak were seen brawling behind the scenes.

Tessa Blanchard picked up a victory over Keyra. Following the contest, Blanchard declared that it felt great to be competing in MLW, and she later confronted Zamaya backstage. In the championship division, the MLW National Openweight Championship bout between Austin Aries and Matt Riddle reached the 30-minute time limit to end in a draw. Aries addressed the crowd afterward, saying he knew everyone wanted five more minutes but refused to give it to them. Riddle responded by knocking Aries out and taking the title belt. In another featured bout, Ultimo Guerrero defeated Templario and Titan.
