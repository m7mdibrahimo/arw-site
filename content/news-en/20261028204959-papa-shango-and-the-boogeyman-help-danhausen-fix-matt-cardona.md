Danhausen hosted his Halloween special, which featured appearances from two iconic spooky WWE figures, Papa Shango and The Boogeyman.

The show included worm-eating adventures before the trio attempted to help Matt Cardona, who is currently sidelined with a triceps tear. During the special, Danhausen, Shango, and Boogeyman planned to replace Cardona's injured arm with a new one, while his wife Chelsea Green left them to their work.
