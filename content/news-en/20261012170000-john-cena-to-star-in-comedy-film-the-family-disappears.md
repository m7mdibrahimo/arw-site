WWE legend John Cena will star in a new comedy film titled "The Family Disappears," according to Deadline. The project will also feature David Harbour and Dan Matteucci, with Jonatan Etzler serving as director. Filming is scheduled to begin on November 10 in Atlanta, Georgia. The story follows an overburdened father who discovers that his family has vanished and a stranger has taken over his home.

Fifth Season is producing the film, having previously worked on projects like "The Lost Daughter" and "Killing Eve." This venture adds to Cena's busy acting schedule following the release of his films "Coyote vs. Acme" and "Little Brother" earlier this year, with "Matchbox: The Movie" and other titles upcoming.

Although he admits to missing wrestling, Cena recently shared that he enjoys watching the sport as a fan following his WWE retirement. This transition allows him to fully dedicate himself to his blossoming Hollywood career.
