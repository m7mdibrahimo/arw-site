Cody Rhodes recently shared that gaming is a major part of the culture within the WWE locker room. In an interview with ExtraTV, Rhodes explained that there is always a monitor set up backstage for wrestlers to play games, and the environment is very welcoming to newcomers.

> The locker room, actually, the Usos were always really big on Tekken. It's still played in the locker room to this day. There's always a little monitor set up. Also, no gatekeeping. You can jump right in, and they want you to jump right in because these guys have gotten really good. Nakayama, who's responsible for the modern Street Fighter, I also played him in Australia. Dog walked me. So I need to get better at the modern franchise versus the vintage, the OG.

Rhodes noted that he is currently working on improving his skills in modern fighting games. Roman Reigns also recently discussed the gaming culture among WWE talent in a separate interview.
