AEW is heading into the end of the year with the Continental Classic season. Following AEW Full Gear 2026, the selection show is scheduled for Monday, with the tournament beginning on the November 18 episode of "AEW Dynamite."

During the post-show press conference at Full Gear, AEW CEO Tony Khan discussed the possibility of expanding the tournament format. With fans frequently requesting a women's version of the tournament or a tag team tournament, Khan expressed openness to the idea.

> "I do think there are potential for other tournaments, too. They don't necessarily have to have the same branding as the C2, but there's a potential for a lot of great tournaments we can do. I think the C2 has been hugely, hugely successful and it's a round robin format... There are some things about it that are unique to its identity that I think are very strong."

Khan highlighted the tournament's rule banning outside interference, which he considers a key part of its identity. He noted that in nearly 100 matches across three tournaments, the rule has never been tested.

> "It would be the end of that person. Nobody will ever do it. There will never be outside interference in it, and I think that's part of what makes it so unique because I can't think of another circumstance that's been tested this many times."

While Khan praised the tournament's unique scoring system, he did not announce any official plans for new tournament brands.
