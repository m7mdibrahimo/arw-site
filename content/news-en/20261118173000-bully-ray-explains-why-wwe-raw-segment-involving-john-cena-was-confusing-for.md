Bully Ray discussed John Cena's recent segment on WWE Raw involving Liv Morgan and whether it was the right decision to bring Becky Lynch into the angle. On the latest episode of WWE Raw, Cena made an appearance to promote the John Cena Classic, inviting WWE Women's World Champion Liv Morgan to participate. Cena acknowledged Morgan as a talented competitor whom fans support because they recognize her potential. During the segment, Lynch interrupted, an unexpected moment that Ray noted on Busted Open caused some confusion among the fans. Ray explained that if Cena endorses someone, the audience respects that choice because of Cena's status. However, when Lynch interrupted and criticized Morgan, the crowd became unresponsive because they were not going to cheer for Lynch insulting someone Cena had just praised and selected for his tournament. Ray also pointed out that Cena had aligned himself with Morgan in his promo by noting their shared struggles and paths to success. Lynch then accused Morgan of holding back her Judgment Day partners, Roxanne Perez and Raquel Rodriguez, and claimed Morgan could not have achieved her success without the group.
