Cody Rhodes recently discussed the strongest wrestlers he has ever faced during an appearance on the Double Coverage Podcast. Rhodes highlighted several names known for their immense power, including Mark Henry and Big Show, noting their unique "reserve strength." He also praised the power of Big E, Cesaro, John Cena, and Bron Breakker.

However, Rhodes singled out Brock Lesnar for a different quality. While acknowledging Lesnar's imposing physical presence, Rhodes emphasized that Lesnar's speed was what truly set him apart. He noted that watching Lesnar compete in the UFC made it clear that his agility was what made him a one-of-a-kind performer. Rhodes stated, "His speed was genuinely terrifying from a performative standpoint. He just was one of one, unicorn."
