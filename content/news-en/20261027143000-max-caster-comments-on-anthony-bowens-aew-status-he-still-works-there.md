Max Caster has commented on the AEW status of his former tag team partner Anthony Bowens. Earlier in October, reports indicated that both Bowens and Caster were free agents after parting ways with All Elite Wrestling. While Bowens addressed his future during a media appearance without clarifying his official status, Caster recently stated at a virtual signing that Bowens remains with the company. Caster noted that Bowens is still wrestling there and expressed that he believes Bowens still works for AEW. Additionally, Bowens recently shared a photo on social media of himself training at the NJPW Dojo.
