Chelsea Green’s celebration at WWE Crown Jewel 2026 turned into a nightmare when Tiffany Stratton betrayed her to win the WWE Women’s Championship. Green had just defeated Liv Morgan to capture the Women’s Crown Jewel Championship, leading to a celebratory segment with Paul Levesque.

During an emotional speech about her 13-year career, Green was attacked by Morgan. Stratton rushed to the ring, initially appearing to assist Green against Morgan and other interferences from Roxanne Perez and Raquel Rodriguez. However, Stratton quickly turned on Green, cashing in her Money in the Bank contract.

Stratton executed the Prettiest Moonsault Ever to secure the victory and the WWE Women’s Championship. While Green retained the Women’s Crown Jewel Championship, she lost the WWE Women’s title in the process. The betrayal served as a major turning point for Stratton, who capitalized on an exhausted opponent to claim the championship.
