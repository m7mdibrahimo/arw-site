WWE talent have safely arrived in Saudi Arabia for the Crown Jewel 2026 Premium Live Event, scheduled for November 7 at the ANB Arena in Riyadh. According to Bryan Alvarez of Wrestling Observer, all scheduled WWE performers have reached the country without encountering any problems. Personnel involved with Power Slap, which has been operating in the region throughout the week, have also reported no issues. The arrival comes after weeks of uncertainty regarding the company's decision to proceed with the event amid regional security concerns, including reports of rebel attacks targeting infrastructure. TKO CEO Ari Emanuel had previously stated that WWE was monitoring the situation closely. The event was moved from the outdoor Riyadh Season Stadium to the indoor ANB Arena, and WWE has shifted a significant portion of its broadcast production to be managed remotely from WWE Headquarters in Stamford, Connecticut. While a production crew remains on-site in Riyadh to handle cameras and audio, the remote setup reduces the number of staff members required to travel. With the roster in place and production adjustments finalized, WWE is moving forward with the show as planned.
