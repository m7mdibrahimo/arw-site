Joe Hendry believes he can make a major impact in WWE. During an interview with Scott Fishman of TV Insider, Hendry discussed his career goals and his desire to be a top star for the company.

"I’m just always looking to get better at what I do. I know in my heart that I can be a top draw for WWE. I know people have things to say about that, but the truth is I know that I can be a top guy, I know that I can be an asset to the professional wrestling business because I have the metrics to prove it," Hendry said.

Hendry has been a regular presence on WWE RAW since joining the brand earlier in the year. He has also been mocking JD McDonagh using a Funko Pop on recent shows and stars in WWE's first microdrama Agent of Shadows.
