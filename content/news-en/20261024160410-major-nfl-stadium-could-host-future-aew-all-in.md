AEW running All In at the home of the Philadelphia Eagles is a genuine possibility for the future, according to referee and travel department manager Bryce Remsburg. Speaking on The Certified Mark Show, Remsburg noted that Lincoln Financial Field has already hosted WrestleMania and that Philadelphia is one of AEW's strongest markets, making the stadium a strong candidate if the company looks for a domestic venue. While he admitted that the timing might not be right for next year due to current demand, Remsburg emphasized that his assessment is based on actual insider knowledge rather than just fan speculation, leaving the door wide open for a potential stadium show down the line.
