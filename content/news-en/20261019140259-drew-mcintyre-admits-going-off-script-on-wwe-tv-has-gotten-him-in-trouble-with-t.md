Drew McIntyre recently opened up about his creative freedom in WWE, revealing that altering his promos during live television broadcasts has sometimes landed him in trouble with Triple H. Speaking on a podcast, McIntyre explained that while WWE's creative team establishes the basic direction of a storyline, he relies on real-time crowd reactions to guide his words on the microphone. He noted that if fans are not responding to the planned material, he is willing to change direction, which can result in either a high five or a reprimand from the boss backstage.
