Paul Heyman spoke about a terrifying phone call he received from his daughter during the COVID-19 pandemic after she was involved in a severe car accident, emphasizing that his only concern was making sure she was safe.

Speaking to Graham Bensinger, Heyman explained that his daughter left home to grab food and called him minutes later to report the crash. He rushed to the location with his son Jacob, where he saw the heavy damage to her vehicle after another driver distracted by a mobile phone had crashed into her.

Seeing the totaled car made Heyman realize how easily the night could have ended in tragedy. He noted that his reaction of putting his child's safety above all else came from his late father.
