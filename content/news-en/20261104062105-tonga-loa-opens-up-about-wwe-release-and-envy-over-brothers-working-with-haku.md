Tonga Loa opened up about his recent release from WWE and shared his thoughts on his second run with the company. During a virtual signing for K & S WrestleFest, Loa reflected on his time on the main roster alongside his brothers, Tama Tonga and Talla Tonga, and their father, Haku. When asked about his departure, Loa admitted that he had a feeling it was coming after a wave of releases and being let go the following week alongside Jeff Cobb. He also expressed a bit of envy that his brothers get to work on the road with their father, though he remains happy for them.
