Game Changer Wrestling held its GCW The Crossroads event on November 7 from the Fountain Square Theater in Indianapolis, IN. The show aired on TrillerTV+.

In the main event, Atticus Cogar defeated Joseph Sawyer to win the GCW World Championship. Following the match, Mance Warner and EFFY attacked Cogar.

Here are the full results from the event:

GCW World Tag Team Championships: KJ Orso and Sam Stackhouse (c) def. Davey Bang and August Matthews to retain the titles.

Tim Bosby def. Isaiah Broner.

Gabby Forza def. Calvin Tankman.

Bustah and The Brain (Alec Price and Jordan Oliver) def. Joey Janela and Ryan O’Neill.

SLADE (w/ Amy Rose) def. Gary Jay.

Rachel Armstrong, Logan Cavazos, Sal Mistretta, Kavron Kanyon, and Jimmy Lloyd def. Leo Fox, Zach Thomas, Katrina Creed, Victor Analog, and Aaron Williams.

Deathmatch: Vipress, Anakin Murphy, and Scotty Vortekz def. Sicko The Clown, Nino Extremo, and Mr. Danger.

Spyder Nate Webb def. Haley J.
