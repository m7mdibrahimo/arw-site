AEW executive Jeff Jarrett was pleased to see the promotion experiment with new concepts during the WrestleDream pay-per-view. The event featured the debut of the Clockwork Carnage match, in which Will Ospreay successfully defended his AEW World Championship. Jarrett praised the show overall and was particularly impressed by the innovative match type, which he believes will be a success.

> "I just sat there and looked at the players in the Clockwork Carnage, and I said, I think there's probably people that are not really sure because you've never seen it how it's going to play out. But when I kind of walked through it and thought it through, I'm like, this is going to steal the show," he said. "I thought that was fantastic."

Jarrett, who has a history of creating unique match types like the King of the Mountain match in TNA Wrestling, noted his appreciation for innovation. "I love innovative. I love trying new things. I love having the audience get engaged at a different level," he added. "I just think the progression, I loved it." While Jarrett was high on the concept, host Conrad Thompson disagreed, stating he felt the match should have been reserved for a television special rather than main-eventing a pay-per-view.
