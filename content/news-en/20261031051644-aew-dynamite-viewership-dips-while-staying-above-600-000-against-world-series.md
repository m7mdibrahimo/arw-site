According to Wrestlenomics, the October 28 episode of AEW Dynamite saw a decline in viewership compared to the previous week. The broadcast on TBS averaged 620,000 viewers, falling from the 661,000 viewers recorded on October 21.

The program earned a 0.14 rating in the 18 to 49 demographic, a slight decrease from the 0.15 rating achieved the week before. The show faced stiff competition from the fifth game of the World Series featuring the Milwaukee Brewers and the Tampa Bay Rays. Although the event was also simulcast on HBO Max, specific viewership data for that platform remains unavailable.
