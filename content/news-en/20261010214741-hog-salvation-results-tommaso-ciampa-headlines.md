House of Glory Wrestling presented HOG Salvation on October 9 from the NYC Arena in Jamaica, NY. The event was broadcast on YouTube.

Amazing Red def. Marcus Mathers to retain the HOG Crown Jewel Championship.

Motor City Machine Guns def. Miracle Generation.

Daron Richardson def. Smiley.

The Mane Event def. The Skywalkers.

Mantequilla def. Vic Vendetta, Angel Jacquez, Onyx Amagi, Tiny Giant, and JJ Doze in a six-way scramble match.

Malakai Black and Xelina def. Lio Rush and Shotzi Blackheart.

Tommaso Ciampa def. Charles Mason in the main event.
