Drew McIntyre recently shared the screen with Elijah Holyfield in Agent Of Shadows. Speaking on The Joe Vulpis Podcast, McIntyre lauded the NXT talent for possessing a remarkable presence and tough demeanor.

> "Holyfield’s kid, Elijah, he was in a scene with me like face to face. I have not watched him wrestle for one second, sorry if he's been on TV, I have not watched him. But, he has a great face and a great presence and just looks like a man, like a hard man. I'm hoping he clicks in the ring as well as the promo stuff because he did a great job in the scenes I was in with him."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dr4Mv8I_IMY

Holyfield has not yet appeared on WWE NXT television, competing instead on EVOLVE and LFG, with future AAA plans ahead.
