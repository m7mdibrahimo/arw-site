Chad Gable quickly shifted his focus after losing the Intercontinental Championship to Ethan Page on the November 2 episode of WWE RAW at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn. During a Q&A video on his Instagram, Gable addressed the loss, vowing to win back the title. Gable stated that despite the setback, his trip had been incredible, but he promised to take care of the situation and get the championship back. He outlined his immediate plans, explaining that after one more show and a brief stop at home, he would head straight to WWE RAW to chase the title again. During the physical opening match at Barclays Center, Gable targeted Page's leg with an ankle lock and executed moves like Chaos Theory and an Olympic Slam onto the announce table. Page ultimately escaped the ankle lock, sent Gable into the turnbuckles, and hit an Egoplex to secure the three-count. Gable's remarks make it clear that Page should expect him to remain fully focused on the championship picture.
