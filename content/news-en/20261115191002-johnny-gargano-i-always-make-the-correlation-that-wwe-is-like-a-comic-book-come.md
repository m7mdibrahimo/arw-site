Johnny Gargano discussed the upcoming Worlds Collide event and the Avengers: Doomsday tie-in during a recent interview with WDW News Today. Gargano noted that he always compares WWE to a comic book come to life, pointing out that there is something for everyone. He expressed his excitement for the Disney World event, praising the unique synergy between WWE and the Marvel crossover. Gargano encouraged fans, especially those in the Orlando area, to experience a live show firsthand.
