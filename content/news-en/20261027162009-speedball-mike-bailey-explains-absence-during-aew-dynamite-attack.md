Speedball Mike Bailey has revealed why he and Andrade El Idolo did not step in to help Will Ospreay during a recent attack on AEW Dynamite, according to Fightful. The incident occurred after a tag team match where Ospreay and Adam Copeland faced Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight, leading to a beatdown by the Don Callis Family. While other wrestlers came to make the save, United Empire was notably missing from the scene.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=glgQlRip0k4

Speaking in his latest vlog, Bailey explained that he was preparing for his own match while the assault took place. He joked that Andrade El Idolo was likely relaxing on a boat with friends at the time.
