Drew McIntyre is expanding his career beyond WWE by securing a voice role in Disney’s upcoming animated film, Hexed. The movie, which features a magical adventure about a teenage girl named Billie Doe, is scheduled for theatrical release on November 25, 2026.

Disney released the official trailer on October 12, showcasing a cast that includes Hailee Steinfeld, Rashida Jones, and Jodie Foster. While McIntyre is listed in the film’s full cast on IMDb, Disney has not yet disclosed which character the former WWE Champion will portray. Fans will have to wait until the movie arrives next month to see how the Scottish Warrior fits into the world of Hexe.
