Ricky Sosa has outlined his goals if he wins the TNA World X-Cup at Destination X, stating that he will pursue Leon Slater immediately. Destination X will feature the TNA World X-Cup with six TNA talents from different nations, including Belgium's Ricky Sosa.

During a new interview with Love Wrestling, Sosa explained that he intends to challenge Leon Slater without hesitation if he captures the trophy.

> "I think that I'm going straight to Leon Slater. I feel like if you win a World Cup… you're a world class player, right? So you should go for the world title, and I feel like me and Leon Slater will have a great, great match, and I think I have my chance to beat him too, you know? So of course, this is where I'm going if I'm winning the World Cup."
