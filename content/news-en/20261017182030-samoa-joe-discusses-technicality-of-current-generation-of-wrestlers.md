Samoa Joe recently shared his thoughts on the technical level of today's professional wrestlers, noting that it is significantly higher than in past generations. During an interview with Shak Wrestling, Joe was asked what modern wrestlers do better than those from his era. He highlighted adaptability and coordination as key areas where today's talent excels. Joe compared the evolution of wrestling to MMA, noting that lighter weight classes often display greater technical skill and closer matchups due to fighters absorbing different types of punishment, whereas past eras were heavily dominated by a heavyweight style.
