Ever since WWE took over the territories to build a global wrestling promotion, the company has stayed at the top of the industry. Over the years, WWE acquired rival promotions like WCW and ECW, though many fans argued those integrations fell short of success. Last year, WWE announced a partnership with the Mexico-based Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide, and while fans anticipated similar results, commentator John Bradshaw Layfield believes the promotion maintains its core identity while occasionally overlapping with WWE programming. During an episode of Something To Wrestle, Layfield stated that WWE never managed an integration properly until now, pointing to AAA as the first successful example. JBL explained that past attempts failed due to the mindset Vince McMahon developed during the territory days, as he never forgot how rival regions tried to shut down WWE before it grew, vowing to keep his company at the forefront. Meanwhile, former two-time AAA Mega Champion El Hijo del Vikingo shares JBL's enthusiasm for the AAA and WWE relationship, describing it as a win-win situation. In an interview earlier this year, Vikingo noted that fans of both promotions get introduced to new wrestlers, while the performers experience fresh audiences and unique matches they would not have had otherwise.
