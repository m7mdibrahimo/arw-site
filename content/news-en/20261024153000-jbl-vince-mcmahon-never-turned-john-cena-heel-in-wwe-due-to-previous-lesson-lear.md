John Cena was one of the biggest babyfaces in WWE history, but many fans often called for him to turn heel during the peak of his popularity. However, Vince McMahon consistently refused to pull the trigger on a heel turn for Cena.

According to John 'Bradshaw' Layfield on an episode of "Something To Wrestle," McMahon learned a harsh lesson from turning Stone Cold Steve Austin heel in the past. JBL explained that Austin's merchandise sales dropped significantly after his heel turn, which made McMahon very cautious about altering Cena's character.

> You wonder why they never turned Cena? 'Cause they turned [Stone Cold Steve] Austin, and Austin was, the merch that Austin – Steve – was selling was ridiculous, and Vince learned his lesson

Austin himself has previously admitted that his WrestleMania X-Seven heel turn was a major mistake and one of the worst calls of his career. Ultimately, McMahon chose to protect Cena's massive marketability rather than risk his merchandise sales by turning him to the dark side.
