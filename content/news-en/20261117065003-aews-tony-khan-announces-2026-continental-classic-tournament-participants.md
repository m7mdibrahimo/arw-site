Tony Khan has announced the participants for the 2026 Continental Classic, which is scheduled to begin on the upcoming episode of AEW Dynamite. Defending Continental Champion and 2025 winner Jon Moxley joins the Gold League alongside Kevin Knight, Orange Cassidy, Nick Wayne, Mike Bailey, and Tommaso Ciampa. Meanwhile, the Blue League features Adam Page, Brody King, Bandido, Jay White, Daniel Garcia, and Ricochet. The tournament will conclude at AEW Worlds End on December 19.
