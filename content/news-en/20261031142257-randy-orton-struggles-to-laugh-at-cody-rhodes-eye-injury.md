Randy Orton watched Cody Rhodes deliver a warning on WWE SmackDown from home, recording his reaction on Instagram while watching the promo ahead of their Last Man Standing Match at Crown Jewel.

Rhodes spoke about the damage he plans to inflict on Orton and apologized in advance to Orton's wife and five children before showing a photo of the eye injury he suffered after taking a punt from Orton at WrestleMania 42. Orton tried hard to keep a straight face while viewing the image on his television.

When Rhodes mentioned that he won the match despite the injury, Orton fired back by saying, "Did ya?" The winner of their upcoming bout will become the number one contender for the Undisputed WWE Championship.
