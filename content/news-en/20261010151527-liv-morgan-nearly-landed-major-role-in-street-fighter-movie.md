Liv Morgan was reportedly close to securing a major role in the upcoming Street Fighter movie before her shoulder injury changed her plans. According to a former Paradigm talent agent, Morgan met with Legendary Entertainment executives several times to play Cammy and had a strong chance of landing the part.

However, her shoulder injury prevented her from being medically cleared for physical work during the filming in Australia. The agent also noted that securing approval from the studio for multiple WWE stars like Roman Reigns and Cody Rhodes made adding a third star a difficult challenge.
