AEW Full Gear 2026 is just hours away, and the final betting odds from BetOnline.ag are now out for all nine matches on the card. Will Ospreay is a heavy favorite to defend the AEW World Championship against Kyle Fletcher, while Mercedes Moné is also heavily favored to put her Women's World Championship on the line against Marina Shafir. Meanwhile, the Young Bucks are the biggest favorites on the entire card against Jake Doyle and Mark Davis. Other marquee bouts feature a Texas Deathmatch for the AEW World Trios Championship, an Anarchy in the Arena match, and Kazuchika Okada defending the AEW International Championship against Adam Copeland. The event takes place tonight at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona, starting with the pre-show at 7 PM ET and the main pay-per-view at 8 PM ET.
