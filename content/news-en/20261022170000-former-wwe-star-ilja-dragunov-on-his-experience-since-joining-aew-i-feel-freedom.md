Former WWE star Ilja Dragunov recently opened up about his transition to AEW, describing his experience as a refreshing new start. After being released by WWE earlier this year, Dragunov made his promotional debut at the AEW WrestleDream pay-per-view. Following the event, the former WWE United States Champion spoke with The Takedown on SI to share his initial impressions.

"It lets my heart beat faster, and I feel like I re-fell in love with professional wrestling in some kind of way. The experience that I've had so far is having people around me that really want to portray everything that is going on in my mind, that really understand what I'm doing," Dragunov said. "They want to enhance what I'm doing and they want to put it into the right light, what I'm doing. And I feel if you can describe it with some word, I feel freedom."

Dragunov noted that his stint so far has been collaborative, with both him and the creative team aligning on various decisions. His journey to AEW included a last-minute visa renewal, and his departure from WWE stemmed from the promotion failing to offer a satisfactory contract. Dragunov recently secured a major victory by defeating Mike Bailey in the main event of the October 21 episode of AEW Dynamite.
