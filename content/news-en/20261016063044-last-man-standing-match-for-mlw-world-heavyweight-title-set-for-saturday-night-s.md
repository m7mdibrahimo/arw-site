Major League Wrestling announced that Killer Kross will defend the MLW World Heavyweight Championship against Josh Bishop in a Last Man Standing match at Saturday Night SuperFight on November 7 at the sold-out Cicero Stadium in Chicago. The event will stream live worldwide on VEEPS. Their previous encounter for the title ended with Kross retaining via submission, but Bishop recently pinned the champion during a New York City Street Fight featuring Donovan Dijak and Matt Riddle. IWGP Heavyweight Champion Yota Tsuji is also scheduled to appear at the event.
