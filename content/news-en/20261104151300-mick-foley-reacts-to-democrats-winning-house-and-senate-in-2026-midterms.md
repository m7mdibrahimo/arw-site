Mick Foley reacted to the Democratic victories in the 2026 midterms, expressing happiness with the results and emphasizing unity rather than celebrating a political win. Following the midterm elections, Foley shared a message on his Instagram Story reflecting on the outcome. "Very happy with tonight's results. We have so much more in common that unites us than divides us," he wrote. Democrats won control of both the House and Senate in the midterms, shifting power in Congress after Republicans previously controlled both chambers. Days before the election, Foley had released a video on his YouTube channel urging Americans to vote Democrat to provide a stronger check on Donald Trump. In the video, he criticized Trump's path toward autocracy and called for decency and sanity in Washington. Now that the election is over, Foley has shifted his focus away from the political fight, emphasizing instead that Americans share more common ground than differences.
