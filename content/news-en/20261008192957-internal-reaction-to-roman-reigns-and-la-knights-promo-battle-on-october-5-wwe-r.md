The promo battle between Roman Reigns and LA Knight on the final WWE RAW before Money in the Bank generated significant internal praise among WWE officials and production staff. According to WrestleVotes Radio on Fightful Select, the segment received considerable acclaim behind the scenes, with officials particularly impressed by the high level of emotion shown by Reigns. Discussions followed regarding the potential effectiveness of utilizing this more emotional side of Reigns in appropriate future settings. During the October 5 edition of RAW, Knight confronted the World Heavyweight Champion to stake his claim on the title, while Reigns fired back by bringing up past attacks and questioning Knight's status.
