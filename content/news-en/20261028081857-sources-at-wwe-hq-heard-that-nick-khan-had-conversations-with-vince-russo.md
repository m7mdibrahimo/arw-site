Reports indicate that Vince Russo may have held discussions regarding a return to the WWE creative team. Conrad Thompson noted in an article for Uncrowned that rumors circulated this past summer about Russo potentially rejoining the company.

According to Fightful, sources at WWE headquarters confirmed they heard Nick Khan had conversations with Russo, though the seriousness of these talks remains unclear. Sources familiar with the situation stated that Russo was either at the company headquarters or considered for a visit, but the information was not shared with the general staff.
