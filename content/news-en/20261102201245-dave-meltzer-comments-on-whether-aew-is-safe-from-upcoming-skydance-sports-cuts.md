Now that the merger between Paramount Skydance and Warner Bros. Discovery has created Skydance, many fans are wondering if this will affect AEW. According to Dave Meltzer on Wrestling Observer Radio, AEW's TV rights agreement is currently safe. Meltzer noted that Skydance does not classify AEW as a sport, which protects the promotion from potential cuts in the sports catalog. While AEW is safe for now, the media rights deal is set to expire in late 2027, with an option for 2028.
