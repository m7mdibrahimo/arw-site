Grayson Waller discussed breaking into Tony D'Angelo's home on WWE NXT, confirming that his actions came in response to his rival's remarks. Waller stated on a radio show that he did exactly what he knew he could do, comparing the incident to his previous actions with Johnny Gargano, and noted that he is playing a game few dare to enter. Grayson Waller will defend the NXT Championship against Tony D'Angelo at WWE NXT Halloween Havoc.
