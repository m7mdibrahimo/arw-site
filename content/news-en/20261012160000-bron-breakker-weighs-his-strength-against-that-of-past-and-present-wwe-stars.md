Bron Breakker recently compared his strength to several past and present WWE stars, acknowledging that legends like The Rock, Brock Lesnar, and Mark Henry are stronger than him. During an interview with ESPN Ringside ahead of Money in the Bank, Breakker discussed various names, noting that John Cena would be a close matchup while dismissing any comparison to his uncle Scott Steiner. Breakker continues to build an impressive career in WWE, following a path similar to other powerhouse athletes.
