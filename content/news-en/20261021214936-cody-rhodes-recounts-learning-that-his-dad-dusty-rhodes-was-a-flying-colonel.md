Cody Rhodes recently shared a story about discovering that his late father, Dusty Rhodes, held the status of a Flying Colonel with Delta Air Lines. Speaking on The Roundtable, Cody explained that he used to be frustrated when WWE booked his father in coach, especially when he needed him for a storyline involving Rey Mysterio in Sacramento. Around that time, Cody was discussing airline status with his financial manager when Dusty walked by. When the manager asked Dusty about his status, Dusty replied that he was a Flying Colonel. Cody later checked his dad's ticket and upgraded it to first class. When they boarded the flight, the gate agent and flight attendants immediately recognized Dusty as a Flying Colonel, revealing it was a defunct customer loyalty program from Delta that started in 1940.
