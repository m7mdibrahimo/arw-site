Amazing Red opened up about his unforgettable night at TNA Bound for Glory, where he entered the TNA Hall of Fame and captured the X-Division Championship. The 44-year-old star announced his participation in the seven-way scramble earlier in the event before securing his fourth title reign.

> This whole day has been a blessing. This whole day, I cannot put into words, I could never put into words, but one word if I can use it right now, it's been amazing

Red reflected on his deep connection to the division and his recent struggles, describing the victory as a full-circle moment after years of setbacks. He thanked his supporters and vowed to elevate the championship during his new reign.
