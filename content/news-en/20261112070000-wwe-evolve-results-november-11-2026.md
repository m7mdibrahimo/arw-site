WWE aired the latest episode of WWE EVOLVE on November 11 on Tubi. Anya Rune defeated Sloane Jacobs. The match between Cutler James and Sam Holloway ended in a double countout. A vignette for Vanta The Unknown aired. The MOG Squad announced that Charlie Dempsey will face one of them next week. Timothy Thatcher noted there are uncertainties regarding Harlem Lewis' injuries and stated he may have to forfeit the title if he is unable to defend it. Harley Riggins said he would take the title if Lewis gives it up. Lewis knocked Riggins out of the ring and stated he is good to go for next week. Cappuccino Jones and Chazz “Starboy” Hall cut a promo on Shido Ash and Viktor Zanov. Aaron Rourke and Laynie Luck defeated Kale Dixon and Alix Brooks.
