While Survivor Series is focused on WarGames this year, WWE is bringing back the 5-on-5 Survivor Series match, featuring Cody Rhodes' Team USA against Rey Mysterio's Team Mexico. LA Knight and Je'Von Evans have joined Rhodes and CM Punk for the US, while Mysterio has recruited Penta, Rey Fenix, and Dragon Lee, leaving one spot open on each team. Bully Ray, speaking on "Busted Open Radio," suggested two ideal candidates to fill those final slots: Chad Gable and El Grande Americano. Bully argued that despite El Grande Americano not being Mexican, he and Gable would be perfect because of their rivalry in Mexico earlier this year. He noted that since Survivor Series is in Houston, Texas, the crowd would support them, and believes the two could steal the show if positioned correctly in the match.
