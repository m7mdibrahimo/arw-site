Drew McIntyre believes many younger wrestlers are prioritizing fame over their in-ring development. Speaking on Raw Talk, McIntyre expressed his frustration with talent focusing on social media platforms like TikTok and Twitch before establishing themselves as performers. He emphasized that he only pursued outside ventures like acting after proving his worth in professional wrestling.

> "Have you thought about getting good at wrestling first and then doing that stuff? Before I did the movie stuff, I got good at the wrestling first. I used the platform to get famous just as a side effect of being on television because you love wrestling—or you should love wrestling."

McIntyre clarified that his comments were not directed at CM Punk, whom he acknowledged as someone who genuinely loves the business despite their personal animosity. He argued that wrestlers should focus on mastering their craft, noting that outside opportunities will naturally follow once they succeed in the ring. McIntyre, who has spent 26 years in the industry, insisted that total commitment is essential for long-term success.
