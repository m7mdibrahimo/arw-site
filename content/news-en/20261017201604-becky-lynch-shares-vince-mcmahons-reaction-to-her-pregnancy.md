Becky Lynch was one of WWE's top stars when she discovered she was pregnant, but informing Vince McMahon about her departure caused her severe anxiety. Lynch recently shared details of that conversation, emphasizing that McMahon's response was completely different from what she feared.

During the 2026 espnW: Women + Sports Summit, Lynch explained that WWE provided incredible support when she shared her news while holding a championship. McMahon did not hesitate to offer congratulations immediately, expressing immense happiness for the wonderful news.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IAQB0MMFcv0

This support extended to her eventual return, as the company never pressured her to come back before feeling completely ready. Lynch announced her pregnancy on WWE television in May 2020 and relinquished her title before making her official return at SummerSlam in August 2021.
