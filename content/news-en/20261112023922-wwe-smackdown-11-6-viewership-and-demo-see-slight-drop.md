Viewership for the November 6 episode of WWE SmackDown saw a decline. According to Dave Meltzer of the Wrestling Observer, the show drew 1.1 million viewers on the USA Network, down from the 1.162 million viewers reported for the October 30 episode. In the 18 to 49 demographic, the broadcast recorded a 0.25 rating, a slight decrease from the 0.26 rating achieved the previous week. The episode was taped on Monday and faced competition from College Football on ESPN.
