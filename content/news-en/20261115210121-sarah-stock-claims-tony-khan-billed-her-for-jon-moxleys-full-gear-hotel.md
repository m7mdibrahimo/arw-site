Sarah Stock recently took another jab at AEW by claiming that Tony Khan billed her for Jon Moxley’s hotel stay during Full Gear weekend, providing a photo of a guest folio from the Phoenix Airport Marriott as proof. The document lists the guest as Good/Jon alongside All-Elite Wrestling as the associated company for a stay from November 13 through November 15. However, the folio shows a zero balance and cash payment, leaving questions about how Stock received the paperwork. Stock left AEW in April 2025 and has frequently criticized the company since her departure.
