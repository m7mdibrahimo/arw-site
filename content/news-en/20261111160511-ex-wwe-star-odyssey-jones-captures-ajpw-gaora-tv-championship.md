Odyssey Jones has won the GAORA TV Championship in All Japan Pro Wrestling, marking his first major title win since leaving WWE over two years ago. Jones defeated Dragongate veteran Masaaki Mochizuki to capture the belt and celebrated the milestone on social media. During a recent podcast appearance, Jones discussed his time on the independent scene and shared his thoughts on AEW, naming Swerve Strickland, Kenny Omega, MJF and Prince Nana as talents he enjoys watching while keeping his options open for the future. Jones was released by WWE in September 2024 following domestic violence allegations, which he has disputed while pursuing legal action related to the matter.
