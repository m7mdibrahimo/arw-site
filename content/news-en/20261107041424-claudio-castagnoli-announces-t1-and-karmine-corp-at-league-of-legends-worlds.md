AEW wrestler Claudio Castagnoli made a special appearance at the 2026 League of Legends World Championship quarterfinals. The event, held at the Credit Union of Texas Event Center in Allen, Texas, featured a matchup between T1 and Karmine Corp.

Castagnoli introduced both teams to the crowd before the first game began. Following T1's victory in the opening match, he participated in a brief interview. He explained his interest in the game, noting that he participated in an exhibition match eight years ago and has been a fan ever since.
