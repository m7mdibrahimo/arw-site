Maria Kanellis recently discussed her wrestling career during an appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet. She recalled a specific singles match from November 2006 on WWE Raw against the late Edward Smith Fatu, known as Umaga.

Kanellis explained that she worked herself into a nervous state backstage to ensure her performance appeared authentic to the audience. She praised Umaga for his kindness and his commitment to keeping her safe during their encounter.

> "I remember working myself up backstage so that when I came out, I was shaking so that it would look more real and more believable. Because Umaga is one of the nicest, sweetest people in the world, and I didn’t feel that bump at all. He held me so tight, I didn’t feel hitting the mat."

She also addressed the Samoan Drop she took, noting that while Umaga warned her about her head placement, the move was still quite impactful. Umaga passed away in 2009, and his son, Zilla Fatu, is currently part of the WWE NXT brand.
