Former WWE star Jinder Mahal praised Sami Zayn, stating that the Undisputed WWE Champion is delivering some of the finest character work in professional wrestling today. Speaking on "Busted Open," Mahal highlighted Zayn's unique ability to guide fans through a journey and display various layers of his persona, despite drawing negative reactions while speaking like a babyface. Mahal compared the crowd response to Bret Hart in 1998, noting that Zayn and Roman Reigns currently offer the top television presentation. Zayn is preparing to challenge Roman Reigns for the Crown Jewel Championship in Saudi Arabia after demanding respect on WWE Raw.
