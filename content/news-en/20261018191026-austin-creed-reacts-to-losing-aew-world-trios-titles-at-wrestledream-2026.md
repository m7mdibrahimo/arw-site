Austin Creed is not letting a championship loss slow down The New Level after dropping the AEW World Trios Championships at WrestleDream. Creed shared a message on social media following the October 17 pay-per-view, making it clear that the group's run together is only beginning.

> "They got us last night. But we are just getting started…" Creed, Kofi, and Swerve Strickland lost the titles to Hangman Adam Page, Brody King, and Bandido in a Two-Out-of-Three Falls Match. The New Level secured the first fall before the challengers fought back, with Bandido locking in the second fall using a low 450 splash on Strickland. The chaotic finish saw Strickland attempt Big Pressure, which Bandido countered into a pin to regain the titles for Page and Brodido. Strickland walked away in frustration and dropped his belt on the canvas, while Creed remained calm and focused on the future. The trio previously won the titles from Page and Brodido at All In London before dropping them back at WrestleDream.
