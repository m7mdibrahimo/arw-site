Johnny Gargano loves Disney cruises, but the WWE SmackDown schedule creates challenges for him, his wife Candice LeRae, and their son to plan vacations. Speaking with MickeyBlog.com, Gargano shared that these voyages have turned into a favorite family pastime, highlighting a recent excursion aboard the Disney Destiny.

He noted that SmackDown talent usually travel on Thursdays, work on Fridays, and keep traveling through the weekend, which conflicts with many cruise departures leaving from Orlando. When the interviewer jokingly asked if he planned to request a transfer to Monday Night Raw just to make scheduling easier, Gargano did not actually ask for a roster change.

Disney holds a special place in the couple's lives, as they married at Disney's Grand Californian Hotel in September 2016. Despite the demanding travel requirements of WWE television, Gargano intends to keep working around the schedule to arrange future trips.
