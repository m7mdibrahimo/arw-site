Dave Meltzer believes that the final decision regarding whether WWE Crown Jewel 2026 will take place in Saudi Arabia rests with the Saudi government, particularly Turki Al-Sheikh. While TKO CEO Ari Emmanuel and TKO President Mark Shapiro will make the ultimate call on whether the WWE roster travels, the decision depends on whether Al-Sheikh gives the green light. Meltzer notes that Al-Sheikh's recent social media post emphasizing loyalty during hard times has put pressure on TKO not to cancel the event. Although WWE reportedly has a backup plan in place due to safety concerns and recent security incidents in Riyadh, the massive financial stakes and the long-term future of the partnership through 2028 complicate any potential relocation.
