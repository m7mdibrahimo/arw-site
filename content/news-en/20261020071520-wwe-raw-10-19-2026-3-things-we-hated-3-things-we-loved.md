This week's edition of WWE RAW featured a chaotic opening segment involving Roman Reigns and Sami Zayn, a standout performance from Penta, and a title match that lived up to its high expectations. The WINC staff breaks down the highlights and lowlights from the October 19, 2026, episode. 

Hated: The opening segment between Roman Reigns and Sami Zayn felt disjointed. The discussion regarding The 946 and Carmelo Hayes felt forced and unrelated to their debate over championship status, making the segment difficult to follow.

Loved: Penta made a strong impression. After confronting Reigns and Zayn, he defeated Bron Breakker in a clean victory with help from Je'Von Evans, establishing himself as a serious threat to the top titles.

Hated: The ongoing feud between Joe Hendry and JD McDonagh reached a low point. The reliance on the "Funko Pop" costume and the destruction of the prop during a comedy match felt forced and overshadowed Hendry's talent.

Loved: Chad Gable defended the Intercontinental Championship against Rey Mysterio and Dragon Lee in a stellar match. The technical quality and fluid exchanges made it one of the highlights of the night.

Hated: Lyra Valkyria's storyline with Bayley continues to suffer. The "mind games" and constant backstage ambushes have stalled her momentum, making the character work feel repetitive.

Loved: Roman Reigns finally got physical. He intervened in the main event brawl, assisting The Bloodline against The 946. This development adds necessary heat to the ongoing faction war ahead of Survivor Series.
