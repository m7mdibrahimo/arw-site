Tony D'Angelo has spent five years in NXT, yet he remains waiting for a promotion to WWE RAW or SmackDown. According to a recent backstage report from Self Made Pro, his continued presence in NXT may be due to Triple H's lack of interest in his character.

> "The reason why Tony D hasn't been called up is because Triple H doesn't think much of him, or he didn't in the old gimmick. He didn't care for the mafia gimmick and beyond. He's been in NXT for five years."

This report contrasts with D'Angelo's own public statements. Earlier this month, the former NXT Champion expressed his ambition to reach the main roster and eventually become WWE Champion. He also noted his desire to become the first wrestler to achieve a Grand Slam across both NXT and the main roster. Despite his status as one of NXT's most recognizable stars, he has yet to receive the call while others have moved up.
