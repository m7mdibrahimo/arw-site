Major events unfolded at the conclusion of GCW The Crossroads. Atticus Cogar reclaimed the GCW Championship in the main event by defeating TNA's Joseph Sawyer. The match ended when Cogar hit an air raid crash through a glass pane, followed by a side headlock DDT onto the glass-filled canvas. Sawyer had previously claimed he would take the championship with him if he won.

Following his victory, Cogar's celebration was cut short by the return of Mance Warner and EFFY, who attacked him. Neither Warner nor EFFY had competed for GCW since WrestleMania weekend in April. Sawyer had previously walked out on a GCW title defense against John Jeffrey at GCW Score on September 26, citing a knee injury, though he later appeared at other bookings.
