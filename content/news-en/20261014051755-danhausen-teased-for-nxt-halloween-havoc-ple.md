Danhausen may be serving as the host for the upcoming NXT Halloween Havoc premium live event. The final segment of the October 13th episode of NXT on The CW featured General Manager Robert Stone finding a note on his desk. The note demanded that Stone name the author as the host for NXT Halloween Havoc on October 31st, or he would be cursed. The signature sound of Danhausen played in the background, hinting at his involvement. Danhausen has recently been appearing on WWE television, where he has been following GUNTHER on SmackDown, leading to physical confrontations between the two. The current card for NXT Halloween Havoc includes Kelani Jordan defending the NXT Women's Championship against Wren Sinclair, and Grayson Waller defending the NXT Championship against Tony D'Angelo.
