AEW star Gabe Kidd recently answered fan questions on Instagram Stories regarding potential WWE opponents, offering a blunt and vulgar response to the idea of a dream match. When asked if there was anyone in WWE he would like to wrestle, Kidd dismissed the entire roster with explicit language, stating that none of them were worth mentioning. Kidd has previously expressed similar sentiments about WWE talent while recovering from a shoulder injury sustained at AEW Dynasty in April. During a frustrated rant from his hotel room at the time, he also targeted wrestlers from other promotions while discussing his eventual in-ring return. Seven months later, his negative view of the WWE locker room remains unchanged, confirming he has no interest in facing any of its stars.
