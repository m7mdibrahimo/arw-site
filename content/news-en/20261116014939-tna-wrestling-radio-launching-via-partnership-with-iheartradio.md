TNA Wrestling is launching its own 24/7 radio station through a new partnership with iHeartRadio. Wrestler Elijah announced the news during the TNA Wrestling Countdown to Destination X pre-show, calling it a major update for the company.

Commentator Tom Hannifan provided additional details about the new service, stating that TNA Wrestling Radio will grant fans full access to the TNA content library. The radio service will also produce and feature original podcasts for listeners.
