John Cena is expected to add two more participants to the John Cena Classic during the November 13 episode of WWE SmackDown. Early run sheets reveal that Cena is scheduled for a backstage segment to invite members of the Fatal Influence faction to his December event.

According to early production plans, Fallon Henley and Lainey Reid are set to receive invitations during the show. WWE has not yet officially announced their participation. The run sheet does not specify whether fellow Fatal Influence member Jacy Jayne will also receive an invitation or if the offer is intended only for the tag team. Jayne is scheduled to face Charlotte Flair on the same show.

Cena has been appearing across WWE programming to recruit talent for the event on December 14 at Disney's ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex in Orlando, Florida. Confirmed participants include Cody Rhodes, Zilla Fatu, Noam Dar, Romeo Moreno, and Kelani Jordan. The event will air on Netflix as a special episode of WWE RAW featuring main roster stars against WWE NXT talent.
