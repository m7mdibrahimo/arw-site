Following a run as a fan favorite, Tiffany Stratton shifted her alignment at WWE Crown Jewel by attacking Chelsea Green and cashing in her Money in the Bank contract to capture the WWE Women's Championship.

Stratton has worked most of her WWE career as a villain, which she believes suits her better. During an appearance with Dave LeGreca on Busted Open, Stratton discussed how she adapted to the industry quickly.

> "I know I'm better as a heel. I know how to be a bitch and cuss people out. I was the girl who always talked bad to her dad growing up. I leaned into that I really tried to find a way to make it authentic. I naturally found ways to be my bitchy self, and it's worked."

On WWE SmackDown, Stratton mocked Green by stating that Green would be confined to catering going forward, according to Fightful.
