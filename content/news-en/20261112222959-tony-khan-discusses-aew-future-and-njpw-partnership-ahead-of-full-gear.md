AEW President Tony Khan held a media call on Thursday, November 12, 2026, to preview Saturday’s AEW Full Gear pay-per-view. Khan expressed his excitement for the event and stated that he feels more optimistic about the company's future than ever before. Regarding plans for 2027, Khan revealed he has met with Skydance CEO David Ellison four times. He noted that Ellison is a strong supporter of AEW, though they have not engaged in contract negotiations. Khan also discussed the company's relationship with NJPW, expressing his happiness to support Wrestle Kingdom. He highlighted that ties between the organizations have improved significantly following the efforts of Rocky Romero and the launch of Forbidden Door. Finally, Khan addressed the ongoing storyline involving Will Ospreay, Kyle Fletcher, and Callum Newman.
