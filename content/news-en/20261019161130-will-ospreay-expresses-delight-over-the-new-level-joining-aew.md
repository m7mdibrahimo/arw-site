Will Ospreay recently shared his thoughts on The New Level joining AEW, expressing his delight at having them as part of the roster. At AEW All In 2026, Kofi and Austin Creed made their surprise AEW debuts, teaming up with Swerve Strickland to challenge for the AEW Trios Championships. The trio successfully captured the titles that night before losing them at the AEW WrestleDream event.

Speaking in an interview with Brandon Kravitz, Ospreay praised the duo for their attitude and willingness to contribute to the company's growth.

> "Oh, my, it's been a joy here, man. Creed (and Kofi) both. I met him at a Wembley. They were both sound guys. Like a super nice. So like, (I told them) you're just like, you fit the vibe. You fit everything that's like going along. So just the fact that he's here and he's like so willing to help and give back to our little person promotion that's just growing and growing. I'm just so happy that they're a part of the team."
