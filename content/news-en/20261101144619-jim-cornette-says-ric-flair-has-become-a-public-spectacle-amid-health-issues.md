Jim Cornette expressed sadness over Ric Flair's recent health issues and public lifestyle, stating that the 77-year-old wrestling legend has become a public spectacle. Speaking on his YouTube channel, Cornette discussed Flair's recent fall in Tampa that left him hospitalized and wearing an eye patch, following previous issues with a blood clot in his leg.

While acknowledging that Flair has always enjoyed partying, Cornette contrasted his current behavior with the professional discipline he showed during their time traveling together in the past. He questioned why Flair still feels the need to maintain such a public presence despite serious health concerns and plenty of past achievements.

Flair, meanwhile, has dismissed concerns about his health by pointing to his long career and previous survivals. However, Cornette remains saddened by the ongoing situation as he remembers a version of Flair who knew when to balance partying with professional responsibilities.
