Rob Van Dam recently discussed the possibility of returning for another run in AEW, noting that the company has proven itself over time. Speaking on his podcast, RVD praised AEW for giving wrestlers more creative freedom and a culture driven by the talent compared to his experiences in WWE. While he is not actively pushing for bookings, RVD named several familiar veterans he would love to work with if he returns, including Chris Jericho, Christian Cage, Adam Copeland, and Jeff Hardy.
