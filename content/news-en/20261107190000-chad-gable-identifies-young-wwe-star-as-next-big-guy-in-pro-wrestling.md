Chad Gable has spent a long time in professional wrestling and knows how to spot future talent with the "It factor." During a recent appearance on Busted Open, Gable discussed Je'Von Evans, noting that he spent considerable time with him while doing media work for SummerSlam and saw a different side of him. Gable praised his in-ring skills and highlighted his mature mindset regarding his goals, ambitions, and background. "But hearing him talk about his goals, his ambitions, and the way he talks about wrestling was kind of what lit a fire under me," Gable said. He added that Evans is his pick as the next major star in the industry. Gable also pointed to Evans' promo before Money in the Bank as an example of what will help him connect with fans once he gets more opportunities.
