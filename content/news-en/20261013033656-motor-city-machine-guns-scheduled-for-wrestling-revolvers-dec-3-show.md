Motor City Machine Guns are set to appear at the Wrestling REVOLVER event on December 3. The promotion announced that Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin will be featured on the "Season Finale" show in Dayton, Ohio. Tickets for the event go on sale this Friday at 8 p.m. ET. Shelley and Sabin signed with AEW in late July 2026, though they have not wrestled for the promotion since an AEW Collision taping in early September. Since then, the duo has competed for House of Glory and is also scheduled for upcoming appearances with West Coast Pro Wrestling.
