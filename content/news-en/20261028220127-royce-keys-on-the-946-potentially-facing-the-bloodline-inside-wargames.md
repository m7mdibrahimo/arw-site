Royce Keys recently discussed the possibility of The 946 facing The Bloodline inside WarGames and what that potential clash would mean for the group. Since arriving on the WWE main roster, The 946 has kept its focus entirely on The Bloodline. The two factions have been at war, and Roman Reigns entered the fray on WWE RAW last week, leaving fans anticipating a collision inside the WarGames double cage structure at Survivor Series.

Speaking in a new interview with Bleacher Report, Keys explained that the members knew each other well before forming the faction. "I knew everybody before we actually all got together. So, 946 is us. We've taken parts of how we grew up and where's we're from and put them together, so it's something that's authentic and real," Keys said. Keys also highlighted the unique strengths each person brings, describing Carmelo Hayes as a pit bull and noting that Jaida Parker hits harder than some men, comparing the group to a pot of gumbo filled with different flavors.

Looking ahead at a potential WarGames match against The Bloodline, Keys expressed clear enthusiasm. "It's something we would love to be a part of," Keys said. "If we are in it, just expect the unexpected, and just take what we've done now and multiply it a hundred."
