U.S. intelligence issued a fresh warning regarding escalating Iranian efforts to target Americans abroad, creating additional security concerns ahead of WWE Crown Jewel on November 7 in Riyadh.

The Director of National Intelligence sent a warning memo to the Secretary of State stating that plotting against U.S. citizens has reached advanced stages in the Middle East and other countries. Although the alert does not mention a direct threat against WWE or its roster, the U.S. government implemented broad precautionary measures, including withdrawing non-essential diplomats and their families from Saudi Arabia amid rising tensions and missile and drone attacks intercepted over Riyadh.

Conversely, TKO executive Ari Emanuel confirmed that a dedicated team is monitoring the situation daily to evaluate circumstances and explore potential alternatives if necessary, while WWE Crown Jewel remains scheduled for its planned date without any official changes from the company.
