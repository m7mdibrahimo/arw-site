Cody Rhodes has addressed the persistent rumor that he once requested to lose the WWE Championship, labeling the claim as completely false. Speaking on the Double Coverage podcast, Rhodes expressed frustration that an old report from approximately eight months ago continues to be treated as fact. He explained that a top-tier champion at the peak of their career would never volunteer to give up their "meal ticket." Rhodes noted that while an aging performer might suggest a final opponent to conclude their career, the idea of a current champion casually asking to drop the title is nonsensical. He emphasized that if a wrestler is holding the championship, they should expect someone to come and take it from them rather than pitching to lose it. Rhodes also highlighted how online wrestling discourse can often become disconnected from the reality of the arena experience. He encouraged fans to attend a live WWE show to see for themselves that the atmosphere is overwhelmingly positive, contrasting sharply with the negativity often found in online discussions.
