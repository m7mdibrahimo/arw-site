Zack Sabre Jr. recently addressed whether he considers himself the greatest technical wrestler of all time ahead of his upcoming match against Hechicero. CMLL and New Japan Pro-Wrestling are teaming up to present Lucha Kingdom at Arena México on November 16th, featuring Sabre Jr. challenging Hechicero for the CMLL World Heavyweight Championship. Appearing on CMLL's Esto es Lucha Podcast, Sabre Jr. was asked about his legacy in the sport.

> "Uh, ask me in 20 years time, and hopefully I can say yes," he laughed. "The thing that drives me every match is that I want to be the greatest technical wrestler of all time. It’d be self-indulgent for me to say. But yeah, that’s the driving force." In addition to this upcoming bout, Sabre Jr. recently competed at Taichi and NJPW's event at Taichi's former middle school, where Dangerous Tekkers reunited and defeated Naomichi Marufuji and Yoshinobu Kanemaru.
