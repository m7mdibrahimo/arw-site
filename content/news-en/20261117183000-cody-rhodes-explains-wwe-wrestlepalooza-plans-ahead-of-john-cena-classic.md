Cody Rhodes has addressed his participation in the upcoming John Cena Classic and how it fits into WWE's busy schedule ahead of Wrestlepalooza on December 12. With a tour of Australia happening simultaneously, Rhodes noted the logistical challenge of traveling back for the John Cena Classic, which he will take part in as its very first invitee.

Speaking on The Ebro Laura Rosenberg Show, Rhodes clarified the purpose behind the event. He explained that the tournament is designed to give young NXT talent a platform to face established main roster stars, comparing it to John Cena's debut when he confronted Kurt Angle.
