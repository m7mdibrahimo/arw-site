Billy Gunn has enjoyed the unique experience of seeing both of his sons, Austin Gunn and Colten Gunn, follow in his footsteps to become full-time professional wrestlers in AEW. In an interview with Inside The Ropes, according to Wrestling Inc., Gunn discussed the differences in his children's personalities.

> "Austin is my youngest, he is me amped up to about a thousand. Colten is not anywhere near me, but he is very [much] the opposite of me and Austin, in that he is very analytic."

Gunn reflected on the storyline betrayal where his sons attacked him with a chair before he aligned with The Acclaimed. Despite the rough assault, Gunn expressed immense pride in his sons, stating that he genuinely believes they are talented wrestlers.

> "I wanted my kids to actually suck so they didn't do this job, but unfortunately..." 

Before giving them his blessing to train, Gunn required both of his sons to complete their education. Austin asked to begin his training immediately on his graduation day, while Colten had a completely different life plan mapped out until he decided to change course.

> "Colten, on the other hand, he was more put together, because he had his life planned out until he was 60, when he was gonna retire, and then it all went haywire for him, which was a total shock for me and Paula — Paula is my wife."

Gunn concluded by noting that Colten expressed a desire to avoid any future regrets by entering the business, and proved to be exceptionally skilled at it.
