The CMLL Día de Muertos event took place on October 30, 2026, at Arena México in Mexico City, featuring several competitive matches.

Herederos (Felino Jr., Hijo de Stuka Jr. & Cobarde) def. Amos del Aire (Magia Blanca, Rey Pegasus & Tornado).

Persephone def. Lluvia & Olympia in a three-way match.

Mina Shirakawa def. India Sioux.

Soberano Jr. & Bárbaro Cavernario def. Depredadores (Volador Jr. & Vegas Depredador) and Galeon Fantasma (Zandokan Jr. & Difunto) in a three-way tag match.

Hechicero def. Darby Allin.

In the main event, Xelhua def. Calavera Jr. I, Rafaga Jr., and Guerrero Maya Jr. to win the Rey del Inframundo tournament.
