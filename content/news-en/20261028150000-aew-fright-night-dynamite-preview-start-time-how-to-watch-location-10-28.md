AEW Dynamite takes place on October 28, 2026, at the Chaifetz Arena in St. Louis, MO, starting at 8 pm ET on TBS and Max. The match card features Darby Allin defending the TNT Championship against Josh Alexander, and Persephone defending the TBS Championship against Queen Aminata. FTR and Motor City Machine Guns clash in the third match of their best-of-five series, currently tied 1-1. Additionally, Triangle of Madness faces Hazuki, Koguma, and Fuwa-chan for the Artist Of Stardom Championships.
