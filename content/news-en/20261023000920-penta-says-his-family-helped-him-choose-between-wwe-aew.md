Penta revealed that his family played a crucial role in helping him choose between WWE and AEW when he became a free agent in 2024. The former AEW World Tag Team Champion ultimately decided to join WWE, where he officially debuted in January 2025.

Speaking on Busted Open Radio, the veteran luchador recalled asking his wife and daughters to help him make the difficult decision. He remembered gathering his family at home in Mexico and asking them directly which company they preferred for his future. His family immediately chose WWE, giving him the exact clarity he needed to move forward.

> "I took my daughters and my wife with me and say, 'Okay, guys, this is the plan. It's very simple. What do you prefer for me? This company or WWE? What do you think?' Immediately, 'WWE.' Okay, this is the answer I need."

After getting his family's input, Penta instructed his agent to finalize the move to WWE and began preparing for his new chapter. He currently holds the Men's Money in the Bank briefcase. His brother Rey Fenix later joined WWE in April 2025 on the WWE SmackDown brand, and both brothers have since crossed paths in singles and tag team action.
