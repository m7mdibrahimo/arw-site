Gorgeous George, whose real name was George Wagner, was a pioneering professional wrestler in the 1940s and 1950s, becoming one of the industry's first national stars due to his charismatic character work. More than six decades after his passing, Amazon Prime is featuring Wagner in their limited series about Muhammad Ali titled "The Greatest," which briefly highlights how Ali drew inspiration from the Gorgeous George persona. Chris Jericho of AEW portrays George in the series, and during an interview with Christopher Smith, he discussed how he landed the role and what he hopes fans will take away from his performance.

Jericho explained, "I auditioned and it turns out they were looking for Gorgeous George for a while and couldn't find anybody." He added that he drew inspiration for his portrayal from an old movie starring Wagner, studying the late wrestler's distinct personality and cadence. Jericho noted that Wagner played a crucial role in shaping Ali's legendary persona after the two met in Las Vegas, helping him develop the showmanship that defined his career. Reflecting on why he was chosen for the part, Jericho suggested it was because he could accurately capture the essence of Gorgeous George rather than simply being a wrestler, stating that he wanted to remain true to who Wagner was as a person.
