Chris Jericho recently revealed that a television series based on his early professional wrestling career is currently in development and approaching the pilot filming stage. During an interview on Skratch N’ Sniff, the former AEW World Champion explained that the project draws from his first autobiography, A Lion's Tale, rather than a standalone movie. Jericho noted that the production has made significant progress behind the scenes, stating that they have already found an actor from the series Letterkenny who bears a strong facial resemblance to him during his early days in the business. While he chose to keep the actor's identity under wraps for now, Jericho confirmed that he has been deeply involved in the creative development process since day one. Although a network, streaming platform, and official filming schedule have not yet been announced, the project's movement toward a pilot episode brings his long-term goal of bringing his wrestling origins to the screen closer to reality.
