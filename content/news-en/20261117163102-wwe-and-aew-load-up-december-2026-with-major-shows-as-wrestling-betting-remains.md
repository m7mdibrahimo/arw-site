The 2026 wrestling calendar is heating up for December, with WWE heading to Australia, AEW traveling to Mexico, and multiple major events scheduled for the same night. However, placing real-money bets on professional wrestling remains difficult due to its predetermined outcomes. While DraftKings offers free prediction pools, major sportsbooks generally avoid regulated wagers because outcomes are scripted and known internally. TKO President Mark Shapiro previously confirmed that WWE has no plans to alter its creative security to accommodate gambling markets.

Despite the betting limitations, the live event schedule features significant international shows. WWE is preparing for a three-show weekend in Perth, Australia, starting with Friday Night SmackDown on December 11, followed by Wrestlepalooza on Saturday, December 12, and Monday Night RAW on December 14. This follows a successful multi-year agreement with Western Australia. Meanwhile, AEW is heading to Monterrey, Mexico, for Winter Is Coming on December 9 at Arena Monterrey, featuring Dynamite and Collision tapings.

AEW's December schedule also includes the Continental Classic, leading into Worlds End on December 19 at the Target Center in Minneapolis. The tournament format features round-robin matches that build toward the year-end pay-per-view. AAA is also holding Guerra de Titanes on December 19 at Arena Guadalajara in Guadalajara, Mexico, following major title changes at TripleMania 34, where El Grande Americano won the AAA Mega Championship, El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr. and Galeno captured the AAA World Tag Team Championships, and La Catalina ended Flammer's 1,128-day reign as the AAA Reina de Reinas Champion.
