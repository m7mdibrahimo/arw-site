Dijak reflected on the difficulty of getting Vince McMahon's attention while portraying the T-Bar character on WWE RAW between 2020 and 2022. He noted that while McMahon was never rude, their rare conversations left him feeling that his character was very low on the priority list.

Dijak explained that he repeatedly submitted creative ideas and asked how he could contribute, but McMahon simply told him to write down his pitches without giving a clear roadmap for advancement. Although he was not treated poorly, Dijak felt McMahon was focused on bigger storylines like The Bloodline, preventing him from building a meaningful direction.
