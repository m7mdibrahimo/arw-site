Stephanie Vaquer’s status in WWE has become a major topic of interest following an illness that derailed her schedule last week. Hours before the November 2 episode of WWE RAW at Barclays Center, footage captured Vaquer arriving at the arena. She was seen accompanied by security and greeted fans before entering the building. Vaquer was forced to miss the October 26 episode due to an illness, which caused WWE to cancel her planned match against Becky Lynch and replace her with Liv Morgan. Dave Meltzer reported on Wrestling Observer Radio that Vaquer’s absence was not due to an injury or a storyline, describing the illness as not terribly serious but indeterminate in terms of a return timeline. While her arrival at Barclays Center does not guarantee she has been medically cleared for in-ring action, it marks her return to the WWE environment. WWE now has the option to resume her storyline with Lynch whenever she is cleared to compete.
