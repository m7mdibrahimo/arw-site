Backstage reports reveal that Bron Breakker and Roxanne Perez were originally slated to win the Money in the Bank ladder matches instead of Penta and Tiffany Stratton. Penta later showcased a custom briefcase with pyro on WWE RAW, following a strong impression made during his main event match against Roman Reigns in Mexico, which has fueled speculation about a potential rematch. Meanwhile, Stratton entered the match alongside her friend Chelsea Green, who holds the WWE Women's Championship on WWE SmackDown, with hopes of both holding titles following Stratton's past successful cash-in against Nia Jax.
