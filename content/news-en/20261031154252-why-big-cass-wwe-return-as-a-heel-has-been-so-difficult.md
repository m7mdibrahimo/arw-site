Big Cass admits that returning to WWE after an eight-year absence brought a major challenge, as fans already knew who he was. Because his previous run featured him as one-half of a wildly popular babyface tag team alongside Enzo Amore, getting those same fans to boo him has not been easy. Speaking on The Sal Licata Show, Cass explained that coming back as a heel meant instantly fighting years of nostalgia.

"It definitely does, yes, because I'm coming back into WWE as a heel, right? I think we can talk about heels and faces on your show, I'm pretty sure. I'm coming back as a heel, and the last time anybody in that viewing audience saw me, that they recognized me from, I was in one of the hottest babyface acts in the company," Cass said, adding that he considers their former act to be easily among the top three babyface acts in the entire company at the time.

That history followed him right back when he returned on the August 3 episode of RAW. Instead of Enzo standing by his side, Cass returned alone as a heel and immediately attacked Je’Von Evans. While he has since formed a partnership with Ethan Page, fans have not forgotten his past. Cass revealed that supporters still shout his old catchphrases during matches, though he suspects they do so simply to annoy him. Ironically, Cass sees an upside to this behavior, noting that if fans are trying to get under his skin by bringing up his past, they are treating him exactly how a heel should be treated.

Ultimately, Cass acknowledges that having a completely clean slate would have made his return much simpler. Instead, he must convince a crowd that once cheered for him to reject his current persona. As he teams up with Ethan Page and continues his heel run, he hopes to build a fresh WWE identity that moves past the nostalgia of the past.
