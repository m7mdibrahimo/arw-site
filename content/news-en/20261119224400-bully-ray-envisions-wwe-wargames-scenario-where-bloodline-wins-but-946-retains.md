The main event of WWE Survivor Series will see the Bloodline face off against The 946 in a WarGames match following months of rivalry. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, Bully Ray shared his thoughts on how the match should play out. While he acknowledges the momentum behind Royce Keys' faction, he does not believe they are established enough to overcome World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns and his family.

Bully Ray stressed that 946 must exit the match looking strong and dangerous, suggesting WWE could use the clash to wrap up existing storylines. He floated the idea of LA Knight getting involved to target Solo Sikoa, which could create a memorable angle regardless of who wins on paper.

> "Straight up, on paper, yes... I want to see 946 win, because I believe in their future. I think they need to win. If you want a happy compromise, fine, they don't win, but they leave with so much heat that you forgot they lost."

He added that if the Bloodline walks away with the official victory, 946 must deliver absolute destruction in the aftermath. Bully Ray explained that the brawl should be so brutal that fans forget the outcome entirely, cementing 946 as top-tier villains coming out of the event.
