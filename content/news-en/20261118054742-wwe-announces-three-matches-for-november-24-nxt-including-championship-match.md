WWE has announced three major matches for the November 24 episode of WWE NXT, featuring an NXT Championship number one contender match, a Women's North American Championship defense, and an eight-person tag team match. Mason Rook will face Shiloh Hill to determine the next challenger for Grayson Waller's NXT Championship, following Rook's recent return from injury. Zaria will defend her NXT Women's North American Championship against Izzi Dame, who earned the title opportunity after being invited to the John Cena Classic. Additionally, Noam Dar, Romeo Moreno, Hank Walker, and Tank Ledger will team up to face The Vanity Project's Jackson Drake, Brad Baylor, and Ricky Smokes, alongside Myka Lockwood, following ongoing tensions on the show.
