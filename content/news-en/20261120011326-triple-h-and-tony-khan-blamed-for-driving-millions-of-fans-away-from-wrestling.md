Vince Russo believes that Triple H and Tony Khan are directly responsible for the declining casual audience in professional wrestling. Speaking on a recent episode of Coach and Bro, the former WWE writer argued that both leaders have become so focused on producing shows that cater to their own personal preferences that they have driven millions of viewers away from the business.

Russo rejected the notion that casual audiences should be blamed for not understanding wrestling history, insisting the issue lies with the creative decisions made in WWE and AEW. He claimed that the industry has lost millions of fans because its top promotions are being written for an audience of two, pushing mainstream viewers away from the sport entirely.
