According to Wrestling Observer Radio, Oba Femi's recent call-out of Roman Reigns following his victory over Bronson Reed at WWE Money in the Bank was not part of a long-term booking plan. Dave Meltzer reported that Femi reacted to the crowd's energy during the post-show appearance. While the match is currently planned for WWE WrestleMania 43, it is not scheduled for the upcoming Survivor Series: WarGames. Reigns is currently set to challenge Sami Zayn for the Crown Jewel Championship at Crown Jewel in Riyadh. Meanwhile, Femi has finished his storyline with The Vision and is currently operating as a solo act after rejecting an alliance with Paul Heyman.
