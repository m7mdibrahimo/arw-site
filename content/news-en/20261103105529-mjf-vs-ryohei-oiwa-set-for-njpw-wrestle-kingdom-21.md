MJF and Ryohei Oiwa are officially set to collide. At the NJPW Power Struggle event on November 3, 2026, T.M.D.K. members Ryohei Oiwa and Hartley Jackson defeated The Swirl, consisting of Lee Johnson and Blake Christian. After the match concluded, the arena lights went out and a video message from AEW star MJF appeared on the screen. MJF addressed Oiwa, belittled him, and challenged him to a match at NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 21 on January 4, 2027. MJF carried a personal version of the AEW World Championship during the promo, despite not being the actual champion. Following the video message, Oiwa accepted the challenge. As of this writing, MJF versus Ryohei Oiwa is the latest match added to the Wrestle Kingdom 21 card.
