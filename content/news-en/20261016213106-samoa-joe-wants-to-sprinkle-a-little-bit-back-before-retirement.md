Samoa Joe is looking toward the future of his wrestling career as he contemplates his eventual retirement. In a recent interview with CBS Sports, the veteran reflected on his nearly three-decade journey, noting that his perspective has shifted from personal accolades to the legacy he will leave behind in the industry.

> "Really, it was this grand adventure that I got to meet a ton of cool people and go to a ton of cool places and learn to love the world, which is a rare opportunity."

Joe credited his extensive travels for shaping his worldview and fostering a deep appreciation for the people he has encountered. Rather than focusing on championship titles or personal recognition in his final years, he is prioritizing mentorship and contributing to the next generation of talent.

> "I like to sprinkle a little bit back before I'm done and hopefully leave the pro wrestling industry a much better place than I found it."

Joe has consistently emphasized the importance of earning respect and sharing the knowledge he has gained throughout his career. While he remains active inside the ring, it is clear that he is already focused on ensuring he leaves a positive impact on the wrestling business once his time as an active competitor comes to an end.
