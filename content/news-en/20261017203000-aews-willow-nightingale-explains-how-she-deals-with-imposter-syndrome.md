AEW star Willow Nightingale recently discussed how she handles imposter syndrome during her rise in the women's division. Appearing on The Mick and Kenny Show, Nightingale explained that she sometimes questions how she reached such a prominent position in the company. To counter these feelings, she reminds herself before major shows that she earned her spot through hard work and that nobody achieves this level by mistake. She also noted that internal psychological struggles can sometimes be tougher to manage than physical injuries like a broken hand, adding that wrestling is ultimately her job and she just has to focus on performing. Following her recent title match against Mercedes Moné at AEW All In, Nightingale has remained active by competing against Thekla in a number-one contender's match at AEW All Out and appearing at AEW Grand Slam: France.
