Dralistico has sparked rumors regarding his AEW status after posting a cryptic photo on X. The luchador shared an image of himself in handcuffs behind bars with the caption, "Maybe not for long?" This post comes amidst ongoing speculation about his contract status, as he has not appeared on AEW television since the June 6 episode of AEW Collision. Bryan Alvarez previously reported that Dralistico's contract is nearing its end, noting that he and his brother, Dragon Lee, have recently trademarked the name "Hermanos Lee." The imagery bears a striking resemblance to a post made by Rey Fenix in early 2025 during his own period of uncertainty with the company. While there is no official confirmation of his departure or a potential team-up with his brother, the timing and content of the post have led many fans to believe he is counting down the days until his deal expires.
