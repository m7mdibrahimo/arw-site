Camping World Stadium in Orlando is currently the leading candidate to host the 2028 WWE Royal Rumble. The Orlando Business Journal reported that the Greater Orlando Sports Commission is working to bring four WWE and UFC events to the city, including the 2028 Rumble and WrestleMania 47 in 2031. Orange County has already approved funding for this initiative. According to Fightful Select, there is a strong consensus within WWE regarding the venue as the top choice for the event. 

> "There are those within WWE who absolutely believe Camping World Stadium in Orlando is the clear frontrunner to host the 2028 Royal Rumble."
