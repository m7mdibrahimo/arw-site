Stephanie Vaquer is enjoying her time in Hawaii following WWE's tour, sharing photos of herself in a black bikini from her hotel room. The WWE star posted a collection of images on Instagram, showing her posing in front of the mirror from different angles. Beyond her hotel room selfies, Vaquer explored the local beaches, went out on a boat, and enjoyed her downtime in paradise. She captioned her post with enthusiasm, writing, 

> "Aloha, Hawaii! 🌺🌴 I absolutely loved it here! ❤️ Mahalo! 🤙🏻"

This trip follows a recent ankle scare at Money in the Bank, where she struggled to walk after losing the Women's World Championship to Liv Morgan. Vaquer later clarified that she had simply twisted her ankle and only needed a few days of rest. Her quick return to the ring in Hawaii and her relaxing getaway demonstrate that the minor injury is fully behind her.
