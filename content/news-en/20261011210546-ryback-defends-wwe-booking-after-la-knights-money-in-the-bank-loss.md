Ryback recently took to social media to defend WWE and LA Knight following his loss to Roman Reigns at Money in the Bank. Many fans expressed frustration over Knight failing to capture another world title, with some also criticizing the decision to open the event with their match. However, Ryback argued that wins and losses in professional wrestling do not define a performer's talent or overall value. He emphasized that wrestling is a scripted art form focused on storytelling, character work, and entertaining audiences rather than athletic competition alone. LA Knight remains one of the most popular stars in WWE, and supporters continue waiting for him to secure his first major world championship.
