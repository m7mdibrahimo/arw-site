WWE and Montez Ford are being sued by a Cook County Sheriff’s officer who claims he was subjected to an unwanted sexualized dance during a July 6 episode of WWE RAW. Noel Lucas, who was working security at the Allstate Arena, filed the lawsuit Wednesday against WWE, Ford, and WWE subsidiary Event Services, LLC, alleging 14 counts including assault, battery, and violations of the Illinois Gender Violence Act.

The complaint alleges that during The Street Profits' entrance, Ford approached Lucas and performed a sexualized dance without consent. "Defendant Crawford then began performing a sexually provocative dance immediately in front of and over Plaintiff [Lucas], repeatedly thrusting and gyrating his hips and pelvis towards Plaintiff, performing a sexualized ‘lap dance.’" Lucas claims that the interaction was filmed and shared on social media, leading to public ridicule and humiliation at his courthouse job, as well as bullying of his children at school.

Lucas is seeking unspecified damages and an injunction to prevent the further use of his likeness. WWE appears to have edited the interaction out of the version of the episode currently available on Netflix. As of Thursday, WWE had not yet issued a formal response to the legal filing.
