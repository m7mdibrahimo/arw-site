New Japan Pro-Wrestling has revealed the full lineup of teams for the 2026 Super Jr. Tag League tournament, which kicks off on October 24. Twelve teams have been divided evenly between the A and B blocks, featuring top international talents and crossover stars from AEW.

Block A is headlined by current IWGP Junior Heavyweight Tag Team Champions El Desperado and Mistico. They are joined by Ryusuke Taguchi and Kazuma Sumi, Master Wato and KUUKAI, KUSHIDA and Nick Wayne, Taiji Ishimori and Robbie X representing Unbound Co., and the House of Torture pairing of DOUKI and Yoshinobu Kanemaru.

Block B features AEW duo The Swirl (Lee Johnson and Blake Christian), along with YOH and HYO, Titan and Daiki Nagai, Robbie Eagles and Kosei Fujita, Francesco Akira and Jakob Austin Young, and SHO alongside Dick Togo. The tournament final will take place on November 3 at NJPW Power Struggle, with the winners earning an IWGP Tag Team Championship match.
