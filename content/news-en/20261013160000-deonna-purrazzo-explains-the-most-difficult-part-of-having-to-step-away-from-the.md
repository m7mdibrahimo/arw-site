Deonna Purrazzo recently opened up about the challenges she faced after suffering an injury earlier this year. During an ROH taping in June, Purrazzo was injured while facing Steph De Lander. She eventually received medical clearance to return and later battled De Lander at the Death Before Dishonor event.

Speaking with The Ledgera magazine, Purrazzo reflected on the most frustrating aspect of her time away from the ring.

> "Getting injured in July was most difficult but I felt like for the first time in my time in ROH/AEW, I was firing on all cylinders. I was doing my best work and the fans were recognizing that. Having that momentum suddenly taken away from me was probably the hardest part."

Purrazzo noted that the injury occurred during a floor spot. Looking toward the future, she plans to prioritize safety by keeping her movement grounded.

> "Being a technical wrestler, I am not much of a high flier or risk taker anyway. So, I think moving forward I will continue to keep my feet planted on the ground as much as possible!"

During the same interview, Purrazzo also discussed her tenure as the ROH Women’s Pure Champion.
