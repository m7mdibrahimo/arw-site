Ring of Honor announced a new addition to the upcoming ROH Final Battle lineup.

Action Andretti is scheduled to defend the ROH Television Championship against Adam Priest and ELP in a triple threat match. The event is set for December 4 at the Boeing Center at Tech Port located in San Antonio, Texas, marking the second official match revealed for the card.

The announced lineup so far features Athena (c) vs. Hyan for the ROH Women's Championship, along with the ROH Television Championship triple threat bout.
