Léi Yǐng Lee has been absent from TNA television for several months, leading to speculation about her status with the promotion. According to PWInsider, Lee’s hiatus is due to an extended trip back to China to visit her family, whom she had not seen in many years. Despite her time away from the screen, Lee remains under contract with TNA and is not considered to have departed the company.

Lee last appeared on TNA television during the July 1 episode of TNA iMPACT, where she challenged Xia Brookside for the Knockouts World Championship in a No Disqualification match. Prior to that, Lee had been a prominent figure in the division throughout 2026, holding the Knockouts World Championship twice, including a successful title defense against NXT’s Zaria at Genesis. Her absence is simply a personal break to spend time with her family, and she is still part of the active roster.
