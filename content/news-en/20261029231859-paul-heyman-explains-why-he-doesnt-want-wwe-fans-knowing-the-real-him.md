Paul Heyman is well-known for his confident persona on WWE television, but he prefers to keep his true self out of the public eye. In a conversation with Graham Bensinger, Heyman admitted that he feels uncomfortable being himself in public because it involves revealing his personal flaws. He noted that he is a private person and does not feel the need to hide any major secrets, but rather he is simply a flawed individual like everyone else.

> "It's vulnerable. It's flaws and all. It's just me and I'm very private."

> "I'm just a terribly fu*ked up person like everybody else. I just embrace my f*cked-up-ed-ness."

Heyman emphasized that he does not feel the need to pretend he is perfect just because of his status in WWE. He explained that he is comfortable with his own issues and respects that others have their own personal struggles as well.

> "I'm special. I'm different. I'm f*cked up. You're f*cked up in your way. He's f*cked up in his way. She's f*cked up in her way. All respect to everybody in the room. And I embrace it."

While Heyman is more than happy to entertain audiences as the "Wise Man," he draws a clear line between his character and his private life.
