The Rock is preparing to appear at the upcoming WWE Survivor Series: WarGames event in Houston to celebrate the 30th anniversary of his 1996 debut. Following this anticipated return, Charlotte Flair suggested that The Rock might make further appearances at major upcoming events.

During an interview on The Screen Chatter Interview Page, Flair noted that the 40th edition of the Royal Rumble could be an ideal setting for his return.

> "I'm...not saying he would, but I'm saying the Rumble is that big."

If he participates, The Rock could potentially face top champions such as Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn or World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns. The 2027 Royal Rumble is scheduled for Saturday, February 6, at State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona.
