Konosuke Takeshita is returning to DDT Pro-Wrestling to compete at the upcoming Ultimate Party 2026 show. Takeshita, who currently performs under contract with AEW, New Japan Pro-Wrestling, and DDT Pro-Wrestling, is scheduled for a high-profile three-way match on November 3rd at Ryōgoku Sumo Hall in Tokyo, Japan, where he will face Danshoku Dino and Daisuke Sasaki.

This upcoming DDT appearance follows a busy stretch for Takeshita across multiple promotions. At AEW WrestleDream, he competed in the intense Clockwork Carnage match for the AEW World Championship. Earlier in September at NJPW Destruction in Kobe, his impressive 212-day reign as NJPW World Television Champion officially came to an end after he was defeated by Shun Skywalker.
