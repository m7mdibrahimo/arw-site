The participant list for the inaugural Clockwork Carnage match at AEW WrestleDream 2026 is finalized. AEW World Champion Will Ospreay will defend his title against Kazuchika Okada, Konosuke Takeshita, Samoa Joe, "Speedball" Mike Bailey, Ricochet, Darby Allin, and Adam Copeland.

According to Wrestling Observer Radio, Jay White was originally slated for the match but was sidelined due to an injury sustained at AEW All Out 2026. Adam Copeland stepped in as his replacement after his tag team partner, Christian Cage, suffered three broken ribs and a collapsed lung at the same event. With Cage out of action, plans for their team were scrapped. Copeland expressed his goal to capture the AEW World Championship before his planned retirement, as 2027 will be his final year as an active performer.
