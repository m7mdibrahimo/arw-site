All Elite Wrestling recorded its Halloween-themed episode of Collision on Wednesday, October 28, at the Chaifetz Arena in St. Louis, Missouri, immediately following the live broadcast of Dynamite. The Fright Night installment is scheduled to air on Saturday, October 31, across TNT. The lineup featured the initial in-ring appearance for The Hurt Syndicate since All In, a trios battle involving The Cut and The Conglomeration, and a singles match pitting Ilja Dragunov against The Beast Mortos.

The Hurt Syndicate (Bobby Lashley and Shelton Benjamin) def. Griff Garrison, Nick Comoroto and Aaron Solo
Triangle of Madness (Thekla, Julia Hart and Skye Blue) def. Hazuki, Koguma and Fuwa-Chan
Divine Dominion (Megan Bayne and Lena Kross) def. Nixi XS and Leela Feist
Tommaso Ciampa def. Matt Sydal
Konosuke Takeshita def. Adam Priest
Mike Bailey def. Darius Martin
The Cut (Juice Robinson, Austin Gunn and Colten Gunn) def. The Conglomeration (Orange Cassidy, Kyle O'Reilly and Roderick Strong)
Ilja Dragunov def. The Beast Mortos

The evening's television tapings concluded with those matches.
