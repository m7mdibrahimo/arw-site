Jeff Jarrett discussed his illustrious career on an episode of My World, revealing one major match he never got to have. Jarrett stated that he wishes he had secured a rematch with Shawn Michaels following their encounter at In Your House. The two WWE Hall of Famers previously faced off at WWF In Your House 2: The Lumberjacks in July 1995 for the Intercontinental Championship, where Michaels won to begin his third reign. Jarrett noted that the only other time they shared a ring was in a 10-man tag team match in November 1994 alongside Diesel, Jim Neidhart, and Owen Hart. Co-host Conrad Thompson joked about booking a rematch after Jarrett famously walked out of WWF following the event due to creative tensions with Vince McMahon. Jarrett laughed and admitted, I didn't get to do it! I killed it!
