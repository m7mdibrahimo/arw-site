Drew McIntyre recently discussed his promo style and criticized some unnamed WWE colleagues for relying on catchphrases. Speaking on a recent podcast appearance, McIntyre explained that he prefers to make fans care about the actual material and story rather than becoming one of WWE's catchphrase merchants. He praised Roman Reigns for mastering a restrained approach that draws the audience in. McIntyre noted that he is currently working on dialing down his own intensity to achieve a similar effect. He also revealed plans to prove a point upon his return by deliberately manipulating a live crowd into a specific reaction to show he could easily create a catchphrase if he wanted to.
