Scott Garland, known as Scotty 2 Hotty, recently discussed the origins of his classic WWE Attitude Era tag team Too Cool during an appearance on "Insight with Chris Van Vliet." Garland recalled that before WrestleMania 14 in Boston, he had been home for months with no creative plans and was ready to be released by the company. He was then randomly paired with Brian Christopher for the opening tag team battle royal, despite having wrestled each other previously as heels and babyfaces. After performing under the name "Too Much" for roughly a year, Vince Russo rebranded the duo as Too Cool. Later, the addition of Rikishi led to iconic moments, including their memorable dance routine during the 2000 Royal Rumble match.
