Tonight's WWE SmackDown, airing October 9, 2026, from The Cajundome in Lafayette, Louisiana, serves as the final stop before WWE Money in the Bank. The show features the return of CM Punk and a confrontation involving Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn and Kevin Owens. 

In the opening segment, CM Punk addressed his participation in the Money in the Bank Ladder Match, aiming to become a three-time winner. Kevin Owens interrupted to challenge Punk, followed by Sami Zayn, who mocked both men for their past failures to win the title. 

Tiffany Stratton, Jacy Jayne and Lash Legend def. Sol Ruca, Lola Vice and Roxanne Perez in a 6-Woman Tag Team Match. The finish saw Sol Ruca accidentally hit Lola Vice with a Sol Snatcher, allowing Lash Legend to secure the victory with a Lash Extension. Following the match, Lash Legend attacked Tiffany Stratton.

Backstage, an attack by Jade Cargill, B-Fab and Michin on Charlotte Flair led to the postponement of the WWE Women’s Tag Team Championship match. Alexa Bliss was named as Charlotte's replacement to team with Tatum Paxley for a future date.

MFT (Tama Tonga and Talla Tonga) def. The War Raiders (Erik and Ivar) in a non-title match. The finish came after the MFT hit the Brothers' Keeper. Following the match, Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki attacked both teams.
