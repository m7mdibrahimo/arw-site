Andrade El Idolo revealed that he was initially confused when AEW fans chanted for him to take his pants off during matches. Speaking on Renee Paquette's Close Up, Andrade explained that he asked the referee what the crowd was saying, and once he understood, he decided to lean into his heel persona. He noted that he always wears trunks underneath just in case his pants tear. Andrade successfully defended the AEW National Championship against Tommaso Ciampa at AEW WrestleDream, though his signature pants almost cost him the match.
