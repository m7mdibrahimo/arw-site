Shiloh Hill shared that he felt nervous when he first began working alongside legends in WWE NXT, worrying that established wrestlers might judge him because of his nerdy personality and interest in computers. Instead, Hill found that Shawn Michaels is completely different from the intimidating image he had pictured, praising him in an interview as one of the most wonderful and coolest people he has gotten to know behind the scenes. Hill emphasized that the locker room environment in WWE NXT is welcoming and open, with many stars sharing diverse interests outside of the wrestling ring, which made him feel completely at ease.
