Ricky Saints is disputing the controversial finish of his AAA World Cruiserweight Championship match against Rey Fénix on the October 16, 2026 episode of WWE SmackDown, insisting he did not intentionally shove referee Charles Robinson. The match ended in a disqualification victory for Fénix after he lost his balance on the ropes and Saints capitalized with a Roshambo. Robinson refused to count the pin, leading to a moment where Saints shoved Fénix into the ropes, which caused Robinson to get knocked into them and immediately call for the bell. Following the event, Saints addressed the situation on X, writing:

> "I didn’t intentionally push this man, let the record be clear." This response came after WWE posted about the finish, claiming Saints tried to cheat to win. Saints was furious after the bell, dropping Fénix with another Roshambo and losing his cool over the official decision. While the result stands, Saints maintains the contact was accidental and believes he was robbed of the title.
