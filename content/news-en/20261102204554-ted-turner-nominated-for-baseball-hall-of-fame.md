Ted Turner was selected among eight candidates for the National Baseball Hall of Fame Class of 2027 in recognition of his time running the Atlanta Braves. The former WCW owner passed away on May 6, 2026, at the age of 87, having owned the team from 1976 to 1996 and helped turn it into a national draw through TBS prior to winning the World Series in 1995.

His nomination is being evaluated by the Contemporary Baseball Era Committee, which requires a 75 percent vote from a 16-member panel for induction. Major League Baseball announced that voting results will be revealed on December 6 on MLB Network, with the induction ceremony scheduled for 2027.
