Zack Sabre Jr. stated in a recent interview that he would never claim to be the leader of the TMDK faction, emphasizing that the group belongs to Australian wrestling. Speaking on CMLL's Esto es Lucha Podcast, Sabre Jr. explained that Shane Haste and Mikey Nicholls started TMDK back in 2006 and 2007, and Haste smartly came up with the title 'The Frontman' because there is no official leader. He added that seeing younger members like Ryohei Oiwa and Kosei Fujita succeed with TMDK colors and win major tournaments in Japan is much more fulfilling for him than claiming leadership. The faction also includes members such as Robbie Eagles, Bad Dude Tito, Hartley Jackson, and WWE's Bronson Reed.
