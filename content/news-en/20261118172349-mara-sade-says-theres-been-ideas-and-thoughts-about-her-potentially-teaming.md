Mara Sadè revealed that there have been discussions about her potentially teaming with her real-life partner, Kevin Knight, in the ring. While speaking in a recent interview, Sadè was asked about the possibility of joining forces with Knight to take on mixed tag team duos such as Killer Kross and Scarlett Bordeaux, as well as Lio Rush and Shotzi Blackheart. She expressed total enthusiasm for the idea, noting that people are talking about it and that it would make for great entertainment.

> "Oh, a thousand percent. I mean, everybody's thinking about it. You've gave two perfectly well examples. that would be like for some good entertainment. I will not lie. Low-key. So I will just say there's been ideas and thoughts about it, but nothing's ever fallen through. So make it happen, world."
