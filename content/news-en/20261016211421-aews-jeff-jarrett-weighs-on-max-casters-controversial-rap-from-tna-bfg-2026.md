AEW's Jeff Jarrett recently addressed Max Caster's controversial rap promo from TNA Bound for Glory 2026, which referenced the recent attacks in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. Caster was released from TNA immediately after his match against Moose and later issued a public apology. Speaking on his My World podcast, Jarrett called Caster a young talent who did not think things through, adding that there are consequences for actions and questioning who approved the segment. Reports indicate that TNA President Carlos Silva made the decision to terminate Caster's deal, making him a free agent.
