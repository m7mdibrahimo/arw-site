The Young Bucks (Matt Jackson and Nick Jackson) successfully retained their AEW World Tag Team Championship at AEW Full Gear. Their championship defense was made official after Mark Davis and Jake Doyle scored a victory over the champions on the October 21 episode of AEW Dynamite.

During the title match, Matt and Nick Jackson landed multiple superkicks on Doyle before delivering a BTE Trigger. Matt attempted a pin, but Doyle kicked out so powerfully that Matt was driven out of the ring. Late in the contest, Matt tried a hurricanerana on Doyle, who caught him in midair. Nick then leapt off the top turnbuckle to assist his brother before diving onto Davis on the outside to prevent him from breaking up the pinfall, allowing Matt to pin Doyle and ensure The Young Bucks remained champions.
