Booker T revealed that when he first joined WWE, he was warned by a terrified wrestler that taking naps backstage could cause serious heat with management. Instead of following this unwritten rule, Booker decided to test it by telling Jim Ross directly that he was going to sleep in his car. Ross simply acknowledged it without issue, and Booker continued taking naps throughout his career without anyone ever punishing him or explaining why it should be a problem.
