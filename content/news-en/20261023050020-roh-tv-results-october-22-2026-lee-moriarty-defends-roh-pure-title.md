Ring of Honor presented the latest episode of ROH TV on October 22, streaming live on Honor Club. In the main event, Lee Moriarty successfully defended the ROH Pure Championship against Mance Warner. The show also featured Shane Taylor cutting a promo praising Shane Taylor Promotions. Bishop Kaun defeated Oro in singles action, followed by a post-match attack that was interrupted by Bandido, who later received a world title challenge from Kaun. In other matches, The Rascalz defeated Cole Radrick and Matt Diesel, The Dark Order secured a victory over Premier Athletes and Beef, Zayda Steel picked up a win against Xanti Li, and The Swirl defeated Il Cartello Grillo, while Mina Shirakawa emerged victorious over Isla Dawn.
