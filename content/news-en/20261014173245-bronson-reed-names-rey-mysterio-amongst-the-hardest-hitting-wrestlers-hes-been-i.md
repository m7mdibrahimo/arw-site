Bronson Reed has named Rey Mysterio as one of the hardest-hitting wrestlers he has shared the ring with, alongside Bron Breakker and Gunther. During an interview prior to WWE Money In The Bank, Bronson discussed his experiences facing some of the toughest competitors in professional wrestling, including Kazuchika Okada and Roman Reigns. When asked about the most impactful strikers he has encountered, Bronson highlighted Bron Breakker's weekly dominance, Gunther's intense chops, and Rey Mysterio's surprising power inside the squared circle.
