John Cena officially retired from in-ring competition last year, and Adam Copeland is scheduled to do the same next year. Their former colleague Nic Nemeth is not showing any signs of slowing down, but he has begun thinking about what his own road to retirement might look like.

> "I would like to do a cool thing," Nemeth said on "Busted Open Radio." "I would like to do my last show in LA, my last show in Cleveland, my last time fighting for a title, all these different things and go, 'This is my last one.' We'd all like to have a last year run. I don't know how that works. I know it can work out for some people, and a lot of people in our business don't get to call those shots. But when you do and you mean something, hopefully we see more and more of those, because I know we like it."

Nemeth started his professional career in WWE in 2004, working his way through the developmental system alongside names like The Miz and Mickie James. He hopes that all the wrestlers from his generation receive a moment of appreciation before they step away from the ring. Nemeth currently competes in TNA and holds the TNA World Tag Team Championship, following his TNA World Championship loss to Leon Slater at Bound for Glory on October 11.
