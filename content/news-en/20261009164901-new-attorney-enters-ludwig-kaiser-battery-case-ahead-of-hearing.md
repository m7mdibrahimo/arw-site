According to Ringside News, a new legal development has emerged in the ongoing battery case involving Ludwig Kaiser just days before his upcoming Orlando court appearance. Attorney Bruce A. Mount Jr. filed a notice of appearance to represent Richard Reap, the alleged victim, ahead of the scheduled October 19 hearing.

The situation stems from May, when Kaiser—whose legal name is Marcel Barthel and who performs as El Grande Americano—was arrested following an April dispute at an Orlando apartment complex. Reap claims he was attacked inside an elevator, while Barthel’s defense argues he acted in self-defense to protect himself and his girlfriend, Andrea Bazarte, relying on Florida’s Stand Your Ground law.

He recently captured the AAA Mega Championship by defeating Dominik Mysterio at TripleMania last month, while everyone now awaits the October 19 court decision regarding the dismissal request.
