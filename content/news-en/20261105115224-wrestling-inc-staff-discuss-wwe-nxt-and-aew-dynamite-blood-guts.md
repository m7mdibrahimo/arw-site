Wednesday brought a loaded night of professional wrestling featuring a head-to-head battle between WWE NXT and a special Blood & Guts edition of AEW Dynamite. The Wrestling Inc. staff broke down the biggest highlights and low points from both broadcasts.

Among the negatives, the Vanity Project suffered another defeat on NXT in a six-man tag team match against main roster stars Chad Gable, Dragon Lee, and Rey Fenix. Furthermore, the heavy focus on Blood & Guts on Dynamite hindered AEW's ability to fully build toward AEW Full Gear 2026. Finally, the finish to the men's Blood & Guts match felt abrupt when Adam Copeland surrendered to prevent a screwdriver attack, cutting short the anticipated table spot outside.

On the positive side, an official Texas Death match was booked for Full Gear for the AEW World Trios Championships between the team of Hangman Page, Brody King, and Bandido against Swerve Strickland, Austin Creed, and Kofi Kingston following a wild backstage brawl. The men's Blood & Guts match also delivered incredible moments, featuring Darby Allin scaling the cage and Kazuchika Okada bringing a leaf blower to clear tacks from the mat. Finally, NXT was bolstered by appearances from notable main roster stars.
