Britt Baker made her CMLL debut at Arena México on Friday night, teaming with Persephone to defeat Thunder Rosa and Tessa Blanchard in a tag team match. Baker secured the victory in the semifinal match by forcing Rosa to submit using her Lockjaw submission hold. CMLL celebrated the win on social media, highlighting the dominant performance of the AEW star and Persephone against Rosa and Blanchard. Baker competed in American-themed gear for her first appearance at the famous venue in Mexico City. The match was officially announced on Tuesday following Persephone teasing Baker's arrival the previous week. It remains to be seen whether Baker's successful debut will lead to more appearances in CMLL.
