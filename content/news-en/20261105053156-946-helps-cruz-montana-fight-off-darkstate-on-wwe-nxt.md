Cruz Montana faced Dion Lennox in an Unsanctioned Match on the November 4 episode of WWE NXT, with UFC fighter Michael Chandler serving as the guest referee. Montana recently debuted on Monday Night Raw as the newest member of the 946 faction alongside Royce Keys, Carmelo Hayes, Jaida Parker, Bronco Nima, and Lucien Price, where they attacked The Bloodline.

During the match on WWE NXT, Lennox and Montana brawled around ringside, and Lennox had a brief verbal argument with Chandler. DarkState members Osiris Griffin and Cutler James arrived to help Lennox, but the Performance Center lights went out. When the lights returned, the 946 faction appeared in the ring and neutralized Griffin and James. Montana then drove Lennox through a table and hit the 'Spin the Block' to secure the win. The 946 faction stood together in the ring to close out the segment.
