John Cena recently shared his thoughts on Halloween during an interview on The Romesh Ranganathan Show, where he and Jessica Biel discussed costume parties. Cena admitted that when he needs a last-second costume, he simply relies on his wrestling gear as a fail-safe. He explained that he wears jorts, knee pads, sneakers, a ball cap, a t-shirt, double wristbands, and a rally towel.

Cena confessed that he uses his wrestling attire as a crutch because he lacks the motivation to dress up in creative costumes. However, he makes up for his lack of costume creativity by handing out large amounts of candy to trick-or-treaters. He proudly stated that he gives out king-sized candy bars, joking that they are practically guaranteed to cause diabetes.
