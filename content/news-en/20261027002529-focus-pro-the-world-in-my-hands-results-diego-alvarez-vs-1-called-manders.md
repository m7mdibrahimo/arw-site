Focus Pro Wrestling hosted its The World In My Hands event on October 24 at Sonia in Cambridge, Massachusetts. The show was broadcast on IWTV, according to Fightful.

Austin Luke def. LJ Cleary via disqualification for the Fight Factory Pro Wrestling Irish Junior Heavyweight Championship; Cleary retains the title.
Dolphin Funeral def. Freeze Time, Above All, and Smooth Criminals for the Focus Pro Tag Team Championships to retain the titles.
JB Anderson vs. Anakin Murphy in a Relaxed Rules match.
Gypsy Mac def. Izzy Moreno in a Deathmatch for the number one contender spot for the Focus Pro Women’s Championship with Steph De Lander as the special guest referee.
Super Crazy def. Mani Ariez.
Dr. Redacted def. Mike Graca for the NHB American Deathmatch Championship to retain the title.
Diego Alvarez def. 1 Called Manders for the Focus Pro Championship to retain the title.
Danny Demanto def. Brian Bayside under Bayside High Rules.
