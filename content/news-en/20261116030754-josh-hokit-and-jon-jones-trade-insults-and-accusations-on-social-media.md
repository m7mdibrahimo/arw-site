Josh Hokit experienced the first defeat of his MMA career on Saturday at UFC 334, falling by unanimous decision to Cyril Gane in a UFC Heavyweight Title bout. Following the match, Jon Jones commented on social media.

> "Bro, I know it’s the craziest coincidence. Almost every single fighter that talks crap about me ends up losing the next fight. I swear it’s been that way for years. Weird."

Hokit responded aggressively to the post.

> "When you are looking up at me from hell. You’ll realize that Satan protects his best workers, you women beating, gay, drug addict piece of shit."

Hokit once trained at the gym owned by Greg Jackson, where Jones is a long-term student. Jones addressed their history and the recent loss.

> "I can’t think of anything I’ve ever done wrong to Josh. I allowed him to train in my personal training camp and always treated him with the utmost respect. In return, I’ve gotten nothing but disrespect from him, left and right. I enjoyed last night way too much. It was great seeing an ass get humbled. I’ll offer one piece of advice: Let’s stop blocking punches with your face. Your career won’t last very long if you keep taking that kind of damage."

Jones further claimed that Hokit folded during the championship rounds and asserted that Hokit could never defeat him. Hokit later accused Jones of ruining the life of their former teammate, Simone.

> "The wrath of God is coming for you, pussy. Our teammate Simone was a good Christian kid who had his whole life ahead of him. There was nothing wrong with him. He trusted you. Then he started hanging around you, getting involved with drugs with you. Not too long ago, I saw him downtown in Albuquerque, barking and talking to himself, seemingly completely detached from reality. It was heartbreaking to see what he’d become. That’s someone who trusted you, and you should be ashamed of the role you played in his downfall… for that, burn in hell."

Jones has not competed since November 2024, and his future in the sport remains uncertain.
