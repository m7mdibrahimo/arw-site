AEW CEO Tony Khan recently addressed Adam Copeland's upcoming retirement announcement for 2027, expressing his support and noting that Copeland wants next year to be his final run in the ring. Khan also discussed the recent injuries sustained by Christian Cage during their AEW Tag Team Championship Ladder Match at All Out, where Cage suffered multiple broken ribs and a collapsed lung. Cage is currently recovering and remains absent from AEW programming, while Copeland recently competed in the Clockwork Carnage match at WrestleDream for the AEW World Championship before falling short against Will Ospreay.
