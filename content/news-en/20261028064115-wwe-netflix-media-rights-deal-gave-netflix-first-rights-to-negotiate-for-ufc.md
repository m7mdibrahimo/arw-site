WWE's media rights agreement with Netflix included a provision that gave Netflix the first opportunity to negotiate for UFC's domestic media rights, according to an unredacted document obtained by Brandon Thurston of Wrestlenomics during his coverage of the WWE shareholders' lawsuit.

> "Unredacted: WWE's media rights deal with Netflix gave Netflix first rights to negotiate for UFC domestic media rights. Those rights ultimately went to Paramount, not Netflix."

Thurston further explained: 

> "WWE's attorneys raised this in the merger lawsuit as part of litigation about whether post-merger media rights evidence should be included at the ultimately canceled trial. WWE argued merging with UFC benefited media rights deals, contrary to plaintiffs' expert's assertion."

These details emerged following the recent revelation of the WWE portion of the merger lawsuit settlement.
