Dwight Howard watched Monday night's episode of WWE Raw from the audience at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn, New York, according to Fightful. The former basketball star observed the Intercontinental Championship match between Chad Gable and Ethan Page alongside the crowd.

During the event, Howard visited the backstage area to converse with Triple H. The executive welcomed him to stick around and informed him to reach out whenever he wished to slam someone. Howard previously participated in a WWE tryout during SummerSlam weekend in 2022 and stated in 2024 that Ric Flair advised him his knees were too worn down for a professional wrestling career. He concluded his NBA career in 2022 with the Los Angeles Lakers.
