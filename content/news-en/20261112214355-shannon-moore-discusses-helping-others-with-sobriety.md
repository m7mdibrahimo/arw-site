Shannon Moore spoke about his role in helping individuals battle addiction and achieve sobriety during the latest episode of the Insight with Chris Van Vliet podcast. Moore explained that the wrestling community has evolved significantly from the past. He recalled that a list of who might die next used to float around locker rooms, and said that "dead pool" has become a "living pool" where people know they can reach out for help, crediting peers such as Dan Spivey and Jeff Jarrett and the WWE Wellness Policy for saving many lives. He added that he receives constant calls from people inside and outside wrestling seeking help after sharing his story.
