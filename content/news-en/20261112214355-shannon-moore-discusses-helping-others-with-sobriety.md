Shannon Moore spoke about his role in helping individuals battle addiction and achieve sobriety during the latest episode of the Insight with Chris Van Vliet podcast. Moore explained that the wrestling community has evolved significantly from the past. He noted that health policies and efforts from WWE have helped transform negative locker room trends into a genuine support network for those in need. He added that he receives constant calls from people inside and outside wrestling seeking help after sharing his story.
