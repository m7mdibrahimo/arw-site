NJPW King of Pro-Wrestling 2026 takes place on October 12, 2026, at Ryogoku Sumo Hall in Ryogoku, Japan, starting at 3 AM ET, and will stream live on NJPW World. The lineup features Yota Tsuji defending the IWGP Heavyweight Championship against Ryohei Oiwa, YOH putting the IWGP Junior Heavyweight Championship on the line against Robbie Eagles, Gabe Kidd defending the IWGP Global Heavyweight Championship against Shota Umino, and Aaron Wolf defending the NEVER Openweight Championship against Kosei Fujita. In addition, United Empire defends the NEVER Openweight 6-Man Tag Team Championships against Unbound Co., while Konosuke Takeshita and Toru Yano face House of Torture, Hechicero and Boltin Oleg take on TMDK, Yuya Uemura, Taichi and El Desperado meet House of Torture, and Goto Revolutionary Army faces Unbound Co.
