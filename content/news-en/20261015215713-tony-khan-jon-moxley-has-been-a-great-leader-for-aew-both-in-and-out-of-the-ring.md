Tony Khan continued to praise Jon Moxley during his media call ahead of AEW WrestleDream on October 17. Khan was asked about the impact Moxley has had on the company during a challenging period that included the recent losses of Andy Williams and PAC. Khan emphasized that Moxley has been a vital part of AEW since its 2019 inception and expressed deep respect for him as a leader inside and outside the ring. Regarding PAC's tribute show in September, Khan shared that Moxley felt it was important to perform and honor his friend. Additionally, Khan briefly commented on Adam Copeland's upcoming retirement during the same call.
