WWE Hall of Famer Paul Heyman recently discussed the differences between working with Vince McMahon and Paul "Triple H" Levesque during an appearance on "In Depth with Graham Bensinger." Heyman described McMahon as a "dictator" who ran the show with his own money and ideas, requiring talent to constantly pitch and sell their concepts to him. In contrast, Heyman praised Levesque as a "collaborator" rather than a dictator, calling him the best editor he has ever worked with. He noted that while McMahon preferred a blank slate, Levesque listens to ideas from smart people without holding expectations of his own vision.
