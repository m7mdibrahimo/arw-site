Andrade is treating his second run in AEW with high dedication, stating that he owes Tony Khan for this opportunity and plans to repay that debt through his work inside the ring. During an appearance on Close Up with Renee Paquette, Andrade explained that returning to the company was about proving his true worth and abilities. He emphasized that his goal is to defend his title regularly and contribute positively to the entire locker room. Andrade noted that he wants to represent AEW in the best possible way and ensure the management made the right decision by bringing him back.
