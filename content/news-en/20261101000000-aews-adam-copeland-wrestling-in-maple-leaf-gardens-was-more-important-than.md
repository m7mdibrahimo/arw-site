Adam Copeland, currently in the final years of his wrestling career, has revealed that performing at Maple Leaf Gardens held more significance for him than any other venue, including Wembley Stadium. Speaking to Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling's YouTube channel, the AEW star reflected on the venue where he spent his youth watching wrestling events. Copeland noted that he never anticipated the opportunity to compete there, especially after his nine-year retirement. He explained that once he returned to the ring, the logistics of his career made a match at Maple Leaf Gardens seem like a "pipe dream." The opportunity became a reality after he joined AEW and began coordinating with Scott D'Amore, the owner of Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling and former TNA president. With 2027 confirmed as his final year in the sport, Copeland feels he has achieved his major professional goals, with the Tokyo Dome as one of the few remaining locations on his list.
