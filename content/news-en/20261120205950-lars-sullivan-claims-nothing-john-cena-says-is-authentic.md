Lars Sullivan recently criticized John Cena's online presence, suggesting that his social media posts feel artificial. The tension began after The Rock confirmed he would appear at WWE Survivor Series on November 28 in Houston to celebrate the 30th anniversary of his debut. John Cena tweeted in support of The Rock, highlighting their shared history and their undefeated record as a team at the event.

> “I’ve been lucky enough to have several history making moments facing and alongside The People’s Champ! I mean we are an undefeated team at #SurvivorSeries! Can’t wait to feel electricity in Houston as we celebrate 30yrs of @TheRock!”

Sullivan responded to the tweet, dismissing Cena's message as insincere. 

> “Nothing you say is authentic. It's almost like ai posts.”

Sullivan provided no further explanation for his comment, and Cena has not responded to the accusation.
