ATTACK Pro Wrestling held night one of ATTACK! Goosebumps 11 on October 24 at Cathays Community Centre in Cardiff, Wales, with the event airing live on YouTube.

In the opening matches, Kid Lykos II defeated Larry The Caddy to capture the ATTACK! Pro 24/7 Championship, but Josh Holly subsequently defeated Kid Lykos II to win the title. Orsi defeated Karnivora, and Mark Andrews secured a victory against Andy Roberts. In the Weird & Wonderful World Cup finals, Leon Cage picked up a win over Chuck Mambo.

Moving to the tag team action, Principle Of Brilliance (Aaron McRae and James Ellis) successfully defended the ATTACK! Pro Tag Team Championship against Greedy Souls (Brendan White and Danny Jones). Matt Gatlin also defeated Chicago Desailly.

In the main event, Drew Parker successfully defended the ATTACK! Pro Championship by defeating Kid Lykos.
