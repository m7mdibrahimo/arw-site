WWE Hall of Famer Bully Ray has shared high praise for WWE star Blake Monroe, suggesting she has the potential to become the female version of ECW legend The Sandman.

Monroe joined the main roster in early October and immediately entered a rivalry with Giulia on WWE SmackDown. The feud rapidly escalated into a street fight on WWE SmackDown, prompting Ray to discuss Monroe's talent on Busted Open Radio. Ray praised her ability to deliver compelling hardcore matches, noting how well her extreme style contrasts with her appearance.

> "I have enjoyed Blake Monroe's work since before she was Blake Monroe. And there's just something about her when she does hardcore matches, when she does extreme matches, when she's telling a story based in any type of match that is not a regular wrestling match. Blake Monroe, she's a gorgeous woman. And then she goes and does all of this hardcore stuff. And to me, it just works. Like, no girl who is that hot should be able to do anything this violent. And I don't think that they've even scratched the surface of what Blake Monroe could really do."

Ray expressed a desire to see Monroe evolve into a dedicated hardcore female wrestler, an archetype he feels WWE currently lacks. He then made the direct comparison to his former ECW colleague.

> "Knowing what she's done in the past, I'd love to see Blake evolve into something that WWE does not have at all, which is a hardcore female wrestler. I'm going to say something pretty outlandish, but tell me if you get my point or if you can see it. Blake Monroe is the female version of the Sandman."

Monroe has previously competed in street fights across both WWE NXT and WWE SmackDown, earning victories over Jaida Parker and Giulia.
