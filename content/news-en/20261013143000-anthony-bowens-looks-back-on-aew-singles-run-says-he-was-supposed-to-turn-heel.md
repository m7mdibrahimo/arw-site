Anthony Bowens recently reflected on his recent singles run in AEW during an interview with Michael Fairman. Bowens addressed the sentiment that his singles run flopped, explaining that it was intentionally designed to be a slow-burn setup for a heel turn.

"Well, what I was working towards last year was being a heel. Creative switched and it ended up going into a brief kind of Acclaimed reunion. But that’s what I think people don’t understand. They think that the return was a flop, which it wasn’t. It was made to be that way so that I can go off the deep end, blame Billy Gunn for my not being able to win the big matches, blame the fans for choosing Max Caster over me, adding to his delusion of being 'The Best Wrestler Alive' and then I would go into what 'The Pride of Pro Wrestling' was. Yes, it’s a very fun play on words but honestly it has really nothing to do with me being gay. The Pride of Pro Wrestling is an ambassador to the company that feels like he’s being overlooked and he will do anything to get his flowers. That’s really what it was."

Bowens also discussed his subsequent alignment with The Opps, which features Samoa Joe, HOOK, Katsuyori Shibata, and Action Andretti. He praised the group and the opportunity to work alongside close friends and veterans.
