Trick Williams is expanding his creative footprint beyond the squared circle. During an appearance on Double Coverage with Mr. Exact, Williams revealed that he is currently working on a full music project.

Williams discussed his friendship with Lil Yachty, noting that Yachty has become an important creative voice in his WWE career as the two spend time together in the gym and recording studio. When asked if fans can expect more music from them, Williams confirmed that an entire project is actively in the works.

While he did not reveal a release date or tracklist, Williams shared that he has been writing raps since he was around 10 years old. He even recited a rap from a notebook he still keeps from his elementary school days, proving that music has been a lifelong passion.
