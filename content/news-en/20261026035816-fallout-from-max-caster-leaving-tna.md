Details have emerged regarding the fallout from Max Caster departing TNA. According to Fightful Select, Max Caster was originally only scheduled to work TNA Bound for Glory, but he was subsequently added to the tapings in Dothan, Alabama. The shows had to be rewritten after he was removed from them. Caster debuted at TNA Bound for Glory by answering Moose's open challenge, during which his pre-match rap included a mention of Saudi bombings. TNA severed ties with him the following day, and he has since stated that he will be stepping away from wrestling.
