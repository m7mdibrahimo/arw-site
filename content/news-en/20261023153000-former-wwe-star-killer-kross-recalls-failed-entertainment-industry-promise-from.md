Former WWE star Killer Kross recently recalled a failed promise from TKO regarding opportunities in the entertainment industry. During an interview on "The Wrestling Classic," Kross explained that during an in-house meeting a couple of years ago, bosses discussed how the merger with TKO would help bridge the gap into different entertainment lanes, similar to what Cody Rhodes and Roman Reigns have achieved. Kross stated that he followed up with WWE to pitch himself as a film ambassador, but after being told to follow up in Los Angeles, nothing ever happened. Following his frustration, Kross pursued independent filmmaking on his own and recently worked on his upcoming film "Godshot."
