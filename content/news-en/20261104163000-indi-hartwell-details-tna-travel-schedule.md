Indi Hartwell discussed the major changes in her travel schedule since moving from WWE to TNA, noting that the new system has given her the chance to spend much more time at home.

During a recent interview with "Busted Open Radio," Hartwell explained that TNA's relatively light schedule has helped her return to her original home in Australia and visit her family more frequently, unlike her previous busy schedule during her time with WWE that only allowed her to travel home once a year.

> "For me, especially when people ask me how do I find the TNA schedule, I'm able to go home to Australia much more often than I could a few years ago where I was maybe able to go home once a year for four days over Christmas. That's really the most important thing to me because family is so important to me."

Hartwell added that her current life has a better balance between wrestling and personal interests, such as exercising regularly and visiting family, because the TNA schedule does not require traveling and committing to weekly shows all the time.
