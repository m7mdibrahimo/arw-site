Jonathan Coachman is criticizing WWE for involving John Cena in the current storyline between Nikki Bella and Paige. The former commentator argued that the company is disrespecting Cena by repeatedly referencing his past relationship with Nikki. Speaking on The Coach & Bro Show, Coachman questioned the reliance on a relationship that concluded years ago, especially since Cena has moved on.

> "How long ago did Nikki Bella date and do that reality show with John Cena? Probably more than five years ago. Cena is either married or in a long-term relationship with a beautiful young lady that resembles Nikki Bella."

Coachman’s concerns intensified after the October 16 episode of WWE SmackDown, where Nikki and Paige utilized Cena's signature moves, including the Five Knuckle Shuffle, the STF, and the Attitude Adjustment, against one another. He believes these callbacks are a deliberate attempt to make Cena’s history part of a personal rivalry.

> "So you put Nikki and Paige in a rivalry, and in the match, they do the ‘AA,’ and the announcers say, ‘Clearly, that’s disrespecting because she dated Cena.’ Then Nikki did an STFU because that’s one of Cena’s moves."

Coachman further noted that newer fans might not understand the context behind these references, questioning why WWE would jeopardize the dignity of one of its biggest stars for a storyline. He urged the creative team to allow Nikki and Paige to develop their feud without constantly dragging Cena into the narrative.
