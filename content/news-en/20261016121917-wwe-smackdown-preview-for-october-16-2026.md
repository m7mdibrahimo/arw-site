The October 16 episode of SmackDown delivers high drama following Money in the Bank. Cody Rhodes and Randy Orton meet face-to-face before Crown Jewel, per Rhodes' request to General Manager Nick Aldis. Nikki Bella and Paige collide again to settle their ongoing rivalry, and R-Truth battles Damian Priest in a singles match after their recent tension. The action broadcasts live from the Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, California, on USA Network and Netflix.
