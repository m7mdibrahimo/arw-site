Comedian Kyle Fields released a new parody video on Instagram titled "Bret Hart Reviews Halloween Candy," using a bag of sweets to poke fun at several prominent wrestling figures through his impression of the Hall of Famer. In the video, Fields turns various classic candies into punchlines targeting Shawn Michaels, Vince McMahon, Bill Goldberg, and Eric Bischoff.

During the sketch, fake Bret Hart compares Hershey's Krackel candy to the sound of his neck after taking a kick from Goldberg. He also takes shots at Eric Bischoff while reviewing Airheads candy:

> "I'll tell you who was an airhead — Eric Bischoff. He's gotta be the dumbest, stupidest person. You know, he legally can't make toast."

Other candies like Snickers, Dots, Sugar Daddy, and Starburst were used to make jokes about Shawn Michaels and Vince McMahon, as well as Bret's history of getting cut open in the ring during his WWE career. This parody follows previous videos by Fields, including a fake Bret Hart Cameo session and a Calgary traffic school sketch.
