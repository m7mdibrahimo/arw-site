Donovan Dijak has detailed his daughter's wrestling training following a recent GCW spot, expressing his excitement to continue guiding her on her journey. Dijak shared that his daughter regularly trains with him, running drills and exercises. He noted that after discussing the spot with her and ensuring she was comfortable, she participated in what became her in-ring debut. Dijak praised her athleticism and instincts, stating that she works hard to become an elite professional wrestler and will continue training with him as she pursues her goals.
