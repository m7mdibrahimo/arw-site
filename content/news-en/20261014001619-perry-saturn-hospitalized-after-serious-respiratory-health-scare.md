Former WWE and ECW star Perry Saturn has returned home following a severe health scare that required multiple hospital visits throughout the past week. Maven provided an update on Saturn's YouTube channel, explaining that he hosted the video because Saturn was too unwell to appear himself.

According to Ringside News, the trouble began when Saturn felt ill and requested to reschedule a recording. His condition worsened by the following day, leading to his hospitalization. Maven noted that Saturn dealt with a "whole slew of respiratory issues." The situation grew alarming when Saturn stopped responding to calls for several days, causing significant concern for friends like Sonny Onoo.

> "The reason you're here, the reason Perry's not here, is about a week ago now, he was starting—he wasn't feeling good… Tuesday came around, and he ended up going into the hospital."

> "He's been having some—we'll call it respiratory issues, and it's a whole slew of respiratory issues. He ended up in and out of the hospital pretty much all of last week."

> "Just got out now last night, and I'll be fully transparent here. We could not even get in touch with him for a couple of days. I was talking to Sonny Onoo, who's very close with Perry, and we were all very concerned, calling, you know, constantly."

> "Luckily, he surfaced last night, had just been discharged. Seems to be on the right track now, but he's probably going to be another at least few days, if not another week or maybe even over a week until he's back."
