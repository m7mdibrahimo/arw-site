Danhausen is set to host a special episode of WWE SmackDown on October 30, titled 'Danhausen’s Very Nice, Very Evil SmackDown.' The event will take place in Charleston, West Virginia, just one day before Halloween. While Danhausen has not been confirmed for any matches on the card, he is expected to make several appearances throughout the show. Several other matches have already been announced for the episode.
