CM Punk made sure Roxanne Perez received a special birthday message this year as she celebrated turning 25 years old. On November 5, 2026, Punk shared a photo of himself alongside Perez on Instagram Stories, highlighting their close mentor-student relationship.

Punk referred to Perez as his shortest daughter in the birthday post. He wrote, "Happy birthday to my shortest daughter may all the snacks be on lower reachable shelving."

Perez has frequently credited Punk and AJ Lee for helping her develop as a performer and offering guidance during her time in WWE NXT. Their playful dynamic has continued to entertain fans outside the ring as Punk playfully embraces his role as her wrestling dad.
