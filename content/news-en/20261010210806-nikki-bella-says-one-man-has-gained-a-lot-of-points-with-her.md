Nikki Bella is opening up about her current dating life, revealing that while she is not actively searching for a relationship, she is open to possibilities. Speaking to Us Weekly, the former WWE star addressed her previous comments about being in a "single and celibate era," admitting that she might have used the term incorrectly but remains close to that lifestyle. Despite her stance, she revealed that she has been texting a few people and one man in particular is standing out.

> I might text a few people. I have some guys in my text messages that make me smile. Actually, one, you know, he's gained a lot of points making me smile.

Bella explained that she is not looking to build a "team" or a formal relationship, noting that she often feels like she is not looking for anyone when she is out in public. She previously expressed frustration with friends attempting to set her up on awkward FaceTime calls, but now admits that she is not completely closing the door on romance. While she is not actively searching for "Mr. Right," she acknowledged that if someone continues to impress her, the situation could evolve.
