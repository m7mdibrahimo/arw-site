WWE star Je'Von Evans recently discussed the aspects of his wrestling career that he is focused on improving, including his microphone skills and physical conditioning. During an appearance on The Rich Eisen Show, Evans admitted that cutting promos has been a challenge for him since he started wrestling on the independent scene at a young age. However, he noted that signing with WWE has helped him lock in and improve. Evans also mentioned that he has been working with Paul Heyman recently to refine his delivery. Additionally, Evans is focused on building more muscle mass to improve his physical longevity in the ring while maintaining his signature explosiveness.
