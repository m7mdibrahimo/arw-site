AJ Styles is expected to gain more influence in WWE scouting. Bobby Roode has also been considered for a backstage role similar to those held by Bruce Prichard or Michael Hayes, according to Fightful Select. However, Roode's potential role is far from finalized. A source denied rumors that Styles has been running the Performance Center, but noted that he recently filled in to finish a class and remains a constant and positive presence at the facility.
