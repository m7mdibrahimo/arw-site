Former WWE star Eva Marie opened up about her recent divorce, revealing that she gave up significantly more in the settlement than most people realize. Speaking with Page Six, she confirmed that her divorce from Jonathan Coyle was legally finalized on August 22, 2025, after which she sold their Texas home and sorted out their remaining assets. Despite making major sacrifices during the split, Marie stressed that there was one thing she refused to lose.

> "I walked away in my divorce giving a lot more than what people probably could imagine that I did, but I made sure that I fought for my name. That’s the one thing that God gave me and my parents did, so I wasn’t going to let anybody try to take that from me." Marie admitted that Jonathan Coyle offered some resistance regarding the branding value of Natalie Eva Marie, but she stood her ground. She has since legally dropped her married surname and updated all her official records, ensuring her identity remains entirely her own as she enters her next chapter.
