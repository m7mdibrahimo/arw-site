John Cena recently revisited his classic theme song during a media tour to promote 'Matchbox: The Movie'. During his appearance with Capital, the 17-time WWE World Champion performed 'My Time Is Now'. Cena released his album 'You Can't See Me' back in 2005, which sold around 140,000 copies in its first week and featured 17 tracks, including 'The Time is Now', 'Right Now', 'Just Another Day', and 'If It All Ended Tomorrow'. Following his retirement from in-ring competition in December 2025, Cena now acts as an ambassador for WWE. Additionally, Cena and WWE are scheduled to present the John Cena Classic in December 2026.
