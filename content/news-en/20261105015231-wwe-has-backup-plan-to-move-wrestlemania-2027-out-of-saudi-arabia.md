WWE remains committed to holding Crown Jewel in Saudi Arabia despite regional conflict, but the company has established a contingency plan for WrestleMania 2027. During TKO's November 4 investor call, Mark Shapiro addressed concerns regarding WWE's operations in the Middle East. With Crown Jewel set for November 7 in Riyadh, Shapiro emphasized that the company sees minimal risk. "I would tell you that the risk is extremely minimal. I think we’ve endured the tough climb thus far this year, right? We’ve had no events interrupted. We’ve staged all of our events in the Middle East flawlessly. We have three events left this year, one being November 7th with Crown Jewel, which should be a big one for us. We are all systems go."

Shapiro acknowledged the unpredictability of the region but noted that WWE intends to proceed with its partners. Regarding WrestleMania 2027, which is scheduled for Saudi Arabia, Shapiro revealed that TKO has already secured half of its payment. If the event must be relocated, the company is guaranteed the remaining funds. "But we have already been paid half upfront and received the funds from our partners in Saudi. If and when our event ever had to be moved, because it would never be canceled, it would be moved, we will certainly and definitely see payment on the other half. So, come rain or shine, we’re getting the other half payment. We’re sitting in a good place."

Should a relocation be necessary, Shapiro stated that multiple U.S. cities are prepared to host. "As far as contingency goes, it’s a long list of cities, primarily in the U.S., that we would move WrestleMania to. Of course, you wouldn’t get the FIP, Stephen, because it would be last minute, but nonetheless, you’d get the sellout. So we have cities lining up, kind of raising their hands, standing willing, ready, and waiting. We’ll cross that bridge when we come to it. But frankly, we’re not concerned."
