TNA Wrestling is bringing back a classic match type for its upcoming event. The promotion announced that the Triple Threat Revolver will return during the iMPACT! tapings in Calgary, Alberta, Canada, on November 13. The winner of the match will earn a future opportunity at the TNA World Championship, currently held by Leon Slater.

TNA has not yet announced the competitors for the match. The Triple Threat Revolver was last held at Victory Road in 2022. The match features eight competitors and begins as a triple threat. When a wrestler is pinned or submitted, they are eliminated, and a new competitor enters to maintain the triple threat format until only three wrestlers remain. The next person to score a pinfall or submission after that point wins the match.
