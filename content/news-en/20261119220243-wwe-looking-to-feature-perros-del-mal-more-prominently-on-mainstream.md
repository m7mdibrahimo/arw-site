Perros del Mal has caught the attention of WWE management. WrestleVotes reported that there have been recent creative and marketing discussions regarding the reigning AAA World Trios Champions. The company is exploring ways to capitalize on the hype surrounding the group, with a concerted effort to continue emphasizing their presence in AAA while looking to feature them more prominently on WWE's mainstream programming as a legitimate tag team in the coming years.
