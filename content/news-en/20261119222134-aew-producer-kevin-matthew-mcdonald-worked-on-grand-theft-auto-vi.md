AEW producer Kevin Matthew McDonald revealed that he worked on the highly anticipated video game Grand Theft Auto VI. Taking to Instagram, the producer shared that he spent the past five years performing motion capture work for the game, bringing various characters to life including security guards, bouncers, street thugs, and MMA fighters.

McDonald expressed his excitement about contributing to such a massive project, thanking stunt coordinators Manny Siverio and Chris Colombo for the opportunity to join the iconic franchise and work alongside the team.

In addition to his backstage producing duties, McDonald occasionally competes in squash matches for AEW and ROH. Fans may also remember his time with TNA from 2017 to 2019, as well as his earlier work on the independent wrestling circuit.
