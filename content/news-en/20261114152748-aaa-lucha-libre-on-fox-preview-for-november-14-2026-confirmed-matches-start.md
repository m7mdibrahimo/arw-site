Lucha Libre AAA will air another live show on FOX, building towards the upcoming Worlds Collide event with a loaded card. Rey Mysterio is scheduled to address infidelity accusations following last week's show. In other action, Pimpinela Escarlata will face Omos, while AAA Cruiserweight Champion Rey Fenix searches for new contenders to step up for a tournament. The event will air live from the Gimnasio Olímpico in Mexico City, Mexico, starting at 10 PM ET on FOX for fans in Latin America, and on YouTube for viewers in the US and worldwide.
