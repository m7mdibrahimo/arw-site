Cody Rhodes recently shared that 50 Cent completely overshadowed the rest of the cast during the production of the upcoming Street Fighter movie by renting out an entire theme park. During an appearance on Complex's Sneaker Shopping, Rhodes explained that the cast members had turned gift-giving into a friendly competition to treat the crew. While Cody organized a yacht outing in Sydney featuring custom Sonic Boom cocktails, 50 Cent took things to another level by booking out a classic local theme park for the entire cast and crew to enjoy all night long, complete with a magician at the end of the evening. The film features a star-studded lineup including Cody as Guile, Curtis 50 Cent Jackson as Balrog, and Roman Reigns as Akuma, alongside Jason Momoa, Noah Centineo, and Andrew Koji. Rhodes also noted that his role in the project expanded significantly after he initially impressed the production team.
