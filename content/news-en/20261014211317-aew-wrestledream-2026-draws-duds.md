AEW WrestleDream 2026 is heading to Orlando, Florida, featuring a stacked card with a championship on the line for every currently announced match. The event highlights a mix of anticipated bouts and creative choices that have fans talking.

Among the standout matches, Swerve Strickland and The New Level will defend their AEW World Trios Championships against Hangman Page and Brodido in a rare two-out-of-three falls match. Additionally, Will Ospreay's unique Clockwork Carnage match will make its debut, offering a chaotic battle for the AEW World Championship.

However, not all matchups have generated excitement. The decision to pair Harley Cameron and Kris Statlander as Wrathlander to challenge for the AEW Women's World Tag Team Championships, as well as Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher stepping in to face The Young Bucks, has drawn criticism for missing the mark on compelling storytelling.
