Elayna Black didn't want to start a bidding war after WWE fired her. The former Cora Jade explained in a documentary on TNA Wrestling's YouTube channel that financial offers were not her priority. Following her departure, she wanted to avoid environments that could negatively impact her mental health, prioritizing her love for wrestling over the biggest paycheck.

Black stepped away from professional wrestling in July 2025 before making her return during TNA's AMC debut on January 15, 2026, and officially signing on January 28. She noted that joining TNA's Knockouts division gave her a renewed sense of belonging and a place to start over after questioning her future in the industry.
