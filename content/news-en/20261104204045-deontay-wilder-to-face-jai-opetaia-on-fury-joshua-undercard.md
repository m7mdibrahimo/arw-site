Deontay Wilder is set to return to the ring on December 11 to face Jai Opetaia. The bout will take place on the undercard of the upcoming event headlined by Tyson Fury and Anthony Joshua. Netflix announced that the heavyweight division is preparing for a major clash as Wilder, known for his devastating punching power, takes on the undefeated cruiserweight champion Opetaia. The event will stream live globally on Netflix from Principality Stadium in Cardiff, Wales.
