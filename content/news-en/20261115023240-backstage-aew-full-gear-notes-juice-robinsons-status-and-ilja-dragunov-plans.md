As AEW Full Gear 2026 approaches, several backstage updates have emerged regarding tonight's event. According to Fightful Select, Juice Robinson is not confirmed for the show and is expected to take time away from the ring. This follows a bloody storyline on the November 7 episode of AEW Collision that resulted in him being taken to the hospital. The Cut and The Death Riders are scheduled to compete in an Anarchy in the Arena match at the pay-per-view.

Regarding the championship match between Ilja Dragunov and Tommaso Ciampa, reports indicate that this bout was planned around the time Dragunov joined the promotion. Dragunov confronted Ciampa on the November 11 episode of AEW Dynamite after Ciampa defended the AEW National Championship against Mike Bailey. Additionally, AEW has secured several licensed songs for the event, including a mainstream hit. Mark Davis and Clark Connors handled media appearances this week, with Davis set to challenge The Young Bucks for the AEW World Tag Team Championships and Connors participating in the Anarchy in the Arena match.
