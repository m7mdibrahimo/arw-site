WWE RAW aired live from the Pechanga Arena in San Diego, California, featuring key confrontations and high-stakes matches.

The show opened with Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn addressing the crowd before being joined by WWE World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns. Reigns proposed that Zayn's Honorary Uce title held more weight than his current championship, while Zayn asserted his authority as the head of his own table on SmackDown. Reigns countered by claiming the World Heavyweight Championship is the top title in the industry before walking off and encountering Penta backstage.

In the opening contest, Penta defeated WWE World Tag Team Champion Bron Breakker after Je'Von Evans struck Breakker with the Money in the Bank briefcase behind the referee's back.

In backstage news, Roman Reigns told reporter Jackie Redmond that he had a lot on his plate and needed to go back to the drawing board before leaving the arena in his vehicle.

In the tag team action, WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green and IYO SKY defeated WWE Women's Intercontinental Champion Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez when SKY pinned Rodriguez with a roll-up.

Matches & Results:

Singles Match: Penta def. Bron Breakker

Tag Team Match: Chelsea Green and IYO SKY def. Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez
