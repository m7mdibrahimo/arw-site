Mick Foley has issued a public message to his colleagues within WWE regarding their personal ties to USA President Donald Trump. As the 2026 Midterm Elections come to a close, Foley took to social media to address those in the company who maintain a personal relationship with the President. Foley wrote:

> "Should things not go their way, I truly hope some of my @wwe colleagues who have a personal relationship with the president will put country over party and speak up for the sake of democracy."

WWE Chief Content Officer Paul 'Triple H' Levesque currently serves as the Vice-Chair of The President's Council on Sports, Fitness, and Nutrition. Several WWE talents have participated in events alongside USA Secretary of Health and Human Services Robert F. Kennedy Jr. in recent times.
