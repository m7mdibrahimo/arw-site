Matt Hardy believes that a WWE Hall of Fame induction for him and his brother Jeff is inevitable, and he would be open to accepting the honor while they are still active wrestlers. During a recent episode of The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy, co-host Jon Alba suggested the Hardys as potential headliners for the 2027 WWE Hall of Fame. Matt Hardy confirmed he would gladly accept the induction on behalf of both brothers, stating, "Yes, I would accept for both him and I." He also noted that he would not rule out an induction as soon as the upcoming class, emphasizing that the timing is ultimately up to WWE. Regarding the ceremony format, Matt admitted he would prefer the traditional WrestleMania weekend experience, though he would not turn down an alternative if offered. Furthermore, he sees potential benefits to being a Hall of Famer while still competing, noting that it could serve as a unique promotional tool for active performers. Matt concluded that retirement is not a requirement for him to accept the recognition.
