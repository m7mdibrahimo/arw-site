WWE NXT aired live on The CW and Netflix on October 27, 2026, from the Performance Center in Orlando, Florida. The show featured the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic and a title match. In the tournament, Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno defeated Vanity Project (Brad Baylor and Ricky Smokes) with Myka Lockwood. Later, Hank and Tank (Hank Walker and Tank Ledger) defeated The Wagner Brothers (Galeno and El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr.), with Dr. Wagner Jr. watching from the crowd. In the NXT North American Championship match, Jackson Drake, accompanied by Ricky Smokes, Brad Baylor, and Myka Lockwood, successfully defended his title against EK Prosper. In other action, Chantel Monroe defeated Lizzy Rain, and Sean Legacy defeated Saquon Shugars. During the event, Tony D'Angelo left the building after learning Grayson Waller was at his house, only to find a letter upon returning to his restaurant directing him to meet where it all began. Grayson Waller and Tony D'Angelo brawled in the parking lot to end the broadcast. Additionally, it was announced that Myles Borne and Tavion Heights will face each other in a Weapons Wild Match at Halloween Havoc, and Robert Stone confirmed that Dion Lennox will face Cruz Montana in an Unsanctioned Match next week. Izzi Dame also vowed to expose Zaria, while Birthright laid out Zilla Fatu during his promo.
