Orange Cassidy feels he cannot escape Wheeler Yuta. Since mid-2021, the pair has faced off in 38 matches across AEW, including high-stakes bouts like Blood & Guts and Stadium Stampede. In a recent interview with Going Ringside, Cassidy jokingly remarked that their rivalry seems perpetual. "I don't think it'll ever end. Just when I think I get away from him, there he is, always right around the corner. I think he got a new haircut, I guess? I don't know," Cassidy said.
