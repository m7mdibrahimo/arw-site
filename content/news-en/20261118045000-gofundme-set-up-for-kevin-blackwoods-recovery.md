A GoFundMe has been established to support independent wrestler Kevin Blackwood during his recovery. Blackwood recently suffered a broken fibula and tibia, and it was subsequently discovered that he developed a blood clot. The fundraiser's description details the incident: "It Dies Today guest listed Kevin to our hometown show at Rec Room on November 7th. As a diehard fan of ours for many years, this was the first opportunity he was able to see us live. What we didn't know, is that would turn into a major health event for him. Kevin, like hundreds of others, were enjoying our show, and in his case, moshing to our heaviest song when he was kicked. The kick snapped his leg, breaking both his fibula and tibia bone- causing it to require surgery to repair. If that wasn't bad enough, he had to go back a couple of days ago, only to find he has a blood clot and needs to be on blood thinners." The campaign aims to assist Blackwood with medical bills and living expenses while he is unable to work over the next few months.
