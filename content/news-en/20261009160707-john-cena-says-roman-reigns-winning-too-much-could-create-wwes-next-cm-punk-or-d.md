John Cena believes that if Roman Reigns continues winning continuously, it could eventually create the same kind of audience backlash that pushed CM Punk and Daniel Bryan into stardom. During an appearance on Busted Open Radio, Cena discussed whether LA Knight has a chance to defeat Roman, comparing his own career and the days when fans held up signs reading spoiler alert Cena wins. Cena praised Triple H for having an open mind and being responsive to the audience compared to the rigid direction of Vince McMahon. He explained that constant winning by a top star eventually fuels a fan rebellion that helps build a new star. Cena also expressed high respect for LA Knight and placed Roman in his conversation of the greatest wrestlers of all time.
