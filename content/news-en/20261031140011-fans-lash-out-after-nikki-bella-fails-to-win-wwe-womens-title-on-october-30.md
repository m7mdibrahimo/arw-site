Nikki Bella fell short of capturing the WWE Women's Championship on the October 30 episode of SmackDown, sparking immediate frustration among fans. Despite hitting Chelsea Green with the Rack Attack 2.0, Nikki ultimately lost following a roll-up pin where Green managed to get her feet on the ropes. Viewers took to social media to voice their anger over the finish, criticizing the lengthy buildup leading to a cheap victory and questioning the creative direction for returning legends under Triple H.
