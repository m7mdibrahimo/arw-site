Kevin Knight expressed a strong desire to return to NJPW to showcase the significant growth in his career since his last appearance with the promotion in May 2025. This conversation comes as Knight continues to achieve notable success in AEW, where he is preparing to team with Kyle Fletcher to face The Young Bucks for the AEW World Tag Team Championships at WrestleDream.

In an interview with TMZ Inside The Ring, Knight discussed Kenny Omega's previous experience in NJPW, noting that Omega achieved massive accomplishments there before moving to AEW. Knight explained that his return would be different because NJPW fans remember him from his earliest days as a Young Lion, but he asserts that he is eager to come back and prove the evolution of his character and in-ring performance to those fans.
