Former WWE NXT star Adriana Rizzo recently discussed her experience working under Shawn Michaels, revealing that he was the most difficult person to reach and speak with backstage.

During her appearance on Talk Is Jericho with Chris Jericho, Rizzo explained that Michaels serves as the heartbeat of WWE NXT and acts as the primary figure responsible for shaping the brand's overall identity and developing talent, which keeps him extremely busy at all times.

Despite the daily communication challenges caused by his heavy workload, Rizzo emphasized that she received full support from him when she suffered a serious partial ACL tear. She met with him and the coaching staff to discuss treatment options, expressing great respect and appreciation for his efforts with rising talent.
