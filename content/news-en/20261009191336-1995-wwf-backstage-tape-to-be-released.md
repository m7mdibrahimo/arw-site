Unreleased footage from 1995 is finally seeing the light of day. Joe Dombrowski of Pro Wrestling Library announced that a long-rumored videotape filmed by Darryl Peterson, known as Man Mountain Rock, during his time with WWF back in 1995 is scheduled to be released. Dombrowski wrote, "It's the story that's waited over 30 years to finally be told. Next week, the trailer finally drops. Later this month, you all get to find this lost footage." The trailer features Kevin Nash and Henry O. Godwinn riding on a bus. The video on demand is set to be released on October 29. Petersen previously noted that The Kliq makes fun of him in the footage, a topic Nash has addressed in past shoot interviews. Petersen wrestled for WWF from January to October 1995.
