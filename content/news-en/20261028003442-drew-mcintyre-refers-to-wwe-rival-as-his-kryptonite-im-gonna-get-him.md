Former Undisputed WWE Champion Drew McIntyre reflected on his greatest career rivalries during a recent interview on Insight with Chris Van Vliet. While discussing his history with names like CM Punk, Sheamus, and Seth Rollins, McIntyre singled out Roman Reigns as his ultimate kryptonite.

McIntyre stated that he has still not defeated Reigns and expressed his desire to settle the score with him and his family. The two have faced each other five times in singles competition, most recently at Clash at the Castle 2022.

McIntyre has frequently blamed Reigns and the Bloodline for blocking his championship opportunities. With Reigns heading into WarGames as World Heavyweight Champion, McIntyre continues to tease his eventual return to WWE following his appearance at WrestleMania 42.
