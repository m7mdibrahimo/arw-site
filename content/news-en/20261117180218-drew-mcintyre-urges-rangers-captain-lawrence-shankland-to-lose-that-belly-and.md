WWE superstar Drew McIntyre has called on Rangers captain Lawrence Shankland to improve his fitness and start scoring more goals for the club. McIntyre, who has been away from WWE television since April to pursue acting opportunities, is a well-known supporter of the Scottish team. In a recent interview with Away Days, the superstar addressed Shankland, who joined the club over the summer, and challenged him to get into better shape. "Mate, cut back on the burgers," McIntyre said. "I promise if you get off them when you get back from injury and score a bunch of goals, I will bring you In-N-Out burgers on a private jet to Scotland. Lose that belly mate, and score some goals for Rangers! No offence but seriously, we need you."
