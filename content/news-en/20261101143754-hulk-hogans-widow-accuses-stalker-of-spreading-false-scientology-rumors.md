Sky Daily has opened up about the false Scientology rumors that have followed her for years, pointing the finger at a man she describes as her stalker. Daily, the widow of Hulk Hogan, took to social media to shut down the speculation, explaining that she has been a practicing Christian for nearly two decades. She noted that while she was born into Scientology, she later embraced her grandparents' Christian faith and raised her children accordingly. According to Daily, a man who began stalking her years before she met Hogan started the rumors by demanding she appear on his anti-Scientology show and repeatedly contacting her online. Daily believes that her marriage to Hogan only amplified the lies, giving the stalker a larger platform to claim she was secretly trying to funnel Hogan's money into the church. She also addressed rumors surrounding her son's education, clarifying that he attends an online school utilizing study technology simply to earn high school credits so he can pursue electrician training, entirely unrelated to Scientology. After years of dealing with the accusations, Daily admitted the ordeal has taken a heavy toll and diluted Hogan's Christian message, but she emphasized that she chooses to respond with kindness rather than bitterness.
