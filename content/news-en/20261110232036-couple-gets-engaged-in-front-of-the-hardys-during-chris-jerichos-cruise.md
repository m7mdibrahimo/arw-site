Matt and Jeff Hardy experienced a first during a meet-and-greet on Chris Jericho’s Rock ‘N’ Wrestling Rager at Sea. While meeting the legendary tag team, a fan suddenly dropped to one knee and proposed to his girlfriend right in front of them. Matt and Jeff watched from the front row, cheering and applauding as the woman accepted the ring. Matt Hardy’s podcast Instagram account shared the moment, noting that it was a first for The Hardys during any of their meet-and-greets.
