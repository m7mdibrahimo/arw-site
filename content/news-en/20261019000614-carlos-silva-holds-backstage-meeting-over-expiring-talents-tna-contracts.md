TNA Wrestling management is preparing for major contract discussions as several talent agreements approach their end. President Carlos Silva recently gathered the roster during television tapings to address the situation directly. According to reports, the promotion has been operating on short-term deals, with numerous contracts set to expire over the next two months.

Silva informed wrestlers that official negotiations will begin around the TNA Bound for Glory event. The initiative aims to provide clarity to the roster while allowing creative organizers to plan storylines further in advance. A second wave of contract expirations is also expected in December, giving management a tight timeframe to finalize agreements.

No roster departures or re-signings were announced during the gathering, as the meeting was intended solely to outline the upcoming negotiation timeline. TNA management now faces the task of securing its key performers before their deals officially expire.
