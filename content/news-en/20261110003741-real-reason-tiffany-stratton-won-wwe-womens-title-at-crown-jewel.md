Tiffany Stratton's WWE Women's Championship win at Crown Jewel was designed to spark a major character change after her babyface run failed to reach the expected level of stardom, according to WrestleVotes.

WWE wanted to pivot her back to a heel role to test if she could achieve greater success. During the event, Chelsea Green defeated Liv Morgan to win the Women's Crown Jewel Championship. Stratton initially appeared to assist Green amid interference from Roxanne Perez and Raquel Rodriguez, but then cashed in her Money in the Bank contract on the exhausted champion, delivered the Prettiest Moonsault Ever, and left Riyadh with the title.
