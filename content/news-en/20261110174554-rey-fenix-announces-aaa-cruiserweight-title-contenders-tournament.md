Rey Fenix announced a new tournament to find the top challenger for his AAA Cruiserweight Championship.

The competition will feature four three-way bouts in the upcoming weeks, culminating in a four-way match involving the four victors at AAA Guerra de Titanes on December 19. The winner of that four-way clash will then challenge Fenix for the title on January 2 at the AAA event in Acapulco.

Previously, Fenix successfully defended the belt in an open challenge at AAA Heroes Inmortales by beating Jack Cartwheel, Laredo Kid, and Takuma. He was originally booked to face Mini Vikingo, but an injury sidelined Mini Vikingo for the remainder of the year. Additionally, Fenix earned a shot at the WWE Intercontinental Title on Monday's WWE Raw by defeating Big Cass and Chad Gable.
