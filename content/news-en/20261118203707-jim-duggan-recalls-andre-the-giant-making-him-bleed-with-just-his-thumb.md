Jim Duggan recently spoke about a memorable moment from his career when Andre The Giant made him bleed during a match using only his thumb. Throughout his career, Duggan frequently worked as a babyface against various giants, including The Big Show, Yokozuna, and Andre The Giant. In an interview with Sporf, Duggan recalled facing Andre and approaching him with his chin on the giant's belly to show he was not afraid. When Andre reached out to grab him, his thumb struck Duggan's lip, causing blood to pour down his chest. Duggan noted that the unplanned injury actually worked to their advantage during the match. WWF ultimately went off the air with Duggan standing over Andre The Giant, covered in blood.
