An independent wrestling event in Hidalgo, Mexico, escalated into a criminal investigation after referee Gerardo German Pineda, known as El Buho, collapsed and died after a spot in the ring. The incident took place during the main event at Centro Deportivo Cultural 11 de Julio in Mineral de la Reforma between the night of October 6 and the early hours of October 7. Authorities subsequently detained wrestler Hijo de Fishman as part of a probe into possible manslaughter, according to Ringside News.

Reports state that Pineda sustained physical impact during the bout, including a movement described as a martinete, which is the Mexican term for a piledriver. Officials stopped the match and summoned emergency responders when it became clear Pineda was severely harmed. Paramedics attempted life-saving measures, but Pineda was pronounced dead at the venue.

Police detained Hijo de Fishman on site and transferred him to the Public Prosecutor's Office. The Attorney General’s Office in Hidalgo opened an investigation into the matter as potential manslaughter, though officials noted that his detention does not automatically denote criminal guilt. Hijo de Fishman was kept in custody during an initial 48-hour period to see if prosecutors would present formal charges to a judge.

El Patron Espectaculos, an entity affiliated with the show, issued a public statement confirming it secured legal counsel for the wrestler while remaining in touch with the referee's family. The company outlined its official stance on the developing legal situation.

> "Our company is attentive to the developing situation and has arranged accompaniment and legal assistance for Hijo de Fishman, so that the corresponding process is carried out in accordance with the law and before the competent authorities."

> "The lawyer and legal representative of the company maintains contact with the family of Mr. Gerardo Germán Pineda, ‘El Búho’, and with his legal representatives, to handle the case in the best way and guarantee respect for all parties involved."

> "The legal process is still ongoing, so, out of respect for the authorities and due process, this company will refrain from issuing additional information that could interfere with or prejudice the facts."

> "We deeply regret the loss of a life and express our solidarity with his family, friends and colleagues in the wrestling business."

Before his nearly 30-year officiating career in Hidalgo, Pineda competed in the ring for roughly 15 years under the ring name El Buho Negro II. Throughout his career, he served as a referee for high-profile performers including Mil Mascaras, Canek, Dos Caras, El Hijo del Santo, Ultimo Guerrero, and Blue Panther. The investigation regarding any ultimate criminal fault remains active.
