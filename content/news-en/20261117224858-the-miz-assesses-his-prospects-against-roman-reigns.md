The Miz has come a long way since his early days as a tag team act or an undercard heel. Despite holding the WWE Championship multiple times, some fans may find the idea of him facing Roman Reigns far-fetched. However, the WWE star believes he can convince the audience that he can stand toe-to-toe with Reigns. "I guarantee if you put me against Roman Reigns, I can make you believe any given moment that I can defeat Roman Reigns," he stated during an interview on Z100 New York. While Miz did secure a few victories over Reigns nearly a decade ago, Reigns has since reinvented himself as a dominant force. Miz was notably one of the first to publicly suggest that Reigns should adopt a heel persona, arguing in 2018 that Reigns was suffering from the same fan fatigue that John Cena once faced. Miz believed Reigns needed to embrace his own personality, a shift that eventually led to the creation of "The Tribal Chief" character.
