Matt Cardona is uncertain about when he will be medically cleared for an in-ring return. Cardona has not wrestled since the September 3 episode of WWE Main Event, where he faced Kit Wilson and suffered a torn tricep.

Cardona provided an update on social media regarding his recovery process. "I tore this tricep and got surgery just over 2 months ago. I’ve been going to rehab and making up workouts in the gym to work around my injury. In my head, I’m ALMOST ready. I don’t know when I’ll be cleared to return to the ring, but I do know that I’M NOT FINISHED!"

During an interview with Sean Ross Sapp, Steve Maclin also discussed his friendship with Cardona.
