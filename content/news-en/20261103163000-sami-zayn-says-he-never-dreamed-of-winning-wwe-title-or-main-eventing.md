Sami Zayn is among an elite list of wrestlers who have both main-evented WrestleMania and won a WWE world championship. However, he recently revealed that he never dreamed of achieving either milestone when he was a young fan, explaining that his approach was always to work toward general goals rather than mapping out specific accomplishments.
