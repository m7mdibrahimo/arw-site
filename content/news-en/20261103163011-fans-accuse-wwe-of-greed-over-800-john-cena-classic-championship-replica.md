WWE quickly turned the new John Cena Classic Championship into merchandise, but the price tag drew instant backlash online according to Ringside News. WWE Shop announced that a limited-edition replica of the title is available for $800, sparking widespread anger among fans on social media.

Supporters expressed their frustration on X, with one comparing the cost to a monthly mortgage payment and others criticizing the price during tough economic times. Some followers argued that the manufacturing cost is far lower, calling the high price a complete ripoff.

> "The greed never fails to amaze me, $800 for a belt? That’s damn near a mortgage payment 😮‍💨"

> "charging $800 in this economy for a toy belt is crazy 😭"

> "$800 for a toy belt that only takes $40 to make. What a ripoff. Garbage man."

> "800 bucks???? Nah, yall can keep that"

This release happened one day after John Cena unveiled the championship on RAW at Barclays Center. WWE designed the belt for the John Cena Classic, which takes place on December 14 at Disney World in Orlando, with Cody Rhodes announced as the first participant.
