Actor Kieran Culkin appeared on Wednesday's episode of The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon to promote The Hunger Games: Sunrise On The Reaping. During a game of Catchphrase alongside Jimmy Fallon and Julia Garner, Culkin wore a shirt featuring Sami Zayn, which was also visible in pictures taken behind the scenes.

Sami Zayn captured the WWE Championship by defeating CM Punk during the September 11 edition of SmackDown. Across his reign, the champion has successfully defended the title against opponents such as Kevin Owens, Trick Williams, and Je'Von Evans.
