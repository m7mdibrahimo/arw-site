AEW is preparing for a three-hour live special combining AEW Dynamite and AEW Collision on November 11. This event serves as the final stop before the highly anticipated AEW Full Gear, where Will Ospreay is scheduled to defend his AEW World Championship against Kyle Fletcher.

During the latest episode of AEW Collision, it was officially announced that Will Ospreay and Kyle Fletcher will have a face-to-face segment during the upcoming show. The promotion also added a tag team match featuring Brodido against New Level, consisting of Austin Creed and Kofi. Additionally, a mixed tag team match was confirmed, pitting Mercedes Moné and Jay White against Marina Shafir and Jon Moxley. These additions round out much of the card for the event.
