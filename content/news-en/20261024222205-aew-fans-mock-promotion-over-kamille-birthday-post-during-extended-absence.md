AEW faced criticism from wrestling fans after publishing a social media post celebrating the birthday of Kamille on October 24. Rather than sending warm wishes, many fans used the opportunity to highlight her continued absence from television programming. Some fans questioned if she was still on the roster, while others mocked the long spell without appearances.

Reports confirm that Kamille remains under contract with AEW. Her creative direction was put on hold following her storyline with Mercedes Moné, with no long-term plans established after her removal from television.

Kamille made a brief return at Dynasty on April 12, where she attacked Willow Nightingale backstage and won a pre-show match against Big Anne. She subsequently lost a TBS Championship match to Willow Nightingale on AEW Dynamite three days later and has not appeared on AEW programming since then. Aside from an appearance for Diamond Championship Wrestling that aired in August, her recent match activity has been limited.
