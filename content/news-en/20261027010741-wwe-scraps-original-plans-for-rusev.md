WWE scrapped original plans for Rusev due to an injury, forcing the company to alter its upcoming television storylines. According to WrestleVotes, Rusev was initially supposed to team up with Ethan Page on RAW before a neck injury sidelined him.

Big Cass ultimately took that spot alongside Page, while Rusev suffered his injury against Je’Von Evans on the July 27 episode of RAW. Though Rusev received medical clearance and returned to the ring at an October 16 live event in Honolulu, his television comeback still requires a new direction.
