During a recent episode of Something To Wrestle, WWE Hall of Famer JBL addressed the long-standing rumor regarding backstage tension between Shawn Michaels and Vader following their SummerSlam 1996 match for the WWE Championship. JBL firmly dismissed the claims that Michaels disliked working with Vader because of a stiff wrestling style, stating that the allegation is entirely untrue. He acknowledged that Michaels was a perfectionist regarding in-ring work at the time, but emphasized that HBK did not mind physical contact and respected Vader's toughness.

JBL explained that Vader's struggles in WWE stemmed primarily from the timing of his arrival and the physical toll his career had taken on him by 1996. He noted that Vader had to stop using steroids upon joining the promotion, which significantly impacted his size, strength, and overall physical capabilities. Furthermore, JBL pointed out that Michaels had expected to face the dominant version of Vader seen in Japan and WCW, and that the heavy pressure of the New Generation Era on top stars like Michaels and Bret Hart contributed to the mounting frustrations during that period.
