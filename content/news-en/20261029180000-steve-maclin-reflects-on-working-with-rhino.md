TNA Wrestling star Steve Maclin has opened up about his experience working alongside ECW and WWE legend Rhino. The two shared a series of matches against each other in TNA Wrestling during 2022 and have continued to collaborate in Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling.

Speaking with Fightful's Sean Ross Sapp, Maclin expressed how surreal it feels to share the ring and brainstorm match ideas with veterans he grew up watching on television.

> "I love working with Rhino, just because it's ECW, it's WWE, that nostalgia feeling for me of guys that I've watched and here I am throwing ideas at them and like, 'Oh, yeah, I like that.' Dude, it's still surreal to be able to do that. Because normally the old ways is you just sit there and you shut your mouth and you listen to what the veteran wants to do and you do what they [say]. But here I am with two guys that are willing to do anything and have the faith in me and the trust in me too. It just speaks volumes and I can't thank them enough for the opportunity."

During the conversation, Maclin also discussed the preparation behind his match against Adam Copeland at MLP Northern Rising.
