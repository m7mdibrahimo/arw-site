Adam Page and Brodido defeated Swerve Strickland and the New Level in a Two Out of Three Falls match at AEW WrestleDream to recapture the World Trios Championship. The bout featured intense brawls and interference, with Brody King attacking Strickland before Page intervened as a voice of reason. After the New Level secured the first fall via the Midnight Hour on Bandido, Bandido evened the score following chaos that included a Buckshot from Page. Bandido ultimately secured the victory by pinning Creed after delivering an avalanche flipping bodyslam and reversing a JML Driver into a roll-up. Following the match, Strickland tossed his title belt in front of Page before walking away, marking a conclusion to their recent rivalry after dropping the titles to Strickland and the New Level at AEW All In.
