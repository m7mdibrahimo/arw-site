In a recent interview with TMZ Sports Inside the Ring, Chris Jericho discussed the early days of AEW and revealed that he personally helped recruit Jon Moxley and PAC to the company. Jericho explained that convincing PAC to leave Dragon Gate at the time was not an easy task due to the star's loyalty to the promotion, but he eventually agreed to join. PAC was the top foreign star in Dragon Gate during AEW's formation, and his commitment to defending the Open the Dream Gate Championship delayed his in-ring debut for AEW until late August 2019, after he lost the title to Ben-K in July.
