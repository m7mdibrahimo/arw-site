John Cena made a surprise appearance at the inaugural Banana Bowl Championship on October 10 at Nationals Park. He arrived in a military-style Humvee with a police escort before delivering the ceremonial game ball for the championship game between the Savannah Bananas and the Texas Tailgaters. The Tailgaters won the game 2-1 to claim the title. Cena previously appeared at a Savannah Bananas exhibition game in 2024.
