Kam Hendrix has addressed the ongoing speculation regarding his personal life and his connection to Maxxine Dupri and Austin Theory. During an appearance on NotSam Wrestling, Hendrix was asked by Sam Roberts about fans on social media who feel entitled to comment on his relationship with Dupri. Hendrix maintained his character and insisted that there is no relationship to discuss. "I love it, but I don't know what you're talking about — personal relationship? Kam Hendrix is single. I don't know what you're talking about with that, but honestly, with people's comments, honestly, people are talking, and that's all I care about."

Hendrix appears unbothered by the discourse surrounding him, Dupri, and Theory. He seems satisfied as long as he remains part of the conversation. This follows his approach from October, when he parodied the viral photo of Buddy Matthews and Rhea Ripley by recreating the pose with Dupri. Hendrix continues to treat the storyline with humor rather than addressing real-life relationship rumors.
