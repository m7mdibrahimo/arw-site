Clark Connors believes the War Dogs faction successfully established its own identity. Speaking with Fightful's Sean Ross Sapp ahead of AEW Full Gear 2026, Connors reflected on the War Dogs' time as a sub-group within the BULLET CLUB in NJPW. Connors stated that the group was only part of the BULLET CLUB in name, as the promotion wanted to keep the brand alive for merchandise sales.

> "The War Dogs were BULLET CLUB only in name. We’ve said that before and we’ll continue to say that because it was one of those things that they wanted us to do. They wanted us to keep BULLET CLUB around because it sells a lot of merch but you know what also sells a lot of merch? The War Dogs, and by the end of that whole thing, people forgot about BULLET CLUB. We were the number one merch sellers for a long time out there in Japan, just being the War Dogs. So, coming over here… we made our names for ourselves over there so we wanted to spread the gospel here so, I think we did a pretty good job to start out if you looked at how we’ve been doing so far this year, so, we’re gonna keep it going."

Connors, David Finlay, and Gabe Kidd transitioned from NJPW to AEW in early 2026 and have been with the company for nearly a year.
