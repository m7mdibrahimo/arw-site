Tony Khan recently discussed the possibility of AEW signing Kairi Sane now that she is a free agent. Speaking at the post-AEW WrestleDream media scrum, Khan praised her immense talent while stopping short of confirming any official talks, though he emphasized that he is very interested in her status.

> "She’s a fantastic wrestler. I can’t speak to that at this time, but I am very aware and excited to see that she’s a free agent."

> "And that’s very, very interesting. And I’m very intrigued by the possibility of that."

Meanwhile, reports indicate that Kairi has not yet signed a contract with the promotion despite ongoing rumors, while fans await her appearance on Chris Jericho's upcoming cruise.
