WWE Hall of Famer Ric Flair is facing a serious health issue as he deals with a blood clot in his leg, forcing him to limit his travel and cancel a scheduled appearance at Washington State Horror Con in Seattle. Flair explained that doctors have restricted his trips to a maximum of three to four hours, making Seattle too far for now. He expressed his sincere apologies to his agent Steve Solomon, the convention, and his fans, emphasizing that his health and recovery must come first. Flair noted the severity of blood clots, referencing recent health complications faced by NFL star Jalen Hurts. Fortunately, a treatment plan is already in place, and Flair is scheduled to undergo a procedure to strip the affected vein, which doctors expect will allow him to resume normal travel in the future. This health scare follows previous battles, including melanoma and a torn rotator cuff in 2025. For now, Flair's primary focus remains on his recovery as he prepares for the upcoming procedure.
