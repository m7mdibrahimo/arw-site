BUSHI is officially returning to NJPW for upcoming events. New Japan Pro-Wrestling's Unbound Co. faction is presenting the Bound for Unbound show on November 20th at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo, Japan, adding a new match to the card featuring the comeback of former IWGP Junior Heavyweight Champion BUSHI. He is scheduled to face Phillip Marlowe Jr., an alternate persona belonging to IWGP Heavyweight Champion Yota Tsuji.

NJPW stated in an announcement that Yota Tsuji informed Titan about his friend Phillip Marlowe Jr. wanting to compete in a match to decide the top masked wrestler in Unbound Co., while Titan answered that he had an earlier commitment in Arena Mexico and suggested BUSHI as an alternative. BUSHI's appearance happens 18 months after he departed from the promotion in May of 2025.
