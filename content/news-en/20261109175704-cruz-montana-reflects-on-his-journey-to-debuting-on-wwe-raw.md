Cruz Montana, the newest member of the 946 faction in WWE, recently reflected on his difficult path to the main roster. In a post on Instagram, he shared how his life changed dramatically over the last three and a half years, transitioning from a dark period in rehab to performing on WWE Raw. Montana expressed deep gratitude, crediting his daughter for saving his life and inspiring him to rebuild everything from the ground up. He concluded his message by encouraging others to trust themselves and believe in their own potential.
