Will Ospreay and Callum Newman will challenge for the IWGP Tag Titles at NJPW Power Struggle on November 3. At NJPW King of Pro-Wrestling, United Empire lost the NEVER Openweight Six-Man Tag Team Titles to Unbound Co after Ospreay secured a visual pinfall on Yuto-Ice while the referee was down. Following the match, Newman attacked OSKAR, but Ospreay stopped him and dared Newman to swing his sword at him. Ospreay then challenged Unbound Co for the tag team titles, and they agreed while choosing Newman as Ospreay's partner.
