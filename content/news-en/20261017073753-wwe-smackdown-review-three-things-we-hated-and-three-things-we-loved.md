The October 16, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown featured a mix of segments and matches that drew varied reactions from the Wrestling Inc staff. Here is a look at three highlights and three low points from the broadcast.

Among the negatives, the match between Nikki Bella and Paige was criticized for being sloppy and clunky, resembling a contest between inexperienced performers. Additionally, the bout ended with a fluke roll-up victory for Paige that relied on the ropes. The match between Rey Fenix and Ricky Saints for the AAA Cruiserweight Championship also disappointed, ending in a disqualification after Saints pushed the referee into the ropes. Finally, the main event segment involving Cody Rhodes was marred by the involvement of Baron Corbin, who inserted himself into a situation meant for Randy Orton, resulting in a repetitive disqualification finish.

On the positive side, the Women's Tag Team Championship celebration featuring Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley was saved by an interruption from Fatal Influence. Jacy Jayne, Fallon Henley, and Lainey Reid provided effective heel work that successfully engaged the crowd. Kevin Owens and CM Punk also delivered a standout, albeit brief, backstage segment where Nick Aldis attempted to keep the rivals separated. The tension between the two remained a high point of the show. Lastly, the show concluded with a moment of intensity when Cody Rhodes delivered a punt kick to Baron Corbin. After Randy Orton interfered in the main event, Rhodes countered Corbin with a Cody Cutter before landing the punt, leaving Orton stunned on the entrance ramp.
