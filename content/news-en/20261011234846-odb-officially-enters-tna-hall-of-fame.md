ODB officially entered the TNA Hall of Fame on October 11, 2026, during a special ceremony ahead of Bound For Glory. Al Snow and Traci Brooks handled the honors, sharing memories of ODB's unique career and her refusal to change for anyone. Snow recalled meeting her during the first Tough Enough auditions in 2001, while Brooks praised her authenticity and lasting friendship. TNA President Carlos Silva presented ODB with her plaque to seal her historic induction.
