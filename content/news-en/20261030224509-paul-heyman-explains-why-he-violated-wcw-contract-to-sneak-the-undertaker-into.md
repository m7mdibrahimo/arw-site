Paul Heyman recently discussed his decision to violate his WCW contract in 1990 to help get The Undertaker signed by WWE. Speaking on In Depth with Graham Bensinger, Heyman recalled meeting Mark Calloway in May 1990 after returning to WCW, where Ole Anderson was leading the creative department. Heyman noted that Anderson was out of touch and dismissed Calloway as a talent who would never draw money. When Calloway's contract approached its expiration in September 1990 and WCW attempted to lowball him, Heyman arranged for him to secretly meet with Vince McMahon. Heyman acknowledged that he broke his agreement with Turner Broadcasting to make the meeting happen. "Ole didn't believe in Mark to the point where, when Mark's contract came up in September 1990 and they tried to lowball him, there was someone in WCW that violated their contract with Turner Broadcasting and WCW and snuck Mark into a meeting through a mutual friend, or a friend that I had in WWE that snuck him into the meeting with Vince McMahon," Heyman stated. The meeting successfully led to Calloway signing with WWE and adopting his iconic persona. Reflecting on his actions, Heyman explained that he believed WCW was about to waste an extraordinary talent who went on to become one of the greatest acts in wrestling history.
