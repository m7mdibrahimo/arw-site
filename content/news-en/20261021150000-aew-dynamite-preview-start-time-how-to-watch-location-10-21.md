AEW Dynamite takes place on October 21, 2026, at the Watsco Center in Coral Gables, FL, airing live at 8 p.m. ET on TBS and Max. The match card features Ilja Dragunov facing Speedball Mike Bailey, and Will Ospreay teaming with Adam Copeland to take on Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher. Additionally, Maya World battles Queen Aminata in an AEW TBS Championship number one contenders match, while The Young Bucks square off against Mark Davis and Jake Doyle in an AEW Tag Team Championship eliminator match.
