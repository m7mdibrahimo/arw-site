Chris Jericho recently shared his thoughts on DeLorean cars, explaining that seeing one up close ruined the appeal for him. During an episode of Talk Is Jericho, Jericho and Brad Gilmore discussed the film Back to the Future Part III, leading Gilmore to mention how fans still seek out the iconic vehicles. Jericho admitted he does not understand the obsession, calling the cars clunky and poor in quality upon closer inspection. He noted that seeing a DeLorean owned by WWE star Sheamus only confirmed his opinion. Although Jericho remains a fan of the movie franchise, he made it clear he has no intention of ever buying one for himself.
