Former Divas Champion Kaitlyn opened up about her departure from WWE in 2014, explaining that she felt she could finally breathe after asking for her release due to immense mental and physical struggles. During an appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, she recalled feeling that she had not done enough with the massive platform given to her.

Kaitlyn noted that she experienced immediate relief and a sense of liberation upon leaving, wanting to build a new identity and start a business rather than immediately planning another wrestling run. However, she later wondered if things might have turned out differently if she had known how to articulate her feelings and talk to someone within the company.

She also shared that she did not inform her close friends backstage, including AJ Lee and Big E, because she simply wanted to leave without making a major backstage situation. WWE eventually granted her a final match against AJ Lee on Main Event, and she later made a one-night return for the 2018 Mae Young Classic.
