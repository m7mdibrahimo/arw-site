John 'Bradshaw' Layfield revealed on an episode of "Something To Wrestle" that Vince McMahon chose Rio De Janeiro as the location for the fictional 1979 tournament to crown the inaugural Intercontinental Champion simply as a rib on Pat Patterson. Layfield explained that McMahon knew Patterson could not pronounce the city's name correctly due to his French accent. Patterson was handed the title after the fictional tournament and went on to become a legendary figure in WWE until his passing in December 2020. Today, the Intercontinental Championship legacy continues, with Chad Gable currently holding the belt after defeating Penta at SummerSlam, and Gable is set to defend his title against Rey Fenix on the upcoming WWE RAW.
