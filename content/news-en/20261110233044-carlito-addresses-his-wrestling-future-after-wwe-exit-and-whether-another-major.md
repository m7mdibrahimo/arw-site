Carlito is not actively seeking a return to WWE or another major wrestling contract following his departure from the company. During a recent appearance on the Hitting the Turnbuckle Podcast, the former Intercontinental Champion discussed his current career status and his approach to independent wrestling. Carlito emphasized that he is not putting pressure on himself to secure a full-time television deal.

> "Um yeah, I'm just whatever the future is, I don't really know what the future is."

He noted that he is enjoying his current freedom and is in no rush to commit to a specific path. When asked about potentially competing for independent championships in the United Kingdom, Carlito expressed openness to the idea.

> "Sure, why not? Let's just we got a I got a couple left in me at this point still."

He also joked about his criteria for pursuing another title, suggesting he would prefer to face the "weakest champion." While Carlito is not ruling out future opportunities, he remains content with his current situation after his latest run with The Judgment Day.
