Nic Nemeth recently spoke about how the wrestling industry's workload has changed over the years, reflecting on his time with WWE. He noted that working five or six days a week for years took a heavy toll on performers before schedules became lighter following the COVID-19 pandemic.

Nemeth revealed that he once pitched an idea to Vince McMahon to create fake time off and manufactured returns for talent, because the grueling year-round schedule made it impossible to get a normal break. He suggested using storyline write-offs, such as being attacked and sidelined, to give performers a rest and create exciting comeback moments for the fans.
