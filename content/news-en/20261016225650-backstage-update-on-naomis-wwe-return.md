Recent backstage reports indicate that the return of Naomi to WWE television could happen sooner rather than later. According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, there have been internal discussions about her potential comeback. Naomi has been away from the ring since August 2025, when she vacated the Women's World Championship on WWE RAW to announce her pregnancy with her husband, Jimmy Uso. Since then, the couple welcomed a baby boy following some minor pregnancy complications, while keeping details mostly private.
