Will Ospreay recently discussed his deep passion for professional wrestling and the extreme lengths he would go to in order to continue performing. Speaking with WKMG News 6 ahead of AEW WrestleDream, Ospreay reflected on his journey from wrestling on a trampoline in his backyard to his formal training in the industry. He emphasized how grateful he is for his current career, noting that his dedication to the sport goes far beyond mainstream success. Ospreay stated, "I'm so blessed to be able to be in this position, to be able to just do something that... I love doing, like I've said it so many times, if wrestling became like illegal tomorrow, like I'd be in my garden wrestling. Like I'd happily, like I'd be like the criminal mastermind of like an underground, Mafia wrestling run promotion. Do you know what I mean? That's just me."
