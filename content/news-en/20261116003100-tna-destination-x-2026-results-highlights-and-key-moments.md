TNA Destination X aired live on November 15, 2026, from the Edmonton Expo Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. Travel complications altered the event's lineup, forcing the cancellation of the scheduled TNA World Tag Team Championship match.

On the Countdown To TNA Destination X Pre-Show, AJ Francis cut a promo demanding to be added to the Ultimate X match. Later, Vincent from The Righteous interrupted Elijah's musical segment, leading to an impromptu singles match.

---  
Pre-Show Match: Singles Match  

Elijah faced Vincent in an unannounced encounter after Vincent attacked him during a musical performance. Elijah secured the pinfall victory after delivering the Highwayman's Farewell.

🏆 Winner: Elijah  

---  
Match 1: TNA Knockouts World Championship #1 Contender’s Ultimate X Match  

All six competitors battled for the suspended X, with Shayna Baszler using her submission moves to control the action early on. Shots of Singapore Cane strikes from Kiera Hogan cleared Shotzi Blackheart from the ropes, allowing Tasha Steelz to retrieve the X.

🏆 Winner: Tasha Steelz  

---  
Match 2: No DQ Match  

Matt Hardy called out Syx after travel issues disrupted his original match, leading to an attack by Joseph Sawyer. Director of Authority Santino Marella made the match official under No DQ rules. Hardy used trash cans and chairs before executing a Twist of Fate through a chair for the pinfall.

🏆 Winner: Matt Hardy  

---  
Match 3: 2026 World X-Cup 5-Way Elimination Match  

Home Town Man eliminated Amir Jordan after a Home Town Slice. Fabian Aichner pinned Home Town Man after a boot. Aichner then hit a moonsault on Jason Hotch to eliminate him. In the final stretch, Ricky Sosa hit Fabian Aichner with a cutter off the top rope followed by a roll-up to win the match.

🏆 Winner: Ricky Sosa  

---  
Match 4: Call Your Shot Gauntlet Match  

Mustafa Ali and Frankie Kazarian wrestled a back-and-forth contest until both men shoved the referee while brawling near the ropes. The referee threw out the match, declaring it a no contest. Following the decision, Ali attacked Kazarian with the ring bell and a steel chair.

🤝 Result: No contest  

---  
Match 5: TNA X-Division Championship #1 Contender’s Ultimate X Match  

Director of Authority Santino Marella added TJP as a surprise entrant. TJP sprayed red mist into KC Navarro's eyes during the match. Despite the attack, Navarro pulled himself onto the X rope, retrieved the X while trapped in an electric chair setup by AJ Francis, and earned the victory.

🏆 Winner: KC Navarro  

---  
Main Event: TNA World Championship Match  

Trey Miguel arrived at the arena in time for the main event against TNA World Champion Leon Slater. Both competitors exchanged heavy strikes and high-flying moves, including a Styles Clash attempt by Slater and a meteora by Miguel. Slater avoided an exposed turnbuckle attack and executed a Swanton 450 to secure the pinfall and retain his title.

🏆 Winner: Leon Slater
