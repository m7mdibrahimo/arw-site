TNA Destination X took place on November 15, 2026, at the Edmonton Expo Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. Severe travel delays forced several changes to the lineup, including the cancellation of the scheduled TNA World Tag Team Championship match.

On the Countdown pre-show, Tom Hannifan and Matthew Rehwoldt broke down the altered card and aired video features covering the main event between TNA World Champion Leon Slater and Trey Miguel, who was still in transit. AJ Francis came to the ring to boast about his private jet arrival and declared his intention to win the X-Division title and cash in Option C. Backstage, Shayna Baszler vented about Indi Hartwell's absence before Santino Marella booked her into the Knockouts Ultimate X match.

Elijah then appeared in the ring with a guitar to reveal that TNA Radio is heading to iHeartRadio. Vincent of The Righteous cut him off, mocking his inability to capture the World Championship. A brawl broke out, prompting a referee to start an impromptu singles match.

Elijah def. Vincent via pinfall after hitting the Highwayman's Farewell.

Director of Authority Santino Marella addressed the crowd, praising the roster for enduring severe travel disruptions to get to Edmonton.

The main pay-per-view card began with the Knockouts World Championship #1 Contender's Ultimate X match.

Tasha Steelz def. Shotzi Blackheart, Kiera Hogan, Shayna Baszler, M By Elegance, and Heather By Elegance to become the number one contender for the TNA Knockouts World Championship after retrieving the X from the cables.

TNA World Champion Leon Slater stated in a promo that he is ready to take on whoever steps into the ring with him.

Matt Hardy came to the ring and expressed his anger that his scheduled opponents were unable to make the show. He called out anyone in the back, leading to an attack from Syx member Joseph Sawyer. Santino Marella came out and sanctioned an immediate No Disqualification match.

Matt Hardy def. Joseph Sawyer in a No DQ match after executing a Twist of Fate using a steel chair wrapped around Sawyer's neck.

World X-Cup 2026 continued with an elimination match featuring talent from across the division.

Ricky Sosa def. Fabian Aichner, Jason Hotch, Home Town Man, and Amir Jordan in a 5-Way Elimination Match. Home Town Man eliminated Jordan by pinfall. Aichner then eliminated Home Town Man by pinfall. Moments later, Aichner eliminated Hotch with a moonsault pinfall. Sosa outlasted Aichner, scoring the final pinfall after a series of uppercuts.

Backstage, officials confirmed that Trey Miguel arrived at the building, locking in the TNA World Championship main event against Leon Slater as scheduled.
