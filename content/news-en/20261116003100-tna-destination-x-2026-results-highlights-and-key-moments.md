TNA Destination X took place on November 15, 2026, live from the Edmonton Expo Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. Tom Hannifan and Matthew Rehovdt welcomed fans to the pre-show, discussing travel issues that led to changes on the card, including the cancellation of the TNA Tag Team Championship match. Trey Miguel and Leon Slater were featured in documentary video packages highlighting their journeys to the main event TNA World Championship match. AJ Francis cut a promo claiming he would win the Ultimate X match, defeat Amazing Red for the X-Division Championship, and cash in Option C. Shayna Baszler cut a backtage promo stating she would rip apart ligaments. Elijah performed a concert in the ring before Vincent of The Righteous interrupted and attacked him. In the singles match, Elijah defeated Vincent after executing a tombstone piledriver and pinning him.
