TNA Destination X aired live on November 15, 2026, from the Edmonton Expo Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. The event featured TNA World Champion Leon Slater defending his title against Trey Miguel, alongside high-stakes Ultimate X matches. The pre-show included a documentary on Trey Miguel's career and his journey to the main event, as well as a look at Leon Slater's path to the championship. During the pre-show, Elijah defeated Vincent in a singles match after hitting the Highwayman's Farewell. 

In the opening match of the main card, Tasha Steelz won the TNA Knockouts World Championship #1 Contender’s Ultimate X Match, defeating M By Elegance, Heather By Elegance, Shotzi Blackheart, Kiera Hogan, and Shayna Baszler. Later, Matt Hardy defeated Joseph Sawyer in a No DQ match after brawling to the floor and using the Ultimate X structure to his advantage. The event also featured promotional segments, including an announcement from Elijah regarding TNA Radio and a promo from Leon Slater asserting his status as the champion.
