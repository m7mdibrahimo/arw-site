TNA Destination X took place on November 15, 2026, at the Edmonton Expo Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. The event featured several high-stakes matches, including TNA World Champion Leon Slater defending his title against Trey Miguel and TNA Knockouts World Champion Indi Hartwell defending against Shayna Baszler. The pre-show, hosted by Tom Hannifan and Matthew Rehwoldt, addressed travel issues that forced changes to the card, including the cancellation of the TNA Tag Team Championship match.

During the event, AJ Francis expressed his desire to win the Ultimate X match and eventually cash in for the TNA World Championship. Elijah also appeared to announce the upcoming launch of TNA Radio on the iHeartRadio network before engaging in an impromptu match against Vincent of The Righteous.

Elijah def. Vincent

Tasha Steelz def. M By Elegance, Heather By Elegance, Shotzi Blackheart, Kiera Hogan, and Shayna Baszler in an Ultimate X match to become the #1 contender for the TNA Knockouts World Championship.

Matt Hardy def. Joseph Sawyer in a No DQ match after Hardy attacked Sawyer with a chair and a trash can, finishing with a Twist of Fate.

Ricky Sosa def. Home Town Man, Amir Jordan, Fabian Aichner, and Jason Hotch in a 5-Way Elimination match to win the World X-Cup.

Mustafa Ali vs. Frankie Kazarian ended in a no contest after an altercation with the referee. Following the match, Ali continued his assault on Kazarian using the ring bell to send a message to management and the fans.
