Joe Hendry is currently navigating a difficult period in his WWE career, which he believes stems from the injury suffered by Logan Paul. Before Paul was sidelined, Hendry was engaged in a heated feud with him, even releasing a parody song that called for WWE to fire the YouTuber. The former TNA World Champion acknowledged that Paul's absence has hindered his momentum, but he remains focused on his ultimate objective.

"Well, I mean, you know, I'm in a tough spot right now. So, I was in a feud with Logan Paul. He got injured, so I couldn't take that where it needed to go, maybe do some more songs about Logan. Who knows? Then I had problems with The Vision and The Judgment Day. I got problems with everybody. So, I'm in a tough spot here. I got problems with everyone," Hendry said on the "Michael Fairman Channel."

Despite the setbacks, Hendry is determined to become a top star in WWE. "But, I mean, for me, my goal is to be a champion in WWE. You know, when I got to NXT and I became NXT Champion, I knew I could do it. I know that I can be a top star in WWE. I have the tools to do it. So, that's what I'm trying to do. I'm trying to become one of the top guys over a long period of time. That's my long-term goal."

Hendry, who is currently involved in a feud with The Judgment Day and recently suffered a loss to JD McDonagh on "WWE Raw," is also targeting the Intercontinental Championship currently held by Chad Gable. While he admits he still has work to do, he is fully committed to the effort required to reach the world championship level.
