Swerve Strickland and The New Level became the new AEW World Trios Champions at AEW Full Gear on November 14, 2026, defeating Hangman Page and Brodido in a chaotic Texas Death Match. The match quickly devolved into violence as all six men utilized various weapons. Hangman Page and Brodido used wooden skewers on Strickland, Austin Creed, and Kofi Kingston, while Creed retaliated with a barbed wire-wrapped trombone. The brutality escalated with staple guns, a barbed wire table, and a Power Glove-induced explosion in Brody King's face. After a series of intense exchanges, including a Vertebreaker on the steel steps and a frog splash onto thumbtacks, Strickland secured the win by knocking out Page with a House Call. The referee's count confirmed the victory for Strickland, Creed, and Kingston, concluding a match defined by blood and weapons.
