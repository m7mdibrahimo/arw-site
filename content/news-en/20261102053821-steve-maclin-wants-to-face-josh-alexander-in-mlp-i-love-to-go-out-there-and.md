Steve Maclin discussed his time in TNA Wrestling and his future in Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling. Maclin was released from TNA on June 7, 2026, after a five-year tenure. Speaking with Fightful's Sean Ross Sapp, Maclin stated that he made the most of every opportunity given to him during the different regimes, including those of Scott D'Amore and Carlos Silva. He noted that many people from his past, such as Jimmy Jacobs and D'Lo Brown, are now working behind the scenes at Maple Leaf Pro, creating a familiar environment.

Regarding his goals in Maple Leaf Pro, Maclin expressed a strong desire to face Josh Alexander. 

> "First that comes to mind is Josh Alexander. I love to go out there and have a war with my best friend. I know he wouldn't hold anything back and I wouldn't hold anything back because we know what's on the line and that would be the Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling Heavyweight Championship, which is the most prestigious Canadian championship that there is."

Maclin also mentioned Stu Grayson as another potential dream opponent. He praised the card for MLP Northern Rising, noting that it showcased the variety of professional wrestling. He added that the possibilities for his future opponents in Maple Leaf Pro are endless.
