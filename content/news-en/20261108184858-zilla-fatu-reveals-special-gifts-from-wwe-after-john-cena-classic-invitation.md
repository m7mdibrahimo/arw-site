Zilla Fatu recently shared his special welcome package from WWE after being invited to the John Cena Classic. Fatu posted a video on his YouTube vlog to show off a customized briefcase filled with exclusive merchandise, including a Rise Above Hate jacket, a watch, and playing cards. The package also contained two mysterious coins and a sealed letter explaining their specific purpose for the tournament. John Cena announced on WWE NXT that Fatu is the second competitor and the first NXT talent to join the tournament, which takes place on December 14 at Disney World. Fatu joins Cody Rhodes as the only announced participants so far.
