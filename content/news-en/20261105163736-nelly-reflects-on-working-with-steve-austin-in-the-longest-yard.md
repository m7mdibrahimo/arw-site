The 2005 film The Longest Yard featured several professional wrestlers, including Steve Austin, The Great Khali, Goldberg, and Kevin Nash, alongside former American football players and rapper Nelly.

Austin portrayed a racist prison guard who attempted to provoke the character played by Nelly, a running back on the inmate team. During the movie, Austin delivered offensive remarks to Nelly's character, telling him, "That's how a white man runs the football."

While appearing on 7 PM In Brooklyn with Carmelo Anthony, Nelly discussed his experience working with Austin by stating, "He was super cool. Stone Cold, everybody was super cool. He had to sell it. He sold the shit out of that motherfucker. He was super cool." Furthermore, former WWE writer Kazeem Famuyide noted that Austin fully committed to the role with all his energy while filming those scenes.
