This week's "TNA Impact" featured a mix of title picture developments and a lackluster singles debut. The show included a four-way number one contender's match, the introduction of Shayna Baszler to the Knockouts division, and a Fire N' Flava reunion.

In the main event, Jason Hotch defeated Mustafa Ali in a two-out-of-three falls match to retain his title. Following the bout, Hotch and Leon Slater celebrated their status as foundational members of the TNA roster.

Regarding the negatives, Cedric Alexander's path to an X-Division title rematch was criticized for being unnecessarily complicated, as he had to survive a four-way match to secure a shot at new champion Amazing Red. Additionally, Avery Styles' singles debut against Joe Alonzo was poorly received; the match was described as sluggish and highlighted the inexperience of the younger Styles.

On the positive side, Shayna Baszler's arrival in the Knockouts division is seen as a major upgrade, providing a fresh threat to champion Indi Hartwell. Furthermore, the reunion of Tasha Steelz and Kiera Hogan as Fire N' Flava was praised, as it allows both competitors to return to their most effective character personas. Finally, the ongoing storyline involving Harley Hudson and The Righteous was noted for having potential, though it was criticized for its current lack of clarity.
