Looking back at his memorable WrestleMania 38 match against Johnny Knoxville, Sami Zayn opened up about the unique dynamic and the intensity of their feud during an appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet. Zayn recalled how the bout relied heavily on Knoxville's signature Jackass stunts rather than traditional wrestling maneuvers, featuring unforgettable spots like a giant hand slap, a mousetrap table, and a bowling ball to the groin. Reflecting on Knoxville's performance, Zayn praised his dedication while playfully admitting his lingering frustration. "He was perfect. I think he was just perfect. I mean, I hate him," Zayn said, adding that no other pairing could have replicated that exact match.
