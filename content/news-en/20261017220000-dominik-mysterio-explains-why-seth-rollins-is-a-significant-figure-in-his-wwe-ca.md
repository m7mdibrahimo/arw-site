Dominik Mysterio recently opened up about his WWE career and discussed why Seth Rollins holds a major significance in his journey. Speaking on the Six Feet Under podcast, Mysterio reflected on his wrestling roots, recalling his early appearances and his debut match against Rollins in a street fight during the Thunderdome era in 2020. Mysterio praised Rollins for his incredible mind for the wrestling business, explaining how working with him helped develop his technical skills and sparked his creativity. Since his first 15 to 20 matches were all against Rollins during the pandemic, Mysterio credited him with guiding him through the unique circumstances of that era and always knowing how to maneuver him inside the ring.

> "I really enjoyed working Seth," Mysterio said. "I think just coming up, like having my first match with him and seeing how he works and how his mind works with the whole business and with wrestling and how he puts a match together, he's on another level when it comes to stuff like that."

> "I don't know if it's because he had my first match and he just kind of like saw it," Mysterio explained. "Honestly, my first 15, 20 matches were all with Seth, because it was all during the pandemic, so he kind of just like guided me through this whole thing. So, I think that's why he knows what to do and how to maneuver me around and he's the man."
