Nia Jax recently commented on Tiffany Stratton successfully replicating her own Money in the Bank cash-in strategy during the WWE Crown Jewel event against Chelsea Green. Stratton is a two-time women's Money in the Bank winner who secured world titles by cashing in on her close friends, Nia Jax and Chelsea Green.

During a recent interview with Jimmy V3, Jax addressed Stratton's method of befriending champions before betraying them to claim their titles.

> "The person who got cashed in on, that kind of bothers me, but… from like a strategy standpoint, she's got a really good strategy, like she gets in with the, with, she wins the briefcase and then she becomes besties with the champ. Like it really is kind of a good strategy. Like I can't knock that, she, she's, you don't, I didn't expect it, and I doubt Chelsea expected it, even though I warned Chelsea. It's a good strategy that she's using. I can't knock it."

In another portion of the interview, Jax discussed the origins of her ring name.
