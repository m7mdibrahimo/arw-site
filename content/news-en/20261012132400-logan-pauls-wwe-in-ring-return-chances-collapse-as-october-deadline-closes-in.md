Logan Paul's chances of returning to a WWE ring before the end of October have dropped significantly to just five percent. Paul has been recovering from a torn triceps suffered during a World Tag Team Championship defense at Saturday Night's Main Event on May 23. Medical staff and officials, including Bruce Prichard and Triple H, have informed Paul that he will not be cleared until at least November. Despite wanting an early return, Paul's hopes for an October comeback have effectively ended as the company takes a cautious approach to his recovery.
