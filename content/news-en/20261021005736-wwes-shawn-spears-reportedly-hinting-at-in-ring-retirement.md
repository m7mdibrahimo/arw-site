Is the career of WWE NXT star Shawn Spears nearing its end? A recent incident at a WWE live event in Kitchener, Ontario, has sparked speculation regarding his future in the ring. According to the latest Wrestling Observer Newsletter, Dave Meltzer reported that Spears addressed the crowd following an eight-man tag team match, stating that the bout would be his final performance in Canada. Although Spears later competed in Sudbury, Ontario, the following night, his initial comments have led many to wonder if the 45-year-old veteran is preparing to retire soon.

Spears had been largely inactive during the latter part of the summer, with his recent live event appearances marking his first matches since July. Before this brief hiatus, he had been featured on television as the leader of The Culling stable, eventually being written out of the group in late June. Since returning to WWE in early 2024, Spears has balanced his in-ring career with a backstage role as a producer for NXT. Prior to his WWE comeback, he spent four years in AEW, where he notably feuded with Cody Rhodes and served as a key member of The Pinnacle alongside MJF, Wardlow, FTR, and Tully Blanchard.
