Adam Copeland recently revealed on the October 14th edition of AEW Dynamite that he plans to retire from professional wrestling in 2027. Following this announcement, AEW President Tony Khan discussed the news during the WrestleDream media call, noting that Copeland's decision had been in the works for some time.

During the same media session, Khan was questioned regarding the potential for Adam Copeland to perform at the Tokyo Dome, a venue the veteran has expressed interest in competing at throughout his career. Khan remained tight-lipped on the subject, stating, "I'll leave some mystery to that." With Wrestle Kingdom 21 scheduled for January 4th, 2027, the wrestling world continues to watch for potential collaborations, as AEW stars like Kenny Omega, Will Ospreay, MJF, and Jon Moxley remain active in NJPW storylines leading up to the event.
