During a recent appearance on Shannon Sharpe's Club Shay Shay, Roman Reigns explained why he and other top WWE stars like John Cena, The Rock, and Batista tend to keep to themselves backstage before a show. Reigns noted that after reaching the top of the industry, they share a similar mindset focused on conserving energy for their performances. He compared it to a football mentality where production matters most, admitting he had to choose between being one of the boys in the locker room or becoming a megastar. Reigns acknowledged this approach might make him seem standoffish, but he remains focused on delivering his best in the main event.
