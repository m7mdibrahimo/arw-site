AEW WrestleDream 2026 is set to take place tonight, October 17, at the Addition Financial Arena in Orlando, Florida. The event features a stacked card, headlined by a Clockwork Carnage match for the AEW World Championship where Will Ospreay defends his title against seven challengers: Samoa Joe, Mike Bailey, Darby Allin, Kazuchika Okada, Ricochet, Adam Copeland, and Konosuke Takeshita. In other championship action, Mercedes Moné defends the AEW Women's World Championship against Thekla, The Young Bucks defend the AEW World Tag Team Championship against Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight, Swerve Strickland and The New Level defend the AEW World Trios Championship against Hangman Adam Page and Brodido in a Two-Out-of-Three Falls match, Andrade El Idolo defends the AEW National Championship against Tommaso Ciampa, and Jon Moxley defends the AEW Continental Championship against Mark Davis. Additionally, Persephone defends the TBS Championship in a Four-Way match against Hyan, Dani Luna, and Queen Aminata, Chris Jericho faces Nick Wayne, FTR takes on the Motor City Machine Guns in a best-of-five series opener on the preshow, and Divine Dominion battles the Sisters of Sin on the preshow. The Tailgate Brawl preshow starts at 7 PM ET on TNT and streams on HBO Max, while the main show begins at 8 PM ET on HBO Max, Amazon Prime, PPV.com, YouTube, and traditional cable and satellite providers.
