AJ Styles may be taking on a significantly larger role behind the scenes in NXT than previously known. According to Andrew Baydala, Styles has been quietly "running things" at the WWE Performance Center for months. This development aligns with persistent rumors that Styles could assume a major leadership role in NXT once Shawn Michaels moves on from his current position following WrestleMania 43. While WWE has not officially named Styles as Michaels' replacement, his increased involvement is not entirely unexpected. Styles has previously disclosed that he has been filling in for coaches, producing matches, and working directly with younger talent at the Performance Center. These responsibilities have sparked interest from figures like Matt Hardy, who believes Styles' extensive background on the independent wrestling circuit gives him a unique perspective on talent development. Hardy noted that Styles is likely more attuned to the current independent wrestling scene and perhaps more willing to provide opportunities to emerging stars than Michaels. Although there has been no official announcement regarding a permanent transition, the expectation among those within the Performance Center is that Styles will take on even more responsibility once Michaels departs.
