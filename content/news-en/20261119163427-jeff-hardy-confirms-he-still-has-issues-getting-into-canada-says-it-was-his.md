Jeff Hardy recently discussed his memorable TNA appearance where he was seemingly eliminated in a brutal segment. During an interview with Chris Van Vliet on Insight, Hardy explained that it was entirely his own idea to be put in a trash can and chopped with axes by Samuel Shaw and Joseph Sawyer, a scene that effectively killed off his character in the storyline.

Reflecting on the unique segment, Hardy shared details about the creative process behind the Slaughterhouse match. “I’m on the other side. I’m just a hologram," Hardy said. " Yeah, in the pro wrestling world, I’m on the other side out there in the stars. It was nasty. But that was one of those things. This Slaughterhouse match idea they came up with, and it was like we had to be real creative to come up with what we were going to do in that match, and it all turned out pretty cool, I thought. Especially when they get done with that, and then you edit it and you watch it back. It’s always so fun, making a little movie. But yeah, that was pretty nasty, man. That’s a hell of a way to take me out.”

Hardy also revealed the humorous reasoning behind the segment's name, noting that his brother Matt came up with a fitting joke. “Matt had a really witty name for that thing. He said, ‘This is the real Canadian Destroyer’ because I have issues getting into Canada, and actually, that was a good joke on Matt’s part. It just looked great watching it back, and I just loved it. That was my idea, and so it’s always cool when you dream up something in your imagination, and it comes out so good and so well in reality, and especially in front of a live audience. But yeah, as of right now, storyline-wise, I’m on the other side.” Hardy has previously faced legal issues related to DUIs, and TNA recently traveled to Canada for Destination X along with TV tapings in Edmonton.
