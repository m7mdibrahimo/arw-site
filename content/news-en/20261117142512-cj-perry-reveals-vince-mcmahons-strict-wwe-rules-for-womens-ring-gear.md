CJ Perry recently discussed the strict rules Vince McMahon enforced regarding women's ring gear during WWE's PG era. Known during her time in WWE as Lana, Perry explained that female talent faced heavy restrictions on what they could wear, including a ban on bikini tops and outfits that showed too much skin. She shared that she regularly had to wear mesh shorts underneath her wrestling attire and tights to prevent her backside from showing because McMahon wanted more coverage. During an appearance on The Joe Vulpis Podcast, Perry pointed to Maxxine Dupri as a modern star enjoying more freedom today, noting that Dupri's outfits bring back memories of the Divas and Attitude eras. Perry recalled that she was not allowed to show side boob, a restriction she found frustrating due to her modeling background, and noted that earlier periods in WWE history were even stricter. She also mentioned that performers like Nikki and Brie Bella, Kelly Kelly, and Natalya worked under similarly tight guidelines before the standards gradually began to loosen over time.
