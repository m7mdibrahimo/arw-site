WWE Money In The Bank 2026 brought a mix of highs and lows, featuring thrilling ladder action, a shocking ambulance return by Je'Von Evans, and some controversial title changes. In the women's ladder match, Tiffany Stratton secured the briefcase, a decision that left many fans wishing for a fresh face like Lash Legend, Sol Ruca, Lola Vice, or Roxanne Perez to take the spot instead. Meanwhile, the opening match saw LA Knight put up a stellar performance before losing to Roman Reigns in a World Heavyweight Championship bout, though Royce Keys and the 946 made their presence known in the crowd afterward. In another notable match, Oba Femi defeated Bronson Reed after interference from The Vision, kicking out of multiple brutal blows including a Tsunami. The men's ladder match delivered an unforgettable spectacle, capped off by Je'Von Evans driving an ambulance back to ringside, getting launched through its roof, and Penta ultimately climbing the ladder to grab the briefcase and cement his path to the world title. Finally, the evening saw Liv Morgan capture the Women's World Championship in a triple threat match against champion Stephanie Vaquer and Becky Lynch, sparking mixed reactions regarding the title's booking moving forward.
