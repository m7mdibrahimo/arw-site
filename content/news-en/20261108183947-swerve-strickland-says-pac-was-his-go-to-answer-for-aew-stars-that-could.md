Swerve Strickland recently revealed that PAC was his immediate answer when asked which AEW star could potentially become AEW World Champion. Speaking in an interview with Sportskeeda, Strickland discussed his time at the top of the men's division and noted that PAC was previously his go-to choice for the title. Strickland also named several other talents he believes will eventually hold the world championship, including Andrade, Kevin Knight, Kyle Fletcher, and Ricochet, alongside Megan Bayne for the women's division. Earlier this week, it was confirmed that Strickland and The New Level will face Hangman Page and Brodido in a Texas Deathmatch at AEW Full Gear.
