Randy Orton is currently working as a heel in his feud with Cody Rhodes, but fans continue to cheer him regardless of his actions. Dave Meltzer discussed the issue on Wrestling Observer Radio, noting that Orton's legendary status and 25-year career make it difficult for audiences to boo him. Meltzer pointed out that the problem could become even more pronounced at Crown Jewel in Saudi Arabia, where international crowds often give Orton an overwhelmingly positive reaction. With Orton and Rhodes set to collide in a Last Man Standing match, WWE faces the challenge of a crowd that is far more interested in cheering the veteran than jeering him.
