Ethan Page won the Intercontinental Championship on WWE Raw by defeating Chad Gable. Despite achieving his ultimate goal so quickly, Page admitted he feels weird about it and needs to reassess his goals. He also noted that everyone else gets to watch his career from the outside, and told fans who are unhappy with his win to focus on him now.
