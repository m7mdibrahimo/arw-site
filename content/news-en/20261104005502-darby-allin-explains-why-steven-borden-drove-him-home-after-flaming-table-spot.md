Darby Allin credits Steven Borden with a big assist following their brutal encounter at Blood & Guts 2025. Speaking in a new interview with Going Ringside, Allin reflected on the match where PAC put him through a pair of flaming tables near the entrance tunnel, and revealed why Borden drove him home afterward. "Actually last year when I got thrown through the flaming table, he drove me home. It was about a four hour car ride and I had my shirt off because I didn't want — my whole skin on my back was pretty much gone. I didn't want it to stick to the seat. So, I was [leaning forward] the whole time while he was driving me. He saw the pain I was in last year, so I don't know if that gets him excited for tomorrow," Allin said. The two are set to team up in this year's Blood & Guts match.
