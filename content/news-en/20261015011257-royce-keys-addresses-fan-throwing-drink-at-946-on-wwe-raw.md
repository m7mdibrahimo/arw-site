Royce Keys says he is not bothered by fans throwing drinks at 946 during WWE RAW. In an appearance on The Ebro Laura Rosenberg Show, the WWE star noted that the incident reminded him of the chaotic atmosphere of Mid-South wrestling, suggesting that the faction's ability to provoke such strong reactions is a positive sign. Keys explained that the group's growing popularity has led to fans frequently approaching him in public to shout the faction's name. During the October 12 episode of WWE RAW, 946 made their entrance through the crowd for a match against Jimmy Uso, Jey Uso and Solo Sikoa. Footage surfaced online showing a fan throwing a drink toward Lucien Price, which resulted in the fan being escorted from the arena by security. Keys, who saw the clip on Instagram, expressed that he enjoyed seeing the crowd so emotionally invested in their work. The night was a major one for the group, as their match against The Bloodline featured the arrival of Carmelo Hayes, who attacked Solo Sikoa with a tire iron to align himself with the faction.
