Lucha Libre AAA will run an event at Arena GNP Seguros in Acapulco on January 2. The venue regularly hosts tennis tournaments and can seat approximately 12,000 spectators.

Promotional material for the show advertises El Grande Americano, La Catalina, Rey Mysterio, Dominik Mysterio, and Omos. Perros Del Mal, Psycho Circus, Mr. Iguana, and La Parka are also featured on the graphics.

The Undertaker hyped the upcoming card in a statement:

> "New year. New memories. One unforgettable night in Acapulco. 🌴🔥 On January 2, welcome the new year together at Arena GNP Seguros, surrounded by the warmth of the coast and the energy of an unforgettable night."
