Mercedes Moné opened up about winning the AEW Women's Championship from Willow Nightingale at AEW All In in her newsletter, Moné Mag. She recalled sitting in the crowd as a fan three years prior and knowing Wembley Stadium was where she belonged. Despite suffering a broken nose early in the bout, she powered through the pain just as Willow competed with a broken hand. Moné is scheduled to defend her title against Marina Shafir at AEW Full Gear.
