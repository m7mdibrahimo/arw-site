Taryn Terrell has officially revived The Dollhouse in TNA Wrestling. A promotional vignette first aired on the November 5 episode of Thursday Night iMPACT to build anticipation for the group's return. Terrell, an original member of the faction, initially brought Harley Hudson into the fold before the duo added Mila Moore to finalize the lineup.

During the Destination X event on November 15, TNA confirmed that The Dollhouse will make their live presence felt on the November 19 broadcast of Thursday Night iMPACT. The promotion is holding television tapings leading directly into the upcoming episode.
