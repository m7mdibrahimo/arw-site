Netflix is currently developing a new documentary focused on the career of Dana White. According to reports, the project is scheduled for release in early 2027 and will be directed by Gotham Chopra.

Initially intended to be a film about Las Vegas, the documentary evolved to center on the 57-year-old UFC president. The narrative tracks his journey from convincing Frank and Lorenzo Fertitta to purchase the promotion for $2 million in 2001, to the eventual sale of the company to an Endeavor-led group for $4 billion fifteen years later.

This production arrives as Netflix holds the rights to WWE Raw in the United States and all WWE programming internationally. Meanwhile, Paramount+ secured the rights to UFC events in a seven-year, $7.7 billion deal following the promotion's departure from ESPN.
