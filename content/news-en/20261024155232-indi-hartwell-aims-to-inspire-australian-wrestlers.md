Indi Hartwell looks forward to inspiring Australian professional wrestlers in the future as the TNA Knockouts World Champion. She successfully captured the title for the first time during the Bound for Glory event held on October 11.

During a recent interview, Indi Hartwell discussed her challenging journey to reach this moment, noting that all the difficult days served as educational experiences that contributed to her development. She expressed pride in being the first Australian woman to win the world title in the promotion, emphasizing her desire to encourage more Australian talents to achieve their dreams.
