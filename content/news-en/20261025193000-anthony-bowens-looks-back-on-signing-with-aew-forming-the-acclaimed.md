Anthony Bowens recently looked back on his journey to All Elite Wrestling and the formation of The Acclaimed. During an interview with the Michael Fairman Channel, Bowens recalled his decision to sign with AEW in October 2020 after previously agreeing to a deal with WWE. He explained that he felt AEW offered a better style and more freedom for younger talent. Shortly after his debut, Bowens and Max Caster joined forces to create The Acclaimed, eventually capturing gold as World Tag Team and Trios Champions. Both wrestlers have now become free agents, with Caster currently taking a break from in-ring competition.
