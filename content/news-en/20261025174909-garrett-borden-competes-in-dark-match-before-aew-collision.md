Garrett Borden stepped back into the ring prior to the October 24 episode of AEW Collision, squaring off against Kiran Grey in a singles match and securing the win. Throughout the bout, Borden executed multiple Stinger Splashes, displaying athleticism reminiscent of his legendary father Sting and his brother Steven Borden.

Borden clinched the victory using the TKO, a fireman's carry into a cutter. He previously wrestled a dark match ahead of AEW Collision in September, facing Kiran Grey as well.
