According to Ringside News, Ludwig Kaiser's legal case is moving into 2027, with the WWE Superstar facing a new court hearing as his battery case continues.

Court scheduling records show that Kaiser is scheduled for a hearing on January 4, 2027, at 9:00 AM before Judge Andrew L. Cameron, along with a pretrial conference on December 17, 2026, before the same judge. This comes as Kaiser's defense team seeks medical records for his accuser, Richard Reap, who claimed he suffered a concussion and torn rotator cuff during an April 23, 2026, confrontation at an Orlando apartment complex.

Kaiser, whose real name is Marcel Barthel, maintains that he acted in self-defense, while his attorney, Thomas D. Sommerville, continues challenging the evidence regarding the alleged injuries. With new court dates set for December and January, the legal battle for the WWE Superstar will see further developments before reaching a final resolution.
