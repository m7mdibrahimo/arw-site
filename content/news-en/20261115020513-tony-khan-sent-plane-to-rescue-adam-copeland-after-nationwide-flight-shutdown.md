Adam Copeland almost missed AEW Full Gear 2026 due to a nationwide flight shutdown, but Tony Khan intervened to ensure he arrived on time. After Copeland's commercial flights were repeatedly canceled, the AEW President arranged a private plane to transport him to Phoenix for his match against Kazuchika Okada.

Copeland detailed the travel ordeal in a video posted on X on Saturday, November 14. He explained that he was already on the runway headed to Denver when the pilot announced a nationwide ground stoppage. Copeland returned home and watched as his subsequent flight options were canceled one after another.

"I called Beth and she came and got me. I went home, no idea if I was going to be able to make it today. All of my flights started getting canceled, canceled one after the other after the other."

Khan then stepped in to send a private jet. Tony Schiavone also joined the flight, arriving early at Copeland's house to wait for the departure. "So Tony Khan sent a plane to come get us and poor Schiavone got there early. So he was sitting there at 9 o'clock, I think. We weren't taking off to 11. I was like, come on, have a coffee. Come and pet my dogs. He's a dog guy."

Copeland noted that the travel chaos turned into an unexpected family visit before they finally departed for Phoenix. Despite the disruptions caused by the FAA flight shutdown, Khan ensured everyone required for the main card reached the venue. Copeland is set to challenge Kazuchika Okada for the AEW International Championship at AEW Full Gear in Phoenix, Arizona.
