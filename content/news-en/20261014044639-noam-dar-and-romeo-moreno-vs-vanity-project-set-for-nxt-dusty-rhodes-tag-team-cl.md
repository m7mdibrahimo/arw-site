The first semifinal matchup for the 2026 NXT Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic has been finalized. During the October 13 episode of NXT on The CW, NXT Tag Team Champions Vanity Project, consisting of Brad Baylor and Ricky Smokes, defeated Mini Vikingo and Chazz 'Starboy' Hall to advance in the tournament.

Later in the same broadcast, Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno secured their spot in the semifinals by defeating Fraxiom, which includes Nathan Frazer and Axiom. This victory confirms that Dar and Moreno will face Vanity Project in the next round.

Winners of the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic earn a future opportunity at the NXT Tag Team Championship. The last team to win this tournament while holding the titles was The Undisputed ERA.
