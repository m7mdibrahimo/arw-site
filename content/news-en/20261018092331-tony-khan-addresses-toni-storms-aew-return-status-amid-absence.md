Tony Khan provided an update on Toni Storm’s mysterious absence from AEW during the post-AEW WrestleDream media scrum, confirming that the former AEW Women’s World Champion will eventually return to the company. While Khan did not announce a specific return date, he noted that the women’s division has stepped up in her absence, with Mercedes Moné currently holding the title. Storm has been off television since March 18, when she was mysteriously attacked backstage and replaced by Mina Shirakawa in a scheduled match against Marina Shafir. Dave Meltzer previously reported that AEW learned of the situation on the day of the show and that it was not an injury. Khan confirmed that Storm will eventually return to seek revenge against her attacker.
