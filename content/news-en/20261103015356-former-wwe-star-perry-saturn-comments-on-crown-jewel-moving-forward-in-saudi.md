Less than a week before WWE Crown Jewel takes place in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, former WWE star Perry Saturn expressed his strong concerns about the event. In a video on his YouTube channel, Saturn argued that WWE should hold the show elsewhere due to security risks in the region. Saturn stated that he believes the event poses too high a risk given ongoing security challenges. While he expects the show to proceed without incident, Saturn questioned how TKO would handle the financial and human consequences if a tragedy were to occur.
