Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre held the event "Lunes Clásico" on November 2, 2026, at Arena Puebla in Puebla, Puebla, Mexico. Full results from the show are detailed below.

Rencor and King Jaguar def. Amnesia and Dreyko.

Tiger Boy and Meyer def. Perversos (Perverso and Hijo del Perverso).

Fuego and AtrapaSueños (Rey Cometa and Espíritu Negro) def. Prayer, Espanto Jr. and Rey Apocalipsis.

Fuerza Poblana (Stigma, Arkalis and Rayo Metálico) def. Máquina Letal and Hermanos Calavera (Calavera Jr. I and Calavera Jr. II).

Three-Way Match: Mascara Dorada def. Volador Jr. and Capitan Suicida.

Templario, Dulce Gardenia and Neón def. Soberano Jr. and Infernales (Averno and Euforia).
