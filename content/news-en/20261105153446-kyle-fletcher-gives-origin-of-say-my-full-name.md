Kyle Fletcher recently discussed how his signature phrase "say my full name" came to be during an appearance on What Happened When with Tony Schiavone and Conrad Thompson. Fletcher explained that he first used the line in a promo on a whim, after which someone backstage texted him to suggest he keep using it.

According to Fletcher, the idea was inspired by the respectful way one should address a person of stature, noting that he wanted fans to show him proper respect. Conversely, as a heel, he uses single names or shorthand when referring to others to show disrespect in return. Fletcher's team recently picked up a victory in Blood & Guts on Wednesday, and he is now set to challenge Will Ospreay for the AEW Title at AEW Full Gear.
