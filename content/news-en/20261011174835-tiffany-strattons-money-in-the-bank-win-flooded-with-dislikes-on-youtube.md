Tiffany Stratton left Money in the Bank with the briefcase, but fans are still unhappy with the decision. After social media backlash following the finish, the reaction has carried over to WWE's official YouTube channel. The video of the Women's Money in the Bank Ladder Match has received around 35,600 dislikes compared to only 4,900 likes, meaning nearly 88 percent of the reactions are dislikes. Many fans argued that WWE should have chosen Roxanne Perez or used the opportunity to elevate a new star instead of giving the briefcase to someone already established near the top of the division. While this initial reaction shows widespread disappointment, opinions could change if Stratton delivers a memorable run and cash-in.
