Linda Hogan has decided to leave TikTok, citing disturbing content as the reason for her departure. The ex-wife of Hulk Hogan announced on Facebook that she officially deleted her account after repeatedly encountering graphic animal abuse videos on the platform. Hogan expressed her outrage, noting that the content she saw included footage involving various animals, which she claimed originated from other countries.

> "I just deleted my TikTok account. I cannot stand it. There is just way too much animal abuse exposed on that platform from other countries from Third World. I don’t know people fucking camels hitting fish on the head with hairbrushes boiling dogs alive. I don’t know why they are allowed to show that on TikTok."

While Hogan did not name specific accounts or provide direct links to the videos, she made it clear that the nature of the content was deeply upsetting. She questioned the platform's policies regarding the moderation of graphic animal cruelty. This is not the first time Hogan has used social media to share personal frustrations; earlier this year, she made headlines for comments about her daughter, Brooke Hogan, and for promoting Hulk Hogan's Netflix documentary. For now, deleting her account serves as her response to the situation.
