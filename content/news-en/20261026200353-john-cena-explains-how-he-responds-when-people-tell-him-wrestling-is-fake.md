John Cena says he does not get angry when people tell him professional wrestling is fake. Instead, he views it as a sign that the person is simply uninformed about the business. During an appearance on The Romesh Ranganathan Show, Cena compared the comment to people who claim London has bad food. He explained that both opinions stem from a lack of current knowledge or experience. Cena noted that he often tells people who make the comment that they are viewing the industry through an outdated lens. He prefers to treat it as an opportunity to explain what modern professional wrestling actually involves rather than taking offense. For Cena, who has spent decades in the ring, defending the legitimacy of the craft is no longer a priority. He believes that those who dismiss it are the ones missing out on the reality of the business.
