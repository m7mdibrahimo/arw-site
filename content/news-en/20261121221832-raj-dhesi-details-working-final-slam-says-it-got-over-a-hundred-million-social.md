Raj Dhesi recently shared his experience working the Final Slam event, which took place on November 14 at the Plenitude Arena in Nanterre, France. The show, hosted by Twitch streamer Amine, drew 18,000 fans. Describing the event to MuscleManMalcolm, Dhesi admitted he was initially skeptical when the offer arrived, assuming it might be a small convention match.

However, after seeing the scale of the press conference—which Dhesi noted was larger than most indie wrestling shows—he realized the production was aiming for a massive spectacle. "I'm going all out for this. This is going to be bigger than Monday Night Raw. The production is going to be crazy," Dhesi recalled being told. Upon arriving at the arena, he was impressed by the massive screens and the giant ramp, noting that he had not experienced such a venue in years.

Reflecting on the event's success, Dhesi highlighted the impressive reach of the show. "I was seeing some of the numbers today, over a hundred million social media impressions," he said. The full three-to-four-hour broadcast has already surpassed a million views on YouTube since its release. Dhesi praised the streamers involved for their unique approach to wrestling, noting that they think like entertainers rather than traditional wrestlers. He emphasized that the event proved that the traditional framework of professional wrestling is evolving, noting that it was the first French wrestling event to be broadcast live on television in many years.
