CMLL held an edition of Lunes Clásico on October 26, 2026, at Arena Puebla in Puebla, México. In the main event, Esfinge and Sky Team (Máscara Dorada and Neón) defeated Guerreros Laguneros (Stuka Jr., Último Guerrero and Gran Guerrero). Xelhua secured his spot in the Rey del Inframundo finals by winning the Puebla eliminator match against Stigma, Rayo Metálico, Arkalis, Rey Apocalipsis, and Kheops. Other results included Johnnie Robbie defeating Alice Crowley, and the team of Sanely and Princesa Sugehit picking up a win over Marcela and Magnífica. Additionally, Reyna Isis and Valientes (Olympia and Hera) defeated India Sioux, Skadi, and Kira, while the team of Águila Egipcia and Espartano won a three-way match against Asturiano and Astro, and Lucky and Black Onix.
