Marina Shafir is preparing to face Mercedes Moné for the AEW Women's World Championship at AEW Full Gear. This marks Shafir's first one-on-one title match in AEW since July 2023. The pay-per-view will also serve as a tribute to PAC, who passed away in September. In an interview with Renee Paquette, Shafir discussed how she has been processing the loss. "I think for each of us individually, it’s happened privately. It definitely happened as a group, I think France was the one where it hit different. Personally, I’m just gonna let it hit me when it’s supposed to hit me. I’m not running from it, I’m not hiding from it. The grieving just hasn’t happened yet for me."

Reflecting on the timing of her title opportunity, Shafir admitted it has been difficult to feel positive. "I think about it all the time. Sometimes I think, if Ben wouldn’t have passed, would I be sitting here with you? I think eventually yeah, imminently no. It was hard for me to feel good about having that opportunity with her," she said. Shafir shared that she spoke to Moné immediately following their match. "I straight up told her after the match, I went right to her and I said, ‘I have a lot of respect for you and I always wanted to stand across the ring from you, but not under these circumstances.’ Unfortunately for her, she's gonna feel my grief. I don't know what that's going to look like, I don't know. I hate thinking about it."
