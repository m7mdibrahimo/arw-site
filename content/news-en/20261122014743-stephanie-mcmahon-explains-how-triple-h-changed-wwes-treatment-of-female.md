Stephanie McMahon recently credited Triple H with transforming how WWE treats female wrestlers, noting that women were not given the same training opportunities as men before he took over developmental. During an appearance on What's Your Story? With Steph McMahon, she explained to Diamond Dallas Page that Triple H changed the recruitment process by targeting female athletes instead of relying primarily on modeling backgrounds, ensuring they received the same training and match time as the male roster.

Stephanie recalled that these changes eventually led to some of the biggest reactions on NXT shows, particularly for the Four Horsewomen of Sasha Banks, Bayley, Charlotte Flair, and Becky Lynch. However, she noted that the main roster had not caught up at the time, pointing to a famously short RAW match involving Paige, Emma, and The Bella Twins that sparked the worldwide Give Divas a Chance movement. That widespread fan backlash ultimately forced Vince McMahon to publicly acknowledge the demand for longer matches and better storylines, paving the way for historic milestones like women headlining WrestleMania.
