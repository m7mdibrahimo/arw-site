Veronica West has shared another American Psycho parody video on social media, channeling Patrick Bateman's famous daily routine. The former WWE EVOLVE star previously recreated the movie's infamous axe scene earlier this month. In her latest video, West walks viewers through an exaggerated version of Bateman's obsessive morning habits, including stretching and skincare products. She also referenced her irritable bowel syndrome in the clip, following up on her previous axe-wielding parody that featured the classic Huey Lewis and the News speech.
