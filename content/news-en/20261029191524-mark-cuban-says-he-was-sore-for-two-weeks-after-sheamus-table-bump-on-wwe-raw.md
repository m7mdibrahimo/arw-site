Mark Cuban recently opened up about his memorable appearances in WWE during a new interview with Build Or Break. Cuban recalled receiving an RKO from Randy Orton at Survivor Series in November 2003, noting that he had two rules when the company first reached out to him after he bought the Dallas Mavericks: he had to take a hit, and he had to give one.

Cuban then reflected on his 2009 appearance on WWE RAW, where he got involved in a physical segment with Sheamus. He explained that Sheamus put him through a real table during the show, recalling the instructions he received beforehand. Cuban said, "He's going to put you through a table. It was a real table. They told me, ‘He’s gonna put you right in the center of the table, it'll just crack in half. Keep your head down because if your head is up when you hit, you'll break your neck.’"

Cuban added that despite pretending to be knocked out after the spot, he was sore for two weeks following the heavy impact. Praising the athleticism and toughness of the roster, Cuban concluded, "Those guys deserve so much respect. It may be orchestrated, but it's still athletic and it's still painful."
