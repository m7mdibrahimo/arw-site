Ilja Dragunov says he is not concerned about being overlooked on AEW's loaded roster. The former WWE star explained that he has spent his entire 14-year career betting on himself, and he does not fear getting lost in the shuffle. Speaking to TMZ's Inside the Ring, Dragunov addressed concerns about standing out among AEW's talent.

> "I mean, we basically talked about this right now. I didn’t consider how stacked the roster is because, like, that’s a fear I don’t have."

Dragunov noted that his confidence was a key factor in his decision to leave WWE. He feels that AEW has already demonstrated it sees his unique value, citing the presentation of his arrival at AEW WrestleDream before Tony Khan confirmed his signing.

> "And the way the people — the company — introduced me, I think it speaks for itself that I stick out from all the rest in that roster."

He believes that wrestlers who try to act like everyone else are the ones who struggle to get noticed. Dragunov remains confident in his experience and his distinct style as he begins his journey in the promotion.

> "But I haven’t been overlooked once in my 14 years of career."
