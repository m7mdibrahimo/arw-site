According to Fightful, Kenny Omega will prioritize NJPW over his AEW schedule if he recaptures the top belt. Omega is officially scheduled to challenge Yota Tsuji for the IWGP Heavyweight Championship at Wrestle Kingdom 21.

In an interview with NJPW1972.com, Omega was asked how he would handle things if he won the IWGP Heavyweight Championship again, explaining that his commitments there would come first.

> "I’m very cognizant of when (Konosuke) Takeshita was the title holder and there was concern that the fans felt he had misplaced his priorities, that he needed to appear more for the Japanese fans. That was a real learning experience for me. As much as I want to hold that belt on American television, I can’t abandon the fans that supported me here. So if I become the champion in New Japan, that becomes my number one priority. I have (a) contract with AEW, but it is 50:50 and it’s time to stop thinking that this is one-sided. So my commitment is that if I am champion, New Japan matches will be prioritized over my AEW schedule, and I will face any rightful challenger that comes my way. I want to strengthen all sides on this relationship."

Wrestle Kingdom 21 is January 4th, 2027, at the Tokyo Dome.
