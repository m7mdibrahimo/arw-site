Veteran WWE commentator Michael Cole recently discussed his potential retirement plans during an interview with OutKick, revealing that he signed a multi-year deal last year. While he is not planning to step away immediately, Cole shared his thoughts on his future in the industry.

"I'm 59, going to be 60 this week, actually. I want to get to retirement age," he said. "I'd like to get to 65, if I can. But, one of the things that I've been very adamant about, and I've talked to Paul Levesque about this. I've talked to my producers, my boss, Lee Fitting, and I've made sure, I want them to know that if they believe that I'm slipping at all I want to know, because I want to get out at the top of my game. I don't want to hang on and do this 'til I'm 75 or 80 years old."

Cole emphasized that he wants to leave the business while he is still performing at a high level, noting that he has discussed this with WWE Chief Content Officer Paul Levesque and his boss Lee Fitting. He also mentioned that his day-to-day life has become much simpler since he decided to focus solely on his broadcasting talent role rather than managing other announcers.
