Jim Cornette has offered his perspective on the 2026 midterms, asserting that Donald Trump is solely responsible for the Republican party's poor performance at the polls. According to Cornette, Trump alienated several voting groups that had previously supported him, and he is now calling for the new Congress to investigate the events of Trump's administration. Speaking on his YouTube channel, Cornette described the election outcome as a "blue tsunami."

Cornette specifically highlighted the shift in Hispanic voters, suggesting that Trump’s immigration policies negatively impacted the Republican party. He also criticized Trump's handling of economically struggling Americans, claiming that many voters turned against him due to his actions in office. Cornette believes that Republicans who aligned themselves closely with Trump suffered the consequences, while those who distanced themselves might have fared better.

Looking ahead, Cornette urged Congress to initiate public hearings and sworn testimony to investigate alleged wrongdoing by Trump, his family, and his inner circle. He argued that establishing a clear historical record is essential to prevent similar events in the future and to dismantle the political myth surrounding Trump.
