Cody Rhodes dismissed claims that WWE is in decline, stating that the company continues to grow and move upward. Speaking to The Sportster, Rhodes noted that negative headlines about weekly ratings make him laugh because they do not match the reality behind the scenes. He pointed to top stars like CM Punk, Roman Reigns, Rhea Ripley, Liv Morgan, Charlotte Flair, Lash Legend, and Trick Williams as proof of the roster's strength and depth.

Rhodes also emphasized that professional wrestling has become more global than ever before, highlighting the presence of three wrestlers in the upcoming Street Fighter movie as a major milestone. Despite differing opinions from fans, Rhodes remains confident in the company's future, pointing to established stars, rising talent, and new crossover opportunities as clear signs of progress.
