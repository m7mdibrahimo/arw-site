Christyan XO recently shared insights into her professional wrestling journey and her inclusion in the Shane Taylor Promotions faction within ROH. The group currently features Shane Taylor, ROH Men's Pure Champion Lee Moriarty, Trish Adora, Cpt. Shawn Dawn, and Carlie Bravo, with Christyan XO serving as the newest addition.

During a virtual signing with Captain's Corner, Christyan XO detailed how she initially connected with AEW and ROH, according to Fightful. She explained that her path began with extra work late last year.

> "Last December, I did some extra work with them… So, they offered me a few times after I did extra work and yeah, it was pretty awesome."

She noted that while her first extra work appearance took place in Virginia, the official offer came while she was in New York. Christyan XO expressed deep appreciation for her stablemates, specifically highlighting Carlie Bravo and Trish Adora for their guidance.

> "Amazing person," she said about Carlie Bravo. "I love the opportunity to be able to work beside him. I wouldn’t wanna be standing beside anybody else… The whole group, all amazing people, and they make everything super fun, and they help guide me and help me these past couple of months so, amazing people. Very happy I’m with them."

Regarding Adora, she added, "I love Trish (Adora) as well. She always helps me out if I’m feeling nervous and stuff. Again, she’s like a sister to me. She’s amazing."
