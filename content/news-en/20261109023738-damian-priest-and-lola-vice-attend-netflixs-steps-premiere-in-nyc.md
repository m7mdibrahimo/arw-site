Damian Priest and Lola Vice stepped away from their WWE commitments to walk the red carpet in New York City for the November 8, 2026, premiere of the new Netflix animated film, Steps. The pair posed for photos together, with Priest wearing a dark patterned suit and orange-tinted sunglasses, while Vice opted for a black cutout top and long black skirt.

Priest’s appearance was more than just a red carpet outing, as he provides the voice for Cracktor, a troll overlord in the movie. The film is a new take on the Cinderella story, featuring the voices of Ali Wong, Stephanie Hsu, Amanda Seyfried, Daniel Radcliffe, Bette Midler, and Nikki Glaser.

Priest previously discussed his transition to voice acting with Netflix, noting that his experience in WWE helped him prepare for the role. "As WWE Superstars, we use our voices as an extension of our onscreen character. I enjoyed channeling that energy into making Cracktor feel intense and larger-than-life. I think people are going to be surprised by how much of a show-stealing character he is."

Steps is scheduled to arrive on Netflix on November 20, 2026. Priest and Vice have been public about their relationship, which began after two years of friendship, and they frequently share updates on their life outside of the ring.
