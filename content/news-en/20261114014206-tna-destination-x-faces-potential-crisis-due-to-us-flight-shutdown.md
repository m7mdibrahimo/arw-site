TNA Wrestling is dealing with a significant challenge ahead of its Destination X 2026 event. A nationwide ground stop issued by the FAA has disrupted travel across the United States, creating uncertainty for the company as most of its talent is slated to fly into Canada on Saturday for the upcoming pay-per-view.

According to Bryan Alvarez of the Wrestling Observer, the promotion is in a difficult position regarding the event held on Sunday, November 15, at the Edmonton EXPO Centre in Alberta. Since the majority of the roster is not scheduled to travel until November 14, the ongoing air traffic control issues could make it extremely challenging to get everyone to the venue on time.

Alvarez noted that the situation could have a direct impact on the show, stating:

> "Most everyone working Destination X should be flying in tomorrow, so if this lasts the expected six hours it could conceivably heavily impact (no pun) that show."

If flight disruptions persist, TNA may be forced to make emergency adjustments to its plans.
