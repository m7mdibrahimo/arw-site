Drew McIntyre believes his Hell in a Cell match against CM Punk at WWE Bad Blood in 2024 rivals the historic first-ever iteration between The Undertaker and Shawn Michaels at WWE Badd Blood 1997. During an appearance on the Hall of Fame podcast, McIntyre explained that the intense personal rivalry and genuine animosity between him and Punk elevated the match to legendary status. He noted that he lost due to blood loss rather than being bested fairly by Punk. Wrestling veteran Eric Bischoff also praised McIntyre's performance and emotional delivery during the brutal encounter.
