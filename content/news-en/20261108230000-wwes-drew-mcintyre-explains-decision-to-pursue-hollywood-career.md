Drew McIntyre has not been seen in WWE since losing to Jacob Fatu at WrestleMania 42, although he recently posted a training video hinting at a potential return to the ring. During his time away, McIntyre has been busy filming the Highlander reboot alongside Henry Cavill, starring in The Last Druid with Russell Crowe, and voicing a character in the animated film Hexed, following his appearance in The Killer's Game with Dave Bautista in September 2024.

In an interview on CNN, McIntyre explained that breaking into Hollywood was never part of his plan, and he had always wanted to be a professional wrestler until Bautista called him and encouraged him to audition for The Killer's Game. He noted that opportunities have unfolded organically rather than through active pursuit, and while Hollywood has welcomed him, his career in WWE is far from over.

Expressing gratitude to Nick Khan and Triple H for allowing him to explore the film industry, McIntyre confirmed his eventual return to WWE. He also mentioned that Agent of Shadows brought together several WWE Superstars and legends, bridging the gap between his two worlds until the time is right for his comeback.
