It has been a busy week in professional wrestling as WWE builds toward WWE Survivor Series, while AEW hosted AEW Full Gear and TNA presented TNA Destination X. The past seven days brought both major highlights and significant creative missteps across the industry.

Loser: Women's WarGames Build (WWE)
- The build for the women's WarGames match on WWE SmackDown has felt confusing and thrown together compared to the men's match.
- Participants like Fatal Influence, Charlotte Flair, Alexa Bliss, Jade Cargill, Michin, B-Fab, and Tatum Paxley were mixed together without a clear reason for a WarGames match.
- Adding Bayley and Stephanie Vaquer to even out the babyface team felt random, especially with Vaquer being brought in by a simple phone call from Flair instead of using IYO SKY.

Winner: Survivor Series Tradition (WWE)
- WWE is bringing back the traditional five-on-five elimination match to celebrate the 40th anniversary of WWE Survivor Series.
- Cody Rhodes will captain Team USA against Team Mexico, which is captained by Rey Mysterio and includes Penta and Rey Fenix.
- The format allows top stars outside of the Bloodline storyline to shine on the card, with young talent like Je'Von Evans getting a major opportunity.

Loser: TNA Destination X (TNA)
- TNA Destination X suffered from a lack of creativity, despite the promotion's effort to pull the show together amid travel disruptions.
- An impromptu match between Mustafa Ali and Frankie Kazarian ended in a non-contest, while a longer match between Matt Hardy and Joseph Sawyer failed to impress.
- Both Ultimate X matches were too short, leaving only two matches on the card that felt up to pay-per-view standards.

Winner: DMX Music Integration (AEW)
- AEW successfully integrated the music of the late rapper DMX during the Anarchy In The Arena match at AEW Full Gear.
- The song 'Party Up (Up In Here)' added massive energy to the chaotic and violent match.
- Fan reaction to the music choice was overwhelmingly positive, making it one of the most talked-about elements of the event.
