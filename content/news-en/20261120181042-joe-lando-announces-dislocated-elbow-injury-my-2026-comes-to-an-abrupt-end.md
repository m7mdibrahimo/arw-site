Independent wrestler Joe Lando announced that a dislocated elbow has brought his year to an end. Lando, a regular on the UK independent wrestling scene, shared on social media that he suffered the injury this past Sunday during a singles match against Luke Jacobs. X-rays revealed a fracture after multiple attempts to reset the joint left it labeled as unstable. Lando stated that he is currently awaiting updates on whether he will need surgery and what his recovery timeline will look like.
