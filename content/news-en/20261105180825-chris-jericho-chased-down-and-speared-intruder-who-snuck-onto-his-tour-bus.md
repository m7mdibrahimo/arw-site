Chris Jericho is known for his toughness in the ring, but that intensity apparently carries over to his life on the road. During an appearance on The O Show, guitarist Frank Sidoris shared a story he heard about an incident involving Jericho's tour bus. According to Sidoris, an individual allegedly snuck onto the bus without permission.

> "A cool, fun story that I heard from the road a long time ago. Some guy—it was cool because it just kind of shows that Chris will throw down if he has to actually fight someone. So his band was on, and I don't know if some guy, some fan, tried to jump onto his bus, like open the door, got on the bus and did something, and then ran away. And Chris was just coming onto the bus, I believe."

Jericho reportedly did not wait for security to arrive. Instead, he chased the intruder down himself.

> "He legitimately chased this dude down and speared him, like full blast, and the guy was removed."

Sidoris noted that Jericho handled the situation personally rather than calling for help.

> "But it was incredible that there was no, like, 'I need security.' He's like, 'I am security.' It's pretty impressive, dude."

Jericho apparently took the individual down with enough force to end the confrontation immediately, earning the respect of those who witnessed the scene.

> "Yeah. He didn't kill the guy. Didn't he just knock the wind out of him? Just took him out, and everyone was just like, 'Bravo, man. That's respectable.'"
