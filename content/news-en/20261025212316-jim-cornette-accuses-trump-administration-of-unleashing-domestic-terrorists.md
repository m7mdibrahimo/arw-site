Jim Cornette reacted to a video showing police using force against civilians outside a restaurant, launching a sharp attack on the administration of US President Donald Trump. Cornette accused the administration of unleashing domestic terrorists on the country and urged people to channel their anger into the November 3 election to vote current officials out of office. This follows a previous tirade in which Cornette lashed out at WWE Hall of Famer Kane for welcoming Trump back to Tennessee. In his latest remarks, Cornette kept his focus strictly on the administration and the footage, pushing his followers to make their voices heard at the polls.
