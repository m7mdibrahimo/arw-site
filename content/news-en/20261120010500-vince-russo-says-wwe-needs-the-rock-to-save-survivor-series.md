The Rock is officially heading back to WWE for Survivor Series, but former writer Vince Russo believes the company has a much bigger reason to need him right now. Russo is convinced that WWE has been struggling creatively for months and that the return of The Rock will provide the massive boost it is missing.

During The Pat McAfee Show on November 18, 2026, The Rock confirmed his upcoming appearance and revealed that he rearranged his Hollywood promotional schedule to travel to Houston. Speaking on Coach and Bro, Russo expressed surprise that WWE did not announce the appearance weeks in advance, pointing to creative problems around Crown Jewel, the Survivor Series lineup, and the John Cena Classic.

Jonathan Coachman noted the business side of the situation, mentioning that thousands of tickets remain available for Survivor Series and that the appearance gives the star a chance to promote his upcoming film. With Roman Reigns already scheduled for the event, Russo warned that bringing back a major star without meaningful ongoing plans could create problems, emphasizing that WWE faces immense pressure to maintain strong storylines after The Rock leaves Houston.
