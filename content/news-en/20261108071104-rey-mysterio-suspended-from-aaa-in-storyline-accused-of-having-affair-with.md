Rey Mysterio has been suspended from Lucha Libre AAA in a storyline following shocking accusations made by Dorian Roldán during the third week of Héroes Inmortales. Roldán, the son of AAA matriarch Marisela Peña, interrupted Rey Mysterio's Hall of Fame induction for his mother to level multiple grievances against the general manager. Roldán claimed that Mysterio sent AAA talents to unsafe working conditions in the USA, citing El Hijo del Vikingo and Mini Vikingo, and noted that Mysterio struck Omos at AAA Ola de Calor after suspending him. Furthermore, Roldán criticized Mysterio for inserting himself as the referee in the match where El Grande Americano defeated Dominik Mysterio for the AAA Mega Champion title, and questioned why his mother chose Mysterio over him for both the general manager role and the Hall of Fame induction. Finally, Roldán alleged that he has proof of an extramarital affair between Mysterio and Marisela Peña.
