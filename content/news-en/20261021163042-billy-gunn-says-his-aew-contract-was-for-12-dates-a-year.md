Billy Gunn revealed that his original contract with AEW was for only 12 dates a year when he was initially brought in as a coach and producer. He noted that he did not plan to wrestle much, but Tony Khan eventually used him frequently across both shows. Gunn wrestled 23 matches in 2020 and 34 matches in 2021, and has not competed for AEW since January 2026.
