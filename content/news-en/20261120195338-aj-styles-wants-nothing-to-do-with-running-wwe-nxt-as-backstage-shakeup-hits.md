AJ Styles has no interest in taking over WWE NXT from Shawn Michaels, explaining that he retired to spend time with his family rather than take on another demanding full-time job. While backstage speculation previously linked Styles to the developmental brand, Dave Meltzer reported in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter that Styles has personally stated he does not want the position. Although he has occasionally coached at the Performance Center, his actual involvement has focused on scouting and the WWE ID program.

Meltzer also noted that the broader backstage shakeup after WrestleMania 43 may not unfold as initially rumored, with Shawn Michaels potentially joining the main roster to work alongside Triple H while Bruce Prichard transitions to working from home. Meanwhile, CM Punk has also been mentioned in connection with the NXT role for the future, but he recently addressed rumors in Detroit by confirming he is signed for five more years and intends to keep wrestling actively.
