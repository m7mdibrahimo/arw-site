David Finlay recently opened up about his decision to leave New Japan Pro-Wrestling after eleven years and sign with AEW in March 2026. Prior to joining AEW, Finlay considered offers from both sides, as his father Fit Finlay and brother Uriah Connors were in WWE NXT, while his former Bullet Club War Dogs stablemates Gabe Kidd and Clark Connors were in AEW. Ultimately, Finlay chose AEW because it offered him more creative freedom and a better schedule, allowing him to reunite with his friends. He also admitted that as a former two-time IWGP Global Heavyweight Champion who wrestled at the Tokyo Dome eight times, he did not want to start from scratch in WWE NXT. Finlay also noted that his departure from NJPW was driven by a desire to spend more time with his family, particularly his young child, after years of touring in Japan. He knew he was going to leave NJPW a year in advance and gave his all during his final year out of respect for his mentor Gedo.
