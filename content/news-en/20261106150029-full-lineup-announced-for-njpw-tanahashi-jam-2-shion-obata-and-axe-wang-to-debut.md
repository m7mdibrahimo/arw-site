The lineup has been finalized for NJPW TANAHASHI JAM 2. Hiroshi Tanahashi announced the complete match card for the event, which is scheduled for November 19 at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo, Japan. The main event will feature Zack Sabre Jr., Kosei Fujita, and Sosuke Shoda squaring off against Ryohei Oiwa, Hartley Jackson, and Kaito Kiyomiya. In the opening bout, amateur wrestler Shion Obata will make his official in-ring debut. Other notable matches include Shun Skywalker defending the NJPW WORLD TV Championship against Toru Yano, as well as Shota Umino facing Axe Wang, and Shion Obata taking on Boltin Oleg.
