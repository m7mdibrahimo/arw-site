The October 10, 2026 episode of AEW Collision on TNT experienced a notable decline in viewership compared to the prior week, according to Ringside News. The broadcast brought in 372,000 viewers alongside a 0.04 rating in the key 18 to 49 demographic.

In comparison, the October 3, 2026 show attracted 415,000 viewers with a 0.08 demographic rating, marking a loss of 43,000 viewers. On a year-over-year basis, the program improved compared to the same week in 2025. These figures track linear television broadcasts on TNT only and exclude digital streaming data.
