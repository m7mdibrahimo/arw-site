Bron Breakker suffered a scary landing on his neck during a WWE live event on October 17. The incident occurred during a match in which Breakker teamed with Bronson Reed and Austin Theory to face Oba Femi, LA Knight, and Penta. While attempting a Frankensteiner on Knight from the top rope, Breakker lost his footing as Knight moved, causing Breakker to crash awkwardly onto his neck and upper body. Although the referee checked on him immediately, Breakker was able to finish the match. There is currently no official update regarding his health status. This incident is particularly concerning because Breakker had a similar dangerous landing on his neck during a Doomsday Device spot at Survivor Series: WarGames in November 2025. At that time, Bryan Alvarez noted on Wrestling Observer Radio how frightening the situation appeared, stating, "So then they almost killed Bron Breakker. They tried to Doomsday Device, but he did not flip over, landed right on his neck, thought this guy was dead." Given the history of such landings, fans remain concerned about his condition until WWE provides further information.
