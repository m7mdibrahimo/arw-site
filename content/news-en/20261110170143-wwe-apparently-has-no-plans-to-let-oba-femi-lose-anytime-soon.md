Oba Femi continues to make a strong impression on WWE television, but according to Dave Meltzer, the company has no immediate plans to let him suffer a clean defeat. During the November 9, 2026, episode of WWE RAW, Femi faced Bron Breakker in a hard-hitting match that ultimately ended in a disqualification after outside interference from The Vision. Meltzer noted on Wrestling Observer Radio that neither wrestler could afford to lose at this point, leading to the inconclusive finish.

Meltzer believes WWE is determined to keep Femi looking unbeatable for the foreseeable future. This follows Femi's pivotal role at Crown Jewel 2026, where he interfered to help Penta and Je'Von Evans capture the WWE World Tag Team Championships by taking out Bronson Reed. While WWE originally planned a six-man tag team match involving Femi, Penta, and Evans against Breakker, Theory, and Reed, those plans were adjusted to facilitate the title change.

While Meltzer's assessment reflects his analysis of WWE's booking rather than confirmed backstage directives, the repeated disqualification finishes highlight the company's high expectations for Femi. However, as the rivalry continues, fans are increasingly eager to see a definitive winner emerge from his clashes with Breakker.
