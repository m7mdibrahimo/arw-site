Austin Creed hints that something could be in the works regarding a potential DaParty reunion. During a sit-down interview at PAX Australia, Creed was asked about the possibility of reuniting with Adam Cole, Claudio Castagnoli, and Tyler Breeze now that all members are no longer with WWE. Creed stated that he would not confirm or deny whether anyone has been filming or creating assets for a new YouTube channel. He also discussed his current status with AEW, noting that building rapport in the locker room is his primary focus before considering any new video game content. Creed added that WWE owns the UpUpDownDown channel, which has not been updated in several months.
