Zoey Serrano revealed that she once struggled to breathe during a match against Shayna Baszler in WWE. While caught in Baszler's Kirifuda Clutch, Serrano felt like she was going to pass out, but Triple H repeatedly instructed the referee to tell her to keep going. Serrano explained that she was already exhausted because the match had gone over its allotted time. She felt like she was constantly moving, getting up, and going down while Baszler remained attached to her back. Baszler clarified that Triple H was not telling Serrano to simply lie there, but rather wanted her to continue fighting. Baszler noted that Serrano's lack of submission training at the time, being a white belt, likely made the experience more difficult. Serrano joked that she genuinely believed she was going to pass out during the encounter.
