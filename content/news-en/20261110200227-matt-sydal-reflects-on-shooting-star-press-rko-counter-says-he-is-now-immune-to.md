Matt Sydal recently reflected on taking an iconic RKO from Randy Orton while attempting a Shooting Star Press in WWE, noting that he is now immune to the move. During the July 12, 2010, episode of WWE Monday Night RAW, Sydal, then known as Evan Bourne, fell victim to one of the most memorable RKOs of all time when Orton caught him mid-air. In a new interview with MuscleManMalcolm, Sydal looked back at the moment and joked about his immunity. "I thought I had him, brother. I thought I had him. No, it was just one of those moments because the most painful thing that can happen on a shooting star is just bellowing. I thought that was the most dangerous thing to happen. You go for a shooting star, they move out of the way, you belly flop. It hurts. You belly flop in a pool, it hurts, let alone onto a desk. Surely, that's the most painful thing that could happen. Well, on that day, it was top because I had never actually had someone contribute. I know a lot of people try and catch me with that, but I have a lot of experience with RKOs and nobody's going to catch me with any RKOs ever because I've learned to become immune to them after taking the most devastating one of all time all time but you know like that's what wrestling's about I'm trying to make people jump out of their seats and I think that won't work."
