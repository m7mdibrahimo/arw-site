Zack Sabre Jr. wants to defend his new title at The Dome. He won the CMLL World Heavyweight Championship after defeating Hechicero at the joint event.

Following his victory, Sabre Jr. told the press he hopes to return to Mexico before the end of the year to defend his belt. Meanwhile, he is heading to Japan to compete in the World Tag League alongside his partner Taichi.

The wrestler stated that he aims to defend his championship at NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 21 on January 4, expressing a desire to face new generations, notably Blue Panther.
