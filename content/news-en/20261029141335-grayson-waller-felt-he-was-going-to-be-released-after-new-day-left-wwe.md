Grayson Waller recently revealed that he feared he was close to being released by WWE following the departure of New Day earlier this year. Speaking on Chris Van Vliet's Insight, Waller explained that his TV appearances had become sparse and his defeat to Oba Femi on WWE Raw left him in a vulnerable position. "100%. I could feel it, especially when the New Day left," Waller said, noting that he had to take matters into his own hands rather than let his dream fade away. Waller eventually made his way to WWE NXT, where he called out the roster and set the stage for his current run as NXT Champion. He won the title at NXT Heatwave and is scheduled to defend it against Tony D'Angelo at NXT Halloween Havoc.
