RJ City believes professional wrestling should be more honest about its nature. During a recent interview, The Stunner's Rob Pasbani compared some of RJ City's earlier work to a masculine version of Blanche Devereaux. City responded by discussing how the industry has largely avoided acknowledging how homoerotic it is throughout its history.

> "Is the business not gay enough? Acknowledge it, it's there. We just need to acknowledge it. Let me put it this way, it's need to be more self-aware of how homoerotic it is. It's there. Someone tell JBL what is up."

City feels that the business should openly embrace and recognize these elements rather than ignore them.
