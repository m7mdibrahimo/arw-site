The November 11, 2026, episode of AEW Dynamite/Collision aired live from the Maverik Center in West Valley City, Utah. The show featured the AEW Full Gear go-home show, highlighted by AEW National Champion Tommaso Ciampa defending his title against Mike Bailey, and a mixed tag team match featuring Mercedes Moné and Jay White against Marina Shafir and Jon Moxley.

Will Ospreay and Kyle Fletcher had a tense face-to-face segment. Ospreay questioned Fletcher's loyalty, while Fletcher admitted his jealousy regarding Ospreay's success and his desire to take the AEW World Championship. The segment concluded with Fletcher slapping Ospreay.

In tag team action, The New Level vs. Brodido ended in a double count-out after both teams brawled throughout the arena, with Kofi putting Brody King through a table. David Finlay defeated Orange Cassidy in a singles match, using his shillelagh behind the referee's back to secure the win. In a non-title match, CMLL World Women's Champion Persephone defeated Gypsy Mac.
