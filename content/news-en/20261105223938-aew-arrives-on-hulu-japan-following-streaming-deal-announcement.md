AEW is officially expanding its reach in Japan, with the company's programming now available on Hulu Japan. This launch follows the streaming agreement announced on September 16, 2026. The deal provides exclusive streaming access to AEW Dynamite, AEW Collision, and live pay-per-view events for Hulu Japan subscribers. According to the service's schedule, weekly episodes of Dynamite and Collision will begin streaming on November 10. Additionally, the platform has added approximately 20 episodes from September and October to help new viewers catch up on current storylines. AEW All Out and WrestleDream are included with standard subscriptions at no extra cost, while future live pay-per-view events, starting with Full Gear on November 15, will require a separate purchase. This partnership provides a dedicated home for AEW in a market where stars like Kazuchika Okada, Kenny Omega, Will Ospreay, and Konosuke Takeshita already have significant fanbases. Hulu Japan, operated by HJ Holdings, remains separate from the American Hulu service, and this agreement does not impact AEW's existing distribution in the United States.
