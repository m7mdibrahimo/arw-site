Charlotte Flair is scheduled to appear alongside John Cena during tonight's WWE SmackDown, according to Viper Reports on November 13, 2026. Early plans have her working with Cena, though WWE has not officially announced her involvement.

> "Viper Reports can reveal that Charlotte Flair is set to be in the John Cena segment tonight on SmackDown."

Cena has been traveling across WWE shows to personally invite talent to the John Cena Classic on December 14. With Flair expected in his segment, she could potentially be added to the upcoming event.
