WWE SmackDown viewership for the October 16 episode remained steady, drawing 1.327 million viewers on USA Network. This is a slight decrease from the 1.352 million viewers the show recorded on October 9. The episode earned a 0.32 rating in the 18 to 49 demographic, matching the rating achieved the previous Friday.

Top clips from the show included Cody Rhodes punting Baron Corbin in front of Randy Orton, Trick Williams confronting Sami Zayn, and Gunther being duped by Danhausen. Additionally, the broadcast featured a tense backstage staredown between CM Punk and Kevin Owens, as well as a bout between Paige and Nikki Bella.
