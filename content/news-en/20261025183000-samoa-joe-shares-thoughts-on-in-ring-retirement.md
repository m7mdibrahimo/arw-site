As several prominent stars from the previous generation of professional wrestling prepare to hang up their boots, Samoa Joe has shared his thoughts on his own eventual in-ring retirement. During a recent interview with SHAK Wrestling, the AEW star discussed his career approach and mindset at this stage. He stated that his current priority is simply putting on fun matches for the fans while he still has the opportunity. Joe also mentioned his growing acting career, including his role as Sweet Tooth in the Twisted Metal television series and video game voice work, but confirmed he remains committed to giving back to the wrestling industry.
