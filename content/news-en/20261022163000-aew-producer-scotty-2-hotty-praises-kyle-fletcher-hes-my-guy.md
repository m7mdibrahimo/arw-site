AEW producer Scotty 2 Hotty has praised Kyle Fletcher, highlighting the wrestler's impressive growth over the past year and expressing excitement for his future in the company. During a recent appearance on Insight, Scotty spoke about working with top talent like Will Ospreay and Swerve Strickland, while singling out Fletcher as someone he deeply enjoys collaborating with. Scotty noted that Fletcher's confidence has grown significantly, praising his professionalism, in-ring ability, and overall presentation. Fletcher has enjoyed a dominant twelve months in AEW, capturing the TNT Championship twice, the International Championship once, and the AEW World Trios Championship as part of the Don Callis Family. Don Callis and fellow wrestlers like Mike Bailey also hold Fletcher in high regard, predicting a bright future for the Australian star.
