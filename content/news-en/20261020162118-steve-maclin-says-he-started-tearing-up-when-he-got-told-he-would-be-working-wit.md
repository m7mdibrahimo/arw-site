Steve Maclin recently spoke about finding out that he would be working with Adam Copeland in Maple Leaf Pro. Speaking during a Maple Leaf Pro YouTube documentary, Maclin discussed how he teared up when he learned about the match. 

> "I got told that I was working with Adam Copeland, and I literally just started tearing up, and I was just like, this is like out of happiness, and my wife was there, and it was just, ah. It's just a moment you don't ever think is going to happen in your career"

Maclin also shared how he suffered a broken leg just two days after finding out, dealing with a spiral fracture in his fibula. Despite the injury, he worked hard to make it to the October 3rd event for MLP Northern Rising at Maple Leaf Gardens, where Copeland ultimately came away with the victory.
