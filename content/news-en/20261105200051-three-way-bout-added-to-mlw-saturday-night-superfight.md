Major League Wrestling has added a three-way match to the MLW Saturday Night SuperFight event scheduled for November 7. The match will feature Titán, Último Guerrero, and Templario competing in a triple threat showdown. MLW stated that the event in Chicago is sold out, and fans can watch the event live on VEEPS starting at 8 PM ET. The promotion described the bout as a clash between three distinct styles of lucha libre, highlighting Titán's aerial attacks, Último Guerrero's power, and Templario's combination of both.
