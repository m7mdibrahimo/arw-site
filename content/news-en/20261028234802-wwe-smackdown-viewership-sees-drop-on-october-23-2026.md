New ratings data released by Wrestlenomics shows a decline for the October 23, 2026 episode of WWE SmackDown on USA Network compared to the previous week. The episode drew 1,100,000 viewers, down from 1,327,000 viewers on October 16, 2026. In the key P18–49 demographic, the show recorded a 0.24 rating, a decrease from the 0.32 rating achieved the prior week. Year-over-year, the October 23, 2026 episode fell 4.1% in total viewership and 17.2% in the key demographic compared to the October 24, 2025 episode, which drew 1,147,000 viewers with a 0.29 demo rating. These figures represent linear television viewership on USA Network and do not account for streaming or digital platforms.
