A championship match for the TNA X Division Title is confirmed for the October 29 episode of TNA iMPACT. Cedric Alexander earned his opportunity after defeating Fabian Aichner, K.C. Navarro, and Rich Swann in a Fatal 4-Way match, according to Fightful. Alexander received assistance from The System members Brian Myers and Bear Bronson to secure the victory and challenge the champion, Amazing Red.

The upcoming broadcast will also feature a title defense for the TNA Knockouts Television Championship. The announced card is as follows:

Amazing Red vs. Cedric Alexander
John Skyler vs. A.J. Francis
M by Elegance vs. Jada Stone
Elijah vs. Ryan Nemeth
