Fatal Influence, consisting of Jacy Jayne, Fallon Henley, and Lainey Reid, has officially received an invitation to participate in the John Cena Classic. The announcement came after John Cena, known as The Cenation Leader, was walking backstage following his earlier announcement regarding Charlotte Flair's inclusion in the tournament. During his walk, Cena crossed paths with the members of Fatal Influence, who questioned his decision to invite Charlotte to the event. The interaction concluded with Cena extending an invitation to all three members of the group as well. The updated participant field for the John Cena Classic now features Cody Rhodes, Zilla Fatu, Noam Dar, Romeo Moreno, Kelani Jordan, Charlotte Flair, Jacy Jayne, Fallon Henley, and Lainey Reid.
