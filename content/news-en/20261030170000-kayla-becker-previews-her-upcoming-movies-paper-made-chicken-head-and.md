Since leaving WWE in 2024, former ring announcer Kayla Braxton, now known as Kayla Becker, has been pursuing an acting career with a focus on horror films. In an interview with Fightful, Becker shared details about three of her upcoming projects. She described 'Paper Made' as a martial arts film, 'Chicken Head' as a horror-comedy, and 'Stranglehold' as a project she filmed two years ago that is finally nearing release.

Becker also discussed her personal taste in horror, naming 'Drag Me to Hell' and 'The Shining' as favorites. She praised recent films like 'Talk to Me' and 'Obsession' while noting that she did not enjoy 'The Passenger'. During the conversation, she also took time to pay tribute to Paul Heyman.
