CJ Perry provided an update on her upcoming novel, 'Bulletproof Angel,' which is scheduled for release in November. In an interview with TMZ Inside The Ring, Perry revealed that a film adaptation of the book is already in development. "But also, it is going to be made into a movie. We’re already in the works with it. Very exciting. We’re already starting to work on the packaging. It's like a James Bond spy thriller. There's some woman leads, which is really exciting."

Perry confirmed that she will not star in the film, though she expects to have a cameo role. "No, we’re going to get a bigger actress, like a bigger name. I’m producing it though. I'll probably get a little cameo because why not, I love acting. But the biggest thing for me as a producer, I want to get names that move the needle, the best actresses. Even though I'm phenomenal, there is people that are better."

Additionally, Perry stated that Rusev is set to portray a villain in the movie. In the same interview, Rusev discussed his current absence from WWE.
