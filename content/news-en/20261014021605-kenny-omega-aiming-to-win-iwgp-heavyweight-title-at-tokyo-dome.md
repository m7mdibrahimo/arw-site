Kenny Omega has his sights set on the IWGP Heavyweight Championship at the Tokyo Dome. After a surprise appearance at NJPW King of Pro-Wrestling, it was announced that Omega, Oleg Boltin, and IWGP Heavyweight Champion Yota Tsuji will compete in a three-way non-title match at NJPW Power Struggle on November 3. If Omega wins that match, he will earn a future title shot against Tsuji.

Following his return, Omega spoke with Tokyo Sports about his goals. He emphasized that he wants to capture the championship in front of the New Japan Pro-Wrestling fanbase at the biggest event possible.

> "Japan is my second home, so I want to win the belt in front of New Japan fans, and that deserves the biggest stage of all. So, of course, I’m aiming for the Dome."

NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 21 is scheduled for January 4, 2027. Omega last held the title in 2019, when he lost it to Hiroshi Tanahashi at the Tokyo Dome.
