Mr. Kennedy is back under the WWE umbrella and has his sights set on a future match with CM Punk. Speaking with Nick Teodoro, Ken Anderson admitted that Punk is the first name that comes to mind when he thinks of opponents he still wants to face. The two stars had brief overlaps during their early days in OVW and on SmackDown, but they rarely crossed paths inside the ring. The timing of this desire comes shortly after Anderson signed a WWE Legends deal nearly 17 years after his 2009 release. While he is not expecting the contract to guarantee a major comeback, Anderson acknowledged that a proper match with Punk remains high on his personal bucket list.
