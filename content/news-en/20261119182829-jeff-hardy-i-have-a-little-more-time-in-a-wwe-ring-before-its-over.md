Jeff Hardy remains one of the most iconic superstars in WWE history, known for his unique charisma and high-risk style. While Jeff has not competed on the WWE main roster since 2021, he did make a brief return to the company in 2025 for a few matches in NXT, including a short run as an NXT Tag Team Champion. In a recent appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Hardy discussed the possibility of a full-time return to WWE.

"In a way, I feel like I will. I feel like it’s kind of inevitable that something will happen, whether it be something leading up to the Hall of Fame or whatever, for it to feel right. But yeah, I feel like I have a little more time in a WWE ring before it’s all over. It’s just a matter of time," Hardy stated. WWE previously offered Jeff a spot in the Hall of Fame in 2021, but he declined because his brother Matt Hardy was not included in the offer. During the same interview, Jeff also addressed his ongoing neck issues.
