The New Level, featuring Austin Creed and Kofi, made their arrival in All Elite Wrestling at All In 2026. They were revealed as Swerve Strickland's surprise partners in the Trios Roulette Royale and went on to win the AEW World Trios Championship.

Speaking to Phid McAwesome for Fightful at PAX Australia, Creed discussed the creative freedom wrestlers enjoy in AEW, highlighting that performers have room to experiment even if it means taking risks.

> "There's definitely more space for that. More space to be allowed to fail is how I would say it, and that's what I thrive on is the opportunity to fail. Let me go out and try something. I don't want it to fail, but that adrenaline rush that I get is because I'm going out on national television. I'm going out in front of a crowd of 20,000 people and I'm trying something that I don't know if it's going to work or not, and then it's successful and I go, cool, I am good at my job. Or it doesn't work and I go, 'Ah! Okay, well, what can I change next time so that this does work?' Getting the chance to have that process is what I really enjoy about anything creative but specifically about wrestling, because that's fulfillment for me. Getting the chance to try, not knowing if you're gonna be successful."

The New Level have since completed four successful title defenses of the AEW World Trios Championship.
