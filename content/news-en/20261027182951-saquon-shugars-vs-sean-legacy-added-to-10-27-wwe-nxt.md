Saquon Shugars is set to meet Sean Legacy in the ring during the upcoming episode of WWE NXT. Following a backstage confrontation between the two, their match was made official for the October 27th broadcast on The CW. General Manager Robert Stone revealed the updated match card for the show, which serves as the final stop before the Halloween Havoc Premium Live Event on October 31st.
