According to Ringside News, Matt Hardy and Jeff Hardy received significant creative control for the October 15 episode of TNA Impact, though TNA adjusted the original running order Matt envisioned. Matt wanted a tables match between The Righteous and The Sicks as part of the Hardys' 35th anniversary celebration, noting how closely he and Jeff are associated with such stipulations.

> "And I was like, ‘Huh, who would we put? Who would I put if I was legitimately booking the show?’ I feel like I'd probably book two of our most hated rivals, you know, The Righteous and The Sicks, and maybe they can destroy one another. They'll do us a favor by just sticking them together in a match."

Matt preferred placing the tables match right after the opening segment, but TNA scheduled the Nic Nemeth and Ryan Nemeth segment there instead, moving the tables match later. He suggested the adjustment likely occurred for production reasons to give the tables match more time.
