Kyle Fletcher recently spoke about his debut in the Blood and Guts match during the latest episode of AEW Dynamite, where he secured the victory for his team by threatening Will Ospreay with a screwdriver until Adam Copeland surrendered. In a new interview with TMZ Sports, Fletcher discussed the possibility of stepping inside the cage again in the future. "I mean, you say, would I do it again? I don't really think it's my choice. I think there's a pretty good chance that I will be put in that cage again at some point. It's terrifying," Fletcher said. He described the daunting feeling of knowing there is no escape until the match ends, surrounded by six opponents aiming to make you bleed. Despite the brutal nature of the match, he expressed gratitude for escaping relatively unscathed and emerging victorious, noting that he believes a return to the structure is likely at some point.
