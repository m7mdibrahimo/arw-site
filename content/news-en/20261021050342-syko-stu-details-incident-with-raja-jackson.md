Independent wrestler Syko Stu spoke about the 2025 incident involving Raja Jackson during an appearance on the SOS Podcast. Stu recalled confronting Raja outside the venue before the match and hitting him with a beer can, thinking it was a worked spot. After the match started, Raja slammed Stu and delivered over 20 unanswered strikes to his head before being pulled away. Stu suffered a severe traumatic brain injury, lost two teeth, and shattered his maxilla, requiring him to relearn how to walk and stay off work for a year. Raja later pleaded no contest to felony battery and served 90 days in prison. Stu clarified that he is not holding a grudge against Raja, though he noted that Rampage Jackson previously trashed him on various podcasts.
