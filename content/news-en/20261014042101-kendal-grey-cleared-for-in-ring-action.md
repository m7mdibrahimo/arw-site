Kendal Grey has received medical clearance to return to the ring. During the October 13 episode of NXT on The CW, Grey made an appearance while Wren Sinclair was discussing her upcoming NXT Women's Championship match against Kelani Jordan at NXT Halloween Havoc. Grey, who had been sidelined due to a right ear injury sustained during an NXT Underground match against Lola Vice, confirmed she is now ready to compete. She expressed her full support for Sinclair as she prepares for her title opportunity.
