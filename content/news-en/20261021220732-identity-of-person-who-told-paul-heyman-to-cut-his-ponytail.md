Jacob Heyman recently revealed that he was the person who convinced his father, Paul Heyman, to cut off his iconic ponytail. Speaking during a roundtable discussion alongside Cody Rhodes, Charlotte Flair, and O'Shea Jackson Jr., Jacob shared that he suggested the change to his father. Paul Heyman eventually cut the ponytail in February 2014, though he had previously considered having CM Punk cut it during their 2013 WWE feud. Jacob also spoke about his close relationship with his father, describing him as a hero and best friend who is entirely different from the on-screen heel character fans have watched for decades.
