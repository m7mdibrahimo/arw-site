Rey Mysterio is proud of his daughter Aalyah Mysterio's wrestling progress. Although Aalyah has been announced as part of an incoming WWE Performance Center recruiting class and has not yet made her TV debut, she has quickly adapted to in-ring training. Rey told Stephanie McMahon on What's Your Story? that Aalyah first stepped into the ring for an hour last October, showing natural ability with ropes and rolling. After she moved to Florida in late July to train full-time, she spent two weeks at the PC in February. By the end of that period, she participated in a PC live match coached by Norman Smiley. Rey noted that while he may be biased, he feels there is something special about her potential.
