Josh Barnett revealed that he offered to take over the entire NJPW of America program before it ceased operations. New Japan Pro-Wrestling recently announced that its North American subsidiary is shutting down and entering liquidation, though the promotion still plans to hold shows in the region. Speaking about the closure, Barnett explained that he wanted to manage training, classes, and local events. He believed the program was an underused asset that could become a major highlight with proper direction. However, he noted that the debt compared to the costs was ultimately too high, leading to the decision to close. The last NJPW event in the United States was the start of the 2026 G1 Climax tournament in Hoffman Estates, Illinois, in July, while the final NJPW of America event took place in Ontario, California, in May 2025.
