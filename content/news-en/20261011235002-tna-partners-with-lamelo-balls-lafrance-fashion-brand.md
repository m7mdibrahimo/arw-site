TNA announced a new partnership with LaFrancé, the lifestyle fashion brand created by NBA star LaMelo Ball. The announcement was made by commentator Tom Hannifan right before the opening match of the main card at TNA Bound for Glory on October 11, 2026. As part of this collaboration, TNA referees will wear official shirts featuring the LaFrancé logo during their matches. LaMelo Ball recently moved to the Minnesota Timberwolves after spending six seasons with the Charlotte Hornets. Wrestling fans may also remember that LaMelo, along with his brother Lonzo and their father LaVar Ball, previously appeared on WWE RAW in June 2017 for a segment with The Miz.
