Former WWE referee Jimmy Korderas recently criticized Darby Allin for taking too many dangerous stunts, arguing that audiences have become completely desensitized. Speaking about Allin getting thrown off a steel cage through a pile of tables on the November 4 episode of AEW Dynamite: Blood & Guts, Korderas pointed out that the crowd reaction has drastically faded. He compared it to wrestlers performing piledrivers too frequently until they lose their impact, emphasizing that Allin needs to change his approach. Furthermore, Korderas took aim at AEW's original roster members, suggesting that a lack of character evolution over seven years is contributing to the promotion's live attendance struggles.
