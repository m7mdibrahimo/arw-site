Jon Moxley is heading back to NJPW. Shingo Takagi has been calling out the Death Riders leader and AEW Continental Champion since defeating group member Claudio Castagnoli at NJPW Destruction in Kobe.

At NJPW King of Pro-Wrestling on October 12, Shingo Takagi and Daiki Nagai defeated Hirooki Goto and Tatsuya Matsumoto when Takagi pinned Matsumoto with the Death Rider. The victors mocked Moxley's entrance walk before the Ryōgoku Sumo Hall lights went out and a recorded message from Moxley, accompanied by Marina Shafir, played on the screen.

Moxley did not say Takagi's name, but he confirmed his return to NJPW. Takagi then took a microphone to note that Moxley omitted his name, demanding a match in the ring if he is coming back.
