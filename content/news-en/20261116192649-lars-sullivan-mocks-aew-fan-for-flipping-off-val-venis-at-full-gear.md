Lars Sullivan recently weighed in on a viral moment from AEW Full Gear, where a fan was filmed flipping off Val Venis. Instead of addressing the fan's verbal protest, Sullivan targeted the fan’s physical appearance and used the incident to criticize AEW’s audience and leftist politics. The incident occurred on November 14 outside the Full Gear venue, where Venis had been conducting interviews. Footage showed a fan shouting at Venis to leave before giving him the middle finger, to which Venis responded sarcastically, "Thank you brother, appreciate it." Sullivan later shared his thoughts on X, stating, "Shaped like the average aew fan. Why are the most hideous looking people drawn to leftist politics?" Venis had been promoting his presence outside the event for weeks, intending to debate fans on political issues, including abortion. The middle-finger incident became the catalyst for Sullivan’s broader critique of the AEW crowd.
