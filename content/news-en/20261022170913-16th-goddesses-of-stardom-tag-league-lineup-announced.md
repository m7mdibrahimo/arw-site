STARDOM has announced the official lineup for the 16th Goddesses of STARDOM Tag League. The round-robin tournament is scheduled to begin on November 1 and conclude on November 29. The participating teams for the Blue Goddess block include Suzu Suzuki and Rina Yamashita, Natsupoi and Rian, Ranna Yagami and Akira Kurogane, Hina and Lady C, Hazuki and Koguma, Dark Silueta and Valkyria, Konami and Rina, Natsuko Tora and Ruaka, as well as Saya Iida and Utami Hayashishita. Meanwhile, the Red Goddess block features Aya Sakura and Sayaka Kurara, Saori Anou and Fuwa-chan, Yuna Mizumori and Anne Kanaya, Maika and HANAKO, Hanan and Waka Tsukiyama, Mei Seira and Ema Maishima, Kikyo Furusawa and Kiyoka Kotatsu, Starlight Kid and Maki Itoh, alongside Momo Watanabe and Saya Kamitani.
