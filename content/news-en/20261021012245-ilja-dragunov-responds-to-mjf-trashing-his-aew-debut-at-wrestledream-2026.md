Ilja Dragunov is not bothered by MJF's recent criticism of his AEW debut at WrestleDream 2026. After MJF mocked his entrance and called him a "Russian/German loser" on social media, Dragunov addressed the comments during an appearance on the Battleground Podcast.

When asked for a response, Dragunov dismissed the insults, stating, "Nothing." He emphasized that he prefers to focus on relevant challenges rather than social media arguments, noting, "I’m Russian-German. We handle problems of relevance." Dragunov explained that his upbringing taught him not to be hurt by words, adding, "If I got hurt more by words, I wouldn’t be sitting here nowadays."

Instead of trading insults with MJF, Dragunov stated he is focused on his upcoming in-ring work in AEW, preferring to let his actions speak for themselves. "Chops and elbows and boots and throws and... come on. Like, I handle problems of relevance."
