Kevin Nash recently shared his thoughts on Paul Wight, known to WWE fans as Big Show, explaining that while Wight was one of the kindest people in the industry, there were times when he lost his temper backstage. On the Kliq This podcast, Nash and Sean Oliver discussed their old travel stories, noting that Wight was a genuinely nice person in a business that often lacked such individuals. Nash recalled how Wight would sometimes snap after enduring mistreatment from others over a long period. "He was so nice in a business of just not nice people. So he had to, he would sometimes, he'd flip out because he just, he'd had enough of it." Nash rejected the idea that Wight was overly sensitive, clarifying that he was simply a good person who eventually reached his limit. Wight currently serves as a commentator for AEW Collision. Nash praised his work in that role, noting that their past backstage experiences were never about Wight having a bad attitude, but rather a reaction to dealing with a difficult environment.
