Cody Rhodes discussed the global release of Street Fighter, stating that the new film is a proud moment for the entire wrestling industry. The movie adapts the famous video game franchise and features professional wrestlers Cody Rhodes, Roman Reigns, and Hirooki Goto.

During a recent interview, Rhodes shared his thoughts on the project. 

> "I really think it's a moment for all of wrestling. to be proud and, you know, it's kind of our moment, right? I'm so lucky that i got offered this role and I'd work your whole life and you want these things and you know this is you literally, you've been on the up in the up in the up, your upward mobility is amazing to watch, which is why i'm so happy that you're here, but I'm excited for all of wrestling at three wrestlers in a movie. Think of the days we can get, you know, I remember going to see Guardians (Of The Galaxy) and people, being curious how Dave Bautista would be, and then the first big laugh was a Drax moment, was a Batista moment. I felt like, 'yeah, that's the wrestler, that's the guy.' So the fact that you have Roman, you have Goto and me all in this, and everyone's vastly different, I'm excited for wrestling."
