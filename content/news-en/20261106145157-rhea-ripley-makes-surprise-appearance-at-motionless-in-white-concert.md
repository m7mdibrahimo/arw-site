Rhea Ripley made a surprise appearance on stage at a Motionless in White concert while recovering from knee surgery. Band vocalist Chris Cerulli thanked her for her support and for bringing attention to rock and metal worldwide before dedicating the song Fight Like Hell to her. Ripley continues her recovery after undergoing meniscus surgery in August and has noted that she is not yet ready to return to the ring.
