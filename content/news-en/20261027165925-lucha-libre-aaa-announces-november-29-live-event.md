Ector County Coliseum in Odessa, TX announced that Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide will hold a live event on November 29. This event takes place a day after WWE Survivor Series at Daikin Park in Houston, TX. Additionally, the promotion will host a show on November 27 at 713 Music Hall in Houston, TX.

Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide delivers fast-paced action, energetic atmospheres, and unique personalities to provide fans with an unforgettable entertainment experience.
