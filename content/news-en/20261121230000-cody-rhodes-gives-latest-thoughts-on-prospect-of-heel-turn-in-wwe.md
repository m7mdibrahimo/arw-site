Four years into his WWE run, Cody Rhodes is facing growing calls from fans and pundits to turn heel, particularly as crowds have begun siding with Randy Orton during their recent feud. Speaking on "The Ebro Laura Rosenberg Show," Rhodes remained noncommittal about a potential turn while reiterating that he is not opposed to the idea. "I'm open to anything if it's something that moves the ball forward for WWE," Rhodes said.

He noted that some fans already view his persona as performative and pretentious, adding that he is open to the concept if the opportunity arises, though he believes the longer they go without a definitive heel turn, the less likely it becomes. Rhodes also shared how he envisions a potential turn, stating he would aim to be a genuinely disliked heel rather than a cool one, explain his reasoning only once, and utilize a manager to handle his promos moving forward.
