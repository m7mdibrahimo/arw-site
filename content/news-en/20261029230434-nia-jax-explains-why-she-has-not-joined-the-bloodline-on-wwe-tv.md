Nia Jax possesses deep roots in the Anoa'i wrestling family, yet WWE has never officially placed her inside The Bloodline on television. During an appearance on the Keep It Aloha podcast, Jax addressed why she remains outside the faction on screen, explaining that she already views herself as part of the group behind the scenes.

> "Well, technically, I am in The Bloodline. I don't know if you saw the chart that Dwayne put up. I'm up there. But, you know, it all just depends on storyline for wrestling, right? Everybody wants, like, ‘Oh, why isn't it just automatic being there?’ But I actually really appreciate the fact that I can carve out my own name by myself."

Jax noted that while she enjoys establishing her own standalone identity, she remains ready to assist if a creative direction ever requires female members of the family.

> "But if the boys ever needed me, you know, they've got Naomi, they've got me, they've got Tamina. They can always call us whenever is needed. But technically, I am in The Bloodline, just not on TV."
