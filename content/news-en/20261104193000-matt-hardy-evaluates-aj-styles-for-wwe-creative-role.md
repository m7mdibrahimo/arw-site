AJ Styles seems headed toward a backstage job in WWE NXT, a move that Matt Hardy thinks fits him well. Recent rumors point to NXT leader Shawn Michaels potentially heading to the main roster, with Styles considered to replace him.

Speaking on The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy podcast, Hardy stated that Styles possesses the background and mindset to succeed in this role. He noted Styles' desire to aid young talent, recalling his past agent work in TNA and his extensive independent wrestling experience.
