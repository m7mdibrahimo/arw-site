Kyle Fletcher believes that a future match against Ilja Dragunov in AEW is inevitable, noting that he would be deeply disappointed if the two never met in the ring. Since joining AEW, Dragunov has competed against several notable stars, including The Beast Mortos and Speedball Mike Bailey.

In a recent interview with TMZ Sports, Fletcher discussed the prospect of facing his former independent scene rival. The two previously wrestled in the UK during 2019 and 2020. Regarding a potential reunion, Fletcher stated:

> "I think I would be incredibly disappointed if it never happened. I would like to think it will happen sooner rather than later. I have always thought the world of Ilja. We had a few matches when we were both on the UK independent scene in, I believe, 2019 and 2020. Obviously, we’ve been on incredibly different paths since then, and it’s pretty cool that now our paths have aligned again. I’m so excited to see what he can accomplish, the matches that he can put on, the people that he could get in the ring with. I think there’s a lot of magic to be made there, and I think whenever we get in the ring, I think it’s inevitable. I think it’ll be magic, too."
