B-Fab and Michin retained the WWE Women's Tag Team Championship on SmackDown after their match ended in a no contest amid widespread chaos. The bout devolved into a brawl involving multiple competitors before Charlotte Flair made a surprise return. She stood face to face and exchanged blows with her opponents. The show concluded with a massive ring-clearing brawl featuring Flair, Bayley, Alexa Bliss, and Tatum Paxley as they prepared for upcoming battles.
