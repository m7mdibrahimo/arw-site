UFC 334: Gane vs. Hokit took place on Saturday, November 14 from Madison Square Garden in New York, NY, and aired live on Paramount+.

Full results from the event are below:

Evan Elder def. Samuel Sanches by unanimous decision (30-26, 30-26, 30-26)

Juan Diaz def. Alatengheili by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 30-27); Diaz missed weight for the bantamweight bout

Bia Mesquita def. Macy Chiasson by submission (rear-naked choke) at 3:23 of Round 1 in a women's bantamweight bout

Melquizael Costa def. Jared Gordon by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28) in a lightweight bout

Cesar Almeida def. Donte Johnson by TKO (punches) at 3:20 of Round 1 in a 190-pound catchweight bout

Terrance McKinney def. Jim Miller by knockout (punches) at 1:53 of Round 1; McKinney missed weight for the lightweight bout

Chris Duncan def. Drew Dober by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 29-28) in a lightweight bout

Stephen Thompson def. Charles Radtke by TKO (kick) at 2:54 of Round 3 in a welterweight bout

Luis Gurule def. Bilal Hasan by split decision (28-29, 29-28, 29-28) in a flyweight bout

Uros Medic vs. Kevin Holland ended in a majority draw (29-27, 28-28, 28-28) in a welterweight bout

Caio Borralho def. Yousri Belgaroui by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 30-27) in a middleweight bout

Kayla Harrison def. Amanda Nunes by submission (rear-naked choke) at 1:29 of Round 4 to retain the UFC Women’s Bantamweight Championship

Ciryl Gane def. Josh Hokit by unanimous decision (48-47, 48-47, 48-47) to retain the UFC Heavyweight Championship
