UFC 334: Gane vs. Hokit took place on Saturday, November 14, at Madison Square Garden in New York, NY. The event aired on Paramount+. Below are the full results.

Evan Elder def. Samuel Sanches by unanimous decision (30-26, 30-26, 30-26).

Juan Diaz def. Alatengheili by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 30-27).

Bia Mesquita def. Macy Chiasson by submission (rear-naked choke) at 3:23 of Round 1.

Melquizael Costa def. Jared Gordon by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28).
