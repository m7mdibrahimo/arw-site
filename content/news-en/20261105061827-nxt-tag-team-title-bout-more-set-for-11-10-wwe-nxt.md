An NXT Tag Team Championship match has been officially scheduled for the November 10 episode of WWE NXT. Following their victory in the 2026 Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic tournament, Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno were awarded their championship opportunity by Cody Rhodes, who presented them with the tournament trophy on television. Dar and Moreno will challenge Vanity Project for the NXT Tag Team Championship. The updated match card for the November 10 program features Vanity Project defending the NXT Tag Team Championship against Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno, E.K. Prosper versus Kam Hendrix, Wren Sinclair versus Izzi Dame, and Lizzy Rain versus Chantel Monroe.
