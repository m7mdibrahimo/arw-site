WWE and AEW stars have several upcoming movie releases scheduled across theaters and streaming services. Drew McIntyre leads the pack with Disney's animated film Hexed, which premieres on November 12 at the El Capitan Theatre in Hollywood, followed by a theatrical release on November 25 in the United States and November 27 in the United Kingdom. McIntyre and Pat McAfee both contributed voice work to the project. McIntyre is also attached to the Highlander reboot and The Last Druid, though neither has a confirmed release date. CM Punk stars in Netflix's Guarding Stars, premiering on November 18, where he plays a security team member named Doghouse alongside Leighton Meester and Jared Padalecki. AEW World Champion MJF joins David Harbour in the action-comedy sequel Violent Night 2, arriving in theaters on December 4. The Rock returns on December 25 in Jumanji: Open World alongside Kevin Hart, Jack Black, and Karen Gillan, while Fast Forever is scheduled for March 17, 2028, with Johnson reprising his role as Luke Hobbs. He is also working on Lizard Music. Adam Copeland appears alongside Jason Statham in The Beekeeper 2 on January 15, 2027. Liv Morgan's Bad Lieutenant: Tokyo, which opened in Japan on October 23, awaits a confirmed U.S. theatrical date from NEON. Meanwhile, Street Fighter, featuring Cody Rhodes, Roman Reigns, and Hirooki Goto, has grossed $99,249,281 worldwide since opening on October 16.
