Kurt Angle hasn't wrestled in a long time, but the WWE Hall of Famer proved he hasn't forgotten how to execute the Ankle Lock during a recent appearance for MCW Pro Wrestling.

The show featured a verbal confrontation and in-ring altercation between Kurt Angle and James Ellsworth. Ellsworth attempted to kick Angle before the Olympic gold medalist caught his leg and quickly dropped him to the mat, locking in his signature hold.

Ellsworth sold intense pain as Angle applied pressure before releasing his leg and leaving the ring with help from others. Meanwhile, Ellsworth shared video footage of the incident on X, claiming that Kurt Angle attacked him for no reason.

Although Angle's days of taking bumps and working full matches are behind him, moments like this give fans a chance to remember his storied days without requiring an official return from retirement.
