Ric Flair's long-discussed biopic with The Rock's Seven Bucks Productions is still active, with updates expected in the coming weeks. Flair stated that he anticipates learning more about the film shortly. More than two years after its initial announcement, Flair confirmed that Seven Bucks and Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson remain involved. While the project is still missing a lead actor, Flair revealed that multiple potential stars have been approached. Flair admitted that his personal choice for the role is Extraction star Chris Hemsworth, though no official casting has been announced. The movie was originally reported in February 2024, with Johnson, Dany Garcia, and Hiram Garcia producing alongside Kevin Misher. Flair expressed confidence that the project is moving forward and noted that he expects a financial payout if the film is made.
