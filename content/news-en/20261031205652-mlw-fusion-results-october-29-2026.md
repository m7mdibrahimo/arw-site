Major League Wrestling aired the latest episode of MLW Fusion on October 29, streaming on Tubi. In the opening Opera Cup quarterfinal match, LaBron Kozone defeated Diego Hill. Following that, Trevor Lee accepted an open challenge from Yota Tsuji for MLW Saturday Night SuperFight. Later in the show, Shotzi Blackheart interrupted an interview with Zamaya, who stated that Shotzi did not save her, but rather chose her. Meanwhile, Matt Riddle and Austin Aries agreed to face each other at MLW Saturday Night SuperFight. In other action, Wolfgang defeated Beastman. Masha then challenged Zamaya for MLW SuperFight, and Mistico was officially confirmed for the event. The broadcast also featured footage of Glasgow Boys on Top attacking CJ Gallows, as well as Josh Bishop and Killer Kross battling in the parking lot, which concluded with comments from Kross. Additionally, a Tessa Blanchard video package aired, and Donovan Dijak told Josh Bishop that he calls the shots. In the main event, Matt Riddle defeated Ikuro Kwon in an Opera Cup quarterfinal match.
