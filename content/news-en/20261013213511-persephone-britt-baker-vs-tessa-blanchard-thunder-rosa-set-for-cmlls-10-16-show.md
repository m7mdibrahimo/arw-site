Britt Baker and Persephone are set to team up at the upcoming CMLL all-women's event on October 16 at Arena México. The show will be headlined by the CMLL Amazonas Universal Championship tournament final. Baker will partner with her ally, the reigning CMLL World Women's and AEW TBS Champion, Persephone. The card also features several other tag team matches, including Kira vs. Hera, Garra Negra & Johnnie Robbie vs. Dark Silueta & Reyna Isis, Keyra & Zeuxis vs. Momo Watanabe & Emersyn Jayne, and India Sioux & Lluvia vs. AZM & Miyu Amasaki. The event concludes with Olympia vs. Skadi for the CMLL Amazonas Universal Championship.
