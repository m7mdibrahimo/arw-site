Shawn Michaels acknowledges that building long-term stories in WWE NXT comes with a frustrating challenge because WWE can pull his top stars to the main roster at any moment, often cutting storylines short. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, Michaels explained how unexpected call-ups disrupt creative plans, though everyone working on the brand has learned to anticipate such shifts. He noted that experience helps him sense when a talent might be moving up, but emergency situations on WWE RAW or SmackDown, such as a sudden injury during an active feud, can force WWE to pluck an NXT talent as an immediate replacement rather than choosing someone who lacks momentum.

Because of this, Michaels and the NXT creative team must remain prepared for a wrestler to vanish without a traditional goodbye match or storyline conclusion. While they can briefly acknowledge the departure on television, having backup plans is essential. Michaels admitted that constant pivoting makes his job difficult, yet he finds the unpredictability exciting because it keeps him on his toes. Ultimately, the reality of running WWE's developmental brand means a single phone call from the main roster can alter creative plans instantly.
