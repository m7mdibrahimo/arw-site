Kam Hendrix did not get the answer he wanted when he tried to secure a spot in the John Cena Classic during NXT. Following the November 4 episode of NXT, Hendrix took to social media to react to his backstage exchange with John Cena, making it clear he is not giving up on the tournament. Hendrix had approached Cena backstage, assuming his invitation was already waiting and asking who his opponent would be. Cena declined to hand him a spot, telling him instead to focus on his opponent for the following week, E.K. Prosper. Hendrix responded on X by writing, "Delays Are Not Denials #DAND", treating the setback as a mere delay rather than a closed door.
