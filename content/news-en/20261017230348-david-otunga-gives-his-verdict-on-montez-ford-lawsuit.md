Former WWE wrestler and attorney David Otunga has shared his legal verdict on the recent lawsuit filed against Montez Ford and WWE by a security guard over a lap dance incident during the July 6 episode of WWE RAW. Otunga analyzed the available video footage on his YouTube channel and noted that he could not see clear physical contact being made, suggesting the interaction appeared to be a harmless, playful joke intended to entertain the crowd rather than intentional battery or cause for severe emotional distress. While acknowledging that other camera angles or future evidence could potentially alter the legal analysis, Otunga expressed skepticism regarding the plaintiff's emotional distress and battery claims, while also noting that WWE and Ford hold a strong defense should the case proceed further.
