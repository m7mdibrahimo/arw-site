Will Ospreay recently discussed his perspective on being asked about WWE during interviews, noting that while it comes with the territory, he prefers to focus his energy on AEW. During a conversation with Brandon Kravitz, Ospreay was asked if he is tired of the frequent questions regarding his competitors.

> Oh, it's I just I know it's a part of the gig. Like, people always asking, like, I can only give my opinion. I don't really care about it. Like, it's just I'd rather focus on AEW and I'd rather focus on, like, what we're able to do here, and, like, what I believe AEW is changing the face of professional wrestling, and I love what we present. I love what we do. I love our wrestling. I love everything that like we're all about. So like, I couldn't really care less about any other promotion.

Ospreay emphasized his commitment to the AEW product and the impact he believes the company is having on the professional wrestling landscape. During the same interview, he also touched upon the recent addition of the group known as The New Level to the AEW roster.
