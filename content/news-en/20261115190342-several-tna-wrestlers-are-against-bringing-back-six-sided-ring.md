Trey Miguel wants TNA to bring back the six-sided ring for Destination X, but he has encountered plenty of backstage resistance. Miguel revealed that several talents have complained about the idea because they do not want to take bumps inside the unforgiving ring. Speaking with The Indy Taker on WrestleThings, Miguel said Destination X would be the perfect night for the setup. His opponent, Leon Slater, is firmly on his side and previously said he would love to defend the TNA World Championship inside it.
