Expect The Unexpected Wrestling put on ETU Show Me Vol. 1 on October 25 at The Mecca located in Ridgefield Park, New Jersey, with the show streaming live on IWTV, according to Fightful.

Devious Cass def. Isaiah Broner via disqualification.

Leah Night def. Nessa Ferrari.

Joseph Alexander def. Troy Parker to retain the BST Championship.

Marcus Mathers def. J Boujii.

Janai Kai def. Jordan Blade to retain the ETU Coastal Crown Championship.

Ikuro Kwon def. Dustin Waller to retain the ETU Key To The East Championship.
