Triple H praised WWE's next generation after Money in the Bank 2026, stating that the youth movement stole the show. Speaking at the post-show press conference, he highlighted the performances of Oba Femi, Bron Breakker, Je’Von Evans, Sol Ruca, and Lash Legend.

Triple H specifically pointed to the ladder matches and Oba Femi's hard-hitting clash with Bronson Reed as standout moments. He noted that the rising stars proved they are ready to seize opportunities and carry the company into the future.
