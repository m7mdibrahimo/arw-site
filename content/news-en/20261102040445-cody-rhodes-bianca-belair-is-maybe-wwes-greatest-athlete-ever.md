Cody Rhodes believes Bianca Belair may be the greatest athlete in WWE history. Belair has not wrestled since WrestleMania 41, where she publicly announced her pregnancy. Speaking on the Double Coverage Podcast, Rhodes discussed the modern trend of recruiting athletes with "defensive tackle builds" for WWE. While discussing the versatility of modern performers, Rhodes stated, "Bianca Belair is maybe WWE's greatest athlete ever." Rhodes emphasized that Belair represents the "melting pot" of talent, arguing that elite athleticism provides a significant edge in the ring, whether it complements a performer's charisma, promo ability, or technical savvy.
