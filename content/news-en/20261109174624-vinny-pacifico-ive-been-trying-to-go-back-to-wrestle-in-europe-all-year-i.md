Independent professional wrestler Vinny Pacifico recently spoke about not being able to compete in Europe this year, expressing his strong desire to return to the continent.

Speaking with Fightful Overbooked's In The Weeds, Pacifico explained that other commitments, such as film projects, have repeatedly delayed his plans to travel abroad. He noted, "Definitely putting together some European stuff. Like I've been trying to go back to Europe all year, but just like things come up like film wise or other, other stuff wise that I'm like, 'ah, shit, I can't go to Europe yet.' But definitely miss Europe very much. Cause Europe, the wrestling scene in Europe is just absolutely amazing."

In addition to his wrestling career, Pacifico has been active in acting and content creation, including recent appearances with Big Boom AJ.
