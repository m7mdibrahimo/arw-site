Indie wrestling star Emily Jaye shared heartbreaking news with her fans, revealing that her father has passed away. Jaye first posted on X to tell supporters that she was going through one of the hardest days of her life and urged everyone to hold their loved ones close. In a follow-up post, she confirmed the tragic loss with a simple and touching message. Jaye also included two personal photos in her tribute, showing her with her father during her childhood and later in life while wearing her wrestling gear. Fans and followers have flooded her with condolences during this difficult time.
