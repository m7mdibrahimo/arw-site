Nikki Bella is enjoying a successful WWE return behind the scenes, according to WrestleVotes Radio on Fightful Select. Officials are reportedly thrilled with her strong social media engagement, and her merchandise sales have also surpassed internal expectations.

> “WrestleVotes is told that Nikki Bella has been drawing praise within WWE as of late, with her social media numbers continuing to impress week after week.”

This positive reception arrives as Bella prepares to challenge Chelsea Green for the WWE Women's Championship on SmackDown. Bella previously competed against Paige in an intense match featuring John Cena's signature moves. Although Paige secured the victory using the ropes, Bella remains a key part of the plans as WWE considers an even larger television spotlight.
