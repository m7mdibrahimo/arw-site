AEW is presenting AEW Blood & Guts on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, at the VyStar Veterans Memorial Arena in Jacksonville, FL. The event starts at 8 pm ET and airs on TBS and Max.

The men's Blood & Guts match features Will Ospreay, Adam Copeland, Darby Allin, Steven Borden, and The Young Bucks (Matt Jackson and Nick Jackson) vs. The Don Callis Family (Kazuchika Okada, Kyle Fletcher, Wardlow, Kevin Knight, Mark Davis, and Jake Doyle).

The women's Blood & Guts match features Triangle of Madness (Thekla, Julia Hart, and Skye Blue), Divine Dominion (Lena Kross and Megan Bayne), and Persephone vs. The Brawling Birds (Jamie Hayter and Alex Windsor), Wrathlander (Harley Cameron and Kris Statlander), Willow Nightingale, and Queen Aminata.
