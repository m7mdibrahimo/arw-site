New details have emerged regarding the length of the contracts for Austin Creed and Kofi in AEW, with reports indicating they plan to stay with the promotion for the long run. Creed and Kofi made their debut at All In and quickly captured the World Trios Championship alongside Swerve Strickland. According to Fightful Select, the duo signed five-year deals with Tony Khan's promotion, with Strickland playing a significant role in bringing them in. The trio recently lost their titles to Hangman Adam Page and Brodido at WrestleDream.
