Kevin Nash recently shared his thoughts on Roman Reigns during an episode of the Kliq This podcast, defending the WWE star against claims that he does not belong in the GOAT conversation. While a fan argued that Reigns benefited from a stronger push than Cody Rhodes, Seth Rollins and Jon Moxley, Nash emphasized that Reigns simply looks and sounds like a legitimate superstar. When asked if he would trust Reigns to carry the company as the head of creative, Nash responded with an emphatic yes and added that he is a fan. Nash noted that Reigns checks all the boxes required to be the face of a promotion, drawing on his own experience at the highest level in WWE and WCW.
