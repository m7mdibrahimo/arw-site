David Finlay and Clark Connors, key members of The Cut, recently shared details regarding their transition to AEW. During an interview on "What Happened When," Connors explained that he joined the promotion in late January to assist Gabe Kidd. Finlay noted that he spent more time negotiating his contract before officially signing in March. Both wrestlers expressed satisfaction with their current tenure at AEW, specifically highlighting the financial benefits. They agreed that the compensation in AEW is significantly higher than what they earned in NJPW.
