Zelina Vega is speaking out about the creative limits she faced during her time in WWE. Following her departure from the promotion, the former Women's United States Champion shared that she now enjoys full freedom over her promo work, ring style, and overall presentation without worrying about backstage restrictions.

Speaking with Ring the Belle, Vega revealed that WWE officials previously placed boundaries on her promo segments to keep her from overshadowing others. She explained that management controlled multiple aspects of her character, including her attire and hair choices.

> "Like I don't have a well you can get in there and have this promo but you can't outshine this person or you can't do this or you can't wear that or you can't have your wigs look this color."

> "I don't have any more restrictions. It's literally freedom."

During her final run in WWE, Vega captured the Women's United States Championship by defeating Chelsea Green. Although she achieved her goal of winning a singles title, Vega expressed a desire to defend the championship more frequently, even hoping to take the title to external promotions like AAA and Stardom. Moving forward, she plans to present an authentic version of herself with sharper promos and greater flexibility in her matches.
