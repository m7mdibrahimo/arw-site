WWE has announced a singles match between Cruz Montana and Dion Lennox for the October 20 episode of WWE NXT. According to Fightful, the rivalry intensified on October 13 when Lennox attacked Montana backstage. Lennox then used Montana's phone to call his mother and threatened to send her son back to the dark space he once occupied.

The event will also feature the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic tournament. The lineup for the show is as follows:

Cruz Montana def. Dion Lennox
Vanity Project (Jackson Drake, Ricky Smokes & Brad Baylor) def. E.K. Prosper, Mini Vikingo & Chazz 'Starboy' Hall
The Wagner Brothers def. BirthRight
Creed Brothers def. Hank and Tank
