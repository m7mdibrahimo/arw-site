Hijo de Fishman has been released on bail following his arrest earlier this week. El Patrón Espectáculos CEO Edgar Sandoval and the law firm Magallan Reyes y Asociados announced the release, explaining that a reparative agreement was reached regarding the tragic passing of referee Gerardo German Pineda, known as El Búho. The incident occurred on October 6 when an underhook piledriver left the referee unconscious, leading to a manslaughter charge against Fishman.
