According to Ringside News, wrestler El Cuatrero spoke about the difficult period he experienced over the past two years following a domestic violence case involving Stephanie Vaquer, confirming that his legal battle is far from finished. Cuatrero explained in an interview that he spent nearly two years detained due to personal circumstances, noting that the experience completely changed his life. Despite returning to the wrestling ring, he stressed that legal matters still need to be resolved and he is ready to move forward until the end to clarify all the facts.

Cuatrero added that his main concern during his detention was not wrestling, but checking on his family and worrying about what would happen to them. He expressed gratitude for being back with them and working again while the case continues its legal path.
