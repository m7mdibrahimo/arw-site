AEW Dynamite aired live from the Watsco Center in Coral Gables, Florida, on October 21, 2026. Following the AEW WrestleDream event, Ilja Dragunov made his debut and was introduced by Renee Paquette.

In an AEW World Tag Team Championship Eliminator Match, Mark Davis and Jake Doyle def. The Young Bucks. Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight def. Will Ospreay and Adam Copeland in a tag team match. Swerve Strickland def. Tome Filip. Orange Cassidy and Jay White def. The Death Riders. In an AEW Continental Championship Eliminator Match, Jon Moxley def. Roderick Strong.

During the show, Mercedes Moné addressed her recent title retention against Thekla, claiming she is the most dominant woman in professional wrestling. Steven Borden and Darby Allin challenged The Don Callis Family to a Blood & Guts Match. Following the main event, David Finlay and Clark Connors attacked Roderick Strong, leading to a brawl involving The Bang Bang Gang and Claudio Castagnoli, which revealed a new alliance between Finlay, Connors, The Dogs, and a heel Bang Bang Gang.
