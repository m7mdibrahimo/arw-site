Rusev believes his recent injury has not derailed his momentum in WWE. He remains confident that he can return to television, pick up exactly where he left off, and resume his pursuit of the Intercontinental Championship, a title he has been chasing for over a year.

Speaking on TMZ’s Inside the Ring, Rusev addressed concerns that his absence came at a poor time. He explained that injuries are an inevitable part of a physical profession and noted that he has overcome significant setbacks before, including a broken neck and shoulder surgery. 

> "I don't think I can get upset by that. Life is life. It's a high-impact sport, especially if you do it the right way — things happen. I've broken my neck before, tore my knee, shoulder surgery. I don't think it slowed down my momentum at all."

Rusev dismissed the idea that he would need to rebuild his status from scratch upon his return to WWE RAW. He feels his quality as a performer will allow him to seamlessly re-integrate into the roster. 

> "When you're good, you're good. You come back and plug yourself right back in and kick ass and take names. It didn't slow my momentum at all."

Regarding the Intercontinental Championship, Rusev confirmed his goal remains unchanged. He joked that his only remaining uncertainty is whether he will capture the title before or after the birth of his son, Bear.

> "I've been following the Intercontinental Title for over a year now, and I believe when the time is right, I will bring it back home — whether it's before my son Bear or after Bear."
