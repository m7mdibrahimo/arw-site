According to Ringside News, Ludwig Kaiser is heading back to court as his legal team attempts to have his battery charge dismissed. A new court filing submitted Monday morning, October 19, by attorney Thomas D. Sommerville lists Andrea Martinez Bazarte as a defense witness ahead of the Orlando hearing.

The case stems from an April incident at an Orlando apartment complex involving Richard Reap, who claimed Kaiser pushed and punched him inside an elevator. Kaiser's legal team stated that Reap became aggressive, used offensive language, and threatened to call immigration authorities on Bazarte. Kaiser admitted to pushing Reap but maintained he was acting in self-defense to protect himself and his girlfriend.

Kaiser's lawyers asked the judge to drop the misdemeanor battery charge entirely under Florida's Stand Your Ground law. With Bazarte officially added to the defense witness list hours before the proceedings, fans and observers await the outcome inside the Orlando courtroom.
