Cody Rhodes recently shared details about the bus fire he experienced during WrestleMania 40 weekend, explaining how an exploding air-conditioning unit caused the blaze outside Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia. Rhodes revealed that amid the chaos, he only grabbed two things before escaping: his essential wrestling boots and a family photograph. He then ran to seek refuge on Seth Rollins' bus while firefighters handled the situation.

Two days later, WWE required footage of Rhodes stepping out of his tour bus to fulfill a sponsorship commitment. Despite the vehicle being completely destroyed, production had him walk out of the burned-out shell as if nothing had happened.

> "I walked out of my burnt, hollowed-out bus as if it hadn't burnt down and we hadn't talked about it in the media the last two days, just so they could get that sponsored shot."

Rhodes chuckled at the bizarre request, emphasizing that the company always goes above and beyond to satisfy its commercial partners.
