Viewers of "WWE Raw" were surprised when "NXT" star Cruz Montana appeared and revealed himself as the newest member of The 946. According to Dave Meltzer on "Wrestling Observer Radio," the move was such a secret that even the leadership in "NXT" was unaware of his impending call-up. Meltzer expressed skepticism that Shawn Michaels was truly in the dark, but noted that everyone else was blindsided, as they had plans for Montana in "NXT" right up until the day he debuted on Monday. Montana, a former two-time TNA World Heavyweight Champion, had been involved in a heated rivalry with Dion Lennox in "NXT" and recently defeated him in an Unsanctioned Match last Wednesday.
