The 13-year veteran took to social media to announce his achievement, lifting 155 pounds and noting that the closest competitor lifted 138 pounds in a higher weight class. WARHORSE continues to compete actively on the independent wrestling scene, having worked for numerous promotions throughout his career, including AEW.
