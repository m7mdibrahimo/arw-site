Another week of professional wrestling brings a fresh batch of winners and losers from the Wrestling Inc. staff. Carmelo Hayes had a massive week, joining The 946 on WWE RAW alongside Royce Keys just days before his wedding to NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan. After months of being left off TV, Hayes made a surprise return by attacking Solo Sikoa with a crowbar in the main event. On the downside, WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green struggled after a loss to Roxanne Perez on RAW, following harsh words from Women's World Champion Liv Morgan, plus an incoming title defense against Nikki Bella. In AEW, Max Caster took responsibility for his recent controversies, announcing he would step away from wrestling to attend therapy and work on himself. Meanwhile, Andrade's pants became the most discussed topic after moving on their own during his AEW National Championship defense against Tommaso Ciampa at AEW WrestleDream 2026, sparking online debate about comedy in wrestling. Finally, Jon Moxley fought through his grief to defeat Mark Davis in an AEW Continental Championship match at WrestleDream, cementing his spot as AEW's top star.
