Jessica Biel discussed the possibility of switching up her career during a recent interview with Kiss alongside John Cena. The actress noted how she enjoys aspects of the professional wrestling industry, explaining that she loves the costumes and the persona of everyone getting to play big, crazy characters. Biel stated that she thinks she would be an amazing wrestler, while Cena agreed that she would be incredible and joked that she has a future in the business if acting does not work out for her. Biel also expressed her enthusiasm for slamming people around in the ring.
