Tommaso Ciampa praised Andrade El Idolo ahead of their upcoming match at AEW WrestleDream, highlighting his exceptional talent. Ciampa is scheduled to challenge for Andrade El Idolo's National Championship later tonight, marking his second title match in two pay-per-views following his International Championship match against Kazuchika Okada at AEW All Out. Speaking in a recent interview, Ciampa discussed the upcoming bout.

> "We always say to all performers, you find yourself at numerous different times throughout your journey. Right now things are just clicking on all cylinders for him. So for me, like, that's the Andrade I want to compete against. It's very cool that it's like, okay, I feel like both of us have kind of reinvented ourselves in some capacity. We've both found this - I don't know if it's second or third wind or whatever it is, where things are clicking again, so for us to somehow have managed to cross paths and come to this point... it'll be special, man, because he is so darn talented."
