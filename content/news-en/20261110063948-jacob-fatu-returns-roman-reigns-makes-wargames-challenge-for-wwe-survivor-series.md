Jacob Fatu made his return on this week's episode of WWE Raw during a heated confrontation between The Bloodline and The 946. The segment was scheduled to feature Royce Keys and Roman Reigns, but it quickly devolved into a faction brawl. Cameras shifted to the parking lot where The Usos and Solo Sikoa stood before a burning truck, followed by the dramatic arrival of Fatu.

Reigns attempted to fight off The 946 alone, but the group's numbers eventually overwhelmed him. The Bloodline arrived to assist, and Fatu put Carmelo Hayes through the announce table with a top rope splash.

Following the chaos, Reigns challenged The 946 to a WarGames match at Survivor Series.
