Drew McIntyre has not wrestled in WWE since WrestleMania 42, but he is already planning several surprises for his comeback. The former WWE Champion spent months transforming his physique, and he is now moving better than he did in his twenties. He promised to introduce fresh maneuvers alongside classic holds that fans have not seen from him in years.

During an interview, McIntyre explained that his time away allowed him to completely revamp his training regimen. Instead of solely focusing on gaining mass, he prioritized balance, flexibility, and overall physical conditioning. He now weighs 285 pounds after adding new muscle, confirming he is in peak physical shape and promising a violent, distinctive style upon his return.
