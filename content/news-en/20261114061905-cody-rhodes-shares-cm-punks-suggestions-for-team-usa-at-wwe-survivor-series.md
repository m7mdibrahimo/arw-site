Cody Rhodes has revealed the list of suggestions that CM Punk gave him for Team USA at WWE Survivor Series. The upcoming event will feature a 5-on-5 match between Team USA and Team Mexico, with Rey Fenix joining Team Mexico on the latest episode of WWE SmackDown. During the segment, Punk offered Rhodes several suggestions for the team and asked him to keep an open mind.

The list shared by Rhodes includes Hacksaw Jim Duggan, Ken Patera, 1987 Nikita Koloff, Jason Voorhees, Mr. America, Bruce Springsteen, and A.J. Lee in a red, white, and blue bikini. Rhodes noted that A.J. Lee was his top pick, but he did not want to play favorites. The suggestions also featured The Patriot, Sgt. Slaughter, The Undertaker with a Betsy Ross flag duster, and John Cena, though Rhodes joked that Cena is not retired and they would just need to pay him.
