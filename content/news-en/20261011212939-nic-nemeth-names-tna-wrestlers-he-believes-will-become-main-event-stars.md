Nic Nemeth recently named several TNA wrestlers he believes will become permanent fixtures in the main event scene. During an interview, Nemeth highlighted talents such as Sosa, BDE, Jason Hotch, and Mustafa Ali, while also discussing the growth of the Elegance Brand and praising challenger Leon Slater ahead of their match at TNA Bound for Glory 2026.
