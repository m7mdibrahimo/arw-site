Jason Sensation was set to return to WWE television following his initial success during the Attitude Era, but those plans were abandoned after the tragic death of Owen Hart in May 1999. Sensation explained that WWE officials felt his return would be problematic because his most well-known character was an impression of the late wrestler.

During an interview with Chris Van Vliet, Sensation discussed how his momentum stalled after immigration issues at the United States border ended his regular appearances on WWE RAW. He had become popular for his impersonations, particularly his portrayal of Owen Hart during the DX parody of the Nation of Domination. Although WWE eventually made plans to bring him back to host various shows, those efforts were halted after Owen Hart died following a fatal accident at the WWE Over the Edge event.

Sensation noted that the company canceled all scheduled Canadian tour dates where he was set to make his comeback. Furthermore, officials believed that having him back on television would serve as a constant reminder of Owen during a period of intense legal scrutiny involving the Hart family. Consequently, he remained under contract with limited opportunities, occasionally working local Canadian events with executive Carl DeMarco, but he never received the television return he had been promised.
