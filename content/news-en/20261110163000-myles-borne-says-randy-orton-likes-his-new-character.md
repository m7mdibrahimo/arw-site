Randy Orton's opinion carries great weight in WWE, and NXT star Myles Borne has earned his approval. During a recent interview, Borne revealed that the 14-time world champion praised his new on-screen persona, which is more cunning and calculated than before.

> "Randy likes it. I mean, he sent me a text a while back saying good stuff," Borne said. "He's always been very supportive of every path that I've taken here at NXT, and he knows better than anybody the path that I'm on right now."

These developments follow Borne's heel turn at NXT Heatwave, where he attacked his long-time friend Tavion Heights after losing the NXT North American Championship. The two later squared off in a Weapons Wild Match at NXT Halloween Havoc.
