Cody Rhodes recently discussed how some wrestling fans focus too much on backstage gossip rather than enjoying the in-house performances. Speaking with Mythical Kitchen, Rhodes pointed to the match between Shawn Michaels and Hulk Hogan at SummerSlam 2005 as a prime example of an underrated encounter that gets overshadowed by historical rumors. He explained that Michaels was always performing and praised him as one of the greatest to ever step in the ring. Rhodes also noted that people outside the industry often claim intimate knowledge of famous events like the Montreal Screwjob, suggesting that fans would have a better experience if they simply enjoyed the show on screen.
