Paul Wight says that he has learned a few things from Shaquille O'Neal due to their shared experience as giants in the industry. Over the years, basketball icon Shaquille O'Neal has made several appearances in professional wrestling, including in both WWE and AEW. Paul Wight, who has also crossed paths with him, noted that they share the experience of being large in size. While speaking to Going Ringside, Wight praised his peer.

> "Shaq is a wonderful, wonderful human being. We laugh every time we're around each other. We have experienced all the same things as far as being bigger in size and whatnot. He loves wrestling. He's got a great attitude. Super keen, super keen business mind. I know that Kevin Hart and a lot of comedians give him a hard time about him talking slow. Let me tell you something. That brother is smart. I've learned quite a few things. Even the little conversations we've had about things, I was like, 'eh, that guy's on to something.'"
