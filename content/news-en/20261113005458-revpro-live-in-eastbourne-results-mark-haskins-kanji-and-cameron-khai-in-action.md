Revolution Pro Wrestling held its RevPro Live In Eastbourne event on November 8 at the Winter Garden in Eastbourne, East Sussex, England. The card featured a mix of singles and Great British Tag League tournament matches.

In the opening singles match, Mark Haskins defeated Owen Swain. Following this, Jay Joshua secured a victory over Thomas Shire.

The event also featured two matches in the Great British Tag League. In a Block A contest, Trew And Lacey, consisting of Kieron Lacey and Mark Trew, defeated Michael Oku and Nino Bryant. In a Block B matchup, CPF, featuring Danny Black and Joe Lando, defeated FRIENDS, a team made up of Leon Cage and Maverick Mayhew.

Two championship matches took place on the card. In a RevPro Undisputed British Women’s Championship match, Kanji successfully defended her title against Aluna. Additionally, Cameron Khai retained the RevPro Undisputed British Cruiserweight Championship by defeating Rich Swann.
