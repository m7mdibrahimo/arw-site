Ric Flair is ready to return to public appearances following a recent fall and hospitalization that left him needing further medical treatment. NCG Wrestling announced that the two-time WWE Hall of Famer will appear at Harpos in Detroit on November 21 for a meet-and-greet event. Flair suffered a fall in Florida on October 23, resulting in facial injuries and an overnight stay at Tampa General Hospital, and later revealed he needs another operation and a skin graft. Despite these health challenges, Flair recently pushed back against critics questioning his physical condition by highlighting his long and demanding career. He is also dealing with a blood clot in his leg, but he clearly has no plans to step away from his fans while recovering.
