Penta holds the Money in the Bank briefcase, granting him a guaranteed title shot at any WWE championship at any time. Speaking at the post-show press conference, Penta hinted at his upcoming plans. 

> "This is a big surprise. I have a lot of ideas in my mind right now, but for now, I am focused on what is next. Something in my soul feels, Penta will soon become a World Champion. Very soon, I have a big surprise for everyone. 

> "I know why Roman Reigns is the best in the universe, but now I have the best key in the world. Champs, watch your back."

Penta also expressed respect for his opponents in the ladder match, including Trick Williams, Bron Breakker, Kevin Owens, Je'Von Evans, and CM Punk.
