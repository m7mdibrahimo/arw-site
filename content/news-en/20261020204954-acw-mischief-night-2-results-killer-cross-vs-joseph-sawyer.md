Awesome Championship Wrestling hosted its ACW Mischief Night 2 event on October 17 from the MJN Convention Center in Poughkeepsie, NY, airing live on TrillerTV+. In the opening match, Liamo defeated Buddy Tomas and Real1 in a triple threat match. J-Rod picked up a victory over Janai Kai. In a title defense, ACW National Champion Zack Clayton successfully retained his belt against Max Caster, after which Max Caster announced he is stepping away from wrestling. In a hardcore match, Mance Warner defeated Marcus Mathers. Sent2Slaughter, consisting of Shawn Donavan and Dan Maff alongside Josh Shernoff, defeated The Vetrano Brothers, Mook Vetrano and Stock Vetrano, with Josh Shernoff subsequently named the new King of Poughkeepsie. Leo Sparrow emerged victorious in a triple threat match against FEAR and Jordan Oliver to capture the ACW Cruiserweight Championship. Matt Riddle secured a win over Richard Holliday, who was accompanied by Ben Bishop. In a match with Matt Hardy serving as the special guest referee, The Righteous, Vincent and Dutch, defeated The NOW, Hale Collins and Vik Dalishus, to win the ACW Tag Team Championships. Shotzi Blackheart triumphed in a casket match against Steph De Lander and Indi Hartwell to win the ACW Women’s Championship. In the main event, ACW Heavyweight Champion Killer Kross, accompanied by Scarlett Bordeaux, defeated Joseph Sawyer in a no disqualification match to retain his title.
