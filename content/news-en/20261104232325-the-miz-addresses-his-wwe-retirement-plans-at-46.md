The Miz has observed John Cena and AJ Styles begin to plan for life after wrestling, but he is not ready to join them yet. At 46 years old, Miz stated that he still feels excellent in the ring, even if the physical toll of the WWE schedule is more noticeable than in his younger years. Speaking to Z100 New York, Miz was asked if retirement was on his mind as other veterans wind down their careers. He emphasized that his body has not signaled that it is time to quit, noting that he does not feel slow or sluggish during his matches.

> "I still feel good, man. Like, if I started feeling aching and pain and my energy was low, I don't feel that right now. I'm 46 years old, and I still feel very, very good in the ring. Like, when I'm in the ring, you know when you're feeling slow? I don't feel slow or sluggish. I feel as good as I've ever felt."

Miz believes his experience has made him a smarter performer, allowing him to construct matches effectively and understand exactly what the audience requires. However, he acknowledged that the constant travel and the recovery time required after longer matches have become more difficult. He explained that the non-televised live events held between televised shows contribute significantly to the accumulation of physical wear and tear.

> "So when Cena said that, like, his body, when he knew it was time, you kind of understand. AJ said the same thing, and I do understand. The travel is what really gets you, and the long matches really take a toll on your body, and it takes a longer time for my body to heal when I have a longer match."

> "So when we do, you know, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday, we have live events that are non-televised that people don't see, but we're still doing those matches. And so the bumps and bruises, the pain, it takes a lot longer to go away."

Miz also highlighted the stark difference in recovery between himself and younger stars like Je'Von Evans, who was not even born when Miz began his WWE career. While he enjoys watching the high-risk style of younger athletes, he has no desire to emulate it at 46. Miz intends to continue his career as long as he feels sharp in the ring, regardless of when other veterans choose to retire.
