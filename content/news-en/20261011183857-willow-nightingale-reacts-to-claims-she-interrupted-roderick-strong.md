Willow Nightingale responded to claims that she interrupted Roderick Strong during the AEW Grand Slam France Collision event. During the show, the Conglomeration faced the Death Riders in an eight-person mixed tag team match. In the match, married couple Roderick Strong and Marina Shafir clashed in the ring when Shafir applied her submission hold on Strong, but Nightingale broke it up with a senton. Nightingale later took to social media to address fans who joked about the moment, defending her actions by stating she was simply trying to win the match.

> "I see y’all claiming “Roddy didn’t want to be saved” or “Willow cock blocked” but hey dudes, I’m not here to shame anyone for whatever they might be into…I’m here trying to win."
