Several wrestlers sustained injuries at recent Lucha Libre AAA tapings, including top star Rey Fenix. According to False Finish, Rey Fenix and El Carnicero were both injured during the AAA on FOX tapings this past Saturday.

Regarding Rey Fenix, the report noted, "Fénix suffered an injury to his hand at the latest AAA tapings. Fénix was able to finish the match but was favoring the injury throughout." Fenix is currently set to perform at WWE Survivor Series as a member of Team Mexico in the five-on-five elimination match against Team USA. He also holds the AAA Cruiserweight Championship.

El Carnicero reportedly picked up his injury during a match against Psycho Clown. Further details on the severity of these injuries remain unknown at this time.
