According to Fightful Select, Brooke Havok was at one point considered and even planned to dethrone Laynie Luck as the WWE ID Women's Champion prior to suffering an ACL tear in September. Monica Monroe ultimately ended Luck's title reign, following which Luck departed the WWE ID program.
