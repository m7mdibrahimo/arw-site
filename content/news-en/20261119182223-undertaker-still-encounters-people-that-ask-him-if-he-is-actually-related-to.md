The Undertaker still gets questions about being Kane's brother in real life. On the latest episode of his Six Feet Under podcast, the WWE Hall of Famer talked about encountering fans who are just finding out the truth. "There's not a day — honestly, if I'm around people on the outside, it's hardly a day goes by where either I get the question or I get the thing like, 'I just found out that you and Kane really weren't brothers. That was a 25 year story, I think that's why they get so disappointed when they find out like, 'Ugh, really?'"
