Tommaso Ciampa and Charles Mason shared a surprising moment before engaging in a violent confrontation at HOG Salvation. Making a rare independent wrestling appearance, Ciampa faced Mason in a hard-hitting main event where neither competitor held back. During the bout, the two wrestlers pressed their foreheads together and shared a kiss before resuming their physical battle. Mason later took to social media to comment on the encounter, stating that the feeling was mutual. Tommaso Ciampa ultimately secured the victory, marking his first win outside of WWE or AEW in a decade.
