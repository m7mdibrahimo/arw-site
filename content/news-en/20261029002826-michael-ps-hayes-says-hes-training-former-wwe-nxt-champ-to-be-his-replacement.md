Michael PS Hayes has been a staple behind the scenes in WWE since 1999. In a recent episode of "What's Your Story?", Hayes discussed his long tenure and joked that while the new generation of producers is talented, they are not quite on his level. However, Hayes revealed that Paul "Triple H" Levesque has been proactive about the future, telling him that he will not be around forever.

Hayes recalled Triple H telling him, "You're our Pat Patterson, I need you to make Bobby Roode [our] Michael." Hayes expressed high praise for Roode, who transitioned from an in-ring career to a producer role in August 2023 following neck surgery. "Boy, has [Roode] come along. Bobby's really, really good. He's going to be great... I see great things for him," Hayes said. He added that Roode is learning to navigate the industry's uncontrollable challenges without wasting unnecessary energy.
