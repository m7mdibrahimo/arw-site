Former WWE and AEW wrestler RYZIN is preparing to retire from professional wrestling after a career spanning over two decades. Rob Kimball, known in the ring as RYZIN, announced that 2027 will be his final year in the sport as he shifts his focus toward a career outside the ring. According to Fightful, he will accept only a limited number of bookings in 2027 before officially hanging up his boots, planning to dedicate more time to advertising, marketing, hosting, and acting. Reflecting on his journey, Kimball stated that he is proud of his accomplishments and ready for a new chapter. He noted that he wants to make the most of his remaining dates before stepping away.

Throughout his more than twenty years in the industry, RYZIN built his reputation on the independent circuit before earning opportunities with major promotions. He participated in a WWE Performance Center tryout and made several appearances on WWE NXT between 2014 and 2018. During the pandemic era, he joined AEW and competed in 40 televised matches, primarily on AEW Dark, before parting ways with the company in 2023. Following his departure from AEW, he continued to wrestle on the independent scene, working regularly for ALW in 2026, with his most recent match taking place in June. As he approaches retirement, these appearances will become increasingly rare.

Outside of wrestling, Kimball already manages a busy professional life. He founded Ringmaster Marketing, an Orlando-based firm specializing in branding, social media, video production, and consulting, and he works full-time at one of Orlando's largest advertising agencies while pursuing additional hosting and acting roles. RYZIN is ready to close the chapter on his wrestling career on his own terms in 2027.
