WWE Hall of Famer Glenn Jacobs, known as Kane, has emerged as the favorite to succeed Marsha Blackburn as US Senator for Tennessee. According to reports from The Washington Sun and Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, the former Knox County mayor is a leading candidate for the appointment as Blackburn vacates her seat to assume office as governor. Blackburn will be officially sworn in on January 16. Two Republican sources indicate that Jacobs holds an advantage due to his long-standing relationship with Blackburn and her advisors, alongside his strong regional recognition in Shelby County and the Memphis metropolitan area. Observers note he possesses a unique ability to connect with both establishment Republicans and working-class voters. While speculation previously suggested that President Donald Trump might seek to install Pete Hegseth in the seat during his House impeachment vote, that motion was blocked and Trump confirmed he wanted Hegseth to remain at the Pentagon. Ultimately, the authority to appoint a successor rests with Blackburn upon her resignation. Meltzer noted that while her original term was scheduled to conclude in 2031, her replacement will stand for election in 2028. Jacobs previously contemplated a run for governor before endorsing Blackburn and serving as her treasurer, and he has confirmed he would seriously consider accepting the Senate appointment if chosen.
