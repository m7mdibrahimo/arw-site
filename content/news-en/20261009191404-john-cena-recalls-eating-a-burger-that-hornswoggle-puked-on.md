John Cena recently shared a funny story about a time he was stuck in Ireland with Hornswoggle and Christian Cage due to a volcano eruption. During an appearance on Trevor Noah's What Now, Cena explained that he ate a hamburger that Hornswoggle had previously thrown up on. According to Cena, Christian Cage had tucked Hornswoggle into bed after a night out and placed the room service tray in the hallway. Later, Cena found the untouched burger in the hallway and decided to eat it. He only discovered the truth the next morning when his friends explained what had happened to the food.
