Tatum Paxley recently revealed that her finishing move, the 'Cemetery Drive,' was inspired by STARDOM star Suzu Suzuki. Paxley, who recently captured the WWE Women's Tag Team Championship alongside Alexa Bliss by defeating Fatal Influence (Fallon Henley and Lainey Reid) on SmackDown, discussed the move during an appearance on Off The Ropes.

> "The finish, I had seen a wrestler — who goes by Suzu Suzuki — pull it out… When I had seen that move, I had not been so drawn to a move that heavy before. I had to take inspo from it, and I even tried different variations of my current finish. No matter what I do, I have to add this twist into it. It has to have this twist, and I just worked out on it for such a long time and then when I finally got to pull it out, I was like, ‘Yeah, this is perfect.’ It’s one of those things you look at and you’re like, huh!? That’s what I want people to think when they see me so my finish works really well with that."

Paxley noted that the move has been instrumental in her success, helping her secure the NXT Women's North American Title and the NXT Women's Title during her rise in WWE.
