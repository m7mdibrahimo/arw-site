Roman Reigns appeared in a new TikTok video alongside the viral personality Gangster Granny. The pair participated in the Couples Challenge, a game where participants answer questions to reveal details about their relationship dynamic.

The questions covered topics such as who expressed interest first, who said I love you first, who is more protective, who eats the most, and who handles the cleaning. Reigns and Granny showed humorous agreement throughout the session.
