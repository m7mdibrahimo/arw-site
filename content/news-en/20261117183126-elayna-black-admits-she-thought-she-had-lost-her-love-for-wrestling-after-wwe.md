Elayna Black, formerly known as Cora Jade, recently revealed that she questioned her future in professional wrestling after leaving WWE. In an interview with The Wrestling Classic, she admitted that she had convinced herself she no longer enjoyed the business, leading to a period of deep reflection during her time away from the ring.

> "I think I told myself that I had fallen out of love with wrestling and I think a big part of me did but I don't think it ever fully left me because even when I took that break for a few months I think even a month in I was already kind of itching to wrestle again even though I knew that I needed to still take some more time and despite me feeling like okay maybe I feel ready like I was I knew I wasn't fully actually ready."

Black spent about a decade in the industry and struggled to determine if she genuinely missed wrestling or simply didn't know how to function without it. She explained that taking a break was necessary to separate those feelings from the passion that originally drew her to the sport. As time passed, she began generating new ideas and reconnecting with friends, which helped her realize that her love for the business had not disappeared.

> "So, I think after I passed that month of thinking about it and then I kept thinking about it more and then I kept thinking about it more and then the wheels started turning and then I start, you know, getting ideas and I start, you know, talking to people and friends and stuff like that. I think it never really left me."

Black noted that her difficult experiences, including personal losses, injuries, and her WWE release in May 2025, had clouded her perspective. She is grateful for the time away, as it allowed her to return to TNA Wrestling in January 2026 with a renewed focus on enjoying her career rather than worrying about the future.
