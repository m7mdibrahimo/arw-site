WWE has announced several major matchups for the October 23 episode of WWE SmackDown. Following an in-ring segment between Blake Monroe and General Manager Nick Aldis, a Street Fight was officially made between Monroe and Giulia for next week's broadcast.

The card will also feature a championship defense, as WWE Women's Tag Team Champions Tatum Paxley and Alexa Bliss put their titles on the line against B-Fab and Michin. Additionally, tag team action is scheduled between Bakusai, consisting of Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki, and The MFT, featuring Tama Tonga and Talla Tonga.

These high-stakes clashes add significant momentum to the blue brand as rivalries continue to escalate heading into next week's show.
