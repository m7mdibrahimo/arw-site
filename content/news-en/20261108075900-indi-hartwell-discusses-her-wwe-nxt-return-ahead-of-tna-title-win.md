Indi Hartwell recently reflected on her return to WWE NXT and the emotions surrounding her comeback to her former promotion. The current TNA Knockouts Champion participated in a number one contender's match for the NXT Women's Championship shortly before securing the Knockouts title at Bound for Glory.

During an appearance on Unseen with Joey Karni, Hartwell explained her mindset regarding the return. She admitted to feeling anxious about how the audience would receive her given how quickly the wrestling landscape changes.

> "Yeah, I think I was so happy and smiley because I was kind of worried that the audience wouldn't remember me or, not that they wouldn't remember me, but that they wouldn't care anymore because the business moves fast and a lot has happened in NXT since I was there. So, that's why I was so happy, and yeah, I guess it did give me a sense of closure."

She noted that the experience allowed her to reconnect with former colleagues. Hartwell added:

> "I was able to, you know, talk with old coaches, producers, and people that I worked with there because when you get released, you don't get to say bye to some people."

The appearance provided momentum for her title match at Bound for Glory, where Indi Hartwell def. Xia Brookside. The NXT event also allowed her to face Zaria for the first time.

> "So, yeah, I feel like it was a bit of closure, also a lot of momentum going into Bound for Glory. I got to have a moment with Zaria in the ring, which was cool. Her and I have never ever crossed paths before. So, yeah, two Aussies facing off in the NXT ring on TV. I think it's pretty special."

Wren Sinclair won the number one contender's match and later challenged Kelani Jordan for the NXT Women's Championship at NXT Halloween Havoc.
