Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson has confirmed his attendance at the upcoming Survivor Series: WarGames event. Speaking on "Busted Open Radio", WWE Hall of Famer Bully Ray discussed potential scenarios for Johnson's appearance, noting that if he gets involved with The Bloodline and their opponents from the 946 stable, WWE must have a clear endgame in mind. Ray emphasized that any physical involvement or cameo from Johnson needs to lead toward a definitive payoff, such as a final match against Roman Reigns in Saudi Arabia or a rematch against Cody Rhodes. According to Ray, Johnson has left the WWE Universe hanging too many times in the past, making a concrete follow-up to Survivor Series crucial if he decides to make his presence felt.
