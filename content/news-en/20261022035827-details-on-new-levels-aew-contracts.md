According to Fightful, the members of New Level have signed five-year contracts with AEW. Austin Creed and Kofi Kingston, formerly known as Xavier Woods and Kofi Kingston in WWE, joined AEW as New Level at AEW All In. They formed a group with Swerve Strickland and captured the AEW World Trios Championship in their debut match at the event.
