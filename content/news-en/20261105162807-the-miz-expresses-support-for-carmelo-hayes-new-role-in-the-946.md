The Miz has offered his perspective on Carmelo Hayes joining forces with Royce Keys to form The 946. Hayes transitioned to WWE Raw after spending several years on WWE SmackDown, where he previously worked alongside The Miz. The new faction has continued to expand, recently adding Cruz Montana to its ranks during the November 2 episode of WWE Raw.

During an appearance on Z100 New York, The Miz reflected positively on the departure of Hayes from his guidance to pursue this new storyline. He highlighted the progress of the group as they prepare for a potential WarGames match against The Bloodline at WWE Survivor Series.

> "Carmelo, who was under my branch, who kind of left my branch, I allowed it. But now what he's doing with The 946 has been amazing, I love seeing what they’re going to do next."
