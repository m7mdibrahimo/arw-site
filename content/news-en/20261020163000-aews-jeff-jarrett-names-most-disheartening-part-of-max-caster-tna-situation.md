AEW's Jeff Jarrett discussed Max Caster's recent TNA Wrestling debut and subsequent firing on his My World podcast, pointing out what he finds most disheartening about the entire situation. Jarrett stated that Caster should have immediately taken responsibility and apologized on social media. He also emphasized that politics and religion are two personal topics that should be avoided in professional wrestling to prevent alienating the audience. Caster eventually apologized to fans and TNA before announcing he would take a break from pro wrestling. Although many speculated his departure was linked to WWE's partnership with TNA and Saudi Arabia, reports indicate that WWE had no involvement in his exit.
