Rob Van Dam clarified that fans laid the blame on him when TNA Wrestling got banned from Twitch over a provocative 2020 television segment, stressing that he was not the person calling the creative shots. The WWE Hall of Famer explained that TNA creative wrote the bedroom scene featuring himself, Katie Forbes, and Jennifer Barlow, and he simply agreed to perform it, according to Ringside News.

On the November 11, 2026 episode of his podcast, RVD recalled the January 14, 2020 segment that depicted him in bed with Forbes and Barlow using whipped cream and censor bars. The suggestive footage ultimately caused TNA Wrestling to get banned from Twitch. He pointed out that the censor bars led some viewers to believe they had watched actual pornography on the promotion's broadcast.

Van Dam expressed frustration over being blamed for the segment airing, questioning how he could be held responsible when he lacked any control over the final editing process. The fallout prompted the company to remove the controversial segment from YouTube.
