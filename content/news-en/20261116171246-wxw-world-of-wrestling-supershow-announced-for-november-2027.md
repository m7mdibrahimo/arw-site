Westside Xtreme Wrestling has revealed plans for the largest show in company history, announcing the wXw World of Wrestling Supershow for November 13, 2027. The historic event will be held at the Grugahalle in Essen, Germany.

Legendary ring announcer Thomas Giesen revealed the project in a video recorded inside the arena, marking professional wrestling's return to the Grugahalle after more than 30 years. The announcement reflected on the promotion's humble roots on Christmas Eve 2000, when only 14 fans witnessed the inaugural show.

Across more than 1,000 shows, wXw has served as a launchpad for major stars including Gunther, Ilja Dragunov, Zack Sabre Jr., and Toni Storm. Tickets will go on sale through Eventim on November 20 at 10 AM CET, offering an early-bird discount through Christmas Eve.
