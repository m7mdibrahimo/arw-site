Pre-sale ticket information on Ticketmaster revealed details for the upcoming 2026 Ring of Honor Final Battle pay-per-view. The event will take place on December 4 at the Boeing Center at Tech Port in San Antonio, Texas.

This year marks the 25th presentation of the Final Battle event. The 2025 edition was held on December 5 in Columbus, Ohio. That show featured Athena successfully defending the ROH Women's World Championship against Persephone. Athena has held that title for more than 1,400 days.
