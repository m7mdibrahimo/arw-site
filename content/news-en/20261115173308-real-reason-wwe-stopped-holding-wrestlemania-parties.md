Cody Rhodes recently shared his perspective on why WWE discontinued its iconic WrestleMania parties. Speaking on the Ebro, Laura, Rosenberg show, Rhodes recalled that the extravagant celebrations eventually began to feel less like private gatherings and more like routine meet-and-greet sessions. Before explaining the shift, he remembered a high point of the party era when he and Santino Marella hosted a bodybuilding competition called Musclemania, with The Undertaker calling out poses and Linda McMahon playfully giving him a thumbs-down. Rhodes clarified that he does not blame Musclemania for the end of the traditions, but rather the dilution of the exclusive fraternity atmosphere as more people gained access. Although the massive parties are gone, Rhodes noted that current WWE stars still gather privately on tour buses.
