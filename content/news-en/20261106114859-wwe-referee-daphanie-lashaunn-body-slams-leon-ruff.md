WWE referee Daphanie LaShaunn body-slammed former NXT North American Champion Leon Ruff during a Twitch stream. LaShaunn posted a video of the moment and wrote in her caption, "HAPPY ZELDA DAY TO ALL THOSE CELEBRATING!!! I SACRIFICED LEON IN HONOR OF PRINCESS ZELDA🔺 Watch our hangout and replay on Twitch! Link in bio!"

LaShaunn and Ruff got married in September 2022. Ruff signed with WWE in 2020 and spent just under a year with the company. LaShaunn, formerly known in the ring as Aja Perera, last wrestled in January 2020 and held the SHINE Nova Title, SHINE Tag Team Titles, and the WWE 24/7 Championship.
