WWE is adjusting its television schedule for the Christmas season, opting to host both RAW and SmackDown in the same venue. The company officially announced that the Capital One Arena in Washington, D.C., will serve as the site for a combined event on December 21, 2026.

RAW is scheduled to broadcast live on Netflix that evening, while SmackDown will likely be taped in advance to air on Christmas Day, December 25. This strategy allows the promotion to record both shows before the holiday, with tickets becoming available for purchase on November 6.

Promotional materials for the Washington, D.C., show highlight several stars, including Seth Rollins, Oba Femi, Liv Morgan, Sol Ruca, Penta, and The Usos for RAW, alongside Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, Tiffany Stratton, Jade Cargill, Trick Williams, and Gunther for SmackDown. A similar combined event is planned for December 28 in Tampa, Florida, to record the January 1, 2027, episode of SmackDown.
