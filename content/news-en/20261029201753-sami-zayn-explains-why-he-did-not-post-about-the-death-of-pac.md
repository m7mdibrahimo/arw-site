Sami Zayn recently discussed his decision to refrain from posting on social media following the death of Benjamin "PAC" Satterly last month due to bronchial asthma. During an interview on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, the WWE Undisputed Champion explained that he struggled to find the right words to honor his late friend publicly.

Zayn noted that the deep bond he shared with Satterly, whom he grew up with and traveled the world alongside, felt impossible to capture in a simple online post. He admitted to being a perfectionist and felt that any digital tribute would fail to do their friendship justice.

> "I wasn't able to post anything, I haven't posted anything (pauses) because what do you even say in a tweet, you know what I mean?"

While Zayn acknowledged feeling guilty for not posting, he ultimately concluded that his grief was a private matter for their inner circle.
