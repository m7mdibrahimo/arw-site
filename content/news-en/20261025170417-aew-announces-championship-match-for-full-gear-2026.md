AEW has announced a new AEW World Trios Championship match for Full Gear 2026, giving Swerve Strickland another opportunity to compete for the titles. Strickland will team with The New Level to face Hangman Page, Brody King and Bandido on November 14 at Full Gear in Phoenix, Arizona. Meanwhile, Strickland is demanding that the bout be changed to a Texas Death Match, though Page has not agreed to the stipulation yet. This match serves as a rematch from WrestleDream, where Page and his partners captured the titles before Strickland decided to invoke his rematch clause and demand harsher rules.
