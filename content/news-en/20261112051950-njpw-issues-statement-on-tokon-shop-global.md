Following the closure of the LA Dojo and the cessation of operations for New Japan of America, NJPW has confirmed the future of its official US store, Tokon Shop Global. While the shop will remain open, the promotion noted that operational changes are necessary because the LA Dojo facility currently functions as the store's warehouse.

In an official statement, the promotion said:

> "Thank you for supporting New Japan Pro-Wrestling. On November 6, NJPW announced that subsidiary New Japan of America will be ceasing operations. The official international store of New Japan Pro-Wrestling, Tokon Shop Global, will continue operations after the dissolution. However, because NJoA's LA Dojo serves as the store's warehouse, operational changes will be made. Sales of pre-order items and products imported from Japan will be halted."

NJPW will fulfill existing orders and issue refunds for delayed pre-orders. Wrestle Kingdom tickets purchased through the site remain valid for pickup at the event.
