The 946 member Royce Keys stated that he does not need Roman Reigns to hand him a pair of Jordans, declaring that he would simply steal them if he desired them.

During an interview with Complex, Keys dismissed a question about Reigns offering him sneakers, asserting that he does not require permission and takes whatever he wishes.

His remarks come amid an ongoing rivalry between The 946 and The Bloodline, which recently saw Carmelo Hayes join The 946. According to Ringside News, taking the shoes would not be unprecedented in WWE, as Bronson Reed previously took Reigns' footwear twice during their feud last year.
