TNA announced major additions to its upcoming Destination X event during the broadcast of Thursday Night iMPACT. The special show will take place on November 15 at the Edmonton EXPO Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. Two Ultimate X matches will be featured on the card, with both the men and women competing under the iconic stipulation. The winner of the women's match earns a future shot at the Knockouts World Championship, while the men's winner will challenge for the X Division Championship.

The event will also witness the return of the World X-Cup tournament, bringing in talent from across the globe. Competitors will face off in two separate Fatal 4-Way matches, with the winners advancing to meet in the tournament final later in the evening.

These matches join the previously announced main event for the TNA World Championship, where champion Leon Slater will defend his title against Trey Miguel.
