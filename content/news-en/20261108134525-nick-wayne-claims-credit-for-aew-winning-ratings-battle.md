Nick Wayne credited himself for AEW Dynamite beating WWE NXT in the latest Wednesday television ratings race, overlooking management and his fellow roster members. Following the publication of the November 4 numbers, Wayne shared a post on X declaring himself the victor.

> "NICK WAYNE WON THE WEDNESDAY NIGHT WAR"

Wayne has frequently boasted about personal milestones, such as previously claiming two victories over Chris Jericho, and he now adds the ratings triumph to his accomplishments.

AEW Dynamite pulled in 792,000 audience members on TBS alongside a 0.24 demographic score, whereas WWE NXT gathered 552,000 viewers on The CW with a 0.13 demo figure.

These remarks arrived after AEW President Tony Khan reacted to false reports claiming NXT had won, replying with a GIF once the real figures confirmed Dynamite led by 240,000 viewers.
