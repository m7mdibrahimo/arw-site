Brie Bella managed to complete a WWE Royal Rumble promotional appearance while suffering from a severe bacterial infection that had lasted for weeks. Following severe flight disruptions on her way to Phoenix, her symptoms flared up significantly. Speaking on The Nikki & Brie Show, Nikki Bella noted that the sisters were originally scheduled to fly on Friday for their promotional appearance at an Arizona Cardinals game, but they experienced numerous delays and cancellations before finally securing a flight on Sunday morning. Nikki also recounted a bizarre airport announcement where staff claimed they did not know the locations of the pilots or flight attendants.

Once they arrived in Phoenix, Brie was dealing with a bacterial infection in her throat, nose, and head that she had been fighting for a month and a half. Nikki witnessed how ill her sister was when they shared a hotel room on Sunday night. Brie noted that her condition was visible in her face during the Royal Rumble media events, prompting her to constantly sanitize her hands to protect others. She was ultimately too unwell to travel on Monday and did not return home until Tuesday. After arriving at her mother's house, Brie was able to rest with prepared meals, antibiotics, and care from her mother.
