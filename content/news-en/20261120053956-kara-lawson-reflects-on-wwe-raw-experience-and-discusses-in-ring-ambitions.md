Kara Lawson, the women's basketball head coach for Duke University and Team USA, shared her excitement after attending the November 16 episode of WWE Raw at the Lenovo Center in Raleigh, North Carolina. Lawson attended the event courtesy of Charlotte Flair and noted how much she enjoyed being seated right near the action.

Following Duke's 111–44 victory over the Fordham Rams on November 19, reporter Chris Lea asked Lawson about her night at Raw. She emphasized that she is completely serious about stepping into the ring for a match in the future.

> "I'm dead serious. If they have a draft or something, they need to draft me. I will come in and I will wreck some stuff in that ring. So they need to call me."

Lawson mentioned she is brainstorming a ring name and delivered a message to Triple H and Stephanie McMahon. She expressed her readiness and stated that she plans to attend any future WWE shows that come her way.

The show featured appearances from stars like Cody Rhodes, The Miz, John Cena, and Oba Femi, along with a tag team match involving The Usos.
