Natalya recently shared high praise for Rhea Ripley during an interview with The West Sport. Having shared the ring nearly 20 times since 2019, Natalya highlighted Ripley's incredible unselfishness and generosity as an in-ring performer. She noted that Ripley will return in the best shape of her life, feeling physically ready and mentally rejuvenated after being out of action since June. Although her official in-ring return date remains unconfirmed, Ripley is scheduled for the Wrestlepalooza Perth Pre-Show event.
