Elayna Black opened up about her time in WWE, revealing that she was told she would never become a champion due to her physical appearance. Speaking in a documentary on TNA Wrestling's YouTube channel, the former Cora Jade explained that despite putting in the work and following all instructions, she was passed over for opportunities. "I felt like I was doing everything right. I felt like I was taking all the advice. I felt like I was improving in every aspect that any coach was telling me to. I felt like I was doing it, but I wasn't getting the opportunities that other people were getting, and I kind of was watching myself being passed over, and I didn't understand why," she said. She added, "I was told that I was not and would not be champion in that company because I looked like I couldn't even crack an egg." Black noted that the constant scrutiny severely impacted her confidence after joining the company at just 19 years old. Now back in TNA Wrestling, she feels much more comfortable and confident in who she is both personally and professionally.
