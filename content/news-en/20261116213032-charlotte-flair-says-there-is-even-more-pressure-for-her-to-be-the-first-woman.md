Charlotte Flair recently discussed the possibility of becoming the first woman to win three Royal Rumble matches. Flair, who is chasing the world title record held by John Cena, already holds the distinction of being the only woman to win two Royal Rumbles. In a new interview with Outkick, Flair spoke about the pressure that comes with a potential third victory.

> "For me, I feel like because I have won two, there's even more pressure. First woman to win two, are the odds forever in my favor? Could I win three?"

Flair also noted that the Royal Rumble is unique because it features a mix of past, present, and future stars. She expressed excitement about seeing which NXT stars might make their debut during the match. Flair emphasized the importance of the event, as it provides a direct path to WrestleMania and a guaranteed opportunity to compete for a world championship.
