WWE NXT held its annual Halloween Havoc event on Saturday at the Performance Center, delivering a show that blended world title defenses with the feel of a go-home episode. Critics shared three things they hated and three things they loved about the night.

Among the highlights, the Weapons Wild Match between Tavion Heights and Myles Borne stood out as a brutal and intense encounter, culminating in Heights securing a victory. Zilla Fatu also captured a win over Charlie Dempsey in an Anything Goes match that featured interference from BirthRight. Additionally, the cinematic confrontation between Shiloh Hill and Naraku delivered a spooky and memorable visual experience.

On the negative side, the main event featuring NXT Champion Grayson Waller defending against Tony D'Angelo fell flat with a predictable and overbooked finish. Critics also noted that the entire event felt less like a major premium live event and more like an elongated episode of weekly television designed to build up the upcoming Wednesday show against AEW Dynamite, capped off by a surprise challenge from CM Punk.
