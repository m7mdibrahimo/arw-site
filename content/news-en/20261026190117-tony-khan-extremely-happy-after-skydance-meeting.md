AEW President Tony Khan met with executives from Skydance in Los Angeles on Friday. Reports indicate that Khan left the discussion in high spirits regarding the ongoing relationship following the recent company merger.

According to Dave Meltzer on Wrestling Observer Radio, Khan was noticeably pleased with the outcome of the discussions. Meltzer noted that Khan appeared happier than he has been since the partnership began, despite Skydance already making changes to other wrestling programming under its umbrella.

> "I don't know any of the details yet, but I do know that he came out of the meetings extremely happy. Much happier than he's been about this relationship — he's always been happy about this relationship, he's always been beaming about it since Day One — but he definitely came out of these meetings the happiest he's been."

While Skydance canceled a Roku channel airing Women of Wrestling and NWA content, no cuts or negative changes were reported for AEW following the meeting.
