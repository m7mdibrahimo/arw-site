Orange Cassidy recently shared some advice for the participants in this year's AEW Blood And Guts match, which is scheduled for the upcoming episode of AEW Dynamite. Last year, Cassidy competed in his first ever AEW Blood And Guts match as part of Darby Allin's team against The Death Riders inside the double steel cage structure. During an interview with STL Sports Central, Cassidy was asked what advice he would offer to the other competitors in the bout.

> "You better hope that the people… that are in there with you on your team, you can trust them and they're good because you rely heavily, heavily, heavily on your team. So you better have good teammates."

Reflecting on his reluctance to join the match last year, Cassidy added that he never wanted to participate until Mark Briscoe pushed him to do it. He noted that even though he preferred not to be involved, he fought because his friends and teammates wanted him to. Elsewhere in the interview, Cassidy also discussed the possibility of competing in this year's Continental Classic.
