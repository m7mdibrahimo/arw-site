Trey Miguel believes his upcoming match against Leon Slater offers a stronger narrative than what fans currently anticipate. Miguel is scheduled to challenge Slater for the TNA World Championship at the TNA Destination X special on November 15.

During an appearance on Busted Open, Miguel addressed the expectations surrounding the bout. While he acknowledged that many fans anticipate a high-flying spectacle, he emphasized his desire to showcase a more complete wrestling performance.

> "I read the internet; I know what people are expecting this match to be. They want a 15, 20-minute flip-off, and I think Leon (Slater) and I have such a better story to tell than that."

Miguel noted that he rejects the stigma of being labeled solely as a high-flyer. He views this title match as an opportunity to demonstrate his growth and reach a new level within TNA. Meanwhile, Slater recently teamed with Amazing Red, with that match airing on November 5.
