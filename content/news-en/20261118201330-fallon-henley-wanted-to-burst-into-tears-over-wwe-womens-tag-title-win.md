During an appearance on "The Collection with Brad Gilmore," Fallon Henley opened up about winning the WWE Women's Tag Team Championships alongside Fatal Influence stablemate Lainey Reid in July, when they defeated Paige and Brie Bella.

"I may or may not have wanted to just burst into tears," Henley said. She explained that watching WWE's "Total Divas" growing up inspired her journey, making it surreal to defeat childhood idols like Paige and Brie and Nikki Bella. She also noted that capturing the titles at Madison Square Garden made the moment even more special, describing the experience of walking into the locker room with the women she idolized as realizing that she finally made it.
