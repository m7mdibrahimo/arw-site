William Regal believes professional wrestling is suffering from a credibility crisis caused by performers who fail to make their matches look realistic. On October 16, 2026, the veteran wrestler, who has 42 years of experience, shared a message for talent across the industry, noting that while he works for WWE, his observations apply to all companies. Regal specifically criticized the way wrestlers handle pin attempts and kickouts, arguing that these moments should look like a desperate fight for survival.

> "We are portraying trained pro fighters with instincts (even comedy bouts). If you don’t win you would make less money in the story we’re telling. Every TV bout, if there’s a pin, the cameras focus in. Please pin and kick out like your life and career depend on it. In story, it does."

Regal cited a match between AJ Styles and Amazing Red from the early 2000s as a gold standard, praising their aggressive pin attempts and realistic kickouts. He warned that the industry has lost something over the last two decades, urging performers not to insult the audience's intelligence by neglecting these details. Drawing a comparison to film fight scenes, Regal emphasized that while fans know wrestling is entertainment, they will stop watching if the action stops feeling convincing. He concluded by challenging any wrestler who is unwilling to put in the effort to make these moments look legitimate.
