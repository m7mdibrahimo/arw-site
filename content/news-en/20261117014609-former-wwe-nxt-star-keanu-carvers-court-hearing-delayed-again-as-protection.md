Former WWE NXT star Keanu Carver, whose real name is Kevin Robertson, will not be returning to court in the near future. His ongoing Florida court case, which involves allegations of stalking and domestic violence filed by fellow NXT star Arianna Grace, has been granted another continuance. On November 13, 2026, Robertson’s attorneys requested a delay in Seminole County, Florida. The court accepted the request without opposition from Grace’s legal team, rescheduling the next hearing for February 16, 2027. The existing protection order remains active, meaning Robertson is prohibited from contacting Grace or coming within 500 feet of her. This case, which began in September 2026, stems from a 49-page petition filed by Grace, whose real name is Bianca Carelli, following the end of their relationship. The petition details allegations of stalking, harassment, and physical abuse, including claims that Robertson threatened to kill her and her pets. While Robertson was released from WWE in October 2026 and his social media accounts were deactivated, he has not faced criminal charges. The matter remains civil, and the latest delay means the legal proceedings will continue into 2027.
