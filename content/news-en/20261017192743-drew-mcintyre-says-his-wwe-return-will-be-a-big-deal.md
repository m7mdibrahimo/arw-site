Drew McIntyre recently discussed his absence from WWE and what his eventual return will mean. McIntyre has been off television since his unsanctioned match against Jacob Fatu at WrestleMania 42, as he has been busy filming Highlander and The Last Druid.

Speaking on Going Ringside, McIntyre was asked about a potential return during the upcoming Road to Rumble European Tour. He responded, "Yeah, I'll be back eventually. WWE is my home, but at the same time, Hollywood Drew is busy right now. I’m focused on Hollywood Drew. So when I get back eventually, it's going to be a big deal."

He added, "I've never been out for six months. Since I was 15, I started wrestling professionally. I've never had six months off in this entire time. So when I come back, I think it's going to be a big deal for the fans. It's going to be a big deal for me, but it's not right now."
