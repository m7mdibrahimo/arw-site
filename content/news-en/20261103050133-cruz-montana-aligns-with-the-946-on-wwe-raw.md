Cruz Montana has joined forces with The 946 on this week's episode of WWE Raw. The show concluded with a singles match between Solo Sikoa and Royce Keys that ended in a no contest when The 946 interfered and attacked Sikoa.

Sikoa brought out The Bloodline for support, leading to a massive brawl that drew a huge reaction from the audience. The sides remained even until a masked man entered the ring and struck Roman Reigns in the face with a steel chair. Cruz Montana then unmasked, and The 946 proceeded to dominate The Bloodline.

The segment came to a close when Sami Zayn ran in and laid out Roman Reigns with a Helluva Kick.
