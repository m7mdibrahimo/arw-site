WWE Superstar Drew McIntyre is aiming to reclaim the world championship once he returns to the ring. In a recent appearance on the "Joe Vulpis Podcast," McIntyre shared that he has discussed potential on-screen ideas with Academy Award winning actor Russell Crowe. The two worked together on films like "The Last Druid" and "Highlander."

> "Russell was legitimately talking about...he wanted to do something a couple of times. Oh, he got all excited about us doing something together in the same team, and talked about WrestleMania, or whatever. He talked about us having a manager, like an assistant, kind of like you. It was more of a heel thing he was coming up with. He had some great ideas. He's willing to do it."

McIntyre noted that any such involvement would first require approval from WWE management. The Scottish star has been away from the ring for nearly 200 days, with his last appearance being a loss to Jacob Fatu in an Unsanctioned Match at WrestleMania 42. Since then, McIntyre has focused on his film career, taking roles in projects such as "Hexed" and the micro-drama "The Agent of Shadows."
