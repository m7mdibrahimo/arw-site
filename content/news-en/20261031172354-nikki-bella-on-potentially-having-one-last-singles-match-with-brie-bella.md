Nikki Bella recently discussed the possibility of facing off with her twin sister Brie Bella one more time before they both retire from wrestling. Throughout their careers, the pair have been tied together as tag team partners and as opponents in past feuds.

While speaking to Justin Dorazio in an interview, Nikki noted that injuries and the end of their in-ring careers make another singles match unlikely. She compared her chemistry with Brie to her past matches with Paige.

> "I mean, now, like, after our injuries, I don't think so. I just don't think… We're so, like, at the end of our in ring careers that I feel like it just has to be together at this point. I would have loved that though before, because I really loved that match with Brie. It's a lot like when I have my matches with Paige, there's certain people you work with and you have great chemistry with and you give a lot to each other, and I remember that match. We just are timing, like, you know, you just have chemistry and I would have loved to have one more match, especially how I am now. I would love for Brie to, like, if it was still Paige and I in a story, for her to come in and call me out and be like, what? But, like, by the time she heals and comes back, I don't think it'll happen, unfortunately."

This follows an appearance on WWE SmackDown where Nikki challenged Chelsea Green for the WWE Women's Championship.
