Chad Gable revealed that he and CM Punk have formed an unexpected texting friendship centered around fitness and workout equipment. According to Gable, the two have become total gym nerds behind the scenes, trading messages about training angles, workout equipment, and nutrition. Gable explained that they frequently discuss their daily carb intake and compare notes on meals while backstage at WWE events.

Gable mentioned that working with fitness coach Jay Ferruggia changed his own training approach, leading him to use less volume and rely more on machines. He noted that Punk is also working with Ferruggia and has become fully committed to his training regimen. Gable admitted that their detailed conversations about range of motion, strength curves, and healthy treats might bore outsiders, but the shared dedication provides mutual motivation.
