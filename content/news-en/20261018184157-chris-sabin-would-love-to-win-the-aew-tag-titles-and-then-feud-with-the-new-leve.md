Chris Sabin hopes that the Motor City Machine Guns can capture the AEW World Tag Team Championship and then engage in a six-month feud with The New Level. During a recent interview, Sabin discussed his desire to work with the team, noting their history together dating back to their days in TNA. He praised their entertaining style and mentioned that they rank right alongside The Young Bucks on the duo's list of dream opponents in AEW.
