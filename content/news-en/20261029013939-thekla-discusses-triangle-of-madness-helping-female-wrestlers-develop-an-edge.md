Thekla is highlighting the impact of the Triangle Of Madness stable. Since forming in July 2025, Thekla, Julia Hart, and Skye Blue have continued to raise their profiles within AEW. Thekla has emerged as the most successful member of the group, even winning the AEW Women's World Championship earlier this year. In a new interview with Playboy, Thekla was asked if her group has helped empower female wrestlers to be more aggressive or violent. Thekla said, "I do think that. Women have, for a very long time, tried to make up for a lack of technique and stamina in the ring. We just have to get a little bit more insane. Just come up with crazy shit that we can do to humiliate each other, use psychological warfare and weapons, whether it’s a crazy happy Skye Blue covered in blood or if it’s Julia Hart misting left and right, or if it’s me getting out my belt and starting to punish the referee."
