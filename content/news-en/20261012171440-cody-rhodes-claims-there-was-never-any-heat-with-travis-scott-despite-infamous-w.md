Cody Rhodes recently addressed his infamous physical altercation with Travis Scott during their WWE storyline, claiming there was never any real heat between them. Speaking on Complex’s Sneaker Shopping, Rhodes recalled the rapper's powerful open-hand slap during the 2025 Elimination Chamber angle involving The Rock and John Cena, which he previously noted left him with a busted eardrum. Despite the severe strike and a temporary ban on Scott's sneakers in his household, Rhodes stated that he loves Travis Scott and would welcome him back to WWE. Scott later interfered in Rhodes’ WrestleMania 41 match against John Cena before taking a CrossRhodes, but Cody insists everything remains on good terms.
