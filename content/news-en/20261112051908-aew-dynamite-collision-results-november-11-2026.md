AEW Dynamite and Collision aired live on November 11, 2026, from the Maverik Center in West Valley City, Utah. The broadcast featured several high-stakes matches and segments leading into AEW Full Gear.

The show began with a recap of last week’s Blood & Guts. Backstage, Will Ospreay, The Young Bucks, Steven Borden, and Adam Copeland discussed their loss. Ospreay questioned Copeland for surrendering, but Copeland defended his decision, citing his past neck injury and retirement. He warned Ospreay against risking his health, before walking away.

Kyle Fletcher, accompanied by Don Callis, confronted Will Ospreay in the ring. Fletcher addressed their history, acknowledging Ospreay's success since his neck surgery. Ospreay countered by highlighting his achievements, including beating Kenny Omega for the AEW World Title. The tension escalated until Fletcher slapped Ospreay, who remained composed before leaving the ring.

The New Level (Austin Creed and Kofi) faced Brodido (Brody King and Bandido) in a tag team match. The bout ended in a double count-out after all four men brawled to the back and through a table. Security intervened as the chaos continued.

Orange Cassidy defeated David Finlay in a singles match after Finlay used his shillelagh to secure the win while the referee was distracted. Andrade El Idolo later addressed his recent title loss, vowing to become the first Latino AEW World Champion.

In a non-title match, CMLL World Women’s Champion Persephone defeated Gypsy Mac. Persephone remained focused on retrieving her TBS Championship from Queen Aminata.

Motor City Machine Guns (Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin) defeated FTR (Cash Wheeler and Dax Harwood) in the fifth match of their best-of-five series. The Guns secured the win and will challenge for the AEW World Tag Team Championship after Full Gear.

Tommaso Ciampa successfully defended his AEW National Championship against Mike Bailey in a hard-hitting contest. After the match, Ilja Dragunov confronted Ciampa, signaling their upcoming title match at Full Gear.

In the main event, The Death Riders (Jon Moxley and Marina Shafir) defeated Mercedes Moné and Jay White in a mixed tag team match. Shafir forced Moné to tap out to the Brutalizer. Post-match, a massive brawl broke out involving The Cut, The Dogs, Orange Cassidy, and others, ending with Moné attacking Shafir with her title.

The Elite (Matt Jackson, Nick Jackson, and Jack Perry) faced the Don Callis Family (El Clon, Brian Cage, and Kevin Knight) in a trios match. The match saw intense action, culminating in Kevin Knight hitting a UFO Splash, though the pin was broken up by The Young Bucks.
