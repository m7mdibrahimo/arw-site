Taya Valkyrie has officially entered free agency after deciding not to accept a contract extension offer from AEW. According to Valkyrie's team, she was given the opportunity to stay with the company after her previous contract expired, but chose to move on instead. Valkyrie took to social media to announce her departure, expressing her gratitude for her time with the promotion. "I am incredibly grateful for my time with AEW and the opportunity to be part of the organization. While there, I wrestled and worked alongside exceptional talent, and built lasting relationships within the AEW family and deepend my relationships in the wrestling community," she wrote. Valkyrie made it clear that she is not slowing down and is already looking ahead to the next chapter of her career. "I am beyond thankful to the fans for their continued support of me, not only in my time with AEW but also as I step into this new chapter as a free agent. I can't wait for what's to come and have a lot more to leave in the ring," she added. Now operating as a free agent, Valkyrie plans to explore various opportunities beyond just professional wrestling, noting her interest in television personality work and creative roles, following recent ventures like appearing on "The Traitors Canada" and releasing her debut EP "FAKE".
