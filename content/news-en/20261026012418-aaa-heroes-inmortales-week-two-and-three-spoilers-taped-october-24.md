Lucha Libre AAA taped the second and third weeks of AAA Heroes Inmortales on October 24. According to False Finish, the following matches took place.

Bravo Americano def. Takuma and Cruz del Toro.

AAA World Cruiserweight Championship Open Challenge: Rey Fénix def. Laredo Kid, Jack Cartwheel, and Kento to retain the title.

AAA World Tag Team Championship: The Wagner Brothers def. Taurus and Hysteria to retain the titles.

Pagano and El Carnicero def. Dave The Clown and Murder Clown. Following the match, Pagano and El Carnicero attacked the Psycho Circus, but Psycho Clown made the save.

Abismo Negro Jr. vs. El Fiscal ended in a no contest.

Additionally, Dorian Roldán interrupted the Hall of Fame induction ceremony for Marisela Peña. Roldán accused AAA General Manager Rey Mysterio of maintaining a secret relationship with Peña, claiming this is why she does not punish him for his actions.
