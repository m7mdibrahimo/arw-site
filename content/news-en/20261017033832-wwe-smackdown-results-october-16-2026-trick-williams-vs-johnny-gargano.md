The October 16, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown emanated from the Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, California. The show featured a non-title match between United States Champion Trick Williams and Johnny Gargano, as well as a singles match between Paige and Nikki Bella.

Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn opened the show, addressing his recent challengers and setting his sights on Roman Reigns for next week's WWE RAW. Trick Williams interrupted, leading to a confrontation that resulted in a match against Johnny Gargano. Williams defeated Gargano with a Book End. In the women's division, Paige defeated Nikki Bella after using the ropes for leverage. Additionally, Damian Priest defeated R-Truth via referee stoppage following an injury to Truth during the match.

Nick Aldis confirmed a Street Fight between Giulia and Blake Monroe for next week, and a future match between Nikki Bella and Chelsea Green. Kevin Owens and CM Punk also had a tense backstage segment, with Aldis urging them to stay apart until they are both healthy.
