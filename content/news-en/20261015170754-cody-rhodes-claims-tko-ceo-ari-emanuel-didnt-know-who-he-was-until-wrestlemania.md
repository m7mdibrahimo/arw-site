During a recent media tour for the upcoming Street Fighter movie, WWE star Cody Rhodes revealed that TKO CEO Ari Emanuel did not know who he was until WrestleMania 40 in 2024. Rhodes headlined both nights of the event, after which he signed with WME, the talent agency where Emanuel serves as Executive Chairman. Rhodes noted that the agency helped set up a meeting that led to his casting in the film. While a TKO spokeswoman declined to comment, Rhodes expressed his appreciation for the backing of Emanuel and other executives as he expands his career outside the ring.
