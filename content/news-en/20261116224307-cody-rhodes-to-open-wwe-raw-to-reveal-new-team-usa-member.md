According to Viper Reports, Cody Rhodes is scheduled to open tonight's episode of WWE Raw from the Lenovo Center in Raleigh, North Carolina, to announce the next member of Team USA. The segment leads up to the traditional five-on-five elimination match against Team Mexico at Survivor Series: WarGames on November 28. Rhodes captains Team USA alongside CM Punk and Je'Von Evans, while Rey Mysterio and Penta lead Team Mexico.

Tonight's show also features Raquel Rodriguez defending the Women's Intercontinental Championship against IYO SKY. Additionally, The Usos will face Carmelo Hayes and Cruz Montana in a match to determine the advantage for WarGames, and John Cena will announce more participants for the John Cena Classic.
