Bully Ray expressed great surprise after discovering that Cruz Montana was the masked individual and newest member of the 946 faction on WWE Raw. Montana, an NXT roster member since July, made a surprise appearance at the end of the show by attacking Roman Reigns and his associates. Ray stated on his radio show that nobody knew about the plan, explaining that the reaction from the crowd inside the Barclays Center reflected a genuine shock when the mask came off.

Ray shared his initial guesses while watching the segment, believing at first that it was LA Knight before noticing differences in body movement and size, and then briefly suspecting Angelo Dawkins before the reveal. The legend noted his questions regarding the reasons for Carmelo Hayes joining the group without a clear explanation from WWE yet, expressing his view that the former TNA star currently feels more important compared to the rest of the group.
