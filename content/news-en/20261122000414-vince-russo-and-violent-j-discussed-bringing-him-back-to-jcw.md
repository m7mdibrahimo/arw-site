Although Vince Russo is finished working full-time with JCW, the door may not be completely shut on a future return. Speaking on Two Man Power Trip with John Poz, Russo revealed that Violent J has already brought up the idea of him coming back for occasional appearances.

Russo stated that they still talk regularly and that there is no personal animosity between them. He praised Violent J as a great human being with a big heart, explaining that his issues were with the chaotic way the wrestling side of the company was run rather than with Violent J personally.

Russo left his position last month after experiencing heart palpitations from the intense stress of the job, deciding that wrestling was not worth risking his health over once he realized the operational style would not change.
