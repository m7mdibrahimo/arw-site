Jack Perry took some very dangerous bumps during the October 28 episode of AEW Dynamite, leaving fans concerned about his health after the show. Speaking on Wrestling Observer Live, Bryan Alvarez reported that Perry has been telling people he is feeling sore following his match against Kyle Fletcher. Perry took several brutal impacts involving his neck and back during the hard-hitting bout, most notably when Fletcher powerbombed him onto the exposed edge of the ring frame.

Despite the terrifying nature of the landing, Perry managed to finish the match, which ultimately ended when Fletcher hit a Brainbuster to secure the victory. Alvarez admitted he was shocked that Perry avoided a more severe injury after taking such a punishing blow directly into the ring structure. For now, the only update on Perry's condition is that he is dealing with severe soreness, with no indications of any serious long-term damage.
