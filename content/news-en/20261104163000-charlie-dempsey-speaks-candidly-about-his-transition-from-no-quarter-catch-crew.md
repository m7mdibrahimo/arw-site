Charlie Dempsey recently opened up about his transition from No Quarter Catch Crew to BirthRight in WWE NXT during a guest appearance on Busted Open. Reflecting on the early days of N.Q.C.C., which originally consisted of himself, Drew Gulak, and Bobby Steveson, Dempsey noted that the group truly found its footing once Tavion Heights, Myles Borne, and Wren Sinclair joined, creating a strong sense of cohesiveness.

"So, starting with No Quarter Catch Crew, it was something I was thrown into with Drew Gulak at the time," Dempsey said. "It was what it was. It really got off its feet I feel like when Tavion Heights, Myles Borne, Wren Sinclair all joined it, and I felt like we had a real nice cohesiveness to us."

Dempsey also discussed stepping out on his own and eventually leaning into his famous lineage. Although he initially wanted to forge his own path without relying on his father's name, he eventually realized it made sense to embrace it, joining other second-generation stars in WWE like Arianna Grace, Lexis King, and Uriah Connors. Most recently, Dempsey represented BirthRight at NXT Halloween Havoc, where he faced Zilla Fatu in a Falls Count Anywhere match and came up short despite interference from his stablemates.
