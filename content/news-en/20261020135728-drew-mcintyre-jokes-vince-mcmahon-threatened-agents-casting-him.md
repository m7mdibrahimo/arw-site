Drew McIntyre recently discussed how his Hollywood opportunities have expanded under the current WWE leadership of Nick Khan and Triple H compared to the Vince McMahon era. McIntyre explained that McMahon was fiercely protective of top talent for live events, jokingly noting that McMahon even threatened agents who tried to secure him for outside projects. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, McIntyre acknowledged that while live events previously required constant top-star presence, the new management is much more open to mutually beneficial outside projects. McIntyre has been off WWE television since WrestleMania 42, utilizing his time filming in Spain to maintain his in-ring training.
