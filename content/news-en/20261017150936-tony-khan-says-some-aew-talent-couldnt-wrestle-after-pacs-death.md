AEW President Tony Khan revealed that several wrestlers were too emotionally shaken to compete following the sudden death of PAC, also known as Benjamin Satterley. Speaking on WPRK 91.5 FM, Khan explained that he made the recent Ring of Honor TV taping optional for the talent. "Well, it was voluntary and there were some people that definitely were not feeling well and understandably could not wrestle that day," Khan said. He had to completely rework his original card to accommodate those who stayed home.

Despite the difficult circumstances, many wrestlers chose to perform, leading to an emotional night in Jacksonville. Bandido delivered one of the standout moments by placing a PAC armband on the ROH World Championship after successfully defending it against AR Fox. Khan praised the tribute and acknowledged how heavy the atmosphere has been for the roster, especially following the recent losses of both PAC and Andy Williams.
