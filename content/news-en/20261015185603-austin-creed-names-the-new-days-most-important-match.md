Austin Creed recently reflected on his past career and future goals during an interview with Cameron Hawkins for Uncrowned, coming nearly a month after he and Kofi Kingston debuted in AEW as The New Level. Alongside Swerve Strickland, the trio currently holds the AEW World Trios Championship. Reflecting on their time in The New Day alongside Big E, Creed believes their most significant team match took place while paving the way to WrestleMania 35, when they secured Kofi Kingston's opportunity at the WWE Championship.

> "I think with my definition (of important)," he says, "the most important match that we've had as a team was when me and (Big) E won that match to get Kofi a title shot."

Discussing their current run in AEW, Creed explained that jumping straight into the trios division allowed them to immediately face multiple formidable tag teams. Creed, Kingston, and Strickland are accompanied by Prince Nana as they hold the championship. On October 17th at AEW WrestleDream, the trio will defend the AEW World Trios Championship against Hangman Adam Page, Bandido, and Brody King in a two-out-of-three falls match.
