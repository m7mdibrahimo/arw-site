Following his match against Roman Reigns for the Men's Crown Jewel Title at WWE Crown Jewel, Sami Zayn reflected on the experience of facing the man he considers the industry's biggest star of the last two decades. While the match ended in a victory for Reigns, Zayn noted that he was determined to earn the respect of "The Original Tribal Chief" during their encounter.

Speaking on the post-show stream, Zayn explained that he had previously done and said things to provoke Reigns because he needed to face him at his absolute best. "I might have said some stuff. I might have did some stuff to Roman because I needed the OTC. I didn't want a half measure. I wanted Roman at his best," Zayn stated. Although he ultimately wanted to win the match, Zayn emphasized that he achieved his true goal by earning the respect of his opponent. "I got the respect that I deserve from that man. If that man, the biggest star in this industry over the last 20 years, can show me that respect, I expect it across the board for the last real good guy going forward."
