AEW is set to debut a new match concept called Clockwork Carnage at the upcoming AEW WrestleDream 2026 event. The match, created by Will Ospreay, features eight participants entering the ring every three minutes. Any wrestler pinned or submitted during this first stage is immediately eliminated from the contest. Once the final participant enters, a 15-minute second stage begins, with the winner earning the Men's World Championship by scoring the most falls.

Will Ospreay and Samoa Joe have already secured their spots in the match. Additionally, Speedball Mike Bailey is scheduled to face Lee Moriarty on AEW Collision to determine another qualifier.

According to Wrestling Inc, Dave Meltzer analyzed the remaining five spots in the main event. He listed several logical candidates for the match, though he noted that RUSH recently stated on social media that he does not expect to compete in the near future.

> "Given who is already booked, if we're going to look at the roster for the other five spots in the main event, those who can logically be put in would be Adam Copeland, Darby Allin, Claudio Castagnoli, Nick Wayne, Gabe Kidd, Josh Alexander, Konosuke Takeshita, Orange Cassidy, Ricochet, Rush, and Wardlow."

AEW WrestleDream takes place on October 17 at the Addition Arena in Orlando, Florida.
