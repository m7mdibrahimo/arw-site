Persephone and Queen Aminata have been added to the Women's Blood and Guts match at AEW Blood and Guts 2026, which takes place on November 4. During AEW Fright Night Collision, Persephone announced that one spot remained in the match and called out Queen Aminata, leading Tony Khan to make the bout official. Persephone will team with Triangle of Madness and Divine Dominion, while Queen Aminata joins forces with The Brawling Birds, Wrathlander, and Willow Nightingale. The updated card for the November 4 event features the Men's Blood and Guts match between Adam Copeland, Steven Borden, Darby Allin, the Young Bucks, and Will Ospreay against The Don Callis Family, alongside the Women's Blood and Guts showdown.
