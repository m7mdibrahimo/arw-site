Drew McIntyre recently opened up about how addressing hidden foot and balance issues has made him feel stronger and more stable in the ring than he has in over a decade. Speaking on the What’s Your Story? podcast with Stephanie McMahon, McIntyre explained that working with a new trainer over the past year has completely transformed his approach to physical preparation and joint health.

McIntyre revealed that he previously struggled with balance issues in both his everyday life and inside the wrestling ring due to neglecting the foundation of his body. By incorporating specific routines such as rolling out his feet, doing toe raises, and calf raises, he has managed to build a much sturdier base. Now weighing a career-high 285 pounds, he feels physically better than he has in roughly 12 years and is even confident enough to bring back high-impact moves he had previously abandoned.
