Ricochet has explained that his current heel persona in AEW draws inspiration from DC Comics characters like the Joker, Lex Luthor, and the Reverse-Flash. Speaking on The Takedown On SI, Ricochet noted that he connects with the Joker because he simply wants to cause chaos. He also credited strategic masterminds like Lex Luthor and Reverse-Flash as influences for his intellect and planning. Ricochet has been off TV since losing to Will Ospreay in the first-ever Clockwork Carnage match for the AEW Men's World Championship at WrestleDream.
