John Cena recently discussed his relationship with Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson and explained why he would never pressure him to collaborate on a Hollywood movie project. Speaking on the Happy Sad Confused podcast with Josh Horowitz, Cena noted that Johnson has a very busy schedule and that he prefers not to hand him a pitch deck when they spend time together. However, Cena made it clear that he would be completely open to starring alongside Johnson in a film if movie executives and directors decide they would be a great fit together, either as allies or rivals. Reflecting on their shared history, Cena also recalled their memorable on-screen reunion at last year's Elimination Chamber, where Cena turned heel for the first time in his career.
