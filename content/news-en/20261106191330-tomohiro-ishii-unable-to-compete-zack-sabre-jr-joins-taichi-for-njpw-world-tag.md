Dangerous Tekkers are reuniting for the 2026 NJPW World Tag League, which begins on November 23. While Taichi's partner in the B block was previously unannounced, Zack Sabre Jr. confirmed he is stepping in to reform the team.

Following the Dradition Pro-Wrestling event on November 6, Sabre Jr. explained that Tomohiro Ishii cannot participate in the tournament. He noted:

> "Tomohiro Ishii is unable to compete in the tournament, so he's filling in."

Sabre Jr. and Taichi are three-time IWGP Tag Team Champions. Ishii has been inactive since February 2026 due to a neck injury.
