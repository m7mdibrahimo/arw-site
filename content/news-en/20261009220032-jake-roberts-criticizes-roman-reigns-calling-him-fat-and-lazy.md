WWE Hall of Famer Jake Roberts recently shared a blunt assessment of Roman Reigns. Speaking on Inside the Ropes, Roberts expressed his dissatisfaction with Reigns' current performance, suggesting the star has become complacent.

> "I just don't like Roman with what he does out there. I think that he has better stuff in him yet, but I think that maybe he got a little fat, got a little lazy. You know, sometimes when you're given so much, you quit producing."

Roberts also pointed to WWE's creative process as a potential factor in the decline. He argued that modern wrestlers are too dependent on scripts compared to past generations.

> "And I feel like then either that or he just needed better writers. Maybe that was it. You know, I forget that today that you don't think about your—they write everything for you."

Reflecting on these changes, Roberts stated he would struggle to perform in the modern WWE environment because he values the freedom to write his own promos. He emphasized that no writer could capture his character as effectively as he could himself.
