Kendal Grey continues her absence from WWE rings due to injury, but she made sure to catch her fans' attention through a new post on Instagram.

Grey published a new beach photo in a bikini, confirming she remains away from the action and wrote in her caption:

> "Still out of action, but not out of your head."

Grey has been away from competition since suffering an injury at NXT Heatwave and has not specified an official return date yet, while maintaining ongoing engagement with her fans throughout her recovery.
