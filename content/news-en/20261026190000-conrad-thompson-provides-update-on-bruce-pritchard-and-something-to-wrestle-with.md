Conrad Thompson has provided an update on the status of Something To Wrestle With. In recent years, Bruce Pritchard has maintained a part-time schedule for the podcast, which launched in 2016, with John Bradshaw Layfield stepping in during his absences. Speaking in a new interview with Sean Ross Sapp, Conrad Thompson explained that Pritchard views the podcast as a dependable backup plan should any issues arise with his career in WWE.

> He knows that too. We did very, very well. I'm sure if and when the time is right and he is no longer living in Stamford, then then we'll be able to figure something out. But I think that's going to be the foreseeable future for a little while.

Elsewhere in the interview, Conrad discussed the possibility of collaborating with Joey Styles on a future podcast project.
