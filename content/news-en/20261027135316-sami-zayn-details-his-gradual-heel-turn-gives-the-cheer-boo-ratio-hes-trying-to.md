Sami Zayn spoke about his gradual transition to a heel persona during an appearance on Chris Van Vliet's Insight podcast. Zayn explained that the process started after he lost to Drew McIntyre at WWE Royal Rumble, followed by winning the WWE United States Title from Carmelo Hayes and his feud with Trick Williams. Zayn noted that he wanted the shift to feel organic rather than sudden, aiming for a specific crowd reaction balance between cheers and boos.
