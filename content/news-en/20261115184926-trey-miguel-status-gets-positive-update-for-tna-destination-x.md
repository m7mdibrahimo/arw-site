TNA Wrestling may not need to alter the Destination X main event after all. Following a Saturday where it seemed highly unlikely he would reach Edmonton, Trey Miguel experienced a positive turn in his travel circumstances, reviving hope for his scheduled TNA World Championship match against Leon Slater.

The doubts arose after a nationwide flight shutdown on November 13 stranded various TNA wrestlers in airports across the United States. According to PWInsider, TNA talent believed on Saturday that a miracle would be necessary for Miguel to arrive in Edmonton on time for the Sunday pay-per-view. However, Bryan Alvarez noted on Wrestling Observer Radio that the situation improved progressively as Saturday advanced, shifting away from an almost certain absence.

This represents a significant shift from previous expectations that had TNA preparing for potential replacements or other modifications to the TNA World Championship bout. Although his arrival or transportation has not been definitively confirmed, the current indicators show the match is in a much better position than initially anticipated.
