Syuri and Asuka have reunited. The two wrestlers have faced each other in the past across various promotions when Asuka performed under the ring name Kana. Their most recent encounter took place at Kana Pro Mania: Pulse, where they teamed with Hikaru Shida against the trio of Arisa Nakajima, Rina Yamashita, and Ryo Mizunami. Syuri shared the news on social media, stating:

> "I met up with Kana for dinner for the first time in a while 🍚 I was so happy we got to talk so much ♡♡ Thank you for the meal ✨✨ I’m looking forward to seeing you again 🫶 Thank you so much 🥺"

Asuka has also recently reunited with KAIRI.
