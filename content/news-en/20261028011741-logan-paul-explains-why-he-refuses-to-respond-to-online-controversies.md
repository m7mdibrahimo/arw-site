Logan Paul has spent about a decade dealing with internet drama and has learned how to handle it. Speaking on the Impaulsive podcast, he explained that the average news cycle online lasts only about 36 hours before people move on to the next topic. Paul stated that responding only resets the clock and keeps the conversation going, which is why he prefers to put his phone down and stay silent. However, he draws a line when someone makes false and potentially damaging allegations against him or his businesses rather than simply sharing an opinion. In those situations, he is fully willing to take legal action, noting that he has spent significant amounts on lawyers and is even currently drawing up a new lawsuit.
