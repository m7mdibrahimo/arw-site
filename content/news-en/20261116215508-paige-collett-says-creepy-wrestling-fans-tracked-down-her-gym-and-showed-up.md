JCW star Paige Collett recently opened up about the unsettling behavior she has experienced from some wrestling fans who have crossed the line into her personal life. Speaking on the Headlocks, Heels, & Havoc: Wrestling Unscripted podcast, Collett revealed that fans have gone as far as tracking down her gym and workplace to harass her. Collett explained that the situation became so serious that she had to stop checking into locations publicly. "Craziest fan experience is when people show up to like my gym just because they find out what gym I go to and that happened in Cincinnati when I was living in Ohio," she said. The harassment escalated when she worked a regular job outside of wrestling. According to Collett, fans discovered her place of employment and began showing up uninvited, with some individuals arriving while intoxicated. "When I had a shoot job, like my brother, people would find out where my shoot job was and they would come to my shoot job and harass me. And there was men that once showed up drunk and showed up at my shoot job and that was just very vulnerable and really weird," she added. Collett also shared that men have tracked down her personal social media accounts after seeing her on dating apps, often sending messages to her professional pages when she did not match with them. She emphasized that she appreciates her supporters, but she draws a clear distinction between meeting fans at scheduled appearances and having strangers invade her privacy. "I just it felt like so violating is when PE fans enter my personal life cuz it's like I love you guys. You guys invented me. You guys created you guys got behind my story. And so I love the fans," she stated. Collett is not asking fans to stop supporting her at wrestling events, but she wants people to understand that following a wrestler does not give them the right to follow them everywhere.
