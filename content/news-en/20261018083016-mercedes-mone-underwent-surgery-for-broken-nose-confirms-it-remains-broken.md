AEW Women's World Champion Mercedes Mone shared a significant medical update following her successful title defense at AEW WrestleDream on October 18. Mone confirmed during the post-show media scrum that she had undergone surgery for a broken nose suffered when she won the championship from Willow Nightingale at AEW All In 2026. Despite missing several weeks of action before returning in late September, the champion stated that her nose is still not fully healed.

> "It was definitely one of the hardest-fought battles I’ve ever had and one of my greatest matches and greatest moments in my whole career. I love, love that moment at Wembley. Just a full circle moment for me as well. But yeah, I’m one month and a half post-surgery on my nose. It’s still very much broken, and I’m pushing through every single week but I love what I do, I love defending my Women’s Championship, and I’m here to be the best, and just having a broken nose, it’s not gonna faze me; it’s not gonna stop me, it’s not gonna stop me from defending this, and this is my destiny so I’m ready to give everything that I have for it."

Mone has now completed two successful defenses of the title since capturing the gold. AEW now turns its attention to its next pay-per-view, AEW Full Gear, scheduled for November 14.
