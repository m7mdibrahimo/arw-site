Beyond Wrestling faces the potential end of its Wrestling Open Rhode Island weekly brand by December unless fan attendance improves or sponsor revenue increases, according to Fightful.

Officials stated in a release that their venue contract at Rhodes On The Pawtuxet runs through the end of 2026, setting December 28, 2026, as the final weekly show if metrics do not rise.
