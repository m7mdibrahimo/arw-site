Roman Reigns discussed his mindset in the world of wrestling during a recent interview with Sam Roberts. While Reigns enjoyed his time filming the upcoming Street Fighter movie alongside a cast of actors, he noted that the collaborative environment in Hollywood differs significantly from the locker room in wrestling.

> "There's a great camaraderie in wrestling. I mean, being one of the boys, I can't even explain it because I'm in the small fraternity, I'm in that 1%. Honestly, that should just be for us. But, the difference is I wasn't running around thinking I'm Noah [Centineo], you know what I mean? There’s plenty of dudes in the locker room that are running around thinking they're Roman Reigns, thinking they're of my level. It's crazy. It's just ridiculous."

Reigns emphasized that he views wrestling as his own craft, distinct from the team-oriented background he had in football. He explained that his shift toward taking complete ownership of his career occurred before the COVID-19 pandemic, noting that he was unhappy with his creative direction at that time.

> "Look, I come from football, so I'm always going to look at a teamwork aspect but wrestling is different. That's my craft, I own that shit. That is mine, you know what I mean? Like mine. I don't give a shit about any of them, it’s mine."
