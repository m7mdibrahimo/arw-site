Chavo Guerrero believes that professional wrestlers crossing over into Hollywood can only benefit the wrestling industry. In an interview with Fightful's Sean Ross Sapp, Guerrero reflected on the historic stigma surrounding wrestlers entering the film industry, referencing Hulk Hogan's departure from Vince McMahon Sr. after taking a role in Rocky III in 1981.

> "Wrestling back in the day did not want the wrestlers to make it somewhere else. We all the know the story of Hulk Hogan leaving Vince McMahon Sr. because he got that Rocky III offer. Vince Sr. was like, 'Are you a wrestler or are you an actor?' That's when Hulk Hogan left and went back to the AWA to do the show. Vince Jr. brought him back, he brought him back and was the biggest star ever."

Guerrero explained that the success of stars like John Cena and The Rock outside the ring helps elevate the sport when they return.

> "Us as wrestlers, we love wrestling. We really do. So, for us to venture somewhere else and make popularity and come back to wrestling, it's only better for wrestling and our brand. Look at a John Cena, look how much bigger John Cena is than when he was just wrestling. Look at The Rock, look how much bigger he is or Hulk Hogan, they keep coming back to wrestling and making wrestling bigger with these mainstream people. The Rock, number one movie star in the world, coming back to wrestling. It can only help wrestling and it can only help the wrestlers. That's my opinion 100%, I've seen it time and time again."
