Wrestling fans worldwide continue to honor and remember Eddie Guerrero, particularly as the anniversary of his passing approaches. However, according to Ringside News, seeing those memories everywhere carries an entirely different weight for his daughter, Kaylie Marie Guerrero.

Kaylie shared an emotional message on X asking supporters to consider her feelings and refrain from tagging her in posts featuring photos and memories of her father. She noted that people generally have good intentions, but emphasized that Eddie was her dad rather than simply a larger-than-life character remembered by millions.

> "I know most of you mean well by sharing memories of my dad. Most people talk about him, like he’s just some character. To me, though, he was simply my dad, and I miss him so much. Being tagged in photos of him right now is really difficult for me."

Eddie passed away on November 13, 2005, at the age of 38 following heart failure in his hotel room. More than two decades later, supporters consistently share photos and matches of Eddie every November, which constantly places Kaylie face-to-face with reminders of her loss. She highlighted that the stretch between November 13 and November 18 is the hardest time for her to be on the internet, asking everyone to understand her emotions and avoid tagging her name in those posts.

> "Please be mindful of that. The days around November 13–18 are particularly hard for me to be online. 🙏🏼💔."
