Ric Flair expressed his great happiness following a significant legal victory alongside boxing legend Mike Tyson in a $50 million lawsuit involving a cannabis company. On Tuesday, November 10, 2026, a federal judge in Illinois refused to dismiss the lawsuit filed against five individuals connected to Carma and Tyson 2.0, and Flair celebrated on X.

> "The Legal System Does Work! Thank You!"

The lawsuit includes accusations against several individuals for exploiting company funds and spending more than $1 million on private jets, yachts, and home renovations, alongside collecting secret kickbacks from business deals without the duo's knowledge. Despite attempts by defendants Chad Bronstein, Adam Wilks, Nicole Cosby, James Case, and Christopher Volo to have the lawsuit dismissed, the judge allowed the case to proceed, with Flair and Tyson demanding massive damages.

This decision does not mean the defendants have been found guilty or that the case is finally settled, but rather permits the legal pursuit to continue instead of being dismissed early. The legal battle remains ongoing to recover lost funds and missed deal revenues amid widespread interest from fans.
