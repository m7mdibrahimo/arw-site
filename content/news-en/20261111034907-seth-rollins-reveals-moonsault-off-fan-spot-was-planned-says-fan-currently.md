Seth Rollins recently discussed a viral clip from his early career as Tyler Black, which shows him performing a moonsault off a fan's chest during a match against Matt Sydal in IWA Mid-South. Rollins revealed on The Kittle Things that the spot was entirely planned. He explained that he and the fan, whom he identified as Timothy Regal, had practiced the maneuver during the day to ensure safety and stability. Rollins noted that Regal is a friend of his and currently works as a commentator for the SCW Pro promotion, which is co-run by Marek Brave, Rollins' partner at the Black and Brave Wrestling Academy. Rollins recalled that he was 19 at the time and was not concerned about the risks, stating he was "impervious to pain." He added that the clip gained popularity years later when fans began trading DVDs of the event. Rollins last appeared on WWE programming in August 2026 at SummerSlam, where he faced Roman Reigns.
