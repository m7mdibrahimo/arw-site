Alpha-1 Wrestling is making a comeback after previously announcing its closure. The Hamilton, Ontario-based promotion last held a show on November 1, 2026, which was presented at the time as its final event. However, the organization officially announced that it will return with a new event on Sunday, January 17, 2027, in Hamilton.

According to the promotion's announcement, the upcoming event will feature fan-voted matches and stipulations. The lineup includes WWE Intercontinental Champion Ethan Page, who originally founded Alpha-1 Wrestling, alongside Tyler Breeze, Channing Thomas, Jody Threat, Warhorse, Kody Lane, and Sam Holloway. Page also reacted to the official announcement on social media, expressing his excitement for the promotion's return.
