AEW Full Gear is just hours away, and fans are getting their first look at the stage setup inside the Mortgage Matchup Center. The stage features a massive centerpiece with oversized gears, LED lighting, and highway-style signs, including one that reads “West Phoenix.” This design gives the entrance an industrial, highway-inspired look that stands out from standard weekly television setups. Tony Khan has confirmed that everyone scheduled for the main pay-per-view card has arrived in Phoenix following nationwide flight disruptions, meaning the lineup remains unchanged despite some adjustments to the earlier Tailgate Brawl. Full Gear’s main card begins at 8 PM ET.
