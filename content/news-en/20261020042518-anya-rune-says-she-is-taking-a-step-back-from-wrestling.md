Airica Demia, known as Anya Rune in WWE, is stepping away from wrestling for the time being. Rune announced the decision on social media, explaining that it is best for her physical and mental health. She stated that she has fallen out of love with the sport.

> Hi everybody. I know a post like this is gonna let a lot of you down but I feel like its very necessary. Hell, I never thought I would end up making a post like this. I need to take a step back from wrestling. Both for my physical and mental health. I don't know how long this is going to last but I'm hoping to find a new love for it in the time I will be spending away. I will be keeping my social media somewhat active, it'll just be less wrestling related content. I have unfortunately fallen out of love with wrestling but that doesn't mean I don't love everything its given me. I love and appreciate every single last one of you guys who have continued to support me through everything. Thank you so much

Rune was released by WWE on September 4. She had been competing regularly on WWE EVOLVE as part of the WWE ID program, and she performed under the name Airica Demia on the independent scene before earning her WWE ID contract in March.
