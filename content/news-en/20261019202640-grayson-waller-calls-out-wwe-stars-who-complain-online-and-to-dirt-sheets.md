Grayson Waller shared his thoughts on WWE stars who complain online and to dirt sheets when they are unhappy with their television time. The current NXT Champion explained that he could have taken that path himself, but instead chose to return to NXT and fix his own situation. Speaking on NotSam Wrestling, Waller discussed why going back to NXT was necessary to remind himself of his abilities and send a message to the main roster.

> Part of the reason that I wanted to go back to NXT was because I needed to remind myself first — that took the first few weeks. Now that I’m the NXT Champion, I know how good I am. Now my job is to remind the guys on RAW and SmackDown — hey, you fumbled the ball back, and you’re gonna regret it.

Waller contrasted his approach with wrestlers who immediately turn to social media or wrestling dirt sheets to air their frustrations. He stated that talent have two options when facing a slump, and he chose to take control of his career rather than publicly complain. By returning to NXT, Waller successfully rebuilt his momentum and captured the NXT Championship, proving that taking action yields better results than complaining.
