During his time in WWE, Nunzio spent years as a mid-carder and enhancement talent, primarily competing on WWE Velocity. Alongside Chuck Palumbo and Johnny Stamboli, he led the Full Blooded Italians stable, which he believes helped raise his profile in the company. Speaking on Rewind Recap Relive, Nunzio recalled how the partnership eventually led to a feud with The Undertaker in 2003.

That rivalry produced a memorable spot where Nunzio made the sign of the cross before the Deadman dropped him with The Last Ride. According to Nunzio, the moment was completely improvised on the spot.

> "It just happened. I know it went viral or whatever, and I hear about it all the time. ... I'm glad, I'm glad!" he said. He added that Pat Patterson later praised the gesture in the dressing room, and because it drew so much attention, Nunzio continued doing it at live events.

Nunzio wrestled for WWE from 2002 until 2008. Although he never made a full-time return, he stepped back into a WWE ring in 2024 to face Tony D'Angelo on WWE NXT.
