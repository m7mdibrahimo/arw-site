Big Cass noted that rumors of reuniting with Enzo Amore complicated his WWE comeback. Speaking on The Sal Licata Show, he explained that speculation spread quickly after his signing, leading fans to expect a joint return.

He returned alone on RAW and attacked Je’Von Evans as a heel, which confused some fans. However, he feels he has successfully drawn heat from crowds.

WrestleVotes reported that creative staff felt those expectations hindered establishing his singles character.
