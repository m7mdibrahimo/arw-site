John Cena has been vocal about his retirement tour, but he is not shy about admitting when things did not go as planned. In a conversation with Uncrowned, Cena opened up about his WrestleMania 41 match against Cody Rhodes, where he secured his 17th world championship in April 2025. Cena offered a blunt critique of that performance, stating that he and Rhodes "stunk up the joint."

Cena explained that he shares this experience with younger talent to teach them about taking risks. He noted that even at the highest level, a wrestler can aim for a major creative moment and miss. Rather than hiding that failure, he uses it to encourage others to be bold. "And then I'll look back, especially on that last year because the retirement tour did have a lot of eyes, and I'll say I took a big swing at WrestleMania and we stunk up the joint. We failed," Cena said.

Despite the lackluster performance at WrestleMania, Cena feels the story eventually found its footing. He credited the company for adjusting their plans, which led to a stronger conclusion at SummerSlam. "But what did I do? I got back up, we dusted ourselves off, course-corrected the ship, and we culminated with a fantastic finishing of the story, as Cody would put it, at SummerSlam," he added. Rhodes eventually won the championship back from Cena in a Street Fight at SummerSlam, capping off their rivalry.
