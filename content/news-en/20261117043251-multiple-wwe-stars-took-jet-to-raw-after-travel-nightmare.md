CM Punk, Bayley, and Kali Armstrong utilized a private jet to travel to the November 16, 2026, episode of WWE RAW in Raleigh, North Carolina. The group faced significant travel difficulties following a nationwide FAA ground stop on Friday, which disrupted plans across the industry. According to Bryan Alvarez of Wrestling Observer, the three stars had to secure special transportation to get from an NXT live event in Chicago to the RAW taping. This was the latest in a series of travel hurdles for Punk and Bayley, who had previously endured a 19-hour drive from Fort Lauderdale to Detroit to reach a Saturday NXT show. Despite the grueling schedule, all three wrestlers participated in NXT live events over the weekend before making the flight to Raleigh. WWE had been concerned about potential talent absences due to the ongoing travel situation, which also impacted AEW and TNA schedules. Punk remains a central figure as WWE continues its buildup toward Survivor Series: WarGames.
