AEW star MVP recently revealed that he has been diagnosed with Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome, a condition affecting the heart's electrical system. Speaking on his podcast, MVP discussed the recent death of PAC and other losses in the wrestling community, which inspired him to get his own heart checked. After undergoing a CT scan and consulting with his cardiologist, he was reassured that his heart condition is not fatal and that he is healthy overall. MVP used the opportunity to urge other men in their 40s, especially those who have lived a strenuous wrestling lifestyle, to undergo CT scans and prioritize their health before it is too late.
