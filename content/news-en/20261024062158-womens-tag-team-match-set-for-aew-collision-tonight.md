AEW has added a new women's tag team match to the lineup for AEW Collision tonight, October 24. Promotion president Tony Khan confirmed that Divine Dominion, consisting of Megan Bayne and Lena Kross, will take on the team of Wrathlander, featuring Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron.

This matchup comes after the events of Wednesday's AEW Collision, where Divine Dominion teamed up with Sisters of Sin to launch a surprise attack on both Wrathlander and Brawling Birds. The tension between these teams continues to build ahead of upcoming events.

Wrathlander is already scheduled for a major championship opportunity, as Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron are set to challenge Brawling Birds members Jamie Hayter and Alex Windsor for the AEW Women's Tag Team Titles at AEW Full Gear.
