During a recent appearance on Mythical Kitchen's Last Meal, Cody Rhodes revealed that his favorite promo from his father, the legendary Dusty Rhodes, is not the famous Hard Times speech. While many fans point to that iconic promo, Cody explained that he had already heard it beforehand when his father cut it on Ole Anderson in Georgia Championship Wrestling. Instead, Cody's favorite promo took place in WCW in 1994, specifically a segment featuring his brother Dustin Rhodes titled The View Never Changes, where Dusty talked about wanting to be his partner and apologizing for neglecting him. Cody noted that being able to share that moment with his brother allowed his father to express his love and apologize in a unique way. He also expressed gratitude that his father spent his final chapter as a coach in WWE NXT, where his legacy continues to live on through top stars like Seth Rollins, Becky Lynch, and others at the WWE Performance Center.
