Cody Rhodes recently discussed how the WWE executive team simplified his transition to filming Street Fighter, which hit theaters on October 16 with Roman Reigns also starring. Appearing on The Rich Eisen Show, Rhodes noted he captured the Undisputed WWE Championship from John Cena at SummerSlam 2025 and went straight to the movie set, bypassing traditional extended absence storylines. He praised Nick Khan and Triple H for their flexibility, crediting his peers for stepping up during his time away.

> "I was so lucky, I won the title from John Cena at MetLife for SummerSlam, then went and filmed without doing the, the usual wrestling, put him out, stretcher him out, he's gone and then big return. It was just… I think the world is all aware of what we're doing now in terms of, oh, he's going to film Street Fighter. He doesn't need to do all that. Modern WWE with its executive core and, you know, Nick Khan and Triple H allowing it to go that way made it very easy, made it very easy. Other guys filled in while I was gone and I was able to come back and get right back into it."
