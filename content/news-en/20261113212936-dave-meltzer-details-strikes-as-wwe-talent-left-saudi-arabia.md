According to Wrestling Inc, Dave Meltzer has provided details about the strikes that took place in Riyadh as WWE performers departed Saudi Arabia following Crown Jewel. The promotion successfully held its November 7 event in Riyadh despite security concerns and deadly airstrikes from Yemeni Houthis, collecting a reported sum of $50 million to $55 million for the show.

Meltzer wrote in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter that it represented a choice of profits over safety, though the likelihood of an incident was considered low. On the day of the event, authorities intercepted a cruise missile above Riyadh, and a Houthi military spokesman asserted responsibility for attacks targeting King Khalid International Airport, Aramco properties, and a National Guard camp located in Najran.

Meltzer explained that while wrestlers bypassed the Riyadh airport upon arrival by landing in Bahrain and taking a five-hour automobile trip to Riyadh, they departed through King Khalid International Airport the following day. As they exited, ballistic and cruise missiles flew toward Riyadh and other areas in the Kingdom. Debris from a downed missile struck the Saudi Aramco administrative tower inside the King Abdullah Financial District, shattering windows on multiple floors. Although reports initially indicated all talent had departed prior to the November 8 strikes, it later came to light that Bronson Reed, a medical staff member, and an international executive stayed behind after Reed was hospitalized.
