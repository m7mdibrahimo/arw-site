Former WWE Champion Sheamus recently reflected on his main roster debut, which took place on the June 30, 2009 edition of "WWE ECW" against Oliver John. Speaking on "The Cooligans," Sheamus recalled feeling nervous because the show had to air live on Syfy for the West Coast. He defeated John quickly before winning the WWE Championship from John Cena less than six months later. Despite spending nearly 20 years in WWE and feuding with top stars like Triple H, Randy Orton, and Daniel Bryan, Sheamus stated that he never felt he had a truly substantial storyline. "I didn't really get many storylines. I didn't have the luxury. So I went out there and I just put on the best match I possibly could," Sheamus said. Known for his intense physical style in the ring, Sheamus recently departed from WWE after turning down a restructured contract extension.
