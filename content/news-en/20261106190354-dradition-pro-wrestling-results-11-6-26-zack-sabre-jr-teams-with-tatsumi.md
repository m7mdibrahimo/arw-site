Dradition Pro-Wrestling held its latest show on November 6, 2026, at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo, Japan. The promotion is run by six-time IWGP Heavyweight Champion Tatsumi Fujinami. In the main event, Tatsumi Fujinami and Zack Sabre Jr. defeated Aaron Wolf and Tatsuya Matsumoto. Here are the results from the show:

Hiroyuki Suzuki & MAZADA def. Ryunosuke Nagai & Sanshu Tsubakichi
Shuji Ishikawa def. Katsushi Takemura by referee's decision (1:31)
Daisuke Sekimoto & Kuroshio TOKYO Japan def. AKIRA & Manabu Soya
Kendo Kashin def. Nobuyuki Kurashima
LEONA vs. Strong Machine J - Time Limit Draw (15:00)
Kazushi Sakuraba & Masakatsu Funaki vs. Katsuhiko Nakajima & Mitsuya Nagai - Time Limit Draw (20:00)
Tatsumi Fujinami & Zack Sabre Jr. def. Aaron Wolf & Tatsuya Matsumoto
