WWE has filed a new trademark for the ring name 'Serena Thorn' with the United States Patent and Trademark Office on November 3rd. The filing covers a wide range of entertainment services, including professional wrestling exhibitions and performances across broadcast and online media, as well as fan club and newsletter services. It remains unclear who the new ring name is intended for, as WWE frequently registers names before new talent debuts on television.
