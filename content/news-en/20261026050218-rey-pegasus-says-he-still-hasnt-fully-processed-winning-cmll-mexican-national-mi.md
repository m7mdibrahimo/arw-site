Rey Pegasus opened up about winning the Mexican National Middleweight Title after earning his opportunity through online fan voting. Pegasus reflected on his journey to the championship match against Guerrero Maya Jr. at CMLL Noche de Campeones, where he ultimately secured the belt. 

Speaking with Fightful’s Luis Pulido, Pegasus expressed his lingering disbelief over the victory, especially competing alongside legendary names like Blue Panther and Brillante Jr. "Come to think of it, several weeks have already gone by, and I still can't wrap my head around it," Pegasus said. He added that having his family present at Arena México made the moment even more special.

Pegasus also touched upon being part of Los Amos del Aire during the conversation. Fans can check out the full interview on Fightful's YouTube channel.
