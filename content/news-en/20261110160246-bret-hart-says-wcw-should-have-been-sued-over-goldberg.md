The legendary Bret Hart stated that WCW should have been sued for allowing Goldberg to enter the ring without adequate training to protect his opponents during an appearance on the Johnny I Pro Show.

Hart explained that his spotless safety record is the biggest achievement of his career, noting that Goldberg hurt him because he never received proper instruction. He stated, 

> "It's not like Goldberg. Goldberg just hurt me because he didn't know how to do anything different. No one taught him, no one showed him, and no one explained to him, ‘Bill, it's not really real. You can't actually kick somebody in the head as hard as you can. You just can't.’"

He added that WCW bore the primary responsibility for pushing an inexperienced wrestler into the ring without guidance.

Comparing his professionalism with everyone including Shawn Michaels, Hart maintained that personal disputes stay in the dressing room while everyone acts professionally in the ring. Hart concluded that his true pride lies in every opponent leaving the ring safely without injuries, viewing the company's failure as worthy of a lawsuit.
