Drew McIntyre has been absent from WWE television since WrestleMania 42 following his loss to Jacob Fatu. During his time away, he has been busy filming movies such as The Last Druid and Highlander. During an appearance on Busted Open Radio, McIntyre revealed that he continued to train in the ring while staying in Barcelona for three months to film. He explained that after finding an indie wrestler working as a stuntman on set, he arranged to use a local ring to stay sharp and beat up some local wrestlers during private training sessions.
