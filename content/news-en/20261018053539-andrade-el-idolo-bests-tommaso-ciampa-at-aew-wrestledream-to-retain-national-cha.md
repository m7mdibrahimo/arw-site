Andrade El Idolo successfully defended his AEW National Championship against Tommaso Ciampa at AEW WrestleDream. This victory marked El Idolo's seventh title defense since winning the championship in July. During the encounter, Ciampa engaged in several antics, including taking a photo at ringside and mocking Andrade by wearing trunks that read 'I Just Know.'

Ciampa attempted to gain an advantage by attacking Andrade's pants, even hitting his finisher on the pants and demanding a count, which drew a reaction from the crowd. After a back-and-forth exchange, Ciampa tried to use the championship belt as a weapon, but Andrade countered by spiking him onto it. Andrade then hit his finishing maneuver to secure the win and retain his title.
