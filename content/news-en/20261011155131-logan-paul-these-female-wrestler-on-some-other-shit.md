Logan Paul took to social media following WWE Money In The Bank to praise the performances of the women's roster. At the event, six competitors featured in the women's ladder match won by Tiffany Stratton, while Liv Morgan reclaimed the Women's World Championship from champion Stephanie Vaquer and Becky Lynch. Paul also appeared on the show to support his Vision faction mate Bronson Reed in his bout against Oba Femi, though Femi ultimately secured the victory.
