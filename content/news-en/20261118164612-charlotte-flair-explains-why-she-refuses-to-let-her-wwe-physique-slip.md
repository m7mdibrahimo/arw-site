Charlotte Flair is dedicated to maintaining her physical conditioning, and she views her intense training regimen as an essential part of her WWE persona. Flair, who has built her reputation as a "genetically superior athlete," explained to The Screen Chatter Interview Page that her workouts are more than just a health routine; they are a professional commitment. "I feel like, is part of not only an investment in myself, but an investment in the character. And also, you know, never wanting to miss a step and always wanting to be prepared," Flair stated. She emphasized that after establishing such a high standard for her physique, she is determined not to regress. "I feel like I've set that standard as like the genetically superior athlete. So, like wanting to always look in tip-top shape is part of it, too," she added. Flair credited her discipline to her upbringing, noting that her father, Ric Flair, taught her the importance of self-care. She also clarified that these expectations are self-imposed rather than mandated by WWE. For Flair, this level of dedication is a key factor that distinguishes her from other competitors.
