Brendan Cobbina, who went viral on the internet as the “Black John Cena” due to his resemblance to the WWE legend, is now officially a professional wrestler one year after finally meeting John Cena in person.

Reflecting on his journey, Cobbina marked the anniversary on X, recalling how nervous he was before meeting Cena and the simple advice he received from someone close to him. “I remember upon this moment happening I had no idea what to do nor how to approach it. Incredibly nervous I was stuck between wearing the suit I was gifted and coming across as ‘The Black John Cena’ or to do it the way that felt most natural to me. I have someone dear to me who simply advised me two days before I went to ‘be yourself’ and for me that kick started everything in following my journey.”

Cobbina was amazed to discover that Cena already knew who he was, and when he shared that he was training to become a professional wrestler, Cena offered words of encouragement. “Meeting Mr Cena was my biggest honour. He knew who I was. He was incredibly polite to me. And when I told him I was practising to be a Professional Wrestler he gave me some unforgettable advice then congratulated me when I told him about my possible 🇺🇸 adventures to which he quoted ‘that’s awesome, give everyone over there my love.’”

Now, exactly one year later, Cobbina has stepped inside the ring to compete and already holds championship gold. “And now a year later, as a Professional Wrestler under my own terms, staying true to myself and doing it my way; I can 100% be proud of what I’ve achieved.” Cobbina added that while he is just getting started, he is excited to share more of his wrestling journey with fans.
