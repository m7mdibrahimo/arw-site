Ilja Dragunov secured his first victory in AEW during the latest episode of AEW Dynamite. The show was headlined by a singles match between Dragunov and Speedball Mike Bailey, marking the in-ring debut for the Mad Dragon, who signed with the promotion at WrestleDream. Dragunov claimed the pinfall win over Bailey after delivering two D-Bomb finishers, targeting the knee and the head. This marks Dragunov's first singles victory since defeating The Miz on SmackDown in January. According to Fightful, Miz has not won a televised singles match since February 2024.
