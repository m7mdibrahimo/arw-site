Jay White was reportedly close to joining the Death Riders last year, but an injury prevented the angle from moving forward. According to a report discussed on a Self Made Session, AEW originally intended for a major plot twist during Jon Moxley’s victory over Swerve Strickland at AEW Dynasty 2025. Instead of the finish that aired, White was supposed to interfere and strike Strickland with the Blade Runner to officially align himself with the faction. "The original finish wasn’t BTE Trigger, Death Rider. One, two, three. It was supposed to be a Blade Runner. Like, Jay White was supposed to join the Death Riders."

The plans were ultimately abandoned due to White suffering an injury. This forced AEW to pivot, leading to a different direction following Dynasty, which included setting up a program between the Young Bucks and the team of Will Ospreay and Swerve Strickland. "Because, yeah, because instead they set up the Bucks versus Ospreay and Swerve." Consequently, the conclusion of the match at Dynasty was not the company's original endgame, as White was intended to be the newest member of the Death Riders.
