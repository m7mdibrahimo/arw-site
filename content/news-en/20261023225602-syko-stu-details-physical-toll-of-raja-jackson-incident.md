Over a year after being attacked by Raja Jackson at a Knokx Pro Wrestling event, Stuart Smith, known as Syko Stu, has opened up about his recovery. Jackson, the son of former UFC fighter Quinton "Rampage" Jackson, threw real strikes during the match that left Stu hospitalized for nearly a week with a severe traumatic brain injury. Jackson eventually pleaded guilty to felony battery charges and served 90 days in jail. Stu told the SOSPodcast that he has little memory of the event until he regained consciousness days later. "I was going in and out of consciousness the whole time," Stu said. "I do remember waking up on Tuesday and seeing some of the wrestlers. Rikishi was there." Stu revealed that he had to relearn how to walk and suffered shattered facial bones and tooth loss. The recovery process kept him out of work for an entire year, as his job required significant cognitive focus. Stu noted that he is still undergoing medical and dental treatment for his injuries. As part of his plea deal, Jackson was ordered to pay over $81,000 in restitution and is currently serving two years of probation.
