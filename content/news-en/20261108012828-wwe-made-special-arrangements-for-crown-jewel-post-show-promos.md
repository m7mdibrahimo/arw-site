WWE Crown Jewel featured several major moments inside the ring, but some of the post-show reactions were not as spontaneous as they appeared to fans. According to False Finish, WWE had most of its stars record their promos before the event was even finished so everyone could leave Riyadh as quickly as possible.

The promos featuring Cody Rhodes, Tiffany Stratton, Penta, and Je'Von Evans that aired during the post-show were actually taped while the event itself was still taking place, with Roman Reigns and Sami Zayn serving as the exceptions. This meant Cody's comments after defeating Randy Orton in a Last Man Standing match were captured beforehand, along with Stratton's reaction after cashing in her Money in the Bank contract to become WWE Women's Champion.

Penta and Je'Von Evans also finished their media work early after defeating Bron Breakker and Austin Theory to become the new WWE World Tag Team Champions. Roman and Sami could not follow the same schedule because their match took place later in the show, where Reigns defeated Zayn, requiring their post-match reactions to wait until the contest was officially over.

By recording almost all of its post-show content while the event was still running, WWE could hit the final bell and begin packing immediately without keeping talent and crew in Riyadh longer than necessary.
