Kyle Fletcher recently shared his thoughts on his relationship with Skye Blue, stating that he believes they are a truly unique couple. Both wrestlers participated in this year's Blood And Guts matches, where Fletcher secured a victory for his team, while Blue's team suffered a loss after her partner, Julia Hart, submitted.

Reflecting on their experiences during the Blood And Guts event in his latest Protolog, Fletcher remarked on their shared resilience. "I actually feel pretty pretty okay didn't get too bloodst i got a bit bloodst. Yeah, I don't know, man. This is just what we do. We talk about our level of bloodness. That's the kind of couple we are. I don't think there's another couple like us in the world, you know? I think we're pretty unique. Very, very special. At least half us. We have bloods and guts. Oh, I won my match. Did you win your match? You didn't win your match. I won, she lost. We both did some good bloods and some guts."

In the same video, Fletcher also touched upon the possibility of writing a book in the future.
