Tony Khan revealed that Adam Copeland informed him well before his public announcement that 2027 would be his final year as a professional wrestler. Khan confirmed during the AEW WrestleDream media call that the retirement revelation made during the October 14 episode of AEW Dynamite was a planned discussion between them. While Khan accepted the decision, he noted that the exact timeline for Copeland's final matches in 2027 remains undetermined.

Copeland announced his retirement after defeating Josh Alexander in a qualifier for the WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage match. He shared that he suffered three broken ribs and a collapsed lung during his TLC match against Christian Cage at AEW All Out, injuries that occurred within the first five minutes. Despite these physical setbacks, Copeland intends to remain active and has set his sights on chasing the AEW World Championship before concluding his career.
