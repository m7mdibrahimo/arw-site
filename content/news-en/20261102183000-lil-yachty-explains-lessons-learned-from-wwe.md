Lil Yachty has detailed how his time in WWE differs from his music career, explaining that the wrestling business requires strict discipline compared to the freedom he has in the music industry.

He recently appeared on WWE SmackDown, interfering in the match between Trick Williams and Sami Zayn. The rapper expressed his deep passion for wrestling, noting that he might even give up his music career to become a full-time wrestler in the future.
