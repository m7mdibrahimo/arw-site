Several WWE Superstars have raised concerns regarding the WWE ID program following the recent departures of Laynie Luck and Marcus Mathers. According to Fightful Select, Laynie Luck's contract was not renewed after a 320-day reign as the WWE Women's ID Champion, which ended when she dropped the title to Monica Monroe. Meanwhile, Marcus Mathers also announced his exit from the initiative. These departures have sparked internal questions among multiple talent regarding the direction and stability of the WWE ID program.
