The Iron Survivor Challenge may be moving from its traditional December slot in NXT, but WWE is reportedly not abandoning the match type. WrestleVotes recently indicated that Bruce Prichard is a significant proponent of the concept and has considered moving it to the main roster before his expected retirement next year. "I heard a rumor. They like the concept. Bruce Prichard is one of those guys who really likes the concept himself. With him retiring, maybe one more before I go — I'm bringing this to the main roster as my Pat Patterson moment of the Royal Rumble, so we'll see if that's something that will happen."

While this remains a possibility rather than a confirmed plan, the discussion arose following the announcement of NXT TLC for December 12 in Orlando. The Iron Survivor Challenge, which features a unique format including timed entrances, falls, and a penalty box, has become a staple of NXT's December schedule. Moving the concept to the main roster would represent a significant legacy move for Prichard as he nears the end of his tenure.
