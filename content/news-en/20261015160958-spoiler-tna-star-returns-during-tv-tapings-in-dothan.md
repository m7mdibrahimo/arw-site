Following the Bound for Glory pay-per-view, TNA Wrestling hosted television tapings in Dothan, Alabama, on October 13 and October 14, 2026. During the October 13 tapings, Kiera Hogan turned on Allie after a promo segment and reunited Fire 'N Flava with Tasha Steelz, leading to a two-on-one attack. Shotzi Blackheart made a surprise return to TNA to save Allie, marking her first appearance for the company in seven years. Shotzi previously competed for Impact Wrestling in 2018 and 2019 before her WWE run, and she immediately entered the ongoing storyline with Fire 'N Flava as the company builds toward Destination X.
