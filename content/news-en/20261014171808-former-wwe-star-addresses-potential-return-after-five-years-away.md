Kalisto has addressed a potential return to WWE more than five years after his departure from the company. Speaking in a recent interview, the former star was asked if he has unfinished business in WWE or if that chapter is closed. Kalisto did not give a definitive answer, explaining that his next steps will depend on his health and current projects.

> "I listen to my body a lot and also, too, where things can take me. It all depends on what I’m doing, you know, my projects that I’m working on and everything."

While he stopped short of confirming a comeback, Kalisto admitted that returning to WWE has crossed his mind. He also expressed a strong interest in coaching and giving back to the younger generation of wrestlers.

> "Yeah, I think about it. I think about it, too. But also, too, I love giving so much back that that’s something, too, that I look forward to in the future—either with coaching, worldwide, not just near, you know. I just—I love to teach. I love to give back. And it makes me feel grateful, too. That’s how I see myself in the future."

For now, Kalisto's immediate focus remains on the recovery of his knee. He is taking things step by step and keeping his options open before making any major career decisions.

> "But for now, let me see where my knee takes me. And I’m kind of—I’m all ears right now. Yeah. You know, so it’s just—that’s one thing that did cross my mind, but I’m just keeping my ears open, how I listen to my body, and we’ll just take it from there, you know."

Reiterating that retirement is not on his mind, Kalisto made it clear that he never gives up and will keep pushing forward no matter what.

> "I’m a person that I never give up. I keep pushing no matter what. I get hard-headed with that so much. But, yeah, that’s something, too, that—we’ll see."
