Elayna Black recently opened up about a period of intense personal struggle that coincided with her exit from WWE. In a documentary released on TNA Wrestling's YouTube channel, the wrestler formerly known as Cora Jade detailed how her life seemed to collapse as she faced a series of consecutive losses, including the end of a relationship and the death of her dog.

Black previously dealt with a life-threatening ectopic pregnancy in January 2023 and a severe knee injury in January 2024, which involved tearing her ACL, MCL, and both meniscuses during a match against Lyra Valkyria. Following these health challenges, her release from WWE in May 2025 left her feeling as though she had lost everything, leading her to become afraid of caring for anything new.

> "Ectopic pregnancy, boyfriend leaves me, lose my knee, dog dies. And then, I got fired a few months later."

Although she anticipated the release, losing the career that defined her identity was devastating. She stepped away from wrestling in July 2025 due to the negative impact on her mental health and recurring anxiety attacks. Black eventually returned to the ring by signing with TNA in January 2026, noting that her primary goal was to rediscover her passion for the sport rather than focusing on financial gain.

> "I didn't care about the money. I didn't care about who was going to pay me the most. I did not care about any of that. I just wanted to love wrestling again."
