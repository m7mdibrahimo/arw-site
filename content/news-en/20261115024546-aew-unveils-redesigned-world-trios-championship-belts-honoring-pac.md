AEW has revealed a new look for its World Trios Championship belts, featuring a special tribute to the late Benjamin Satterley, known to fans as PAC. Ahead of Full Gear 2026, the company shared photos of the redesigned belts, which include gold plates, black leather straps, and a memorial inscription on the back reading "In Memory of Benjamin Satterley 1986-2026." The belts also feature an engraved side plate honoring his wrestling career. This tribute holds extra significance as PAC was a former AEW World Trios Champion, having held the titles as part of Death Triangle and later with the Death Riders. PAC passed away on September 27, 2026, and AEW has continued to honor his legacy, notably through a dedicated tribute episode of Dynamite. The new championships were unveiled just hours before AEW Full Gear 2026 in Phoenix, Arizona, where Hangman Page, Brody King, and Bandido are set to defend the titles against Swerve Strickland, Kofi Kingston, and Austin Creed in a Texas Deathmatch.
