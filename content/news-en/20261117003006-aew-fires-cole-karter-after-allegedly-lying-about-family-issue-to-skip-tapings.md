AEW has fired Cole Karter after discovering he was dishonest about a family issue used to excuse his absence from recent Ring of Honor television tapings. According to Fightful Select, Karter was released several weeks ago, though the news is only now surfacing among wrestlers. The situation is particularly notable because Karter had been offered a new AEW contract earlier this year, an agreement that has now been terminated. Karter had spent most of his four-year tenure with AEW working on ROH programming as part of The Frat House faction, which included Griff Garrison, Preston Vance, and Jacked Jameson. Before his time in AEW, Karter previously competed in WWE NXT as Troy "Two Dimes" Donovan, a run that ended in 2022 due to a Wellness Policy violation. Meanwhile, AEW has also confirmed that Tay Melo is no longer with the company. Melo was removed from the official roster page on November 11 after requesting her release earlier this year, a request Tony Khan had previously indicated he intended to grant.
