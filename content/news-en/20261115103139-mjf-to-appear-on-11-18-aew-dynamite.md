MJF is scheduled to appear on the November 18 episode of AEW Dynamite. At the Full Gear pay-per-view, Will Ospreay successfully defended the AEW World Championship against Kyle Fletcher in the main event. Following the bout, Ospreay celebrated in the ring alongside members of the United Empire, including Alex Windsor, Jamie Hayter, Speedball Mike Bailey, and Andrade El Idolo, before they left him alone to greet the fans.

As Ospreay thanked the crowd, a masked assailant ambushed him in the ring. The attacker unmasked to reveal former AEW World Champion MJF. During the post-show media scrum, AEW President Tony Khan officially announced that MJF will address the audience on the upcoming broadcast of AEW Dynamite.

The lineup for the November 18 show also features the start of the 2026 AEW Continental Classic tournament, as well as a singles match pitting Samoa Joe against Jack Perry.
