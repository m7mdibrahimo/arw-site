Kevin Nash has responded to critics who questioned his ongoing friendship with Glenn Jacobs, known in WWE as Kane. Despite holding opposing political views, Nash made it clear that their personal bond remains unchanged.

Addressing the situation on social media, Nash highlighted that disagreement on political matters should not lead to personal hatred. He noted that both men respect each other's opinions and maintain a strong friendship regardless of their differences.

> "The amazing piece of information is that @GlennJacobsTN and myself are friends. His political views are his as mine are mine."

Nash also criticized the negativity on social media and encouraged fans to focus on using their vote rather than letting political debates destroy personal relationships.
