Steven Borden, the son of wrestling legend Sting, is facing pressure to stop taking dangerous bumps in AEW, with Booker T believing his father needs to intervene to prevent early career risks.

Speaking on his podcast, Booker T discussed Borden's frightening incident at AEW All Out on September 26. Borden suffered a concussion after colliding with Kevin Knight during a tag team match, which prompted the referee to immediately stop the contest and send him to the hospital. Booker T urged Sting to advise his son against dangerous dives and focus instead on developing his in-ring skills rather than copying other performers.

Booker T noted that Borden's training with Darby Allin likely influenced his high-risk style, especially since Allin is known for extreme maneuvers. Although Borden has since recovered, returned to the ring, and recently teamed with his brother Garrett for their first official tag team match together, the clear advice remains to avoid unnecessary risks to protect his future.
