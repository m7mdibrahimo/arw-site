The October 27, 2026, episode of WWE NXT aired live from the Capitol Wrestling Center in Orlando, Florida. In the main event, Jackson Drake of The Vanity Project defended the WWE NXT North American Championship against EK Prosper. The show also featured the semifinal rounds of the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic tournament.

The Vanity Project’s Brad Baylor and Ricky Smokes def. Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno in a Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic semifinal match.

Chantel Monroe def. Lizzy Rain in a singles match.

Sean Legacy def. Saquon Shugars in a singles match.

Hank Walker and Tank Ledger def. The Wagner Brothers (Galeno and El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr.) in a Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic semifinal match.

Jackson Drake def. EK Prosper to retain the WWE NXT North American Championship.

During the broadcast, Tony D’Angelo searched for NXT Champion Grayson Waller following a home invasion, leading to a brawl between the two in the parking lot to close the show. Additionally, Danhausen announced that Myles Borne and Tavion Heights will face off in a Weapons Wild Match at Halloween Havoc.
