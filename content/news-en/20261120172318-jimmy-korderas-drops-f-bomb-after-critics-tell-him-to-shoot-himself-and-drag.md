Former WWE referee Jimmy Korderas has faced intense personal backlash following his harsh review of AEW Full Gear. Korderas revealed that critics sent him horrific messages telling him to shoot himself, dragging his wife into the abuse, and even referencing a tragic event in wrestling history. In response to the harassment, Korderas posted a defiant message on X telling those responsible to jump in a tub of profanities. Korderas had previously criticized AEW Full Gear as horrible and messy, pointing to the heavy use of weapons, blood, and chaotic matches like Anarchy in the Arena as reasons for losing his interest in the product.
