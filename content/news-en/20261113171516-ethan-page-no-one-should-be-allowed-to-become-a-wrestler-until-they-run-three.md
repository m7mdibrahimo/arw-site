Ethan Page has proposed a bold requirement for aspiring professional wrestlers. Speaking on The Masked Man Show, Page argued that no one should be allowed to step into a ring or even purchase wrestling boots until they have successfully organized three independent events of their own. Page, who balances his role on the WWE Raw roster with running his own Alpha-1 promotion in Canada, believes this experience would transform the industry. He explained that the "honeymoon phase" of creative control quickly fades once the reality of promotion sets in. According to Page, this process would teach performers to respect the business and understand the immense effort required to draw an audience. He stated that after running three shows, wrestlers would better appreciate their bookings and perform more professionally, especially when appearing in front of an audience they did not personally draw. Page also shared his thoughts on the John Cena Classic during the interview.
