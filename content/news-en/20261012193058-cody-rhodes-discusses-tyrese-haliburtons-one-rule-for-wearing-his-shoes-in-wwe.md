Cody Rhodes recently discussed Tyrese Haliburton's frequent appearances on WWE programming and revealed a specific rule that Haliburton has regarding his signature shoes. Speaking on the latest episode of Sneaker Shopping with Complex, Rhodes noted that Haliburton feels like a part of the WWE family, recalling how Haliburton previously provided weapons for John Cena and Rhodes at last year's SummerSlam. Rhodes explained that when Haliburton gifted him sneakers, he insisted on one condition: if Rhodes wears them on television, he must be winning and beating someone up rather than taking a back bump, calling them a win-only shoe. Rhodes also touched upon wanting to see Travis Scott work with WWE again during the same interview.
