CM Punk almost recruited a WWE Hall of Famer for Survivor Series: WarGames, but the plan quickly unraveled during the November 20, 2026 episode of WWE SmackDown. Punk revealed "Hacksaw" Jim Duggan as the final member of Team USA, only for Duggan to withdraw after finding out who his opponents were.

Punk walked out to the ring in Evansville, Indiana, preparing to unveil the fifth partner, when Cody Rhodes interrupted. Rhodes questioned Punk's unilateral decision-making, noting that while they were co-captains, they had never settled on their last teammate together. Punk then summoned Duggan, who sparked energetic USA chants from the crowd and thanked Punk for the selection.

Rhodes voiced strong skepticism about Duggan wrestling again, pointing out that the veteran has only one kidney and had not competed in a WWE ring in years. Rhodes also doubted whether Duggan could pass medical testing. Punk pushed back by highlighting Duggan winning the inaugural Royal Rumble match, insisting his country needed him. Rhodes eventually agreed to the pick, praising Duggan's survival against cancer.

Punk then queued a video package highlighting Team Mexico so Duggan could evaluate the opposition. Upon watching the preview, a terrified Duggan immediately changed his mind, shouting, "Noooooooooooo!" before rushing out of the ring. His hasty exit left Punk and Rhodes searching for a fifth member once again heading into Survivor Series.
