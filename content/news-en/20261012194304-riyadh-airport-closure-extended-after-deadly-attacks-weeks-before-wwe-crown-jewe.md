Riyadh's main international airport will remain closed until October 17 following a series of deadly attacks, raising concerns ahead of WWE Crown Jewel on November 7. King Khalid International Airport has been shut down since a missile strike on October 10 killed 12 people and injured over 300 others, with another projectile hitting a terminal the following day. The Houthis claimed responsibility for the attacks, which impact roughly 700 flights per day. Despite the security situation and a U.S. Embassy travel advisory, WWE has not announced any changes to the event. Meanwhile, Saudi General Entertainment Authority chairman Turki Alalshikh confirmed that an upcoming boxing match between Canelo Alvarez and Christian Mbilli on October 31 will still take place as planned after Alvarez stated he fears nothing.
