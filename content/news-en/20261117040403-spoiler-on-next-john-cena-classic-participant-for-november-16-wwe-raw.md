John Cena is reportedly scheduled to invite Liv Morgan to compete in the John Cena Classic during the upcoming November 16 episode of WWE RAW. While WWE has advertised Cena's appearance to announce more participants, Morgan's involvement has not been officially confirmed yet. If the plans hold, Morgan will become the tenth confirmed participant in the tournament, joining Cody Rhodes, Zilla Fatu, Noam Dar, Romeo Moreno, Kelani Jordan, Charlotte Flair, Jacy Jayne, Fallon Henley, and Lainey Reid. Cena has spent the past several weeks visiting various WWE shows to recruit competitors for the event, which takes place on December 14 at Disney's ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex in Orlando, Florida, and will air live on Netflix as a special edition of WWE RAW.
