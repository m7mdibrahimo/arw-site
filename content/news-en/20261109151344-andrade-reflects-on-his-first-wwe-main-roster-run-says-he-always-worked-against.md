During a recent appearance on Talk Is Jericho, Andrade reflected on his first run with the WWE main roster following his time in NXT. He noted that while he enjoyed his time and respected his colleagues, he felt creatively stuck because his storylines constantly pitted him against Mexican wrestlers. Andrade stated that he frequently worked in programs against Rey Mysterio, Dominik Mysterio, Humberto Carrillo, and Angel Garza. He recalled telling his wife that he needed more success and felt he was not thriving, leading him to ask for his release from the company in March 2021. After spending time in AEW, Andrade returned to WWE before heading back to AEW, where he recently lost the AEW National Title to Tommaso Ciampa on AEW Collision.
