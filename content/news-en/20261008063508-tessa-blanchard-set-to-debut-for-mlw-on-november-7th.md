Tessa Blanchard is officially heading to MLW. During a sit-down interview on the October 7th episode of CMLL Informa, it was confirmed that Blanchard will be joining the promotion. She is set to make her debut at the MLW SuperFight event, which is scheduled for November 7th. Although she was originally expected to appear for the organization at an earlier date, she is now confirmed for the upcoming show.
