Tommaso Ciampa will defend the AEW National Championship against 'Speedball' Mike Bailey on the upcoming three-hour episode of AEW Dynamite and AEW Collision on November 11. Ciampa recently won the title from Andrade El Idolo, and Bailey is now seeking to bring the belt back to United Empire. The upcoming show will also feature a face-to-face meeting between AEW World Champion Will Ospreay and Kyle Fletcher, alongside a tag team match pitting Brodido against New Level. Additionally, Jon Moxley and Marina Shafir will team up to face Jay White and Mercedes Moné.
