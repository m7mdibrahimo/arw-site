The initial bout has been made official for the upcoming November 23 broadcast of WWE Raw, according to Fightful. During a commercial break on the recent Monday program, Lyra Valkyria interrupted a promo delivered by Sol Ruca to pave the way for their upcoming collision.

This matchup was verified via an inset graphic positioned in the bottom corner of the screen while The Usos battled the 946 team in the evening's headliner. Because this installment serves as a double taping alongside WWE SmackDown, the show is scheduled to get underway at 6 PM ET.
