Logan Paul wants to face Joe Hendry in a one-on-one match as soon as he is cleared to return to the ring. In recent months, Hendry has targeted Paul with signature music videos and songs, including a track titled Fire Logan Paul, which has repeatedly frustrated Paul while he has been sidelined with an injury. Speaking in his latest vlog, Paul expressed his desire to step into the ring with Hendry upon his return. Hendry had previously taunted Paul in the same video regarding a Pokémon card. Paul stated that he would gladly take the opportunity to wrestle Hendry first once cleared, noting his frustration with Hendry rubbing things in his face.
