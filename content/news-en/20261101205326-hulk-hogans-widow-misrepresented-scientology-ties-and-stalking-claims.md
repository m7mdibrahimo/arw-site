Sky Daily, the widow of Hulk Hogan, recently attempted to put rumors regarding her past with Scientology to rest. However, reports indicate her explanations contained inconsistencies, particularly regarding a man she has accused of stalking her for years. According to sources familiar with the situation, the individual in question was not a random obsessed stranger, but a former friend with a shared history in the church. Daily claimed the man harassed her, followed her with a camera, and even headbutted a former boyfriend, prompting her to install security cameras. While Daily has stated she left the church nearly 20 years ago and does not financially support it, she admitted to enrolling her son in an online educational program associated with Scientology to help him earn high school credits. The revelations have brought renewed scrutiny to her past rather than putting the controversy to rest.
