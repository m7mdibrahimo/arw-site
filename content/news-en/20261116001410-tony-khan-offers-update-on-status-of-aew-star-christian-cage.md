Former AEW World Tag Team Champion Christian Cage has missed approximately eight weeks of action due to a collapsed lung and multiple broken ribs. Speaking on the AEW Full Gear media call, AEW President Tony Khan expressed optimism regarding the wrestler's eventual return.

> "I am very optimistic that we will see Christian Cage back, but I can't say when."

Khan noted that Cage is one of the most important figures in AEW history and one of his all-time favorite performers. He added that Cage is undergoing a very challenging recovery process while everyone sends him their support.

The injuries occurred when Cage collided with a ladder during the AEW World Tag Team Championship Ladder Match at AEW All Out on September 26. In that encounter, The Young Bucks successfully captured the titles from Cage and his partner Adam Copeland.

While Cage remains sidelined, Copeland transitioned to singles action and pursued the AEW International Championship at AEW Full Gear. However, Kazuchika Okada successfully defended the title using a Rainmaker.
