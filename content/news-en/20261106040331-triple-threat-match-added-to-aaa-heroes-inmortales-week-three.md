A new triple threat match has been added to the lineup for week three of Lucha Libre AAA's Héroes Inmortales. The show will air on WWE and AAA's YouTube channels on November 7th. The newly added bout features Cruz Del Toro, Kento, and Bravo Americano facing off in a three-way match. Additionally, the event will feature Marisela Peña's induction into the AAA Hall of Fame, as well as a singles match between Abismo Negro Jr. and El Fiscal.
