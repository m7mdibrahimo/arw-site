Nia Jax addressed criticism regarding her being in WWE due to nepotism, pointing out that her family members are not stepping into the ring for her. Since debuting in WWE, Jax has been a regular presence across WWE NXT and the main roster, capturing multiple singles and tag team championships. As a member of the prominent Anoa'i family, she has frequently faced claims from fans that her success stems from her lineage. Speaking on a recent episode of the Battleground podcast, Jax embraced her background and stated that nepotism is beneficial.

> "I mean, people always get upset with, 'oh, nepotism.' It's like, well, nepotism is a great thing.. I don't like, I'm not going to sit there and negate all the accomplishments my family has made in this business. Like, and I get to be a part of it. It's incredible, and I'm not like- I got an opportunity to try out just like everybody else does, and I made it. So there's no reason for me not to be proud of my family and not to be proud of what they've accomplished, and, it's funny. Cause when I came into this business, it was a lot of people like, 'Oh, you're only here because of him. You're only here because of that.' It's like, okay. Duh. Of course I'm here because my family's in it. Like, you know, but I'm also doing it myself and they're not getting in the ring for me. They're not traveling for me. They're not getting injured for me. Like these are all things that I'm going through and that I get, I have to do and make my own path. So I, I actually don't mind it when I get, I get, you know, the flack of, yeah, it's because your family. I have an incredible family and you would too, if you were a part of this Bloodline."
