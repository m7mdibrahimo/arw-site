Mike Bailey recently reflected on his career and his first match against Mustafa Ali, which took place in February 2024 at Prestige Wrestling's A Moment Of Violence event in Los Angeles, California. Speaking in an interview, Bailey discussed their subsequent program in TNA, including a memorable X-Division Championship match in Montreal featuring referee Earl Hebner.

Bailey emphasized his dedication to continuous improvement, stating, "I think my greatest accomplishment in pro wrestling is constant progress and I always think that my best match is my last match." He also highlighted the unique freedom of competing on the independent scene with minimal television restrictions, which allowed them to deliver a standout bout.
