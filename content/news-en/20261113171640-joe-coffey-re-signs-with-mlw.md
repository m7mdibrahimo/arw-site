Joe Coffey has officially re-signed with Major League Wrestling, according to a report from Sports Illustrated. While the exact details and duration of his new contract were not disclosed, Coffey made his promotional debut earlier this year at the MLW Battle Riot event in January. He later teamed with his brother Mark Coffey to challenge The Good Brothers for the MLW Tag Team Championship at MLW Saturday Night SuperFight. The Coffey brothers continue to perform alongside Wolfgang as part of the Glasgow Boys on Top faction.

MLW CEO Court Bauer praised Coffey following the agreement, highlighting his extensive experience and performance over the past year. Bauer stated that Coffey spent 17 years grappling across the world and delivered a standout year in the promotion. He added that getting the deal finalized was an important priority for MLW, as Coffey backs up his words in the ring and is ready to face any opponent placed in front of him.
