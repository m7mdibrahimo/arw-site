Alexa Bliss reflected on a terrifying medical complication during her pregnancy that doctors initially failed to notice. During her appearance on What's Your Story? with Stephanie McMahon, Bliss shared that she suffered from preeclampsia after welcoming her daughter, Hendrix. Despite her mother having a doctorate in nursing and pointing out unusually high blood pressure readings, medical staff initially dismissed the symptoms. Three days after giving birth, Bliss experienced severe swelling and felt as though she was drowning when lying down. After sending photos to a neighbor who worked at the hospital, Bliss walked across a connecting bridge for blood work, which immediately led to her admission. Doctors informed her that she was at risk for heart and kidney failure, warning her that some mothers in her condition do not make it home with their families. Bliss was given Lasix and lost eight pounds of water weight within the first hour. Although she was eventually cleared after an extended 48-hour hospital stay to monitor her heart, the harrowing experience left a lasting impression. Bliss admitted that the ordeal has made her hesitant to have another child, especially while balancing the demands of life on the road with WWE.
