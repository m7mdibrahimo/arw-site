WWE announced during NXT Halloween Havoc that NXT TLC will officially take place this December. The event is scheduled for Saturday, December 12, 2026, in Orlando, Florida, starting at 7 PM ET/4 PM PT on The CW. This marks the first time NXT is receiving its own version of the Tables, Ladders & Chairs event, which has historically been part of WWE's main roster pay-per-view schedule. No matches have been announced yet for the December 12 show, leaving the card and featured championships open as the company builds toward the event.
