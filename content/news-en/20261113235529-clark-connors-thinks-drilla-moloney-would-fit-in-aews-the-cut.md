Clark Connors hopes to bring Drilla Moloney into All Elite Wrestling as part of The Cut, a newly formed faction born from an alliance between The Dogs and the Bang Bang Gang. The stable currently features Jay White, David Finlay, Connors, Juice Robinson, Austin Gunn, Ace Austin, and Colten Gunn. During a discussion with Fightful's Sean Ross Sapp, Connors named Moloney and El Phantasmo as ideal additions to the group.

Connors explained that Moloney has built up his reputation in Japan and is ready for a change, stating that AEW could utilize him effectively.

> "If we’re looking specifically at The Cut, I know my boy Drilla Moloney. We left him back there in Japan because he wanted to keep making a name for himself but, I think he did. I think he made more of a name for himself but it’s time for him to go to greener pastures over here where we could really use him in a very efficient and a way that everyone could see how great he is."

He also expressed interest in bringing in El Phantasmo due to his history with the Bullet Club and his friendships with members of the stable.

> "Another one would be El Phantasmo, ELP. He’s a former BULLET CLUB guy as well and he’s cool with Jay; he’s cool with me and Fin and everybody so… I would love to have him around."
