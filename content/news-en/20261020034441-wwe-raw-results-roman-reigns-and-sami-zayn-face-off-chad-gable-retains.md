The October 19, 2026, episode of WWE RAW aired live from the Pechanga Arena in San Diego, California. The show featured a tense confrontation between Roman Reigns and Sami Zayn, as well as several high-stakes matches.

Roman Reigns opened the show and addressed Sami Zayn, questioning his motives for returning to the show and his current standing with The Bloodline. Zayn insisted he is carving his own path on SmackDown, leading to a heated exchange where Reigns criticized the current value of the Undisputed WWE Championship compared to the World Heavyweight Championship.

In the opening match, Penta defeated Bron Breakker following interference from Je'Von Evans. The women's tag team action saw Chelsea Green and IYO SKY defeat Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez after SKY rolled up Rodriguez for the win. JD McDonagh picked up a victory over Joe Hendry after smashing a guitar over his head. Lola Vice secured a win over Lyra Valkyria following a distraction from Bayley. In the main event, Chad Gable successfully defended his WWE Intercontinental Championship in a Triple Threat match against Rey Mysterio and Dragon Lee by pinning Mysterio with a small package.

Results:
- Penta def. Bron Breakker
- Chelsea Green and IYO SKY def. Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez
- JD McDonagh def. Joe Hendry
- Lola Vice def. Lyra Valkyria
- Chad Gable def. Rey Mysterio and Dragon Lee
