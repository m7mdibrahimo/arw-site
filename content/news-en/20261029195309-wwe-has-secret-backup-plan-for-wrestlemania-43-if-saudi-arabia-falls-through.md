WWE reportedly has a secret backup plan for WrestleMania 43 in case world events prevent the company from holding the event in Saudi Arabia. According to WrestleVotes on Fightful Select, sources confirmed that The Sphere in Las Vegas is not being considered as a backup location despite internal interest from WWE, as the two sides have not held serious discussions. Dave Meltzer also reported in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter that Las Vegas has scheduling conflicts and financial limitations that rule it out as a replacement. WrestleMania 43 is officially scheduled to take place in Saudi Arabia, although exact dates in April 2027 have not been announced. The company is moving forward with its plans, supported by a significant financial incentive, but a domestic backup blueprint remains in place secretly if circumstances require a change.
