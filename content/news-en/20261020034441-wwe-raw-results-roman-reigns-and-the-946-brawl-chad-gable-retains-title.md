WWE RAW on October 19, 2026, aired live from the Pechanga Arena in San Diego. The show featured a confrontation between Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn and Roman Reigns, leading to tension regarding the status of the World Heavyweight Championship. In the main event, Chad Gable successfully defended his WWE Intercontinental Championship against Rey Mysterio and Dragon Lee in a Triple Threat match. Following the match, Ethan Page and Big Cass launched a surprise attack on the participants. The show concluded with a chaotic brawl between The Bloodline and the group known as 946, which was broken up after a series of strikes. Penta def. Bron Breakker in a Singles Match. Chelsea Green and IYO SKY def. Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez in a Tag Team Match. JD McDonagh def. Joe Hendry in a Singles Match. Lola Vice def. Lyra Valkyria in a Singles Match. Chad Gable def. Rey Mysterio and Dragon Lee in a Triple Threat Match for the WWE Intercontinental Championship.
