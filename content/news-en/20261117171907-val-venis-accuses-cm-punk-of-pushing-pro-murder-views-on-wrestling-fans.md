Val Venis is hitting back at Bryan Idol after being confronted over his plans to protest outside the WWE Royal Rumble on February 6, 2027. Idol had criticized Venis for bringing political debates about abortion to wrestling events, arguing that it ruins the experience for fans. "@ValVenisEnt i shouldnt have to explain the damage you are doing to pro wrestling when you choose to stand outside the building of ANY company protesting issues or anything that can take away from a fans experience," Idol wrote. Venis defended his actions, claiming he only speaks to people who voluntarily engage with him on public sidewalks. He then shifted his focus to CM Punk, pointing to a shirt Punk wore that featured a political message regarding abortion rights. Venis characterized Punk’s stance as "pro-murder" and expressed frustration that Punk was allowed to display such views within a WWE-produced product. "On the other hand you had CM Punk IN THE PRODUCT PRODUCED PUSHED BY A BILLIONAIRE WITH GLOBAL REACH, RAMMING HIS PRO-MURDER POLITICAL POSITIONS DOWN THE THROATS OF PRO WRESTLING FANS, and your moronic ass claims I'm ruining wrestling," Venis stated. Venis previously protested outside AEW Full Gear on November 14, and he remains committed to appearing at the Royal Rumble despite Idol's objections.
