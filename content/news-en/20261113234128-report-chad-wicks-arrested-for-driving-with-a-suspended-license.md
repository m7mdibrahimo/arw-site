Chad Wicks was reportedly arrested on November 12. PWInsider reports that Wicks, also known to fans as Chad Dick, was booked into the Winnebago County Jail in Forest City, Iowa, at 5:47 PM local time for driving with a suspended license. Wicks previously wrestled in WWE as part of a tag team named The Dicks. Earlier in November, he was also arrested by the Cerro Gordo County Sheriff's Office on multiple counts of failing to appear in court before being released on a $1200 bond.
