Trey Miguel secured a championship opportunity after winning a ladder match on the latest episode of TNA iMPACT. The show featured the Hardys as guest bookers to celebrate their 35th anniversary in wrestling, setting up a high-stakes ladder match involving Miguel, Moose, Ricky Sosa, Elijah, and International Champion Jason Hotch for a shot at Leon Slater's title at Destination X. During the bout, Mustafa Ali interfered to cost Hotch his chance, while Nic Nemeth also got involved and sabotaged Elijah. Miguel ultimately stopped Nemeth from retrieving the contract, pulled him off the ladder, and claimed the victory. Destination X is scheduled for November 15 in Edmonton, Canada.
