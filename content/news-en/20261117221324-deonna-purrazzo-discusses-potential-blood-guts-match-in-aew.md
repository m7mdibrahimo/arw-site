Deonna Purrazzo addressed the possibility of competing in an AEW Blood & Guts match during a recent interview with Fox News/Outkick. She explained that she focuses entirely on storytelling when considering such violent stipulations.

> "I think that it would take a lot to get me there, but if we could tell the right story and have the right feud. When I started at AEW, I kind of had that going with Thunder Rosa."

She noted that she would require another deep rivalry on par with her past program against Thunder Rosa in order to participate in the grueling structure.

> "We had a no disqualification match, we had a lumberjack match, we ended up having the first ever women's televised Texas Bull Rope match on a ‘Collision’ right before All In 2024. So, I feel like that kind of story is what it takes to get to Blood and Guts, and I would love to be able to tell that story."
