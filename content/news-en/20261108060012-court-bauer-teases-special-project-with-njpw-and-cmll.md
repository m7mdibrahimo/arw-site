Court Bauer teased a special project involving MLW, NJPW, and CMLL. During MLW Saturday Night SuperFight, the MLW founder brought NJPW Chairman Naoki Sugabayashi into the ring to discuss their ongoing partnerships.

Bauer expressed gratitude to both promotions and revealed that they are collaborating on a "special project" set to arrive in 2027. The event also featured IWGP Heavyweight Champion Yota Tsuji facing Trevor Lee as part of the continued talent exchange.
