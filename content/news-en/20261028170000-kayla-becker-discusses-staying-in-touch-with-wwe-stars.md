Kayla Becker confirms she still keeps in touch with several people from WWE. Even though she left the promotion in 2024, the former Kayla Braxton continues to talk with a number of her former co-workers.

Becker revealed this during an interview, noting that colleagues constantly reach out to check on her or send congratulatory messages for special occasions.

> "A lot of people from the company still reach out and it always means a lot because it'll come out of nowhere just like to check and see how I'm doing or send a happy birthday message or I'm thinking of you message. It's such a family, that place. We all will part ways and go on our different journeys. I talked to Big E recently, I know he's still there to talk to him, I talk to the other boys of the New Day. Kevin Owens will reach out every once in a while. Bayley. Yeah, it's nice."
