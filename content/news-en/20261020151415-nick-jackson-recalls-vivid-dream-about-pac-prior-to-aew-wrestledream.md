Nick Jackson recently recalled a vivid dream he had about PAC prior to AEW WrestleDream and explained how he felt his presence at the event. PAC sadly passed away the day after AEW All Out, leading to numerous tributes from the AEW roster, including The Young Bucks. Speaking during the latest episode of Being The Elite, Nick Jackson shared details of his dream leading up to AEW WrestleDream. He recalled a scenario where they were told last minute about a singles match, noting that PAC reacted by worrying he was not good enough to do it last minute. Jackson added that the dream felt extremely real, noting that he could even feel PAC breathing on him. At the end of the match, Jackson felt his presence, looked up to the skies, and thanked PAC.
