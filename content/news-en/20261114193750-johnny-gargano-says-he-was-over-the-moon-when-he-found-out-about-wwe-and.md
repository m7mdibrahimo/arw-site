Johnny Gargano recently shared his excitement about WWE's partnership with Disney, revealing that he was thrilled when he first heard about the collaboration. Gargano and his wife Candice LeRae are well known for their love of Disney, often wearing ring gear inspired by Marvel and other Disney properties.

During an interview with WDW News Today, Gargano discussed how he and Candice reacted when they received a Disney-related questionnaire from the company. He said, "Oh, yeah. Immediately. As soon as I found out there was even an inkling of a talk between Disney and WWE and ESPN and things like that, I was over the moon." He added that he filled out the questionnaire in extreme detail, breaking down his favorite subjects like Star Wars, Marvel, and theme parks.

Gargano also noted that the partnership feels like a dream come true given the history of professional wrestling at Disney World. He concluded by saying that the news brought immense excitement to both him and Candice.
