During her interview on Ring The Belle, former WWE star Zelina Vega, now wrestling under the name Xelina, discussed her second run with the company and revealed that she was initially hesitant to become her husband Aleister Black's manager. Vega explained that despite management's plans to pair them together in that role, she made it clear she wanted to forge her own singles path instead. Although they were eventually paired for tag matches and other segments, Vega noted that things did not go the way she originally intended before they were both released from WWE in April as part of annual post-WrestleMania cuts. Now operating as free agents, both Vega and Black are currently making appearances for independent promotions such as House of Glory and PROGRESS Wrestling.
