The full lineup is set for NJPW x CMLL Lucha Kingdom, which takes place on November 16 from Arena Mexico. The event will be headlined by Mistico and Ultimo Guerrero facing Yota Tsuji and Hirooki Goto in a Relevos Increibles match. Other notable bouts include Hechicero defending the CMLL World Heavyweight Championship against Zack Sabre Jr., Syuri defending the IWGP Women's Championship, and several other interpromotional matchups featuring talent from both NJPW and CMLL.
