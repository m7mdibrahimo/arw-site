Sherilyn Guerrero recently made her in-ring debut at Reality of Wrestling's Fall Fury on November 14, defeating Mystii Marks by escaping a Gory Special and securing a roll-up pin with a handful of tights. Following the strong reception to her first match, Sherilyn discussed her future in an interview with Brad Gilmore, noting that she is not rushing her next steps.

Reflecting on her journey, she mentioned focusing heavily on training rather than mapping out her destination, expressing gratitude for the overwhelming response. While she is open to exploring opportunities with major promotions like WWE, AEW, and TNA, she is also happy to continue building her career at Reality of Wrestling.

Sherilyn shared that her mindset is inspired by her father Eddie Guerrero's career path, particularly his desire to learn and grow by wrestling in Japan and Mexico. Ahead of her debut, she gained experience at the WWE Performance Center and attended Bayley's Lodestone camp, and she now aims to keep growing and discovering her identity as Latina Heat.
