Conor McGregor is hinting at a transition to bare-knuckle fighting. As a stakeholder in Bare Knuckle Fighting Championships, he confirmed he intends to compete in that discipline soon. Speaking to Spitfire Gym via MMA Fighting, McGregor expressed his enthusiasm for the sport, noting it requires significant courage.

> "Bare knuckle; I really enjoy bare knuckle fighting. I don’t think there’s any chance of a boring fight in bare knuckle."

McGregor has one fight left on his UFC contract. Following an ACL and meniscus injury at UFC 329 against Max Holloway, his return date remains unknown.
