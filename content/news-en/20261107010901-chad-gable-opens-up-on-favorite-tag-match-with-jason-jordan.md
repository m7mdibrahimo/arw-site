Chad Gable recently opened up about his favorite tag team match with Jason Jordan during a recent social media Q&A. The two famously teamed up as American Alpha in WWE, capturing both the NXT Tag Team Championship and the SmackDown Tag Team Championship before parting ways in 2017. Reflecting on their time together, Gable recalled a memorable night in Blackpool, England, where the crowd chanted his name endlessly before the bell even rang. "There was a night in Blackpool, England, where the crowd applied a soccer/football chant to my name…They did it endlessly on loop to this chant that we had never heard before, before the bell even rang. It was one of those magical nights. The arena was awesome and just the atmosphere, it was like something we'd never felt. I think Jason and I both felt like we just took a massive leap forward that night in terms of terms of just chemistry and connecting with the crowd, all that stuff. That stands out big time in my mind," Gable shared. Elsewhere in the Q&A, Gable also discussed his Intercontinental Title loss to Ethan Page.
