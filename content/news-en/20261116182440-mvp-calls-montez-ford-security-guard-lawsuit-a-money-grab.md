MVP recently weighed in on the lawsuit filed against Montez Ford following an incident with a security guard named Noel Lucas during the July 6 episode of WWE RAW. Speaking on the November 16 episode of the Marking Out podcast, MVP expressed his opinion that the legal action is primarily an attempt to secure a payout. He noted that while Ford may have crossed a line with his actions, he does not believe the security guard suffered any physical harm.

> "I definitely think that on the part of the security guard, it's definitely a money grab — an easy opportunity to get paid. Did he get physically hurt? No. But were his feelings hurt? Probably. Embarrassment and humiliation — and nowadays, you get paid for that in court."

MVP believes the situation will likely be resolved through a settlement and a non-disclosure agreement. He also suggested that WWE management likely addressed the matter with Ford backstage to ensure such interactions do not happen again. 

> "I'm sure they had the conversation in the locker room — ‘DO NOT DO THIS.’ It's a good lesson for everyone, all pro wrestlers. There are certain lines that we shouldn't cross when interacting with fans or even with our own staff."

Lucas is suing Ford, WWE, and Event Services, LLC, alleging assault, battery, and intentional infliction of emotional distress. Ford is currently away from WWE television, though reports indicate this is due to the birth of his child with Bianca Belair rather than the lawsuit.
