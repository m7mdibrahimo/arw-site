IYO SKY captured the WWE Women's Intercontinental Championship on the November 16, 2026, episode of WWE RAW, defeating Raquel Rodriguez in a chaotic title match. The contest saw significant interference from The Judgment Day and other superstars throughout the bout.

Rodriguez utilized her size advantage early, but SKY countered with high-flying maneuvers, including a moonsault to the outside. Despite a dominant performance from Rodriguez, who repeatedly escaped the Tejana Bomb, SKY eventually turned the tide. Roxanne Perez attempted to distract SKY, but the challenger used the interference to her advantage, sending Rodriguez into Perez. Liv Morgan and Becky Lynch also joined the fray, brawling at ringside and adding to the disorder.

In the final moments, SKY countered a Tejana Bomb attempt and hit her signature Over the Moonsault to secure the pinfall victory. This win marks the start of SKY's reign as the new WWE Women's Intercontinental Champion, following an opportunity granted by Adam Pearce. The fallout from the interference by The Judgment Day and the ongoing tensions with Rodriguez, Morgan, and Lynch are expected to continue as Survivor Series approaches.
