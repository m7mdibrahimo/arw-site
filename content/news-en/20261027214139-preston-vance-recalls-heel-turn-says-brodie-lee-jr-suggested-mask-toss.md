Preston Vance discussed his transition to a villainous persona during a recent appearance on The Certified Mark Show. Vance recalled a November 2022 episode of AEW Rampage, where he departed from the Dark Order to join La Facción Ingobernable. During his exit, he encountered Brodie Lee Jr. and threw his Dark Order mask at the child's feet. Vance revealed that this specific moment was actually the idea of Brodie Lee Jr. himself. Vance noted that he was initially hesitant but ultimately followed the suggestion, describing the moment as a significant breakthrough in his wrestling career. Currently, Vance is involved in an ROH storyline where he is attempting to rejoin the Dark Order, though John Silver remains opposed to the idea.
