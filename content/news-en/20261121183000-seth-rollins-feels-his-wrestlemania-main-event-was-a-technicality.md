At the conclusion of WrestleMania 31, Seth Rollins captured the WWE World Heavyweight Championship by cashing in his Money in the Bank briefcase during the title match between Brock Lesnar and Roman Reigns. Although fans view the moment as legendary, Rollins feels the milestone is not as significant as supporters believe.

During an interview on "The Kittle Things," Rollins discussed how the booking unfolded.

> "Most people consider it the main event, like part of the main event, if you look at it on the line up, it would be because I changed the match to a Triple Threat, but I was not on the marquee."

> "So, at this point, there's many, many arguments to be had. I feel like I have never been in a main event at WrestleMania."

Rollins pointed out that he has featured in Night One main events and assisted with the Night Two main event at WrestleMania 40 alongside Cody Rhodes and Roman Reigns, but he was never the individual featured on the marquee to close out Night Two. He conceded that securing that specific main event spot remains a tough sell, while noting that he has several years remaining in his career. Even though he appeared on the poster for WrestleMania 40 alongside Reigns, Rhodes, and The Rock while serving as World Heavyweight Champion, he still declines to classify it as his own true main event encounter.
