Samoa Joe stated that his retirement timeline is still not quite nailed down and that he wants to leave as many good memories as he can for fans. During a recent interview with SHAK Wrestling, the AEW star talked about his career and future plans, explaining that his main focus is on putting on meaningful and fun matches.

Joe has competed across major promotions including TNA, Ring of Honor, WWE, and AEW throughout his extensive career. Regarding his future in the ring, he noted that while he does not have a specific retirement timeframe locked down yet, his goal is to make the most of his opportunities and create memorable moments for the audience.
