Will Ospreay will face Callum Newman at NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 21. Tensions have grown between the two since Ospreay left Newman in charge of United Empire, while Newman chose his own path. The two teamed up at NJPW Power Struggle to challenge the Knockout Bros for the IWGP Tag Team Titles, but the match ended in a disqualification when Newman used a chair. After a physical dispute during and after the match, Newman challenged his mentor for Wrestle Kingdom 21.
