Cody Rhodes recently survived a Last Man Standing Match against Randy Orton at WWE Crown Jewel. Following the event, Rhodes expressed interest in Rey Mysterio's proposal for a traditional five-on-five match between Team USA and Team Mexico at WWE Survivor Series. Rhodes stated that he would love to captain Team USA. Mysterio previously revealed on Stephanie McMahon’s podcast that he discussed the concept with Adam Pearce and Nick Aldis, intending to lead Team Mexico himself. With both veterans stepping up to lead their respective sides, the potential showdown continues to gather significant interest.
