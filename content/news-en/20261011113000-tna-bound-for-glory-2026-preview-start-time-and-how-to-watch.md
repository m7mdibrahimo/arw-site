TNA Bound for Glory takes place on October 11, 2026, at the Yuengling Center in Tampa, Florida. The event starts at 4:30 p.m. ET and streams live on TNA+ and via pay-per-view. The lineup features several championship matches and high-stakes bouts. Nic Nemeth defends the TNA World Heavyweight Title against Leon Slater, while Xia Brookside defends the TNA Knockouts World Title against Indi Hartwell with Mickie James serving as the special guest referee. Mustafa Ali puts the TNA International Title on the line in a four-way match against Special Agent 0, John Skyler, and Jason Hotch, and Rich Swann faces Ryan Nemeth, Cedric Alexander, Fabian Aichner, KC Navarro, and BDE in a six-way match for the TNA X-Division Title. Additionally, Allie and Kiera Hogan defend the TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Titles against Shayna Baszler and Zoey Serrano. The card also includes a Call Your Shot Gauntlet match, a falls count anywhere tag team match between The Hardys and Syx, and singles matches featuring Moose against Max Caster and Eddie Edwards against Ricky Sosa.
