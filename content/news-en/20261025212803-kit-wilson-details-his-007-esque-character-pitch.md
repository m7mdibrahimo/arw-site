Kit Wilson has shared details regarding his pitch to adopt a James Bond-inspired character. Speaking on the Battleground Podcast, Wilson discussed how he envisioned the persona, noting that it would require a slight haircut to fit the look. Wilson explained that being British makes a Bond connection natural for him, and he wanted to incorporate a full suit and a distinct entrance, walking down the ramp and removing his jacket and shirt before wrestling in his black suit trousers. Elsewhere in the interview, Wilson mentioned that he would love for BLACKPINK to perform his WrestleMania entrance.
