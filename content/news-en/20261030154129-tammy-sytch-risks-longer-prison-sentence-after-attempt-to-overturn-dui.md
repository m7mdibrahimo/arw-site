Tammy Sytch is facing the risk of a longer prison sentence after her attempt to overturn her DUI manslaughter conviction was dismissed. According to PWInsider, Sytch filed a motion seeking to throw out her conviction based on claims of ineffective assistance from her defense attorney regarding the March 2022 crash that resulted in the death of 75-year-old Julian Lasseter. Her motion cited pre-existing medical conditions, toxicology findings, and a potential conflict of interest involving the prosecution, but a judge dismissed the filing as procedurally insufficient. The court warned that if she successfully challenges her negotiated plea, prosecutors would no longer be bound by the deal and could reinstate dropped charges, potentially exposing her to up to 26 years in prison compared to her current 17.5-year sentence.
