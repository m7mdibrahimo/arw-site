Jacy Jayne recently discussed her experience since joining the WWE main roster as part of Fatal Influence. Jayne admitted that she felt a sense of emptiness before securing the WWE Women's United States Championship from Tiffany Stratton on the August 14 episode of WWE SmackDown.

> "Oh, I love it. You know, it felt really weird not having a title, because I had a title for so long before I came up. I felt naked, like, ‘Ugh, I don’t feel like me.’ Then I won this off Tiffany Stratton and I feel whole again."

Beyond her own success, Jayne expressed pride in her stablemates' achievements. She noted that watching Lainey win gold felt like seeing a family member succeed after overcoming self-doubt. Jayne is currently preparing for the WWE Women's Money In The Bank match.
