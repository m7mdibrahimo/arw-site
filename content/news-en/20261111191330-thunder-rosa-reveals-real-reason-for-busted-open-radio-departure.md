Thunder Rosa has provided further insight into her exit from Busted Open Radio after a four-year tenure. Months after departing the SiriusXM show, she explained that she would have remained if not for shifting circumstances behind the scenes. During an appearance on the I Love Wrestling Podcast, Rosa credited Dave LaGreca for her initial opportunity in wrestling radio and noted that management changes ultimately led to her departure.

> "David LaGreca, there's another one that gave me my breakthrough in radio. He gave me that opportunity. He believed in me, and I was on radio for four years, and I would have stayed on the radio, but, you know, management changes and things happen."

Rosa stated she holds no grudges and misses the platform. Her exit occurred in July as WWE increased its presence on SiriusXM, leaving her out of the new lineup. She emphasized that this is not the end of her media career, as she plans to launch her own podcast to speak freely on any topic.
