Drew McIntyre is currently away from WWE, having been off television for over six months. While his return date remains uncertain, the former champion still feels he has unfinished business with several stars in the company. During a recent appearance on The Joe Vulpis Podcast, McIntyre addressed his current status.

> "Unfinished business with more people than I can count. I think a lot of them got unfinished business with me because they're all quite frankly jealous. Imagine how jealous they are that I'm Hollywood Drew now. Yeah, Hollywood Drew here. He's got places to be and people to see. Got this watch here, recommended by Russell [Crowe]. I mean, realistically, I've not been full of myself but you know, WWE and the fans need me. I don't need y’all, but I'm still going to be there for you because I truly am a good man. The last last real good guy."

McIntyre noted that he still considers himself the "last real good guy" in the industry. He also touched upon his past rivalries with CM Punk, Roman Reigns, and Jey Uso, while discussing the possibility of working with Russell Crowe in WWE.
