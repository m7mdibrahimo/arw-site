Juice Robinson was reportedly taken to the hospital following a bloody backstage scene on the November 7, 2026 episode of AEW Collision. Earlier in the night, Jay White led his group into a confrontation with Jon Moxley and Death Riders after defeating Shane Taylor Promotions. The altercation turned physical, with Robinson and Ace Austin assisting in taking down Moxley as Mercedes Moné watched and laughed. White subsequently executed a Blade Runner on Moxley, leaving Death Riders laid out.

Later in the broadcast, Lexy Nair presented a backstage area where blood covered the floor alongside two forks. Robinson was absent and later confirmed to be hospitalized, while White retrieved one of the forks and signaled his intent to pursue Death Riders. Although the segment strongly implied Death Riders were responsible for Robinson's condition, AEW did not air the actual attack or definitively prove who carried it out, intensifying the ongoing rivalry ahead of Full Gear.
