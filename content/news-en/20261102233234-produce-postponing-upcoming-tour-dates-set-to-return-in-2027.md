PRODUCE is taking a hiatus. The promotion announced on Monday that it is postponing its upcoming tour dates following its partner shows in New York on November 14 and 15, including previously scheduled events in Florida and Canada. The company intends to return in February 2027 for its "Most Wanted" event in Los Angeles.

In a statement, the promotion cited several reasons for the decision, including "unforeseen family medical circumstances, increasing travel costs, and ongoing uncertainty in the live events industry." The company noted that it will use this time to reset and work with new partners to ensure the project's long-term health. PRODUCE added that it will continue working with creative and promotional partners on content releases and collaborative opportunities for its fighters.

Tickets for the postponed shows in New York, Florida, and Montreal will be refunded. The company is scheduled to return to The Vermont in Los Angeles on February 25, 2027, for PRODUCE: Most Wanted.
