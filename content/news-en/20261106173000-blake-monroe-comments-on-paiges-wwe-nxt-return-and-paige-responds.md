Paige recently competed on WWE NXT for the first time in 12 years as the surprise challenger for NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan, though Jordan managed to retain her title. While supporters celebrated the appearance, Blake Monroe took to social media to dismiss the comeback as a nostalgic act. Paige fired back at Monroe, challenging her to earn a comparable fan reaction.

The two athletes previously competed in AEW, where they squared off multiple times, including a 2024 Owen Hart Foundation Tournament quarterfinal match won by Monroe, who wrestled under the name Mariah May at the time. After her recent loss to Jordan, Paige disclosed that she entered the championship bout at the eleventh hour following a late alteration to the original plans.
