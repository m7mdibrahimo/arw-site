Jeff Cobb recently reflected on his time in WWE as JC Mateo, specifically comparing the experience of wearing face paint to wearing a mask in Lucha Underground. While performing with the MFT faction alongside Tama Tonga and Talla Tonga, Cobb was required to wear paint. According to Fightful, Cobb found the paint more annoying than a mask because it was difficult to remove. He noted that officials mistakenly told him he would wear paint like he did in Japan, despite never having done so. 

> "Paint. Because I've never done that before. Although, to be fair, I've never done the mask thing before. But the paint was truly different. Because I was told, you're going to wear the paint like you did in Japan, and I never wore paint in Japan. I think they got me confused with somebody else. But yeah, paint was hard because I had to find paint. But, yeah, the paint was definitely the hardest just for the fact that, like, with the mask, I could put it on. I couldn't breathe, but I could put it on, and then when I'm done, I could take it off. But the paint took forever to get off. I had to use the wipes. Then I found out the wipes that I used got recalled."
