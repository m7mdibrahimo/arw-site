AEW Full Gear 2026 delivered an eventful night featuring a Texas Death match, Anarchy in the Arena, new women's tag team champions, and MJF returning to confront the world champion. Reviewing the show, Wrestling Inc. highlighted three things they hated and three things they loved. On the negative side, the women's tag team match lacked sufficient build, suffering from Jamie Hayter's injury and poor placement right after the intense Texas Death match, leading to a tired crowd.

Additionally, the critique noted that putting on two massive, chaotic stipulation matches like Texas Death and Anarchy in the Arena on the same night made the event exhausting. Finally, MJF's return to bring back his 'real world champion' storyline felt predictable, on a night with few other surprise returns or debuts, possibly because of travel delays. On the positive side, The Young Bucks delivered an exceptional David versus Goliath match against Mark Davis and Jake Doyle, showcasing expert storytelling.

Furthermore, the Trios World Championship Texas Death match, in which New Level and Swerve Strickland recaptured the titles, was hailed as an incredible, violent, 40-minute war of attrition. Lastly, the main event between Will Ospreay and Kyle Fletcher delivered an all-time classic, with raw emotion and a brilliant closing stretch.
