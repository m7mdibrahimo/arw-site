Rob Van Dam admitted he actually feels guilty about influencing certain modern deathmatch wrestlers because they copied the crazy spots without understanding the wrestling behind them. During an appearance on Stories With Brisco And Bradshaw, RVD explained that while he is glad to have inspired future hardcore wrestlers, some participants in modern deathmatch wrestling do not even need to know how to wrestle to participate. RVD noted that people saw him in ECW performing high-flying moves and hitting tables without realizing the foundational wrestling training that supported it.

He insisted there is a massive difference between the hardcore style he performed and some of the extreme deathmatch insanity happening today. RVD previously criticized AEW's violent approach following Jon Moxley's spike-covered 2×4 spot, maintaining that extreme punishment does not automatically make something good wrestling.
