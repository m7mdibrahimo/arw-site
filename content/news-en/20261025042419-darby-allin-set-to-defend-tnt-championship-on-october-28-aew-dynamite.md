Darby Allin will defend his TNT Championship against Josh Alexander on the October 28 edition of AEW Dynamite: Fright Night in St. Louis, Missouri. The match was made official following Alexander's dominant victory over Blade Brown on the October 24 episode of AEW Collision. After the win, Alexander, a member of the Don Callis Family, grabbed a microphone to express his desire for a championship. He specifically challenged Allin, promising that if he wins the TNT title, he intends to defend it against Adam Copeland. Copeland confronted Alexander and the Don Callis Family, leading to a brawl that resulted in Mark Davis and Jake Doyle attacking Copeland. AEW confirmed the title match shortly after the confrontation.
