Nikki Bella mocked Chelsea Green's WWE entrance during an NFL game in Paris while promoting their upcoming WWE Women's Championship match on SmackDown. Bella posted a video on Instagram wearing Green's signature hat and mimicking her entrance before switching to her own theme song. She also took a shot at the champion by reminding her that she is already in the WWE Hall of Fame. Green earned momentum heading into the title match by defeating Roxanne Perez on SmackDown, despite interference from Raquel Rodriguez and IYO SKY. Green is also scheduled to face Liv Morgan at Crown Jewel on November 7.
