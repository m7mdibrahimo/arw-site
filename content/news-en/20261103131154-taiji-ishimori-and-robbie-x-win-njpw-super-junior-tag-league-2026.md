Unbound Co. consisting of Robbie X and Taiji Ishimori won NJPW Super Junior Tag League 2026 after defeating Ichiban Sweet Boys featuring Robbie Eagles and Kosei Fujita in the final at NJPW Power Struggle. The conclusion saw Ishimori pin Fujita with an inside cradle. Following the match, Mistico appeared carrying both tag team titles as one half of the champions, announcing he was vacating the championships due to the injury and current absence of his partner El Desperado before Ishimori proposed a three-way match.

This marks the first time Ishimori and Robbie X have captured the tournament crown after losing the 2025 final to House of Torture. Unbound Co. topped A Block with a 4-1 record during the round robin stage.
