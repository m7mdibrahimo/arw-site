There has been much discussion about the death of the independent wrestling scene after PRODUCE and AIW announced hiatuses. These hiatuses are not the first in recent years, as promotions like DEADLOCK Pro and Prestige have also gone on hiatus or lost venues. The topic of independent wrestling being dead seems to surface every couple of years. With the indies facing uncertainty once again, independent wrestlers have shared their thoughts on the current state of affairs. Veteran Dominic Garrini wrote a guest column on POST Wrestling to discuss the topic.
