Jay White has officially named his new AEW alliance, but not before hinting at a potential Bullet Club comeback. During the October 24, 2026, episode of AEW Collision, White addressed the partnership between the Bang Bang Gang and The Dogs, confirming the two groups will now operate under a single name. The announcement followed a major alliance formed on AEW Dynamite, where David Finlay and Clark Connors joined Jay White, Juice Robinson, Ace Austin, Austin Gunn, and Colten Gunn after an attack on the Death Riders. During his segment on Collision, White teased using the Bullet Club name, referencing his and Finlay's history with the group in New Japan Pro Wrestling. However, he ultimately revealed that the unified faction will be known as The Last Outfit. This new group merges the Bang Bang Gang and The Dogs into one seven-member unit, adding a significant new layer to the company's build toward Full Gear. The formation of The Last Outfit leaves the Death Riders with a major challenge as the group establishes its presence in AEW.
