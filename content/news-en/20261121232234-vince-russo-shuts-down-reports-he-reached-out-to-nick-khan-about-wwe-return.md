Vince Russo has strongly pushed back against reports claiming he contacted WWE President Nick Khan to seek a return to the company. Speaking on Two Man Power Trip with John Poz, the former WWE head writer addressed the rumors that gained traction in recent months, making it clear that he never sought work from Khan.

> "One thing I will say that never, ever happened. I've never, John, I will tell you this, since I left in '99, so in 27 years, I've never reached out to the WWE for a job, especially at 65 years old, bro. That's such an effing joke."

Russo questioned the logic behind the reports, pointing out that he has never met Nick Khan or had his contact information. He stated that if he ever wanted to discuss a creative position, he would have called Triple H instead, given their past relationship. Russo also emphasized that he has no financial need to chase work with WWE, citing the steady revenue generated by The Brand over the past 14 years alongside his work with Jonathan Coachman and JCW. He reiterated that his exit from JCW was completely unrelated to WWE.
