A recent report has clarified the reason for Komander’s absence from AEW and ROH programming. The luchador has been sidelined due to an injury, though current expectations suggest his return to the ring could be near.

According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, Komander has not competed in recent weeks because of this injury. Meltzer did not provide specific details regarding the nature or severity of the injury, but he expressed hope that the wrestler will be able to return to action soon.

Komander last wrestled on September 20, teaming with Bandido against Mascara Dorada and Soberano Jr. for CMLL. Prior to that, he challenged Titan for the CMLL World Welterweight Championship on September 18. His final AEW appearance took place on the September 2 episode of Collision against Nick Wayne. There is currently no confirmed information regarding his return date or future plans.
