Trick Williams has addressed Damian Priest questioning whether he really deserves a World Championship opportunity. Speaking on Double Coverage with Mr. Exact, Trick explained his background and everything it took for him to reach WWE. He recalled playing football for 20 years, earning a scholarship to Hampton University, and walking on at the University of South Carolina to become a starter under Steve Spurrier.

After a tough senior year under Will Muschamp, he worked as a substitute teacher and coached kids while trying to make it to the NFL without an agent. He made it to the Philadelphia Eagles camp before being cut and eventually finding his way to a WWE tryout. Trick detailed how COVID disrupted his training in Philadelphia, leading him to move to Los Angeles where he split rent with strangers, delivered food for DoorDash and Uber Eats, and woke up at 4 a.m. to teach kids on the East Coast while training in wrestling with Rikishi at KnokX Pro.

When Priest claims he does not deserve a World Championship match, Trick feels that dismisses his entire journey and relentless work ethic. He stated that someone spending 15 years on the independent circuit does not automatically make their journey more valid than his, though he acknowledged that veteran wrestlers may look more deserving on paper.
