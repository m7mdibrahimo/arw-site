Rey Mysterio revealed that he is in talks with Adam Pearce and Nick Aldis about organizing a classic five-on-five Survivor Series match between Team USA and Team Mexico at this year's Survivor Series event. Speaking on Stephanie McMahon's What’s Your Story? podcast, Mysterio tied the idea to the growing global influence of lucha libre and WWE's partnership with AAA, pointing out stars like Penta, Rey Fénix, Dragon Lee, and El Grande Americano. Mysterio has not yet confirmed whether he will compete in the match himself, noting that they are still figuring out the details.
