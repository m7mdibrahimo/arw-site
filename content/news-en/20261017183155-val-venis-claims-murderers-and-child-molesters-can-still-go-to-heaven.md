Former WWE star Val Venis shared a controversial perspective during a recent interview, stating that even murderers and child molesters can attain salvation and enter heaven if their acceptance of Jesus Christ is completely genuine. Venis acknowledged that this idea is difficult for people to accept on a human level, but he emphasized his belief that Christ's sacrifice applies to all sins, provided there is real repentance. The conversation also touched upon the late Chris Benoit, with Venis noting that Benoit had portrayed himself as a believer, though ultimately only God knows the true state of his soul before his tragic death in 2007.
