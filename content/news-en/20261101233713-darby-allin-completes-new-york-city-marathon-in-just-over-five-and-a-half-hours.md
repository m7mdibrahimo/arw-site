Darby Allin participated in the New York City Marathon on Sunday, finishing with a time of five hours, 34 minutes, and 34 seconds. The event covered 26.219 miles through the five boroughs, with over 60,000 runners taking part. Allin maintained an average pace of 12 minutes and 46 seconds per mile.

This athletic feat follows Allin's climb of Mount Everest last year. He has been active this week, making his CMLL debut on Friday against Hechicero. Allin is also set to compete in the Blood & Guts match this Wednesday, where he will team up with Steven Borden, the Young Bucks, and Will Ospreay to face the Don Callis Family.
