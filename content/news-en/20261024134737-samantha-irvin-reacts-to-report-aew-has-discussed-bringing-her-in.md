Samantha Irvin has reacted to reports regarding AEW's interest in bringing her into the company. The former WWE ring announcer is nearing the end of her two-year non-compete agreement following her departure in 2024. Reports indicate that AEW has internally discussed bringing her in for an on-screen role, though an immediate television appearance is not currently planned. Irvin responded to the news on social media with a playful message acknowledging the internal discussions. While she has connections to AEW through her husband, Ricochet, and has attended several backstage events, she has not yet confirmed any formal negotiations or plans to join the promotion.
