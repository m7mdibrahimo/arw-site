Swerve Strickland does not believe WWE and AEW will ever hold a crossover event in his lifetime. In an interview with Billboard, Strickland explained that the main obstacles are control, marketing, and the need for both companies to protect their top stars. He noted that top wrestlers like Gunther, Roman Reigns, Cody Rhodes, Rhea Ripley, Liv Morgan, and Chelsea Green must be protected, making it difficult to decide who wins dream matches like Seth Rollins versus Will Ospreay. While Strickland mentioned he and his friends in the locker room, such as Carmelo Hayes, Jevon Evans, and Oba Femi, would happily have these matches without caring about wins and losses, he feels the business side makes a supershow nearly impossible.
