Kacy Catanzaro reflected on her title history in WWE, specifically looking back at her reign with the NXT Women's Tag Team Championship alongside Kayden Carter. The duo held the NXT titles for 186 days before later capturing the WWE Women's Tag Team Championship for a 39-day reign.

Speaking with Fightful, Catanzaro recalled the long storyline buildup leading to their eventual victory in NXT after multiple failed attempts.

> "I'm really glad the way it happened because when we finally did it was just so worth it. So I just remember those moments. It's hard because when someone tells you that you're going to get something, it's like, 'Okay, they're just saying you're getting it.' But to feel from the crowd and the people on the journey with you that you've earned it."

Catanzaro added that both of their championship wins felt validating, as it showed the company trusted them with the titles.
