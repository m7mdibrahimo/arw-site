AJ Lee and CM Punk celebrated Halloween this year by transforming into characters from the Resident Evil franchise. The former WWE stars channeled the iconic survival horror series, with AJ Lee dressing as Jill Valentine and CM Punk taking on the role of Leon S. Kennedy. AJ Lee shared the transformation on Instagram, featuring a detailed photoshoot inspired by the games. She recreated Jill Valentine's look from the original title, while CM Punk mirrored Leon S. Kennedy's appearance from Resident Evil 4, complete with the character's signature coat, hairstyle, and weapons. The couple utilized backdrops resembling Raccoon City to complete the setting. AJ Lee captioned the reveal with a nod to the franchise, stating: 

> "I’ll give you S.T.A.R.S… Happy Halloween from Jill Valentine and Leon S. Kennedy! Designed by @sandymimpson @bakingjason"

The couple, known for sharing their interest in gaming culture, managed to blend two different generations of the series for their costumes. Fans have since been debating which wrestler delivered the more authentic performance.
