Nic Nemeth recently discussed his long-standing reputation for being one of the best sellers in professional wrestling. During an interview, Nemeth explained that he initially had to tone down his style after starting his training during the Reality Era. He noted that while he wanted his bumps to look like MMA knockouts, his high-level selling often drew concern from Vince McMahon.

> 20 or 30 times I came to the back with Vince going, 'Oh, my God, are you okay?' And I go, 'This is what you pay me for, brother.'

Nemeth revealed that a high-ranking official once pulled him aside and explicitly asked him to stop being so effective at selling and putting his opponents over. Despite this, he struggled to change his approach because he took great pride in his work. He also recalled how frequently taking the Brogue Kick from Sheamus eventually impacted his trajectory, noting that he could not realistically be positioned as a World Champion while constantly being booked to take major finishes every week.
