All Elite Wrestling presented a new episode of Dynamite on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, broadcast live on TBS from the Mission Ballroom in Denver, Colorado. The show featured a main event clash between Bandido and Hangman Adam Page.

Orange Cassidy def. Jon Moxley in a Gold League Continental Classic match.

Following the match, Nick Wayne interrupted Orange Cassidy and Jon Moxley, asserting that he would emerge victorious in the Continental Classic tournament.

Nick Wayne def. Mike Bailey in another Gold League Continental Classic match.

Darby Allin delivered a message declaring that he was coming for Wardlow and Kyle Fletcher. Don Callis cut a promo announcing that he had a plan for every championship in All Elite Wrestling.

The Young Bucks and Motor City Machine Guns appeared to challenge FTR and the Kasper Brothers for Winter Is Coming, and Stokely Hathaway accepted the challenge.

Kasper Brothers (Drew Kasper and Jacob Kasper) def. two enhancement talents.

MJF appeared for a promo detailing three specific instances involving himself and Will Ospreay, ultimately declaring himself the true champion of AEW. The Death Riders delivered a backstage promo, with PAC calling out The Dogs and Daniel Garcia stating that it was time for the playoffs.

Samoa Joe def. Jack Perry.

Ilja Dragunov was interviewed backstage before Don Callis interrupted him. Dragunov informed Callis that he would not join the family, though he remained willing to defend his title against anyone in it, prompting Callis to announce that Dragunov will face El Clon on AEW Collision.

Swerve Strickland and The New Level (Austin Creed and Kofi) appeared for a promo to celebrate recapturing the Trios Tag Team Titles. Don Callis interrupted to offer them a spot in the family, but they rejected the proposal, leading the entire family to surround the ring. After Strickland and The New Level brandished chains, Prince Nana stated that they should now be known as The Dangerous New Level.

The Cut and Mercedes Moné, accompanied by the USA Women's Freestyle Wrestling Team, appeared for a promo celebrating their wins at AEW Full Gear. AZM cut the celebration short and forced The Cut and Moné out of the ring.

Queen Aminata def. AZM, Maya World and Mina Shirakawa to retain the AEW TBS Championship.

Hangman Page was interviewed backstage, and Swerve Strickland interrupted to state that Page still owed him. Andrade El Idolo was interviewed by Tony Schiavone, declaring his intention to pursue the AEW World Title. Kyle Fletcher interrupted to request a rematch against Will Ospreay for the World Title. After Andrade told him to step away from Ospreay's show, Fletcher challenged El Idolo to a match for the following week.

Bandido def. Hangman Adam Page in a Blue League Continental Classic match in the main event.
