WWE creative staff reportedly have concerns that ongoing audience discussion regarding a potential return for Enzo Amore is overshadowing the current singles push of Big Cass. According to WrestleVotes via Fightful Select, some writers believe fans are more focused on waiting for Amore than engaging with Cass as an independent competitor. This expectation complicates plans since Amore is not currently signed with WWE. Dave Meltzer previously noted on Wrestling Observer Radio that management initially wanted to keep the two apart to maximize Cass as a solo star. However, recent booking decisions, including a loss to Dragon Lee on the September 28 episode of RAW, have raised questions about WWE's long-term vision for Cass as a singles competitor.
