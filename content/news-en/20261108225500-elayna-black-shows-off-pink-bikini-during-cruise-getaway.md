Elayna Black is taking some time away from the ring to enjoy a vacation. The former Cora Jade recently shared several photos from a cruise trip on her Instagram, showing her relaxing in a pink bikini while posing on the ship. While the update was mostly about her time in the sun, the final slide of the post included a mysterious message that read: "they just ain’t you." Black has been navigating life outside of WWE since her departure, even launching an OnlyFans account after she felt forced to explore new opportunities. She eventually returned to wrestling with TNA, but her latest social media post focused entirely on her cruise experience, leaving fans to speculate about the meaning behind her cryptic final message.
