Tatum Paxley is reflecting on her recent championship success. On the October 9th episode of WWE SmackDown, Paxley and Alexa Bliss defeated Fatal Influence (Fallon Henley and Lainey Reid) to capture the WWE Women's Tag Team Championship.

During an appearance on Off The Ropes, Paxley discussed the surreal nature of the moment.

> "Literally everything that I’m doing was something I could not imagine. It’s just so unbelievable. I remember years ago — over five years ago at this point — sitting at the job I was working at, at a supplement store. I would have my phone propped up against a computer and I was just watching matches of hers on YouTube. Just going, ‘Wow, this looks really fun. I think I can do this. I would like to start doing this.’ Then to fast forward, I have a match against her, and then to fast forward, now I am a champion with her on SmackDown."

Paxley emphasized her gratitude for the opportunity.

> "I feel like when I look back to it and I was watching those matches of hers, it was like I was just manifesting this whole thing and I didn’t even realize it. I’m just so unbelievably thankful and blessed to be in the position that I am in right now, and even when I was holding those titles up in Lafayette and I look over and I see Alexa Bliss and I look out and I see all the fans, I hear the pyro — I think that was the first time I had pyro. I was like, ‘Whoa. This is real.’ It’s real!"

This victory marks Paxley's first title win on the main roster, while Alexa Bliss is now a five-time WWE Women's Tag Team Champion.
