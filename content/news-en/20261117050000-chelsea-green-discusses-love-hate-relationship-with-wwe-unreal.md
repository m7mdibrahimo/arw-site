Wrestler Chelsea Green recently discussed her experience with the Netflix series "WWE Unreal," which offers fans a behind-the-scenes look at the lives of WWE executives and Superstars.

During a recent episode of "What Do You Wanna Talk About," Green revealed that she experiences mixed feelings regarding the show, explaining:

> "It's a love-hate relationship because, if I'm being very honest, I was told that Unreal was going to be something different."

She noted that despite the exhaustion of constant camera tracking, the outcome ultimately benefited her.

Over the course of three seasons, the program has showcased Green's journey toward championship success, the construction of her dream home, and her concerns regarding starting a family. The series also highlighted management's assessment of her position, with Bruce Prichard labeling her as a versatile asset and Michael Hayes suggesting she was meant to elevate others rather than headline. Hayes later apologized for his comments prior to Green capturing a world title at the 2026 SummerSlam premium live event.
