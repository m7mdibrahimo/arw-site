Ilja Dragunov has officially joined AEW. The former WWE star made his debut at AEW WrestleDream on October 17, 2026, ending weeks of speculation regarding his future. Renee Paquette introduced Dragunov to the crowd, and he appeared with his familiar character name. Dragunov walked to the ring, circled it, and left without speaking or engaging in a match. The announcers confirmed his status as "All Elite."

His arrival was anticipated, as AEW had been teasing it with vignettes during the October 14 episodes of AEW Dynamite and AEW Collision. These clips featured a man conducting an orchestra, a metronome on a mat, and someone running around a ring, which were later confirmed to be for Dragunov. Dragunov became a free agent in August following the expiration of his WWE contract. His debut was reportedly delayed due to visa issues, but he is now officially signed to the company.
