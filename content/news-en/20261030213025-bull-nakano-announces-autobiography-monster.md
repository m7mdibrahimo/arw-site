WWE Hall of Famer Bull Nakano announced on social media that her new autobiography, titled Monster, will be released by Wani Books on January 8, 2027.

The book covers behind-the-scenes stories from her long career, detailing her historic matches against Aja Kong, her time in the Gokumon-to faction, golden cage matches, and her life after retirement.

Nakano wrestled for over 30 years before her retirement in November 2017, and she was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2024.
