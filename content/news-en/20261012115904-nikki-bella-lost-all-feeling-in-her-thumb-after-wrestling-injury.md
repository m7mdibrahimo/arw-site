Nikki Bella revealed that she still cannot feel her thumb due to an untreated injury from her WWE career. During an appearance on The Nikki & Brie Show with Landon Dickerson, she explained that the injury occurred during a match when her thumb got trapped underneath an opponent during a quick victory roll pin. Despite her thumb remaining out of place and losing all sensation, she never underwent the surgery that doctors originally recommended. She noted that she can still grip items despite the permanent damage, serving as a reminder of the lasting physical toll wrestling can take.
