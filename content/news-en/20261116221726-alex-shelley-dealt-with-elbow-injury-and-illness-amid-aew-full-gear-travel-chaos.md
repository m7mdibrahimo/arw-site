Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin faced significant travel difficulties before and after their match at AEW Full Gear. The Motor City Machine Guns experienced a three-hour delay, a five-hour flight, and another three hours sitting on the tarmac before arriving. Shelley also revealed that both wrestlers suffered from an upper respiratory infection, and he burst a bursa sac in his elbow. Despite these issues and a flight cancellation the following morning, the duo rented a car and drove from San Francisco to Phoenix to ensure they made the pay-per-view. Shelley emphasized that they enjoyed the experience, stating that they would do it all over again because the work is rewarding.
