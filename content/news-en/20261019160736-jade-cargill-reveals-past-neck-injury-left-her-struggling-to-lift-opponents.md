WWE Superstar Jade Cargill recently opened up about a neck injury she dealt with approximately two years ago, revealing that a pinched muscle severely affected her strength and made it difficult to lift opponents. Speaking in a workout video on YouTube, Cargill explained that performing simple exercises like pull-ups has become deeply meaningful to her as she works to maintain her physique. She noted that getting older in the wrestling industry has made her place greater importance on stretching, recovery, and overall body maintenance rather than constantly pushing for heavier weights in the gym.
