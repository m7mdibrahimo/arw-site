Nia Jax entered her WWE tryout believing her physical size would prevent her from joining the company. Instead of being pressured to alter her appearance, she received a supportive reaction from the coaching staff.

During an appearance on the Keep It Aloha podcast, Jax recalled her early insecurities while surrounded by women from fitness and cheerleading backgrounds. She credited Joey Mercury, Billy Gunn, and Sarah Amato for recognizing that her frame was an asset. According to Jax, the coaches never once suggested she slim down.

> "So I had that leg up that I didn't realize when I first came in. I was like, 'Oh, no. I got to lose all this weight. I got to be this. I got to be that.' But they were like, 'No.' Not once did they ever tell me you got to lose weight. They were like, 'No, you're perfect. Like, we love this. This is the way you are."

Jax noted that her Polynesian heritage helped her embrace physical strength. Rather than conforming to a traditional fitness model image, she built her identity around being imposing, which helped establish her as the Irresistible Force.
