Ilja Dragunov recently commented on GUNTHER's current momentum in WWE following his high-profile retirement of John Cena. Dragunov and GUNTHER share a long history dating back to their time in WWE NXT UK, where Dragunov famously ended GUNTHER's historic 870-day title reign. Reflecting on GUNTHER's recent career trajectory, which also includes wins over Goldberg and AJ Styles, Dragunov noted that his push appears to have plateaued. During an appearance on The Ariel Helwani Show, Dragunov shared his thoughts on the matter.

> I guess my feelings are the same feelings as a lot of other people share. If you are the person to retire John Cena, it's kind of like a standard that you should keep a certain level of that, in my opinion.

Dragunov praised GUNTHER as one of the all-time greats and a dear friend since 2014 or 2015, expressing confidence in his future success. However, he admitted that the standard set by such a massive milestone makes his current booking feel slightly lower by comparison. Dragunov left WWE this past August after seven years and made his AEW debut at WrestleDream. Meanwhile, GUNTHER is currently feuding with Finn Balor while awaiting another world title opportunity.
