Queen Aminata discussed her emotions following her victory over Persephone to win the AEW TBS Championship on "AEW Fright Night Dynamite." In a video published on her YouTube channel, she explained that she did not expect to capture the title this quickly, expressing immense joy at the culmination of her five-year journey.

Aminata became emotional and shed tears while remembering her late father, wishing he could have witnessed her success in professional wrestling and dedicating the major achievement to him. She also thanked all her fans for their continuous support and the congratulatory messages she received after winning the title.
