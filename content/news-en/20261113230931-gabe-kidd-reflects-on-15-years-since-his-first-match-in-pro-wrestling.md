Gabe Kidd recently celebrated the 15th anniversary of his professional wrestling debut. Kidd is currently a member of the Death Riders in AEW and is scheduled to compete in an Anarchy in the Arena match at AEW Full Gear. Taking to social media, Kidd marked the milestone, writing:

> "12th November 2026 marked 15 years since my first professional wrestling match. We did it kid. And the most beautiful part, the work is nowhere near done. Keep going, Young Legend."

Recently, Kidd also shared an emotional moment involving Jon Moxley during PAC's tribute show.
