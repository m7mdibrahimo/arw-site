Professional wrestling legend Lance Storm has criticized the upcoming John Cena Classic, arguing that the event undermines the core principles of the industry. Speaking on an episode of Wrestling Observer Live alongside Bryan Alvarez, Storm took issue with the tournament's format, which relies on fan votes to determine the winner, pointing out that it allows wrestlers to potentially sabotage one another. Storm compared the situation to WCW Starrcade 1997, where he claimed Hulk Hogan undermined Sting's return to protect his own spot. He also argued that the event trivializes the value of being a champion and devalues fans' investments in wins and losses. The John Cena Classic is scheduled to take place at the ESPN Wide World of Sports in Walt Disney World on December 14, with the winner decided by fan votes via Club WWE.
