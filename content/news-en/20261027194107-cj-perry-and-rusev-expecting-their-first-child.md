CJ Perry and Miro are expecting their first child together after a challenging and emotional IVF journey. Perry revealed that she is pregnant with a baby boy and is due on April 28, 2027. The couple began IVF in 2025 and completed their embryo transfer on August 10, 2026, followed by a tense nine-day wait before confirming the pregnancy. Perry shared that hearing their son's heartbeat for the first time brought immense joy and gratitude after navigating the hurdles of the process. They have already chosen the name Bear Petrov Barnyashev for their son, with Miro planning far ahead for the child's future.
