During a recent appearance on Stephanie McMahon's What's Your Story? podcast, WWE producer Michael Hayes discussed his role within the company and revealed that Chief Content Officer Paul 'Triple H' Levesque has already identified a potential successor for him. Hayes shared that Triple H told him he is viewed similarly to Pat Patterson and wants him to help groom Bobby Roode to take over that lead producer position in the future. Roode has consistently received strong internal praise from within WWE for his dedication and work behind the scenes as a producer.
