MJF has replaced Billy Gunn at Create-A-Pro Base Brawl. WWE released the top 10 moments from the October 19 episode of WWE RAW. Nick Gage and Mr. Danger have been announced for GCW Highest In The Room 5. Tavion Heights will appear on Busted Open After Dark following WWE NXT, and Shotzi Blackheart will return to Beyond in November. Darian Bengston is set for Crimson City Fights in January, while Gringo Loco will host an open challenge at WR's Jerry Lynn Invitational. Marko Stunt expressed interest in writing a song with Joe Hendry. Anthony Bowens, Christopher Daniels, and Scorpio Sky recently attended a Clayface screening, and AEW's Mikey Rukus completed his first feature film score in nearly a decade. Maple Leaf Pro released a mini documentary for Adam Copeland's debut. Rich Palladino will announce NHB 102 in Boston, and Lio Rush along with Shotzi Blackheart will release their entrance music on October 23. Classic matches featured include Brock Lesnar vs. Eddie Guerrero from SmackDown on November 7, 2002, and Bret Hart teaming with Shawn Michaels against Jerry Lawler and Hakushi from the WWE Vault.
