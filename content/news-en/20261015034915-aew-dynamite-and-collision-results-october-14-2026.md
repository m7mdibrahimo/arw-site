The October 14, 2026, episode of AEW Dynamite and Collision aired live from the Wolstein Center in Cleveland, Ohio. In the opening match, the Don Callis Family (Wardlow, Jake Doyle and Mark Davis) def. The Death Riders (Jon Moxley, Gabe Kidd and Daniel Garcia) in a Trios Match. Adam Copeland def. Josh Alexander in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage Qualifying Match. After the match, Copeland announced that 2027 will be his final year in professional wrestling. Kevin Knight, Lance Archer and Kyle Fletcher attacked Copeland, but Konosuke Takeshita, Orange Cassidy, Kyle O'Reilly and Roderick Strong made the save. Konosuke Takeshita def. Brian Cage in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage Qualifying Match. FTR and The Motor City Machine Guns agreed to a Best of Five Series, starting at AEW WrestleDream.
