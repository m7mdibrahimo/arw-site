The October 14, 2026, episode of AEW Dynamite and Collision aired live from the Wolstein Center in Cleveland, Ohio. The show featured several qualifying matches for the upcoming AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage match.

In the opening Trios Match, Don Callis Family (Wardlow, Jake Doyle and Mark Davis) def. The Death Riders (Jon Moxley, Gabe Kidd and Daniel Garcia).

Adam Copeland def. Josh Alexander in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage Qualifying Match. Following the bout, Copeland announced that 2027 will be his final year in professional wrestling, after which Kevin Knight, Lance Archer and Kyle Fletcher attacked him before being chased off by Konosuke Takeshita and his allies.

Konosuke Takeshita def. Brian Cage in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage Qualifying Match.

Kazuchika Okada def. Myron Reed to retain the AEW International Championship in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage Qualifying Match.

Triangle of Madness (Thekla, Julia Hart and Skye Blue) def. Mercedes Moné and Divine Dominion (Megan Bayne and Lena Kross) in a Trios Match.

Samoa Joe and Tommaso Ciampa def. The United Empire (Will Ospreay and Andrade El Ídolo) in a Tag Team Match. It was announced that Tommaso Ciampa will challenge Andrade El Ídolo for the AEW National Championship at AEW WrestleDream.

Ricochet def. Ace Austin in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage Qualifying Match.

The show also featured a segment where FTR and The Motor City Machine Guns proposed a Best of Five Series, and Nick Wayne and Chris Jericho confirmed a rubber match for AEW WrestleDream.
