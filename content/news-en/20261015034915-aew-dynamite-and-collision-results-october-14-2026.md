The October 14, 2026, edition of AEW Dynamite and Collision emanated from the Wolstein Center in Cleveland, Ohio. The night featured several AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying matches.

In the opening contest, Don Callis Family’s Wardlow, Jake Doyle, and Mark Davis def. The Death Riders (Jon Moxley, Gabe Kidd, and Daniel Garcia) in a trios match. Davis secured the victory for his team after hitting a piledriver on Moxley.

Adam Copeland def. Josh Alexander in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match. Following the bout, Copeland announced that 2027 will be his final year in professional wrestling. He was then blindsided by Kevin Knight, Lance Archer, and Kyle Fletcher, prompting Konosuke Takeshita and his allies to make the save.

Konosuke Takeshita def. Brian Cage in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match via submission with a Dragon Sleeper.

Kazuchika Okada def. Myron Reed to retain the AEW International Championship in an AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying match after hitting the Rainmaker.
