Cody Rhodes discussed his ongoing desire to form a stable with other colleagues on WWE programming, having spent most of his current run as a solo star who captured the Undisputed WWE Championship. During an appearance on the program What Do You Want To Talk About?, Rhodes explained his intentions regarding a potential alliance.

> "It's no secret that I'm actively trying to maybe get a little bit of a group on TV. Maybe it is a secret. I thought I've easter egg'd it multiple times, but I'm not doing it hard enough."

Rhodes is set to lead Team USA, alongside CM Punk and Je'Von Evans, against Team Mexico at Survivor Series. Additionally, Rhodes and guest Chelsea Green reflected on the All In event.
