The October 9, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown emanated from The Cajundome in Lafayette, Louisiana. The show featured CM Punk, Kevin Owens, and Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn engaging in a heated segment ahead of the Money in the Bank ladder matches. In the night's only match, Tiffany Stratton, Jacy Jayne, and Lash Legend def. Sol Ruca, Lola Vice, and Roxanne Perez in a 6-woman tag team match. Following the contest, Lash Legend attacked her own teammates. Backstage, Charlotte Flair was attacked by Michin, B-Fab, and Jade Cargill, leading Nick Aldis to postpone the scheduled WWE Women's Tag Team Championship match. Alexa Bliss was set to replace Flair, but Aldis ultimately banned everyone from ringside for the rescheduled bout.
