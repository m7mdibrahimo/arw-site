CM Punk suffered a surprising defeat against Chad Gable during the WWE Road to Survivor Series: WarGames live event on October 29, 2026, at the Covelli Centre in Youngstown, Ohio. Punk faced Gable in the semi-main event during their first-ever singles encounter. Despite Punk's major status, Gable secured a clean victory by pinning him in the middle of the ring without outside interference, achieving one of the biggest wins of his career.

Footage of the finish spread across social media to confirm the clean pinfall. After the match, Punk showed good sportsmanship by shaking Gable's hand and raising his arm in recognition. Gable, who holds the WWE Intercontinental Championship, continues a strong run of performances culminating in this major accomplishment against a star of Punk's caliber.
