Brooke Hogan revealed that she already mourned the loss of her relationship with Hulk Hogan before he died, but a discovery after his passing hit her harder than expected. Speaking on the Leave Then Cleave podcast, she explained that their final falling out stemmed from opening up to her father, only for him to tell her they should go their separate ways. She realized then that he always prioritized himself and others over her.

Brooke noted that she stood by her father through surgeries and difficult moments over the years, yet he rejected her attempts to mend their bond. According to Ringside News, she shared that the most painful realization after his death was learning he had called wrestlers and friends in his final days to say his end was near, choosing to leave things unresolved with her anyway.
