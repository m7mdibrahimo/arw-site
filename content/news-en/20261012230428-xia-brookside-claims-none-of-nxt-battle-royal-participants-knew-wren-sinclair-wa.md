According to Xia Brookside, none of the participants in the recent WWE NXT Battle Royal realized that Wren Sinclair was disguised as AAA's Lady Shani. Sinclair, who was suspended from WWE NXT at the time, entered the match to determine the next challenger for Kelani Jordan's NXT Women's Championship, eventually winning the bout before revealing her identity.

Speaking on Busted Open After Dark, Brookside explained that the participants were told 'Shani' did not speak English well, which limited interaction. She noted that the imposter was not present for most of the day, making it easy to miss her arrival.

> "I was surprised, because I had no idea either. I had no idea. None of us had any idea for the whole day that that was happening, that that was who that was. None of us. It was very much like, ‘Well, she doesn’t speak English,' so, ‘Hi.’ That’s it. I was like, ‘OK.’ Found out in the match when everyone else found out, so that was a crazy turn of events. I thought it was great. I thought the reveal was awesome and the fact that none of us knew either just made it even better."

Brookside added, "She wasn’t even there for most of the day and none of us had counted to even notice one was missing." NXT General Manager Robert Stone is scheduled to address the situation in the next episode of WWE NXT.
