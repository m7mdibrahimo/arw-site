When Grayson Waller returned to NXT earlier this year, he dropped his boxing shorts for traditional wrestling tights, a change that aligned with a more serious character and his first NXT Title win. Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Waller noted that he understood why people viewed him as a joke in his previous gear.

He debuted the tights at the Royal Rumble, where Roman Reigns pulled him aside and offered high praise.

> "Hey, that’s the look. You look like a champion now."

That encouragement led him to keep the pants and adopt a serious vibe upon returning to NXT.

Grayson Waller will defend the NXT Title against Tony D'Angelo at NXT Halloween Havoc on Saturday.
