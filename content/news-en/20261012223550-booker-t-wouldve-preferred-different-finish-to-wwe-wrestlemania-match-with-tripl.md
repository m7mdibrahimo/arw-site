Booker T's WWE WrestleMania 19 match against Triple H remains a frequent topic of fan debate. Given the rivalry's racial undertones, many believed Booker T should have defeated the heel champion, but he ultimately lost. In a recent interview on "Insight with Chris Van Vliet," Booker revisited the feud and admitted he would change the outcome if he could. Booker stated he never discussed winning the match beforehand and did not engage in backstage politics. He expressed gratitude for the fans who supported him but noted that he did not realize how much he wanted the win until he reached the main event stage. While he acknowledged the match was a great story, he noted that the bout took place in the middle of the card rather than serving as the main event.
