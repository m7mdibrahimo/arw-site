WWE does not intend to change its schedule despite AEW announcing that Dynamite: New Year’s Smash will air live on Friday, January 1, 2027. This date puts the AEW show directly opposite WWE SmackDown, marking a rare head-to-head night for the two promotions.

According to WrestleVotes Radio on Fightful Select, WWE plans to maintain its existing plan for the January 1 episode of SmackDown. The company will record the episode in advance, with a double taping scheduled for Monday, December 28, in Tampa, Florida. A WWE source noted that this decision was made to ensure that talent would not have to work on New Year’s Day, and the company is not interested in altering that plan regardless of the competition.

Internally, WWE reportedly does not expect the holiday episode to draw massive numbers. There is an expectation that the taped New Year’s Day edition of SmackDown could draw a lower rating than a standard live broadcast, whether or not it faces live competition from AEW.
