Dax Harwood discussed FTR's upcoming second match in their best-of-five series against the Motor City Machine Guns. On the October 24 episode of AEW Collision, FTR consisting of Dax and Cash Wheeler will face the Motor City Machine Guns, featuring Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin, following their initial encounter at AEW WrestleDream. During a recent interview with The Danny Ocean Show and 3NT Wrestling, Harwood shared his thoughts on the upcoming bout.

> "Well, obviously, any sports team can tell you that being down 0-1 is not how you want to start a series. But the good thing is we're coming home. You know, hometown advantage for the hometown boys, FTR. I feel pretty confident going into this match. Motor City Machine Guns, one of the greatest tag teams of all time, along with, obviously, FTR, the greatest tag team of all time. So I'm feeling confident. I'm feeling confident that FTR will pull out a victory. I'm feeling confident with my, again, having the home field advantage, and I look forward to tomorrow night." In another recent interview, Harwood also discussed FTR working alongside Stokely Hathaway.
