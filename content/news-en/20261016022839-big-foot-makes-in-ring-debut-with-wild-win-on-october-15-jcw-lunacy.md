Big Foot made his official in-ring debut on the October 15 episode of JCW Lunacy, securing a victory in a chaotic tag team match. Big Foot teamed up with Big Foot Hunter Mike to face The Backseat Boyz, Tommy and JP Grayson. The match ended in a bizarre fashion when Big Foot hit a double chokeslam on both opponents, followed by a chokeslam on his own partner, Big Foot Hunter Mike, onto the pile. Big Foot then covered all three men to score the win. Immediately after the match, Big Foot fled the venue, ending his unusual debut abruptly.
