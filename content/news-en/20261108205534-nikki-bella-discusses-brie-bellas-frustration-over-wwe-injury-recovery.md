Nikki Bella shared during an interview with SportsRadio 94WIP that Brie Bella is struggling with the slow healing process following her shoulder injury at WWE SummerSlam 2026. Because she avoided surgery, Brie is depending entirely on physical therapy, which has kept her from exercising normally and caused her significant frustration.

> "It's, you know, slow process cuz she didn't have surgery, but she couldn't wear the location of the breaks were. Um but she's doing well. She's in PT a few days a week. So it's kind of one of those things that you're just waiting for those bones to heal."

Nikki added that her sister cannot work out at all right now due to the nature of the scapula injury. Brie is awaiting a new X-ray to check on her bone growth after previous scans showed the bones had not yet fused.
