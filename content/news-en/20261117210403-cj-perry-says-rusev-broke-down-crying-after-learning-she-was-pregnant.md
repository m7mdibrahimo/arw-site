Miro, known as Rusev during his time in WWE, could not hold back his tears after learning that his wife, CJ Perry, was pregnant. Speaking with Andro Mammo, Perry opened up about their long IVF journey, revealing that Miro broke down crying in his car before he could even make it home from the gym. Perry explained that she told her mother first while waiting for Miro to return from the gym, but he quickly figured out what was happening during a phone call.

Perry shared that she still surprised him when he arrived home, but he was already extremely emotional and thankful. The couple went through four IVF cycles, facing painful injections, hormone treatments, and difficult egg and embryo counts, before a successful embryo transfer in August 2026. They are now expecting a baby boy named Bear Petrov Barnyashev, with a due date of April 28, 2027.
