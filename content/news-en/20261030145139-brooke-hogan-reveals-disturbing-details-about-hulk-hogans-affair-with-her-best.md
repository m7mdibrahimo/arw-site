Brooke Hogan recently revisited her father Hulk Hogan's affair with her best friend and artist representative, Christiane Plante, during an appearance on the Leave Then Cleave podcast. While the relationship itself was painful, Brooke revealed that the most disturbing part was discovering that Plante had allegedly been sharing private details about Brooke's personal life and relationship with her father.

Brooke explained that during the affair, Hulk would occasionally make surprisingly specific remarks about her relationship, leading her to briefly wonder if her phone was being hacked. She ultimately found out about the affair from her then-boyfriend during dinner in Miami, completely blindsided by the revelation that her father and best friend were involved.
