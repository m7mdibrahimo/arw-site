Paige Collett recently revealed that a promo directed by Vince Russo for her Ring Rat character was banned from Juggalo Championship Wrestling television. During an appearance on Headlocks, Heels, & Havoc: Wrestling Unscripted, Collett explained that the segment involved her character talking about "German style" and ultimately got pulled from TV.

Collett admitted that she helped come up with some of the material, stating, "I got so much trouble for it. I'm like, 'Oh, this is my fault.'" Although JCW has previously aired controversial segments involving her character, this particular Russo-directed promo was deemed too wild for broadcast. Collett expressed her determination to eventually release the footage online so fans can see what was kept off television.
