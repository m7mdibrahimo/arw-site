Brie Bella has decided to stop sharing photos of her children on social media after her six-year-old son, Buddy, was targeted by online abuse. Speaking on The Nikki & Brie Show, the WWE Hall of Famer revealed that an anonymous bot-like account left around 20 hateful comments and images mocking Buddy on a Halloween post she shared. The experience deeply shook her and convinced her to delete the post entirely, vowing never to post photos of her children online again.

Brie also discussed how such accounts can easily target young users who run their own social media pages, suggesting that parents should disable comment sections to protect children. The conversation then expanded to how much kids absorb from adult conversations around the house, with Nikki Bella sharing how her son Matteo overheard details about a plane crash and grew worried. Both agreed that parents must be more mindful of what they discuss near children and take proactive steps to protect them in the digital age.
