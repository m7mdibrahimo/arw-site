Nia Jax has revealed the remaining goals on her pro wrestling bucket list, expressing her desire to compete in a steel cage match on WWE television and to face another powerhouse female wrestler in a hard-hitting collision.

Jax has been with WWE for over a decade, capturing multiple titles and taking part in major bouts throughout her tenure. Speaking to WPLG Local 10, she explained that she still has ambitions she wants to fulfill inside the ring.

> "So I would love to, you know, get into a cage match. I haven't done that cage match on TV. I've done it on live events, but never on TV. I would love to have like a big hoss match, you know, big, two big women who possibly break the ring. You know, you never know. There's so many different things like that. I feel like in WWE, no matter how long you've been here, you still feel like you haven't accomplished everything that you can because there's always something. But right now, those are two that I would love to get to."
