Andrade El Idolo recently dropped the AEW National Championship, but he is already setting his sights on a much bigger prize. Following the November 7 episode of AEW Collision, Andrade took to social media to announce that his next goal is the AEW World Championship, which is currently held by Will Ospreay. Rather than viewing his recent loss as a major setback, Andrade is treating it as a learning experience that will push him forward.

"You learn more from losing than winning. You learn how to keep going! Face up The next goal, the AEW World Championship. Waiting for the perfect time and place. Where and against whom would you like me to face off? Thank you AEW FANS, for believing in me! See you today in EL PASO TEXAS"

Tommaso Ciampa captured the title from Andrade on Collision after hitting a roll-up following a chaotic match aboard the Norwegian Jewel. With the championship loss behind him, Andrade is now looking toward the world title picture, though he has not yet revealed when he plans to challenge Ospreay.
