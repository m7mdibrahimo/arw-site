Orange Cassidy recently discussed the story behind his entrance music, revealing that he currently uses two very expensive theme songs that he enjoys equally. While Cassidy has long been associated with Jefferson Starship's "Jane," he explained that AEW initially struggled to secure the rights for that track. Tony Khan then suggested The Pixies' "Where Is My Mind?" as an alternative. Once Cassidy began using the new song, Jefferson Starship eventually cleared the rights for "Jane" as well. Cassidy told STL Sports Central that he is fortunate to have both options, noting that each track suits him well in different moments. He continues to alternate between them depending on the situation.
