Bronson Reed says that he has the exact same mindset heading into his upcoming bout with Oba Femi that he had before facing Roman Reigns. Reed and Femi are scheduled to face each other in singles action at Money In The Bank. Having previously defeated Roman Reigns, Reed is no stranger to major victories in WWE. In a recent interview with Rock and Pop FM, Bronson Reed discussed his approach to the upcoming match. "I have that exact same mindset that you're talking about. When I faced Roman Reigns, you know, I think at that time in Australia, and then around the world, fans had no belief that I could beat Roman Reigns. When I did it, I shocked the world, and now I've been gone with an injury and we've seen this rise of over Femi and he is on top of the world when it comes to wrestling at the moment. I think again, a lot of the fans watching in their minds, they think I can't beat him, but I'm going to beat him on Saturday and prove everyone wrong."
