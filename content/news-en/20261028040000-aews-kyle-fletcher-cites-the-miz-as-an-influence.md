AEW star Kyle Fletcher recently discussed how WWE veteran The Miz has influenced his promo work. During an interview on the Battleground Podcast, Fletcher explained that he admires Miz's ability to interact naturally with the crowd and rely on spontaneity rather than memorized scripts. He noted that watching performers like The Miz and John Cena react to live audiences inspired him to trust his own speaking abilities in AEW. Fletcher admitted that while overthinking is common when preparing promos, seeing this spontaneous approach encouraged him to adopt a similar style since joining The Don Callis Family.
