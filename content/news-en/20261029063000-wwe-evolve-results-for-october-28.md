According to Fightful, WWE broadcast an episode of WWE EVOLVE on October 28, 2026, from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, via Tubi.

Harley Riggins def. Milosh Jovic
Jax Presley def. Jasper Troy
Elijah Holyfield def. Braxton Cole and Marcus Mathers

Riggins kept up his assault on Jovic after the bell. Jasper Troy claimed that the brand is his new home base. The MOG Squad discussed Max Abrams and his recovery from facial reconstruction surgery. Nikkita Lyons hosted her celebration, which Anya Rune interrupted. Timothy Thatcher booked Sloane Jacobs against Rune with a title opportunity on the line, and stated that Lyons will put her EVOLVE Women's Title on the line against Thea Hail, Skylar Raye, and Wendy Choo in a four-way match. Viktor Zanov is currently in Australia, Shido Ash is transitioning to singles competition, and Aaron Rourke and Laynie Luck resolved to team up against Kale Dixon and Alix Brooks.
