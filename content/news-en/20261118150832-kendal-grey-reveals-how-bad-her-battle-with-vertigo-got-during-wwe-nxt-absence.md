Kendal Grey opened up about her challenging recovery from an ear injury and vertigo during her recent three-month absence from WWE NXT. Speaking on Busted Open After Dark, Grey admitted that she felt extremely low and doubted whether she would perform at the same level upon her return. Following her return and her November 17 NXT Women's Championship match against Kelani Jordan, Grey expressed disappointment with the disqualification finish, noting that she remains determined to bounce back and work toward capturing the title.
