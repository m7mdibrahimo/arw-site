Alyssa Daniele’s time in WWE’s developmental system has hit a major setback. The NXT rookie revealed on November 18 that she suffered a serious shoulder dislocation and will require surgery. Daniele, who joined the company in April, will be sidelined for several months as she recovers. She expressed her frustration with the timing, noting that she felt she was building momentum before the injury. However, she is already looking ahead to a return in the spring of 2027.

> "I’ve gotten a lot of questions about where I’ve been in the ring.. unfortunately I suffered a pretty nasty shoulder dislocation that requires surgery so I’ll be out for a few months. But I’ll be back and better than ever this spring. Stay with me, we’re just getting started ⚡️❤️🔥"

> "getting injured just as the momentum was starting is tough, but being away gives me time to get better in every way possible and makes me even hungrier to come back. ❤️🔥 the locker room should definitely be ready for when I’m cleared."

Daniele was part of the April 29 rookie class alongside Garrett Beck, Nicholas Panicali, and Zoe Hines.
