India Sioux recently discussed the significance of her CMLL-Japan Women's Championship reign. Sioux defeated Hazuki on March 7, 2025, to capture the title. Reflecting on her journey, she emphasized the personal importance of returning to compete in Japan.

> Every achievement I’ve had has been very special. Since my return up to this point, these past two years have been wonderful. After so many years out, returning to Japan was, first and foremost, a very important goal for me.

Sioux noted that defending the title against top-tier talent like Hazuki and Rina has been a major focus of her career. She highlighted that winning the championship on foreign soil required immense concentration. Sioux also briefly touched upon her participation in the CMLL Amazonas Universal Championship tournament earlier this year.
