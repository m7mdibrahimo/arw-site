Drew McIntyre recently discussed his acting ambitions outside of WWE, revealing that he would love to star in a horror-based project. During an interview with Sidewalks Entertainment, the former WWE Champion explained that he has no limitations as he explores various acting projects. When asked whether he would prefer playing a villain or a protagonist, McIntyre shared a unique perspective.

> "Nothing particular in mind. Something horror-based, maybe. But I'm just going to take it as it comes. I just have no limitations right now. It's just let's see what the future holds in my wrestling career and otherwise."

Standing at 6'5 and weighing 285 pounds, McIntyre noted that playing a terrified protagonist rather than a traditional monster would be much more fun and unexpected. He suggested that having a smaller character be the brave one while he is constantly in fear sounds like an interesting dynamic.
