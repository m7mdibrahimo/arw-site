India Sioux has shared her experience working on the CMLL project Mitos del Ring, which blended Mesoamerican culture with Mexican wrestling. Speaking in an interview, Sioux explained that while the idea initially sounded strange, she quickly became fascinated after stepping into the ring as Mayahuel. She noted that most appearances involved meet-and-greets at Arena México and praised the unique creative direction of the project. Due to other commitments, Sioux had to step down midway through, allowing Olympia to take over the role. Sioux believes the project has strong potential and hopes it could eventually expand to other parts of the world.
