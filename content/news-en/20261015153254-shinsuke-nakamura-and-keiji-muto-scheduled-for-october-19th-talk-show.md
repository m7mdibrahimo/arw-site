Keiji Muto and WWE star Shinsuke Nakamura are scheduled to host a live talk show stream on October 19th. The announcement was shared via the official social media account for Keiji Muto, also known as the business page for Great Muta. The event, streamed live on TwitCasting Premier, will feature the two wrestling legends discussing their time on the world stage, the past and future of professional wrestling, behind-the-scenes stories, and their legendary encounters. Fans will also be able to send in questions and comments during the stream. Nakamura currently performs on the WWE SmackDown brand and has recently been teaming with five-time IWGP Junior Heavyweight Champion Hiromu Takahashi.
