Adam Copeland discussed how his transition to AEW allowed him to perform in historic venues that he never thought he would reach. Copeland noted that after returning to WWE in 2020, he seriously doubted he would ever get the chance to wrestle in arenas like Arena Mexico, Wembley Stadium, or Maple Leaf Gardens. Moving to AEW changed everything, enabling him to check off all three locations within a single calendar month. "At this stage of my career, this deep into it, to still have things to go after I think is kind of what keeps you young," Copeland said during an interview on Daily Faceoff, adding that these milestone experiences continue to keep him energized during his current run.
