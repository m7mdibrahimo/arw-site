The Syx have been making waves in the TNA tag team division, but Samuel Shaw, Joseph Sawyer and Erick Redbeard recently took a break from their serious demeanor to participate in a viral social media trend. Fans at a recent ACW event were surprised when the trio suddenly broke into the "Tip Toeing In My Jordans" dance while the Riff Raff track played over the speakers.

Samuel Shaw led the performance, performing the exaggerated tiptoe dance to the delight of the crowd. Joseph Sawyer followed suit, adding his own flair by dropping to his knee for a move that resembled a mini Spinaroonie. Erick Redbeard then joined in, completing the trio's routine as the audience cheered them on. The dance is a recreation of a popular meme featuring a character from the 2014 Riff Raff song "Tip Toe Wing In My Jawwdinz."

While this lighthearted moment was a departure from the intense persona TNA fans have come to expect, it demonstrated that the three men are capable of having fun outside the ring. The group has quickly become a significant presence in the TNA tag team landscape, but their ACW appearance provided a rare glimpse of their personalities.
