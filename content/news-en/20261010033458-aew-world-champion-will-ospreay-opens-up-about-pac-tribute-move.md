During the September 30 episode of AEW Dynamite, AEW World Champion Will Ospreay and Ricochet competed in a match dedicated to their late friend, Pac, who both men consider a significant influence on their careers. Ospreay honored Pac by executing his signature Black Arrow finishing move, a maneuver he had not performed in eight years. Reflecting on the moment during an interview on Mike Bailey's video blog, Ospreay explained the difficulty of the tribute. 

> "That was a tough one. I ain't done that in eight years is the last time I did it and I was ten kilos lighter, I don't know what that is in pounds. But that was one of the most difficult matches I've ever done in my life, and when it comes to myself and Trevor (Ricochet), obviously there is always hard feelings between us because egos always get in the way and you always want to be sitting on top of the throne and I know he wants this championship. But to be able to put our differences aside and do a great tribute to a very good friend of the pair of us, I wanted to do it right."

Ospreay noted that many fans and peers believed he and Ricochet were the only wrestlers capable of performing the Black Arrow due to its technical complexity. Bailey concluded the segment by praising the match as a fitting tribute to Pac.
