The 2019 WWE Crown Jewel plane story has taken another turn. Following comments from George Barrios suggesting the incident was exaggerated, another former WWE executive contacted Conrad Thompson to claim that WWE was deliberately targeted when trying to leave Riyadh. According to Thompson's report in Uncrowned, the anonymous executive stated that certain countries are problematic upon departure when they know a company needs to travel soon and is carrying cash from merchandise sales. Barrios previously admitted he did not know the exact cause of the delay but recalled paying airport fees to resolve travel issues in other countries. Despite the conflicting accounts, this six-year-old controversy continues to draw new claims from former company insiders.
