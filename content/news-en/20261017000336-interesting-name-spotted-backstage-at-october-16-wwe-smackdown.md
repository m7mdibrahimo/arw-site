The Boogeyman has made a surprise appearance backstage at the October 16 episode of WWE SmackDown. The WWE legend visited the Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, California, and even walked around the ring while in character. He documented the visit on Instagram, stating,

> "Just because you don't see me, doesn't mean I'm not there. I'm the Boogeyman and I'm still coming to get you."

This appearance follows the veteran's public interest in returning to the ring. In July 2025, he posted a workout video calling out Triple H, saying,

> "Put me in the game Triple H… #WWELEGEND BOOGEYMAN."

Although The Boogeyman has not been a regular on television since his 2009 release, he has remained involved with the company through a Legends deal and various character appearances over the years.
