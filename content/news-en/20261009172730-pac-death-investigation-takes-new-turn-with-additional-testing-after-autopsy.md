The investigation into the death of PAC has taken a new turn as the Cook County Medical Examiner's Office ordered additional testing following the initial autopsy. Dave Meltzer reported in the latest Wrestling Observer Newsletter that ordering further studies generally indicates that examiners did not find an obvious cause of death right away. According to a source familiar with the office, major natural causes such as heart attacks, strokes, or aneurysms are typically apparent during the first examination. Because PAC was a high-profile figure, officials are likely proceeding with extra caution before releasing conclusive details. The additional testing is widely believed to involve toxicology reports, which can take several weeks to complete. Police previously reported that there were no obvious signs of foul play or accidental injury when PAC passed away at the age of 40. First responders rushed to the scene after receiving a 911 call from a woman who found him unresponsive inside a running rental vehicle. Medical personnel attempted life-saving measures, but he was pronounced dead at approximately 4:08 p.m. The official cause of death remains pending while authorities await the final results of the ongoing medical tests.
