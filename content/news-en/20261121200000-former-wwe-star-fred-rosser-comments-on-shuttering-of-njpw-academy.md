Fred Rosser, known in WWE as Darren Young, spent over three years as a trainer at the NJPW Academy and the NJPW Los Angeles dojo after signing with the promotion in August 2020. Earlier in November, it was announced that NJPW would close its American subsidiary, including the training academy. Rosser recently took to Instagram to share an emotional statement regarding the closure, reflecting on his time coaching students from around the world.

> "The wins, the championships and the accomplishments are great, but watching people grow in confidence, character, and belief in themselves is the real reward for me," Rosser wrote. He added that the relationships and memories will remain with him forever, emphasizing that coaching was always his true purpose. While Rosser did not specify his future plans following the shutdown of New Japan Pro-Wrestling of America Inc. and its academy, his most recent NJPW match was a loss to David Finlay at NJPW The New Beginning USA in February. The American subsidiary was originally founded in 2019, and the company announced that North American events will continue with the full support of the TV Asahi group, which became the new owner in May.
