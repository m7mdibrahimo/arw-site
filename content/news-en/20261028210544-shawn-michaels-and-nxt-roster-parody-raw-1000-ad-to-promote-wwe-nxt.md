Shawn Michaels and various members of the WWE NXT roster have parodied the historic WWE RAW 1000 advertisement to promote the upcoming episode of WWE NXT. The show is scheduled to air on Wednesday instead of its usual time due to the US elections, and the promotion is working to ensure fans are aware of the schedule change.

In the new video, Michaels appears alongside stars including Zilla Fatu, Wren Sinclair, Hank and Tank, and Shiloh Hill. They recreate the classic ad's premise, which features a humorous game of telephone where the roster misunderstands messages passed between them.

Next week's WWE NXT will feature special appearances by John Cena and Cody Rhodes. Additionally, WWE NXT General Manager Robert Stone recently discussed his time in TNA, where he balanced his wrestling career with teaching.
