Tony D'Angelo has opened up about his career in WWE NXT, sharing his proudest moment, top opponents, and his ultimate dream match. Having been a cornerstone of the brand for several years, D'Angelo has achieved significant success, including becoming a Grand Slam champion in WWE's developmental brand. As he prepares to challenge Grayson Waller for the NXT Championship at Halloween Havoc, he reflected on his journey in a new interview with Denise Salcedo.

When asked about his proudest accomplishment in NXT so far, D'Angelo was clear:

> "I'd have to say becoming the first ever Grand Slam champion in NXT history."

He also highlighted the toughest competition he has faced during his time with the company.

> "Oba, Ilja, and Dijak."

Looking toward the future, D'Angelo revealed his ultimate dream match, a contest that would feature four prominent NXT alumni at WrestleMania.

> "Fatal four-way WrestleMania. Me, Bron Breakker, Trick Williams, Julius Creed."

Throughout the interview, D'Angelo also discussed how he has managed to adapt to the ever-changing landscape of the NXT roster.
