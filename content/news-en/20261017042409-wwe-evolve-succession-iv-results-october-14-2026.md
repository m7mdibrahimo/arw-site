WWE aired the latest special of WWE EVOLVE, Succession IV, on October 14 via Tubi. In the opening match, Harlem Lewis successfully defended the EVOLVE Championship against Harley Riggins. Following the contest, Riggins launched an attack on Lewis. Additionally, the broadcast featured a video segment showcasing Josiah Jean securing a WWE ID contract. In other action, Vanta the Unknown defeated Laynie Luck. During the show, Elijah Holyfield expressed his desire to face Braxton Cole, only to be interrupted by Marcus Mathers and It's Gal. In the main event, Nikkito Lyons retained the EVOLVE Women's Championship against Wendy Choo in a Last Woman Standing match. The segment concluded with Skylar Raye confronting Lyons.
