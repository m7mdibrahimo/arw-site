The audience figures for the November 10, 2026 episode of WWE NXT on The CW Network saw a slight increase compared to the previous week. According to Wrestlenomics, the show drew 588,000 viewers with a 0.11 rating in the key P18–49 demographic. In comparison, the November 4 episode drew 552,000 viewers and a 0.13 demo rating, marking an increase of 36,000 viewers and a decrease of 0.02 in the demo. Compared to the same week in 2025, when the show drew 554,000 viewers and a 0.07 rating, viewership increased by 6.1% and the demo rating rose by 57.1%. These numbers reflect linear TV viewership on The CW Network only and do not include streaming or digital platforms.
