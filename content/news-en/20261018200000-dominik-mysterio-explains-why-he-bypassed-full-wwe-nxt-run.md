Dominik Mysterio explained why he never completed a traditional run through WWE NXT after joining the company's main roster early in his career. Mysterio noted that a busy schedule featuring appearances on WWE RAW, WWE NXT, and WWE SmackDown, alongside live events throughout the weekend, left no time for standard developmental classes. He added that learning directly from veteran talent on the road proved more valuable than completing a regular developmental stint. Mysterio eventually competed on WWE NXT later in his career, capturing the NXT North American Championship twice and defeating rivals like Trick Williams and Dragon Lee.
