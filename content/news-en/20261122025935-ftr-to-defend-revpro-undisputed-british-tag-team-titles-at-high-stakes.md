Revolution Pro Wrestling announced that Dax Harwood and Cash Wheeler are heading back to the promotion in February. The duo is scheduled to put the RevPro Undisputed British Tag Team Titles on the line at RevPro High Stakes on February 6.

Harwood and Wheeler will square off against the winners of the British Tag League for their initial title defense. They captured the vacant championships from Cowboy Way members 1 Called Manders and Thomas Shire in August. A planned defense at York Hall in November was scrapped because the venue shut down. Meanwhile, AEW is scheduled to hold AEW Dynasty in Liverpool on February 7.
