Je'Von Evans discussed his WWE theme music, noting that he wanted something that would get the crowd in a good mood. Evans signed with WWE in late 2023 and moved to the main roster at the beginning of this year, becoming a fan favorite in WWE NXT. Along with his brash attitude, he uses the theme song "Who Want It" by J Frost and Def Rebel. In an interview with KSGO Radio, Evans explained his choice.

> "I think when I came into NXT, or came into WWE in general, I wanted something that would kind of just get the crowd in a good mood, you know what I mean? So once I heard the beat of this and the lyrics, I was like, okay, I feel like this will get the crowd, you know, jumping a little bouncy, you know what I mean?"

Elsewhere, Evans recently spoke about crashing through an ambulance roof at WWE Money In The Bank.
