Nikki Bella took a personal jab at Chelsea Green during the October 9, 2026 episode of WWE SmackDown. While confronting Green regarding the WWE Women's Championship, Bella dismissed Green's title reign and questioned her standing. Bella stated, "You're nothing but a bootleg Nikki Bella. And I think that WWE Women's Championship deserves an upgrade to the real thing. I mean, ask your husband. I'm sure he wants one too." By referencing Green's husband, Matt Cardona, in the context of an "upgrade," Bella made the backstage segment significantly more personal. Before the insult, Bella warned Green that she intends to win the championship after finishing with Paige, claiming she will do to Green exactly what she plans to do to her current rival.
