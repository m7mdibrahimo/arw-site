Chris Jericho recently discussed his time leading various stables in AEW and his current singles run. In an interview with TMZ's Inside The Ring, Jericho explained that his past groups were a product of necessity to help introduce newer talent to the audience. He noted that factions like The Inner Circle and the Jericho Appreciation Society were created to give rising stars a bigger platform. Jericho added that his recent return in April marked a shift to a solo run, as he feels his name alone is enough to represent his long career. He also stated that his current focus is to work with as many different wrestlers in AEW as possible.
