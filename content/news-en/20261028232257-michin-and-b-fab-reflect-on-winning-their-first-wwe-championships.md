Michin and B-Fab have officially claimed their first gold in WWE. Continuing their recent success, the duo defeated Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley to win the WWE Women's Tag Team Championships on the October 23 episode of WWE SmackDown.

Speaking with Complex Graps, Michin shared her feelings about capturing her first title after nearly ten years with the company and almost two decades in wrestling. She admitted that while being in WWE was her childhood dream, achieving gold felt uncertain at times, especially while working as a fan favorite.

> "It's still surreal for me because I've been in WWE for almost a decade, but I've been wrestling for almost two decades now... It was a dream to get gold in WWE, but as time went on I kinda accepted that maybe it wasn't in the cards for me."

Michin added that teaming up with B-Fab and Jade Cargill reignited her determination to achieve her goal. Winning the titles in her hometown made the moment even more special for her.

B-Fab also reflected on her journey, describing her transition from managing teams like Hit Row and The Street Profits to becoming an active competitor in the ring.

> "It's definitely surreal, it hasn't sunk in fully... Getting to transition from that spot of being in the manager position to being able to wrestle in the ring, it's just crazy to see how everything has really just kept elevating from that spot."
