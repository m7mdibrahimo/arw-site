Noam Dar recently revealed that he and Romeo Moreno had no idea about John Cena's invitation to the inaugural John Cena Classic until Cena surprised them backstage during the November 10 episode of WWE NXT. Dar noted that their on-screen reactions were completely genuine, and he appreciated that WWE kept the surprise hidden. Cena also presented them with a mysterious briefcase containing a personal letter and special items, which Dar described as deeply meaningful. Despite winning the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic with Moreno, Dar admitted he did not expect a tournament spot, adding that failing to win the Cruiserweight Classic a decade ago still motivates him as he approaches this new opportunity.
