Sami Zayn successfully defended the WWE Championship against Je'Von Evans in an impromptu match on WWE RAW. Zayn came to the ring to address a lack of respect, mentioning Trick Williams, CM Punk, and Evans. This led to an exchange with Evans, after which Adam Pearce informed Zayn he would defend his title immediately. Evans dominated early with a dropkick and a frog splash, but Zayn escaped outside. Penta appeared with a referee and Money in the Bank backing, forcing Zayn back in. After Bron Breakker interfered, Evans missed a springboard attack, allowing Zayn to hit an Exploder and a Helluva Kick to retain the title.
