Chelsea Green secured a major upset victory at WWE Crown Jewel 2026, defeating Liv Morgan to win the Women's Crown Jewel Championship. Despite facing interference from Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez, Green overcame the odds, kicking out of multiple Codebreakers and eventually countering Morgan's obLIVion finisher into a sudden rollup to get the three-count. The win leaves Riyadh with another major accomplishment for Green, adding to her WWE Women's Championship reign, while Morgan must deal with the frustrating loss.
