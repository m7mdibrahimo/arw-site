Ash By Elegance is expecting a baby boy, sharing the difficult journey that preceded this joyful moment with her fans. The former WWE Superstar, previously known as Dana Brooke, announced that she is 32 weeks pregnant following a painful miscarriage, a severe health ordeal that required intensive care, and a diagnosis of Crohn's disease.

During an interview on November 17, 2026, Ash explained that she first became pregnant in August 2025, which led to her decision to step away from the ring as she relinquished the TNA Knockouts World Championship at Victory Road at the time. Her health worsened in February 2026 when she suffered severe stomach pain and internal bleeding that required hospitalization and three blood transfusions, before her condition was diagnosed as Crohn's disease last May, explaining the serious health issues she faced.

Ash expressed her joy and considered her upcoming child, whom she named Kairos, a true miracle after everything she went through. She noted that her Crohn's disease has entered clinical remission during the pregnancy, although doctors are still treating her case as a high-risk pregnancy, with a C-section scheduled for January 11, 2027. Regarding her professional future, she confirmed that her TNA contract expires at the end of December with the possibility of returning in the future, as she hopes to get back in the ring so her son can see what she is capable of achieving.
