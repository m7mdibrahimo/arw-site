Tony D'Angelo recently discussed whether he views himself as one of the all-time greats in WWE NXT.

D'Angelo has spent several years on the brand, breaking out during the NXT 2.0 era, and he is a WWE NXT Grand Slam Champion who is challenging for the WWE NXT Championship at Halloween Havoc.

During an interview with Love Wrestling, Tony D'Angelo was asked about his status as a potential all-time great in the promotion.

> "I try not to think of it, honestly. I'm just trying to keep my nose to the grindstone, and you know what? At the end of the day, if people want to say I'm one of the pillars or people held of high standard when it comes to NXT and championships and all that, then that would be awesome, and more power to me and my family and what I've done. But honestly, I'm not too focused on that."

He also discussed the possibility of returning to his Don of WWE NXT persona.
