During the November 21, 2026, broadcast of AEW Collision from Green Bay, Wisconsin, a Continental Classic match between Daniel Garcia and Brody King ended suddenly. The referee stopped the match after Garcia appeared to be in pain following a maneuver by King. King had controlled much of the Blue League contest, using his size to his advantage. The situation became concerning when King executed a rolling powerbomb-style move that left Garcia appearing shaken. The referee immediately checked on Garcia and called for the bell. King was awarded the victory, securing three points in the Continental Classic. There is no official confirmation yet on whether Garcia sustained a legitimate injury or if the stoppage was part of the planned finish.
