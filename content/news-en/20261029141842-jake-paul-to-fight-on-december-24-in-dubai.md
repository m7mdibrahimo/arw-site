Jake Paul is scheduled to return to the ring on December 24 in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, without an opponent announced yet. This marks Paul's first fight since his knockout loss to Anthony Joshua in December 2025, after which he underwent multiple jaw surgeries. In a press release, Paul said, "I've spent years proving people wrong, taking risks, and pushing myself against opponents nobody thought I'd face. Nothing that's happened has changed my belief in myself or what I'm working toward. I'm hungrier than ever, and on Thursday, December 24, I'm returning to the ring in Dubai to start the next chapter of my journey." He added, "Dubai is a city that thinks big, breaks boundaries, and doesn't believe in limits. That's exactly the kind of place I want to make my comeback. We've got plenty of names on the list; let's see who signs the contract. Merry Christmas, Dubai. Santa Paul is coming to town, and somebody's getting knocked out." MVP co-founder Nakisa Bidarian stated that they are finalizing a worthy opponent for the return and will share more details in the coming weeks.
