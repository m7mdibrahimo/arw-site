Chelsea Green spoke about traveling to WWE Crown Jewel and performing in front of Saudi Arabian fans. For the women's roster, they wear bodysuits covering themselves from neck to toe, with each woman having unique designs. Speaking to Good Karma Wrestling, Green discussed the long flights, noting that it is rare for WWE superstars to get eight hours of sleep, making the travel time a welcome chance to rest. 

Green then shared her thoughts on the fans, saying:

> "I mean, the travel is always really, really rough on your body because you're going so far. But I think what people don't realize, because my regular friends will always be like, 'how on earth are you gonna sit on that plane for 20 plus hours?' I think what people don't realize is that's the only time we get to sleep."

She added:

> "So it's funny because we love going to Saudi Arabia and putting on those one piece outfits. Like the girls love wearing them, love designing them, love performing in them, but we never know how the fans are going to react as females in the ring."

Green praised the crowd at Crown Jewel, stating that they were loud from start to finish, sang everyone's songs, and gave her and Liv Morgan the reactions they wanted during their match.
