AEW Dynamite viewership and key demographic numbers are in for the November 4, 2026 episode on TBS, marking one of the strongest increases of the year. According to PWInsider, the broadcast drew 792,000 viewers with a 0.24 rating in the key 18-49 demographic. This is up from the October 28, 2026 episode, which drew 620,000 viewers with a 0.21 rating, representing an increase of 172,000 viewers and 0.03 in the demo. Compared to the same week in 2025, when the November 5 episode drew 497,000 viewers and a 0.09 demo rating, this represents a year-over-year increase of 59.4% in viewership and 166.7% in the demographic. These figures reflect linear TV viewership on TBS only and do not include streaming or digital platforms.
