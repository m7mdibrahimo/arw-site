New Japan Pro-Wrestling presented NJPW Power Struggle on November 3 from Hiroshima Sun Plaza Hall in Hiroshima, Japan, with the action streaming live on NJPW World, according to Fightful. 

Aaron Wolf, Master Wato and KUUKAI def. United Empire (Francesco Akira, Jakob Austin Young and Zane Jay)
Mistico, KUSHIDA and Masatora Yasuda def. Unbound Co. (Titan, Gedo and Daiki Nagai)
TMDK (Ryohei Oiwa and Hartley Jackson) def. The Swirl (Lee Johnson and Blake Christian)

During the event, MJF appeared on the video screen to challenge Ryohei Oiwa at Wrestle Kingdom 21, and Oiwa accepted the challenge.

Yuya Uemura, Toru Yano, Ryusuke Taguchi and Kazuma Sumi def. House of Torture (Ren Narita, Shun Skywalker, DOUKI and Yoshinobu Kanemaru)
YOH, Hyo and Shota Umino def. House of Torture (SHO, Dick Togo and Yujiro Takahashi)
