Kyle Fletcher recently discussed the possibility of branching out into acting, according to Fightful. While speaking in an interview with TMZ Sports, the AEW star was asked about potentially appearing in movies and television.

He expressed enthusiasm about the prospect of taking on acting roles, noting his lifelong creative side. He stated that he is currently entirely dedicated to his wrestling career.

> "I think I am an incredibly creative person. I always have been my whole life. So the thought of doing a movie or a TV show or something in acting is, is something that excites me. I will just say that right now I am so very focused on becoming the best pro wrestler that I possibly can be, and I know that doing anything in film or TV, it takes a huge dedication, a huge amount of time, and that's time that I would have to spend away from the ring, which I don't think I'm ready to do right now, especially on the run that I'm on."
