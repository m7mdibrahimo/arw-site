Recent backstage details have emerged regarding the short Women's World Championship reign of Stephanie Vaquer and Liv Morgan's subsequent victory at WWE Money in the Bank. According to Dave Meltzer on Wrestling Observer Radio, Vaquer's title win at a live event in Chile was intended as a special one-night moment for her home country before WWE quickly put the championship back on Morgan. Meltzer noted that management views Morgan and Rhea Ripley as the top two stars in the women's division on that brand.
