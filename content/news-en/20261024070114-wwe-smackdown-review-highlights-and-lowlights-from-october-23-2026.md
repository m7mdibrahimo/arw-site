The October 23, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown featured intense rivalries and championship build-ups, though some segments drew criticism. The WINC crew highlighted both the successes and failures of the broadcast.

Among the negatives, the show featured a fake-out Money in the Bank cash-in attempt by Tiffany Stratton on Liv Morgan. Critics felt the referee's slow reaction made the segment feel forced rather than unpredictable. Additionally, a scheduled match between Bakusai and MFT never occurred after Kyoki stole Haku's walking stick, leading to a brawl instead of wrestling. The main event was also criticized for being messy; what began as a tag match became a six-man tag after interference, ending with Trick Williams pinning Ricky Saints.

On the positive side, Giulia and Blake Monroe delivered a compelling street fight. Monroe secured the victory after using a fire extinguisher and a Neutralizer onto a pile of chairs. Cody Rhodes and Randy Orton brought significant intensity to their segment, where Nick Aldis confirmed their Crown Jewel match. Rhodes upped the stakes by declaring it a Last Man Standing match. Finally, WWE announced that CM Punk and Kevin Owens will face off in two weeks in Brooklyn, New York, following an intense pre-recorded promo segment.

Trick Williams def. Ricky Saints
