Mercedes Moné has achieved massive success in pro wrestling, but she considers winning the AEW Women's World Championship at All In as one of her proudest moments. Speaking on her MoneMag show, Moné reflected on what it meant to capture the world title after a turbulent period in her career.

"Winning the AEW Women's World Championship meant everything to me. Three years earlier, I had been sitting at All In as a fan, unsure of where I stood in wrestling or where my career was going next. I didn't have all the answers, but I remember watching AEW that night and knowing in my heart that it was the place for me," Moné said. She added that returning to Wembley three years later to win the title was beyond her imagination.

Moné explained that her only choice for an opponent at All In was Willow Nightingale, during a match where she broke her nose, recalling a similar injury when she broke her heel against Nightingale in NJPW. Despite dealing with injuries, both stars pushed through the pain to deliver an unforgettable match. After winning the title, Moné wished she could tell her past self what the future held, and noted that she received a warm round of applause and congratulations from her peers backstage.
