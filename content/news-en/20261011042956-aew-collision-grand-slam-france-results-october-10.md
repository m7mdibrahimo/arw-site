All Elite Wrestling held AEW Collision Grand Slam France at the Adidas Arena in Paris, France. The event featured several championship bouts and qualifiers for upcoming matches.

The Death Riders def. The Conglomeration
Nick Wayne def. Chris Jericho
Mike Bailey def. Lee Moriarty
Swerve Strickland def. Carlie Bravo
Andrade El Idolo def. Michael Oku
Harley Cameron & Kris Statlander def. Divine Dominion and Sisters Of Sin
The Young Bucks def. The Dogs

During the broadcast, Chris Jericho promoted an upcoming live edition of Collision from the Jericho Cruise in November. Konosuke Takeshita expressed his desire to compete in the Clockwork Carnage match, a sentiment echoed by Ricochet.

Jon Moxley addressed the audience, stating that the conflict is between the Death Riders and the world. Marina Shafir added that their actions represent a war for the love of professional wrestling, asserting that no one loves the sport more than the Death Riders.

Following the AEW National Championship match, Tommaso Ciampa confronted Andrade El Idolo. After the AEW Tag Team Championship match, Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher launched an attack on The Young Bucks.
