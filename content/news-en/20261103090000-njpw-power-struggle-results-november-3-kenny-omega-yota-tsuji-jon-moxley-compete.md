NJPW hosted its Power Struggle event on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at the Hiroshima Sun Plaza Hall in Japan. The card was broadcast for fans on NJPW World.

Aaron Wolf, Master Wato & KUUKAI def. United Empire (Francesco Akira, Jakob Austin Young & Zane Jay)
Mistico, KUSHIDA & Masatora Yasuda def. Unbound Co. (Titan, Gedo & Daiki Nagai)
TMDK (Ryohei Oiwa & Hartley Jackson) def. The Swirl (Lee Johnson & Blake Christian)

During the proceedings, MJF appeared on the video screen to issue a challenge to Ryohei Oiwa for Wrestle Kingdom 21. Oiwa accepted the proposal.
