Ilja Dragunov recently addressed the tribalism among wrestling fans following his move to AEW. In an interview with Jon Alba on The TakeDown on SI, Dragunov explained that he views professional wrestling as an emotionally driven business where the specific company fans choose matters less than the emotional connection they feel. He noted that tribalism ultimately stems from the same passion that makes wrestling work.

Dragunov also discussed his personal philosophy in the ring, describing his style as a combination of legitimate-looking combat and art, inspired by role models such as William Regal, Terry Funk, and Kobashi. He believes that AEW is the ideal place for his dramatic, fight-heavy approach, allowing him to bring his unique vision to a company that actively seeks it.
