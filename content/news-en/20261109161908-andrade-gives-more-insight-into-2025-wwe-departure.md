Andrade discussed his second departure from WWE during an appearance on Talk Is Jericho with Chris Jericho, according to Fightful. He left the promotion in mid-September 2025 following a month off television, and later returned to AEW.

He explained that his divorce from Charlotte Flair contributed to his discomfort because he had originally returned to work with her, and the creative direction provided to him was not strong.

> "I had my divorce (from Charlotte Flair). I went back to WWE to work with my ex-wife, and now that I'm divorced, I felt a little weird. I wasn't feeling at home or comfy. The story they gave, it wasn't a good or specific story or direction. Later, they wanted to send me to AAA. 'It's a good opportunity.' I moved here to work with WWE. I know AAA and respect AAA, but WWE is worldwide; AAA is just in Mexico. People in Mexico know me. I wanted people to know me around the world, like in Australia, Arabia, Europe, and other countries where WWE is. I made my name in Mexico; why go back to Mexico? I wanted to work in WWE with the top guys. I didn't feel good. We were talking; I didn't feel comfortable, and they gave me my release," he said.
