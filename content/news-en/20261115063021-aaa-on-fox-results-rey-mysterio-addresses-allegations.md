Lucha Libre AAA ran its AAA on FOX show on November 14 from Gimnasio Olímpico in Mexico City, Mexico, with broadcasts on FOX Latin America and YouTube, according to Fightful. Dorian Roldan kicked off the program by labeling Rey Mysterio a cheater who misused his authority. Mysterio answered the claims by stating that Roldan was humiliating his mother. Roldan stated that Mysterio remained suspended and that he served as the interim general manager, later calling Mysterio a terrible person as his evidence of cheating.

Jack Cartwheel def. Kento and Lince Dorado

Following that, Roldan revealed a Trios Championship match for the following Saturday featuring Perros Del Mal defending their titles against El Grande Amercano, La Parka, and Rey Fenix.

Omos def. Pimpinela Escarlata
