TNA World Champion Leon Slater recently spoke about his experience wrestling at John Cena's final WWE event, Saturday Night's Main Event in December 2025. Slater teamed up with Je'Von Evans to face AJ Styles and Dragon Lee in a tag team match during the show. Slater expressed his gratitude for being selected as one of the few young talents featured on the card, especially as a contracted TNA wrestler. He noted that it was an unforgettable feeling to represent TNA on such a major stage alongside other rising stars like Sol, Oba, and Evans. Slater also shared his excitement about meeting Cena and taking the opportunity to learn from him during the event.
