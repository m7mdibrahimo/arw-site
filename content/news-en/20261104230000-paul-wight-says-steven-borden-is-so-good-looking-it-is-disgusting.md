Paul Wight recently discussed the appearance of Steven Borden during a new interview, noting that he wishes he could look like him for just one day. Borden is the son of Sting and has appeared in AEW this year, stepping in for his father as a tag team partner for Darby Allin.

Borden is scheduled to compete in the Blood and Guts match this Wednesday on AEW Dynamite, though he could face further trouble if Wight decides to act on his intrusive thoughts. Speaking with First Coast News, Wight praised Borden's looks:

> "Steven Borden, who is Sting's son, who is the 6'5", 250-pound… No, he is so good looking, it's disgusting. I would like to look like him for one day. No, I'm serious. He's got the hair. He's all that and a bag of chips, and it's just like, you know, I'd like to punch that face."

In the same interview, Wight also described Darby Allin as the "ultimate babyface."
