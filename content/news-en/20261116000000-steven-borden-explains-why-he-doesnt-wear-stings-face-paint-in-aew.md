AEW's Steven Borden has emerged as a fan favorite since teaming with Darby Allin, often paying tribute to his father, Sting. However, unlike his father, Steven does not wear face paint or use a moniker, despite being introduced to AEW fans in that manner. During a recent interview on "101XL – Jacksonville's Sport Radio" alongside Allin, Steven explained his decision to present himself differently. "The last thing I want to do is be playing The Greatest Hits," Steven stated. He added, "I think, you know, you can do callbacks when time – when it makes sense, at the right time, but I think if I were to come out with face paint, and a trench coat, and a bat, like, I probably could have been booed out of the building in the beginning; and I'm still in the beginning right now." While Steven aims to build his own wrestling legacy, the physical similarities between him and his father are clear to industry veterans like Jim Ross. Though Steven is less than a year into his in-ring career, he remains undeterred by early setbacks, including a concussion, and maintains a readiness to face pressure without hesitation.
