Cody Rhodes revealed that he is still dealing with the effects of an orbital fracture he suffered during his WrestleMania 42 match, which may have been caused by Randy Orton's punt kick. Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Rhodes admitted that the injury has impacted his peripheral vision and work. Despite the lingering effects, Rhodes is not slowing down and is set to face Orton in a Last Man Standing match at WWE Crown Jewel next month. Rhodes acknowledged that he tends to push through serious injuries, though he advised younger wrestlers not to follow his example.
