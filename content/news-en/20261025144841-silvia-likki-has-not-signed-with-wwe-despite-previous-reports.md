Silvia Likki has not signed with WWE despite previous reports naming her as one of four new female recruits heading to the WWE Performance Center. According to Ringside News, the Italian CrossFit athlete did not sign a contract with the company. The confusion began after Bryan Alvarez of the Wrestling Observer reported that Likki had signed alongside Gabriela Martinez Asensi, Elly Finch, and Tayleigh Robertson, with all four women relocating to Orlando, Florida, following WWE tryouts. While Martinez Asensi, Finch, and Robertson remain part of the recruiting class, Likki is not an official addition to the roster. Likki is a CrossFit athlete from Italy with a large social media following, including over 200,000 followers on Instagram.
