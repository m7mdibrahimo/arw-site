Drako Starx reflected on his departure from WWE a full year after his contract was terminated on October 10. Starx took to social media to share a photo of his official release letter while highlighting his career milestones since that day.

> "A year ago today I was released from my WWE Contract One year later I have: -Wrestled in Barbados -Wrestled in South America -Wrestled for LUCHA Shows -Wrestled for multiple championships -Jumped a promoter, choked out his son, & took over his company Flip the Switch 👊🏼👌🏼"

Starx originally signed with WWE in 2023, performing for the WWE EVOLVE brand and appearing at numerous WWE NXT live events. The same day also brought a similar release for Lady Leigh, who shared her own thoughts with fans regarding her departure.
