Former WWE star Layla recalled that during her time as Divas Champion in 2012, a head writer told her that people in production meetings simply did not care about the women's division. Speaking on the Lay Talk podcast alongside Katie Lea Burchill, Layla discussed the limited opportunities female wrestlers faced during the Divas Era. Burchill reflected on her own career, noting that while she tried pitching storylines, she wished she had spent more time improving in FCW. Layla pushed back against the idea that the wrestlers were simply not ready, pointing out that even after returning from injury and winning the championship, she was told directly by a head writer that management did not care about the women.
