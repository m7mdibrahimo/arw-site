The Rock has officially confirmed his return to WWE for Survivor Series 2026, and Danhausen has wasted no time in mocking the legend. Following WWE's announcement that The People's Champion will be appearing in Houston, Danhausen took to X to share a photo of himself recreating one of The Rock's most infamous '90s looks. The image features Danhausen in his signature face paint, paired with The Rock's classic black shirt, jeans, silver chain, and fanny pack, all while posing next to a Christmas tree.

The Rock confirmed his appearance during a segment on The Pat McAfee Show, noting that he did not want to miss the company's celebration of his 30-year career. "Here's the truth. I would not miss next week for the world, man. I'm going to Houston. We don't have a problem. And I'm gonna go to Survivor Series," The Rock stated. He further revealed that he even worked with Sony to adjust his promotional schedule for his movie, Journey, to ensure he could make the trip to Houston.

Danhausen’s playful jab follows a previous interview in May 2026, where he jokingly named The Rock as a superstar he wanted to curse. While there is no indication that the two will interact at the upcoming event, Danhausen seems content with trolling the legend online. WWE Survivor Series: WarGames 2026 is scheduled for Saturday, November 28, at Daikin Park in Houston, Texas.
