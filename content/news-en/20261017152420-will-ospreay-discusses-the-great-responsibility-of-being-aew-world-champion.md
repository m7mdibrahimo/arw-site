Will Ospreay recently discussed the heavy responsibility that comes with holding the AEW World Championship and leading the company through challenging times. In a recent interview, Ospreay praised the AEW locker room for how they have handled adversity over the past few months. He explained that carrying the promotion's top title adds a significant level of pressure to his role. Ospreay emphasized his dedication to the team and his desire to deliver great shows for the fans, especially with upcoming events on the schedule. He understands what it takes to represent the company as world champion during a demanding period.
