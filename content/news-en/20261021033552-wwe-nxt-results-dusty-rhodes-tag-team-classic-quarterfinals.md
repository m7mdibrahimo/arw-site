The October 20, 2026, episode of WWE NXT from the Performance Center in Orlando featured intense action in the 2026 Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic. The show included a confrontation between Tony D'Angelo and Grayson Waller, as well as a brawl between Tavion Heights and Myles Borne.

El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr. and Galeno def. BirthRight in a 2026 Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic Quarterfinals match.

Izzi Dame def. Layla Diggs.

Following her victory, Izzi Dame accepted a challenge from Zaria for a match at Halloween Havoc. Additionally, NXT General Manager Robert Stone announced that Jackson Drake will defend his North American Championship against EK Prosper next week.
