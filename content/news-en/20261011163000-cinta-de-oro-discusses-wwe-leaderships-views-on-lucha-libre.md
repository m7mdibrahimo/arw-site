Cinta de Oro, formerly known as Sin Cara in WWE, recently discussed how WWE leadership viewed lucha libre during his time with the company under both Vince McMahon and Triple H. During an interview with TMZ's Inside The Ring, he explained that the style was often treated more as general entertainment than traditional professional wrestling or authentic lucha libre. Despite the challenges, he expressed gratitude for working with Attitude Era stars like Big Show, Kane, and The Undertaker, while noting that he frequently had to improvise matches in FCW without a proper match card. He also reiterated his past frustrations regarding the lack of opportunities for Latino wrestlers in WWE, recalling how he once asked for a one-year television run to prove his star power. While he remains critical of past booking decisions and questions current trends in AAA following its acquisition by WWE, he praised efforts to give younger Latino talent a chance to showcase their abilities on a bigger stage.
