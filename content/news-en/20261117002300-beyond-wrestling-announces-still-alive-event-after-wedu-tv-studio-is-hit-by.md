Beyond Wrestling has announced a new event titled "Still Alive" to take place alongside their scheduled "Rent Free" supershow. The addition comes after a lightning strike hit the WEDU TV studio, forcing NWA to cancel their event that was scheduled for Saturday, November 21.

Because contracted wrestlers Kylie Alexa, Liviyah, and Love Doug are local to New England, Beyond Wrestling added a matinee card featuring $10 tickets and a 3 pm bell time. This will lead into the "Rent Free" supershow at 8 pm, with both events broadcasting live on IWTV from Rhodes On The Pawtuxet in Cranston, Rhode Island.
