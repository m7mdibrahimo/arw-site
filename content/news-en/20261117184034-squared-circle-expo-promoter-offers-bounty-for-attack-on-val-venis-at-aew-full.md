Outside the AEW Full Gear event on November 14, former WWE wrestler Val Venis faced verbal hostility from a fan who told him to leave. Simultaneously, Ed Gonzalez, the promoter for Squared Circle Expo, sparked significant controversy by posting on Facebook that he would provide two VIP tickets to his event to the first person who assaulted Venis in the parking lot and provided video evidence.

Facebook eventually removed the post for violating community standards. Gonzalez dismissed the backlash, claiming the post was a joke. He later issued a sarcastic apology, stating he was only sorry because the tickets he offered were already sold out.

Venis had previously announced his intention to conduct political interviews with fans outside the venue, leading to the initial confrontation where he responded to the fan with a sarcastic thank you.
