Drew McIntyre recently revealed on Insight with Chris Van Vliet that he once pitched an idea to Vince McMahon to use his sword to cut off someone's arm during a backstage segment, but the idea was rejected. McIntyre explained that he was inspired by the realistic amputee effects in Saving Private Ryan and wanted to finally use the sword for something physical after carrying it to the ring for years. However, he noted that Vince had never seen the film and ultimately did not understand the pitch. McIntyre is now starring in the upcoming vertical short Agent of Shadows produced by WWE.
