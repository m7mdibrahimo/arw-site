The November 11 go-home episodes of AEW Dynamite and AEW Collision ahead of Full Gear delivered contrasting viewership outcomes. Both shows took place at the Maverik Center in Utah.

AEW Dynamite averaged 742,000 viewers, according to Wrestlenomics. While this surpassed the program's four-week average of 697,000 viewers, it dropped from the 792,000 viewers drawn on November 4. The key 18-49 demographic rating fell by 33 percent, sliding from 0.24 the previous week to 0.16.

In contrast, AEW Collision experienced significant growth directly following the flagship broadcast. The show averaged 500,000 viewers, representing a 33 percent increase from the previous week's 376,000 viewers. That marked the program's highest audience in the past month. The 18-49 demographic rating rose to 0.12, up 50 percent from the prior week's 0.08.

Both AEW broadcasts are posting strong numbers in the fourth quarter of 2026 compared to the same period in 2025. AEW Dynamite has increased its viewership by 42 percent, while AEW Collision has grown by 63 percent.
