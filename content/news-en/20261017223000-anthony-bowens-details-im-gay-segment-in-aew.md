Anthony Bowens recently discussed his memorable moment on the June 23, 2023 episode of AEW Rampage, where he rejected Harley Cameron's advances by stating "I'm gay." The segment, pitched by QT Marshall, generated a massive crowd reaction in Chicago and became a celebrated piece of LGBTQ+ representation. Bowens revealed that AEW originally planned a similar segment earlier, and he loved that this version was lighthearted. Although the segment was slightly edited for television due to not airing live, he recalled the intense crowd reaction and the amusing reactions from Billy Gunn and Max Caster during the filming.
