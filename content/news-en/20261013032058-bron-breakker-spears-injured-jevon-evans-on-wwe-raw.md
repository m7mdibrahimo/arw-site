Bron Breakker attacked an injured Je’Von Evans with a spear during the October 12, 2026 episode of WWE RAW. The show opened with Penta celebrating his Money in the Bank victory and reflecting on his journey to the briefcase. Evans then interrupted the celebration while wearing a sling, with taped ribs and a noticeable limp following the ladder match. Evans admitted he was banged up but wanted to congratulate Penta face-to-face and show respect. Breakker suddenly appeared and blasted the vulnerable Evans with a spear before leaving him to be checked by officials. Evans' condition is now a major question, and Breakker made it clear he will target anyone at their weakest.
