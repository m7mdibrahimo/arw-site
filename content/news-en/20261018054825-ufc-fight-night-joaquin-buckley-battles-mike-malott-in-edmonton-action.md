UFC Fight Night took place at Rogers Place in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, featuring a full card of action broadcast live on Paramount+.

Match 1: Bantamweight Match
Su Young You defeated Cody Chovancek by submission with a kimura at 4:19 of Round 3.

🏆 Winner: Su Young You

Match 2: Women's Bantamweight Match
Chelsea Chandler defeated Melissa Croden by submission with a kimura at 4:52 of Round 1.

🏆 Winner: Chelsea Chandler

Match 3: Bantamweight Match
Steven Koslow defeated Chad Anheliger by submission with a rear-naked choke at 4:31 of Round 3.

🏆 Winner: Steven Koslow

Match 4: Women's Flyweight Match
Katlyn Cerminara defeated Mackenzie Stiller by TKO with strikes at 4:46 of Round 1.

🏆 Winner: Katlyn Cerminara

Match 5: Heavyweight Match
Joel Faglier defeated Javad Mahjoub by TKO with punches at 2:11 of Round 2.

🏆 Winner: Joel Faglier

Match 6: Middleweight Match
Nick Galanti defeated Julien Leblanc by submission with a face crank at 1:26 of Round 1.

🏆 Winner: Nick Galanti

Match 7: Heavyweight Match
Tanner Boser defeated Jhonata Diniz by TKO with punches at 4:26 of Round 3.

🏆 Winner: Tanner Boser

Match 8: Lightweight Match
Mandel Nallo defeated Nate Landwehr by TKO with a leg kick and punches at 0:47 of Round 2.

🏆 Winner: Mandel Nallo

Match 9: Bantamweight Match
Timmy Cuamba defeated Louis Jourdain by submission with a neck crank at 2:48 of Round 2.

🏆 Winner: Timmy Cuamba

Match 10: Middleweight Match
Marc-Andre Barriault defeated Kyle Daukaus by TKO with strikes at 4:28 of Round 1.

🏆 Winner: Marc-Andre Barriault

Match 11: Lightweight Match
Cristian Perez defeated Kyle Nelson by knockout with punches at 0:27 of Round 1.

🏆 Winner: Cristian Perez

Match 12: Women's Flyweight Match
Jasmine Jasudavicius defeated Erin Blanchfield by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28).

🏆 Winner: Jasmine Jasudavicius

Main Event: Welterweight Match
Mike Malott defeated Joaquin Buckley by knockout with a knee at 2:32 of Round 2.

🏆 Winner: Mike Malott
