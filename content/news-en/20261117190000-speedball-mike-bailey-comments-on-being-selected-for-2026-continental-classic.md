Ahead of the 2026 Continental Classic, Mike "Speedball" Bailey has expressed his excitement after being named as a participant. AEW CEO Tony Khan recently announced the field for the upcoming tournament, which marks Bailey's second appearance in the event. Following the news, Bailey took to X to share his enthusiasm and commitment to the challenge.

> "I was made for the Continental Classic. I have dedicated my entire life to be ready for opportunities like this. I will pour my heart and soul into every single match and I will be the winner. It's starts this Wednesday! #AEWDynamite."

Bailey has been placed in the Gold League, which also includes reigning champion Jon Moxley, his former tag team partner Kevin Knight, Nick Wayne, Tommaso Ciampa, and Orange Cassidy. The Blue League consists of Ricochet, Brody King, Bandido, Adam Page, Daniel Garcia, and Jay White. In last year's tournament, Bailey competed in the Gold League and finished at the bottom of the table, recording wins against Knight and Kyle Fletcher, while suffering losses to the late PAC, Kazuchika Okada, and Jack Perry. The 2026 Continental Classic begins this week on AEW Dynamite, with the final scheduled for Worlds End on December 19.
