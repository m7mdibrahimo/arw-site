Rosemary is currently sidelined from in-ring competition in TNA Wrestling due to a torn ACL, an injury she sustained while wrestling at an independent event this past summer. According to Fightful Select, the recovery process could keep her out of action for an additional nine to ten months. Despite the injury, Rosemary remains active within TNA. She has been working at television tapings in an agent, producer, and coaching capacity, utilizing her decade of experience to help train other talent. The injury previously forced TNA to alter plans for the Knockouts World Tag Team Championships, where Kiera Hogan eventually stepped in as Allie’s partner. While TNA aired a vignette on the September 24 episode of iMPACT teasing her return, it is clear that her official return to the ring will take significantly longer than originally anticipated.
