Diamond Dallas Page used a Kevin Nash meme based on his old WWE Diesel gimmick to mock high gas prices. DDP shared an image on X designed like a gas station price board, listing Diesel at $6.10 9/10 alongside regular, plus, and premium prices. He joked that the last time Diesel was that high, Nash was owning WWE. Nash famously portrayed Diesel during his first WWE run in the mid-1990s, capturing the WWE Championship in 1994 and holding it for nearly a year.
