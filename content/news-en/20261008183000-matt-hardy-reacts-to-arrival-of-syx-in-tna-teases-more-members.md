Matt Hardy recently discussed the debut of former Wyatt Sicks members Samuel Shaw and Joseph Sawyer in TNA Wrestling, following their attack on The Hardys during the September 24 episode of TNA iMPACT. Speaking on his podcast, Hardy praised their star power and impact. "I think they bring a lot of star power. WWE didn't obviously utilize them to their maximum potential. But they did have that powerful debut and they were going to be a faction that carried on, you know, kind of Bray Wyatt's vision and and kind of character and spiritual and almost supernatural type work that he loved to do and he loved be involved in," Hardy stated. He also addressed the possibility of Bo Dallas joining the group and hinted at future additions. "I don't think they decided to title the faction 'Syx' just because they thought they were going to be two," Hardy said, teasing that more members are coming. Matt and Jeff Hardy are set to face Syx in a Falls Count Anywhere match at the upcoming Bound For Glory event.
