Tony Khan revealed that Red Velvet lacks medical clearance to perform at Final Battle, resulting in the ROH Women's Television Championship being vacated. A new titleholder will be decided through a tournament, with the final match taking place at Final Battle. The quarterfinal matches feature Zayda Steel vs. Robyn Renegade, Isla Dawn vs. Viva Van, Trish Adora vs. Dani Luna, and Vert Vixen vs. Mina Shirakawa.

The updated lineup for ROH Final Battle includes Athena (c) vs. Hyan for the ROH Women's Championship, Action Andretti (c) vs. Adam Priest vs. ELP for the ROH Television Championship, and Bandido (c) vs. TBD in a Survival of the Fittest match for the ROH World Championship, alongside the tournament final for the ROH Women's Television Championship.
