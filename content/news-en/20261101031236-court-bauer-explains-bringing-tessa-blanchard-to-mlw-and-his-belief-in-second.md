MLW founder Court Bauer has detailed the decision to sign Tessa Blanchard, who will make her promotion debut against Keyra at MLW Saturday Night SuperFight. Blanchard returned to prominent wrestling through TNA Wrestling in 2024, after being largely absent from major platforms since 2020 due to allegations of bullying and racial slurs directed at La Rosa Negra.

Appearing on Wrestling Observer Radio, Bauer shared his philosophy on granting talent another opportunity:

> "I'm a firm believer in second chances. If I didn't get a second, third, fourth chance, maybe, I mean, I wouldn't be in the position I am because someone opened the door for me. You can't just grade everyone on, like, the same, you know, system. It's not black and white. There's grays in this, and you wanna see kind of where they are, where their head's at when you talk to them, and you converse, and they gotta kinda see what's happened. How they've changed, do they feel differently? How can they reflect? Are they just a sociopath?"

Bauer noted that fans often support authentic comeback stories when individuals show genuine growth. Blanchard had previously denied the accusations and shared a photo alongside Negra in 2023.
