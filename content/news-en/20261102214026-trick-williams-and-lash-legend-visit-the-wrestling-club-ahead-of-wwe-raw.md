Trick Williams and Lash Legend made a surprise visit to The Wrestling Club on November 2, ahead of the double taping of WWE RAW and WWE SmackDown at the Barclays Center. The Wrestling Club, which is run by Victor Taylor Perry, is located at KIPP AMP Middle School in Brooklyn. The organization provides young fans with a space to watch wrestling and build community. Several WWE stars, including Willow Nightingale and Mercedes Moné, have visited the students previously. During this visit, Trick Williams and Lash Legend were greeted with excitement by the attendees, who broke into chants of "Let's Go Trick" upon their arrival.
