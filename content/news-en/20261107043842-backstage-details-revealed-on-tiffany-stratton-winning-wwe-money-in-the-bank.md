New backstage details have emerged regarding Tiffany Stratton winning the Women's Money in the Bank Ladder Match. WWE had already decided on Stratton as the briefcase winner in the weeks leading up to the event, with no major last-minute changes made to the creative outcome. However, the competitors in the match were only informed of the result on Thursday night, just prior to the show.

Creative plans were also being developed for Stratton to eventually challenge Chelsea Green and defeat her for the championship. While those plans remain an option, her Money in the Bank victory provides WWE with additional narrative directions. Meanwhile, the outcome of a six-woman tag team match on WWE SmackDown was altered on the day of the show. Stratton teamed with Jacy Jayne and Lash Legend to defeat Lola Vice, Roxanne Perez, and Sol Ruca, despite initial plans calling for the opposite team to win.
