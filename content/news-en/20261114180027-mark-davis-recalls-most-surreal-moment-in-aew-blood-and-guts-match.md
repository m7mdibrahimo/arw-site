AEW wrestler Mark Davis recently shared his thoughts on participating in this year's men's Blood And Guts match, identifying the experience as one of the highlights of his career.

The match featured several wild moments, including exploding backpacks, threats with a screwdriver, and a fall from the top of the double cage structure. Speaking to PWTorch, Davis highlighted the moment when Darby Allin was thrown off the cage by Wardlow as the most memorable part of the night.

> "Oh, it was awesome, dude, it felt great. And then the most surreal moment from it was obviously standing inside of the ring looking up, seeing Darby Wardlow on the cage and then I looked from the screen, so I watched like the view of Wardlow throwing him on the screen and then I just saw Darby’s body fall in front of the screen through the tables. I was like, ‘Yeah,’ It was awesome. It was great."

Davis remains focused on his ongoing work with AEW following his involvement in the high-stakes match.
