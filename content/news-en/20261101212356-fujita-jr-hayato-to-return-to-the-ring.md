Fujita Hayato plans to wrestle again in 2027. He stated that he is leaving his position as the commissioner of Michinoku Pro so he can challenge the reigning Tohoku Junior Heavyweight Champion, Sangre Azteca Jr.

Back in late 2018, he shared that doctors found a spinal tumor and ependymoma. He made a comeback to competition in 2022 and captured the MPW Tohoku Junior Heavyweight Championship and LIDET UWF Championship, though he stepped away once more in 2024 when his cancer symptoms came back, prior to participating in an exhibition match in June 2025.

Even though he spent most of his career with Michinoku Pro, he has also wrestled for GLEAT, Pro Wrestling ZERO1, NJPW, and additional promotions throughout Japan, according to Fightful.
