The October 13, 2026, episode of WWE NXT aired live from the Capitol Wrestling Center in Orlando, Florida. The show featured Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic tournament action and a number one contender's match for the WWE NXT Championship.

The Vanity Project (Ricky Smokes and Brad Baylor) def. Mini Vikingo and Chazz "Starboy" Hall in a Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic tournament quarterfinal match.

Tristan Angels def. Saquon Shugars in a singles match.

During the show, Wren Sinclair appeared to address her recent victory in the WWE NXT Women's Championship number one contender's battle royal. NXT General Manager Robert Stone also confirmed that Kali Armstrong would receive a match for the AAA Reina de Reinas Championship against La Catalina later tonight. Additionally, Tony D’Angelo confronted Myles Borne ahead of their upcoming title contender's bout.
