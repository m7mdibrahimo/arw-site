WWE hosted an NXT live event on November 7 at the MLK Center in Gainesville, Florida.

In the opening match, Elio LeFleur defeated Ulka Sasaki. Next, Andi Summers and Kenzie defeated Alix Brooks and Zoe Hines in a tag team match. Elijah Holyfield then defeated Niko Vance.

In a singles match, Myka Lockwood, accompanied by Brad Baylor and Ricky Smokes, defeated Cara Brown. Next, Zaria successfully defended her NXT Women's North American Championship against Vanta The Unknown.

DarkState, consisting of Cutler James and Osiris Griffin with Dion Lennox in their corner, defeated Garrett Beck and Milosh Jovic in a tag team match. Later, EK Prosper defeated Kam Hendrix.

In another title match, Nikkita Lyons successfully defended her EVOLVE Women's Championship against Skylar Raye.

In the main event, the team of Shiloh Hill, The Dropouts (Jackson Goonrey and Tyran Tuckey), and Zilla Fatu defeated BirthRight (Channing Lorenzo and Uriah Connors) and The Vanity Project (Brad Baylor and Ricky Smokes) in an elimination match.
