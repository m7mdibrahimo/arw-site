In life, death and taxes are the only certainties, but according to Eric Bischoff, fellow WWE Hall of Famer Ric Flair might be immune to at least one of them. Speaking on an episode of "83 Weeks," Bischoff and co-host Conrad Thompson discussed Flair's remarkable resilience through numerous health challenges, particularly a recent fall that left him bleeding heavily from an eye. Bischoff stated that after humanity is gone, Vince McMahon and Ric Flair will still be walking the earth because Flair is indestructible like a cockroach. On October 23, Flair suffered a sudden fall outside a Tampa restaurant, cutting his head and eye and requiring emergency surgery at a hospital. Thompson, who is also Flair's son-in-law, noted that "The Nature Boy" sustained significant soft tissue damage but managed to return home by the following morning. Thompson explained that a blood clot in Flair's leg has complicated his mobility, which also forced him to cancel his appearance at Washington State Horror Con after preventing him from traveling. Bischoff expressed hope that recent social media photos of Flair back in a bar were accurate and that he is recovering well enough to enjoy himself.
