Becky Lynch began her wrestling career at a young age, but she stepped away for six years starting at age 19. She returned in 2013 to sign with WWE. During an interview on "So U Think U Can Sports?", Lynch admitted she was unsure if a future existed for her in professional wrestling until WWE offered her an opportunity.

Lynch explained that she had a deep passion for the sport and struggled to find success in other areas. She noted that leaving wrestling created a significant void in her life regarding fighting, art, and creativity. She expressed a strong desire to return, but after signing with WWE, she lived in constant fear of being fired for several years.

According to Wrestling Inc, Lynch clarified that her anxiety was not due to previous injuries, but rather her belief that she was not good enough and her tendency to overthink everything. She frequently feared losing her job during her early days in NXT, but she credited Dusty Rhodes for supporting her backstage, as he likely empathized with her status as an outsider in the locker room.
