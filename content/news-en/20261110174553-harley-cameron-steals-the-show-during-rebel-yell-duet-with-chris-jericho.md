During the latest Chris Jericho Rock ‘N’ Wrestling Rager at Sea, Harley Cameron joined Chris Jericho on stage for a memorable duet of Billy Idol’s classic track “Rebel Yell.” Fozzy’s official Instagram account shared footage from the performance, which featured Jericho dressed as Billy Idol and Cameron taking the lead on vocals for several portions of the song to the delight of the crowd. Fozzy highlighted the collaboration, noting that it was not Cameron’s first time sharing the stage with the band during the cruise. Cameron continues to blend music and comedy into her wrestling career, showcasing her microphone skills beyond her usual on-screen promos and capturing plenty of attention from fans during the event.
