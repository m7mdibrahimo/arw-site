AJ Styles may not be taking over WWE NXT as some previously believed. Despite weeks of speculation regarding him potentially replacing Shawn Michaels, recent reports indicate that WWE has different plans for the former champion that focus on discovering and developing new talent.

According to PWInsider, WWE intends to utilize Styles to "assist in scouting and the WWE ID program" rather than tasking him with running the brand. His increased presence in the NXT backstage area was partly due to Terry Taylor recovering from knee surgery, which sparked rumors about his future responsibilities.

Instead of managing NXT television, Styles will have an influential role in identifying independent wrestlers who receive opportunities within the developmental system. This allows him to apply his extensive experience to help the company build its future.
