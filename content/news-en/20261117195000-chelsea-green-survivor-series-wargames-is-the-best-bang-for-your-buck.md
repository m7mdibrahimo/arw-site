Chelsea Green considers Survivor Series WarGames to be the best value for fans among all WWE premium live events. Known as one of the promotion's premier shows, Survivor Series has a rich history of iconic tag team bouts. In recent years, the company has integrated WarGames into the main roster, making it a yearly staple of the event. During an interview with Brad Gilmore and The Collection, Green explained why she believes the show offers the most for fans.

> "Oh, my gosh. I mean, I feel like when I say expect the unexpected with wrestling and with WWE, Survivor Series War Games is the epitome of that because we never know what could happen at that PLE. Like, anything could happen. Anyone could come out. Any weapons could be used. Seriously, I think this is the best bang for your buck in terms of PLE because you're going to get a little bit of everything. So just know whether I'm involved or not, I will be there watching because crazy stuff happens at War Games."

Green emphasized that the unpredictability of the matches and the variety of action make it a must-watch event, regardless of her own participation.
