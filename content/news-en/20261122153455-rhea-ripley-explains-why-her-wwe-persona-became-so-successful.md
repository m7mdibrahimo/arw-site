Rhea Ripley has grown into one of WWE's premier performers, and she credits her rise to dropping artificial expectations and showing her authentic self. Speaking on The Wrestling Classic podcast, the former Women's World Champion reflected on transitioning from her early NXT appearance into the darker, dominant character that reshaped her career.

Ripley revealed that the change followed a challenging stretch at the WWE Performance Center, where trainers criticized her and she felt pressured to act like someone she was not. She eventually decided to embrace her real personality and personal tastes, including her passion for metal bands like Motionless in White and Suicide Silence, which directly inspired her signature look.

> "I was trying to be someone that I knew I wasn't just to fit into the mold that I thought this place wanted. But I couldn't do it anymore. Like I didn't like being fake. I just wanted to be comfortable and confident in my own skin."

She added that her growth came naturally over the years as she accepted her own flaws and quirks. Ripley believes fans respond to her work because they can feel when a performer is genuinely authentic on screen.
