Bayley recently shared her perspective on why wrestlers trying too hard to play traditional characters can sometimes hinder their performances. During an appearance on The AJ Styles Podcast, the WWE veteran discussed her career and offered advice to independent wrestlers looking to reach WWE.

Bayley explained that her own career breakthrough happened when she stopped overthinking her persona and instead leaned into her genuine emotions during her time in NXT. She noted that fans can easily tell when a performer is genuinely having fun in the ring compared to when they are just faking it.

AJ Styles agreed with her viewpoint, suggesting that a successful wrestling persona often involves amplifying one's actual personality rather than inventing something entirely new. Bayley acknowledged that while having a standout character remains important, connecting with true raw emotion creates a much more authentic connection with the audience.
