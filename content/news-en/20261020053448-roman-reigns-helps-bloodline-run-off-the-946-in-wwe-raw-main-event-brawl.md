World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns arrived to assist The Bloodline during a chaotic main event segment on "WWE Raw." After Jimmy and Jey Uso called out the 946 faction to address recent attacks on their family, including Solo Sikoa and Jacob Fatu, a brawl ensued. Sikoa initially targeted Royce Keys, but the numbers game turned against them when Carmelo Hayes interfered with a crowbar. As Keys threatened Sikoa, Reigns' entrance music played, and he joined his family to clear the ring. Reigns landed a Superman Punch on Keys and a spear on Bronco Nima, while Sikoa hit a Samoan Spike. The Bloodline stood tall as the 946 faction retreated through the crowd.
