During a recent interview on Flagrant, Cody Rhodes discussed the accomplishments that have eluded him in WWE, noting that while he still has a chance to win the Money In The Bank briefcase, he believes the United States Championship is unlikely to be part of his future. "I haven't won Money In The Bank. I think the only other title on the board for WWE is the United States title, which I don't think will probably ever happen in my career. But those are the things," Rhodes said. He also expressed a desire to achieve unexpected milestones, adding, "I think the things that haven't been done, not to sound grand or vague or both, are things we haven't seen coming yet." Additionally, Rhodes shared a story involving Solo Sikoa, Brock Lesnar, and Roman Reigns.
