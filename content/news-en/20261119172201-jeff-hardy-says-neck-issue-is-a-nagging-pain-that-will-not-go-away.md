Jeff Hardy has provided an update on his current status and neck issue. Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Hardy discussed his ongoing physical struggles. "I’m feeling alright. It’s just a nagging pain that will not go away," Hardy said. He admitted to dealing with significant fear before his matches, worrying about a potential herniated disc. Hardy recently performed a major Swanton bomb at a TNA pay-per-view, acknowledging that it is hard for him to resist those high-risk spots when he feels confident. Hardy is currently off television following a cinematic match where he was attacked by Syx (Samuel Shaw and Joseph Sawyer).
