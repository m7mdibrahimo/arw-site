Andrade El Idolo discussed his recent runs in WWE and AEW over the past few years. Speaking on an episode of "Talk Is Jericho" with Chris Jericho, Andrade explained that AEW grants him greater freedom in shaping his character and delivering promos to the audience.

Conversely, Andrade noted that while he preferred his second run in WWE over his first, he felt constrained by stereotypical expectations tied to his Mexican heritage. He stated on the podcast:

> "In WWE they never gave me a real character, I was always the Mexican guy. They used to write promos for me to practice, but they didn't sound like me. Whereas in AEW they just give you the general idea, which gives you more freedom to express yourself."

Even so, Andrade emphasized that WWE treated him well and offered more opportunities during his second stint, though he aimed to showcase abilities beyond what management asked of him. Since returning to AEW this past January, he expressed delight at the cultural diversity and varied wrestling styles there, alongside the chance to collaborate with incredible talents from around the globe.
