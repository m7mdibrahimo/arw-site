Former WWE co-president George Barrios recently discussed the rapid success of the WWE Network during an appearance on "My World with Jeff Jarrett," according to Wrestling Inc. Barrios explained that the company recognized the potential of its untapped content library and decided to pivot away from the $50 monthly pay-per-view model shared with cable companies by offering a direct service for $10 a month.

> "That becomes the essence of the WWE Network."

Barrios noted that pay-per-views served as the anchor while additional programming was intended to retain casual fans. According to the former executive, Vince McMahon was initially skeptical about the venture.

> "George... How much wrestling do you think people can watch?"

Following the launch, between 500,000 and 700,000 subscribers signed up within hours, stunning everyone involved. Barrios later recalled teasing McMahon in a meeting about the initial doubt, telling him that people apparently wanted to watch quite a bit of wrestling.
