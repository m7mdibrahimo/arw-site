Shannon Moore recently reflected on his time with WWE and discussed the infamous over-the-top-rope belly-to-belly suplex he took from Brock Lesnar on WWE SmackDown. During an appearance on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Moore explained that the dangerous spot ultimately saved his job. He recalled that before the show, Terry Taylor told him he needed to do something that would make Vince McMahon want to keep him.

Moore approached Lesnar with the idea of being thrown from the center of the ring to the floor. Although Lesnar initially hesitated due to safety concerns, Moore insisted, and the high-risk bump successfully prevented his release.
