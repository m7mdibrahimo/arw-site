During the November 6 The Crash Lucha Libre show, Hijo del Rey Misterio faced Xtreme Tiger in a mask versus mask contest. Hijo del Rey Misterio emerged as the winner of the encounter. Following the match, while his opponent stayed in a corner ahead of removing his mask, a spectator climbed onto the apron near Hijo del Rey Misterio who stood on the opposite side. The individual struck the competitor in the midsection, leaving him visibly surprised and unprepared. The attendee then landed a harder blow to the same area, prompting Hijo del Rey Misterio to instantly floor him with a strike that sent the person off the apron.
