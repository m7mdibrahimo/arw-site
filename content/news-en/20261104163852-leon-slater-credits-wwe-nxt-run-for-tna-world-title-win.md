TNA World Champion Leon Slater recently discussed the significant impact his time in WWE NXT had on his career, emphasizing that he wouldn't have captured the world title without that experience.

During a new interview, Leon Slater explained that his appearances in WWE NXT served as a crucial stepping stone that elevated his skills and provided him with valuable lessons he brought back to TNA.

Leon Slater also noted that the current partnership between promotions allows talent to work with experienced veterans such as Nic Nemeth, Matt and Jeff Hardy, and Frankie Kazarian.
