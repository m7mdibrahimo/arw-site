Chad Gable recently spoke about losing his Intercontinental Championship to Ethan Page on the November 2 episode of WWE Raw. During a new interview, Gable reflected on finally receiving the singles run he wanted later in his career.

> "To finally have this chance now where — it's crazy right because it's so late in your career. I feel like I'm finally having the chance to have the run I’ve always wanted. But, I think the time that I had with it, I really delivered on what I personally wanted to do, both for myself and the business overall and the company in just providing matches and the quality of work that I think a title like that deserves."

Rey Fenix secured the right to challenge for the Intercontinental Championship by pinning Gable and Big Cass during the November 9 episode of Raw.
