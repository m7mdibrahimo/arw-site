Diamond Dallas Page believes that Cody Rhodes should eventually turn heel, pointing to a similar character shift that revitalized Hulk Hogan's career.

Speaking on TMZ's Inside the Ring, DDP discussed Rhodes and his history of fan interaction, recalling how he would spend hours talking and signing autographs after shows during the early days of AEW. DDP compared this to Hogan's legendary transition into the leader of the nWo, suggesting that Rhodes could benefit from a similar transformation while he remains a top star. He also shared his thoughts on WWE Unreal, noting that he appreciates how such projects pull back the curtain to show the people behind the characters.
