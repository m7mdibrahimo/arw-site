All Elite Wrestling has announced two additional tag team matches for the upcoming October 14 edition of AEW Dynamite and Collision. The three-hour broadcast will feature AEW World Champion Will Ospreay and AEW National Champion Andrade El Idolo facing Samoa Joe and Tommaso Ciampa. Additionally, Britt Baker and Persephone will compete against Dani Luna and Hyan. These bouts join a packed card featuring Clockwork Carnage qualifiers and various championship contests.
