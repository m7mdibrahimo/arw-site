Tony Khan had to adjust plans for the November 11 episode of AEW Dynamite and Collision due to significant travel difficulties affecting the talent roster. During the AEW Full Gear media call, Khan explained that while the situation in Salt Lake City was challenging, he was pleased with how the three-and-a-half-hour broadcast turned out. The show featured a TBS Championship match between Queen Aminata, Hyan, and Maya World as the main event. Khan revealed that Hyan did not arrive at the arena until the show was already underway. He also noted that Nick Wayne arrived late in the night, partly due to his involvement with the Jericho cruise. Khan stated, "You've got numerous flight issues and you're trying to put a great wrestling show together, imagine how I feel at the end of the night when I think it was a great show and everything came together great." He credited the network for allowing flexibility with the schedule and expressed pride in the roster's ability to pivot under pressure.
