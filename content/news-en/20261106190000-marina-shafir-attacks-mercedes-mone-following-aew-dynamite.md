Marina Shafir expressed her intense frustration with Mercedes Moné following this week's episode of AEW Dynamite, vowing to make her pay for her recent comments. The two came face-to-face during the broadcast, where the AEW Women's World Champion mocked Shafir by calling her a follower of Jon Moxley and claiming her relevance only stemmed from that alliance.

Shafir continued her verbal assault on social media, criticizing Mone's status and resources.

> "Mercedes, Miss CEO, you spoiled little s*. Yeah, I don't have the lights. I don't have the money. I don't have the production of an entire company telling the world how special I am. No, because if I had any of that, I wouldn't be the woman that I am today. But what do I know? I'm just Mox's little bih."

Shafir claimed she was the primary motivator behind the Death Riders' recent actions and promised to make Mone cry when they meet at AEW Full Gear on November 14. This encounter marks their first singles match and Shafir's first world title opportunity since 2022.

> "You know, how about you ask Mox if I'm a little bih? How about you ask Claudio? How about you ask Yuta, Daniel, Gabe? What do you think they're gonna tell you? What they're gonna tell you is I was the instigator. I was the motivator that helped a group change professional wrestling forever. But Mercedes, you're no one's little bih, right? At Full Gear, I'm gonna make you cry like one."
