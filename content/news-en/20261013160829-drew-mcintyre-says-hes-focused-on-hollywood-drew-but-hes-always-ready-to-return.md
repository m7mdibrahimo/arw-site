Drew McIntyre is currently prioritizing his acting career, having secured roles in projects such as The Last Druid, Highlander, and the animated film Hexed, following his 2024 appearance in The Killer's Game. The multi-time WWE Champion, who last competed at WrestleMania 42 in April, discussed his potential return to the ring during an interview with OutKick.

> "We’ll see. You know, I’m as big as I’ve ever been. I’m 285 right now. I’m 10 pounds bigger than I was for Agent of Shadows. I’m in the best shape of my life. I posted myself running the ropes the other day, you know? Keep myself in ring shape. I’m always ready to go. I think it got like over eight million views just bouncing off the ropes a few times — across my social media platforms — but right now, I’m focused on ‘Hollywood Drew.’ So, we’ll see. We will see. I’m not giving anything away."

McIntyre previously held the WWE Championship for a third time in early 2026 after defeating Cody Rhodes, though he lost the title back to Rhodes two months later.
