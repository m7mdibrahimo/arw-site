Claudio Castagnoli says that competitive League of Legends and pro wrestling share notable similarities within their communities. Speaking with Summoner's Sofa, Castagnoli compared the passionate fan bases of both industries, noting how dedicated supporters are quick to embrace newcomers and share their enthusiasm. He also highlighted the matching presentation elements, such as team entrances, announcers, and crowd reactions, while expressing gratitude to fans who still remember his past match appearance. Elsewhere in the interview, Castagnoli discussed sewing his own ring gear during the early days of his career.
