Clark Connors discussed his decision to sign with AEW following his run in NJPW. Connors, who is currently part of The Cut stable, spoke with TMZ’s Inside The Ring about what convinced him to join AEW and whether other companies showed interest. He explained that joining AEW had always been a goal of his. He noted that he did not actively shop his name around too much because he had the opportunity to work back and forth with New Japan. However, once he joined AEW, he realized he did not want to keep traveling back to Japan for long periods, noting the difficulties of the flight. When asked about Tony Khan's pitch, Connors admitted that financial incentives played a major role in his decision to try out the promotion.
