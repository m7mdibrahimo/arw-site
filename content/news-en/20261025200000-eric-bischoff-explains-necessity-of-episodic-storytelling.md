WWE Hall of Famer Eric Bischoff discussed the importance of relying on episodic and dramatic storytelling in professional wrestling shows to continuously attract fans. During a recent episode of his "83 Weeks" podcast, Bischoff explained that wrestling shows need a dramatic feel similar to soap operas to ensure their success, emphasizing that fans should look forward to seeing how events unfold the following week. He added that what set WCW apart in the past under his management was the reliance on these interconnected stories that gave matches real meaning rather than being just pointless encounters. Bischoff previously criticized the storytelling style in AEW, while praising the efforts of Triple H and Bruce Prichard in regaining WWE fans after the final period of Vince McMahon, according to Wrestling Inc.
