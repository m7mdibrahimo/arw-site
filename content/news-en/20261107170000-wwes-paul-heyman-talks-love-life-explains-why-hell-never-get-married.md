WWE Hall of Famer Paul Heyman recently discussed his personal life and explained why he has no plans to ever get married. During an interview with Graham Bensinger, Heyman opened up about his views on romance and relationships, describing marriage as a trap. He explained that a relationship should be based entirely on choice rather than a piece of paper, emphasizing his desire for partners to remain with him out of absolute freedom to leave. Heyman noted that while he has shared close, family-like bonds with top talents such as Brock Lesnar, Roman Reigns, and CM Punk through his professional career, his personal pursuits and his children always come before romantic relationships. Speaking of his family, Heyman shared that his son Jacob has recently launched his own talk show called Roundtable, with Paul serving as executive producer.
