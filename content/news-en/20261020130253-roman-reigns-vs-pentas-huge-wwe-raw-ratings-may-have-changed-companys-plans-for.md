WWE may have found a new formula for drawing viewers, and Penta is a major reason behind it. His match against Roman Reigns in Mexico City delivered some of the highest ratings for WWE RAW this year, leading to belief that the company has taken notice. Speaking on Wrestling Observer Radio, Dave Meltzer discussed the impact of the match and whether RUSH could eventually land in WWE. Meltzer noted that WWE is now looking at Mexican talent differently, especially after Penta challenged Reigns for the World Heavyweight Championship on September 14. Meltzer stated that the match delivered the second-biggest worldwide audience of the year for WWE RAW, highlighting that the strong numbers came from global markets including the United States. The September 14 episode drew more than three million global Netflix views, leaving both WWE and Netflix extremely pleased. Meltzer also pointed out that Nielsen is placing greater emphasis on Hispanic households, making Mexican wrestlers in major television roles increasingly valuable. He suggested this could lead to bigger opportunities on both WWE RAW and WWE SmackDown, while advising AEW to also position Mexican wrestlers as legitimate stars who can draw viewers. Meltzer added that Penta's strong performance likely helped change plans regarding the Money in the Bank briefcase, ultimately putting him in position for a major championship opportunity.
