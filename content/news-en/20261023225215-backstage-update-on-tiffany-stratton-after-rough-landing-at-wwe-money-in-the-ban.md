Tiffany Stratton captured her second WWE Women's Money in the Bank briefcase, but the victory came with a significant physical toll following a dangerous fall on October 10. Stratton, a prominent star on WWE SmackDown, missed last week's episode after landing hard on her head and neck during a high-risk Senton Bomb in New Orleans. The ladder shifted violently during the spot, causing her to fall short of Charlotte Flair, Lash Legend, and Lola Vice. During a post-show interview, Stratton admitted her neck was hurting but expressed her determination to secure the win. According to the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, conducting this interview raised questions about the severity of her injuries and whether she received an immediate concussion examination at a hospital. As of this report, her status for tonight's show at the Toyota Arena in Ontario, California, remains unconfirmed.
