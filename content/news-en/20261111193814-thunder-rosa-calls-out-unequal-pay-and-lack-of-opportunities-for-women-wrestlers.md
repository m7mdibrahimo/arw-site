Thunder Rosa believes women in professional wrestling are still dealing with many of the same obstacles they have faced for years. Speaking on the I Love Wrestling Podcast, Rosa was asked about the biggest challenges women encounter in the business, quickly noting that the loaded topic could get her into trouble.

Rosa addressed the issue of unequal pay directly, explaining that the problem extends across sports as female athletes continue fighting for equal compensation in 2026. She also pointed out that opportunities are often dictated by industry hierarchies and stereotypes, recalling how promoters initially tried to categorize her strictly as a Mexican luchadora rather than a professional wrestler.

While acknowledging that institutional changes by previous generations have opened doors for diverse talent, Rosa stressed that the work is far from finished. She hopes future generations of women wrestlers can define themselves on their own terms without being limited by industry labels.
