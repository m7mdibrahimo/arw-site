Conrad Thompson recently shared a story about an attempt by The Ultimate Warrior to scam him before the wrestling legend's passing in 2014. Warrior reached out to Thompson to sell several title belts, claiming they were used on television. Thompson, who was interested in the items, reached out to belt maker Dave Millican to verify the authenticity of the claims.

> "So I was introduced to him through his website. He had a bunch of photos up there and there was a photo that was no longer linked, but he claimed it was the SummerSlam 1990 belt that he wore against Ravishing Rick Rude. So I took those pictures and I sent them to belt maker Dave Millican. I said, 'Hey, is this right?' and he goes, 'Absolutely not. That's not the ring used belt.'"

Thompson confronted Warrior about the discrepancy via email, but Warrior insisted that the belt was legitimate and dismissed Millican's expertise. Thompson then attempted to negotiate a deal for the yellow Intercontinental Championship and the World title from WrestleMania 6. He arranged for Millican to accompany him to authenticate the purchase, but Millican revealed that Warrior had already sold the Intercontinental belt years prior to a private collector. When Thompson presented this information to Warrior, the deal fell through. Thompson expressed that while he remained a fan of the Ultimate Warrior character, the experience was disappointing.
