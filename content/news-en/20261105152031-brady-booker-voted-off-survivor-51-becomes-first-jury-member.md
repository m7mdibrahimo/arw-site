Brady Booker, also known as Bodhi Hayward, was eliminated from Survivor 51 on Wednesday's episode. He was the eighth contestant voted off the show, losing in a 9-4 vote against Eric Macksoud. Following his elimination, Booker became the first member of the jury. Angelica “Jelly” Loblack spearheaded the move against Booker, claiming he was "bringing a level of toxicity." During his time on the island, Booker had a conflict with Mike Pinsky, whom he accused of orchestrating the vote flip. Booker previously competed in WWE from 2022 until his release in November of that year, where he was part of the original Chase U. Since then, he has performed on the independent scene, including appearances for AEW on AEW Dark and regular bookings at ROH TV tapings and independent events like 1FW in Atlanta, GA. He has frequently teamed with Billy Gunn during his recent independent appearances.
