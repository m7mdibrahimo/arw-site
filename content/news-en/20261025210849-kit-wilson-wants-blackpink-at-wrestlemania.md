Kit Wilson revealed his big wish to have the K-pop girl group BLACKPINK perform his live entrance music at WrestleMania. Speaking on the Battleground podcast, the WWE star discussed his musical preferences.

> "I'm a lover of music. I got so much to explore in that world. But my music… I need female vocalists. So I'm sorry to all the male artists, but you're out for this. I'm on a bit of a K-pop binge at the moment. I really am. I'm really enjoying it. So I'm going to get the four girls from BLACKPINK and I would love them to hit that WrestleMania performance. To be honest and, those guys are triple threats. They can sing, they can dance, they can perform, they can act, they can do it all. That's more than a triple, but they can do it. So I'll leave the singing and dancing to them and I will just strut with a serious face down that ramp cause that's a long ramp. We know it is. So I'm going to let the magic happen behind me. I'm just going to focus on the camera and I'm going to walk."

Wilson currently appears alongside The Miz on WWE programming, and both men previously took part in a WrestleMania segment this year featuring John Cena and Danhausen.
