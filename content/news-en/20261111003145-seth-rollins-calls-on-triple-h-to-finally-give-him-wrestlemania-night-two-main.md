Seth Rollins has participated in many iconic WrestleMania moments, including his legendary Money in the Bank cash-in at WrestleMania 31. However, one major career milestone remains elusive: closing the show on the second night of WrestleMania. During a recent appearance on The Kittle Things podcast, Rollins discussed his history at the event, specifically noting that he does not consider his WrestleMania 31 victory a true main event because he was not advertised in that spot. While he has main-evented Night One before, he has yet to be the final wrestler on the marquee for Night Two.

Rollins expressed his desire to change that, directly addressing Triple H. "Mr. Triple H, let's work on that. Whoever's in charge. It's a tough sell. It's a tough sell. Maybe I've got a few years left. We'll see if I can get there." Acknowledging that the two-night format complicates the definition of a main event, Rollins noted that he wants to be the person officially advertised to finish the show on Night Two. With his career potentially winding down in the coming years, Rollins remains focused on achieving this final goal.
