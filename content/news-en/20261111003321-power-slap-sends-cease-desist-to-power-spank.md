Power Slap has issued a cease-and-desist letter to Power Spank, a parody competition run by Lingerie Fighting Championships (LFC) and fronted by former MLW executive Mister Saint Laurent. The parody event features female competitors spanking each other on the rear end, directly mirroring Power Slap's face-slapping format. Based in Las Vegas, Nevada, LFC plans to make the situation public to comply with regulations for public companies. LFC CEO Shaun Donnelly stated, "We respect Power Slap and its founders, but we will not be bullied into abandoning protected speech. We will defend our right, and the right of every American, to free speech, and if that takes us to the Supreme Court, we are prepared to go." MMA Junkie reached out to Dana White for comment, but received no response.
