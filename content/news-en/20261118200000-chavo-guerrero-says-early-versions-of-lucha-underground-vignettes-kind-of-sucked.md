Chavo Guerrero was among the first to recognize the potential of Lucha Underground. While the promotion's visual style later became a highlight for fans, Guerrero revealed that the early backstage vignettes were not initially successful. "We still really didn't know what it was. We were still filming the first season when the first episode aired, that happens sometimes in Hollywood. GLOW was the same thing," Guerrero told Fightful's Sean Ross Sapp. "When that first episode aired and I went home and watched it, Skip Chaisson, was our back vignette director and he was brought in by Robert Rodriguez, he was one of Robert’s guys. At first, they started filming these backstage vignettes and they kind of sucked."

Guerrero explained that the arrival of Skip Chaisson significantly changed the production quality. "They weren't really that good. They brought in Skip and man, Skip made it like this film. The cinematography was so crazy and we started colorizing it like a comic book. They were bringing me in on the editing process and learning how to do all that kind of stuff with that," he added. After seeing the first episode, Guerrero motivated the roster to match the high production standard.

> "I pulled all the wrestlers aside and explained to them that usually, its production trying to keep up with us. We have to step up our game and keep up with that production because if you saw that first episode, I've never seen anything like it before. I remember reading internet sites and at first they’re kind of like, hm, I’m not sure about this. This is different, okay, give it a chance. By the third, fourth, fifth episode man, people were hooked."
