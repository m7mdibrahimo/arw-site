Cody Rhodes has addressed his future in acting after filming his role in Street Fighter, emphasizing that wrestling remains his central priority and that he will never walk away from it for Hollywood.

During an appearance on The Rich Eisen Show, Rhodes discussed his recent film work and confirmed his desire to pursue additional roles. He credited stars like Dave Bautista, John Cena, and The Rock for paving the way so wrestling talent can enter Hollywood with respect.

Rhodes highlighted that modern WWE allows performers to pursue outside opportunities without leaving the squared circle. He expressed gratitude toward Triple H and Nick Khan for accommodating his movie schedule, maintaining that WWE remains his essential day job.
