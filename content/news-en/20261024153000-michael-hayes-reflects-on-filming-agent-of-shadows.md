WWE producer Michael Hayes recently looked back on his experience filming the ReelShort movie Agent of Shadows during an appearance on What's Your Story? with Stephanie McMahon. The film's cast features several wrestling stars including Drew McIntyre, Jacob Fatu, Joe Hendry, Ethan Page, Booker T, and Hayes himself. Hayes shared that he was grateful for the freedom to ad-lib and noted that the filming process moved quickly.

Hayes also shared an amusing anecdote about a misunderstanding with the film's assistant director, who he realized spent a lot of time yelling. Hayes humorously recalled telling the assistant director that he would start yelling back if it happened again, before someone clarified that it was simply part of the job. At 67 years old, Hayes continues his work as a producer behind the scenes in WWE.
