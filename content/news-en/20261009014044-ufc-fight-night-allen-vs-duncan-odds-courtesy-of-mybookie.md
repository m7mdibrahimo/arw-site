UFC Fight Night: Allen vs. Duncan is set for Saturday, October 10, at the UFC APEX in Las Vegas, Nevada. The event will be broadcast on Paramount+. MyBookie has released the betting odds for the full fight card. Brendan Allen is currently a -126 favorite against Christian Leroy Duncan, who is listed at -101. Other notable matchups include Matheus Camilo versus Jai Herbert, Lupita Godinez against Ketlen Souza, and Andre Fili facing Kai Kamaka III. The event also features bouts between Julius Walker and Gerald Meerschaert, Malcolm Wellmaker and Otari Tanzilovi, Francisco Prado and Ismael Bonfim, Niko Price and Leon Shahbazyan, Felipe Franco and Brendson Ribeiro, RJ Harris and Allen Frye Jr., Darya Zheleznyakova and Alice Pereira, and Ernesta Kareckaite versus Melissa Gatto.
