Orlando is no longer considered the definitive favorite to host the 2028 WWE Royal Rumble. While Camping World Stadium was previously viewed as the top candidate, new reports suggest the city is no longer the clear frontrunner. Bryan Alvarez of Wrestling Observer reported on November 20, 2026, that Orlando remains among the top three potential locations but is not leading the race.

Alvarez also noted that the open-air nature of Camping World Stadium poses a potential weather concern for an event scheduled for late January or early February. This update contradicts earlier claims from WrestleVotes Radio, which indicated that WWE officials strongly believed Orlando was the favorite for the event. The Greater Orlando Sports Commission has been actively pursuing the 2028 Rumble with approved funding, aiming to strengthen its partnership with WWE as the city also eyes a future WrestleMania. Despite these efforts, the host city remains officially unannounced, and multiple locations are still under consideration.
