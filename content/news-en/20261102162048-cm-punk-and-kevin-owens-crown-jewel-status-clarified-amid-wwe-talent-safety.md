CM Punk and Kevin Owens will not wrestle at WWE Crown Jewel 2026, and rumors claiming they refused to travel to Saudi Arabia are false. According to Dave Meltzer on Wrestling Observer Radio, their absence is unrelated to any travel refusal. Instead, Punk and Owens are scheduled for a No Holds Barred match on the November 6 episode of WWE SmackDown, just one night before Crown Jewel. Meltzer noted that this SmackDown match was always the plan and that Punk vs. Owens was never on the original Crown Jewel card. Meltzer also addressed concerns regarding talent reluctance to travel to Saudi Arabia due to ongoing security issues in the region, confirming that some wrestlers indeed have reservations. However, he emphasized that Roman Reigns is making the trip, which sets a definitive precedent for the roster. Executive meetings led by Mark Shapiro and Nick Khan on October 30 reassured employees about safety while explaining why the event is moving forward.
