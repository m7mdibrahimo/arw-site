Looking back on his first program with Savio Vega at WWE SummerSlam 1996, John "Bradshaw" Layfield recalls that while they were rivals on television, they have shared a strong 30-year friendship in real life. JBL praised his former opponent and mentioned Vega's famous nickname from his WWE days. "I had a great relationship with Savio," JBL said on "Something to Wrestle." "Savio was my trial match, just happened by happenstance...You couldn't ask for a better person to wrestle with then Savio. Savio was an incredible worker. I don't know anybody who's ever had anything bad to say about Savio...Hunter [Triple H] used to call him 'bumblebee' because bumblebees aren't supposed to be able to fly. And Savio would get up at the top rope...Savio could actually fly, and he's like the bumblebee. He Could do things that physics said that he shouldn't do." Currently, JBL and Vega work closely together in WWE's sister promotion, Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide, where Vega serves as a producer, color commentator, and translator alongside JBL.
