Je'Von Evans recently reflected on the high-stakes moment during WWE Money in the Bank, where Bron Breakker pushed him from the top of a ladder through the roof of an ambulance. Despite the severity of the spot, Evans managed to appear on the following episode of WWE RAW. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, Evans shared his thoughts on the impact of the stunt.

> "Like you said, it’s going to be remembered for decades. I’m grateful that I can be part of such a soon to be iconic moment. I wouldn't say it’s iconic yet, but we’ll see next year when they play it back and everybody’s like, ‘Oh, that was crazy.’ Until then, I feel like that it's just been wild. Who can say they've been part of a Money In The Bank ladder spot, which is insane, at 22. Not a lot of people can say that."

Evans also described his mindset during the high-pressure match, noting that he went in with an open mind despite his nerves. He emphasized that being part of his first Money in the Bank ladder match was an exciting, albeit nerve-wracking, experience. Looking ahead, Evans and Penta are scheduled to challenge for titles at WWE Crown Jewel.
