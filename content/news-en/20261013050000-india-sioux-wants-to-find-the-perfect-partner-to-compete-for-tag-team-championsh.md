India Sioux has her sights set on competing for tag team championships in CMLL. In a recent interview, she expressed her desire to find the perfect partner to pursue the titles, noting that the current tag team scene in CMLL is heavily dominated by ruda teams. Sioux highlighted her willingness to take on new challenges and mentioned her ongoing rivalry with Sanely, stating she is ready to put their masks on the line whenever Sanely is prepared. She also praised Olympia and Hera as the current Mexican National Women's Tag Team Champions, calling them tough competitors and the team to beat in the division. Sioux previously worked at Mitos del Ring and continues to look for new opportunities within CMLL.
