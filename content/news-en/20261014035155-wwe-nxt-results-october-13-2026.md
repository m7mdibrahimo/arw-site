The October 13, 2026, episode of WWE NXT aired live from the Capitol Wrestling Center in Orlando, Florida. The show featured two Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic tournament quarterfinal matches, a AAA Reina de Reinas Championship bout, and a WWE NXT Championship number one contender match.

The Vanity Project (Ricky Smokes and Brad Baylor) def. Mini Vikingo and Chazz “Starboy” Hall in a Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic quarterfinal match.

Tristan Angels def. Saquon Shugars in a singles match.

La Catalina def. Kali Armstrong to retain the AAA Reina de Reinas Championship.

Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno def. Fraxiom (Nathan Frazer and Axiom) in a Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic quarterfinal match.

During the broadcast, Wren Sinclair addressed her recent suspension and her status as the number one contender for the NXT Women’s Championship. General Manager Robert Stone lifted her suspension and confirmed she will challenge for the title at Halloween Havoc. Kelani Jordan interrupted the segment, expressing her refusal to face Sinclair. Additionally, Dion Lennox ambushed Cruz Montana backstage, promising a confrontation next week.

In the main event, Myles Borne faced Tony D’Angelo in a WWE NXT Championship number one contender’s match.
