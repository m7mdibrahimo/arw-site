WWE has officially announced that the 2027 WWE Royal Rumble will take place on Saturday, February 6, at State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona. As part of the weekend festivities, the Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale will host both WWE SmackDown and AAA Road to Rey de Reyes on Friday, February 5. This marks the third time Arizona has hosted the event, following editions in 2013 and 2019. The Premium Live Event will feature the 30-man and 30-woman Royal Rumble matches, with the winners earning a championship opportunity at WrestleMania 43. Tickets will be available via SeatGeek, with a general on-sale starting Wednesday, November 18, and various presale opportunities beginning November 16. This event is part of a multi-year agreement between TKO Group Holdings and Arizona sports organizations to bring high-profile events to the region. The Rumble will stream on the ESPN App in the U.S. and on Netflix internationally.
