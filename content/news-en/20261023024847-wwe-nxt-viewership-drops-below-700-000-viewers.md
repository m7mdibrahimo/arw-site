WWE NXT viewership and ratings saw a decrease for the October 20 episode on CW Network. According to Wrestlenomics, the show drew 655,000 viewers, falling below the 700,000 mark and dropping from the 732,000 viewers recorded the week before. In the key 18 to 49 demographic, the episode scored a 0.10 rating, down from the 0.14 rating from the previous week. Meanwhile, YouTube highlights for the episode featured Zilla Fatu, Cruz Montana, Dion Lennox, Kelani Jordan, Kendal Grey, Wren Sinclair, Grayson Waller, and Tony D’Angelo.
