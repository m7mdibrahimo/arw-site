Roman Reigns reportedly used his influence backstage to change the main event of SummerSlam, despite facing resistance from some within WWE. According to Conrad Thompson in his Uncrowned column for Yahoo Sports, Reigns is expecting to defend his WWE World Championship against Oba Femi at WrestleMania 43. Thompson reported that Reigns has been advocating for Femi to be presented as a top-tier star on the road to that event.

"The biggest match of WM43 weekend is almost certainly Roman Reigns defending his WWE world title vs. white-hot babyface Oba Femi. Roman himself certainly expects that match to happen, and the rumor mill is he's using his voice backstage to keep Femi as strong as possible until their match."

This push reportedly extended to the booking of SummerSlam. Thompson noted that Femi's Hell in a Cell victory over Brock Lesnar was originally slated to open Night 1, while CM Punk's WWE Undisputed Championship defense against Cody Rhodes was set to close the show. Reigns allegedly intervened to swap the order. "According to what I call ‘rumors and innuendo,’ in the days leading up to the two-day SummerSlam, Reigns strongly advocated for Femi's Hell in a Cell victory over Brock Lesnar to replace CM Punk's WWE Undisputed Championship defense vs. Cody Rhodes as Saturday's main event."

There was reportedly pushback regarding this change. Some argued that the large Hell in a Cell structure was better suited for the beginning of the show to clear the stage, while others felt that sending the Minneapolis crowd home after seeing hometown favorite Lesnar lose was a questionable decision. Ultimately, Reigns' perspective prevailed.
