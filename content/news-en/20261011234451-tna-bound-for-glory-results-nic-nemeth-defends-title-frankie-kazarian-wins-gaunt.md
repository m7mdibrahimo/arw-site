TNA Bound For Glory took place on October 11, 2026, at the Yuengling Center in Tampa, Florida. The event featured several title changes and the TNA Hall of Fame induction ceremony.

In the main event, TNA World Champion Nic Nemeth successfully defended his title against Leon Slater. In a Falls Count Anywhere Match, The Hardys defeated syX. Zoey Serrano and Shayna Baszler captured the TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Championship from Allie and Kiera Hogan. Additionally, Indi Hartwell defeated Xia Brookside to become the new TNA Knockouts World Champion, and Jason Hotch won a 4-Way Match to claim the TNA International Championship. Amazing Red won the 7-Way Scramble Match to become the new TNA X-Division Champion, while Frankie Kazarian emerged victorious in the Call Your Shot Gauntlet Match. In other action, Ricky Sosa defeated Eddie Edwards, and Moose secured a victory over Max Caster.

The TNA Hall of Fame induction ceremony honored Amazing Red, Konnan, and ODB for their contributions to the industry.
