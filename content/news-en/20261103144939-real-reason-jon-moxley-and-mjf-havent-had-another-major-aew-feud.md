Jon Moxley and MJF have spent years as two of AEW's top stars without crossing paths in another major feud, and reports indicate this is entirely intentional due to their contrasting philosophies on wrestling. According to Self Made Pro during a Self Made Session, the two competitors view the sport differently, which complicates the process of building a sustained program together. "They don't see wrestling the same way, and that makes it hard to produce wrestling together," the report notes. While management could book them together if necessary, they are otherwise allowed to pursue independent paths.

The history between the two includes a major 2020 feud where MJF challenged Moxley for the AEW World Championship at AEW All Out, followed by their 2022 rivalry that culminated at AEW Full Gear, where MJF defeated Moxley with assistance from William Regal to claim the title. Although a natural storyline exists for a potential third chapter, AEW has yet to revisit the matchup.
