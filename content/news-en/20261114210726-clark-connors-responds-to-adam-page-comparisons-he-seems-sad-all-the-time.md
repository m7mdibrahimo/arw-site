Clark Connors recently responded to fans comparing him to Hangman Adam Page, as both wrestlers are part of the AEW roster. Speaking on TMZ’s Inside The Ring, Connors addressed the comparisons regarding his aura. "Well, anybody uses the word aura in the first place, I probably shouldn’t be talking to them because they're under 18 years old. So I'm going to leave that," Connors said. He then compared their personalities, stating that Adam Page seems sad and gets angry easily, while Connors considers himself cool as hell. "He drinks because he's sad, and I drink because it's fucking bad," Connors added.
