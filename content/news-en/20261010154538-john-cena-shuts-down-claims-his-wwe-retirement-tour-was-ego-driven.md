John Cena has addressed criticism surrounding his recent WWE retirement tour, firmly rejecting claims that his farewell run was motivated by personal ego. Speaking on a recent podcast appearance, Cena explained that his primary goal was to help the business grow and create new opportunities rather than simply celebrating his own career. He noted that the decision to launch a comprehensive year-long storyline came from his own pitch to WWE management, offering them a choice between a structured farewell tour or a quiet social media announcement. Cena admitted that while not every creative direction resonated with all fans, he is proud of the engagement and the emotional investment generated throughout the year, adding that he has no regrets about how his final run concluded.
