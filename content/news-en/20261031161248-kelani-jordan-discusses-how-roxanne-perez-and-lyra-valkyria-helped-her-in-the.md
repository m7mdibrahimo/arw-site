Kelani Jordan has discussed the wrestlers who helped her the most in the ring, naming Roxanne Perez and Lyra Valkyria. In a new interview with Kron 4, the current NXT Women's Champion credited her former fellow NXT roster members and coaches for her development. When asked who she has learned the most from, Jordan explained, "Yeah, so besides, of course, the coaches here at the Performance Center, we have amazing coaches, and Coach Smiley helped me a lot beginning off. But some of the talent that helped me a lot, of course, Melo helped me a lot in the ring. Not just, like, actual rings, but also just brain, like, getting my brain right for what we do. But Roxy (Roxanne Perez) helped me a lot in the ring. Lyra (Valkyria) would train with me a lot in the ring, especially when I was first starting off, and we all were here in NXT." Elsewhere in the interview, Jordan spoke about looking up to Bianca Belair.
