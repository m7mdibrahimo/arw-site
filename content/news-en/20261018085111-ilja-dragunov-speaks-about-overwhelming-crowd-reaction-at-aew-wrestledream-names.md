Ilja Dragunov discussed his AEW debut and the crowd reaction he received at AEW WrestleDream after signing with All Elite Wrestling. Speaking alongside Renee Paquette and Tony Khan at the post-event media scrum, Dragunov shared his initial thoughts on joining the company.

> I would say very, very overwhelming. It’s a new breath of fresh air for me. It is a totally new environment for me. But the way I’ve got welcomed here, it only feels plainly right.

When asked who he wants to wrestle in AEW, Dragunov expressed eagerness to face various roster members.

> A better question is: Who not? For real, if I think about all the competitors here in AEW, it is hard to say who I do not want to wrestle. Because it starts with a (Jon) Moxley, it goes on with a (Samoa) Joe, it goes on with a (Kyle) Fletcher. So many amazing competitors in that ring and since I’m here and I felt this — when I went through this — to this presentation in front of all of you, I feel like I’m starving. I’m starving for competition and I know that whoever I will step up against has a serious problem because I am here. Now I am here, and this is gonna be a serious problem for so many people.

Dragunov previously competed in WWE starting in 2019 before opting not to return and entering free agency.
