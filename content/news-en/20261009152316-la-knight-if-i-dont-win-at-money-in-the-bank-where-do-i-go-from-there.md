LA Knight admitted that he is unsure where his career will head if he fails to capture the world title at the upcoming WWE Money In The Bank event. This weekend's premium live event will feature a World Heavyweight Championship match between Roman Reigns and LA Knight, marking Knight's fifth opportunity at a world championship since 2023.

In an interview with TMZ Inside The Ring, Knight described the upcoming bout as a crucial turning point. > "I mean, part of the issue with that is I've probably kind of framed it that way a little bit because it just feels — look, can I necessarily say it is my last one ever? I don’t know. I mean, it could be. Who knows, I might retire next week, you never know. It's just a matter of it's been so long that this has just been hanging by a string where it's like, so many people ask how has LA Knight not been World Champion yet? That is a big time question where it's almost like put up or shut up, do or die. This is the time to do it. If it doesn't happen here, it's just kind of like, where do I go from there? I think that's really kind of the moral of that story that anything."
