Nick Wayne holds two victories over Chris Jericho, but he was unable to escape the green mist of Great Muta on the Jericho Cruise.

Muta made an unexpected appearance during a tag team match featuring Jericho and the Hardys, Matt Hardy and Jeff Hardy, taking on Wayne, Jay Lethal, and Blake Christian. Wayne confronted Muta on the ramp, where the Japanese legend surprised him by spitting green mist into his face.

Keiji Muto retired from active competition in 2023 following his final official match against Masahiro Chono.
