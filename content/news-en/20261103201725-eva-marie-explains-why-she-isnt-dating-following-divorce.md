Eva Marie is officially single following the finalization of her divorce, but she is not looking to jump back into the dating scene anytime soon. The former WWE star explained to TMZ’s Inside the Ring that she is currently not dating because she feels out of practice and uncertain about how to navigate the process. After being married for about a decade, she joked that she is unsure if she still has any romantic "game." Eva Marie further explained that her lifestyle makes meeting people organically difficult, as she does not drink alcohol and therefore does not frequent bars. She added that she has never used a dating app and finds the prospect of doing so intimidating due to the mixed feedback she has heard. Additionally, at age 42, she noted that many potential partners are likely to be parents, which introduces a new dynamic she has not navigated before. Her divorce from Jonathan Coyle was legally finalized in August 2025. For now, she is in no rush to figure out the dating world.
