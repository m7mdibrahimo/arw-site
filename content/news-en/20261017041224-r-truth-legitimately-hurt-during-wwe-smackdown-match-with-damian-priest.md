R-Truth suffered a legitimate injury during his singles match against Damian Priest on the October 16, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown. The injury occurred following an awkward landing after Truth executed a missile dropkick while Priest was recovering from an impact with the ring post. The referee immediately halted the match to assess Truth's condition and ultimately called for an early stoppage, declaring Damian Priest the winner.

According to reports, the injury was not part of the planned storyline, and the match did not conclude as originally intended. The stoppage forced a change in the post-match sequence; while Priest had initially planned to bring a steel chair into the ring, he was interrupted by Fraxiom, who came out to assist the injured Truth. There is currently no official update regarding the nature or severity of Truth's injury, though the unexpected end to the match has raised concerns.
