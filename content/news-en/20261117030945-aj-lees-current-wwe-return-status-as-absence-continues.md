AJ Lee’s future with WWE has remained a subject of speculation since her appearance at WrestleMania 42. During a recent Q&A session, Joey Votes of WrestleVotes addressed whether fans should expect to see her back in the ring before the end of 2026. According to Votes, a return this year is not in the plans, with the 2027 Royal Rumble being the best-case scenario for her next appearance.

> "Will AJ Lee wrestle again this year? No. Best-case scenario is her showing up at the Royal Rumble, so that's in 2027. So no, she won't wrestle again this year."

This confirms that Lee’s absence from WWE competition will extend beyond the end of the year. While her name was previously linked to storylines involving Paige and the Bella Twins at SummerSlam, WWE creative sources indicated she was not actually planned for that program before Brie Bella’s injury. Lee herself has remained open to the possibility of wrestling again, noting in September that her return to the ring gave her confidence in her abilities. However, she emphasized that any future match would need to be driven by a compelling story rather than just for the sake of wrestling. For now, no such story is expected to materialize before 2027.
