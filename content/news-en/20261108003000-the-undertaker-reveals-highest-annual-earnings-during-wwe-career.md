WWE Hall of Famer The Undertaker previously maintained a mysterious persona and avoided discussing behind-the-scenes business. However, following his retirement, the release of his "Last Ride" documentary in 2020, and the launch of his "Six Feet Under" podcast in 2023, he has become more open.

In a recent interview with "The School of Hard Knocks" on Instagram, according to Wrestling Inc., The Undertaker discussed his financial success during his WWE tenure. He revealed that he became a millionaire at age 27 after joining the company at 25, following a brief stint in WCW.

When asked about his most profitable year, he stated:
> "Just shy of $15 [million]."

He explained that wrestling is about filling seats and standing out in a cookie-cutter industry. He also shared financial advice from his father:
> "Always live beneath your means. You never know what's in store for you tomorrow, and if it all goes away, what are you going to do?"
