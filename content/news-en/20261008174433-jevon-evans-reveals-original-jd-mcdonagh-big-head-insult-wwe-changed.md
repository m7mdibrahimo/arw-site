Je'Von Evans recently shared that he had a different insult planned for JD McDonagh during their segment on WWE RAW, revealing he wanted to use the word cranium instead of calling his head huge. Speaking on a podcast, Evans explained that he felt the word might be too complex for a broad television audience, though he still plans to sneak it into a future promo eventually. He also expressed gratitude for the chance to showcase his microphone skills alongside his in-ring athleticism.
