Ring of Honor aired the latest episode of ROH TV on October 15 on HonorClub. AR Fox and Top Flight defeated JD Ink, Matt Mako, and Charles Crowley. The Beast Mortos defeated QT Marshall. ROH Women's Pure Champion Deonna Purrazzo defeated Emi Sakura to retain her title. The Premier Athletes defeated Parker Li, Adrian Alanis, and Nic Nolan. ROH Women's World Champion Athena faced Sirena Linton in a Proving Ground match. Matt Sydal defeated Bryan Keith. ROH World Television Championship match between Action Andretti and ELP ended in a time limit draw.
