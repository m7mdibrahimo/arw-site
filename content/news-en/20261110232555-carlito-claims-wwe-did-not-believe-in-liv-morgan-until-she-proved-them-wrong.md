Liv Morgan is currently one of WWE's top female stars, but Carlito believes the company did not always share that view. During his appearance on the Hitting the Turnbuckle Podcast, the former Judgment Day member suggested that Morgan had to fight to earn the company's confidence.

Carlito noted that when Morgan first won the SmackDown Women's Championship in July 2022 by cashing in her Money in the Bank contract on Ronda Rousey, WWE management still seemed hesitant. "I feel like the company didn't believe in her. So I'm very proud of her that she made them believe in her."

He believes Morgan was not initially one of the company's preferred choices for a major push. "So I'm very proud of her for doing that, cuz I'm sure she wasn't one of the chosen ones, but she made them choose her."

Carlito credits Morgan for forcing WWE to take her seriously and establishing herself as a central figure in the women's division, regardless of whether the company originally saw her as a long-term main event player.
