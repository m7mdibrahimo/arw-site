Liv Morgan is currently one of WWE's top female stars, but Carlito believes the company did not always share that view. During his appearance on the Hitting the Turnbuckle Podcast, the former The Judgment Day member suggested that Morgan had to fight to earn the company's confidence.

Carlito noted that when Morgan first won the SmackDown Women's Championship in July 2022 by cashing in her Money in the Bank contract on Ronda Rousey, WWE management still seemed hesitant. "Like she kind of won and then and now they you know I feel like the company didn't believe in her. So you know I'm very proud of her that she you know she made them believe in her, you know what I mean?"

He believes Morgan was not initially one of the company's preferred choices for a major push. "So that's cool when someone that you know there's not you're not one of you're not a company person eventually the the company adopts you, you know what I mean? So I'm I'm very proud of her for doing that cuz she I'm sure she wasn't one of the chosen ones, but she made them choose her."

Carlito credits Morgan for forcing WWE to take her seriously and establishing herself as a central figure in the women's division, regardless of whether the company originally saw her as a long-term main event player.
