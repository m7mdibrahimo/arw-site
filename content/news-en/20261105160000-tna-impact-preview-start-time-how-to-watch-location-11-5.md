TNA iMPACT! returns on November 5, 2026, at the Dothan Civic Center in Dothan, Alabama, starting at 9 p.m. ET. The event will be broadcast on AMC and AMC+.

The lineup for the show includes Harley Hudson’s initiation into The Righteous. In a high-stakes singles match, Eddie Edwards faces Bear Bronson; if Edwards wins, Bronson must join The System, but if Bronson wins, he gains control of the faction. The TNA World Tag Team Championship will be on the line as the Nemeth Brothers (Nic Nemeth and Ryan Nemeth) defend against Leon Slater and Amazing Red. Additionally, Fire ‘N Flava (Kiera Hogan and Tasha Steelz) take on Shotzi Blackheart and Allie, while Frankie Kazarian and Avery Makes make their tag team debut.
