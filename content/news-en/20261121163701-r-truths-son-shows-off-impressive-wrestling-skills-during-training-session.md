R-Truth's family may feature a new wrestler in the future, as his son Christopher was spotted performing impressive moves inside the ring with complete confidence and comfort. R-Truth had previously spoken about the massive sacrifices he made throughout his career in WWE, along with the championships and moments he missed out on as a father.

Footage showed R-Truth training with his son Christopher inside the ring, where the young man did not simply learn basic fundamentals. Instead, he displayed high physical fitness and smooth maneuvers, which included leaping over his father's head and executing a crisp arm drag takeover as though he possessed years of experience.

These clips gain special significance when recalling R-Truth's previous statements regarding the overwhelming fan support he received, and how his son Christopher helped him accept years of sacrifice and absences from family occasions when he told him:

> "Dad, it was worth it."

If Christopher decides to pursue professional wrestling seriously, he will not have a better coach than his veteran father, who knows the intricacies of the rings very well.
