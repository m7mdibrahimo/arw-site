TNA iMPACT's Thanksgiving Special will not air on AMC. The promotion announced that the episode scheduled for that week will be preempted by Thanksgiving programming. Instead, the special will be available to stream on AMC+ and will broadcast on TNA+ worldwide and Sportsnet 360 in Canada. TNA Wrestling released a statement on social media: “PROGRAMMING NOTE: Due to the Thanksgiving holiday, next week's Thanksgiving Special episode of #TNAiMPACT will not broadcast on @AMC_TV, but will be available on @AMCPlus. The episode will broadcast on TNA+ worldwide and @Sportsnet 360 in Canada as normal.” Results for the show will be available in our results section.
