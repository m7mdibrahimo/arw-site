Netflix has censored a notable moment during the replay of Roman Reigns defending the World Heavyweight Championship against LA Knight at WWE Money in the Bank. In the original live broadcast, LA Knight flipped off Reigns, but the replay features a different camera angle that entirely hides the gesture. This follows a growing trend of content adjustments by the streaming platform on WWE replays. Previous edits on WWE RAW include cutting to black during Jacob Fatu's car incident, muting a door slam by Royce Keys, blurring blood, and censoring profanity. While the match outcome remains unchanged, replay viewers are receiving a modified version compared to the live audience.
