During the latest SiriusXM stockholders' call, CEO Jennifer Witz highlighted the significant growth of WWE Radio. She reported that listenership has increased by nearly 20 percent since the service launched its rebrand in July. Witz noted that sports programming continues to be a major driver of listener engagement, uniting fans around live events and team coverage. SiriusXM also reported a 4 percent year-over-year increase in monthly active sports listeners across its connected car and streaming platforms. Among 360L subscribers, the average time spent listening to sports grew by 7 percent, indicating that listeners are engaging more deeply with the content. WWE Radio features a variety of programming, including Busted Open hosted by Dave LaGreca, Bully Ray, Nic Nemeth, and Mark Henry, along with various subsidiary shows. Additionally, NXT commentators Vic Joseph and Booker T provide play-by-play coverage for WWE Premium Live Events on the platform.
