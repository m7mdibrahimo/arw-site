John Cena is set for a busy week in early November, with appearances scheduled for both "WWE Raw" and "NXT." During his time on "Raw," Cena is expected to provide full details regarding his upcoming John Cena Classic competition, which is slated for December. While the specifics remain under wraps, Dave Meltzer shared his thoughts on the timing during Tuesday's "Wrestling Observer Radio."

"It's going to be very interesting," Meltzer said. "We'll see if it's December the 19th. I'm kind of betting December 19, because that's the day of the AEW PPV."

AEW is scheduled to host its final pay-per-view event of the year, Worlds End, on December 19 at the Target Center in Minneapolis, Minnesota. If WWE schedules the John Cena Classic for that same date, it would likely compete directly against the finals of AEW's Continental Classic tournament. WWE has frequently scheduled programming against AEW events in recent years, often utilizing "NXT" or AAA to counter AEW shows. The two promotions are already set to go head-to-head next week, as "NXT" will air on Wednesday to avoid conflict with mid-term election coverage.
