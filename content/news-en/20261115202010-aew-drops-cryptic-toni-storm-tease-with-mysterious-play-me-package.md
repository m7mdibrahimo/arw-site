AEW shared a cryptic video on Instagram featuring a package with the words “Play Me” written on it alongside a tube of lipstick, pointing toward the ongoing mystery surrounding Toni Storm’s absence. The “Play Me” message matches the cassettes sent to select media members around WrestleDream in October, which were labeled “Toni Storm Tapes” and featured Renee Paquette narrating recordings from a device Storm carried. Fightful Select previously reported that the tapes were planted to build toward Storm’s return. Tony Khan has confirmed that she will eventually return to AEW.
