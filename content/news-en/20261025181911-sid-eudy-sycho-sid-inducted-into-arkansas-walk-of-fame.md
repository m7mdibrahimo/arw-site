Sid Eudy, the wrestling legend known to fans as Sycho Sid, has been honored with an induction into the Arkansas Walk of Fame. The ceremony took place on October 24, 2026, as reported by The Sentinel-Record. Eudy, who passed away in August 2024, previously received a posthumous induction into the WWE Hall of Fame's Legacy Wing in April 2026. A two-time WWF Champion, Eudy's legacy was also celebrated in late August at the Sycho Sid Memorial Benefit Show in Memphis, Tennessee, an event that featured WWE performer Jordynne Grace.
