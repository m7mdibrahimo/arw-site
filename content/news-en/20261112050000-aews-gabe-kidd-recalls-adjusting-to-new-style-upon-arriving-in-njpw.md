Looking back at his early days in New Japan Pro-Wrestling, AEW star Gabe Kidd recently discussed the difficulties he faced when adapting to the promotion's distinct style. Speaking on "Talk Is Jericho," Kidd noted that despite having a decade of experience when he arrived in 2019, he had to start from scratch as a young lion at the Dojo. He admitted that checking his ego was a major challenge, recalling how Katsuyori Shibata corrected his rope-running technique to match the New Japan way. Kidd acknowledged that while confidence is important in professional wrestling, his early struggles humbled him and taught him valuable lessons about improvement.

In addition to the stylistic changes, Kidd faced a tough language barrier and a demanding daily lifestyle. With no fluent English speakers in the dojo, he struggled to learn Japanese while handling chores like cleaning and cooking for the veterans alongside his heavy training schedule. Although he eventually felt ready for a prominent role, Kidd felt he never gained the company's full backing, which ultimately led to his departure and move to AEW.
