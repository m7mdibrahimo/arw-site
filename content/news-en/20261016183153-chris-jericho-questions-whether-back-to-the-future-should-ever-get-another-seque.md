Chris Jericho recently discussed whether the Back to the Future franchise should ever return with another sequel. During an episode of Talk Is Jericho, Jericho and Brad Gilmore explored the possibility of a new movie, noting that Robert Zemeckis and Bob Gale have long controlled the franchise and that Michael J. Fox would be irreplaceable as Marty McFly. They both agreed that legacy sequels often fail to live up to the original classics and that the trilogy should remain untouched as a complete story.
