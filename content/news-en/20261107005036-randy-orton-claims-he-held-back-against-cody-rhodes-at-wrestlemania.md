Randy Orton is making a bold claim regarding his WrestleMania match against Cody Rhodes, suggesting he deliberately held back. In an interview ahead of their Last Man Standing match at WWE Crown Jewel, Orton stated that he did not unleash his full aggression during their encounter at WrestleMania. Despite landing a punt kick to Rhodes' head, Orton now believes he may have been restrained by their personal history. "Jackie, maybe at WrestleMania, I didn't wanna go to that dark place. Maybe I didn't wanna even do the punt to Cody. Maybe I held a little something back, so at Crown Jewel, I'm gonna let him have 100% of my foot right in his face. Jackie, I don't know that Cody will ever come back after this," Orton told Jackie Redmond. Orton, who once served as a mentor to Rhodes during their time in Legacy, emphasized that he views Rhodes as a younger brother. However, he remains determined to secure his 15th world championship and believes that defeating Rhodes at Crown Jewel is a necessary step toward that goal.
