Cody Rhodes knows what it is like to get lost in online wrestling forums, but he eventually stepped away because the constant negativity started ruining how he enjoyed watching the product. Speaking on the Double Coverage podcast, Rhodes explained that while he initially loved finding fans who shared his passion, the overwhelming negativity pushed him away. He noted that he does not want his viewing experience altered by online discourse, though he is careful never to dismiss anyone's opinion. Rhodes also discussed how fandoms across other entertainment mediums, such as movies, suffer from the same toxic arguments, suggesting that people could benefit from sharing cute dog videos instead of endless online debates.
