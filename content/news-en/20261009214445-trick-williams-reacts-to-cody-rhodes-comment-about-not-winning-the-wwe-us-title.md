Trick Williams does not believe Cody Rhodes will win the United States Championship anytime soon. During a recent appearance on the Flagrant podcast, Cody Rhodes discussed the championships he has yet to capture in WWE, noting that the United States Championship is likely something he will never win in his career. Williams, who recently regained the title from Baron Corbin at Sunday Night’s Main Event and is preparing for the Men’s Money in the Bank ladder match, responded to the remarks on social media by stating that it will not happen anytime soon.
