Andrade El Idolo recently shared a message of support for Penta following his massive victory. The conclusion of the 2026 WWE Money in the Bank event saw Penta holding the coveted briefcase aloft after winning the ladder match, securing a guaranteed opportunity at a world championship whenever he chooses. Andrade El Idolo, the reigning AEW National Champion, posted on X to send his warm regards to his peer. "Congratulations to a good friend and teammate @PENTAELZEROM Viva Mexico, you bastards!!" Following his triumph, Penta revealed during the post-show press conference that a major surprise is on the horizon.
