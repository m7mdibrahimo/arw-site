Hulk Hogan's beachfront Florida mansion has officially been sold for $5.9 million, which is nearly $5 million below its initial asking price of nearly $11 million earlier this year. According to TMZ, the Clearwater property where the late WWE legend spent his final years was offloaded by his estate, along with a neighboring guest cottage that sold for $2.8 million. Hogan originally bought the home for $3.3 million in 2012. Meanwhile, the estate is also dealing with a lawsuit from Carma HoldCo Inc. over Hogan's Real American Beer venture.
