Clark Connors recently compared his current run in All Elite Wrestling to his early days in New Japan Pro-Wrestling. Connors joined AEW earlier this year after competing for NJPW from 2018 to 2026. Speaking on TMZ's Inside The Ring, he reflected on the stark contrast between the two experiences. "My first year in Japan, I was doing a thousand squats a day and doing like Nakanishi's laundry. I think whatever I'm doing now is better. Not getting paid fucking in Chonko. Now this year I couldn't be happier. I think a lot of the American audience didn't necessarily, if they knew who I am, it was from small little shots here and there. But, I got to show everybody that I am that dude. I'm proving it every week and weekend out with The Cut, with The Dogs, with [unintelligible], baby," Connors said. At AEW Full Gear, Connors and his stablemates in The Cut will compete in the Anarchy in the Arena match against Death Riders and The Conglomeration.
