Jordynne Grace’s prolonged absence from WWE television recently fueled rumors that she might be leaving the company. Speculation intensified when fans noticed that the label "WWE Superstar" had been removed from her social media bio. However, according to Fightful Select, Grace remains under contract with WWE. The report clarified that the change to her bio actually occurred several months ago and is not a recent development related to her status. Furthermore, while Grace has been using her time away from the ring to prepare for a bodybuilding competition, sources indicate that this pursuit is not the reason for her television hiatus. Reports from October 19 suggested that her absence is simply due to her not currently being part of WWE's creative plans for SmackDown, as the brand has a limited amount of television time for its female roster. WWE had previously explored the idea of pairing Grace with Ivy Nile, with vignettes reportedly filmed for the potential team, though it remains unclear if those plans will ever come to fruition.
