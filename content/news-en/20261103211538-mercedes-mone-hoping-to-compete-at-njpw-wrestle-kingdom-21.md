Mercedes Moné has expressed her desire to compete at the Tokyo Dome as the match card for New Japan Pro-Wrestling's Wrestle Kingdom 21 takes shape following the 2026 Power Struggle event. After an X user reacted to the current lineup and hoped that the AEW Women's World Champion would be part of the show, Moné replied, "Me too". Moné last wrestled at the Tokyo Dome in January 2025 at Wrestle Dynasty, where she became a double champion by defeating Mina Shirakawa to win the RevPro Undisputed British Women's Championship alongside her NJPW STRONG Women's Title.
