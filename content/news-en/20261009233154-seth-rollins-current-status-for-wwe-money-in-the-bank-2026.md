Seth Rollins has addressed his status for WWE Money in the Bank 2026 this weekend. While Rollins will be in New Orleans, he clarified that he will not be making a surprise return to television. According to Dave Meltzer, Rollins is currently dealing with a minor injury, and WWE is waiting for the right moment for his comeback.

Rollins confirmed on The Rich Eisen Show that his trip to New Orleans is solely for a scheduled appearance and to support his wife, Becky Lynch, during her match for the Women's World Championship.

> "I will be in New Orleans. As I’m flying down there tomorrow... I have a pre-advertised appearance there. So, I’ll be there to meet some of our fans and, you know, enjoy the festivities for a little while. And then I will be on hand to watch my wife, who’s competing in a triple threat match for the Women’s World Championship."

Rollins emphasized that he will not be involved in the show in any capacity. He stated that fans should not expect to see him on screen during the broadcast, noting that he will be watching the action from backstage while Lynch competes for the title.
