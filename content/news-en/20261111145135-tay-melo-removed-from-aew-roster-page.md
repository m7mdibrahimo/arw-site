Tay Melo is no longer listed on the official AEW roster page, according to Fightful. Her removal from the website took effect on November 11.

Tony Khan disclosed in July that Tay requested her departure from AEW and that he intended to grant it. Khan stated:

> "Tay had said she wanted to step away from wrestling. She has said, ‘I love AEW and I love the people here but I wanted to step away from AEW and step away from wrestling,’ and that’s what she told me."

Tay stated in September that she has not retired from the sport.
