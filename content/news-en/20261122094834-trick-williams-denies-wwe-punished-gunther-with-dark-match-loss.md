Trick Williams has dismissed speculation that WWE punished Gunther by having him lose a dark match following the November 9 episode of WWE RAW in Greenville, South Carolina. The loss occurred just two days after the WWE Crown Jewel event, where Gunther was originally scheduled to face Finn Balor before plans were dropped when Gunther decided against traveling to Saudi Arabia. The timing led some fans to speculate that the pinfall loss to Williams was a form of backstage punishment.

Speaking on the Double Coverage Podcast, Williams rejected the theory and stated that his victory was simply the result of his own performance. He emphasized that he earned the win over Gunther in the ring and mocked the idea that WWE was penalizing the former champion.

> "People say, 'Oh my goodness, you know, Gunther got punished,' this, blah, blah, that, by losing to Trick Williams. Gunther got his ass whooped. Point blank, period. I mean, he's in the ring with Trick Williams, the United States Champion, Mr. Lemon Pepper Stepper himself. What'd you expect was going to happen?"

Williams added that he holds high respect for Gunther's abilities and physicality, noting that he had long wanted to work with him. He explained that no wrestler remains dominant forever and that winning the match was a major personal achievement.
