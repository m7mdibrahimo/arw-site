TNA Bound for Glory took place on October 11, 2026, at the Yuengling Center in Tampa, Florida. The event featured the TNA Hall of Fame induction ceremony and a series of championship bouts. Amazing Red, Konnan, and ODB were inducted into the TNA Hall of Fame. Following the ceremony, the in-ring action commenced with a variety of matches.

Ricky Sosa def. Eddie Edwards in a singles match.
Amazing Red def. Fabian Aichner, KC Navarro, Cedric Alexander, Rich Swann, Ryan Nemeth, and BDE to win the TNA X-Division Championship in a 7-Way Scramble match.
Zoey Serrano and Shayna Baszler def. Allie and Kiera Hogan to win the TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Championship.
Frankie Kazarian won the Call Your Shot Gauntlet match.
Jason Hotch def. Mustafa Ali, John Skyler, and Special Agent Zero to win the TNA International Championship in a 4-Way match.
Indi Hartwell def. Xia Brookside to win the TNA Knockouts World Championship, with Mickie James as special guest referee.
Moose def. Max Caster in a singles match.
Syx (Joseph Sawyer and Samuel Shaw) def. The Hardys in a Falls Count Anywhere match following interference from Erick Redbeard.
