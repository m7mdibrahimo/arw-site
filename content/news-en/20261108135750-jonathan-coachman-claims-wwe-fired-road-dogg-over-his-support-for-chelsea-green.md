Jonathan Coachman claimed that Brian James, known as Road Dogg, was fired from WWE because he supported Chelsea Green and believed the company should have done more with her. During an episode of The Coach and Bro Show with Vince Russo, Coachman discussed Green's recent success and noted that someone backstage lost his job after fighting for her opportunities. He recalled how James publicly congratulated Green after she won the Interim WWE Women’s Championship at SummerSlam in August, while also questioning why it took the company so long to recognize her potential and criticizing what he called top guys vision for underutilizing talent.
