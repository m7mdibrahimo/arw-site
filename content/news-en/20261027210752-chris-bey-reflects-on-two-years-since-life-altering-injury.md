Chris Bey recently took to social media to reflect on the two-year anniversary of the severe neck injury and paralysis he suffered at a post-TNA Bound for Glory television taping. In his emotional post, Bey expressed gratitude for his recovery, noting his progress in walking and training in the gym during the first two years. He also thanked his supporters, medical professionals, and his partner BB, while looking ahead to his continued journey. Despite the injury, Bey has made several on-screen appearances for TNA, including a notable appearance at the 2025 Bound for Glory pay-per-view to introduce Ricky Sosa.
