During a recent episode of Busted Open, Bully Ray discussed Je'Von Evans' performance at WWE Money in the Bank, where he competed in the men's ladder match alongside Penta, CM Punk, Trick Williams, Bron Breakker, and Kevin Owens. While Evans had a major spot crashing through an ambulance, Ray argued that a different moment stood out more.

"There was a moment in time in that match that meant more to me, for Je'Von Evans and his future, than that spot. When he pulls up to the ring, and Bron Breakker looks inside the driver's side, and Je'Von opens that door with such force, Bron Breakker goes flying, did you see Je'Von Evans when he jumped out of that ambulance and started beating the sh*t out of Bron Breakker?" Ray said. The Hall of Famer praised Evans for his intensity and convincing punches, arguing that such moments build more connection with the crowd than high-risk bumps. Evans' night ultimately ended when Breakker threw him from the top of the ladder onto the ambulance, and Breakker later continued the attack on WWE Raw.
