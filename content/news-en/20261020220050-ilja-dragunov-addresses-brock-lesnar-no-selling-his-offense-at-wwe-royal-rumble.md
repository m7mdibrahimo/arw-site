Ilja Dragunov recently addressed his brief showdown with Brock Lesnar at the Royal Rumble, where Lesnar quickly eliminated him and seemingly brushed off his offense. Speaking on The Ariel Helwani Show, Dragunov explained that facing a monster like Lesnar aligns with his character's gritty persona. Despite backstage reports that several wrestlers rolled their eyes at the moment, Dragunov holds no frustration and views his vulnerability as a key component of his career success.
