Japanese combat sports veteran Shinya Aoki has officially signed a contract with DDT Pro-Wrestling. Aoki has competed for the promotion as a freelancer since 2018, but his official signing was announced following his match on October 18, 2026. After the bout, he returned to the ring wearing a DDT shirt to confirm the agreement.

Aoki revealed in an interview with Tokyo Sports that former DDT President Sanshiro Takagi and Yuki Ueno convinced him to join full-time after his knockout loss against Mikuru Asakura at a Super Rizin event in September.

> "It’s because Sanshiro Takagi and Yuki Ueno reached out to me when I was at a loss after my match against Mikuru Asakura in September. Far from just getting too caught up in DDT, I’ve finally joined the promotion. How about that, guys? Jealous, aren’t you!?"

Aoki will continue to compete on independent shows alongside his new commitment. His first match under an official DDT contract is set for November 3 at DDT Ultimate Party, where he will face Zack Sabre Jr.
