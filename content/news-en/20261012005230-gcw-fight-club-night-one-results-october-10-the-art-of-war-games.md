Game Changer Wrestling hosted its GCW Fight Club Night One event on October 10 at The Showboat in Atlantic City, New Jersey. The event was broadcast on TrillerTV.

In a scramble match, Vinny Talotta defeated Austin Luke, Devious Cass, Katrina Creed, and Kavron Kanyon. Terry Yaki secured a victory over Juni Underwood. The team of Cowboy Way, consisting of 1 Called Manders and Thomas Shire, defeated Jeffrey John and SLADE.

Pitbull Gary Wolf picked up a win against Jimmy Lloyd. In a title match, GCW World Tag Team Champions KJ Orso and Sam Stackhouse defeated The Bustah And The Brain, represented by Alec Price and Jordan Oliver, to retain their championships.

Joey Janela defeated Tony Deppen, and Nick Gage emerged victorious against Marcus Mathers. In the main event, The Art of War Games match saw the team of VNDL48, featuring Atticus Cogar, Otis Cogar, Christian Napier, Bobby Beverly, and Eddy Only, defeat Team Vipress, which included Vipress, Anakin Murphy, Conor Claxton, Mr. Danger, and Bear Bronson.
