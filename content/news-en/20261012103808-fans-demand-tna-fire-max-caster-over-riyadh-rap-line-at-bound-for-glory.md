Max Caster made his TNA debut at Bound for Glory, but fans were left outraged over a line in his entrance rap referencing a recent tragedy in Riyadh. During his pre-match rap, Caster delivered the line, "I drop bombs, you don't even have to fly to Riyadh." The lyric drew immediate backlash on social media from viewers who felt it crossed the line following a real-life attack that left civilians dead and injured in the city. Fans slammed both Caster and TNA for turning a recent tragedy into a cheap punchline, with many demanding a public apology, disciplinary action, and even the termination of his contract. While some viewers noted that wrestling heels often push boundaries, the timing of the joke just hours after the incident led many to argue it went far beyond acceptable character work. TNA and Caster have yet to address the mounting criticism surrounding the controversial segment.
