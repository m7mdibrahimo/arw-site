WWE's decision to quickly end Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley's title reign was reportedly not driven by a major storyline, but rather by a desire to shake up the women's tag team division. According to WrestleVotes, WWE is currently willing to rotate the championships frequently to create movement and give opportunities to different teams when a major narrative is absent. Michin and B-Fab's victory on the October 23 episode of WWE SmackDown was part of an effort to generate fresh interest in the division. The title change itself was far from clean, as Jade Cargill interfered behind the referee's back to stop Paxley, allowing Michin to hit a Styles Clash and secure the win. The abrupt change has sparked questions among fans regarding the direction of the division and whether rapid title shifts enhance the prestige of the championships.
