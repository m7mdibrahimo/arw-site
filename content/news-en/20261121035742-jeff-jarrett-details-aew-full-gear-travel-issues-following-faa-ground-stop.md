The recent nationwide FAA ground stop for departing flights created chaos across the professional wrestling industry, affecting promotions ranging from TNA to WWE Raw. Jeff Jarrett was among those impacted, missing his scheduled appearance on the Buy-In for AEW Full Gear.

Speaking on his My World podcast, Jarrett discussed his travel difficulties and recalled a conversation with his wife, Karen, before leaving home.

> "While I was leaving the house on Friday, the queen had some magical words. She looks at me and she said, 'Okay, hun. I'm see you in a little while.' I'm like, 'Okay, that's not a vote of confidence.' And off I went to the airport. Sure enough, I got to come back in the house about... three hours later."

Although rebooked for Saturday morning, warnings from a friend about heavy airport congestion prompted Jarrett to wake up early and travel from Nashville to Minneapolis. However, his departure faced a prolonged delay because the flight lacked a first officer. Realizing he would arrive after the Buy-In concluded, Jarrett ultimately decided to secure the final seat on a flight back home.
