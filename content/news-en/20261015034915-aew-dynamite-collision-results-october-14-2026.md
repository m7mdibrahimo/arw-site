The October 14, 2026, episode of AEW Dynamite/Collision took place at the Wolstein Center in Cleveland, Ohio. The show featured several AEW WrestleDream Clockwork Carnage qualifying matches. In the opening contest, Don Callis Family (Wardlow, Jake Doyle and Mark Davis) def. The Death Riders (Jon Moxley, Gabe Kidd and Daniel Garcia) in a trios match. Adam Copeland def. Josh Alexander in a Clockwork Carnage qualifying match. Following his win, Copeland announced that 2027 will be his final year in professional wrestling before he was attacked by Kevin Knight, Lance Archer and Kyle Fletcher. Konosuke Takeshita def. Brian Cage in a Clockwork Carnage qualifying match. Kazuchika Okada def. Myron Reed to retain the AEW International Championship in a Clockwork Carnage qualifying match. In a trios match, Triangle of Madness (Thekla, Julia Hart and Skye Blue) def. Mercedes Moné and Divine Dominion (Megan Bayne and Lena Kross).
