Channing "Stacks" Lorenzo has officially returned to the ring, just weeks after a terrifying incident at an NXT live event left him stretchered out and hospitalized. Stacks competed on November 8, jumping right into the action. He revealed on his Instagram Story that he wrestled at the NXT live event in Gainesville, Florida, marking his first match since his injury at a house show on October 16.

For his return, Stacks teamed with his BirthRight partner Uriah Connors and the NXT Tag Team Champions, The Vanity Project's Brad Baylor and Ricky Smokes. They faced off against the team of Shiloh Hill, "The Main One" Zilla Fatu, and The Dropouts, Scott Green and "The Tuckman" Tyran Tuckey. Zilla ultimately emerged as the lone survivor in the traditional Survivor Series 4-on-4 elimination match.

Just weeks prior, there were major concerns for Stacks following a severe fall during an eight-man tag team match in Sudbury, Ontario. After taking a back body drop over the top rope, he crashed onto the floor, leading WWE to halt the match for medical assistance. He was stretchered out and taken to a hospital, where he later posted a photo wearing a neck brace while joking about his condition:

> "Doc was concerned about my ‘attitude’.. Told ’em it’s a preexisting condition. The show goes on."

At the time, Stacks did not disclose the specific nature of his injury or a return timeline. Now, he has already resumed his in-ring career with a high-stakes elimination match.
