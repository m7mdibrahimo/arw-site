Paul Heyman once tried to bring Rob Van Dam into ECW, but his initial proposal fell far short of what it would take to secure him. The WWE Hall of Famer shared that Heyman wanted him to travel to Philadelphia, drop a match to Mikey Whipwreck, and accept a mere couple hundred dollars for the appearance. At the time, RVD was earning substantial money working in Japan.

Working for All Japan Pro Wrestling meant Giant Baba paid him considerably more than independent bookings in the United States, giving him no reason to accept Heyman's proposal. However, Sabu was determined to bring him into the promotion and repeatedly urged him to reconsider, assuring him that Heyman would call again and instructing him to demand better pay.

Sabu told him,

> "You getting over, me and you are the most over guys here. You should be getting paid more than everyone else."

RVD eventually joined ECW and evolved into one of the greatest stars in the promotion's history, continuously protecting his financial value throughout his professional wrestling career.
