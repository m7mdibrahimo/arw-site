John Cena recently reflected on the struggles of his early acting career and how he was once told that his time in Hollywood was essentially over. Speaking on Josh Horowitz's Happy Sad Confused podcast, Cena discussed his 2006 film The Marine and explained that his primary focus at the time was helping WWE grow rather than building an acting career. He admitted that his heart was not fully invested in those early projects because he did not approach them with the same preparation and intensity that he brought to professional wrestling. Eventually, his early Hollywood run failed, and his representative informed him that they would no longer be making movies. Cena noted that his biggest disappointment was feeling like he had let people down financially after they invested in him. Fortunately, his career rebounded when he was encouraged to try hosting and later took on a parody role in the Fred films, which finally sparked his passion for acting.
