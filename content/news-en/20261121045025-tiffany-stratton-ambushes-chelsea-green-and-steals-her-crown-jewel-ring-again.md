Chelsea Green faced another tough night backstage during the November 20, 2026 episode of WWE SmackDown. Following a video recap detailing their ongoing tensions, Cathy Kelley interviewed Green in the backstage area. Green explained that while she could not fix her ruined friendship, she had at least managed to get her Crown Jewel ring back.

The conversation was interrupted when Blake Monroe arrived and openly mocked Green, sarcastically calling her rise an inspiring Cinderella story from rags to riches. Green responded immediately by slapping Monroe across the face. Before she could react further, WWE Women's Champion Tiffany Stratton ambushed Green from behind and snatched the Crown Jewel ring once more.

The chaotic scene escalated into a brawl between Green and Monroe. The ongoing feud between Stratton and Green has now grown even more intense, with Monroe inserting herself into the conflict and creating another major issue for Green.
