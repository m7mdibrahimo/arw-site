Mason Rook stated that only a select few are aware of the severity of the issue that kept him off WWE NXT television. Prior to the September 29 edition of WWE NXT, it was announced that Rook was not medically cleared to compete. He remained absent from the program until the November 10 edition of the show, where he returned by attacking NXT Champion Grayson Waller alongside Shiloh Hill. Before his absence, Mason Rook had been scheduled to challenge for the NXT title. In a social media post following his return, Rook noted that he worked ahead of schedule to make his comeback. "Only a select few know the severity of what I’ve come back from. I worked my ass off to get back ahead of schedule. Strap in, because I’m gonna cause a Rookus."
