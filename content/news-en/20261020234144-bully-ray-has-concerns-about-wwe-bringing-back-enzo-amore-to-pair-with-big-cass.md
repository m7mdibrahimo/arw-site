Rumors about a potential WWE reunion between former WWE NXT stars Enzo Amore and Big Cass began circulating after Amore was spotted at the WWE Performance Center in May. Cass eventually made his return to the company as a heel in August following his departure from AEW. During a recent episode of Busted Open Radio, Bully Ray and Dave LaGreca discussed what the future holds for Cass on WWE RAW and whether a reunion with his former tag team partner makes sense. Bully Ray expressed concern that bringing Amore back now would frame Cass as a failed experiment that management needs to rescue. LaGreca noted that Cass currently has only one victory on WWE RAW and needs someone to help him regain his identity. Bully Ray also questioned whether today's WWE audience remembers their classic act well enough to chant along with it, noting that the rest of the locker room and fans would require significant reprogramming to accept them as equal partners again.
