WWE's next Premium Live Event is scheduled to be the annual Crown Jewel show, returning to Riyadh, Saudi Arabia on November 7 following last year's event in Perth, Australia. However, ongoing conflicts in the Middle East have raised questions about whether traveling to Saudi Arabia next month is advisable. Following a missile strike on King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh over the weekend, which resulted in twelve fatalities and over three hundred injuries, no official announcement has been made regarding whether Crown Jewel will proceed as planned or be relocated. Speaking on the latest edition of Wrestling Observer Radio, Dave Meltzer noted that Crown Jewel was not mentioned during the WWE Money in the Bank 2026 broadcast, suggesting that WWE intends to move or cancel the show only as an absolute last resort. Meltzer stated that nobody wants to travel to the region under the current high-alert conditions, and while a relocation is likely, the company will likely wait until the situation forces a decision. Furthermore, Meltzer speculated that the lack of updates may be due to the logistical difficulties of securing an alternative arena on such short notice with less than a month remaining before the event.
