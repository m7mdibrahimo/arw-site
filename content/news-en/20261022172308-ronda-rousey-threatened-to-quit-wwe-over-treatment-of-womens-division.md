Ronda Rousey once threatened to quit WWE after six minutes were cut from her pay-per-view match, according to Shayna Baszler. Speaking on a podcast, Baszler revealed that Rousey was so upset about the time cut that she bought her own plane ticket to WWE headquarters to confront the creative team. Baszler praised Rousey's dedication to advancing the women's division, noting that she cared deeply about the locker room despite fans sometimes doubting her commitment. Rousey reportedly paid for her own travel to ensure the meeting could not be canceled by the company, showing how serious she was about fighting for better treatment for women.
