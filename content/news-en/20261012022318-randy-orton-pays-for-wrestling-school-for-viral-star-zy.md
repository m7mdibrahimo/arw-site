Randy Orton has shown his support for an aspiring wrestler. Fans may recognize Zy from his viral "Be Like" videos on Instagram, where he imitates legendary stars including Randy Orton, Kurt Angle, Booker T, Triple H, Batista, Rob Van Dam, and Roman Reigns. Beyond his viral impressions, Zy is actively training to become a professional wrestler. Zy shared on his Instagram stories that Randy Orton paid $2,400 to cover his wrestling school tuition. Before gaining fame for his wrestling content, Zy went viral for imitating basketball players. He is now focused on his professional wrestling career.
