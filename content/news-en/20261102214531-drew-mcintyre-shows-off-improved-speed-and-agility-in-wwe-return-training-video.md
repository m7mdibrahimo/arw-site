Drew McIntyre continues to tease fans with glimpses of what to expect upon his eventual WWE return, sharing another training video that highlights a noticeable boost in his speed and conditioning. In the new footage posted to X, McIntyre showcased a much faster pace, incorporating moves like shoulder tackles and big boots that he has not regularly used in recent years.

Despite weighing around 285 pounds, McIntyre has heavily focused on his agility rather than maintaining a strictly powerhouse style. This shift was evident when he recently hit a flying arm drag from the top rope during practice. Reflecting on his time away, McIntyre told Chris Van Vliet on INSIGHT that his conditioning is currently the best it has been in over a decade, allowing him to move better than ever while also reviving maneuvers he had previously abandoned.

While there is still no official word on when McIntyre will make his in-ring return following a hiatus that began after WrestleMania 42, his latest updates make it clear he is using the time away to evolve his moveset and physical conditioning.
