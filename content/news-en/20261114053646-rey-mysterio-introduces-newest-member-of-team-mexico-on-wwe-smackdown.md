Team Mexico has added its newest member for the upcoming traditional 5-on-5 Survivor Series match. The match was made official on WWE Raw, with Cody Rhodes set to captain Team USA and Rey Mysterio leading Team Mexico.

During a backstage segment on WWE SmackDown, Team USA members Rhodes and CM Punk were discussing Sami Zayn and Kevin Owens when Mysterio walked up to introduce Rey Fénix as the newest addition to Team Mexico. Fénix, who holds the AAA Cruiserweight Championship, expressed that it was an honor to share the ring with them, stating that he is competing for himself, his country, his family, and for the love of lucha libre.

Both teams had previously turned down an offer from Dominik Mysterio to join their ranks on Monday night, which led Punk to offer his services to Rhodes. Rey Mysterio later invited Penta to join Team Mexico, while Penta's tag team partner Je'Von Evans revealed backstage that Rhodes had recruited him for Team USA, warning that he would not go easy on his partner. The event will also feature both Men's and Women's WarGames matches.
