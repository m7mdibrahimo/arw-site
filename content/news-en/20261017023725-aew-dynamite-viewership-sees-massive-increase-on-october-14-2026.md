The October 14, 2026 episode of AEW Dynamite on TBS experienced a significant week-to-week increase in viewership and ratings. According to Wrestlenomics, the show drew 715,000 viewers with a 0.14 rating in the key P18-49 demographic. This compares to the October 6, 2026 episode, which had 495,000 viewers and a 0.11 rating, representing a rise of 220,000 viewers and 0.03 in the demo. Year-over-year figures also showed growth, up 33.9 percent in viewership and 27.3 percent in the demographic compared to the October 15, 2025 episode. These numbers reflect linear TV viewership on TBS only.
