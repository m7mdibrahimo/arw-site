Joe Hendry has established himself as a fan favorite with his catchy entrance theme and high-energy persona, making it difficult for WWE audiences to cheer against him. However, the former TNA World Champion has not ruled out an eventual heel turn in WWE.

Speaking on the "Michael Fairman Channel," Hendry addressed the idea of shifting away from his current character. He explained that his persona was originally created to draw heat from the crowd, making its positive reception a surprise.

> "Interesting question. I mean, never say never. You just never know. Anything can happen in the WWE. It's funny. Originally, this is the character that I've designed, and the person that I'm portraying was designed to be hated and people just happen to like it. So I guess that's always intriguing. It was absolutely designed to be hated by everyone and people just liked it, so yeah. You can't you can't force it. Like you say, [the audience] are a huge part in dictating our paths."

Hendry often incorporates music into his presentation, utilizing a guitar, microphone, and internet meme culture, including his popular theme song video. In the ring, Hendry competed on the October 28 edition of WWE Main Event against Bronson Reed, following a loss to JD McDonagh on the October 19 episode of WWE RAW.
