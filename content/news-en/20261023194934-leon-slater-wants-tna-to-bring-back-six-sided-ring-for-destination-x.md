TNA World Champion Leon Slater hopes the company will bring back the iconic six-sided ring for the upcoming Destination X pay-per-view event. Speaking on the I Love Wrestling Podcast, Slater admitted he has no inside information on whether the ring will return, but he would love the chance to wrestle inside it. While acknowledging that wrestlers have often noted the six-sided ring is harder and hurts much more than a traditional four-sided setup, Slater remains eager to experience it. Destination X is scheduled for November 15 at the Edmonton Expo Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, marking the first time TNA has held the event in nine years. Slater is set to defend the TNA World Championship against Trey Miguel at the show, though TNA has not yet confirmed if the six-sided ring will make a comeback.
