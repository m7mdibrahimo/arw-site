Dawn Marie recently explained how Paige Collett became her wrestling daughter and why she wanted to mentor the younger talent. Speaking on Velvet Ropes, Dawn recalled a past lesson from Jimmy Snuka about passing down knowledge to the next generation so a legacy does not end. She admitted she felt sad for years because she thought she never got the chance to do that during her active career. However, after meeting Collett at an appearance in Philadelphia alongside Marty Jannetty, Dawn immediately knew she was special. > “Get over here right now and you get under my wing because I’m going to take care of you because you have something very, very special. So, she’s my wrestling daughter.” Dawn praised Collett for genuinely loving the business from her heart and noted that she regularly reaches out for advice. She now proudly guides Collett by teaching her the old-school lessons she learned from veterans years ago.
