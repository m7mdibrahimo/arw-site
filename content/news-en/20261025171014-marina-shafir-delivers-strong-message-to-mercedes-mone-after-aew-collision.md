Marina Shafir delivered a direct message to Mercedes Moné following AEW Collision, reassuring the champion that she is not afraid. Shafir previously interrupted Mone on AEW Dynamite while she was celebrating her title defense from AEW WrestleDream.

In a digital exclusive after AEW Collision, Shafir addressed Mone directly about their differences.

> "Mercedes, we all know you're not a pussy. I just needed to get your full attention. I actually have a lot of respect for you. You are in a league of your own, and as much as it might pain you to think or to even say, you know that I am too. But that's where the difference lies between you and me. I know. You need to swing your dominance in between your legs, and I don't. I can't even compliment myself. I don't need bells and whistles. I don't need affirmations. I don't need a dance. I don't need production. I just need the opportunity to work. All of the work that I have been doing these last three years has gotten me here. Here. But there's something else that I need. I need to stand across the ring from you and I want to show everyone exactly who I am. But most importantly, I need to show you."

In a separate digital exclusive, Jay White referred to The Last Outfit as The Cut.
