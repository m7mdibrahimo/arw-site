Royce Keys of The 946 recently discussed the intense reactions he and his stablemates have received from WWE fans since initiating a feud with The Bloodline on WWE Raw. Keys revealed that he has received numerous threats in his direct messages, noting that fans are extremely protective of Roman Reigns. He specifically recalled an incident in Bossier City, Louisiana, where a fan threw a beer at them during their in-ring debut on the October 12 episode of WWE Raw, a six-man tag team match where Carmelo Hayes interfered to join The 946. Keys also mentioned that the most hostile crowd he encountered was in Hawaii due to the Anoa'i family's deep roots there, where even young children hurled insults at them. Despite the overwhelming hostility, Keys remains steadfast in his pursuit of Roman Reigns to capture the WWE World Heavyweight Championship, aiming to dismantle the dominant faction. Both groups are set to collide inside WarGames at Survivor Series later this month.
