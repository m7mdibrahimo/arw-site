Becky Lynch questioned Roxanne Perez's association with Liv Morgan, suggesting that it is not helping her career progress. Perez joined The Judgment Day in June of last year and has been aiding Morgan since then, experiencing only brief moments of tension without breaking out on her own. Lynch believes Perez is holding herself back within the group.

Speaking in a recent interview, Lynch expressed her confusion over what Perez is gaining from the alliance, noting that Perez was once widely regarded as a major prodigy in the industry. Lynch also criticized Perez's current presentation, stating she is tired of her entrance and wants to see her fight aggressively instead of relying on cutesy behavior.

Elsewhere in the interview, Lynch briefly addressed Raquel Rodriguez for maintaining her alliance with Liv Morgan as well. Fightful reported these comments from the latest discussion.
