Ilja Dragunov recently opened up about his career and the influence of his long-time rival and former WWE colleague, GUNTHER, whom he holds in the highest regard. The two wrestlers crossed paths extensively in Germany before taking their intense rivalry to NXT UK. Reflecting on their shared history, the AEW star credited GUNTHER with shaping him into the performer he is today.

> Gunther [is] a dear friend of mine since, I don't know, [since] 2015. Like, so for such a long time, no other person in professional wrestling forged me more than he. [He's] A mastermind. He is one of the greatest, and he will be one of the greatest of all time when he decides to leave the boots in the ring,

Dragunov noted that his greatest career advancements always came from stepping into the ring with GUNTHER, praising the latter's straightforward approach and directness in bringing out the best in his opponents. While GUNTHER previously predicted that Dragunov would make a major impact on the main roster, Dragunov's main run proved brief. Meanwhile, GUNTHER has continued to cement his legacy in WWE by capturing the WWE World Heavyweight Championship and defeating legendary names.
