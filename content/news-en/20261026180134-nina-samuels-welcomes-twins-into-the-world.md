Nina Samuels has announced the birth of her twin boys. After an 11-year career in professional wrestling, Samuels is now stepping into the role of motherhood. Sharing the news with her fans, she posted a photo with the caption, "Welcome to the world my gorgeous boys 💙."

Samuels last competed in the ring this past March for Pro-Wrestling: EVE, where she challenged Willow Nightingale for the TBS Championship. During her career, she also spent several years signed to WWE as part of the NXT U.K. brand.
