WWE Hall of Famer Mick Foley recently opened up about his fears regarding the long-term health effects of his storied wrestling career. During a video on his YouTube channel, Foley discussed political matters before making a sobering admission about his physical condition. He noted that decades of taking brutal bumps and suffering repeated head trauma have left him concerned that he could eventually develop dementia. Foley made it clear he has not received a medical diagnosis, but rather fears what the future holds given the punishment his body and brain have absorbed over the years. He pointed to specific infamous moments from his career, including his legendary June 28, 1998 Hell in a Cell match against The Undertaker, as sources of lasting damage. Despite the grim outlook, Foley quickly returned to discussing politics, comparing his career sacrifices to financial figures associated with the Trump family.
