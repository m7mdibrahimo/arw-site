Steve Austin has faced many legendary opponents, but he considers Bret "The Hitman" Hart to be the absolute standard for realism in professional wrestling. During an appearance on The Wrestling Classic, Austin reflected on their iconic rivalry, noting that Hart's influence on his career was profound. Austin recalled that Hart recognized his potential while he was still in WCW and specifically requested to work with him after returning from knee surgery in 1996. Their classic submission match at WrestleMania 13 remains a career-defining moment for Austin, who admitted the topic still makes him emotional. Beyond their personal bond, Austin praised Hart's technical approach, stating that when it comes to believable action, grit, and intensity, nobody surpassed him. Austin believes Hart played a major role in his rise to superstardom and maintains that the Hitman's ability to bring realism to the ring remains unmatched decades later.
