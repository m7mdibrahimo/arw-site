During a recent episode of Something to Wrestle, WWE Hall of Famer John Bradshaw Layfield and Conrad Thompson looked back at the 25th anniversary of The Invasion, when Vince McMahon acquired WCW. JBL discussed several of McMahon's business quirks and creative decisions during the Monday Night Wars. JBL explained why McMahon held a bias against southern wrestling and chose not to bring in certain creative minds, saying: "Vince had a perception about southern 'wrasslin” that he didn't like."

Despite overlooking figures like Jim Cornette, Dutch Mantell, and Kevin Sullivan, McMahon eventually brought in Bruce Prichard and other southern creatives to help build the team that ultimately surpassed WCW in April 1998, leading to his eventual buyout of the company in March 2001.
