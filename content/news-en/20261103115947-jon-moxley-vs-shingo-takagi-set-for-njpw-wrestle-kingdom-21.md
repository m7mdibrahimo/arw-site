According to Fightful, Jon Moxley will face Shingo Takagi at an upcoming NJPW event. The Death Riders, consisting of Jon Moxley and Wheeler Yuta, made their return to New Japan Pro-Wrestling at the 2026 Power Struggle event. They competed in a match against Unbound Co.'s Jeff Cobb and Shingo Takagi, with Cobb ultimately securing the victory over Yuta.

Following the match, a brawl broke out between Moxley and Takagi, which ended with Takagi forcing Moxley to scramble away. Takagi then delivered a Death Rider DDT to Yuta before challenging Moxley to a match at Wrestle Kingdom 21 at the Tokyo Dome. The updated lineup for the January 4, 2027 card features MJF vs. Ryohei Oiwa and Jon Moxley vs. Shingo Takagi.
