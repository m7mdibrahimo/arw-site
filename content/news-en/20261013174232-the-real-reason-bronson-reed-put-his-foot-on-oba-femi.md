The viral moment where Bronson Reed placed his foot on Oba Femi's back during their rivalry was not a display of genuine animosity. According to a report from Self Made Pro, Reed personally requested the spot to help build heat before eventually putting Femi over.

> "That's the actual reason why, like, when Bronson put his foot on Oba, part of it was like, 'Yo, you're gonna beat me, bro. Let me get some heat. Please, let me get some heat on you, dude.' Which is why he put his foot on him."

This dynamic continued after their match at WWE Money in the Bank. Fans noticed Femi standing over Reed with his foot on his back, which appeared to be a form of retaliation for their previous encounters. However, Self Made Pro reports that Reed actually instructed Femi to execute the spot.

> "And then at the end, you know, when they wrestled at Money in the Bank, he looked over to Oba and said, 'Put your foot on me. You beat me. Put your foot on me,' which is very professional."

Reed aimed to ensure that Femi received the final word, helping to elevate the King of the Ring winner at the end of their feud.
