CMLL and NJPW have announced the schedule for the 2027 Fantastica Mania tour, which will kick off in February and run for three weeks. The tour will visit several cities, including Osaka, Tokyo, Sendai, and Nagoya. The events will take place across multiple venues, featuring three consecutive nights at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo to close out the tour.
