Reby Hardy is once again dealing with fans who assume her social media posts are veiled attacks on her husband, Matt Hardy. She has grown tired of the constant speculation and recently decided to address the situation with a sarcastic approach on Instagram.

After a follower commented asking her to stop hating on Matt, Reby posted a video response urging fans to consider that her content is not always about him. She stated:

> "I said I want you to stop and think that maybe everything posted is not about you or your favorite wrestler. Just consider the possibility."

In her caption, she joked that even a simple post about the sky being blue would lead fans to ask how it relates to her husband. This fan sensitivity stems from a public disagreement in September 2025, when Reby criticized Matt for referring to her as "Rebecca Hardy" during a stream with Kai Cenat. She felt he missed an opportunity to promote her actual brand. Now, Reby seems more interested in roasting these fans than convincing them that Matt is not the subject of her every post.
