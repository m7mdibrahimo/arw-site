Women's United States Champion Jacy Jayne recently discussed emotional wrestling moments during an appearance on "The Wrestling Classic," revealing that Damian Priest cashing in his Money in the Bank contract deeply moved her.

Jayne recalled attending WWE WrestleMania 40 in Philadelphia live, explaining that she wept in the audience because she understands Priest's long journey through the independent circuit.

> "When Damian Priest cashed in at WrestleMania, I was in the audience," Jayne said.

Priest successfully cashed in on Drew McIntyre, who had just defeated Seth Rollins for the World Heavyweight Championship before CM Punk attacked him.
