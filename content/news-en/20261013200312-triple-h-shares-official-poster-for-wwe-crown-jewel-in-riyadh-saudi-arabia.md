Triple H has shared the official poster for WWE Crown Jewel, confirming that the event will take place in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. Taking to X, the WWE chief content officer posted the poster featuring the word Riyadh front and center alongside the caption "Champions vs. Champions, WWE Crown Jewel is LIVE from Riyadh. Saturday, November 7 at 12 p.m. ET." The announcement follows recent speculation regarding the event's location due to security concerns in the region and a Level 3 travel advisory issued by the US Embassy. Despite these concerns and potential backup plans, WWE is moving forward with the trip, having already announced marquee matches for the card during WWE RAW, including Roman Reigns facing Sami Zayn and Chelsea Green defending her title against Liv Morgan.
