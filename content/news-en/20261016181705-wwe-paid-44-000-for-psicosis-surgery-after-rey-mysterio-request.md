WWE covered the cost of hip replacement surgery for lucha libre legend Psicosis after Rey Mysterio pushed the company to help his longtime friend and rival, according to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter. Nick Khan ultimately approved the expense of about $44,000 because Psicosis holds a Legends merchandise contract with the organization.

> “WWE, through a decision by Nick Khan, paid for Dionicio ‘Nicho’ Castellanos (the original Psicosis) hip replacement. Castellanos is under a merchandise ‘legends’ deal with the company. The price tag was in the $44,000 range. Rey Mysterio spearheaded this.”

Meltzer noted that Psicosis, whose legal name is Dionicio Castellanos, played a vital role in the early career of Rey Mysterio through extensive matches together across AAA, ECW, WAR, and WCW. This medical assistance coincided with Psicosis appearing with a cane for mobility support while receiving the Lucha Libre Award at the 2026 Cauliflower Alley Club reunion, where Mysterio attended to celebrate with him.
