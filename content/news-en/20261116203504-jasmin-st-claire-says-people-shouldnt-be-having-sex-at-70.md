Jasmin St. Claire believes that people should stop having sex once they reach the age of 70. The former ECW personality gave a strange reply to a question posted on X by an account named American Citizen about things people should stop doing when they hit 70.

St. Claire answered with a post saying that people should no longer be doing sex at that age.

St. Claire did not clarify whether she was joking or genuinely believed that seniors must retire from sex at that age. St. Claire is known for being bold when talking about such matters and for taking part in bizarre storylines, such as her recent pregnancy angle in Juggalo Championship Wrestling which featured former WWE star Dijak.

Whether St. Claire was looking for a laugh or serious about her opinion, she chose a controversial topic to place an age limit on, which will certainly face opposition from many people who do not tie this aspect to a specific age.
