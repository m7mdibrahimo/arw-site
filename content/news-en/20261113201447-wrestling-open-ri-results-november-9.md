Beyond Wrestling hosted the latest edition of Wrestling Open RI on November 9 at Rhodes On The Pawtuxet in Cranston, Rhode Island. The event was broadcast on IWTV. The results from the card are as follows:

Brett Ryan Gosselin def. Danny Cabral
Aaron Rourke def. Anthony Vecchio
Jodi Aura def. Haddy
The Verdict (Bryce Donovan & Vinny Scalice) def. All Go Nelli & Guzman to retain the WO Tag Team Championship
Devin Reno def. Junior Benito
Star Struck (Anthony Greene & Channing Thomas) def. DJ Powers & Rain Conway
Liviyah def. Amira
Bobby Orlando def. Dustin Waller

Bobby Orlando emerged victorious in the main event of the evening.
