Claudio Castagnoli recently discussed the possibility of pursuing an esports career, noting that he does not believe he could manage it while active as a pro wrestler. Like many wrestlers of the current generation, Claudio is an avid gamer, with League of Legends being his most-played title currently, according to Fightful.

Castagnoli recently attended the 2026 League of Legends World Championships and considered the idea of building a career in esports. While speaking in an interview with Paul Delos Santos for The Load-In, he addressed the potential venture.

> "I guess we have to wait and see, but it's just fun to watch. For me, it's… I don't think I could do it while being active. It takes a whole lot of time and it takes a whole lot of effort, and I just know how hard it is for somebody to get into wrestling. So it's probably the same. I mean, it is the same getting into the league, especially getting into the league at that level. So, you just have to wait and see."

During the same interview, Claudio also compared esports achievements with his accomplishments inside the wrestling ring.
