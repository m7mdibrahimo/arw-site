CJ Perry recently opened up about the emotional and mental health struggles she faced after leaving WWE. Speaking on Nick Club Wrestling Happy Hour, Perry explained that adjusting to life without the adrenaline rush of performing in front of massive crowds was one of the biggest personal challenges she has ever faced. She admitted that she went through a period of feeling manic and struggling with her mental health as she tried to find happiness outside the professional wrestling industry.

Perry noted that the intensity of performing live in WWE is nearly impossible to recreate, comparing the feeling of performing in front of 100,000 fans to an instant adrenaline rush that no drug can satisfy. However, she eventually realized that constantly chasing that feeling was unhealthy and chose to focus on appreciating her past accomplishments rather than wanting more. She also discussed her journey with sobriety, working on her marriage, and learning to embrace the next chapter of her life.
