WWE Hall of Famer Rikishi recently took to social media to express his deep pride in The Bloodline, highlighting their recent achievements and their ongoing efforts to uphold the Anoa'i family legacy. Following the events of this week's WWE Raw, where The Bloodline retaliated against team 946 and Roman Reigns challenged the faction to a WarGames match, Rikishi shared a heartfelt message on the platform X.

"My heart is full watching my family carry our legacy forward. Proud Samoans representing our culture with pride, inside the ring and beyond. Always love and respect this industry, the people who paved the way, and the fans who stand beside you. Keep God first, stay close, and remember your roots Bloodline is who we are. Proud of you all. 🩸☝🏾🌴🇼🇸🇦🇸🙏🏾🤙🏾Fans throw up the ☝🏾's #Bloodline #SamoanPride #Rikishi #Fatu #Anoai @wwe @wwe_on_netflix," the Hall of Famer wrote.

The stable currently features both Usos alongside Solo Sikoa and Jacob Fatu, with Roman Reigns serving as the leader of the group following their recent reunion earlier this year. Looking toward the future, Rikishi has previously advocated for his nephew, Zilla Fatu, who is currently gaining experience on WWE NXT as the son of the late Umaga, to eventually join the faction and make the group even stronger.
