Charlotte Flair went all-out for Halloween this year, transforming herself into Princess Diana by recreating one of the late royal’s most famous looks. Flair shared photos on Instagram showing her in Diana’s iconic black “revenge dress,” completed with a short blonde wig, sunglasses, and white arm accessories. She captioned the post,

> "the people’s princess ♾️ & QUEEN. p.s. only do what your hearts tells you ❤️ GLAM @drehidalgo happy halloween 🫶🏻"

Flair also included several quotes by Princess Diana throughout the post, highlighting themes of feeling unloved and following one’s heart rather than standard rules. By channeling Princess Diana, Flair blended the royal's famous nickname and defiant fashion moment with her own WWE persona.
