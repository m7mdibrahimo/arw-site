Tony Khan remains enthusiastic about the future of AEW. Following the acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery by Paramount, which resulted in the formation of Skydance, Khan has been engaging with the new leadership. AEW continues to broadcast its programming on TNT, TBS, and HBO Max as part of the WBD family.

Speaking on X, Khan revealed he recently traveled to Los Angeles for a meeting with Skydance officials. "I just got back from Los Angeles, I had a great visit out there with the Skydance team," he stated. He expressed optimism regarding the future of the promotion under the Skydance banner.

Additionally, Khan confirmed that AEW will host its annual Fright Night event this Wednesday in St. Louis, which will be attended by Warner Bros. executives. "We’re going to be joined by the Warner Bros. executives," he added.
