Alexa Bliss has made it clear that she is finished having children after dealing with a severe medical emergency during her first pregnancy. During her appearance on What's Your Story? With Steph McMahon, Bliss opened up about welcoming her daughter Hendrix in November 2023 with her husband, Ryan Cabrera, and explained why she does not plan to expand her family further. Bliss revealed that doctors initially missed signs of preeclampsia because she naturally has low blood pressure. It was her mother, who holds a doctorate in nursing, who noticed that her blood pressure readings were unusually high for her and urged her to get them checked. The situation escalated dangerously after Hendrix was born. Bliss began experiencing severe swelling and trouble breathing while lying down, telling everyone she felt like she was drowning. Three days after giving birth, she reached out to a hospital neighbor who encouraged her to get blood work done. Doctors immediately admitted her to the hospital out of concern for potential heart and kidney failure, warning her that untreated complications could be fatal. She received medication to remove excess fluid and spent 48 hours being monitored. After finally being cleared, Bliss and Cabrera discussed the possibility of more children, but she decided that one is enough. She also pointed out the difficulties of traveling with WWE while raising two children, noting that while her husband might want a larger family, she is happy with the family she has.
