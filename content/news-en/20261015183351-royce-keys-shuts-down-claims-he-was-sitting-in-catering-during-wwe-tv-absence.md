Royce Keys addressed the assumption that he was simply sitting in catering during his time away from WWE television, revealing that Triple H kept him busy with dark matches before every WWE RAW and WWE SmackDown for months. Speaking on NotSam Wrestling, Keys explained that he was only home on Tuesdays and Wednesdays ahead of his debut, working hard to get the necessary repetitions. He noted that Triple H gave him the opportunity to perform in dark matches for about two and a half to three months to help him adjust to the environment and prepare for his television run. Keys is now part of the 946 group, expressing how much he enjoys his current position on television while being able to showcase his true personality alongside the right people.
