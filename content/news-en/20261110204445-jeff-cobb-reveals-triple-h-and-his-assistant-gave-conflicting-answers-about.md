Jeff Cobb recently opened up about his transition from NJPW to WWE, explaining that conflicting messages from Triple H's office complicated his farewell tour. During an interview with Chris Van Vliet on INSIGHT, Cobb addressed claims that he left New Japan on bad terms in 2025. He stated that he originally received permission to finish his remaining dates, but when he followed up through Triple H's assistant, he was told that plans had changed and he needed to leave immediately. Cobb then contacted NJPW to remove himself from the upcoming events. However, the very next day, Triple H personally reached out and told him he could complete his NJPW commitments after all. Because New Japan had already changed its cards, Cobb missed out on the proper farewell he had envisioned. To salvage the situation, he arranged to wrestle Hiroshi Tanahashi on April 19, 2025, just before his WWE contract officially began on April 21. Cobb noted that miscommunication led to online rumors that he had screwed over NJPW, and he wanted to clear the air about what truly happened before his WWE debut at Backlash 2025.
