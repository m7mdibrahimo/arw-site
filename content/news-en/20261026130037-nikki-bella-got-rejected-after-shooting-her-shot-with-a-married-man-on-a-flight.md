Nikki Bella recalled an embarrassing moment from years ago when she tried to flirt with a handsome stranger on a plane. During The Nikki & Brie Show, while discussing books with Victoria Arlen, Nikki remembered seeing a man reading The Shack a few rows behind her. Wanting to make the first move, she asked the flight attendant to send him a drink. Unfortunately, the mystery man turned it down because he was married. The flight attendant returned with the rejection, and Nikki responded with a friendly thumbs-up, vowing never to try it again. The man appreciated the gesture, laughed, and exchanged thumbs-ups with her from his seat, making the awkward encounter as pleasant as possible.
