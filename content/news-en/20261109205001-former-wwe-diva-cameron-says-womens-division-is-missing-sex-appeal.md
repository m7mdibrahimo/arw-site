Former WWE Superstar Ariane Andrew, known to fans as Cameron, shared her candid thoughts regarding the current state of WWE's women's division. Appearing alongside fellow former Superstar Layla on a Halloween edition of the Layla Talk Podcast, both performers reflected on the presentation and traditions of the Divas era before turning their attention to today's roster.

While Andrew praised the elevated in-ring capabilities of modern performers, she argued that WWE has drifted too far away from the glamour and allure that once defined the division. Addressing the change in direction, Andrew expressed her view directly:

> "If people want to watch MMA, they're gonna watch MMA, right? But I feel like that's what's missing is that sex appeal. Like guys want respectfully guys want to see a little ass, but I think it's dope for girl like you know girls to show little ass and be able to kick ass."

Layla noted that contemporary talent walk a difficult line because modern audiences expect high-level athletic competition. However, both agreed that performers like Chelsea Green effectively bridge the gap between both eras by blending in-ring skill with classic character presentation.
