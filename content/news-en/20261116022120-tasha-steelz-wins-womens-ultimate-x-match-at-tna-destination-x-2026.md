Tasha Steelz earned an opportunity to challenge for the TNA Knockouts World Championship by capturing the Women's Ultimate X match at TNA Destination X on Sunday, November 15, 2026. According to Ringside News, Steelz emerged victorious over Shotzi Blackheart, Kiera Hogan, Shayna Baszler, M By Elegance, and Heather By Elegance during the six-woman encounter at the Edmonton Expo Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada.

The action commenced with all six participants brawling inside the squared circle as Baszler targeted M By Elegance's arm and Hogan alongside Blackheart executed dives to the outside. Heather ascended the structure to knock Blackheart down before performing a moonsault, and the chaos escalated through competing elevation attempts toward the hanging prize. After Hogan used a Singapore cane to thwart Blackheart, Steelz scaled the cables and retrieved the X to secure the win.

This triumph grants Steelz the status of number one contender to the TNA Knockouts World Championship held by Indi Hartwell. TNA adjusted its original card when travel difficulties prevented Baszler from receiving her scheduled title opportunity at the event, allowing Steelz to capitalize on the shift and position herself for a future championship bout.
