The Rock's production company, Seven Bucks Entertainment, is set to produce a new live-action 'Medusa' project for Disney. According to a report by Deadline, Michael Montemayor is writing the first draft of the original film, which Seven Bucks is developing under its deal with Disney. The Rock's partners, Dany Garcia and Hiram Garcia, will also serve as producers on the project. Plot details remain strictly under wraps. Meanwhile, WWE is preparing to celebrate the 30th anniversary of The Rock's debut.
