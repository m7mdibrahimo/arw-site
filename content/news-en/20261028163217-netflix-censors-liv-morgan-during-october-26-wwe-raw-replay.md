Netflix has been editing WWE replays recently, and Liv Morgan became the latest wrestler to face this treatment during the October 26 episode of WWE RAW. After her match against Becky Lynch, Dominik Mysterio carried Liv away on his shoulders while the live broadcast captured the moment from various angles. However, the subsequent replay on Netflix featured censorship applied to portions showing Liv's backside, apparently to address an accidental wardrobe malfunction. This follows similar editing instances on the platform, such as cutting away when LA Knight gave Roman Reigns the middle finger at Money in the Bank, and muting the sound or cutting the screen during Jacob Fatu's car incident on the September 28 RAW replay.
