Sherilyn Guerrero, the second-oldest daughter of the late WWE Hall of Famer Eddie Guerrero, is set to make her professional wrestling in-ring debut. During the Reality of Wrestling Sherri Martel Classic event, Guerrero was involved in an altercation with Mystii Marks, who blamed her for a previous loss and struck her. After the incident, Guerrero grabbed a microphone and challenged Marks to a match for the November 14th show. Guerrero previously participated in the 2026 Lodestone camp hosted by WWE star Bayley and has visited the WWE Performance Center.
