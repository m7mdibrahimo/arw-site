Becky Lynch believes Liv Morgan's Women's World Championship reign is dragging down the entire women's division. Speaking on WWE Now, Lynch argued that Morgan is best known for avoiding title defenses rather than her actual accomplishments in the ring. "Yes. 100% I do. 100% I do. Yes. Because what, like, like her biggest claim to fame is that she didn't defend her title for a hundred days," Lynch said. While acknowledging Morgan's in-ring ability, Lynch criticized her for relying on the rest of Judgment Day and sitting on the sidelines.

Following a heated confrontation on the November 9 episode of WWE Raw, Lynch challenged Morgan to face her without interference. Adam Pearce subsequently announced that Morgan will defend the Women's World Championship against Lynch at Survivor Series.
