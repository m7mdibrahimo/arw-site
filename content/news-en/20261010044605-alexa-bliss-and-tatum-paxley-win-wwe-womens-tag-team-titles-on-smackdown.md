In a surprise title change just 24 hours before Money in the Bank, Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley captured the WWE Women’s Tag Team Championship. During the October 9, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown, Bliss and Paxley defeated the defending champions, Fatal Influence’s Fallon Henley and Lainey Reid.

Fatal Influence dominated much of the contest, repeatedly isolating Paxley to prevent a tag. The momentum shifted once Bliss entered the fray, utilizing a series of strikes and a DDT to regain control. The chaotic finish saw Bliss take Reid out on the floor with a Sister Abigail, leaving Henley vulnerable in the ring. Paxley capitalized by hitting the Cemetery Drive to secure the pinfall victory.

This win marks a significant shift in the WWE women’s tag team division as the new champions head into the Money in the Bank weekend.
