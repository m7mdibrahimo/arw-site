Cody Rhodes has shared a surprising influence behind his iconic American Nightmare logo during an appearance on the podcast What Do You Wanna Talk About? with Andrew Schulz and David Dastmalchian. Rather than turning to traditional wrestling imagery like skulls or guns for inspiration, Rhodes looked to the villainous organization Shadaloo from the video game franchise Street Fighter.

Rhodes explained that he wanted a unique visual identity when establishing his brand after his time with Bullet Club, which led him to adapt elements from the classic fighting game art style. When Dastmalchian questioned whether he was joking, Rhodes insisted he was completely serious and had been waiting for a chance to share the connection.

The American Nightmare logo has since become one of the most recognizable symbols in the industry, appearing across merchandise and even as a tattoo on Cody's neck.
