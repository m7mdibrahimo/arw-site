Big Damo announced that Tommy End and Xelina will miss the upcoming PROGRESS Wrestling event due to a personal matter.

PROGRESS Wrestling's Chapter 199: Bonestorm takes place on October 25 at the Electric Ballroom in London, England. Co-owner Big Damo shared the news on X, stating that they plan to bring the pair back in 2027.

> "Unfortunately Tommy End and Xelina will no longer be able to compete at Chapter 199: Bonestorm. They are dealing with a personal issue. I am gutted we won't have them with us next week but we will have them with us again in 2027. I believe in family first and I am wishing them the very best. They are incredible people and have been a pleasure to deal with. For anyone who bought tickets just to see them then obviously you are welcome to get refunds. However we are working on bringing in other talent right now and will announce them shortly. We know it'll be an incredible show."
