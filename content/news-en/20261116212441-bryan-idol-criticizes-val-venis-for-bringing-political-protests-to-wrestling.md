Former wrestler Val Venis is crossing boundaries by bringing political debates to public events, with plans to target the 2027 WWE Royal Rumble. NWA star Bryan Idol believes Venis must stop these actions, noting that his presence outside venues to engage in heated arguments with fans harms the wrestling industry more than it helps.

Idol expressed his frustration on X after Venis announced his intention to confront WWE fans outside the Royal Rumble, scheduled for February 6 in Glendale, Arizona, where he plans to discuss abortion issues with attendees. Idol questioned the behavior, stating:

> "What does any of this have to do with pro wrestling? What’s wrong with this guy"

Idol did not stop there; he posted another message directed at Venis, accusing him of damaging the wrestling industry by forcing political debates into spaces where fans come solely to enjoy their time. Idol's comments followed Venis's appearance outside AEW Full Gear on November 14 in Phoenix, where he attempted to interview fans about political issues and received an angry reaction from a passerby.

Despite that reception, Venis insists on repeating the experience outside State Farm Stadium at the Royal Rumble. Idol believes former wrestling stars should respect the industry that built their fame rather than turning events into arenas for political confrontation, while Venis continues to hold onto his plans without backing down.
