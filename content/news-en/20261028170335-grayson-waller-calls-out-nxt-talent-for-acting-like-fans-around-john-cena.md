Grayson Waller has criticized the NXT locker room for acting like fans ahead of John Cena's upcoming appearance on the November 3 edition of WWE NXT. During an appearance on Busted Open After Dark, Waller revealed that he was backstage when the graphic announcing Cena was shown, and he was unimpressed by the excited reactions of the younger talent.

"So, I was backstage tonight when the graphic came up. You should have heard the kids. ‘Oh, John!’ It was tough. I felt like I was at a fan meet-and-greet or something," Waller stated. He noted that while he respects the legends, wrestlers need to treat established stars as equals rather than lining up for merchandise or photos, comparing his own attitude of dropping his fan card on day one to the mindset the developmental roster should adopt.
