Claudio Castagnoli revealed in a new interview during his appearance at the League Of Legends Worlds that he is not currently medically cleared to compete.

On the October 28 episode of AEW Dynamite, Claudio attempted to save his Death Riders teammates during a brawl with The Cut, which included a trash can throw that bounced back toward him. The Cut ultimately wore down the star, leading to his absence from AEW television ever since.

While speaking to The Load-In at the event, Claudio discussed his current health status by stating:

> "No, it's been great. I'm not medically clear right now, so I was just sitting around at home. So they asked me if I wanted to come here, see Worlds, and I'm like, yeah, definitely, 100%. I'll be there. So what impressed me, and I had the chance to go to China for the LPL a few years ago, and then here again, it's the passion of the fans. It's the passion of the fans for their team, for the game, and how they show it. It's very similar to pro wrestling. They have signs, they come early, they dress up their jerseys it's like they show their passion and they're proud. Proud of their heart."
