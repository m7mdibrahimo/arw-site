This week's AEW Full Gear airing November 14, 2026, features action-packed matches live from the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona. In the main events, Will Ospreay defends the AEW World Championship against Kyle Fletcher, Tommaso Ciampa defends the AEW National Championship against Ilja Dragunov, and Kazuchika Okada defends the AEW International Championship against Adam Copeland. The event also features Mercedes Moné defending the AEW Women’s World Championship against Marina Shafir, an Anarchy in the Arena match, a Texas Death Match for the AEW World Trios Championship, and more high-stakes bouts.
