This week's AEW Full Gear aired live from the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona, featuring championship matches and high-stakes action. In the opening match, Kazuchika Okada defended the AEW International Championship against Adam Copeland, securing the win with a Rainmaker. Next, The Young Bucks successfully defended the AEW World Tag Team Championship against Davis and Doyle after a hard-fought battle, followed by an appearance from The Kasper Brothers and FTR. In a brutal Texas Death Match, Swerve Strickland and The New Level defeated "Hangman" Adam Page and Brodido to capture the AEW World Trios Championship. Wrathlander also made history by defeating The Brawling Birds to become the new AEW Women's World Tag Team Champions. Meanwhile, Ilja Dragunov captured the AEW National Championship by defeating Tommaso Ciampa, and Mercedes Moné retained the AEW Women's World Championship against Marina Shafir. In the chaotic Anarchy in the Arena main event, The Cut emerged victorious over The Death Riders and The Conglomeration.
