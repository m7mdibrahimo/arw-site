AEW Full Gear 2026 took place on November 14, 2026, live from the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona, airing on TNT and streaming platforms like Prime Video. In the opening match of the pay-per-view, Don Callis Family's Kazuchika Okada defended the AEW International Championship against Adam Copeland, retaining his title after a hard-fought encounter. Next, The Young Bucks defended the AEW World Tag Team Championship against Mark Davis and Jake Doyle of the Don Callis Family, securing the win to keep their titles. Following the match, the Motor City Machine Guns came out to celebrate, but were confronted by the newly debuted Kasper Brothers (formerly known as Brutus Creed and Julius Creed in WWE), who attacked The Young Bucks before FTR arrived to join the brawl.
