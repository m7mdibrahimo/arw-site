AEW Full Gear aired on November 14, 2026, live from the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona, featuring a stacked card with multiple championship matches and intense brawls. In the main event of the pre-show, Queen Aminata successfully defended the AEW TBS Championship in a four-way match against Skye Blue, Hyan, and CMLL World Women's Champion Persephone, picking up the win by hitting Off With Her Head on Hyan. Other pre-show results saw Jack Perry defeat The Beast Mortos with a Muscle Buster, the Don Callis Family trio of El Clon, Kevin Knight, and Wardlow defeat the team of Jayden Monroe, RJ Wild, and Jordan Cruz, and the team of Big Boom! A.J., QT Marshall, Darius Martin, and Matt Sydal pick up a win in an eight-man tag team match. On the main pay-per-view card, Kazuchika Okada defeated Adam Copeland to retain the AEW International Championship after interference and a hard-hitting bout. The Young Bucks defeated Mark Davis and Jake Doyle to retain the AEW World Tag Team Championship, followed by a post-match angle where the debuting Kasper Brothers and FTR confronted the champions and Motor City Machine Guns. Swerve Strickland and The New Level captured the AEW World Trios Championship by defeating Hangman Page and Brodido in a brutal Texas Death Match. The event also featured video packages, backstage segments, and intense build-ups for upcoming storylines.
