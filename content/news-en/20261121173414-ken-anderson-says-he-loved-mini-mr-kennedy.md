Ken Anderson recently shared his thoughts on the 2008 Royal Rumble match that featured mini versions of WWE superstars. During an appearance on Rewind, Recap, Relive, Anderson recalled the special match on an episode of WWE RAW which included mini versions of Mr. Kennedy, Batista, and Kane, along with Hornswoggle. Reflecting on the segment, Anderson expressed his fondness for the performance, particularly praising the actor who portrayed mini Mr. Kennedy. 

"So I was actually, I think I was injured at the time that they did that. So I didn't get to see it in person, but I loved it," Anderson said. He also noted a funny detail regarding the use of props, adding, "That guy passed away, actually, unfortunately. The guy that played mini Kennedy. But like just the fact that the microphone came down and he couldn't reach it and he was jumping for it, and also somebody and Vince used to jump on me about chewing gum. He hated the fact that I chewed gum, but they made sure that the mini Kennedy was chewing gum."
