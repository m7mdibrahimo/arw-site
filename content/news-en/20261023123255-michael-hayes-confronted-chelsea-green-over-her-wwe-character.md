WWE Hall of Famer Michael Hayes recently revealed that he once confronted Chelsea Green about her matches lacking her signature character work. Speaking on Stephanie McMahon's What's Your Story? podcast, Hayes explained that while Green puts on technically sound matches, he wanted to see more of her personality inside the ring. Hayes praised Green as a smart and fantastic performer who immediately understood his advice, though he joked that simply mentioning her name tends to get him into trouble given their past history.

The two previously clashed when Green was unhappy with footage of Hayes questioning her potential, a situation that even led to a confrontation with her mother at SummerSlam. Despite that rocky past, Hayes had nothing but praise for Green this time around, emphasizing that his critique was simply about making sure her vibrant character always shines through during her matches.
