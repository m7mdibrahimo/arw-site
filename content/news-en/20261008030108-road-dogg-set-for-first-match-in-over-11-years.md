WWE Hall of Famer Road Dogg is preparing to step back into the ring for the first time in over a decade to participate in a major anniversary event in Tennessee. The veteran performer will join forces with Jeff Jarrett and Jay Lethal as part of Jeff Jarrett's All-Star Super Slam on October 22 at Veterans Park in Hendersonville, Tennessee, during the annual Hendersonville Pig Fest.

The main event will feature Jarrett celebrating 40 years in professional wrestling by teaming with Road Dogg and Jay Lethal to face QT Marshall, Elijah Drago and Brady Booker. According to Cagematch, the last official match for Road Dogg took place on January 25, 2015, at the WWE Royal Rumble event, where he and Billy Gunn lost to The Ascension. That means more than 11 years will have passed between his previous appearance and his upcoming return.

The comeback arrives as a surprise given that Billy Gunn stated earlier this year that physical wear and tear would prevent Road Dogg from ever wrestling again. According to Ringside News, Gunn dismissed the possibility of another New Age Outlaws run and indicated that his former partner was completely done taking bumps in the ring.

Aside from his in-ring absence, Road Dogg has remained active in the industry, previously departing the WWE SmackDown creative team and later appearing backstage at TNA Slammiversary amid discussions about a potential role. Those talks ultimately stalled, leading the wrestling legend to dust off his boots for the upcoming Tennessee card.

The rest of the event features several unique matchups, including a Concession Stand Brawl and a 10-man battle royal. Jeff Jarrett is marking four decades in the business, but Road Dogg stepping back between the ropes stands out as a major talking point for the show.
