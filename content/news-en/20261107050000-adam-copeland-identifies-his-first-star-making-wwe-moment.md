Adam Copeland is approaching the end of his in-ring career, aiming for the AEW World Championship before an official retirement in 2027. Prior to signing with AEW, Copeland achieved massive popularity and success as Edge during his extensive tenure with WWE.

During a recent appearance on "The Mick And Kenny Show," Copeland reflected on his time in WWE and pinpointed the exact moment he felt he became a star, pointing to the historic ladder match against The Hardys at No Mercy in Cleveland.

> "To answer your question about it feeling like the first star-making moment: it was actually in Cleveland. No Mercy, the ladder match against The Hardys ; it was the first tag team ladder match ever"

Copeland shared that WWE Hall of Famer Mick Foley met all four competitors behind the curtain after the bout and told them that they had just become stars, making the validation even more impactful.

Reflecting on another iconic moment, Copeland discussed his legendary leap off a ladder to deliver a spear to Jeff Hardy during the Tables, Ladders, and Chairs match at WrestleMania X-Seven, noting that he did not feel nervous before executing the high-risk spot.
