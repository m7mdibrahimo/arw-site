Independent wrestling star Alejandra the Lion was recently involved in a terrifying car accident that completely destroyed the front of her vehicle and totaled the car. Alejandra took to Instagram to share photos and videos of the wreckage, which showed deployed airbags and severe front-end damage. Fortunately, she made it clear immediately that she escaped without any physical injuries. "First of all, I’M OK! Zero injuries and physically I’m totally fine. Mentally I’m extremely heartbroken!"

According to Alejandra, the collision occurred while she was driving to the gym when a 16-year-old driver attempted to cross a main intersection without checking for oncoming traffic. She expressed immense gratitude that both she and the other driver walked away safely from the crash. However, losing her car has created a major challenge for her independent wrestling career, as she constantly travels and practically lived out of her vehicle. Despite the uncertainty, she remains determined to push forward through her faith and resilience.
