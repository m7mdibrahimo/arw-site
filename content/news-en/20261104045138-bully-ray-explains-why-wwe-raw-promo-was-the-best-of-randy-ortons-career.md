Randy Orton's greatest in-ring moments remain up for debate, but WWE Hall of Famer Bully Ray believes his recent promo on WWE Raw clearly stands above the rest. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, Ray praised Orton's short and direct promo targeting Cody Rhodes on the November 2 episode of WWE Raw. "The best promo I've ever heard Randy Orton cut in his entire career," Ray said. The veteran added that the promo landed blow after blow, noting that Orton pointed out Rhodes' lack of experience in Last Man Standing matches ahead of their clash at WWE Crown Jewel. Orton also highlighted his loyalty to the wrestling business, unlike peers who pursued Hollywood. Ray noted that under Vince McMahon, Orton would have used the term sports entertainer, but Paul Levesque's creative direction allows performers to embrace the wrestling aspect of WWE.
