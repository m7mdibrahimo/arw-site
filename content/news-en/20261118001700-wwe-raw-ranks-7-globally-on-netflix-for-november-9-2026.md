New ratings data for the week of November 9, 2026, shows that WWE Raw ranked #7 globally and #4 in the United States on Netflix. The episode reached 2,400,000 global views, a decrease from the previous week's ranking of #5. Compared to the same week in 2025, when the show pulled 2.8 million views, global viewership saw a year-over-year decline of 14.3%.
