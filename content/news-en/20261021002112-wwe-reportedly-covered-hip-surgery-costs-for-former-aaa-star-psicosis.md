According to the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, WWE paid for the hip replacement surgery of Dionicio Castellanos, known as Psicosis and Nicho el Millonario. The procedure, costing around $44,000, was approved by WWE President Nick Khan, aided by Psicosis holding a WWE Legend's Contract. The operation was spearheaded by WWE star Rey Mysterio, who shares a storied history with Psicosis from their early days in AAA, ECW, and WCW. Psicosis famously competed in the opening match at Bash at the Beach 1996 and had a later run in WWE as part of the Mexicools stable.
