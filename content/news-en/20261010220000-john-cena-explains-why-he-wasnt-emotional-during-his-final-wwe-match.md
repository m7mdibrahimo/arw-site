John Cena recently reflected on his final in-ring performance, which took place at WWE Saturday Night's Main Event on December 13, 2025. While fans were deeply emotional as the legendary career of the 17-time world champion came to an end, Cena remained composed throughout the night.

Speaking on Busted Open Radio, Cena explained that he had already mentally processed his retirement before stepping into the ring for the last time.

> "What I do and how I perform is not for my personal satisfaction. It is for the reaction of the audience. And if they felt emotional the day that I stopped performing in the ring, that's the feels that they have. A lot of people who were super emotional on my last day, I didn't share those emotions. I'm completely sympathetic, but I had already compartmentalized my journey. I know it's a chapter in my life. I work every day on self-worth, stuff like that. Man, I finished my last match. I have left nothing on the table. There is nothing more I could give."

In that final match, Cena faced GUNTHER, the winner of "The Last Time Is Now" tournament. Despite a valiant effort that featured his signature moves, Cena ultimately tapped out to a sleeper hold applied by GUNTHER. Cena viewed the loss as a peaceful conclusion to his storied career.
