Boca Raton Championship Wrestling revealed the full lineup for Cruisin' For A Bruisin' 4, which is scheduled for Friday, November 6, 2026, at the DoubleTree by Hilton Miami Airport Convention Center in Miami, Florida. The event features eight matches, with six championships on the line.

In the main event, Matt Riddle defends the BRCW Championship against A.J. Francis in his first title defense since winning the belt at Summer Smash on August 16. Matt Taven and Stallion Rogers square off on the same card to determine the number one contender for the championship. 

The BRCW Six-Man Tag Team Championship match features Samuel Shaw, Erick Redbeard, and Joseph Sawyer challenging champions Gangrel and The Righteous. Lacey Lane puts the BRCW Women's Championship on the line in a triple threat against Harley Cameron and Shotzi. Additionally, the Grizzled Young Veterans make their promotional debut by challenging the Sons of Boca for the BRCW Tag Team Championship.
