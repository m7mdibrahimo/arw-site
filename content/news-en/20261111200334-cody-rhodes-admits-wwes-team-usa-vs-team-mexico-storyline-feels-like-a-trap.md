Cody Rhodes is looking forward to representing Team USA in the traditional 5-on-5 elimination match at WWE Survivor Series on November 28 in Houston, but he is concerned about the potential pitfalls of the storyline. Rhodes, who will captain Team USA alongside CM Punk and Je’Von Evans, admitted during an appearance on ESPN Radio’s Unsportsmanlike podcast that the "Team USA vs. Team Mexico" narrative is a delicate balance to maintain.

Rhodes noted that while he is excited to pivot to a fan-focused concept after his intense feud with Randy Orton, he is wary of how the promotion will handle national pride. Rhodes said, "I've gotta do three weeks of promos talking about how we're gonna beat Team Mexico. How do you do that without getting in any trouble? How? You can't, you can't, you can't. It's impossible." He expressed concern that the storyline could easily cross the line, especially when facing a legend like Rey Mysterio. Rhodes added, "The whole thing feels like it's a trap. It's a setup. But I'm gonna see how good I can navigate these waters. I think Team USA might be the bad guys here."
