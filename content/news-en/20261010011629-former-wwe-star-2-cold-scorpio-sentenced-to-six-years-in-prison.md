Former WWE, WCW and ECW wrestler 2 Cold Scorpio, whose real name is Charles Scaggs, was sentenced on October 9 to six years in the Missouri Department of Corrections. The sentence follows his guilty plea in a Kansas City assault case stemming from a violent June 2024 incident at a travel stop, where Scaggs was working security and was involved in a stabbing.

According to court records, the judge also ordered Scaggs to complete a 120-day shock incarceration program. The legal proceedings were complicated by a bond violation on September 17, when Scaggs admitted to brandishing a firearm while out on bond. At the time of that violation, the court noted: "Court finds and believes Defendant to be clearly in violation of his bond conditions, and that said violation is a danger to public safety."

Despite having returned to the ring briefly after his initial guilty plea, Scaggs is now facing his prison term. The court's final ruling brings a definitive end to the criminal case that has spanned more than two years.
