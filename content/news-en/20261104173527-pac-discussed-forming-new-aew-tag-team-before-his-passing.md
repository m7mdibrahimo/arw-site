Gabe Kidd revealed that he and PAC recently discussed the possibility of forming a new tag team in AEW. Reflecting on his late colleague, Kidd shared that they had talked about portraying two rough English wrestlers on American television. Their relationship goes back to 2011 when Kidd helped set up the ring for a Dragon Gate UK event in Nottingham, and they grew much closer while working together in the Death Riders.

Kidd noted that PAC also shared unexpected musical tastes, such as a love for dancehall and dubstep. During a dinner in Charleston, West Virginia, they compared their similar journeys moving from England to Japan as young men. The tag team idea ultimately never became a reality, leaving Kidd with lingering questions about what they could have achieved together.
