Julius Creed and Brutus Creed officially departed WWE after their contracts expired, but a new revelation shows that WWE once held massive expectations for Julius. According to Dave Meltzer on Wrestling Observer Radio, individuals inside the company initially believed Julius had the potential to become one of its top stars. Meltzer noted that during the recruitment process, some top figures within WWE even compared his potential to John Cena.

Despite an early strong start and capturing the NXT Tag Team Championships, the duo's momentum slowed after moving to the main roster in 2023 and joining Chad Gable's group. Meltzer suggested that limited television time and a lack of character development hindered their rise, while adding that AEW should consider signing them to showcase their exceptional athleticism.
