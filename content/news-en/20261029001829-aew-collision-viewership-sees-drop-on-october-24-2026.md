New ratings data for the October 24, 2026 episode of AEW Collision on TNT indicates a decline in viewership. According to Dave Meltzer, the episode drew 449,000 viewers, with a 0.1 rating in the key P18–49 demographic. This is a decrease of 65,000 viewers compared to the October 14, 2026 episode, which recorded 514,000 viewers and a 0.1 demo rating. When compared to the same week in 2025, the show saw a year-over-year increase of 96.9% in total viewership and 150.0% in the key demographic. These figures represent linear TV viewership on TNT and exclude digital or streaming data. Ringside News will continue to monitor AEW Collision as the company prepares for its next major live event.
