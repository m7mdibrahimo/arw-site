AEW star Bandido has named Kenny Omega and newly signed star Ilja Dragunov as his dream opponents in the company. Since joining All Elite Wrestling, the luchador has competed against several top names, including MJF and Andrade El Idolo.

Speaking to Good Karma Wrestling, Bandido revealed the two wrestlers he hopes to face in singles action. He noted that Kenny Omega remains at the top of his list, while also expressing interest in facing Dragunov following their past history in PWG.

> "Well, I always say Kenny Omega will be one of my biggest top names. But now I share a time with the new guy who came to the company in the past in PWG. His name is Ilja Dragunov. So probably that would be good for me to have singles with him. Yeah, I would love to have singles with him too."
