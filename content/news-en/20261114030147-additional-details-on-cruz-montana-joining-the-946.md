New details have emerged regarding the main roster call-up of former NXT star Cruz Montana, who previously held the TNA World Championship. His arrival as the newest member of Royce Keys' 946 faction on the November 2 episode of WWE Raw was kept secret until his surprise appearance.

According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, a meeting was held in late October before NXT Halloween Havoc to discuss Montana joining the group. Because there were existing creative plans for him in NXT, staff at the Performance Center were reportedly blindsided by his move to the main roster.

Meltzer noted that NXT head of creative Shawn Michaels has hinted that main roster decisions often catch him off guard, though Meltzer previously expressed skepticism regarding how much Michaels truly knows. Michaels stated in September that while preparing talent for the main roster is a primary goal, the high volume of call-ups this year has challenged his creative team.

Following his Raw reveal, Montana competed in an unsanctioned match against Dion Lennox of DarkState. With help from his 946 stablemates, Montana secured the win to a strong crowd reaction at the Performance Center.
