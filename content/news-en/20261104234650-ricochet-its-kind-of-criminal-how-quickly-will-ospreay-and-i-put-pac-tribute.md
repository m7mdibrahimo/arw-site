Ricochet recently discussed his match against Will Ospreay on the September 30 episode of AEW Dynamite, which served as a tribute to PAC. The encounter was highly praised by fans and critics alike, with some calling it one of the best matches in the promotion's history. In an interview with The Takedown on Sports Illustrated, Ricochet explained that the match came together very quickly because of their familiarity with one another and their shared history with PAC. "Honestly, the match kind of helps you. The match helps the day go by, because not being able to think about your match, you're stuck thinking about sad times basically," Ricochet said. He noted that their long-standing relationship made the preparation seamless. "It's funny, it's kind of criminal how quickly he and I got all that together. It's kind of criminal how quickly he and I were like, 'Oh yeah, okay, well I guess that's it, I'll see you later.' Because again, we worked so well together and we just knew Ben so well." Ricochet added that focusing on the performance helped him navigate a difficult day.
