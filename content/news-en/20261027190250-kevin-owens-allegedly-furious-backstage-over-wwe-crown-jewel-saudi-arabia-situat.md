Kevin Owens reportedly is not happy about WWE's upcoming trip to Saudi Arabia, with Jonathan Coachman naming him as one of the disgruntled backstage talents. According to Coachman speaking on Two Count Tuesday, several WWE stars are furious behind the scenes over the Crown Jewel situation, though none have addressed it publicly yet. JD noted that WWE booked Kevin Owens versus CM Punk as a taped match for the Barclays Center next week, suggesting the bout may have been moved from Crown Jewel because Owens did not want to travel to Saudi Arabia. WrestleVotes previously reported that while talent can refuse the trip, they will not be paid and could face potential career consequences. Meanwhile, WWE continues preparations for the November 7 event at Riyadh Season Stadium, while a team evaluates alternatives in case plans must change.
