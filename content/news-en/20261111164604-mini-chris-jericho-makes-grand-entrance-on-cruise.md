A miniature version of Chris Jericho stole the spotlight during the Rock 'N' Wrestling Cruise by fully embodying his persona aboard the ship.

James Davies posted footage on Instagram showing a person portraying a mini version of Chris Jericho wearing a glowing gold jacket identical to Jericho's famous attire, with his entrance theme playing as he struck his signature pose.

Fans widely enjoyed the entertaining appearance as the performer replicated the wrestler's movements and atmosphere, adding great fun to the fan-filled cruise.
