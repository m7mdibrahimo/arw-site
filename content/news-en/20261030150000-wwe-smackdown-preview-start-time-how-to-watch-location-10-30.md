Preview for the October 30, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown from the Charleston Coliseum in Charleston, WV. The show airs at 8 pm ET on USA Network in the US and on Netflix internationally. The lineup features Danhausen presenting 'Danhausen's Very Nice, Very Evil SmackDown', along with Sami Zayn defending the Undisputed WWE Championship against Trick Williams. Chelsea Green defends the WWE Women’s Championship against Nikki Bella, and Rey Fenix defends the AAA World Cruiserweight Championship against Ricky Saints.
