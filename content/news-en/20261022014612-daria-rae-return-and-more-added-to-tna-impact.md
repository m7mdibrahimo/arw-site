Daria Rae is scheduled to make her return on the upcoming episode of TNA iMPACT! Total Nonstop Action Wrestling announced that the Director of Operations will return during the October 22 show following her suspension in September. Additionally, former TNA World Knockouts Tag Team Champions Allie and Kiera Hogan will speak after losing their titles to Syna Baszler and Zoey Serrano at TNA Bound for Glory.

The updated lineup for the Thursday broadcast features a TNA International Championship 2-Out-Of-3 Falls match between champion Jason Hotch and Mustafa Ali. Other scheduled bouts include Avery Styles versus Joe Alonzo, along with a four-way number one contender match for the TNA X Division Championship featuring Rich Swann, K.C. Navarro, Cedric Alexander, and Fabian Aichner.
