CJ Perry has some ambitious Hollywood plans for her husband, Rusev. The former WWE Superstar is working on a movie adaptation of her new spy thriller novel, Bulletproof Angel, and she already has a specific role in mind for him. Perry revealed during an appearance on The Joe Vulpis Podcast that she envisions Rusev playing a mafia villain in the film. She explained that his background as a WWE bad guy makes him a perfect fit for the part. "We want Rusev in it. He's going to play one of the villains because, he obviously is Eastern European, and anytime he's ever played an Eastern European bad guy, he's always crushed it. He does look like he could be a mafia guy, you know, crushing people's souls."

Perry is currently in discussions with an iconic director and a major actress to bring the project to the screen. She intends to serve as a producer and take on a smaller acting role, leaving the lead female parts open for bigger Hollywood names. While Rusev's involvement is not yet officially confirmed, Perry is clearly determined to see him trade his wrestling gear for a gangster suit. Bulletproof Angel, which follows a CIA operative undercover in Russia, was released on November 10, 2026.
