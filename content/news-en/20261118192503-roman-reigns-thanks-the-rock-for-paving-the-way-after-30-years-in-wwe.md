Roman Reigns is acknowledging The Rock's contribution to WWE as the latter celebrates 30 years since his debut. Reigns took to social media to thank his famous cousin for "paving the way" for the next generation of wrestling stars, including himself. He shared a photo of the two from WrestleMania XL in 2024, where they teamed up to defeat Cody Rhodes and Seth Rollins. Reigns expressed deep respect, stating, "30 years of The Great One. Eternal love & respect for @TheRock. Thank you for paving the way for so many of us."

Triple H also honored the anniversary, reflecting on their shared history in the business, from wrestling in small towns to selling out stadiums during the Attitude Era. WWE is celebrating the milestone with special content and limited-edition merchandise, including a $700 replica of the Intercontinental Championship. The Rock originally debuted as Rocky Maivia at Survivor Series on November 17, 1996, at Madison Square Garden. As Survivor Series approaches on November 28, with Reigns preparing to lead The Bloodline against The 946 in WarGames, fans continue to speculate about a potential reunion or a long-awaited singles match between the two, though nothing has been officially announced.
