Netflix has released the first trailer for the upcoming film Guarding Stars, giving fans a glimpse of CM Punk in his next movie role. In the preview, Punk appears during a comedic makeover sequence, delivering a brief line that highlights his character. The film stars Leighton Meester and Jared Padalecki and is based on Katherine Center's novel The Bodyguard. Guarding Stars is scheduled to premiere on Netflix on November 18.
