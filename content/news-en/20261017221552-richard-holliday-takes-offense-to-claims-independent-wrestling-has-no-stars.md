Richard Holliday has made it clear that he takes personal offense to claims that the independent wrestling scene lacks true stars, pointing to his own career as definitive proof to the contrary. During an appearance on the Direct Discussions podcast, Holliday reflected on his busy schedule throughout 2026 and the high-caliber opponents he has faced, including Karrion Kross, Mike Santana, Matt Riddle, Nic Nemeth, and Matt Cardona. Holliday stated that matches with Kross and Santana consistently deliver magic whenever they share the ring.

> "You know, some of my favorite opponents that stick out would be Killer Kross. Anytime I get in the ring with Killer Kross, it's magic. Santana, Mike Santana, now Cruz Montana, anytime with him was magic. Matt Riddle. I've been able to share the ring with Nick Nemeth, Matt Cardona on several occasions."

Shifting his perspective, Holliday expressed that he no longer views himself merely as an independent wrestler fortunate enough to work with recognizable names. Instead, he believes he has reached a level where facing him represents a major milestone for his opponents. He noted that wrestlers frequently cut promos framing their bouts against him as the biggest test of their careers. While acknowledging that such a mindset involves a degree of cockiness, Holliday argued that self-belief and a touch of arrogance are essential for survival and success in the professional wrestling industry.
