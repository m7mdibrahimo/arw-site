It took nearly two months for Ilja Dragunov to debut at AEW WrestleDream this past Saturday following his WWE departure. Dave Meltzer reported on "Wrestling Observer Radio" that visa complications delayed his arrival, and suggested WWE played a role in the situation.

> "He's been signed, but they had to work out the visa situation, because, you know, his visa was with WWE, and of course WWE is going to make it difficult."

Meltzer noted that AEW received confirmation of Dragunov's visa at 2 a.m. on Wednesday while taping "Dynamite" and "Collision," allowing them to finally air his vignette. Dragunov is now scheduled to compete in his first AEW match this Wednesday on "Dynamite."
