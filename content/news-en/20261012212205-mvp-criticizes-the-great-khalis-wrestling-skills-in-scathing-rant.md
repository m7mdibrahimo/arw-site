MVP recently shared his blunt assessment of The Great Khali's in-ring work during an appearance on the Marking Out podcast. While discussing wrestlers who transitioned from monster gimmicks to comedic roles, MVP praised Khali's business impact in India but was highly critical of his wrestling ability.

> "He—I mean, he made money for WWE because of the Indian audience, and I respect that, and the big, you know, I think Sony TV deal and all that, but he was trash. And there's a billion people that hate me because I feel that way."

MVP recounted several anecdotes about the difficulty of working with Khali, including a moment during a European tour where The Undertaker jokingly touched Khali's heel, nearly causing the giant to lose his balance. He also shared a story involving Batista, who was selling on all fours during a match when Khali accidentally knocked himself over because he lacked ring awareness.

> "Don't never ask me about him in a wrestling context."

Despite the harsh critique, MVP acknowledged that Khali was a pleasant person to be around backstage, even if he remains unimpressed by his professional performance inside the ring.
