Independent wrestler Vinny Pacifico recently discussed the challenges of the entertainment industry, noting that many wrestlers do not realize how difficult the field truly is. Pacifico, who has balanced his wrestling career with pursuing acting roles over the past two years, credits his background in the ring for his success in film and television.

In an interview with Fightful Overbooked's In The Weeds, Pacifico reflected on his journey: "This is what a lot of wrestlers don't realize. Entertainment is such a hard industry. I've been doing this since I'm 14. So for me, it's just like what I grew up doing. Now I'm getting into acting and film and all these different things and TV, and it's a whole… I'm learning so much. I'm seeing so much. I'm a sponge."

Pacifico emphasized that he would not be the actor he is today without his wrestling experience, stating, "I wouldn't be getting the opportunities I'm getting in film if I wasn't wrestling. You know, that's hands down. I fully believe that. So that's why I think wrestling deserves a lot of credit and wrestling is, it's tough."

He concluded by expressing his support for his peers, saying, "People who wrestle and go out there and beat their bodies up and do these matches and train so hard. It is not easy. So I, just like you said, I love seeing people do well and making money and just thriving. I love seeing that. It makes me really happy to see."
