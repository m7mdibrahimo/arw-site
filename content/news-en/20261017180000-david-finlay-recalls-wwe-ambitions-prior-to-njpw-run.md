During an appearance on Talk is Jericho, David Finlay recalled his early career plans to eventually wrestle for WWE. Given that his father, Fit Finlay, works as a producer for the promotion, and his brother Brogan Finlay competes in NXT as Uriah Connors, joining WWE seemed like a natural path. However, after planning to spend a brief period in Japan following advice from Nattie Neidhart, Finlay ended up staying with New Japan Pro-Wrestling for eleven years. During his time with NJPW, he captured the IWGP Global Championship twice, the NEVER Openweight Championship, and the IWGP Tag Team Championships with Juice Robinson, before eventually bringing his success to AEW alongside Gabe Kidd and Clark Connors.
