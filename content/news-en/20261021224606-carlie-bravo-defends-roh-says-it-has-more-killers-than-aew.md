Carlie Bravo recently defended Ring of Honor in an interview, stating that the promotion has more hungry and dangerous talent than AEW. Bravo highlighted the roster's drive, mentioning names like Red Velvet, Dalton Castle, The Outrunners, and AR Fox. He also pointed out current and former champions such as Mistico, Mascara Dorada, Deonna Purrazzo, and Lee Moriarty, questioning who can compete with that level of talent.
