Liv Morgan reflected on her career and admitted that she might not have survived WWE's developmental system if she started today. During an appearance on Hot Ones with Sean Evans, Morgan discussed her early days and how being a lifelong fan gave her the illusion that she would naturally succeed. She explained that the rigorous training was far more difficult than she expected, noting that she initially believed she would become champion within a year. Morgan expressed gratitude for starting back in 2014, believing that the company's patience and the extra time given to develop her skills were crucial to her survival and eventual success in the industry.
