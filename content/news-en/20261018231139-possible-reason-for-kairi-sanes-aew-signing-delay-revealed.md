Questions continue regarding Kairi Sane’s move to AEW, as recent reports point to a specific reason for the delay in finalizing the deal. According to Dave Meltzer in the October 18 edition of the Wrestling Observer, the holdup is not due to failed negotiations but rather the process of obtaining a work visa.

Meltzer explained that the belief is that Sane is waiting for her visa paperwork to be completed so she can work for AEW within the United States. This explains her ability to participate in the Chris Jericho's Rock 'N' Wrestling Rager at Sea, as the event takes place in international waters rather than on U.S. soil.

> "The belief is Kairi is waiting for her visa to come in regarding AEW. She can work the Jericho Cruise because that will be in the waters rather than U.S. soil."

Despite ongoing mutual interest, Sane’s future with AEW remains pending until the visa issue is resolved and a contract is officially signed. Until then, fans must wait to see her final destination.
