Charli Evans, a prominent figure in the Australian independent wrestling scene, has officially relocated to the United States and is now based in St. Louis. While Evans had previously operated out of her home country, she maintained a consistent presence on the independent circuits in both the UK and the United States despite the travel demands. In a recent social media update, Evans shared the news of her move and expressed her commitment to her career. "I had to swallow the hard truth that I wasn't going to be fed staying in Australia. And if I did, that hunger would turn into regret and it would eat me alive. I made phone calls. I booked flights. I consulted tarot cards on nights I couldn't stop crying to ask them if what I was doing was the right choice. I had to be scared and take chances and believe in myself because I refused to let making it happen be something I just said. All to find a way to get to the table I deserve to sit at. So if you've been wondering where I have been, I have been starving. Luckily, the next course has just been served. Visa granted. I am here. I am here to be where the best wrestle. I am here to make it happen. Welcome to the United States of Chev's country."
