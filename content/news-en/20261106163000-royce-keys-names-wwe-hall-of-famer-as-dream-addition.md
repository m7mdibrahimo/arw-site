Royce Keys revealed his desire to add WWE Hall of Famer Mark Henry to his 946 faction. Keys explained during an interview with The Sportster that Henry would be the ideal addition due to his strength and experience.

> "It's got to be my wrestling dad. The world knows he's my wrestling dad and that would be Mark Henry. You know, you got someone with a title, and it was proven that he's the 'World's Strongest Man'. Like why wouldn't you have 'The World's Strongest Man' by your side, you know?"

The 946 group has been feuding with The Bloodline, recently adding Jaida Parker and Carmelo Hayes as they head toward a potential Survivor Series matchup.
