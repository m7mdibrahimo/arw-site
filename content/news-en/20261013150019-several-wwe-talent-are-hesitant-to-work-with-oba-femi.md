According to a recent backstage report from a Self Made Session, several WWE talent are hesitant to work with Oba Femi. Self Made Pro noted that the hesitation has nothing to do with Oba personally, but rather with concerns about dropping down the card after the feud concludes. He explained that wrestlers worry they do not gain much when helping build the next major star. Self Made Pro also claimed that Gunther is another wrestler whom some talent are reluctant to work with for similar reasons.
