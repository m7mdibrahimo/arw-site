Satoshi Kojima faces a unique challenge for the upcoming NJPW/Taichi co-presented show on October 17th. Taichi has officially announced that if Kojima can successfully drop to the target weight of 100kg, he will face IWGP Junior Heavyweight Champion YOH in a non-title match. The stakes are high for Kojima, as a victory over YOH would earn him a future opportunity at the title.

Taichi's event, which is being held at his former middle school, features a diverse card. The lineup includes a Falls Count Anywhere match on school grounds between Toru Yano and El Desperado against Unbound Co. (Yuto-Ice and Gedo), as well as a DDT Universal Championship match between Ryusuke Taguchi and Dick Togo. NJPW noted that Taichi and Kojima share a complicated history, having previously teamed up during the World Tag League. Taichi is now offering his former tag partner a chance to potentially pivot his career by competing in the junior heavyweight division.
