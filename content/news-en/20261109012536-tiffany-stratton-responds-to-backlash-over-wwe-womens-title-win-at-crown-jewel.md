Tiffany Stratton is embracing the negative reaction following her WWE Women’s Championship win at Crown Jewel. After cashing in her Money in the Bank contract on Chelsea Green, fans expressed frustration, with many feeling the outcome was predictable. Stratton took to her Instagram Story to address the criticism, posting a photo of herself with the message, "Tiffy 2 X Hoes Mad," accompanied by the song "Hoes Mad." 

Data from WWE’s official YouTube channel shows the intensity of the fan response, with the upload of the cash-in receiving approximately 28,464 dislikes compared to 9,004 likes. The controversy stems from Stratton cashing in on Chelsea Green immediately after Green had defeated Liv Morgan to win the Women's Crown Jewel Championship. Stratton initially appeared to be helping Green before turning on her, hitting the "Prettiest Moonsault Ever" to secure the championship in Riyadh. Despite the backlash, Stratton appears focused on her reign rather than the criticism.
