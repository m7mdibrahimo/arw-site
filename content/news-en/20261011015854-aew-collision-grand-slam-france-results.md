All Elite Wrestling presented AEW Collision Grand Slam France on October 10 from the Adidas Arena in Paris, France. According to Fightful, the broadcast aired across TNT, HBO Max, and MyAEW.

The Death Riders def. The Conglomeration

Chris Jericho appeared live to promote Collision emanating from the Jericho Cruise in November. Nick Wayne defeated Chris Jericho in singles action. A vignette aired for Konosuke Takeshita expressing his desire to compete in Clockwork Carnage, while Ricochet also stated his wish to enter the tournament.

Mike Bailey def. Lee Moriarty

Swerve Strickland def. Carlie Bravo

Jon Moxley stated that it is the Death Riders versus the world. Marina Shafir added that the group is waging a war on the love of professional wrestling.

Andrade El Idolo def. Michael Oku

Tommaso Ciampa confronted Andrade El Idolo after the championship match. Harley Cameron and Kris Statlander won a number one contenders match against Divine Dominion and Sisters of Sin. The Young Bucks defeated The Dogs to retain their titles, followed by a post-match attack from Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher.
