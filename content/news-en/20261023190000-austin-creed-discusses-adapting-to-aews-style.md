Austin Creed has expressed complete confidence in his and Kofi's ability to quickly adjust to AEW following a 16-year run in WWE, according to Wrestling Inc. During an interview, Creed referenced his past tenure in TNA before joining WWE in 2010, noting that his roots in the X-Division provide him with the flexibility to handle any new work environment.

> "From my point of view, your boy was born in the [TNA] X-Division. So, I mean, this ain't no thing. We can switch it up as much as we want to," said Creed.

> "I feel like Kofi and I are very much chameleons in that aspect. As soon as you drop us in an environment, we see what needs to be done and we are able to adapt fairly quickly."

Kofi added that they can efficiently fulfill any requested role, expressing excitement over the diverse opportunities outside the ring that AEW provides following a strong start featuring a Trios Championship reign before losing it at WrestleDream.
