Ken Anderson, known to fans as Mr. Kennedy, has officially returned to WWE after nearly 17 years. According to Ringside News, he has signed a WWE Legends deal with the company that released him in 2009.

Anderson debuted in 2005 and became popular for his bold personality and unique ring introductions. He won the United States Championship in 2006 and the Money in the Bank briefcase at WrestleMania 23 in 2007, but an injury scare led WWE to have him lose the briefcase to Edge, and concerns about his in-ring safety played a major role in his release on May 29, 2009. Following his release, he found success in TNA Wrestling, where he became a two-time TNA World Heavyweight Champion.

Legends contracts allow WWE to feature former stars in merchandise, video games, and other projects. The agreement does not currently include plans for Anderson to return to television or compete in the ring.
