Pro Wrestling EVE held its EVE 157: Elite Encounters event on November 8 at The Hanger in Wolverhampton, England. The show was broadcast on MyAEW and featured several championship changes.

Full results from the event are as follows:

Chantal Jordan def. Stephanie Maze
Session Moth Martina, Nyla Rose, Nightshade, Lana Austin & JGU def. Rhio, Alexxis Falcon, Skye Smitson, Molly Spartan & Violet Nyte in a Riot of Violence 10-women tag team match
Maika & HANAKO def. Anita Vaughan & Safire Reed to win the EVE Tag Team Championships
Skye Blue def. Lucy Sky to win the Spirit of EVE Championship in a deathmatch
Marina Shafir def. Miyu Yamashita to win the EVE International Championship
Kay Lee Ray def. Kris Statlander to win the EVE Championship
