ROH aired the latest episode of ROH TV on November 19, available on ROH HonorClub. In the opening match, AR Fox defeated Tony Nese. Following the match, Maya World discussed Athena's attempts to recruit Hyan. Hyan interrupted, stating she knows where her home is and that it is alongside Maya.

In title action, Action Andretti successfully defended the ROH Television Championship against Soleil. Elsewhere, Emi Sakura defeated Janai Kai, while Lee Moriarty addressed his history with AR Fox. The Outrunners (Turbo Floyd and Truth Magnum) teamed with Dalton Castle to defeat the trio of Troy Hollywood, Stunt Marshall, and Darian Bengston. Preston Vance picked up a win over Jacked Jameson.

Don Callis announced that Rocky Romero and Volador Jr. are set to challenge for the ROH Tag Team Titles. In a multi-man tag match, the Grizzled Young Veterans (James Drake and Zack Gibson) teamed with The Kingdom (Matt Taven and Mike Bennett) to defeat the team of Jordan Oliver and Alec Price, alongside Scorpio Sky and Matt Sydal. Gravity defeated Alex Zayne, and in the final match of the night, Hyan and Maya World defeated Billie Starkz and Diamante.
