Roman Reigns recently spoke about the advice he shares with younger WWE stars, drawing a parallel to his work in the upcoming movie Street Fighter. Reigns discussed how signature moves become a key part of the show and how wrestlers must make those signatures integral to the program so fans want to see them. The 16-year veteran is having a successful year after winning the 2026 Royal Rumble and defeating CM Punk for the World Heavyweight Championship at WrestleMania 42, followed by successful title defenses against Jacob Fatu, LA Knight, Penta, and Seth Rollins.
