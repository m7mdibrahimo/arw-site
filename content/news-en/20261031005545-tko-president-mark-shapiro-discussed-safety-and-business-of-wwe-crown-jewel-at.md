On Wednesday, TKO held a town hall meeting led by TKO President Mark Shapiro to address employees. A major topic of discussion was WWE Crown Jewel, which is scheduled to take place in Riyadh on Saturday, November 7. Safety concerns have arisen in Riyadh following an airport attack earlier this month that resulted in 13 deaths, prompting the US Embassy to issue multiple travel warnings against visiting Saudi Arabia.

Speaking at the town hall, Shapiro noted that shareholders are closely monitoring the situation as TKO aims to meet its financial targets for 2026. He acknowledged that while they have successfully avoided event cancellations so far, unlike other global sports businesses, there are no guarantees. He stated that if the event had to be canceled, it would have a very negative impact on their financial year.

Shapiro also addressed the safety situation directly, emphasizing that human safety comes first. He stated that if there is danger or further escalation of conflicts in the region, they will not hesitate to cancel the events, regardless of the budget. He concluded by noting that TKO has upcoming events in the Middle East and will never put anyone in harm's way.
