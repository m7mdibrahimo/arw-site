WWE NXT will take place on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, at the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida. The show airs live at 8 pm ET on the CW Network in the United States and streams internationally on Netflix.

The announced match lineup features:

NXT Tag Team Championship: Vanity Project (Brad Baylor & Ricky Smokes) (c) vs. Noam Dar & Romeo Moreno

E.K. Prosper vs. Kam Hendrix

Wren Sinclair vs. Izzi Dame

Lizzy Rain vs. Chantel Monroe
