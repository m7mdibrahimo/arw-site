During an interview on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Sami Zayn reflected on his career and credited Canadian wrestler Sexxxy Eddy with providing the biggest break of his career. Zayn explained that back in his early years, getting booked in the United States had nothing to do with him directly, but rather happened because Eddy traveled to the US, bled profusely during a match, and captured everyone's imagination. That memorable performance led to US bookings for Canadian wrestlers like Zayn. Zayn added that he cannot imagine his career without The Bloodline, because everything in WWE is a domino effect.
