Kyle Fletcher reflected on his match for the AEW World Championship at AEW All Out 2025, where he lost to Hangman Page. As he prepares to challenge Will Ospreay for the same title at AEW Full Gear 2026, Fletcher recalled how devastating that previous defeat was for his mindset.

Speaking to TMZ Sports, Fletcher shared how deeply the loss affected him after coming so close to realizing his lifelong dream. He noted that the experience left him questioning his career path and his abilities as a competitor.

> "I can't even put into words how shattering it was to lose that match against Hangman last year. You know, you dream about winning the world title your entire life, and you get so close and you can taste it and you can visualize it and it's right in your fingertips and then you fail, and then you literally- I felt heartbroken. I was shattered in every different way, and I was questioning everything, questioning everything I'd done, everything I'd been through up till that point, if it was worth it, if I was ever going to be good enough. So I think the biggest thing this time is that I remember how that feels and I'm going to do anything and everything I can to never feel like that again."

Fletcher explained that remembering the pain of that defeat is driving his preparation for his upcoming title opportunity, as he is determined to avoid feeling that way again.
