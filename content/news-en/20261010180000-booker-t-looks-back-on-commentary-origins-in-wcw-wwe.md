WWE Hall of Famer Booker T recently discussed his journey into commentary during an appearance on "Insight with Chris Van Vliet." He recalled starting out in WCW by entertaining wrestlers in the locker room, a habit that eventually led to an official television role in WWE thanks to the support of Michael Cole. Booker noted that while working under Vince McMahon was restrictive, his experience in WWE NXT under Shawn Michaels allowed him to truly be himself and focus on helping rising talent.

> "This thing goes back to WCW"

> "I love Michael Cole, he's been my biggest supporter. I wouldn't be doing commentary if it wasn't for Michael Cole"

> "Shawn goes, 'I don't know what you said, but I liked it!' So I was like, 'Oh man, I love this!"'
