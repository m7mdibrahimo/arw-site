During a recent appearance on a podcast, Brian Cage recalled his AEW debut match at AEW Double or Nothing 2020 against Darby Allin. Cage revealed that he refused to perform a dangerous stunt proposed by Allin involving a ladder.

"The only thing I've ever said no to Darby is actually my debut in AEW. He wanted me to duct tape his legs around a ladder, and then duct tape his arms around a ladder, and then pick the ladder up over my head and throw it to the outside," Cage explained. He noted that the spot reminded him of a famous WWE moment where Rey Mysterio suffered a concussion, and despite Allin's insistence that he would be fine, Cage refused to risk it.
