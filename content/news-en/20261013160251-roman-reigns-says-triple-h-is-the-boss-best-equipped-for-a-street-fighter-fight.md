Roman Reigns believes Triple H is the boss he has worked under who is best suited for a Street Fighter-style battle. With the Street Fighter movie set for release later this week, Reigns is starring in the film as the character Akuma. During a recent interview with ExtraTV, Reigns was asked which of his former or current bosses would perform best in a Street Fighter scenario.

> I don't even know. That's a tough one there. Yeah, yeah, yeah. That's a tough one there. Well, I'll go with Triple H right now. I think, you know, there's a bunch of good bosses that I've had over the years. But he probably is the most equipped physically to handle a Street Fighter type of fight.

In the same interview, Reigns also discussed how video games have served as a healthy alternative to the extracurricular activities that wrestlers frequently engaged in during past eras.
