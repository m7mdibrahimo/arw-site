During a recent appearance on Planet Tyrus, Eric Bischoff discussed the immense pressure wrestlers faced in the past to maintain their personas outside of the ring. Bischoff explained that Hulk Hogan often lost sight of who Terry Bollea was beneath the gimmick because he lived his character in public constantly. Tyrus also weighed in, reflecting on the controversies throughout Hogan's career and emphasizing that the late veteran always owned his mistakes and apologized sincerely rather than shifting the blame.
