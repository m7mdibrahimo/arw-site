Jeff Cobb is finally speaking out about the plans that never came to fruition during his time with WWE. In a recent interview on the INSIGHT podcast with Chris Van Vliet, Cobb reflected on his tenure with the company, noting that he had high expectations when he signed. Cobb confirmed that WWE discussed several creative directions for him before he arrived, but many of those ideas were never executed. "Uh, well, I try not to say I don't want to just like say anything like I'm trying to bury anybody or anything like that. So, I mean, there's just a couple things. Oh, we're going to do this with you. We're going to do this with you. All right, cool. But then it just didn't pan out."

Cobb arrived in WWE in May 2025 at WWE Backlash, joining Solo Sikoa's faction under the name JC Mateo. While he was initially presented as a dominant force, his presence faded before he was released in May 2026. Dave Meltzer reported in the May 8, 2026, Wrestling Observer Newsletter that WWE had originally considered a significant singles push for him, though Cobb did not explicitly confirm those were the exact plans he was referencing. Cobb expressed understanding regarding the volatile nature of the business, citing injuries and roster changes as common factors that force creative shifts. "And you know, I can't can't complain too much. I mean, I'm not a writer. I'm not It's not my company. And and you know, things happen where like some people get released, some people got from injuries, some people get hurt and changes up the plans. I get it."

Despite the frustrations, Cobb is moving forward and has returned to NJPW. He noted that he is no longer dwelling on the decisions that led to his departure, stating, "So, it's it's neither here nor there anymore."
