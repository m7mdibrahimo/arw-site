Drew McIntyre revealed that his authentic approach to promos has occasionally caused friction backstage. During an appearance on the Soder podcast, McIntyre explained that some colleagues took his television and social media remarks personally, failing to distinguish between his character and his real-life personality. McIntyre stated that after returning to WWE, he decided to stop playing a character and instead mirror his natural, unfiltered Scottish demeanor on screen. He noted that he often jokes around in real life, and he chose to bring that same energy to his work.

> "The past few years, when I was outside the company, I really worked on the microphone stuff. I came back, found my voice, finally found my character—which is basically me. There’s a lot of truth in there." McIntyre added that the confusion often stems from his habit of trash-talking.

> "And I really started letting people have it to the point they were forgetting they’re playing a character. They’re like, ‘You said that about me.’ No, I did. I said that about your character on Twitter. Don’t be such a loser. It’s not you. It’s the character I’m talking to." While these blurred lines have led to awkward backstage moments, McIntyre believes this commitment to realism is why his segments have become must-see television.
