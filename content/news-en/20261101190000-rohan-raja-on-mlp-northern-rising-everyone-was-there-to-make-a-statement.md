Rohan Raja discussed the importance of Maple Leaf Pro and its journey since launching in October 2024. Following a media rights deal in March that brought the Mayhem show to TSN2 in July, Raja noted the massive impact of having a dedicated Canadian promotion. Speaking on Fightful Overbooked's In The Weeds podcast, he recalled the immediate backstage energy of the very first show and the excitement of securing a major TV home.

Reflecting on the recent MLP Northern Rising event at Maple Leaf Gardens, Raja shared an emotional moment involving Adam Copeland. He stated, "This guy has done everything in his career and he's stepped in and he has that immediate emotional reaction." Raja added that everyone from the pre-show to the main event delivered a strong performance.
