WWE NXT star Osiris Griffin, who performs alongside Cutler James in DarkState, encountered an unfortunate situation outside the ring when he discovered severe damage to his car ahead of NXT Tuesday.

Griffin revealed the incident on X by sharing a photo displaying major destruction to the front end of the vehicle, though he did not name the person responsible. He approached the situation with humor by referencing his faction's catchphrase while he and his partner continue making waves in the WWE tag team division.
