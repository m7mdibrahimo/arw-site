TNA Wrestling is facing more contract decisions as Xia Brookside's deal approaches its expiration before the end of 2026, according to Ringside News. The promotion is working to prevent her departure.

According to Fightful Select, Brookside's agreement runs out at the end of December. TNA has already presented a new proposal, though it remains unconfirmed if she has accepted it. Brookside joined TNA in 2024 following her time with WWE NXT UK, and the company must secure a new agreement to retain her for 2027.

> "Former TNA Knockouts Champion Xia Brookside sees her contract expire at the end of the year. We’ve heard that there have been contract talks, and TNA has communicated an offer in hopes that Brookside stays."

Brookside is not the only talent facing an expiring deal, as Trey Miguel revealed his contract also concludes before year's end after signing two six-month deals in 2026. Mustafa Ali and Rich Swann also face expiring contracts on December 31, alongside AJ Francis's situation. TNA President Carlos Silva previously addressed these matters in a backstage meeting, noting several deals the company must resolve.
