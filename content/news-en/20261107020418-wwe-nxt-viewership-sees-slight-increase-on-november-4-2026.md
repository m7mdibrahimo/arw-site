According to PWInsider via Ringside News, the November 4, 2026 episode of WWE NXT on The CW Network saw a slight increase in viewership compared to the previous week, drawing 552,000 viewers. 

The show secured a 0.13 rating in the key 18–49 demographic, improving upon the prior week's figures of 520,000 viewers and a 0.09 rating. 

These numbers reflect exclusively linear TV viewership on The CW Network and exclude streaming or digital platforms as WWE builds toward its next major live event.
