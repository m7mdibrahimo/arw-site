Zilla Fatu and Charlie Dempsey will spin the wheel at the upcoming NXT Halloween Havoc Premium Live Event on October 31st to determine their match type. Fatu wanted to spin the wheel on the October 27th episode of NXT, but he was distracted by Arianna Grace and then ambushed by BirthRight members Charlie Dempsey, Uriah Connors, Lexis King, and Channing 'Stacks' Lorenzo. During the attack, Dempsey struck Fatu's previously injured hand with a shovel. Their match remains official for the event, and the stipulation will be decided by spinning the wheel live at the show. The updated match card for NXT Halloween Havoc features Kelani Jordan defending the NXT Women's Championship against Wren Sinclair, Grayson Waller defending the NXT Championship against Tony D'Angelo, ZARIA facing Izzi Dame, Shiloh Hill taking on NARAKU, and Tavion Heights battling Myles Borne in a Weapons Wild Match, alongside the wheel-spin bout between Fatu and Dempsey.
