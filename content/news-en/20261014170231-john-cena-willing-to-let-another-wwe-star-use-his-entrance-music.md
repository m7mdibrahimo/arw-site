John Cena recently reflected on his retirement from in-ring competition, admitting that he thinks about wrestling every day and that his body could no longer handle the physical demands. Speaking in an interview, Cena praised WWE's current talent roster and discussed his iconic entrance music, joking that it was time for him to retire because the song is a classic. When asked if he would allow a younger WWE star to use his entrance theme now that he is retired, Cena stated that he has no issue with it and considers the music available for anyone to take.
