WWE presented a new episode of WWE EVOLVE on October 21, broadcast on Tubi from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida.

During the broadcast, Aaron Rourke declared that he has never backed down from a challenge. Additionally, a vignette aired highlighting Elijah Holyfield. Following her victory, Skylar Raye called out Nikkita Lyons. Later in the show, Alix Brooks and Kale Dixon claimed that their issues with Rourke would conclude by the end of the night.

Full results from the event are listed below:

Chazz "Starboy" Hall and Cappuccino Jones def. The MOG Squad (Jacari Ball and Santi Rivera)
Skylar Raye def. Sloane Jacobs
Kale Dixon def. Aaron Rourke
