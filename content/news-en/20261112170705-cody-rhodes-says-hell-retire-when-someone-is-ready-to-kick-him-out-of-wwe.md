Cody Rhodes says he has no immediate plans to leave WWE, but he already knows what will lead to his retirement. During an interview with Adam Glyn, Rhodes explained that he intends to keep wrestling until a newcomer is ready to take his place. He noted that he still feels great physically, despite the heavy schedule and the physical toll of facing heavyweights like Randy Orton. Rhodes also pointed to WWE's massive success and its television deals with ESPN and Netflix as major reasons to stay in the business. Rather than setting a specific date, Rhodes is waiting for the right person to arrive. While he previously mentioned having six or seven years left and recently signed a new contract to finish his career with WWE, he made it clear that the next generation will ultimately decide when it is time for him to step aside.
