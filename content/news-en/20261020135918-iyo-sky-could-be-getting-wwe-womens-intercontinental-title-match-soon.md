IYO SKY could soon challenge for the WWE Women's Intercontinental Championship after pinning Raquel Rodriguez on the October 19 episode of WWE RAW. Speaking on Wrestling Observer Radio, Dave Meltzer discussed SKY teaming with Chelsea Green to defeat Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez, noting that SKY pinned the champion cleanly with a cradle. Meltzer expects this finish to lead directly to a title match within the next few weeks, especially given the existing tension between the two competitors on screen. SKY previously faced Rodriguez in a Queen of the Ring semifinal match in June and has a history with The Judgment Day. While WWE has not officially announced the matchup or a date, Meltzer believes the recent pin gives the company a clear direction for the division.
