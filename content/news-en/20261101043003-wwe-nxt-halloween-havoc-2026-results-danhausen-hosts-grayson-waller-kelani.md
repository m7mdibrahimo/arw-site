Full results for WWE NXT Halloween Havoc on The CW and Netflix on October 31, 2026, live from the Performance Center in Orlando, Florida. Danhausen hosted the show. In the opening matches, Myles Borne defeated Tavion Heights in a Weapons Wild Match, while Izzi Dame secured a victory against Zaria. Shiloh Hill also defeated NARAKU. Backstage, Sean Legacy startled Tristan Angels, and NARAKU and Shiloh Hill brawled before misting each other. Noam Dar, Romeo Moreno, and Hank and Tank previewed the Dusty Cup Finals, and Robert Stone announced that Vanity Project will face Rey Fenix, Dragon Lee, and Chad Gable. Cruz Montana and Dion Lennox had a face-to-face encounter ahead of their upcoming Unsanctioned Match. In the special stipulation match, Zilla Fatu defeated Charlie Dempsey, who was accompanied by Lexis King, Arianna Grace, Uriah Connors, and Channing “Stacks” Lorenzo. Kelani Jordan successfully defended the NXT Women's Championship against Wren Sinclair, after which Robert Stone informed the champion that she will defend her title against a mystery opponent this Wednesday. Danhausen expressed his desire to clone Kam Hendrix, only for EK Prosper to launch an attack on Hendrix. In the main event, Grayson Waller successfully defended the NXT Championship against Tony D’Angelo. Following the match, CM Punk appeared to confront Waller, announcing that he will challenge for the NXT Title this coming Wednesday.
