The November 7, 2026, episode of AEW Collision on TNT experienced a notable rise in viewership and key demographic figures compared to the previous week.

According to Programming Insider, the broadcast drew 376,000 viewers and a 0.08 rating in the P18–49 demographic. This marks a clear improvement over the October 31 episode, which recorded 302,000 viewers and a 0.06 rating, reflecting an increase of 74,000 viewers and 0.02 in the key demographic.

Year-over-year, the show also saw growth compared to the November 8, 2025, episode, which had 289,000 viewers and a 0.06 rating. This represents a 30.1 percent increase in total viewership and a 33.3 percent rise in the demographic. These figures cover linear TNT broadcasts only.
