Becky Lynch and Seth Rollins have taken their AMO Coffee brand on the road with a newly customized bus known as the AMObile. Lynch recently shared a video on Instagram showcasing the bright yellow vehicle, which features a custom exterior, a QR code, and a complete coffee setup inside. The interior is equipped with a coffee machine, grinder, pour-over equipment, and plenty of extra product storage, along with a washing machine near the back. Lynch even joked that the bright design is so loud it could be spotted from space and encouraged fans to keep an eye out for the bus on the road.
