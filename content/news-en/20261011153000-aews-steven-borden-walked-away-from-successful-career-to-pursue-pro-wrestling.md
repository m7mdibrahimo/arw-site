AEW star Steven Borden revealed that he left a successful career managing a sports technology company to pursue professional wrestling. Borden admitted that balancing a demanding 80-to-90-hour workweek with training became impossible, leading him to walk away from his previous life to follow in the footsteps of his father, Sting. After making his debut in October 2025 and receiving guidance from Darby Allin, Borden quickly transitioned to the AEW main roster. However, his promising career recently faced a setback when he suffered a concussion at AEW All Out 2026 during a tag team match alongside Allin against Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight. Fortunately, Borden has fully recovered, though there is currently no word on when he will return to the ring.
