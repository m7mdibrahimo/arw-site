Conan Lycan has adopted a new ring name in WWE, where he is now performing as Kato Sykes within the company's development program. Sykes began his professional wrestling career in 2018 and competed for several promotions, including GCW, ACTION Wrestling, AEW, TNA, and Black Label Pro, before joining WWE.

He previously competed in dark matches against Marcus Mathers and Brooks Jensen prior to NXT broadcasts on The CW Network. Under his WWE I.D. deal, he was previously known as Mariano, following reports in March 2026 regarding the promotion's interest in signing him.
