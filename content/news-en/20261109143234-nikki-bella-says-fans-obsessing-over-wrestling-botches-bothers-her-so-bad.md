Nikki Bella is tired of wrestling fans obsessing over minor mistakes and labeling them as botches. During a recent appearance on The Nikki & Brie Show, Cathy Kelley brought up how critical fans can be over the tiniest missteps during live WWE television. Nikki agreed, explaining that mistakes happen to everyone in everyday life and that performers should not be held to an impossible standard of perfection. Kelley added that the intense pressure can get inside a performer's head, but Nikki simply wants fans to appreciate the incredible difficulty of performing live without magnifying every single human error.
