WWE has confirmed that the November 4th edition of NXT will air live on a Wednesday. This show is scheduled to go head-to-head with AEW Blood & Guts at the VyStar Veterans Memorial Arena in Jacksonville, Florida. The event will feature the finals of the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic tournament. Multi-time Undisputed WWE Champion Cody Rhodes will make an appearance to present the trophy to the winning team.
