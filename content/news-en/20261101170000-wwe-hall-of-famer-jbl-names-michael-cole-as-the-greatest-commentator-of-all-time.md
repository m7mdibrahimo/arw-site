During an appearance on "Something to Wrestle," WWE Hall of Famer JBL named his former traveling partner, Michael Cole, as the greatest commentator in professional wrestling history.

JBL highlighted Cole's exceptional career longevity, noting that he has called more matches and WrestleManias than anyone else. While praising legendary voices like Jim Ross and Jerry Lawler, JBL stated that Cole edges them out due to his sustained success and durability.

JBL also pointed out that Cole achieved long-term stability under Vince McMahon while many other commentators struggled. He added that Cole's work as a heel on commentary showcased his incredible skill and ability to connect with audiences.
