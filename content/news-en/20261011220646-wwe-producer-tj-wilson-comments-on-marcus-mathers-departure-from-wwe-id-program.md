WWE producer TJ Wilson has praised Marcus Mathers following his recent departure from the WWE ID program. Mathers confirmed his exit from the initiative earlier this week, opening a new chapter in his career. During his time in the program, Mathers made a strong impression, wrestling on the EVOLVE brand and participating in the tournament for the Men's WWE ID Championship. He also trained at The Dungeon, a training facility operated by Wilson and Natalya. In response to the news, Wilson shared high praise on social media, writing that if he owned a wrestling company, Mathers would be his first hire, noting his work ethic and passion for the sport.
