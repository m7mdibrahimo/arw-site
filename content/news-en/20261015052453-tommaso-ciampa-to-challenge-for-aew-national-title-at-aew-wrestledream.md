AEW has announced a major championship match for the upcoming AEW WrestleDream event. During this week's episode of AEW Collision, it was confirmed that Tommaso Ciampa will face champion Andrade El Idolo for the AEW National Championship.

This announcement followed a tag team match where Tommaso Ciampa and Samoa Joe defeated Andrade El Idolo and Will Ospreay. This marks Ciampa's second recent title opportunity, following his unsuccessful challenge against Kazuchika Okada for the AEW International Championship at AEW All Out.
