Max Caster has commented on his future in professional wrestling following his recent situation at TNA Wrestling's Bound for Glory pay-per-view. Caster, who spent five years with All Elite Wrestling and held the AEW World Tag Team and World Trios Championships, spoke about the possibility of returning to AEW during a virtual signing for K & S WrestleFest. When asked about a potential comeback, Caster stated, 'Never say never,' but added that he is currently declining all wrestling bookings. He explained that he agreed to the virtual signing months ago and intends to honor it out of respect for Brian Myers, noting that this appearance will be his last wrestling-related event for an undefined period.
