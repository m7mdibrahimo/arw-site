Don Callis believes Kyle Fletcher will ultimately eclipse every prominent star he has ever managed, including Kenny Omega, Konosuke Takeshita, Will Ospreay, and Chris Jericho.

Speaking to Poisonrana ahead of Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling's Northern Rising event, Callis highlighted Fletcher as his crowning achievement.

> "Everything I've done wite the Don Callis Family, getting Kyle Fletcher ready for becoming what I believe he's going to be the greatest wrestler in the history of the sport."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6wSjwd_2Pgg

Fletcher has spent nearly three years as an essential member of the Don Callis Family in AEW. During that run, he captured the AEW TNT Championship twice, the AEW International Championship, the AEW World Trios Championship, and the ROH World Television Championship.

Fletcher and stablemate Kevin Knight are scheduled to challenge The Young Bucks for the AEW World Tag Team Championship at AEW WrestleDream 2026 on October 17. Meanwhile, Fletcher also remains focused on the AEW International Championship he lost to Kazuchika Okada at AEW All In London 2026 after underhanded tactics.
