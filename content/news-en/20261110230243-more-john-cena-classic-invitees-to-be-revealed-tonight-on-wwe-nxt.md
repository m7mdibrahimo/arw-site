According to a tweet from WWE, more participants for the John Cena Classic will be announced on tonight's episode of "NXT". Cena expressed his excitement, stating, "I'm excited to invite more Superstars from @WWENXT to the John Cena Classic! The future is here and now, Let's C who enters!" Last week on "Raw", Cena revealed that the tournament will take place during a special episode of "Raw" on December 14, marking one year since his retirement from in-ring competition. The event will be broadcast live from Disney World in Orlando. Cody Rhodes and Zilla Fatu have already been announced as participants. In an interview with The TakeDown on Sports Illustrated, Cena noted that the tournament will feature separate brackets for men and women, with winners determined by a fan vote on Club WWE, similar to the format of "American Idol". Tournament winners will be awarded a special John Cena Classic title belt.
