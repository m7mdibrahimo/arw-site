CMLL and NJPW will host their joint CMLL x NJPW Lucha Kingdom event on Monday, November 16, 2026, live from Arena Mexico in Mexico City. The event is scheduled to begin at 6:00 pm ET and will stream live via the CMLL YouTube Channel for Fan Leyenda members and as a pay-per-view on MyAEW.

The lineup features multiple high-profile title matches and cross-promotional bouts. Hechicero will defend his CMLL World Heavyweight Championship against Zack Sabre Jr., while Syuri puts her IWGP Women's Championship on the line against Kira. Additionally, Los Hermanos Chávez (Ángel de Oro and Niebla Roja) defend the NJPW STRONG Openweight Tag Team Championship in a Three-Way Tag Team match against United Empire (Francesco Akira and Templario) and Unbound Co. (Taiji Ishimori and Titán).

Other notable matches on the card include Místico and Último Guerrero teaming up to face Yota Tsuji and Hirooki Goto, Konosuke Takeshita taking on Bárbaro Cavernario, and Soberano Jr. facing Shota Umino. HOUSE OF TORTURE (DOUKI and SHO) will also compete against Guerrero Maya Jr. and Xelhua, alongside a multi-man tag team clash and a six-man tag team match.
