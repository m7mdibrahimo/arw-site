AEW Collision viewership for October 3 reached 415,000 viewers on TNT, according to Wrestlenomics. This represents an increase from the 337,000 viewers the show recorded on September 19, which was the previous Saturday airing.

The episode achieved a 0.08 rating in the 18 to 49 demographic, rising from the 0.04 rating recorded during the last Saturday broadcast. The program ranked 8th on cable for the day. While the show also aired on HBO Max, those specific viewership figures are unavailable.
