Rey Mysterio reflected on accidentally breaking Undertaker's nose during an episode of SmackDown in May 2010. The injury occurred when Mysterio overshot a West Coast Pop and landed directly on his opponent's face.

While speaking with Adrian Hernandez on Unlikely, Mysterio admitted that he was terrified following the accident.

> "I actually did it off the West Coast Pop. Yeah, I was scared for my life after that. Yeah, trust me."

Elsewhere in the interview, Mysterio discussed his 2023 match against Roman Reigns.
