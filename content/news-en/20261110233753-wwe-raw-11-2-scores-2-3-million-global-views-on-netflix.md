WWE Raw viewership numbers for the November 2 episode are in. According to Tudum, the show drew 2.3 million global views on Netflix, ranking fifth globally and fifth in the United States. For comparison, the episode on October 26 also drew 2.3 million global views, though it ranked seventh globally and eighth in the U.S. at that time. Among the most-viewed WWE Raw clips on YouTube, Cruz Montana leveling Roman Reigns and joining The 946 led the way with 1.8 million views. Other popular segments included John Cena announcing the John Cena Classic, Sami Zayn hitting Roman Reigns with a Helluva Kick, Sami Zayn firing back at Roman Reigns, and Randy Orton sending a message to Cody Rhodes.
