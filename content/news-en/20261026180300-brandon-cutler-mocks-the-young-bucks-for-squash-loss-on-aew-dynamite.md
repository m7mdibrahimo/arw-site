Brandon Cutler poked fun at The Young Bucks following their first career squash match loss on last week's AEW Dynamite. According to Fightful, the AEW World Tag Team Champions, Matt and Nick Jackson, faced Mark Davis and Jake Doyle shortly after defending their titles against Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight at AEW WrestleDream.

The champions were unable to mount any offense as Davis and Doyle secured a quick victory. During the latest episode of Being The Elite, the brothers expressed confusion regarding the defeat. Brandon Cutler, who is familiar with such losses, joined them to offer his perspective.

> "Coming from a lot of experience, what you guys just experienced for the very first time in your careers is a squash match."
