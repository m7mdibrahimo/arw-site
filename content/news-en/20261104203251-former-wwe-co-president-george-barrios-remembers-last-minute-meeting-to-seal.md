As controversy continues to surround WWE's working relationship with Saudi Arabia, former WWE co-President George Barrios recently looked back at how the partnership was originally sealed. Speaking on Jeff Jarrett's podcast, Barrios recalled being sent to negotiate the deal in October 2018, shortly after the assassination of Jamal Khashoggi, while Vince McMahon debated backing out of the agreement out of fear that the partnership could ruin the company. After no one else wanted to handle the negotiations in person, Barrios took the trip to Saudi Arabia and described the experience as unlike anything he had ever encountered.

Barrios detailed arriving at a dark compound surrounded by armed guards before entering a room resembling a mancave, where one of the kingdom's senior princes sat in boxer shorts and smoked a hookah. Following a tense initial shouting match over the deal's financial terms, Barrios successfully finalized the agreement with the prince's second-in-command. Looking back, Barrios expressed pride in the decision to move forward, believing that the ongoing partnership has allowed both WWE and Saudi Arabia to thrive under the country's Vision 2030 goals.
