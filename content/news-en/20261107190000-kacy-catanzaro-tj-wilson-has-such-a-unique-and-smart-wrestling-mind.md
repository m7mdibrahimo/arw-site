Kacy Catanzaro recently spoke about her time in WWE and praised TJ Wilson for his incredible wrestling mind. During a new interview with Fightful's Sean Ross Sapp, Catanzaro discussed how working with various coaches backstage shaped her career. She credited Wilson with giving her some of the most memorable realization moments during her matches. Catanzaro stated that she was always inspired by watching him compete and learning from his extensive experience in the ring. Elsewhere in the conversation, she also touched upon a potential future in AEW or ROH alongside Lacey Lane.
