Goldberg’s custom car cover company, HeroCovers, is set to appear on an upcoming episode of Shark Tank on ABC. The business specializes in personalized photo-realistic covers for cars, trucks, boats, and motorcycles, and has previously partnered with brands like Hot Wheels, the NBA, and LASD Motorsports. Goldberg serves as the co-founder of the company. HeroCovers announced on social media that they will pitch their business on the November 4 episode of Shark Tank, which is titled 'Kevin in a Headlock' and scheduled to air at 10 p.m. ET. While Goldberg has not explicitly confirmed his on-screen appearance, he previously mentioned during a June interview that the show was one of his upcoming television projects.
