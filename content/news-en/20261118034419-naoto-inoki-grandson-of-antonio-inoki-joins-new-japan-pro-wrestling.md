Naoto Inoki has officially joined NJPW. The promotion announced that Naoto Inoki, the grandson of NJPW founder Antonio Inoki, is now part of the company. NJPW President Hiroshi Tanahashi welcomed Naoto and posed for a photo with him. A press conference to discuss the signing is scheduled for November 18. Antonio Inoki, who passed away in the fall of 2022, founded New Japan Pro-Wrestling in 1972. The company recently used an A.I. message from Antonio Inoki to announce Wrestle Kingdom 21 and 22.
