Austin Creed and Swerve Strickland are seeking revenge after Bandido attacked Kofi on this week's "AEW Dynamite: Blood & Guts." The New Level has been locked in a heated feud with Bandido and Adam Page for several weeks, which continued despite the latter's victory at WrestleDream. Bandido ambushed Kofi during "Dynamite" before attacking Creed and Strickland as well. An incensed Creed delivered a backstage promo afterward, stating that they were done showing respect and that Bandido would soon face their wrath.

> "Look, ever since we came to AEW, it's been nothing but respect from our end. Bell to bell, in the ring, to find out who is the best. And now, Bandido, who obviously has a problem with Swerve, instead of addressing Swerve first and only, he felt the need to leave the ring, to come in the back and put his hands on Kofi, to put his hands on my brother. So, Bandido, now you and I have a very serious problem. This is no more fun and games. This is no more respect," warned Creed. "Next week, it's Brodido against The New Level, and you are going to realize that our kindness is so much further from weakness than you could ever imagine."

Strickland then handed Creed a chain he had previously used on Adam Page, which Creed accepted. Strickland later posted a photo of the encounter on social media with the caption "More crime." The New Level will face Bandido and Page at the upcoming Full Gear pay-per-view, where the AEW World Trios Championships will be on the line in a Texas Deathmatch.
