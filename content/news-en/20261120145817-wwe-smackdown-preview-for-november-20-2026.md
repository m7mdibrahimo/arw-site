The November 20, 2026, episode of WWE SmackDown continues the build toward the upcoming Survivor Series Premium Live Event. A major focus of tonight's broadcast is the announcement of the final member of Team USA, who will join Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, LA Knight, and Je'Von Evans. They are set to face Team Mexico, which currently consists of Rey Mysterio, Penta, Rey Fenix, and Dragon Lee.

With one spot remaining on each team, fans are anticipating Cody Rhodes' reveal of the fifth American representative. The event takes place at the Ford Center in Evansville, Indiana, beginning at 8 PM ET on the USA Network. International viewers can stream the show on Netflix.
