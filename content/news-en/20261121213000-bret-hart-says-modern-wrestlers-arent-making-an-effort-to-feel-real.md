WWE Hall of Famer Bret Hart recently discussed the current state of professional wrestling during an appearance on the "Johnny I Pro Show." Hart criticized modern performers for not making enough of an effort to make their matches appear authentic compared to older generations.

"I always have a line that I use regularly: the best pro wrestling has to always pretend to be real. And I don't think they're trying hard enough anymore to make it appear real," Hart stated. He recalled watching 1960s wrestling clips featuring a style closer to Greco-Roman wrestling, which he prefers. Although he acknowledged that fans today do not value kayfabe as much, he noted that he and his peers always suspended disbelief. "I was always a fan of good, real wrestling," Hart added. "I love a lot of wrestlers that are working today, but you know, for God's sake, maybe they could put a hold on once in a while?"
