Tavion Heights, who joined WWE in August 2022 after an amateur wrestling career, revealed that his Olympic-style belly-to-belly suplex was a non-negotiable part of his moveset. In a recent interview on "No-Contest Wrestling," Heights explained that the move was his primary finisher in amateur wrestling and he insisted on keeping it for his professional career on WWE NXT. "That was my go-to, the peak move that I mastered in Olympic wrestling," he said. "When I came to WWE I knew what my finish was. That was the one thing that was like non-negotiable."

Heights, whose real name is Tracy G'Angelo Hancock, represented the United States at the 2020 Tokyo Olympics and won a bronze medal at the World Wrestling Championships the following year. He currently competes on WWE NXT and is involved in a feud with his former No Quarter Catch Crew stablemate Myles Borne.
