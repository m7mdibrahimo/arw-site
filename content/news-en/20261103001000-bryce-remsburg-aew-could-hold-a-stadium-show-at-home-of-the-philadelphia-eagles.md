Referee Bryce Remsburg discussed the potential for AEW to host a major event at Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia, the home of the NFL's Philadelphia Eagles. During an appearance on "The Certified Mark Show," Remsburg noted that the venue previously hosted WrestleMania, proving its capability for large-scale events. He added that Philadelphia has remained one of the promotion's strongest markets since its inception.

Although rumors suggest a stadium show may occur in New York City this April, Remsburg believes hosting an event at the Philadelphia stadium remains a possibility in the future.
