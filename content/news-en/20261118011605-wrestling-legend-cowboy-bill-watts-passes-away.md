The wrestling world is mourning the loss of "Cowboy" Bill Watts, who has passed away at the age of 87. Bill Apter shared the news after receiving a text from Watts' son, Erik Watts, which read, "Dear Bill, my father, Cowboy Bill Watts, passed away today. I wanted to let he loved you and had a very special place in his heart. Love you, my friend."

Watts began his legendary career in 1962, competing across the country and eventually reaching All Japan Pro-Wrestling. During his time in the ring, he engaged in a notable feud with Bruno Sammartino over the WWWF Championship. Beyond his in-ring work, Watts became a highly influential booker, running Mid-South Wrestling from 1979 until 1987. He later served as the Executive Vice President of World Championship Wrestling in 1992 and assisted with WWE booking in 1995. Watts is widely credited with pioneering the episodic television style that became a staple of wrestling. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2009. We send our condolences to his family, friends, and fans.
