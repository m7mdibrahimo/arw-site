Samoa Joe recently shared his thoughts on the traditional wrestling rule about protecting finishing moves, stating that the idea does not hold true for every performer. During an interview with SHAK Wrestling, Joe explained that success in the industry depends entirely on the individual rather than a strict formula. While some wrestlers heavily protect their finishers to maximize their impact, others have achieved massive success without doing so. Joe noted that looking at the historical data reveals successful stars using both approaches, making a rigid stance unnecessary. He also has plenty of personal experience with a recognizable finisher, having used the Muscle Buster to win the AEW World Championship at Full Gear 2025 and upon his return to AEW Dynamite in April 2026.
