WWE had no involvement in TNA's decision to fire Max Caster following the controversy surrounding his rap at Bound for Glory, according to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter. Sources within WWE stated that the company played no role in the release, noting that most key decision-makers were unaware of the controversial Riyadh reference until after TNA had already severed ties. TNA President Carlos Silva made the independent decision to cut ties with Caster, who was working on a per-appearance basis without a long-term contract. The rap was neither scripted nor approved by TNA, leading the promotion to issue a public apology and remove related content. While some in AEW believed TNA should have anticipated the situation given Caster's history of freestyle raps, the report confirms that TNA acted entirely on its own accord.
