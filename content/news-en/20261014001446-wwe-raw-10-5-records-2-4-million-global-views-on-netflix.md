WWE Raw on October 5 drew 2.4 million global views on Netflix, according to Tudum. This figure represents a slight decrease from the 2.5 million views recorded on September 28. Despite the drop, the episode ranked seventh globally and fourth in the United States. This marks an improvement in ranking from the previous week's episode, which placed eighth globally and fourth in the United States.

The most-viewed clips from the October 5 episode on YouTube included Roman Reigns recruiting Solo Sikoa for The 946 battle with 2 million views, and Oba Femi brawling with The Vision at 1.7 million views. Other popular segments featured LA Knight and Roman Reigns in a fiery faceoff, Paul Heyman discussing Oba Femi, and Stephanie Vaquer's announcement regarding her title defense against Liv Morgan and Becky Lynch.
