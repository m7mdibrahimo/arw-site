Veteran wrestler Chavo Guerrero recently discussed his time with the short-lived promotion Aro Lucha. Debuting in 2017, the company featured stars like Rey Mysterio, Swerve Strickland, Ricky Saints, and Penta, but ceased operations in 2018 after only four events.

Guerrero, who served as both a producer and wrestler, told Fightful that he views the experience as a personal achievement. He noted that while the project had significant momentum and involved Ron and Don Harris, it ultimately struggled to sustain itself.

> "That's a very short lived thing, something that we thought was maybe going to take off. I believe it was Ron and Don Harris, great dudes, awesome guys that also got into that space. Sometimes things take off or sometimes the money dries up. I don’t really know what happened there, but we were looking to really make an impact on it and it just didn't get off the ground. That’s Hollywood, that happens all the time. It's wrestling as well too, you know. That was something, another little feather in my cap there."
