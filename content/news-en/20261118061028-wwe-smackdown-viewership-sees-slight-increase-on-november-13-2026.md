Audience metrics for the November 13, 2026 episode of WWE SmackDown on USA Network show a slight growth in viewership, according to data from Wrestlenomics. The program reached 1,169,000 viewers and secured a 0.27 rating in the key 18–49 demographic.

By comparison, the November 6, 2026 episode brought in 1,100,000 viewers with a 0.25 rating in that same demographic, marking an addition of 69,000 viewers and a 0.02 rise in the rating. These metrics account strictly for linear television broadcasts on USA Network and exclude digital streaming numbers.
