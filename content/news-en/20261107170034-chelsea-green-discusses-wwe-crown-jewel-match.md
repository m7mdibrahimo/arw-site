Chelsea Green spoke about the significance of her Crown Jewel Championship match against Liv Morgan at WWE Crown Jewel. Speaking to Corey Graves on WWE's Countdown show, Green noted that Morgan was previously an underdog who fought her way to the top and stated that being underestimated by Morgan works to her advantage.

Reflecting on her journey and the upcoming bout, Green shared her thoughts on reaching the pinnacle of her career.

> "I feel like I'm living my dream. I wanted this for so long. I worked for it for so long, and I'm just trying to take every single day and be grateful that this is happening while also working extremely hard. This means everything to me because there have been so many times where in these 13 years I've doubted myself. Now I'm at the top of Mount Everest. I need to prove that I belong there. I need to stay there."
