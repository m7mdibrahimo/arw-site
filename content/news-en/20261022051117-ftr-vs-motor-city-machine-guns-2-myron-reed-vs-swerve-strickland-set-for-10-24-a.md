AEW has announced the lineup for the October 24 episode of AEW Collision. The show will feature the second match in the best-of-five series between FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns. Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin secured their first victory in the series at AEW WrestleDream, with the third match scheduled for AEW Fright Night Dynamite. In the singles division, Swerve Strickland will face Myron Reed as he looks to extend his current winning streak. More matches and segments for the show will be announced in the coming days.
