John Cena recently reiterated his strong support for bringing WrestleMania to London, expressing his belief that the city has earned the right to host the event. Speaking on Off The Menu with Ed Gamble and James Acaster, Cena noted that the upcoming WrestleMania outside of North America serves as proof of concept that international events are possible. He also reflected on his surprise announcement at WWE Money in the Bank 2023, emphasizing London's passionate energy and status as a great travel destination for fans. While WrestleMania 23 is set for Saudi Arabia and officials previously noted no ongoing 2028 talks, Cena remains hopeful about future possibilities in the UK.
