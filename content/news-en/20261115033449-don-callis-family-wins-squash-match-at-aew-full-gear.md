The Don Callis Family had an easy night at AEW Full Gear. During the Tailgate Brawl, the group's members Wardlow, Kevin Knight, and El Clon quickly defeated Jaden Monroe, RJ Wylde, and Jordan Cruz. The match ended after Wardlow hit a powerbomb symphony, followed by a UFO Splash from Knight. This victory helps Knight and Clon bounce back after their recent loss to The Young Bucks and Jack Perry on AEW Collision.
