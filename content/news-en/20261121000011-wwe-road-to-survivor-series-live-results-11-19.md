WWE hosted a live event on November 19 from the Von Braun Center in Huntsville, Alabama. According to Fightful, full results from the show are as follows.

Trick Williams def. Damian Priest
Charlotte Flair def. Jacy Jayne via DQ
Charlotte Flair, Alexa Bliss, & Tatum Paxley def. Fatal Influence (Jacy Jayne, Fallon Henley, & Lainey Reid)
Kevin Owens def. Johnny Gargano (with Candice LaRae)
Oba Femi & Je’von Evans def. The Vision (Bron Breakker & Austin Theory) (with Maxxine Dupri)
Danhausen def. The Miz
The MFTs (Talla Tonga & Tama Tonga) def. Fraxiom (Nathan Frazer & Axiom), The War Raiders (Erik & Ivar), and Bakusai (Shinsuke Nakamura & Kyoki)
Sami Zayn def. Cody Rhodes and Gunther
