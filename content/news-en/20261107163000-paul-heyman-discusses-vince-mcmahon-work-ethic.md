Paul Heyman recently discussed the demanding work ethic and relentless drive of Vince McMahon during an interview on In Depth with Graham Besinger. Heyman highlighted McMahon's extraordinary energy and noted that his intense approach to work was both inspiring and thoroughly exhausting for those around him.

He explained that McMahon's difficult childhood and complex family background heavily fueled his ambition and relentless pursuit of success, adding that McMahon expected everyone to match his frantic pace without taking vacations or missing life milestones. While Heyman expressed admiration for his former boss, former WWE writers have previously described the working environment under McMahon as harsh due to his exceptionally demanding expectations.
