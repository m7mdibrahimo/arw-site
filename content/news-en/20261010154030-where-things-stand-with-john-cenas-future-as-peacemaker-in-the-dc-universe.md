John Cena is not worried about when he might play Peacemaker again, leaving the decision entirely up to James Gunn. Speaking on the Happy Sad Confused podcast, Cena explained that he has not worn the helmet recently but remains ready whenever Gunn calls.

> No. And when I put that helmet on, I told James, I'm like, 'Dude, I will put this helmet on as long as you need me to.' I love working with him. I love the environment he creates. He makes me a better performer, so I love being around him.

Cena acknowledged that Gunn now manages an entire cinematic universe at DC Studios rather than a few isolated characters, meaning major names like Superman and Batman take priority.

> Superman, Batman, Wonder Woman, dog. There's so many valuable IPs. I'm not in the leadership spot, but if I was, I'd be making those plays first. So that's okay.

Looking back at his career, Cena compared the situation to his retirement from professional wrestling, viewing fan demand as a compliment rather than a source of frustration. Rather than stressing over things he cannot control, he prefers to focus on doing good work wherever the next opportunity leads.
