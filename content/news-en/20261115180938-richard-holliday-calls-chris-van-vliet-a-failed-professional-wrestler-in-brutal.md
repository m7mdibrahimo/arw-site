Richard Holliday recently turned a wrestling trivia event hosted by Chris Van Vliet into a personal roast session and teased a potential in-ring match against him. During the Pro-Wrestling Halloween Trivia Jam at Yellow King Brews in Wallingford, Connecticut, Holliday took the stage and mocked Van Vliet for becoming a podcaster while accusing him of inserting his Canadian nose into Holliday's business. Holliday then targeted Van Vliet's past wrestling experience under the name Chris Sharp, bluntly calling him a failed professional wrestler and questioning his involvement in the industry. Although Holliday offered a brief sarcastic compliment about Van Vliet doing a phenomenal job, he immediately followed it by offering himself as a guest to improve the show. After uploading a video of the segment to X, Holliday posted a direct challenge asking his followers who wanted to see him face Van Vliet's former wrestling alter ego.
