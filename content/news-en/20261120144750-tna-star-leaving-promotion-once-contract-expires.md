Rich Swann has officially decided not to renew his TNA contract, confirming he will become a free agent on December 31. After months of speculation about his future, Swann took to Instagram to announce his upcoming departure and asked fans where they want to see him next. Swann initially signed a short-term deal in July, expressing his belief in TNA's resurgence and his happiness with the company.

However, despite competing for the TNA International, World, and X-Division Championships throughout 2026, he has chosen to test the open market. Swann has a long history with TNA dating back to 2018, notably winning the World Championship in 2020 and headlining Rebellion 2021 against Kenny Omega. With his contract expiring at the end of the year, he is now looking forward to listening to new offers.
