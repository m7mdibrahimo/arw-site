Former TNA writer Vince Russo feels that the company has intentionally removed him from its historical records. While watching the recent Hall of Fame inductions for ODB, Konnan, and Amazing Red, Russo noted that his name was never mentioned despite his decade-long tenure with the promotion.

> "I swear, I nearly — like, I was watching some TNA stuff over the weekend on YouTube. I'm watching the Hall of Fame inductions. I worked with all of them — ODB when I was there, and Konnan, and every single one, Amazing Red. I've known him since he was 19. I swear to God, bro, for whatever reason, I think I've been totally erased from TNA history. I spent 10 years there. There's never any mention — like, nothing, like whatsoever."

Russo clarified that he holds no personal grudge against the current TNA leadership, as he does not know the individuals running the organization. However, he remains convinced that his contributions have been deliberately wiped away. 

> "And I don't even know the dude that runs the place. I don't know him, never said anything about the dude, but I swear, bro, I think they wiped me from TNA history."

Whether his assessment is accurate or not, the Hall of Fame ceremony served as a reminder of his long, albeit controversial, history with the promotion during one of its most prominent eras.
