Chad Gable is looking to completely overhaul his WWE entrance visuals, sharing a wild vision that includes an '80s aesthetic, workout footage, and cruising in a Corvette. Speaking with Chris VanVliet, the Intercontinental Champion explained that his current presentation needs to reflect his true personality rather than feeling forced. While he is happy with his hard-rock entrance music, he wants the video screen graphics to match that same energy. Gable described ideas that include driving a car with the top down in a style reminiscent of Miami Vice. The star recently captured his first singles title in WWE by defeating Penta at SummerSlam 2026, and he is now focused on making every aspect of his character entirely authentic.
