In 2018, Cody Rhodes and the Young Bucks produced All In, the largest independent wrestling event in history, which featured a women's four-way match between Tessa Blanchard, Dr. Britt Baker, Chelsea Green, and Madison Rayne. Appearing on an episode of "What Do You Want To Talk About?," Green reflected on the historic event, ranking it as the second-best moment of her career behind her WWE Women's Championship win at WWE SummerSlam. Rhodes praised Green's proactive approach during the event's preparation, revealing that she was the very first talent to approach him with creative pitches. "You were the first person that came to me and asked me what I wanted," Rhodes recalled, noting that her initiative opened the floodgates for other ideas. Green shared that she still has the match layout saved in her phone's notes app, having written it on her way to the venue before presenting it to the participants, who approved it without any changes. AEW currently owns the All In intellectual property, transforming it into their flagship annual event.
