Harley Cameron and Kris Statlander earned a shot at the AEW Women's Tag Team Titles by winning a three-way match at AEW Collision Grand Slam France against Divine Dominion and Sisters of Sin. Statlander secured the victory by hitting Saturday Night Fever on Julia Hart. It was later confirmed that Cameron and Statlander will challenge the Brawling Birds for the titles at AEW Full Gear. The match was originally scheduled for AEW WrestleDream, but Jamie Hayter was not cleared to compete. The titles are currently held by the Brawling Birds, consisting of Alex Windsor and Jamie Hayter.
