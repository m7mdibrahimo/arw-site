WWE gave Oba Femi a significant moment on the November 16 episode of WWE RAW, but many viewers in the United States missed it because it aired during a commercial break. Femi made his entrance and delivered a promo explaining his return to target The Vision, noting that watching them repeatedly beat down Penta and Je'Von Evans convinced him to get involved again. 

"They ask me why I returned to get involved with The Vision when I said that I was done with The Vision," Femi stated. "The answer is simple, I watched them run up the score against Penta and Je'Von week after week. Beatdown after beatdown. So I took it upon myself to even the score, I flew all the way to Saudi Arabia to make their lives a living hell. And when I'm done with them, and they have nothing left, the reason for that will be OBA FEMI."

The decision to broadcast the promo during a commercial break sparked immediate backlash online. Fans expressed their frustration on social media, with one viewer writing, "Oba Femi made his entrance and delivered a promo during the commercial break. WTF" Another fan joked by sharing a clip of Brock Lesnar smashing a television, while others criticized the company for interrupting Netflix viewers watching with ads or directly tagging WWE and Triple H to demand an explanation for the poor timing.
