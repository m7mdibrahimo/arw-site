RYZIN intends to manage the direction of his final year as an active wrestler. The veteran recently announced that 2027 will mark his last season competing in the ring, and he remains receptive to any prospects that arise before he transitions into different professional paths.

During a recent interview, RYZIN discussed his flexibility and upcoming plans.

> "I'm kind of flying by the seed of my pants and seeing what comes to me for the new year. I don't way to say — I don't want to go back on my word in anyway either. I want to be very strategic about what I select."

RYZIN noted that his opening bout in January is scheduled against Carlito for USA Pro Wrestling in Orlando. Additionally, he intends to participate in the tenth anniversary celebration for Atomic Legacy Wrestling this February. He prefers to keep his remaining schedule flexible.

> "Everything else is a big question mark and I tend to like that. I kind of like being able to control the narrative of my final year and be open to opportunity of what comes my way."
