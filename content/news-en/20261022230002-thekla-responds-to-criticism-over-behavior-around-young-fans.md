AEW wrestler Thekla is unconcerned about criticism regarding her interactions with young fans, which has evolved into a running joke about her toxic toddlers.

During a recent interview, Thekla explained that the situation started when cameras caught her using a frequent insult while two children stood beside her on the stage. Renee Paquette later joked during an interview by asking whether she was really for the children, prompting Thekla to enthusiastically declare that she represented her toxic toddlers.

Despite the awkward moment, the father of the two kids reached out to reveal they are actually big fans of hers. Thekla joked that someone had to teach them those words eventually. Rather than avoiding the controversy, she has fully integrated the incident into her on-screen persona.
