Kyle Fletcher stated that he will rectify his loss to Darby Allin in due time, while also labeling him as a cheating bastard following their match at AEW Grand Slam: France. During the special Tuesday night event, Darby Allin defended the AEW TNT Championship and successfully retained it against Fletcher. Speaking on his latest vlog, Fletcher shared his thoughts on the event and the atmosphere in Paris.

> "Fuck, I forgot what it was again. Grand Slam France or Grand Slam Paris? Grand Slam France, I think. First one ever, because I'm sure after tonight it's probably gonna happen again. Grand Slam France numero uno. Good day, bad loss. Long day, I'm very tired. It's been, I've been here for 36 hours or something like that. What's between 36 and 48? Can you do that math real quick? 42, 42 hours. in the country of France, in Paris. Thanks for the fun times, everybody. The crowd was absolutely buzzing. It was a good atmosphere in the building. First time ever with Darby Allin, had a fun time. Cheating bastard had to cheat to beat me. But we will rectify it all in due time."

Elsewhere in the vlog, Fletcher also paid tribute to PAC by leaving a memorial for him in Paris.
