Arianna Grace continues to appear regularly on WWE NXT television as part of the BirthRight group, but she recently took some time away from the ring for a relaxing break.

Grace posted photos to social media on November 7 from a spa day. The NXT wrestler shared images of herself wearing a blue bikini, including a mirror selfie in a robe and a photo inside a sauna, captioning the post with:

> "Much needed spa day 😮💨"

The post marks a brief departure from her ongoing storylines in WWE NXT. Grace remains a featured performer on the brand, and the downtime comes during a busy period of television appearances for the BirthRight faction.
