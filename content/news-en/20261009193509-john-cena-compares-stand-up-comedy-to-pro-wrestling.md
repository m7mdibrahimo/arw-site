John Cena recently compared stand-up comedy to professional wrestling during an appearance on the What Now podcast with Trevor Noah. Cena discussed the various parallels between the two professions, noting that performers in both fields handle their own travel, gear, and logistics while performing in front of live, vocal crowds. He explained that both forms of entertainment require quick thinking, precise timing, and the ability to read the audience and adapt on the fly. Cena also recalled his longevity in WWE was driven by timing and crowd awareness rather than pure athletic skill. Elsewhere in the interview, Cena shared a story about eating a burger that Hornswoggle had vomited on.
