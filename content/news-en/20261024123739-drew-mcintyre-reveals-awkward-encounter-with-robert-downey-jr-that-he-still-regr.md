WWE star Drew McIntyre revealed an awkward encounter with actor Robert Downey Jr. that he still regrets. While promoting Agent of Shadows, McIntyre explained that he ran into Downey outside a Los Angeles restaurant and tried to respect his personal space by immediately walking away after a brief greeting. However, he quickly realized that Downey wanted to continue the conversation, but it was already too awkward to turn back. McIntyre also shared a more positive celebrity encounter with Sam J. Jones, the actor who played Flash Gordon, during a signing in Knoxville, Tennessee.
