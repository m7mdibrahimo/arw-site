Paige has reached her limit with fans falling for impersonation scams. On November 15, a fan tagged her on X to complain that his brother was repeatedly sending money to someone pretending to be her, even sharing screenshots of WhatsApp conversations where the scammer requested Apple gift cards. The fan expressed frustration that Paige had not responded to his previous messages to clarify the situation.

Paige did not hold back in her response, clearly frustrated by the ongoing issue. She replied, "You can’t be this stupid. Please.. there’s only so many times I can tell you guys. ATP it’s on you."

This follows a recent incident at Rhode Island Comic Con where Paige encountered a man who had spent eight months communicating with an impersonator. She has repeatedly clarified that she only uses her official, verified accounts on X, Instagram, and TikTok, and she does not have any private or secret profiles. Paige emphasized that if anyone claiming to be her slides into a WhatsApp conversation to ask for gift cards, they are not talking to her.
