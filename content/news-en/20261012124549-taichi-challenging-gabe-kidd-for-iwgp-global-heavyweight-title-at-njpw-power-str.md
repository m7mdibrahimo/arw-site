Taichi is stepping up to challenge Gabe Kidd for the IWGP Global Heavyweight Championship. At New Japan Pro-Wrestling's King of Pro-Wrestling show on October 12th, Gabe Kidd successfully defended his title against Shota Umino by using the Brutalizer submission. Following the match, Kidd demanded a new challenger, prompting Taichi to emerge and stake his claim to the championship. Kidd officially set the title match for the upcoming NJPW Power Struggle event on November 3rd. The updated card for Power Struggle also features the IWGP Tag Team Championship match between the Knock Out Brothers and United Empire.
