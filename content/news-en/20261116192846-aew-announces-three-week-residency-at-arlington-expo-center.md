AEW has announced a three-week residency at the Arlington Expo Center in Arlington, Texas. The venue, formerly known as Esports Stadium Arlington, has hosted AEW events in the past. AEW Collision will air from the location on January 9, January 16, and January 23. AEW CEO Tony Khan has previously praised the venue, stating, "The wrestlers love it, the fans love it, the staff loves it, I love it. There's a biological connection between the wrestlers, the staff, the fans, the building, everybody, the way it all clicks, the cameras, we all have a great chemistry and magic together there that could only happen organically."
