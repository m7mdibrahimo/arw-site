Sherilyn Guerrero opened up about her wrestling debut on November 14 at ROW Platinum - Fall Fury, where she defeated Mystii Marks in the main event.

Sherilyn Guerrero def. Mystii Marks

Speaking with Brad Gilmore, Guerrero explained that she kept her composure throughout the day while believing she would be in the semi-main event.

> "I was nervous, but the nerves didn't really hit me until I was in my gear. When I changed into my gear, and I was walking around, I was like, ‘Oh.’ What's actually funny is that I was good and then when I thought we were semi-main… I was like, ‘Cool, can't wait.’ Ryan’s just like, ‘Oh, I need to talk to you.’ I'm like, ‘Yeah, you know, some people were saying I'm not semi-main anymore. I want to tell my family and friends when I'm coming on, they're watching and he's like, ‘Yeah because you're main.’ I was like, ‘Oh okay.’ …I went to the locker room and I went to the sauna and I was like, ‘Okay. Yeah it's real.’ That was probably when I was like praying every other 30 seconds between like going over the match. All day, I think, I was really I was good at keeping my composure and then when he told me we were main that's when my palms started sweating."
