Travel chaos caused by grounded flights on Friday nearly disrupted AEW Full Gear, leaving several talent stranded or forced to make last-minute plans. Maya World was unable to reach the event due to travel issues, while AEW World Tag Team Champions The Young Bucks had to take matters into their own hands to make it to Phoenix, Arizona on time.

Speaking on Being the Elite, Matt Jackson explained that he and Nick Jackson decided to rent a car and drive six and a half hours from California alongside Head of Talent Relations Christopher Daniels. Matt Jackson noted that he did not want to risk waiting for a morning flight and facing added stress. Their efforts paid off as they successfully defended the AEW World Tag Team Championship against Mark Davis and Jake Doyle.

Following the match, the champions were confronted by the Motor City Machine Guns, who had also faced travel difficulties getting to the show. However, both teams were attacked by FTR and the debuting Kasper Brothers, leaving the tag team division in complete turmoil.
