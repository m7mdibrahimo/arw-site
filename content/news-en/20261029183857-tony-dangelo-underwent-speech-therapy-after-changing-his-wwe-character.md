Tony D’Angelo revealed that he had to undergo speech therapy after evolving his WWE character, noting that his former mob-boss accent had become so deeply ingrained that changing how he spoke required conscious effort. Speaking on the Battleground Podcast, D’Angelo explained that moving away from 'The Don' persona meant breaking old habits. "Well, you know, I had to go to speech therapy because I had a little bit of an accent," he said. Transitioning to his new presentation also meant giving up suits to establish a clear distinction from his previous gimmick. D’Angelo wanted to demonstrate his versatility and prove to management that he is capable of playing different roles beyond just a character-heavy mob boss. "I can do the character stuff. I can go that route if you need me to, but I can also be a badass and fit into that role," he added, emphasizing that he wants to give WWE creative options.
