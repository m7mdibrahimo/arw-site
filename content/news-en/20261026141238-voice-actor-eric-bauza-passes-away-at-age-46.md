Actor Eric Bauza passed away at the age of 46, according to Fightful. He was famous as the modern voice of Bugs Bunny and Daffy Duck, and he starred in Coyote vs. Acme alongside John Cena.

WWE fans knew him from animated films, voicing Dino and Bam in The Flintstones & WWE: Stone Age SmackDown!, Rolf Rodriquez in The Jetsons & WWE: Robo-WrestleMania!, and Big Earl in Scooby-Doo! and WWE: Curse of the Speed Demon.

His career included roles in many popular shows and films, earning him multiple Children's and Family Emmy Awards.
