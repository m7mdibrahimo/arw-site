Divine Dominion consisting of Lena Kross and Megan Bayne secured a victory against Sisters of Sin featuring Julia Hart and Skye Blue at AEW WrestleDream. During the match, Kross stopped Hart from executing a tornado DDT, allowing Kross and Bayne to deliver a double chokeslam to secure the win. Divine Dominion previously held the AEW Women's World Tag Team Championship before losing the titles to the Brawling Birds at AEW All In.
