WWE Chief Content Officer Paul 'Triple H' Levesque revealed that Roman Reigns was the one who requested to start the 2026 WWE Money in the Bank event. During the Countdown show, Triple H told Cathy Kelley that Reigns demanded his WWE World Heavyweight Championship match against LA Knight be the opening contest of the PLE. The updated card for the event includes the following matchups: WWE World Heavyweight Championship: Roman Reigns (c) vs. LA Knight; WWE Women’s World Championship: Stephanie Vaquer (c) vs. Becky Lynch vs. Liv Morgan; Men’s Money in the Bank Match: Bron Breakker, CM Punk, Je’Von Evans, Kevin Owens, Penta, and Trick Williams; Women’s Money in the Bank Match: Jacy Jayne, Lash Legend, Lola Vice, Roxanne Perez, Sol Ruca, and Tiffany Stratton; Singles Match: Oba Femi vs. Bronson Reed.
