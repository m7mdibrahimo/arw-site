The U.S. Embassy in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, issued a new security alert on October 7, urging U.S. citizens to exercise heightened vigilance. The advisory warns of potential flight disruptions and airspace closures due to ongoing security concerns and public threats from the Houthis against airports. Citizens are advised to avoid travel near the Saudi Arabia-Yemen border and to steer clear of large gatherings or areas with a heavy police presence.

The U.S. Mission noted that the military conflict has the potential to escalate. Officials recommend that travelers verify flight statuses directly with airlines and monitor local media for updates. While the embassy remains operational, U.S. government employees face travel restrictions within 20 miles of the Yemen border and require special authorization for certain provinces and cities. The alert also reminds citizens to keep a low profile and to stay away from windows or glass doors in the event of an aerial attack.

WWE Crown Jewel is scheduled to take place in Riyadh on November 7. Reports from late September indicated that there is significant concern within WWE regarding the event.
