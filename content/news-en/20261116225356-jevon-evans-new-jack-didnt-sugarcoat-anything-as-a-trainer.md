Je'Von Evans recently discussed what it was like to be trained by New Jack early in his career during an interview with Joe Baiamonte of Soundsphere.

Evans praised New Jack for his brutal honesty, recalling how New Jack once bluntly told him that a promo he cut at age 14 or 15 was "horrible" and made him do it again. "Me being so young I didn't want anybody to sugarcoat anything. And New Jack, that was somebody who wasn't sugarcoating nothing! So I really appreciate him being so honest with me, especially at such a young age, because that got me from A to B," Evans said. He also shared the most important lesson he learned from New Jack, noting that the veteran told him to simply be himself rather than playing an artificial character. New Jack passed away in May 2021.

Evans is currently one-half of the WWE World Tag Team Champions alongside Penta.
