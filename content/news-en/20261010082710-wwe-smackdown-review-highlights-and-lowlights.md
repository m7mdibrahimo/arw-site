Welcome to the latest review of "WWE SmackDown," which served as the final stop before Money in the Bank and featured the crowning of new tag team champions, according to Wrestling Inc. The show delivered a mix of memorable promos and questionable booking decisions as the blue brand prepared for the weekend ladder matches.

Among the negatives, the scheduled match between GUNTHER and Danhausen was abruptly canceled. Danhausen got cold feet in the ring and tried to stop the bell from ringing, leading to a beatdown where Finn Balor made the save without any actual match taking place. Additionally, the Women's Tag Team Championship scene became complicated when Tatum Paxley won the titles alongside Alexa Bliss instead of Charlotte Flair, who was taken out backstage by Jade Cargill. Finally, the main event angle intended to hype the men's Money in the Bank match descended into a chaotic mess with multiple outside interferences, leaving the broadcast with an incomplete and rushed conclusion.

On the positive side, CM Punk and Kevin Owens kicked off the program with a sharp war of words atop ladders regarding their Money in the Bank aspirations, capped off by an appearance from Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn. The women's division also delivered a strong six-woman showcase featuring participants from both brands, highlighting their readiness for the ladder match before Team SmackDown secured the victory. Lastly, Bron Breakker and Trick Williams delivered a hard-hitting main event encounter before the interference began, showcasing their top-tier potential inside the ring.
