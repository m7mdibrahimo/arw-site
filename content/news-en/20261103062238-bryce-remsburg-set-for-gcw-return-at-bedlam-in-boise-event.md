Bryce Remsburg is returning to GCW for their Bedlam In Boise event on Sunday, November 15. The show will take place at the Treefort Music Hall in Boise, Idaho. Remsburg was a regular for the promotion before he signed with AEW in 2019. This appearance will mark the 47th state in which he has refereed. AEW has never held an event in Idaho. GCW Bedlam In Boise will feature other AEW and ROH stars including Jordan Oliver, Alec Price, Megan Bayne, and Billie Starkz.
