Gabe Kidd has opened up about the emotional toll of the passing of Benjamin Satterley, known to fans as PAC. Satterley tragically passed away in late September, and his loss has deeply affected the wrestling community, particularly those close to him like Gabe Kidd and the Death Riders. Speaking on Chris Jericho's podcast, Kidd admitted that he struggled to keep his emotions in check during the day of the tribute show held in PAC's honor.

> "I'm holding in this emotion all day because I know if Ben could see us, if he was there and could communicate with us, he'd be like, ‘Don’t mope around all day, you've got a job to do.’ That's the backbone of the Death Riders. Regardless of the situation, we're at work, we have to put on a show."

After participating in the main event tag team match, Kidd said he could no longer suppress his feelings. He then shared a heartfelt moment with Jon Moxley. 

> "I'm holding in all this emotion and then we do the match and it just comes out. I just dropped to my knees and I feel someone wrap their arms around me. I look up, it's Mox. I'm not going to share what we said to each other because that's between me and him, but he said something to me that's just real."

Kidd emphasized that the Death Riders share a bond that will never be broken, and that they are continuing their work to honor Satterley's memory. In the same interview, Kidd also revealed that Aussie Open was originally planned to participate in the 2023 G1 Climax.
