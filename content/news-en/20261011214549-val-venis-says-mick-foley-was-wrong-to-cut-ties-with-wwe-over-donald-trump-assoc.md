Val Venis recently shared his thoughts on Mick Foley cutting ties with WWE due to the company's association with Donald Trump. While Venis expressed his personal fondness for the WWE Hall of Famer, he argued that Foley made a mistake by mixing politics with business. Venis stated that people can maintain professional relationships despite political disagreements through proper dialogue and debate.

Venis further claimed that Foley's perspective leaves little room for discussion, stating that he believes Foley chose to cut ties rather than engage in rational debate. However, Venis acknowledged that Foley has not actively slandered WWE since leaving his Legends deal. The situation stems from Foley voluntarily giving up a portion of his income by ending the agreement, a decision he previously stated he would make again.
