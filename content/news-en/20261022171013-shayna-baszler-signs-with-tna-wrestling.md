Shayna Baszler has officially signed with TNA Wrestling following her championship victory at Bound for Glory. The former WWE star confirmed the signing, noting that she was so confident about winning the TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Titles alongside Zoey Serrano that she knew she would need to stick around and defend them against any challengers. Baszler explained that her recent Bloodsport match against Natalya served as a turning point, reminding her of what she is still capable of inside the ring. While she has spent time coaching and producing at WWE NXT and EVOLVE, Baszler is not ready to transition fully into coaching just yet. Baszler and Serrano defeated Allie and Kiera Hogan to capture the titles at Bound for Glory on October 11.
