Former WWE Co-President George Barrios recently looked back on his time working alongside Vince McMahon. Appearing on an episode of "My World," Barrios discussed his initial impressions of the former chairman, noting that he grew up watching him simply as an announcer before joining the company.

Barrios recalled being stunned by how quiet and introverted McMahon was in person, contrasting sharply with his bombastic television persona. Despite working together for over a decade, Barrios stated that he never heard McMahon raise his voice, adding that he believes the former boss is often misunderstood and misperceived by the public.
