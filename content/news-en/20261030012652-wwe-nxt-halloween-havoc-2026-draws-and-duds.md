This Saturday, WWE NXT celebrates Halloween with the 2026 edition of Halloween Havoc. Hosted by Danhausen, the premium live event features six matches, including two title bouts. Wrestling Inc. staff analyzed the announced card to determine which matchups are the biggest draws and which are the biggest duds.

Shiloh Hill vs. Naraku is considered a draw. The feud has effectively utilized social media to build interest, with Hill's humorous approach to researching the ancient mist fitting the NXT brand perfectly. While it may not be a technical masterclass, the storytelling and the gimmick of the mist make it a fun, thematic addition to the card.

Conversely, Zaria vs. Izzi Dame is viewed as a dud. The storyline lacks direction, as Dame focuses on "exposing" Zaria's past rather than pursuing the Women's North American Championship. With Zaria's title not on the line, the match feels low-stakes and unnecessary.

Kelani Jordan vs. Wren Sinclair is another draw. Sinclair earned her spot by winning a battle royal disguised as Lady Shani, and the personal nature of the rivalry, involving Kendal Grey, adds intensity. Jordan remains one of the top talents on the roster, making this a marquee attraction.

Finally, Tony D'Angelo vs. Grayson Waller for the NXT Championship is labeled a dud. Despite their shared history, the rivalry feels manufactured and uninspired. The match risks being a lackluster main event for a division that needs to move forward.
