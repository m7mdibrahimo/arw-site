EA Sports has announced that a PRIDE FC expansion is coming to EA UFC 6. The update will be available for players starting on December 11, 2026. This expansion brings the legendary Japanese promotion back to life with a massive update that changes the game's visuals and mechanics. The inclusion of PRIDE FC has been one of the most requested features by the community since the launch of the EA SPORTS UFC franchise in 2014.

The update introduces authentic PRIDE rules, including a 10-minute first round and the ability to land grounded knees to the head, soccer kicks, and standing foot stomps. Players will also experience a new 50,000-person arena, an opening ceremony, and new Japanese commentary. Additionally, the Grand Prix Mode will allow players to choose from a roster of 20 iconic PRIDE legends in a grueling single-elimination tournament where damage carries over between bouts. Every fighter in EA SPORTS UFC 6 will be able to compete under these rules in various game modes.
