Karmen Petrovic has addressed the strange rumors that falsely claimed she had passed away. The situation began after an exhausting work schedule, including appearances at AAA Worlds Collide, a WWE recruiting event at the Virginia Military Institute, and an episode of WWE NXT. 

Petrovic explained that after the busy stretch, she went home and slept for 10 hours. When she woke up, she found her phone filled with notifications from people who believed she had died.

> "I woke up and my phone was just blowing up, and I’m like, ‘What the hell happened?’ And then I’m getting all these notifications, and apparently I died. So I’m like, ‘Okay, let me check myself.’” 

She quickly realized the reports were entirely false and confirmed she was alive and well. Petrovic noted that the bizarre incident was simply a result of her taking a much-needed rest after a grueling weekend of work.
