Jacy Jayne recently reflected on her time in WWE NXT, revealing that she feared she would be released just weeks before winning the NXT Women's Championship. During an interview with Denise Salcedo, Jayne explained that she experienced panic attacks because she felt she was in a do-or-die situation every time she appeared on television. Jayne recalled spending four years in NXT, where she initially debuted with Toxic Attraction before eventually falling down the card. After the faction disbanded and she faced a period of frustration with her booking, she decided to take matters into her own hands. She personally approached Shawn Michaels to ask for an opportunity to prove herself, which eventually led to a match against then-champion Stephanie Vaquer. That breakthrough performance helped kick-start her momentum, leading to her title win and eventual promotion to the main roster alongside her Fatal Influence stablemates Fallon Henley and Lainey Reid.
