David Harbour and director Tommy Wirkola recently discussed MJF's starring role in the film Violent Night 2, where he appears alongside Harbour as Santa Claus and Kristen Bell as Mrs. Claus. Speaking with Liam Crowley of ScreenRant, the director and star shared their thoughts on the AEW wrestler's performance.

> "Max was a great sport. Very enthusiastic. He brought his wrestling energy. I really think he did a great job"

Wirkola stated. Harbour shared similar praise for him.

> "He’s very funny in the movie. He’s also great at the stunts. We would do things together where he’d be like, ‘I only want to do this a couple of times. Just hurl me across the room.’ ‘Alright, man.’ He looks fantastic in the movie"

The movie is scheduled to come out on December 4. Meanwhile, MJF has been away from AEW programming since AEW All In, where he took part in the Casino Gauntlet match won by Andrade El Idolo.
