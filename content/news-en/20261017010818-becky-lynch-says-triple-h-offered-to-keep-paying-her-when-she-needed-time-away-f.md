Becky Lynch revealed that Triple H offered to continue paying her through her existing deal when she needed to take time away from WWE due to burnout. Speaking at the 2026 espnW: Women + Sports Summit, Lynch discussed how supportive WWE has been throughout her career as both a performer and a mother. She recalled that when she first became pregnant while champion, Vince McMahon congratulated her immediately and the company placed no pressure on her to rush back. Years later, when her daughter was three years old and her contract was expiring, Lynch began feeling burned out and told Triple H that she needed a break. Instead of trying to keep her on the road, Triple H offered to prolong her arrangement so she could continue receiving pay until she was ready to return. Lynch noted that this gesture meant a great deal to her and highlighted the ongoing support WWE provides to mothers. She also discussed how she and Seth Rollins manage their travel with their daughter, and how WWE has accommodated her schedule by keeping her off live events and only bringing her in for television.
