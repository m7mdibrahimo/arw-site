WWE hosted Money In The Bank 2026 in New Orleans, featuring intense ladder matches and unforgettable moments. Several stars stood out as winners on Saturday, while others left with disappointment.

Je'Von Evans delivered an unforgettable performance in his first ladder match, launching himself off a ladder and through the roof of an ambulance in a dangerous spot that will live on in wrestling history. Penta secured a major victory by winning the Money In The Bank briefcase, cementing his status as a top star capable of fighting champions and maintaining his spot in the spotlight.

Roman Reigns faced a lukewarm reaction from fans who seemed tired of his constant presence, amid a long wait for his anticipated showdown against Oba Femi. Tiffany Stratton won the briefcase in a chaotic and unorganized women's ladder match, a choice seen as a reheated repeat rather than creating a new star.
