A spokesperson for the Cook County Medical Examiner’s Office confirmed that Benjamin Satterley, known as PAC, died from bronchial asthma. The death was officially ruled as natural. The New York Post first reported the finding. AEW announced Satterley's passing on Sunday, September 27, just one day after he competed against Andrade El Idolo in the opening match at AEW All Out. Wrestlers from across the industry have been sharing tributes to Satterley, who also competed as Neville in WWE. Our condolences go out to his family, friends, and fans.
