The John Cena Classic tournament continues to take shape, with a second participant now officially confirmed. During WWE NXT, John Cena made a special appearance to announce that Zilla Fatu will enter the competition in December. Cena addressed the crowd and spoke about what the developmental brand means to him, describing it as a place built on potential and opportunity, before revealing the next name in the tournament.

Zilla Fatu, the son of the late Umaga, joined Cena in the ring for the announcement. Cena shared an emotional moment as he talked about his personal connection to Fatu's family, joking about feeling like an honorary Samoan due to his history with Rikishi, The Rock, Roman Reigns, and Solo Sikoa, before expressing his deep respect and love for Umaga. Visibly moved to tears, Fatu thanked Cena for the opportunity and the two shared a hug in the middle of the ring as the field for the December tournament continues to grow.
