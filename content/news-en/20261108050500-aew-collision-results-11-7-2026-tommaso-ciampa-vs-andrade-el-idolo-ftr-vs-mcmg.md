The November 7, 2026, edition of AEW Collision took place at the Norwegian Jewel in Miami, Florida. The show was headlined by an AEW National Championship match between Andrade El Idolo and Tommaso Ciampa. Below are the full results from the event:

Chris Jericho def. Blake Christian.

FTR (Dax Harwood & Cash Wheeler) def. Motor City Machine Guns (Alex Shelley & Chris Sabin) in the fourth match of their best-of-five series.

Nick Wayne def. Marcus Mathers.

Death Riders (Jon Moxley, Daniel Garcia & Gabe Kidd) def. STP (Shane Taylor, Carlie Bravo & Shawn Dean).

Persephone def. Mina Shirakawa.

Tommaso Ciampa def. Andrade El Idolo to win the AEW National Championship.

During the event, Nick Wayne bragged about his 2-0 record against Jericho, while The New Level appeared to declare they were not manipulated by Swerve Strickland. Following the Death Riders match, Mercedes Moné interrupted and insulted Marina, leading to an attack on the Death Riders by The Cut (Jay White, The Gunns, The Dogs & Juice Robinson). After Persephone's win, Queen Aminata made the save when Persephone attempted to attack Shirakawa. Jay White addressed the hospitalization of Juice Robinson, vowing to make those responsible pay.
