AEW star and ROH Pure Champion Lee Moriarty has announced his engagement to his longtime partner, La Cherry. Cherry shared the happy news on X, posting several scenic photos of the couple together while proudly displaying her engagement ring. The couple kept the announcement simple and sweet, adding a short caption with a heart emoji. Moriarty has been enjoying major professional success inside the ring as the ROH Pure Champion, and now he celebrates a major milestone away from wrestling as they prepare to get married.
