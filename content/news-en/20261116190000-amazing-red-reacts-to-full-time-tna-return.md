Amazing Red talked about his ongoing run with TNA Wrestling and described it as a special moment that brought him back to his roots. The veteran returned to the promotion earlier this year to enter the Hall of Fame and to continue his active in-ring career.

The 44-year-old captured the X Division Championship for a fourth time at Bound for Glory during the same weekend he received his Hall of Fame induction. He spoke on The Wrestling Classic about his feelings regarding this comeback, saying:

> "Very surreal, full-circle moment where I thought I would never be back into something like this, let alone be back into TNA. Especially where I'm at now in life, like mentally, physically, it was probably no better position or way to be back in TNA than now. And I'm just so blessed, man. I'm so happy to be here. Again, it just feels like this feels like a dream. It feels like a dream for sure"

Red noted that TNA has constantly stood apart from other organizations by blending independent wrestling elements with a higher level of production, emphasizing that the promotion has kept evolving to reach its peak form today. He added that he has grown smarter in his ring choices to prevent injuries and maintain his safety instead of taking unneeded risks.
