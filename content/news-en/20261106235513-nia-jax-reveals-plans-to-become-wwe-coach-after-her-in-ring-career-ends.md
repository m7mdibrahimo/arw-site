Nia Jax does not plan to leave WWE once her active wrestling career concludes. The former WWE Women's Champion is already considering her future and hopes to help develop the next generation of Superstars. During an appearance on the Keep It Aloha podcast, Jax discussed her future and whether she sees herself remaining with the company. While she does not have a specific timeline for her retirement, she made it clear that she wants to stay within the WWE family.

"I definitely want to stay within the WWE family as long as possible, you know, if it's like if I'm not in the ring I'd love to help at you know, there we're not far from the training center where I live."

Jax explained that coaching younger talent would be a natural next step for her, as she wants to pass on her experience. She believes that athletic ability alone is not enough to succeed in WWE; aspiring wrestlers must also fall in love with the business, including the storytelling and psychology behind matches. "So, I will say this like you can grow up watching wrestling and be a big fan. You can be super athletic and be good at it. It unless you fall in love with wrestling in the WWE in the process and putting the matches together and the psychology of it and loving the history of it, it's not going to be like your fit."

Jax currently has no plans for retirement and remains focused on her in-ring career. However, she is already envisioning a future behind the scenes at the WWE Performance Center, where she hopes to share her firsthand knowledge of the industry with upcoming talent.
