TV host Marc Summers recently reflected on his legendary career, including his time on the classic show Double Dare. Summers recalled a unique crossover during the height of wrestling's popularity. 

> "We did Super SlopAMania 1. Gorilla Monsoon vs. Bobby The Brain Heenan. Wrestling could not have been bigger. In fact, WWE wanted to hire me from Nickelodeon and put me on as announcer on their show, but they offered me zero money, so I wasn’t going to do it."

Summers also shared fond memories of Bobby Heenan, whom he first met as a teenager while working at Starlight Musicals in Indianapolis. He described Heenan as a brilliant and funny individual, while also praising Gorilla Monsoon for his intelligence. The Super SlopAMania episode aired in February 1989.
