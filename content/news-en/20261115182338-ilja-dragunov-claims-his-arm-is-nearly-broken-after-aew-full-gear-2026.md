Ilja Dragunov stated that his arm felt nearly broken following his brutal battle against Tommaso Ciampa at AEW Full Gear. In a promo shared by AEW on X after the event, Dragunov explained that enduring the immense pain was entirely worth it to claim the AEW National Championship. Ciampa relentlessly targeted Dragunov's arm throughout the match, hitting him with an avalanche Air Raid Crash and dropping him onto the edge of the steel steps. Despite the severe damage, Dragunov survived the onslaught and put Ciampa away to capture his first title in AEW, ending Ciampa's reign at just seven days.
