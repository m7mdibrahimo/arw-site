Following his victory over Bronson Reed at WWE Money in the Bank 2026, Oba Femi addressed the audience during the October 12 episode of WWE Raw. Femi reflected on his experience in New Orleans, noting that he had expected a difficult challenge but ultimately found success. He reiterated his dominance, stating that anyone who opposes him will be forced to kneel, citing his recent victories over both Reed and Bron Breakker.

Paul Heyman then interrupted the segment to praise Femi's performance at Money in the Bank. Heyman lauded Femi for kicking out of Reed's Tsunami and described him as a vision of the future of WWE. Heyman's attempt to align with Femi was met with resistance, as Femi cut him off and discarded the microphone. Femi clarified his stance, stating, "I am not Brock Lesnar, nor is he Roman Reigns because they are 'Paul Heyman Guys.' Femi declared himself an 'Oba Femi Guy' before exiting the ring, leaving Heyman visibly shaken.
