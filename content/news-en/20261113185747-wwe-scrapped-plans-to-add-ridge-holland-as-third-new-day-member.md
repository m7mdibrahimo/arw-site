WWE reportedly considered a major creative shift for The New Day following their betrayal of Big E. According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, the company discussed adding Ridge Holland as a third member of the group after Kofi Kingston and Xavier Woods turned heel. However, the idea was ultimately scrapped.

This decision would have been highly controversial given the history between Big E and Holland. Big E suffered a broken neck on the March 11, 2022, episode of SmackDown after a belly-to-belly suplex from Holland went wrong outside the ring. While Big E recovered, the injury ended his wrestling career.

Big E previously revealed that he had his own creative ideas for his revenge, including ruining one of Kofi Kingston’s signature Royal Rumble saves. He pitched that he and his fiancée could have sat at ringside wearing low-brimmed hats before interfering, but creative had other plans and told him to save the idea for later. Big E expressed frustration with that direction, noting that he preferred to capitalize on strong ideas immediately rather than waiting.
