Ricky Saints recently reflected on his time in WWE, sharing that his tenure with the company feels like a full decade. Saints debuted in NXT in February of last year and moved to the main roster this May. He has captured championships in NXT but has not yet won a title on the main roster.

During an interview with KLFY News 10, Saints discussed how quickly the past year has passed and described living his lifelong dream in his home state of Louisiana as a surreal experience.

> "It's been a great ride. Wow, a year has gone by so quick for me. I feel like I've been here for ten years already. I feel pretty seasoned right now. But it's been great. You know, this was a lifelong dream of mine. So to be living it out, to be back in Louisiana, because I'm from here, it's like a fever dream. It's very odd to me."
