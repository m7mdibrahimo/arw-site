Sol Ruca recently addressed her transition to the WWE main roster and how she overcame feelings of imposter syndrome. The former NXT Women's North American Champion advanced to the main roster in April 2026, secured the Women's Intercontinental Championship shortly after, and became a regular fixture of the WWE Monday Night RAW women's division.

During an interview with the Fresno Bee, she discussed experiencing imposter syndrome as an athlete.

> "It was kind of like, ‘Wow, I do know what I’m doing. This is where I should be. Especially as an athlete, I feel like a lot of us have a bit of imposter syndrome, thinking, ‘I don’t know if this is right for me. This isn’t something I was passionate about when I was a little kid.’ But now I’m passionate about it, and that was definitely a bit of reassurance that this is where I’m supposed to be, I’m in the right place, and this is something I want to continue doing.”

In another recent conversation, she mentioned being told previously that she would likely never play a heel character in professional wrestling.
