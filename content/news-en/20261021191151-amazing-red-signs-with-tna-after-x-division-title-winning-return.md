Amazing Red's TNA return has become permanent after he officially signed with the promotion following his X-Division Championship win. Just ten days after entering Bound for Glory as a Hall of Famer and capturing the title, reports from PWInsider confirm he has committed to a new run with the company.

Red made a surprise appearance at Bound for Glory to enter the vacant X-Division Championship match during his Hall of Fame induction speech. He defeated KC Navarro to secure the title for the fourth time in his career, marking his first reign with the belt since September 25, 2010. While TNA has yet to announce the deal officially, the agreement is already complete as Red continues to run House of Glory Wrestling in New York City alongside Brian XL.
