Former WWE star Maven has opened up about a serious health battle he has been facing for nearly a year. In a recent YouTube video, he revealed that he has been dealing with daily headaches and brain fog since November 2025. Maven explained that he has sought medical help to return to his normal life, but the symptoms persist. According to the information he has gathered, the headaches are caused by fluid buildup in the back of his brain. The pain has become so severe at times that he admits he often looks forward to sleeping just to get a break from the constant pressure in his head. Maven stated that surgery is "definitely on the horizon," though he remains realistic that it may not be a complete cure. He even reached out to former Tough Enough contestant Chris Nowinski, who has become a prominent advocate for brain injury research, for guidance. Despite the severity of the situation, Maven emphasized that he is not sharing his story to seek sympathy, noting that he feels fortunate for the life he has lived.
