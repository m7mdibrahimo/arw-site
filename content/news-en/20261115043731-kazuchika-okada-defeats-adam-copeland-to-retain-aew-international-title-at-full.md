Kazuchika Okada defended the AEW International Championship against Adam Copeland at the 2026 Full Gear pay-per-view, marking his third successful defense of the belt. Copeland targeted Okada's right arm and shoulder throughout the bout to prevent him from hitting The Rainmaker at full power. Okada later mocked Copeland's spear setup, while Copeland mocked Okada's middle finger to the hard camera. The finish came when Okada hit Copeland with an impaler DDT on the exposed floor outside the ring before rolling him back inside and finishing him with The Rainmaker for the win. Following the match, Okada attacked Copeland again and hit him with another Rainmaker.
