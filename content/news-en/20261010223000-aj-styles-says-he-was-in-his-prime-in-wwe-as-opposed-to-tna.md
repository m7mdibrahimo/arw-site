AJ Styles believes his true prime occurred during his tenure in WWE rather than TNA Wrestling. While Styles gained significant recognition during his years in TNA, he feels his best work came later in his career after joining WWE.

During a recent appearance on Cody Rhodes' "What Do You Want To Talk About?" podcast, Styles discussed his evolution as a performer. He emphasized that his time in WWE allowed him to refine his understanding of in-ring storytelling rather than relying solely on high-risk maneuvers.

> "A lot of people say, 'Well, that prime AJ Styles was in TNA.' I disagree. I was in the best shape of my life when I got to WWE, but I understood psychology. So there wasn't a dive every second and all these things that didn't need to be done. There was things that I thought was needed for that part of the match. And just because you can do something doesn't mean you should do something."

Styles noted that younger wrestlers often struggle to grasp that concept, but maturity helps them prioritize what makes sense for the audience to follow. Rhodes agreed, noting that Styles reached his peak physical condition and health management upon arriving in WWE. Styles has previously shared this philosophy on longevity with other talents, including Will Ospreay.
