Former WWE Superstar Eva Marie has opened up about the reasons behind her divorce from Jonathan Coyle after ten years of marriage, explaining that their relationship eventually transformed into a transactional business partnership rather than a traditional marriage. In a recent interview, she shared that reaching the age of 40 made her reevaluate her life and realize that neither of them was receiving what they truly deserved. 

Eva noted that there was no single explosive incident or major scandal that caused the split, but rather a slow realization that being comfortable was not enough to sustain a marriage. She explained that spending time in California to work on her recovery centers helped set the change in motion. Although they remained on good terms and stayed close friends, she decided to part ways to pursue genuine fulfillment for both of them.
