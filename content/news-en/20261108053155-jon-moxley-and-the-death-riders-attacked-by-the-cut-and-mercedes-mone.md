Jon Moxley and The Death Riders believed they had everything under control during AEW Collision on November 7, 2026, but their celebration turned into a complete disaster following a vicious assault by Jay White and The Cut. Earlier in the evening, Moxley teamed with Gabe Kidd and Daniel Garcia to defeat Shane Taylor Promotions in a six-man tag team match.

Moxley spoke on the microphone after the contest, only to be interrupted by AEW Women's World Champion Mercedes Moné, who warned Moxley to keep Marina Shafir under control ahead of their anticipated clash at Full Gear. Moxley responded to Moné and Garcia while criticizing her for using inappropriate language, before Jay White emerged with The Cut to surround the ring and launch a massive offensive against The Death Riders.

Juice Robinson and Ace Austin proceeded to attack Moxley amid laughter and applause from Moné at ringside, before White executed a Blade Runner to put away Moxley. The segment concluded with The Cut and Mercedes Moné standing tall over the defeated group in preparation for Full Gear.
