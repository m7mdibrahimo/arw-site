Road Dogg returned to the ring after more than 11 years without an official match, competing at Jeff Jarrett's All-Star Super Slam on October 22. The WWE Hall of Famer teamed with Jeff Jarrett and Jay Lethal for a special anniversary match during the Hendersonville Pig Fest event. His last official match took place at WWE Royal Rumble on January 25, 2015, alongside Billy Gunn as the New Age Outlaws. Despite previous doubts about his physical condition from his former tag team partner, Road Dogg officially ended his long wrestling drought.
