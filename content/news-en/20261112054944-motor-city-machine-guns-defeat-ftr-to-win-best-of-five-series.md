The Motor City Machine Guns have defeated FTR to win their best-of-five series on this week's episode of AEW Dynamite. The two teams entered the deciding fifth match with two victories each, with the winner earning a future opportunity to challenge for the AEW World Tag Team Championships following AEW Full Gear. The match was closely contested and appeared to be heading toward a time-limit draw. However, in the final seconds, Alex Shelley countered a Shatter Machine attempt from FTR. Shelley knocked Cash Wheeler out of the ring and rolled up Dax Harwood to secure the pinfall victory. As a result, Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin are now the number one contenders for the AEW World Tag Team Championships and will face the champions after AEW Full Gear.
