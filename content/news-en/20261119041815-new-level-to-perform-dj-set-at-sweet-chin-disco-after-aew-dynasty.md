New Level (Kofi and Austin Creed) are set to perform a DJ set at Sweet Chin Disco in Liverpool on February 7, following the AEW Dynasty event. Sweet Chin Disco announced the news, noting that Creed has visited the venue as a guest several times and previously discussed the possibility of DJing there. The event will feature entrance themes from various eras and promotions, sing-alongs, cosplay, and a dance floor for wrestling fans. The venue also teased that fans might see surprise appearances from the AEW roster. Mercedes Moné previously performed a guest DJ set at the venue following AEW All In. New Level currently holds the AEW World Trios Championship alongside Swerve Strickland.
