Maria Kanellis recently revealed a significant pay discrepancy from her time in WWE during the Divas era. According to Kanellis, she earned more money from a single Playboy photo shoot in 2008 than she made during an entire year as a WWE performer.

Speaking on the Chris Van Vliet show, Kanellis shared that she consulted her younger sister before accepting the offer. Once her sister learned about the financial compensation, she supported the decision. Kanellis noted that this pay gap was a well-known issue among the women's roster at the time.

> "I had to call my sister, though, because she was still in high school and be like, ‘Hey, so they asked me to do Playboy. Are you okay with that?’ And she’s like, ‘How much are they paying you?’ And then she was fine with it."

> "Yes. One shoot. More than the year."

Kanellis explained that while the situation was frustrating, it served as a motivator during her career. She also revealed that she was not the company's first choice for the cover, estimating she was the third or fourth candidate approached, though she enjoyed the experience regardless.
