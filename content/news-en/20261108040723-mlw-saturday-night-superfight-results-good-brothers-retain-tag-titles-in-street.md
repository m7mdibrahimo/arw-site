Major League Wrestling held its MLW Saturday Night SuperFight event on November 7 at Cicero Stadium in Chicago, IL. The show aired live on the VEEPS platform.

In the main event, Good Brothers (Karl Anderson & Doc Gallows) defended the MLW Tag Team Championships against Glasgow Boys On Top (Joe Coffey, Mark Coffey, & Wolfgang) in a Street Fight. Karl Anderson and Doc Gallows secured the victory to retain their titles.

In another featured matchup, Masha defeated ZAMAYA in singles action.

Before Mistico could begin his match, Big Damo and Brick Savage launched a sudden attack on him. Meanwhile, Josh Bishop and Donovan Dijak were involved in a brawl backstage during the event.
