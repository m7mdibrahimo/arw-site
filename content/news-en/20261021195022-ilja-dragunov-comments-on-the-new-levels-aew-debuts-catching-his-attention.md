Ilja Dragunov recently discussed how The New Level's AEW debuts caught his attention, noting the way they performed since joining the promotion. Speaking in an interview, Dragunov admitted that it would be a lie to say their performances went unnoticed, praising their happiness and connection with people they previously shared time with.

Dragunov is currently preparing for his in-ring debut against Speedball Mike Bailey on AEW Dynamite, following his first appearance over the weekend at AEW WrestleDream. The New Level made their debut for the promotion at AEW All In, joining the growing list of former talents to make the jump.
