Brian Ortega has officially announced his retirement from mixed martial arts following a unanimous decision loss to Dan Hooker at UFC Fight Night: Jiri vs. Stirling. This event marked Ortega's debut in the lightweight division after spending his entire UFC career competing at featherweight.

The defeat represents his third consecutive loss, as he has secured only one victory since the beginning of 2021. Ortega, who began his professional career in 2010, concludes his journey in the sport with a final record of 16 wins, 6 losses, and one no-contest.
