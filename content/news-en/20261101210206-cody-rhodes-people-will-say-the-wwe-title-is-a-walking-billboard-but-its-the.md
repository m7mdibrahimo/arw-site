During a recent appearance on the Double Coverage Podcast, Cody Rhodes opened up about his favorite title in professional wrestling and discussed the history of the WWE Championship. Rhodes noted that while some critics view the championship as a walking billboard, it holds deep personal significance because of the legendary names tied to its legacy. He pointed out that the belt is connected to icons like John Cena and Roman Reigns, as well as himself. Rhodes also reflected on the polarizing history of the spinner belt, recalling how heavily criticized it was during its era compared to how fans view it fondly today.
