Friction between Gabe Kidd, Clark Connors, and David Finlay followed them outside of AEW according to Fightful. PRODUCE held Hell Comes To Dogtown in Los Angeles, California on October 22. The former trio was originally scheduled to promote the event together before their partnership collapsed a day prior when Connors and Finlay attacked Kidd on AEW Dynamite to join the Bang Bang Gang.
