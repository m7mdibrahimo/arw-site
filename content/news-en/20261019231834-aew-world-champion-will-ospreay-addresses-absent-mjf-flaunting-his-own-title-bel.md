Following his successful title defense at Clockwork Carnage, AEW World Champion Will Ospreay addressed the situation regarding absent star MJF, who continues to carry around his own Triple B belt and claim he is the true champion. During the post AEW WrestleDream press conference, Ospreay jokingly asked AEW owner Tony Khan how he plans to deal with MJF walking around with a belt despite not being present on television. Khan noted that MJF has not been around recently, leading Ospreay to quip that the rival has been hiding. MJF has been away from AEW since AEW All In after losing a Casino Gauntlet match to Andrade El Idolo, and has instead focused on NJPW by challenging G1 Climax winner Ryohei Oiwa, though many expect him to eventually return to AEW and renew his feud with Ospreay.
