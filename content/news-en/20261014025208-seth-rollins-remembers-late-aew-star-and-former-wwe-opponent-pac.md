In the weeks following the tragic death of AEW star Pac, who performed as Neville in WWE, fellow wrestlers have shared their grief and memories of the late athlete. WWE's Seth Rollins discussed his friend and former opponent during an appearance on the No Contest Wrestling podcast. Rollins noted that this was his first time speaking on camera about the star, whose real name was Benjamin Satterley. 

> "He was just an awesome dude. He was so funny and so unique. He was also super loyal and he was down to earth. He was just one of those people that... We looked up to Ben. We admired him, I think, because he was so incredible at what he did in the ring but he was also so humble about that part of his life. And the way he treated people with kindness and love and there was a warmth about him."

Rollins recalled meeting Satterley when he was 21 during his first trip to Europe, where he was immediately impressed by the star's in-ring ability. He joked that seeing Satterley perform made him question his own athletic future. Rollins hopes that Satterley will be remembered not just for his wrestling skill, but as an incredible human being.
