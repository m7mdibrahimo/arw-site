Becky Lynch recently criticized Liv Morgan's impact on the Judgment Day during an appearance on the "RAW Recap." Lynch specifically questioned Roxanne Perez's association with the faction, asking what she is getting out of standing in the shadows. Lynch also pointed out how Morgan has negatively affected other members, claiming she dragged Dominik Mysterio down and questioning JD McDonagh's current role as a lackey.
