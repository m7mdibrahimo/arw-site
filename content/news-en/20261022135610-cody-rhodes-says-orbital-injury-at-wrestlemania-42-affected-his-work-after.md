Cody Rhodes revealed that an orbital injury he suffered against Randy Orton at WrestleMania 42 affected his in-ring work afterward. Speaking on Insight with Chris Van Vliet, Rhodes admitted that he pushed through the injury too quickly and struggled with his peripheral vision. He compared his relentless attitude to his famous performance wrestling with a torn pec at Hell in a Cell 2022. Rhodes and Orton are still feuding, with Rhodes now utilizing the punt kick.
