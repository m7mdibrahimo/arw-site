WWE is set to receive a massive $150 million payout for bringing WrestleMania to Saudi Arabia. According to Dave Meltzer on Wrestling Observer Radio, this figure is nearly three times the roughly $55 million the company normally earns for a standard event in the region. Meltzer noted that the deal brings significant financial stakes, especially amid ongoing uncertainty surrounding other sporting events in the Middle East due to regional conflicts. While the $150 million has not been officially confirmed through WWE contract documents, Meltzer reported the number based on information shared by his sources.
