According to reports, WWE Hall of Famer Bruce Prichard is set to undergo surgery to repair a broken neck. Conrad Thompson announced on his podcast that Prichard has been dealing with a neck injury that stems from an incident 35 years ago when he was attacked by The Ultimate Warrior during a taping of WWF Superstars. Prichard has been absent from his backstage role as WWE's executive director of creative writing and talent since WrestleMania 42 while recovering from a back injury.
