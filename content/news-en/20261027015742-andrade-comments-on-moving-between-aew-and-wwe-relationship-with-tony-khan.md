Andrade El Idolo has frequently moved between WWE and AEW, first joining WWE in 2015 as 'Andrade "Cien" Almas' before leaving in 2021. He competed in AEW until 2023, then returned to WWE for the 2024 Royal Rumble, only to return to AEW in 2025. During an interview on "Close Up with Renee Paquette," Andrade discussed his relationship with Tony Khan and the difficulty of choosing between promotions.

> "Personally, [it] was good, while professionally it was a little sad because 1) my [AEW] contract was expired at this moment, so I need to pick a decision, like I need to go for a personal life – because I was married at this moment – or my professional life, so I picked my personal life."

Andrade emphasized that his departure from AEW was professional and that he remains grateful for the second opportunity. He stated his commitment to working hard to repay Khan for the trust placed in him.

> "Now is the moment to show who I am! I need to pay to Tony, you know, like I need to pay with my work in the ring?"

Andrade aims to contribute to the locker room and elevate AEW during his current run, which has already seen him win the AEW National Championship.
