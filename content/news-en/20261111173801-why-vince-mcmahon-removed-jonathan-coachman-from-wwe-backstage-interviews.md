Jonathan Coachman recently revealed that Vince McMahon removed him from WWE backstage interviews because of his large physical size. Speaking on Boots To Boots, Coachman explained that standing at 6-foot-3.5 and weighing up to 270 pounds made WWE Superstars look too small on television. McMahon grew frustrated after an awkward camera shot exposed Coachman performing the splits during a backstage segment to keep his height down. The following week, McMahon approached Coachman at headquarters and decided to shift him into an on-screen character who was willing to take physical beatings. This major change eventually led to Coachman's memorable heel turn at SummerSlam 2003 when he attacked Shane McMahon with a steel chair.
