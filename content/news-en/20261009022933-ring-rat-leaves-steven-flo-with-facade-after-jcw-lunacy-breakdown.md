Steven Flo suffered a double loss during the October 8 episode of JCW Lunacy. Following a chaotic match against Facade, Flo's frustration boiled over after a mishap involving his guitar. As Ring Rat attempted to assist him, the instrument broke, distracting Flo and allowing Facade to secure a roll-up victory. 

After the match, Flo confronted Ring Rat over the incident and physically slammed her to the mat. Facade intervened, attacking Flo before checking on Ring Rat. The segment concluded with Ring Rat choosing to depart the arena alongside Facade, effectively ending her alliance with Flo.
