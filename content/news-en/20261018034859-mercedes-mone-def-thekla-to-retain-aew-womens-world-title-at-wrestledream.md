Mercedes Moné successfully defended her AEW Women's World Championship against Thekla at the 2026 AEW WrestleDream pay-per-view. This marked Moné's second defense of the title. During the closing moments of the match, Moné removed the faceguard she was wearing for her broken nose and used it as a weapon against Thekla. Although Thekla countered a subsequent attempt at the Moné Maker into a near-fall pin, Moné ultimately hit the maneuver to secure the victory and retain her championship.
