Val Venis is planning to take his political debates outside WWE Royal Rumble in 2027. After appearing outside AEW Full Gear to debate fans, Venis announced on X that he will be outside State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona, on February 6, 2027. He stated that he wants to debate people specifically about abortion and when personhood begins. Venis previously appeared outside AEW's pay-per-view in Phoenix to conduct man-on-the-street interviews and challenge people he described as leftists, following through despite mixed reactions from fans.
