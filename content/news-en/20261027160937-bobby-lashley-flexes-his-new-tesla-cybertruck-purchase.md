Bobby Lashley has a new ride and a specific reason for buying it. The AEW star joined the growing list of wrestlers with a Tesla Cybertruck by sharing a video on Instagram. The clip started with the question, "Why would you even get a Tesla?" Lashley answered by showing himself sitting casually behind the wheel while the truck appeared to drive itself with orchestra music playing in the background. He also compared himself to the bulky vehicle in the caption by writing, "When you and your truck have the same build." Lashley is not the first wrestling star to get a Cybertruck, as Jade Cargill, Liv Morgan, Booker T, and Kane previously purchased their own.
