ECW legend Francine is testing out a new way to earn extra money on Cameo, which led to a confrontation with a fan online. After revealing that she was open to filming custom foot videos, she found herself defending her finances, her marriage, and her age.

Francine shared on X that fans had been sending requests for foot-related Cameo content. While she had not offered that service before, she decided to see what ideas fans had in mind. However, one fan mocked her decision and claimed she must be broke to sell such videos, while also incorrectly stating that she is 60 years old and questioning her husband's thoughts on the matter.

Francine quickly responded to clear up the claims, stating that she is not broke and sees no problem with making easy extra money. She also clarified that she is 54 years old rather than 60. She added that anyone with a problem regarding her choices can take their criticism elsewhere.
