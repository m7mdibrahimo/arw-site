WWE honored the late “Cowboy” Bill Watts during the November 17, 2026, episode of WWE NXT. The company aired a tribute graphic for the WWE Hall of Famer shortly after the show began its second hour. The graphic featured a black-and-white photo of Watts in his trademark cowboy hat, with the text “WWE Remembers” and his lifespan, 1939–2026. Watts passed away earlier that day at the age of 87. Wrestling journalist Bill Apter announced the news after receiving a message from Watts' son, Erik Watts. WWE released an official statement honoring Watts' legacy, noting, “WWE is saddened to learn that WWE Hall of Famer ‘Cowboy’ Bill Watts has passed away.” Watts was a prominent figure in the territory era, particularly for his leadership of Mid-South Wrestling, where he helped launch the careers of stars like Ted DiBiase, Junkyard Dog, and Jim Duggan. He later served in WCW before his 2009 induction into the WWE Hall of Fame. Triple H also recognized Watts' contributions earlier in the day, highlighting his role in shaping the wrestling industry.
