Drew McIntyre is already planning big things for his eventual return to WWE, and he expects those plans to lead straight back to the world championship. During a recent appearance on The Joe Vulpis Podcast, McIntyre opened up about his unfinished business in the company, making it clear that CM Punk and Roman Reigns remain at the top of his target list. "Obviously, the Punk stuff will live forever because he’s genuinely a piece of crap," McIntyre said. He also called Reigns his kryptonite because he has never secured a pinfall victory over him during their many battles.

McIntyre's frustrations extend to the rest of The Bloodline, specifically blaming Jey Uso for interfering in his world title matches over the years. "And his stupid, stupid family that constantly screw me over of every world title," McIntyre stated, adding that he has no intention of simply forgiving them because fans have changed their minds. The Scottish star explained that he wants his upcoming storyline to revolve around the heavyweight championship, believing the narrative will be strong enough to demand it. McIntyre has been away from WWE television since WrestleMania 42 while pursuing outside projects, and he has not yet announced when he will make his comeback.
