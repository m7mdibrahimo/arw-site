WWE SmackDown star Alexa Bliss revealed on social media that she has been dealing with an ongoing illness over the last two months. Bliss posted on X about her constant cold-like symptoms, writing, "Anyone else been just sick non stop for the last 2 months??" When another user described a similar mix of a flu, a sinus infection, and bronchitis, Bliss responded, "Yes! This has been me non stop for the last 2 months – CONSTANT flu feeling & sinuses – it just won't stop."

Bliss has had an active year in 2026, competing in the Royal Rumble and Elimination Chamber matches, releasing a single with her husband Ryan Cabrera, and having a brief run with the WWE Women's Tag Team Championships alongside Tatum Paxley. Fans in the replies offered well wishes and remedies, ranging from honey and bourbon to allergy medication.
