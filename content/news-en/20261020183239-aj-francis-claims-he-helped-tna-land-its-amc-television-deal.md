AJ Francis believes he deserves significant credit for helping TNA secure its television deal with AMC. Speaking on The Wrestling Classic, Francis explained why he signed another deal with TNA, noting that the company recognizes what he brings to the table and his role in helping it grow. While acknowledging contributions from stars like The Hardys, Nic Nemeth, and Moose, Francis argued that his work building babyfaces over the past two years has been just as important. He cited wrestlers like Joe Hendry, Mike Santana, KC Navarro, Rich Swann, and Elijah as examples of talent he has helped elevate. Francis also compared his creative freedom in TNA to being a quarterback calling the plays, highlighting his recent storyline with Elijah as some of the best work of his career.
