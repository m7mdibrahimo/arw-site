The Mysterio family legacy is expanding in WWE, with Aalyah becoming the latest member to join the company. WWE Hall of Famer Rey Mysterio recently discussed his daughter's journey during an appearance on "What's Your Story," noting that her path to WWE began with a visit to the Performance Center last autumn.

"October [2025], I took her to the PC, and we jumped in the ring for a while," Rey recalled. "She was very natural. [My wife] Angie was there, and Angie was looking at her. She would look at me and she goes, 'Wow.' Hitting the ropes, rolling, stuff that I have seen other students take time to pick up, she was picking up very fast. So that happened in October. She really liked it." After graduating from UCSD with a human biology degree, Aalyah spent two weeks training at the Performance Center on her own. Her rapid progress impressed officials, leading to an opportunity in a PC Live Match under coach Norman Smiley.

"On the last day, they asked her like, 'Hey, I think you're ready to have a PC live match,'" Rey said. "Norman Smiley was her coach and she had her first match. When I saw it, I was like, 'No, there's no way.' She's my daughter, I'm probably biased, but there's something special there that it's crazy." Aalyah officially relocated to Florida in July to begin full-time training in August, and WWE confirmed her signing in September alongside a new recruitment class that included Cara Brown, Katie Reynolds, Ana da Silva, Jackson Goonrey, Tyran Tuckey, and Zoe Sager. While she has yet to be assigned to a specific brand or make her televised debut, her journey follows in the footsteps of her past appearances alongside her father and her 2020 storyline involvement with Buddy Murphy.
