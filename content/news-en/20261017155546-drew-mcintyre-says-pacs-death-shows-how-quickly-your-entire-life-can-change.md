Drew McIntyre recently opened up about the sudden death of PAC, explaining how quickly life can change without warning. During an appearance on Stephanie McMahon’s podcast, McIntyre reflected on personal losses and urged people not to waste time on meaningless arguments. He recalled his early days working with PAC in the UK back in 2006 and 2007, noting how they predicted each other's future success. PAC tragically passed away at the age of 40 on September 27 after suffering from bronchial asthma, just a night after competing at AEW All Out.
