Tommy Dreamer has shared a teaser video on social media for a new project titled 'The Midnight Dreamer'. Earlier this year in June, it was announced that Dreamer had parted ways with TNA after a ten-year run. Reflecting on his departure, Dreamer stated that it was a mutual decision with Carlos Silva and that he harbours no ill will toward the company. Meanwhile, Scott D'Amore has also discussed Dreamer's exit from TNA in recent comments. The new teaser clip features an animation of a man riding a horse and concludes with a 'Coming soon' message.
