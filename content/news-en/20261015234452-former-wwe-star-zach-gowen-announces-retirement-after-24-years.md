Former WWE star Zach Gowen has officially retired from professional wrestling following a 24-year career. He shared the news on Facebook, posting his first and final promotional photos to mark the end of his journey.

Gowen expressed gratitude to the promoters who provided him with opportunities and thanked the fans for accepting him as a legitimate athlete rather than a spectacle. He noted that wrestling provided direction for him as a lost child, allowing him to fulfill his lifelong dream.

https://www.facebook.com/zachgowen/posts/pfbid02U9Tj8pRszz6cxtQ1yhPXuqzMKwZHFoDnwZ5fM9RJuenyhDL9MUZdifKPfhHvSrhXl

> "The best moment of my career was wrestling for AEW in front of my wife, kids, and parents."

He highlighted his AEW appearance as his career highlight, citing it as the culmination of his recovery from drug addiction and his personal comeback.
