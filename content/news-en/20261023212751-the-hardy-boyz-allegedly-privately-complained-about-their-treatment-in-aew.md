The Hardy Boyz were reportedly unhappy with their time in AEW, and D-Von Dudley claims he heard about their frustrations directly from Matt and Jeff Hardy. Speaking on the Duke LovesRasslin podcast, the WWE Hall of Famer discussed AEW's handling of established talent and noted that many prominent wrestlers eventually find themselves sitting on the sidelines.

Dudley explained that he heard complaints from the legendary brothers during his backstage visits while exploring a producer role. He expressed surprise that a decorated team like The Hardys experienced such treatment, contrasting it with their successful run and prominent positioning in TNA Wrestling.
