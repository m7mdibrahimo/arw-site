Nick Jackson discussed his working relationship with AEW president Tony Khan following The Young Bucks' match at AEW WrestleDream, expressing strong loyalty and admiration for him. Since the inception of All Elite Wrestling in 2019, The Young Bucks have played a central role in the company's growth while serving as both executive vice presidents and active talent working closely with Khan.

Reflecting on a backstage moment on the latest episode of Being The Elite, Nick Jackson shared,

> "I go, at the end, I go Tony, I don't know how much more of these pay-per-views I could do. ... I walked through the curtain. Tony goes, 'I can't believe you said that!' Gives me a big hug. I don't know why I said it, but, man, I would do anything for Tony at this point. I love that man. We've given our entire careers to him, and he's given us so much. So I don't think he gets enough praise and credit from us."
