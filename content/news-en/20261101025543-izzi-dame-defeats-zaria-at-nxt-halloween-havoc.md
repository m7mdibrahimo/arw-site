Izzi Dame secured a victory over ZARIA in a non-title singles match at the 2026 NXT Halloween Havoc event. Dame previously declined a title opportunity, stating she wanted to expose the champion first and make her vulnerable before challenging for the NXT Women's North American Championship.

During the encounter, ZARIA began building momentum until individuals appeared wearing the exact outfits shown in her childhood photos, which Dame had recently revealed on NXT television. The champion was then distracted when the title belt was brought inside the ring, allowing Dame to capitalize and secure the pinfall. Following the match, Dame made it clear that she is now targeting the championship.
