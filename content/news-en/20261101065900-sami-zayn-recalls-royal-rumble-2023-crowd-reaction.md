Sami Zayn recently looked back at his betrayal of Roman Reigns and The Bloodline at WWE Royal Rumble 2023, stating that the audience response was unlike any noise he had previously encountered. During an appearance on Insight, he noted that the tone and pitch of the cheers inside the Alamodome in San Antonio, Texas, when he struck Reigns with a chair, stood out from other legendary pops in the industry.

> "From a pitch and a tone perspective, when I hit Roman with that chair, it's this pitch that I've never really heard before. It's like they're all saying 'yeah' at the same time. It's like the pop doesn't start with an 'H.' It starts with a 'Y.'"

Zayn concluded by describing the storyline as one of the finest projects of his career, emphasizing that the success came from collaborative teamwork and many contributors sharing ideas to build a complete narrative.
