El Texano Jr. has a new partner in AAA. The Wagner Brothers, El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr. and Galeno, kept the AAA World Tag Team Championship on the November 21 edition of AAA on Fox, beating El Mesías and Mecha Wolf.

Once the bout ended, Texano and Demonio Inferna jumped the champions. The commentary team did not name Inferna during the segment. Together, Texano and Inferna go by Los Nuevos Misioneros de la Muerte.

It marked Inferna's first AAA appearance since WWE bought the company, and Texano's first outing since he won the Copa Bardahl Cibernetico at Triplemania.
