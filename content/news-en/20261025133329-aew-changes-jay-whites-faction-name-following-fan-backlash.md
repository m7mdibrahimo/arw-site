AEW quickly changed the name of Jay White’s new faction after receiving negative feedback from fans. White had introduced the group as The Last Outfit during an episode of AEW Collision following his alliance with the Bang Bang Gang and The Dogs, but the name immediately faced heavy criticism online. Following the backlash, AEW released a new video on X featuring White cutting a promo under a completely different name, The Cut. In the promo, White took aim at Jon Moxley and the Death Riders while retaining the same group members, including Juice Robinson, Ace Austin, Austin Gunn, Colten Gunn, David Finlay and Clark Connors.
