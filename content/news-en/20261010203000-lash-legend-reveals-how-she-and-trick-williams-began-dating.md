Lash Legend and Trick Williams are now a married couple traveling on the road with WWE, but their relationship had a slow start. Speaking on "What's Your Story," Legend revealed that she initially friend-zoned Williams after meeting him at the WWE Performance Center.

> "I remember the first time I saw him, and I was like, 'Oh no, no, no,'" Legend recalled. "He was my type. I remember feeling that feeling, and I'm like, 'No, I'm gonna stay away.'" Legend explained that she wanted to stay focused on her career and personal life outside of work. However, after getting to know Williams as a person, she grew to admire his character and values, though she kept him in the friend zone for two years. Williams previously shared that he had to build up the courage to pursue her after rumors about them started circulating at the Performance Center. Eventually, Legend says he won her over. The couple got engaged and officially tied the knot in Jamaica in June 2026. Both are scheduled to compete at the 2026 Money in the Bank event in New Orleans.
