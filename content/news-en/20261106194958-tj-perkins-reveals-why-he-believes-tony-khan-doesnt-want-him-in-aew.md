TJ Perkins has many friends in AEW and has even had people within the company pitch ideas for him, but none of those efforts have led to a regular role. According to Perkins, the reason may be simple: he does not believe he is the type of wrestler Tony Khan prefers.

During an appearance on the I Love Wrestling podcast, Perkins was asked why he has rarely appeared in AEW despite his long-standing relationship with NJPW. He recalled working around the time of Forbidden Door, but noted that he never actively pursued a spot in AEW whenever he was available.

> "Yeah, like, let's see. I did the Forbidden Door one, and I did a TV that week. So maybe it was like a Ring of Honor match or something. I don't remember what it was, but it was just—it was all for Forbidden Door. Like, it was a lead-in for Forbidden Door. And it's not come up a lot over the years. It's not been a place—and this isn't because I dislike it or anything—like, it just was not a super high priority to check in over there, I guess, like when I'm available."

Perkins noted that the wrestling landscape has changed as promotions form tighter alliances, making it more important for performers to understand how their career path limits their options. He explained that friends have often suggested he join AEW, including Katsuyori Shibata, who previously wanted Perkins to join his team. However, Perkins believes he simply isn't on Tony Khan's radar.

> "And it's come up a few times. I mean, I have so many friends there. They'll ask for certain things. Sometimes they'll ask beyond me. Like, Shibata wanted me to join his team, stuff like that. But honestly, I don't think that I'm on Tony's radar. Like, I don't think that he—I'm not really the type of guy that he—I'm not the type of wrestler that he enjoys, I guess."

Ultimately, Perkins stated that whenever the possibility of him joining AEW has been raised, the promotion has passed. He expressed no bitterness, noting that he feels better suited for the promotions where he already works.
