La Jarochita is currently sidelined with a left wrist injury. During the October 14th episode of CMLL Informa, she appeared wearing a wrist brace and confirmed that she will be out of action for three to four months. She explained that she had recently received medical clearance to return from a previous injury before suffering this new setback. La Jarochita has been a staple of CMLL since 2016 and currently holds the CMLL World Women's Tag Team Championship alongside Lluvia.
