TNA Impact continued its path toward Destination X, which takes place a week from this Sunday. Despite the anticipation, many questions remain regarding the World X-Cup and the Ultimate X matches. The Wrestling Inc. crew shared their thoughts on the November 5, 2026, episode, highlighting both positive and negative moments, including Shotzi Blackheart's return to the ring, Xia Brookside joining The Righteous, and the opening segment featuring Moose. For a full breakdown of the show, check the results page.

Things We Hated:

- Moose's opening segment: The segment involving Moose calling out Syx, which led to a brawl with Matt Hardy, felt disjointed. Moose has no established connection to Syx, and his involvement seemed forced based on an unseen backstage conversation with Matt. Furthermore, the storyline between Syx and The Hardys should have concluded with the Slaughterhouse Syx Match last Thursday, as that served as a perfect cinematic climax.

- Xia Brookside joining The Righteous: Harley Hudson's initiation into The Righteous was interrupted, leading to Xia Brookside appearing and attacking her instead. The entire angle feels unnecessary, as The Righteous thrive best in the tag team division, and Brookside does not seem to fit the group's aesthetic or narrative utility.

- The System's leadership: After three years, the group remains stagnant. Eddie Edwards kept his role as leader after a match with Bear Bronson, leaving fans wondering if any meaningful change will ever occur within the stable.

Things We Loved:

- Avery Styles' improvement: Despite suffering an apparent injury during his tag team match with Frankie Kazarian, Styles showed significant growth in the ring. His execution of the Spiral Tap, a move inspired by his father AJ, was a standout moment.

- Daria Rae's in-ring debut teased: TNA is building toward an eventual in-ring debut for the authority figure Daria Rae. Her heated rivalry with Indi Hartwell has created genuine intrigue, leaving fans eager to see if they will eventually face off in the ring.

- Knockouts tag team match: Shotzi Blackheart and Allie displayed great chemistry, while the reunion of Tasha Steelz and Kiera Hogan provided a thrilling and ferocious performance that energized the Knockouts division.
