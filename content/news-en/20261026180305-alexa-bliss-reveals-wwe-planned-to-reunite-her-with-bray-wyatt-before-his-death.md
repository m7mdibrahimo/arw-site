Alexa Bliss recently shared that WWE had plans to reunite her with Bray Wyatt before his tragic passing in August 2023. During an appearance on Brandi Rhodes's Shot of Brandi, Bliss explained that their storyline was supposed to continue after WrestleMania, where she had turned against him during his match with Randy Orton. However, her pregnancy and Wyatt's subsequent health issues prevented the planned reunion from ever taking place.

Reflecting on their time together, Bliss mentioned that a past interview where Wyatt stated they would always share a connection has brought her a sense of comfort. She also spoke about participating in a Bray Wyatt-inspired attraction at Universal Orlando for Halloween Horror Nights, describing how stepping into the Firefly Fun House setting finally helped her process his death and say goodbye.
