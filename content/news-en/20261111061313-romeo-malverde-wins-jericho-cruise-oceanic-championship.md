A new champion was crowned on the Chris Jericho Rock N Wrestling Rager at Sea as Romeo Malverde captured gold. Formerly known as Santos Escobar in WWE, he revealed that he won the Jericho Cruise Oceanic Championship during the current cruise.

On social media, he wrote: 
> "The Emperor of the Ocean!! #andnew what a surreal experience to be onboard of the #chrisjerichocruise see you next year when I will defend this title. In the meantime this championship will be defended… no limits no borders no forbidden doors… 😉 gracias @jericho_cruise gracias @chrisjerichofozzy"

Romeo Malverde defeated Mo Jabari to win the title. He earned his title shot after defeating Ricky Knight Jr in a number one contenders match. Malverde is currently a free agent following his WWE release in April.
