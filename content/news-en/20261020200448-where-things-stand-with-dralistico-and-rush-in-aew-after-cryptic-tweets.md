Dralistico's cryptic social media post has sparked speculation about his future with AEW, while RUSH's mysterious message appears to be related to visa issues rather than an exit. According to an update from Bryan Alvarez, Dralistico's contract is set to expire in December, and a recent photo of him behind bars suggests he may want out of the company. Alvarez noted that Dralistico has not wrestled for AEW since June, when he teamed with RUSH and Sammy Guevara. Meanwhile, RUSH tweeted 'See you 2027,' leading fans to question his status, but Alvarez clarified that his absence is entirely due to an expired visa that is not expected to be resolved until the new year. Alvarez added that there is no indication RUSH is unhappy in AEW, leaving Dralistico as the primary focus regarding potential contract departures as his deal nears its end.
