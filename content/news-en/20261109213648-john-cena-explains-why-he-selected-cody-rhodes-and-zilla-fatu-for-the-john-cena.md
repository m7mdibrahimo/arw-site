John Cena has explained why he decided to select Cody Rhodes and Zilla Fatu as participants in the upcoming John Cena Classic. The tournament is scheduled to take place on December 14th, 2026, featuring superstars from the WWE main roster and NXT, with the announcement made during a recent episode of WWE RAW. Speaking in a new interview, Cena stated that he wanted Rhodes as the first entrant because of his status as a franchise cornerstone who deeply respects every aspect of the wrestling business. Discussing Fatu's inclusion, Cena noted it was somewhat honorary, drawing parallels to his strong history with Rikishi, Dwayne Johnson, and Roman Reigns. Cena also reflected on his past willingness to put over Solo Sikoa in their singles match.
