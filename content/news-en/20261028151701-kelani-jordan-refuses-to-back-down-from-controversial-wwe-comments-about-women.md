NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan is standing by her controversial remarks regarding how certain women gain attention in WWE through their physical appearance and catchphrases rather than their in-ring skills. During an October 28, 2026 interview with The Wrestling Classic, Jordan addressed a past NXT promo where she criticized fans for focusing on women who fit specific narratives instead of recognizing elite athletes. She confirmed that she still believes those words and views the moment as a major turning point in her career. Jordan explained that she felt her athletic achievements and championship reigns, including her time as the inaugural NXT Women's North American Champion and TNA Knockouts Champion, were often overshadowed by her cheerful personality. She noted that her previous bright image led people to see her as a cute underdog rather than a serious threat, prompting her to adopt a more aggressive approach to ensure she is no longer overlooked.
