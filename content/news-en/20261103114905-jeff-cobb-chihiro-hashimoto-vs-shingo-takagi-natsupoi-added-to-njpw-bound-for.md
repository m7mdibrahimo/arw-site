Chihiro Hashimoto successfully defended her wXw Women's Championship against Anita Vaughan at a Sendai Girls' Pro-Wrestling event. Following her victory, Hashimoto announced that she will compete for New Japan Pro-Wrestling. She is scheduled to team with Jeff Cobb to face the married couple Shingo Takagi and Natsupoi at NJPW Bound for Unbound on November 20. The event takes place at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo and is presented by New Japan's Unbound Co. faction, led by IWGP Global Heavyweight Champion Yota Tsuji, with Cobb and Takagi also belonging to the group. The updated card for the November 20 show features Drilla Moloney against Daiki Nagai, alongside the mixed tag team match.
