Blake Monroe has opened up about the prolonged delay before her in-ring debut on WWE SmackDown, admitting that the waiting period tested her patience and caused moments of self-doubt. After five months of waiting following her arrival on the roster, Monroe confessed that she began questioning why WWE was not utilizing her, especially as she watched other female wrestlers receive opportunities.

Monroe explained that injuries and shifting creative plans behind the scenes were the primary reasons for the delay rather than a single specific issue. She adopted a persistent approach, bringing multiple ideas to creative each week until officials finally approved a plan. That persistence ultimately paid off when she faced Giulia and secured her first official victory on the October 2 episode of WWE SmackDown.
