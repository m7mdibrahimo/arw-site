Lyra Valkyria’s social media presence has been shrouded in mystery for months. The WWE Superstar’s X account, @Real_Valkyria, currently appears to be suspended, while her Instagram account, @real_valkyria, has completely vanished. Fans first noticed the disappearance of both accounts in late June 2026, shortly after Valkyria turned on her former tag team partner, Bayley, during the June 22 episode of WWE RAW.

While some fans speculated that the deactivation was a strategic move to align with her new villainous persona, there has been no official confirmation from WWE or Valkyria herself. The timing of the disappearance remains suspicious, as it occurred within a week of her heel turn. As of October 18, 2026, the status of her X account remains unclear, and her Instagram profile has not returned. Despite her online absence, Valkyria remains active on WWE television, most recently competing against Sol Ruca on the October 14 edition of WWE Main Event.
