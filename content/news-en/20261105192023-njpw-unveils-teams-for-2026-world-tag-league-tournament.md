New Japan Pro-Wrestling has announced the official blocks and teams for the upcoming 2026 World Tag League tournament. The tournament is scheduled to begin on November 23rd, culminating with the final on December 13th.

The tournament features two competitive blocks. Block A includes the IWGP Tag Team Champions Knockout Brothers, Monster Sauce, Yuya Uemura and Yuji Nagata, Aaron Wolf and Boltin Oleg, Unbound Co., T.M.D.K., United Empire, and House of Torture. Block B features Bishamon, United Empire, Unbound Co., House of Torture, Shota Umino and Satoshi Kojima, Toru Yano and Togi Makabe, Taichi and a partner to be announced, and another House of Torture team.

The winners of the 2026 World Tag League will earn a championship opportunity for the IWGP Tag Team Championship at Wrestle Kingdom 21 on January 4th, 2027. Additionally, if the Knockout Brothers win the tournament, they will select their own opponents for the event.
