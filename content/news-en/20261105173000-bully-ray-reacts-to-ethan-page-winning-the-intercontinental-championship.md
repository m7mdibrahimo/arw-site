Ethan Page surprised many by defeating Chad Gable for the Intercontinental Championship on WWE RAW. Page secured his first main roster title using underhanded tactics, sending Gable into an exposed turnbuckle before hitting his finisher.

Discussing the match on Busted Open, Bully Ray expressed his reaction to the title change. He stated:

> "It's a big win for Ethan Page last night. Huge step forward for him. I'm shocked Gable lost."

Ray noted that he does not want to see a continued storyline between the two competitors. He also shared his admiration for the physical transformation Page has undergone since his time in NXT.

Ray expressed confusion regarding the decision to take the title from Gable, noting that the former champion had planned to be a workhorse titleholder similar to GUNTHER. He highlighted that Gable is one of the few stars capable of performing a high-spot-heavy style. Gable's reign, which began at SummerSlam with a victory over Penta, lasted exactly three months.
