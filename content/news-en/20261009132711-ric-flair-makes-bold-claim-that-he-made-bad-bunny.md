Ric Flair recently accepted a wedding invitation from a fan on video under the condition that he receives two tickets to bring his signature style to the event. During the video message, the WWE Hall of Famer also made a bold claim regarding global music star Bad Bunny. Flair stated that he was responsible for the success of Bad Bunny, before concluding his message with his trademark catchphrase. Bad Bunny is no stranger to the wrestling industry, having previously competed in WWE and faced Damian Priest in a singles match at Backlash 2023. Meanwhile, Flair continues to make headlines through his public statements, having recently addressed his future in the industry and his current standing with AEW.
