Bandido has set a major milestone in Ring of Honor history. On October 14, he officially surpassed Jay Lethal to claim the record for the most overall days as the ROH World Champion, reaching over 710 days across two reigns. Lethal previously held the record with 707 total days during his two championship runs. Bandido's current reign began in April 2025 at AEW Dynasty, and he is now eyeing Samoa Joe's record for the longest single continuous reign of 645 days.
