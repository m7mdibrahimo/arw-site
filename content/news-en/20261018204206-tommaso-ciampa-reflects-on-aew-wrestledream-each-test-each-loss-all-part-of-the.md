Tommaso Ciampa has reflected on his recent appearance at AEW WrestleDream, where he challenged Andrade El Idolo for the AEW National Championship. Although Andrade retained his title, Ciampa remains optimistic about his future in All Elite Wrestling as he pursues the AEW World Championship.

In a post-event statement, Ciampa addressed his performance and his long-term goals.

> "THESE F’N PANTS!! Jokes aside, I came to AEW to test myself… “where the best wrestle”. Jericho, Okada, Andrade: each test, each loss, all part of the journey. I run marathons, not sprints. The goal remains the same: collect every single AEW Title on my way to the World Title. Back to work. ✌️ PS: epic dad fail on that selfie. No clue what happened to Willow’s face. But shout out to the nature boy in the background. Woooo!"

During the media scrum, Tony Khan noted that he specifically instructed the commentary team to highlight the moment Ciampa pinned Andrade by his pants.
