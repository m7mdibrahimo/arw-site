WWE star Cody Rhodes recently opened up about the frustrations of having his intense work ethic constantly attributed to his legendary father, Dusty Rhodes. Speaking on HeymanHustleTV Presents: The Roundtable alongside other second-generation stars like Charlotte Flair, Jacob Heyman and O'Shea Jackson Jr., Cody explained that outsiders often assume he feels a heavy responsibility to live up to his family's legacy. However, Cody made it clear that his actual drive comes from a different source entirely, pointing out that his mother deserves the credit for teaching him how to work hard. Cody also noted that his father never pressured him to surpass his own achievements in wrestling. Instead, the persistent narrative from fans and media has sometimes created a complicated feeling toward what should be one of his life's greatest blessings.
