Averno & Bárbaro Cavernario def. Volador Jr. & Neón in a Trios Tournament Seeding Battle Royal
Neón, Péndulo & Alexius def. Depredadores (Volador Jr., Kimba & Tenochca) in a Trios Tournament Semifinal
