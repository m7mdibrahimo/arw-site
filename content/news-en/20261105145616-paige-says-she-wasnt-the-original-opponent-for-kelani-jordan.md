Paige returned to WWE NXT on the November 5 episode, serving as the mystery opponent for Kelani Jordan. This appearance marked her first match in the brand since 2014, as she was the inaugural NXT Women's Champion. During an appearance on Busted Open Radio, Paige revealed that she was not the person originally scheduled for the match. "I got called last minute. It wasn't actually supposed to be me. Triple H pulled me aside in Brooklyn at the double taping and said, 'Do you mind going down to NXT?' I was like, 'Oh shit. Yeah, definitely.' It didn't give me much time to prepare. Maybe if I prepared a little bit more, I would've won. It is what it is. I know what to do next time if I get a little bit of time," Paige stated. Jordan ultimately secured the victory in the contest.
