Elayna Black is having a great run in TNA, but achieving fame has taught her to be much more careful with her statements after seeing innocent remarks transformed online.

During an interview with Women’s Wrestling Talk, Black explained that she now exercises greater caution regarding her speech and personal trust, noting that individuals frequently manipulate statements to create entirely different narratives.

> "So I think you just have to be more protective of how you say things, how you word things, who you trust, who you kind of open up to and stuff like that because people like to twist words and people like to turn things into stories."

Despite needing to safeguard her privacy, Black stressed that she does not experience any limitations within TNA, finding genuine joy in wrestling alongside her friends while enjoying complete creative freedom.
