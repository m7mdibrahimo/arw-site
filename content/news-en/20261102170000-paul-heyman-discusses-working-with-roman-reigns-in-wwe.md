According to Wrestling Inc, Paul Heyman recently explained why Roman Reigns was his ideal choice to manage in WWE following his time alongside Brock Lesnar. Speaking with Graham Bensinger, the Hall of Famer praised Reigns as a top-tier athlete with unmatched in-ring knowledge.

> "There is no one else I could have followed up my run with Brock Lesnar with than Roman Reigns."

Heyman admitted that he actually learned more from Reigns than the wrestler learned from him during their time together.
