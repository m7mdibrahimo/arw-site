New Japan Pro-Wrestling has announced a mystery match for the upcoming NJPW Bound For Unbound event on November 20. Gedo revealed he extended an offer to a specific opponent he has never defeated in a one-on-one encounter. According to Fightful, the mystery man has not yet accepted the proposal. If the individual agrees, the match will be made official, but Gedo noted that if the offer is declined, the contest will not take place. The event card also features Yota Tsuji vs. Ares, Drilla Moloney vs. Daiki Nagai, Natsupoi and Shingo Takagi vs. Chihiro Hashimoto and Jeff Cobb, Robbie X vs. Dragon Lee, OSKAR vs. Lance Archer, and Yuto-Ice vs. Fuminori Abe.
