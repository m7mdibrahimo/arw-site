Another original member of the WWE ID developmental program has officially announced his departure. Sam Holloway confirmed on October 12 that his two-year run with the initiative has come to an end. Holloway stated that while his chapter with the program is over, he is excited for what lies ahead in his career.

> "My time in the WWE ID Program has come to an end. Thank you for an incredible two years filled with unforgettable experiences, opportunities, and lessons that will stay with me throughout my career. I'm beyond grateful for this chapter, but even more excited for what's ahead. The work continues and I'm ready to takeover!"

His exit follows recent departures from the original class, including Marcus Mathers and Laynie Luck, as well as Veronica Haven earlier this year. Meanwhile, WWE continues to introduce new talent to the developmental program as it evolves.
