Westside Gunn's feud with WWE appears to be over. Almost a year after publicly cutting ties with the company, Gunn left a meeting with Nick Khan on good terms. Gunn posted on X on November 10 that he had just wrapped up one of the biggest meetings of his life and thanked Khan for making things right between the two sides.

Gunn expressed his excitement about the future and even tagged WWE alongside his own 4th Rope promotion while hinting at potential collaborations. This marks a massive change from November 2025, when Gunn claimed he was kicked out of WWE RAW in his hometown of Buffalo just minutes after the show started, losing over 5,000 dollars on travel and tickets. Reports later indicated that WWE was unhappy with Gunn using a photo of Ted DiBiase and Virgil in Times Square and his crew sleeping during SummerSlam. While Gunn left the meeting optimistic, neither side has announced any official plans yet.
