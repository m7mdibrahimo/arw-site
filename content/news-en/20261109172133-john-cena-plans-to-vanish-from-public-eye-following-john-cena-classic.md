John Cena is planning to completely disconnect from his professional schedule after the inaugural John Cena Classic. Speaking with Rick Ucchino of The Takedown on Sports Illustrated, Cena revealed that he will shut down his schedule around December 15 and stay off the radar until January 4 or 5. Since the John Cena Classic is scheduled for December 14, his holiday break will begin almost immediately after the tournament.

> "I make sure around [December] 15th, I am a ghost and I will resurface about January 4th or 5th. And in that time, I make sure to spend 10 days in Massachusetts with my family and 10 days in Canada with my wife's side of the family. I'd say the past six years, that's been my path of action. And boy, it's really been rewarding and great."

Cena noted that this holiday routine is not a new luxury, as he has followed the exact same schedule for about six years despite his busy commitments outside WWE. Although he has retired from regular in-ring competition, balancing Hollywood, business obligations, and his personal life requires serious planning to secure three weeks off during the holidays.
