It has been four years since Big E last wrestled following a serious neck injury, and he is content with his retirement. During an interview on "HaangEmHiigh," Big E admitted that while he loved his time in the ring, he reached a point of acceptance regarding his career.

"Honestly, because of my time in 2020, really working on myself, getting into meditation and mindfulness, it really was not a hard decision for me, because I just, I'd gotten to a point where I think I was just able to accept that this was the right decision," Big E said. "And yeah man, I think meditation has really allowed me...meditation is really just a lot about letting go, a lot of what guided meditation teaches you is letting go of things that don't serve you anymore."

Big E noted that his history of injuries, including torn ligaments and herniated discs, influenced his decision to prioritize his long-term health. He has since found fulfillment in his broadcasting role with WWE and is focused on building a new chapter in his life.
