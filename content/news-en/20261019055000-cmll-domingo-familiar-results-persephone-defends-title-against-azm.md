Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre held CMLL Domingo Familiar on October 18, 2026, at Arena México in Mexico City, Mexico. In the main event, Persephone defeated AZM to retain the CMLL Women's World Championship. Additionally, Hermanos Chávez successfully defended the CMLL World Tag Team Championship against Fuerza Poblana.

Full results from the event include:

- Fuerza Poblana def. Dinastía Pantera
- Emersyn Jayne & Miyu Amasaki def. India Sioux & Sanely
- Momo Watanabe def. Reyna Isis
- Depredadores def. Fuerza Poblana via Disqualification
- Valientes def. Infernales
- Hermanos Chávez def. Fuerza Poblana
- Persephone def. AZM
