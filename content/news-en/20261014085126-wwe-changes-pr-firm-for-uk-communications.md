WWE has appointed a new public relations firm to handle its communications in the United Kingdom, according to PRWeek. The Romans P.R. Agency had managed WWE's U.K. PR duties since 2020. A spokesperson for the agency stated: “We enjoyed our six years of laying the SmackDown with WWE, and they were an important part of our Sport and Entertainment division’s formative years. But all good tag teams eventually run their course. Fortunately, this one came to an end without a People’s Elbow in sight.” London-based PR firm Ready10 is taking over the account to manage WWE's live event schedule, which includes WWE RAW in Glasgow, Scotland on January 18, 2027, and WWE SmackDown in Manchester, England on January 22, 2027. Ready10 Managing Partner Sophie Diner commented: “When it comes to entertainment brands, they don’t get much bigger than WWE. There is not a week of the year that WWE isn’t creating and delivering content to a global audience, so we’ve got plenty to get stuck into, and adding them to our client list has made childhood dreams come true for a lot of the Ready10 team!” The account will be led by Diner and Ready10 Director Rob Leary, reporting to Joel Zietcer, Senior Director of Communications at WWE.
