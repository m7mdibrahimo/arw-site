Paul Walter Hauser is leaving MLW. The Emmy winner announced his departure on social media, explaining that his decision was simply based on creative differences rather than any drama. Hauser expressed gratitude for his time in Major League Wrestling, thanking Sami Callihan for introducing him to Court Bauer, who gave him an opportunity at Battle Riot 6 and beyond. He noted that he learned a lot about in-ring work, producing backstage segments, and understanding the wrestling business. Hauser mentioned that while he doesn't think his best matches were in MLW, he did the best he could, whether that meant getting MLW writeups in the Wall Street Journal, having clips featured on Jimmy Kimmel Live, or bringing snacks and helping the crew. He also thanked wrestlers like Minoru Suzuki, Tom Lawlor, Trevor Lee, Ikuro Kwon, Beastman, Josh Bishop, and others for their generosity and camaraderie, while noting he is moving on.
