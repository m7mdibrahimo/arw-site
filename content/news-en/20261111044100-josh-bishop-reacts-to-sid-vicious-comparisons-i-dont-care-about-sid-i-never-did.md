MLW World Heavyweight Champion Josh Bishop is not interested in being compared to the late Sid Eudy, also known as Sid Vicious or Sycho Sid. During an interview with Fightful's Sean Ross Sapp, Bishop addressed the frequent comparisons he receives. Bishop stated, "It’s fine, I guess, because did I ever wanna be like Sid? Did I ever grow up saying, oh my God, I’m such a Sid Vicious — no. I don’t care about Sid. I never did and I never will." He added that his only memories of Eudy were from his childhood, noting, "To me, Sid was the guy that wrestled Heath Slater on Monday Night Raw one time. He was the guy from Botchamania, and the guy that wrestled Heath Slater to me as a kid." Sid Eudy passed away in August 2024 and was posthumously inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2026 as part of the Legacy wing.
