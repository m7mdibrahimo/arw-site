Big Mami made a surprise appearance at the first week of Lucha Libre AAA's Héroes Inmortales event to save Niño Hamburguesa. During the AAA World Trios Championship opening match, Perros del Mal (Daga, Berto, and Angel) defended their titles against Niño Hamburguesa, Mr. Iguana, and La Parka. Karmen Petrovic helped her group retain the titles by distracting the referee. After the match, Perros del Mal attacked La Parka, Iguana, and Hamburguesa. Petrovic continued the assault on Hamburguesa outside the ring until Big Mami, who holds the AAA World Mixed Tag Team Titles with Hamburguesa, came out to make the save. Big Mami attempted a hip attack on Petrovic, but Daga pulled her to safety. Big Mami had not wrestled for AAA since 2025.
