WWE is not stopping its efforts to counterprogram AEW, and it appears the company has a much larger objective in mind. According to Bryan Alvarez on Wrestling Observer Live, WWE aims to weaken AEW ahead of its next round of television negotiations. With the media landscape shifting and AEW approaching a contract year, these upcoming talks are critical.

Alvarez noted that recent media sales create uncertainty regarding future television deals. He explained that WWE is determined to chip away at AEW's audience whenever possible. "As long as there is that chance, WWE is going to want to make that happen. And so they are going to counterprogram all these events. And if they can even take away 5% of viewership, 10% of viewership, they want to chip away as much as they can because that's just what they do."

While Alvarez expects AEW to secure a similar deal to remain on TBS and TNT, WWE is making the process as difficult as possible. This strategy continues with the upcoming head-to-head battle on November 4, where WWE NXT will face AEW Dynamite's Blood & Guts special. After gaining confidence from previous ratings victories, WWE is prepared to challenge AEW more aggressively than before.
