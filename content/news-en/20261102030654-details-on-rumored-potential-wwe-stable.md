There are new details regarding a rumored potential stable within WWE. According to Fightful Select, as of last week, plans for a stable featuring Big Cass, Rusev, and Ethan Page had not been relayed to them. Big Cass and Ethan Page teamed up on the most recent episode of WWE RAW to defeat Chad Gable and Dragon Lee. Meanwhile, Rusev has been absent from television due to an injury, though he and Page had been teaming on RAW throughout the spring.
