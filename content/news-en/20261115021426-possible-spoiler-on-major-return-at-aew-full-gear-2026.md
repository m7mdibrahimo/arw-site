AEW Full Gear 2026 could feature a major return tonight, as MJF is expected to make his comeback after more than two months away from television. According to Fightful Select, his return was originally planned for much earlier, but AEW made a slight pivot in its plans. MJF is expected to travel into Phoenix for Saturday's pay-per-view and will likely jump straight into a championship feud upon his return. He has not appeared on AEW television since All In on August 30, though he recently made a surprise appearance as a guest referee at Create A Pro Wrestling Academy. MJF previously addressed his absence by stating that he was waiting for the right moment to return and wanted to target the AEW World Championship.
