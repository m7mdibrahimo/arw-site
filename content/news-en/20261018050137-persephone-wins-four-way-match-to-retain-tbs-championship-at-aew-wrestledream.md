Persephone successfully defended her TBS Championship in a four-way match against Dani Luna, Hyan, and Queen Aminata at AEW WrestleDream. Despite the challenging odds, she secured the victory by breaking up Hyan's pin attempt on Luna, dropping Hyan to the canvas, and executing a moonsault press for the pinfall.
