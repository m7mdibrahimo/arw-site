LA Knight announced he will briefly adopt the nickname Louisiana Knight during the WWE Money In The Bank weekend. The moniker originated from a previous interaction with R-Truth, and Knight is embracing the joke while in the state.

> "Oh, man, I was coined Louisiana night a couple years ago here, wasn't I, by, well, R-Truth. But, yeah, you know, it's funny."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eziqN2H2ymU

According to Fightful, Knight plans to challenge Roman Reigns for the World Championship in the event's opening match.
