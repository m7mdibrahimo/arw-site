According to Fightful, the updated AAA roster features divisions for men, women, and tag teams, alongside backstage and broadcast crews. The men's division highlights names like Dominik Mysterio, El Grande Americano, and El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr., while the women's division includes La Catalina and Lady Shani. In management and behind the scenes, Rey Mysterio serves as general manager, and Triple H leads the creative side alongside a team of prominent producers and writers.
