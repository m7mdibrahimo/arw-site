Bobby Lashley reflected on his first WWE Championship victory in 2021, sharing that he didn't even go to sleep the night he won the title. Speaking in a new interview, Lashley recalled how the achievement felt after 17 years in the business and discussed the overwhelming reaction from family, friends, and colleagues.

> "It was a big deal, man, because I always say I fall in love with the journey. So I wasn't even really looking at titles. I know it was a big deal. But when I got that title that night, I didn't even go to sleep. I was getting so many people that called me from colleagues of mine to just… friends, family, everybody was just calling me and the feelings started coming up. Because sometimes I get so drawn into just the workout, like working out, waking up in the morning, eating right and doing all that stuff that I forget about the goals at the end of it. So when the goal came, I was like, damn, this is beautiful, and then it started hitting me and the heart started beating hard and everything like that. I was like, 'I love this, man,' and that's what it's all about. You know, just reaching those plateaus of your career It took me 17 years. But I don't look at it as 17 years. I look at it as I enjoyed every bit of it, and then I got a goal in the 17 years."
