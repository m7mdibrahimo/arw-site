Tony D'Angelo defeated Myles Borne in the main event of WWE NXT to become the number one contender to the NXT Championship, setting up a title match against Grayson Waller at Halloween Havoc. D'Angelo controlled the early stages of the bout before Borne used a reversal, a bodyslam, and an attack on the ringside barricade to take the lead. D'Angelo eventually fought back, knocking Borne off the top rope and delivering a spinebuster and a backdrop onto the steel steps before the final commercial break. Despite Borne's injured back, he managed several near-falls, but D'Angelo ultimately countered a striking exchange with a chokeslam to secure the pinfall victory. Following the match, Tavion Heights, who had been disguised as a cameraman, entered the ring to attack Borne and put him through the announcer's desk.
