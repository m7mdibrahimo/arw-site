Sherilyn Guerrero has reflected on her experience training at the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida. She visited the facility in October 2026 as part of her preparation to make her professional wrestling debut in November.

Speaking with Brad Gilmore, Guerrero expressed her gratitude for the opportunity to learn from the coaching staff. She noted that the environment was welcoming and respectful, allowing her to take notes and learn from coaches like Robbie Brookside, Sarah Stock, Matt Bloom, Terry Taylor, Steve Corino, and Finlay.

> "I'm so thankful for that opportunity. That was an amazing environment, very welcomed there. Everybody was just so respectful, I learned so much. Shout out to coach Brookside, coach Lindsay, coach Bloom, coach Terry Taylor, coach Mox, coach Finn. I got to watch, take notes and embrace into that, what they're teaching people. It was a experience I'm so grateful for. I am forever indebted to learning this business wherever it is. I'm glad, I got to go to the Mecca of it but it helped me see that this is where my heart is. It helped me say, I'm ready to really make this. Obviously I already made that decision but being there, it only just lit the fire so much more and help me. It was another milestone to help me see that this is where I'm supposed to be."

Guerrero added that training at the center solidified her passion for the industry and gave her extra motivation as she steps into the ring.
