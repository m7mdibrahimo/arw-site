El Desperado has been sidelined and will miss both the Super Jr. Tag League tournament and the CMLL x NJPW Lucha Kingdom event. New Japan Pro-Wrestling confirmed that the popular star was diagnosed with spinal canal stenosis and a hernia, forcing him to step away from scheduled competition.

In an emotional video update, Desperado revealed that severe nerve pain and weakness in his left leg left him unable to stand, walk, or sit properly for extended periods. He admitted to pushing through warning signs and taking painkillers since early summer, but his body finally reached its limit. To keep plans intact, he selected young talent Masatora Yasuda to replace him as Mistico's tag team partner in the tournament.

Despite his physical limitations, Desperado confirmed that his self-produced show, the Gaspe Invitational scheduled for November 9 at Shin-Kiba 1st Ring, will still proceed as planned. NJPW and fans across the globe have offered their support as he begins his medical recovery.
