Speedball Mike Bailey recently reflected on his match against Ilja Dragunov, expressing excitement about what the future holds for Dragunov in All Elite Wrestling. On the October 21 episode of AEW Dynamite, Dragunov made his promotional debut by facing Bailey in the main event. Despite Bailey's best efforts to spoil the debut, Dragunov secured the victory. Speaking in his latest vlog, Bailey praised his opponent and shared his thoughts on the match.

"I have been thinking a lot about Wednesday. Good match. You know, good times. I got exactly what I wanted, which is yet another one of the world's best wrestlers. Finally, where the best wrestle. Shout out to Ilja. He fucking brought it. He's going to do amazing, and I'm very excited to see where this goes in the next couple months," Bailey said.

Bailey also discussed his own position in the company, noting that his strong performances continue to resonate with the AEW audience even without a win. However, he admitted that the journey is tiring as he remains focused on pursuing a singles championship.
