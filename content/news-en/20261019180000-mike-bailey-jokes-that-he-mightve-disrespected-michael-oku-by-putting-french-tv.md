Mike Bailey recently reflected on his trip to France for AEW Grand Slam: France and joked that he might have disrespected Michael Oku during a media appearance. Speaking in an interview, Bailey discussed applying a half crab submission hold to a French television host. He joked that since Oku is known as the master of the half crab, using the move in Europe felt like stepping on his turf. Bailey explained that a submission hold was a much safer choice than a high elbow guillotine or an open hand slap, noting that once the hold is locked in, he maintains full control.

Bailey also mentioned that sitting on the host's lower back ensured he could remain in control comfortably for as long as he wanted. During the same interview, Speedball Mike Bailey also spoke about his Prestige Wrestling match against Mustafa Ali.
