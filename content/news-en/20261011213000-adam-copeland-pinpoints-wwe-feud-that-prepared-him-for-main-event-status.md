Adam Copeland recently discussed how his intense blood feud with Matt Hardy transformed his approach to wrestling and prepared him for main event success. Before becoming a top star, Copeland famously teamed with Christian Cage in the first-ever TLC match at SummerSlam 2000. Reflecting on his career during an interview on the Daily Faceoff podcast, Copeland explained that his rivalry with Hardy taught him how to work with television cameras and think like a director. 

> "[That was] when I started to put together what exactly was going to move me beyond where I was at, and that was taking my time and making sure that, that camera could get that shot," he stated.

He noted that memorable moments and emotional connections with fans matter far more than executing high-risk moves. Copeland also criticized generic audience chants like "This Is Awesome," arguing that true success comes when fans connect directly with the wrestler rather than just the show itself.
