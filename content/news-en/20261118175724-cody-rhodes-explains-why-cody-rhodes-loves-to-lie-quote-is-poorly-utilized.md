In February 2022, Bryan Danielson stated, "Cody and I have been friends for a long time, we’d ride together in WWE and that kind of stuff. I don’t know if you guys know this, this is probably something that the boys know, but I’m a big-time liar. I love to lie. My love of lying, I wasn’t a very good liar until I rode with Cody. Cody taught me how fun lying is. Just making up, saying something straight to somebody’s face, the most absurd lie." This quote has frequently been used as a meme whenever a wrestler makes a statement fans doubt. Speaking on the Nikki & Brie Show, Cody Rhodes discussed why he feels the quote is poorly utilized. "He put that quote out there, 'Cody loves to lie. He taught me how to lie.' It's so poorly utilized. I know what he's saying. We didn't make up stories. We're not talking about, 'They had a death in the family.' No, these are just fun stories. You tell the boys, 'This guy has a hairpiece. Did you know that? He puts it on at 10 AM every morning.' That's a fun story. The guy doesn't actually have a hairpiece. We're not revealing anything. It's one of the worst quotes ever. Any time I say something dumb, which we're interviewed all the time, you're bound to say something: 'Cody Rhodes loves to lie,'" Rhodes said. During the same interview, Rhodes also noted that The Rock was aware of the details regarding his meeting for the "Street Fighter" film before he was.
