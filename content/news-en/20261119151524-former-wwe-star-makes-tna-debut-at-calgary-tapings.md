Pinky Montoya wrestled her first match in TNA during the latest TNA iMPACT! tapings at the WinSport Event Centre in Calgary, Alberta, Canada, when she faced Elayna Black.

Montoya spent the past period rebuilding her name across independent wrestling shows following her departure from WWE in October 2025. Montoya used that time to change her previous persona and adopt a new look based on bright pink colors.

Even though this step represents a prominent milestone in her career after leaving WWE, it has not been officially announced yet whether she has signed an official contract with TNA, keeping the matter for now as just a first and important appearance on the card.
