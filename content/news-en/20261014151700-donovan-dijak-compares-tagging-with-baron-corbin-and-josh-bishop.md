Donovan Dijak recently discussed the differences between teaming with Baron Corbin and Josh Bishop in Major League Wrestling, according to Fightful. Since leaving WWE, Dijak has competed regularly on the independent scene and in MLW, where he partnered with Baron Corbin before Corbin re-signed with WWE and led to a new alliance with Josh Bishop.

Speaking on Fightful Overbooked's In The Weeds, Dijak explained that his own wrestling experience closely matches Baron Corbin's due to their similar starting periods in the industry. He noted that Josh Bishop is newer to the squared circle but brings immense positive energy and incredible physical strength.

Dijak praised his experiences working with both competitors in tag teams and rivalries, expressing excitement for fans to witness their upcoming content.
