Drew McIntyre spoke about his acting career and his ongoing loyalty to WWE in a new interview with CNN News 18.

While discussing whether he prefers acting to professional wrestling, McIntyre made it clear where his true priorities lie. "Yeah, I mean, it's WWE. It's my first love. It's my most dysfunctional relationship, my longest marriage, and we'll be together as long as we can be together," he said. He added that even Batista shares a similar sentiment about missing the energy of live crowds, noting that there is nothing quite like the passion of WWE fans, particularly those in India.

McIntyre also touched on his acting projects, such as the upcoming WWE-related project Agent Of Shadows, but emphasized that he is not stepping away from the ring anytime soon.
