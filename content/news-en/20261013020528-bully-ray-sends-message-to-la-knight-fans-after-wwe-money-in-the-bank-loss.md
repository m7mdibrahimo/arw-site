WWE Hall of Famer Bully Ray shared his thoughts on Busted Open Radio regarding LA Knight's performance against Roman Reigns at WWE Money in the Bank. Despite the loss, Bully Ray praised Knight for delivering the best match of his career, while noting that Reigns played an essential role in that success. He urged fans to appreciate both competitors rather than focusing solely on the outcome. Bully Ray also highlighted the storytelling elements of the match, including the action around the commentary desk and the decision to open the show with this marquee bout.
