Despite turning 50 years old recently, AEW's Bobby Lashley has no plans to step away from the ring. In a recent interview with "MuscleManMalcolm," Lashley dismissed the idea that his career is winding down, even after his recent victory over Joe Alonzo to capture the 4th Rope Heavyweight Championship.

> "I still have so much left. I have a lot left in my tank. And see, that's what people don't understand, cause sometimes in this era right now, everybody's talking about the young guys, the young guys, the young guys...Man, I've still got a lot to give to this business of wrestling. And I've put so much into it, and I don't just want to throw it away because I'm a little bit older. I still have a lot more to give. So I want to have some good matches with some good people, and I want to do a lot of things in this business still."

Lashley has maintained a lighter schedule in AEW compared to his 2024 debut, when he and Hurt Syndicate teammate Shelton Benjamin quickly captured the AEW World Tag Team Championships. While Lashley, Benjamin, and manager MVP have appeared less frequently in 2026, they remain active, including a recent squash match on "AEW Collision."
