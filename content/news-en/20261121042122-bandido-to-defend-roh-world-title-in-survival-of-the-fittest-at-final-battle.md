Tony Khan officially announced a Ring of Honor World Title match for ROH Final Battle, with champion Bandido scheduled to defend his title in a multi-man elimination Survival of the Fittest match at the event scheduled for December 4.

Bandido previously successfully defended the ROH World Title in the same match type at ROH Final Battle 2025 by defeating Blake Christian, The Beast Mortos, Komander, Sammy Guevara, and Hechicero. Qualifying matches will begin on upcoming episodes of ROH TV, with Mark Davis facing Darius Martin in the first qualifying bout.

The event will also feature additional title matches, as Athena defends the ROH Women's Championship against Hyan, and Television Champion Action Andretti takes on Adam Priest and ELP in a three-way match.
