AEW Dynamite will not air opposite WWE SmackDown on January 1, 2027, as originally anticipated. Dave Meltzer clarified on Wrestling Observer Radio that Dynamite will broadcast significantly earlier on New Year's Day, airing after a college football game. Meltzer admitted his initial scheduling information was incorrect and explained that the show is expected to begin between 3:30 PM and 4:00 PM Eastern time, concluding well before SmackDown begins. This change allows AEW to reach fans already watching college football rather than directly competing with WWE for Friday night viewers.
