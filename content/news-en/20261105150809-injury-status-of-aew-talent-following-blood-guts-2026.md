AEW Blood & Guts 2026 lived up to its reputation for carnage on November 4, featuring two intense cage matches. Despite the high-risk spots and heavy use of weapons like barbed wire and broken glass, it appears all participants emerged without significant injuries. According to Bryan Alvarez of the Wrestling Observer, everyone who competed in both the men’s and women’s matches is believed to be fine. There was a brief scare regarding Darby Allin, who appeared to strike his own face with his knee while taking a Michinoku Driver, raising concerns about a potential tooth injury. However, the situation was quickly resolved, and Allin was confirmed to be okay. This outcome is a positive result for AEW, especially compared to last year's event which resulted in several minor injuries for the competitors.
