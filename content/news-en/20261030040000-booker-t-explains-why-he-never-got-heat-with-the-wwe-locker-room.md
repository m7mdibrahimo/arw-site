After making the jump to WWE following a successful run in WCW after the promotion closed, many wrestlers risked getting buried or humiliated. However, Rey Mysterio, Goldberg, and Booker T all saw their careers thrive upon arriving in WWE. Despite stepping into an established locker room after years with WCW, Booker T stated that he never experienced any backstage heat during his time with the company.

"Nah, I never got heat. I always understood, you know, the locker room etiquette and stuff like that," he explained during an episode of his "The Hall of Fame" podcast. "I was always, you know, one of the guys you know, I always thought the guys could turn to as far as that goes." He also noted that he was outspoken and unconcerned with backstage politics because the locker room wasn't signing his paychecks.

"I always thought if I didn't got heat with the boss, I was okay, and I don't remember ever getting heat with the boss," he added. Booker T further pointed out that bullies continue targeting individuals who fail to fight back, arguing that backstage heat remains a frequent topic of discussion only because many performers were historically too afraid to speak up due to fear of termination.
