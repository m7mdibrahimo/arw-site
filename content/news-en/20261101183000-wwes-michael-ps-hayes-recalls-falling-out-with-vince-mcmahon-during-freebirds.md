Michael P.S. Hayes recently appeared on "What's Your Story? With Steph McMahon" to discuss the real story behind the Fabulous Freebirds' departure from WWE in 1984. Hayes explained that the group walked out following creative disagreements and an incident with André the Giant. He recalled a chaotic night where he drank heavily and took valium after mistakenly thinking he was off duty, leading to an awkward encounter with André where he fell face-first upon arrival.

Hayes noted that despite these issues, Vince McMahon repeatedly tried to get them to stay. However, a final meeting where Hayes accidentally fell asleep after having a beer with him permanently broke that trust. Years later, Hayes eventually returned to WWE in a backstage role and briefly worked as 'Dok Hendrix,' acknowledging that he knew it was a sink-or-swim situation.
