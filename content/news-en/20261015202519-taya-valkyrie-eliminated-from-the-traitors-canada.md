Taya Valkyrie has been eliminated from The Traitors Canada. The former TNA Knockouts Champion was banished from the show on the fourth episode that aired on Monday, October 12. She received ten votes after being blindsided by her teammates, Spicy Vee and Sherod. Following her elimination, Valkyrie revealed that she was a traitor in the game. Valkyrie was announced as part of the cast in September. As of the time of publication, she has not wrestled since January 2026 when she taped a match for ROH On HonorClub, and the reason for her absence from the ring remains unknown.
