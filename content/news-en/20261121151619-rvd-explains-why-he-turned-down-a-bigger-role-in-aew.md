Rob Van Dam recently revealed that he could have secured a larger role in AEW, but he chose to protect his guaranteed WWE Legends contract instead. Speaking on his 1 Of A Kind podcast, RVD addressed social media claims that his previous criticism of AEW's creative freedom changed only after he landed his WWE deal. He clarified that his WWE Legends agreement was already in place before Tony Khan ever contacted him.

Reflecting on how his AEW appearances began, RVD explained that Jack Perry initially reached out with creative ideas and later informed him that Tony Khan would call. During their conversation, Khan proposed a recurring position to help fill gaps left by injured wrestlers. However, RVD declined a regular schedule to safeguard his reliable WWE income, noting his recent Hall of Fame induction and merchandise production. Although he continued to make sporadic one-off appearances for AEW, such as the 420 Challenge and matches with Swerve Strickland, he maintained that a guaranteed financial safety net always came first.
