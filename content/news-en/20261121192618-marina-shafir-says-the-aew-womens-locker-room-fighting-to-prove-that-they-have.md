Marina Shafir discussed the ongoing effort by the AEW women's division to prove they have just as much to offer as the men. Shafir, a key member of the Death Riders faction, addressed the rivalry between the men's and women's locker rooms during a recent interview with WFRV Local 5.

Shafir emphasized that the competition for television time creates a constant drive for female wrestlers to showcase their value.

> "I think I am currently breaking barriers with defining what the real competition in this business is. I am very proud of the locker room we have, and I think for women in general, like we are in competition with for time and our biggest competition is the male locker room, and that is going to be a forever battle."

She also highlighted how embracing this competitive dynamic could ultimately expand the scope of professional wrestling.
