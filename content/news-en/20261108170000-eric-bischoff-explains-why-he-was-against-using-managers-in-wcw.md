During a recent episode of "83 Weeks," Eric Bischoff discussed his early executive tenure in WCW during the early 1990s, revealing his decision to reduce tag teams and managers to cut financial costs.

Bischoff explained that he was not looking for ways to utilize managers, but rather trying to stop using them because they lacked economic value, with rare exceptions like Paul Heyman. He noted that he preferred to focus on wrestlers who could speak for themselves on camera without needing someone to represent them.

Despite his stance early on, Bischoff later used certain figures as managers as the promotion grew in popularity, including Sonny Onoo, Sherri Martel, and Ted Dibiase, before eventually becoming a manager himself when he joined the New World Order faction in 1996.
