Ulka Sasaki is returning to Pro Wrestling NOAH. A hype video aired during the Wrestle Odyssey II show on October 25, confirming that Sasaki will compete at the promotion's New Year's Eve event on December 31 at the Yokohama Buntai in Kanagawa, Japan. NOAH will also hold an event titled NOAH The New Year on January 1, 2027.

Sasaki has been on excursion with WWE since making his NXT in-ring debut in February 2026. While he has not yet appeared on NXT television, he has competed for WWE EVOLVE, including a match on the September 16 episode. Sasaki is a former GHC National Champion and GHC Tag Team Champion.
