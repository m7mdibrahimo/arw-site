Jake Hager recently suffered a brutal TKO loss to Johnny Magna at Power Slap on September 17. Hager bled from the eye during the bout, which led to a stoppage. Following emergency surgery, Hager's fiance Patricia Blackburn revealed to Slap News that he had a detached retina and required an additional procedure to reattach it. Blackburn stated that the latest surgery went better than expected. Hager currently holds a 1-2 record in Power Slap after signing a six-fight deal with the promotion, though his future in slapping remains uncertain.
