The November 9 episode of "WWE Raw" continued the buildup to Survivor Series 2026. This week's show featured significant developments, including the official setup for a traditional Survivor Series elimination match between Team USA and Team Mexico, and a major brawl that saw the return of Jacob Fatu. The WINC staff shared their thoughts on the episode's most interesting moments.

What we hated:
- The ongoing Oba Femi vs. Bron Breakker feud feels exhausted. Despite their dominance, the repetitive nature of their matchups, including a disqualification finish this week, undermines the momentum of both wrestlers.
- The execution of the Survivor Series buildup felt cluttered. While the concept of Team USA vs. Team Mexico is promising, the segment involving JD McDonagh, Dominik Mysterio, and the team captains felt forced and lacked clear direction.
- The World Heavyweight Championship feels like an afterthought. Roman Reigns is currently occupied with the Bloodline's war against The 946, leaving the title without any credible challengers or a clear path for defense.

What we loved:
- The traditional Survivor Series elimination match format is a welcome change. The USA vs. Mexico theme adds a fresh layer of intensity, and the inclusion of stars like CM Punk, Penta, and Je'Von Evans makes the teams feel significant.
- The partnership between Penta and Je'Von Evans continues to be a highlight. Their genuine chemistry and excitement about representing their respective teams at Survivor Series provided a needed emotional anchor to the show.
- Jacob Fatu's return was executed perfectly. His involvement in the brawl, capped off by a massive splash through the commentary desk, provided a high-impact moment that successfully heightened the stakes for the upcoming WarGames match.
