Charlotte Flair revealed that she did not witness a Royal Rumble event live until she officially joined WWE in 2013. Charlotte stands out as one of the most prominent second-generation wrestling stars as the daughter of legendary 16-time world champion Ric Flair, who notably won the Royal Rumble match in 1992.

Despite her father's illustrious career, Charlotte explained during an interview with Good Day Sacramento that she never attended the massive event in person until she signed her contract and began her journey with WWE NXT in 2013 before reaching the main roster in 2015.

> "My gosh, probably when I started at WWE in 2013, made it to the main roster in 2015. I don't know if I ever went to a Royal Rumble to watch my dad. Obviously when he won (in) 92′, I was six. So definitely wasn't there. But no, probably my first Rumble that I attended was with the company."
