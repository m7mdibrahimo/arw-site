Santino Marella recently reflected on his memorable confrontation with Mick Foley during the 2012 Royal Rumble match, where the two stars faced off using The Cobra and Mr Socko before Cody Rhodes interrupted them.

Speaking on the We R Wrestling podcast, Santino Marella shared that he came up with the idea for the spot and enjoyed seeing Foley's enthusiastic reaction to it.

> "I pitched it. I kind of came up with it. But my favorite part about it, is when I was explaining it to Mick Foley, the excitement in his face, and he was just brought back to… he was like a fan, you know, he was picturing and visualizing it. He just had this huge smile on his face, and then when we executed it, you know, we didn't time it out, but it took the exact same time to put on our socks and our cobras. Then we were actually, we had more planned. We were supposed to go like all the way to one side and then kind of all the way back to the other side. But Cody Rhodes, he jumped us too early, and I remember saying, we had more planned, man. But, at least we got to do the most important part, like just the showdown."

Last month, it was announced that Santino Marella signed a new contract with TNA.
