At 46 years old, WWE star The Miz is aware that his wrestling career is steadily winding down, though he has no immediate plans to step away from the ring. Speaking on Z100 New York, the former WWE Champion addressed his eventual retirement and explained that he still feels healthy enough to perform at a high level.

> "I still feel good. [I'd think about retirement] if I had started feeling aching and pain and my energy was low. I don't feel that right now."

Despite having spent over two decades in the business, The Miz believes his current ring work is as strong as ever. He noted that his experience allows him to wrestle smarter, knowing how to structure matches and connect with the audience effectively. Addressing John Cena's decision to retire after realizing he could no longer perform at his peak, The Miz acknowledged that he understands that mindset, admitting that long matches and constant travel take a heavier toll on his body than before.

> "It takes a longer time for my body to heal when I have a longer match. The bumps, the bruises, the pain? It takes a lot longer to go away."
