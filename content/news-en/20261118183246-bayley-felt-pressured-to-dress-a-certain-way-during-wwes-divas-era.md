Bayley appeared as a guest on The AJ Styles Podcast and opened up about the early days of her WWE career. She admitted that she struggled with her confidence during the tail end of the Divas era because she felt she did not fit the mold of what the company expected from its women at the time. Bayley explained that she believed she had to dress a certain way or appear in a bikini to succeed, which made it difficult for her to be herself.

> "The hardest was when I was first coming up, I would say, because it was very still Divas, you know, era. So it was hard for me to try to fit that mold of being this, thinking I had to dress a certain way or go out there in a bikini or wear certain things. And for a while it was like that. We had to wear certain outfits at the airport or to TVs and things like that. So it took a long time for me to feel confident that I was ever going to make it."

Bayley noted that she had to work through those feelings to reconcile the person she is, Pam Martinez, with the character of Bayley. She credited her high school sports background with helping her develop the social skills and chemistry needed to succeed in the locker room and the ring. Bayley added that she has learned to adapt her style to match her opponents, citing her recent match with Meiko Satomura as an example of how she adjusts her approach to bring out the best in others.
