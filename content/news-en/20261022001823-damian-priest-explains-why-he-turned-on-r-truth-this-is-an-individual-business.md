Damian Priest explained why he turned on R-Truth in an interview with WWE Radio's Off The Ropes. Since ending his partnership with Truth in September, Priest said he has adopted a new mindset focused on himself and his own career rather than trying to please others. "It's one of those things that I'm just looking around, looking and reading the room and I seeing everybody or a lot of others achieve things that I want and progressing," Priest said. He added that he realized he was just playing and pretending while others were working toward their goals, emphasizing that WWE is an individual business rather than a team sport.
