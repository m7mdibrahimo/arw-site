Nikki Bella has cast doubt on the possibility of a final one-on-one showdown against her twin sister, Brie Bella. Although Nikki previously hoped for another match, she now believes their history of injuries and the limited time left in their in-ring careers make a reunion unlikely.

During an interview with SportsRadio 94WIP, Nikki reflected on their memorable 2014 rivalry, which culminated in a match at WWE Hell in a Cell. While she cherished that experience, her perspective has shifted due to their physical condition. "I mean, now, like after our injuries, I don't think so. I just don't think we're so like at the end of our in-ring careers that I feel like it just has to be together at this point," Nikki stated.

Nikki also noted the strong chemistry she shared with Brie, comparing it to her past work with Paige. She had even envisioned a scenario where Brie could interrupt her ongoing feud with Paige, but she acknowledges that Brie's recovery from multiple scapula fractures suffered at WWE SummerSlam on August 1, 2026, makes this difficult. With both sisters currently on two-year contracts that are expected to conclude their wrestling careers, Nikki remains pessimistic about a final match ever taking place.
