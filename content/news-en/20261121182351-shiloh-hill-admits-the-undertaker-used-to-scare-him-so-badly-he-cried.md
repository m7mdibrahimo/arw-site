NXT star Shiloh Hill recently admitted that The Undertaker used to scare him so badly as a child that he would cry during the WWE legend's entrance. Speaking on Good Karma Wrestling, Hill explained that his early childhood terror eventually turned into deep respect and appreciation for the character as he grew older. Years later, that frightening childhood memory transformed into a surreal working relationship when The Undertaker served as his mentor and coach on WWE LFG.

Hill recalled that having the WWE legend guide him felt completely unreal, comparing his current career to playing a realistic version of a 2K video game. Hill eventually won WWE LFG Season 2 and secured an NXT contract in October 2025 after receiving high praise from The Undertaker. He added that having face-to-face conversations with Shawn Michaels gives him that same surreal feeling of stepping inside a video game backstage scene.
