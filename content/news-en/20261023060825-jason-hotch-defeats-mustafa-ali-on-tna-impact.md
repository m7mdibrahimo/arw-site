Jason Hotch successfully defended his International Championship against Mustafa Ali in a Two Out of Three Falls match during the main event of TNA Impact. Ali secured the first fall with a quick roll-up, but Hotch leveled the score in the second fall after the referee disqualified Ali for using the championship belt as a weapon.

The final fall featured intense action as Ali attempted to use illegal tactics and the belt to regain control. Hotch fought back, retaliating with the belt before hitting his signature GTH maneuver to secure the victory. Hotch previously captured the title by defeating Ali at Bound for Glory, continuing a heated rivalry that has spanned both TNA and Warrior Wrestling events.
