Kacy Catanzaro is not ready to return to wrestling just yet. Catanzaro has not wrestled since the April 18, 2025 episode of WWE SmackDown. A few weeks later, she was released by WWE along with her tag team partner, Kayden Carter, who now goes by the ring name Lacey Lane. Speaking with Sean Ross Sapp, Catanzaro addressed the possibility of teaming up with Lacey Lane in AEW or ROH.

> "For right now, it's not a thing. But I'm also a never say never person. For me, I was ready to let my wrestling journey take a break in that moment and see where life wanted to take me after that. Never say never."

Earlier in the interview, Catanzaro mentioned that she misses the connection with the fans.

> "I feel like, honestly, the fans. I miss having those moments where you're in the ring, you can feel them there with you, them supporting you, whatever that might be. Those interactions, even if they're small, when you're out there on TV, like I missed that environment of having the crowd with you and having them there."
