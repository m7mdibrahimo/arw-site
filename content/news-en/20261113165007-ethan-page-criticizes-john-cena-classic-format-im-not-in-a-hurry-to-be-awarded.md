Ethan Page is not interested in competing in the John Cena Classic tournament. During an appearance on The Masked Man Show, Page explained why he has no plans to enter the competition, which will take place at Disney World in December.

Page criticized the tournament's format, pointing out that fan voting could dictate the outcome even if he dominates his opponent. He added that he is not eager to win merchandise featuring another person's name and a free watch when he can afford his own jewelry and prefers items with his own name.

John Cena is scheduled to appear on the November 13 episode of WWE SmackDown.
