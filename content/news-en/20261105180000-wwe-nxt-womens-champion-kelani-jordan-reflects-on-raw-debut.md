WWE NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan recently shared her thoughts on her experience making her main roster debut. Jordan appeared on the September 14 edition of WWE Raw, where she competed in a triple threat Money in the Bank qualifying match. While the victory went to her former NXT rival Lola Vice, Jordan expressed that she was happy with the opportunity to perform on a larger stage.

> "It was so cool [to make my Raw debut]. It's just crazy to see the difference, like, when you're performing in front of however many people. I don't even know the number there."

Jordan also highlighted the significance of sharing the ring with Raquel Rodriguez, noting that it was a milestone in her career. She described the appearance on Raw and the show's presence on Netflix as a major turning point. Although she did not reveal her full character, she is eager for a future main roster call-up to either Raw or SmackDown. For now, she remains focused on her reign as NXT Women's Champion and is open to facing new challengers, such as Lizzy Rain and Kali Armstrong.

> "When the time is right, be able to get on the big show, whether it's Raw or SmackDown, whatever it is. I want to be able to show what I can do on a different brand. But right now, my focus is just to have an iconic NXT Women's Championship reign and a legacy here in NXT."
