James Ellsworth recently opened up about his departure from WWE, revealing that the company actually kept paying him even after letting him go in November 2017. During an appearance on Developmentally Speaking, Ellsworth explained how the situation unfolded before his eventual return to television. 

> Well, it was weird how it happened. Like in November 2017, they like let me go, but then they brought me back a couple of months later. So they never like, they never stopped paying me. I was getting paid that whole time.

Ellsworth eventually returned to WWE television in 2018 to work alongside Carmella, but he realized his time was truly ending when Paige, serving as SmackDown General Manager, fired him on screen. Reflecting on his run, Ellsworth believes WWE viewed him as a unique character who could be brought back years later for a strong reaction, much like the Boogeyman, and he admitted he would gladly return as a manager if given the opportunity.
