TNA iMPACT! aired on November 5 from the Dothan Civic Center in Dothan, AL, on AMC. Moose opened the show addressing Matt Hardy, who suggested he return to his old self. Moose stated he is now focused on SYX. After Samual Shaw and Joseph Gacy attacked Moose, Matt Hardy intervened to make the save. Trey Miguel announced he will provide commentary for the tag team title match.

Indi Hartwell informed Daria Rae that she enlisted Shayna Baszler because she could not handle the task herself, while Brian Johnson ensured no physical altercation occurred. Frankie Kazarian and Avery Styles defeated Hunter James and Alexander Lev. Mustafa Ali warned Kazarian that Styles would eventually betray him, leading to Kazarian punching Ali. The System agreed to stay out of the match between Eddie Edwards and Bear Bronson. Eddie Edwards then defeated Bear Bronson, and The System emerged to celebrate.

In other segments, Daria Rae noted that Brian Johnson does not need to file an incident report, and Saninto praised his performance. Allie and Shotzi Blackheart defeated Fire ’N Flava (Tasha Steelz and Kiera Hogan), though the latter duo attacked them after the bell. Mila Moore broke up with John Skylar, and Elayna Black attempted to manipulate Ash By Elegance and Heather By Elegance. Xia Brookside attacked Harley Hudson, joining The Righteous in the process. Moose confirmed he is targeting SYX and will face them alongside Matt Hardy at Destination X.

In the main event, The Nemeths (Nic Nemeth and Ryan Nemeth) defeated Amazing Red and Leon Slater to retain the TNA World Tag Team Championships. Elijah chased off the champions after the match. Harley Hudson expressed her lack of friends, leading Taryn Terrell to welcome her to the Dollhouse.
