Mick Foley stated that AEW has become the wrestling show that keeps pulling him back because the company treats the entire roster with importance rather than focusing only on a few top stars. Speaking on The Mick and Kenny Show, Foley explained that he connected with AEW more than WWE after taking a break from following wrestling. He noted that AEW gives genuine importance to wrestlers throughout the card, making the middle of the card feel truly meaningful. Foley discussed this with Tony Khan, asking how the company manages to give every wrestler a clear sense of purpose every week. While clarifying that he did not want to knock WWE, Foley emphasized that AEW's roster depth makes it believable that almost anyone could step up and challenge for the world title on any given night. He also praised AEW's tag team division, stating that the promotion respects dedicated tag teams rather than simply throwing two established singles stars together.
