Steve Maclin recently discussed the praise he received from Adam Copeland following their match at MLP Northern Rising. Copeland stated that Maclin should be one of the most wanted free agents in the industry. Reflecting on these remarks, Maclin expressed how deeply meaningful the validation was after his 15 years in professional wrestling. He shared how he felt while lying on the mat next to his wife after she had been Gored, listening to Copeland's words and tearing up. Maclin noted that receiving such praise from a Hall of Famer and a legend of the Attitude Era meant a great deal to him, and he intends to carry that pride forward every time he steps into a ring.
