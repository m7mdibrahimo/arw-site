Shotzi Blackheart has officially made her debut on TNA iMPACT as the newest member of the Knockouts division. Following the events of Bound for Glory, former champions Allie and Kiera Hogan addressed their future, with Hogan calling Allie the weakest link of their team. Tasha Steelz then rushed to the ring to attack Allie alongside Hogan. Blackheart ran out to make the save and even the odds, forcing Hogan and Steelz to retreat while Allie celebrated her new ally. Blackheart previously wrestled for TNA in 2018 and 2019, but this marks her first official roster appearance. She departed from WWE in mid-2025 and subsequently captured the MLW Women's Featherweight Championship.
