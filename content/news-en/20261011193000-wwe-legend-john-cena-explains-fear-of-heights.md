During a recent interview with E! News to promote his upcoming film Matchbox: The Movie, WWE legend John Cena opened up about developing a fear of heights after years of taking bumps in the ring. Cena performed his own stunts for the movie, which included hanging from a helicopter midair, but he admitted that the experience forced him to confront his anxieties. "I think my fear of heights stems from falling down most of my life in the WWE," he explained. Cena praised director and former stuntman Sam Hargrave for keeping him safe during the intense shoot, noting that while he did not completely overcome his phobia, trusting his director allowed him to push past his discomfort. Matchbox: The Movie is scheduled to be released on October 9 in the United States and will stream on Apple TV, featuring a cast that includes Jessica Biel and Kristofer Hivju.
