Avery Styles recently discussed his strong bond with TNA colleague Rich Swann, noting that the two developed an immediate connection outside of the ring. According to Wrestling Inc, Styles shared his admiration for Swann during a recent radio appearance.

> "He's hilarious, one of the funniest guys I've ever met."

Styles, the son of WWE legend AJ Styles, recently competed in the Call Your Shot battle royal at Bound for Glory, reaching the final four. He praised Swann’s energy and expressed a desire to face him in a match soon.

> "We just hit it off."
