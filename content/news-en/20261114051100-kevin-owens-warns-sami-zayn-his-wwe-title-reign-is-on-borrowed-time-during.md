Kevin Owens made it clear during the November 13 episode of WWE SmackDown that he is still pursuing Sami Zayn's Undisputed WWE Championship. Owens confronted Zayn in the ring, explaining his motivation for wanting the title and delivering a warning following a physical altercation with Johnny Gargano. Zayn had come to the ring alongside Johnny Gargano and Candice LeRae, addressing his recent Crown Jewel loss to Roman Reigns and expressing his desire for respect from the WWE Universe.

Interrupted by Kevin Owens, Zayn listened as Owens acknowledged him as one of WWE's best wrestlers who deserved the championship. However, Owens revealed his determination to take the title, explaining how his long injury absence robbed him of the chance to return and win the championship for his family. He blamed Zayn for standing in their way and shoved him, prompting Johnny Gargano to intervene and defend his friend. After Gargano attacked Owens from behind and Candice LeRae slapped him, Owens fought back, dropped Gargano with a Stunner, and grabbed the microphone to warn that Zayn's title reign was on borrowed time before throwing Gargano over the top rope.
