Nia Jax has revealed the origins of her ring name and the television show that inspired it during a recent interview. Speaking with Jimmy V3, Jax explained that she adopted her surname from the character Jax Teller in the series Sons of Anarchy, which she was obsessed with at the time. She added that her first name, Nia, is a traditional Samoan name.

Jax has established a successful career in WWE since joining the NXT roster in 2015, eventually rising to become a multi-time women's world champion on the main roster. Elsewhere in the interview, she discussed the possibility of The Bloodline reaching out to her, Naomi, or Tamina Snuka.
