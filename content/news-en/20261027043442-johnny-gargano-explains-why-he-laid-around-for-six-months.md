Johnny Gargano recently explained why he spent six months laying low backstage instead of wrestling before his return to the ring against CM Punk in Cleveland in August. In a vlog, Gargano opened up about the emotional toll he experienced, revealing that he felt like he was letting people down and that his son Quill's reactions to his losses broke him.

> "Everyone wants to know, 'Why were you laying around and feeling sorry for yourself for six months? What was going on?' It's complicated, but the easy answer is right there [shows his title wall]. I feel like everyone expected me to be that guy, and I tried to be that guy, and I couldn't. I feel like I was letting a lot of people down. I hated that."

He added that it felt easier at the time not to try rather than face failure and disappointment. Gargano expressed gratitude to the fans for their continued support and thanked Sami Zayn for showing him the way forward.
