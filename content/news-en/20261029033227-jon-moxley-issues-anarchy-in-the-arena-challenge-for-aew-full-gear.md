A major challenge has been issued for AEW Full Gear. This week's episode of AEW Dynamite began with a tag team match featuring Jon Moxley and Daniel Garcia against Jay White and David Finlay of The Cut, with White and Finlay securing the win following interference from Ace Austin.

A brawl involving members of the Death Riders and The Cut erupted after the match. During the chaos, Jon Moxley challenged his opponents to an Anarchy in the Arena match at AEW Full Gear. Further details regarding the match are expected to be announced soon.
