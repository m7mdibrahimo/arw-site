Paul Heyman came close to leaving professional wrestling before ECW started, planning to work on New York morning radio against Howard Stern after WCW fired him following disputes with Bill Watts over contract cuts. However, Jim Crockett Jr. convinced him to become a booker for a new promotion by challenging his criticisms of traditional bookers. Heyman accepted, turning down the radio deal and eventually joining Eastern Championship Wrestling, which shaped his career.
