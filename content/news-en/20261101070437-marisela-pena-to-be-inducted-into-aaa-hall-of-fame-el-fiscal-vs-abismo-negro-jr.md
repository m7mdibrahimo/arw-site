Marisela Peñan is set to enter the AAA Hall of Fame during week three of Lucha Libre AAA's Héroes Inmortales. The event takes place on November 7th, with Rey Mysterio handling the induction ceremony for Peña. Additionally, a singles match between El Fiscal and Abismo Negro Jr. is scheduled for the show.
