Syx, consisting of Joseph Sawyer and Samuel Shaw, made an immediate impact in TNA by attacking the Hardys. The pair, formerly known as the Wyatt Sicks in WWE, have been teaming on the independent circuit since their WWE departures in April. During an appearance on Busted Open Radio, Sawyer expressed his excitement about being able to talk on the microphone more frequently in TNA.

> "For me, it was pretty easy. I was just excited to get a microphone in my hand a little bit. Anyone that watched us for the two years we were on SmackDown, we didn't really get that chance too much to do that. For me, I relished every moment that I had that I was able to actually speak. That was awesome. As far as the transition, I think we'll be able to do a little bit more of what we had a chance to do previously, and it's something we wanted to do. We're very ready to do that."

Shaw highlighted their past success as tag team champions in WWE and their desire to evolve their characters in TNA. He noted that the promotion has been receptive to their creative ideas and goals. Syx is scheduled to face the Hardys in a Falls Count Anywhere match at TNA Bound For Glory.
