NXT Women's Champion Kelani Jordan shared some heartbreaking news with her fans, revealing that her beloved guinea pig Pabi has passed away after eight years by her side. Jordan took to her Instagram Stories to share photos and videos of Pabi, noting that he passed away the night before. She recalled getting him during her sophomore year of college as an emotional support buddy, explaining that he had been with her through an entire chapter of her life. Jordan paid tribute to Pabi, writing, "Last night, my little guy Pabi went to guinea pig heaven 🤍 8 years together 🥹" She added, "I got Pabi my sophomore year of college as an emotional support buddy. he’s been with me through it all 💗 hope you get all the chin scratches and celery in heaven buddy, gonna miss your little squeaks 😔" Jordan also addressed anyone who might dismiss Pabi as "just" a hamster, explaining that guinea pigs are social creatures that recognize their owners and form real attachments with unique personalities. She noted that while she now performs in front of thousands of people, Pabi was with her long before that and will be deeply missed.
