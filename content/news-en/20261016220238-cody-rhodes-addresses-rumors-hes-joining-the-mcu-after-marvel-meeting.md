Cody Rhodes recently addressed the rumors surrounding his mysterious meeting with Marvel Studios, keeping fans guessing about a potential role in the MCU. Speaking with Brandon Davis, the former Undisputed WWE Champion noted that people have been throwing out potential characters all day, but he refused to confirm if any of those guesses are accurate. Rhodes explained that he has not discussed the details of the visit, stating that he only shared a photo from a wonderful office after a great meeting with wonderful people. He also mentioned that his connection to Marvel goes back to childhood through trading cards and animated series, adding that anyone visiting their library could easily study up on a character if approached hypothetically.
