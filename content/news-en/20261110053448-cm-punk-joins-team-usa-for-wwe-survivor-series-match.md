CM Punk has officially joined Team USA for an upcoming match at WWE Survivor Series. The announcement unfolded during an in-ring segment that began with a meeting between Team USA captain Cody Rhodes and Team Mexico representative Rey Mysterio. Dominik Mysterio soon interrupted the discussion and asked his father for a spot on Team Mexico, but Rey rejected the request. Dominik then turned to Rhodes to ask for a place on Team USA, which Rhodes also declined.

CM Punk made a surprise appearance shortly after. He joked about his patriotism before asking Rhodes for a spot on Team USA. Rhodes accepted the request, officially adding Punk to the lineup. The segment concluded with Punk, Rhodes, and Rey teaming up to clear Dominik Mysterio and JD McDonagh from the ring.
