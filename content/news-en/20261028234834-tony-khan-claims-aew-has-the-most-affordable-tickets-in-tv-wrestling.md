Tony Khan recently praised the affordability of AEW tickets while promoting the upcoming Blood and Guts special. Speaking with First Coast News, the AEW President discussed the double steel cage match scheduled for Wednesday, November 4, at the VyStar Veterans Memorial Arena in Jacksonville. Khan emphasized that the company offers unbeatable value for live fans. 

"Blood and Guts is a two-ring, double steel cage match with some of the greatest wrestlers in the world fighting it out, and the only way to win the match is to make your opponent submit or surrender," Khan said. "And it is just absolutely as advertised. You're going to see some blood, and you're going to see great wrestlers, great wrestling, and it's going to be hard-hitting. It's going to be exciting."

Khan further highlighted ticket pricing as a major priority for the promotion. "AEW also has the most affordable tickets in TV wrestling. I guarantee you we're not going to take an arm and a leg for this. This is going to be a good night at the wrestling matches, and I am very proud of what we've been able to do here," he added. Tickets for the Jacksonville event are currently available on AEWTIX.com.
