FTR defeated Motor City Machine Guns on the November 7 episode of AEW Collision, tying their best-of-five series at 2-2.

Motor City Machine Guns (Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin) entered the match leading the series 2-1 against FTR (Dax Harwood and Cash Wheeler). Late in the match, Big Stoke created a distraction for Shelley. Dax Harwood capitalized on the moment and secured the pinfall victory via a rollup.

With both teams now tied with two wins each, the high-stakes series moves toward a decisive fifth match to determine the overall winners.
