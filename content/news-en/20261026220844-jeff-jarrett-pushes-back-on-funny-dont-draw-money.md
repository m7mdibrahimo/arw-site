WWE Hall of Famer Jeff Jarrett recently pushed back against the long-standing belief that comedy wrestling does not draw money. Speaking on his My World podcast, Jarrett discussed the humorous pants-ripping spot between Andrade El Idolo and Tommaso Ciampa during their AEW National Championship match at AEW WrestleDream.

Jarrett defended the creative choice, noting the positive live crowd reaction and stressing that creative is subjective. He pointed to his extensive career across multiple promotions to emphasize that if a live audience responds well, the segment achieved its goal. Meanwhile, the rivalry between Andrade and Ciampa continues to escalate following a recent attack on AEW Collision, with speedball Mike Bailey stepping in to interrupt.
