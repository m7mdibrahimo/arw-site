AEW star Anna Jay has confirmed she will undergo shoulder surgery within the next one to two weeks. Jay explained during a Twitch stream that she spent weeks consulting multiple doctors and surgeons to ensure surgery was the correct choice. The injury previously sidelined her during the summer when AEW had planned for her return to television. While the setback is disappointing for the twenty-eight-year-old, she aims to properly fix the issue and avoid future complications.
