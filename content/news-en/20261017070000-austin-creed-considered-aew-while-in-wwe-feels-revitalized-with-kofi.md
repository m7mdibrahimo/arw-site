Austin Creed revealed in an interview at PAX Australia that he previously thought about moving to AEW while still under contract with WWE. The New Level member explained that he wanted to experience a different in-ring style, noting that his roots trace back to the fast-paced action of the X Division.

Creed stated that joining AEW alongside Kofi breathed new life into their careers. The duo recently aligned with Swerve Strickland at AEW All In 2026 to capture the AEW World Trios Championship, and Creed praised the intense atmosphere created by passionate fans as well as the ongoing hostility between Strickland and Hangman Adam Page.

The stable has also begun branching into singles competition within the promotion. Kofi recently competed for the ROH World Championship against Bandido at AEW Dynamite: Grand Slam France.
