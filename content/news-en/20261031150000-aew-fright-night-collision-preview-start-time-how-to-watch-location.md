AEW Fright Night Collision takes place on October 31, 2026, at the Chaifetz Arena in St. Louis, MO, starting at 8 pm ET on TNT and HBO Max. The lineup features Tommaso Ciampa vs. Matt Sydal, The Conglomeration against The Cut, Ilja Dragunov vs. The Beast Mortos, and an Artist of Stardom Championship match between Triangle of Madness and Hazuki, Koguma, and Fuwa-chan. Additional matches include Konosuke Takeshita vs. Adam Priest, Mike Bailey vs. Darius Martin, plus appearances by Hurt Syndicate and Divine Dominion. Fightful provides live coverage every Saturday at 8 p.m. ET followed by a post-show review podcast on YouTube.
