Jim Cornette made it clear that he has reached his limit with AEW, expressing that the Anarchy in the Arena match at AEW Full Gear 2026 left him considering walking away from professional wrestling completely. During a recent episode of his podcast, Cornette delivered a harsh review of the chaotic 38-minute encounter that featured Jon Moxley's Death Riders alongside The Conglomeration taking on Jay White's faction, The Cut.

The chaotic battle included broken glass, fork attacks, a gorilla mascot, outside interference, and a flaming table. The bout concluded when Jay White pinned Jon Moxley to secure the victory for The Cut. Despite the dramatic elements, Cornette remained completely unimpressed, arguing that AEW sacrificed all credibility and believability just to stage an over-the-top spectacle.

Cornette wrapped up his critique by cautioning that the promotion's heavy reliance on dangerous stunts rather than traditional wrestling matches poses a serious threat to the entire industry. He warned that casual viewers might lose interest in the entire genre after witnessing such programming.
