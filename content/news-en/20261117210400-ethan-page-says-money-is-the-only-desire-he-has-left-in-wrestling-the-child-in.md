Ethan Page has accomplished a lifelong goal and captured the Intercontinental Championship on the November 2 episode of Raw by defeating Chad Gable. Speaking with The Sportster, Page revealed that his ego and childhood dreams are now completely fulfilled. He stated that he no longer wrestles for himself or out of love for the business. "It’s great, I can be done. I'm not doing this for myself anymore. I have now sold my soul to corporate wrestling and I will go out of my way to make every dollar that I can to never give back a cent of my time, my personal life, or my physical health to wrestling the second I can leave. I don’t love this, I don’t enjoy doing it. The child in me is completely satisfied," Page said. He added that he now only cares about the paycheck and enjoys being in the company with the most money. "My dreams? Satisfied. I'm just here for money and everything that comes with it."
