Following the passing of AEW star PAC, WWE star and TKO board member Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson paid tribute to his former colleague on social media. While expressing condolences, Johnson also praised AEW and its president, Tony Khan, calling the promotion a phenomenal wrestling company and Khan a good friend. According to a report by Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, many within WWE were surprised by the remarks, and some still feel a lingering sting regarding the praise for a competing promotion. Meltzer noted that Johnson likely meant no harm, simply offering respects due to his deep ties to the wrestling industry, and neither Khan nor AEW mentioned the comments during their tribute show, AEW Dynamite: A Tribute to PAC.
