Velvet Sky recently shared her thoughts on one of TNA’s most infamous segments, a mandatory roster meeting held by Dixie Carter on the November 5, 2009, episode of TNA iMPACT! The meeting, which took place shortly after Hulk Hogan and Eric Bischoff joined the company, was filmed and aired on television, a decision that many found bizarre at the time. In the footage, Carter urged the roster to align with the company's new direction or consider working elsewhere.

After the clip resurfaced on X in 2026, a fan account joked that the wrestlers in the room likely did not care about the speech. Sky, who was present at the meeting, confirmed this, stating, "Not one person. Especially me." When asked why TNA would choose to air such an awkward segment, Sky was blunt, accusing Carter of being a "huge mark for herself." She added, "Because she was a huge mark for herself."
