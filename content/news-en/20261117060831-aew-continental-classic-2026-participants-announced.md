AEW has revealed the lineup for the 2026 Continental Classic. According to Fightful, the round-robin tournament will get underway during the November 18 episode of AEW Dynamite and wrap up at AEW Worlds End to crown a new Continental Champion. Both league finals as well as the title match will occur at Worlds End on December 19, with Jon Moxley holding the title currently.

The Blue League consists of Jay White, Hangman Page, Ricochet, Daniel Garcia, Brody King, and Bandido. Meanwhile, the Gold League features Jon Moxley, Kevin Knight, Orange Cassidy, Nick Wayne, "Speedball" Mike Bailey, and Tommaso Ciampa.

Tournament matches carry a 20-minute time limit, awarding three points for a victory and one point for a draw, while everyone is barred from ringside.
