John Cena recently explained his unique approach to social media, revealing why he tries to follow as many people as possible on X while keeping his Instagram completely blank of follows. Speaking to BuzzFeed UK, the WWE legend noted that his follow spree on X began while he was filming Fast 9 in Kensington, where he noticed people bragging about their follower counts. Instead of showing off how many followers he has, Cena prefers the idea of letting others allow him into their conversations. He explained that he wants to hear different opinions and perspectives that might differ from his own, noting that he would gladly follow around 12 million accounts if the platform actually allowed him to move past its daily limits. Cena also acknowledged that a simple follow from him can put a smile on a fan's face and make them feel heard, making it an easy way to give back to those who have supported him for years.
