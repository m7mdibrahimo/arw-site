AEW star Ilja Dragunov has reflected on his brief in-ring encounter with Brock Lesnar during the men's Royal Rumble match earlier this year. During the match, Lesnar famously no-sold Dragunov's offense before eliminating him shortly after. Speaking in an interview on The Ariel Helwani Show, Dragunov explained his perspective on the moment, noting that his character has always been defined by facing tough obstacles.

> "If you really understand the persona that I have been in the ring most of the time, like, I've been facing monsters. At the end of the day, you can't change certain things. You have a person like Brock Lesnar standing in front of you; most people would run away as soon as that happens, you know?"

Dragunov added that he focused on staying in character and showing mental toughness despite Lesnar's reaction. He emphasized that his character relies on vulnerability and fighting back rather than dominating opponents, which is why he does not view the moment as a defeat or hold any regrets about his time in WWE.
