Full results for WWE NXT Halloween Havoc on The CW and Netflix on October 31, 2026, live from the Performance Center in Orlando, Florida. Danhausen hosted the show. In the opening Weapons Wild Match, Myles Borne defeated Tavion Heights. Backstage, Sean Legacy jump-scared Tristan Angels, and NARAKU and Shiloh Hill brawled before misting each other. Izzi Dame defeated ZARIA. Noam Dar, Romeo Moreno, and Hank and Tank previewed the Dusty Cup Finals in a backstage segment. Robert Stone announced that Vanity Project will face Rey Fenix, Dragon Lee, and Chad Gable. In a Very Nice, Very Evil Match, Zilla Fatu defeated Charlie Dempsey with assistance from Lexis King, Arianna Grace, Uriah Connors, and Channing “Stacks” Lorenzo. Cruz Montana and Dion Lennox had a face-to-face confrontation ahead of their Unsanctioned Match. In the NXT Women's Championship Match, Kelani Jordan defeated Wren Sinclair to retain the title. Shiloh Hill defeated NARAKU. Robert Stone informed Kelani Jordan that she will defend her championship against a mystery opponent. In the NXT Championship Match, Grayson Waller defended his title against Tony D'Angelo. Fightful's post-show podcast streamed on YouTube and Twitch.
