The NFL is partnering with CMLL for a special event at Arena Mexico on Wednesday, November 18. This show, titled Nights of Helmets and Masks, serves as a celebration of the upcoming game between the Minnesota Vikings and the San Francisco 49ers, which takes place at Banorte Stadium in Mexico City on Sunday, November 22. The event is set to feature top talent from the Mexican wrestling scene. Tickets for the show will be available for purchase starting Friday, October 16.
