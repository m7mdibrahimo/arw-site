Kizuna Tanaka is preparing to make her return to the ring. In January 2026, Tanaka wrestled at a Dream Star Fighting Marigold event, where she suffered a fractured and dislocated left ankle after taking a diving body move. Following months of recovery and emergency surgeries, she is officially scheduled to compete again. Tanaka will face Shinno at the Marigold Grand Destiny event on October 24. At 21 years of age, she has been wrestling since 2023, making appearances for Sareee-ISM, Sendai Girls', STARDOM, NOAH, and WAVE, and has been with Marigold since its launch in 2024.
