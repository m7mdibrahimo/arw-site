Cody Rhodes is looking to expand his professional involvement with WWE beyond his status as a top performer. During a recent appearance on the What Do You Wanna Talk About? podcast hosted by Chelsea Green, Rhodes explained that he frequently visits the company headquarters to sit in on meetings unrelated to creative direction to better understand the organization's internal operations.

Rhodes admitted he has actively searched for a corporate role to fill, though he has not yet identified a suitable position. He emphasized that his interest is not in influencing television storylines, but rather in learning about project management and the external vendors the company utilizes. Green shared similar sentiments, noting that her own interactions with staff revealed various specialized roles within the company that she previously did not know existed.

Rhodes also discussed advice from Bruce Prichard, who tells visiting wrestlers that the office staff works for them. Rhodes interpreted this as an encouragement to ask questions and engage with the business side of the company. While he has not secured an office role, Rhodes is clearly preparing for a future beyond his in-ring career.
