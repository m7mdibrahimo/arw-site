Looking back at his famous 2016 Talking Smack promo with Daniel Bryan, WWE star The Miz revealed that he genuinely thought he was going to get fired after the heated exchange. During the segment, Bryan called Miz a coward and criticized his wrestling style, which led Miz to fire back with a memorable line about Bryan being the one who would never wrestle in a WWE ring again. The shocking comment caused Bryan to walk off the set, while Miz feared he was about to get punched or fired. However, moments later, he received positive text messages from Kevin Dunn and Vince McMahon, who loved the segment and praised the performance. Miz noted that the interaction helped elevate the Intercontinental Championship into a main event-level title, though he admitted he was already having a rough day after being drafted to WWE SmackDown and finding out he was only scheduled for a dark match.
