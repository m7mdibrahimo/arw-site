Carmelo Hayes shocked WWE fans when he joined the 946 faction on the October 12 episode of WWE RAW. According to a report from False Finish, this alliance was planned months in advance. WWE reportedly had Hayes penciled in for the group as early as July. Hayes had been absent from television since his loss to Baron Corbin on the August 14 episode of WWE SmackDown, which ended his pursuit of the United States Championship. His return on October 12 saw him attack Solo Sikoa with a tire iron before joining Bronco Nima, Lucien Price, and Royce Keys to end the show. This suggests his absence was not a lack of creative direction but a long-term plan for his debut with the faction.
