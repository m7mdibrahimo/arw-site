WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green suffered a loss to Roxanne Perez on a recent episode of WWE RAW, followed by a post-match beatdown from Perez, Liv Morgan, and Raquel Rodriguez. While the angle aimed to build toward the upcoming Green versus Morgan match at Crown Jewel, Bully Ray expressed confusion during an appearance on Busted Open Radio. He questioned why Green, as a current world champion, had to lose the match in the middle of the ring, noting that it seemed to halt her momentum. Bully Ray argued that if Perez needed to be protected or kept strong, she should have won the women's Money in the Bank match instead, and he was frustrated that Green lost even after showcasing her wrestling ability.
