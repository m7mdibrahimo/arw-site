WrestleMania will head outside of North America for the first time in history when WrestleMania 43 takes place in Saudi Arabia. TKO CFO Andrew Schleimer shared details about the agreement during the company's third-quarter earnings call.

> "There are no KPIs in the agreement on visitation. Our partners in Saudi do retain the box office. That does not ignore our benefit, unlike some of the other models we're seeing now that the FIP effort has been developed more as we go to market. We get paid. We've received half our fee already. Despite where folks come from or what that sellout looks like in terms of demos, we're getting paid."

Regarding the broadcast, Schleimer noted that the event is part of the existing deal with ESPN. 

> "On the partnership side, this is an event that was legislated in our deal with ESPN that will be US prime for a WrestleMania PLE. In terms of the media value, we are very excited that we will be able to sell through to our existing partners that will get exposure as if this were a regular PLE. It may start just a bit earlier than some PLEs, but no issue on the partnership side. We will be working closely with our partners on selling local partnerships and sharing revenue with them, but otherwise, nothing to be concerned about there."

No official date has been set for WrestleMania 43.
