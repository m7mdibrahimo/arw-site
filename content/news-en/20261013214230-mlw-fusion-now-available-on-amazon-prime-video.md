Major League Wrestling announced that MLW Fusion is now streaming on Amazon Prime Video to broaden its global audience. Supporters can buy full seasons for $26.99 or separate episodes for $1.99, with options to stream or download them across various devices.

> "MLW belongs on the biggest platforms, and Amazon Prime Video is another significant step in that direction."

MLW Founder and CEO Court Bauer noted that the launch helps fans follow major rivalries and bouts. Meanwhile, the promotion is preparing for SuperFight on November 7, which marks Tessa Blanchard's MLW debut.
