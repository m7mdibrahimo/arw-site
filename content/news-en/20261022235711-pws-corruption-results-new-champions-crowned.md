Pro Wrestling Symphony held its PWS Corruption event on October 16 at 6th & Peabody in Nashville. The show was broadcast on IWTV.

In the opening match, Brian Hitt defeated Erron Wade. Following that, the team of Joiya Blake and Hyena Hera, known as Creature Feature, defeated The Goons.

In a title unification match for the PWS Tag Team Championships and the ACTION Tag Team Championships, Top Team (Jay Lucas and Terry Yaki) defeated Royal Prestige (Majestic and TeVon Jordyn) to become the new PWS Tag Team Champions.

In individual competition, Kenzie Paige defeated Bobbi Thunder. In the main event, Tim Bosby defeated Colby Carter and Adrian Thomas to win the PWS Championship.
