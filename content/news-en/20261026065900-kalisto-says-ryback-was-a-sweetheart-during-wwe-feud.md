Former WWE talent Ryback has faced a controversial reputation since leaving the company, partly due to his aggressive social media presence. Looking back at their 2016 feud over the WWE United States Championship, Kalisto recalls a very different experience working with him. "Oh, my God, man, he was nothing but a sweetheart," Kalisto said during a recent interview with TMZ Sports. "I do get asked that a lot, too, and I always say, 'Man, he was like the best human being on earth with me.' He was awesome, he took care of me, I took care of him." Kalisto believes Ryback was frustrated with his own career trajectory at the time, but noted that Ryback was the one who specifically requested to work with him. The former champion promised to take the heat to help Kalisto get over during their program. "Man, we just clicked. When we got in the ring? We just clicked! ... When we started working with each other, man, just things started flowing," Kalisto added. He considers Ryback one of his favorite opponents in WWE, noting that their contrasting sizes made for a satisfying physical style. "I loved working with him," Kalisto concluded. "I wish I can work him again."
