Although the recent double taping of AEW Dynamite and AEW Collision in Utah appeared seamless to fans, Dave Meltzer reported that the Chris Jericho Rock N' Wrestling Rager at Sea caused significant logistical challenges. According to Wrestling Observer Radio, the difficult travel schedule for talent returning from the cruise to Salt Lake City forced AEW to rearrange plans at the last minute.

> "A lot of the show was done on the fly and rearranged because they were having trouble with getting the people from docking yesterday to get to Salt Lake City."

Despite these issues, many stars from the cruise made it to the event, including Mercedes Moné, Andrade El Idolo, and Persephone. All previously advertised matches proceeded as scheduled.
