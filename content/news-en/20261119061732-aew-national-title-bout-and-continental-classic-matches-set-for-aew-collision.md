AEW announced new matches for the upcoming episode of AEW Collision, featuring continued Continental Classic tournament action. In Blue League competition, Ricochet faces Jay White, while Brody King takes on Daniel Garcia. Gold League action features Kevin Knight battling Tommaso Ciampa.

Additionally, Ilja Dragunov is set to defend his AEW National Championship against El Clon. This title match was scheduled after Dragunov refused to join the Don Callis Family in recent appearances.
