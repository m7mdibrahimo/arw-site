Dawn Marie remains deeply critical of her former ECW rival Francine, stating that their past backstage conflicts continue to impact her perspective today. During an appearance on Velvet Ropes, Dawn Marie contrasted her positive experiences in WWE with the hostile environment she encountered in ECW. She explicitly stated her disdain for Francine, noting that she would not help her even in a dire situation.

> "Like I truly had no problems in WWE and, and, and, you know, listen, there’s no secret, like I hate Franny, you know. Wouldn’t piss on her if she was on fire."

Dawn Marie claimed that Francine made her time in ECW miserable because she felt threatened. She argued that while she focused on improving her wrestling skills, Francine spent her energy trying to push other women out. According to Dawn, Francine originally targeted Tammy Sytch, which allowed Dawn to establish herself before becoming the next focus of Francine's hostility.

> "By the time Tammy screwed up and was gone, I already had established myself, right? So I wasn’t going anywhere. So now she then tried to make it where it was so uncomfortable and miserable that I would leave, or that I would do something politically incorrect so that I would get thrown out as a response, you know?"

Dawn Marie alleged that Francine would often refuse to speak to her directly, instead communicating through others like Lance Storm. This behavior led Dawn to become protective of other women joining the promotion. The two were both present for the 2024 WWE NXT event at the former ECW Arena in Philadelphia, where Dawn Marie served as a referee for a match between Lola Vice and Jaida Parker.
