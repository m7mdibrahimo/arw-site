Carmella is regretting a recent cosmetic procedure after finding it difficult to move her upper lip. Speaking on her Hot Mom Sh*t podcast, she revealed that she decided to get a Botox lip flip ahead of her 39th birthday, but things did not go as planned. She explained that she received a little more Botox than usual, which left her unable to properly control her upper lip. She admitted that she has been constantly trying to massage and move her lips in hopes of making the Botox wear off faster, but she now faces months of waiting for the effects to fade. She issued a simple warning to anyone considering the same procedure, saying she regrets it and advises others against it.
