The U.S. State Department has issued a new travel warning for the Middle East due to potential flight cancellations, airport closures, and escalating attacks, just weeks before WWE Crown Jewel in Riyadh. The warning highlights hostilities from Iranian-backed Houthi forces against Saudi Arabia, which have previously targeted civilian airports and critical infrastructure. Despite the security concerns, WWE is still planning to hold the event on November 7 at King Khalid International Airport, though some international flights remain suspended and other major sporting events in the region have been postponed or canceled. WWE's parent company, TKO, faces pressure to keep the event on schedule, and while the company has a backup venue in the United States, no relocation has been announced as preparations continue.
