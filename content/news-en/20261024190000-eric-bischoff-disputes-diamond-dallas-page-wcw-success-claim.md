While many fans credit the Diamond Cutter as the main reason for Diamond Dallas Page achieving immense popularity in WCW, WWE Hall of Famer Eric Bischoff has a different perspective. Speaking on his podcast, Bischoff debated the true catalyst behind the success of the star whose move inspired Randy Orton's RKO.

> "I know, for Page, that finish, in his mind, is what made him; I disagree"

Bischoff acknowledged that the finisher was impressive, but he insisted that the real turning point was when Page dropped theatrical gimmicks and simply displayed his authentic self to the audience. By shedding those older personas, Page connected genuinely with fans, which ultimately made his finishing maneuver feel much more impactful after he had already won over the crowd.
