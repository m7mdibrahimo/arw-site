Former WWE EVOLVE star Veronica West has opened up about why she decided to abandon the Fallyn Grey ring name after leaving the company. West explained that Fallyn was the character she created when she first entered professional wrestling, spending years building that identity on the independent scene. However, after signing with WWE ID and becoming Veronica Haven, she had to conform to creative demands and could not continue using the original name following her release.

Despite having the option to return to Fallyn Grey, West chose not to, citing constant mispronunciations and comparisons to other wrestlers. She wanted a fresh start with a name she could truly make her own. Her new persona, Veronica West, combines elements of Fallyn Grey and Veronica Haven with a more vulnerable side, reflecting her journey through loss and rebuilding.

West recently confirmed that her October 7 match against Airica Demia marked her final appearance in WWE EVOLVE, following her departure from the WWE ID program in July. She is now heading back to the independent scene, eager to continue her career under her own terms and connect with fans who supported her through her time with the promotion.
