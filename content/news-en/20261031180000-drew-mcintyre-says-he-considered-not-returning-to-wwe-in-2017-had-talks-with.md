Drew McIntyre has revealed that he was close to signing with NJPW before making his return to WWE in 2017. After rebuilding his character in various promotions following his initial departure, McIntyre considered remaining outside WWE. In an interview on The Joe Vulpis Podcast, he explained that when his time with TNA came to an end, he was open to moving to Japan. However, a conversation with Triple H changed his mind, convincing him that returning to WWE was the right choice. McIntyre noted that within two minutes of speaking with Hunter, he realized it was time to come back. He returned after three years away, and after reaching out through William Regal, he ultimately rejoined the company via WWE NXT.
