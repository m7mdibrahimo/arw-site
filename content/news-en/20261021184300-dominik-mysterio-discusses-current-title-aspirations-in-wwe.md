Dominik Mysterio has spoken about the various championships in WWE and his opportunities if he decides to pursue them, including tag team titles. He currently lacks a championship belt following his loss of the MEGA Championship to El Grand Americano at AAA TripleMania.

During a new interview with Bleacher Report, Dominik outlined the titles he is targeting in WWE. He mentioned a desire to resolve his issues with Roman Reigns and recalled his past rivalry alongside Sami Zayn and Kevin Owens as part of The Judgment Day. He also expressed interest in pursuing the Intercontinental Championship or teaming with JD McDonagh to chase the tag team titles.

In a related interview, Dominik recalled the attack he suffered from Brock Lesnar during a shared segment, confirming he had no prior knowledge of the assault.
