Motor City Machine Guns star Chris Sabin recently expressed his strong belief that All Elite Wrestling boasts the greatest tag team division in the history of professional wrestling. Speaking in a new interview, Sabin reflected on sharing the ring with iconic tandems like Adam Copeland and Christian Cage, as well as his longtime rivals, the Young Bucks, noting that the talent roster in AEW is unmatched. Following their signing with the company earlier this year and their involvement at AEW All Out, Sabin and Alex Shelley are currently focused on their high-profile best-of-five series against FTR.

> "It was a pretty surreal moment, especially, like, being out there with, like, Adam Copeland and Christian Cage. Like, these are two guys that I watched growing up. So then we have them. We have the Young Bucks out there who was, like, if anyone knows the history of the Motor City Machine Guns, were tied with the Young Bucks. You know, we've wrestled them so many times. TNA, Independents, PWG, everywhere. You know, we've never wrestled FTR before. Never had a match. So that was a cool moment to be out there with them. Yeah, man, I don't know. I just think AEW has the greatest tag team division in the history of wrestling. I think that you can't argue that, and we're just excited to get in the mix with everyone, you know?"
