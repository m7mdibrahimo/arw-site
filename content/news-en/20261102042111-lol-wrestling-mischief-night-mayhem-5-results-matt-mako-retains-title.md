Labor of Love Wrestling hosted its LOL Wrestling Mischief Night Mayhem 5 event on October 30 at Thunderbird Hall in Philadelphia, PA. The show was broadcast on YouTube. In the main event, Matt Mako successfully defended his Labor Of Love Citywide Championship against Sé Morrigan.

Lee Moriarty def. JuleZee
Frankie B def. Sloane Jacobs
The Mecca def. Big Callux in a Casket Match
Allie Katch def. RULLO, Rajan Husher, 2Die4 (Shannon LeVangie, Paris Van Dale, & Armani Kayos), Vinny Talotta, Sean Henderson, Magnifico, Kingsley, Gabby Ortiz, Kongo, Jose Zamora and The Crusher in a Ghouls, Goblins, and Gimmicks #1 Contenders Match
Citywide Street Gang (Martin Hughes & Tom LaRosa) def. The Outfielders (Shea McCoy & Weber Hatfield) to win the Commonwealth Cup
Matt Mako def. Sé Morrigan to retain the Labor Of Love Citywide Championship
