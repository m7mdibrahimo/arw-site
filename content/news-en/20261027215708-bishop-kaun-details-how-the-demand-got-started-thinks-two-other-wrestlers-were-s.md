Bishop Kaun recently discussed the formation of The Demand, the AEW faction featuring himself, Toa Liona, and Ricochet. During an appearance on The Certified Mark Show, Kaun explained how he and Liona ended up aligning with Ricochet. 

> "Honestly, we forced his hand," Kaun responded when asked how he and Toa Liona were paired with Ricochet. "We saw him cutting this promo. It was like, hey, he’s looking for people. He wants to start this group, he needs a faction, and Toa and I were just looking at the landscape. We were like, ‘Okay, here’s possible people that could be in this group with him.’ Nah, that’s ours for the taking. Fortunately, we’re having tag matches at that time. Every time Toa and I wrestle, our backs are against the wall. We can’t mess up. We have to show out every single time. We feel like we are still not respected after being here for how many years. So we had a match; Ricochet was on commentary. ‘Hey, we gotta show out for this guy. We need to show him and we’re gonna take this opportunity, and no, we’re in this group with him.’ So, literally, we had our match; we get to the back. A producer pulls us aside. He’s like, ‘Yeah, it’s y’all three. He wants you. You impressed him,’ so, yeah, I know we impressed him. We were beating ass out here so, we truly forced his hand. I think it was supposed to be two other guys — nah — we stepped over them, got our spot."

Kaun noted that he believes two other wrestlers were originally intended to be in the group, though he suspects they are currently part of the faction's current on-screen alliance with ROH World Six-Man Tag Team Champions Lethal Twist.
