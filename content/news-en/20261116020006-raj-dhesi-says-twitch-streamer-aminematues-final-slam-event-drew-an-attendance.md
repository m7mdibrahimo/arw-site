According to Raj Dhesi, the Final Slam wrestling event, hosted by Twitch streamer AmineMaTue on November 14, 2026, drew a reported attendance of 18,000. The show, which aired live on Amine's Twitch channel, took place at the Plenitude Arena in Nanterre, France. The event featured several stars, including Raj Dhesi, AEW’s Jordan Oliver, and various French wrestlers and content creators. In the main event, Amine defeated French YouTuber Squeezie to become the inaugural Final Slam Champion. Raj Dhesi, who issued an open challenge, defeated French YouTuber DJILSI. Other results included Anyme and Mecca defeating Brawks and Kuro, and GregMMA defeating IbraTV. Nate Prince won the Final Rumble match by eliminating Maxime Biaggi.
