Enzo Amore and Joe Alonzo were backstage at the November 4th episode of WWE NXT. The show, which aired on The CW, was moved to Wednesday due to midterm election coverage. Fightful Select reports that both Amore and TNA Wrestling's Joe Alonzo visited the WWE Performance Center during the event. Reports from earlier this year indicated that Amore had also tested the waters with AEW.
