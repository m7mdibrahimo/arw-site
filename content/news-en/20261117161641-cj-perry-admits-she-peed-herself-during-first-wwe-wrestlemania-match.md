CJ Perry has revealed an embarrassing moment from her early career, admitting she urinated in the ring during her first match at WrestleMania 32. Appearing on The Joe Vulpis Podcast, the former WWE Superstar, widely known as Lana, explained that the massive crowd of 106,000 fans at&T Stadium left her extremely nervous. The incident occurred on April 3, 2016, in Arlington, Texas, during a 10-woman tag team match where she teamed with Naomi, Tamina, Emma, and Summer Rae against Brie Bella, Paige, Natalya, Alicia Fox, and Eva Marie. Perry admitted she lost control while performing a move in the ring and spent the match hoping no one noticed.

Reflecting on her time in WWE, Perry contrasted her anxiety with Rusev's calm demeanor before his match against John Cena at WrestleMania 31. She noted that as she gained more experience, the nerves became easier to handle, though stepping back into the ring today would likely bring back the same anxiety. While she has spent years away from in-ring competition, Perry has not ruled out a return to WWE, noting she would prefer a manager or on-screen role over chasing championships.
