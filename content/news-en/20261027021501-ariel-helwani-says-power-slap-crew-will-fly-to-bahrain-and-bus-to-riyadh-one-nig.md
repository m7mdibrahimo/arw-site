Potential travel plans for WWE Crown Jewel have emerged as Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport has faced closures and limited flights following recent security events. According to Ariel Helwani, the Power Slap production crew is scheduled to fly into Bahrain and take a five-hour bus ride to Riyadh one night before WWE Crown Jewel, unless the local airport opens up further. It remains unclear if World Wrestling Entertainment will adopt a similar travel arrangement for its talent and staff ahead of the event. Meanwhile, TKO CEO Ari Emanuel has confirmed that officials are closely monitoring the situation as the scheduled date approaches.
