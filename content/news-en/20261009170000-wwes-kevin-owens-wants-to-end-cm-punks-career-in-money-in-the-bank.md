Kevin Owens has made it clear that his primary goal in the upcoming men's Money in the Bank ladder match is to end the career of CM Punk. The two wrestlers have a long-standing rivalry that extends beyond their time in WWE. While acknowledging the other talented competitors in the match, Owens admitted that his focus remains entirely on targeting Punk.

"Well, you know, there are five other competitors in the match, but for me, there's really just one other competitor," Owens said in an interview with WWE. He added, "One of them, the biggest one, is to hopefully end scumbag CM Punk's career. I don't like him. I will never like him. I don't want him around."
