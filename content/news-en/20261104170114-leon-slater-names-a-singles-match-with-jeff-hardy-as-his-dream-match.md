Leon Slater has revealed that his ultimate dream match in professional wrestling is a singles bout against Jeff Hardy. Slater has climbed the ranks in TNA, capturing both the X-Division Championship and the TNA World Heavyweight Championship, and has recently aligned with The Hardy Bros. Speaking in an interview with The Extreme Channel, Slater explained that despite checking off many milestones, he has yet to face Hardy in a singles match. When asked about a potential match with Kazuchika Okada, Slater noted that it could happen one day.
