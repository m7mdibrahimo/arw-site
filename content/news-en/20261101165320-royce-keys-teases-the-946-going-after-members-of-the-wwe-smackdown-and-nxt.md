Royce Keys has teased that The 946 could target members of the WWE SmackDown and NXT rosters in the future. Since forming on the main roster, the group consisting of Royce Keys, OTM, Carmelo Hayes, and Jaida Parker has left no one safe. While the faction is currently focused on entering War Games to face The Bloodline, they also have broader plans. In a recent interview with Soundsphere Magazine, Royce Keys discussed their goals for the remainder of 2026, noting their primary focus on Roman Reigns and The Bloodline, while also hinting at potential appearances on SmackDown and NXT. "Yeah, War Games, we definitely have our eyes on that. We have our eyes on a lot of things, mainly The Bloodline, Roman Reigns. So, you know, we like to keep it short and sweet, and, you know, ain't nobody safe, so I can be on Raw, SmackDown, NXT. It doesn't matter," he said.
