A major matchup is confirmed for WWE Crown Jewel. World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns will face WWE Champion Sami Zayn, with the winner earning the Crown Jewel Championship.

Last year, Seth Rollins defeated Cody Rhodes to win the title. Additionally, WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green will battle Women's World Champion Liv Morgan for the Women's Crown Jewel Championship.
