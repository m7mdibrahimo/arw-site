Sheamus might have just given wrestling fans a reason to keep an eye on AEW Full Gear after requesting an early departure from a convention on the same day. According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, Sheamus is scheduled to appear at Big Event NY on November 14, which is the same day AEW will hold Full Gear. Meltzer reports that Sheamus asked this past week whether he could leave early to attend a wedding. While there is no confirmation linking his request to AEW, the timing has sparked heavy speculation. Sheamus officially became a free agent after his WWE contract expired in early October, and TNA has also expressed interest. Although no deal has been reached yet, veteran names within AEW have previously pushed for his signing.
