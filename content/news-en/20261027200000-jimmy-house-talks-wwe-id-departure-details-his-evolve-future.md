Jimmy House discussed his departure from WWE ID in a new interview with Fightful's Sean Ross Sapp, confirming that he was released from the program a little over a month ago. House noted that he has been trying to build momentum since returning to the independent circuit and using the lessons he learned during his time with WWE ID. He also revealed that he has several matches and segments that will appear on future episodes of WWE EVOLVE, noting that it was his first true television experience and expressing gratitude for the opportunity to apply those skills moving forward.
