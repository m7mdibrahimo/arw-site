During the November 2 episode of WWE Raw, John Cena made a live appearance to reveal key details regarding the John Cena Classic tournament. Cena confirmed that the special event will take place on December 14 at Walt Disney World in Orlando, Florida, as a special episode of WWE Raw. This broadcast will follow a separate WWE Raw show airing earlier the same day from Perth, Australia.

Cena unveiled the prize for the tournament winner, presenting the new WWE John Cena Championship belt. The title features a design reminiscent of Cena's signature spinner championship from past years. He also noted that subscribers to Club WWE will have a role in voting for the tournament outcome.

The tournament features main roster stars competing against talent from WWE NXT. Cena will appear on WWE NXT this week to announce the NXT participants, but he confirmed Cody Rhodes as the first official entrant from the main roster. Additional participants will be revealed in the coming weeks, including at WWE Survivor Series in Houston, Texas. Randy Orton then interrupted to warn Rhodes about their upcoming Last Man Standing match at WWE Crown Jewel.
