AEW star Kyle Fletcher recently discussed the idea of writing a book about his professional wrestling career. While speaking on his latest Protolog, Fletcher noted that he believes he could write a strong memoir, jokingly adding that he has plenty of people to criticize. "I think about that a lot. I'm probably, I think I'm gonna write a pretty good book. Done a lot of shit to talk about. A lot of people to throw under the bus. I don't actually hate anybody, read the book to find out," he said.
