David Otunga has completely changed his perspective on the lawsuit involving Montez Ford after reviewing new camera angles. The former WWE star and attorney previously leaned toward Ford and WWE, but now believes the plaintiff has a much stronger battery case than initially thought. Otunga pointed out that the new footage shows what appears to be repeated contact, which makes it harder to argue the interaction was accidental. He also noted that Ford's positioning, which he described as straddling, strengthens the argument regarding restricted movement under the Illinois Gender Violence Act. While Otunga believes the new evidence gives the plaintiff significantly more leverage and makes a potential settlement more realistic, he emphasized that proving liability and securing substantial damages remain separate legal battles. Regarding WWE, Otunga stated that Ford improvised the interaction rather than receiving approval beforehand, but noted that lack of pre-approval does not automatically protect the company from responsibility if the action occurred within the scope of work.
