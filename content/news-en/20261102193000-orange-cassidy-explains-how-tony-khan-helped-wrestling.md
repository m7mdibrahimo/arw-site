AEW star Orange Cassidy discussed the major role Tony Khan has played in developing the pro wrestling industry, stating that his entry into the field benefited everyone.

In an interview with STL Sports Central, Cassidy expressed that he is fortunate to work under someone who loves wrestling so much, explaining that Khan opened the door for audiences in America to watch different styles of wrestling from around the world.

He added that having a boss who puts wrestling first makes working there wonderful, praising Khan's intelligence and his management vision that allowed diverse styles to be presented in AEW.
