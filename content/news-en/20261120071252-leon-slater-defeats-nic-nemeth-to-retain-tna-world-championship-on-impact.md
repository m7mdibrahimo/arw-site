Leon Slater retained the TNA World Championship by defeating Nic Nemeth in the main event of TNA iMPACT. The rematch came together after Trey Miguel expressed his respect for Slater earlier in the night and seemed set to request another title clash. Ryan Nemeth and Nic Nemeth interrupted to mock and attack Miguel, prompting Slater to run in for the save and establish the headlining bout.

Nemeth came out firing with an immediate headbutt and superkick in an attempt to finish the champion quickly. Slater weathered the initial storm and turned the clash into a competitive back-and-forth contest. Outside interference flared up when Ryan Nemeth tried to get involved, but Miguel chased him up the ramp to neutralize the threat.

The closing moments turned chaotic after the referee took a bump. Nic Nemeth grabbed a steel chair to gain an illegal edge, but Elijah made a surprise run-in and shattered a guitar across Nemeth's back. Slater capitalized as Nemeth stumbled back into the ring, hitting a direct kick to the face followed by the Swanton 450 to secure the pinfall victory.
