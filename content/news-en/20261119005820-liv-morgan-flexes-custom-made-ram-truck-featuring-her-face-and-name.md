Liv Morgan has made it clear that her Tesla Cybertruck didn’t turn out to be the dream purchase she expected. Now she has another massive truck featuring her name and face. Ram Trucks uploaded an Instagram video showing Morgan with a customized red truck that includes her face, eye decals, and name in the design. Morgan posed in front of the vehicle, while Ram simply wrote in the caption: "No promo needed."

This new ride comes after Morgan admitted earlier this year that her Tesla Cybertruck was her worst investment. During an April appearance on the You Wish podcast, she revealed that she barely drives the vehicle she purchased in 2024, calling it her biggest waste of money. This time, Morgan didn’t have to design the truck herself, and with her face and name prominently displayed, there is no doubt about who owns it.
