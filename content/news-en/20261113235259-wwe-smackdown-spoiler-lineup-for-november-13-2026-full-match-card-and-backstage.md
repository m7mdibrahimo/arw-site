WWE SmackDown is scheduled for November 13, 2026, featuring a variety of matches and rivalries. According to Viper Reports, the show includes several segments and bouts designed to shift the current landscape of the promotion. The card features John Cena addressing the WWE Universe, a WWE Tag Team Championship match between The MFTs and Bakusai, and a singles match between Finn Balor and Gunther. Additionally, Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn and WWE Women’s Champion Tiffany Stratton are both set to address the WWE Universe. Damian Priest is also scheduled to face Axiom. Backstage notes indicate that CM Punk is reportedly being groomed to replace Shawn Michaels as the head of WWE NXT, and AJ Styles’ role within the brand is being clarified amidst these changes. As always, plans remain subject to change before the broadcast goes live.
