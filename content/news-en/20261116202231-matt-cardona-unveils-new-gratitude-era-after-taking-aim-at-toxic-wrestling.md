Matt Cardona is shifting his perspective on his WWE return, moving past the criticism to launch what he calls the "Gratitude Era." In a video posted to X, Cardona addressed those who label his current run a disappointment, noting that while he has lost more matches than he has won and remains far from his goal of becoming WWE Champion, he refuses to see his return as a failure. Cardona reflected on his 14-year journey to become a WWE Superstar, expressing appreciation for every moment of that experience. He explained that after being fired, he made a conscious decision to prove himself on the independent scene rather than dwell on bitterness. "Zack Ryder needed to die for Matt Cardona to live," he said, highlighting his growth during his five years away from the company. Now back in WWE, Cardona is choosing to view every loss as a lesson rather than a setback. He emphasized his desire to move away from the negativity prevalent on social media and within the wrestling industry. "There's so much negativity online, in the world, and in WWE, but I'm not gonna add to it. I'm gonna change it because I'm grateful. I'm grateful to be here, for every opportunity, for being a WWE Superstar." With this new mindset, Cardona is officially moving on from past eras and focusing on a more positive outlook. "It's time to start focusing on what we do have, not what we don't have. It's time to usher in a new era in WWE — the Gratitude Era."
