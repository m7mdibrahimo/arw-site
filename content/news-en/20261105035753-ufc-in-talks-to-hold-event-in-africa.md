TKO Group Holdings President Mark Shapiro confirmed that the UFC is actively discussing the possibility of hosting its first-ever event in Africa. During the 2026 TKO earnings call on November 4, Shapiro stated that the organization recently returned from a trip to the continent to explore potential venues and logistics.

> "Lawrence Epstein at the UFC, we have just returned from a trip to Africa, where we are discussing staging our first-ever UFC event. So more to come on that…" The UFC currently has upcoming shows scheduled for Doha, Qatar, on November 21, followed by a trip to Riyadh in December.
