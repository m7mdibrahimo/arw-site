Cody Rhodes believes that the earning potential for WWE Superstars is reaching new heights, noting that top-tier pay is now comparable to that of major professional athletes. During an interview on "Flagrant," Rhodes discussed the evolution of WWE's compensation structure, crediting the insight of company President Nick Khan.

> "Nick Khan had a great quote about 'These guys are going to do well. These guys are going to do really well and then these couple guys, they're going to do next level.' And that next level pay is now comparable with sports."

Rhodes expressed pride in the profession, noting that while wrestlers have historically been recognized globally, they previously lacked the income levels of those in the NBA or Major League Baseball. He stated that under the modern TKO leadership and Nick Khan, that gap has closed significantly. Rhodes described current payouts as "very good" and noted that WWE contracts now offer guaranteed amounts, adding that his late father, Dusty Rhodes, would be "shocked" by current earnings. Since his 2022 WWE return, Rhodes has become a main event staple, holding the Undisputed WWE Championship three times and currently navigating a high-profile feud with Randy Orton.
