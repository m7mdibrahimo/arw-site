JCW wrestler Paige Collett celebrated reaching 56 days without sugar by sharing new photos of herself in black lace underwear on X. Collett used the post to raise awareness about Complex Regional Pain Syndrome, writing, "🧡Today’s Day 56 without sugar #crpsawareness 🧡". She has previously shared that cutting sugar helps her manage chronic pain from the condition, which she says was triggered by a training injury under WWE Hall of Famer Rikishi. Collett also recently defended her use of medical marijuana, explaining that cannabis plays a major role in helping her manage pain and continue her wrestling career.
