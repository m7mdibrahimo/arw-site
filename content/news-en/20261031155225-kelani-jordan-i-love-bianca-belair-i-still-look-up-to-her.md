Kelani Jordan discussed who inspired her in the professional wrestling industry, noting that she loves and still looks up to Bianca Belair. While speaking in a new interview with Kron 4, Jordan explained her background and her wrestling influences. "Well, I never… One, I didn't know how to become a wrestler, so I didn't even think I could do it, and I would say, one, yes, I took a lot of inspiration from… Like, I love Bianca. Like, I looked up- I still look up to her. Becky Lynch, I loved, and also RVD, Rey Mysterio, Shawn Michaels, a lot of inspirations, Eddie Guerrero. But yeah, I just really never knew how to become a wrestler. So I couldn't ever say like, oh, when I grew up, I want to be a wrestler because I didn't even know it was possible. So at the time when I grew up, when I was like, 'oh, when I grow up,' I wanted to be an Olympic gymnast. That was like my goal," Jordan said. Jordan also mentioned that she originally aimed to become an Olympic gymnast before discovering professional wrestling.
