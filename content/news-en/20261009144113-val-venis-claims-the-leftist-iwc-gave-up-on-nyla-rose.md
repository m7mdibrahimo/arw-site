Val Venis recently returned to social media to target Nyla Rose once again, claiming that the online wrestling community has abandoned her. In a post on October 7, Venis wrote that the leftist IWC gave up on Nyla Rose, continuing a pattern of criticism that spans several years. Venis previously faced heavy backlash in 2024 for controversial comments about Rose, including remarks made after Dustin Rhodes defended her against online scrutiny.

Meanwhile, Rose has taken a broader approach to addressing hostility and misconceptions surrounding transgender individuals. During an interview in September, she explained that many people form negative opinions based on misinformation and political rhetoric before ever meeting a transgender person. She noted how inaccurate portrayals often shape people's reality, though she typically chooses not to respond directly to Venis and remains focused on her career in AEW.
