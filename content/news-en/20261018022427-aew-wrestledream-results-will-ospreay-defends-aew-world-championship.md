AEW WrestleDream took place on October 17, 2026, at the Addition Financial Arena in Orlando, Florida. In the main event, Will Ospreay defended his AEW World Championship against Samoa Joe, Mike Bailey, Adam Copeland, Konosuke Takeshita, Kazuchika Okada, Ricochet, and Darby Allin in a Clockwork Carnage Match. Mercedes Mone successfully defended her AEW Women's World Championship against Thekla. The Young Bucks retained their AEW World Tag Team Championship against Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight. Persephone defended her TBS Championship against Hyan, Queen Aminata, and Dani Luna in a Four-Way Match. Jon Moxley successfully defended the Continental Championship against Mark Davis. Andrade El Idolo retained his National Championship against Tommaso Ciampa. Nick Wayne defeated Chris Jericho. In the Saturday Tailgate Brawl, The Motor City Machine Guns defeated FTR in the first match of a Best of Five Series, The Death Riders and The Dogs defeated The Bang Bang Gang and The Conglomeration, and Divine Dominion defeated Sisters of Sin.
