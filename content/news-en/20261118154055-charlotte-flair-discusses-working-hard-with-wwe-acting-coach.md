Charlotte Flair recently discussed the possibility of exploring acting outside of professional wrestling and noted that she hopes to feature in more projects in the near future. Flair previously appeared in Psych: The Movie in 2017 and is set to star as a serial killer in an upcoming indie horror film titled You Lose You Die. During an interview with Screen Chatter, she revealed that she has been putting in significant effort with her acting coach through WWE.

> "No, so I've been…Really working hard with my acting coach through WWE. His name is Ignacio, so I'll give him a shout out, Nacho. So I hope one day that something happens for me. Yeah, I'd love to."
