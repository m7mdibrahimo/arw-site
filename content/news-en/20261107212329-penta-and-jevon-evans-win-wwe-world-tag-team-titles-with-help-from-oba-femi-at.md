Penta and Je’Von Evans captured the WWE World Tag Team Championship at WWE Crown Jewel. The Vision (Bron Breakker and Austin Theory) defended their titles in the second match of the event, which featured interference from Maxxine Dupri and Bronson Reed. The champions appeared to have the advantage until Oba Femi arrived at ringside. Femi took out Reed, creating a distraction that allowed Penta to leap from the top rope onto Breakker. Inside the ring, Penta delivered a Mexican Destroyer to Theory, followed by an OG Cutter from Evans to secure the victory.

Penta and Je’Von Evans are now the new World Tag Team Champions.
