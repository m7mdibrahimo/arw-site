Paige Collett opened up about a terrifying health scare during an appearance on Headlocks, Heels, & Havoc: Wrestling Unscripted, recalling how taking just three bumps in a wrestling ring last summer sent her straight to the emergency room with severe heart palpitations. She stated that medical staff feared she might be going into cardiac arrest. However, instead of receiving proper care, Collett claims the ER staff had never heard of complex regional pain syndrome (CRPS), the chronic condition she has battled for years, and suspected she was merely seeking prescription drugs.

Collett explained that the experience was the most frightening moment of her life, especially after spending nearly seven years and enduring four surgeries on her feet and legs trying to get a proper diagnosis following a training injury under Rikishi. She revealed that she often had to advocate fiercely for herself while dealing with the intense chronic pain. Rather than opting for ketamine treatments, Collett has chosen medical marijuana to manage her symptoms, noting that it saved her life and urging people not to judge those living with chronic pain for how they choose to cope.
