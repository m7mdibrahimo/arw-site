Bubba the Love Sponge believes a private 42-second recording of Hulk Hogan may reveal that Hogan and his wife, Sky Daily, were living separately before his death. On his YouTube channel, Bubba played a clip of Hogan speaking with a woman he identified as Daily. During the conversation, Hogan mentioned an alleged stroke, and Bubba noted that Daily seemed completely unaware of the health issue. Bubba stated he had been told that Hogan and Daily were staying at different residences, with Hogan living on El Dorado and Daily residing on Devon Street with her children. Bubba argued that if they were in a healthy, cohabitating marriage, Daily would have known about such a significant medical event. Bubba suggested that Hogan may have been living largely alone with caretakers, while Daily lived elsewhere, only visiting occasionally. Bubba admitted this is his personal interpretation based on what he heard, but he believes her reaction on the recording confirms that the couple may have been estranged.
