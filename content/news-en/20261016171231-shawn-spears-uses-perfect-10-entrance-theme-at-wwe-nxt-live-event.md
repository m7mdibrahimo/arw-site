Shawn Spears treated longtime WWE fans to a nostalgic moment by using his classic Perfect 10 entrance music during an NXT live event in Kitchener, Ontario, on October 15. Spears participated in an eight-man tag team match, joining Shiloh Hill, Tavion Heights, and Tony D’Angelo to face BirthRight.

https://x.com/ringsidenews_/status/2111097284599697576

Before the match, Spears surprised the crowd by walking out to the iconic theme associated with his Tye Dillinger persona, which gained massive popularity in 2015. Since returning to WWE in 2024, Spears has performed under his real name and recently formed The Culling with Niko Vance and Izzi Dame. It remains unclear if this was a one-time tribute to his past or a hint at a character shift for television.
