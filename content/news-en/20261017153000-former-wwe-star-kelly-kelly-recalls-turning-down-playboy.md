Former WWE star Kelly Kelly recently recalled turning down an offer to model for Playboy during her early days with the company. Speaking on the "LayTalk" podcast with former colleague Layla El, Kelly explained that she was only 21 at the time and ultimately decided against it because she worried the photos would haunt her and appear on the internet if she decided to have children. Although her family supported whatever choice she made, she stuck with her initial decision and focused entirely on her wrestling career. That dedication later led her to capture the WWE Divas Championship by defeating Brie Bella at a 2011 event, holding the title for 104 days before dropping it to Beth Phoenix at Hell in a Cell 2011. Though she left WWE in 2012, she has made several special appearances over the years.
