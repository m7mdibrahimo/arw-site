WWE already has a major match in mind for WrestleMania 43, but fans hoping to see Cody Rhodes face CM Punk might be disappointed. According to Dave Meltzer in the latest Wrestling Observer Newsletter, Roman Reigns versus Oba Femi is currently planned for the event, while a match between Rhodes and Punk is not on the schedule right now. Meltzer noted that there has been plenty of discussion about booking Rhodes versus Punk for Night One and Reigns versus Femi for Night Two, but he was told that only one of those matchups is actually locked in. Rhodes versus Punk would make sense since both stars compete on SmackDown and have been kept apart since their SummerSlam match on August 1, but management is not pulling the trigger on it yet. Meltzer also reminded fans that it is only October, meaning plans could easily change multiple times before the show due to injuries or other creative shifts. WrestleMania 43 is expected to take place on April 10 and 11, 2027, in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.
