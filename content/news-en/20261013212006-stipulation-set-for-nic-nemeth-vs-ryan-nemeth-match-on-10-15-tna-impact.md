Matt and Jeff Hardy are set to run the October 15th edition of TNA iMPACT, the first show following Bound for Glory. The Hardys have announced that the main event will feature TNA World Tag Team Champions Nic Nemeth and Ryan Nemeth facing each other. There is a high-stakes stipulation attached: if either brother intentionally loses the match by count-out, disqualification, or by laying down for the pin, both will be stripped of their TNA World Tag Team Titles. Nic Nemeth enters this match after losing his TNA World Championship to Leon Slater at Bound for Glory.
