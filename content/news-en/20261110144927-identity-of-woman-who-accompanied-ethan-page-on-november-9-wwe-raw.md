Ethan Page arrived at the November 9 episode of WWE Raw accompanied by Tommi Janae Roberts, a singer and member of the band Daisy Chains. Roberts later acknowledged her appearance on Instagram Stories, sharing a photo from the show and writing: "Tonight on Netflix." Page arrived before the show in a car surrounded by four women and later walked to the ring with several women. Once in the ring, he bragged about beating Chad Gable and mocked Gable's confidence, identity, and family. Gable, Big Cass, and Rey Fenix then interrupted him to demand title opportunities. Adam Pearce booked a Triple Threat Match to determine the number one contender, which Fenix won. Meanwhile, Roberts' unannounced cameo added an unexpected crossover element to Page's celebration.
