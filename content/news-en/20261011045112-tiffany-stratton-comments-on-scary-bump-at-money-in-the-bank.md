Tiffany Stratton reflected on the dangerous landing she experienced during the Women's Money in the Bank ladder match. Stratton made history by becoming the second woman to win the briefcase twice, but the victory came at a physical cost after she executed a Swanton Bomb off a ladder and hit her head hard on the floor. Following a quick check by the referee, she insisted on finishing the match.

Speaking on the post-show, Stratton admitted that she is still dealing with the physical aftereffects of the high-risk maneuver. "Oh my gosh, I haven't even recovered yet. I'm sitting here, my neck still hurts, but I wanted to do anything it took to get that briefcase. So, I told medical, 'I was okay. I want to still power through this match and get through it.' Because what's a ladder match without Tiffy in it? So, I powered through it and we're back in the end," she said.
