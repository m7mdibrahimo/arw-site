Logan Paul revealed in his latest vlog that he no longer believes in Joe Hendry, stating that he finally realized how much of a jerk he is. This comes amid their ongoing feud, which began with Hendry's song Fire Logan Paul, a track that frustrated the social media star. Despite Paul being out of action with an injury, their rivalry has escalated significantly since Hendry joined the main roster. In his video, Paul remarked:

> "Dude, I used to believe in Joe Hendry, not anymore. I used to, yeah, and then I realized what a dick he is. Bro, has he not learned? You can't fire me, bro. Regardless, I'm gonna go end this man with my mouth."
