John Cena recently reflected on one of the most bizarre rumors he has ever seen about himself online, recalling the time the internet falsely claimed he had passed away. Speaking in an interview, Cena joked about the strange experience of waking up and reading reports of his own death just to get clicks and likes. He noted that while the misinformation was wild, he simply had to look around to confirm he was still alive and well, realizing how strange it is to check facts on your own obituary without needing any public statements to clear things up.
