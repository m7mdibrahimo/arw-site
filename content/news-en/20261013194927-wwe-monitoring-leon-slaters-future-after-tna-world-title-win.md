Leon Slater recently secured the biggest victory of his career by winning the TNA World Championship. According to PWInsider, WWE is already monitoring his future as his current TNA contract is expected to expire at the end of 2026.

There are two primary scenarios being discussed regarding his next move. Slater could potentially sign a short-term extension with TNA to enjoy a meaningful run as World Champion before eventually moving to WWE NXT. Alternatively, he might drop the title quickly and join WWE as soon as his contract concludes.

Many within WWE have long expected Slater to eventually land in NXT. The main uncertainty remains the timing of his arrival, whether that occurs at the beginning of 2027 or after he has spent more time building his legacy in TNA.
