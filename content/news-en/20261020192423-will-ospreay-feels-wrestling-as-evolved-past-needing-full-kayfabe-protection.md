Will Ospreay feels that wrestling has evolved past needing full kayfabe protection in modern shows. Speaking in a recent interview, Ospreay discussed how professional wrestling presents its stories today compared to decades past, noting that fans no longer expect complete secrecy regarding the business.

While comparing wrestling to cinematic universes like Marvel and DC, Ospreay explained that audiences readily accept scripted storytelling without demanding strict kayfabe maintenance. However, he also expressed reservations about certain backstage documentaries and television specials revealing too much of the inner workings, such as timing cues and commercial breaks, which he believes diminishes the magic of the performance.
