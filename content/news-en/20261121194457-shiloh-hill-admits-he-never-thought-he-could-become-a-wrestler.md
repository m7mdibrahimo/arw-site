Shiloh Hill, one of the rising stars on WWE NXT, revealed that he never imagined a professional wrestling career was possible for him despite growing up as a passionate fan of the company. During an appearance on Good Karma Wrestling, the former Stanford football player explained that the television personalities seemed entirely different from an ordinary person.

> "Oh, I never thought I was going to be able to do it."

He recalled wondering how a regular kid could fit in when performers had bizarre backgrounds or unusual previous occupations. The concept never crossed his mind until the organization reached out directly to ask if he wanted to enter the industry, leading to his immediate acceptance.

Before chasing his wrestling dreams, Hill walked away from a software development position at Tesla after completing his studies in psychology, computer science, and sustainability science at Stanford. That major life change brought him to the WWE developmental system, where he competed on WWE LFG under the mentorship of The Undertaker. He eventually triumphed in the second season of WWE LFG and earned an NXT contract.

Hill is now getting ready for his upcoming bout in the John Cena Classic on December 14, 2026, following a personal invitation delivered by Cena on NXT.
