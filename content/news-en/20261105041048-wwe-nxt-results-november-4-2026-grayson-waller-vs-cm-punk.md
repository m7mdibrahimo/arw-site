Welcome to Wrestling Inc.'s coverage of WWE NXT on November 4, 2026, coming to you live from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida. Grayson Waller defended the NXT Championship against WWE SmackDown star CM Punk in the main event. Kelani Jordan successfully defended the NXT Women's Championship against Paige in an open challenge. Romeo Moreno and Noam Dar defeated Hank Walker and Tank Ledger to win the 2026 Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic, with Cody Rhodes presenting the trophy. Cruz Montana faced Dion Lennox in an Unsanctioned Match with Mike Chandler as the special guest referee. Chad Gable, Dragon Lee, and Rey Fenix defeated Vanity Project in a Six Man Tag Team Match. Additionally, John Cena made an appearance to announce the competitors for the upcoming John Cena Classic.
