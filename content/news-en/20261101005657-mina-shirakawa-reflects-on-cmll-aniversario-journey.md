Mina Shirakawa recently discussed her career progression within CMLL. In an interview with Fightful's Luis Pulido ahead of her match at the October 30 CMLL event, she shared her perspective on transitioning from a fan in the crowd at the 2018 CMLL Aniversario to a competitor at the 2026 edition.

> "When I watched the Aniversario (in 2018), I couldn’t imagine I would wrestle in the ring. During the show, I was just like, “Oh, wow, everybody is fantastic, everybody flies, jumps so fast; maybe they are not human” (*chuckles*). So I could not imagine that. Wrestling, sometimes, makes miracles."

> "The last time I entered the ring at Aniversario (2026), I almost. cried because of my emotions. Finally, I'm in the ring of an Aniversario, so yeah, that day I was very nervous. Sometimes, that kind of miracle, that kind of thing happens, so I love wrestling, you know. Some things I cannot imagine happen, so I love wrestling."

Mina Shirakawa competed against India Sioux at the October 30 CMLL event.
