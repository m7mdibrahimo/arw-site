According to Ringside News, Hijo de Fishman has been freed from custody following an agreement with the family of referee Gerardo Germán Pineda, also known as El Búho, which completely halts the criminal proceedings against the wrestler.

El Patrón Espectáculos CEO Edgar Sandoval and the legal team announced the release, confirming that the agreement included compensation for the incident and successfully closed the judicial file without requiring further investigation.

The situation began at an event in the Centro Deportivo Cultural 11 de Julio in Mineral de la Reforma, where the official suffered a fatal medical emergency, leaving the wrestler free ahead of a planned press conference.
