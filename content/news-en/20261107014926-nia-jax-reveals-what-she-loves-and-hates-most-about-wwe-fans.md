Nia Jax spoke candidly about her relationship with WWE fans, explaining that the exact same people who make her job enjoyable are also the ones who frustrate her. Speaking on the Keep It Aloha podcast, she gave the same answer when asked about her favorite and least favorite parts of professional wrestling.

Jax stated that the audience is vital to the success of shows, noting that the COVID-19 pandemic clearly demonstrated that live crowds make up the vast majority of what wrestlers provide inside the ring. She loves their energy and interaction, but simultaneously dislikes the harsh criticism that the same audience directs at performers.

Despite the difficulty of handling constant criticism regarding her performance and appearance, Jax noted that she accepts it as a natural part of working in the spotlight. She added that demanding travel has given her the chance to experience numerous cultures, emphasizing that crowd interaction in both its positive and negative forms remains the core element that creates the excitement of WWE.
