Tiffany Stratton's second Money in the Bank victory sparked significant online backlash, but according to a new report, the reaction was part of WWE's long-term strategy. Speaking on a Self Made Session, Self Made Pro revealed he was told that WWE anticipated the frustration over Roxanne Perez's loss. The company reportedly views this negative fan response as a success because their goal is to establish Stratton as a heel.

> "Another thing that was said to me was that they knew that this would get some fan online heat on Tiffany Stratton, which is what they want because apparently the long-term plan for Tiffany is to be a heel."

WWE allegedly wanted to avoid a predictable outcome to generate genuine heat for Stratton while simultaneously increasing fan support for Perez.

> "The gist was basically, we wanted to do a thing because people expected the logical thing, and we want this to make people mad and then get mad at Tiffany Stratton and also get more on the side of Roxanne Perez."

Self Made Pro noted that this explanation was provided to him directly following the event. It remains to be seen if this intended heel trajectory for Stratton will unfold as planned in the coming months.
