TNA Wrestling referee Michael Romo’s departure from the company appears to be tied to an alleged personal conduct violation. According to Fightful Select, Romo was escorted out of the company's recent television tapings in Calgary and will not be returning.

TNA confirmed on November 20, 2026, that Romo was no longer with the promotion, releasing him alongside George Iceman, though the company used noticeably different wording regarding the referee's exit. Sources indicated that a business Romo ran had served as a sponsor for TNA, creating a business relationship that extended beyond his officiating work.

TNA's original statement offered no details and stated that the company would not be commenting further on the matter. The exact nature of the alleged personal conduct violation remains undisclosed.
