Roman Reigns reflects on his WWE career and admits he wishes he had more confidence to push back against creative decisions during his early days on the main roster. During an appearance on Club Shay Shay, Reigns discussed how he lacked the leverage needed to reject ideas when he was still trying to prove himself. He specifically pointed to the infamous 'suffering succotash' promo from over a decade ago, noting that fans still bring it up today despite his subsequent success. While he wouldn't change much overall, Reigns acknowledged that there are a few promos, segments, and match finishes from his past that he would handle differently today given his current influence and status in WWE.
