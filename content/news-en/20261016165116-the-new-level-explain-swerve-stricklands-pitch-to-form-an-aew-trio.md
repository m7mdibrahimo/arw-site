The New Level quickly adapted to All Elite Wrestling as Austin Creed and Kofi currently hold the AEW World Trios Championship alongside Swerve Strickland. While both stars knew Strickland prior to signing with AEW, the former AEW World Champion assisted them significantly in settling into their latest positions.

Speaking with Uncrowned, Creed explained that Strickland originally pitched joining forces as a trio. Beyond contending for championships, the goal was to help both newcomers get acclimated immediately, with Creed detailing how the trios division allowed them to square off against numerous top tandems right away.

The champions will defend their AEW World Trios Championship this Saturday at AEW WrestleDream 2026 against Hangman Page and Brodido. The contest marks the first time a Two-out-of-Three Falls stipulation will be utilized for a trios bout in AEW history. The titleholders previously defeated their challengers last month at AEW All Out 2026.
