Dann Read recently highlighted the significant positive influence Kay Lee Ray has on Pro Wrestling EVE. According to Fightful, Read noted that her presence elevates the quality of the promotion's events and fosters a superior environment within the locker room.

> "You're only limited in wrestling by the creativity of those involved, and so when you're working with someone in our case, you know, like, like Kay Lee Ray, who is.. she is a creator. She comes up with so many ideas and she's always thinking, I can't tell you how much it means to have Kay Lee Ray back in EVE. It's like, she's still living in the States, but we're flying her in every single show because like Kay Lee Ray and EVE just works, just feels right. I'm not saying that like we didn't do great work without her, but we certainly do better when she's with us, because that's just that it's that would be that no matter the promotion, no matter what promotion Kay Lee Ray is a part of, you do better work when you have Kay Lee Ray in your locker room and you have a much better locker room as well."

Since departing WWE earlier this year, Kay Lee Ray has returned to the independent circuit in the United Kingdom. She has since become a staple performer for the all-women's promotion.
