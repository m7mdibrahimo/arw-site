Ilja Dragunov recently discussed the visa renewal process that allowed him to make his debut at AEW WrestleDream. Appearing on The Ariel Helwani Show, Dragunov explained that while the procedure took about a month, the company provided full support. He recalled receiving the notification about his passport in the middle of the night while talking to his girlfriend, wrestler Lady Leigh, and immediately arranging his quick return to the United States.
