Game Changer Wrestling hosted GCW Shotzi Blackheart's Drive-In From Hell on October 25 from the Brownsville Drive-In Theater in Grindstone, Pennsylvania. According to Fightful, the show streamed live on TrillerTV+, featuring a variety of singles, tag team, and stipulation bouts.

Kavron Kanyon def. Zach Thomas, Blizzy Blake, Beck Reeves and Felix Koz in a Scramble Match.
Juni Underwood def. Leon King.
Bustah & The Brain def. The Legion Of Rot.
Vipress def. Dani Mo to retain the GCW Ultraviolent Championship.
Ryan O’Neill def. Sal Mistretta.
VNDL48 def. Facade Squad.
SLADE def. Priscilla Kelly.
Conor Claxton def. Jimmy Lloyd.
Shotzi Blackheart & Lio Rush def. Janelasus in a Doorway To Hell Match.
