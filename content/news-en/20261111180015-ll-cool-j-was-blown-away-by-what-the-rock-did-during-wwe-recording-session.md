The Rock left LL Cool J completely stunned during a WWE recording session after knocking out an entire award acceptance speech on his first try without needing to watch playback. According to Jonathan Coachman, his backstage duties during his early WWE career included recording promotional material and acceptance speeches for stars who could not attend ceremonies. Coachman explained that The Rock understood timing better than almost anyone, often stretching his catchphrases to fit any required duration.

That ability was put on display when LL Cool J happened to be visiting Coachman's recording room to promote a movie. The Rock walked in and learned he needed to record a 60-second acceptance speech for an entertainment award he won in Europe, rather than flying overseas. After getting the details, The Rock delivered the speech and adjusted his catchphrase to land perfectly within the allotted time, then walked out immediately without checking the recording. LL Cool J was left in absolute disbelief, noting that the same task would have taken half a day in Hollywood and questioning how he could skip reviewing the footage. Coachman explained that it came down to trust and experience, noting that The Rock was already working like a seasoned professional long before his movie career took off.
