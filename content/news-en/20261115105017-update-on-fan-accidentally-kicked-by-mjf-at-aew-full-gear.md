MJF made a surprising return at AEW Full Gear on November 14, but his exit brought an unintended accident when he hopped the barricade after attacking Will Ospreay. As he swung himself over, his foot accidentally caught a female fan in the face and knocked her down. Sean Ross Sapp provided an update on the fan's condition, with sources within AEW indicating that she was alright.

> "Early indications from people at AEW were that the fan that got accidentally kicked by MJF at Full Gear was okay and laughed the situation off."

The incident followed Will Ospreay's victory over Kyle Fletcher to retain the AEW World Championship. A masked attacker then ambushed Ospreay from behind, and MJF unmasked to stand over him with his Triple B championship belt.
