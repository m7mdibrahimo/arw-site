Adam Copeland may be moving toward a darker character arc in AEW. During the November 4 episode of AEW Dynamite, the conclusion of the Blood & Guts match appeared to set the stage for a potential heel turn. Following the match, Will Ospreay was visibly frustrated by Copeland's decision to surrender, creating immediate tension between the two.

The match itself was chaotic, with Kyle Fletcher and the Don Callis Family gaining the upper hand. Fletcher gave Copeland an ultimatum: surrender or watch his teammates suffer. Copeland chose to submit, but that did not prevent Wardlow from throwing Darby Allin off the top of the cage, rendering Copeland's sacrifice useless. Dave Meltzer and Bryan Alvarez on Wrestling Observer Radio suggested that this finish was designed to establish Kyle Fletcher as a top contender while simultaneously pushing Copeland closer to a villainous role. If his teammates hold him responsible for the loss, Copeland's frustration could easily evolve into resentment, providing AEW with a clear path to turn his character against those he tried to save.
