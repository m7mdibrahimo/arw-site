All Elite Wrestling celebrated the Halloween season on Wednesday, October 28, with the Fright Night edition of "AEW Dynamite" in St. Louis, Missouri.

Queen Aminata def. Persephone
Darby Allin def. Josh Alexander

Kyle Fletcher also challenged Will Ospreay to a match for the AEW World Championship, setting up the main event for AEW Full Gear 2026.

Regarding television ratings, Wrestlenomics confirmed that the Fright Night episode averaged 620,000 viewers. This marks a 6% drop from the 661,000 viewers of the previous week and an 18% decline from the trailing four-week average of 758,000 viewers. It stands as the lowest rating since the Grand Slam France special that aired on a Tuesday and the lowest in the regular Wednesday timeslot since the Rebel Heart special on September 9.

Despite this decrease, the current average for October and the fourth quarter of 2026 sits at 665,000 viewers, which is 38% above the October 2025 average and 34% higher than the fourth-quarter average from last year.

In the key 18-49 demographic, the program drew a 0.14 rating, falling 7% from the 0.15 rating recorded seven days prior. This special was also 18% lower than the trailing four-week average of 0.17.

The show still secured second place for the night in its timeslot behind the NBA, according to Dave Meltzer of the Wrestling Observer. Meltzer additionally reported that the broadcast achieved a 0.21 rating in the 25-54 demographic.
