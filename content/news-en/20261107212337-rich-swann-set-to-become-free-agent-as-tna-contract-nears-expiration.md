TNA Wrestling may be preparing to part ways with a major name before the year concludes. Former TNA World Champion Rich Swann is approaching free agency, and there is currently no indication that he has signed an extension with the promotion. According to Fightful Select, Swann's short-term contract is scheduled to expire on December 31, 2026. The 35-year-old performer, who returned to the company during the summer of 2025, has remained a fixture in the championship picture throughout 2026, competing for the TNA International, World, and X-Division titles. Despite his active role in TNA programming, the lack of news regarding a new deal has left his future uncertain as the promotion heads into 2027. Swann, who previously captured the WWE Cruiserweight Championship during his tenure with WWE, has experience across multiple major promotions, giving him significant leverage should he decide to test the open market.
