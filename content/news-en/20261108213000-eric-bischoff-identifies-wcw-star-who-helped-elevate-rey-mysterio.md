Rey Mysterio is widely regarded as one of the most prominent figures in professional wrestling today, but when he joined WCW in 1996, he was simply a newcomer trying to establish himself. During a recent episode of '83 Weeks,' Eric Bischoff discussed Mysterio's early days and pointed out that his feud with Dean Malenko over the Cruiserweight Championship was the key to helping the young luchador find his footing on American television. Bischoff stated that Malenko brought out the best in Mysterio and helped him make a massive impact. The rivalry spanned from Mysterio's debut at Great American Bash 1996 through Halloween Havoc that same year, leaving a lasting impression on viewers. Bischoff also argued that Malenko's contributions and the early cruiserweight division's influence are often underrated. Additionally, Bischoff noted that he personally preferred Malenko over Mysterio during their time in WCW because Malenko felt more believable to him. Mysterio has previously expressed respect for Malenko, aligning with Bischoff's assessment of their dynamic inside the ring.
