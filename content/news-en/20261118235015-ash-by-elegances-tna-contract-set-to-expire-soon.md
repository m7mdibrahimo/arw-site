Ash By Elegance is approaching free agency as her current contract with TNA Wrestling is scheduled to expire on December 31, 2026. The former TNA Knockouts World Champion confirmed the expiration date during an appearance on TMZ Inside The Ring while discussing her pregnancy and future in professional wrestling. She revealed that TNA has told her she can return and renegotiate whenever she is ready.

> "They are like, ‘Whenever you are ready, just know that the door is open for you. Just know that you are able to come in and we can renegotiate, we can work on your contract.’ I am under contract until December 31st of this year, and then my contract is expired."

Ash is currently expecting her first child in January 2027, following major personal changes. Despite her contract ending, she expressed a strong desire to return to the ring, noting that becoming a mother has given her even more motivation to wrestle again. Her husband is fully supportive of her ambitions, and she believes the TNA locker room will welcome her son when she comes back.

Ash previously relinquished the TNA Knockouts World Championship in 2025 before signing a new agreement in January 2026. With just weeks remaining on her deal and no confirmation of a new one, she is set to enter 2027 without a contract, though TNA remains open to working with her in the future.
