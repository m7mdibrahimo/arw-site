David Finlay and Clark Connors revealed that they originally wanted to use the name Raw Dogs when arriving in AEW, but avoided it due to WWE's Monday Night Raw. Speaking on the What Happened When podcast with Tony Schiavone, the former Bullet Club members discussed their time in NJPW and how they ultimately settled on the much simpler name The Dogs. While they liked the edge that the original name carried, they realized the connection to the WWE flagship show would present a clear problem for their television run.
