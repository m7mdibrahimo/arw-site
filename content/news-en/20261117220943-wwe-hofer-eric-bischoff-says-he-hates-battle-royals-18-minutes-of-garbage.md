Eric Bischoff made it clear that he is not a fan of Battle Royals while discussing them on his "83 Weeks" podcast. Bischoff did not hold back his criticism of the match style, calling them "18 minutes of garbage" and stating that nobody looks good during these bouts. He explained that with so many competitors in the ring, wrestlers simply walk around looking for a spot to get noticed, which lowers the overall quality and makes it difficult to showcase true character work. Bischoff also reflected on World War 3, the WCW pay-per-view he produced from 1995 through 1998 featuring a three-ring Battle Royal, admitting that the concept sounded much better on paper than it actually was in reality.
