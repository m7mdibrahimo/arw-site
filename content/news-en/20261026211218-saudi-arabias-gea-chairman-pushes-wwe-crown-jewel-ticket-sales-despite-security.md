WWE Crown Jewel is still set to take place in Saudi Arabia, and Turki Alalshikh is actively promoting ticket sales for the event. Despite growing security concerns surrounding the November 7 trip to Riyadh, the chairman of the General Entertainment Authority released a new promotional package featuring the Roman Reigns vs. Sami Zayn match for the WWE Crown Jewel Championship. This marks the first time he has directly called on fans to buy tickets for this year's show. The timing stands out as safety questions persist following a U.S. State Department order for non-emergency government employees and their families to leave Saudi Arabia due to Houthi-related risks. While WWE officials continue to monitor the situation, TKO executive Ari Emanuel confirmed that a team is tracking developments daily and considering alternative locations if necessary. However, preparations in Riyadh are moving forward without any changes to the schedule.
