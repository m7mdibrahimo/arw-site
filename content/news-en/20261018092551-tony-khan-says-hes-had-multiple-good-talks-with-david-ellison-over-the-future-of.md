Tony Khan recently spoke about the future of AEW under the Skydance banner following the merger of Warner Brothers and Paramount, with David Ellison at the helm. Speaking at the AEW WrestleDream press conference, Khan was asked if he had spoken with Ellison about the future of AEW. He confirmed having several great conversations with Ellison, noting that the new leader is very excited to partner with AEW. Khan highlighted that having Warner Brothers and Paramount under the same roof brings exciting opportunities, increased resources, and a stronger Warner Brothers. He added that Ellison, who is a wrestling fan and around his same age, wants to keep TBS, TNT, and Warner Brothers streaming under the same roof, which aligns well with AEW's presence on HBO Max, TBS, and TNT. Khan expressed great enthusiasm for the company's future under Skydance.
