Jaxson Ryker recently stated that WWE's former black-and-gold NXT brand achieved so much success that Vince McMahon could not stand it. Speaking on Developmentally Speaking, the former WWE star explained that the creative freedom provided by Triple H and Shawn Michaels created a product completely different from the main roster. Ryker recalled his run from 2017 through the pandemic as a golden era, noting that NXT felt like a legitimate third brand rather than a standard developmental territory. According to Ryker, McMahon was unhappy with the high quality of the shows produced by Triple H's team. He contrasted the collaborative environment in NXT, where talent had a voice in their music and match layouts, with the micromanagement he later experienced on WWE RAW and WWE SmackDown.
