TNA Wrestling is coming to Sport1 in Germany starting November 3, 2026. The network will air the wrestling shows weekly at 10:15 p.m. on free-to-air TV, featuring a German commentary duo consisting of Denis Pötzsch and Walandi Tsanti. Andreas Gerhardt, Chief Content & Distribution Officer, noted that the partnership allows the brand to reach both die-hard fans and new viewers. Commentator Denis Pötzsch expressed excitement about his return to calling TNA action, while Walandi Tsanti brings his years of experience from WWE SmackDown to the microphone. TNA will bring over 20 years of history and a dynamic modern product to German television screens.
