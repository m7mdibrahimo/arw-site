In a recent interview with TVInsider, AEW star Samoa Joe opened up about the emotional atmosphere backstage following the tragic passing of both Pac and Andy "The Butcher" Williams just weeks apart. Joe explained that the locker room has been leaning on one another to process the profound loss.

> "There's no real established protocol beyond how anybody grieves amongst friends and loved ones. I think in times like this, we try to be there for each other and available for that late-night call if you need to talk about it or something like that."

Joe noted that the impact of these losses has been felt throughout the professional wrestling industry, serving as a stark reminder of the precious nature of life. He believes that maintaining their work schedule has provided a necessary outlet for the talent to cope.

> "I think, if anything, we're grateful that we even have the opportunity to have the show go on."

AEW recently honored both men with tribute shows, including 10-bell salutes. Williams was remembered with a 12-man battle royal on "AEW Collision," while the "AEW Dynamite" tribute to Pac featured a 10-man tag team match between his former Death Riders stablemates and The Don Callis Family. Joe commended AEW President Tony Khan for his support during this difficult time.

> "I think in these times we band together. We definitely cherish and have a better understanding that our time on this earth is precious. We should tell the people that we care about them, and we love them when we get the opportunity."
