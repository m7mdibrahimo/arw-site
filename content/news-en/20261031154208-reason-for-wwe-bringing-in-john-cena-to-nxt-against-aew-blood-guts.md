WWE is ensuring that NXT avoids a lopsided ratings defeat against AEW Dynamite and its major Blood & Guts special by bringing in John Cena, according to Dave Meltzer on Wrestling Observer Radio.

While discussing the Wednesday night showdown, Meltzer explained that NXT had to move from its usual Tuesday slot because of election coverage on The CW, placing it directly against one of AEW's biggest annual events.

According to Meltzer, WWE President Nick Khan was worried about how poorly NXT might perform against Blood & Guts since AEW was airing on its regular night.

> "Nick said, you know, I mean, I heard, you know, he was like, Nick says, we got to — we can't go in, you know, they don't want to go in there and get killed."

To prevent an embarrassing loss, WWE turned to Cena as one of its most powerful television attractions.
