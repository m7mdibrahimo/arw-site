Tony Khan has officially welcomed Ilja Dragunov to the AEW roster. Shortly after Dragunov made a surprise appearance at AEW WrestleDream on October 17, 2026, the AEW President confirmed the signing on social media.

> "He has arrived in AEW tonight at #AEWWrestleDream, and now it’s official: @UNBESIEGBAR_ZAR is All Elite!"

Dragunov was introduced by Renee Paquette at the event, where he walked to the ring before departing without a match or promo. His arrival had been anticipated for weeks, following mysterious vignettes and a report from PWInsider that he would appear at the show. Dragunov became a free agent in August after his WWE contract expired, with his AEW debut delayed due to visa processing. While no immediate storyline has been announced, Dragunov is now officially part of the AEW roster.
