Nic Nemeth recently compared his current run as TNA World Champion to his previous world title reigns in WWE. During an interview with TVInsider ahead of his defense against Leon Slater at TNA Bound for Glory, Nemeth reflected on his second WWE World Heavyweight Championship reign. He recalled that after cashing in his contract, Vince McMahon bluntly told him he was only keeping the belt warm for a few weeks so Alberto Del Rio could regain momentum. Nemeth contrasted this experience with TNA, where management openly expresses full support for his work, media appearances, and ability to deliver strong main events.
