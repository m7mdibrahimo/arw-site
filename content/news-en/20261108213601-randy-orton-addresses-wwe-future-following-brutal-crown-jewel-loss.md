Randy Orton may have left WWE Crown Jewel battered and bloodied, but he is not planning on retiring anytime soon. After a fan reached out to him on X following his loss, Orton offered a clear update on his status. He stated that he is not finished and plans to return. "Whether it’s sooner, or later, I’ll be back. Guaranteed. WWE is my home away from home. I still have more to do, and until I am unable, I will continue to keep doing it."

Orton's comments followed a brutal Last Man Standing Match against Cody Rhodes in Riyadh. The two men used steel steps, kendo sticks, trash cans, tables, and a ladder, before Rhodes eventually left Orton unable to answer the referee’s ten-count. Rhodes even used a Punt Kick to secure the win. Despite the physical toll of the match, Orton interacted with fans before heading backstage, where footage showed him wearing an Arabic shemagh. While he did not specify when he will return, his promise leaves little doubt about his intentions to keep competing.
