Mark Davis recently spoke about the matching attire he and Jake Doyle wore during the Blood and Guts match on the November 4 episode of AEW Dynamite. Appearing in an interview with PWTorch, Davis reflected on the surreal experience of competing in the double steel cage structure, noting that the fireworks and chaos quickly prompt a shift into work mode.

Addressing their choice of matching dungarees and white shirts, Davis explained that it was a practical decision rather than a planned gimmick. "That's just what we usually wear. That's what we show up to work in, so we just thought we'd just wear that for the match. No, I think we just knew we were gonna get nitty and gritty and get dirty, so we just wanted to wear the appropriate attire," he said. Davis and Doyle are scheduled to challenge for the AEW World Tag Team Championships at AEW Full Gear.
