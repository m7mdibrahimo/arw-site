WWE may not be done with the Iron Survivor Challenge after all. Although the match is absent from the December NXT schedule, reports indicate that the company has considered moving the stipulation to the main roster. WrestleVotes Radio on Fightful Select noted that the match has been discussed for main-roster use in the past, though its exclusion from this year's NXT calendar does not confirm that a move is imminent.

One potential path for the match involves current main-roster storylines. According to a source, the Iron Survivor Challenge could fit into several ongoing narratives, including the Undisputed WWE Championship picture. WrestleVotes also reported that Bruce Prichard is a fan of the concept and had previously suggested it could serve as a unique match type for the main roster. Whether the company decides to implement the match remains to be seen, but the concept is clearly still on the table.
