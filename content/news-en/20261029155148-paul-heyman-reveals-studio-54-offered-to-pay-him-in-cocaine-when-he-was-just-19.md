Paul Heyman revealed that when he was working as a 19-year-old photographer at the legendary Studio 54 in New York City, management offered to pay him in cocaine instead of money. While speaking with Graham Bensinger, Heyman explained that he was given three options for his paycheck, including cash, tax-reported earnings, or a quantity of cocaine. Choosing to avoid trouble, Heyman opted to be paid legally and focused on his work while others were intoxicated. This gave him a clear advantage and allowed him to secure the head photographer role at just 19, eventually leading him to produce events and book wrestling legends like Ric Flair and Dusty Rhodes before turning 20.
