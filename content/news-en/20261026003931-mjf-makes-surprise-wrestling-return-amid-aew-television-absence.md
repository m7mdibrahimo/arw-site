MJF has made a surprise return to a wrestling ring, even as his absence from AEW television continues. The former AEW World Champion appeared at the Create A Pro Wrestling Academy on October 25, where he served as the special guest referee for a 12-man tag team match. MJF was greeted with a loud reaction from the crowd as he entered, playing to the audience in his signature style before the match began. During his entrance, the star humorously shoved his Dynamite Diamond Ring into his pants for safekeeping. MJF has not appeared on AEW television since the AEW All In event on August 30. During a September 24 interview with Pro Wrestling Illustrated, he explained that his time away is part of a deliberate plan.

> "I don’t want to give anything away. I don’t want to give anything away, but just know whenever I come back because I’m waiting for the right time, the right moment."

MJF emphasized that when he does return, his primary goal will be the AEW World Championship. He stated, "What you can expect from me coming back is I’m going to whoever has the fake title, I’m going to beat them and prove that I am the one true champion emphatically. I’m going to become the first ever undisputed world champion in the history of my company."
