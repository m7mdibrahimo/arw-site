Drew McIntyre recently discussed his extended absence from WWE television while working on various Hollywood projects. During his appearance on the Raw Talk podcast, McIntyre explained that he has been using this time to focus on his physical conditioning, noting that he feels better and moves faster than ever at 41 years old. He also revealed that he has been training inside the ring without an audience to sharpen his skills and speed. While McIntyre did not provide an exact return date, he made it clear that when he finally comes back to WWE, he will not act as if the company did him a favor. Instead, he warned fans that they should be grateful for his return because he believes WWE and its audience need him more than he needs them.
