Ilja Dragunov has learned who his opponent will be for his upcoming in-ring debut in AEW. Tony Khan announced that Dragunov will step into the ring against Speedball Mike Bailey on the October 21 episode of AEW Dynamite. This marks Dragunov's first match with the company following his arrival at AEW WrestleDream, where Bailey competed in the Clockwork Carnage match for the AEW World Title during the main event.
