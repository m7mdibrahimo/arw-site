Steve Maclin recently opened up about his experience wrestling Adam Copeland in a Street Fight at the Maple Leaf Wrestling Northern Rising event. The bout occurred just ten weeks after Maclin suffered a broken fibula while competing at NCG Drive To Glory against Isaiah Broner. Speaking with Sean Ross Sapp, Maclin reflected on the match and his recovery process.

> "I'm feeling great. I'm still buzzing from Northern Rising. It's like a dream come true of a matchup and a day that I will cherish and now probably chase the rest of my career, just for that feeling of comfort and being an adult in this business, but then also still having that inner child in me enjoy every moment and then looking across at one of my heroes. Dude, it's so cool."

Regarding his health, Maclin confirmed that he is back to full strength and has been cleared for action since before the Northern Rising event. While he plans to continue rehabilitation for the foreseeable future, he stated that he is ready to compete wherever he is needed. Fans can view a two-part documentary about his journey to Northern Rising on the Boots To Boots YouTube channel.
