Paul Heyman has responded to recent comments from Stephen A. Smith, who criticized the WWE Hall of Famer for becoming too likable on television. Smith expressed disappointment during a recent appearance, arguing that a great villain should be universally hated and claiming that Heyman has lost some of his edge over time. Heyman took to social media to address the remarks, making it clear he plans to respond further while also taking issue with how the program described his role. The ongoing back-and-forth continues to fuel speculation about whether Smith might eventually make an appearance in WWE.
