WWE NXT aired live on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, from the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida. The event featured Grayson Waller defending his NXT Championship against CM Punk, following a challenge issued by Punk via a video message after Halloween Havoc. Kelani Jordan defended her NXT Women's Championship in an open challenge against Paige. The Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic concluded with Romeo Moreno and Noam Dar facing Hank Walker and Tank Ledger in the finals. Additionally, Cruz Montana faced Dion Lennox in an Unsanctioned Match, and a six-man tag team match pitted Jackson Drake, Ricky Smokes, and Brad Baylor against Chad Gable, Dragon Lee, and Rey Fenix. John Cena also appeared to announce participants for the upcoming John Cena Classic.

Chad Gable, Dragon Lee, and Rey Fenix def. Jackson Drake, Ricky Smokes, and Brad Baylor.

Kelani Jordan def. Paige to retain the NXT Women's Championship.

Romeo Moreno and Noam Dar def. Hank Walker and Tank Ledger to win the 2026 Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic.

Cruz Montana def. Dion Lennox in an Unsanctioned Match.

Grayson Waller def. CM Punk to retain the NXT Championship.
