Darby Allin successfully defended his TNT Championship against Josh Alexander in a hard-fought battle on AEW Fright Night Dynamite. The Don Callis Family member came out aggressive, but Allin managed to fire back with a series of counterattacks.

The action spilled outside with brutal collisions, including Alexander using steel chairs to try to put the champion away. When Trent Beretta and Rocky Romero ran in to interfere, Steven Borden appeared from the crowd in a clown mask to stop them, allowing Allin to capitalize on the distraction and secure the win.
