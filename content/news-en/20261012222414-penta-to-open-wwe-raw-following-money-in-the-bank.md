WWE is preparing for tonight's episode of WWE RAW, with preliminary reports indicating that Money in the Bank briefcase winner Penta is scheduled to open the show. Internal plans place Penta in a central role at the start of the broadcast, positioning him at the heart of the action following his major victory at the Premium Live Event. According to Ringside News, these plans remain subject to change before the show goes live. Tonight's card features several high-profile matches, including Chad Gable defending the Intercontinental Championship against Rey Fenix and a six-man tag team match featuring The Usos and Solo Sikoa against 946. Additionally, Liv Morgan will make her first appearance since regaining the Women’s World Championship. WWE will also provide a medical update on Je’Von Evans, and Rey Mysterio is set to face Big Cass. The event airs live from the Brookshire Grocery Arena in Bossier City, Louisiana, on Netflix at 8 PM ET.
