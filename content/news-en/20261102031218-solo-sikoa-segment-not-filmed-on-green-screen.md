Details have emerged regarding the Solo Sikoa segment on WWE Raw. According to Fightful Select, WWE sources clarified that the segment was not filmed using a green screen and was simply shot the previous week. During the broadcast, Solo cut a promo on Royce Keys as The Usos and Roman Reigns watched and reacted from the background. While The Bloodline did not appear live on Raw, they had been present the prior week.
