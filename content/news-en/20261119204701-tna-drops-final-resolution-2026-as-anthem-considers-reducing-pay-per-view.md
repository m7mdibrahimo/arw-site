TNA reportedly will not close out 2026 with an additional pay-per-view event. Final Resolution has been removed from the December schedule, and larger changes could be on the horizon for the company's pay-per-view calendar next year. According to PWInsider, TNA never made an official announcement for Final Resolution, but the outlet has now confirmed that the company will not run the show next month, meaning those anticipating the annual December event should not look for it.

A strong indication comes from TNA's parent company, Anthem, with sources within confirming that none of its networks received instructions to allocate television time for a December TNA Kickoff Show, which typically runs alongside a pay-per-view. The broader development involves what lies ahead in 2027, as PWInsider also notes that discussions have taken place within Anthem regarding a potential reduction in the number of TNA pay-per-views next year. No details have emerged on how many shows might be cut or which specific events could vanish, leaving Final Resolution as the sole confirmed removal.

Nevertheless, these reported discussions point toward a lighter pay-per-view schedule for TNA in 2027. For the present, Final Resolution serves as the immediate casualty, and despite TNA having never publicly announced the December show, the report confirms no eleventh-hour addition will take place to conclude the year.
