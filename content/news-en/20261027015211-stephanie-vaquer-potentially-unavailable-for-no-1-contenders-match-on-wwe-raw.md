Questions have arisen regarding Stephanie Vaquer's status for Monday's episode of WWE Raw, where she was scheduled to face Becky Lynch in a number one contender's match for the Women's World Championship.

WWE posted an update on X stating that Vaquer's medical availability is currently uncertain heading into the show. General Manager Adam Pearce is set to address the situation during tonight's broadcast to clarify the status of the match.

> "Questions have arisen regarding Stephanie Vaquer's medical availability heading into #WWERaw," the post read. "Adam Pearce will address everything on tonight's show."

Vaquer previously suffered an ankle injury during her title defense at Money in the Bank, where Liv Morgan reclaimed the championship. Although she competed in live events shortly after, her physical condition remains in question following recent backstage segments.
