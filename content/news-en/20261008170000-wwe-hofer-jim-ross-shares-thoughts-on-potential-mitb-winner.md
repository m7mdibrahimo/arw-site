WWE Hall of Famer Jim Ross recently shared his predictions for the upcoming men's Money in the Bank ladder match. Speaking on his Grilling JR podcast, Ross named CM Punk as his top pick to retrieve the briefcase and secure a future world title opportunity.

Ross also highlighted Kevin Owens and Bron Breakker as strong contenders, noting their impressive skills and potential. He praised Owens as one of his favorite in-ring talents and acknowledged his exceptional return, while describing Breakker as a star with unlimited upside.

The star-studded ladder match will feature CM Punk making his first appearance in the bout since 2013, alongside Bron Breakker and Je'Von Evans making their debuts. They will be joined by Kevin Owens returning to the match for the first time since 2021, along with Trick Williams and Penta.
