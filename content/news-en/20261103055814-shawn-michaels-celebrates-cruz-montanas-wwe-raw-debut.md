Shawn Michaels recently took to social media to celebrate the main roster debut of Cruz Montana, who appeared on this week's episode of WWE Raw to join The 946. Michaels highlighted the success of WWE NXT in preparing talent for the transition, noting that 19 superstars have made the jump to WWE Raw and WWE SmackDown over the past 12 months.

> "Another one moving on up! Congratulations to @Montana_NYPR on making the jump to the main roster! That’s 19 NXT Superstars making the leap to Raw and SmackDown in the last 12 months. Pretty proud of that number, but even prouder of the talent and crew who make it happen. Keep showing the world what #WWENXT is all about! See the future of WWE this week on Wednesday night at 8/7c on @TheCW and the @espn Unlimited app"
