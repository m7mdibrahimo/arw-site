TNA has announced a new tag team match for the November 19 episode of TNA iMPACT! The bout will feature Indi Hartwell, Gabby Forza, Shotzi Blackheart, and Allie facing off against Apex, consisting of Shayna Baszler and Zoey Serrano, and Fire N Flava, comprised of Kiera Hogan and Tasha Steelz.

This matchup stems from the Knockouts Championship picture, as Tasha Steelz earned a future title shot against the current champion, Indi Hartwell, by winning the Ultimate X match at Destination X. Shayna Baszler was previously scheduled to challenge Hartwell for the title at that event, but the match was canceled due to travel issues involving Hartwell.

The Dollhouse, featuring Mila Moore, Harley Hudson, and Taryn Terrell, is also scheduled to appear on the program.
