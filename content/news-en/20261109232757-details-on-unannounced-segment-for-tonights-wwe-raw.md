According to Viper Reports, tonight's episode of WWE RAW will feature a segment between Cody Rhodes and Rey Mysterio to begin building toward a Team USA vs. Team Mexico match at Survivor Series. Rey previously announced plans for a traditional elimination match, with himself leading the Mexican side and Cody Rhodes volunteering to lead Team USA. Cody previously expressed his interest in the idea, stating:

> "I'd love something light, something fun… Rey Mysterio's… talking about Team Mexico and Team USA… He's gonna lead Team Mexico. I'd love to lead Team USA. Rey Mysterio, if you're listening, let's make that happen at Survivor Series."

Additionally, World Tag Team Champions Penta and Je’Von Evans are scheduled to open the show, while Roman Reigns and Royce Keys are listed for the closing segment. The event takes place at the Bon Secours Wellness Arena in Greenville, South Carolina.
