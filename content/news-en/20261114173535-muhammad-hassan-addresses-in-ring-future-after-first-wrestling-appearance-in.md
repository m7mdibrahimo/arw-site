Former WWE star Muhammad Hassan spoke about his wrestling future following a recent appearance where he got physical inside a ring for the first time in years. In an interview, Hassan explained that his appearance alongside Krule took place back in May, and he admitted he felt the difficulty of taking his first bump after an eight-year absence.

He stated that he would not mind appearing again in quick run-in segments without wrestling a full match, but he confirmed at the same time that he does not plan to ever wrestle a full match.
