AEW has confirmed that Kazuchika Okada will put his championship on the line against Adam Copeland at AEW Full Gear. The promotion made the bout official during the latest episode of AEW Collision.

This title match stems from recent weeks of tension between the two competitors. Okada previously interfered to cost Copeland and Will Ospreay a tag team bout against Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight on the October 21 episode of AEW Dynamite.
