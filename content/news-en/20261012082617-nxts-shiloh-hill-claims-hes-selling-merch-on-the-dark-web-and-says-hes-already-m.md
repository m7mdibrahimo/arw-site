NXT talent Shiloh Hill claims he has created a site on the dark web to sell his custom shirts and has already made sales. Hill posted on Instagram that he launched an onion site to sell his shirts through the Tor network. He admitted the stunt could be a great idea or a disaster, but noted he is the only WWE wrestler selling merchandise this way. Hill also urged fans who do not know what they are doing to simply buy the shirt through the WWE Shop instead.
