WWE reportedly explored the idea of giving the faction known as The 946 a licensed mainstream song for their entrance theme. The goal was to provide the group with an entrance that reflected their street-culture presentation and carried a larger impact. According to Joey Votes on WrestleVotes Radio, the concept was discussed internally but did not progress far enough to reach the company's legal department before being tabled. While the idea is currently on hold, it remains a concept WWE could potentially revisit in the future. During the discussion, names like Tupac and Snoop Dogg were mentioned as potential styles, though these were not artists WWE had actually contacted. There were also concerns that a recognizable mainstream track might make the group appear "cooler" to fans at a time when they are intended to be heels.
