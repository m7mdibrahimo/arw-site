AEW President Tony Khan addressed the company's media rights situation ahead of the 2026 AEW Full Gear event. During a pre-event media call, Khan spoke about the current deal with Skydance, which remains in effect through 2027. He noted that there is no immediate pressure to negotiate a renewal because of the time remaining on the existing contract.

Khan expressed optimism regarding the potential for the Paramount and HBO Max bundle, suggesting it could provide a platform to increase AEW's viewership. He emphasized his focus on performance rather than contract talks, stating:

> "I’m not the person that’s gonna chase people around and ask about contracts. I really just wanna put my head down and deliver and do a very good job."

He recalled conveying this same commitment to David Zaslav during his tenure at Warner Bros., highlighting his dedication to the product. Khan added that while the company needs revenue to grow, his primary motivation remains his passion for professional wrestling.
