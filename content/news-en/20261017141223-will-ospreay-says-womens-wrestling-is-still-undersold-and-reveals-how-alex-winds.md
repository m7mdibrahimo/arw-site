Will Ospreay believes that women's wrestling continues to be undervalued and is actively working to change that through his involvement with Pro Wrestling EVE. The AEW World Champion shared that he frequently sends EVE matches to Tony Khan, which directly resulted in his wife, Alex Windsor, signing with AEW after Khan watched her match against Jamie Hayter. Ospreay explained that he enjoys using EVE as a platform to test new ideas and help women gain the recognition and opportunities they deserve.

Ospreay emphasized his commitment to pushing women's wrestling forward, noting that many female competitors have worked hard for years without getting enough attention. By serving as a bridge to AEW, EVE provides these athletes with a real pathway to the big stage. Windsor's success stands as proof that this approach works, and Ospreay hopes to see more promotions and executives open doors for deserving talent.
