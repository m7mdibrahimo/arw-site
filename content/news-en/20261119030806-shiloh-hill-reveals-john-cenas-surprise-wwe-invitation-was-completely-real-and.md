NXT Superstar Shiloh Hill recently shared that his surprise invitation to the John Cena Classic was entirely genuine and unscripted. Hill explained that he was backstage during the November 17 episode of WWE NXT, believing he was assisting the production crew with a camera test, when John Cena unexpectedly appeared to hand him a briefcase. Hill noted, "If you saw the video that got posted yesterday with him, that was completely real, I did not know that was happening. They told me I needed to stand there to, like, test out the cameras for a shot they're going to do later. And then all of a sudden, I was shaking John Cena's hand."

The briefcase contained an invitation for Hill to make his Monday Night RAW debut in the John Cena Classic, a special event scheduled for December 14 at the ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex in Florida. Hill expressed his gratitude to Cena and his supporters, acknowledging that many of his followers support him even outside of professional wrestling. Other NXT stars, including Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno, have also confirmed that WWE kept them unaware of Cena's surprise invitations, resulting in authentic reactions.
