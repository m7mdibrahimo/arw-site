Rob Van Dam has addressed fans who claimed they lost respect for him following his criticism of Jon Moxley and AEW. Speaking on his 1 Of A Kind podcast, RVD reacted to headlines about his recent comments regarding Moxley's use of weapons and his claim that AEW's creative freedom has resulted in the inmates running the asylum. He pointed out that many fans reacted to headlines without listening to his full argument, specifically noting those who threw his ECW past back in his face.

While the reactions did not bother him much, RVD specifically targeted individuals who stated they had lost respect for him over the controversy. He questioned how genuine that respect could have been if a single headline was enough to erase it, comparing it to someone saying they would never speak to their father again just because of a minor disagreement. RVD made it clear that he has no interest in winning those critics back, delivering a direct message to anyone threatening to leave.

The controversy began after RVD criticized the heavy reliance on weapons in Moxley's AEW matches, leading fans to accuse him of hypocrisy due to his ECW background. However, RVD has maintained that he always considered that style of wrestling to be garbage, even admitting he thought some of The Gangstas' weapon spots were stupid back in ECW.
