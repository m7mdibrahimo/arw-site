Blake Monroe is looking to impress SmackDown General Manager Nick Aldis. Since joining the brand in April, Monroe has made a strong impact, including two high-profile victories over Giulia, the second of which was a highly praised Street Fight. In an interview with Busted Open Radio, Monroe discussed the importance of winning over the audience, stating that she loves to entertain and make people feel something through her matches.

However, Monroe also expressed frustration with Nick Aldis, noting that she has been on the SmackDown roster for five months and believes she is ready for a world championship opportunity. She specifically criticized Aldis for not booking her in a match against Chelsea Green, while pointing out that he previously booked himself in a match at SummerSlam. Monroe challenged Aldis, saying, "He fantasy booked himself. Well, fantasy book me because I'm the biggest star on the show and I deserve to be world champion. If they match doesn't prove it, what will?"
