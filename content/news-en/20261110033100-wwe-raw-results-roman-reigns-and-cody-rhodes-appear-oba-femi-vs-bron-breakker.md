The November 9, 2026, episode of WWE RAW aired live from the Bon Secours Wellness Arena in Greenville, South Carolina. The show featured appearances by Roman Reigns and Cody Rhodes following the 2026 WWE Crown Jewel premium live event.

Je'Von Evans and Penta opened the show to celebrate their victory as the new WWE World Tag Team Champions. Their celebration was cut short by Bron Breakker, who attacked them and called out Oba Femi. This led to a match between Breakker and Femi, which ended in a disqualification after The Vision attacked Femi. Evans and Penta eventually intervened to help Femi.

In a Triple Threat Match for the #1 Contender spot for the WWE Intercontinental Championship, Rey Fenix defeated Big Cass and Chad Gable. Following the match, Royce Keys and his crew, OTM, attacked all three participants. It was announced that Roman Reigns and Royce Keys will face off later in the night.

Cody Rhodes addressed his Last Man Standing win over Randy Orton at WWE Crown Jewel. He was joined by Rey Mysterio to discuss the upcoming Survivor Series match, but they were interrupted by Dominik Mysterio and JD McDonagh. CM Punk arrived to align himself with Rhodes and Mysterio, leading to a brawl that sent The Judgment Day packing.

In women's division action, Bayley defeated Michin in a singles match. Post-match, Jade Cargill and her crew attacked Bayley, drawing out Charlotte Flair, Tatum Paxley, and Alexa Bliss, leading to a chaotic scene.

In the main event, IYO SKY and Becky Lynch faced The Judgment Day's Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez. The match ended in a victory for Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez after Liv Morgan interfered, allowing Rodriguez to hit a Tejana Bomb on Lynch.
