Swerve Strickland has addressed the criticism surrounding the level of violence in AEW, especially during his rivalry with Hangman Page, pointing out that extreme wrestling has been a part of the industry long before AEW existed. During an appearance on The Radio Big Mack Show, Strickland noted that while AEW pushes boundaries on live television, it certainly did not invent the style, recalling extreme moments from ECW and past generations.

Strickland took aim at veterans, writers, and wrestling personalities who criticize modern content while ignoring the dangerous spots of the past, mentioning Shane McMahon and Jeff Hardy taking extreme risks long before Darby Allin ever jumped off a cage. He recalled watching Shane McMahon face Kurt Angle at King of the Ring and Bubba Ray Dudley putting Mae Young through tables, emphasizing that today's evolution comes from a long history of violence in the sport.
