Aalyah Mysterio made an appearance during the October 20 broadcast of WWE NXT on The CW. A backstage segment aired to provide context for an upcoming match between Layla Diggs and Izzi Dame, who were involved in a heated verbal exchange inside the locker room.

Among the talent present in the background was Aalyah, the daughter of WWE Hall of Famer Rey Mysterio. During the broadcast, she was officially announced as a new recruit at the WWE Performance Center.
