Drew McIntyre is pitching himself as the perfect choice to play James Bond. The former WWE Champion believes his Scottish accent, physical build, and Hollywood experience make him an ideal candidate for the role, even though Robert Pattinson has already been cast as 007. 

WWE's UK Instagram account recently shared a video of McIntyre making his case. "Bond. Clearly Hollywood Drew McIntyre. Who the heck else is going to play James Bond? Just look at my posture, look how I stand. We need a James Bond that's jacked as well. I'm cool, calm, collected. I've got a Scottish accent just like Sean Connery before me," McIntyre said.

McIntyre also shared his vision for the rest of the cast. He named Paul Heyman as his top choice for the villain, noting that Heyman has the acting range to handle a serious role if he adjusts his character. "I'd like Heyman, I think, in the villain role. I could imagine, like me and Heyman been really good in that environment and having spent time in that environment and knowing what he's capable of, especially when he dials it down from his big personalities up here in our show," he explained. 

When asked about a Bond girl, McIntyre agreed with a suggestion to cast Becky Lynch. "There you go. It's Becky he chose, so I'm not gonna keep anyone. Becky would be a good one, genuinely," he added. 

McIntyre has been actively pursuing an acting career, recently appearing in the film "The Killer's Game" alongside Dave Bautista. He is also set to appear in the "Highlander" reboot and "The Last Druid," and he provided a voice for the upcoming Disney project "Hexed."
