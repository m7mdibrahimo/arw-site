Carmella recently shared on her Hot Mom Sh*t podcast that she has been diagnosed with Lynch syndrome, an inherited condition that increases the risk of developing certain cancers. She had been aware of the potential for years due to her mother's history with endometrial cancer, but she previously avoided testing. Becoming a mother shifted her perspective, as she now prioritizes her health to ensure she remains present for her children.

Following her diagnosis, Carmella experienced significant anxiety after receiving an abnormal Pap smear. She feared the worst, stating:

> "I had an abnormal Pap smear come back a couple weeks ago, and in my mind I'm thinking, ‘Oh my God,’ because I recently found out I have Lynch syndrome, which puts me at a much higher risk of ovarian cancer, endometrial cancer. My mom had endometrial cancer. So I'm thinking, ‘Oh my gosh, it's an abnormal Pap, and now I probably have cancer,’ and I'm just going, like, ‘How am I going to tell the kids?’”

Fortunately, follow-up procedures confirmed that everything was fine, and she is now focused on managing her health through regular screenings.
