Kairi is heading out to sea with Chris Jericho, but she made it clear that she will not compete in any matches during the trip amid ongoing speculation about her AEW future and visa status. Speaking with Weekly Pro Wrestling magazine, Kairi revealed that she is training and working on her conditioning to prepare for her in-ring return, adding that she has almost finished a new outfit. She received an invitation to join Chris Jericho's Rock 'N' Wrestling Rager at Sea and viewed it as a fun chance to bring her Pirate Princess persona to a real ship, wanting her first activity after WWE to be something meaningful. 

> “I’m continuing to train and work on my physical conditioning in preparation for my return. I’ve also started working on a new costume, and it’s almost finished.”

Despite rumors connecting her to AEW and reports from Dave Meltzer regarding her visa, Kairi stressed that she will only be signing autographs, selling merchandise, and meeting fans who have supported her since her WWE days, leaving her official return destination a mystery.
