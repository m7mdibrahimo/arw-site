The October 19, 2026, episode of WWE RAW emanated from the Pechanga Arena in San Diego, California. The broadcast opened with Joe Tessitore and Wade Barrett, followed by a recap of the ongoing rivalry between The 946 and The Bloodline, as well as Carmelo Hayes joining Royce Keys’ faction.

World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns arrived at the arena and confronted Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn in the ring. The two discussed their respective titles and the state of The Bloodline before Reigns departed. 

In the opening contest, Penta def. Bron Breakker. The match featured interference from Maxxine Dupri and Austin Theory, but Je’Von Evans ultimately struck Breakker with the Money in the Bank briefcase, allowing Penta to secure the pin.

In tag team action, WWE Women’s Champion Chelsea Green and IYO SKY def. WWE Women’s Intercontinental Champion Raquel Rodriguez and Roxanne Perez. The match saw significant chaos outside the ring, concluding when SKY rolled up Rodriguez for the victory.

In the final match, JD McDonagh def. Joe Hendry. Despite Hendry’s efforts, Dominik Mysterio interfered on behalf of McDonagh. After a guitar was smashed over the head of Hendry’s prop doll, McDonagh delivered a flying headbutt to earn the win.
