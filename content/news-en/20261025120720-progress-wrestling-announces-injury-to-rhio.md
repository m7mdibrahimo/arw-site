PROGRESS Women's Champion Rhio has suffered an injury and will be unable to compete at the Chapter 199: BONESTORM event on October 25th in London, England. Promotion officials announced the news, wishing her a speedy recovery. With Rhio sidelined, her scheduled challenger Raven Creed will now face MARZ, an Australian talent making her debut for the promotion. MARZ has recently wrestled in Japan for TJPW, Ice Ribbon and Pro Wrestling Diana.
