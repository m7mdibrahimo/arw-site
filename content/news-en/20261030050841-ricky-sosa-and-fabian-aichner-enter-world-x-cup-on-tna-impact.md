TNA is reviving the World X-Cup tournament following a hiatus of nearly ten years. During the latest TNA iMPACT, Ricky Sosa and Fabian Aichner confirmed they will compete in the event on Sunday, November 15, at Destination X. Aichner will represent Italy, while Sosa competes for Belgium.

The tournament structure involves wrestlers from various promotions facing off in two fatal-four-way matches, with the winners advancing to a final round to determine the overall champion. Sosa, coming off a win against Eddie Edwards at Bound for Glory, feels his recent victories over Eric Young and The System have prepared him for this moment. Meanwhile, Aichner aims to secure his first major trophy since joining the promotion in May.

Destination X will also feature Ultimate X matches to decide top contenders for the X-Division and Knockouts World Championships. Leon Slater is scheduled to put his TNA World Championship on the line against Trey Miguel in the show's headlining match.
