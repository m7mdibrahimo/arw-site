Bully Ray discussed Cody Rhodes' actions at WWE Crown Jewel on the latest episode of Busted Open Radio. Rhodes defeated Randy Orton in a Last Man Standing match by hitting him with a punt kick, which Bully Ray believes has compromised him and put him on a dark path. While he does not feel Rhodes has reached a point of no return yet, he noted that Rhodes' actions will eventually catch up with him.

Bully Ray also compared Rhodes to Anakin Skywalker, noting that Rhodes is telling people what they want to hear just like Skywalker did. He pointed out that Rhodes hesitated to use the punt kick because he knew it was wrong, but ultimately gave in. Bully Ray stated that he would have more respect for the character if Rhodes had simply stuck to his convictions.
