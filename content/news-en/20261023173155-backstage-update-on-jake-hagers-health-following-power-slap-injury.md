Former WWE and AEW star Jake Hager suffered a serious eye injury at last month's Power Slap 23 event, which required emergency surgery. According to a new report from Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, Hager is slowly regaining some sight in his left eye, but he may require additional surgeries in the future following the ruptured eyeball he sustained.

During the event in Lubbock, Texas, Hager faced independent wrestler Johnny Magna in a slap match. In the second round, Magna landed a powerful blow that caused Hager's left eye to bleed. Although Hager and his corner argued that the strike was a foul, officials awarded the match to Magna via referee stoppage, leaving Hager with both a defeat and a severe eye injury.

Following the incident, Hager's partner Patricia Blackburn confirmed that he underwent an operation. While further details were initially kept private by his family, Hager remains active across various combat sports. After previously stepping away from pro wrestling in 2025, he returned to the ring in June 2026 for the World Wrestling Council in Puerto Rico, before taking on this Power Slap competition.
