Ame Marie Barnett appeared before the October 27 episode of WWE NXT, where she introduced herself as "The Duchess." She was accompanied by Adam Brazier, and the pair were interrupted by Harley Riggins. Reports indicate that Barnett has signed with the promotion. She previously played the role of "Database Bot" in the fourth and sixth episodes of the Disney series Visionquest. Barnett participated in the WWE tryouts held in London this past May and has a background in dance and modeling. Her signing has not yet been officially announced by WWE. Additionally, Elio LeFleur returned from injury prior to Tuesday's episode of NXT.
