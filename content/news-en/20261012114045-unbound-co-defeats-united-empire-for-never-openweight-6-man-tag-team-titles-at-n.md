Unbound Co. captured the NEVER Openweight 6-Man Tag Team Titles from United Empire at NJPW King of Pro-Wrestling. Jeff Cobb, Yuto-Ice, and OSKAR defeated the team of Will Ospreay, Great-O-Khan, and HENARE to win the championships. During the match, Callum Newman interfered and accidentally caused friction within United Empire, leading to the finish. After the bout, Newman attacked OSKAR and Yuto-Ice with a sword before being confronted by Ospreay.
