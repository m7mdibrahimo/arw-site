Swerve Strickland recently shared his thoughts on the greatest professional wrestling match in history, pointing to a legendary NJPW classic. During an interview with Sportskeeda, Strickland was asked about his picks for the best matches of all time and named Kenny Omega versus Kazuchika Okada at NJPW Dominion 6.9 in 2018 as his top choice, alongside the iconic WrestleMania encounter between The Rock and Hulk Hogan. When discussing his personal favorites and the most underrated bouts, Strickland also highlighted the storied rivalry between Hangman Page and Kenny Omega and The Young Bucks. Elsewhere in the interview, Strickland discussed several AEW stars he believes will capture the AEW World Championship in the future, while also mentioning PAC as a former go-to choice.
