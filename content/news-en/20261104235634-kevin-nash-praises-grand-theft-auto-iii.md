On the Kliq This podcast, WWE Hall of Famer Kevin Nash offered praise for the Grand Theft Auto video game series. He specifically highlighted Grand Theft Auto III, noting how enjoyable it is to play. Nash also mentioned other titles developed by Rockstar, such as Bully, stating that most of what the company has released consists of very playable games.
