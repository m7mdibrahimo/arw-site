The scheduled match featuring AEW Women's World Tag Team Champions The Brawling Birds at Pro-Wrestling: EVE has been postponed. Jamie Hayter and Alex Windsor were set to face the team Hyperactive, consisting of Anita Vaughn and Safire Reed, at an event on November 8th. However, following their participation in the violent women's Blood & Guts match, promotion management and the wrestlers agreed to adjust their schedule.

Pro-Wrestling: EVE promoter Dann Read addressed the change during an appearance on the In The Weeds show. He explained that after watching the intense Blood & Guts match and considering the team's upcoming title defense at Full Gear, all parties decided to play it safe and reschedule the encounter for a future date.

> "The decision was to play safe and to reschedule the Hyperactive versus Brawling Birds match for as soon as we can do it in the future basically. It’s not a thing that’s being forced on us or anything along those lines. It’s literally just a case of talking with a talent, what they’re comfortable with, after what has happened, and what they’ve got coming up."

The Brawling Birds will defend their AEW Women's World Tag Team Titles against Harley Cameron and Kris Statlander at AEW Full Gear on November 14th.
