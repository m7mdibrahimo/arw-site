Rob Kimball, known in the ring as RYZIN, has announced that 2027 will be his final year competing in professional wrestling. The decision comes after a lumbar injury sidelined him, and he plans to take on a very limited number of matches before retiring, though no specific final date has been set.

Over a career spanning more than 20 years, RYZIN competed on the independent circuit and for major promotions. His background includes a WWE Performance Center tryout, several appearances on WWE NXT, and a two-year run with AEW during the pandemic era, where he wrestled in 40 televised matches.

After stepping away from the ring, he intends to focus on his career outside wrestling in advertising, marketing, hosting, and acting. He owns an Orlando-based marketing company and was recently featured in a film project.

> "I’ve given a huge part of my life to wrestling, and I’m proud of what I’ve been able to do in this business," said Kimball. "Now I’m ready to put that same energy into building my marketing career, taking on more hosting opportunities and pursuing acting. I’m not walking away overnight. I’m making 2027 my final year, and I want to make the most of the dates I have left."
