The U.S. Embassy in Riyadh issued a security alert on October 22, advising U.S. citizens to exercise extreme caution when using airports in Saudi Arabia. The advisory notes that operations remain suspended at King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh, while airports in Jeddah and Dammam are operational but experiencing delays and cancellations. The Embassy stated that Iranian-supported Houthis have conducted attacks on civilian airports and infrastructure, leading to casualties and travel disruptions. U.S. government employees are required to obtain special authorization to use any Saudi airport. Citizens are urged to monitor local guidance and check flight status directly with airlines. As of now, WWE Crown Jewel is still scheduled to take place in Riyadh on November 7.
