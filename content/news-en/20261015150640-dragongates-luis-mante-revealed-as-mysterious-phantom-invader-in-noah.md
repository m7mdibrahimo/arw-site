Luis Mante has been unmasked as the Mysterious Phantom Invader during Pro Wrestling NOAH's event at Korakuen Hall in Tokyo, Japan on October 15, 2026. HAYATA teamed with GHC National Champion Naomichi Marufuji to face Shuji Kondo and M.P.I. After the match, Marufuji demanded that the masked wrestler reveal his identity. Mante eventually unmasked near the entrance area, disclosing his identity as a Dragongate Japan Pro-Wrestling talent who arrived to assist BUSHI. Mante is scheduled to challenge Marufuji for the GHC National Championship on October 25 at NOAH's Wrestle Odyssey II event.
