Veteran wrestler Kevin Nash has shifted his stance on a Trump administration official, walking back his prior call to oust FBI Director Kash Patel while maintaining that Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. needs to be removed.

Speaking on the November 16 episode of Kliq This, Nash and Sean Oliver discussed the recent measles outbreaks. Nash sharply criticized Kennedy and made his position clear regarding the cabinet official.

Conversely, Nash defended Patel and noted that the FBI director is doing good work by disrupting plots, stating that he has no issue with Patel remaining in his role at this time.
