The Rock has revealed his plans for his upcoming return at WWE Survivor Series on November 28, stating that he is heading to Houston simply to express his gratitude to the fans. Speaking during College GameDay in Miami, The Final Boss explained that he wants to say thank you and share a personal message with the WWE Universe. He noted that he would not be where he is today without the University of Miami and the WWE Universe, making his return about showing appreciation to the people who supported him.

His announcement came as a surprise to many within WWE, as Ringside News previously reported that talent had been kept in the dark about his plans, and WrestleVotes noted that headquarters employees were not expecting the news. Whether he is simply celebrating 30 years or if there are more surprises in store remains to be seen.
