Luke Hawx has announced a new documentary focusing on his life and career in the professional wrestling and entertainment industry. Hawx, who began his wrestling career in 1999 and has worked across numerous independent promotions and WWE television, is shifting his focus as he winds down his in-ring career. He shared that entertainment has shaped his life since childhood and guided his path through wrestling, acting, stunt work, and production. The documentary is a major project for his Louisiana-based production company, Ra-Ra Stories, in collaboration with Bludot Productions, with an official release scheduled for 2027.
