Royce Keys recently discussed the guidance he received from The Undertaker. In a new interview with Hot 97, the leader of The 946 revealed that the Hall of Famer gave him a simple but important piece of advice: to protect himself. Keys explained that this applies to both his life inside the ring and outside of it. He noted that he is comfortable with the way people react to him when he is out in public, as it is part of his character. Earlier this year, Keys made his WWE debut and eventually formed The 946, which has been engaged in a major feud with The Bloodline on WWE RAW.
