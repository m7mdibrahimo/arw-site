Kit Wilson spoke about a creative pitch he made to WWE involving Chelsea Green that ultimately did not happen. Wilson explained that he envisioned a James Bond-inspired character alongside Green as part of the Secret Hervice. "I was a James Bond/Secret Hervice mix and I really would have loved that at the time. It would have been some fun stuff, but it was not to be just yet," Wilson said. He also discussed his recent work in WWE alongside The Miz following his tag team partner Elton Prince's injury, noting his surprise that WWE was open to elements of his toxic masculinity gimmick.
