Chris Sabin of the Motor City Machine Guns has shared the team's primary objective following their return to AEW in July 2026. After a four-year absence from the promotion, Sabin and his partner Alex Shelley are focused on capturing the AEW World Tag Team Championship.

Sabin noted that winning the titles would mark a historic milestone, as they would become the first team to hold tag team gold in WWE, AEW, TNA, and NJPW. He highlighted that a rivalry with The Young Bucks remains their top priority due to their extensive shared history.

The duo is currently engaged in a best-of-five series against FTR, which is tied at 1-1. A victory in this series could lead them to challenge The Young Bucks for the championships.
