AEW star Kevin Knight recently criticized MJF during an interview on TMZ’s Inside the Ring, following a social media exchange where MJF mocked his roller skating. Knight explained that MJF believes he is the smartest person in any room and the best professional wrestler in the world, pointing to his top ranking in the 2026 PWI 500. However, Knight argued that this extreme confidence has become MJF's biggest flaw, noting that he lives inside his own head without realizing it. Knight also defended his lifestyle outside of the ring, explaining that he enjoys diverse hobbies like roller skating, playing the guitar, and reading, whereas MJF is only good at one thing. Knight recently joined the Don Callis Family and captured the TNT Championship, with his sights set on eventually winning the AEW World Championship.
