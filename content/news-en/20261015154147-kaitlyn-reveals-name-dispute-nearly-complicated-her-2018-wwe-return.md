Celeste Bonin recently revealed that her 2018 WWE return almost hit a roadblock over a dispute regarding her ring name. When WWE reached out about bringing her back for the Mae Young Classic, Bonin did not want to compete as Kaitlyn anymore after being away from the company for nearly four years. 

Bonin explained that she no longer felt a connection to the character and wanted to use her real name, while management initially believed she was trying to promote her business, Celestial Bodiez. Despite the hesitation, she ultimately returned for the tournament and delivered a memorable match against Mia Yim, which she considered a perfect way to close that chapter of her career.
