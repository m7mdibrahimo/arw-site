Chavo Guerrero recently discussed the nature of contracts in the professional wrestling industry during an interview with Fightful.

Guerrero noted that wrestling contracts are notoriously one-sided and heavily favor promotions, comparing them to fighting contracts in combat sports. He pointed out that while unionization comes with pros and cons, being part of the Screen Actors Guild has provided him with the benefit of having representation and established protocols to follow. Guerrero also stated that he wants to help bridge the gap between wrestlers and promoters to reach a fair middle ground where everyone makes money and continues working together.
