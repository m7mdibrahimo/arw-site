Jacob Fatu has been absent from WWE television since being struck by a car on the September 28 episode of RAW, but his return appears to be imminent. According to PWInsider Elite, Fatu is not far from an on-screen comeback and has been filming material with other wrestlers expected to compete in the WarGames match at Survivor Series. The current creative direction suggests a major Bloodline reunion, with Roman Reigns, The Usos, Jacob Fatu, and Solo Sikoa potentially teaming up to face The 946 inside WarGames. WWE previously utilized the parking lot incident involving Jaida Parker to write Fatu off television, though reports indicated the company never intended for his absence to last for months. With Fatu now filming segments alongside expected WarGames participants, his return to the storyline seems to be moving forward quickly.
