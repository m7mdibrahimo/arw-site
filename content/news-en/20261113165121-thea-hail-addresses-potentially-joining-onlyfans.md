Thea Hail recently responded when a fan asked whether she would ever consider starting an OnlyFans account. During a Q&A video on her personal YouTube channel, the NXT star addressed various topics ranging from her wrestling career to her life outside the ring. When asked about OnlyFans, Hail laughed off the question and directed attention to her current television appearances. She stated, "Would I ever make an OnlyFans? Let's be real. I wrestle with not many clothes on. 8:00 p.m., The CW Network, Tuesday nights." While the former NXT Women's North American Champion is not launching an account, she used the moment to promote her matches on WWE NXT.
