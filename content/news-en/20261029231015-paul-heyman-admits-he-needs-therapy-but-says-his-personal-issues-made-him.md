Paul Heyman knows he needs therapy, but he has no plans to visit a therapist anytime soon. The WWE veteran believes his personal issues are a major reason for his success and has no interest in changing who he is. Speaking with Graham Bensinger, Heyman revealed that he has never sought therapy for his personal struggles because those very issues helped build his career.

"Being as f*cked up as I am has made me who I am. It's made me successful. I embrace my flaws," Heyman stated. He admitted to likely having childhood trauma, ADHD, a dopamine addiction, and overcompensatory behavior, noting that he is completely fine with it because it makes him who he is. Heyman also pointed to his upbringing around survivors of the Holocaust and the Great Depression as factors that shaped his personality. After decades in professional wrestling, he sees no reason to change now, believing his flaws are an essential part of being the Wise Man.
