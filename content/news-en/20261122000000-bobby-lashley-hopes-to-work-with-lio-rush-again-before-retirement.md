Although Bobby Lashley is currently finding great success alongside his Hurt Syndicate stablemates MVP and Shelton Benjamin, many fans continue to associate him with his former partner Lio Rush due to their successful collaboration in WWE during the late 2010s. With both men now signed to AEW, the possibility of a reunion has emerged. During an interview with MuscleManMalcolm, Lashley expressed his deep admiration for Rush and his desire to work with him once more before he retires from professional wrestling.

> "He is one of my favorite people in the world. Do you know how talented he is? Everything he does, everything he touches his gold. And I just saw him the other day, it was his birthday just the other day. The 23 piece of gold, the 23 year old piece of gold. Now he's 32. That's what I was going to send him. I love Lio, and I always say good things about Lio. Every time when people ask me about Lio, from Tony to before 'Impact,' I always said 'Man, the dude is so talented. He can do anything in this business.' I hope before I do retire I get an opportunity to work with him again."

According to Wrestling Inc, the two have crossed paths briefly during their time in AEW. Lashley continues to perform alongside Benjamin and MVP on AEW Collision.
