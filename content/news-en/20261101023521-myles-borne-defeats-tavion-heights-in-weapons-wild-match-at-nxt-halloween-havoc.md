Myles Borne emerged victorious in the opening match of the 2026 NXT Halloween Havoc Premium Live Event, defeating Tavion Heights in a Weapons Wild Match. The bout served as the culmination of the feud between the former NXT Tag Team Champions.

During the contest, Heights gained an advantage by trapping Borne’s ankle between two chairs and striking it. Heights attempted to secure a submission with an Ankle Lock, but Borne managed to escape by driving Heights into an exposed turnbuckle. The match concluded when Borne shoved Heights through a ringside table before returning him to the ring to secure the pinfall victory.
