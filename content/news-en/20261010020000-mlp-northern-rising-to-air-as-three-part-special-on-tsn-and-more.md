Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling announced that their sold-out MLP Northern Rising event will be broadcast as a three-part television special. The series begins airing on Wednesday, October 14, on TSN, MyAEW, and Fight Nation. The event, which originally took place live on pay-per-view on October 3 at the Mattamy Athletic Centre, featured a street fight between Canadian wrestling legend Adam Copeland and Steve Maclin. According to MLP President Scott D'Amore, the special provides fans with a chance to experience the atmosphere of the historic venue formerly known as Maple Leaf Gardens. The episodes are scheduled for three consecutive weeks: October 14, October 21, and October 28. The broadcast will be available on TSN in Canada, via MyAEW in over 100 countries, and on Fight Nation for European French-speaking audiences.
