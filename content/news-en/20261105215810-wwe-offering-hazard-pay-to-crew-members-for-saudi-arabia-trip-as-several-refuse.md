WWE is reportedly offering a $3,000 bonus to crew members willing to travel to Saudi Arabia for the upcoming Crown Jewel event on November 7 in Riyadh, amidst safety concerns in the region. According to WrestleVotes on Fightful Select, this additional hazard pay is a rare move for the company, which typically does not offer extra compensation for standard behind-the-scenes roles involving production and equipment. Despite the financial incentive, several crew members have declined to make the trip. Safety concerns have also affected talent, with multiple WWE Superstars requesting not to be booked or questioning their safety, though officials have assured them that precautions are a priority. Meanwhile, the United States Department of State has issued a Level 3 travel advisory urging Americans to reconsider travel to Saudi Arabia, but Crown Jewel remains scheduled as planned.
