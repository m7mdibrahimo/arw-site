Chris Cerulli, founding member and lead vocalist of Motionless in White, has opened up about his close friendship with Rhea Ripley following her recent appearance at one of the band's concerts. Cerulli shared a heartfelt tribute on social media, expressing how lucky he feels to have formed such a deep connection with her. He thanked Ripley for inspiring countless people with her strength and vulnerability, noting that he always wants to see her succeed inside and outside the ring. Ripley responded to the touching tribute, expressing her gratitude and noting that they were meant to support each other.
