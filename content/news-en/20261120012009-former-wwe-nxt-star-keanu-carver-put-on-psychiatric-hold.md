Former WWE NXT star Keanu Carver (Kevin Robertson) was placed on an involuntary psychiatric hold in Florida after allegedly threatening suicide during an incident involving a knife and alcohol at a woman's home, according to TMZ. Weeks prior, his ex-girlfriend, WWE star Arianna Grace (Bianca Carelli), obtained a temporary protective order against him.

According to the police report, the Orange County Sheriff's Office responded to a call from a woman stating Carver had entered her unlocked home, demanded tequila, and brandished a knife while expressing a desire to end his life. When deputies arrived, Carver refused to follow instructions and moved toward the woman, prompting officers to restrain him. Carver admitted to wanting to kill himself and to drinking throughout the week.

Deputies took Carver into custody under Florida's Baker Act for a psychiatric examination. The incident follows a September judge's order protecting Grace from Carver after accusations of repeated unwanted contact, further escalating his legal and personal troubles.
