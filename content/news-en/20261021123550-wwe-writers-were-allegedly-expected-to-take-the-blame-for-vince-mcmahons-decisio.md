Former WWE writer Jimmy Jacobs recently discussed the misconceptions surrounding the company's creative team during Vince McMahon's tenure. Jacobs explained that writers were advisers who pitched ideas, but they ultimately lacked the authority to make final decisions. According to Jacobs, when wrestlers heard that creative had nothing for them, it usually meant McMahon simply did not want them on his show. He noted that absorbing the blame and taking the heat for McMahon's choices was simply part of being on the writing team.
