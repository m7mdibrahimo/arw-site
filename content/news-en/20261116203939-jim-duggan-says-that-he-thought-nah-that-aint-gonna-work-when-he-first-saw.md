Jim Duggan recently spoke about the advice he gives to aspiring young wrestlers and reflected on his initial impressions of Bryan Danielson. Speaking in a new interview with Clutchpoints, Duggan discussed his limited interaction with Vince McMahon, viewing him strictly as a boss rather than a friend. Duggan compared professional wrestling to the major leagues, noting the high level of competition and the harsh reality that anyone can be shown the door. Duggan also shared the advice he offers to young talents, emphasizing that while athleticism matters, having that special intangible quality is crucial for success.

Reflecting on Bryan Danielson, Duggan admitted that he initially doubted the star's potential based on his first look at him. Duggan recalled thinking that Danielson's look would not work out, but acknowledged that Danielson ultimately proved him wrong by becoming a massive star. Duggan humorously compared Danielson's trajectory to his own unconventional career, noting that few would have guessed a wrestler carrying a two-by-four and nicknamed Hacksaw would achieve lasting success.
