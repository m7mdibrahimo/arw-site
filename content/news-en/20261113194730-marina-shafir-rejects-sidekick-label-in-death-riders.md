Marina Shafir stated that she is not a follower or assistant to anyone. Prior to their upcoming match at AEW Full Gear, Mercedes Moné did not hold back her views on Shafir, even labeling her as Jon Moxley's helper. During a recent interview, Marina Shafir, known as "The Problem", discussed her absolute rejection of this label.

> "We are teammates. They are my brothers, I am their sister. There is no sidekick mentality here. This isn't a fucking Batman and Batboy type situation and I feel like whenever someone tries to lessen it, it's because they see how fucking big it is really is. They don't see this type of sisterhood and brotherhood in this business. This group in itself is so special because whatever assumptions people had about groups in professional wrestling, we have revolutionized that for like future professional wrestling. We have opened the doors to so many new possibilities that it’s invigorating."

Marina Shafir continued her remarks to reveal the legacy she hopes to leave in the wrestling industry, explaining her perspective on setbacks and goals.

> "As far as me coming out and establishing myself, I’ve established myself as a fucking solid teammate. I've established that I'm dependable, I’m resilient, I'm like the fucking company’s cockroach. I will never fucking die. You can beat me, I might lose but I don't really fucking lose. I don't. I have learned to lose, I've learned how to lose and that is a skill that a lot of people haven't learned for themselves. I had to learn how to lose. I lost in judo, I lost in fighting, I've lost in motherhood, I've lost in being a wife, I've lost with being fucking everything. That's what separates me from everyone. I think that is the essence of who I am. You solve me once and you get ten more problems, that's just poetically what I've always wanted to be. My objective is to make professional wrestling better. I just want to bring more to it. That's always been my goal. I really don't want to be selfish, I want to be more disciplined, I've said that time and time again. I think that is what transcends me and this business. If I leave any type of legacy in this business, I want it to be that."
