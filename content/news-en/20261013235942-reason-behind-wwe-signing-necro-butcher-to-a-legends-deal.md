The recent signing of hardcore icon Necro Butcher to a WWE Legends deal was not a coincidence, but rather a strategic move with a specific purpose. According to PWInsider, the primary motivation for this contract is to facilitate the inclusion of the veteran wrestler in future WWE video games.

Reports indicate that WWE President Nick Khan personally advocated for the deal, recognizing the significant value the deathmatch star brings to the industry. Butcher, who never competed for WWE, noted the irony of the offer, stating that it arrived just one day after he left his regular job.

Butcher spent three decades building his legacy in promotions like Ring of Honor and CZW, becoming a defining figure in American hardcore wrestling. This partnership introduces a cult icon to a new generation through WWE gaming platforms.
