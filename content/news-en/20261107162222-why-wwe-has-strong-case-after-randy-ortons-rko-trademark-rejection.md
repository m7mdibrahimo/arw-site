David Otunga believes WWE still has a strong case to secure the RKO trademark despite facing a recent refusal from the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office. During his Attorney at RAW segment, Otunga discussed the November 3 nonfinal refusal regarding WWE's attempt to register the term for wrestling broadcasts. While RKO Pictures holds existing trademarks for film and television production, Otunga noted that Orton has used the RKO name for over two decades, giving fans a clear association with the wrestling star. WWE has until February 3, 2027 to respond to the decision and can submit further evidence to support its application.
