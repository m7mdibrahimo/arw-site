Kaylia Capri was scheduled to compete at Reality of Wrestling's Sherri Martel Classic in Texas City, Texas, on October 24, but a car accident on the same day forced her to miss the event. Capri, who wrestled as Alexis Gray during her time in WWE, shared the news on social media to apologize to fans, confirming that she sustained minor injuries but was heartbroken over missing the show. Although she did not disclose the cause of the accident, hospital treatment details, or a timeline for her in-ring return, she expressed gratitude for being able to walk away from the crash. Since her WWE release in September 2023, Capri has been active on the independent wrestling scene, and she has not yet announced when she will return to action.
