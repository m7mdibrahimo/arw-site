Former WWE Superstar Eva Marie recently discussed her divorce from Jonathan Coyle, admitting she was left speechless when asked about their current relationship status. After a decade together and filing for divorce in December 2024, Eva noted that while she does not harbor any bad blood, they are not close friends either. She explained that she simply wishes him well from a distance, expressing surprise at how the question caught her completely off guard during a recent interview.
