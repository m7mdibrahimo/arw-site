TNA Wrestling star Xia Brookside has purchased a new Ford Bronco. The 27-year-old wrestler shared photos on Instagram of her white SUV parked outside a dealership, complete with a giant red bow on the hood. The vehicle appears to be a Ford Bronco Outer Banks model, which features a 10-speed automatic transmission, four-wheel drive, and a starting MSRP of $48,685. While Brookside did not disclose the final price or financing details, she expressed her happiness with the new purchase, simply captioning her post, "God is good 🙏🏻". Fans and fellow wrestlers flooded the comments with congratulatory messages as she enjoys her time away from the ring.
