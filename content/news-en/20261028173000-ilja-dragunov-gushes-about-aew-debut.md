Ilja Dragunov, the newest signee for AEW, opened up about his emotions and reaction to his debut at WrestleDream, which took place months after his departure from WWE. During an appearance on "Battleground Podcast", the star described the moment as feeling like a fairytale.

> "What is definitely going through my mind is just rethinking about the beautiful moments to come out to my entrance at WrestleDream on the pay-per-view on Saturday, which was absolutely incredible, the welcoming of the crowds on my side, the music I was craving to hear again for such a long time because it carried me through my whole beginnings of my career. And have this perfect presentation of just to transfer through my entrance what I'm all about. That was a fairytale," he said.

He added that he felt a strong sense of nostalgia and intense excitement before stepping into the ring to give his all with complete devotion.

Dragunov expressed admiration for how dedicated AEW is to presenting his character in a distinct and precise way. He also revealed that he spoke with Tony Khan immediately after his first appearance, noting that the AEW CEO praised his debut on television and felt the timing was completely ideal.
