Gunther made it clear he is unhappy about Danhausen receiving a special edition of WWE SmackDown, according to Ringside News. In an unaired backstage interview with Cathy Kelley, Gunther mocked the Halloween-themed special and criticized the company for giving him extra attention.

Although Gunther did not share specific details about his plans, he promised to appear personally on October 30 to make sure the night is memorable for Danhausen. The upcoming episode features Sami Zayn defending the Undisputed WWE Championship against Trick Williams, alongside Chelsea Green defending the WWE Women’s Championship against Nikki Bella.
