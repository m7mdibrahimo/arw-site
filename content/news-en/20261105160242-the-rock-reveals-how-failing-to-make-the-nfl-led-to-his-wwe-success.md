Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson recently opened up about his journey from college football hopeful to global superstar. On November 5, 2026, Johnson shared on Instagram that he once viewed the NFL as his only ticket out of poverty, with specific plans to buy his parents their first home. However, his dreams were sidelined when he failed to secure an NFL roster spot while at the University of Miami, where he found himself on the bench behind future Hall of Famer Warren Sapp.

Johnson admitted that missing out on the NFL was a pivotal moment that changed his life. Instead of making excuses, he recognized that he simply was not good enough for the league at the time. This setback fueled a deep hunger in him that ultimately led to his legendary career in WWE. "Making it to the NFL was the best thing that never happened for me because it set me down a road in a path with a chip on my shoulder," Johnson stated. 

Reflecting on the current landscape of college sports, Johnson expressed his support for name, image, and likeness (NIL) deals, noting that he understands the financial struggles athletes face. He concluded by sharing advice for those building a career, emphasizing the importance of hard work and taking care of one's family. Johnson noted that while he still misses aspects of football, he would not change the path that led him to become a household name.
