Ahead of the upcoming WrestleDream pay-per-view, AEW presented a super-sized double edition of AEW Dynamite and AEW Collision in Cleveland, Ohio, featuring multiple Clockwork Carnage qualifiers and title defenses. The Wrestling Inc. staff reviewed the three-and-a-half-hour broadcast, highlighting the elements that worked and those that fell short.

Among the things they hated were too many predictable Clockwork Carnage qualifiers on a single show, particularly with titles needlessly on the line, as well as a comedic segment featuring Mojo Brookzz and The New Level that failed to connect with the audience, and Adam Copeland's retirement announcement being abruptly interrupted by vague threats from Don Callis and Brian Cage.

On the positive side, the staff praised the hard-hitting match between Konosuke Takeshita and Brian Cage, Samoa Joe and Tommaso Ciampa securing a victory ahead of WrestleDream in a solid main event against Will Ospreay and Andrade El Idolo, and the highly entertaining, predictable yet excellent match between Darby Allin and Hechicero.
