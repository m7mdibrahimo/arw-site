Parker Boudreaux has officially joined the GLEAT roster. GLEAT owner Hiroyuki Suzuki made the announcement while backstage at the promotion's November 20th show. Boudreaux, who has previously competed for WWE and All Elite Wrestling, first began working with GLEAT in 2024. He currently holds the Global Championship for the Oceania Pro Wrestling promotion.
