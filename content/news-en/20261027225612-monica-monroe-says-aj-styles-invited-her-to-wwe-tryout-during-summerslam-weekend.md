Monica Monroe was recruited by AJ Styles. Monroe's career changed significantly at the beginning of October when she defeated Laynie Luck to win the WWE ID Women's Championship, which also earned her a WWE ID contract. Months before winning the title, Monroe attended a WWE tryout during SummerSlam weekend in Minnesota, and she revealed that it was AJ Styles who personally invited her to the event.

> "It wasn't just a typical, you know, the WWE Recruit page reached out to me. It was actually AJ Styles that reached out to me. Yeah, so when I got the call, I freaked out, I wanted to cry obviously because he's an incredible wrestler and just one of the greatest of all the time and just one of the best athletes out there. One the things he said that he noticed was that I was really athletic. Coming from him, I'm like, ‘Are you joking me? You're telling me I’m athletic? That's crazy.’"

Monroe explained that while AJ Styles initiated the contact, the process then moved to Gabe, who manages the ID program. She noted that her success was the result of five years of hard work at Reality Of Wrestling, and receiving the call from Styles was a validating moment that led to emotional celebrations with her friends.
