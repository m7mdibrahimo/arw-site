Cody Rhodes recently discussed his complex professional and personal relationship with Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson. During an appearance on the "Last Meals" podcast, Rhodes noted that their interactions have been unpredictable, ranging from brutal on-screen attacks to friendly invitations to film premieres. "I'm always wondering why he invites me to these things, because it's like I'm the only one [from WWE] who gets invited, and are we going to fight? Are we hugging? We're hugging! Okay, yeah," Rhodes said. "That's the most complicated male relationship I have in my life." Rhodes highlighted the deep-rooted ties between their families, noting that Johnson has known his mother, Michelle Rubio, for years. He also reflected on their intense storyline leading up to WrestleMania XL, where Johnson returned to challenge his position. Rhodes praised Johnson's "Final Boss" character as one of the best and most untapped personas in the history of professional wrestling.
