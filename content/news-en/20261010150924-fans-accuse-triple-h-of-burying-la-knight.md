WWE management faced harsh criticism from fans after revealing details for the upcoming Money in the Bank event. During an episode of SmackDown, a World Heavyweight Championship match between Roman Reigns and LA Knight was confirmed to open the show instead of serving as the main event.

This decision upset many viewers, who felt that placing the bout at the start diminished LA Knight's chances of winning and completely eliminated any possibility of a Money in the Bank cash-in against Roman Reigns later in the night. Numerous fans directed their anger straight at Triple H on social media, criticizing the booking and arguing that the current order killed the excitement and removed all element of surprise before the event even began.
