The professional wrestling world was deeply saddened by the passing of AEW star Pac, also known as Benjamin Satterley, shortly after AEW All Out 2026 where he competed for the AEW National Championship. In the days following his death, numerous wrestlers from both AEW and WWE shared their memories and grief. WWE released a touching YouTube video featuring talents opening up about their time with him.

Cody Rhodes, who shared the ring with Satterley across multiple promotions, revealed during an interview that it was close friend Kevin Owens who organized a camera setup backstage during a WWE TV taping. This allowed the talent to record their personal tributes and farewell messages for Ben.

Reflecting on their history, Rhodes spoke about their classic encounters, including their matches during his time as Stardust and Satterley's run as Neville, as well as their shared locker room experiences in AEW. Rhodes noted that Satterley possessed a rare and intimidating sense of self-awareness regarding his character, adding that knowing and learning from him made him a better wrestler and storyteller.
