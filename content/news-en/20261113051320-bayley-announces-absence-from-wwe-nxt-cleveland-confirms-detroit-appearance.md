Bayley has confirmed she will not appear at the WWE NXT live event scheduled for tonight in Cleveland. She took to social media to address the fans regarding her absence.

> "Dear #nxtcleveland I was really looking forward to returning to the Agora theatre but unfortunately I won’t be making the show tomorrow night. I GOT SOME BUSINESS TO HANDLE."

Bayley included the WWE SmackDown logo in her post, signaling her focus on the upcoming episode of WWE SmackDown this Friday. She is currently involved in a rivalry alongside Charlotte Flair, Tatum Paxley, and Alexa Bliss against Jade Cargill, B-Fab, Michin, and Fatal Influence. Despite missing the Cleveland show, Bayley confirmed she will participate in the WWE NXT event this Saturday in Detroit and has officially been added to the lineup for Sunday's event in Chicago.
