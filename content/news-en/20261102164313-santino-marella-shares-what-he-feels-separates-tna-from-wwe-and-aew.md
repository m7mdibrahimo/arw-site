Santino Marella recently discussed what he believes separates TNA from its competitors, WWE and AEW, highlighting the promotion's creative freedom. Marella has been with TNA for several years working as an authority figure and recently signed a new contract, serving as a primary ambassador for the company. Speaking on the We R Wrestling Podcast, he explained why he thinks TNA's production style offers a distinct advantage to the talent on the roster.

"So TNA is not overproduced. We don't have 15 writers or we don't have 15 producers. We have more freedom. The individuals in the ring have a little more freedom, and when you have freedom, because you hear about back in the golden day of wrestling, they didn't write their promos for them. They knew their characters. They came from other territories, and you'll hear guys like Roddy Piper back in the day say, yeah, they didn't write any promos for me, and that was the golden era," Marella said. He added that while television requires some level of quality control to protect sponsors, individual creative freedom remains one of TNA's biggest strengths.
