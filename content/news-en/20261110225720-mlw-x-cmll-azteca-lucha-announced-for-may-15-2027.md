Major League Wrestling and CMLL will join forces for a special event in 2027. MLW announced that Azteca Lucha will take place on May 15, 2027, at Cicero Stadium in Chicago, Illinois.

The event will serve as a Fusion TV taping for Tubi. Tickets for the show go on sale this Wednesday, November 11, at 10 a.m. CT, with prices starting at $10. Fans can purchase tickets exclusively at LuchaTickets.com.

MLW returns to Chicago following a sold-out weekend at the same venue. During the November 7 Saturday Night SuperFight event, Court Bauer revealed that MLW is currently working on a special project involving both CMLL and NJPW. Further details and match announcements for the Azteca Lucha event will be revealed in the coming months.
