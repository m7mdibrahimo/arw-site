Brie Bella has shared an update on her recovery from a shoulder injury sustained during the tag team match at SummerSlam, where she and Paige faced Fatal Influence. During a recent episode of The Nikki & Brie Show, Brie explained that her bones have not yet fully fused, meaning she remains medically cleared to compete in the ring.

> So, on the solo episode, I told you guys all that I just got back from my scans. Well, the results came in, and unfortunately, my bones have not fused. So, that's okay. It's really three months where they feel like the bones would be fused. I'm still on the right path of healing and still all the things. So, if you see me pull into Sacramento with my mom, please don't assume I'm wrestling. But so I think I will be there watching you [Nikki].

While Brie initially hoped to begin working out by October 1, her recovery is taking longer than anticipated. Consequently, she will not be competing when WWE SmackDown visits Sacramento, California, this weekend, though she will be in attendance to support her sister. Nikki Bella is scheduled to face Paige in her first match since the pair turned on their former partner at SummerSlam.
