Brady Booker is bringing his reality TV experience to All Elite Wrestling and Ring of Honor after competing on Survivor 51. During the latest edition of AEW Collision, a video package highlighted his time on the CBS reality show, where he became the first member of the jury before his elimination on the November 4 episode.

The feature showcased his stint on the islands of Fiji alongside his appearances in AEW and ROH rings. Booker, who previously performed in WWE as Bodhi Hayward, expressed his readiness to test himself against elite competition in AEW and ROH.
