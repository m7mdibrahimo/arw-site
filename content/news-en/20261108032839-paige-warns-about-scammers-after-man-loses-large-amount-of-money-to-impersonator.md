WWE star Paige is warning fans about fraudulent accounts after discovering a scammer stole a substantial amount of money from an unsuspecting man. The situation came to light during Rhode Island Comic Con, where a sweet fan approached her and revealed he had been communicating with someone pretending to be her for eight months. On November 7, Paige, also known as Saraya, posted a video on social media to alert the public and make it clear that she only maintains three official profiles. She stated in the video,

> "The only thing I have is Saraya on Twitter or X Saraya on Instagram or Saraya on Tick Tock. There is nothing else. I don't have any private side accounts, nothing. You are not talking to me." Paige expressed deep sympathy for the victim, who had been completely deceived by the impersonator. She urged her followers to remain vigilant and avoid falling victim to online scams. She added in the video,

> "I had a gentleman come up to me today and unfortunately he was so, so sweet. And he for. He was talking to me for the past eight or so months and got scammed out of some money, a lot of money by the sounds of it." Although she did not disclose the exact financial loss, she emphasized that the sum was significant enough to cause real concern. Paige concluded by reminding everyone to stay safe and double-check accounts before interacting online.
