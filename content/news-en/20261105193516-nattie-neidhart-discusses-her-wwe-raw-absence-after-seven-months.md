Nattie Neidhart recently addressed her lengthy absence from WWE RAW, which has now exceeded seven months. The veteran wrestler stated that she does not feel frustrated by her time away from television, noting that she trusts the process and is waiting for the right moment and storyline to return.

Nattie has not appeared on the program since the March 16 episode, where she defeated Maxxine Dupri. She highlighted the significant changes to the roster during her hiatus, including the arrival of new talents like Lola Vice and Sol Ruca, as well as the addition of Jaida Parker to the 946 group.

Regarding her personal health, Nattie underwent major dental surgery following her appearance at TripleMania in September. She has since confirmed that she is medically cleared to compete whenever needed.
