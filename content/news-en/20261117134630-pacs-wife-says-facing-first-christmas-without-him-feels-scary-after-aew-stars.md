Natalie Satterley, the wife of the late PAC, is still struggling to come to terms with losing her husband, admitting that the approaching Christmas season makes everything even harder. Nearly two months after the former AEW star's sudden passing at the age of 40, Natalie shared a heartbreaking message and personal photos on Instagram, explaining that seeing holiday decorations and ads has become painful.

Natalie recalled that PAC absolutely adored Christmas, noting how difficult it is to contemplate a holiday without him. She shared that everyday moments, like spotting items in shops or hearing familiar songs, still trigger the instinct to message him before the painful reality sets in.

Reflecting on the future they lost after years of building their dream home, Natalie expressed gratitude for the outpouring of support from family, friends, and wrestling fans. The wrestling world recently honored PAC ahead of Full Gear on November 14, when AEW unveiled redesigned World Trios Championship belts featuring a tribute to the former champion.
