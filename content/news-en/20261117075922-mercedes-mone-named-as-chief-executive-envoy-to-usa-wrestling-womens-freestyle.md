USA Wrestling and Mercedes Moné have formed a new partnership, with Moné appointed as the Chief Executive Envoy to the USA Wrestling Women's Freestyle wrestling team. Media entrepreneur Kevin Undergaro is also part of the initiative, which aims to promote U.S. female wrestlers, raise the profile of women's wrestling, and support athletes in building their individual brands and careers beyond competition. The partnership focuses on connecting the U.S. women's wrestling team with mainstream American audiences ahead of the 2028 Olympic Games in Los Angeles.

"The initiative is designed to help promote U.S. female wrestlers, elevate the profile of women's wrestling and support athletes in building their individual brands and careers beyond competition. The 2028 Olympic Games, being contested on home soil in Los Angeles, provide a unique opportunity to connect the U.S. women's wrestling team with the mainstream American audience, a focal point of this partnership," USA Wrestling Executive Director Rich Bender stated.

Moné will leverage her global fanbase and social media reach to mentor, promote, and raise awareness for U.S. female wrestlers and the program as a whole.

"Ten years ago I was part of an evolution that transformed women's professional wrestling. Today marks the first day of the Women's Amateur Wrestling Evolution - an evolution I'm honored to lead along with Team USA. These athletes are world-class competitors and warriors who deserve the highest recognition," Moné said.

Additionally, All Elite Wrestling has joined the initiative as the Official Content Partner for the U.S. women's wrestling team. AEW will support the athletes through social media promotion, collaborative content, and exposure across its platforms, while select wrestlers will have opportunities to attend weekly AEW events, engage backstage, and connect with fans and talent.

"On behalf of All Elite Wrestling, we are thrilled to be named the Official Content Partner of the USA women's wrestling team. It is a true honor to work with these amazing athletes and introduce them to a whole new audience across AEW's platforms, shining a global spotlight on their passion and dedication to the sport," AEW CEO, GM, and Head of Creative Tony Khan stated. This announcement follows Moné's successful defense of the AEW Women's World Title against Marina Shafir at Full Gear.
