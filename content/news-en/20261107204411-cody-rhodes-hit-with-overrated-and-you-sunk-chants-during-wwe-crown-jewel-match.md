Cody Rhodes defeated Randy Orton in a brutal Last Man Standing match at WWE Crown Jewel, but the crowd in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, made it clear they were not fully behind him. Throughout the contest, audience members repeatedly chanted “Overrated!” and “You suck!” at Rhodes. The physical bout saw both competitors use steel steps, kendo sticks, trash cans, and tables, with Orton sending Rhodes through a table with a powerbomb. Rhodes later avoided a Punt Kick and delivered a Cody Cutter followed by three consecutive Cross Rhodes moves. After Orton began bleeding, Rhodes stopped the referee's count and delivered a vicious Punt Kick of his own to secure the victory, continuing to display the ruthless side of his character.
