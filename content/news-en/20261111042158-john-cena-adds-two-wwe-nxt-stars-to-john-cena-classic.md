John Cena has officially announced two additional participants for the upcoming John Cena Classic. During a backstage segment on the November 10 episode of WWE NXT, Cena personally invited Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno to compete in the event.

Cena presented both men with special briefcases containing gifts and a book detailing the event's rules and information. This invitation follows their recent victory in the 2026 Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic, where they defeated Hank Walker and Tank Ledger, earning a future opportunity at the NXT Tag Team Championship against The Vanity Project.

Dar and Moreno join Cody Rhodes and Zilla Fatu as the confirmed participants for the tournament. The John Cena Classic is scheduled for December 14, featuring a mix of main roster and NXT talent to honor the legacy of the legendary performer.
