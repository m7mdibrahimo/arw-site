Tom Grennan, a singer-songwriter from the UK, recently recalled almost paralyzing himself and breaking his back after attempting a botched Swanton Bomb while wrestling with his brother in his youth. Speaking on The 2 Johnnies Late Night Lock In, Grennan discussed his childhood fandom of WWE and his favorite wrestler, Jeff Hardy, whose signature move inspired the dangerous backyard stunt. "Yeah, man. WWE was my childhood. Yeah, but there's a story. We had a trampoline at home and I'd just beat my brother up, and I loved a wrestler called Jeff Hardy," Grennan shared. He explained that during a jump, he bounced out of the trampoline and landed on a tree stump cut by his father, immediately injuring his back before crawling into the house to ask for help.

Grennan noted that his father initially dismissed the injury, telling him to get up. However, after weeks of persistent pain, his father finally took him to the hospital. "Mate, I had a broken back. One vertebrae from being paralysed," Grennan said, describing the doctor's reaction upon seeing the X-rays and realizing the severity of the injury that had occurred weeks prior.
