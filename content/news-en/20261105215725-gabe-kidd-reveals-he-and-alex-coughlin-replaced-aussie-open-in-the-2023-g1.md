Gabe Kidd recently discussed his experience in the 2023 G1 Climax tournament. During a new interview with Chris Jericho, Kidd revealed that he and Alex Coughlin were not initially scheduled to participate in the event. Instead, Kyle Fletcher and Mark Davis of Aussie Open were originally booked for the tournament before they signed with AEW.

Kidd explained that being a replacement motivated him to prove his worth. "It's funny how everything loops around. Aussie Open was supposed to be in [the G1], but they ended up signing here. So they took them out and they put me and Alex Coughlin in. The second that happened, I went, you put me here on a replacement, I'm gonna blow everything out of the water. You are going to regret every single thing that you've ever doubted about me," Kidd stated. He added that he felt he was the MVP of the tournament despite not reaching the semifinals. Gabe Kidd is scheduled to defend his IWGP Global Heavyweight Championship against Yuya Uemura at Wrestle Kingdom 21.
