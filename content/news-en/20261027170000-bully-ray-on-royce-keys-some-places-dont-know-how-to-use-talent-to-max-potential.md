Bully Ray recently discussed Royce Keys, formerly known as Powerhouse Hobbs, and his current run in WWE compared to his time in AEW. Keys debuted in WWE earlier this year after spending six years in AEW, and he has quickly secured a prominent role on television, leading the 946 against the Bloodline.

Speaking on Busted Open, Ray noted that he is not surprised by the immediate impact Keys has made, pointing out that certain promotions simply fail to maximize their wrestlers' potential. Ray emphasized that timing plays a crucial role in a star's success, stating that the timing was right for Keys in WWE.

WWE has previously found success pushing former AEW stars like Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, and Penta, and Keys could be next in line. Reports indicate that WWE views him as a potential future world champion, with he and his 946 group currently preparing to face the Bloodline at Survivor Series in late November.
