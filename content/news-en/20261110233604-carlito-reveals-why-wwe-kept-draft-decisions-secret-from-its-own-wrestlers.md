Carlito recently explained why WWE used to keep draft decisions a secret from its own wrestlers. Appearing on the Hitting the Turnbuckle Podcast, Carlito discussed whether he received advance notice when moving between WWE RAW and WWE SmackDown. While he noted that he was personally told ahead of time, he explained that many other wrestlers were not as fortunate.

The former Intercontinental Champion noted that changing brands involved significant behind-the-scenes adjustments for talent. Wrestlers had to navigate new travel schedules, altered social circles, and potential separation from partners who were drafted to different brands. Carlito explained that WWE often withheld draft information to prevent major surprises from leaking to wrestling news websites before they aired on television. He defended the company's approach, emphasizing that management wanted to protect its storylines rather than punish anyone on the roster.
