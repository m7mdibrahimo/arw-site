Sami Zayn appeared to find what he needed at Crown Jewel after headlining the show in a singles match against Roman Reigns for this year's Crown Jewel Championship. Even though he lost the bout, according to Fightful, Zayn maintained high spirits.

He took to social media on November 9 to share a message indicating that the encounter with Reigns was essential for him, quoting a famous line.

> "You can’t always get what you want, but if you try sometimes you’ll find you get what you need."
