Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson made Halloween special for a young fan named Brooklyn after seeing her detailed costume for a contest. Brooklyn dressed up as Hollywood Rock, complete with wrestling tights, a fake muscle bodysuit, a bald cap, and a replica of his Samoan tattoo. Despite her efforts, she did not win first place in her Halloween competition.

The Rock shared a video of her costume on Instagram and decided to reward her himself. He announced that he is flying Brooklyn and her entire family to California in December as his special guests for the Hollywood premiere of Jumanji: Open World.

> "SURPRISE 🚨 I’m flying you and your family out to California, so you can be my special guests at our red carpet WORLD PREMIERE OF ‘JUMANJI: OPEN WORLD’ in Hollywood this December, just in time for Christmas 🎄🍿"

The Rock mentioned that as a father of three girls, including a 10-year-old daughter, seeing Brooklyn's dedication meant a lot to him. He confirmed that his team will contact her mother to arrange the trip.
