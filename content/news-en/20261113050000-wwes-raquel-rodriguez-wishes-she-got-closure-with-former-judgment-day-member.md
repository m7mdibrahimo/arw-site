WWE star Raquel Rodriguez recently opened up on "Insight with Chris Van Vliet" about her time alongside former Judgment Day member Carlito, expressing regret that their on-screen flirtation did not reach a definitive conclusion. Carlito abruptly departed WWE in June 2025 when his contract expired, though he later noted that management had removed him from television several weeks prior.

Reflecting on their brief storyline, Rodriguez admitted she wished they had received a proper send-off or a final date segment before his exit, calling it a missed opportunity that would have been fun to explore further. While the idea was discussed between them, it never materialized during his second run with the promotion.

Despite the abrupt end to his tenure, Rodriguez remains optimistic that a follow-up could happen eventually, noting that the door is not completely closed for the future. Meanwhile, Carlito has shared his own perspective on his departure, believing that Paul "Triple H" Levesque was never fully invested in his work.
