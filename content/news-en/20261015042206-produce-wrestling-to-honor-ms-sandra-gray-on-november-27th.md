PRODUCE Wrestling announced that its November 27th event, Vol. 7: FRIDAY, will feature a special presentation honoring legendary fashion and costume designer Ms. Sandra Gray. The event will take place at the Roulette Intermedium in Brooklyn, New York, and is co-produced by Darius Lockhart and Darian Bengston.

> PRODUCE ANNOUNCEMENT PRODUCE presents Vol. 7: FRIDAY live from Brooklyn, NY on November 27. Co-produced by DARIUS LOCKHART and DARIAN BENGSTON. Featuring a SPECIAL PRESENTATION to honor MS. SANDRA GRAY. Ms. Sandra's iconic costume designs have been seen in rings and on screens around the world across 4 decades. Join us alongside the best independent wrestlers in the world as we celebrate her illustrious career. TICKETS ON SALE TOMORROW AT 12PM EST

Gray began designing wrestling gear in the 1990s and enjoyed a long tenure with WWE from 2001 to 2015. She later joined AEW in 2019, cementing her reputation as one of the most respected designers in the industry.
