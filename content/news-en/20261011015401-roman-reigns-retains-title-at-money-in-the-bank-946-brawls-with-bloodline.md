Roman Reigns defended his title at WWE Money in the Bank. In the opening match of the 2026 WWE Money in the Bank event, Roman Reigns successfully defended the WWE World Heavyweight Championship against LA Knight. This marked Reigns' fifth defense of the belt and Knight's fifth challenge for a WWE World title. During the match, Reigns became the first person to kick out of Knight's B.F.T. finisher. Knight nearly secured a pinfall after countering Reigns with a spear, but Reigns ultimately sealed the victory with multiple Superman Punches and a spear.

Following the match, 946 members Royce Keys, Bronco Nima, and Lucien Price appeared from the crowd to send a message to Reigns. They were immediately confronted by The Bloodline, featuring Jimmy Uso, Jey Uso, and Solo Sikoa. A massive brawl broke out and spilled outside the arena, ending when Jaida Parker arrived in a truck to extract her 946 stablemates. 946 and The Bloodline are scheduled to face each other in a six-man tag team match on the October 12 episode of WWE RAW.
