Since August 2024, Marina Shafir has been aligned with Jon Moxley as a member of the Death Riders. Speaking to Rob Wolkenbrod of Forbes, Shafir revealed the best advice she has received from Moxley. "Simply, you’re supposed to have fun, and sometimes in the moment you have to work to have fun, and it may not happen imminently," Shafir said. She added that Moxley emphasized that having fun is the goal because it resonates with the audience. Shafir noted that outlaws have their own way of having fun, which helped her relax and open her mind to new possibilities. She mentioned that she has become addicted to not knowing what is going on, as she loves the element of surprise in professional wrestling. Shafir also recalled that Anarchy in the Arena was the first time she really felt that advice, noting that she enjoyed using a frying pan during the match to pay respect to her mother, who immigrated to the country with nothing but a cast-iron pan. Shafir is set to challenge Mercedes Moné for the AEW Women's Championship at AEW Full Gear.
