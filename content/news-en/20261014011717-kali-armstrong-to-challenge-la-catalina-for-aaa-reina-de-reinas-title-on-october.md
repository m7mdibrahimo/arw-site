Kali Armstrong is set to challenge La Catalina for the AAA Reina de Reinas Championship on the October 13 episode of WWE NXT. The matchup was arranged after Armstrong visited NXT General Manager Robert Stone to voice her frustration over being eliminated from a 20-woman Battle Royal by Wren Sinclair, who had been disguised as Lady Shani. In response, Stone coordinated with AAA General Manager Rey Mysterio to book the title match. The updated lineup for the show includes: Myles Borne vs. Tony D'Angelo in a #1 Contender's Match for the NXT Title, Saquon Shugars vs. Tristan Angels, Vanity Project vs. Mini Vikingo and Chazz 'Starboy' Hall in a Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic bout, Noam Dar and Romeo Moreno vs. Fraxiom in a Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic bout, a segment featuring Robert Stone and Wren Sinclair, and La Catalina defending her AAA Reina de Reinas Championship against Kali Armstrong.
