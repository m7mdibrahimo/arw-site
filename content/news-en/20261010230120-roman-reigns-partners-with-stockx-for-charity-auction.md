Roman Reigns has teamed up with StockX to auction off several items, including pieces worn in the upcoming Street Fighter movie and a pair of autographed Air Jordan 12 Retro 'Bloodline' sneakers. The auction items include wardrobe pieces used by Noah Centineo as Ken Masters and Andrew Koji as Ryu. Proceeds from the sale will go to the Joe DiMaggio Children’s Hospital Foundation. The auction began on October 9 at 12 PM EST and is scheduled to close on October 12 at 8 PM EST. Roman Reigns and Cody Rhodes are both involved in the Street Fighter film, portraying the characters Akuma and Guile, respectively. Reigns recently discussed his preparation for the role of Akuma in an interview.
