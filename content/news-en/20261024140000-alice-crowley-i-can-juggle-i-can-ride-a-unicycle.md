Alice Crowley recently addressed a question posed by Jody Threat and Joel Pearl regarding her unicycle and juggling skills. During an interview, Pearl and Threat wondered if Crowley could juggle since she rides a unicycle. Speaking with Luis Pulido, Crowley confirmed her unicycle background and introduced her signature maneuver. 

> Yes, I do, yes, she rides her skateboard and I ride a unicycle that is probably one of the first things that we ever bonded over is that we both ride, you know, and yeah, no I can I've been able to ride a unicycle ever since I was like 11 or 12 when I got it yeah I like one wheel it's better than 4 in my opinion, but Yeah, no, it's a, it takes a lot of skill you gotta have pretty good balance to be able to ride one wheel like that, but no I I've done it in a wrestling match before I've taken the A unicycle put it in the ring and hit it off of the unicycle while riding, that's where the my my invention of what I like to call it the unilariat. But yes, a lot of people mistake my normal lariat for this lariat, but my unilariat is the lariat that I do off of my unicycle, which is where the UNI comes from.

Crowley also spoke about her juggling abilities and whether she could combine the two skills. 

> I can juggle, and I can ride a unicycle, but I've never tried them together. That probably would be hard, I would assume, because it's too different. Obviously, you're double-tasking, but yeah, I can juggle, and I can't ride a unicycle. I don't know if I can do it at the same time to find out, though, so I guess I'll know what I'm doing when I get back home.

The CMLL Women's Grand Prix kicked off on October 23rd.
