Since leaving WWE in 2025, Shotzi Blackheart has been a key figure in the MLW women's division, holding the MLW Women's Featherweight Championship for most of 2026. However, she is now moving to TNA. Fightful Select reports that Blackheart has signed with TNA following her appearance at TV tapings in Alabama on Tuesday. It is currently unknown how long her contract is for. Blackheart's TNA appearance raised questions about her MLW status, as her most recent six-month contract expired on October 5. While she remains on good terms with MLW and had committed to future dates in 2027, her new TNA deal will likely prevent that. Sources indicated that MLW was aware of the possibility of her departure and had contingencies in place. Although Blackheart recently lost her championship to Masha Slamovich, the title change has not yet aired on TV, meaning she may still appear on upcoming episodes of MLW Fusion. Blackheart is still scheduled to appear on Chris Jericho's Rock N' Wrestling Rager at Sea cruise next month. This marks her first full-time stint with TNA, though she previously worked spot shows for the promotion in 2018 and 2019.
