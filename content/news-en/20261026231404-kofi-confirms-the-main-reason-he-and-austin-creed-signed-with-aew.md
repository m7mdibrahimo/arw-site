During an appearance on The Mick and Kenny Show, Kofi revealed that the main reason he and Austin Creed signed with AEW was the opportunity to face The Young Bucks and Kenny Omega. Kofi explained that they previously had a video game challenge and a faceoff with them while still under contract with WWE, despite the company's strict rules about interacting with talent from other promotions. Now that they are in AEW, they are closing in on making their dream matches a reality, having already crossed paths with The Young Bucks at AEW All In and on the following episode of AEW Dynamite, while also positioning themselves in the tag team title picture.
