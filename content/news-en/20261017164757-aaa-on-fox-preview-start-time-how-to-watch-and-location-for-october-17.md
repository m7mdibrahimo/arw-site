Lucha Libre AAA presents AAA on FOX Latin America on October 17, 2026, live from the Domo de la Feria in Leon, Mexico. The event starts at 8 p.m. ET in Mexico and 10 p.m. ET in the United States, with live broadcasts available on FOX in Latin America and YouTube in the US. The lineup features the return of AAA Reina de Reinas Champion La Catalina. Additionally, the Wagner Brothers, consisting of El Hijo de Dr. Wagner Jr. and Galeno, will defend the AAA World Tag Team Championships against El Mesías and Mecha Wolf. Fightful will provide coverage starting at 10 p.m. ET.
