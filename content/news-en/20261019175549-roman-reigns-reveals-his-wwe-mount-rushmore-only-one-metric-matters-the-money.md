Roman Reigns revealed his WWE Mount Rushmore during an appearance on Club Shay Shay, explaining that his choices are based purely on business. Reigns stated that the only metric that matters is money, recalling advice his father gave him about the industry. His Mount Rushmore consists of Hulk Hogan, "Stone Cold" Steve Austin, Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson, and John Cena. Reigns discussed his thoughts on who stands out as the greatest of all time, praising Hogan's global impact and Austin's influence, while also highlighting the unprecedented speed of Johnson's success.
