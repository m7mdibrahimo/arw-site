CM Punk has landed a role in the upcoming romantic comedy 'Guarding Stars'. The film is scheduled to premiere on Netflix on Wednesday, November 18. Punk joins a cast that includes Jared Padalecki, Andie MacDowell, Leighton Meester, and Siobhan Williams. Netflix released the first trailer for the movie on Wednesday, October 14, showcasing brief glimpses of Punk in several scenes. This project marks Punk's second film appearance this year, following his role in 'Night Patrol' which was released in January. Additionally, he provided a voice role in 'Zootopia 2', which premiered in theaters in November 2025.
