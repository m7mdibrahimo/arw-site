Nikki Bella is speaking out against matchmaking attempts, explaining on a recent podcast episode that she is frustrated by friends and acquaintances trying to set her up on dates. The WWE Hall of Famer shared that people have even ambushed her with surprise FaceTime introductions without warning. While she admitted that some of the men were attractive and even featured accents that caught her attention, she made it clear that she is not interested in dating. Nikki noted that she simply does not have the energy for a relationship right now, pointing to her busy schedule traveling, running her wine company, hosting her podcast, and raising her son Matteo. Brie Bella agreed that her sister's hectic life leaves little room for romance, adding that Nikki prefers to spend her rare free nights relaxing at home rather than getting dressed up for strangers. Nikki previously opened up about being single at 42, explaining that she is content focusing on her personal life and business ventures rather than rushing into anything new.
