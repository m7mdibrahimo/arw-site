Sherilyn Guerrero recently made her professional wrestling debut at Reality of Wrestling's Fall Fury, where she secured her first career victory by defeating Mystii Marks on November 14. Following the match, Sherilyn revealed that Dominik Mysterio was among the first people to reach out to her. Dominik had been watching her debut live and sent her a direct message congratulating her on starting out with a 1-0 record. 

Before taking her first steps into the wrestling business, Sherilyn had actually turned to Dominik for advice on whether she should pursue a career in the industry. Her family also showed tremendous support, with her grandfather watching alongside her uncle Eric in Arizona, and her aunts organizing a watch party. Sherilyn's sister, Shaul Guerrero, also sent an enthusiastic voice message celebrating her debut. 

Moving forward, Sherilyn feels reassured knowing she has the backing of her family and peers as she begins her journey in professional wrestling.
