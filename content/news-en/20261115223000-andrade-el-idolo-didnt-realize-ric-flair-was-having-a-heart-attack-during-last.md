Ric Flair's career had a storybook conclusion at WWE WrestleMania 24 in 2008, where he lost to Shawn Michaels in a highly emotional match. However, the legendary "Nature Boy" later decided to return to the ring in 2022 for what he billed as his true "Last Match." Flair teamed with his then-son-in-law, Andrade El Idolo, to face Jeff Jarrett and Jay Lethal. During the bout, Flair suffered a heart attack. Andrade recently shared on "Talk Is Jericho" that he was initially unaware of the medical emergency.

> "I [heard] later, like, a couple of months later! ... At the time, I [thought he was selling]!" Andrade recalled.

He admitted that he had been nervous before the match, fearing that something might go wrong.

> "Before the match, I [talked] to my ex-wife, I said I was scared. If something happened, what [would] I say to my ex-wife? Hey, I was there? What [could] I do? I'm happy that nothing happened."

Andrade explained that Flair seemed fine after the match and was even drinking backstage, so they only learned about the heart attack months later during a doctor's visit.

> "That was surprising! Like, oh, that's why he stayed [down]? I was scared in this moment, I remember in this moment thinking he was selling, but I didn't know he had a heart attack!"

Despite the scare, Andrade still holds Flair in high regard as a person and believes the fact that he wrestled through a heart attack only adds to his legendary status.
