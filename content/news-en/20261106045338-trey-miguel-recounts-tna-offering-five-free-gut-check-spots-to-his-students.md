Trey Miguel wanted Jason Hotch to earn his spot in TNA Wrestling on his own. TNA has a tryout program called Gut Check, where the winner earns a contract. The 2022 winner, Jason Hotch, was trained by Trey Miguel. During an appearance on Busted Open, Miguel discussed his training school and recalled how TNA offered him five free spots in Gut Check for his students. However, Miguel chose not to give one to Hotch, wanting him to pay the entry fee himself so that no one could claim his success was handed to him.

> "I told Jason I didn’t want him to get one of those free spots. I wanted him to pay for it so that way when he won it like I knew he was going to win it, no one could tell him that it was given to him."

Miguel noted that he was nervous waiting for the results alongside D'Lo Brown, but was thrilled when he heard Hotch had won. Miguel praised Hotch's growth from "zero to hero," highlighting his work this year, particularly his matches with Mustafa Ali. Miguel expressed interest in a World Championship match against Hotch, noting that they had a 20-minute singles match in Brampton, Ontario, that received two standing ovations. Miguel concluded by commending Hotch's character and willingness to take risks in the ring, calling him a "perfect example" of a dedicated wrestler.
