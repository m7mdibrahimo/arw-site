Stephen "Wonderboy" Thompson closed out his MMA career on a high note, securing a third-round TKO victory over Radtke at UFC 334. Late in the third round, Thompson delivered a spinning wheel kick that knocked Radtke off balance before he went down, prompting the referee to halt the bout. Following the win, UFC honored Thompson with a career tribute video. Thompson officially retires with a record of 18-9-1.

Thompson made his UFC debut in 2012 with a knockout victory over Dan Stittgen. He built an impressive seven-fight winning streak, earning victories against notable fighters including Patrick Cote, Rory MacDonald, Johny Hendricks, Jake Ellenberger, and Robert Whittaker. That streak led to a UFC Welterweight Championship shot against Tyron Woodley. Their first encounter ended in a majority draw, while Woodley secured a majority decision victory in the rematch. After suffering three consecutive losses, Thompson capped off his career by returning to the win column at UFC 334.
