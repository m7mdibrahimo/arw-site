Mayu Iwatani captured the Marigold Triple Crown Championship at Dream Star Fighting Marigold's Grand Destiny 2026 event. Ryogoku Sumo Hall in Tokyo, Japan, hosted the show on October 24. The main event featured a three-way match between Superfly Champion Mayu Iwatani, United National Champion Victoria Yuzuki, and Marigold World Champion Miku Aono. Iwatani secured the victory by hitting Yuzuki with a Dragon Suplex into a pin to become the new Triple Crown Champion. Iwatani previously left STARDOM in early 2025 and joined Marigold in May of that year.
