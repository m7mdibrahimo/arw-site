Cody Rhodes recently opened up about his reputation for bending the truth in the wrestling business. During an appearance on the Flagrant podcast, the WWE star broke down what he considers the formula for one of his signature stories.

> "A Cody lie is that it's got to be rooted in truth, but you just wanna put a little sauce on it."

Rhodes famously influenced Bryan Danielson regarding the amusement of weaving tall tales behind the scenes. During the same interview, Rhodes also shared strong praise for NJPW star Hirooki Goto and the Japanese promotion as a whole.
