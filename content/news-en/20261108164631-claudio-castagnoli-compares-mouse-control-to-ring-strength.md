Claudio Castagnoli discussed the difference between mouse control skills in esports and his strength feats in professional wrestling. Castagnoli is recognized as one of the strongest wrestlers globally, repeatedly displaying his power through maneuvers like The Giant Swing on massive opponents such as The Great Khali and Big Show.

During an interview with Paul Delos Santos for The Load-In at the League Of Legends World Championships, Castagnoli was asked which feat is more impressive. He explained that lifting heavy weights is more relevant to his career, noting that the two skills do not correlate due to differences in mass control and timing.

> "Well, for me, for my job, I would say it's lifting 400-pound men, and if it comes to me, the one doesn't correlate to the other because of mass control and my timing in that and my timing in the ring. You would think it would match, but it absolutely doesn't. I'm the worst. I just misclick and I click the wrong buttons."
