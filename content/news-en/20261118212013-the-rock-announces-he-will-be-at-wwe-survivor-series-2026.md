The Rock stated on The Pat McAfee Show that he will attend WWE Survivor Series 2026 later this month. WWE is currently celebrating the 30th anniversary of his professional wrestling career with exclusive merchandise and other celebrations.

While appearing on the show on November 18, the star confirmed that he will be present at the event scheduled for November 28 in Houston. He stated:

> "You guys are my boys, man, we're live, and when we're live like this, this is where I live and with a live microphone. Here's the truth, I would not miss next week for the world, man. So I'm going to Houston. Houston, we don't have a problem. I'm going to Houston and I'm going to go to Survivor Series. As a matter of fact, I actually as you know, I said next week, we start our big Jumanji tour next weekend. I actually talked with Sony about three months ago and pushed the tour a couple of days, actually by week. So I can make this event, and I can't wait."
