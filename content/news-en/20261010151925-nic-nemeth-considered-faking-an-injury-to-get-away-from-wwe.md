Nic Nemeth opened up about a particularly frustrating period during his long tenure with WWE, revealing that he was so desperate for a change that he considered faking an injury just to get away from the company. Speaking in an interview, Nemeth explained that after spending nearly twenty years on the main roster, he felt completely stuck and even begged for a release or a chance to work in NXT. Eventually, an opportunity arose when John Laurinaitis asked if he would be interested in a short program with Bron Breakker in NXT to help evaluate the young star. What was initially planned as a brief three-or-four-week stint quickly changed course after management realized the collaboration was a massive success, leading to an extended storyline and an NXT Championship reign for Nemeth.
