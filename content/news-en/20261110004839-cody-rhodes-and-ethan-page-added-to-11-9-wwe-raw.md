World Wrestling Entertainment has announced two new additions for the upcoming episode of WWE Raw. Cody Rhodes and Ethan Page are scheduled to appear on the November 9 broadcast. Rhodes is coming off a victory over Randy Orton in a Last Man Standing match at WWE Crown Jewel. Meanwhile, Page captured the Intercontinental Championship from Chad Gable last Monday on Raw. The current lineup for Monday's show features fallout from WWE Crown Jewel along with appearances from Page and Rhodes.
