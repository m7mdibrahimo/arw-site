The taped WWE SmackDown go-home show for Crown Jewel experienced an increase in viewership alongside a minor decline in ratings. According to Wrestlenomics, average viewership for the November 6, 2026, broadcast from the Barclays Center in New York climbed from 1,162,000 to 1,195,000 viewers, marking a 3% gain.

This viewership marked the highest figure in three weeks, following the October 16 show that drew 1,327,000 viewers. Despite the growth, the total stayed beneath the four-week average of 1,235,000 viewers. Within the key 18-49 demographic, the rating decreased slightly by 0.01 compared to the October 30 episode, landing at 0.25 on November 6. Comparing Q4 metrics to the same period in 2025, both viewership and ratings saw an increase, with viewership rising by 11% and ratings by 12%.

The New York event was headlined by a hard-hitting No Holds Barred match between Kevin Owens and CM Punk, with Kevin Owens securing the win. The program additionally featured final preparations for Crown Jewel, including a segment with Sami Zayn and Jimmy Uso, as well as appearances by Women's Crown Jewel Championship contenders Chelsea Green and Liv Morgan.
