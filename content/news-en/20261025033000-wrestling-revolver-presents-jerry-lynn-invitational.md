Wrestling Revolver is hosting the Jerry Lynn Invitational event live on Saturday, October 24th at 9pm ET and 8pm CT. The tournament features several first-round four-way matches, including BDE against Trey Miguel, Juni Underwood, and Marcus Mathers, as well as Steve Maclin against Crash, Colby Carter, and Troy Parker, with Jerry Lynn serving as a special referee for the tournament finals. The card also includes a Revolver World Title match where champion Damian Chambers defends against Jake Painter with Kayla Kassidy in his corner, a Revolver Remix Title falls count anywhere match between champion Zachary Wentz and Cole Radrick, a trio street fight between Alpha Sig and Tye or Die, a match featuring The Outbreak and The Production, and a 24/7 Scramble Title match with Gringo Loco's open challenge.
