Laynie Luck will become a free agent tomorrow as her WWE ID contract is not being renewed. According to reports, she recently dropped the WWE ID Championship after a 320-day reign. Luck lost the title to Monica Monroe on October 3 after 23 successful defenses. During her time as the WWE ID Women's Champion, she appeared on WWE EVOLVE programming.
