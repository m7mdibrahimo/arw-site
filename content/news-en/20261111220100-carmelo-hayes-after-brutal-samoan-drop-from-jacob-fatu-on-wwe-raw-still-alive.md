Carmelo Hayes has responded to fans discussing the brutal Samoan Drop he received from Jacob Fatu on this week's episode of WWE RAW. Hayes has been a prominent member of The 946 faction since joining the group on WWE RAW, participating in each brawl and beatdown the stable has initiated. During these attacks, Hayes has frequently used a top rope assisted double stomp on the faction's victims, a move that has become a recurring topic of discussion and humor among fans on social media. In a new post on Wednesday, Hayes addressed the physical toll of the pop-up Samoan Drop delivered by Fatu. He stated that he is still alive and intends to continue using his double stomp against The Bloodline.
