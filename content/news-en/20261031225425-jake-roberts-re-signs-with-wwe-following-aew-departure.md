Jake "The Snake" Roberts is back with WWE after finishing his six-year stint with AEW. The WWE Hall of Famer casually revealed his return to the company, noting that he has already signed a new contract and started working on projects for the promotion.

Speaking with Nick Teodoro, Roberts shared the news while discussing his recent activities. He confirmed that he is no longer with AEW and is now back with WWE, though he did not specify whether his new role is on-screen or behind the scenes.

> "Staying busy. Not in AEW anymore. Re-signed with WWE, doing some stuff for them and just enjoying life, man."

Roberts joined AEW in 2020, where he primarily served as the manager for Lance Archer. Following his departure earlier this year, Roberts expressed immense gratitude toward AEW President Tony Khan, describing him as a great boss and a breath of fresh air.
