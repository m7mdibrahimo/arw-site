Brian Cage is frustrated after being left off the AEW WrestleDream card. On October 16, Cage posted a video on X expressing his anger over missing another pay-per-view, despite his recent performances. He stated that he has proven his ability to deliver on the microphone and in the ring, citing his recent matches as evidence.

> “I told myself I wouldn't do this again. I wouldn't be doing this again in the car. Yet here we are.” Cage argued that he has been performing at a high level and feels he deserves a spot on the biggest shows.

> “Every time I go out there, you damn right I own the spotlight. Have the match of the night. I get all of my stuff in. The athletic anomaly, the physical phenomenon. You just saw it all in person. You saw it there live on TV. And who's there this weekend at WrestleDream? That's right, not me.” He pointed out that AEW has held more pay-per-views this year than ever before, yet he remains excluded. Cage insisted he does not need a massive push, just a small opportunity to showcase his talent. He confirmed he will appear on the upcoming AEW show in Coral Gables, Florida, this Wednesday.

> “It may not be at WrestleDream in Orlando, but Coral Gables, Florida, this Wednesday, Brian Cage is gonna be there. I'm gonna be there.”
