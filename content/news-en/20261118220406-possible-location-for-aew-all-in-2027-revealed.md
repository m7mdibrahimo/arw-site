AEW All In 2027 could leave London once again, as a new potential host city has emerged. While Wembley Stadium has served as the primary home for the company's biggest pay-per-view, AEW is currently considering bringing the event to Toronto, Ontario, Canada, next year. Even though no final decision has been made, the Canadian city is emerging as a strong contender.

According to reports, sources within AEW have stated that Toronto has been talked about as a top potential destination for All In 2027, though no specific venue has been chosen yet. Interestingly, Adam Copeland, who hails from the Toronto area, has expressed to Tony Khan and others in AEW that he wants his final match to take place in Toronto. With Copeland planning to retire in 2027, hosting the show in his hometown could make the event even larger.

Still, this does not confirm that Copeland's retirement bout will happen at the show, as it could simply be one of his final appearances. AEW previously moved the event to Globe Life Field in Arlington, Texas, in 2025 due to scheduling conflicts at Wembley Stadium before returning there in 2026, and the option to move to Canada is now strongly on the table.
