Neil Rosan has joined the AEW team as an Associate Producer of Motion Graphics. Rosan announced his new role on social media, stating: "All in for my next chapter. I’m excited to start as an Associate Producer of Motion Graphics with @AEW!" AEW is currently preparing for a three-hour Dynamite and Collision show on November 11, which serves as the go-home episode for Full Gear.
