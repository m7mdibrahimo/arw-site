Ilja Dragunov explained why he was able to keep his name after leaving WWE. Unlike many wrestlers who must rebrand, Dragunov kept his ring name because he used it throughout his independent career before joining WWE. Speaking on the Battleground Podcast, Dragunov noted that the company never asked him to change it.

Dragunov confirmed that he kept the rights to his name, which is deeply connected to his entire wrestling journey. Having used the name across the independents, NXT UK, NXT, the main roster, and now AEW, he stated that there was never any other identity for him.
