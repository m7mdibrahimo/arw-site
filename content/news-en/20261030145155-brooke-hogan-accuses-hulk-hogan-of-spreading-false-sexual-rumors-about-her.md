Brooke Hogan discussed her difficult relationship with her late father, Hulk Hogan, on a recent podcast appearance, revealing a particularly hurtful rumor he allegedly spread about her. During her time on the Leave Then Cleave podcast, Brooke claimed that Hulk told people she slept with her real estate instructor to pass the exam and get her license. She firmly denied the accusation, explaining how hard she worked to study, failed the first time, and eventually passed on her second attempt. Brooke admitted that hearing these false stories made her question what else her father might have said behind her back and whether it damaged her relationships with her family members, including her brother and mother. She also recalled a similar situation involving Hulk's second wife, Jennifer McDaniel, whom Brooke was very close with. Brooke stated that Hulk had previously claimed Jennifer cheated on him with a gym trainer, an allegation Brooke strongly doubted because she personally knew Jennifer's strict gym routines and how dedicated she was to taking care of Hulk.
