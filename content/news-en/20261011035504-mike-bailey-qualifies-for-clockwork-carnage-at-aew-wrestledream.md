Mike Bailey is headed to AEW WrestleDream after securing a big win on the road. During AEW Collision Grand Slam France, Speedball Mike Bailey defeated Lee Moriarty to punch his ticket to the upcoming Clockwork Carnage match at WrestleDream. The finish of the bout saw Bailey deliver his signature Ultima Weapon to pick up the victory. The multi-man contest features eight competitors entering the ring at three-minute intervals, with eliminations allowed until all participants have entered. Once the field is complete, a 15-minute time limit begins, and the competitor who secures the most falls within that timeframe will be crowned the AEW World Champion. Will Ospreay enters the match as the reigning champion, while Bailey and Samoa Joe join the field as two of the confirmed participants.
