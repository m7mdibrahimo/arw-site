Charlotte Flair has reassured fans regarding the health of her father, WWE legend Ric Flair, following his recent fall. Ric previously revealed on Friday that he was dealing with a blood clot in his leg, and reports later confirmed he fell outside a Florida restaurant. He was transported to a hospital to receive treatment for cuts sustained to his head and eye during the incident.

Later that evening, Charlotte provided an update on her father's condition with a touch of humor:

> "Not my dad telling the doctor he fell bc of warrior dropping him on his head…(ps: he’s going to be ok)"

https://x.com/MsCharlotteWWE/status/2113787368889254205

Ric Flair last faced the late Ultimate Warrior in 1992.
