A clue regarding the whereabouts of Toni Storm has surfaced at AEW WrestleDream in Florida, where content creator Astrid Pizarro discovered what appear to be the Toni Storm Tapes. The cassette tape was sealed with a kiss. Pizarro reported that the tapes began with a narration stating that no one had seen or heard from Toni since March 18, 2026, and that a package containing five analog cassettes arrived at the AEW office in Jacksonville, Florida last Wednesday with no return address. The tapes feature Toni offering advice, engaging in conversations, and going about her daily life. Storm last competed at AEW Revolution, where she defeated Marina Shafir, and it was subsequently revealed on the following episode of Dynamite that she had been murdered, though the initial investigation eventually went cold. Fans can check Tony Khan's comments on her potential return for more details.
