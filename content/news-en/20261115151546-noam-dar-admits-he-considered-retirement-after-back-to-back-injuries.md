Noam Dar recently opened up about how close he came to retirement during his grueling recovery from two consecutive long-term injuries. Speaking on The Certified Mark Show, Dar explained that the second injury took a massive toll on his mental health, making him feel like he was reliving the exact same difficult year all over again. While he had been wrestling professionally since the age of 14 and was never used to spending months on the sidelines, the support of the WWE team, his mentors, and his friends helped him push through the rehabilitation process one day at a time. Now, just a year after wondering whether he should hang up his boots, Dar has won the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic with Romeo Moreno and is preparing for an opportunity in the John Cena Classic, proving that his wrestling career is far from over.
