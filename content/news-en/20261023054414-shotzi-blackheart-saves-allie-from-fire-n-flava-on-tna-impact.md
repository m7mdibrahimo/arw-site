Shotzi Blackheart made her official TNA debut by saving Allie from an ambush during the October 22 episode of TNA iMPACT. This incident followed the loss of the TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Titles at Bound for Glory.

During the broadcast, Kiera Hogan blamed Allie for their title loss, calling her the weakest link before ending their partnership. Tasha Steelz then joined Hogan to attack Allie, but Blackheart rushed to the ring to force them away. TNA subsequently confirmed that Blackheart has signed a contract with the promotion.
