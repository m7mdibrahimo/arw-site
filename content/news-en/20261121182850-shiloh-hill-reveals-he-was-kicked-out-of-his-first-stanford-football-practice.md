WWE NXT star Shiloh Hill revealed that he was kicked out of his very first Stanford football practice after getting into a fight. Speaking on Good Karma Wrestling, Hill shared that his linebacker coach immediately predicted his future in professional wrestling following the incident, asking him how long it would take him to reach WWE. Hill estimated about six years, a prediction that remarkably came true when he signed with WWE almost to the day six years later. Before adopting the Shiloh Hill ring name and winning the second season of WWE LFG to secure his NXT contract in October 2025, Hill played football at Stanford and earned a master's degree focusing on artificial intelligence. Now, he is working alongside major stars like John Cena.
