Jason Sensation recently shared that he nearly brought his wrestling impressions to WCW after his WWE career stalled, with Vince Russo expressing a strong interest in signing him. Sensation noted that Russo indicated a position would be waiting for him if he could secure his release from WWE.

During an interview, Sensation reflected on the frustrating period following his appearances with DX during the Attitude Era. After gaining recognition for his Owen Hart impressions, he found himself off television for long stretches. When Russo moved to WCW, Sensation asked his friend Dave Curran to gauge interest in a potential jump. According to Sensation, Russo was eager to bring him on board.

> "Tell Jason he has a home in WCW. If he can get out of his contract, we will hire him right away. You tell him he has a home here, bro."

Although Sensation never joined WCW, he noted that talent like Scott Steiner recognized his work. Sensation also explained that Vince McMahon discouraged him from using agents like Barry Bloom, leading him to stay with WWE. He admitted he sometimes wonders if he should have pursued the other path.
