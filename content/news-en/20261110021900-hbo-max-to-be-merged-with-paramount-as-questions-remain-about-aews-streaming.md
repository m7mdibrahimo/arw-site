HBO Max is set to merge into a combined streaming service with Paramount+, bringing major changes for AEW. Skydance confirmed plans to unite the two platforms following its acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery, although Tony Khan remains confident about his company's position.

Skydance announced that Paramount+ and HBO Max will offer a joint subscription starting in early 2027, with a full unification of both platforms expected within roughly a year after completing the Warner Bros. Discovery purchase. This overhaul affects AEW, as Dynamite, Collision, and pay-per-view events currently stream on HBO Max. While Skydance is also cutting costs and evaluating its sports properties, Khan expressed enthusiasm about working with the new owners following their acquisition in October.

Khan's current media deal with Warner Bros. Discovery runs through at least 2027 with options into 2028, and no programming changes have been announced. However, as the streaming landscape changes and billions in cuts are planned, AEW will need to prove its long-term value to the new leadership beyond Khan's positive relationship with Skydance CEO David Ellison.
