Will Ospreay reflected on the Clockwork Carnage match after successfully defending his AEW World Title at AEW WrestleDream. Ospreay came up with the idea for this experimental match and expressed his gratitude to AEW and Tony Khan for trusting him to try something new. He shared his thoughts on social media, thanking fans for watching and stating that he is proud of himself for taking a bold step.

> "Thank you all for watching #AEWWrestleDream & being part of Clockwork Carnage. The fact a company like @AEW & @TonyKhan put faith in trying something that’s not been done before. Grateful for all the reviews for it proud of myself for daring to try something new. All love guys"
