WWE faced significant problems regarding the original concept for the John Cena Classic tournament. John Cena introduced the championship on WWE Raw, confirming the event for December 14 at the ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex located in Orlando, Florida. He named Cody Rhodes as the first participant and stated that Club WWE members would vote, though he did not explain the voting mechanism.

According to Dave Meltzer on Wrestling Observer Radio, the initial plan involved fans voting on who advanced regardless of who won the match inside the ring. Meltzer noted that critics pointed out major flaws since the company could not plan matches ahead without knowing the winners, leading WWE to reconsider the concept.
