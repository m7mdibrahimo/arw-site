The October 19, 2026 episode of WWE RAW closed in pure chaos as Roman Reigns, The Usos, and Solo Sikoa engaged in a violent brawl with the faction known as 946. Jimmy and Jey Uso entered the ring armed with weapons to call out the group, prompting Royce Keys to confront them from atop the announce table alongside his crew. Solo Sikoa quickly ambushed Keys, igniting a wild physical clash that briefly turned against the family when Hayes struck Sikoa with a crowbar and Jaida Parker brought out handcuffs.

Just as Keys attempted to restrain Sikoa to the ring ropes, Roman Reigns made an unannounced entrance to turn the momentum. Reigns took out Keys with a Superman Punch and flattened Nima with a Spear, forcing 946 into a full retreat through the crowd alongside Price, Hayes, and Parker. Despite past animosity and deep family rifts, the original Bloodline members and Sikoa stood united in the ring as the broadcast concluded.
