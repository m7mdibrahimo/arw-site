AEW Dynamite aired live from the Watsco Center in Coral Gables, Florida, on October 21, 2026. Following AEW WrestleDream, Ilja Dragunov made his debut, followed by a match against "Speedball" Mike Bailey. In the show's opening match, AEW World Tag Team Champions The Young Bucks were defeated by Mark Davis and Jake Doyle in an Eliminator Match. Later, Will Ospreay and Adam Copeland teamed up to face Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight. Fletcher pinned Copeland for the win after Kazuchika Okada interfered. Following the match, Darby Allin and Steven Borden arrived to challenge The Don Callis Family to a Blood & Guts Match. In the final match, Swerve Strickland defeated his opponent with a House Call and a Vertebreaker. It was also announced that Maya World will face Queen Aminata in a number one contender's match for the TBS Championship.
