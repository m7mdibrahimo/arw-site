Dave Meltzer recently addressed the speculation surrounding a potential AEW World Championship match between Will Ospreay and Adam Copeland following their encounter at AEW WrestleDream. During the October 19, 2026 episode of Wrestling Observer Radio, Meltzer noted that AEW has been teasing a showdown between the two top stars. At AEW WrestleDream on October 17, Ospreay successfully defended the AEW World Championship in an eight-man Clockwork Carnage Match, where Copeland came close to winning before being pinned in the closing seconds. Following the match, Copeland shoved Ospreay before the two eventually shook hands.

> Yeah. So, you know, at the end of the pay per view, they did the thing where Osprey and Copeland shook hands. It was pretty clear that they're going to do that match.

AEW subsequently released backstage footage showing Ospreay bringing up Copeland's plans to retire in 2027 and urging him to secure a match date before hanging up his boots. Meltzer suggested that the anticipated championship bout could happen sooner rather than later, floating AEW Full Gear as a possible location for the main event. While nothing has been officially confirmed by the company, Meltzer remains convinced that the match is in the works, dismissing concerns that Copeland cannot keep pace with Ospreay in the ring.

> Will Ospreay is one of the best wrestlers in the world, if not the best. Adam Copeland is very smart. He works his ass off. He's very good.
