Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre held its CMLL Domingo Familiar event on October 25, 2026, at Arena Mexico in Mexico City, Mexico.

The opening match saw Pequeño Sky Team (Galaxy, Kaligua, and Pequeño Magia) defeat Metales Explosivos (Full Metal, Pequeño Pólvora, and Rostro de Acero).

In a Two Out of Three Falls match, Fuerza Poblana (Stigma, Arkalis, and Rayo Metálico) defeated Virus, Cancerbero, and Shoma Kato.

Tag team action followed as Zeuxis and Kira defeated Alice Crowley and Mei Seira.

In a Lightning Match, Skadi defeated Masha Slamovich.

In another Two Out of Three Falls match, Bárbaro Cavernario, Yutani, and Niebla Roja defeated Dragones (Dragón Rojo Jr., Dragón de Fuego, and Dragón Legendario).

In a second Lightning Match, Queen Aminata defeated India Sioux.

In the main event, Esfinge defeated Volador Jr.
