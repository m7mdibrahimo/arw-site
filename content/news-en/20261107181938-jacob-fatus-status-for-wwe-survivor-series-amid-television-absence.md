Jacob Fatu has been absent from WWE television for over a month following a brutal hit-and-run angle on the September 28 episode of WWE RAW, where he was run over by a black sedan driven by The 946 after a parking lot brawl. According to Fightful Select, Fatu is expected to return and join The Bloodline for the upcoming WarGames match at WWE Survivor Series: WarGames. Although WWE has not officially announced the match yet, the current plan features Roman Reigns, The Usos, Jacob Fatu, and Solo Sikoa teaming up to face The 946 inside the WarGames structure. Recent reports indicated that Fatu's return was approaching, as he had reportedly been filming material alongside other competitors for the WarGames match.
