Waves and Curls (Jaylen Brandyn & Traevon Jordan) def. DJ Powers & Rain Conway

Aaron Rourke def. Nick Robles
Devin Reno def. Jermaine Marbury
Dustin Waller & The Verdict (Bryce Donovan & Vinny Scalice) def. Bobby Orlando & StarStruck (Anthony Greene & Channing Thomas) in a six-man tag team main event.
