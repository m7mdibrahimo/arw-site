Ilja Dragunov has enjoyed a strong start in AEW, making a thunderous debut at AEW WrestleDream and defeating "Speedball" Mike Bailey on "AEW Dynamite" shortly after. During recent media appearances, Dragunov has reflected on his time in WWE and his choice not to renew his contract. In an interview with Jon Alba for "The Takedown on SI," Dragunov noted that he had been considering a career shift well before his deal ended.

> "It's been a very complicated time for me. But you see, I came back from my ACL tear...[which] that happened in the most unexciting way possible. I worked my way through three rehabs, and was training very hard and was very dedicated. I came back, and I won the United States Championship, basically on my return, which was an awesome, memorable moment."

Dragunov explained that after losing the title and continuing his run, he felt a shift in his perspective. He emphasized that he holds no ill will toward WWE and maintains deep respect for the seven years he spent there. However, he felt it was necessary to seek a new chapter. Dragunov expressed his satisfaction with his early time in AEW, noting that he has felt a sense of "freedom" since his debut at WrestleDream.
