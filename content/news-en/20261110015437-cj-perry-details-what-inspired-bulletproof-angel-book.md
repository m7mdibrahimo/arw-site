CJ Perry recently detailed the inspirations behind her new spy thriller book, 'Bulletproof Angel.' In an interview with Sean Ross Sapp, Perry explained that the book draws heavily from her own life experiences and hobbies, such as her love for firearms and shooting ranges. She noted that the lead character is a model and a secret American-Russian spy who dates a Russian mafia member as a cover story. Perry also shared that her lifelong fascination with James Bond movies influenced the story, as she always wondered why the woman could not be the one doing the saving.
