According to Fightful, WWE filed two new trademarks on November 19. The company submitted paperwork to trademark both Gringo Anonimo and Anonimo Gringo.

The filings cover several entertainment and media services. These include live wrestling exhibitions, broadcast media, online content, and fan club activities.
