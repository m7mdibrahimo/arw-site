Nikki Bella made it clear on a recent episode of her podcast that she is currently single, but she definitely is not looking for anyone to help her find a partner. While speaking on The Nikki & Brie Show alongside Gillie and Wallo, Brie Bella attempted to play matchmaker after discussing pickup lines in wrestling. Nikki quickly shut down the idea, stating, “Okay, I’m not, like, for sale.” The lighthearted exchange followed an awkward hotel incident the previous night, where a man repeatedly approached the twins during dinner despite security intervention.
