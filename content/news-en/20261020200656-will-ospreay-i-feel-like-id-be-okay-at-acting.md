Will Ospreay recently discussed the possibility of branching out into acting during an interview with Real Radio Monsters. While acknowledging that professional wrestling and mainstream media are increasingly intersecting through stars like The Rock, John Cena, and Roman Reigns, Ospreay admitted he struggles with reading scripts. Despite this, he expressed a willingness to take on a role if given the opportunity by director Guy Ritchie, stating he would put his mind to it.
