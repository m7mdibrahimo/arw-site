ROH action is heading to Arena Mexico for an upcoming championship match. On the November 19 episode of Ring of Honor TV, Don Callis announced that Rocky Romero will team up with Volador Jr. to challenge El Sky Team, consisting of Místico and Máscara Dorada, for the ROH Tag Team Titles.

Callis referred to Volador Jr. as the lost son of the Don Callis Family. Volador Jr. and Rocky Romero first began teaming together in 2025, although Romero usually teams with Trent within the Don Callis Family faction.

Callis noted that the title match will take place in Arena Mexico. An exact date for the championship bout has not yet been announced.
