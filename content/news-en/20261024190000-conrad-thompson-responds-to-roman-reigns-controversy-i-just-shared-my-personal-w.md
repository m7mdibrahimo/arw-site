Conrad Thompson has responded to recent criticism regarding his opinions on Roman Reigns and his workload. In an interview with Sean Ross Sapp, Thompson explained that his comments were merely a casual opinion shared during a SummerSlam review with Eric Bischoff. Thompson expressed surprise at the ongoing discussion, noting that he simply stated he was ready for something new in regards to Reigns. Furthermore, Thompson mentioned that he would like to host podcasts with Paul Heyman and Michael Hayes in the future.
