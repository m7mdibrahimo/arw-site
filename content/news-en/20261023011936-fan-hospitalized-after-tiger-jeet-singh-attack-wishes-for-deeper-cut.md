A wrestling fan who was struck in the head by Tiger Jeet Singh's sword at an event stated he feels no anger, describing the incident as a dream come true while expressing regret that the wound did not leave a permanent mark. The 38-year-old Yokohama resident attended the Strong Style Pro Wrestling show at Korakuen Hall on October 21, revealing that the attack occurred during Megumi Kudo's 40th anniversary celebration on October 17 when the 82-year-old legend charged at him with his signature blade.

The strike caused bleeding that led to an ambulance ride to the hospital, where medical staples closed the injury. Despite this, the fan wished the cut had been deeper to provide a lasting souvenir. Tiger Ali Singh defended his father by pointing out that attendees were warned beforehand, while organizer Megumi Kudo offered an apology. Afterward, the fan received a get-well gift from Sareee consisting of a ticket to her December 28 show and an autographed photo.
