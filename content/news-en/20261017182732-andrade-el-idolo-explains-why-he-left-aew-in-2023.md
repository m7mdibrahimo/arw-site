Andrade El Idolo recently explained why he left AEW in 2023 to return to WWE. Speaking on Close Up with Renee Paquette, Andrade reflected on his career moves and his decision to prioritize his personal life at the time, as Charlotte Flair, his wife then, worked for WWE. After parting ways with WWE following their divorce, Andrade returned to AEW and is now focused on representing the company and defending the AEW National Championship against Tommaso Ciampa at AEW WrestleDream.
