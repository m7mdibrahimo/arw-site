Matt Riddle is set to return to combat sports. Misfits Boxing announced that Riddle will face former WWE star Edris Enofe in a boxing match on December 18. This marks Riddle's first combat sports appearance since transitioning to professional wrestling in 2014 following his MMA career, during which he amassed an 8-3 record, including 7-3 in the UFC. Riddle remains active in professional wrestling through independent promotions and MLW. Enofe secured a TKO victory over Big Bobby The Boss at Misfits Boxing in September, following his departure from WWE in September 2025 where he competed in WWE EVOLVE. Andrei Arlovski will headline the event.
