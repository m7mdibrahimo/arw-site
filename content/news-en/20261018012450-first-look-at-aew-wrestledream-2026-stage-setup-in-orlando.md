Newly surfaced photos have revealed the massive stage setup for AEW WrestleDream 2026 inside the Addition Financial Arena at the University of Central Florida in Orlando on Oct. 17, 2026. The special set features tall pillars, unique LED lighting designs that differ from standard Dynamite or Collision setups, and a shortened entrance ramp for the wrestlers.

According to Ringside News, the main card kicks off at 8 PM ET. The event is headlined by Will Ospreay defending the AEW World Championship against seven opponents in the inaugural Clockwork Carnage match, with fans eagerly waiting to see the fully illuminated stage during the live broadcast.
