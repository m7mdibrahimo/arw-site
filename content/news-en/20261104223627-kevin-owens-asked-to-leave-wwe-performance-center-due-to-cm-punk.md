Kevin Owens went to the WWE Performance Center for shoulder treatment before Shawn Michaels spoke with him and asked him to leave the facility, according to Fightful. Owens recorded the discussion, which featured Michaels asking him to depart because CM Punk is preparing to face Grayson Waller for the NXT Championship on Wednesday's NXT. Owens was unhappy with the choice.

Owens and Punk are scheduled to collide in a No Holds Barred match on Friday's WWE SmackDown. The lineup for Wednesday's NXT features Grayson Waller vs. CM Punk for the NXT Championship, and Kelani Jordan vs. Mystery Opponent for the NXT Women's Championship.

The card also includes Romeo Moreno & Noam Dar vs. Hank Walker & Tank Ledger in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic Final, and Chad Gable, Rey Fenix & Dragon Lee vs. The Vanity Project in a six-man tag team match. Cruz Montana vs. Dion Lennox takes place in an unsanctioned match with Michael Chandler as special enforcer, alongside an appearance by John Cena and Cody Rhodes presenting the Dusty Cup trophy.
