Willow Nightingale recently marked four full years since her official signing with AEW by highlighting her major career achievements. Her initial appearance took place on a 2022 episode of AEW Rampage, where she defeated Leila Grey in a singles match. Following the bout, broadcaster Tony Schiavone surprised her with an official announcement that she had signed a contract with the promotion.

Over the years, Willow secured several prominent titles, including two reigns as TBS Champion alongside winning the AEW Women's World Championship and the AEW Women's World Tag Team Championship. To mark the milestone, she posted a message on social media questioning whether her past self from four years ago could have imagined all of her success.
