Roman Reigns defeated his former Bloodline ally Sami Zayn in the main event of WWE Crown Jewel 2026 to become the Men's Crown Jewel Champion. The match was filled with chaos, as both referees were knocked out during the action. Zayn attempted to use a championship belt as a weapon, but Reigns ultimately secured the victory after hitting two consecutive Spears.

Heading into the match, there was significant tension between the two champions. Reigns, the World Heavyweight Champion, had questioned Zayn's credibility, while the Undisputed WWE Champion had been demanding that everyone acknowledge him. Zayn started strong, using the barricade and announce table to his advantage, but Reigns eventually took control with his signature offense. The match descended into disorder when Reigns accidentally knocked out the referee, leading Zayn to hit an exploder suplex and a Helluva Kick with no one available to count the pin. When a replacement referee arrived, Reigns kicked out, prompting Zayn to attack the second official as well.

With both referees down, Zayn grabbed a championship belt, but Reigns caught him with a Superman Punch and a Spear. Although the original referee recovered to count the pin, Zayn managed to kick out. The match grew increasingly personal as they exchanged strikes, but Zayn's attempt to hit a Spear backfired. Reigns intercepted him, delivered two Spears, and secured the three-count. The win adds another title to Reigns' résumé, while Zayn is left to deal with the fallout of another bitter defeat against his former leader.
