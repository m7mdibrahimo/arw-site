Mina Shirakawa is preparing to face India Sioux at the upcoming CMLL show on October 30th. Ahead of the bout, Shirakawa spoke about her opponent and her excitement for the match. 

"I know she is very strong; she is very tough, and she loves a hard-hitting match because I saw that her match against Queen Aminata was very hard-hitting, and I also love hard-hitting matches. So I'm so excited to wrestle her, and I'm so curious about the title she holds, the (CMLL) Japan Women's Championship," Shirakawa said. 

She also expressed her gratitude for being chosen as Sioux's international opponent. "I'm very honored because she sees me as an international star, so I am very, very grateful, and very, very excited for this Friday," she added.
