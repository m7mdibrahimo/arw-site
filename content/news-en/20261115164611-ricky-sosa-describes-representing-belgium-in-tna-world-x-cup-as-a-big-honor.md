Ricky Sosa stated that representing Belgium in the TNA World X-Cup at TNA Destination X is a major and historic honor because he is the first Belgian to compete in the tournament.

During a recent interview with Love Wrestling, Sosa expressed his happiness regarding the opportunities he has received since signing with TNA. He voiced complete confidence ahead of his first-round match against The Home Town Man and Amir Jordan, noting that he considers Jordan a friend.

> "So it's a big, big honor for me to represent Belgium, and I think I'm the first Belgian to be on that tournament too. So it's also a historical moment, and my life has been a blessing since I signed with TNA and being able to get these opportunities are great, and yeah, I'm going to have fun and take the moment and enjoy the moment."
