Dave Meltzer reported in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter that Nick Khan made the decision for WWE NXT to feature bigger attractions for its one-night move to Wednesday. NXT will air head-to-head against AEW on November 4 because the CW network moved the show due to upcoming election night coverage. Meltzer noted that NXT will also compete against Main Event and EVOLVE on the same night. Bryan Alvarez previously confirmed that NXT was instructed to make the episode special to handle the competition against AEW Blood & Guts.
