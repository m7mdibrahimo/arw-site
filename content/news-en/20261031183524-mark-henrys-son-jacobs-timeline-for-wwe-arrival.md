Mark Henry shared a major update regarding his son Jacob Henry's future with WWE, indicating that the second-generation prospect is nearing his official in-ring debut. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, Henry revealed that Jacob is expected to join the company in about a year.

The update follows a recent WWE tryout for Jacob, who signed an NIL deal with the promotion back in 2024. Mark mentioned that Charlie Dempsey recognized Jacob from his recent tryout, noting how passionate his son has become about wrestling. Jacob is now constantly calling his father to discuss weekly programming like WWE SmackDown.

Jacob originally announced his WWE signing at Remix Rumble in August 2024 through an NIL agreement that allowed him to continue his amateur athletic career. He joined the University of Oklahoma wrestling program as a heavyweight and explored football as well, giving WWE a long-term prospect. Now, that transition to professional wrestling appears much closer on the horizon.
