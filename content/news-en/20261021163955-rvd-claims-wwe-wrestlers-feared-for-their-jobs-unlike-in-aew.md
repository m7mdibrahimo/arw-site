Rob Van Dam believes AEW gives its wrestlers the freedom to express themselves, unlike his time in WWE where performers feared for their jobs and simply followed orders. Speaking on his 1 Of A Kind With RVD podcast, Van Dam praised AEW for proving itself as a legitimate alternative after years of skepticism from fans and critics who doubted the company's longevity. He noted that many fans now watch both promotions and discussed how AEW allows wrestlers to have more creative control over their characters and work.

Contrasting the two environments, Van Dam explained that WWE wrestlers often felt too intimidated to push back against creative direction due to job security concerns. In contrast, he feels AEW encourages talent to showcase their skills on their own terms, making the promotion feel much more wrestler-driven.
