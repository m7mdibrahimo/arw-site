In December, Gunther forced John Cena into retirement. He subsequently retired AJ Styles as well, but he stepped back and is currently engaged in a program with Danhausen and Finn Balor.

Speaking on the Ariel Helwani Show, Ilja Dragunov, a former rival and friend of Gunther, addressed his current status.

> "I guess my feeling is the same feeling that a lot of other people share. If you are the person to retire John Cena, it's a status that you should keep a certain level of that. For me, he is one of the best and he will be one of the best of all time. He is a dear friend of mine since 2014-15. I know he's good. I know he will always do good, and whatever he does is going to be awesome. After the standard that he set and how he got portrayed that this is not necessarily the level we're working at right now,"

Dragunov left WWE in August following the expiration of his contract and officially joined AEW at WrestleDream.
