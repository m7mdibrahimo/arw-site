UFC Fight Night: Jiri vs. Stirling took place on Saturday, November 21, 2026, at the ABHA Arena in Doha, Qatar. The event was broadcast exclusively on Paramount+. In the main event, Navajo Stirling defeated Jiri Prochazka by knockout at 4:58 of the second round.

The full results are as follows:

John Mitchell def. Shoaib Yousaf by TKO at 3:50 of Round 1
Diyar Nurgozhay def. Alik Lorenz by TKO at 0:24 of Round 2
Dom Mar Fan def. Nikolas Motta by unanimous decision (30-26, 30-26, 29-27)
Tahir Abdullayev def. Jake Matthews by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 29-28) (Abdullaev missed weight)
Amir Albazi def. Alessandro Costa by submission at 4:34 of Round 3
Tallison Teixeira def. Shamil Gaziev by unanimous decision (30-27, 29-28, 29-28)
Santiago Luna def. Aleksandre Topuria by split decision (30-27, 28-29, 30-27)
Ikram Aliskerov def. Jared Cannonier by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 30-27)
Kyoji Horiguchi def. Asu Almabayev by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 29-28)
Dan Hooker def. Brian Ortega by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 30-27)
Kevin Vallejos def. Aljamain Sterling by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 29-28)
Navajo Stirling def. Jiri Prochazka by knockout at 4:58 of Round 2
