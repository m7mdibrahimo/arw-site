Chris Jericho recently discussed Sammy Guevara's absence from AEW and ROH television during a Q&A session with "The Stunner." Jericho noted that Guevara should be featured more often, praising his personality and stating that he should have a prominent television role.

> "I think he should be on TV more," Jericho said. "He's got a great personality, too. He's got a very punchable face. I think we should at least try to do something with him."

Guevara has been absent from AEW programming for five months, with his last match occurring on the June 6 episode of AEW Collision. Despite his absence, Tony Khan has confirmed that Guevara remains with the company. In addition to Guevara, Jericho mentioned Mike Bailey, Kevin Knight, and Matt Menard as other stars who deserve more television time.
