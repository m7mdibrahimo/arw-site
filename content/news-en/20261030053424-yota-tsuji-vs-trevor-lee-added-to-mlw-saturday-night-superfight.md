IWGP Heavyweight Champion Yota Tsuji is scheduled to complete at MLW Saturday Night SuperFight on November 7th in Cicero, Illinois, after Trevor Lee answered his open challenge. Austin Aries defending the MLW National Openweight Championship against Matt Riddle was also added to the lineup.

On the October 29th episode of MLW Fusion, ZAMAYA and Masha Slamovich had a confrontation. CONTRA's Josef Samael acted as a hypeman during the altercation and stated that the match will decide who is the baddest b*tch in MLW.

The updated card for Saturday Night SuperFight features Keyra vs. Tessa Blanchard, The Good Brothers vs. Glasgow Boys On Top for the MLW World Tag Team Championship in a Chicago Street Fight, and Killer Kross vs. Josh Bishop in a Last Man Standing match for the MLW World Heavyweight Championship, along with Austin Aries vs. Matt Riddle and Yota Tsuji vs. Trevor Lee and Masha Slamovich vs. ZAMAYA.
