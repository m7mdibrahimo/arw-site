Jimmy House, a member of The Mog Squad faction, has found success both in WWE EVOLVE and on the independent circuit, including appearances for Reality Of Wrestling and Coastal Championship Wrestling. In a new interview with Sean Ross Sapp, House discussed his transition into a heel persona with his group. He acknowledged that while he initially wanted to be a hero for children, he realized the necessity of mastering both heel and babyface roles, noting that many wrestlers evolve into better babyfaces after excelling as heels.

House also reflected on the early career advice he received from Dustin Rhodes. He highlighted Rhodes' iconic reinvention as Goldust as a prime example of turning a challenging concept into something legendary. House explained that he learned from Rhodes that being a great worker means having the ability to make any assignment feel real and look great. He credited Gabe Sapolsky for helping open doors for him to work with WWE. Fans can watch the full interview on the Fightful YouTube channel.
