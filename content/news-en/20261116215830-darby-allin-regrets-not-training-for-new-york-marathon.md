Darby Allin is known for pushing his limits in and out of the ring. In 2025, Allin climbed Mount Everest, but he recently faced a different kind of challenge at the New York Marathon. In an interview on "1010 XL," Allin admitted that he struggled during the race because he did not prepare for it.

> "Yeah, dude, I'm feeling every ounce of that. Everyone's like, 'Yo, congratulations!' And then I was like, 'Dude, I wish I trained for that.' I didn't train once for it, and then little did I know, it was only going to be a few days removed from Blood & Guts."

When asked about his next challenge, Allin noted that he does not plan his adventures ahead of time.

> "Honestly, I don't even know; it just comes to me naturally. I never grew up wanting to do Mount Everest, and I never grew up wanting to do a New York Marathon either. It just kind of falls into you."

Allin also reflected on his fear during a Continental Classic match against Kevin Knight earlier this year. He explained that a backflip off a guardrail nearly went wrong, leaving him struggling for the rest of the match.
