Chris Jericho has landed a major acting role. Deadline reports that Jericho will star as Gorgeous George in "The Greatest," an upcoming limited series about the life of Muhammad Ali that will stream on Prime Video. All eight episodes are scheduled to be released on November 4. George's flamboyant style, swagger, and microphone skills famously influenced a young Muhammad Ali. George began his career in 1932 and retired in 1962, and Jericho will appear as the legendary wrestler in the fourth episode. Jaalen Best will portray Muhammad Ali in the series. Jericho's acting resume continues to grow, having appeared on CBS' Tracker and Apple TV’s Margo’s Got Money Troubles this year, with an upcoming role in the second season of Dexter: Resurrection.
