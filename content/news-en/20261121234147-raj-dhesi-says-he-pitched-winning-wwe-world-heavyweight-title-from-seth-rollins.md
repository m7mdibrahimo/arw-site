Raj Dhesi, formerly known as Jinder Mahal in WWE, spoke about his final match with the company against Seth Rollins for the World Heavyweight Championship on the January 15 edition of WWE Raw. Speaking with MuscleManMalcolm, Dhesi revealed that he pitched winning the title even if it was just for one day. He said, "I would have loved to won the championship. Actually, I pitched it even just for one day, or have Damien Priest cash in on me. The next week I lose it. I think it would have just got a lot of buzz because it already got a lot of buzz based off Twitter and Tony (Khan) giving me the little bit of the rub." Dhesi also discussed adjusting the match finish after Rollins injured his knee during the bout, praising Rollins as one of the best in the ring and a favorite opponent.
