Nikki and Brie Bella recently discussed the criticism women face over dressing a certain way as they get older. Nikki admitted that she happily dresses very young and believes people should just wear what makes them feel good. The topic came up on The Nikki & Brie Show when Cathy Kelley joined as a guest and shared a TikTok video by creator Christina Kirkman discussing age-related fashion judgments. Kelley noted the mixed messages women receive from society about dressing their age while also being expected to express themselves. Brie pointed out that judgment often comes from other women, while Nikki suggested that those critics might secretly want to experiment with their own style. Nikki added that she is not about to change her wardrobe just because others think clothes have age limits.
