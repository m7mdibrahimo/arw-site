Sol Ruca made her return to WWE NXT. During a backstage segment in the locker room, Wren Sinclair, Kendal Grey, Kali Armstrong, and Lizzy Rain were chatting before Ruca appeared and mentioned she received an invite from her former ally ZARIA, though she had not seen her around. Izzi Dame then revealed she was the one who sent the invite. Dame explained that she defeated ZARIA in a non-title singles match at Halloween Havoc and chose not to challenge her for the NXT Women's North American Championship at the Premium Live Event because she wanted to break ZARIA down and leave her vulnerable first. Ruca is currently on the WWE RAW roster, where she has held the Women's Intercontinental Championship, alongside her past reigns with the WWE Women's Speed Title and the NXT Women's North American Championship.
